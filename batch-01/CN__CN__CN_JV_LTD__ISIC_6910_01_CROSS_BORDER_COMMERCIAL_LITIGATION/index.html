Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No unified statutory minimum paid-up capital for a Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture; the shareholders set the registered capital in the Articles of Association. Under the 2023 Company Law, subscribed capital must be fully paid-in within five years from establishment unless an industry-specific law provides otherwise. No blocked escrow account is required by national law; however, client funds held by the legal services entity must be maintained in a segregated client trust account (IOLTA-compliant) rather than a capital escrow.

Funding & unlock path: Not applicable for industrial civil defense permits. The office space must obtain a Fire Safety Inspection Certificate from the local fire rescue authority under the Fire Protection Law of the People's Republic of China before operation; maintain fire extinguishers, emergency lighting, evacuation exits, and fire alarm systems; and comply with workplace safety regulations applicable to office workers.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA), accessed through the local competent tax authority's electronic tax bureau portal (Electronic Tax Administration, eTax).

E-government filing portal

The Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture must first complete business registration with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR). After issuance of the business license bearing the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), the entity must complete tax registration at the competent STA tax office within 30 days of commencement of business. The USCC is the sole statutory Tax Identification Number (TIN) for tax filings and VAT invoice issuance. For a foreign non-resident enterprise without an establishment or place of business that derives China-source income, the non-resident enterprise must register for withholding tax identification through its payer or China-based agent, creating a Taxpayer Identification Number for non-resident enterprises, or rely on the withholding tax filing process under the applicable tax treaty and domestic withholding rules. The JV is a resident enterprise taxpayer and will receive a taxpayer identification through the eTax portal upon filing the tax registration information declaration in the electronic tax system.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation (Resident Enterprise Income Taxpayer; Limited Liability Taxpayer under China's Corporate Income Tax Law; default standard VAT taxpayer classification)

Available election forms

None. China does not operate a check-the-box entity classification election. The Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture is mandatorily treated as a Chinese resident enterprise subject to Corporate Income Tax on worldwide income. No hybrid mismatch entity classification election is available. General anti-avoidance rules and controlled foreign corporation rules apply to cross-border arrangements.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory VAT registration as a general VAT taxpayer is triggered when annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000. Small-scale taxpayers are subject to a monthly sales exemption threshold of RMB 100,000; registration for VAT as a small-scale taxpayer is required for any entity conducting taxable sales in China even below this exemption.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export of goods qualifies for 0% VAT when the goods are physically exported and customs export declaration procedures are completed. Cross-border services, including offshore legal and professional consultancy services supplied to non-resident clients, are eligible for VAT zero-rating or exemption if the services are provided and consumed entirely outside China, payment is received from outside China, and the service contract and delivery evidence clearly demonstrate the offshore consumption. The scope is limited to services listed as cross-border taxable services under STA regulations; commercial legal services may qualify when rendered wholly offshore and not linked to real estate or goods located in China.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bill of Entry/Export Customs Clearance Form)
  • Bill of Lading or equivalent multimodal transport document (airway bill where applicable)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (foreign exchange collection proof issued by the bank)
  • Zero-rated export VAT invoice or commercial invoice
  • Cross-border service contract and statement of work proving offshore delivery
  • Visa or stamp certificate from the destination country if required
  • Bank SWIFT payment evidence matching the invoiced foreign recipient
  • Foreign Exchange Settlement Declaration / export proceeds verification record under SAFE

China operates a state-controlled Electronic Invoice Clearance System requiring taxpayers to issue all VAT invoices through the National Unified Electronic Invoice Service Platform (All-Electronic Invoice system). Invoices must be digitally signed and transmitted in real time to STA; there is no separate post-fiscal stamping for digital electronic invoices. Physical tax-control invoices must be issued through the licensed tax-control equipment and upload transactional data to the national invoice management system. VAT reconciliation, invoice validation, and purchaser authentication are performed electronically via the STA invoice verification portal.

Export zero-rating is subject to the strict transactional documentation review by STA. If documentation is incomplete, repurchased by related party, proceeds not repatriated within the verified deadline, or services are found to be consumed inside China, STA will void the zero rating, recover the VAT underpaid, impose late-payment penalties at 0.05% per day on the outstanding VAT, and levy administrative fines ranging from 50% to 5 times the amount of tax lost. Fraudulent claims may additionally trigger criminal liability for tax evasion under the Criminal Law, including imprisonment, monetary penalties, and a permanent disqualification from simplified VAT-exemption procedures.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The RMB is fully convertible on the current account under China's acceptance of IMF Article VIII obligations, meaning trade in goods, services, and routine legal fee remittances can settle freely after verifying underlying documentation. However, the capital account remains heavily regulated: foreign direct investment equity injections, foreign loan drawdowns, and cross-border security arrangements require prior SAFE registration or approval. There is no blanket surrender requirement for export proceeds since the 2012 SAFE reform; enterprises may retain earnings in a foreign currency account or voluntarily convert to RMB, provided repatriation occurs within statutory timelines and transaction data is reported via the Trade Monitoring System. Profit remittance for a Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture is permissible but administratively layered: the enterprise must submit an audited annual financial statement, corporate income tax clearance and filing proofs, a board-approved profit distribution resolution, and a certificate from the local tax authority confirming that withholding tax on dividends has been paid or exempted. The central bank's macroprudential cross-border financing parameter and capital account item approval can delay large inflows; therefore, treasury planning must embed a six-to-twelve week compliance runway for any capital injection or outbound litigation settlement in excess of USD 5 million.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export proceeds must be repatriated into China within 180 days from the date of goods export customs declaration (or within the contractually agreed payment period for services, capped at 180 days under SAFE general rules). The statutory conversion ratio is zero, as no mandatory conversion to RMB is imposed; however, the full amount must be recorded in the SAFE Trade Monitoring System, and banks will verify that the foreign currency receipts are credited to the entity's domestic foreign exchange settlement account. Any shortfall, delay, or routing through an offshore account without SAFE exemption constitutes an administrative violation and can trigger balance-of-payments penalty and customs downgrade.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) and State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Offshore digital EMI platforms, such as multi-currency B2B neobanks or crypto-linked payment corridors, are not viable for a mainland-licensed law firm entity. China does not grant banking licenses to foreign fintech EMIs, and SAFE regulations mandate that RMB and foreign currency settlement for domestic legal entities move exclusively through licensed domestic commercial banks or qualified non-bank payment institutions with official resmi filing. Attempting to route litigation client funds or IOLTA-style trust deposits through an unlicensed offshore EMI would trigger immediate AML regulatory suspension and probable freezing. A limited exception exists for pure foreign-currency receivables from non-resident clients that are not crossed into China; those can be collected into an offshore account with SAFE reporting, but for a JV operating in mainland, the prudent treasury rail is a tier-1 Chinese bank trust account.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity capital for the Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture must be routed through a capital account at a licensed domestic bank. The procedure is: (1) SAFE FDI registration is filed at the local branch before remittance, with the Foreign-Invested Enterprise Establishment Certificate and Joint Venture Contract; (2) after the registration certificate is issued, the bank establishes a Capital Account (FDI); (3) the foreign shareholder wires USD or other LEGAL tender currency via SWIFT with purpose code 'Equity investment in a foreign-invested enterprise' (code 3100 series); (4) the receiving bank verifies the wire against the SAFE registration, crediting the Capital Account; (5) funds may be held in foreign currency or converted to RMB in the capital account transaction bank, with the conversion subjected to comprehensive verification of the enterprise's full previous-year actual operations; (6) an independent Chinese Certified Public Accountant issues a capital verification report (or capital contribution audit opinion) that is filed with the local Administration for Market Regulation. For a law firm, settlement proceeds from a foreign client (e.g., legal fees and court advances) may enter the ordinary current account under service trade codes, but must satisfy the trade authenticity requirement through service contract and invoice screening. Client trust funds received from abroad must be credited to a dedicated client trust account (analogous to IOLTA) and cannot be commingled with the firm's operational funds.Operational outflows for litigation support (e.g., photocopying, expert witness fees, translation services, court filing fees) settle in RMB through the normal current account using domestic clearing rails and standard bank transfer. For cross-border disbursements to international co-counsel, consultants, arbitral institutions, or witnesses, the preferred rail is SWIFT MT103 for high-value fee payments, together with the same electronic advancement system. The bank requires a disbursement instruction sent via SWIFT with purpose code for 'legal services' and a supporting packet: the service contract, itemized invoice, court or arbitration evidence of the case, tax withholding certificate if the payee is subject to China-source WHT, and the firm's client trust ledger showing that the payment draws solely from the segregated trust balance. For settlements involving collection of a judgment or arbitration award, the firm may use a more efficient channel: an authenticated copy of the final award or judgment, a no-appeal certificate, and a consent from the judgment debtor through a local domestic bank in the debtor's country. If the matter involves execution against goods, a Commercial Letter of Credit (incoming or outgoing) is permissible only when the law firm acts as escrow agent or trusted counsel for a cross-border sale; otherwise, the law firm should not handle merchandise title to avoid exceeding the legal service scope. Documentary Collections (D/P or D/A) are discouraged for litigation-related disbursements because their ownership and transfer documentation requirements do not align with evidence chain governance required by Chinese banking compliance committees.Onshore RMB forward contracts with a licensed domestic bank under SAFE's current account hedging framework, available only for verified trade or service payment exposures with a maturity not exceeding one year and long-form documentary proof of the underlying obligationRMB/USD cross-currency swaps permitted for recognized foreign debt and FDI capital account exposures, but only after SAFE approval and macroprudential parameter verification for the enterprise's total cross-border financingOffshore non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) for the RMB are not permissible for onshore entities as a direct hedging tool; any NDF leg can only be executed through the domestic bank's offshore branch under a written SAFE no-action position and strictly for underlying trade exposures, effectively making NDFs a second-choice corridorCurrency option collars or vanilla options bought from a qualified domestic bank are permissible for the underlying litigation fee escalation and settlement receipts, provided the notional amount does not exceed the outstanding invoice cross-border exposure and the bank performs a suitability assessment under the enterprise's derivatives qualification

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% (Enterprise Income Tax Law Article 4)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% on gross dividend distributions paid or credited to a non-resident enterprise shareholder not attributable to a Chinese permanent establishment, reduced from the 20% statutory non-resident rate by State Council implementation rules.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The annual corporate income tax return and final settlement must be filed and paid within 5 months after the end of the tax year; for a calendar-year JV the deadline is 31 May. Quarterly provisional income tax returns are due within 15 days after the close of each quarter.
