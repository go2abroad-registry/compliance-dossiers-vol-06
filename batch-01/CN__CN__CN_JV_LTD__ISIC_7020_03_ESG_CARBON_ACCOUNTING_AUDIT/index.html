Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-up capital for consulting activities under the Chinese Company Law. For a Sino-foreign equity joint venture in the ESG/carbon advisory business, no blocked escrow account or capital verification certificate is required at formation. However, Article 47 of the 2023 Revision requires all subscribed capital to be fully paid in within five years after incorporation, unless a shorter period is mandated by law or administrative regulation. The subscribed amount must be stated in the Articles of Association and disclosed in the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System.

Funding & unlock path: Not Applicable - no manufacturing, warehousing, chemical, assembly, or high-risk physical operations. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA); official e-tax portal: Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax), accessible through www.chinatax.gov.cn and provincial e-tax terminals; central VAT invoice verification platform: National VAT Invoice Verification Platform.

E-government filing portal

The Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture is incorporated via the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR), which issues an 18-character Unified Social Credit Code (USCC); that USCC is automatically adopted by the taxation administration as the enterprise taxpayer identification number (TIN). Tax registration is completed passively through the SAMR-STA interdepartmental data exchange; the entity must subsequently authenticate as legal representative in the Electronic Tax Bureau, file beneficial ownership/shareholding, bank account, accounting policy, and expected business scope data, and obtain a Tax Control UKey or e-invoice certificate in order to issue legal VAT invoices. For a foreign non-resident parent or service provider without a Chinese establishment, the competent tax authority assigns a Non-Resident Enterprise Taxpayer Identification Number (with withholding agent linkage) through the Electronic Tax Bureau function for non-resident taxpayer registration; the foreign entity must provide certificate of incorporation, tax residency certificate from the treaty jurisdiction, articles of association, and identity documents of the responsible officer. If the non-resident entity does not have a withholding agent, it must register directly with the competent local tax bureau for the non-resident taxpayer number and tax filing obligations.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 (5 million yuan) of annual taxable VAT turnover is the mandatory registration threshold for General VAT Taxpayer status; persons below the threshold may register as Small-Scale Taxpayers and use lower simplified levies. General taxpayer registration is compulsory once taxable turnover exceeds RMB 5,000,000; for taxpayers with permanent establishments in China, all VAT registration is done through the Electronic Tax Bureau. Imported goods are subject to VAT at import regardless of the threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating for export of goods applies where the goods are physically exported from the Customs territory of China, the export is evidenced by a valid Customs export declaration, the sales proceeds are received from outside China, and the exporter has complied with the export tax rebate filing rules. Qualifying cross-border services wholly consumed outside China, including management consulting, carbon audit, and carbon offset advisory services supplied to non-PRC-resident enterprises where there is no in-China recipient or in-China use, may be zero-rated or exempt under the cross-border service VAT rules; the supplier must retain written contracts, foreign exchange receipts, and service-consumption evidence to substantiate that the benefit is received entirely outside China. The ESG carbon accounting/audit JV must document each offshore engagement with the overseas client, the payment currency and SWIFT receipt, the place-of-supply analysis, and the absence of a domestic taxable nexus for the supply.

  • Customs Export Declaration for Goods (China Customs export declaration filed via the China International Trade Single Window, the export counterpart of the customs bill of entry)
  • Ocean Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (non-negotiable transport evidence showing export from China)
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List issued against the export sales contract
  • Bank Foreign Exchange Receipt Verification / Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or SAFE export-proceeds confirmation showing receipt from the overseas buyer within the repatriation period
  • Export Tax Rebate (Exemption) Application declaration submitted to the State Taxation Administration for the input VAT refund
  • Certificate of Origin or other destination-country regulatory documents required by the foreign customs authority

The STA requires fully digitalised electronic invoices under the e-invoice service platform and Golden Tax Phase IV. VAT invoices must be issued through the State Taxation Administration's e-invoice system, with real-time upload of invoice data to the central fiscal treasury/tax control platform before delivery to the buyer; issuance uses API-based fiscal clearance, Tax UKey authentication, and automatic anti-counterfeit digital stamping. Special VAT Invoices (for input credit between general taxpayers) and ordinary VAT invoices are both digitised; no paper invoices may be issued for a registered general taxpayer outside the national e-invoice platform. The export zero-rating documentation trail does not require a Special VAT Invoice to the foreign customer but requires the export declaration and shipping/payment records to be retained.

Export zero-rating claims are audited against the STA export-rebate databases, Chinese customs manifests, the SAFE foreign-exchange receipt monitoring system, and bank payment records. If the claimed zero-rating is not supported by actual export, authentic bills of lading, verified inward remittance within the 180-day repatriation window, or where documentation is falsified, the tax authority will deny or claw back the entire VAT refund and impose late payment surcharge. Fraudulent export tax refund is punishable under the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Tax Collection by recovery of the refund, a fine of 1 to 5 times the amount fraudulently refunded, and suspension of the right to apply for future export tax refunds; under Article 204 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, deliberate export-tax-refund fraud may result in imprisonment and criminal fines for the responsible persons, and Customs Law penalties apply for false export declarations.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Under China's Foreign Exchange Control Regulations, the RMB is fully convertible for current account items such as trade in goods and services, but capital account items remain subject to administrative approvals, filing, and registration through the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE). A Sino-foreign equity joint venture may freely use foreign exchange received from current account business after bank verification, but cross-border capital injections, external loans, equity transfers, and direct overseas investments must be registered or approved in advance. Export proceeds must be repatriated to a domestic foreign exchange account, after which the enterprise may either retain the currency or sell it to a designated bank at the market spot rate; there is no enforced conversion ratio. Profit remittance to foreign shareholders is permitted only after statutory audits, corporate income tax settlement, and the submission of a board resolution, audited financial statements, and a tax clearance certificate; the remitted amount must not exceed distributable profits recorded in the capital verification and accounting records. For service-based ESG and carbon auditing income, the bank will apply the service trade payment review rules, requiring genuine transaction documents, invoices, and SAFE codes. Overall, convertibility is liberal on the current account but tightly controlled on the capital account, making early SAFE registration and document preparation essential for unrestricted treasury operations.

Export proceeds surrender rules

The term 'surrender' is a legacy regulatory concept; the current statutory regime requires repatriation rather than forced conversion. Export proceeds must be brought onshore within 90 days after export customs declaration under the SAFE goods-trade foreign exchange verification rules, and must be credited to the exporter's foreign exchange account before any RMB settlement. The mandatory conversion ratio is zero: 100% of export receipts may be retained in foreign currency or converted at the bank's market spot rate. Enterprises classified as Category B/C face shorter settlement windows and additional document reviews.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) in conjunction with the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Not viable. Offshore digital e-money institutions (EMIs) such as multi-currency virtual account providers are not licensed by the PBOC or SAFE to hold RMB accounts or execute foreign exchange settlements for PRC-domiciled enterprises. Chinese law restricts the provision of payment and settlement services to licensed banks and non-bank financial institutions registered with PBOC. A cross-border EMI cannot open an account for a Sino-foreign JV, issue a local IBAN for RMB, or provide corporate FX clearing without establishing a licensed presence. The only compliant digital approach is to use the API channels of a licensed onshore bank or the PBOC's Cross-Border Interbank Payment System (CIPS).

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Foreign exchange capital injections must be remitted directly from the foreign shareholder's own bank account via SWIFT MT103, using the bank's designated FDI remittance instruction and referencing the SAFE Foreign Investment Registration Receipt. The purpose code should be selected as investment in the registered capital of a foreign-invested enterprise, and the recipient account must be the JV's RMB capital account or foreign exchange capital account opened under the SAFE registration. After funds are credited, a Chinese CPA must issue a capital verification report, and the bank must record the inbound remittance in the FX monitoring system. Withdrawals from the capital account for wages, purchase of fixed assets, or payment of carbon-offset brokerage fees require verified transaction documents and must follow the negative-list restrictions on prohibited investments.Trade settlement for ESG and carbon services should be executed through SWIFT MT103 for standard service payments; for high-value goods transactions or documentary evidence of physical carbon credit delivery, Commercial Letters of Credit or Documentary Collections may be used. The JV may also instruct payments via CIPS for RMB-settled counterparties. Before outbound payment, the bank requires a tax withholding certificate if the overseas counterparty is a non-resident service provider, a SAFE cross-border payment code, and proof of underlying transaction such as an invoice, contract, or delivery confirmation. Payments must be matched to the declared ESG/carbon service scope, and carbon credit purchases should reference registry serial numbers to evidence genuine economic substance.Forward FX contracts permissible only with a licensed onshore bank, limited to hedging real current-account exposures such as USD receivables from export clients, requiring underlying transaction documents and SAFE declaration.Cross-currency swaps permissible only for approved capital-account flows, including registered external debt and FDI-related conversions, strictly within the SAFE debt-to-equity ratio ceiling and with matching maturity.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are not permissible onshore for RMB; an onshore enterprise may not enter an NDF because SAFE requires physical delivery and verification of the underlying exposure at maturity.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Under SAT Announcement [2016] No. 42, contemporaneous Local File documentation is mandatory when annual related-party transactions exceed RMB 200 million for tangible property transfers, RMB 100 million for intangible property transfers or financing arrangements, or RMB 40 million for provision of services; Master File and CbC Report thresholds trigger where aggregate related-party transactions reach RMB 1 billion or the group's consolidated revenue exceeds RMB 5.5 billion.

Economic substance scope: China applies the substance-over-form doctrine and General Anti-Avoidance Rule under PRC EIT Law Article 47 and SAT Announcement 2017 No. 6. Entities lacking real business premises, employees, management decisions, and Core Income-Generating Activities will be recharacterized; shell shareholders claiming treaty benefits are denied withholding relief.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Declaration

Statutory sanction: PRC Tax Administration Law Article 63 imposes a fine of 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax and full recovery of unpaid tax; PRC Criminal Law Article 201 provides imprisonment up to 7 years for serious tax evasion.

Director personal liability: Courts may pierce the corporate veil under PRC Company Law Article 20 where directors abuse limited liability; directors can face personal civil restitution, freezing and confiscation of personal assets, travel bans and court-ordered exit restrictions, and criminal imprisonment up to 7 years.
Transfer Pricing / Earnings Stripping

Statutory sanction: PRC EIT Law Articles 41 and 46 authorize special tax adjustments and thin-capitalization interest disallowance (debt-to-equity limit 2:1 generally, 5:1 for financial enterprises). Failure to prepare contemporaneous TP documentation triggers penalties under SAT Announcement [2016] No. 42 plus interest surcharges.

Director personal liability: Directors who approve false TP documentation are jointly and severally liable for the adjusted tax, face travel and exit bans under Tax Administration Law Article 44, and if the transfer pricing scheme constitutes intentional tax evasion may incur criminal responsibility under PRC Criminal Law Article 201.
Shell Company / Treaty Abuse / AML

Statutory sanction: DTA relief is denied under the beneficial ownership test in SAT Announcement 2019 No. 35; withholding is recalculated at the full 10% statutory rate; GAAR recharacterization under EIT Law Article 47; penalties may be imposed under Tax Administration Law Article 62.

Director personal liability: Directors face personal civil liability for the unpaid withholding taxes, blacklisting with restrictions on leaving China until tax liabilities are settled, and if laundering proceeds of tax fraud, imprisonment up to 10 years under PRC Criminal Law Article 191.
