Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None; no statutory minimum paid-up capital and no blocked escrow account requirement for an ordinary Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture limited liability company. The shareholders determine the registered capital and contribution schedule in the JV contract and Articles of Association. Under the 2023 Company Law, shareholders must fully pay subscribed capital within five years from establishment unless a shorter period is specified in the Articles of Association.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China, with electronic administration through the provincial Electronic Tax Bureau (E-Tax Hall) on the STA official portal at www.chinatax.gov.cn.

E-government filing portal

The Sino-foreign equity joint venture is registered with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) and automatically obtains a Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which is the sole legal Tax Identification Number (TIN) for all tax filings in China. Within 30 days of issuance of the business license, the entity must complete tax registration in the E-Tax Hall, confirm taxpayer type, and bind the USCC to the VAT, corporate income tax, and social and administrative records. A foreign non-resident enterprise without a Chinese establishment that derives China-source income or needs treaty relief must obtain an STA-issued non-resident taxpayer identification number through the competent tax authority at the payers or project location, or through the statutory withholding agent, by completing the non-resident enterprise tax registration procedure. A separate VAT ID is not issued because the USCC or non-resident TIN is the single tax identifier.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — The Sino-foreign equity joint venture is registered with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) and automatically obtains a Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which is the sole legal Tax Identification Number (TIN) for all tax filings in China. A separate VAT ID is not issued because the USCC or non-resident TIN is the single tax identifier.

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

None for income tax; China does not provide check-the-box elections or hybrid mismatch entity elections. For VAT only, a business with annual taxable sales below RMB 5,000,000 may elect to register as a general VAT taxpayer to obtain input VAT credits, and a general taxpayer with certain specified transactions may elect simplified VAT treatment where expressly permitted by the Value-Added Tax Law of the People's Republic of China and STA implementation rules.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

0.06%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

All companies are required to complete VAT and tax registration within 30 days of SAMR registration; there is no de minimis turnover threshold for basic tax registration. Mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration applies when annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000; below that threshold, small-scale taxpayer status with a 3% levy may apply. The standard VAT rate applicable to AEO certification and security auditing services (management consultancy, certification, and related modern services) is 6%; the national headline goods VAT rate is 13%.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods: zero-rating is available for goods declared to and verified by China Customs as physically exported, sold to an overseas purchaser, with export proceeds collected within the statutory window and a timely export VAT refund declaration filed by a general VAT taxpayer. Services: cross-border taxable services supplied to an overseas unit or individual and consumed entirely outside China may qualify for zero rating or VAT exemption under the Value-Added Tax Law of the People's Republic of China and the STA/MOF rules on cross-border taxable services. For AEO certification and security auditing services, the supplier must demonstrate that the recipient is offshore, the services are not performed for a China mainland benefit, and the deliverables are used wholly outside China. If the service is outside the zero-rated statutory list but consumed wholly offshore, it may be VAT-exempt rather than zero-rated, meaning no output VAT but no input VAT credit. Supporting export contracts, invoicing, bank receipts, and service delivery records must be maintained to substantiate the treatment.

  • China Customs Export Declaration Form (Export Goods Bill of Entry equivalent) accepted by the General Administration of Customs through the China International Trade Single Window
  • Bill of Lading or master airway bill evidencing international shipment and exit of the goods from China
  • Foreign exchange receipt record or bank inward remittance certificate (Inward Remittance Certificate equivalent) confirmed by the payment rail and reported through the State Administration of Foreign Exchange framework
  • Export contract, commercial invoice, packing list and, where applicable, AEO certification evidence used for customs facilitation

China operates the Golden Tax Phase IV electronic tax administration and the nationwide Electronic Invoice Service Platform. Business taxpayers must use fully digitalized electronic invoices (FDEI) or the legacy VAT invoice management system, with each special VAT invoice bearing a unique invoice code and an encrypted digital stamp issued by the STA. Invoice data is transmitted to the tax authority in near-real time through the official platform interfaces; red-letter credit-note invoices require online verification and filing before use. Export invoices must be reconciled with customs declaration data through the China International Trade Single Window and the STA Export Tax Rebate system before a zero-rating claim is accepted.

Export VAT zero-rating is audited by STA against GACC export declarations, logistics records, payment receipts, and service delivery documentation. If the required documentation is missing, inconsistent, or the foreign exchange receipt does not occur within the applicable repatriation deadline, the tax authority will assess VAT at the applicable 6% or 13% rate together with late-payment interest at 0.05% per day. False or fraudulent zero-rating and refund claims are clawed back and penalized under the Tax Collection Administration Law of the People's Republic of China at 50% to five times the tax involved, with unlimited retroactive assessment for fraudulent concealment, and criminal prosecution under the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China for fraudulent export refunds or falsely issued VAT special invoices. AEO status may be suspended or withdrawn where customs or tax irregularities are identified.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a controlled but progressively liberalised foreign exchange regime. The current account, covering trade in goods, services, salaries, and investment income, is generally fully convertible for bona fide cross-border transactions under a current account verification and monitoring system administered by SAFE and the designated banks. Exporters and service suppliers may settle foreign currency receipts into RMB at their discretion after presenting underlying trade documentation, with no mandated conversion ratio. By contrast, the capital account remains qualitatively restricted. Foreign direct investment into China is allowed, but the foreign investor must complete SAFE foreign exchange registration for the domestic entity, route the inward remittance through a dedicated capital foreign currency account, and comply with the Foreign Investment Negative List. Profit remittance abroad is permissible only after the completion of annual statutory audit, filing of the audited financial statements, payment of corporate income tax and any related withholding tax, and submission of the formal board resolution authorising dividend distribution. In practice, banks require documented evidence of actual business operations and a clean record in the asset and liability survey. The principal hurdles are documentary authenticity, tax clearance, and the requirement that the remitted profit be traceable to audited retained earnings. There is no free repatriation of undistributed funds absent liquidation. Capital account convertibility is therefore granted transaction-by-transaction, not as a general licence.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under PRC foreign exchange rules, export proceeds in foreign currency must be repatriated to a domestic account or deposited with an approved offshore bank in accordance with the goods trade-based administration system, and the exporter must complete trade foreign exchange receipt confirmation within 180 days from the export customs declaration date unless an extension or special scheme applies. There is no mandatory conversion ratio to RMB; the exporter may retain the foreign currency or sell it to a licensed bank at market rate, provided the authenticity and consistency of the customs data, contract, transport documents, and payment advices are demonstrated.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Fintech electronic money institutions such as Wise do not hold a PRC commercial banking licence or a SAFE cross-border payment institution licence and are therefore not legally viable as primary treasury clearing banks for a registered Sino-foreign equity joint venture in China. They cannot open domestic RMB capital accounts, receive FDI capital injections, process SAFE trade verification, or support the statutory capital foreign exchange settlement process. A fintech EMI may be used as an ancillary remittance tool for low-value non-regulated payments executed through a licensed Chinese correspondent bank, but its viability for corporate treasury purposes is Low. Any attempt to route registered capital or trade proceeds through an unlicensed EMI would trigger automatic rejection under the real trade background review and expose the entity to severe AML and regulatory sanctions.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital must flow from the overseas investor directly into the JV's capital foreign exchange settlement account opened with a licensed Chinese bank after the JV has completed FDI foreign exchange registration with SAFE or via the delegated bank. The initial transfer must be denominated in the registered foreign investment currency and use a valid foreign exchange payment purpose code for direct investment; the bank will verify the capital verification report issued by a Chinese registered accounting firm, the board resolution, and the investment confirmation letter. Once received, the capital account funds may be converted to RMB into a capital settlement account strictly for permitted business use such as purchase of fixed assets, payment of raw materials, wages, rent, tax, and acquisition of domestic services, with each disbursement subject to authentic transaction documentation. The JV must never use the capital account for current account settlement without prior RMB conversion or deploy funds into equity purchase, entrusted loans, or prohibited negative-list activities.Outbound treasury settlements for AEO audit service imports, licensing fees, consultancy payments, and overseas disbursements are processed by domestic banks through SWIFT MT103 messages under the current account supervision system, supported by the underlying commercial contract, invoice, service completion certificate, and SAFE reporting. Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% on dividends paid by a Chinese resident enterprise to a non-resident enterprise shareholder, subject to further reduction under an applicable double tax treaty (for example, 5% for a Hong Kong resident corporate shareholder holding at least 25% of the equity of the Chinese payer throughout the 365-day beneficial ownership period).

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File must be prepared when any related-party transaction exceeds: CNY 100 million for tangible asset sales/purchases; CNY 100 million for financial asset transfers; CNY 50 million for intangible asset transfers; CNY 40 million for other related-party transactions. Master File must be prepared when aggregate related-party transactions equal or exceed CNY 1 billion. All documentation must be contemporaneous and in Chinese.

Economic substance scope: China does not operate the zero-tax Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) framework used in some tax-neutral jurisdictions, but the Corporate Income Tax Law, Tax Collection and Administration Law general anti-avoidance rule and judicial anti-avoidance doctrine require real commercial substance. For an AEO supply-chain audit entity, Core Income-Generating Activities (CIGA) — client risk assessment, physical security audits, supply-chain due diligence, certification reporting and management decisions — must be performed by local personnel in China using local offices, systems and payroll. An offshore entity that merely holds invoices, signs agreements abroad or routes profits to a non-resident related party without any local business substance will be recharacterized as a shell company, denied deductions, treated as having a permanent establishment, or subject to controlled-foreign-company and GAAR adjustments.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion via Transfer Pricing and False Intercompany Service Charges

Statutory sanction: Tax Collection and Administration Law Art. 63: imposition of 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax, daily late-payment surcharge of 0.05% of unpaid tax under Art. 32, and a formal transfer-pricing adjustment with the reassessment of 10% WHT on deemed outbound payments. Criminal Law Art. 201: evasion amount over RMB 50,000 and over 10% of tax payable attracts a criminal fine and up to 3 years imprisonment; enormous amounts are punished with 3 to 7 years imprisonment.

Director personal liability: Where directors cause the company to be used as a sham to evade tax, the corporate veil is pierced; directors are jointly and severally liable for unpaid tax, interest, penalties and legal costs. Directors may be placed on the tax authority exit-ban/blacklist (prohibition on exiting China) under TCAL Art. 44, have personal assets seized, be disqualified from serving as directors, and be sentenced to imprisonment alongside the company for tax evasion crimes.
DTA Treaty-Shopping / Dividend Stripping with False Beneficial Ownership

Statutory sanction: The tax authority denies treaty benefits and reimposes 10% statutory WHT on the gross dividends under CIT Law Art. 3 and Art. 37, plus late-payment surcharge and a 50% to 500% penalty under TCAL Art. 63; interest and royalties improperly treated under the DTA are recast as taxable Chinese-source income with the same sanctions.

Director personal liability: A director who signs a false beneficial-ownership declaration or a certificate of tax residence with knowledge of the sham faces personal liability for the unpaid WHT and penalties; the director may be banned from exiting China, blacklisted by the tax authority and prosecuted for tax fraud, capable of imprisonment and asset confiscation; restitution is ordered for diverted dividend amounts.
Shell Company / Absence of Economic Substance in the AEO Supply-Chain Audit Entity

Statutory sanction: Under CIT Law Art. 47 and TCAL Art. 47 (GAAR), deductions are disallowed, profits are recharacterized as assessable income, the entity is deemed to have a taxable representative office/permanent establishment, and net tax is recomputed at 25%; Customs AEO certification is revoked under Customs regulations, preferential clearances and green-channel benefits are cancelled, and export/import privileges may be suspended. Criminal liability arises if taxpayers use forged documents or false invoices.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly create an empty management shell may be held personally liable for the company's tax debts when assets are commingled or removed, leading to civil asset restitution; directors are subject to travel bans, blacklisting by customs/tax authorities, and disqualification from corporate governance; if tax fraud is established, imprisonment of up to 7 years with fines and confiscation applies.
