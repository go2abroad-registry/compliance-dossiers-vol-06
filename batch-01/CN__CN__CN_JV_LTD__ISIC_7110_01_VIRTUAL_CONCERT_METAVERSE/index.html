VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

6%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

All PRC companies must complete tax/VAT registration within 30 days after SAMR business license issuance. Mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration is triggered when annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000; below that threshold the JV may remain a small-scale VAT taxpayer using the simplified collection rate, but must still be registered for VAT and issue ordinary invoices.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under Caishui [2016] No. 36 and STA cross-border service guidance, 0% VAT zero-rating is limited to enumerated outbound supplies such as international transport, software-related services, R&D/design services, and offshore outsourcing services. A virtual concert and digital ticketing platform may qualify for zero-rating if the core tax character is software, cloud hosting, or technology services delivered to non-resident recipients; if characterized as cultural/entertainment services supplied to a non-resident audience, the cross-border service is eligible for VAT exemption rather than 0% zero-rating. In all cases the taxpayer must prove: (1) the service recipient is a non-resident with no PRC establishment; (2) the service is not consumed inside China and no PRC audience is served; (3) offshore contracts, e-invoices and payment evidence exist; and (4) foreign exchange receipts are repatriated to a PRC onshore account through a licensed bank within the statutory deadline. If physical goods are bundled with the virtual experience, the goods component is zero-rated under the standard goods export declaration and export VAT refund regime.

  • Customs Export Goods Declaration Form (Authorized Customs Release Record) - required only when physical goods are exported; pure service exports do not clear customs
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing physical outbound movement for any bundled merchandise
  • Export or service invoice and offshore contract clearly identifying the non-resident recipient and the service/goods character
  • Bank foreign exchange receipt voucher from a licensed PRC bank evidencing onshore repatriation of export proceeds
  • Trade in Services Foreign Exchange Transaction Declaration Form for the service component, as accepted by SAFE and STA
  • Digital service delivery evidence such as metaverse session logs, ticket access records and user geolocation reports demonstrating offshore consumption

China operates a real-time e-invoice clearance regime under the STA Golden Tax Phase IV system. Taxpayers issue invoices via the official e-invoice platform using the national unified electronic invoice schema; each invoice receives a unique digitized invoice code and electronic signature. Fully digitalized e-invoices are transmitted to STA in real time at issuance, and the STA platform performs automatic anti-fraud matching against customs, foreign exchange, and bank transaction data. ERP taxpayers may use STA-certified standardized data interfaces; no physical fiscal stamp or third-party clearance device is required in the fully digitalized e-invoice stream.

The STA may reopen tax assessments for up to 3 years generally and 10 years for fraud. If export zero-rating or exemption is not substantiated by the required documentation, the proceeds are recharacterized as domestic taxable turnover; VAT at 6% is assessed with daily late-payment interest of 0.05%, and a monetary penalty of 50% to five times the underpaid tax may be imposed under the PRC Tax Collection Administration Law. Fraudulent zero-rating or export refund claims also trigger criminal liability under the PRC Criminal Law, including confiscation of the refunded amount and imprisonment of responsible individuals; clawback is retrospective without a limitation period in criminal fraud cases.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a managed floating exchange-rate regime for the renminbi. Current-account transactions are broadly convertible once trade and service authenticity is established, but capital-account flows remain subject to SAFE's 'real need' verification framework, classification-based quotas, and cross-border registration. The Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture may receive registered capital in a SAFE-monitored foreign-exchange capital account, and each conversion of that capital into RMB requires a granular declaration of intended use, supported by invoices, contracts, board resolutions and tax evidence. Profit remittances to the foreign parent are lawful after the JV completes its annual statutory audit, files corporate income tax, and submits to the bank the board's dividend declaration, audited financial statements, and tax clearance certificates. Convertibility is therefore segmented: operational royalties, ticket platform fees, and service payments are treated as current-account items with relatively streamlined verification, while foreign direct investment equity inflows, investor loans, cross-border guarantees, hedging structures, and external debt must pass through SAFE registration and permissible-purpose tests. The JV should treat all foreign-exchange liquidity planning as pre-approved and document-driven, because any mismatch between purpose codes, supporting vouchers, and fund flows will trigger bank-level rejection or SAFE penalties.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export foreign exchange proceeds under goods trade must enter a licensed onshore foreign-currency current account and are generally expected within 90 days after the customs export declaration; deferred collection beyond 90 days requires reporting to the SAFE monitoring system. There is no mandatory conversion ratio as at the current regulatory stage; the JV may retain the proceeds in foreign currency or settle from 0% to 100% into RMB at its own discretion through a designated bank, subject to trade authenticity documentation, the SAFE goods-trade total-amount verification framework, and the bank's anti-money-laundering declaration requirements.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low, with material legal constraints. Offshore digital EMIs are not licensed by the PBOC or SAFE to hold RMB accounts, open domestic basic deposit accounts, or clear cross-border foreign-exchange flows for legal entities in mainland China. Services such as Wise, Airwallex and Treasury-style payment platforms may only function as ancillary reporting layers or as sanctioned-entity screened payment facilitators linked to a licensed onshore bank. Direct EMI settlement of subscription, ticket or ticket-token revenue is not a compliant clearing rail for the Chinese JV, and any USDC-denominated settlement must be excluded from the onshore treasury entirely. The viable architecture is licensed Tier-1 bank collection accounts with Chinese licensed payment institutions and bank-card acquiring rails for the virtual concert ticketing model.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

After obtaining SAFE FDI registration, the JV opens a foreign-exchange capital account at a licensed Tier-1 Chinese bank. The foreign investor remits the equity capital by SWIFT MT103 using the JV capital-account number as beneficiary, while the remittance reference field cites the SAFE FDI registration code and the purpose classification for 'foreign direct investment equity injection into a foreign-invested enterprise'. The receiving bank verifies the MT103 counterpart identifiers, sanctions screens, source-of-funds instructions and the capital account balance against the SAFE registry. Once credited, the JV may apply to convert the capital into RMB by submitting a foreign-exchange settlement purpose declaration, supporting invoices or payment schedules for virtual-concert platform development, software licensing, cloud infrastructure, leasing and payroll. The bank performs post-settlement authenticity review, and a PRC CPA issues the capital verification report for filing with the local Administration for Market Regulation. Any subsequent increase in registered capital requires an additional SAFE registration and updated capital verification before new remittances are accepted.For service fees, licensing, metaverse platform infrastructure and entertainment-rights royalties, the JV should use standard SWIFT MT103 payments from its operating account, attaching license agreements, invoices, withholding tax evidence and the SAFE transaction-basis certificate. For high-value imported hardware such as immersive displays, rendering servers and edge-computing nodes, the JV should issue an irrevocable documentary commercial letter of credit through its Chinese bank, with the beneficiary seller presenting bills of lading, commercial invoices, packing lists and insurance certificates. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low, with material legal constraints. Offshore digital EMIs are not licensed by the PBOC or SAFE to hold RMB accounts, open domestic basic deposit accounts, or clear cross-border foreign-exchange flows for legal entities in mainland China. Services such as Wise, Airwallex and Treasury-style payment platforms may only function as ancillary reporting layers or as sanctioned-entity screened payment facilitators linked to a licensed onshore bank. Direct EMI settlement of subscription, ticket or ticket-token revenue is not a compliant clearing rail for the Chinese JV, and any USDC-denominated settlement must be excluded from the onshore treasury entirely. The viable architecture is licensed Tier-1 bank collection accounts with Chinese licensed payment institutions and bank-card acquiring rails for the virtual concert ticketing model.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Master File is mandatory when the multinational group's annual consolidated revenue exceeds RMB 5 billion; Local File is mandatory if, in the tax year, the JV's related-party transactions exceed any of the following thresholds in RMB: tangible property transfers over RMB 200 million, intangible property transfers over RMB 100 million, financial asset transfers over RMB 100 million, or service transactions over RMB 40 million; a Special File (country-by-country reporting) is also required for groups with total consolidated revenue equal to or greater than RMB 55 billion in the preceding fiscal year and any Chinese constituent entity whose related-party transaction amount exceeds RMB 10 million

Economic substance scope: China follows the arm's-length principle under Article 41 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law; while there is no separate Economic Substances Regulation (ESR) like in the UAE, the State Taxation Administration applies the substance-over-form and beneficial-ownership doctrine through the General Anti-Avoidance Rule (GAAR) under Article 47, and through the 'actual purpose' and 'real substance' tests in the DTA relief rules. For a virtual concert Metaverse JV, core income-generating activities (CIGA) are considered the actual development, improvement, deployment, and maintenance of the virtual Metaverse platform, hosting of live virtual performances, ticket-token minting, and payment settlement; if these functions are exclusively performed by the foreign parent or a third-party contractor, the Chinese JV may be denied the 5% dividend treaty rate and may be recomputed under a profit-split method allocating profits to where the economically significant personnel and assets are actually located; a mere invoice or royalty contract lacking physical office space, technical staff, and local decision-making is an unacceptable shell arrangement and can trigger a special tax adjustment, a 10% or 5.59% compounded interest surcharge, and a 50%-150% penalty

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion through fictitious VAT invoices and fabricated production costs

Statutory sanction: Article 201 of the Criminal Law (逃税罪) exposes the entity and responsible persons to fines of up to 5 times the amount of evaded tax and imprisonment of up to 7 years; Article 205 of the Criminal Law (虚开增值税专用发票罪) provides a penalty of up to 10 years or life imprisonment when the false invoice amount is enormous; under Article 63 of the Tax Administration Law the entity must pay the underpaid tax plus 50%-500% penalty and daily late-payment surcharge of 0.05%

Director personal liability: Directors who sign or approve the filing or who knowingly participate in fictitious invoicing are jointly and severally liable for restitution of the unpaid tax and penalties; a Chinese court may order civil seizure of personal assets, impose an exit-control order restricting directors from leaving China, publish the name on the State Administration of Taxation's serious taxpayer blacklist (joint credit discipline), and impose criminal imprisonment from 3 years to life for the most severe false-invoice cases; personal criminal liability is not shielded by the JV's limited liability in cases of active fraud
Shell company structuring and abusive dividend treaty relief

Statutory sanction: The General Anti-Avoidance Rule under Article 47 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law allows the tax authority to recharacterize or disregard the whole corporate structure and impose tax on the actual economic substance; the SAT Announcement on Beneficial Ownership (No. 35 of 2018) denies the DTA claim and reimposes the statutory 10% WHT with interest and a 50%-150% penalty; the serious-taxpayer publicity rules under the Tax Administration Law place the JV and its controlling persons on a nationwide blacklist that restricts government procurement, financing, and outbound travel

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly incorporate and maintain a shell to abuse the treaty are personally jointly liable to the extent of assets they caused to be shifted; the court may pierce the corporate veil under China's Company Law Article 20, order personal reimbursement of the underpaid WHT, freeze personal bank accounts, and restrict exit from China; the same conduct may constitute criminal tax evasion under Article 201 when the underpaid tax exceeds the criminal threshold, leading to imprisonment of up to 7 years
Transfer pricing profit shifting and failure to maintain contemporaneous documentation

Statutory sanction: Article 41 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law mandates that related-party transactions be priced at arm's-length; SAT Announcement No. 6 of 2017 on Special Tax Adjustments requires the taxpayer to make a comparable-profit adjustment and pay an additional income tax plus interest surcharged by five percentage points above the benchmark lending rate; a fictional transaction that is found to have no business purpose can be recharacterized, and an underpaid tax penalty of 50%-150% is imposed under Article 63 of the Tax Administration Law

Director personal liability: Directors who deliberately cause the JV to omit related-party transactions or who backdate intercompany agreements to create a false contemporaneous file can be held personally accountable under the Company Law and charged with tax fraud under Article 201 of the Criminal Law; they may be liable for the TP adjustment amount out of personal assets, prohibited from serving as directors in China for 3 to 5 years, placed under exit-control by the public security authorities, and subject to criminal imprisonment of up to 3 years for falsifying tax documents
