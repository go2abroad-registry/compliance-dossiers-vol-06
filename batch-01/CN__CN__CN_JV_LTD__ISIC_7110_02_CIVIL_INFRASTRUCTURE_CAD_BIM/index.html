Competent tax authority

E-government filing portal

The Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture (CN_JV_LTD) is incorporated with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) under the multi-license integration reform. The business license issued contains the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which China recognizes as the sole statutory Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) and VAT registration number. Upon incorporation, SAMR automatically transmits registration data to the STA, completing the mandatory tax registration without a separate certificate. For foreign non-resident entities that do not establish a Chinese enterprise but have China-sourced income, the local STA office must be approached to complete non-resident enterprise tax registration and obtain a non-resident tax identification number before any withholding tax can be paid on their behalf. The JV must then complete VAT taxpayer status filing through the e-Tax portal, apply for the digital tax-control account if full digitized e-invoicing is needed, and register for corporate income tax, individual income tax withholding, and payroll/social contributions as part of the initial payroll setup.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

6%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VAT registration is mandatory upon business establishment regardless of turnover. For general VAT taxpayer status, the statutory registration threshold is annual taxable turnover of RMB 5,000,000; below this threshold the taxpayer is normally a small-scale VAT taxpayer, but may elect general taxpayer status. The applicable VAT rate for the engineering design, BIM, and structural drafting services supplied by CN_JV_LTD is 6% (modern services rate), not the 13% general goods rate.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

CN_JV_LTD may claim 0% VAT on cross-border supply of engineering design, BIM, and structural drafting services only when the recipient is an overseas entity, the services are wholly consumed outside of China, and the services qualify under the STA's cross-border zero-rating catalogue for offshore services and design services. The JV must satisfy the 'fully consumed overseas' test through evidence such as the offshore contract, service scope, place of actual performance, overseas recipient's identity, and foreign exchange receipt. If any part of the service is used in or related to real property or construction located in China, or if the recipient is a Chinese resident entity, zero-rating is denied and the 6% VAT liability applies.

China operates the Golden Tax Phase IV system. Taxpayers issue fully digital e-invoices through the STA's unified Electronic Invoice Service Platform, with real-time API-based transmission of invoice data to the tax authority's digital tax-control account. Each e-invoice carries a dynamically generated anti-counterfeit QR code and unique invoice number; physical stamping is not required. General VAT taxpayers must complete the digital tax-control account setup and use the e-Tax portal for invoicing, VAT returns, and export zero-rating/refund declarations; non-compliant invoice data is automatically blocked or flagged for audit.

Export zero-rating claims are scrutinized jointly by the STA, General Administration of Customs, and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange. If the required customs declaration, transport document, service delivery evidence, or foreign exchange repatriation receipt is missing or inconsistent, the claimed 0% treatment is reversed and VAT at 6% is assessed with daily late-payment interest. Fraudulent or unverified export zero-rating claims trigger recovery of the tax refund plus an administrative penalty of 50% to 5 times the tax amount under the Tax Administration Law; serious cases constitute the criminal offense of defrauding export tax rebates under Article 204 of the Criminal Law, punishable by imprisonment up to life imprisonment and confiscation of property.

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates under a managed floating RMB exchange-rate regime enforced by SAFE; the current account is liberalized for bona fide trade-in-services transactions, while the capital account remains controlled and requires case-by-case registration or approval. The Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture may freely settle export-derived BIM/CAD service fees into CNY or retain them in designated current-account FX positions, but every inflow and outflow must be supported by authentic trade contracts, invoices, and electronic delivery evidence. Profit remittance is permitted under a proof-first, anti-disguised-repatriation policy: dividends must be calculated from audited statutory financial statements, fully subjected to corporate income tax and any applicable withholding, evidenced by board minutes, and accompanied by SAFE remittance forms. The practical hurdle is not the legal ability to convert, but the administrative reality that banks act as the implementing agent of SAFE and will not execute a transaction without a clear underlying record. Capital injections have a separate currency route through the FDI foreign-exchange capital account and cannot be used for temporary liquidity or intercompany loans outside the registered business scope. Service-export credits are scrutinized for round-tripping, fake billing, and possible disguised capital inflow/outflow, requiring banks to maintain complete source-and-use documentation for every settlement.

Export proceeds surrender rules

All export-related foreign-exchange receipts must be repatriated to China within 90 days after the date of service completion or, for goods, the date of customs export declaration; the designated bank may grant a one-time 90-day extension for valid commercial reasons and SAFE-recorded cases. As of 2012, SAFE abolished forced surrender, so the mandatory conversion ratio is 0%: the JV may retain 100% of export proceeds in a current-account foreign-exchange deposit account or sell any portion to an authorized domestic bank at the prevailing market rate. Each receipt must be reconciled against SAFE's trade-monitoring database using the export contract, invoice, proof of delivery or BIM/CAD transmittal, and payment instruction.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE), a delegated foreign-exchange administration body of the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Fintech EMIs such as Wise and other non-bank digital wallet providers are legally barred from acting as the onshore treasury account institution for this CJV in mainland China. They do not hold a Chinese deposit-taking banking licence and cannot satisfy PBOC or SAFE reporting obligations for the entity; their services can only appear as offshore payment sub-rails after the domestic bank has executed the CNY/FX conversion. Therefore, the JV must maintain its operational, capital and remittance accounts in a Tier-1 licensed domestic bank, with fintech tools limited to off-channel encashment by non-resident employees or non-Chinese suppliers.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The overseas parent must remit the JV equity via SWIFT MT103 to the JV's designated FDI foreign-exchange capital account in a Tier-1 domestic bank. Before the first deposit, the bank shall complete the FDI foreign-exchange registration in the SAFE digital platform and issue a foreign-exchange registration number. The payment narrative must state 'Equity Contribution to Registered Capital', with the applicable capital-project purpose code. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Under SAT Announcement 2016 No. 42, the Local File is statutorily required when annual related-party transactions exceed RMB 200 million for tangible property transfers, RMB 100 million for intangible property transfers, RMB 100 million for financial-asset transfers, or RMB 40 million for other related-party transactions; the Master File is required when total cross-border related-party transactions exceed RMB 1 billion or when the ultimate parent is obliged to prepare a group Master File, and the annual related-party transaction reporting schedule is required whenever any reportable related-party relationship exists.

Economic substance scope: China applies an economic-substance and substance-over-form doctrine through EITL Articles 41, 46, and 47 and STA Bulletin 2017 No. 6. Intra-group service fees must have actual service delivery, commercial value, and be free of shareholder duplication. Core income-generating activities in China include contract negotiation, engineering performance, project management, risk control, and CAD/BIM production. A shell JV that merely signs contracts and routes profits offshore while lacking employees, premises, decision-making, and risk will fail the functional and economic-substance analysis and be recharacterised, and treaty benefits under the beneficial ownership test will be denied.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion Through Concealed Cross-Border Engineering Revenue and False Invoicing

Statutory sanction: Tax Administration Law Article 63 requires recovery of the unpaid tax plus a fine of 50% to 5 times the tax evaded and daily late-payment surcharge of 0.05%. Criminal Law Article 201 imposes imprisonment of up to three years or criminal detention plus fines where the evasion is relatively large and exceeds 10% of payable tax; three to seven years and heavier fines apply where the amount is especially large and exceeds 30% of payable tax.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil may be pierced under PRC Company Law Article 23 if the director or controlling shareholder abuses limited liability; the director can be personally liable for unpaid taxes and damages to creditors. The legal representative or actual controller may be placed on the serious tax violation blacklist and subjected to an exit-control/travel ban under Tax Administration Law Article 44, barred from senior management roles, and, if criminal liability is established, sentenced to imprisonment with seizure of personal assets and potential foreign-residency consequences.
Transfer Pricing Documentation Failure and Thin Capitalisation / Interest Stripping

Statutory sanction: The tax authority may recharacterise the transactions and adjust taxable income under EITL Article 41, disallow excess interest under Article 46, and impose interest at the benchmark rate plus 5 percentage points under Article 48. Failure to maintain transfer pricing documentation and annual related-party disclosure triggers penalties under Tax Administration Law Articles 62 to 64, including fines up to RMB 10,000 for non-filing and 50% to 5 times tax underpayment penalties if the defect causes underpayment; daily surcharges continue until full payment.

Director personal liability: A director who knowingly signs false TP documentation or moves intercompany prices for personal gain can be found personally liable for restitution to the company and creditors, may be included in the tax blacklisting and exit-control system, and may face criminal liability for tax evasion under Criminal Law Article 201 or embezzlement under Article 271, including imprisonment up to seven years and confiscation or attachment of personal assets.
Shell Conduit and Treaty Shopping on Dividend Repatriation

Statutory sanction: Reduced treaty rates are denied under STA Bulletin 2018 No. 9 and EITL Article 47; the tax authority may recompute the JV's taxable income and recapture withholding tax at the statutory 10% rate, plus daily surcharges and a fine of 50% to 5 times the under-withheld tax under Tax Administration Law Article 63. The denial applies retroactively, with possible additional adjustment for another ten years in serious avoidance cases.

Director personal liability: Directors and controlling persons who approve disguised distributions are civilly liable for damages to the company, minority shareholders, and creditors; the legal representative or actual controller will be placed on the tax blacklist and prevented from leaving China until the tax is settled. If conduit structuring is used to conceal taxable income and divert funds to personal offshore accounts, criminal liability under Criminal Law Article 201 may result in imprisonment of up to seven years, monetary fines, and judicial enforcement against personal assets, including through cross-border mutual legal assistance.
