Virtual offices and commercial mailboxes are categorically rejected for this industry in China. A dangerous goods and battery safety testing laboratory is treated by the State Administration for Market Regulation, the municipal tax authorities, and banks as a high-physical-presence industry requiring leasable, tangible premises with verified environmental, safety, and equipment certifications. Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital for a Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture under the Company Law (2018). The parties must agree on a registered capital amount in the Joint Venture Contract and Articles of Association. Capital contributions must be paid in full within the agreed period (typically within two years from issuance of the Business License) and must be deposited into a designated capital verification account, subject to verification by a certified public accountant.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China, including its provincial/municipal tax service bureaus; online electronic portal: the STA National Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax Bureau) platform, integrated with the Golden Tax-IV system and accessible through the official STA website (www.chinatax.gov.cn).

E-government filing portal

For a resident Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture (JV), tax registration is compulsory under the Tax Administration Law and the Administrative Measures for Tax Registration. After the JV obtains its Business License from the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR), the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) printed on the license automatically serves as the statutory Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) and VAT registration number. This is completed through the Multi-Licence Integration data-sharing mechanism, under which SAMR transmits incorporation data to the STA; the JV should then complete any remaining tax registration, tax-filing period confirmation, VAT taxpayer status set-up, and invoice quota activation through the e-Tax Bureau within 30 days of license issuance. A foreign non-resident entity without a permanent establishment or legal entity in China but deriving China-sourced income must apply for tax registration with the competent tax authority at the place of the mainland Chinese withholding payer and obtain a Non-Resident Enterprise Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) before the Chinese payer is permitted to withhold and remit tax.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation - The JV is treated as a resident enterprise for Corporate Income Tax (CIT) purposes and is taxed at entity level on its worldwide income; no pass-through or partnership classification is available. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation - The JV is treated as a resident enterprise for Corporate Income Tax (CIT) purposes and is taxed at entity level on its worldwide income; no pass-through or partnership classification is available. For VAT purposes, the JV is an independent taxable person and may be either a general VAT taxpayer or a small-scale taxpayer depending on turnover/classification; dividends paid to non-resident shareholders are generally subject to 10% withholding tax unless reduced by an applicable tax treaty.

Available election forms

None - PRC tax law does not provide a check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election mechanism. The only relevant election is the VAT taxpayer-status election: an enterprise with annual taxable turnover below RMB 5,000,000 may elect to be treated as a general VAT taxpayer if it maintains adequate accounting records; otherwise small-scale taxpayer status applies. For non-resident entities, the use of a Chinese withholding agent is a statutory mechanism, not an elective tax classification.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

6%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No turnover threshold for legal-person enterprises: VAT registration is mandatory upon business license issuance and commencement of taxable activities. Mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration applies when annual taxable turnover equals or exceeds RMB 5,000,000; turnover below this threshold may remain small-scale taxpayer unless a voluntary general taxpayer election is made.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods exports are zero-rated under the PRC VAT export-tax-rebate system: output VAT on the export sale is nil and eligible input VAT can be reclaimed after the export is declared to customs, the sales invoice is issued, and the export proceeds are repatriated into China through a licensed bank. Cross-border services, including qualifying technical testing, certification, and R&D-related laboratory services supplied to non-resident clients for consumption entirely outside mainland China, may be eligible for zero-rating or VAT exemption under Cai Shui [2016] No. 36 and STA Administrative Measures for Cross-Border Taxable Services. The services must be genuinely provided to an overseas recipient, the beneficiary must be the overseas recipient, and no domestic entity or person may directly consume the benefit of the services in China; otherwise the domestic 6% VAT rate applies.

  • Customs Export Goods Declaration (electronic customs statement filed through the China International Trade Single Window)
  • Commercial invoice, packing list, and export sales contract or purchase order
  • Bill of Lading (B/L), Air Waybill (AWB), or multimodal transport document evidencing the physical export movement
  • Bank-issued foreign exchange receipt proof or SAFE trade-monitoring record showing receipt of the export proceeds from the non-resident buyer
  • If applicable, customs temporary import/export manifests for test samples moved temporarily across the PRC border

China operates a mandatory electronic invoice fiscal control system. The JV must issue invoices through the STA e-Tax invoice service platform using the Fully Digitalized Electronic Invoice (FDEI) system (also called all-electronic VAT invoices). Invoice data are transmitted in real time to the STA tax control system; QR-code anti-counterfeiting, digital signatures, and electronic tax-control stamps form part of the anti-fraud clearance architecture. Special VAT invoices, required by business customers to claim input VAT credit, must be verified/reconciled through the STA VAT invoice verification platform before the buyer can take the credit. Credit notes and cancellations require red-letter (negative) electronic invoices through the same platform; physical tax-control devices are being phased out in favour of cloud-based digital invoicing.

STA and customs authorities may audit export zero-rating claims through cross-checks of customs declarations, logistics documents, bank receipts, and VAT invoice data. Unsubstantiated or fraudulent zero-rating claims will be rejected, resulting in recapture of input VAT credits, additional output VAT, late payment surcharges at 0.05% per day, and administrative penalties ranging from 50% to five times the tax underpaid. False export declarations and export-tax-rebate fraud may also trigger customs penalties, downgrade of customs credit status, and criminal liability under the PRC Criminal Law for fraudulent export-tax-rebate crimes. In fraud or evasion cases, the tax authority may reassess beyond the ordinary three-year limitation period.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a substantive current-account convertibility system with stringent capital-account controls administered by PBoC and SAFE. The RMB is convertible for legitimate trade and service transactions, but every payment must be processed through a licensed domestic bank that independently verifies contracts, invoices, customs declarations, transaction classification codes and the real business background. Capital account items, including foreign direct investment equity, shareholder loans, cross-border intercompany funding and profit repatriation, remain restricted and require SAFE registration, filing or approval before conversion. For the Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture, inbound foreign shareholding will enter through a SAFE-registered capital injection account, and the funds may be converted into RMB under the willing conversion regime for permitted operating purposes, subject to a negative-list undertaking restricting use in securities, speculative property investments, or domestic lending. Dividend remittances to the foreign parent require the JV's certified public accountant audit report, tax clearance certificate, board resolution declaring the dividend, and a SAFE-form FX remittance application. This dual regime - open current account, controlled capital account - imposes a high documentary burden on every cross-border treasury flow and makes the receiving bank the de facto FX regulatory gatekeeper for both inflow and outflow compliance.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Statutory monitoring timeline and mandatory conversion ratio: goods-trade export foreign-currency receipts are expected to be repatriated to the JV's designated current-account foreign-currency account in Mainland China generally within 180 days from the export customs declaration or invoicing date; any longer-offshore retention or non-repatriation requires prior filing or approval through the bank under SAFE's goods-trade foreign-exchange monitoring framework. The mandatory conversion ratio is 0 percent; the JV may keep 100 percent of export proceeds in foreign currency, convert all or any portion into RMB, or leave the mixture unchanged, provided every transaction is genuine, supported by underlying trade documentation, and reported through the receiving bank's external revenue reporting system.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the People's Bank of China (PBoC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating is 1 out of 10 as a domestic settlement endpoint for the mainland JV. Foreign fintech EMIs such as Wise are not licensed by PBoC to open local-currency bank accounts or provide domestic clearing services for mainland-incorporated legal entities. SAFE's capital-information system requires FDI inflows and export-proceeds collections to be settled through a licensed Chinese bank, not an offshore electronic-money institution. Routing shareholder capital or testing fees through Wise would create a severe reconciliation gap, risk quarantine of funds by the Chinese receiving bank under real-name and beneficial-owner rules, and fail to satisfy statutory FX reporting and tax verification obligations. Wise should be used only as a foreign-side remittance convenience or pre-funding utility by the overseas parent, never as a JV treasury account holder, clearing rail, or FX settlement counterparty.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The foreign shareholder must first complete SAFE Foreign-Investment Basic Information Registration through the chosen tier-1 bank using the capital-project information system, after the JV receives its business license. The bank will open a designated foreign-currency capital injection account in the JV name. The shareholder remits via SWIFT MT103, or MT202 for bank-to-bank cover, naming the JV as beneficiary and showing the SAFE FDI registration reference in the remittance information field, with the transaction purpose classified as foreign direct investment capital injection. The receiving bank verifies the payment against the SAFE registration, applies the correct external revenue transaction code, and requires a certified public accountant issuance of a capital verification report before the funds are treated as available for operating use. RMB conversion is executed under the willing conversion regime with a negative-list undertaking; no capital is automatically converted without the JV's instruction and the bank's compliance release.Outbound treasury disbursements for testing subcontractors, laboratory equipment purchases, royalties, or cross-border service fees will be settled primarily through SWIFT MT103 real-time direct payments, supported by signed service contracts and tax invoices. For larger equipment imports, the JV can open an irrevocable commercial letter of credit under SWIFT MT700 through the tier-1 bank, with presentation of bills of lading, inspection certificates and invoices. Documentary collections under URC522 may be used on a simple documents-against-payment or documents-against-acceptance basis where a standby LC is not required. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File threshold: annual related-party transactions exceeding RMB 100 million for services and intangibles, RMB 200 million for tangible goods, and RMB 100 million for financial asset transfers; Master File preparation is required when the multinational group's consolidated revenue exceeds RMB 5 billion, and Country-by-Country Reporting is triggered at the same RMB 5 billion group consolidated revenue threshold.

Economic substance scope: The PRC does not operate a UAE-style Economic Substance Regulation, but the State Taxation Administration applies the substance-over-form doctrine, the general anti-abuse rule under EIT Law Article 47, and the BEPS Action 6 treaty-shopping rules. A Chinese JV must maintain a real physical laboratory, qualified technical staff, independent risk control, and decision-making functions located in China; the Core Income-Generating Activities (CIGA) of UN38.3 drop testing and CE-RED testing must be performed in mainland China. Failure to demonstrate economic substance can result in denial of DTA benefits, denial of the 15% high-technology enterprise rate, and recharacterisation of the entire structure as a conduit arrangement.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Shell entity and treaty abuse

Statutory sanction: The tax authority invokes the general anti-abuse rule under EIT Law Article 47, recharacterises the JV as a conduit, denies the treaty rate, and re-imposes 25% corporate income tax on the shifted profit, adding a penalty of 50%-300% of the underpaid tax under Article 63 of the Tax Collection and Administration Law plus a 0.05% daily interest surcharge.

Director personal liability: Under Company Law piercing-the-veil doctrine, directors and actual controllers who abused the corporate identity are jointly and severally liable for the tax, interest, and penalties out of personal assets; exit restrictions and blacklisting are imposed under Article 44 of the Tax Collection and Administration Law; criminal prosecution for tax evasion under Criminal Law Article 201 carries imprisonment of up to 7 years and a fine of up to 5 times the evaded tax.
Transfer pricing documentation failure and artificial profit shifting

Statutory sanction: The State Taxation Administration makes a transfer pricing adjustment under EIT Law Article 41, imposing an arm's length return, adjusting taxable income upward by the difference, and levying an understatement penalty of 50%-300% under Article 63 of the Tax Collection and Administration Law; failure to file related-party disclosure forms additionally attracts administrative fines up to RMB 10,000 and daily interest on the adjusted amount.

Director personal liability: Courts may pierce the corporate veil and hold directors personally liable for the additional tax, interest, and penalties when they approved the sham pricing; the responsible legal representative will be placed on the travel ban / blacklist list; criminal liability for issuing false invoices under Criminal Law Article 205 can result in imprisonment of up to life imprisonment and confiscation of personal assets.
Outbound dividend withholding fraud

Statutory sanction: The withholding agent, the Chinese JV, is held liable for the unpaid 10% withholding tax of RMB 5 million under Article 69 of the Tax Collection and Administration Law, plus a penalty of 50%-100% of the unpaid tax and daily overdue interest; the forged TRC triggers criminal liability for forgery under Criminal Law Article 280 and tax fraud under Criminal Law Article 201.

Director personal liability: The directors who authorised the dividend remittance without withholding tax and stripped the JV's assets are personally liable for the tax deficiency under the corporate veil doctrine; the legal representative is blacklisted and prohibited from leaving China; criminal prosecution for foreign exchange evasion under Criminal Law Article 190 carries imprisonment of up to 10 years, and tax evasion under Article 201 carries imprisonment of up to 7 years, in addition to full asset restitution to the company.
