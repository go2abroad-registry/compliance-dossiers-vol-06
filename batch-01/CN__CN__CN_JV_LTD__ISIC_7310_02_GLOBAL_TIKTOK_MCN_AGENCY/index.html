Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum registered capital is imposed for a standard Sino-foreign equity joint venture limited liability company engaged in creator incubation and advertising agency services. Under Article 47 of the Company Law, the full amount of subscribed capital must be paid by each shareholder within five years after the issuance of the business license. The subscription amount and payment schedule are set out in the Articles of Association. No blocked escrow account or paid-up capital verification certificate is required at registration; however, paid-in contributions must subsequently be disclosed through the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; electronic registration and filing are performed through the provincial Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax Bureau) portal and the STA 12366 tax service platform.

E-government filing portal

A Sino-foreign equity joint venture first completes incorporation and obtains a business license from the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR). Upon incorporation, the legal representative or authorized agent must perform tax registration through the competent local tax authority's Electronic Tax Bureau within 30 days of obtaining the license. The taxpayer identification number is the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which is issued automatically at SAMR registration and activated for tax purposes via the e-Tax Bureau. For a foreign non-resident entity without a Chinese establishment, the non-resident must either register directly with the competent tax authority for a non-resident tax code or be registered through a Chinese withholding agent; the procedure requires submission of the home-jurisdiction tax registration certificate, constitutional documents, a China service contract or source documentation, and designation of a domestic contact person or withholding agent.

Standard VAT / GST rate

6%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 (CNY 5,000,000) annual taxable turnover, which is the mandatory threshold for general taxpayer registration; an incorporated joint venture must nevertheless complete basic VAT registration immediately upon establishment regardless of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under the VAT pilot policy, exported goods are eligible for zero-rating when they physically depart the PRC customs territory and the exporter obtains the relevant customs and foreign-exchange documentation. Cross-border taxable services may be zero-rated only if the service type is expressly included in the zero-rating catalogue issued by the Ministry of Finance and State Taxation Administration and the service is consumed entirely outside China. A Creator MCN agency providing talent representation and content management to an offshore platform or foreign creators generally falls into the VAT-exempt cross-border service category rather than the zero-rating category if the services are consumed wholly offshore; in that case input VAT on related purchases is not creditable. If a service is classified as zero-rated, the taxpayer must file the cross-border service declaration, retain the foreign-exchange receipt evidence, and be able to prove that neither the recipient nor the benefit of the service is located in China.

  • China Electronic Customs Export Declaration (Customs Declaration Form for Exported Goods) transmitted through the China Customs integrated system
  • Commercial invoice and packing list for the exported goods
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical departure from PRC customs territory
  • Bank receipt or Foreign Exchange Receipt Instruction evidencing receipt of export proceeds from the overseas buyer within the statutory foreign-exchange time limit
  • Signed cross-border service contract or master services agreement with the offshore counterparty for zero-rated or VAT-exempt service claims
  • Offshore consumption statement or service completion certificate issued by the overseas recipient, demonstrating that the service is used and consumed entirely outside China

China enforces the Golden Tax System (Phase IV). VAT special invoices and digital e-invoices must be issued through the tax-authority-authorized digital invoice platform, carrying a uniquely issued invoice code and digital verification. There is no third-party clearance requirement; the electronic tax bureau performs real-time issuance clearance, anti-fraud digital stamping, and quota management. Enterprises must apply for an e-invoice quota, use the Tax U-Key or approved digital token to issue invoices, and reconcile issued invoices electronically through the Electronic Tax Bureau.

The tax authority may retrospectively reverse zero-rating or VAT exemption when the supporting customs declarations, contracts, or payment evidence are unverified, fraudulent, or when export proceeds are not repatriated within the required period. Reclaimed output VAT, disallowed input VAT credits, and refunded amounts are clawed back with late-payment surcharge calculated from the original filing date. Under Article 63 of the Tax Collection and Administration Law, a tax penalty of 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax may be imposed. Cases involving fraudulent export tax refunds or fabricated export documentation may be transferred to judicial authorities and prosecuted under Article 204 of the Criminal Law, where the maximum penalty can include life imprisonment and confiscation of property.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a managed floating exchange-rate regime under a bifurcated convertibility framework. The Renminbi is fully convertible for current-account items such as trade in goods and services, dividends, and interest, subject to a genuine-transaction review and filing of supporting documentation with the designated foreign-exchange bank. Capital-account items remain heavily controlled: foreign direct investment inflows are admitted through a SAFE registration and a dedicated foreign-currency capital account, while outbound remittances derived from capital accounts are restricted until the enterprise proves an underlying real transaction and satisfies SAFE rules. Export proceeds are subject to repatriation and settlement obligations, although the historical mandatory surrender quota has been relaxed to a repatriation duty with voluntary conversion in practice. Profit remittance is a current-account item once audited financial statements, tax filings, and the foreign-invested enterprise annual FDI registration are verified; however, banks conduct anti-money-laundering and beneficial-ownership screening, and any deviation between the registered investment purpose and actual fund usage will block payment. The JV must therefore maintain a clean capital-chain dossier from injection through settlement and dividend declaration, including FIE annual reports, tax payment proofs, and consistent trade contracts before any clearance is granted.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For general trade, export foreign-currency receipts must be repatriated into a domestic designated foreign-exchange account within the export collection verification period, normally 90 days after customs declaration unless the underlying contract expressly provides longer credit terms and the bank accepts the supporting evidence. There is no fixed mandatory conversion ratio under the current voluntary settlement regime: the JV may retain up to 100% of verified export proceeds in a foreign-currency current account or convert them into RMB at the prevailing market rate, subject to the bank's authentic-transaction review and SAFE reporting within five working days of receipt.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC), acting through the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) as the delegated authority for foreign-exchange administration

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Low. Mainland China does not recognise a domestic EMI licence category comparable to the EU or Singapore e-money regimes. PBOC and SAFE require corporate accounts, foreign-exchange settlement, and cross-border payment clearance to be handled by licensed onshore banks; an offshore digital EMI such as Wise cannot open RMB or FX accounts for a mainland-incorporated JV. The TikTok Creator Payout and Wise B2B rails named in the target payment structure can only be accessed through a licensed bank partner or a properly licensed cross-border payment institution, and Wise may act only as a beneficiary-side payout channel after funds have legally crossed via SWIFT through the onshore bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All foreign-direct-investment capital must be registered with SAFE through the designated bank or the foreign investment information reporting system before the remittance is accepted. The non-resident shareholder sends an SWIFT MT103 with the beneficiary name exactly matching the JV business licence, the invoice or equity-subscription agreement attached, and a remittance purpose classified as foreign direct investment under the SAFE balance-of-payments code. The licensed bank credits the funds to the FDI foreign-currency capital account and the JV then applies for RMB settlement under capital account by submitting a settlement payment application, board resolution, contract or invoice, and a PRC certified public accountant capital verification report for paid-in capital certification. Turnover: There is no quantitative balance-sheet, turnover, or headcount exemption under Chinese law. Under Article 164 of the PRC Company Law (2018 revision, continued in substance by the 2023 revised Company Law effective 1 July 2024), every company, including a Sino-foreign equity joint venture, must prepare annual financial accounting reports and have them audited by an accounting firm licensed in China. The audit obligation exists even for a zero-turnover shelf JV. Additional mandatory audit triggering events include capital reduction, dissolution and liquidation, one-person limited company status, presence of institutional or state-owned investors requiring audited valuations, and any public offering or listing plan.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% on gross dividends paid by a PRC resident enterprise to a non-resident enterprise under PRC Corporate Income Tax Law Articles 3 and 37, subject to a reduced treaty rate when the non-resident shareholder meets beneficial ownership and residency requirements.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File is mandatory when related-party tangible-asset transactions exceed RMB 200 million annually, intangible-asset transactions exceed RMB 100 million annually, service transactions exceed RMB 100 million annually, financial-asset transactions exceed RMB 100 million annually, or other related-party transactions exceed RMB 40 million annually; Master File is mandatory when annual aggregate related-party transactions exceed RMB 1 billion.

Economic substance scope: China does not apply Caribbean-style Economic Substance Regulations, but the PRC Corporate Income Tax Law Article 47 and Tax Collection Administration Law Article 120 require substance over form, commercial reasonableness, and beneficial ownership; a Chinese-incorporated JV is a PRC tax resident whose Core Income-Generating Activities are in mainland China, and any offshore intermediate company claiming treaty benefits must prove that its own core income-generating activities, personnel, premises, and risk control functions are located in the claimed residence jurisdiction.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion - Masked PRC-Source TikTok/MCN Revenue

Statutory sanction: PRC Corporate Income Tax Law Articles 3, 6, and 37; Tax Collection Administration Law Article 63 imposes a penalty of 50% to 500% of the unpaid tax plus daily late-payment interest at 0.05%; criminal referral under Criminal Law Article 201 is available for fraudulent tax declarations exceeding the criminal threshold, carrying imprisonment of up to 7 years for substantial evasion.

Director personal liability: Courts can pierce the corporate veil under PRC Company Law if directors commingle personal and corporate funds, abuse limited liability, or intentionally defraud creditors; directors may be jointly and severally liable for the JV's unpaid taxes and restitution to the state treasury, subject to asset seizure, restricted-departure travel bans, blacklisting, disqualification as directors, and criminal imprisonment for deliberate evasion.
Abusive DTA Relief and Treaty Shopping Through a Shell Company

Statutory sanction: PRC Corporate Income Tax Law Article 47 and Tax Collection Administration Law Article 120 allow full recharacterization; the claimed treaty benefit is denied, the full 10% WHT is collected with daily late-payment surcharges, and penalties of 50% to 300% of the under-withheld amount may be imposed under withholding non-compliance rules; State Taxation Administration beneficial-ownership announcements disallow conduit arrangements lacking real activity.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign or instruct false treaty declarations, beneficial ownership certificates, or residency certificates face administrative penalties, civil joint liability for the resulting tax deficiency, exposure to criminal tax evasion charges, blacklisting of the corporate taxpayer, and travel restrictions that prevent the legal representative and finance director from leaving China until all tax liabilities and penalties are settled.
Transfer Pricing Distortion, Thin Capitalization, and Documentation Failure

Statutory sanction: PRC Corporate Income Tax Law Articles 41 and 46 permit the tax authority to make an arm's-length adjustment, disallow excess interest, add daily late-payment interest, and impose transfer pricing penalties; the Tax Collection Administration Law and special tax investigation rules allow penalties on underpaid tax from mispriced related-party transactions, and failure to maintain contemporaneous documentation exposes the JV to additional penalty assessments and adjusted taxable income.

Director personal liability: Directors and financial officers who knowingly approve false transfer pricing documentation, backdated contracts, or circular funding arrangements may be personally liable for the JV's tax losses under corporate veil-piercing principles; they face asset freezing, restricted departure, blacklisting, and criminal liability for fraud or tax evasion, including imprisonment terms of up to 7 years where deliberate concealment of China-source MCN profits is proven.
