Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China; online taxpayer portal: National Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax Bureau) accessible through https://www.chinatax.gov.cn and the provincial Electronic Tax Bureau endpoints.

E-government filing portal

Upon incorporation of the Sino-foreign Equity Joint Venture with the State Administration for Market Regulation, the JV is assigned an 18-character Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which is the statutory Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) and the VAT registration number. The JV must complete tax registration automatically through the one-stop SAMR-STA data sharing channel within 30 days of issuance of the business license; in practice the local Electronic Tax Bureau account is activated using the USCC, and the legal representative, finance officer and tax agent must pass real-name verification (biometric or e-certification) in the e-Tax Bureau. For VAT, the JV must file the VAT taxpayer status registration; if the estimated annual taxable sales exceed CNY 5,000,000, general VAT taxpayer registration is mandatory and requires evidence of accounting constitution and place of business; a JV below that threshold may register as a small-scale VAT taxpayer. No separate or additional VAT ID is issued other than the USCC. The foreign non-resident shareholder that does not have a permanent establishment in China must separately file Non-Resident Enterprise Tax Registration with the tax authority of the withholding agent (typically the JV or the resident payer) and obtain a Non-Resident Taxpayer Identification Number (NRTIN) before any China-source income payment, dividend distribution or cross-border service fee remittance; this NRTIN is also used for CIT withholding and VAT withholding where the non-resident is the supplier. The JV must also complete any required payroll/social insurance registrations through the one-stop platform, but the tax identity for all tax types is the USCC. A foreign non-resident enterprise with no establishment in China that supplies services into China is generally not required to obtain a local VAT ID; the domestic recipient withholds VAT at source.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation. The Sino-foreign equity JV is a PRC resident enterprise under the Enterprise Income Tax Law and is taxed at the entity level on worldwide income at a 25% CIT rate; it is not pass-through and the foreign shareholder has no direct tax attribute in the JV's income until distribution.

Available election forms

None. China does not operate a check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election regime. The JV cannot elect partnership-style pass-through or branch-equivalent treatment. The only available status election is VAT-related (small-scale taxpayer versus general VAT taxpayer), which is a collection mechanism, not an entity tax classification election, and general taxpayer status becomes mandatory once the CNY 5,000,000 VAT threshold is exceeded.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

6%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

CNY 5,000,000: mandatory threshold for general VAT taxpayer registration based on cumulative taxable sales in any consecutive 12-month period. There is no turnover threshold for registering for tax itself; VAT registration is mandatory from the date of business license issuance. A foreign non-resident supplier without an establishment does not get a turnover threshold; the domestic recipient withholds VAT.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under Caishui [2016] No. 36 Annex 4 and subsequent STA announcements, a Chinese resident VAT taxpayer may apply the 0% VAT rate to qualifying cross-border taxable services provided to non-resident recipients if the service is supplied entirely outside the PRC or is consumed wholly overseas, and the consideration is received in foreign exchange. The JV's ASO/SEO localization service is eligible only if the contract and operating facts place it in an enumerated category (most likely software services, information system services, or business process management services); ordinary advertising or marketing services are not automatically zero-rated and may instead be exempt or subject to 6% VAT unless the activity is documented as an offshore-consumed digital/IT service. The zero-rating claim must be substantiated by a formal cross-border service contract, a 0% VAT invoice, foreign exchange receipt, and proof that the end user is overseas.

  • Cross-border service contract with the overseas recipient, expressly stating that the ASO/SEO localization service is performed in China but wholly consumed outside the PRC
  • Commercial invoice and 0% VAT special invoice or Fully Digitalized Electronic Invoice (FDEI) issued through the National Electronic Invoice Service Platform
  • Foreign exchange repatriation evidence: Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or SAFE cross-border income declaration/bank receipt showing receipt of convertible currency from the overseas client
  • Service delivery record: SEO/ASO campaign reports, target-market performance dashboards, server logs, and completion certificates evidencing that the service was delivered outside the PRC
  • Customs Export Declaration (Export Goods Customs Declaration) and Bill of Lading/Air Waybill - required only for embedded physical goods; not applicable to pure digital ASO/SEO services
  • Tax authority export zero-rating workpaper and declaration filing retained for at least 10 years

China has phased out paper self-printed invoices in favor of the Fully Digitalized Electronic Invoice (FDEI) issued through the State Taxation Administration's national Electronic Invoice Service Platform. Every invoice must be transmitted to the STA in real time at issuance; the platform assigns a unique invoice number, applies a cryptographic anti-forgery digital signature and QR/verification code, and stores the invoice in the national tax database. Large-volume taxpayers may connect through the official e-invoice interoperability API (JSON/XML-based) instead of manual portal entry. Special VAT invoices are issued through the VAT Invoice Management System using the tax-control UKey or the FDEI platform; issuance above the approved monthly invoice quota is blocked by the system. The scheme is an approval-and-clearance model, not a private fiscal-stamp model; there is no third-party clearance.

If the foreign exchange consideration is not collected within 180 days from the service completion or customs declaration date, or if any document in the substantiation bundle is false or missing, the STA will claw back the 0% treatment and deem the supply taxable at 6% output VAT, collect the underpaid tax, impose late payment surcharge of 0.05% per day, and may impose an administrative fine of 50% to five times the tax amount under the Law on Administration of Tax Collection and Administration; where the claim is found to be fraudulent export tax refund, a fine of one to five times the refunded amount applies and criminal liability under Article 204 of the Criminal Law of the PRC may be pursued. There is no statute of limitations for recovering tax from fraudulent zero-rating claims, and taxpayers will be downgraded in tax credit status and subjected to expanded retrospective audits.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a managed floating exchange-rate regime with current-account convertibility and capital-account restrictions. For a Sino-foreign equity joint venture, foreign exchange for trade in goods and services, including ASO/SEO agency fees, app-store commissions and royalties, is convertible on the current account once the underlying transaction is real and documentation is presented to a designated bank; the bank submits the payment data to SAFE's digital services-monitoring system. Capital account items, such as paid-in equity capital, foreign borrowings, and cross-border loans, require advance registration, specific bank accounts, and verification before conversion. Export and foreign-service proceeds are not subject to a forced surrender ratio, but must be repatriated onshore and reported within the statutory verification window. Profit distribution and dividend remittances are permitted only after the eligible annual accounts are audited by a Chinese CPA, income tax is settled, and the board's dividend resolution plus SAFE dividend remittance registration are submitted. The JV must maintain a capital foreign exchange account for FDI injections and an operating foreign exchange account for current-account receipts; commingling the two is prohibited. RMB must be converted to foreign currency at negotiable spot rates intermediated by PBOC's China Foreign Exchange Trade System, and forward deliverables are allowed only for genuine underlying exposure. Any attempt to route capital via personal accounts or payment companies violates SAFE circulars and will trigger penalties, suspension of conversion privileges and potential RMB fund recovery.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE's current verification framework, export proceeds from goods trade must be received onshore into a designated foreign-exchange receipt account within 180 days from the date of customs export declaration, or within 180 days from the bill-of-lading date if customs declaration is not yet filed, with an extension up to 360 days permitted for documented credit terms. There is no fixed statutory mandatory conversion ratio; the exporting entity may retain up to 100% of eligible proceeds in a foreign-currency settlement account or sell up to 100% to a designated domestic bank at the prevailing spot rate as long as the trade documents match the receipt verification declarations. For a pure service and ASO agency, service-trade income must be reported through the bank under SAFE's service-trade reporting system and settled or retained under the same genuine-transaction principle; any intentional over-invoicing, delayed repatriation beyond the statutory window, or illegal parking offshore will trigger verification penalties and loss of settlement simplification privileges.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) and State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The foreign shareholder must remit the equity contribution via SWIFT MT103 or MT202 to the JV's foreign-exchange capital account at a domestic designated bank, referencing the SAFE foreign direct investment (FDI) registration number. The remittance purpose must be classified as paid-in FDI capital under SAFE's capital-account project code, not as a service payment. After the funds arrive, the JV obtains a Chinese CPA capital-contribution verification report, completes post-registration with SAFE, and only then may convert the capital balance to RMB in the capital account or use it for approved operating purposes. Wise or Payoneer capital injections are prohibited, and any personal-account routing will be rejected by SAFE and bank verification controls.For current-account service outflows such as app-store commissions, ASO platform fees, media buying and subcontractor payments, the primary clearing rail is an onshore-originated SWIFT MT103 message supported by the commercial invoice, signed service contract, statement of work, completion evidence, tax certificate and SAFE service-trade purpose code. Commercial letters of credit and documentary collections are not typical for intangible ASO/SEO services but may be used only if the engagement includes a physical media-buy or licensing component requiring bank intermediation; otherwise standard telegraphic transfer is the compliant settlement method. A standby letter of credit may be issued as a performance guarantee, while the underlying money movement still follows verified MT103 settlement.Onshore deliverable CNY/USD forward contracts with genuine current-account exposure and SAFE registration, used for a hedge horizon matching the trade invoice book.Onshore FX swaps and cross-currency swaps permitted when the JV has registered underlying debt or capital-flow exposure, with swap proceeds applied strictly to the registered commercial purpose.Offshore non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are not permissible onshore for a Mainland JV; a separate offshore parent-level hedge would be legally distinct and is not recommended as a substitute for SAFE-compliant onshore hedging.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: Low for primary treasury use. Wise B2B and Payoneer are not licensed as domestic settlement institutions or foreign-exchange banks in China and cannot open local CNY capital or current accounts for a Mainland JV. They may only serve as auxiliary payout rails for overseas collections after the funds pass through a licensed cross-border clearing partner of a Chinese bank; the legal corporate treasury spine must be a Tier-1 domestic bank. Using an EMI for inbound equity injection, statutory dividend remittances, or SAFE-verified trade payments is not compliant with PBOC and SAFE rules.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: CNY thresholds require preparation of a Local File when annual related-party transactions in the prior tax year reach or exceed: RMB 200 million for tangible property transfers, RMB 100 million for financial asset transfers, RMB 100 million for intangible asset ownership or licensing transfers, and RMB 40 million for interest, royalties, service fees, or other related-party transactions. Master File must be prepared when aggregate related-party transactions exceed RMB 1 billion. The Annual Related Party Transaction Reporting Form must be filed with the annual corporate income tax return regardless of thresholds.

Economic substance scope: China does not formally label the requirement as Economic Substance Regulations, but the tax authorities apply the substance-over-form doctrine, general anti-avoidance rule under CIT Law Article 47, and the beneficial-ownership test. The JV must demonstrate Core Income-Generating Activities in China, including contract negotiation, service delivery, pricing analytics, ASO/SEO campaign management, local risk assumption, and key decision-making. A letterbox entity without staff, premises, or substantive functions in China will be denied treaty benefits, recharacterized, and subjected to special tax adjustment.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and False Invoicing in Cross-Border ASO/SEO Services

Statutory sanction: Under Tax Administration Law Article 63, the taxpayer must pay the evaded tax plus interest and a monetary penalty of 50% to 500% of the evaded amount; criminal evasion under Criminal Law Article 201 carries up to three years imprisonment for relatively large evasion, and three to seven years for huge evasion exceeding 30% of payable tax; the enterprise is blacklisted under the Serious Tax Dishonesty List.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil may be pierced under PRC Company Law Article 20 when directors abuse limited liability to evade tax; directors may be personally liable for civil restitution of unpaid tax and surcharges, assets may be frozen, exit restrictions imposed, and criminal conviction may result in imprisonment up to seven years, fines, and confiscation.
Withholding Tax Non-Compliance and DTA Abusive Relief

Statutory sanction: Under Tax Administration Law Article 69, the withholding agent is liable to pay the unwithheld tax and a penalty of 50% to 100% of the unwithheld amount; late-payment surcharges continue to accrue; under CIT Law Article 47, the GAAR may deny treaty benefits and recharacterize the payment as a dividend, triggering additional 10% WHT and penalties.

Director personal liability: Directors who approve untaxed or treaty-abusive outbound payments may become personally liable for the uncollected tax when the corporate form is used as a sham; courts may attach personal assets, issue travel bans, and directors may face criminal liability for fraudulent withholding or tax evasion. Blacklisting and exit-control measures are also available against responsible persons.
Transfer Pricing Misstatement and Shell Company Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Under CIT Law Article 41, the tax authority may make a special tax adjustment and impose compensatory interest at a rate exceeding standard benchmark interest by five percentage points when contemporaneous documentation is absent; Article 47 GAAR may disregard the entire structure; tax penalties under the Tax Administration Law range up to five times the tax deficiency and administrative blacklisting may result.

Director personal liability: Directors who deliberately construct a shell JV or sign false transfer-pricing documentation can be held civilly liable for asset restitution and tax shortfall under veil-piercing principles; travel bans and asset freezes are possible, and criminal prosecution for collusion in tax evasion may result in imprisonment up to seven years for substantial intentional arrangements.
