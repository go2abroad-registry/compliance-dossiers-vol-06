Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA) and its Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax) online portal; national VAT data is processed under the STA's Golden Tax Phase IV information system.

E-government filing portal

After the State Administration for Market Regulation issues the business license, the Sino-foreign equity joint venture must complete tax registration within 30 calendar days via the Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax) portal. The Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) printed on the business license functions as the corporate taxpayer identification number (TIN/VAT registration number). For foreign non-resident enterprises with no permanent establishment requiring direct tax filing or export VAT refund claims, the non-resident taxpayer identification number is assigned by the competent STA office after submission of entity formation documents, non-resident enterprise registration information, evidence of the overseas registered address, appointment of tax representative (if applicable), and bank account/withholding agent details through the same e-Tax portal. The e-Tax registration synchronizes the VAT profile, corporate income tax registration, and payroll social contribution registration with the USCC as the master key.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation; the Sino-foreign equity joint venture is a Chinese resident enterprise taxed on worldwide income at the entity level; no pass-through treatment exists.

Available election forms

None. China does not operate a check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election regime. VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

There is no turnover exemption for corporate tax registration; every business must register after obtaining its business license. The mandatory VAT general taxpayer threshold is annual taxable sales exceeding CNY 5,000,000. Small-scale taxpayers (below the threshold) may use the simplified collection method, and small-scale taxpayers with monthly sales not exceeding CNY 100,000 are exempt from VAT during the applicable preferential policy period.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to goods physically exported from the Chinese customs territory once the export customs declaration is accepted, the goods leave China, the exporter is a VAT general taxpayer, the export sale is accurately invoiced, and export proceeds are collected from the overseas buyer. Qualifying cross-border services, including UI/UX design and digital design subscription services, may be zero-rated (exempt with input VAT refund) when the recipient is a non-resident enterprise or individual, the service is consumed wholly outside China, and the supply is within the STA's cross-border taxable service catalogue; the entity must hold full documentation evidencing the overseas recipient, service performance outside China, and foreign-currency payment.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Export Goods Declaration Form) carrying the export declaration number and export tax rebate classification code
  • Bill of Lading or airway bill proving physical export movement across the Chinese border
  • Commercial invoice and signed sales/service contract with the overseas buyer, including English/Chinese versions and exact service or goods description
  • Bank receipt of foreign exchange collection and the SAFE current-account export collection verification/monitoring record, evidencing funds received from the overseas counterparty within the repatriation period
  • VAT special invoice or ordinary VAT invoice issued for the export sale and recorded in the VAT invoice comprehensive service platform
  • Certificate of export goods or cross-border service transaction record, including the bank filing declaration for cross-border services and the non-resident recipient's legal existence certificate where required

Under the Golden Tax Phase IV digital administration, VAT invoices from full digitalized electronic invoice (e-invoice) issuance must be created through the STA's national e-invoice platform or an STA-integrated ERP/third-party API; each invoice obtains a unique invoice code and an encrypted anti-fraud electronic signature before legal delivery. Invoice data are transmitted in real time to the STA and must be reconciled monthly in the VAT invoice comprehensive service platform and e-Tax portal before the VAT return is filed. Red-letter (credit) invoices for cancellation or adjustment require the same fiscal clearance and must be matched to the original invoice data; paper tax-control invoices are legacy and being phased out except in transitional border/free-trade-zone scenarios.

The STA cross-checks customs declarations, VAT invoice issuance, foreign-exchange collection records, and corporate income tax filings through electronic data matching. A taxpayer that fraudulently obtains export zero-rating or refunds through fictitious exports, false service declarations, inflated values, or forged export collection documents must repay the refunded VAT on demand and is liable under the PRC Tax Collection Administration Law to a penalty of one to five times the tax underpaid, plus late-payment surcharges and interest. If the foreign-exchange proceeds are not repatriated within 180 days without an approved SAFE exception, the zero-rated claim is treated as unsupported and is clawed back retroactively with statutory late fees. Persistent or large-scale fraud is referred for criminal prosecution under the PRC Criminal Law for false VAT invoices and export tax refund fraud, together with cancellation of VAT general taxpayer status and suspension of future export refund rights.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a managed floating exchange rate regime. Current-account transactions are broadly convertible under SAFE supervision, but capital-account transactions remain subject to the Foreign Investment Catalogue, negative list review, and pre-registration or verification procedures. For a Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture, FDI capital can be remitted in CNY or foreign currency after completing Foreign Investment (FDI) registration and opening a dedicated capital account at a domestic designated bank. The foreign exchange in this capital account can be settled into RMB for approved operational use, but must be used within the entity's business scope; unconverted portions remain in foreign currency. Profit repatriation to non-resident shareholders is permitted after statutory audits and tax clearance, provided the open-year accounting statements confirm distributable profits and income tax and any withholding obligations are settled, but the process requires submission of an assurance statement to the settlement bank. The JV must also comply with daily and monthly foreign exchange monitoring reporting, actual-use verification, and SAFE's random inspections. In practice, banks will independently assess the commercial authenticity of each payment, and any mismatch between the stated design-subscription business model and the payment narrative triggers a documentation review and possible suspension, making convertibility operationally simple but procedurally rigorous.

Export proceeds surrender rules

China no longer imposes a fixed percentage statutory surrender ratio for export receipts. Under SAFE's trade-in-goods foreign exchange management regime, all export proceeds must be repatriated into mainland China and credited to the enterprise's current-account foreign exchange account or settled into RMB through a designated bank within the contractually agreed collection period, which SAFE normally expects not to exceed 90 days from the export declaration or bill-of-lading date unless the enterprise has demonstrated and registered exceptional trade credit terms. The enterprise may retain foreign currency in a current account without compulsory conversion, but any late collection beyond the prescribed window requires a supplementary post-verification declaration, supporting sales contracts, customs declarations, and a written explanation to SAFE; false declaration or offshore parking of receivables will expose the entity to penalties and downgrade to B-class trade verification.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) acting through the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Low viability for onshore compliance. Fintech EMI rails such as Wise and Stripe are not licensed by the domestic banking and insurance regulator or SAFE as deposit-taking institutions or foreign-exchange settlement banks inside mainland China. A mainland-registered equity joint venture cannot lawfully use a foreign electronic money institution as its primary treasury account or statutory settlement account, because all RMB and foreign currency receipts and payments must flow through designated domestic banks subject to SAFE's post-verification platform. Stripe may still be used as a downstream collection tool for overseas D2C subscription revenue only if the funds are subsequently swept into the JV's domestic bank account through compliant cross-border collection, and Wise can support ancillary international payment instructions initiated from the domestic bank, but neither replaces the domestic onshore clearing path required for corporate tax, customs, social security, and capital control.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The JV must complete the Foreign Direct Investment record in the national enterprise credit system and obtain the SAFE FDI registration code before any remittance. The foreign shareholder then wires the subscribed capital from its overseas bank account to the JV's dedicated capital account at the domestic Tier-1 bank using SWIFT MT103, stating in field 70 the corporate reference to the FDI registration code, the words PAYMENT FOR SUBSCRIBED CAPITAL or EQUITY CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION, and the applicable balance-of-payments investment purpose code. On landing, the domestic bank verifies the remittance against the FDI registration, monitors actual-use compliance under SAFE, and issues a capital verification letter after the funds are credited. Any subsequent conversion into RMB must be made through the bank's foreign exchange settlement system and earmarked exclusively for categories within the JV's business scope, including employee salaries, rent, subcontractor fees, software licensing, and operating expenses.For recurring operating trade costs such as overseas design-library subscriptions and UI asset licenses, clearing shall be routed through SWIFT MT103 from the JV's RMB or foreign currency general account after submitting the authentic documentation package comprising the service contract, tax invoice, proof of service receipt, and overseas income tax withholding certificates. For high-value import of software or specialized design hardware, a Commercial Letter of Credit is viable for consignment-based trade, with documents presented through the advising bank and payment after acceptance. Documentary collections may be used for small-value shipment-based transactions but are less efficient for digital design services where no physical transport documents exist. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low viability for onshore compliance. Fintech EMI rails such as Wise and Stripe are not licensed by the domestic banking and insurance regulator or SAFE as deposit-taking institutions or foreign-exchange settlement banks inside mainland China. A mainland-registered equity joint venture cannot lawfully use a foreign electronic money institution as its primary treasury account or statutory settlement account, because all RMB and foreign currency receipts and payments must flow through designated domestic banks subject to SAFE's post-verification platform. Stripe may still be used as a downstream collection tool for overseas D2C subscription revenue only if the funds are subsequently swept into the JV's domestic bank account through compliant cross-border collection, and Wise can support ancillary international payment instructions initiated from the domestic bank, but neither replaces the domestic onshore clearing path required for corporate tax, customs, social security, and capital control.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File is mandatory if any of the following annual related-party transaction thresholds are met: (1) tangible asset transfers exceeding RMB 200 million; (2) financial asset transfers exceeding RMB 100 million; (3) intangible asset transfers exceeding RMB 100 million; (4) other related-party transactions (including royalties, interest, and service fees) exceeding RMB 40 million. A Master File is required when the consolidated group revenue exceeds RMB 5.5 billion.

Economic substance scope: Although China has no standalone Economic Substance Regulations akin to offshore financial centres, the core income-generating activities (CIGA) doctrine is embedded in the Enterprise Income Tax Law and the General Anti-Abuse Rule (Art. 47). A Chinese resident JV must demonstrate real business operations, physical office, employees, and board decisions in China. For foreign shareholders claiming treaty benefits, an entity without meaningful substance will be denied 'beneficial owner' status and treaty relief will be rejected.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Concealment of Revenue

Statutory sanction: Under Article 63 of the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection, the tax authority recovers the underpaid tax plus a daily late-payment surcharge of 0.05%, and imposes a fine ranging from 50% to 5 times the underpaid amount. If the evaded amount meets the criminal threshold, Article 201 of the Criminal Law imposes imprisonment for up to seven years (or more for particularly large amounts) and a criminal fine.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil may be pierced under Article 20 of the PRC Company Law, making the director jointly and severally liable for the company's unpaid taxes. The director also faces an exit ban (blacklisting under Article 44 of the Tax Collection Law) and potential criminal prosecution as the direct responsible person, with imprisonment risk.
Transfer Pricing Documentation Failure

Statutory sanction: Under Article 44 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, the tax authority may assess income by using deemed profit methods when documentation is absent. Under the Special Tax Adjustment Measures, an additional 5 percentage points is added to the computed interest on the tax adjustment. Separately, under Article 62 of the Tax Collection Law, failure to file required declarations may attract a fine up to RMB 10,000.

Director personal liability: Where the director directed or approved the concealment of related-party transactions, he or she is treated as the direct responsible person. The tax authority can request immigration control to restrict the director's departure until the adjusted tax, interest, and penalties are paid. Repeat violations can expose the director to criminal liability for fraudulent declaration.
Treaty / DTA Relief Abuse (Unlawful 'Beneficial Owner' Misrepresentation)

Statutory sanction: Under SAT Announcement 2018 No. 9, the tax authority may deny the treaty benefit and re-collect the under-withheld tax, with late-payment surcharge. If the false representation amounts to intentional concealment, Article 63 of the Tax Collection Law imposes penalties of 50% to 5 times the underpaid tax. The misuse may also be characterised as tax fraud, leading to criminal prosecution under Article 201 of the Criminal Law.

Director personal liability: The director who signed the false beneficial ownership declaration can be personally assessed for failure to withhold correctly. The corporate veil may be pierced to seize personal assets for the tax deficiency, and the director may be place on the tax blacklist, barred from overseas travel, and subject to criminal imprisonment of up to three to seven years for tax fraud.
