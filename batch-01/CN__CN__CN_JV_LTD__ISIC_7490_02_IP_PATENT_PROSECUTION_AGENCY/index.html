Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None under the 2023 Company Law or the Foreign Investment Law for a Sino-foreign equity joint venture limited liability company. The registered capital is contractually agreed in the Articles of Association and must be fully subscribed, with paid-in contributions effected within five years from incorporation under the contribution schedule. No blocked escrow account is required, but foreign-invested capital must enter through a designated foreign exchange settlement account and remain subject to foreign exchange regulatory restrictions on withdrawal. As a patent prosecution agency, the entity must maintain the statutory number of qualified patent attorneys and a fixed lawful business premises as required by the Patent Agency Regulations.

Funding & unlock path: Not Applicable to an office-based professional services company; no Civil Defense fire permit and no structural load inspection are required. The premises must nevertheless satisfy ordinary office fire safety obligations under the Fire Protection Law of the PRC, including fire extinguishers, accessible exits and emergency lighting, and the employer must comply with occupational safety registration under the Work Safety Law for office workplaces.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; online services via the STA's national integrated electronic tax filing platform (e-Tax Portal).

E-government filing portal

For a newly established Sino-foreign equity joint venture limited liability company, the entity must first obtain its Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) from the local Administration for Market Regulation (AMR) in the corporate registration process. Thereafter, within 30 days of establishment, the company must complete tax registration by submitting the registration information through the e-Tax Portal or at the in-charge State Taxation Administration local office. The tax registration application requires the articles of association, AMR business license, legal representative ID, registered address proof, and the foreign investor's certificate of incorporation (with apostille/legalization if required). After successful verification, the STA issues the tax registration file and confirms the Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN), which is the USCC itself. The foreign non-resident shareholders that are not executing any direct primary business activities in China do not typically require a separate Chinese TIN; instead, they must provide their home-country tax identification number in the tax registration documentation and, where they derive Chinese-source income subject to withholding, the local subsidiary or withholding agent will withhold at source. If the foreign party later establishes a branch or permanent establishment in China, that branch must separately register for a non-resident enterprise tax number with the in-charge tax authority and file monthly returns.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under the Notice on the VAT Policies for Cross-Border Taxable Activities (Caishui [2016] No. 36) and related interpretations, a Chinese VAT taxpayer providing intellectual property prosecution services (including PCT patent applications and international trademark registration) to a foreign client is eligible for the 0% VAT export rate when the service is fully consumed outside China and the conditions are met: the service recipient is located overseas; the service has no direct link to goods or assets inside China; the consideration is received in foreign currency; and the service is genuinely rendered from China to an overseas beneficiary. The provider must retain documentary evidence demonstrating the offshore nature of the consumption, such as contracts, invoices, instructions from the foreign recipient, and outward delivery records.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bill of Entry / Export Declaration Form) — required for any incidental physical goods export or sample dispatch
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill — evidencing international carriage when goods are physically exported
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or bank foreign-exchange settlement voucher — confirming receipt of foreign currency in China within the mandatory period
  • Written service contract and commercial invoice — specifying the cross-border service and overseas recipient
  • Proof of offshore service performance — including correspondence with foreign patent and trademark offices, project reports, and delivery logs

China enforces a centralised VAT invoice management system operated by the State Taxation Administration. Every VAT-registered company is mandated to issue invoices through the STA's e-invoicing infrastructure (the electronic invoice platform) and, unless an exemption applies, must use the tax control equipment or the new full digital electronic invoice regime. All invoice data, including the electronic anti-counterfeit barcode, is cleared in real time with the central tax database through an application programming interface (API) submission channel for high-volume issuers. Issuers must transmit invoice details and monthly VAT return data to the STA through this API, and each invoice must carry the STA-issued unique invoice code and a two-dimensional anti-counterfeit code to be valid for input credit recognition. Failure to comply with the e-invoice issuance mandate leads to rejection of input VAT credit and potential penalties.

Zero-ratings are rigorously examined by the State Taxation Administration and the General Administration of Customs. If a claim is found to be fraudulent, unverifiable, or based on false or incomplete documentation, all previously refunded input VAT is clawed back, and the taxpayer must pay late-payment interest at 0.05% per day on the recovered amount. In addition, a penalty of 50% to 500% of the tax involved may be imposed under the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection. Criminal liability may be pursued under the Criminal Law for export-fraud activities, including imprisonment and fines. Taxpayers may also be placed on a high-risk audit blacklist, losing accelerated clearance privileges and facing enhanced scrutiny of all future export claims.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Under SAFE's current account convertibility regime, RMB is freely convertible for current account transactions such as trade royalties, service fees, and profit distributions subject to documentary validation. Capital account transactions remain tightly controlled and require separate registration and quota approvals. For a Sino-foreign equity joint venture, registered foreign equity capital may be converted into RMB through a capital account settlement process known as FDI conversion after obtaining the FDI foreign exchange registration, and the converted RMB capital must be used within the declared business scope. Profit remittance hurdles include mandatory tax clearance with withholding tax certificates, audited financial statements, prior-year loss verification, and SAFE-compliant documentation; dividends may be remitted only after a tax certificate is obtained and the bank verifies the authenticity of the underlying distribution. Export proceeds surrender requirements have evolved from mandatory percentages to repatriation obligations; enterprises are no longer forced to convert a fixed ratio into RMB, but must repatriate export earnings to domestic accounts within the prescribed timeline. Overall convertibility is asymmetric: current account flows are liberalized, while capital account inflows and outflows, especially outbound remittances of capital-account proceeds, are subject to case-by-case approval, real-demand verification, and post-clearance monitoring. This reality requires a treasury blueprint built on strict SAFE registration discipline and documentary preservation.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Statutory timeline: export foreign exchange earnings must be repatriated to China within 180 days after export customs declaration, unless an offshore retention approval is obtained. Mandatory conversion ratio: none; enterprises may retain 100% of export proceeds in foreign currency or voluntarily settle them into RMB at a domestic bank, subject to truthfulness verification and SAFE reporting.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) and State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Offshore fintech EMIs such as Wise and Revolut are legally barred from providing principal domestic corporate banking services in China; they do not hold a Chinese commercial banking license and cannot open RMB settlement accounts, receive FDI capital, or clear statutory patent office fees on behalf of a domestic JV. They may be used only as supplemental cross-border payment channels when partnered with a licensed Chinese bank or licensed cross-border payment institution, and even then their scope is limited to non-statutory operational invoices, never for capital account injections, tax payments, or official patent registration fees. The correct treasury structure is a licensed domestic Tier-1 bank as the spine and the EMI only as an optional non-core payment railing for low-value professional disbursements.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound FDI capital injection must be routed by SWIFT MT103 from the foreign shareholder's bank to the JV's FDI capital escrow account held at a Chinese Tier-1 bank, using the correct FX purpose code and the SAFE-issued Foreign Exchange Business Registration Certificate number. Upon receipt, the bank performs capital settlement into RMB under the FDI registration, records the transaction in the SAFE system, and a certified Chinese public accountant issues a capital verification report confirming paid-in capital. No inbound capital may be used for unapproved domestic investments or equity transfers without a separate SAFE approval.Trade disbursements for patent prosecution fees, international trademark registration, and professional legal services should be cleared via SWIFT MT103 customer transfers for direct fee payments to official patent offices, via Commercial Letters of Credit where the service contract requires documentary conditioned payment, or via Documentary Collections against signed delivery proofs where the counterparty demands simpler documentary control. All outbound payments must carry the underlying contract, invoice, and the appropriate SAFE service-trade purpose code; tax withholding certificates must be presented where Chinese WHT applies.Onshore RMB forward FX settlement and sale contracts with a licensed Chinese bank, backed by real outstanding trade or service transactions and documentary evidence of expected receivables/payables.FX swaps for temporary liquidity conversion between RMB and a trade currency, with an underlying transaction background and a committed maturity date.Cross-currency swaps for cross-border financing and interest-rate risk management, subject to SAFE approval and external debt registration where applicable.Onshore FX options for hedging anticipated but uncertain future cash flows, permitted under real-demand hedging rules and only with a licensed domestic bank.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are not a permitted onshore hedging instrument for Chinese resident entities; if used, they must be executed offshore with a non-resident licensed counterparty and fall outside the domestic regulatory perimeter, requiring separate board approval and treasury policy sign-off.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Contemporaneous documentation mandatory where total related-party transactions exceed RMB 100 million in a single category or RMB 200 million in aggregate under SAT Announcement [2016] No. 42; disclosure form and contemporaneous file due by the annual CIT filing deadline (May 31). Specific thresholds: RMB 40 million for tangible property, RMB 100 million for intangibles, RMB 100 million for services, RMB 100 million for financial assets, and RMB 20 million for financing.

Economic substance scope: Mainland China enforces economic substance through the General Anti-Avoidance Rule in Article 47 of the EIT Law and SAT Announcement [2017] No. 6, plus the benefificial ownership doctrine under SAT Announcement [2019] No. 35. A Chinese JV engaged in patent prosecution must show real operational substance: employees, decision-making autonomy, premises, books and records, and client contracts. Core Income-Generating Activities (CIGA) comprise drafting, filing, docketing, office-action responses, client consultation, and final approval of patent/trademark filings. Offshoring these activities to a shell without personnel or premise will trigger income reattribution and denial of treaty benefits.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fabricated Expense and Transfer Pricing Abuse

Statutory sanction: Under Article 63 of the Tax Administration Law and EIT Law Article 63, a civil penalty of 50% to 5 times the evaded tax plus daily surcharge of 0.05%; under Criminal Law Article 201, evasion exceeding RMB 50,000 and 10% of taxable amount is punishable by imprisonment up to 3 years and fine; if evasion exceeds 30% of taxable amount, imprisonment of 3 to 7 years with unlimited fines.

Director personal liability: Directors who signed or directed the fraudulent scheme are jointly and severally liable for the unpaid tax, interest, and penalties under judicial interpretation and Company Law Article 20; they are subject to the tax-dishonesty blacklist, travel ban at the border, asset freeze/seizure, disqualification from directorship of any enterprise, and criminal imprisonment up to 7 years, with civil restitution of diverted funds.
Treaty Shopping / Failure to Withhold Dividend WHT

Statutory sanction: Under Article 47 of the EIT Law and SAT Announcement [2019] No. 35, treaty benefits are denied, the conduit is disregarded, and the full 10% WHT is reassessed with interest and a penalty of 50% to 5 times the unpaid tax; failure to withhold under Article 69 of the Tax Administration Law attracts a penalty of 50% to 3 times the amount not withheld.

Director personal liability: Directors who authorized the false TRC submission or approved the dividend without withholding are personally liable for the WHT and penalties; the blacklist triggers exit-control at the border, personal bank account freezes, and denial of high-consumption privileges. Criminal exposure: imprisonment up to 7 years for tax evasion under Article 201; up to 10 years for fraud under Article 266 if forged documents are used, with civil liability for the full amount of unpaid WHT plus statutory interest.
Illegal Cross-Border Profit Repatriation / Beneficial Ownership Concealment & Shell Company Abuse

Statutory sanction: Under SAFE cross-border remittance regulations, illegal transfers are punished by confiscation of illegal gains and a fine of 30% to 100% of the amount; the entity is also fined up to RMB 300,000 for beneficial ownership registration failure under the 2024 Company Law reform. Tax reassessment plus a 50%-5x evasion penalty applies; under Criminal Law Article 191, money laundering of tax evasion proceeds carries imprisonment up to 7 years and fines up to RMB 10 million.

Director personal liability: The director who authorized the sham wire is personally liable for the SAFE fine and the illegal transfer amount; the director is placed on the tax/AML dishonesty list, triggering travel bans, routine border denial, asset freezing, and seizure. Criminal conviction under Article 191 imposes 3 to 7 years' imprisonment and confiscation of personal property; civil liability requires restitution of all diverted funds plus statutory interest; the director is permanently barred from serving as an officer of any company.
