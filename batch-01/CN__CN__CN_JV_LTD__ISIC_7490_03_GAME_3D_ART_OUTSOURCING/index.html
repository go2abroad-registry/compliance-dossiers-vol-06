Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None. The PRC Company Law (2023 revision) imposes no statutory minimum paid-up capital for an ordinary limited liability company, but all shareholders must subscribe to the registered capital in the Articles of Association and must contribute the full subscribed capital within five years from incorporation unless a special statutory exception applies. No blocked escrow account is required. A capital verification report is not generally required unless the registered capital is contributed with non-monetary assets or the local SAMR requests an appraisal report for in-kind contributions.

Funding & unlock path: Not Applicable for a Civil Defense fire permit because the premises are leased office units, not a production or storage site. Occupational safety is limited to workstation ergonomics, electrical safety and personal data confidentiality because the operation is digital and consulting in nature.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China, exercised through provincial and municipal State Tax Service Bureaus; the national online electronic portal is the Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax Bureau) of the STA, accessible through www.chinatax.gov.cn and the provincial e-Tax Bureau service websites.

E-government filing portal

For a Sino-foreign equity joint venture established in China, the foreign investor completes the foreign-invested enterprise incorporation process with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR). The Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) printed on the business license is the statutory Tax Identification Number (TIN), VAT registration number, and enterprise identifier for all tax administration purposes; no separate TIN application is needed for a domestically incorporated entity. Tax registration is completed through the multi-registration integration mechanism, whereby SAMR incorporation data is transmitted to the STA and the enterprise tax registration record is automatically opened on the e-Tax Bureau. For a foreign non-resident enterprise that has no Chinese establishment but derives China-source taxable revenue through services or transactions and needs a Chinese tax identity for withholding or VAT compliance, the non-resident enterprise must file a non-resident enterprise tax registration with the competent local STA office, providing notarized and legalized foreign incorporation documentation, board resolutions, the relevant cross-border contract, bank account details, and evidence of the Chinese withholding agent. The local STA then creates a non-resident taxpayer registration record used for enterprise income tax withholding, VAT compliance, and certificate issuance. In all cases, the foreign investor and the Chinese joint venture must also complete foreign exchange registry formalities with SAFE-qualified banks for outward and inward remittances.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Chinese renminbi is convertible on the current account for legitimate trade and service transactions, including the import/export of goods, royalties, and cross-border service fees, subject to verification of underlying documentation. However, the capital account remains heavily regulated and controlled. Fund injections into a Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture as registered capital are treated as foreign direct investment and require a separate FDI foreign exchange registration with SAFE or an authorized bank. Dividend remittances offshore are permitted only after the completion of annual audit, tax filing, tax clearance, and submission of the relevant board resolution. Profit repatriation is not an immediate right; the enterprise must first verify the authenticity of the profits through audited financial statements and the local tax withholdings. The RMB is not freely tradable outside mainland China on a full capital account basis, and any cross-border capital movements such as shareholder loans, equity transfers, and liquidation proceeds must be submitted to SAFE for approval or filing. Foreign currency receipts from service exports are considered current account receipts and may be retained or converted, but the enterprise must document the underlying trade or service contract. Overall, the convertibility regime is moderate and functional for operational cash flows, but every cross-border capital movement must align with SAFE administrative rules and the domestic banking system's compliance framework.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE's goods and services trade administration, the historical mandatory surrender of export proceeds has been abolished. Exporting and service-providing entities are not required to convert foreign currency receipts into RMB at a fixed ratio. However, all export-related foreign currency proceeds must be repatriated into China through an authorized domestic bank within the collection period specified by the underlying contract and, in the absence of a specifically approved extension, generally within 180 days of the export declaration or service completion date under SAFE's trade monitoring framework. The enterprise may hold such proceeds in a foreign exchange settlement account or sell them to a domestic bank at the prevailing market rate. Each receipt must be matched against the relevant customs declaration, contract, invoice, and receipt verification records in the SAFE digital platform, and any overdue collection must be justified and reported.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

PayPal Fit: High

Offshore digital fintech EMIs such as standard non-licensed European/Singapore electronic money institutions are not legally permitted to maintain domestic corporate accounts for Chinese registered entities. Mainland China requires all RMB corporate accounts to be held with PBOC-licensed banking institutions or licensed payment institutions. International EMI platforms can provide ancillary payment processing for the entity's foreign-currency receivables, such as PayPal collections, but the funds must eventually be settled through an onshore partner bank in RMB or a licensed cross-border payment product approved by SAFE. These EMIs are not a substitute for a corporate bank account and cannot serve as the main treasury rail. The viability of using an offshore EMI is therefore low for primary account infrastructure, while the viability of PayPal as a sales-collection tool is medium, subject to strict reconciliation, tax reporting, and the limitation that PayPal payout is processed through a licensed Chinese partner bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital for the JV must be injected through the FDI Foreign Exchange Capital Account opened with a designated domestic bank after completing the FDI direct investment registration with SAFE or the authorized bank. The overseas investor will remit USD, EUR, or other contractually permitted currency by SWIFT MT103 or MT202, and the remittance instruction must clearly state the purpose as equity investment in the Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture. The corresponding SAFE-approved balance-of-payments purpose code for foreign direct investment capital contribution must be used. Upon receipt, the bank will verify the amount against the FIE establishment approval, the joint venture contract, the negative-list regulatory position, and the FDI registration certificate. The capital must pass the bank's capital verification procedures; a certified Chinese accounting firm will issue a capital verification report confirming that the foreign investor has actually contributed the registered capital. The funds can then be converted into RMB or retained as foreign currency under SAFE rules, but the use of the capital funds within mainland China is restricted to the entity's actual operating purposes and cannot be used for speculative investments, foreign exchange forward bets, or unauthorized lending.For service-based trade settlement such as 3D art outsourcing, SWIFT MT103 is the primary rail for direct telegraphic transfer of service fees to overseas contractors and sub-contractors. The payment must be supported by the service contract, commercial invoice, statement of work completion, and the required Chinese tax withholding certificate for overseas service payments. Commercial Letters of Credit are generally unsuitable for pure 3D art services but may be used if the contract bundles software development with tangible hardware deliverables. Documentary collections may be used for invoice-backed settlement in certain trading relationships, involving the presenting bank forwarding shipping or service acceptance documents to the buyer's bank against payment or acceptance. All outbound current-account payments are subject to audit by the remitting bank; if the amount exceeds a bank-defined threshold, the enterprise must provide the underlying transaction dossier. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% statutory withholding on dividends paid by a Chinese resident enterprise to a non-resident corporate shareholder that has no permanent establishment in China or whose dividends are not effectively connected with such an establishment.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Quarterly EIT prepayment returns are due within 15 days after each quarter-end; the annual Form A100000 is due within 5 months after the fiscal year-end, i.e. on or before 31 May for calendar-year taxpayers.
