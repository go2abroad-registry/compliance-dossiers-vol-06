Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China, operating through the National Unified Electronic Tax Administration Platform (etax.chinatax.gov.cn) and supported by the Golden Tax Phase IV administrative, invoice-control, and data-analytics systems.

E-government filing portal

For the Sino-foreign equity joint venture, tax identity is established through incorporation rather than a separate tax-registration certificate. Step 1: incorporate with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) and obtain the Business License bearing the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC). Step 2: under the multi-certificate integration reform, SAMR automatically transmits registration data to the STA, and the USCC becomes the legal Tax Identification Number and VAT registration number for the entity. Step 3: on the National Unified Electronic Tax Administration Platform, the JV must complete its taxpayer profile, verify the legal representative and financial officer identities, report its accounting system and financial reporting rules, select VAT and CIT filing cycles, register its bank accounts with the STA, and apply for general VAT taxpayer status where annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000 or when necessary to recover input VAT and access export zero-rating. Step 4: for export VAT purposes, the JV must register its export tax refund qualification with the competent tax bureau and maintain foreign exchange receipt records. Step 5: for a foreign non-resident entity without a Chinese establishment, no USCC is generally issued; tax identity is recorded through a non-resident enterprise tax registration and contract registration performed by the domestic withholding agent at the tax authority where the withholding agent is located, using the non-resident's home-country tax identification number or other identifying details acceptable to the STA.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None as to check-the-box or hybrid mismatch elections. China does not allow pass-through treatment for an incorporated JV; the entity is mandatorily a Chinese resident enterprise taxed at entity level on worldwide income at the 25% corporate income tax rate. Dividends paid to foreign shareholders are subject to 10% withholding corporate income tax unless reduced by an applicable double taxation treaty. VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

6%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

All incorporated entities are automatically VAT-registered upon establishment. Mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration is triggered when annual taxable turnover exceeds RMB 5,000,000; below that threshold the JV may be classified as a small-scale VAT taxpayer unless voluntary general VAT taxpayer status is approved. As an export-oriented digital platform, registration as a general VAT taxpayer is recommended to preserve input VAT recovery and export zero-rating rights.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For goods, zero-rating is available when the goods are physically exported from China and the standard export declaration and foreign exchange receipt requirements are satisfied. For cross-border services, zero-rating is limited to the positive list in Cai Shui [2016] No. 36, including software services, information system services, business process management services, R&D and design services, provided to an overseas recipient and completely consumed outside China. The WebNovel subscription service must be carefully classified: if treated as a value-added telecommunications or digital-content service not on the zero-rating list, the supply may qualify only for the cross-border VAT exemption under State Taxation Administration Announcement No. 29 of 2016, subject to proof of offshore recipient, offshore consumption, and payment from outside China. Where Apple App Store or Google Play acts as merchant of record, the JV must retain the store settlement reports, subscriber territory data, and platform remittance statements to prove the export character of the revenue.

  • Customs Export Declaration Form (export cargo customs declaration) with the customs-assigned declaration number and export verification date
  • Commercial invoice and packing list evidencing the exported goods or the digital service invoice in the case of services
  • Bill of Lading, air waybill, or other international transport document proving physical export of goods
  • Bank foreign exchange receipt and settlement certificate (the Chinese FIRC equivalent) or cross-border RMB receipt evidence showing settlement of export proceeds
  • Export contract, purchase order, or platform settlement statement from Apple App Store or Google Play showing the overseas end-customer and net remittance
  • For digital services: service contract, service description, subscriber IP or territory logs, and written declarations confirming that the service is completely consumed outside China

China mandates the issuance of VAT invoices through the STA-controlled electronic invoice system. Under the nationwide full digital electronic invoice reform, VAT invoices are issued through the electronic invoice service platform and carry a unique 20-digit anti-forgery invoice number; special VAT invoices are matched and reconciled through the VAT Invoice Comprehensive Service Platform. The system is not a real-time pre-clearance fiscal stamping regime, but Golden Tax Phase IV provides real-time data monitoring, automatic abnormal-invoice risk screening, and transactional blocking for high-risk taxpayers. The JV must use the correct digital e-invoice tax classification codes for web-novel subscription services and may integrate the STA invoice service platform with its accounting and billing systems through approved API interfaces.

Any export zero-rating or refund claim, whether for goods or cross-border digital services, is subject to retrospective tax audit. If the export declaration, customs data, foreign exchange receipt, or overseas-consumption evidence is false, missing, or unverifiable, the tax authority may claw back the refunded VAT together with a late-payment surcharge of 0.05% per day and impose a fine of 50% to five times the tax evaded. Where fraudulent export tax refund is established, criminal prosecution under Article 204 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China may result in fines, confiscation of property, and imprisonment up to life imprisonment in severe cases. Digital service exporters are similarly exposed to clawback if the evidence of offshore consumption and cross-border payment does not substantiate the claimed exemption or zero rate.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Mainland China operates a managed floating RMB regime with robust current-account convertibility but strictly administered capital-account convertibility. The Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture may freely convert RMB for legitimate trade in services and goods, yet every conversion must pass through a licensed domestic bank that verifies the underlying transaction using contracts, invoices, customs declarations, tax receipts, or SAFE registrations. The inbound capital injection from the foreign investor is a capital-account item and therefore requires SAFE FDI registration, a designated capital account at a licensed bank, and a CPA-issued capital verification report before the funds can be converted to RMB for operational use. Profit remittances to the foreign shareholder are treated as current-account returns on investment once the board has approved a dividend and the enterprise has completed its statutory annual audit, filed financial statements, settled PRC corporate income tax, and handled withholding-tax obligations; the domestic bank will collect documentary evidence for authenticity. Export proceeds and digital-content royalty inflows are receivable in foreign currency or RMB, subject to trade-foreign-exchange verification, but there is no unlimited freedom to make speculative payments. Emergency outbound transfers for loan repayment, IP cross-licensing, or equity disposition must be independently approved by SAFE or handled under specific cross-border macro-prudential regulations.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the current SAFE goods-and-services trade FX administration framework, there is no statutory surrender ratio requiring a fixed percentage of export foreign-currency receipts to be converted to RMB. Export proceeds must be repatriated to a licensed domestic foreign-exchange account and admitted through a bank that matches the SWIFT inflow against the export customs declaration or service-trade contract. The general SAFE expectation is that goods-export proceeds are collected within 90 days from the export customs declaration; if the overseas buyer is granted a longer trade credit period, the exporter must maintain an internal receivable register and the overdue position must be explainable in SAFE's total-amount verification. In practice, an enterprise downgraded to Class B or Class C because of overdue export proceeds or mismatched trade data faces mandatory bank-level verification and restrictive conversion procedures.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE), the delegated foreign-exchange regulatory arm of the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Very low. Foreign fintech EMIs such as Wise, Revolut, or Airwallex are not regulated by the PBOC or SAFE as deposit-taking banks for resident Chinese companies. They cannot open a statutory corporate bank account for the JV, cannot accept capital-account FDI funds, and cannot hold RMB client funds as an authorized deposit-taking institution inside mainland China. Although certain licensed third-party payment agencies can assist the JV in collecting Apple IAP and Google Play proceeds, the JV's primary treasury and FX clearing rails must be domestic tier-1 banks. A foreign EMI may only be used as an ancillary offshore account for the foreign shareholder, never as the main operational account of the Chinese JV, because such use would violate SAFE capital-account administration and PBOC payment-clearing rules.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The foreign shareholder must first complete the FDI registration with SAFE through the local market-regulatory process, obtain the FDI registration/Foreign-Exchange Business Registration Confirmation, and open an FDI capital account at one of the tier-1 domestic banks. The inward remittance should be transmitted via SWIFT MT103 as a corporate capital injection, with the remitter bank using the agreed transaction purpose code for 'Investment in establishment of an enterprise / capital increase' and a clear payment narrative matching the registered injection amount and the JV's official name. The receiving bank will perform a real-time FX/KYC review, credit the funds to the capital escrow account, and report the transaction to SAFE's FDI information system. After the inflow is received, a qualified Chinese certified public accountant must issue a capital verification report; only then may the JV convert the capital into RMB and transfer the converted funds into its RMB basic deposit account, with each disbursement recorded against actual operational invoices and the payment mandate required by SAFE's rules on capital-account fund use. Any later increase, decrease, equity transfer, or early repayment of FDI must be separately registered or amended with SAFE before the cross-border remittance is processed.Outbound treasury payments of the JV should generally be executed through SWIFT MT103 for royalties, content-acquisition fees, author advances, and service-trade disbursements. For each service payment, the JV must submit a copy of the underlying contract, the service-trade foreign-exchange payment application, tax invoice or equivalent invoice, and evidence that PRC withholding taxes have been settled through the tax system. Where the JV purchases physical promotional merchandise, artwork, or printed publishing inventory abroad, the trade desk may issue a Commercial Letter of Credit or process a Documentary Collection through the bank's trade finance window; however, digital webnovel and subscription payments are efficiently and compliantly cleared via MT103, not LC/DC. For cross-border RMB payments to compatible jurisdictions, the JV may use CIPS through a tier-1 bank, provided the transaction is supported by the same underlying commercial documentation and does not circumvent SAFE's capital controls.Onshore deliverable RMB/USD forward contracts booked with a licensed domestic bank under a genuine current-account or registered capital-account exposure, with a tenor aligned to the projected cash flow and no speculative netting.FX swaps and cross-currency swaps for matched inflows and outflows, executed only against real trade or capital obligations and subject to SAFE's underlying-exposure documentary requirements.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are not permissible as a standalone onshore hedging rail for a resident Chinese JV; offshore NDFs cannot be booked as PRC hedge accounting, so the JV should rely on deliverable forwards and avoid unregulated NDF reliance.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Very low. Foreign fintech EMIs such as Wise, Revolut, or Airwallex are not regulated by the PBOC or SAFE as deposit-taking banks for resident Chinese companies. They cannot open a statutory corporate bank account for the JV, cannot accept capital-account FDI funds, and cannot hold RMB client funds as an authorized deposit-taking institution inside mainland China. Although certain licensed third-party payment agencies can assist the JV in collecting Apple IAP and Google Play proceeds, the JV's primary treasury and FX clearing rails must be domestic tier-1 banks. A foreign EMI may only be used as an ancillary offshore account for the foreign shareholder, never as the main operational account of the Chinese JV, because such use would violate SAFE capital-account administration and PBOC payment-clearing rules.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File preparation is mandatory when the aggregate amount of controlled related-party transactions in a fiscal year equals or exceeds CNY 100,000,000 (approximately USD 14 million); Master File preparation is mandatory when the total annual related-party transaction volume reaches CNY 1,000,000,000 (approximately USD 140 million) or when the enterprise's entire consolidated revenue exceeds the statutory sovereign threshold; contemporaneous country-by-country reporting applies to JVs falling under the Chinese CbC reporting regulations.

Economic substance scope: China does not maintain a separate Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) statute, but the General Anti-Avoidance Rule under the Enterprise Income Tax Law Article 47 and the SAT Notice on Anti-Abuse require the JV to demonstrate Core Income-Generating Activities (CIGA) in mainland China, including strategic management, editorial control, risk assumption, and key employment functions. A JV structured as a pure contractual shell or whose directors are all abroad, with no China-based business premises or decision-making, is treated as a conduit; the tax authority may re-assign taxable income to the real economic owner and deny treaty benefits, including the 5% dividend rate.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Under-Declaration of Digital Subscription Revenue

Statutory sanction: Violation of Enterprise Income Tax Law and Tax Administration Law Article 63; penalty of 50% to 500% of underpaid tax, plus daily 0.05% late-payment surcharge; where criminal fraud is established under Criminal Law Article 201, fines and imprisonment up to seven years.

Director personal liability: Courts will pierce the corporate veil upon showing shareholder/ director deliberate abuse of corporate limited liability; the responsible legal representative and financial officer become personally liable for unpaid CIT, VAT, and penalties; personal asset restitution, travel restrictions (exit blacklisting), and criminal sentence of up to seven years plus confiscation may be imposed.
Abusive Transfer Pricing / Profit Stripping through IP Royalties

Statutory sanction: Special Tax Adjustment under the CIT Law Article 41 and Administrative Measures for Special Tax Adjustments; the tax bureau may re-compute taxable income using arm's length methods and impose interest (benchmark loan rate plus 5 percentage points, retroactive) and a monetary penalty for missing documentation (up to CNY 100,000) plus 50% of tax underpaid if the adjustment resulted from concealed related-party facts.

Director personal liability: Directors who signed the intercompany IP agreements and board minutes approving excessive royalties face personal civil reimbursement for the JV's tax debt if the corporate assets are insufficient; the director may be named in a personal tax collection notice, prohibited from leaving China, and disqualified from serving as a director; if the misconduct crosses into intentional tax fraud, criminal imprisonment up to seven years becomes possible.
Shell-Company Treaty Shopping / Unlawful Dividend WHT Failure

Statutory sanction: Rejection of treaty relief and recovery of the 5% under-withheld difference with late-interest surcharge; the applying entity faces a penalty of 50% to 500% of under-withheld tax under the Tax Administration Law, and the tax authority may also impose a criminal charge of fraudulent tax claim under Criminal Law Article 205.

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly and severally liable for the unpaid withholding tax when they caused the JV to rely on treaty benefits without substance; civil asset seizure, blacklisting of the legal representative for departure denial, and personal restitution orders are issued; in egregious and publicly harmful cases, directors face criminal imprisonment up to three years and a ban from serving on any new Chinese corporate entity.
