Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA), administered through the Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax) online portal and local competent tax service bureaus.

E-government filing portal

Upon incorporation of a Sino-foreign equity joint venture with limited liability, the State Administration for Market Regulation issues a business license containing the Unified Social Credit Code, which serves as the sole tax identification number for VAT, corporate income tax, and withholding tax. The enterprise must complete taxpayer registration with the competent tax authority within 30 days of the business license date, using the e-Tax portal and submitting the business license, articles of association, legal representative identification document, board resolution, registered address proof, and bank account information. For a foreign non-resident enterprise that has no Chinese permanent establishment but derives China-source income, the non-resident must complete non-resident enterprise tax registration with the competent tax authority or via e-Tax, submitting the home-country registration certificate, tax residence certificate, appointment of tax agent, and the income-bearing contract. No separate VAT identification number is issued; the Unified Social Credit Code is the single taxpayer identifier for all Chinese tax purposes.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

All newly incorporated entities must complete tax registration within 30 days of establishment regardless of turnover; mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration is triggered when annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000. Below that threshold, the entity may continue as a small-scale VAT taxpayer with the simplified collection method.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A 0% VAT rate applies to goods physically exported from mainland China when the goods are lawfully declared to customs and actually leave the Chinese customs territory. Qualifying cross-border taxable services, including offshore digital publishing and content syndication services, may qualify for VAT zero-rating or exemption when the recipient is located outside mainland China, the services are wholly supplied for use outside China, and the transaction is not in the restricted service negative list. For CN_JV_LTD's digital webtoon chapter unlocking through Apple IAP and Google Play Billing, zero-rating substantiation requires platform settlement records identifying non-resident end consumers, evidence that content downloads and chapter unlocks occur outside mainland China, and matching foreign-exchange collection documentation evidencing receipt of the overseas proceeds.

  • Customs Export Declaration Form of the People's Republic of China (export customs clearance record)
  • Bill of Lading or electronic equivalent issued by the international carrier
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or bank foreign-exchange collection voucher evidencing repatriation of export proceeds

The entity must issue electronic invoices through the State Taxation Administration's official e-Invoice Platform using the Fully Digitalized Electronic Invoice system. Invoice data must be uploaded in real time to the Golden Tax Phase IV system; B2B invoices are subject to a digital anti-fraud stamp, tax-authority-side metadata clearing, and identity authentication through a tax control device or tax UKey. The platform provides API interfaces for automated invoice issuance, delivery, red-letter reversal, and reconciliation, and no invoice is legally usable for input VAT credit before the tax authority validates the digital record.

The tax authority may reopen export VAT zero-rating claims within the statutory assessment period. If the supporting customs, transport, or foreign-exchange documents are incomplete, inconsistent, or fabricated, the claimed 0% rate is denied, VAT is assessed at the applicable supply rate with overdue interest, and penalties of 50% to five times the underpaid tax are imposed under the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection. Fraudulent export claims involving forged customs declarations, bills of lading, platform settlement records, or remittance certificates are treated as tax evasion and may lead to criminal prosecution, revocation of export VAT refund qualification, and customs credit-system blacklisting.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a managed floating exchange-rate regime with qualified convertibility on the current account and strict registration-based controls on the capital account. RMB and foreign-currency flows are convertible for bona fide trade, service and income transactions supported by underlying contracts and by the bank's verification of the SAFE transaction classification code. Export receipts may be retained in foreign exchange or settled to RMB at the entity's discretion under the will-based settlement policy, but every payment must be matched to the SAFE Goods Trade Monitoring System or the service-trade payment/collection confirmation form. Capital-account flows, including foreign direct investment paid-in capital, foreign-currency loans, cross-border guarantees and offshore reinvestment, require prior SAFE registration or filing, and the opening of the corresponding SAFE-approved capital account at a licensed bank. Profit remittance is possible only after the JV has satisfied legal accounting requirements: accumulated profits must be verified by an audit, statutory reserves must be set aside according to Chinese law, and a tax clearance or withholding certificate must be produced for dividend/service-trade payments. Foreign-currency hedging is limited to real-need derivative transactions, and no onshore offshore-deliverable RMB market exists for a domestic entity. Convertibility is therefore substantive but not absolute; every flow must pass through an authorized bank and carry a genuine commercial or financial underlying purpose.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE's will-based settlement framework there is no statutory mandatory conversion ratio: a registered export entity may retain 100% of its export foreign-currency proceeds in a current-account foreign-exchange account or sell them to a licensed bank for RMB. For ordinary goods exports, proceeds must be repatriated to an onshore designated-bank account generally within 90 days from the date of export customs declaration; if collection is delayed beyond this window, the exporter must record the deferral in the SAFE Goods Trade Monitoring System and retain commercial evidence of the non-payment or late payment. For cross-border digital webtoon syndication receipts classified under service trade, there is no fixed conversion ratio, but the platform settlement, licensing agreement and revenue report must be presented to the remitting bank, and the proceeds must be credited to the JV's service-trade foreign-exchange account without using offshore intermediary accounts. Actual surrender to RMB occurs only when the JV instructs the bank to convert, applying a board-approved treasury policy rather than a regulator-mandated percentage.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE), the foreign exchange authority administered under the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Not viable as an onshore treasury substitute. A China-registered joint venture cannot legally open or maintain its primary operating account with a foreign fintech EMI such as Wise, Revolut Business, Airwallex or Payoneer because PBOC and SAFE do not recognise offshore EMI licenses for deposit-taking or RMB settlement activity within China. Such providers can only act as ancillary payment-facilitation corridors for platform collections in foreign markets where a separate offshore entity exists, and they cannot satisfy capital-account registration, capital verification, export proceeds repatriation or tax-clearance requirements for the onshore JV. The JV must maintain its statutory bank account with a Chinese licensed commercial bank; any EMI collection must be swept into that onshore account through a documented payment settlement arrangement and must remain transparent to the Chinese bank.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Under State Taxation Administration Bulletin 2016 No. 42, mandatory Local File is triggered by related-party transactions exceeding: RMB 200 million for tangible property, RMB 100 million for financial/fund assets, RMB 100 million for intangible property transfers, RMB 40 million for all other related-party transactions (including services and royalties), while the Master File is required when the aggregate annual related-party transaction amount exceeds RMB 1 billion. The Annual Related Party Transactions Report must be submitted with the CIT annual return irrespective of thresholds when any related-party transaction exists.

Economic substance scope: China does not operate a zero-tax Caribbean-style ESR. Instead, the equivalent substance-over-form doctrine appears through PRC CIT Law Article 47 (GAAR), the beneficial ownership assessment in tax treaties, and the CIGA/PE analysis in SAT Bulletin 2016 No. 42. For webtoon syndication, the economically substantive core income-generating activities are artistic creation, chapter editorial decisions, platform contract negotiation, pricing, and risk management; if these activities are performed in Mainland China while the legal IP owner is a shell offshore affiliate, tax authorities may attribute the license income to the Chinese JV, recharacterize the royalty, deny deductions, and impose TP penalties.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Under-Reporting Digital Platform Revenue

Statutory sanction: TCAL Article 63: the tax authority recovers the unpaid tax, charges 0.05% daily late surcharge, and imposes a monetary penalty of 50% to 500% of the underpaid amount. Criminal Law Article 201: fraudulent tax evasion can result in a fine, criminal detention, or imprisonment up to three years, rising to three to seven years if the amount is exceptionally large and a large percentage of the tax liability is evaded.

Director personal liability: A court may pierce the JV's corporate veil under PRC Company Law (2023) Article 23 where directors use the legal-person status to evade tax and seriously prejudice the state fiscal creditor; directors who participated in signing false revenue reports are jointly and severally liable to disgorge assets to the tax authority. Under TCAL Article 44, responsible directors may be blacklisted and prohibited from leaving China until tax liabilities and penalties are settled.
Transfer Pricing Abuse / Offshore Shell IP Routing

Statutory sanction: Article 47 CIT Law and SAT Bulletin 2016 No. 42 permit compulsory arm's-length recharacterization of the royalty and transfer; income may be adjusted at the arm's-length consideration, royalty deductions may be denied, and the taxpayer may be assessed with late surcharge plus an additional penalty of 50%-500% under TCAL Article 63 if the arrangement is fraudulent. The taxpayer may also be added to the State Revenue Administration's major serious-dishonesty public information list, triggering joint sanction and exit controls.

Director personal liability: Where directors approve a sham IP migration or payment structure knowing it has no business purpose, the corporate veil is pierced; directors can be held personally liable to answer for the full tax adjustment, and if the conduct crosses into intentional non-declaration or forged documents, Criminal Law Article 201 exposure applies with travel bans and imprisonment for the responsible executive.
Withholding Tax Evasion and DTA Abuse

Statutory sanction: CIT Law Articles 37 and 40 and TCAL Article 69 subject the withholding agent to an order to pay the overdue withholding tax plus 0.05% daily late surcharge and a fine of between 50% and 300% of the tax that should have been withheld. The tax authority may deny the treaty benefit under the principal-purpose test incorporated through China's tax-treaty policy, recharacterizing the distribution as a taxable dividend and adding further interest.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly sign false beneficial-ownership declarations, instruct the CFO to remit without withholding, or cause the JV to disregard a withholding order are jointly and severally liable to the tax authority under the civil veil-piercing regime; if they convert or misappropriate withheld employee or supplier tax amounts, they face criminal accountability and potentially prison terms of up to seven years, plus blacklisting and exit bans.
