Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutorily fixed minimum paid-up capital is required for an ordinary manufacturing WFOE. The shareholders must state a definite registered capital in the Articles of Association, subscribe fully, and pay up the subscribed capital within five years (or a shorter term selected in the Articles of Association). Cash contributions must be remitted into a SAFE-designated foreign exchange capital account opened by the company after incorporation; funds are not blocked in escrow but cannot be freely used until the FDI Foreign Exchange Registration is completed and the capital has been actually settled into RMB under applicable settlement policy. The registered capital must be commensurate with the planned manufacturing scale; unreasonable under-capitalization may trigger tax, customs, and bank scrutiny.

Funding & unlock path: Obtain Fire Safety Acceptance Verification from the local Fire and Rescue Brigade before production, covering fire compartmentation, emergency exits, automatic sprinklers, standpipe systems, fire alarm devices, and fire-resistance ratings of the food-processing and warehouse floors. Complete occupational health and safety clearance under the Work Safety Law of the People's Republic of China and the Occupational Disease Prevention and Control Law, including the Three Simultaneities mechanism requiring work-safety facilities to be designed, constructed, and commissioned together with the project. Provide structural load inspection acceptable to local building administration for palletized raw material storage, a dust explosion prevention protocol for rice flour powder handling areas, occupational hazard pre-evaluation for noise and heat, ventilation testing, and an emergency evacuation drill record prior to startup.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; authoritative electronic portal is the Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax) at chinatax.gov.cn, with local implementation by provincial and municipal taxation bureaus.

E-government filing portal

(1) Foreign shareholders incorporate a WFOE as a limited liability company through the Administration for Market Regulation to obtain a Business License carrying the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC); the USCC becomes the statutory Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) of the Chinese resident entity. (2) Within 30 days of license issuance, complete tax registration through the Electronic Tax Bureau at the competent tax authority of the registered address, submitting the registration articles, lease evidence, legal representative identification, and financial officer real-name verification; the tax authority activates the USCC-based TIN and establishes corporate income tax, VAT, stamp duty, and payroll-related filing calendars. (4) Register separately with the China International Trade Single Window for export customs declaration, and open a foreign exchange settlement account with a designated bank to receive export proceeds. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None. The PRC applies entity-level taxation without US-style check-the-box or pass-through elections for WFOEs. The only material administrative election is VAT classification: enterprises with annual taxable turnover below RMB 5,000,000 may elect between small-scale taxpayer treatment (simplified levy with restricted input credits) and general taxpayer registration; no hybrid-mismatch or fiscal-transparency election exists. A WFOE is always a China tax resident subject to worldwide CIT at 25% unless treaty relief applies to a non-resident counterparty.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 (CNY 5 million) of cumulative annual taxable sales of goods and services triggers mandatory registration as a VAT general taxpayer; below this threshold small-scale taxpayer treatment at a 3% simplified levy rate may apply, but general taxpayer election is permitted and is practically mandatory for export refund qualification.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A registered VAT general taxpayer manufacturing and exporting retort-packaged instant rice noodles qualifies for the 0% export VAT rate provided: (i) the goods are physically exported out of the PRC customs territory; (ii) the enterprise holds export tax refund filing status and customs registration; (iii) the Customs Export Declaration reports the goods as ordinary export trade with actual FOB value; (iv) sales proceeds are received from overseas via designated foreign exchange banks under the SAFE Trade in Goods Monitoring System; and (v) the exported goods are supported by matched purchase invoices and payment evidence. Qualifying cross-border digital services may also be zero-rated under the cross-border taxable services catalog, but the principal export channel for packaged food manufacturing is goods export. Export proceeds must be repatriated into the designated foreign exchange account by the 180th day after export; receipts falling due later than 90 days require a deferred-receipt report filed with SAFE within 30 days after the export date.

  • Customs Export Declaration (export goods declaration) filed and transmitted through the China International Trade Single Window
  • Commercial invoice and packing list evidencing FOB value and HS classification, with HS heading 1902.30 confirmation by customs at export
  • Bill of Lading or multimodal/airway waybill showing actual delivery to the overseas buyer
  • Bank receipt voucher or foreign exchange settlement slip issued by a designated bank under SAFE's Trade in Goods Foreign Exchange Monitoring System, functionally equivalent to an Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC)
  • Special VAT Invoice copies for input materials, packaging, and production inputs used in the exported finished goods, required for the export tax rebate matching and verification system
  • Foreign sales contract or purchase order evidencing commercial terms
  • Export tax refund calculation and declaration data submitted through the e-Tax export refund module

Golden Tax Phase IV digital administration applies: all VAT invoices, including Special VAT Invoices and Ordinary VAT Invoices, must be issued through the unified VAT Invoice Management System integrated with the national e-Tax platform, using tax-control devices or the Fully Digitalized E-invoice (FDEI) mechanism. Invoices are digitally signed, assigned unique invoice and QR codes, and uploaded in real time for tax authority online validation; buyers can verify authenticity and input credit eligibility immediately. For export refunds, the Special VAT Invoice for purchased inputs and the Customs Export Declaration are matched electronically through the Export Tax Rebate and Invoice Verification System, and an anti-fraud red/blue letter invoice monitoring rule prevents duplicate credit claims.

Export VAT refunds are subject to post-clearance audit with digital matching of customs declarations, bank FX receipt records, and VAT invoice data. If zero-rating is claimed without genuine physical export or without timely collection of export proceeds, the refund is clawed back with interest and penalties: under the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Tax Levying and Collection, fraudulent export refunds attract a fine of 1 to 5 times the refunded amount, the enterprise tax credit rating is downgraded to D-level, and export refund eligibility is suspended. Where fraudulent refund amounts exceed RMB 50,000, or cumulative amounts exceed RMB 2.5 million in aggravated circumstances, the case is referred for criminal liabilities under Article 204 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, with penalties ranging from fixed-term imprisonment of 3 years up to life imprisonment, plus fines and asset confiscation. Legal representatives and financial officers can be held personally liable where deliberate invoice falsification, circular trade, or false customs declarations are proven.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China maintains a managed floating exchange-rate regime where the Renminbi is convertible for current account items in accordance with the IMF Article VIII obligations. WFOEs may conduct foreign exchange settlements for trade in goods, services, declared dividends, and current transfers without specific approval, subject to a five-element authenticity and legality review by designated banks. Surrender requirements have been eliminated; enterprises are permitted to retain foreign exchange earnings in approved settlement accounts and to convert them at market rates. However, capital account transactions (including cross-border equity injection, intercompany loans, and repatriation of invested capital) remain tightly regulated. Inbound direct investment, in the form of registered capital, requires a Foreign Exchange Settlement (FDI) registration through SAFE or an authorised bank, after which funds are credited to a capital account and may be converted to RMB for permitted uses. Dividend remittances to foreign parents require audited financial statements, corporate income tax clearance declarations, and a bank review to confirm that the profit is derived from genuinely operating activities. The PBOC imposes the macroprudential Parameter and window guidance to manage foreign exchange flows. All hedging instruments must be executed with licensed domestic banks under a real-demand background. Documentation retention, transaction purpose codes, and concurrent reporting are mandatory.

Export proceeds surrender rules

China abolished mandatory repatriation and surrender quotas for current-account export proceeds in 2012 and subsequent rules. Exporters can retain 100% of foreign currency receipts in a current-account settlement account at any licensed bank, and may voluntarily convert to RMB or use the proceeds for legitimate imports. Statutory reporting requires submission of export collection declarations via the Customs and SAFE electronic systems within the prescribed 30-day window; banks verify the authenticity of the underlying trade (contract, invoice, customs declaration, bill of lading). No minimum conversion ratio applies, but all settlements must be executed at prevailing interbank exchange rates through the bank's pricing.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the People's Bank of China (PBOC), the de facto central bank and FX supervisory authority of the People's Republic of China.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Given SAFE's banking license and physical presence requirements, offshore digital EMI platforms (e.g., Revolut, Wise, Airwallex) cannot service WFOEs as local banks. They lack a China branch licence and cannot open domestic current account or capital account facilities. Such platforms may act as auxiliary cross-border payment gateways partnering with licensed banks, but the ultimate liquidity clearing must route through a Tier-1 onshore bank for regulatory compliance. Therefore viability is Low as a primary onboarding substitute and Medium only as a supplementary remittance tool for low-value transaction streams.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: China thresholds under SAT Public Notice No. 42 (2016): Local File preparation is triggered when annual related-party tangible-property transactions reach or exceed RMB 200 million; intangible-property transfers reach RMB 100 million; financial-asset transfers reach RMB 100 million; or service transactions reach RMB 40 million. A Master File is generally required where the prior-year consolidated revenue of the MNE group reaches RMB 5 billion and the Chinese entity has cross-border related-party transactions. The annual Related Party Transactions Filing Schedule is mandatory for every WFOE with any controlled transaction regardless of these thresholds.

Economic substance scope: China does not impose a UAE-style Economic Substance Regulations code, but the General Anti-Abuse Rule in EIT Law Article 47, SAT Public Notice 2009 No. 2, and the DTA beneficial-ownership doctrine impose a substantive economic-presence standard. The WFOE must demonstrably carry out Core Income-Generating Activities in the Luosifen manufacturing chain, including procurement of rice, production scheduling, food-safety and quality control, retort processing, recipe R&D, inventory and logistics risk management, and invoicing. If the foreign parent strips the Chinese entity of substantive functions and profits are allocated to a low-substance offshore affiliate, China may recharacterize the structure, attribute income back to the substance-holding company, deny treaty benefits, and pierce the corporate veil.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion - Transfer Pricing Manipulation

Statutory sanction: Tax Administration and Collection Law Article 63: recovery of the underpaid CIT plus a daily late surcharge of 0.05%; administrative fine of 50%-500% of the underpaid tax. Criminal Law Article 201: where evasion is large and exceeds 10% of the tax due, responsible persons are punishable by up to 3 years' detention; where the amount is huge, imprisonment of 3-7 years plus fines.

Director personal liability: Directors, legal representatives, CFOs, and actual controllers who ordered or approved the false pricing scheme are jointly and severally liable for the tax loss under the corporate veil-piercing provisions of China's Company Law; the competent tax authority will blacklist the legal representative under the serious-tax-violation joint punishment mechanism, impose an exit ban or travel restriction under Tax Administration and Collection Law Article 44, and criminal liability may include personal fines, asset confiscation, and imprisonment up to 7 years.
Withholding Tax Evasion on Profit Repatriation and Falsified DTA Claim

Statutory sanction: Tax Administration and Collection Law Article 69: a withholding agent that fails to collect or withhold tax is ordered to recover the tax and is subject to a 50%-300% penalty; if recovery from the foreign payee is impossible, the withholding agent remains exposed to the unpaid WHT plus late interest under Article 32; a false DTA claim is rejected and the under-withheld tax is assessed with penalties; Criminal Law Article 201 applies when the outward payment concealment is willful and causes tax evasion.

Director personal liability: The legal representative and financial officers who sign remittance instructions are personally liable for civil restitution of the unpaid WHT; they are blacklisted and restricted from exiting China until the tax is settled; where the conduct is intentional, directors are prosecuted as principals and face criminal fines, asset seizure, and imprisonment up to 7 years.
Shell Company and General Anti-Abuse Rule / Economic-Substance Violation

Statutory sanction: EIT Law Article 47 and the General Anti-Abuse Rule under SAT Public Notice 2009 No. 2: the tax authority may recharacterize the transactions, adjust taxable income, deny treaty and deduction benefits, impose interest, and apply Tax Administration and Collection Law Article 63 penalties of 50%-500% where tax evasion is involved; the artificial entity structure may also be revoked administratively; criminal liability arises where the sham structure is used to materially understate Chinese tax.

Director personal liability: Courts will pierce the corporate veil where the entity is a facade for tax evasion or personal enrichment, rendering directors and actual controlling shareholders jointly and severally liable for unpaid CIT, WHT, interest, and penalties; the legal representative is blacklisted and cannot leave China until the tax debt is cleared; criminal sanctions include personal fines, restitution of misappropriated corporate assets, and imprisonment up to 7 years for intentional abusive arrangements.
