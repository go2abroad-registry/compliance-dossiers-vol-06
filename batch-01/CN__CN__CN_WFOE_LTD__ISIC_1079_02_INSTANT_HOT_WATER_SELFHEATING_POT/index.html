Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum registered capital applies to a wholly foreign-owned enterprise in the ISIC 1079 food manufacturing sector, provided the business scope is outside the Foreign Investment Negative List. The registered capital is stated in the Articles of Association and must be a genuine reflection of the expected operating scale and investment plan. Capital may be contributed in cash or in-kind within the contribution period stipulated in the Articles of Association; under current rules, the contribution period may be up to 30 years for an ordinary WFOE. A blocked escrow account is not statutorily mandated, but any foreign currency capital injection must be reported through the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) registration system and deposited in a dedicated capital contribution account opened with a domestic RMB-Foreign Exchange bank.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety Certification: the facility must obtain a building fire protection design review and a final fire inspection acceptance or filing certificate before occupancy, including emergency exits, fire extinguishers, sprinkler systems, fire alarm systems and smoke control measures; Civil Defense fire clearance is mandatory for the production area. Structural load inspection is required to verify that floors, mezzanine platforms and storage racks can support the self-heating meal assembly equipment and exothermic reaction heaters. Occupational safety clearance must be issued under the Law on Production Safety, including machine guards, emergency stop mechanisms, worker training records, personal protective equipment, heat and steam hazard controls, and an approved emergency response plan. Food factory hygiene approval and a valid food safety management certificate are required for all personnel handling food contact surfaces. The Civil Defense and occupational clearances must be posted at the facility entrance and made available for annual inspections.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; online electronic portal is the nationwide E-Tax Bureau (Electronic Tax Bureau), accessible through the STA official gateway (www.chinatax.gov.cn) and the provincial enterprise E-Tax Bureau clients, with export clearance data exchanged through the China Customs Integrated Single Window.

E-government filing portal

Step 1 - Incorporation and unified tax identifier: the foreign investor files the WFOE (limited liability company) incorporation with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) via the National Enterprise Registration Online Service; the issued business license carries the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), an 18-digit statutory enterprise identity number that automatically doubles as the Tax Identification Number (TIN), so no separate TIN certificate is issued. Step 2 - Supplementary tax registration: within 30 calendar days of the business license date, log in to the E-Tax Bureau using the USCC and initial password, complete the supplementary tax registration, file the legal representative, finance officer and tax officer data, record the accounting policies and books system, and register the enterprise basic deposit bank account. Step 3 - VAT taxpayer status election: in the E-Tax Bureau taxpayer registration module, choose General VAT Taxpayer (mandatory if prior-12-month taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000, or voluntary with complete accounting books) or Small-Scale VAT Taxpayer (annual taxable sales at or below RMB 5,000,000). Step 4 - VAT invoice and export system setup: apply for the VAT invoice quota and format, acquire the tax-control UKey or tax-control disk under Golden Tax System Phase IV (GTS-IV), activate the Fully Digitalized Electronic Invoice capability, and, for the exporting manufacturing WFOE, complete the STA export tax refund recognition, the China Customs exporter registration, and the SAFE-monitored foreign-exchange account notification. Step 5 - Non-resident alternative: a foreign company without a Chinese entity that derives China-sourced income obtains a tax ID through its withholding payer; the payer files the withholding declaration with the competent STA office and obtains a withholding tax certificate naming the foreign beneficial owner. Step 6 - Confirmation: after verification the E-Tax Bureau issues the electronic tax registration notice evidencing the WFOE's tax types (VAT, Enterprise Income Tax, stamp duty, urban maintenance and construction tax, education surcharges, and individual income tax withholding for payroll) and the filing calendar.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation - the WFOE is a PRC resident enterprise established under Chinese law and is taxed at entity level; it is liable to Enterprise Income Tax on worldwide income at the 25% standard rate, to Value-Added Tax as a General or Small-Scale VAT Taxpayer, and to local surtaxes; there is no pass-through to foreign shareholders and no consolidated filing with the foreign parent.

Available election forms

None - the PRC Enterprise Income Tax Law provides no check-the-box or flow-through election for a foreign-owned limited liability entity, and no hybrid entity or hybrid instrument election is available; the STA instead applies the General Anti-Avoidance Rule (GAAR), the special anti-avoidance rules on transfer pricing, thin capitalization and controlled foreign corporations, and the BEPS minimum-standard substance requirements to neutralize hybrid mismatch outcomes.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 in annual VAT taxable sales is the mandatory General VAT Taxpayer registration threshold; an enterprise whose annual taxable sales do not exceed RMB 5,000,000 may register as a Small-Scale VAT Taxpayer and enjoy a monthly RMB 100,000 (quarterly RMB 300,000) VAT exemption, with the small-scale levy rate currently reduced to 1% (standard small-scale rate 3%). Zero-rated export turnover and exempt sales remain countable in the annual taxable sales amount used to determine the threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is available under Article 15 of the PRC Interim Regulation on Value-Added Tax and the Measures for the Administration of Export Tax Refund and Exemption when: (1) the WFOE is registered with China Customs as an exporter and has completed STA export tax refund recognition; (2) the self-heating meal pots are physically exported from mainland China customs territory; (3) the goods are released under a lawful Customs Export Declaration; (4) title passes to a genuine overseas buyer under an export contract; (5) the foreign-currency consideration is declared to and received in the WFOE's SAFE-monitored bank account within the repatriation deadline and verified against the specific export declaration; and (6) the transaction clears GTS-IV risk screening. Qualifying offshore digital services wholly consumed abroad may also be zero-rated; the export VAT treatment for goods is the exemption-offset-refund (exempt, credit, refund) regime with 0% output VAT and refundable input VAT, while small-scale exporters may claim exemption without refund.

  • Customs Export Declaration (the export Bill of Entry) released by China Customs through the Integrated Customs EDI / International Trade Single Window, bearing the statutory 18-digit declaration number and the ratified HS classification, quantity, unit price, and declared export value.
  • Bill of Lading (ocean port-to-port shipment) or duplicate Air Waybill (air shipment) evidencing that the goods physically departed a mainland China customs port to the overseas destination; a combined transport Bill of Lading is acceptable for multimodal routes.
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or the export foreign-exchange receipt and settlement voucher issued by a licensed Chinese settlement bank under SAFE monitoring, evidencing that the overseas buyer's foreign-currency remittance was credited to and settled through the WFOE's designated foreign-exchange account for the matched customs declaration.
  • Export sales contract and the WFOE-issued commercial invoice, strictly matching the customs declaration on product description (instant hot-water self-heating pot), HS classification, quantity, Incoterms rule, currency, unit price and destination.
  • Packing list and weight tally confirming the shipped quantities against the customs declaration, commercial invoice and Bill of Lading.
  • Fully Digitalized Electronic Export Invoice (or the tax-control exported-goods ordinary VAT invoice where still applicable) generated in the E-Tax Bureau, together with the destination-market certificate of origin and, where required, the health or food export certificate for food-contact self-heating products.

China's Golden Tax System Phase IV (GTS-IV) requires issuance of statutory VAT invoices exclusively through the E-Tax Bureau or approved tax-control equipment; the Fully Digitalized Electronic Invoice (All-Digital Electronic Invoice) carries the statutory 20-digit invoice number and an encrypted anti-counterfeit digital stamp, with real-time structuralized upload of every invoice line to the STA national electronic invoice platform. General VAT taxpayers in non-pilot setups must issue through tax-control UKey or Tax-Control Disk and upload to the VAT Invoice Management System before legal effectiveness. The STA exposes API and batch-issuance interfaces for ERP integration and, through the taxpayer digital account, conducts real-time fiscal clearance against filed returns, customs export declarations, bank collection flows and logistics data; any mismatch blocks further invoice issuance, downgrades the taxpayer risk rating and triggers tax risk inspection.

China Customs and the STA jointly cross-match every export refund application against the customs declaration, Bill of Lading or waybill, logistics track, FIRC and bank settlement data under GTS-IV. If the foreign-exchange proceeds are not collected and repatriated within 180 days (or the SAFE-approved extended period) without recognized force majeure or commercial cause, or if any supporting document is found forged, duplicated or mismatched, the STA cancels the zero-rating, claws back the full refund with assessed output VAT and imposes the statutory overdue surcharge of 0.05% per day (approximately 18.25% per annum), plus an administrative fine of 50% to 500% of the evaded tax under the Tax Collection and Administration Law. Fraudulent export refunds involving RMB 50,000 or more constitute criminal export tax refund fraud under Article 204 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, punishable by graduated custodial sentences up to life imprisonment with confiscation of property; the offending enterprise is downgraded to C-class or D-class tax credit, its export refund qualification is voided, and its customs credit rating is demoted to the point where future zero-rated clearances and refund prioritization are blocked.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

RMB is convertible for current account transactions and managed for capital account transactions through SAFE. A WFOE can freely settle trade in goods, services, dividends, royalties, and administrative charges, subject to authenticity review and supporting documentation. Profit remittance is a current account item but requires audited financial statements, corporate income tax clearance, and bank review of the SAFE-designated remittance application. Capital account operations, including FDI capital contributions, foreign shareholder loans, equity transfers, and liquidation proceeds, require registration with SAFE or through an approved domestic bank using the Foreign Exchange Administrative Information System. These funds must enter through a dedicated foreign exchange capital account and cannot be used for unauthorized intercompany lending, local equity investment, or speculative financial market activity. Since the abolition of mandatory surrender, export proceeds no longer need to be converted into RMB but may be retained in foreign exchange settlement accounts or voluntarily converted. The exchange rate regime is a managed floating rate based on market supply and demand with reference to a currency basket. There is no overt legal ceiling on remittance value, but every transaction must be supported by genuine trade, service, or investment documentation; any mismatch between declared purpose and actual cash flow will be rejected by the receiving bank and may trigger SAFE investigation.

Export proceeds surrender rules

SAFE requires export proceeds to be repatriated to a licensed domestic bank and reconciled with customs export data through the Trade Monitoring System. The standard collection window is generally measured from the customs export declaration date; exporters should collect proceeds and complete receipt verification within the monitoring framework, commonly understood as 180 days, with any abnormal overdue receipt requiring a written explanation and supporting trade terms to the bank. There is no mandatory conversion ratio: the exporter may retain 100 percent of export proceeds in a foreign exchange settlement account or voluntarily sell them into RMB. Forced surrender is abolished, but the bank must verify the shipment, invoice, customs declaration, and underlying sale contract before recording the receipt.

Central bank supervision: The People's Bank of China (PBOC) and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Mandatory Audit Threshold Mandatory Audit for All Entities

Turnover: China applies a mandatory annual independent certified public accountant (CPA) audit to all foreign-invested enterprises, including a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise, with no de minimis exemption based on balance-sheet size, turnover, or headcount. The mandatory audit applies irrespective of whether the WFOE has revenue, assets, or employees; zero-revenue holding entities must still produce audited statutory financial statements. Additional quantitative triggers under other PRC rules include exceeding the thresholds applicable to medium/above enterprises under the Ministry of Finance classification, application of CAS for large or listed groups, public issuance of bonds, licensure in financial services, merger/acquisition restructuring, and any regulatory requirement to file audited accounts with SAMR, tax authorities, financial regulators, or stock exchanges.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% on gross dividends remitted by a Chinese resident WFOE to a non-resident enterprise shareholder, unless reduced under an applicable DTA or the China-Hong Kong tax arrangement.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

China's tax year is the calendar year. Quarterly advance CIT returns are due within 15 days after each quarter-end; the final annual CIT return is due on or before May 31 following the tax year-end.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: RMB 200 million for annual related-party tangible goods transactions; RMB 100 million for annual related-party intangible property transactions; RMB 100 million for annual related-party financial transactions; and RMB 1 billion aggregate related-party transactions in the prior year triggers the Master File requirement. The Local File threshold and documentation obligations follow SAT Announcement 2016 No. 42.

Economic substance scope: Mainland China does not impose a separate Economic Substance Regulations regime comparable to offshore centers, but economic substance is directly relevant to anti-avoidance and DTA relief. The WFOE must demonstrate that Core Income-Generating Activities (CIGA) are performed in China: board management, strategic decision-making, operational risk, asset ownership, employment, and premises. If a foreign parent directs operations without separate PRC substance, the tax authority may apply CIT Law Article 47 GAAR, recharacterize the arrangement, deny treaty benefits, reject royalty or management fee deductions, and impose tax with interest and penalties.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False VAT Invoicing

Statutory sanction: The unpaid CIT is assessed with daily late-payment interest; Law on the Administration of Tax Collection Article 63 imposes a penalty of 50%-500% of the evaded tax; Criminal Law Article 201 provides imprisonment up to 3 years for ordinary tax evasion and up to 7 years where the amount is huge, while Criminal Law Article 205 on false VAT special invoices can lead to imprisonment up to life and asset confiscation.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced where the director personally ordered the fraudulent scheme; directors face civil asset restitution for the underpaid tax and penalties, tax authority blacklisting with travel bans and exit restrictions, criminal fines, and imprisonment up to 7 years for tax evasion or up to life imprisonment for false-invoice offenses.
Transfer Pricing Mispricing and Artificial Profit Shifting

Statutory sanction: CIT Law Article 41 mandates a special tax adjustment to the arm's-length price; Article 46 applies thin capitalization adjustment for excessive debt; Article 47 GAAR permits recharacterization of shell arrangements; SAT Announcement 2016 No. 42 imposes documentation requirements; Tax Collection Law penalties can range from 50% to 500% of the underpaid CIT, plus 0.05% daily late-payment interest and additional audit costs.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly approve the non-arm's-length contracts may be held jointly and severally liable for the resulting tax adjustment and interest; courts may pierce the corporate veil and order civil restitution from personal assets; the STA may blacklist the legal representative with a travel ban and exit restriction; criminal tax evasion charges can expose directors to imprisonment up to 7 years.
Dividend WHT Non-Compliance and DTA Abuse / Profit Repatriation Violations

Statutory sanction: CIT Law Articles 37 and 39 authorize collection of the unpaid WHT from the withholding agent or non-resident recipient; Law on the Administration of Tax Collection Article 69 authorizes a fine of 50%-300% of the unwithheld amount; late payment surcharges accrue at 0.05% daily; DTA relief is denied and the full statutory WHT is recovered with interest if the beneficial ownership test is not satisfied.

Director personal liability: The legal representative and chief financial officer may be personally liable to restore the unpaid WHT and fines where the WFOE's assets are stripped; tax authorities may blacklist the directors, impose travel bans and exit restrictions, and treat the deliberate non-withholding as tax evasion, leading to criminal imprisonment up to 7 years and civil asset restitution.
