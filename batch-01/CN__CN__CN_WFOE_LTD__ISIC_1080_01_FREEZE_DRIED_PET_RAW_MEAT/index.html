Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None (no statutory minimum registered capital). Under Article 47 of the 2023 PRC Company Law, the whole subscribed capital of a limited liability company must be paid in full within five years from the date of establishment; the contribution schedule must be stated in the Articles of Association and the paid-in capital is recorded in the capital register and published on the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System. No blocked escrow account is mandated by Chinese company law for a WFOE; however, the competent pet-feed licensing authority will, during the on-site inspection for the Feed Production License, verify the actual paid-in capital and the completed investment in the freeze-dry production line, cold store, clean utilities, environmental protection facilities, and fire-safety installations before the license is issued.

Funding & unlock path: Fire-protection design review and completion acceptance/filing with the municipal Fire Rescue Department under GB 50016-2014 (2018 edition); installation of automatic fire detection, alarm and extinguishing systems in cold stores, freeze-dry tunnels, and packaging halls; structural load inspection certificate covering vacuum freeze-dryers and cold-storage floors; occupational safety completion acceptance for safety facilities and occupational-hazard control effect assessment covering workers exposed to low temperatures, vacuum equipment, and meat-drying dust; special-equipment use registration for pressure vessels, boilers, ammonia or fluorine refrigeration systems and lifting appliances with the local special-equipment safety supervision authority.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China, acting through the national Electronic Tax Administration portal (electronic tax office) in coordination with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) for entity registration and business license issuance.

E-government filing portal

For a wholly foreign-owned enterprise (WFOE) in mainland China, the statutory Tax Identification Number (TIN) is the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) obtained through company registration with SAMR. The procurement procedure is: (1) submit the establishment application and constitutional documents through the SAMR online enterprise registration system; (2) obtain the legally issued business license bearing the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code; (3) within the statutory post-registration time limit, complete tax registration using the USCC as the taxpayer identification number on the Electronic Tax Administration portal; (4) file the enterprise's basic information, legal representative identity, registered address, bank account, and expected business scope with the STA sub-office; (5) receive the electronic tax registration certificate; and (6) activate VAT invoice quotas and export tax refund registration if export operations are contemplated. A foreign non-resident enterprise without an establishment or place of business in China must instead complete withholding qualification registration and obtain a tax code via the payroll or contract withholding procedures administered by the STA.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None; China does not provide check-the-box elections or hybrid mismatch elections. A WFOE is mandatorily taxed as a resident enterprise on its worldwide income at the entity level. The only relevant election is the VAT taxpayer status election between small-scale taxpayer and general taxpayer, subject to statutory turnover thresholds and input VAT credit conditions.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

CNY 5,000,000 in annual taxable turnover, above which mandatory general taxpayer VAT registration applies; below this threshold an enterprise may remain a small-scale taxpayer. Under current STA relief policy, small-scale taxpayers with monthly sales not exceeding CNY 100,000 may benefit from temporary VAT exemption, while the standard applicable small-scale taxpayer levies apply otherwise.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rated VAT treatment applies to goods that are physically exported from mainland China to destinations outside China when: (1) the exporter has completed the lawful customs export declaration through the China International Trade Single Window; (2) the goods leave the jurisdiction under customs supervision with a validated electronic Customs Export Declaration; (3) the export sales proceeds are collected through designated foreign exchange banks and repatriated to China in accordance with State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) rules; (4) the exporter issues the required VAT invoice and export documentation evidencing a genuine cross-border sale; and (5) the corresponding input VAT has been properly recorded, matched, and claimed solely against the exported goods. For qualifying export goods, the output VAT is reduced to zero and the exporter is entitled to refund the input VAT attributable to those exports.

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronic customs clearance record and paper printout)
  • Bill of Lading or air waybill evidencing physical export from Chinese customs territory
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or equivalent bank foreign exchange settlement receipt
  • Export sales contract between the WFOE and the overseas buyer
  • Commercial invoice matching the customs declaration value
  • Packing list
  • Certificate of origin, if required by the destination jurisdiction
  • Export sanitary or veterinary certificate issued by China Customs for animal-derived pet food products

China mandates the digital fully digitalized VAT invoice system, known as digital electronic invoices, administered through the Electronic Invoice Service Platform of the State Taxation Administration. All VAT invoices are issued online with real-time data transmission to STA databases; invoices require pre-assigned digital invoice numbers, encrypted anti-forgery stamps, and automatic fiscal clearing without the use of legacy paper invoice counterfoils. Invoice issuers must connect to the STA platform through the official electronic tax portal and standardized application programming interfaces (APIs), and every invoice issuance, voiding, red-operation, and credit note is reported to the tax authorities in real time or within the statutory reporting cycle. No independent off-platform fiscal device stamping is recognized for fully digitalized invoice issuance, and all invoice data is automatically reconciled with tax returns and export declaration data through the cross-department data sharing mechanism with the General Administration of Customs.

Export zero-rating benefits are subject to rigorous joint audit by the State Taxation Administration, China Customs, and SAFE. If any export declaration, invoice, foreign exchange receipt, or supporting document is found to be false, fabricated, or materially inconsistent, the claimed VAT refund will be clawed back with penalty interest, a tax penalty from 50% to 500% of the tax amount evaded will be imposed under the Law on Administration of Tax Collection, the exporter may be suspended from export tax refund procedures or placed on a tax-blacklist, and if fraud constitutes a criminal offense under the Criminal Law, responsible persons will bear criminal liability including confiscation of goods and imprisonment.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a current-account convertible regime under the Foreign Exchange Administration Regulations, while capital account transactions remain subject to strict SAFE oversight. For the CN_WFOE_LTD, export receipts derived from vacuum freeze-dried pet raw meat qualify as current account income and may be voluntarily converted into CNY at licensed banks, subject to authenticity matching against customs declarations and electronic port data. Foreign currency can be retained in an export proceeds settlement account within the bank-approved quota. Capital account flows, however, require prior SAFE registration, a dedicated capital account at a licensed bank, and documentary proof of underlying investment. Profit remittance is treated as a current account payment but effective blockage can arise if the bank is not satisfied that accumulated statutory reserves have been made, corporate income tax and dividend withholding tax have been settled, or audited financial statements support distributable profits. The substantive hurdle is the commercial bank's authenticity review: any remittance without contracts, invoices, tax certificates, customs records, or board resolutions will be refused, and false trade constructs invite administrative fines ranging from 5% to 30% of the unlawful amount, plus confiscation of illegal gains.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE current practice, there is no mandatory conversion ratio for export foreign currency receipts; the WFOE may voluntarily settle or retain proceeds in an onshore foreign currency settlement account. Statutory repatriation generally requires export proceeds to be received into China within 90 days from the underlying export customs declaration, unless an approved extension is granted by SAFE; funds must enter an onshore licensed bank account and be reported through the trade foreign exchange monitoring system.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC); State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low and legally not viable as a primary channel. Offshore digital EMI providers holding foreign e-money or banking licenses are not authorized foreign exchange banks within China and cannot open domestic settlement accounts, process CNY clearing, or service FDI capital account flows for the WFOE. Such platforms may be considered only for ancillary treasury of overseas affiliated entities using non-CNY currencies, but all statutory capital injections, investment registrations, and current account settlements must pass through a Chinese licensed commercial bank designated under SAFE rules.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound equity must be wired via SWIFT from the offshore investor to the WFOE's dedicated FDI capital account at a licensed Chinese commercial bank. The remittance instruction must reference the SAFE FDI registration code and a clear equity injection purpose text, using the standard bank transaction classification for foreign-funded enterprise capital contributions. Upon receipt, the bank performs authenticity verification against the SAFE filing, onboarding documents, and source of funds; funds are then converted to CNY under the registered capital settlement policy and credited to the operating settlement account. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File contemporaneous documentation is required if annual related-party tangible asset transactions exceed RMB 200 million, intangible asset transactions exceed RMB 100 million, financial asset transactions exceed RMB 100 million, or other related-party transactions exceed RMB 40 million. Master File is required where the multinational group's consolidated revenue in the prior year exceeds RMB 2 billion; Country-by-Country Report filing is required where consolidated group revenue exceeds RMB 5.5 billion. The annual related-party transaction report must be filed with the annual CIT return.

Economic substance scope: China applies substance-over-form principles through the General Anti-Avoidance Rule, the beneficial ownership doctrine, and treaty abuse rules. The WFOE must have real personnel, physical premises, decision-making functions, ownership of relevant manufacturing assets, and assumption of operating risks. Core Income-Generating Activities must be performed in China where the freeze-drying plant, production operations, and strategic procurement decisions are located; a pure booking or conduit entity without economic substance will not be recognised for treaty benefit purposes and may be recharacterised by the tax authority.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion Through Misreported Export Revenue and Related-Party Sales

Statutory sanction: Article 63 of the Tax Collection Administration Law imposes additional recovery of unpaid tax, daily late-payment surcharge at 0.05%, and a monetary penalty of 50% to 500% of the tax underpaid. Article 201 of the Criminal Law imposes imprisonment up to 3 years for relatively large tax evasion and 3 to 10 years plus fines for huge amounts.

Director personal liability: Directors who order, control, or knowingly participate in the evasion are jointly and severally liable for unpaid tax, interest, penalties, and civil asset restitution; they may face travel bans through the seriously untrustworthy taxpayer blacklist and criminal prosecution as directly responsible persons, including imprisonment and fines.
Shell Entity and Treaty Benefit Abuse

Statutory sanction: Article 47 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law permits the State Taxation Administration to make special tax adjustments and disregard the conduit entity; treaty benefits are denied under STA Announcement 35/2019 where beneficial ownership is absent, and underpaid withholding tax is collected with interest and penalties under the Tax Collection Administration Law.

Director personal liability: Directors signing false treaty-benefit declarations face personal liability for unpaid withholding tax and penalties, inclusion in the tax blacklist with exit-control measures and travel restrictions, and criminal liability for forging or using falsified official documents under Article 280 of the Criminal Law.
Profit Stripping Through Thin Capitalisation, Excessive Royalties, and Management Fees

Statutory sanction: Articles 41 and 46 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law and supporting thin-capitalisation rules disallow the excess interest and non-arm's-length deductions; transfer pricing adjustments may be assessed with late-interest surcharges; if the payment is recharacterised as a dividend, the 10% dividend withholding tax becomes immediately payable, and under Article 63 of the Tax Collection Administration Law a penalty of 50% to 500% applies.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly facilitate the sham loan, royalty, or service arrangements are personally liable for restitution of the under-withheld tax, interest, surcharges, and penalties; they may be blacklisted with travel bans and restricted consumption, and face criminal prosecution for tax evasion or invoice fraud under Articles 201 and 205 of the Criminal Law.
