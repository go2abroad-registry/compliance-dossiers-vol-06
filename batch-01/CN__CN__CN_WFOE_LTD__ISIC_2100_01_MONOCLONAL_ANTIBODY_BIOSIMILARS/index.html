Competent corporate registry

State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR), exercised through the local Market Supervision Administration (MSA) at the place of the WFOE's registered address; registration is lodged through the provincial integrated online one-stop enterprise registration service and recorded in the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System.

Governing statute

Company Law of the People's Republic of China (2023 Revision), effective 1 July 2024; Chapter II, Articles 42-66 on formation, articles of association and governance of limited liability companies; Article 47 requiring all subscribed capital to be fully paid within five years; Article 50 on mandatory contents of articles of association; Foreign Investment Law of the People's Republic of China (2019), Articles 4, 28 and 31; Special Administrative Measures for Foreign Investment Access (Negative List); and the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China, Book III, Chapter 3, Section 2 on juridical persons.

Formation / constitutional filings

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None at federal registration level; no minimum paid-in capital or escrow or blocked account is imposed for ordinary WFOE incorporation under the PRC Company Law and Foreign Investment Law. However, the Articles of Association must state the subscribed registered capital and a contribution schedule, and Article 47 requires the shareholder to pay the subscribed capital in full within five years of establishment. Contributions may be cash or appraised non-monetary assets and must be transferred to a dedicated capital contribution account at a PRC licensed bank, with foreign direct investment reporting effected through the bank under the State Administration of Foreign Exchange regime. If non-cash contributions are used, an appraisal report and legal transfer of title are mandatory. For manufacturing entities, the registered capital should also preserve the then-applicable ratio to total investment required by the foreign-invested enterprise capital rules in force at filing.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China, acting through the local competent tax bureau for the WFOE's registered address; statutory administrative e-filing is performed through the STA's national Electronic Tax Bureau portal and, for customs-linked tax and export matters, through the China Customs Single Window.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the WFOE with the State Administration for Market Regulation and obtain the Business License containing the Unified Social Credit Code. Step 2: Use the statutory data-sharing mechanism between the market regulator and the State Taxation Administration; the USCC is automatically recognised as the taxpayer identification number for all tax filings and the WFOE is placed in the tax registration system without a separate paper tax registration application. Step 3: Within 30 days of obtaining the Business License, complete the new taxpayer comprehensive onboarding through the Electronic Tax Bureau by authenticating the legal representative and financial/tax personnel, confirming tax registration information, binding the company bank settlement account, enrolling for VAT and CIT filing obligations, and activating electronic invoice issuance. Step 4: For the non-resident foreign investor, no Chinese TIN is generally needed for dividend withholding because the WFOE acts as withholding agent and remits tax electronically; if the non-resident shareholder must itself file a China-source tax or treaty-benefit claim, the local competent tax bureau may require a separate non-resident tax registration. Step 5: Before exporting monoclonal antibody biosimilar goods, complete Customs registration in the China Customs Single Window so that the WFOE's Unified Social Credit Code and customs registration are aligned for export declarations and VAT export refund processing.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None in the sense of a US-style check-the-box regime; the WFOE is a Chinese resident enterprise taxed on worldwide income at entity level and cannot elect pass-through treatment under the Enterprise Income Tax Law. China does not offer hybrid mismatch elections to domestic taxpayers. The only relevant administrative elections are VAT status choices: a taxpayer below the RMB 5 million general-taxpayer threshold may elect to become a general VAT taxpayer voluntarily, and producers of biological products may elect the statutory simplified VAT levy for domestic sales, but neither election changes the default entity-level CIT classification of the WFOE.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory VAT registration applies from establishment for all active enterprises regardless of turnover; the statutory threshold for mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration is annual taxable sales exceeding CNY 5,000,000. If taxable sales do not exceed CNY 5,000,000, the WFOE may remain a small-scale taxpayer under the simplified levy method, but may voluntarily register as a general VAT taxpayer if accounting records and input-tax invoice controls are adequate.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating for exported biosimilar antibody goods is available where the WFOE is a general VAT taxpayer, the goods are physically exported from China under a valid Customs export declaration, the sale is made to an overseas buyer, the exported goods are not prohibited or restricted, and the export proceeds are collected and repatriated to China within the applicable foreign-exchange collection period. Under the manufacturing export regime, the WFOE applies the exemption-credit-refund method: output VAT on exports is zero, input VAT attributable to domestic purchases and manufacturing costs is offset against output VAT on domestic sales, and any residual input credit is refunded by the State Taxation Administration. For qualifying cross-border services supplied to overseas recipients and consumed wholly outside China, zero-rating requires a written service contract, proof of non-resident recipient and onshore/offshore service use, and bank or customs evidence of the cross-border transaction.

China operates real-time VAT invoice supervision through the State Taxation Administration's Electronic Invoice Service Platform. All VAT special invoices, general invoices and fully digitalised electronic invoices must be issued through tax-regulated invoicing systems; invoice data including invoice code, serial number, buyer and seller Unified Social Credit Codes, commodity description, tax rate, amount and tax added, is uploaded to the STA in near-real time. Input VAT credit is available to the buyer only after the special VAT invoice is authenticated and electronically matched on the STA invoice verification platform. Anti-fraud compliance is enforced through tax-control digital signatures, anti-counterfeiting tax-control codes, and automated cross-matching of invoice data, customs records, VAT returns and export refund declarations. Enterprise resource planning systems may integrate directly with STA electronic invoice interfaces/APIs or use licensed tax-control equipment for machine-to-machine invoice issuance, red-letter invoice cancellation and automatic reporting.

The State Taxation Administration, China Customs and Public Security authorities jointly scrutinise export refund claims by cross-matching Customs export declarations, VAT invoice data, logistics records, and bank foreign-exchange collection data. If zero-rating or export refund is found to be unverified, false, fraudulent, or unsupported by the required customs or foreign-exchange documentation, the STA will deny the claim, claw back the refunded amount plus late-payment interest, and impose a penalty ranging from 50% to 500% of the tax involved under the Tax Administration Law. Serious cases constitute criminal fraud under the Criminal Law, with penalties including imprisonment up to life and confiscation of property. The WFOE can also be suspended from export rebate processing, downgraded in tax credit classification, and barred from simplified export refund procedures. In fraud cases there is no statutory limitation on retrospective reassessment, and the authorities may retrospectively revoke zero-rating if goods are found to have been diverted to the domestic market or if the foreign-exchange repatriation and transport documents are forged or materially inconsistent.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China has accepted Article VIII current-account convertibility, so trade-related foreign exchange can be freely settled through authorized banks if the transaction is genuine and properly documented. The capital account, however, remains a controlled regime: inbound FDI, foreign-currency debt, outbound guarantees, and cross-border equity transfers require prior or post-event registration with the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE). For a WFOE, the registered capital is injected through a designated foreign-exchange capital account and then settled into RMB under SAFE-approved capital settlement, with usage restrictions tied to the business operation and the negative list. Export proceeds are no longer subject to mandatory surrender; 100 percent may be retained in a foreign-exchange account or converted to RMB according to the enterprise's own treasury decision, but the proceeds must be repatriated into China and reconciled with customs declarations in SAFE's trade monitoring system. Profit remittance, though a current-account item, faces procedural hurdles: an audited annual statement confirming distributable profits, a shareholders' resolution, a competent tax authority clearance, and the bank's authenticity check are mandatory. A 10 percent dividend withholding tax applies unless the applicable double taxation agreement reduces the rate. For a biopharmaceutical WFOE, royalties or license fees to foreign parent companies attract additional transfer pricing due diligence.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Currently SAFE does not impose a mandatory conversion ratio; 100% of export proceeds may be retained in a foreign-exchange settlement account or converted into RMB at the exporter's discretion. The statutory timeline is that export proceeds must be collected by the contractually agreed payment date and normally no later than 90 days after the customs export declaration; any deferral or advance beyond 90 days must be registered in the SAFE/AS2 goods-trade monitoring system before the expected money movement, with supporting invoices, contract, bills of lading, and customs release records. Export proceeds received without a matching export declaration will be trapped under the no-match rule and trigger compliance review.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) and State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Viability rating: Low. Foreign fintech EMIs and digital-only banking providers are not legally authorized by the National Financial Regulatory Administration (NFRA) to maintain onshore RMB accounts, clear CNY, process SAFE-regulated foreign-exchange conversions, or execute inbound FDI and outbound trade payments in mainland China. A WFOE may use such EMIs only as ancillary treasury management dashboards or transaction data utilities; they cannot replace the licensed domestic bank layer. Any attempts to route WFOE capital or trade flows through an offshore EMI would violate China's centralized clearing and SAFE reporting rules.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The foreign investor transmits the capital by SWIFT MT103 to the WFOE's designated FDI foreign-exchange capital account at the licensed tier-1 bank, using remittance purpose code 'Foreign direct investment (capital injection)' and referencing the SAFE FDI registration number, the investment contract, and the WFOE unified social credit code. Upon receipt, the bank checks the funds against the SAFE registration, performs a capital-account authenticity review, settles the FX into RMB if the business use falls within the permitted negative-list scope, and deposits the RMB into the WFOE's RMB basic deposit account. The WFOE must then appoint a PRC CPA to issue a capital verification report confirming that the registered capital has been fully paid, and update the enterprise information in the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System. No corporate treasury can deploy the injected funds before the capital verification report is issued.For raw material imports and international equipment purchases, the WFOE should use a commercial letter of credit (L/C) or documentary collection opened through the relationship bank; for services, royalties, and smaller trade payments, SWIFT MT103 is suitable. The bank will require each payment to be lodged in the SAFE goods-trade monitoring platform with the associated customs declaration, commercial invoice, packing list, contract, and tax-filing reference. Payments should include the WFOE's unified social credit code and a SAFE-approved trade purpose code. Mandatory Audit Threshold Mandatory Audit for All Entities

Turnover: Compulsory independent audit is legally mandated for all Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprises (WFOEs) in China regardless of size, because foreign-invested enterprises must submit audited financial statements with SAMR and the tax authority. Additionally, an independent CPA audit is strictly required under Chinese law if the enterprise meets any one of the following quantitative thresholds: (1) total assets exceeding RMB 50 million; (2) annual operating revenue exceeding RMB 30 million; (3) annual headcount exceeding 300 employees; (4) the enterprise is a public-interest or listed-group subsidiary requiring group audit consolidation; (5) the enterprise is engaged in banking, insurance, securities, or other financial activities; (6) the enterprise applies for high-tech enterprise status or government subsidies requiring attested financials; (7) the enterprise has related-party transactions and must file China transfer pricing contemporaneous documentation, especially annual related-party transactions exceeding RMB 200 million; or (8) the WFOE is licensed by the National Medical Products Administration (NMPA) for biopharmaceutical manufacturing, and the pharmaceutical license renewal requires audited financial soundness reports. For this monoclonal antibody biosimilar manufacturing WFOE, the compulsory audit is unconditional due to the foreign-invested nature, pharmaceutical license, and likely asset/turnover scale.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Local File preparation applies where aggregate annual related-party tangible property transactions exceed RMB 200 million, intangible property transactions exceed RMB 100 million, or related-party service fees exceed RMB 40 million; Master File documentation is mandatory where the MNE group's related-party revenue exceeds RMB 1 billion or satisfies the SAT Announcement [2016] No. 42 group financial thresholds

Economic substance scope: China applies an economic substance doctrine through EIT Law Articles 41-48, the General Anti-Avoidance Rule, and the anti-treaty-abuse rule; a biopharmaceutical WFOE must prove Core Income-Generating Activities in China, including bioreactor operations, fermentation, purification, quality control, storage, risk management, and DEMPE functions for intangible property; a low-function contract-manufacturing WFOE should earn only a routine manufacturing return while residual profit belongs to the entity that owns economically substantive risks and intangibles

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Transfer Pricing Manipulation

Statutory sanction: EIT Law Articles 41 and 48 permit special tax adjustments; Tax Collection Administration Law Article 63 imposes a penalty of 50% to 5 times the underpaid tax plus a daily surcharge of 0.05%; false invoice conduct can invoke Criminal Law Article 205 on VAT invoice fraud

Director personal liability: Directors and shadow controllers who devised the scheme face joint and several civil restitution for unpaid tax and interest under PRC Company Law Article 20 or Article 23 (2023 revision); blacklisting, asset freezing, exit and travel bans may be imposed; criminal conviction under Criminal Law Article 201 carries up to 3 years imprisonment for relatively large evasion and up to 7 years where the amount evaded is huge
Shell Entity / Lack of Economic Substance and Treaty Abuse

Statutory sanction: STA may deny treaty benefits under SAT Announcement [2017] No. 9 and apply the GAAR under EIT Law Article 47, recharacterizing the income and imposing additional CIT, interest, and penalties of up to 3 times the shortfall; anti-money-laundering authorities may also apply asset seizure under the PRC AML Law

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced where shareholders abuse limited liability to evade debts and defraud creditors; directors face personal liability for all unpaid taxes, civil damages, and legal costs; travel bans and blacklisting follow; aggravated cases constitute tax fraud or illegal business operation under Criminal Law Articles 201 and 225 with imprisonment up to 10 years
Failure to Withhold and Report China-Source Payments

Statutory sanction: EIT Law Article 37 makes the WFOE the statutory withholding agent; Tax Collection Administration Law Article 69 imposes a penalty of 50% to 3 times the unwithheld tax; late filing adds RMB 2,000-10,000 fines and daily surcharges; the bank remittance may be blocked until compliance is restored

Director personal liability: The responsible director, financial controller, and legal representative are personally liable for the withheld amount if the company cannot pay; frequent or willful non-withholding triggers criminal tax evasion liability, fines, imprisonment up to 7 years, exit bans, and personal seizure of assets under Civil Procedure Law and tax enforcement rules
