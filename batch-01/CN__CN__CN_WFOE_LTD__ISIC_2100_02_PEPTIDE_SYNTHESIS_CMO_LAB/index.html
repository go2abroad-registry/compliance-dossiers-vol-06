Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-in capital is imposed on a wholly foreign-owned enterprise limited liability company for pharmaceutical contract manufacturing; the only binding capital requirement is the amount stated in the Articles of Association. Under Company Law Article 47, the sole foreign shareholder may pay the subscribed capital in instalments but must make full payment within five years from the date of establishment. China does not require a blocked escrow account; instead, the WFOE must open a capital contribution settlement bank account, and the shareholder must remit contributions through a traceable bank transfer. The bank receipt and the company's books evidence the paid-in capital. If the shareholder fails to remit by the agreed schedule, the company may demand payment and the shareholder loses the benefit of time for the unpaid portion. Contributions may be in cash or lawful non-monetary property other than labour services, and such non-monetary contributions must be assessed and transferred to the company.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety clearance requires Fire Protection Design Review and Fire Protection Acceptance or filing with the local Fire and Rescue Section before use, including functioning automatic alarm and suppression systems and an approved evacuation plan. Occupational clearance requires an occupational health hazard assessment, pre-use hazardous chemical hazard evaluation, medical surveillance for exposed workers, and registration of pressure vessels and other special equipment with the market supervision and emergency management authorities. If dangerous chemicals form part of the synthesis process, a worksite safety facility design review and completion acceptance from the Emergency Management Bureau are mandatory before production.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA); online portals include the Electronic Tax Bureau and the fully digitalized electronic invoice service, with the national gateway at chinatax.gov.cn

E-government filing portal

For the inbound WFOE, the statutory Tax Identification Number (TIN) is the Unified Social Credit Code endorsed on the business license issued by the State Administration for Market Regulation. The WFOE must: (1) incorporate as a wholly foreign-owned limited liability company and obtain the business license, which simultaneously creates the tax registration record; (2) complete the supplementary tax registration and taxpayer qualification confirmation with the STA through the Electronic Tax Bureau within 30 days; (3) register the legal representative, financial officer, and tax officer and bind the enterprise to the digital tax risk control system; (4) select the Value Added Tax taxpayer identity, namely small-scale taxpayer or General VAT taxpayer, and complete the initial VAT classification; (5) file the tax bank account and tax settlement account information; and (6) activate the fully digitalized electronic invoice service for VAT invoice issuance and acceptance. A foreign non-resident parent does not require a separate Chinese TIN solely for shareholding; dividends are handled under the WFOE TIN and the statutory dividend withholding regime. For a foreign non-resident entity with a Chinese establishment, the STA assigns a non-resident taxpayer identification code upon tax registration for withholding and settlement of China-source income.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation; the WFOE is a Chinese resident enterprise taxed on worldwide taxable income at the statutory corporate income tax rate of 25 percent, and is automatically an Enterprise Income Tax taxpayer and a Value Added Tax taxpayer in China. No pass-through treatment is available.

Available election forms

None under China's check-the-box or hybrid mismatch rules; the WFOE cannot elect pass-through status. The only relevant statutory election is the voluntary registration as a General VAT taxpayer for a small-scale taxpayer with annual taxable sales below the mandatory RMB 5,000,000 threshold, provided accounting systems are adequate.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VAT registration is triggered by the WFOE incorporation and business-registration record within 30 days; mandatory General VAT taxpayer registration is required when annual taxable turnover exceeds RMB 5,000,000

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The WFOE may claim zero-rated VAT on exported goods where the peptide products physically depart from PRC customs territory under a valid Customs Export Declaration, and may claim zero-rating on qualifying cross-border laboratory/technical CDMO services that are supplied to an overseas enterprise and fully consumed outside China. Substantiation requires transfer of legal title, actual cross-border movement or offshore consumption, collection of foreign exchange from the overseas customer, and matching of customs, logistics, invoice, contract, and banking records.

  • Customs Export Declaration Form (Export Goods Customs Declaration) with export inspection/export rebate notation
  • Commercial Invoice (export invoice) issued by the WFOE
  • Bill of Lading or air waybill evidencing physical export from China
  • Foreign exchange collection receipt or export proceeds settlement slip evidencing repatriation of the export sales proceeds
  • Sales contract or purchase order with the overseas CDMO client
  • For peptide materials, export laboratory certificate/analysis certificate and any statutory export inspection certificate required by customs or health authorities

Mandatory fully digitalized electronic invoicing under the STA Golden Tax IV infrastructure; invoices must be issued, reported, and cleared through the Electronic Tax Bureau real-time tax control system, with anti-fraud digital stamping, institutional invoice coding, and authenticity verification on the National VAT Invoice Inspection Platform.

Export zero-rating is subject to joint STA-customs audit review using Golden Tax III/IV data matching, customs export data, logistics tracking, and bank foreign-exchange collection records. If a zero-rating claim is fraudulent or cannot be substantiated, the tax authority will claw back the refunded or exempted VAT, impose daily late-payment surcharge, and assess a penalty of 0.5 to 5 times the tax evaded under the Tax Collection Administration Law of the People's Republic of China. Serious cases are referred for criminal prosecution under the Criminal Law provisions on fraudulent export tax refunds and false VAT invoices.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Mainland China operates under a managed floating exchange rate system with structured capital controls. For a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE) in peptide synthesis CDMO, current account convertibility is broadly available for trade settlements, services, and profit remittance after tax clearance; however, capital account transactions remain permission-based and require prior registration or approval by SAFE. Inbound foreign direct investment (FDI) into the WFOE must be injected as paid-in capital through a dedicated foreign exchange capital account, supported by a Foreign Investment Information Filing, a valid business license, and a capital verification certificate issued after remittance. Foreign exchange capital funds cannot be used for debt repayment, equity investment outside the registered business scope, or RMB settlement outside approved purpose codes. Profit remittances to the overseas shareholder require annual statutory audit, financial statements, tax filing completion certificate, and bank account confirmation; dividends can be remitted after SAFE/operational bank review. Surplus working capital and principal repayments are not freely convertible. Repatriated export proceeds are subject to trade authenticity checks and may be retained in foreign currency accounts or settled under SAFE rules. This creates a regime of high current-account freedom but strict capital-account gatekeeping, requiring documentary discipline for treasury operations.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE's export foreign exchange monitoring framework, export proceeds must be repatriated to a domestic current-account foreign exchange bank account within 180 days after the date of customs export declaration. The exporter may keep the full amount in foreign currency or sell it to the designated bank for RMB; there is currently no statutory mandatory conversion ratio for general manufacturing exporters. Receipts exceeding USD 50,000 require the bank to verify customs declaration data and the corresponding commercial contract; any unrepatriated balance after 180 days must be explained through the bank's foreign exchange inspection system, with potential inclusion in the 'suspicious foreign exchange income' list.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the People's Bank of China (PBoC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low / Not viable as primary banking rails. Mainland China's banking and payment regulations require any local-currency financial service provider to hold a banking license or a PBoC payment institution license. Offshore digital EMIs are not licensed to maintain RMB settlement accounts, cannot process domestic tax payments or social security contributions, and cannot act as receiving banks for SAFE-sanctioned inbound FDI remittances or export proceeds. They may only support ancillary cross-border collection/remittance outside China, but for the WFOE treasury they cannot replace a designated Tier-1 licensed bank.

Mandatory Audit Threshold Mandatory Audit for All Entities

Turnover: The mandatory audit trigger for a Chinese WFOE limited liability company is legal form rather than quantitative size: the PRC Company Law requires every limited liability company to appoint a licensed PRC CPA firm to audit its annual financial statements. There is therefore no balance-sheet, turnover, or headcount exemption for a WFOE. The audit obligation is reinforced for a pharmaceutical CDMO because NMPA and provincial drug authorities condition the Drug Production License and GMP compliance on audited financial position and continuing viability. For group consolidation purposes, audit also becomes mandatory if the WFOE is material to the foreign parent under the parent's applicable accounting framework, but that is a group reporting requirement rather than a PRC statutory threshold.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% statutory withholding tax under EIT Law Article 37 on dividends paid by the WFOE to a non-resident corporate shareholder. Treaty relief commonly reduces the rate to 5% where the non-resident shareholder directly owns at least 25% of the WFOE's capital and satisfies the beneficial-owner and 365-day holding-period requirements under the relevant DTA.
