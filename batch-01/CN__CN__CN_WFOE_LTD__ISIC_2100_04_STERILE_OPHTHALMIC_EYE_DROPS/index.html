Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum registered capital requirement applies to a wholly foreign-owned enterprise under current PRC law. Foreign investors must, however, declare a registered capital that is reasonably commensurate with the scale of the sterile ophthalmic manufacturing operation and must inject capital through a dedicated foreign-exchange capital account, followed by SAFE foreign direct investment (FDI) filing and independent capital verification by a PRC-licensed accounting firm. The contributed capital is not held in a blocked escrow account; it must remain in the corporate bank account and may not be withdrawn or diverted before the company's legitimate operational obligations are demonstrated to the bank under anti-money-laundering procedures.

Funding & unlock path: Fire protection design approval and final fire-completion acceptance inspection by the Fire and Rescue Brigade, including smoke detection, sprinkler suppression, hazardous-material storage and emergency egress. Civil-defense structural review is required for the physical facility. Occupational-hazard pre-evaluation and effectiveness evaluation are mandatory with workplace air monitoring under GBZ 2.1. A work-safety registration and emergency response plan must be filed. Sterile cleanroom suites must be validated to GMP Grade A, B, C and D classification, with sterilizer safety inspection and cleanroom environmental monitoring certificate obtained before commercial production.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

E-government filing portal

The WFOE, notwithstanding its foreign ownership, is a Chinese resident enterprise and must complete company registration with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) to obtain the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which is the statutory tax identification number for both enterprise income tax and VAT. Within 30 days of issuance of the business license, the entity must complete tax registration at the competent local STA office corresponding to its registered address, including confirmation of taxpayer type, legal representative real-name authentication, digital certificate activation, and bank account reporting. All WFOEs must register for VAT at formation regardless of turnover; mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration applies when annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000, while turnover below that threshold may remain small-scale taxpayer status unless a general taxpayer election is made.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to qualified goods physically exported from China, including sterile ophthalmic eye drops exported as finished pharmaceutical or medical products, when the WFOE is a VAT general taxpayer, has made the export customs declaration in its own name or through a licensed export agent, has received foreign currency settlement or approved RMB cross-border settlement from the overseas buyer, and files an export VAT exemption and refund claim with the STA supported by verifiable transaction documents. Qualifying offshore digital services may be zero-rated only where they are within the statutory cross-border zero-rated services catalogue and the services are consumed wholly outside China.

China operates the Golden Tax Phase IV electronic invoice control system. VAT general taxpayers must issue invoices through the STA-approved e-invoice platform; fully digitalized electronic invoices receive a unique 20-digit electronic invoice number and encrypted tax-control code, with invoice data uploaded to the STA in near real time before issuance. ERP systems must integrate through approved APIs for bulk invoice issuance, legal representatives and financial officers must authenticate via digital certificates and tax U-Key, and high-risk transactions trigger STA risk-control approval or invoice-issuance blocking. Fraudulent or out-of-sequence invoicing is subject to system suspension and statutory penalties.

Export zero-rating is subject to ex-post desk and field audits by the STA and customs authorities. Where zero-rating is claimed without actual export, without due receipt of foreign exchange, with false invoices, or through circular transactions, the STA will claw back all refunded VAT, impose late-payment interest, and assess penalties of 50% to five times the tax evaded under the Law of the People's Republic of China on Tax Collection and Administration. Criminal liability for fraudulent export-tax-rebate claims under the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China may result in fines and imprisonment up to life imprisonment, and the export operating qualifications of repeat offenders may be revoked.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a managed floating exchange-rate regime with current-account convertibility broadly permitted and capital-account transactions strictly controlled by SAFE. Under the Foreign Exchange Administration Regulations, payments and receipts require genuine underlying transaction documentation and must be monitored through SAFE's digital banking systems. For a WFOE manufacturing sterile ophthalmic eye drops, routine imports of active pharmaceutical ingredients, medical-grade packaging, equipment, raw materials and eligible service fees generally qualify as current-account transactions and may be settled or paid after bank due-diligence review of the trade documentation. Remittance of after-tax profits to the foreign shareholder is procedurally allowed as a current-account transfer only after audited financial statements, tax clearance certificates, board resolutions and SAFE registration records confirm that the dividends derive from legitimate operating earnings and there are no unpaid statutory taxes. By contrast, inbound equity capital and shareholder loans are capital-account items requiring SAFE capital project registration, a dedicated capital account and post-receipt capital verification by an authorised Chinese CPA. Currency conversion is not freely elective; the bank must validate each conversion against the stated capital-use plan and underlying trade or investment transaction. There is no fixed mandatory surrender ratio for export receipts, but all export foreign currency must be repatriated through licensed banks and reported via the Goods Trade Monitoring System. Profit-remittance hurdles include delayed tax clearance, incomplete audits, mismatched entity names, absence of SAFE FDI filing, and inconsistent customs declarations; all must be resolved before dividend wires will pass bank compliance.
