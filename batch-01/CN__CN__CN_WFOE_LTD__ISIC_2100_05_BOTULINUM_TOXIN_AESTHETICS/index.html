Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None. There is no statutory minimum paid-up capital or blocked escrow account requirement for a WFOE limited liability company under the PRC Company Law or Foreign Investment Law. The sole shareholder must subscribe the full registered capital within the time limit stated in the Articles of Association, which may not exceed five years from establishment, unless a shorter period is required by special regulatory rules. Funds contributed by the foreign shareholder must enter through a capital account and be used in accordance with SAFE foreign exchange administration requirements. Although no minimum is imposed by general company law, the NMPA drug manufacturing licence review will verify that the registered capital and funding plan are sufficient for the fermentation and lyophilization facility.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA); online services are delivered through the national Electronic Tax Administration Bureau (e-Tax Bureau) operated at provincial STA level.

E-government filing portal

A WFOE must complete tax registration within 30 days after the State Administration for Market Regulation issues its business license. The registration is filed through the local STA / e-Tax Bureau using the electronic business license and legal representative identity authentication; the resulting statutory tax identification number is the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which is recognized for all VAT, corporate income tax, and customs purposes. A foreign non-resident enterprise without a permanent establishment in China but deriving China-source income obtains a tax ID through the payer's withholding registration in the e-Tax Bureau, or by direct non-resident registration with the local STA using country-of-incorporation evidence, apostilled or notarized documents, and a Chinese translation, as required.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Every established taxpayer must register for VAT; annual taxable sales exceeding RMB 5,000,000 triggers compulsory VAT general taxpayer status, while annual taxable sales at or below RMB 5,000,000 may remain under the small-scale taxpayer simplified method unless the taxpayer elects general taxpayer status.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The 0% VAT rate and associated export VAT refund apply to goods physically exported from the PRC customs territory where the exporter is a registered VAT general taxpayer, holds export contracts and customs declarations, demonstrates actual shipment via transport documents, and receives payment in foreign exchange from the overseas buyer. The goods must not be on the export-prohibited or export-restricted list, and qualifying biological toxin products require the applicable pharmaceutical export permits.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Export Goods Declaration Form)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical shipment
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Export contract or sales agreement
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or equivalent bank export-proceeds collection verification certificate

VAT special invoices are issued exclusively through the State Taxation Administration-controlled VAT Invoice Management System; issuance, invoice inventory, and credit invoice data are uploaded to tax authorities in real time. The tax authority's fully digitalized electronic invoice program mandates API-compatible issuance through the e-Tax invoice platform, with statutory invoice codes, machine-readable digital signatures, and automated invoice-logic controls; pharmaceutical and medical aesthetics exports must reconcile invoice flows with customs declaration data to maintain clearance eligibility.

Upon audit, if the tax authority determines that zero-rating or refund documentation was falsified, incomplete, or unsupported by actual export and FX repatriation, the refunded VAT is clawed back with late-payment surcharge, penalties ranging from 50% to five times the evaded tax amount under the Tax Collection and Administrative Law, and possible criminal liability for forgery or fraudulent invoice issuance; unlawful export rebate cases may also suspend or revoke export tax privileges.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a managed floating exchange-rate regime with strict bifurcation between current-account and capital-account convertibility. Current-account receipts and payments are generally convertible after commercial banks complete authenticity and compliance reviews under the SAFE transaction-by-transaction verification framework. Capital-account transactions, including inbound direct investment and foreign debt, require separate SAFE registration, not merely bank filing; FDI capital contributions must be deposited in an overseas-investor capital project account before conversion to RMB. For a wholly foreign-owned enterprise in the medical aesthetics biotechnology sub-sector, FX administration is compounded by product-specific licensing and national restrictions on sensitive biological materials. Profit remittance is permitted after the annual financial statements are audited and audited profits are verified, but the remittance is subject to corporate income tax withholding, clearance of all FX compliance obligations, submission of tax payment certificates, and enhanced due diligence on the transaction purpose and beneficial ownership. Practical hurdles include reconciliation between customs declarations, NMPA import licences, invoices, SAFE monitoring data, and the actual manufacturing of botulinum toxin formulations. Non-trade payments require supporting agreements, while transfers to offshore affiliates are scrutinized for transfer-pricing and round-tripping red flags. The RMB is not freely convertible and controlled market access is maintained through SAFE and PBOC policy.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export foreign currency receipts must be repatriated to mainland China within 180 days of the date of export customs declaration unless SAFE grants a longer period. The statutory conversion ratio is not fixed; foreign currency receipts may be retained 100% in a current-account foreign exchange account or sold to a designated bank for RMB at the enterprise's election. However, 100% of the proceeds must enter the onshore banking system and be subjected to authenticity verification against the customs declaration, contract, invoice, packing list and logistics documents. Failure to repatriate within the statutory timeline can trigger SAFE penalties and a downgrade in the trade-external payment reputation list.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE), operating under the authority of the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Prohibited as a primary banking rail. Offshore digital EMI platforms are not licensed Chinese designated foreign exchange banks, are not connected to SAFE registration or verification networks, and are not eligible to hold a WFOE's RMB settlement account, capital project account, or capital-verification records. The PBOC and SAFE would treat such use as an unlawful cross-border currency conversion arrangement. A fintech EMI may only support ancillary offshore treasury at the parent group level, with no direct touch on the China WFOE's regulated banking books.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% corporate income tax (CIT) under the Enterprise Income Tax Law of the People's Republic of China (EIT Law) for a resident enterprise; a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise is a PRC tax resident and is taxed on worldwide income.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% on gross dividends paid by a PRC resident enterprise to a non-resident enterprise shareholder, unless an applicable DTA reduces the rate. Under the Mainland-Hong Kong Arrangement, the rate may be reduced to 5% when the beneficial owner is a company directly holding at least 25% of the WFOE's capital and satisfying the substantive operation and anti-conduit requirements.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: RMB 20,000,000 for annual related-party tangible property and service transactions; RMB 10,000,000 for related-party intangible or financial-asset transactions. A Master File and Country-by-Country reporting notification are additionally required for Chinese group members when the overseas consolidated group revenue in the preceding fiscal year exceeds RMB 5,500,000,000.

Economic substance scope: China does not apply OECD-style Economic Substance Regulations as a separate statute, but the EIT Law and SAT Announcement 2018 No. 9 impose a substance-over-form and beneficial-ownership doctrine. For a botulinum toxin WFOE, economic substance requires real offices, qualified scientific staff, board autonomy, actual fermentation/lyophilization operations in China, and meaningful management of risks; these constitute the core income-generating activities (CIGA). If the Hong Kong parent owns only legal title to the patent without employees, decision-making or risk-taking capacity, the arrangement may be disregarded and the dividend withholding rate increased to 10%, or the transaction recharacterized under the GAAR (EIT Law Article 47).

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Transfer Pricing and Thin Capitalization Abuse

Statutory sanction: EIT Law Articles 41 and 46; SAT Announcement 2016 No. 42; Tax Collection Administration Law Article 63. The tax authority may make a special tax adjustment, disallow excessive payments, impute interest, levy arrears interest, and impose a penalty of 50%-500% of the underpaid corporate income tax.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly approve false transfer pricing studies or authorize intercompany payments without connection to real services can be jointly and severally liable under Company Law Article 23 (piercing the corporate veil). Personal civil liability includes restitution of corporate assets, freezing of bank accounts, exit restrictions and inclusion in tax non-compliant blacklists; criminal prosecution under Criminal Law Article 201 for tax evasion may result in up to 7 years of imprisonment and fines.
Treaty Abuse and Beneficial Ownership Conduit

Statutory sanction: SAT Announcement 2018 No. 9; EIT Law Article 47 (GAAR) and Article 49; the outbound dividend is recharacterized as taxable at 10% statutory source withholding, with additional 5% WHT, overdue interest, and a 50%-500% penalty on the under-withheld amount. The agent (WFOE) is responsible for collection if it fails to withhold.

Director personal liability: Directors are treated as persons directly responsible for unpaid withholding tax; they can be personally fined up to 50% of the unpaid sum. Asset freezing, travel bans and exit blacklisting are available enforcement measures. If the treaty abuse is executed with forged residency documents or fraudulent invoices, criminal liability under Criminal Law Articles 205 or 201 applies, with imprisonment from 3 to 10 years and disqualification from serving as a director.
Illegal Specialist Drug Distribution, AML and False Invoicing

Statutory sanction: Drug Administration Law Article 114 (licence revocation and fine up to 15 times the illegal product value), PRC Criminal Law Articles 141 (counterfeit drugs), 191 (money laundering), 201 (tax evasion), and 205 (false VAT invoices); VAT Law and anti-money laundering fines under the People's Bank of China anti-money laundering regulations. Tax penalties include disallowance of false deductions, WHT recovery, daily surcharges and 50%-500% understatement penalties.

Director personal liability: Directors who participate in or fail to supervise the illegal export, false invoicing or laundering of payments are personally liable for monetary penalties and civil restitution. Courts may order director bans, travel bans, bank account freezes and asset confiscation. Conviction for money laundering may result in 5-10 years of imprisonment; issuing false VAT invoices carries up to 7 years; tax evasion carries up to 7 years; and the directors may be jointly liable for the entire unpaid corporate tax, overriding the limited liability structure.
