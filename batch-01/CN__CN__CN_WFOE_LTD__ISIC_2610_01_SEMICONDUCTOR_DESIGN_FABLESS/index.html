Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China, administered through the China Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax) online platform and the competent local STA office at the WFOE's registered address.

E-government filing portal

For a China-incorporated WFOE, the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) printed on the business license is the sole Tax Identification Number. The WFOE must, within 30 calendar days from the issuance of the business license, log into the STA e-Tax platform and complete the production/tax identification filing, including declaration of the financial officer and accounting system, report of bank deposit accounts, confirmation of tax collection method, and selection of VAT taxpayer status. The e-Tax system records the USCC as the taxpayer identification code used for all corporate income tax, VAT, customs export rebate, and withholding filings. A foreign non-resident enterprise with no establishment or place of business in China but deriving China-sourced income is registered by the entity that makes the withholding payment; the withholding payer files the non-resident registration using the e-Tax withholding module and the STA issues a non-resident taxpayer identification record for that purpose.

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No revenue threshold for basic tax registration; every WFOE must complete tax registration within 30 days of incorporation. Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of tangible semiconductor products are zero-rated for VAT if the goods are physically exported through customs and the exporter holds the customs export declaration together with proof of foreign exchange receipt. Qualifying cross-border fabless design services, integrated circuit IP-licensing, software development, and related technological services are zero-rated when the recipient is located outside the PRC, the services/intangibles are consumed or used wholly outside China, and the taxpayer holds the cross-border contract, foreign exchange receipt evidence, and supporting proof of offshore consumption as required under Cai Shui [2016] No. 36 and the STA measures on cross-border taxable services zero-rating/exemption. The export documents must be entered in the STA export VAT refund clearing system before the 0% rate is accepted.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Export Goods Declaration Form)
  • Bill of Lading (Original, Electronic, or Air Waybill)
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Export Sales Contract or Purchase Order
  • Foreign Exchange Receipt Record (Bank Receipt of Export Proceeds in Foreign Exchange / SAFE export proceeds monitoring declaration)
  • Export VAT Refund E-declaration Package Verified in the STA e-Tax System

China mandates real-time fiscal clearance through the STA Golden Tax Phase IV system and the Fully Digitalized Electronic Invoice (FD-EI) platform. All VAT taxpayers must issue special VAT invoices or fully digitalized e-invoices through the official e-invoice system; each invoice is digitally signed, assigned a unique invoice code and anti-counterfeiting QR code, and cleared in the STA tax cloud in near real time. ERP-to-tax-platform API integration is available for continuous transmission of invoice data. Invoices not cleared through the STA platform, not timely reported, or without the statutory digital stamp are invalid for input VAT credit, expense deduction, and export VAT refund purposes.

The STA and customs jointly scrutinize export zero-rating/refund claims using Golden Tax Phase IV risk analytics, export price benchmarks, inventory reconciliation, and bank foreign-exchange data. If the required customs declaration, transport documents, or foreign-exchange receipt records are missing, incomplete, fraudulent, or if export proceeds are not repatriated within the applicable deadline, the tax authority must claw back the VAT refund and reverse the zero-rating retrospectively. The taxpayer is also liable for overdue payment surcharges of 0.05% per day and an administrative penalty of not less than 50% and not more than five times the amount of tax refund falsely obtained or evaded under Article 63 of the Tax Collection and Administration Law. Fraudulent export-tax-refund schemes may additionally be prosecuted under Article 204 of the PRC Criminal Law, with criminal fines and imprisonment.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a bifurcated convertibility framework. Current account transactions, including trade in goods and services, interest, dividends, and royalties, are fully convertible under a registration-based system. Authorized banks review supporting documents (contracts, invoices, customs declarations) and settle the trade in RMB or foreign currency without case-by-case SAFE approval. Capital account transactions, in contrast, remain tightly controlled and require ex-ante approval or registration with SAFE for each specific flow. For a WFOE, the initial equity investment must be registered under the FDI scheme, and subsequent capital injections, shareholder loans, and cross-border guarantees require separate registration. Dividend remittances out of audited profits are classified as current account items and are freely remittable once tax clearance and audit are completed, but banks will verify the genuineness of the prior year's financial statements and tax filings. Surrender requirements for current account receipts were abolished in stages, and now exporters may retain 100% of foreign exchange earnings in designated accounts; however, all receipts must be repatriated to China within the statutory period. Profit remittance hurdles include obtaining a recent audit report, verifying the enterprise has met its tax obligations, and demonstrating the absence of outstanding SAFE investigations. The overall system is therefore open for trade but remains a 'managed float' for capital flows, requiring strict documentation and designated bank channels.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export proceeds must be repatriated into China within 180 calendar days from the date of customs declaration (unless a longer grace is approved by SAFE). There is no mandatory percentage conversion ratio to RMB for current account export receipts; enterprises may hold 100% in foreign exchange settlement accounts or voluntarily convert. However, any non-converted balances must be utilized for bona fide trade, services, or equity-related expenditures. The historical 'surrender' obligation now only refers to the repatriation duty, with conversion at the enterprise's discretion.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low feasibility. China's licensing regime requires a domestic payment institution license (PBOC) for any entity offering account-to-account transfers in CNY or holding funds for residents. Offshore fintech EMIs, such as Wise, Revolut, or Airwallex, do not hold the requisite licenses for servicing mainland entities. These platforms are legally barred from opening local currency accounts for WFOEs and cannot act as intermediaries for inbound FDI capital injections, FX settlement, or SAFE reporting. They may be used only for ancillary low-value outbound payments if the counterparty is willing to receive through their global network, but this does not constitute compliant clearing for corporate treasury. All primary banking and FX transactions must be executed through regulated domestic banks.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant routing begins with an FDI registration certificate and SAFE foreign exchange registration. The foreign investor remits the equity contribution via SWIFT MT103 or MT202 to the WFOE's designated foreign exchange capital account at a domestic Tier-1 bank. The bank debits the incoming remittance and, upon satisfaction of the authenticity and consistency check, converts the funds into RMB at the spot rate or via a settlement instruction. The capital must be used within the registered business scope. A Chinese certified public accountant must issue a capital verification report within 30 days of the contribution. All remittances must include the correct FX purpose code, typically '821011' for remittance of foreign currency investment, and the receiving bank must upload the information to the SAFE monitoring system.For operational payments including semiconductor design tools, subcontracting, and IP royalties, use the SWIFT MT103 for high-value, low-frequency payments with full compliance screening. For procurement of manufacturing equipment and raw materials, a confirmed or unconfirmed Letter of Credit (LC) via the domestic bank's advising channel is a standard clearing rail. Documentary collections are less common but acceptable for small-value regular settlements. All outbound transfers must be supported by underlying contracts, invoices, and customs records to pass the bank's current account veracity review.Onshore CNY deliverable forward contracts are permitted for the WFOE's real trade or capital flows, with a bank facility line and no speculative positioning; tenors are typically up to 12 months, extendable to 18-24 months for project-related exposures.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are not available onshore for CNY because the currency is not fully convertible and the onshore deliverable forward market already exists; offshore NDFs are outside the SAFE licensing boundary and cannot be settled with domestic CNY liquidity, therefore only onshore deliverable forwards or option alternatives are permissible.Cross-currency interest rate swaps (CCIRS) are permissible strictly for hedging future foreign-currency debt service or investment cash flows, with a demonstrated underlying exposure, and must be transacted through a licensed domestic bank and reported to the China Foreign Exchange Trade System (CFETS).

They may be used only for ancillary low-value outbound payments if the counterparty is willing to receive through their global network, but this does not constitute compliant clearing for corporate treasury. All primary banking and FX transactions must be executed through regulated domestic banks.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File is mandatory when any annual related-party transaction threshold is exceeded: RMB 100 million for tangible property transfers, RMB 50 million for intangible property transfers, RMB 100 million for financial asset transfers, and RMB 100 million for related services. Master File is mandatory when the MNE group's consolidated revenue exceeds RMB 2 billion. The fabless WFOE must also file the annual Related Party Transaction Report (Form G100000) regardless of the threshold.

Economic substance scope: The PRC has no standalone Economic Substance Regulation (ESR), but the anti-abuse rules (EIT Law Article 47) and the general anti-avoidance rule (GAAR, Article 120 of the Implementing Regulations) deny treaty and statutory benefits to shell arrangements. The revenue authority reviews Core Income-Generating Activities (CIGA) in China: for a fabless design enterprise, the design team, R&D control, and IP management must have real functions, assets, and risks in China. Without active personnel and physical operations in China, treaty relief may be denied and a 50%-500% penalty applied.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion and false declaration

Statutory sanction: Articles 63 and 64 of the Law of the PRC on the Administration of Tax Collection: payment of the underpaid tax plus a penalty of 50%-5 times the amount of the underpaid tax, and daily late surcharge of 0.05%.

Director personal liability: The legal representative and supervising director may be jointly and severally liable for restitution of the underpaid tax and can be subjected to exit denial (blacklisting) by the tax administration for undeclared balances; egregious fraud can trigger criminal tax evasion under Article 201 of the Criminal Law, with imprisonment of up to 7 years and fines.
Transfer pricing manipulation and thin capitalization

Statutory sanction: Under Article 41 of the EIT Law and the Special Tax Adjustment Measures (STA Announcement 6 of 2017), the tax authority may re-determine taxable income and impose a 5-year interest surcharge plus a penalty of 50%-500% of the additional tax where transactions are found not at arm's length.

Director personal liability: If the WFOE is insolvent and cannot pay the adjusted liability, the director/controller may be directly liable to creditors and to the tax authority for fraudulent conveyance; criminal consequences apply where illicit profit shifting is used with intent to evade tax, leading to personal asset seizure and imprisonment for up to 7 years.
Shell company and DTA relief abuse

Statutory sanction: Article 47 (EIT Law) and Article 120 of the Implementing Regulations empower the STA to apply a general anti-avoidance adjustment, recharacterize the transaction, and impose a penalty of 50%-500% of the tax understated, plus interest; denial of treaty relief with retrospective effect.

Director personal liability: Under the Company Law and financial supervisory rules, directors may be held personally civilly liable for the failure of the entity; for cross-border fraud, the same acts can result in placing the director on the STA's serious taxpayer list, which blocks exit from China, freezes personal bank accounts, and exposes the director to extradition if money laundering is also proven.
