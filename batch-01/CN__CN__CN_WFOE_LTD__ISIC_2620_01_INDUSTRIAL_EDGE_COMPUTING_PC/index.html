Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum registered capital and no blocked escrow account requirement for a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise under the revised PRC Company Law and Foreign Investment Law. The registered capital is set by the shareholders in the Articles of Association and must be fully subscribed in cash or kind; under Article 47 of the Company Law (2023), subscribed capital must be paid in full within five years from establishment unless a special industry law provides otherwise. Paid-in contributions must be remitted into the WFOE's licensed RMB or foreign currency capital account in China and verified by the bank; no regulatory escrow arrangement is imposed. The foreign investor may contribute in USD or another freely convertible currency, and SAFE foreign exchange recordal is completed through the receiving bank.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA), operating through the nationwide electronic tax service platform (e-Tax Bureau) and local competent tax offices.

E-government filing portal

A Foreign Wholly-Owned Enterprise (WFOE) is automatically registered for tax purposes upon incorporation, obtaining a Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) from the Administration for Market Regulation, which also serves as the statutory tax identification number (TIN/VAT identification) and is synchronized to the STA. The WFOE must then complete tax registration particulars in the STA electronic tax service platform within 30 days of the business license issuance, including confirmation of VAT general taxpayer status, enterprise income tax filing basis, payroll withholding registration, and bank account reporting. For a foreign non-resident enterprise without a permanent establishment, tax registration must be obtained through the competent STA office using the statutory non-resident tax registration procedure, under which the non-resident enterprise receives a separate tax identification number for fulfillment of China-source withholding tax obligations.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None. Chinese tax law does not provide check-the-box or entity classification elections for a WFOE. A WFOE is mandatorily taxed as a resident enterprise under the Enterprise Income Tax Law. Treaty benefits are applied through filing and documentation rather than elective classification; hybrid mismatch rules are not elective.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 annual taxable sales, which is the mandatory threshold for VAT general taxpayer registration; all VAT taxpayers must register with the STA, but those below the threshold are classified as small-scale taxpayers under the simplified method.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export sales of goods physically shipped out of China qualify for the 0% VAT rate provided the WFOE is a VAT general taxpayer, the goods are actually exported with customs clearance, the export proceeds are repatriated in foreign exchange within the statutory deadline, and the transaction is fully documented. Qualifying offshore services rendered to overseas clients may also be zero-rated under applicable Ministry of Finance and STA regulations. Domestic sales of rugged industrial computers and edge gateways are taxable at 13%.

  • Customs Export Goods Declaration (export bill of entry)
  • Bill of Lading or equivalent multimodal transport document
  • Commercial invoice and foreign sales contract
  • VAT special invoice or export invoice reflecting zero-rating
  • Bank foreign exchange receipt certificate or SAFE-verified export proceeds record
  • Export tax refund/zero-rating declaration and reconciliation ledger

The STA operates the National Electronic Invoice Service Platform. VAT invoices must be issued through STA-certified tax-control systems and fully digitalized electronic invoices are reported in real-time to the platform, which assigns a unique anti-fraud invoice number, dynamic QR code, and digital stamping before the invoice is valid for tax credit or export refund purposes. API-based integration is mandatory for high-volume issuers.

If the STA determines that zero-rating was claimed without actual export, without verified foreign exchange repatriation, or based on false documentation, the refund is disallowed and clawed back with late-payment surcharge of 0.05% per day from the due date. Fraudulent export refund claims are penalized at one to five times the refunded amount under Article 66 of the Tax Administration and Collection Law, with criminal liability for export tax fraud, suspension of export tax refund qualification, and inclusion in the tax non-compliance blacklist.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China maintains a guarded capital account with full convertibility on the current account for bona fide trade and service transactions under the Foreign Exchange Administration Regulations. RMB is convertible for current-account transactions, but capital-account transactions require entity-level registration at SAFE, including foreign debt filing, direct investment registration, and capital account opening. For a WFOE, the paid-in capital injection is treated as foreign direct investment and must be registered with SAFE before wiring; subsequent profit remittance requires submission of audited financial statements, corporate income tax filing proof, and a SAFE-registered bank's verification of the same. Export proceeds are repatriated to the entity's domestic foreign exchange account and may be kept as foreign currency or settled to CNY at the entity's discretion. The practical hurdles to full capital outflow include the requirement to prove the commercial genuineness of each outward remittance, maintain at least a 5-year retention of trade documents, and observe NDRC and MOFCOM reporting thresholds for cross-border transaction flows. Accordingly, currency convertibility is legally open on the current account but functionally constrained by SAFE monitoring and purpose-code verification on the capital account.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export foreign currency receipts must be fully repatriated into the WFOE's domestic designated foreign exchange account and verified against the Customs export declaration via the SAFE trade monitoring system within 30 calendar days. The statutory mandatory conversion ratio is effectively 0%, i.e. no forced surrender into CNY; settlement into RMB is voluntary and can be executed at any time, but any non-settlement in foreign currency requires continuous foreign exchange income reporting to the central bank.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBoC) and State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Low. Fintech electronic money institutions such as Wise, Revolut, or Payoneer are not licensed by the PBoC to provide banking services or maintain onshore clearing accounts for corporates in mainland China. A WFOE cannot rely on an offshore EMI as its primary treasury interface because local FX settlement and capital account receipts must flow through a SAFE-designated domestic bank. Offshore EMIs can only function as a supplementary collection/outreach corridor for small values, and only where the underlying payment is not a capital-account transaction; even then, the WFOE's legal books and tax filings will require full transparency of any offsetting transaction.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound FDI capital contributions for the WFOE must be routed through a designated domestic bank via SWIFT MT103 wire, referencing the unique SAFE Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) Code and an invoice paying purpose code classified as 'Equity Capital Contribution - foreign direct investment remittance'. The bank performs a pre-credit check against the SAFE registration file; the funds are temporarily booked to a suspense account and cannot be used until the accountant issues a formal capital verification report and the local branch completes the foreign currency capital settlement. Only after such verification may the funds be transferred to the WFOE's operating RMB account and disbursed according to the board-approved cash flow.Outbound trade disbursements for production inputs are cleared through the SWIFT MT103 network for T/T settlements, MT700 for commercial LCs where suppliers require a bank undertaking, and SWIFT MT400 for documentary collection against presentation of shipping documents; all outbound wires require an official supplier invoice, proof of customs registration where applicable, and must be reported into the SAFE trade receipts and payments system for verification against the original import declaration.Onshore RMB/USD and RMB/EUR deliverable forwards available only through PBoC- and SAFE-licensed banks, backed by genuine trade exposure with a maximum tenor of 12 months.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are not permitted within the onshore Chinese market for domestic-licensed entities; WFOEs are restricted from executing NDFs in the domestic balance sheet unless booked through offshore subsidiary accounts under a separate SAFE case-by-case approval.Cross-currency swaps are permissible only where the underlying risk is a real converted capital or trade-flow currency requirement, and the transaction must be registered in the bank's counterparty credit and SAFE's derivative trade reporting system.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% under PRC Enterprise Income Tax Law Article 4; resident enterprises (including WFOEs) are taxed on worldwide income. Non-resident enterprises without a permanent establishment are taxed at 10% on China-source dividends, interest, royalties, and rentals, subject to tax treaty relief.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% on dividends distributed by the China-resident WFOE to a non-resident corporate shareholder, imposed under Article 3(3) and Article 4(2) of the PRC Enterprise Income Tax Law and Articles 37/38 of the Detailed Implementation Regulations. No withholding applies to dividends paid to another resident enterprise; DTA reductions may apply to non-resident shareholders.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Quarterly prepayments: within 15 days after the close of each quarter or month. Annual return: within five months after the fiscal year-end. A calendar-year WFOE must file the final annual return and settle by 31 May of the following year.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File: required for a China resident taxpayer if, in the preceding taxable year, any of the following related-party transaction thresholds is exceeded - tangible property transactions above RMB 200 million; financial asset transactions above RMB 100 million; intangible property transactions above RMB 20 million; or other related-party transactions above RMB 40 million. Master File: required where the total aggregate amount of related-party transactions of the consolidated group exceeds RMB 1 billion, or where the enterprise is selected or instructed by the SAT to prepare it.

Economic substance scope: Mainland China does not operate a standalone Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) code in the style of offshore financial centers; nevertheless, the PRC Enterprise Income Tax Law applies substance-over-form and general anti-avoidance under Article 47, and SAT notices on treaty benefit entitlement require the beneficial owner to conduct real economic activity. A WFOE manufacturing rugged industrial computers and edge gateways must demonstrate that its personnel, factories, inventory, pricing decisions, risk management, research if applicable, and core income-generating activities (CIGA) physically and strategically reside in China; a shell that merely signs contracts and invoices while the parent controls all economics will be recharacterized and denied treaty benefits.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion through fabricated cost deductions and false VAT invoices

Statutory sanction: Violates Article 8 of the EIT Law, Articles 21(2) and 63 of the PRC Tax Collection and Administration Law, and Article 204/205 of the PRC Criminal Law (crime of making falsely issued special VAT invoices and crime of evading tax/export tax refund fraud). Financial penalty: recovery of tax underpaid plus 50% to 500% penalty; daily late-payment surcharge at 0.05%; criminal fines up to the amount of evaded tax; imprisonment up to 7 years for tax evasion; up to 10 years for export tax refund fraud.

Director personal liability: Courts and tax authorities may pierce the corporate veil under the PRC Company Law and TCAL implementation rules: the director/legal representative who signs tax returns or directs false invoicing is jointly and severally liable for the corporate tax debt with personal assets; personal bank accounts may be frozen (asset preservation); a heftier travel restriction is imposed through the High Court enforcement blacklist ('restricted high-consumption list'), including prohibition on overseas air/high-speed rail travel; criminal liability attaches personally, including detention and imprisonment, especially when the legal representative orders or acquiesces in the false invoicing.
Shell company structure with no economic substance and treaty shopping for reduced withholding

Statutory sanction: Denial of treaty benefits under the PRC EIT Law Article 47, the general anti-avoidance rule, and the Beneficial Ownership Notice (SAT Announcement 2018 No. 9). The tax authority may recharacterize the transaction, refuse the reduced rate, impose the full statutory 10% withholding plus late-payment surcharge, and assess penalties between 50% and 500% of the tax underpaid.

Director personal liability: A director who causes the WFOE to execute artificial conduit contracts or to facilitate treaty-benefit fraud may be held civilly liable for restitution of the unpaid withholding tax; the competent tax court may order freezing of personal savings, shares, and real estate; an asset preservation order may extend to overseas enforcement under China's international asset-sharing mechanisms; the individual may be subject to a travel ban and placed on the tax-integrity blacklist; in cases of malicious false beneficial-ownership declarations, criminal tax evasion liability may be pursued.
Undervalued export invoices and profit shifting in related-party industrial computer exports

Statutory sanction: Contravenes the arm's length principle under EIT Law Article 41 and the Special Tax Adjustment Implementation Measures (SAT Order No. 6 and SAT Announcement 2016 No. 42); Customs Law and VAT export refund regulations characterize the under-valuation as false declaration; penalty includes customs fines of up to three times the undeclared duty, VAT export refund clawback, and under TCAL Article 64 a 50-500% penalty on the transferred profit; criminal liability for false export/receiving export tax refunds under Criminal Law Article 204 can lead to imprisonment up to 10 years or more for large amounts.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly sign low-priced transfer pricing invoices or instruct the misdeclaration of customs values render the corporate veil pierceable: they are jointly liable for the incremental taxes, customs duties, and export refunds wrongfully obtained; personal assets can be attached by the tax and customs authorities; the director's exit visa is cancelled/blocked by the court; where deliberate evasion exceeds statutory thresholds, imprisonment for up to ten years may be applied, in addition to monetary fines and confiscation of personal gains.
