Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital for a WFOE under current PRC law. The registered capital must be subscribed as stated in the Articles of Association, with a typical contribution period of up to 30 years for manufacturing entities. The first tranche of not less than 20% of the subscribed capital must be paid within 90 days of business license issuance. Capital must be injected into a foreign exchange capital account (FDI account) and verified by a certified public accountant. No escrow account is required, but the capital must be fully subscribed and paid in accordance with the schedule in the Articles of Association.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety Certificate (消防验收合格) from the local Fire and Rescue Department, requiring a fire safety inspection of the factory premises, including fire exits, extinguishers, and sprinkler systems. Occupational health and safety approval from the local Work Safety Commission (应急管理局), including a workplace safety assessment, employee safety training, and compliance with the Work Safety Law of the PRC.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA) and the nationwide E-Tax Bureau electronic portal, operating under the Golden Tax Phase IV integrated information system; customs and foreign exchange matters interface with the General Administration of Customs of China (GACC) and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE).

E-government filing portal

A China-resident WFOE obtains an 18-character Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) from the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) upon incorporation. The USCC is the legal entity identifier and taxpayer identification number used by the State Taxation Administration. Tax registration is completed within 30 calendar days of business licence issuance through the E-Tax Bureau by filing the enterprise registration particulars, investment and shareholder details, directors and finance personnel, bank account information, and accounting/fiscal system details. The tax authority synchronises registration data with customs (GACC) and SAFE via the multi-department information-sharing platform; an electronic tax registration certificate is system-generated. For a foreign non-resident enterprise with China-source income and no establishment or place of business in China, the withholding agent in China is required to register the non-resident for tax matters and obtain/record a non-resident taxpayer identification number. If the non-resident applies directly, it must submit its home-country tax registration certificate, incorporation charter, identity/status documentation of authorised representatives, and evidence of the expected China-source income. The WFOE must also file with GACC for customs registration and foreign trade operator filing before conducting export trade, and must determine its VAT status, whether as a small-scale taxpayer or as a VAT general taxpayer, under the E-Tax Bureau.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None. China does not provide check-the-box elections for a WFOE limited liability company. A WFOE is mandatorily treated as a resident enterprise taxpayer under the Enterprise Income Tax Law and is taxed at entity level on worldwide income; no pass-through treatment or hybrid mismatch election is available for income tax purposes. For VAT, the only available election is between small-scale taxpayer treatment and VAT general taxpayer registration where eligible; this is an administrative tax status election, not an entity classification election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB (CNY) 5,000,000 of annual taxable sales; exceeding this threshold makes registration as a VAT general taxpayer mandatory. A taxpayer below the threshold is generally a small-scale VAT taxpayer and may, subject to conditions, voluntarily elect general taxpayer status, which is also required to access the export VAT zero-rating and refund regime.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A VAT general taxpayer exporting active noise cancelling headphones may apply the VAT zero rate for the export sale with refund or credit of input VAT, namely the exemption-refund method, if the goods are actually exported under customs supervision, the export is evidenced by a valid electronic export customs declaration, the sales proceeds are received and repatriated in foreign exchange in accordance with SAFE requirements, the exporter holds genuine export contracts and transport documents, the export refund declaration is filed within the statutory deadline, and the exported goods are not subject to export prohibition or tax refund restriction. Qualifying cross-border services delivered to overseas customers and consumed outside China are also zero-rated if supported by service contract, delivery evidence, and foreign exchange receipts.

  • Electronic export customs declaration with customs assigned declaration number, issued by GACC
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Bill of Lading, air waybill, or other international transport document applicable to the shipment
  • Export sales contract or order confirmation
  • Foreign exchange receipt confirmation from the settlement bank, or export proceeds receipt record filed in SAFE's Trade in Goods Foreign Exchange Monitoring System
  • VAT invoice issued through the STA e-invoice platform for the export sale, together with input VAT credit evidence for the related purchases
  • Export tax refund or zero-rating declaration and reconciliation data submitted in the E-Tax Bureau export rebate module, cross-matched with customs export data

Invoices must be issued under the State Taxation Administration's nationwide VAT invoice control system integrated with Golden Tax Phase IV. VAT special invoices and ordinary invoices are generated either through the full digital e-invoice platform or approved tax control equipment, namely a U-Key or tax disc, and invoice data is transmitted to the tax authority in real time at issuance. Each invoice carries a unique nationwide invoice number and anti-counterfeit QR or digital watermark requirement; no invoice issued outside the STA system is legally valid for input VAT credit or export refund. Buyers must authenticate invoices on the national VAT invoice verification platform before crediting input VAT. The tax authority performs real-time cross-checking of invoice data against VAT returns, customs export declarations, and corporate income tax data, with automatic risk alerts for mismatch, split invoicing, or false invoicing.

Export zero-rating and refund claims are subject to joint data scrutiny by the State Taxation Administration and GACC, including cross-matching of invoice data, customs declarations, logistics documents, payment trails, and foreign exchange receipt records. If the claimed export is false or an export is not supported by genuine foreign exchange repatriation within the statutory deadline, generally 180 days from export unless an extension is approved by SAFE, the tax authority will claw back the refunded input VAT or zero-rated output VAT, require amended returns, charge interest at 0.05% per day of overdue tax, and impose administrative penalties under the Tax Collection Administration Law of up to five times the evaded or fraudulently refunded amount. Serious cases of fraudulent export tax refund through false declarations or counterfeit invoices trigger criminal liability under the Criminal Law, including significant fines, forfeiture of illegal gains, and imprisonment up to life imprisonment; the taxpayer may also be blacklisted on the tax integrity joint-punishment list, lose existing export refund facilitation status, and be barred from applying export refunds for a specified period.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a managed floating exchange rate regime under a current-account-convertible framework, but capital-account transactions remain strictly controlled by SAFE. For a WFOE, ordinary operational FX flows - export collections, service fees, royalties, and trade-related payments - may flow through the current account with bank-based verification and no pre-approval beyond the trade monitoring system. However, inbound equity contributions, shareholder loans, acquisitions, cross-border guarantees, and repatriation of capital have to pass through the capital account mechanism: SAFE FDI registration, designated foreign exchange capital account, post-arrival use-of-funds examination, and de-registration upon liquidation. Profit remittance is legally permitted once the annual audited financial statements are prepared, corporate income tax has been settled, and a dividend distribution resolution is passed, but the bank will require the full documentary trail to confirm that retained earnings were generated by real business activity and that the remitted amount does not exceed distributable after-tax profits. Strict foreign exchange controls remain in place for speculative or purely financial cross-border flows, and all RMB-FX conversions must be conducted through a licensed bank at market rates. As a result, the WFOE's treasury operations should be designed for submit-then-use verification, not free convertibility.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the People's Bank of China current account management framework, export proceeds from goods must be collected through the SAFE trade monitoring system within 90 days of export customs declaration; if delayed beyond 90 days, the enterprise must apply for deferred collection registration before the deadline. There is no mandatory surrender ratio: after arrival in the domestic bank account, the WFOE may retain foreign currency or convert it into RMB at a licensed bank; repatriation must be made to a domestic account and the matching with the customs declaration must be completed through the bank's verification mechanism.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China; State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) as the dedicated cabinet-level regulator for foreign exchange control.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low. Offshore digital EMI institutions, payment fintech platforms, and virtual multi-currency account providers are not licensed to open regulated current or capital accounts in the name of a PRC-licensed WFOE and cannot complete SAFE/FDI reporting on behalf of the entity. At best, they can serve as supplemental collection or disbursement tools for overseas transactions only when final funding settles through a licensed domestic bank; they must never become the principal treasury rail because the KYC validation and regulatory acceptance failure risk is severe.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The foreign investor must complete SAFE FDI registration before remittance and open a foreign exchange capital account in the WFOE name at a designated domestic bank. Remit funds using SWIFT MT103 with the remitter name matching the registered shareholder and the payment purpose code for direct investment equity contribution; the receiving bank validates the payment against SAFE's capital project information system, credits the foreign exchange capital account, and monitors use against the approved scope. After settlement, a Chinese certified public accountant should issue a capital verification report confirming paid-in capital; RMB conversion for operations must then follow the bank's capital account use rules, not the general current-account convertibility channel.For supplier payments and trade settlements, the WFOE should maintain a basic current account at a licensed domestic settlement bank and use SWIFT MT103 for direct remittance of confirmed open-account invoices, commercial letters of credit issued by the WFOE's bank for strategic or high-value purchases, and documentary collections for repetitive lower-risk shipments. All outbound payments must be supported by valid trade documents, booked with the correct SAFE trade transaction codes, and reconciled against the matching customs and logistics records.Onshore deliverable USD/CNY forward contracts executed with the domestic bank under the real-hedging principle and supported by documented trade exposure.Cross-currency interest rate swaps and FX swap agreements for matching the tenor of capital account and operational treasury flows, subject to bank credit limits and underlying project approval.Deliverable FX options (including call/put combinations) traded onshore when the underlying exposure is a firm forecasted receivable or payable; premium payments are permissible through the domestic account.Offshore non-deliverable forwards (NDF) are not permissible as a direct hedge for the mainland WFOE; only the overseas parent may access CNH NDF outside the PRC regulatory perimeter for consolidation-level exposure, and the WFOE cannot settle or funding such instruments domestically.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low. Offshore digital EMI institutions, payment fintech platforms, and virtual multi-currency account providers are not licensed to open regulated current or capital accounts in the name of a PRC-licensed WFOE and cannot complete SAFE/FDI reporting on behalf of the entity. At best, they can serve as supplemental collection or disbursement tools for overseas transactions only when final funding settles through a licensed domestic bank; they must never become the principal treasury rail because the KYC validation and regulatory acceptance failure risk is severe.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File must be prepared when annual related-party tangible-asset transfers exceed RMB 200 million, intangible-asset transfers exceed RMB 100 million, financial-asset transfers exceed RMB 100 million, or other related-party transactions exceed RMB 40 million; Master File must be prepared when aggregate annual related-party transactions exceed RMB 1 billion; annual related-party transaction disclosure forms must be filed with the CIT annual return regardless of threshold

Economic substance scope: Mainland China does not apply a zero-tax Economic Substance Regulations regime, but Chinese tax authorities invoke the substance-over-form principle under EIT Law Article 47, anti-avoidance rules, and the beneficial-ownership test to recharacterize Shell Company structures. The WFOE's Core Income-Generating Activities (CIGA) for design, procurement, component manufacturing, assembly, quality control, warranty management and IP exploitation must be performed in China with people, premises, assets, functions and risks; a WFOE lacking economic substance while booking low margins and shifting profits to an offshore related party will see its profits adjusted and dividends recharacterized

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Transfer Pricing Manipulation / Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: EIT Law Articles 41 and 47; PRC Tax Collection and Administration Law Articles 36 and 63; the tax authority makes an adjustment to arm's length profit and imposes a penalty of 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax plus a daily late-payment surcharge of 0.05%; where the evasion amount is relatively large and the evaded tax ratio exceeds the criminal threshold, the case is referred under Criminal Law Article 201

Director personal liability: A court may pierce the corporate veil under PRC Company Law Article 20 where abuse of limited liability causes the tax debt, making the directors jointly and severally liable for unpaid tax, penalties and interest; the legal representative can be blacklisted and placed on an exit-control/travel ban until the tax debt is settled; criminal conviction for tax evasion can carry imprisonment of up to 3 years for ordinary amounts and 3 to 7 years for huge amounts, plus confiscation of assets and fines
Failure to Maintain Contemporaneous Transfer Pricing Documentation and Related-Party Disclosure

Statutory sanction: Under the Special Tax Adjustment Measures and EIT Law Article 44, the tax authority may assess taxable income based on available information and impose a penalty of 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax; the failure to produce contemporaneous documentation also reverses the burden of proof onto the taxpayer, exposes hidden margins to full adjustment and triggers interest

Director personal liability: Directors who personally direct the concealment or destruction of transfer pricing documents face joint and several liability for the adjusted tax where the corporate veil is pierced; if false invoices or forged documentation are used to substantiate deductions, the directors also face criminal liability for fraudulent tax declaration under Criminal Law Article 201 and Article 205 in VAT-related cases, with imprisonment up to 7 years and an exit ban
Withholding Tax Fraud and Treaty/Beneficial Ownership Abuse

Statutory sanction: The reduced treaty rate is denied and the WFOE is liable for the additional 5% or more statutory withholding tax, plus late-payment surcharge; under Tax Collection and Administration Law Article 69, the withholding agent that fails to withhold or under-withholds is liable for the withheld tax and may be fined 50% to 300% of the unwithheld amount; false treaty claim documents constitute tax fraud

Director personal liability: Directors who intentionally structure the conduit arrangement are jointly and severally liable for the under-withheld tax, interest and penalties under Company Law Article 20; tax authorities may apply exit-control blacklisting against the legal representative; criminal liability under Criminal Law Articles 201 and 202 for withholding tax embezzlement can result in imprisonment of up to 7 years, criminal fines and judicial confiscation of personal assets benefiting from the arrangement
