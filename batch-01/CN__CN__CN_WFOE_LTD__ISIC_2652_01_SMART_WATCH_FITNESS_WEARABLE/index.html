Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-up share capital applies to an ordinary WFOE engaged in smart wearable manufacturing. The shareholders must stipulate the registered capital amount in the Articles of Association and subscribe all shares in full. Under Article 47 of the Company Law (2023 Revision), shareholders must pay in their subscribed capital within five years from the date of incorporation unless a special law requires a shorter period. No blocked escrow account is required at pre-registration stage, but contributed funds must be remitted from the foreign shareholder to a dedicated registered-capital account in a bank licensed by the People's Bank of China, retained until the business license is issued, and clearly labelled as registered capital contribution. If the company later expands into cellular smart watch equipment requiring a telecommunications licence, the relevant telecommunications regulator may impose additional minimum paid-up capital conditions. For the current OEM smart watch and smart ring manufacturing scope, no mandatory escrow or minimum capital rule applies.

Funding & unlock path: Before commencing production, the factory must obtain a Fire Protection Design Review Opinion and a Fire Protection Completion Acceptance Certificate from the Fire and Rescue Section of the local Emergency Management Bureau. The building must meet the fire-resistance, evacuation, safety spacing and alarm requirements of GB 50016-2014, and the premises must pass structural load inspection certifying that any mezzanine, vibration table or heavy equipment area can support the design load. The company must complete an occupational hazard evaluation under the Law on Work Safety and conduct workplace monitoring for soldering fumes, noise, heat stress and electromagnetic fields at the limits prescribed in GBZ 2.1-2019, with personal protective equipment supplied to workers. A Work Safety Production record or permit must be filed with the local Emergency Management Bureau, and if the factory includes a warehouse storing batteries, separate dangerous goods storage clearance is required. Urban civil defence authorities may additionally impose underground civil air defence construction obligations if the factory building project meets the statutory floor-area threshold, and such approval must be appended to the fire clearance file.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China, acting through the local State Tax Service bureau in the WFOE's domicile jurisdiction; day-to-day filings are made through the STA National Unified Electronic Tax Service Platform (e-Tax).

E-government filing portal

The WFOE's tax identity is created automatically when the Administration for Market Regulation issues the business license with the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code, which is the taxpayer registration number for corporate income tax, value-added tax, and other direct taxes. Procure tax registration as follows: (1) complete online tax registration and supplementary information (corporate income tax, VAT, payroll, accounting/fiscal calendar) in e-Tax within 30 calendar days of business license issuance; (2) apply for VAT taxpayer qualification (Small-Scale or General Taxpayer), tax-control U-Key, invoice quota, and digital electronic invoice service activation at the competent tax service bureau; (3) file the bank deposit account and financial accounting system with the STA; (4) for a foreign non-resident enterprise without a Chinese establishment, the Chinese domestic payer performs withholding registration and obtains a non-resident enterprise TIN (or references the foreign-country TIN and applicable tax treaty) for Chinese-source payments; for the WFOE itself, being a resident Chinese entity, no separate non-resident TIN procedure applies.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None in the U.S. check-the-box or hybrid mismatch sense. For VAT, an enterprise with annual taxable sales below RMB 5,000,000 may elect Small-Scale Taxpayer status or voluntarily register as a VAT General Taxpayer; a taxpayer whose annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000 is required to register as a VAT General Taxpayer. For corporate income tax, the WFOE is mandatorily taxed as a resident enterprise at the entity level; no pass-through election is available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 in annual taxable sales (rolling 12-month period) is the mandatory threshold for VAT General Taxpayer registration. Any newly established enterprise must complete tax registration within 30 calendar days after business license issuance. Below RMB 5,000,000, an enterprise may remain a Small-Scale Taxpayer or voluntarily register as a VAT General Taxpayer if accounting standards are met.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exported tangible goods are zero-rated for VAT when the goods are actually declared for export and physically leave China; the exporter is a registered VAT General Taxpayer or otherwise eligible; the sale is made to a non-resident customer and title and risk pass abroad; a valid electronic Customs Export Declaration has been released; the sale price and terms are at arm's length; export proceeds are collected through lawful banking channels and repatriated to China within the 180-day statutory deadline (or an approved non-receipt exception applies); and the export VAT refund/rebate declaration is filed within the statutory filing window (for goods exported in a calendar year, ordinarily no later than 30 April of the following year). As a manufacturing exporter, the WFOE claims zero-rate VAT and input VAT rebate on self-produced smartwatches and fitness wearables under the VAT exempt-credit-rebate method. Qualifying offshore digital services zero-rating rules do not apply to physical goods.

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronic release record) from the China International Trade Single Window, with export declaration number, HS classification, quantity, FOB value, destination country, and customs release date.
  • Original Bill of Lading (sea transport) or Air Waybill (air transport) issued to the order of or consigned to the foreign buyer or consignee, evidencing physical movement of the smartwatches out of China.
  • Export commercial invoice and packing list issued by the WFOE, showing the foreign-currency amount, Incoterms, shipment marks, and complete description of the wearables.
  • Export sales invoice (foreign-currency export invoice) issued by the WFOE through the STA e-invoice platform, linked to the underlying Customs Export Declaration.
  • Foreign exchange collection voucher from a licensed bank, evidencing inward remittance and settlement of the export proceeds (bank inward remittance certificate or FIRC-equivalent), supported by the SAFE goods-trade foreign-exchange monitoring record.
  • Sales contract or order and, where inputs are purchased from domestic suppliers, the related VAT Special Invoices for those inputs used in the exported goods.

China operates the Golden Tax-IV invoice control environment. VAT invoices must be issued through the STA Digital Electronic Invoice service platform or lawful tax-control equipment; Special VAT Invoices must carry the required invoice classification code, amounts, and tax data, and are uploaded to the STA in real time or near-real time. Buyers must verify and confirm Special VAT Invoices on the STA VAT Invoice Comprehensive Service Platform before claiming input VAT credits. Each Digital Electronic Invoice carries an STA-assigned unique invoice number and anti-forgery electronic seal; there is no separate physical fiscal stamping step, but high-risk transactions are subject to automatic tax-control risk screening and audit flags.

STA cross-matches the export declaration, e-invoice, transport document, foreign-exchange collection data, and input VAT invoices. If export proceeds are not collected and repatriated within 180 days from export and no statutory exception applies, the export sale may be recharacterized as a domestic taxable sale: the previously refunded input VAT is clawed back, 13% output VAT is assessed, and late-payment interest and penalty fees may be imposed. A false export claim (falsified customs or transport documents, over-invoicing, circular fund movements, or fabricated foreign-exchange receipts) exposes the WFOE to recovery of the entire refunded VAT, administrative fines of up to five times the amount fraudulently obtained, suspension or downgrade of export tax-rebate status, and criminal prosecution under Article 204 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China for fraudulent export tax rebates.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a managed floating RMB exchange rate regime under which the currency is fully convertible on the current account in line with IMF Article VIII obligations, but capital account activity remains subject to administrative registration, monitoring, and documentary verification. For this WFOE manufacturing health-monitoring smartwatches and smart rings, trade-adjacent flows (component import payments, OEM export receipts, service fees) settle freely on the current account through licensed domestic banks once the underlying contracts, customs declarations, invoices, and transport documents are presented and verified in the SAFE/FAST system. The capital account is the operative constraint: the offshore investor's paid-in capital injection, conversion of the registered capital into CNY, intercompany loan recycling, and onward dividend repatriation all require a valid SAFE FDI registration, a certified CPA capital verification report, and strict adherence to the approved business scope and use-of-funds negative list. No mandatory surrender of export proceeds has applied since the 2012 reform; enterprises retain their FX receipts in domestic current-account settlement accounts within PBOC-approved ceilings. Profit remittance to the overseas parent is permissible on the current account after the annual audited financial statements are filed, corporate income taxes are settled, and a bona fide dividend resolution is presented, but the settling bank will require documentary alignment with the FDI registration and prior tax clearance evidence. The practical enforcement challenge for a medium-technology manufacturer is the per-transaction genuineness review performed on every large capital or current-account flow, the 5-business-day reporting window after settlement or payment, and prohibitions on speculative cross-border arbitrage; accordingly, the treasury should treat the RMB as effectively convertible for operational trade flows but administratively gated for structural capital flows.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no mandatory surrender ratio or compulsory conversion timeline for export foreign currency receipts under the post-2012 SAFE trade reform; a Chinese exporter is entitled to retain 100% of its proceeds in a domestic foreign-currency current-account settlement account. The statutory repatriation obligation requires the export proceeds to be collected and credited onshore by the bank within 90 calendar days of the export customs declaration date, with a possible one-time 90-day extension for approved open-account terms, and the collection must be reported to SAFE within 5 business days of crediting. Advance export receipts for goods not yet shipped must additionally be registered in the FAST system within 15 days of receipt and confirmed with a matching export contract when the goods are subsequently declared.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) and the delegated State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low viability as the primary treasury rail. Offshore fintech EMIs are not licensed by the PBOC or SAFE to open domestic FDI capital accounts, receive foreign direct investment injections, perform onshore RMB conversion, or provide CNAPS-based CNY clearing for a mainland WFOE; consequently they are legally barred from serving as the entity's principal transactional bank. Their scope is limited to ancillary offshore wallet functionality, vendor disbursements outside China, and visibility dashboards, and any attempt to route statutory capital injections, tax payments, or export collections through a foreign EMI would founder on the settlement bank's genuine-entity checks, the capital verification process, and the SAFE FAST audit trail.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The foreign investor must first complete SAFE FDI registration (directly or through the settling bank's agency channel) and obtain the unique FDI code registered in the FAST system. The capital injection is then remitted from the investor's offshore account via SWIFT to the WFOE's dedicated FDI capital account held at the tier-1 onshore bank, with the remittance advice quoting the BOP purpose code for foreign direct investment paid-in capital and a narrative line such as paid-in capital contribution for smart-watch and fitness-wearable manufacturing. Upon receipt, the bank reports the inflow to SAFE within 5 business days, converts the foreign currency to CNY at the prevailing interbank spot rate upon the WFOE's instruction, and releases money only after the WFOE commissions a licensed CPA to issue the capital verification certificate and uploads it to the FAST portal. Converted CNY can then be deployed exclusively toward approved opex, capex, payroll, and procurement, with each subsequent drawdown supported by underlying invoices, contracts, payroll rosters, or tax vouchers held by the bank for audit inspection.Component and raw-material procurement from overseas OEM suppliers is cleared primarily through SWIFT MT103 telegraphic transfers bearing the applicable BOP current-account goods trade purpose code (e.g., 121010 for general trade) matched against import customs declarations, commercial invoices, packing lists, and bills of lading; where suppliers require payment assurance, the WFOE may open an onshore sight or usance letter of credit through the settling bank. Export collections from downstream distributors of the smart-watch products are received via MT103 against export customs declarations and full shipping document sets, or negotiated under export letters of credit when the buyer requires a documentary mechanism. Services procured from group-related parties (marketing, software platforms, engineering design) are remitted as cross-border services payments under the applicable 226-series BOP code with signed service agreements and transaction-level reporting no later than 5 business days after payment. Mandatory Audit Threshold Small Company Audit Exemption Regime

Turnover: The statutory audit obligation is categorical for every WFOE incorporated as a limited liability company under PRC Company Law; there is no balance-sheet, turnover, or headcount threshold below which a WFOE is exempt. The audit is independently triggered by each of the following conditions: the entity is a PRC limited liability company wholly owned by foreign investors; the entity prepares annual financial reports for shareholder approval; the entity files the annual enterprise information publicity report with SAMR; the entity files the annual enterprise income tax final settlement; and the entity is a customs-registered manufacturer-exporter of smart watches and smart rings. For accounting-standard selection only, small enterprises meeting the Ministry of Finance quantitative size criteria such as annual operating revenue not exceeding RMB 30 million, total assets not exceeding RMB 50 million, and headcount not exceeding 300 may apply the Simplified Accounting Standards for Small Enterprises; enterprises above those indicators must apply full PRC Accounting Standards for Business Enterprises. In all cases the independent certified CPA audit remains statutorily required regardless of whether revenue, assets, or headcount are zero or below any classification threshold.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Transactions with related parties exceeding RMB 100 million annually for a single related transaction category (or RMB 40 million for related-party loans, guarantees, or intangible property transactions) trigger mandatory Local File preparation; Master File is required when controlled transactions exceed RMB 1 billion annually, with Country-by-Country reporting thresholds at RMB 5.5 billion consolidated group revenue.

Economic substance scope: The PRC does not apply Euro-style ESR, but the General Anti-Avoidance Rule (GAAR) and the substance-over-form doctrine require the WFOE to demonstrate real business activity, operating premises, manufacturing personnel, and actual risk-bearing; passive or empty structures attracting Chinese-source income may be recharacterized and assessed with 25% corporate income tax and 10% WHT under Circular 82-related rules where a place of effective management or permanent establishment is found.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False VAT Export Refund

Statutory sanction: PRC Criminal Law Article 204: criminal penalty of 5x the fraudulently refunded amount as a fine plus imprisonment from 5 years to life imprisonment for extremely large amounts; unpaid VAT, surcharges, and administrative penalties recoverable by the State Taxation Administration

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly orchestrate the false export scheme face criminal prosecution, personal fines equal to 1-5 times the defrauded refund, and imprisonment; civil statutes allow piercing of the corporate veil under PRC Company Law Article 20, making directors personally liable for corporate tax debts if abuse of corporate form causes creditor harm.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: PRC Enterprise Income Tax Law Article 41 and Special Tax Adjustments under Articles 44-46: upward TP adjustment, interest charge of 5% plus benchmark lending rate on the reassessed tax, and potential 10% WHT on constructive dividends; under Article 47 GAAR, the structure may be recharacterized and the tax assessed from the effective place of management

Director personal liability: Directors who approve the sham structure may be jointly and severally liable for unpaid corporate tax under Company Law Article 20 (piercing); PRC Cross-Border Tax Enforcement may issue travel restrictions (blacklisting) against legal representatives when tax arrears exceed RMB 100,000 and exit orders are used to enforce collection.
AML / Cross-Border Trade-Based Money Laundering

Statutory sanction: PRC Anti-Money Laundering Law Article 47 and Criminal Law Article 191: money laundering offence punishable by imprisonment up to 10 years and fines of up to RMB 5 million; bank account freezing, customs penalties, and SAFE foreign exchange fines up to 30% of the illegal amount

Director personal liability: Directors and statutory representatives who sign or authorize the transactions are personally exposed to freezing of personal assets, cancellation of residence permits, and criminal conviction; under the corporate veil piercing doctrine, the corporate entity's limited liability is disregarded and directors must restitute the full illicit proceeds, subject to blacklisting and exit bans.
