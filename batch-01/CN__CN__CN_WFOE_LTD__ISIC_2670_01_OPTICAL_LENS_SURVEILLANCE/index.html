Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A virtual office or commercial mailbox is not permissible as the sole registered address for a manufacturing and export WFOE. SAMR requires a fixed physical address within the registered administrative division and a valid lease or ownership document. The tax authority and customs authority require the same physical premise for tax registration, VAT general taxpayer status, export-foreign exchange reconciliation, and customs clearance records. Banks will physically inspect the premises before opening the capital account and basic deposit account; a virtual office triggers enhanced due diligence and, in practice, refusal. Only a genuinely leased serviced office or industrial incubator address in the same city, with a registered lease agreement and confirmation from the municipal market regulator, may be considered, but it will not satisfy manufacturing substance requirements.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-up capital and no blocked escrow account requirement are imposed for a general manufacturing WFOE under the PRC Company Law. The registered capital is determined in the Articles of Association and may be subscribed and paid in accordance with the contribution schedule adopted by the shareholders. The capital contribution schedule must be realistic and enforceable; the foreign investor must remit contributions through licensed banking channels and report the capital receipt to the State Administration of Foreign Exchange. Special minimum capital or escrow rules may apply if the enterprise enters a regulated financial, security, or negative-list restricted business.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA), operating the National Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax) online portal

E-government filing portal

1) The WFOE is incorporated as a limited liability company and obtains a business license from the Administration for Market Regulation; 2) Within 30 days of registration, the WFOE must complete tax registration through the National Electronic Tax Bureau using the unified social credit code, which becomes its Tax Identification Number (TIN) for all tax purposes; 3) The WFOE applies for VAT general taxpayer status as a manufacturing exporter by filing the required electronic application and providing production and sales evidence; 4) The e-Tax portal assigns a taxpayer classification and enables VAT invoice issuance only after the electronic registration and verification process is completed; 5) Foreign non-resident entities with a withholding or VAT collection obligation may register separately through the same e-Tax portal to obtain a non-resident taxpayer ID.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — 1) The WFOE is incorporated as a limited liability company and obtains a business license from the Administration for Market Regulation; 2) Within 30 days of registration, the WFOE must complete tax registration through the National Electronic Tax Bureau using the unified social credit code, which becomes its Tax Identification Number (TIN) for all tax purposes; 3) The WFOE applies for VAT general taxpayer status as a manufacturing exporter by filing the required electronic application and providing production and sales evidence; 4) The e-Tax portal assigns a taxpayer classification and enables VAT invoice issuance only after the electronic registration and verification process is completed; 5) Foreign non-resident entities with a withholding or VAT collection obligation may register separately through the same e-Tax portal to obtain a non-resident taxpayer ID.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None. Chinese tax law does not offer check-the-box or hybrid mismatch elections; a WFOE is a Chinese tax resident company taxed at entity level on its worldwide income, and indirect export VAT zero-rating is claimed by refund rather than by taxpayer election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory VAT registration for all taxpayers; general VAT taxpayer registration is mandatory once annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000; WFOE exporters normally register as general VAT taxpayers to access input VAT credit and zero-rated export refunds.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods by a registered VAT general taxpayer are eligible for zero-rated VAT and input VAT refund when the goods are actually shipped out of China, the customs export declaration has been filed and released, the sale is made to a foreign buyer, and the foreign currency proceeds are collected and repatriated to China within the statutory period. Qualifying offshore services under the cross-border service zero-rating list may also qualify if the services are consumed entirely outside China and all required service transaction documentation is retained.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bill of Entry)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill
  • Foreign Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or bank-issued Export Foreign Exchange Receipt Voucher
  • Commercial invoice issued by the WFOE
  • Packing list
  • Export sales contract
  • VAT invoice and export sales settlement records

All VAT invoices must be issued through the State Taxation Administration's national unified electronic invoice system and transmitted in real time to the electronic tax bureau. Fully digitalised e-invoices carry the tax authority's digital signature and an anti-fraud QR code; issuance requires tax-approved digital credentials and API-based clearance. The Golden Tax Phase IV platform performs automatic cross-checking of invoices, customs data, and VAT returns to prevent fabrication and false export declarations.

The State Taxation Administration, in coordination with customs and the foreign exchange administration, audits export refund files and cross-checks customs export data, foreign exchange receipts, and VAT returns. Any unverifiable or fraudulent zero-rated claim is subject to retroactive clawback of refunded VAT, interest, and monetary penalties under the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Tax Collection; serious export-tax-refund fraud is punishable under criminal law and customs anti-smuggling provisions, with additional restrictions on future refund eligibility.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The RMB is convertible for current-account items under IMF Article VIII, while capital-account items remain subject to SAFE registration and post-investment monitoring. A WFOE engaged in precision optics manufacturing may freely convert genuine trade receipts from optical-lens exports, provided the originating bank can validate the export declaration, commercial invoice, and customs records. Capital inflows also require a bank-managed FDI pre-registration and a foreign exchange capital account. Once registered, the foreign investor can inject equity; after receipt, the funds are held in a Foreign Exchange Capital Account, can be settled to RMB in a Settlement Pending Payment Account, and must be spent in accordance with the PBoC/SAFE permitted-use catalogue. Profit remittances and dividends are treated as current-account payments and can be converted and distributed after the annual audit is completed, corporate income tax paid, and the bank receives a shareholders' resolution and tax-payment proof. In practice, no blanket conversion ratio is applied; instead, the bank's compliance committee performs an authenticity and anti-inversion review for each transaction. B/C-classified enterprises face heightened documentary, purpose and transactional controls. No parallel-market conversion is lawful.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory surrender ratio is imposed. Under SAFE's trade-in-goods administration, export receipts must be repatriated into a domestic foreign-currency current account or RMB trade account within the trade-receipt cycle, generally within 90 days from the export customs declaration unless a longer deferred receipt period has been registered in the Trade Monitoring System. The WFOE may retain the full foreign-currency amount or voluntarily settle it to RMB. For advance receipts or deferred receipts, the WFOE must report through the bank in the monitoring system to avoid overdue reconciliation flags and downgrading of its trade-irregularity classification.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low, and functionally non-viable as a primary clearing rail. Offshore digital EMI providers are not licensed by PBOC/SAFE to open settlement or current accounts for a mainland-China WFOE, to execute RMB-FCY conversion, or to clear inbound and outbound trade payments. They cannot satisfy the local SAFE trade-authenticity filing requirements. An overseas EMI may be used by the offshore shareholder or for global treasury dashboard reporting, but the WFOE's legal clearing rail must reside in a PRC-licensed bank. Some non-bank payment institutions hold local cross-border payment licenses, but an EMI acting as a principal clearing institution for this entity is not permissible.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Prior to receipt, the WFOE must complete the SAFE FDI foreign-exchange registration through its bank and obtain the Foreign Investment Registration Code or filing number. The shareholder then remits equity via SWIFT MT103 or MT202 to the WFOE's Foreign Exchange Capital Account, with the beneficiary reference field containing the WFOE name, shareholder name, remittance purpose FDI equity injection, and the SAFE registration code. On arrival, the bank matches the funds to the registration, records the BOP/SAFE transaction code for FDI paid-in capital, performs source-of-funds screening, credits the capital account, and reports into the SAFE monitoring system. If capital is to be settled into RMB, the bank converts it into the Settlement Pending Payment Account and tracks utilization against the permitted-use catalogue, requiring capital verification by a certified public accountant where the bank deems it necessary.For receivables from optical-lens exports, the foreign buyer pays via SWIFT MT103 against the export invoice, and the bank credits the trade collection account after reconciling the receipt with the corresponding customs declaration. For outbound disbursements to suppliers, service providers, or license holders, the WFOE instructs SWIFT MT103 or MT102 remittances from its settlement account under the appropriate SAFE purpose code, attaching the underlying commercial contract and tax withholding or payment vouchers. Where a commercial letter of credit is used, the bank issues the L/C under MT700 and arranges reimbursement under MT740 or MT750. Documentary collections are executed as D/P or D/A through the presenting bank with compliant shipping documents. 25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: A Chinese Local File must be prepared if the entity's related-party transactions exceed any of these thresholds: RMB 200 million in tangible asset transfers; RMB 100 million in intangible asset transfers; RMB 100 million in financial asset transfers; RMB 50 million in provision of services; or RMB 400 million in aggregate related-party transactions. A Master File is required when the multinational group's consolidated revenue exceeds RMB 5.5 billion, and Country-by-Country reporting applies at the same turnover threshold.

Economic substance scope: China does not apply an offshore Economic Substance Regulations regime in the Cayman-UAE sense, but the substance-over-form principle is embedded in EIT Law Article 47 (General Anti-Avoidance Rule) and in treaty abuse standards. A manufacturing WFOE must maintain genuine business premises, full-time employees, operational control, decision-making functions, and risk ownership aligned with the precision optical lens manufacturing activity. The Core Income-Generating Activities (CIGA) of production, quality assurance, procurement, R&D, and logistical management must be performed and managed in China. If a WFOE is reduced to a contract manufacturer or a shell while related parties elsewhere capture profits, the tax authority may re-characterize the arrangement, reallocate income under the arm's length standard, disregard artificial entities, and deny treaty benefits under the Principal Purpose Test.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Cross-Border Fraudulent Underreporting

Statutory sanction: Violates Articles 63 and 64 of the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection and Levy and Article 201 of the Criminal Law. The tax authority will recover the underpaid tax, impose daily 0.05% interest from the due date, and levy a fine of 50% to five times the evaded tax. When the evaded amount is large, criminal sanctions include an additional fine of one to five times the evaded amount and criminal detention or imprisonment for up to seven years for the directly responsible personnel.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced if the legal representative or financial director directed, approved, or actively participated in the false reporting. Directors can be held personally and jointly liable for the unpaid tax and penalties if the company assets are insufficient and they caused or benefited from the evasion. Personal bank accounts and real estate may be frozen or seized; the director will be placed on the tax exit-ban blacklist and prohibited from leaving mainland China until the tax debt is settled. Criminal conviction can result in imprisonment of up to seven years.
Shell Company / Treaty Abuse and Absence of Beneficial Ownership

Statutory sanction: The EIT Law Article 47 and Article 120 of its Implementation Rules empower the tax authority to apply GAAR and disregard the intermediary. SAT Announcement 2019 No. 35 denies treaty benefits if the beneficial ownership test fails. The tax authority will reassess the arrangement, require the statutory 10% withholding tax plus late-payment interest, and may impose a penalty of 50% to 100% of the underpaid tax for intentional treaty abuse. Criminal penalties under Article 201 also apply if the structure results in a false tax return.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false beneficial ownership declarations or who establish the sham offshore entity can be held personally liable for the shortfall in withheld tax. Their passports may be revoked or travel restricted under the serious tax violation exit-ban rules; they may face civil restitution of assets moved offshore. If the scheme goes beyond civil tax avoidance into fraudulent tax evasion, criminal liability exposes them to imprisonment for up to three to seven years and confiscation of personal assets that were derived from the arrangement.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation and Thin Capitalization

Statutory sanction: Violates EIT Law Articles 41 (arm's length principle) and 46 (thin capitalization). The Chinese tax authority will make a primary transfer pricing adjustment, impose an additional contemporaneous documentation penalty of up to RMB 10,000 for non-compliance with documentation requests, and assess interest equal to the benchmark loan rate plus 5% per annum on the adjusted tax. If intent to evade is proven, the underpaid tax penalty under Article 63 imposes a fine of 50% to five times the tax evaded, and criminal sanctions apply for false or fraudulent filings.

Director personal liability: Directors and the chief financial officer who co-signed the false pricing policy or instructed the creation of the offshore debt can be found jointly liable for the adjusted tax and interest. Personal assets can be used to satisfy the corporate tax debt if the court confirms that the directors ignored arm's length standards and abused limited liability. They face travel bans, credit blacklisting, and potential criminal liability as accomplices to tax evasion where the profit-shifting plan deliberately concealed income from the tax authority.
