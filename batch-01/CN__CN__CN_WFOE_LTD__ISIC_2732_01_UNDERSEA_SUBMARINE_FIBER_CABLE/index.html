Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

Under the PRC Company Law, a company must have a registered domicile that is actually capable of receiving legal documents and serving as its principal business location. For an ISIC 2732 manufacturing WFOE producing armored subsea fiber optic cables, a virtual office, commercial mailbox, or shared-registration agent address is not permissible and will be rejected by the local SAMR, tax authorities, and banks. The tax authority requires a physical place of production and management; banks require physical inspection before opening the RMB basic deposit account; MIIT and environmental authorities require actual plant premises, equipment, employment records, safety permits, and utility bills. Therefore, a physical registered office and meaningful commercial substance must be established before incorporation.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

There is no statutory minimum paid-up capital for an ordinary manufacturing WFOE and no blocked escrow account is required under PRC Company Law. The registered capital shall be a fixed amount stated in the Articles of Association and may be contributed in installments over the period specified therein, ordinarily not exceeding five years for limited liability companies. For the armored subsea cable manufacturing scope, the commercial counterparties and the industrial production license authority may require the paid-up capital to be at least 30 percent of the subscribed capital before public procurement or export contract execution, and the paid-in capital will be verified by a registered PRC CPA before issuance of the industrial production license.

Funding & unlock path: Fire acceptance certificate issued by the Fire and Rescue Section after inspection of automatic fire alarms, smoke exhaust, escape route widths, hydrant flow and fire extinguisher placement. A structural load clearance certificate is required verifying factory floor loading capacity for cable drum rewinds, armoring machinery and storage racking, with required seismic load approvals where applicable. Occupational health clearance is required under the Work Safety Law, including chemical exposure monitoring, ventilation controls, personal protective equipment for workers handling cable fillers, and an emergency response plan for fire and chemical spill incidents.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a managed, IT-enabled foreign exchange control regime under which current account transactions are generally convertible for legitimate trade and service activities, while capital account transactions remain subject to strict registration, approval, and quota controls. For a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE) in the subsea submarine fiber cable manufacturing sector, payments for imported raw materials, components, royalties, consulting services, and routine operating expenses are classified as current account items and may be freely settled through domestic-tier banks, provided the underlying transaction is truthful and supported by authentic documents such as contracts, invoices, customs declarations, and tax payment vouchers. Capital account items, including inbound paid-in capital contributions, foreign-currency loans, dividend remittances, and changes in the equity structure, must first be recorded with SAFE through the local branch or the settlement bank. The WFOE must open a dedicated foreign exchange capital account, obtain a SAFE registration certificate for foreign direct investment, and submit a capital verification report from a Chinese certified public accountant before converting inbound contributions into RMB for operational purposes. Dividend distributions to the offshore shareholder require audited financial statements, corporate income tax settlement certificates, and proof that the profits were generated from actual operations; the withholding tax on dividends must be paid before profit remittance is allowed. Full CNY convertibility is not attained for capital account purposes, and the WFOE must use designated banking channels for all cross-border flows. The foreign exchange system remains administratively rigorous, and non-bank fintech payment entities are not authorized to provide capital account clearing services.

Export proceeds surrender rules

China has abolished the mandatory surrender of export proceeds to the central bank; there is no mandatory conversion ratio for foreign currency earned by exporters. However, export proceeds from goods declared under the customs export declaration must be repatriated into a domestic foreign exchange settlement account within 180 days from the date of export declaration, unless the enterprise obtains a SAFE-approved overseas retention arrangement. Once received, the WFOE may elect to retain the foreign currency or convert it into RMB based on operational cash flow needs, with all conversion undertaken through a licensed domestic bank under genuine transaction verification.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China, operating under the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Fintech EMIs and offshore digital banking platforms are not viable as direct corporate account providers in mainland China. PBOC and SAFE licensing frameworks reserve the ability to open CNY settlement accounts, foreign exchange capital accounts, and capital account clearing capabilities exclusively to licensed Chinese commercial banks. Offshore fintech EMIs such as Mercury, Revolut, or Airwallex can only offer ancillary workflow tools, payment reconciliation technology, or cross-border fee optimization through a licensed bank partner; they cannot legally service a Chinesedomiciled WFOE with a domestic account number or provide the required SAFE reporting for inbound foreign direct investment, export proceeds repatriation, or dividend remittance. Consequently, the viability rating is Low-Nonviable as a direct banking relationship, and the WFOE must maintain its primary treasury relationship with a domestic tier-1 commercial bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital injections must be routed exclusively through the WFOE's designated SAFE-registered capital account at a licensed Chinese bank. The remittance must be made via SWIFT MT103 with transaction purpose code for foreign direct investment contribution, referencing the SAFE foreign direct investment registration code and the unique investment contract number. The receiving bank verifies the remittance against the SAFE registration, performs a real-name KYC check on the remitter, and credits the capital account. Conversion of the foreign currency into RMB is permitted only after the capital verification report has been issued and the bank confirms the intended use matches the scope of operations. Larger capital injections may require a prior capital account opening notice and the submission of a foreign investment working capital payment request.For trade settlements with overseas subsea cable component suppliers and subcontractors, the WFOE may use SWIFT MT103 wire transfers for straightforward raw material and service payments, commercial letters of credit confirmed by first-class international banks for large capital equipment purchases, and documentary collections for lower-risk repetitive trade flows. Each outbound remittance must be accompanied by the relevant current account payment document and passed through the bank's trade authenticity review; customs declarations, invoices, contracts, bills of lading, and tax vouchers are required for cross-border disbursements. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Under SAT Announcement 2016 No. 42, a Local File is mandatory when annual related-party tangible asset transfers exceed RMB 200 million, intangible property transfers exceed RMB 100 million, financial asset transfers exceed RMB 100 million, or related-party service payments exceed RMB 40 million; a Master File is required when total annual related-party transactions exceed RMB 1 billion; the contemporaneous documentation must be prepared before the annual CIT return deadline and submitted to the tax authority upon request

Economic substance scope: For treaty and anti-avoidance purposes, the WFOE must maintain real substance in China, including factory premises, operational employees, manufacturing control, procurement functions, risk management and actual decision-making over submarine cable production and export contracts; although China does not impose a separate Economic Substance Regulations filing, the General Anti-Abuse Rule in Article 47 of the CIT Law and the beneficial ownership tests in SAT Announcement 2015 No. 60 operate as an economic substance doctrine, requiring that any non-resident recipient of dividends, royalties or interest performs real business activities and does not exist solely to obtain treaty rates

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Invoice Fraud

Statutory sanction: Tax Collection Administration Law Article 63 imposes confiscation of unpaid tax and a fine of 50 percent to 500 percent of the underpaid tax plus daily late-payment surcharge of 0.05 percent; Criminal Law Article 201 makes tax evasion a crime when the unpaid tax exceeds RMB 100,000 and is more than 10 percent of the tax due, carrying imprisonment of up to 3 years for ordinary amounts and 3 to 7 years plus fines for huge amounts

Director personal liability: Chinese courts may pierce the corporate veil under Company Law Article 23 when abuse of the limited liability structure causes serious prejudice to tax creditors; directors who order or approve false invoicing become personally and jointly liable for the tax shortfall, penalties and interest; the tax authority may issue an exit ban and place the director on the National Dishonest Judgment Blacklist under Tax Collection Administration Law Article 44; criminal conviction exposes the director to personal fines, asset confiscation and imprisonment
Transfer Pricing Manipulation and Thin Capitalization

Statutory sanction: Article 41 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law and Article 36 of the Tax Collection Administration Law authorize the tax authority to make a transfer pricing adjustment; thin-capitalization interest exceeding the statutory 2:1 debt-to-equity ratio is non-deductible under Article 46 and implementation regulations; the adjusted taxable income attracts the 0.05 percent daily late-payment surcharge, and if the understatement is fraudulent, Article 63 of the Tax Collection Administration Law adds a 50 percent to 500 percent fine

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly execute non-arm's-length agreements and artificial financing structures can be held personally liable for the company's unpaid tax under Company Law Article 23; personal bank accounts and assets may be frozen by the tax authority to satisfy the corporate liability; exit restrictions and blacklisting apply under Tax Collection Administration Law Article 44; if the scheme is prosecuted as tax evasion under Criminal Law Article 201, the responsible director faces imprisonment of up to 3 years or 3 to 7 years for extremely large amounts, together with personal fines
Shell Company, Economic Substance Failure and Treaty Abuse

Statutory sanction: Article 47 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, together with SAT Announcement 2015 No. 7 and SAT Announcement 2015 No. 60, allow the tax authority to deny treaty benefits, recharacterize the conduit structure and impose the full 10 percent statutory withholding rate on dividends, interest and royalties; false treaty benefit declarations attract a fine of 50 percent to 300 percent of the tax shortfall under Tax Collection Administration Law Article 64, and if documents are forged with intent to evade tax, Article 63 and Criminal Law Article 201 apply

Director personal liability: Directors and controlling shareholders who deliberately use a shell structure for treaty shopping cannot rely on limited liability; courts may pierce the veil under Company Law Article 23 and impose joint and several personal liability for unpaid tax, withholding tax, penalties and interest; the tax authority may impose travel bans, asset preservation and cross-border exit restrictions under Tax Collection Administration Law Article 44; criminal conviction under Article 201 for fraudulent treaty claims exposes directors to personal fines, confiscation of assets and imprisonment of up to 7 years
