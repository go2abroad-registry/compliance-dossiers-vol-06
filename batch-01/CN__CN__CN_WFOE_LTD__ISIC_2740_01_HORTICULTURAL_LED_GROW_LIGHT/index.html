Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None by statute; no minimum paid-up capital is prescribed for a manufacturing WFOE under ISIC 2740, but the registered capital must be fully subscribed and paid within five years from incorporation under Article 47 of the PRC Company Law (2024). No escrow or blocked capital account is required by SAMR or SAFE; capital contributions must be remitted to the company's domestic RMB capital account as foreign direct investment, recorded through the SAFE Direct Investment registration at a licensed bank, and used only for lawful operating purposes stated in the Articles of Association.

Funding & unlock path: Before production, obtain a Fire Safety Facility Design Review and Acceptance Filing from the Fire and Rescue Section of the Emergency Management Bureau; fire separation distances, means of escape, fire alarm systems, automatic sprinklers and emergency lighting must comply with GB 50016. For new industrial buildings, satisfy applicable civil air defense requirements through construction of underground shelter space or payment of the civil air defense fee as determined by the local civil defense office. Complete the Work Safety Facility Three Simultaneities design review and the occupational disease hazard project filing with the local Health Commission. Register air compressors, pressure vessels and lifting equipment with the Special Equipment Safety Supervision Bureau and obtain operator certificates for all personnel operating such equipment. Industrial hygiene monitoring must cover soldering fume exposure, noise and lighting levels, and records must be retained for at least three years.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA); electronic filing via the STA Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax) online portal; export and customs declarations filed via the China International Trade Single Window.

E-government filing portal

For a foreign non-resident investor forming a WFOE in China: (1) incorporate the WFOE with the Administration for Market Regulation; the business license carries the 18-digit Uniform Social Credit Code (USCC), which is the statutory taxpayer identification number for enterprise income tax and VAT. (2) Within 30 days after the business license is issued, complete tax registration in the STA Electronic Tax Bureau under the PRC Tax Collection Administration Law; no separate tax registration certificate is issued because the USCC is the unified tax ID. (3) Register VAT taxpayer status, electing Small-Scale VAT taxpayer or General VAT taxpayer, and file the legal representative, bank account, registered address and accounting system information. (4) Activate e-invoicing capability in the STA e-invoice platform. (5) If exporting goods, complete the export tax refund exemption filing with the competent tax authority before claiming zero-rated export treatment.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

China does not provide check-the-box, pass-through, or hybrid-mismatch entity elections. A WFOE is mandatorily taxed at the entity level under the Enterprise Income Tax Law and is a VAT taxpayer. The only available status election is the choice between Small-Scale VAT taxpayer and General VAT taxpayer when annual taxable turnover is at or below RMB 5 million; General VAT taxpayer registration is compulsory above that threshold.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Tax registration is mandatory for all WFOEs within 30 days of business license issuance, regardless of turnover. The VAT turnover classification threshold is RMB 5 million of annual taxable sales (excluding VAT): sales above RMB 5 million require compulsory registration as a General VAT taxpayer; sales at or below RMB 5 million permit Small-Scale VAT taxpayer status and allow voluntary election of General VAT taxpayer status.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating for exported goods applies to a VAT General Taxpayer that has completed export tax refund filing and has actually exported the goods from China customs territory. The horticultural LED grow lights must physically leave China under customs supervision, be declared at zero VAT on the export sales invoice, and have the corresponding input VAT refunded. The exporter is expected to receive the foreign exchange proceeds from the overseas buyer through a designated foreign exchange bank within the statutory period and must retain documents proving the sale, shipment and payment. Goods that are prohibited for export, subject to export duties without exemption, or not actually exported are not eligible for zero-rating.

  • Customs Export Goods Declaration (electronic declaration with customs release date from the China International Trade Single Window)
  • Commercial invoice and detailed packing list
  • Bill of Lading (marine) or Air Waybill (air) evidencing that the goods left China customs territory
  • Export sales contract or purchase order
  • Bank foreign exchange receipt voucher, being the Inward Remittance Certificate equivalent issued by a designated bank, evidencing collection and repatriation of the export proceeds
  • Electronic export VAT invoice (ordinary VAT invoice for export) matching the customs declaration data

China operates a national tax-control e-invoicing system. Fully digitalized electronic invoices, known as Digital E-Invoices, are issued through the STA electronic invoice platform and invoice data is transmitted to the STA in near-real time at issuance. Each invoice carries a unique invoice number, and no private fiscal stamping or third-party clearance intermediary is required. VAT returns must reconcile issued invoice data. For exports, the export invoice and the customs export declaration are data-matched through the China International Trade Single Window; discrepancies automatically flag the claim for audit.

Export zero-rating claims are electronically cross-checked against customs export declarations, logistics records, e-invoice data and foreign exchange collection data. If a claim is unsubstantiated or fraudulent, the STA will claw back the refunded VAT, impose a late payment surcharge of 0.05% per day, and may impose a fine of 50% to 5 times the amount of tax evaded under the PRC Tax Collection Administration Law. In serious cases, criminal liability for tax evasion applies, and there is no statute of limitations on recovery for fraudulent export refund claims. Taxpayers must retain complete export documentation for the statutory record-keeping period.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates under a managed floating exchange rate regime with full current account convertibility for genuine trade and service transactions, but capital account items remain subject to administrative control and SAFE verification. The WFOE is permitted to receive foreign currency share capital from its overseas parent, but the funds must flow into a dedicated FDI foreign exchange capital account at a SAFE-licensed bank and cannot be deployed until the receiving bank verifies the injection against the MOFCOM/SAFE FDI filing and the registered business scope. Trade receipts are current account items and may be retained in foreign currency or voluntarily settled into RMB; there is no mandatory surrender requirement, but all export proceeds must be repatriated onshore and reconciled with customs declarations and the SAFE trade monitoring system. Profit remittance by a WFOE is a capital account outflow that requires an audited annual statutory financial report, board resolution declaring the dividend, tax clearance certificate, and bank-level documentary review before the outward remittance can be executed. In practice, banks impose strict actual-use-principle checks on capital injections, and any use of FDI capital for securities, entrusted loans, or real estate outside the approved business scope will be rejected. The convertibility environment is therefore workable for a compliant manufacturing WFOE, but every cross-border fund movement demands documentary substantiation and advance registration under the PBOC/SAFE framework.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE's Goods Trade Foreign Exchange Administration reform, there is no mandatory surrender ratio for export proceeds; the mandatory conversion ratio is 0% and the WFOE may retain 100% of eligible foreign currency export receipts in its domestic foreign exchange settlement account or convert them into RMB at the onshore spot rate through its settlement bank. The statutory monitoring timeline requires export proceeds to be received onshore within 90 days from the date of customs export declaration; receipts collected after 90 days must be reported through the SAFE Trade Foreign Exchange Monitoring System, and the settlement bank must retain underlying trade documents such as the export contract, commercial invoice, customs declaration, and bill of lading.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE), the foreign exchange administration authority mandated by the People's Bank of China (PBOC) as the central bank of the People's Republic of China

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Offshore fintech EMIs, including Airwallex, have Low feasibility as the primary onshore treasury provider for a PRC WFOE. These entities are not licensed by PBOC or SAFE to open onshore RMB settlement accounts, receive FDI capital injections, perform SAFE trade verification filings, or support statutory profit remittance under Chinese foreign exchange law. Airwallex and similar platforms may be used as an ancillary offshore collection or disbursement layer for non-mainland receivables and payables, but they cannot replace the WFOE's licensed domestic tier-1 bank for regulatory capital account compliance, export proceeds reconciliation, cross-border RMB clearing, or FX hedging. Any use of an offshore EMI must be documented separately so that the onshore settlement bank can reconcile the underlying trade flows and avoid duplicate or unreported cross-border transfers.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Offshore fintech EMIs, including Airwallex, have Low feasibility as the primary onshore treasury provider for a PRC WFOE. These entities are not licensed by PBOC or SAFE to open onshore RMB settlement accounts, receive FDI capital injections, perform SAFE trade verification filings, or support statutory profit remittance under Chinese foreign exchange law. Airwallex and similar platforms may be used as an ancillary offshore collection or disbursement layer for non-mainland receivables and payables, but they cannot replace the WFOE's licensed domestic tier-1 bank for regulatory capital account compliance, export proceeds reconciliation, cross-border RMB clearing, or FX hedging. Any use of an offshore EMI must be documented separately so that the onshore settlement bank can reconcile the underlying trade flows and avoid duplicate or unreported cross-border transfers.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Under SAT Public Notice 42 of 2016, the Local File is mandatory when annual related-party tangible-property transactions exceed CNY 200 million, or when annual intangible-property, financial-asset, or service-related transactions exceed CNY 100 million; the Master File is mandatory when total related-party transactions exceed CNY 1 billion; a Country-by-Country Report is required where the multinational group's consolidated revenue exceeds CNY 5.5 billion.

Economic substance scope: Mainland China does not apply Caribbean-style Economic Substance Regulations, but EIT Law Articles 45, 46, and 47 impose anti-controlled-foreign-company rules, thin-capitalization limits, and a general anti-avoidance rule; tax treaties and the beneficial ownership doctrine require real economic substance. For a WFOE manufacturing horticultural LED grow lights, the Core Income-Generating Activities are the selection and procurement of LED chips and drivers, product design and engineering, substrate and heat-sink assembly, quality control, inventory risk, warranty risk, and sales strategy; these activities must be performed and documented in China to justify the WFOE's profit level, and offshore entities must show personnel, premises, decision-making, and risk-bearing capacity before receiving royalties, management fees, interest, or dividends.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Invoicing

Statutory sanction: Tax Collection Administration Law Article 63 imposes a penalty of 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax plus 0.05% daily late-payment interest; Criminal Law Article 201 imposes fines and imprisonment up to seven years for tax evasion; Criminal Law Article 205 imposes severe penalties, including life imprisonment for huge false special VAT-invoice amounts; the tax authority may order restitution, confiscate illegal proceeds, and refer the case for criminal prosecution.

Director personal liability: A court may pierce the corporate veil under Company Law Article 20 where abuses of limited liability harm creditors and tax claims; directors are personally jointly liable for restitution of diverted assets and unpaid tax to the extent of their wrongful benefit; they face tax blacklisting, exit-control travel bans, asset freezes, and imprisonment of up to seven years for tax evasion convictions.
DTA Treaty Abuse and Shell-Company Beneficial Ownership

Statutory sanction: EIT Law Article 47 and the State Taxation Administration's general anti-abuse measures allow the tax authority to recharacterize the entire arrangement, deny treaty benefits, impose the statutory 10% withholding tax, levy 0.05% daily late-payment interest, and impose penalties from 50% to 500% of the under-withheld tax under Tax Collection Administration Law Articles 63 and 68; the WFOE as withholding agent remains liable for the amount it should have withheld.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly approve a sham SPV structure may be held civilly liable under Company Law Article 20 for the tax loss, ordered to return assets distributed from the shell, blacklisted by the tax authority, barred from leaving China through exit-control measures, and prosecuted for criminal tax evasion under Criminal Law Article 201; if false foreign tax documents are used, imprisonment terms can reach seven years.
Transfer Pricing Noncompliance and Profit Stripping

Statutory sanction: EIT Law Article 41 authorizes the tax authority to adjust the related-party transaction to the arm's length result and impose compensatory interest; Tax Collection Administration Law Article 62 penalizes failure to file the related-party disclosure schedules with fines from CNY 2,000 to CNY 50,000; Article 63 applies 50% to 500% penalties for intentional misrepresentation; failure to furnish contemporaneous documentation can shift the burden of proof, deny the arm's length position, and trigger referral for criminal prosecution if the underpayment is material.

Director personal liability: Directors who direct or participate in false transfer pricing records may be personally liable for civil restitution of shifted profits under Company Law Article 20, jointly and severally with the company for the adjusted tax where funds were distributed to shareholders, subject to travel bans and tax blacklisting under administrative tax enforcement, and exposed to criminal conviction under Criminal Law Article 201 with imprisonment up to seven years plus fines for willful evasion through manipulated intercompany pricing.
