A virtual office, commercial mailbox, or letterhead-only address is not acceptable as the sole registered address for a manufacturing WFOE. Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

There is no statutory minimum paid-up capital for a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise Limited Liability Company under PRC law. The sole shareholder must subscribe the registered capital stated in the Articles of Association and must actually pay in full within five years from establishment, unless a shorter contribution period is required by a specific PRC law or administrative regulation. No blocked escrow account is mandated. The contribution must be remitted from lawful overseas funds into the WFOE's designated foreign settlement or RMB capital account at a PRC-licensed bank after completing the foreign exchange information registration with the State Administration of Foreign Exchange through the designated bank.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA), acting through provincial, municipal and county tax service bureaus; online filing is through the national Unified Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax) portal at https://etax.chinatax.gov.cn, with export transaction data interfaced through the General Administration of Customs (GACC) and the China International Trade Single Window.

E-government filing portal

For a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE) whose parent is a foreign non-resident entity, the PRC statutory taxpayer identity is obtained as follows. The WFOE must first register with the local State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) and obtain a Business License bearing the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which is the WFOE's Tax Identification Number for income tax, VAT, and customs/export tax refund purposes. Within 30 days after the Business License is issued, the WFOE must complete online tax registration in the Unified Electronic Tax Bureau, submitting the USCC, registered address, legal representative and financial officer identity details, paid-in or subscribed capital, bank account, business scope, customs registration, and VAT taxpayer classification. The foreign non-resident shareholder does not need a separate PRC TIN solely for holding equity; however, the WFOE must register the non-resident shareholder for withholding tax reporting in the Electronic Tax Bureau before remitting outbound dividends, royalties, interest, or service fees. A foreign non-resident enterprise with no Chinese establishment that earns China-source income is assigned a non-resident tax registration code by the local STA office upon contract filing. For export operations, the WFOE must additionally complete Foreign Trade Operator registration and Customs Declaring Entity registration with the GACC through the China International Trade Single Window.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None - the PRC has no check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election regime. The only relevant statutory election is the VAT taxpayer status election: when annual taxable sales are below the CNY 5,000,000 threshold, a small-scale taxpayer may voluntarily register as a General VAT Taxpayer; once elected, the entity can only revert after twelve months. There is no entity classification pass-through election for a WFOE.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

CNY 5,000,000 of taxable turnover (excluding VAT) within any consecutive 12-month period; exceeding this amount makes registration as a General VAT Taxpayer mandatory. Below this threshold the taxpayer is normally classified as a small-scale taxpayer.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export sales of robotic vacuum appliances by the WFOE qualify for 0% VAT if the goods are actually exported from mainland China through a formal customs export declaration made in the WFOE's own name and customs registration; the WFOE is a General VAT Taxpayer and Foreign Trade Operator; the export sale is supported by a genuine cross-border contract, invoice, packing list, and transport document evidencing physical export; the export proceeds are collected from the foreign buyer in foreign currency and repatriated through the Chinese banking/SAFE system within the prescribed deadline; and the export declaration value, HS classification, and commodity description are accurate. The 0% rate applies to the export sale itself, with input VAT attributable to the exported goods refundable or retained as export tax rebate. Qualifying services such as offshore digital services are not part of this goods-based export dossier.

  • Customs Export Goods Declaration (export clearance form filed via the China International Trade Single Window)
  • Full set of ocean Bills of Lading or Air Waybills with the WFOE shown as exporter/shipper
  • Export sales contract or purchase order with the foreign buyer and Incoterms
  • Commercial invoice and packing list matching the customs declaration
  • Export VAT invoice (ordinary VAT invoice issued for export sales through the VAT Invoice Management System)
  • Foreign exchange collection and settlement confirmation reflected in SAFE's Trade in Goods Foreign Exchange Monitoring System (bank remittance advice or export proceeds collection certificate)
  • Any applicable export licence/certificate for restricted goods, if the specific robotic vacuum model requires it

The PRC VAT system operates through the STA's VAT Invoice Management System and the Fully Digitalized Electronic Invoice platform. For all B2B and export transactions, invoice issuances are transmitted electronically to the tax authority in near-real time through the Unified Electronic Tax Bureau; each issued invoice receives a unique invoice code, QR code and tax-control digital signature. API-based integration is mandatory for high-volume issuers; paper invoices, where still permitted, must be generated through a tax-control device (Tax U-Key) with encrypted fiscal stamping. The export VAT invoice is cross-matched with the GACC export declaration and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) export collection data. The system also includes real-time red-letter invoice reporting, invoice authenticity verification, and automated risk warnings; any batch invoice that fails validation is blocked from issuance.

The STA, GACC and SAFE conduct automated cross-checking of export declarations, invoice data, logistics records, and foreign exchange receipts before and after export tax refund approval. If zero-rating is not substantiated, the export is recharacterised as a domestic taxable sale and VAT is recaptured at 13% with daily late-payment interest at 0.05% per day. False refund claims trigger clawback of refunded VAT plus administrative fines ranging from 50% to 5 times the evaded tax under the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Tax Collection; fraudulent export tax refund is a criminal offence under the PRC Criminal Law, punishable by imprisonment and fines. In fraud cases, tax assessment and clawback are not time-limited, and the taxpayer may be placed on the serious dishonesty list, lose export tax refund qualification, and have customs credit rating downgraded.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Under China's current-account convertibility regime, RMB is freely convertible for trade and service payments once the bank completes genuine transaction verification; capital-account transactions remain controlled and are permissible only on listed exogenous grounds after SAFE registration or approval. A Mainland WFOE may receive foreign equity funding into a SAFE-registered capital account, convert it in the bank's FX settlement pool, and retain RMB liquidity domestically, but it cannot freely move RMB offshore. Profit remittance to the overseas shareholder is an income-account payment requiring the WFOE's audited financial statements, board/shareholder distribution resolution, tax settlement certificate and a bank real-trade/income verification, with no separate annual reinvestment quota applicable after 2010 but still subject to tax clearance. Dividends must derive from after-tax earnings; the WFOE must comply with withholding tax and provide proof of payment. In practice, the convertibility hurdle arises not from absence of currency but from documentary exactness: wrong purpose code, mismatched customs data, invoicing discrepancies, or unregistered SAFE records cause bank rejection. Treasury teams should pre-map all payments to national-purpose codes and maintain consistent cross-border documentation.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE Goods Trade Administration, export foreign currency proceeds must be repatriated to and declared in China through a designated domestic bank within 90 days after the date of customs export declaration, or within the extended approved credit term where the sales contract supports a longer collection period and SAFE has accepted the trade monitoring file. There is no mandatory conversion ratio: the WFOE may retain 100 percent of export proceeds in a domestic foreign-currency current account or voluntarily convert them into RMB at market rates. Each receipt is screened by the receiving bank against customs and SAFE electronic trade data, with matching export declaration numbers, invoice amounts and payment parties; overdue or unmatched receipts are flagged as trade FX monitoring exceptions and can trigger SAFE inquiry before the next settlement is cleared.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) as the central bank, with the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) as the delegated national foreign exchange control and cross-border payment regulatory authority

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Offshore digital EMIs and fintech banking platforms are not licensed deposit-taking institutions under PBOC or NFRA and are legally barred from opening or maintaining RMB demand deposit accounts for Mainland-domiciled entities. Such EMIs cannot complete SAFE FDI registration, issue a legal capital account, match Chinese tax identity, or satisfy cross-border current-account repatriation and payment-filing obligations for a WFOE. They may be used only as ancillary offshore receivables aggregators for sales booked by an overseas affiliate, but all conversion and repatriation into the WFOE must route through a licensed domestic commercial bank. Viability Rating: Low as a primary treasury banking rail; Medium only as a supplementary collection module for offshore e-commerce revenue that is subsequently swept to China via a compliant trade payment file.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity capital from the overseas shareholder must be wired from the parent bank to the WFOE's SAFE-registered capital account at a licensed domestic bank using SWIFT MT103. The remitter name in the wire must exactly match the shareholder recorded in the WFOE's FDI foreign-exchange registration, and the payment purpose in the remittance instruction must be mapped by the receiving bank to the SAFE International Income and Payments declaration category for foreign direct investment capital contribution. The bank will credit the capital account, verify the beneficiary account number sealed in SAFE's FDI system, confirm that no prepayment or borrowing balance creates mismatch, and then allow FX settlement into RMB only after the WFOE submits its unified social credit code, business scope certificate, and the capital account payment-use negative-list confirmation. A certified public accountant must later issue a capital verification report confirming the registered capital has been fully paid in using lawful FX receipt and settlement records. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: For each fiscal year, a Local File is mandatory if any of the following related-party transaction thresholds are met in RMB: RMB 200 million or more for tangible property transactions (including IoT components, motors, robotic vacuum units); RMB 100 million or more for intangible property transactions (including patents and software); RMB 100 million or more for financial related-party transactions; or RMB 40 million or more for other related-party transactions. A Master File is required if the aggregate related-party transactions of the WFOE in a fiscal year reach RMB 1 billion or more.

Economic substance scope: China does not enact a separate 'Economic Substance Regulation' statute like some offshore hubs, but CIT Law Articles 41, 45 and 47, together with the Special Tax Adjustments Measures, require economic substance through the Core Income-Generating Activities (CIGA) doctrine. The WFOE must demonstrate real manufacturing facilities, employees, procurement decisions, inventory control, quality assurance, logistics coordination, and entrepreneurial risk-taking in China. A mere invoice-pipeline or shell WFOE used to channel profits to a treaty-shopping parent will have its treaty benefits denied and its transactions recharacterised under GAAR.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Transfer Pricing Manipulation / False Related-Party Invoicing / Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: CIT Law Article 41 permits the tax authority to make TP adjustments; Article 44 allows default estimation of taxable income when documentation is absent. Tax Collection Law Article 63 imposes a fine of 50%-500% of the underpaid tax plus 0.05% daily overdue interest. Criminal Law Article 201 imposes criminal liability where evaded tax exceeds RMB 50,000 and exceeds 10% of the total payable tax, with imprisonment up to 3 years for relatively large amounts and 3-7 years for huge amounts, together with fines.

Director personal liability: Directors who consciously designed or approved the sham pricing are directly responsible persons. Under Company Law Article 20 (and the 2023 revision Article 23) they may be forced to make civil restitution for losses caused to the company and creditors. The tax authority blacklists the legal representative on the Serious Tax Dishonesty List and notifies immigration to restrict exit; criminal conviction can result in personal asset forfeiture and imprisonment.
Non-Resident Withholding Tax Evasion / Mischaracterization of Distributions

Statutory sanction: CIT Law Articles 37 and 38 impose a 10% statutory WHT and make the WFOE the withholding agent. Tax Collection Law Article 69 imposes a fine of 50%-300% of the unwithheld tax, and the tax authority may recover the full tax from the WFOE plus daily overdue interest. If the mischaracterization is deliberately fraudulent, Criminal Law Article 201 applies with fines and imprisonment up to 7 years.

Director personal liability: The legal representative and financial controller who ordered the wire transfer can be held personally liable for the unpaid withholding tax where fraud or abuse of the corporate form is established. The tax authority may blacklist them and enforce travel bans under Tax Collection Law Article 44. Criminal conviction for tax evasion may lead to personal asset seizure and imprisonment.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Abuse / Treaty Shopping

Statutory sanction: SAT Announcement 2015 No. 60 denies treaty benefits to non-beneficial owners. CIT Law Article 47 permits the competent tax authority to recharacterise the scheme under GAAR with a retroactive adjustment up to the statutory period for anti-avoidance. Tax Collection Law Article 63 imposes a fine of 50%-500% of the underpaid tax, plus interest. The tax authority may also disregard the offshore entity for tax purposes and assess the WFOE as the true profitable enterprise.

Director personal liability: Chinese courts may pierce the corporate veil under Company Law and hold shareholders and directors personally liable for the unpaid taxes and civil debts of the shell WFOE. Directors' personal assets can be attached, and the legal representative is subject to travel bans and blacklisting. Where they have signed false financial statements or tax returns, they risk criminal prosecution for tax evasion with imprisonment up to 7 years and personal financial restitution.
