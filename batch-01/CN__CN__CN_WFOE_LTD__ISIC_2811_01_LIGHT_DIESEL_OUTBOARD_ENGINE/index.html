For a WFOE whose approved scope is the manufacturing of light diesel outboard marine engines under ISIC 2811, a pure virtual office or commercial mailbox is not permissible as the sole registered address. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China; online electronic portal is the unified Electronic Taxpayer Portal accessed from the official STA website at www.chinatax.gov.cn and through the provincial State Taxation Administration e-tax channels.

E-government filing portal

For the CN_WFOE_LTD, the statutory tax identification number is the Unified Social Credit Code issued by the State Administration for Market Regulation. Step 1: incorporate the wholly foreign-owned enterprise as a limited liability company and obtain the business license containing the Unified Social Credit Code. Step 2: complete tax registration with the competent State Taxation Administration office within 30 days after business license issuance, using the Unified Social Credit Code as the TIN/VAT ID, either in the tax service hall or through the Electronic Taxpayer Portal. Step 3: register for VAT taxpayer status, enterprise income tax registration, payroll withholding registration, and export tax rebate/export VAT status for the marine outboard engine export activity, and connect the entity to the State Taxation Administration electronic invoice service platform. Step 4: open a Chinese bank account, complete trade-in-goods foreign exchange registration with the State Administration of Foreign Exchange, and complete customs exporter registration. Step 5: use the Unified Social Credit Code consistently on all VAT invoices, customs declarations, contracts, banking records, and tax filings. A foreign non-resident shareholder without a Chinese establishment does not obtain a separate Chinese TIN/VAT ID; it uses its home-country tax identification number in withholding filings unless the State Taxation Administration assigns a non-resident taxpayer code for a specific withholding registration event.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None. China does not provide check-the-box entity classification elections or elective hybrid mismatch treatment. A WFOE is classified by statute as a resident enterprise taxpayer under the PRC Enterprise Income Tax Law and is taxed at entity level on worldwide income; the General Anti-Avoidance Rule and controlled foreign corporation rules apply but are not elective.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No monetary threshold for registration of a WFOE as a VAT taxpayer: VAT registration is compulsory upon tax registration. For general VAT taxpayer status, registration is mandatory when annual taxable turnover exceeds RMB 5,000,000; below this amount the entity qualifies as a small-scale VAT taxpayer subject to statutory simplified collection rules and exemptions.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to physical goods, including light diesel outboard engines, that are exported from mainland China under a customs export declaration and for which the seller receives and repatriates export proceeds in foreign currency. A VAT general taxpayer production enterprise may claim the export tax rebate through the exemption, credit and refund method, which zero-rates the export sale and allows input VAT on the exported goods to be offset or refunded. Small-scale exporters may claim exemption without credit or refund. Eligibility additionally requires that the goods are not on the prohibited or restricted export-tax-rebate list, the export transaction is genuine, the customs declaration data is electronically transmitted to the tax authority, and all supporting invoices, cargo movement documents, and bank receipts are consistent and available for audit. Qualifying cross-border offshore services may also be zero-rated only if performed from China, consumed entirely outside China, and listed in the State Taxation Administration cross-border taxable services zero-rating documentation.

  • Export Customs Declaration (export bill of entry/exit customs declaration) with customs release or declaration number
  • Commercial invoice / export sales invoice issued by the WFOE
  • Packing list
  • Bill of Lading (sea), Air Waybill (air) or rail/road consignment note evidencing physical export
  • Export contract or sales order
  • Bank receipt / foreign exchange repatriation record, including Inward Remittance Certificate where required, or the bank's export collection/settlement certificate evidencing receipt of export proceeds within the statutory deadline
  • Electronic export tax rebate declaration submitted through the Electronic Taxpayer Portal, linking the customs declaration number, VAT invoice data, and bank receipt data

All VAT invoices must be issued through the State Taxation Administration national unified electronic invoice system using fully digitalized electronic invoices. Invoice data is transmitted to the tax authority in near real-time at issuance; the system performs automatic anti-fraud validation, assigns a unique invoice code and number, and applies the national anti-counterfeit digital stamp/tax-control verification. High-volume taxpayers must use API-based direct connection to the electronic invoice service platform, while smaller taxpayers use the free web-based platform. Physical/paper VAT invoices are being replaced by this mandatory digital clearance channel, and VAT input credit or export refund claims require matching invoice data in the State Taxation Administration invoice database.

Tax authorities cross-match customs declaration, export VAT invoice, cargo movement, and State Administration of Foreign Exchange receipt data before approving any zero-rated refund. If zero-rating is unsubstantiated, the refund is disallowed or clawed back with late-payment surcharge, and the exporter must pay output VAT on the export sales. Where the false or inflated refund claim is classified as tax evasion or fraudulent export tax refund, the official penalty is 50% to 500% of the evaded or refunded tax amount under the PRC Law on the Administration of Tax Collection, personal and corporate credit rating downgrades apply, and the matter may be referred for criminal prosecution under PRC Criminal Law for fraudulent export refund fraud. In fraudulent cases the tax authorities may recover the tax and penalties on a retrospective basis without limitation period.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates an administered floating exchange rate regime. Current account transactions, including export proceeds for light diesel outboard engines, are generally convertible after genuineness verification by designated banks. A WFOE may retain export foreign exchange or settle it into RMB at its own commercial discretion; there is no mandatory surrender ratio for current account trade receipts. Capital account transactions remain regulated: foreign direct investment equity contributions require SAFE foreign exchange registration and must enter through a foreign exchange capital fund account at a licensed Chinese bank. The use of those funds is limited to bona fide business purposes within the approved business scope and cannot be diverted to prohibited areas such as secondary-market securities investment, entrusted loans, or non-operating real estate investment. Profit remittance to the foreign shareholder is permitted only after the Chinese audited financial statements have confirmed distributable profits, corporate income tax has been fully settled, an annual audit has been completed, and the FDI foreign exchange registration has been updated at the bank. The principal hurdles are the rigorous documentation burden for every current account and capital account payment, the 180-day repatriation rule for export proceeds, the need for bank-level authenticity verification of each trade transaction, and the practical requirement to obtain prior bank approval for capital account outbound payments and hedging instruments.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export foreign currency proceeds must be repatriated to mainland China within 180 days of the export customs declaration date unless SAFE has approved a longer window. There is no mandatory conversion ratio: the enterprise may retain 100% of export proceeds in a foreign currency settlement account or settle them into RMB at the applicable onshore rate. Each export collection must be electronically matched to the corresponding customs declaration through the SAFE goods-trade monitoring system. Late collections, mismatched proceeds, or unreported foreign receipts can result in downgraded trade classification and heighten future compliance scrutiny.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the People's Bank of China (PBoC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low - legally non-viable as an onshore treasury rail. Offshore digital EMIs do not hold a People's Bank of China payment institution licence and cannot open or maintain a domestic settlement account for a Chinese WFOE. SAFE and the PBoC require all domestic FX settlement, export collection, capital account entry, and RMB clearing to be executed through licensed banks in China. An offshore EMI may serve only as an ancillary payment conduit for overseas supplier payments or third-country invoicing, never as the primary account relationship for capital account compliance, tax reporting, or statutory treasury operations.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

The statutory PRC withholding tax on dividends paid by a WFOE to a non-resident enterprise shareholder is 10% on the gross dividend amount. Where the non-resident shareholder is resident in a country with a Comprehensive Double Tax Agreement with China, the treaty rate may be reduced, commonly to 5% where the beneficial owner directly holds at least 25% of the capital of the Chinese payer; otherwise the DTA rate is often 10% but may be lower under certain treaties.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: China does not use a single flat monetary gate. The Local File is required in any tax year where statutory related-party transaction thresholds are exceeded: RMB 200 million for transfers of tangible property; RMB 100 million for transfers of intangible property; RMB 100 million for transfers of financial assets; and RMB 40 million for related-party services. The Master File is generally expected when the multinational group reaches the PRC country-by-country reporting threshold or when the SAT calls for group-level documentation; contemporaneous documentation should be prepared before filing and produced within the regulatory time limit upon audit request.

Economic substance scope: While China does not apply a Caribbean-style Economic Substance Regulations code, it applies a substantive economic-substance doctrine through the General Anti-Avoidance Rule in the CIT Law and through transfer-pricing law. The tax authority evaluates a WFOE's functions performed, assets used and risks assumed, and will require Core Income-Generating Activities (CIGA) such as procurement, manufacturing, quality control, R&D decision-making, warranty reserve management and global sales support to be performed in China with real personnel and assets. A WFOE that operates as a pure invoice issuer or pass-through with no commercial reality while profits are attributed to related foreign entities is exposed to re-characterisation, profit adjustment, denial of treaty benefits and anti-abuse penalties.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion Through Omission of Export Revenue and False Cost Booking

Statutory sanction: Article 63 of the Tax Administration Law requires repayment of the underpaid CIT, a late surcharge of 0.05% per day, and an administrative penalty of 50% to five times the amount of tax evaded; where the evaded amount is substantial, the Criminal Law Article 201 offence of tax evasion may result in fines and imprisonment of up to seven years.

Director personal liability: The courts may pierce the corporate veil under Article 23 of the Company Law where the director or controlling shareholder abuses the limited liability structure to evade tax debts and seriously harms creditors; the director can be made personally and jointly liable for the company's CIT and penalties. The legal representative and responsible directors may also be placed on the tax authority's serious tax violation blacklist, subjected to court-imposed travel restrictions and a ban on high consumption, and, upon criminal conviction, sentenced to imprisonment for tax evasion.
False Transfer Pricing Documentation and Artificial Profit Shifting to Tax Haven Intermediaries

Statutory sanction: China applies the transfer pricing adjustment rules and can re-determine the WFOE's taxable income on arm's length terms, make secondary adjustments to the non-resident related party, charge late interest at special rates, and impose the general tax evasion penalty where the documentation is false or misleading. Failure to provide contemporaneous documentation or to cooperate with an audit is penalised under the Tax Administration Law, and the total exposure is the re-allocated profit, interest, late collection fees and a fine up to five times the underpaid tax.

Director personal liability: A director who knowingly signs false transfer pricing policies, authorises backdated service agreements, or instructs the finance team to conceal material related-party transactions may be found jointly liable with the company under the tort and veil-piercing rules, be blacklisted from leaving China through the tax enforcement cases, suffer personal asset freezing for unpaid tax debts, and face criminal liability for fraud or tax evasion if the scheme is proven beyond reasonable doubt.
Treaty Abuse and Dividend Stripping Through Conduit Non-Resident Shareholders

Statutory sanction: The tax authority may deny the DTA relief under domestic anti-treaty-abuse rules and re-impose the statutory 10% withholding tax plus the 0.05% daily late surcharge; Article 63 of the Tax Administration Law may apply where the prima facie withholding was deliberately understated, giving a fine of 50% to five times the shortfall. The General Anti-Avoidance Rule under Article 47 of the CIT Law permits re-characterisation of the entire arrangement, and Article 120 of the Tax Administration Law allows the tax authority to impose an arrangement-specific penalty where no tax law specific anti-avoidance rule covers the scheme.

Director personal liability: The signatory director of the WFOE can be named as an agent of the withholding obligation and held personally liable for the unpaid withholding tax and penalties if the company is insolvent. Piercing the corporate veil under the Company Law can impose civil liability on directors who caused the company to enter the conduit structure; travel bans and asset attachment may be ordered by the tax or debt-enforcement court, and serious cases involving forged tax residency certificates or fraud against the State may lead to criminal imprisonment for fraud or tax evasion.
