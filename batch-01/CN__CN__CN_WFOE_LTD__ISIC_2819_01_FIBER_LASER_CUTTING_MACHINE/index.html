Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None. The 2023 PRC Company Law has abolished the minimum registered capital requirement for ordinary limited liability companies and no blocked escrow or special paid-up capital account is required for a wholly foreign-owned limited liability company engaged in the manufacture of high-power fiber laser cutting machines. The sole shareholder must subscribe the entire registered capital in the currency and within the time schedule stated in the Articles of Association, with full contribution generally required within five years of incorporation unless another law or administrative license imposes a shorter period. Banks and export control authorities may independently expect a higher demonstrated capitalization for letter of credit and wire transfer transactions, but this is a commercial underwriting requirement and not a statutory capital escrow condition.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety Acceptance Filing and Fire Safety Acceptance Certificate for the factory under the Regulations on Fire Protection in Construction Projects of the Ministry of Emergency Management; structural load inspection and building civil defense clearance from the local civil defense administration if the building is subject to civil defense obligations; occupational hazard project registration and occupational hazard pre-assessment approval under the Law on Prevention and Control of Occupational Diseases; and workplace safety clearance from the local Emergency Management Bureau before production commencement.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA) and its electronic tax filing portal known as the Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax Bureau or 电子税务局 in local reference, though the official English interface is accessible via the STA website)

E-government filing portal

The foreign non-resident entity must first complete the establishment registration with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) to obtain a business license and a Unified Social Credit Code (USCC). That USCC serves as the statutory Tax Identification Number (TIN) under the STA's tax registration system. For the Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE), after the SAMR issuance of the business license, the STA automatically or upon application completes the tax registration and issues a taxpayer identification certificate; the WFOE must then complete the information collection of its legal representative, financial officer, tax accountant, and bank account details, activate the e-Tax Bureau account, and apply for the electronic tax registration certificate. If the non-resident entity has no permanent establishment but derives Chinese-source income, it applies for a tax registration for non-resident enterprises via the e-Tax Bureau to obtain a non-resident TIN (the same USCC-based identifier where applicable or a separate non-resident taxpayer number issued by the local tax authority) and must file withholding tax returns through the appointed withholding agent.

That USCC serves as the statutory Tax Identification Number (TIN) under the STA's tax registration system. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None for general corporate income tax classification. A WFOE limited liability company is by default a Resident Enterprise subject to Entity-Level Taxation on its worldwide income. No check-the-box election exists under Chinese law. For thin capitalization and hybrid mismatch rules, China has statutory documentation requirements for related-party transactions and potential interest deduction limitations; there is no elective hybrid mismatch regime, though treaty entitlement requires the submission of a Certificate of Tax Residence to claim benefits under applicable Double Tax Agreements.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

CNY 5,000,000 annual taxable sales for general VAT taxpayer registration (mandatory once exceeding, although small-scale taxpayers may voluntarily register as general taxpayers; all WFOEs engaged in manufacturing are required to register as general VAT taxpayers upon formation if their projected annual sales exceed this threshold or if they need to issue special VAT invoices and claim input VAT credits).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The export of high-power fiber laser metal sheet cutting machines qualifies for the zero-rated export VAT treatment (VAT rate 0%) under the export tax rebate policy, provided the goods are physically exported out of China and the sales proceeds are repatriated into China through official foreign exchange channels. The WFOE must produce genuine export sales contracts, customs export declarations, and foreign exchange receipts. Services directly related to exported goods and certain offshore digital services may also qualify if they satisfy the cross-border service zero-rating criteria, but for the manufacturing entity the primary zero-rating is for physical goods exports under the export tax rebate (VAT refund) regime.

  • Customs Export Declaration (出口报关单) with approved customs release mark
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing physical export movement
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or bank foreign exchange receipt/collection record evidencing repatriation of export proceeds
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List matching the customs declaration
  • Export sales contract or purchase order
  • Proof of payment of export freight and insurance where applicable

China mandates the use of the new-generation fully digital electronic invoice system, known as the Fully Digitalized Electronic Invoice (数电票) under the Golden Tax Project Phase IV. VAT invoices must be issued through the e-Tax Bureau or approved tax-control equipment and transmitted in real time to the STA's clearing platform. The system applies an anti-fraud digital identification mechanism with a unique invoice code and digital stamping; all special VAT invoices for input VAT credit must pass automatic real-time reconciliation in the national VAT invoice verification system before the buyer may claim credit. Electronic invoices are not manually stamped but carry mandatory digital signatures and QR codes issued by the tax authority clearing system.

Where an exporter falsely declares exports, obtains VAT refunds without actual physical movement of goods, or fails to repatriate export proceeds within the statutory foreign exchange settlement period without lawful justification, the tax authority will claw back the refunded VAT and impose penalties ranging from a fine of 50% to 500% of the falsely obtained refund, plus late payment surcharge at 0.05% per day. Serious cases involving fraudulent export VAT refund invoices are treated as criminal fraud under the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, with potential liability for the responsible legal representative and tax accountants.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

RMB is convertible for current account transactions under the PRC Foreign Exchange Administration Regulations and China's commitments under Article VIII of the IMF Articles of Agreement. Export and import payments, service fees, interest, royalties, and usual business-related current account settlements may be processed through licensed banks on the basis of genuine underlying trade documentation, without case-by-case SAFE pre-approval. Capital account transactions, however, remain controlled. Foreign direct investment into a WFOE requires an FDI foreign exchange registration with the receiving bank, and the capital account remains subject to the national negative list and macroprudential supervision. Profit remittance is permitted after tax, but in practice the WFOE must present audited Chinese financial statements, a board resolution declaring the dividend, tax clearance certificates, and evidence of the completed FDI registration; the bank will scrutinize the genuineness of the underlying earnings and the conversion into foreign currency is executed as a spot transaction. The principal convertibility hurdles are not in the current account but in the capital account, where equity injections, cross-border loans, and foreign debt require registration, use restrictions, and ongoing reporting to SAFE. Adequate corporate treasury planning must therefore distinguish freely convertible operational flows from controlled capital and financing flows.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export proceeds in foreign currency must be repatriated to China within 180 days of the export customs declaration date. There is no fixed mandatory conversion ratio; a WFOE may retain 100% of qualifying export proceeds in a foreign-currency current account with a designated bank, or voluntarily settle 100% into RMB through a licensed bank. However, the foreign-currency balance is subject to bank macroprudential limits, and every conversion must be supported by genuine trade documents filed in the SAFE trade verification system. Proceeds held offshore beyond the statutory 180-day window without a SAFE-approved extension constitute a compliance event and can trigger fines, loss of verification convenience, and suspension of foreign exchange payment privileges.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) and State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low viability. Offshore fintech EMIs are not licensed by the PBOC or SAFE to open domestic RMB settlement accounts for mainland WFOEs, and they cannot access the CNAPS or CIPS clearing systems directly without a local banking partner. Such EMIs cannot replace a Tier-1 Chinese commercial bank for statutory corporate account opening, export proceeds repatriation, capital injection verification, or tax-related payment channels. Their use is limited to ancillary offshore foreign-currency accounts outside China, and any attempt to route operational China-based liquidity through an unlicensed EMI would be considered a serious KYC and regulatory breach.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The WFOE must complete SAFE FDI registration through a qualified Tier-1 bank by submitting the business license, the foreign investor's certificate, the incorporation agreement, and the registered capital schedule. The bank then opens a dedicated FDI capital account in the name of the WFOE. The offshore parent wires the equity contribution using SAFE transaction purpose code 622011 for FDI capital injection. The receiving bank verifies the remitter against the registered foreign shareholder, the amount against the approved registered capital, and the stated purpose against the business scope before converting the foreign currency into RMB. Funds flow from the capital account to the basic operation account only for permitted operating purposes, such as purchase of machinery, raw materials, salaries, rent, and other current expenditures directly related to the manufacturing business. A certified public accountant in China subsequently issues a capital verification report, which is filed with the market regulator and serves as evidence of paid-in capital.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% statutory withholding tax on dividends distributed by a Chinese resident enterprise (WFOE) to its non-resident corporate shareholder where the shareholder has no establishment/PE in China. Treaty relief may reduce this to 5% under agreements such as Mainland-Hong Kong or Mainland-Singapore where the shareholder holds at least 25% of the WFOE's capital and is the beneficial owner; US-China treaty generally preserves a 10% rate for dividends.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The annual Form A return and final CIT payment must be filed within five months after the tax year end, i.e., on or before 31 May of the following calendar year. Quarterly provisional returns are due within 15 days after each quarter-end.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File thresholds: RMB 200 million for annual related-party tangible asset transfer (purchases/sales); RMB 100 million for related-party intangible asset transfer; RMB 100 million for financial asset transfer; RMB 40 million for related-party services. Master File threshold: total annual related-party transactions exceeding RMB 1 billion. Country-by-Country Reporting threshold: consolidated group revenue equal to or greater than RMB 5.5 billion in the preceding fiscal year.

Economic substance scope: China does not apply UAE-style Economic Substance Regulations, but the PRC CIT Law Article 47 (General Anti-Avoidance Rule) and SAT Announcement (2015) No. 60 require arrangements to have reasonable commercial purpose and economic substance. The 'beneficial owner' analysis for treaty relief effectively applies a Core Income-Generating Activities test: the foreign shareholder must demonstrate substantive activity, staff, premises, decision-making and risk management. A WFOE manufacturing fiber laser cutting machines must itself maintain real R&D, procurement, inventory, production, quality control and management in China; an offshore distributor or holding company must perform real CIGA functions before receiving intercompany dividends, royalties, or service fees.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Transfer Pricing Fraud

Statutory sanction: PRC CIT Law Article 41 authorizes the tax authority to recharacterize non-arm's-length intercompany prices; the Special Tax Adjustment Measures impose adjustment interest at the benchmark RMB lending rate plus 5 percentage points. Under Tax Administration Law Article 63, the underpaid tax is pursued and a fine of 50%-500% of the underpaid amount is imposed. Criminal liability arises under Criminal Law Article 201 for tax evasion: imprisonment up to 3 years for substantial evasion, and 3-7 years for especially large amounts exceeding 30% of taxable tax.

Director personal liability: Directors and senior officers who knowingly sign false filings may be considered 'directly responsible persons' under Criminal Law Article 201 and face criminal detention or imprisonment and fines. Under PRC Company Law Article 20, courts may pierce the corporate veil if shareholders or directors abuse limited liability and corporate independence, causing creditors or the State to suffer serious losses; personal assets may be seized for restitution. The legal representative may also be placed on the tax 'blacklisting' serious dishonesty list, subject to exit bans, travel restrictions, and denial of visas.
Treaty Abuse / Shell-Company Dividend Routing

Statutory sanction: Under SAT Announcement (2015) No. 60 and the Beneficial Ownership regulations, the tax authority will deny treaty benefits, re-characterize the income as flowing directly to the ultimate non-resident, and reassess withholding at the full 10% statutory rate. Tax Administration Law Article 63 imposes a penalty of 50%-500% of the unpaid withholding tax; Article 32 imposes daily surcharges; the arrangement may also be attacked as an arrangement without reasonable commercial purpose under CIT Law Article 47.

Director personal liability: Directors signing false beneficial-ownership certificates face criminal exposure under Criminal Law Article 280 (forging official documents) and Article 266 (fraud) if the false documentation is used to reduce Chinese tax. Civil liability: the tax authority may recover understated withholding tax plus penalties from the WFOE's directors to the extent they caused dissipation of assets; legal representatives are subject to travel bans and visa blacklisting under the Serious Tax Violation Information Publication Measures. If the foreign SPV is itself insolvent, the Chinese WFOE's directors could face personal joint liability under Company Law Article 20.
Export VAT Invoice Fraud and False Export Rebate Claims

Statutory sanction: PRC Criminal Law Article 205 (falsely issuing Special VAT Invoices) imposes heavy fines on the entity and imprisonment of the responsible persons, up to life imprisonment in especially serious cases; Criminal Law Article 204 (fraudulent export tax refund) provides imprisonment up to 10 years or life and confiscation of property for large-scale refund fraud. Tax Administration Law Articles 63 and 64 impose administrative fines from 50% to 500% of the evaded tax and cancellation of export tax refund qualification.

Director personal liability: Directors, CFOs, and export managers who knowingly design or sign false VAT invoices and customs declarations face personal criminal imprisonment (up to life imprisonment under Article 205 or Article 204) and fines, in addition to monetary restitution to the tax authority. Under Company Law Article 20, the corporate veil is pierced if the fraudulent conduct is an abuse of separate legal personality, making directors personally liable for the full tax loss. The legal representative will be placed on the serious tax dishonesty blacklist and barred from leaving China; assets may be frozen, and passports canceled.
