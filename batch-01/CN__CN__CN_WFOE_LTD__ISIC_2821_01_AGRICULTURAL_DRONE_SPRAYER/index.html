Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A virtual office, commercial mailbox, or mail-forwarding-only address cannot serve as the sole registered business address for a WFOE engaged in the manufacture and export of autonomous crop-spraying unmanned aerial vehicles. The SAMR registration, tax registration, and banking licensing systems require a definite physical business premises evidenced by a lease in the company's name, a valid property ownership or building inspection certificate, and proof that the address is the actual production and management site. For this High_Risk_Special_License industry, virtual offices are rejected by local tax authorities, SAMR, and banks because the company must be inspectable, must maintain drone assembly equipment, must store hazardous materials and lithium batteries safely, and must demonstrate commercial substance in the jurisdiction. An incubator or industrial-park address may be accepted only where the company occupies actual dedicated workspace and can satisfy the physical inspection of staff, assets, and operational records.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-up capital for a general WFOE under current PRC Company Law, but the registered capital must be set out in the Articles of Association and subscribed within the legally prescribed time limit (generally within 5 years for companies established after 2024 per the newly revised Company Law if applied, or as specified in the amended Articles). For an agricultural drone sprayer manufacturer, the actual paid-in capital must be reasonably commensurate with the registered capital and verified for the purpose of obtaining the Civil UAV manufacturing and sales licensing certificates; no blocked escrow account is statutorily mandated, but funds for registered capital subscription may need to be remitted in foreign exchange through a bank account opened under the relevant SAFE rules and a capital contribution verification report (验资报告) may be required by the licensing authority.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety Certification: obtain a pre-occupancy Fire Safety Inspection Certificate (建设工程消防验收/备案凭证) from the local Fire and Rescue Brigade (Ministry of Emergency Management) for the registered manufacturing workshop and warehouse, confirming compliance with GB 50016-2014 Code for Fire Protection Design of Buildings (fire-resistant construction materials, 2-hour fire resistant separations between production, battery storage and office areas, exit capacity, hydrant coverage, and automatic sprinkler system if floor area > 1,500 m² or storage height > 5.6 m). In addition, the company must obtain an occupancy permit for the use of hazardous storage (lithium battery rooms) and a daily fire safety inspection log that is maintained by a 'civil defense responsible person' with a formal training certificate; Structural Load Inspections: an authorised engineering quality inspection agency must certify that the roof and elevated work platforms can sustain the load of agricultural drone assembly rigs and test fixtures, and a seismic safety certification may be required in high-intensity fortified seismic zones; Workplace Safety Approval: as required by the Work Safety Law of the PRC, the company must complete a workplace hazard identification and have a qualified safety officer (注册安全工程师) on site, obtain a level-specific concealed works certificate for compressed air or high-pressure irrigation test stations, and register high-pressure watermist testing systems with the local market regulator. Fire certificates, structural alignment records, and the occupational safety file must be uploaded to the CAAC facility dossier and maintained for annual inspection.

For an agricultural drone sprayer manufacturer, the actual paid-in capital must be reasonably commensurate with the registered capital and verified for the purpose of obtaining the Civil UAV manufacturing and sales licensing certificates; no blocked escrow account is statutorily mandated, but funds for registered capital subscription may need to be remitted in foreign exchange through a bank account opened under the relevant SAFE rules and a capital contribution verification report (验资报告) may be required by the licensing authority.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA) - National Electronic Tax Office (electronic portal at etax.chinatax.gov.cn); VAT invoice verification through the National VAT Invoice Inspection Platform; customs export declarations routed via the China International Trade Single Window

E-government filing portal

Upon incorporation, the WFOE receives an 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) from the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR); the SAMR-to-STA one-stop data exchange automatically completes tax registration, making the USCC the taxpayer identification number (TIN) and VAT registration number. The legal representative and financial officer must activate the tax account in the National Electronic Tax Office using real-name authentication, register the bank settlement account for tax payments, and confirm the enterprise classification as a PRC resident enterprise. The foreign parent of the WFOE, if a non-resident enterprise with no PRC establishment, obtains a non-resident TIN through the WFOE as withholding agent for China-source dividends, interest, royalties, and service fees. Before exporting agricultural drone sprayers, the WFOE must also complete customs registration with the General Administration of Customs and file the foreign trade and bank settlement details with the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE).

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None for entity classification. PRC law has no check-the-box or fiscal-consolidation election. A WFOE limited liability company is mandatorily a PRC resident enterprise taxed on worldwide income at the 25% corporate income tax rate; no pass-through is available. Hybrid mismatch outcomes are not elective and may be adjusted under the general anti-avoidance rule. Elections are limited to VAT general versus small-scale taxpayer status, depreciation and amortization methods, and transfer pricing methodology choices.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

0.13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VAT registration is effective automatically from business license issuance with no minimum turnover threshold. Mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration is triggered when taxable turnover in any rolling 12-month period exceeds CNY 5,000,000; below this, small-scale VAT taxpayer status is possible, although an export-oriented WFOE should elect general VAT taxpayer status to claim input VAT refunds.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under the PRC VAT Interim Regulations and the MOF/STA Notice [2012] No. 39 on Export Tax Refund (Exemption) for Goods and Services, the WFOE may apply the 0% export VAT rate and claim an input VAT rebate only if all of the following are satisfied: (1) the WFOE is the actual exporter named on the customs export declaration; (2) the agricultural drone sprayers are physically exported from PRC customs territory under customs supervision; (3) the sale is supported by genuine commercial contracts and complete logistics evidence; (4) export proceeds are repatriated through designated foreign exchange bank accounts and verified by SAFE; and (5) the export tax rebate declaration is filed electronically within the statutory deadline. Qualifying offshore digital services are not relevant to this goods manufacturing export model.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaration for Export Goods) from the China International Trade Single Window, bearing the customs declaration number
  • Export VAT invoice (fully digitalized e-invoice) and commercial invoice issued in the name of the WFOE
  • Packing list and weight/volume manifest
  • Bill of Lading, air waybill, or international transport waybill evidencing physical departure from PRC customs territory
  • Sales contract and purchase order evidencing the cross-border trade terms
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or bank foreign exchange receipt record proving SAFE-monitored repatriation of export proceeds into the WFOE's designated foreign exchange settlement account

The STA Golden Tax Phase IV system requires the use of fully digitalized electronic invoices issued through the National Electronic Tax Office for all taxable sales. Each e-invoice is transmitted in near real time to the STA invoice data pool, carries an anti-counterfeit QR code, and must pass the National VAT Invoice Inspection Platform before VAT input credit can be taken. Taxpayers using ERPs must connect through the STA-approved e-invoice API integration to enable real-time issuance, cancellation, and red-letter reversal; no unapproved third-party clearing or digital stamping is allowed. Export sales are evidenced with export e-invoices linked to the customs export declaration data, not by separate paper stamping.

The STA cross-matches customs export declarations, export invoices, tax filings, and SAFE foreign exchange receipt data through Golden Tax Phase IV. If export proceeds are not collected by the SAFE/tax-audit benchmark (normally 180 days) or the required documentation is incomplete, the zero-rating claim may be denied, refunds may be clawed back, and VAT may be assessed retrospectively at the applicable domestic rate (9% or 13%), plus a daily late-payment surcharge of 0.05% from the original filing due date. In fraud cases involving false export declarations, forged documentation, or false VAT invoices, Article 66 of the PRC Tax Collection and Administration Law authorizes recovery of the full amount of fraudulently refunded VAT and penalties of one to five times the amount obtained; criminal liability may also arise under Articles 204 and 205 of the PRC Criminal Law. The general tax recovery period is 3 to 5 years depending on fault, and there is no limitation period for fraudulent tax recovery.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a differentiated FX regime: current-account items are generally convertible for genuine trade and services, subject to bank verification against supporting documents, while the capital account remains tightly controlled and requires SAFE registration or approval for inbound/outbound investment, shareholder loans, and repatriation. RMB is freely usable in trade settlement but cannot be freely exchanged offshore without prudent constraints. For a WFOE, foreign-currency capital contributions must be remitted through an FDI foreign exchange capital account, with received funds subject to the 'capital settlement' rules allowing conversion into RMB only for approved business expenditures. Profit repatriation to foreign shareholders requires verified audited financials, completed corporate income tax filing, and a SAFE-compliant remittance form, but is subject to 'bank as gatekeeper' regulation. Export proceeds must be repatriated to China; current-account receipts may be retained in foreign exchange or converted freely, but no mandatory surrender ratio exists. In practice, convertibility is high on the current account, low on the capital account, and hedging access is limited strictly to real-demand transactions. This creates compliance friction, especially for high-technology UAV exporters subject to dual-use export controls.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export proceeds must be repatriated to China within 180 days from the date of export declaration unless SAFE has approved an overseas retention arrangement, and there is no mandatory statutory conversion ratio: the exporter may retain 100% of foreign currency receipts in a foreign exchange settlement account or convert any portion at the onshore market rate. The bank monitors receipts against customs declaration data, and any mismatch or overdue collection triggers an automatic compliance alert under SAFE's goods-trade monitoring system.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Mandatory Audit Threshold Small Company Audit Exemption Regime

Turnover: The PRC Company Law imposes a universal audit mandate: a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise limited liability company must prepare annual financial reports and have them audited by a certified public accountant for every fiscal year in which the company exists, irrespective of size. Consequently there is no de-minimis balance-sheet, turnover, or headcount exemption; the quantitative trigger is any non-zero balance-sheet total, any non-zero turnover, or any headcount, including an inactive shell year. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: China's contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation thresholds: Local File is mandatory when annual related-party tangible property transfers exceed RMB 200 million; financial asset transfers exceed RMB 100 million; intangible asset transfers exceed RMB 100 million; or other related-party transactions exceed RMB 40 million. A Master File may be additionally required where aggregate related-party transactions exceed RMB 1 billion.

Economic substance scope: Applicable through China's General Anti-Avoidance Rule under EIT Law Article 47, SAT Announcement 2015 No.60, and related-party transaction substance requirements. A Chinese WFOE manufacturing and exporting agricultural drones must demonstrate local employees, premises, management functions, risk absorption, and core income-generating activities in China; without economic substance and commercial rationale, SAT may recharacterize transactions, deny treaty benefits, impute taxable income, and apply penalties.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Corporate income tax evasion and false export revenue reporting

Statutory sanction: Violates EIT Law Articles 41 and 44 and Tax Collection Administration Law Article 63; SAT recovers underpaid CIT, imposes a 0.05% daily late surcharge, and levies a penalty of 50%-500% of the underpaid tax; criminal referral for tax evasion under Criminal Law Article 201 follows where the evasion is intentional and material.

Director personal liability: Directors who cause the WFOE to file false returns or who commingle corporate assets with personal or shareholder assets face civil asset restitution and veil-piercing liability under Company Law Article 20; tax authorities may blacklist the legal representative and impose exit-control restrictions blocking overseas travel; criminal imprisonment up to seven years and fines apply for aggravated tax evasion.
Transfer pricing manipulation and failure to maintain TP documentation

Statutory sanction: SAT may perform a special tax adjustment under EIT Law Article 41, recharacterize the profit split, and impose interest at the PBOC benchmark rate plus up to 5 percentage points; refusal to provide TP documentation attracts penalties under Tax Collection Administration Law Article 44; intentional underpayment is penalized at 50%-500% under Article 63.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly sign false related-party transaction declarations are personally exposed to civil recovery of diverted profits, asset restitution, and indemnification of the company's tax debts in insolvency; travel bans and high-consumption blacklisting apply for unpaid tax liabilities; criminal liability arises where false accounting conceals taxable income, leading to imprisonment under Criminal Law Article 201.
Treaty abuse, shell holding structures, and unlawful outbound profit stripping

Statutory sanction: SAT will deny DTA benefits under Announcement 2015 No.60 and Annual Tax Collection Administration Law enforcement; the company must pay the full 10% dividend/interest/royalty WHT plus daily late surcharge; a withholding agent that fails to withhold is liable for a penalty of 50%-300% of the tax under Article 69; intentional misrepresentation constitutes tax evasion under Article 63.

Director personal liability: Directors and legal representatives who authorize dividend remittances without satisfying withholding obligations face personal civil liability for the unpaid tax and penalties; personal assets may be frozen and the individual placed on border-control blacklists preventing travel; fraudulent treaty claims can result in criminal imprisonment under Criminal Law Article 201 for tax evasion or under general fraud provisions where documents are forged.
