Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A virtual office, commercial mailbox, or shared co-working address is categorically rejected for a WFOE engaged in the manufacture of armored security personnel transport vehicles. The local SAMR will refuse registration unless a lease agreement, premises-use certificate, and property ownership or legal-use certificate are provided. The State Taxation Administration (STA) branch requires physical presence verification before approving General VAT taxpayer status and issuing machine-invoicing quotas. Corporate banks also require an in-person KYC inspection of the registered office and production premises before opening the basic deposit settlement account. Accordingly, a virtual office fails the statutory commercial-substance test and would additionally violate the beneficial-owner verification standards of PBOC/SAMR Order No. 3 and the Company Law of the People's Republic of China.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China; electronic filings are performed through the State Taxation Administration's Electronic Tax Bureau (national online tax portal).

E-government filing portal

For a WFOE incorporated in China, the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) is issued by the local Market Regulation Administration after company registration and serves as the statutory Tax Identification Number (TIN) and VAT registration number. The company must complete tax registration with the competent local tax authority through the State Taxation Administration's Electronic Tax Bureau within 30 days after obtaining the business license, submitting the incorporation documents, approved articles, registered address, legal representative identity, business scope, and banking account details. The tax authority then activates the VAT taxpayer status: small-scale VAT taxpayer by default for annual taxable turnover at or below RMB 5,000,000, or general VAT taxpayer upon application/election or once turnover exceeds the threshold. General taxpayer status requires the installation of invoice-issuance capability (Tax U-Key and digitalized electronic invoice module) and linking of the enterprise bank accounts for tax collection and refund settlement. A foreign non-resident entity not incorporated in China but deriving taxable income from China must complete simplified tax registration with the competent tax authority to obtain a non-resident taxpayer code for withholding compliance.

None (China does not have a check-the-box pass-through election). A small-scale VAT taxpayer may elect to be registered and taxed as a general VAT taxpayer if accounting, bookkeeping, and invoicing conditions are satisfied; there is no available hybrid mismatch election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 annual taxable sales for mandatory registration as a general VAT taxpayer; all entities with VAT-taxable sales must complete VAT tax registration, with entities below the threshold classified as small-scale VAT taxpayers unless they elect general taxpayer status.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export sales of goods physically shipped out of China qualify for the 0% VAT rate with input VAT refund when the seller is a general VAT taxpayer, the export is declared through customs under export procedures, the overseas buyer pays in foreign exchange or cross-border RMB, and the proceeds are repatriated into China through designated foreign exchange banks within the statutory deadline (180 days). For military/dual-use tactical hardware, the export must be separately licensed by the competent national export-control authority and comply with end-use and end-user certification rules. Qualifying offshore services and digital services consumed wholly outside China may be zero-rated or VAT-exempt under the applicable tax-exemption notification; the supply must be supported by service export documentation and foreign-exchange receipt evidence.

  • Customs export declaration (export goods customs declaration; equivalent to the Bill of Entry for export clearance)
  • Original special VAT invoice and export sales invoice
  • Bill of Lading (or airway bill/waybill) evidencing physical export shipment
  • Bank foreign exchange receipt and settlement confirmation (trade foreign exchange receipt/payment information) evidencing repatriation of export proceeds
  • Export contract and, where required for tactical armored vehicles, the national export license and end-user certificate

VAT invoices are issued through the State Taxation Administration's comprehensive invoice service infrastructure (Golden Tax IV System). Issuance data are transmitted in real time to the tax authority, with system-to-system API integration available for high-volume issuers. Special VAT invoices are subject to encryption via a tax control digital stamp/Tax U-Key, and input VAT credit is allowed only after invoice authentication and matching through the VAT Invoice Comprehensive Service Platform.

Unsubstantiated or fraudulent export zero-rating is subject to retrospective clawback of the refunded VAT plus statutory interest. The tax authority may impose a penalty from 50% up to five times the tax involved under the Law of the People's Republic of China on Tax Administration and Tax Collection; false issuance of special VAT invoices and fraudulent procurement of export tax refunds additionally attract criminal liability under the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China (including Article 204 for fraudulent export tax refund crime), with penalties up to imprisonment and confiscation of property. The exporter is also subject to downgraded tax credit status, enhanced post-clearance audit, and suspension of export tax refund treatment.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a managed float and current-account convertibility with strict capital-account controls. RMB is convertible for trade-related transactions with supporting invoices, contracts and customs data; capital account items such as FDI equity injections require SAFE registration, account designation and use of a capital account at a designated bank, while outbound investment and cross-border lending are license/registration-bound. Export proceeds are not automatically confiscated but must be repatriated via SAFE-monitored channels, with banks verifying goods declaration, logistics and purpose codes. Profit remittances by a WFOE require audited financial statements, corporate income tax settlement certificates, and board-approved distribution resolution; once the tax obligation is cleared, dividends may be remitted in foreign currency or RMB. However, military-tactical hardware invoices and related defense payments trigger enhanced documentary checks. There is no full capital account convertibility and no automatic currency swap for speculative purposes; all hedging must be underlying-transaction-driven with tenors aligned to cash flows.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export receipts for this WFOE must be repatriated into China within 180 days from the date of customs clearance; the exporter must lodge the foreign currency in the designated Foreign Currency Settlement Account and match it against customs declaration, invoice, and logistics documentation. Ordinary trade enjoys optional conversion without a fixed statutory surrender ratio, but under the bank's high-risk defense policy, 100% of the received funds must be converted to RMB within 5 business days and cannot be retained abroad; the bank will request the prior-year export declaration and SAFE online spot-check certification.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC), operating through the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) as the delegated FX administration and cross-border payment regulator

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

A financial technology electronic money institution cannot act as the entity's primary China clearing bank. Under PBOC and SAFE rules, only licensed banks with a SAFE account system may hold capital-account funds for a mainland WFOE. Off-group EMIs such as Airwallex, PingPong, or similar digital payment providers lack the Chinese onshore bank account and SAFE registration capability for capital-account receipts, and their remote onboarding will not satisfy Customer Due Diligence for military tactical vehicles. Feasibility: Low; they may be used only as a non-legal payment information layer, never as custodian of China-licensed capital accounts.

Strike-off trigger

Mandatory Audit Threshold Mandatory Audit for All Entities

Turnover: Under PRC Company Law, every company including a WFOE must have each annual financial report independently audited by a licensed CPA firm; there is no asset, turnover, or headcount exemption threshold. The mandatory audit trigger is the fact of registration as a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise. Supplemental triggers: (i) all foreign-invested enterprises are within the MOFCOM annual foreign-investment information reporting regime and must carry an audited financial statement; (ii) a defense/tactical-vehicle manufacturer holding a SASTIND license must maintain audited financial evidence of production capability and compliance; (iii) where the enterprise applies for tax incentives, bank credit facilities in excess of local financing thresholds, or government procurement, the audited financial statement is a legal precondition. Thus the effective quantitative threshold is zero in all balance-sheet, turnover and headcount dimensions.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% on dividends paid to a non-resident enterprise from the WFOE's post-tax profits, unless a China tax treaty reduces the rate, often to 5% for a shareholder owning at least 10% of the paid-up capital.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: A Local File must be prepared when related-party tangible property transactions exceed RMB 200 million, related-party intangible property transactions exceed RMB 100 million, or related-party financial asset transactions exceed RMB 100 million in a fiscal year. A Master File is required when the annual aggregate related-party transactions reach RMB 10 billion or when the State Taxation Administration specifically designates the taxpayer.

Economic substance scope: China does not apply Gulf-style Economic Substance Regulations, but the substance-over-form doctrine is codified in Article 47 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law and Article 120 of its Implementation Rules as a General Anti-Avoidance Rule. Core Income-Generating Activities analysis is used to test whether the WFOE performs actual management, manufacturing, engineering, and risk-bearing functions in China. Conduit entities without local employees, premises, or decision-making authority will be denied treaty benefits and may be recharacterized, causing additional CIT, interest, and penalties.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion via False Invoicing and Under-Declaration of Revenue

Statutory sanction: Article 63 of the Tax Collection Administration Law imposes a civil penalty of 50% to 500% of the unpaid tax; fabricated VAT invoices trigger Article 205 of the PRC Criminal Law and fraudulent export refunds trigger Article 204, with monetary fines and confiscation of property; the unpaid tax is collected with daily surcharges.

Director personal liability: Directors and actual controllers who order or knowingly participate in the evasion are personally liable for civil restitution of the tax and penalties. They face exit denial and blacklisting under Article 44 of the Tax Collection Administration Law, freezing of personal assets, and criminal imprisonment. Large-sum evasion under Article 201 of the Criminal Law carries imprisonment from three to seven years, with additional fines and mandatory restitution.
Shell Company without Economic Substance and Artificially Inflated Deductions

Statutory sanction: The General Anti-Avoidance Rule under Article 47 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law and Article 120 of the Implementation Rules allows the tax authority to recharacterize the arrangement and make a special tax adjustment; Article 63 of the Tax Collection Administration Law adds a 50% to 500% penalty; dividend treaty relief is denied and 10% withholding tax is reassessed, plus late surcharges.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false transfer-pricing documentation, beneficial ownership certificates, and tax filings face corporate veil-piercing liability: personal assets are reached for civil restitution, the individual is blacklisted and prevented from leaving China, removals are blocked, and serious deliberate evasion converts the civil liability into criminal tax fraud with imprisonment of up to seven years.
Unlicensed Export of Military Tactical Vehicles and Sanctioned-Sales Violations

Statutory sanction: The PRC Export Control Law (2020) and the Customs Law impose suspension or revocation of export licenses, confiscation of illegal goods, and fines of up to RMB 5 million or, in serious cases, up to ten times the illegal business turnover; illegal trafficking of military equipment violates Article 125 of the PRC Criminal Law and illegal business operations violate Article 225, each carrying severe fines and imprisonment.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced for directors who orchestrate unauthorized defense exports: they face criminal imprisonment of three to ten years or longer for grave violations, permanent blacklisting from defense industry positions, confiscation of personal property and restitution to the state, foreign directors may be banned from re-entering China, and the entity's export licenses are revoked.
