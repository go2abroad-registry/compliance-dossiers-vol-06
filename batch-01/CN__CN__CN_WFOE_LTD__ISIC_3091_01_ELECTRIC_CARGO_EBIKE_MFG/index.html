Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None at the national level for ISIC 3091. A wholly foreign-owned enterprise may determine its registered capital in the Articles of Association without a statutory minimum paid-up capital; however, under Article 47 of the 2023 Company Law the subscribed capital must be fully paid in within five years from establishment. No escrow or blocked capital account is mandated, but the foreign exchange capital account opened with a designated bank must be used to evidence and receive cross-border capital injections.

Funding & unlock path: Fire protection design approval and completion acceptance certificate from the local fire and rescue brigade; building structural load inspection certificate for the factory floor; installation of fire hydrants, alarm systems, emergency lighting and extinguishers; workplace safety clearance covering welding, lithium-ion battery charging and warehouse areas; occupational disease hazard factor testing; worker medical surveillance program; and a civil defense emergency response plan registered with the local emergency management bureau.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; electronic filings are made through the Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax) online portal operated by the provincial STA offices and accessible through the national electronic tax service platform; customs and foreign-exchange data exchange is integrated via the China International Trade Single Window.

E-government filing portal

For the WFOE, the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) issued on the business license by the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) is the Tax Identification Number (TIN) and VAT registration number. The WFOE must complete tax registration within 30 days of license issuance through the Electronic Tax Bureau portal by uploading the business license, legal representative identity document, articles of association, registered office proof, bank deposit account certificate, shareholding and ownership structure, financial accounting system description, and appointment of financial and accounting personnel; the system then opens files for corporate income tax, VAT, urban maintenance and construction tax, education surcharges, stamp duty, and payroll withholding taxes. A foreign non-resident enterprise without an establishment in China that is liable for VAT or withholding tax must complete non-resident tax registration through the e-Tax portal or through its Chinese collection-withholding agent, submitting home-country tax residency certificates, foreign business registration certificate, director or legal representative passport or corporate identity evidence, board resolutions appointing agents, and the underlying taxable supply contract, to obtain a non-resident TIN for tax return filing and withholding clearance.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Annual taxable sales of RMB 5,000,000 or more mandate general VAT taxpayer registration; below RMB 5,000,000 the entity remains a small-scale taxpayer unless it elects general VAT taxpayer status. Separate VAT registration obligations also arise for non-resident suppliers with taxable sales in mainland China even below the local threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The WFOE may claim the 0% export VAT rate and the associated VAT refund and exemption through the exemption-offset-refund method only if the electric cargo e-bikes are physically exported from China under a customs export declaration filed in the WFOE name, title passes to an offshore buyer, the goods leave the PRC customs territory, the export sale is supported by a genuine contract and commercial documentation, the foreign-currency or RMB export proceeds are collected through the designated foreign-exchange bank within the SAFE collection-verification period, and the exporter has general VAT taxpayer status and complete matching of invoice, customs, logistics, and payment records. Cross-border digital services are outside the current manufacturing scope but would require a separate consumed-outside-China services test and documentary evidence of offshore customer receipt.

  • Customs Export Goods Declaration Form produced by the China International Trade Single Window, including customs release feed-back and manifest or goods clearance record
  • Ocean bill of lading, multimodal transport bill, or air waybill evidencing physical shipment from a PRC port to the overseas consignee
  • Bank inward remittance receipt and SAFE foreign-exchange receipts and payments declaration proving receipt and settlement of export proceeds by the statutory deadline
  • Export VAT refund and exemption application reports filed in the STA export tax refund system, supported by export sales contract, commercial invoice, packing list, VAT invoice copies, and the enterprise export tax rebate calculation worksheet
  • Export collection verification and foreign-exchange payoff records if the exporter is under the macro-monitoring or list-mode category under SAFE trade foreign-exchange administration

China requires real-time fiscal clearance through the Full Digital Electronic Invoice (FDEI) system under the Golden Tax Phase IV digital platform. VAT invoices are issued only through the STA-authorized electronic invoice service platform, which assigns national unique invoice numbers, affixes the mandatory anti-counterfeiting digital stamp and QR code, transmits invoice data to the STA in near real time, and requires API or ERP integration for batch issuance. Handwritten or self-generated invoices are invalid for input VAT credit, export refund substantiation, or buyer corporate deductions.

If the STA or SAFE identifies missing customs declaration data, unverified foreign-exchange receipt, mismatched logistics documentation, returned goods not declared, or fraudulent or forged export supporting documents, the tax authority will disallow the zero-rating, claw back all VAT refunded and any corresponding input VAT credits, assess late-payment interest at 0.05% per day, and impose administrative penalties of 50% to 500% of the tax amount involved, in addition to downgrading the exporter's tax-rebate credit status and mandating enhanced documentary inspection of future exports. Criminal referral follows for false export refunds; the crime of fraudulently obtaining export tax rebates under Article 204 of the Criminal Law of the PRC carries imprisonment and confiscation of property, with aggravated penalties including life imprisonment where the fraudulent amount is especially huge or the scheme is organized.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a managed floating exchange rate regime with layered convertibility. Current account transactions (including trade in goods and services) are generally freely convertible, subject to real transaction verification by banks under SAFE guidelines. Capital account transactions remain controlled: foreign direct investment into a WFOE must be registered with SAFE, and the injected capital must enter a dedicated foreign exchange capital account opened at a local bank. Conversion of foreign currency capital into RMB follows the willing settlement policy, but every conversion requires a valid payment instruction and underlying documentation such as invoices, contracts, or tax payment schedules. Profit repatriation (dividends) is permitted after annual audit and final tax settlement, provided that the profit is derived from legitimate business and documented by board resolutions and external audit reports. Restrictions may still apply if the entity has unresolved SAFE penalties, tax arrears, or negative information in the foreign exchange business integrity index. Repatriation of liquidation proceeds or reduction of capital requires special approval and is often time-consuming. In practice, cross-border funds are heavily monitored through a banking single window, and any discrepancy can lead to suspension of future remittances. Capital verification is not always mandatory for WFOE establishment due to the subscribed capital system, but banks may require evidence of actual injection for operational account credentialing.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under current SAFE rules, export proceeds in foreign currency are not subject to forced surrender. A WFOE may retain its export earnings in a foreign currency settlement account or convert them voluntarily. However, all export receipts must be reported through the bank's cross-border receipts and payments system within 30 days of receipt, and the bank will assess the authenticity against customs declarations and contracts. If the entity opts to sell the foreign currency for RMB, it must present a valid transaction certificate (e.g., commercial invoice, customs declaration, bill of lading). The bank may require additional proof for amounts exceeding RMB 500,000 or where the counterparty appears on sanctions screening lists. Historical retention of foreign currency is permitted, but the statutory timeline for reporting is core; failure to report within 30 days may trigger a SAFE inquiry and administrative penalties. Mandatory conversion ratios are effectively zero, but banks may apply operational conversion pressure for high-forex-holding accounts under macro-prudential assessments.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Fintech electronic money institutions (EMIs) such as Revolut, Wise, or N26 do not possess a Chinese banking license or SAFE approval to provide cross-border payment and foreign exchange services to onshore legal entities. Under the Payment & Settlement Rules and the Foreign Exchange Administration Regulations, all FX operations must be conducted through designated foreign exchange banks or qualified non-bank payment institutions. For a WFOE, using an unlicensed EMI for core treasury would expose the entity to civil and administrative liability, including freezing of funds and monetary penalties. Some EMIs partner with Chinese banks to offer foreign-currency pockets for offshore entities, but the onshore WFOE cannot use these as primary operational accounts. Overall viability is Low; the only prudent approach is to use licensed domestic banks for all CNY and FX flows, with EMIs limited to non-operational, auxiliary international spending if any.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound capital injection routing: The foreign shareholder must transfer equity investment funds directly from an overseas bank account to the WFOE's foreign exchange capital account (or RMB capital account) opened with a designated bank in China. Prior to remittance, the WFOE must complete FDI registration at SAFE (directly through the bank via the Foreign Exchange Integrated Management Platform) and obtain a Foreign Exchange Business Registration Certificate (or registration code). The incoming wire must carry payment purpose code 6220 (or corresponding code for equity investment) and include the FDI registration code in the remittance remarks. Upon receipt, the bank conducts authenticity verification and credits the account; if the funds are in foreign currency and the company needs RMB, a capital settlement transaction is processed under a payment resolution and supporting underlying documents. For distributed capital contributions, each injection is recorded, and banks may request a capital verification report to confirm actual paid-in capital if the company's articles of association require it. At the end of each quarter, the WFOE must update the SAFE foreign investment information report (via state administration) and continue to report changes in beneficial owner.Outbound trade disbursements: For settlement of export receivables, if the buyer requires a documentary credit, a commercial letter of credit (L/C) is established at a Chinese advising bank, and after shipment the exporter presents compliant shipping documents; settlement occurs via SWIFT MT700/MT400 series. For direct remittances, SWIFT MT103 messages are used with correct purpose codes (e.g., 7510 for goods trade). Prior to making any outward payment for raw materials or services, the WFOE must submit to the bank a trade payment application accompanied by customs declarations, purchase contracts, and commercial invoices. The bank will verify the payment is consistent with the declared trade or capital item. For high-value settlements exceeding USD 500,000, banks may require additional supporting import documents. Documentary collections are also permissible but are less common; these would use SWIFT MT501/MT510. All outbound payments require an underlying real transaction, and the bank will perform post-transaction verification against SAFE data.Onshore FX forward contracts with a licensed bank, requiring a valid underlying corporate trade or capital exposure and signed ISDA or CNAPS master agreement.Cross-currency interest rate swaps (CCIRS) for long-term liability hedging, subject to bank credit approval and SAFE reporting.FX options and option spreads provided by A-share banks, limited to hedging and not speculative trading, with tenors matching underlying exposure.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Fintech electronic money institutions (EMIs) such as Revolut, Wise, or N26 do not possess a Chinese banking license or SAFE approval to provide cross-border payment and foreign exchange services to onshore legal entities. Under the Payment & Settlement Rules and the Foreign Exchange Administration Regulations, all FX operations must be conducted through designated foreign exchange banks or qualified non-bank payment institutions. For a WFOE, using an unlicensed EMI for core treasury would expose the entity to civil and administrative liability, including freezing of funds and monetary penalties. Some EMIs partner with Chinese banks to offer foreign-currency pockets for offshore entities, but the onshore WFOE cannot use these as primary operational accounts. Overall viability is Low; the only prudent approach is to use licensed domestic banks for all CNY and FX flows, with EMIs limited to non-operational, auxiliary international spending if any.

Turnover: Under PRC Company Law, every limited liability company, including a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise, must prepare statutory annual financial statements and have them independently audited by a certified public accounting firm registered in China. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% statutory dividend withholding tax under CIT Law Article 37 on gross dividends paid by a PRC resident WFOE to any non-resident enterprise beneficial owner that does not have a Chinese permanent establishment to which the dividend is effectively connected. Qualifying treaty shareholders holding at least 25% of the WFOE capital may reduce the rate to 5% under certain DTAs, and other DTA-specific reductions may apply only with full beneficial ownership and substance substantiation.
