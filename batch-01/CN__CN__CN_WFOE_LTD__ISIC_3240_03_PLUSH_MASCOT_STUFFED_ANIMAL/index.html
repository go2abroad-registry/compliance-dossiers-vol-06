Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; online electronic portal: STA Electronic Tax Platform (e-Tax) at https://tpass.chinatax.gov.cn and local provincial e-Tax service websites

E-government filing portal

For the Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE), the legal incorporation process with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) issues a Business License carrying the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which serves as the enterprise's sole statutory tax identification number (TIN) for all tax authorities. Within 30 days of SAMR issuance, the enterprise must complete tax registration through the STA e-Tax portal or in-person at the competent district tax bureau. Steps include: (1) logging into the e-Tax portal with the USCC and identity verification, (2) filing the taxpayer information supplement form covering legal representative, financial officer, tax payer profile, registered bank account and accounting policy, (3) activating VAT taxpayer status and selecting the applicable taxpayer classification (general vs. small-scale), (4) registering for the electronic invoice system and obtaining digital certificates required for invoice issuance, and (5) registering for social insurance and urban maintenance and construction tax if applicable. For a foreign non-resident enterprise that earns Chinese-source income without an establishment, a separate withholding tax identification number is issued by the STA upon application, but the WFOE itself is a resident Chinese entity and holds the USCC as its definitive TIN.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Under the Enterprise Income Tax (EIT) regime, no check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election exists; the WFOE is uniformly taxed at entity level at the statutory 25% rate. Under the VAT regime, an enterprise with annual taxable sales below RMB 5 million may elect to register as a VAT general taxpayer voluntarily; an enterprise with annual taxable sales at or above RMB 5 million is mandated to register as a general taxpayer. Additionally, a qualifying taxpayer may elect the simplified VAT calculation method for specific goods or services under the VAT implementing rules, subject to STA approval, but this does not change the default entity-level taxation framework.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

There is no turnover threshold for basic tax registration; every enterprise must complete tax registration within 30 days of obtaining its SAMR business license. The mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration threshold is annual taxable sales exceeding RMB 5,000,000. Below this threshold, an enterprise is classified as a small-scale taxpayer subject to the standard levy rates and simplified collection methodology.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export zero-rating (exemption and refund of output VAT) is available for tangible movable goods, including custom plush mascot dolls under ISIC 3240, when the goods are physically exported from the customs territory of China, the exporter is the WFOE, and the export transaction is genuine, pecuniary, and supported by verifiable logistics and payment evidence. The zero-rating applies to the export sale under the Measures for the Refund (Exemption) of VAT on Exported Goods and Services, provided the WFOE holds the full legal documentation bundle, has collected and repatriated the foreign currency proceeds within the statutory deadline, and has submitted the export tax refund declaration to the STA. Service exports and offshore digital services are not within the business scope of this specific plush toy manufacturing entity and therefore cannot be zero-rated.

  • Customs Export Goods Declaration Form (Bill of Entry) cleared by China Customs with the export customs return code
  • Commercial Invoice issued by the WFOE to the overseas buyer
  • Export Contract or Purchase Order stating the terms of sale (INCOTERMS)
  • Packing List detailing the shipped plush toy contents and quantities
  • Bill of Lading (or Air Waybill) evidencing physical movement of goods out of China
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or Bank Foreign Exchange Settlement Receipt issued by the designated foreign exchange bank to prove receipt of the export proceeds
  • Export VAT Special Invoicing or ordinary export invoice as generated through the e-invoice system
  • Customs export VAT rebate proof where the rebate system requires a specially endorsed export declaration

China operates the Golden Tax Phase IV system mandating near real-time reporting of all VAT invoices. The WFOE, as a VAT general taxpayer, must issue all sales invoices through the STA-approved fully digitized electronic invoice ('shu piao') system or tax-certified invoicing software. Each invoice must carry a machine-generated anti-counterfeiting QR code and a unique invoice number pre-registered with the STA. Invoice data, including value, tax rate, and buyer/seller identification, must be transmitted digitally to the STA tax control platform upon issuance. Buyers must verify every invoice through the National VAT Invoice Verification Platform. Non-compliance with electronic clearance and digital stamping requirements results in invoice invalidation and penalties under the Administrative Measures for Tax Invoices.

The STA automatically cross-checks the export declaration data with China Customs and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) electronic systems. Any zero-rating claim that is not substantiated by the complete customs, logistics, and banking documentation bundle, or that fails to satisfy the 180-day foreign exchange repatriation deadline, is subject to immediate clawback of all VAT refunded, plus late-payment interest computed from the date of refund. Under Article 63 of the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection, a false export claim or fraudulent zero-rating filing will trigger an administrative penalty of 0.5 to 5 times the amount of VAT improperly refunded, and the entire refund entitlement for the period may be disallowed. For criminal evasion, Article 204 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China provides for fixed-term imprisonment and criminal fines for fraudulent export tax refunds of significant value. The STA can also impose a 1,000 to 10,000 RMB non-conformity fine on the taxpayer for incomplete recordkeeping, and may disqualify the WFOE from simplified export tax refund privileges for up to three years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China maintains a managed floating exchange rate regime in which the Renminbi is currently fully convertible for current account transactions such as trade in goods and services, while capital account items remain strictly regulated. A Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise engaged in plush toy OEM manufacturing must register its direct investment with SAFE through a designated domestic bank before any inbound equity injection can be made. Once registered, foreign currency capital is credited to a Foreign Exchange Capital Account and can only be settled into RMB under the Payment-Usage Order, meaning each disbursement must be supported by genuine business contracts, invoices, tax records, and, where applicable, prior customs declarations. Profit remittance to the overseas parent requires audited financial statements, tax clearance proof, and bank verification that the distributable profits have been lawfully generated and taxed. There is no fixed mandatory surrender ratio for current account export proceeds because export foreign currency receipts may be retained in a foreign exchange settlement account or voluntarily converted to RMB at market rates; however, the receipts must be repatriated to China and reconciled with customs declarations. The overall assessment is that current account convertibility is liberalized, but every capital movement requires documentary traceability, and any mismatch between the stated purpose, the invoice, and the actual logistics flow will trigger bank-level suspension and possible SAFE penalty.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export foreign currency receipts for plush toy shipments must be repatriated to China and collected in a SAFE-registered foreign exchange settlement account within the statutory collection period, generally 180 days from the customs export declaration date. There is no compulsory mandatory conversion ratio; the WFOE may retain the foreign currency balance or settle it into RMB at the prevailing market rate through a designated domestic commercial bank. Each receipt must be matched against the customs declaration, commercial invoice, packing list, and shipping documents, and significant delays or mismatches must be explained to the bank under the SAFE current account authenticity and matching framework.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low; not viable as a statutory treasury rail for a mainland China WFOE. Foreign digital EMI platforms do not hold PBOC banking licenses and cannot provide lawful RMB basic deposit accounts, participate in CIPS domestic clearing, accept tax and customs duty payments, or open the required SAFE-registered foreign exchange capital accounts. An offshore EMI may only act as an ancillary payment initiation tool for collecting overseas customer card payments, but all RMB settlement, statutory tax payment, FX conversion, capital injection, and trade finance operations must flow through a licensed domestic commercial bank. Attempting to use an unlicensed EMI for core treasury activity in China would create a serious AML deficiency and may be treated as running an illegal payment business.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity capital for the WFOE must first complete FDI foreign exchange registration with SAFE through a designated domestic bank. The overseas parent should remit funds using SWIFT MT103 with a clear payment purpose code such as FDI registered equity injection for the WFOE, routed to the bank-issued Foreign Exchange Capital Account. After arrival, the bank verifies the remitter, the SAFE registration certificate, and the conversion purpose, then settles the foreign currency into RMB under the Payment-Usage Order. Each RMB disbursement from the capital account must be backed by invoices, contracts, and a negative-list compliance check. Turnover: No quantitative balance sheet, turnover, or headcount exemption applies to an ordinary PRC limited liability company. The PRC Company Law mandates that every company, including a wholly foreign-owned enterprise, prepare annual financial accounting reports for each fiscal year and have them audited by a lawfully constituted certified public accounting firm. Therefore, an independent certified CPA audit is compulsory every year with no minimum threshold. Additional compulsory audit and regulatory examination obligations, also without size exemptions, apply to listed companies, financial institutions, state-owned or state-controlled enterprises, and entities operating under regulated-industry licences; for a standard plush-toy OEM WFOE, the governing legal criterion is the general annual statutory audit obligation under the PRC Company Law.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File preparation is mandatory under SAT Public Notice [2016] No. 42 when annual related party transactions exceed: RMB 200 million for tangible property transfers, RMB 100 million for intangible property transfers, RMB 100 million for financial asset transfers or financing transactions, or RMB 40 million for provision or receipt of services and other transactions. A Master File is additionally required when the WFOE belongs to a multinational group and the group's consolidated operating revenue in the prior year exceeds RMB 5 billion or equivalent, and the enterprise has cross-border related party transactions above the statutory disclosure threshold.

Economic substance scope: Mainland China does not use a separately labelled Economic Substance Regulations regime, but the Enterprise Income Tax Law and its special tax adjustment rules require real economic substance under the general anti-avoidance rule in Article 47 and the beneficial owner tests in the DTA approval framework. A WFOE manufacturing plush animals must demonstrate substantive core income-generating activities in China such as direct factory production, local procurement, quality control, employment of resident directors and workers, and actual decision-making over inventory, pricing, and risk. A non-resident shareholder will not obtain treaty relief if it is a shell without the staff, premises, assets, and control necessary to own the shares and receive dividends beneficially.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion Through Shell Company or Artificial Profit Diversion

Statutory sanction: Enterprise Income Tax Law Article 47 empowers the tax authority to adjust transactions lacking bona fide commercial substance; Article 41 of the same law recharacterises related party pricing. The Tax Administration Law Article 63 treats wilful tax evasion as an act punishable by an administrative fine of 50% to 5 times the tax evaded plus late interest; the general anti-avoidance assessment permits a ten-year statute of limitation. Criminal Law Article 201 imposes criminal liability for tax evasion where the amount is large and covers more than 10% of tax payable.

Director personal liability: Article 20 of the Company Law permits the People's Court or tax authority to pierce the corporate veil where shareholders or directors abuse the limited liability structure to harm creditors or evade taxes; directors are jointly and severally liable for unpaid taxes and civil restitution up to the profits diverted. The principal director and actual controller are subject to criminal prosecution under Article 201, leading to imprisonment: three years or more in severe cases, up to seven years where the amount is huge. Tax debtors and responsible directors are placed on the tax blacklist, triggering travel bans, restriction on business exit via international ports, revocation of departure permits, and restrictions on high consumptive behaviour.
Transfer Pricing Misstatement and Fabricated Intercompany Charges

Statutory sanction: Enterprise Income Tax Law Articles 41 to 44 and SAT Public Notice [2017] No. 6 permit the tax authority to adjust taxable income, impose an interest charge at the benchmark lending rate calculated on the additional tax, and apply a penalty of 50% to 5 times the tax shortage if intent is established. Failure to maintain or provide contemporaneous documentation also triggers a specific administrative penalty under the special tax adjustment rules, and the burden of proof shifts to the taxpayer. Criminal Law Article 205 applies where false special VAT invoices are used to fabricate costs and evade taxation, with fines and imprisonment.

Director personal liability: A director who signs false financial statements, participates in the creation of false contracts and invoices, or instructs the finance department to deduct artificially inflated intercompany charges will be treated as the directly responsible supervisor together with the finance executive. Under the unit crime doctrine, the company is fined and the director may be sentenced for tax evasion or false VAT invoice issuance under Criminal Law Articles 201 and 205; personal assets may be seized to satisfy restitution. Travel bans under the tax integrity blacklist and 'Restricting Law-Abiding Persons' system prevent offending directors from exiting China until the tax debt is settled.
Illegal Dividend Repatriation and Evasion of Withholding Tax

Statutory sanction: Article 37 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law imposes withholding at source for non-resident income; Article 69 of the Tax Administration Law authorises the tax authority to impose a penalty of 50% to 3 times the amount that the withholding agent failed to withhold, together with late payment surcharge. If the under-withholding is coupled with a fraudulent entry in the CIT return, the full tax evasion penalty applies and the case may be referred for criminal investigation.

Director personal liability: The directors of the WFOE who cause the company to disguise dividend distribution through false contracts are jointly liable for the unwithheld tax and penalties under the corporate personality piercing rule; they may be ordered to make good the loss to the State and to the minority shareholder where the disguised payment was made to a controlling foreign shareholder. Criminal liability arises if the act constitutes intentional tax evasion or false invoice issuance, exposing the responsible director to imprisonment from three years up to seven years depending on the amount and circumstances. The shareholding non-resident beneficial owner may also be blacklisted in China's joint punishment system, preventing transfer of assets, revocation of licences and visa-imposed exit restrictions.
