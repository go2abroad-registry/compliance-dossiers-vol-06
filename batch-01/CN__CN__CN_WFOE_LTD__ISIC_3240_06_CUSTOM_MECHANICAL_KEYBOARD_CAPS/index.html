Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China, exercised through provincial and municipal tax service bureaus; the online taxpayer system is the STA's National Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax) portal.

E-government filing portal

A WFOE is a PRC domestic resident enterprise, not a non-resident entity. Upon incorporation, the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) issues a Business License containing the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which is legally the taxpayer identification number and VAT registration number. The SAMR issuance is automatically synchronized to the local STA tax service bureau under the integrated multi-certificate tax registration reform, so no separate TIN application is required. Within 30 days of the business license issuance, the WFOE must complete supplementary taxpayer records in the e-Tax portal, including legal representative, financial officer, accounting and bookkeeping standard, domestic bank settlement account, business scope, shareholder information and beneficial ownership declarations, and intended VAT taxpayer status. Before claiming export VAT zero-rating or refunds, the WFOE must also complete the export tax refund/exemption recordation in the e-Tax portal and nominate the bank account used for export refunds.

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

All PRC business entities must complete tax registration upon SAMR business license issuance; there is no economic turnover VAT-registration exemption threshold. The compulsory general VAT taxpayer registration threshold is annual taxable sales exceeding RMB 5,000,000, and manufacturers/exporters below the threshold may voluntarily register as general VAT taxpayers to obtain export refund capabilities.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A general VAT taxpayer may claim the 0% VAT rate on physical exports of custom mechanical keyboard caps that are actually transported out of PRC customs territory, provided the goods have been declared to Customs through the China International Trade Single Window, the sale is made to an overseas customer, and the export proceeds are repatriated through SAFE-supervised bank channels. B2C online sales through Shopify Payments or Stripe are substantiated as exports only when each parcel or batch is cleared under an applicable customs export or cross-border e-commerce export supervision mode and the order, logistics, and payment records are preserved. The 0% rate eliminates output VAT and supports an input VAT refund; qualifying offshore digital services do not apply to this goods-based business.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bill of Entry) accepted through the China International Trade Single Window
  • Bill of Lading or air waybill issued by the carrying carrier evidencing physical departure from PRC customs territory
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC), or the equivalent bank foreign-exchange receipt record and SAFE export-proceeds monitoring certificate
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Cross-border e-commerce retail export order and logistics manifest when cleared under an e-commerce customs mode

VAT invoices and special VAT invoices must be issued through the STA's full-digitalized electronic invoice service platform (e-invoice system) and are transmitted in real time to the Golden Tax Phase IV anti-fraud and risk-control environment. Each e-invoice is protected by anti-counterfeit encryption and a unique invoice identification code; no separate paper fiscal stamp is affixed. The e-invoice data is automatically cross-usable with customs declarations and foreign-exchange receipt records for export refund verification.

The STA, Customs, and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange match the export declaration code, VAT e-invoice data, logistics documents, and foreign-exchange receipt records through the Golden Tax Phase IV system. If export zero-rating cannot be substantiated, the STA will disallow the 0% rate and assess VAT at 13% on the export turnover, claw back any input VAT already refunded, charge a daily late-payment surcharge of 0.05% on the underpaid tax, and impose an administrative penalty of between 50% and five times the tax involved. Fraudulent export-refund claims involving forged customs documents, false bills of lading, fabricated FIRC records, or circular foreign-remittance arrangements are subject to criminal prosecution under PRC Criminal Law, including fines and imprisonment, with life imprisonment possible for especially large fraudulent export tax refund amounts.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The RMB is convertible for current-account transactions, including trade in goods and services, provided each payment and settlement is supported by genuine underlying transaction documentation such as contracts, invoices, customs declarations, and transport logs. Capital-account transactions, by contrast, remain heavily regulated and require explicit SAFE registration and approval before a domestic bank may execute the cross-border transfer. A Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE) established in mainland China can receive inbound equity capital only through a dedicated foreign-exchange capital account after prior SAFE foreign-direct-investment (FDI) registration; the proceeds are subject to a negative list for business scope and cannot be freely converted into RMB for domestic loans, securities investment, or real estate speculation. Profit repatriation to offshore shareholders is lawful only after annual statutory audit and tax clearance, with the dividend remittance executed through a domestic bank using the approved current-account or capital-account purpose code. Prior approval is not generally required for dividends that satisfy all compliance conditions, but banks will apply their own reasonableness review. There is no blanket forced conversion of retained foreign-currency receipts; exporters may hold ongoing export proceeds in a foreign-currency current account or settle them into RMB at the bank's quoted exchange rate. However, all foreign-currency receipts must be repatriated into China through a licensed bank, and any conversion must be matched against customs data and trade contracts. This hybrid system of current-account convertibility and capital-account gatekeeping gives banks the central role in enforcing monetary policy, anti-money laundering rules, and balance-of-payments statistics.

Export proceeds surrender rules

China does not presently mandate a fixed mandatory surrender ratio for export proceeds. The surviving statutory duty is repatriation: a WFOE manufacturing mechanical keyboard keycaps for export must bring all sales proceeds earned from foreign customers back into mainland China without unnecessary delay and bank them through an approved foreign-currency current-account settlement or a RMB settlement mechanism. In practice, banks monitor export receipts against customs declarations and logistics evidence, flagging overdue receipts or significant mismatches. Export proceeds can be retained in foreign currency or voluntarily sold for RMB at a domestic designated bank. Converted RMB proceeds are immediately available for local operating expenses such as factory rent, PBT plastic resin purchases, injection-moulding maintenance, and salaries, subject to standard trade background verification.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC), with delegated foreign-exchange administration powers to the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Offshore fintech EMIs and digital-only cross-border banks are not viable as the primary treasury provider for a mainland China WFOE. Such entities generally do not hold a PBOC banking licence, cannot participate in the domestic CNAPS/CIPS clearing system, and are not permitted to open a compliant RMB settlement account for a Chinese legal entity. They may be used only as auxiliary collection or disbursement channels for e-commerce flows generated by Shopify and Stripe, provided they operate under a lawful cross-border payment service agreement with a licensed domestic institution. However, they cannot satisfy SAFE capital-account registration, import/export trade verification, or corporate statutory audit requirements, so all core treasury balances, tax payments, payroll, and FX settlements must remain in a domestic Tier-1 bank. Feasibility rating: Low as a replacement; Medium as an ancillary non-custodial connectivity layer.

Deadline: · Frequency:
