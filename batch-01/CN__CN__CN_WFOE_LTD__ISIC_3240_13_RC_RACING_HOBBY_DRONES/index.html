Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-up capital is imposed on an ordinary WFOE with this manufacturing scope under the PRC Company Law. The sole shareholder determines the registered capital in the Articles of Association, and each subscription amount must be fully contributed within five years of establishment as required by Article 47 of the 2023 Company Law. Inbound foreign currency capital remittances require a State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) foreign direct investment (FDI) registration through a domestic bank. No blocked escrow account is required by general national company law.

Funding & unlock path: Civil defense and occupational clearance requires Fire Protection Design Review and Completion Acceptance by the local Fire and Rescue Section, including functional smoke detectors, emergency lighting, clear egress routes and portable fire extinguishers. A structural load inspection and approval must be obtained for racking systems, mezzanines or any storage platform exceeding the design capacity. The workplace must pass occupational disease hazard filing, employee exposure monitoring for carbon-fiber dust and noise, and a work-safety three-simultaneous acceptance confirming that the project's safety facilities are designed, constructed and put into use simultaneously with the main production facility.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital is imposed on an ordinary WFOE with this manufacturing scope under the PRC Company Law. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; filings are made through the unified Electronic Tax Administration (electronic tax bureau) and the official STA portal at chinatax.gov.cn.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: The WFOE is incorporated with the local Administration for Market Regulation (AMR), which issues a business license bearing the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC); the USCC is the sole tax identification number for all PRC tax purposes and no separate TIN certificate is needed. Step 2: The AMR automatically shares the registration record with the local STA office; within 30 days of business license issuance (or before issuing an invoice) the WFOE must complete tax registration/payer verification in the Electronic Tax Administration, set up general/small-scale VAT taxpayer status, and activate electronic invoice issuance. Step 3: Because the entity exports goods, the WFOE files an export tax rebate registration in the electronic tax administration and links its tax number to the China Customs Single Window and SAFE trade monitoring records. Step 4: A foreign non-resident enterprise without an establishment in China that receives China-source income from this WFOE (e.g., dividends, interest, royalty or service fees) does not obtain a VAT registration; it completes a withholding registration with the STA at the payer's location and receives a non-resident enterprise tax identification number through that withholding registration, where applicable.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation - the WFOE is a Chinese resident limited liability company taxed at entity level under Corporate Income Tax at the headline 25% rate on worldwide income; no pass-through treatment is available for the foreign investor.

Available election forms

None for entity classification - China does not have check-the-box or hybrid mismatch elections. The only status election relevant to this structure is VAT taxpayer status (small-scale taxpayer vs general VAT taxpayer), which is an indirect tax administrative election rather than an entity classification election. Intra-group restructuring may, if all statutory conditions are met, elect special tax treatment (tax deferral) under the corporate income tax reorganization rules.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Chinese renminbi is convertible for current account transactions under Article VIII of the IMF Articles, and a WFOE engaged in FPV drone manufacturing can freely conduct trade payments and export repatriation through banks that hold SAFE trade-related FX licences. However, capital account convertibility remains strictly controlled. FDI capital injections require SAFE foreign exchange registration, must enter a designated FDI capital account at a domestic bank, and can only be settled into RMB for genuine business purposes with documented underlying transactions. The Foreign Investment Negative List must be checked, although carbon-fiber milling and brushless motor integration for racing hobby drones are not restricted. Profit remittances are permissible but require annual audited financial statements, corporate income tax settlement, appropriate withholding on dividends, and a bank-confirmed tax clearance certificate before funds can be converted and remitted abroad. Funds held in a capital account cannot be used for domestic equity investment, loans, real estate speculation, or portfolio investment without further regulatory approval. External borrowing by the WFOE above certain thresholds would require NDRC/SAFE filing and a separate foreign debt quota. In practice, all major commercial banks operate under a real-demand principle and verify economic substance, tax compliance, and beneficial ownership before facilitating cross-border flows, making capital account operation lawful but procedural and document-intensive.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no mandatory conversion ratio for export proceeds in China. Under the SAFE rules on goods trade foreign exchange administration, all export receipts from FPV drone sales must be repatriated into a domestic goods-trade foreign exchange current account within 180 days after the date of customs declaration, unless SAFE approves an extension for genuine commercial reasons. The statutory mandatory conversion ratio is effectively 0%; the WFOE may retain the foreign currency, use it for current account obligations, or voluntarily convert it into RMB at the prevailing market rate. However, all retained balances and conversions must be tracked in the SAFE digital system and supported by customs declarations, commercial invoices, transport documents, and e-commerce settlement records.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) acting through the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Low regulatory viability as a primary treasury banking rail, but conditional viability as a sales collection channel. Offshore digital EMIs such as Airwallex, Currenxie, or similar platforms cannot replace a licensed domestic RMB basic deposit account for a China-incorporated WFOE. Stripe does not offer direct merchant acquiring in mainland China to domestic-incorporated entities; a China WFOE would normally use a Hong Kong or other offshore entity to access Stripe, which adds cross-border royalty/service fee requirements. PayPal China is a licensed cross-border payment institution and can settle merchant receipts into a WFOE bank account, but the WFOE must still maintain a domestic bank account and comply with SAFE trade monitoring and AML obligations. EMIs are not permitted to hold treasury funds or serve as the statutory bank for tax, VAT, payroll, or FX verification purposes. Therefore the blueprint positions EMIs solely as receivable aggregation channels, never as the official corporate banking book.

Mandatory Audit Threshold Mandatory Audit for All Entities

Turnover: Under PRC Company Law and relevant wholly foreign-owned enterprise regulations, every WFOE with limited liability is statutorily required to have its annual financial accounting report audited by a certified public accountant licensed in the PRC. There is no turnover, asset, or headcount exemption available to a WFOE as a foreign-invested enterprise; all WFOEs must undergo annual audit as a condition of operating lawfully in China. For classification and accounting framework purposes, a WFOE qualifying as a small enterprise under PRC SME criteria (annual operating revenue not exceeding RMB 30 million, total assets not exceeding RMB 50 million, and not more than 300 employees) may apply the Simplified Accounting Standards for Small Enterprises, but this does not remove the mandatory audit obligation. A WFOE exceeding any of those quantitative thresholds must apply the full PRC Accounting Standards for Business Enterprises, and enterprises that are public offering issuers, financial institutions, insurance companies, or members of a consolidated foreign listed group are subject to mandatory appointment of reputable CPA firms, stronger independence rules, and regulatory audit review requirements.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% standard Enterprise Income Tax (CIT) rate for resident enterprises; a 15% preferential CIT rate applies to qualified High and New Technology Enterprises (HNTE) and to certain encouraged zone/project enterprises.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% statutory CIT withholding on dividends paid by the WFOE to a non-resident enterprise under EIT Law Articles 3 and 37; the PRC-Hong Kong DTA may reduce this to 5% where the Hong Kong beneficial owner holds at least 25% of the WFOE capital and satisfies substance/beneficial-ownership conditions.

Annual return and final tax payment must be filed within 5 months after the fiscal year-end (for calendar-year entities no later than May 31 of the following year); quarterly provisional prepayments are due within 15 days after each quarter-end.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: RMB 200,000,000 aggregate annual related-party tangible-property transactions; RMB 100,000,000 for intangible-property or financial-asset transactions; RMB 40,000,000 for other related-party transactions; Master File/Country-by-Country reporting applies where the ultimate parent group has consolidated revenue exceeding RMB 5,500,000,000, all in accordance with SAT Public Notice [2016] No. 42 and subsequent amendments.

Economic substance scope: The PRC does not operate a UAE-style Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) statute, but the tax administration applies a strong substance-over-form doctrine through EIT Law Article 47 (General Anti-Avoidance Rule), beneficial-ownership requirements, and the Principal Purpose Test under tax treaties. A WFOE must demonstrate real Core Income-Generating Activities (CIGA) in China, including strategic decision-making, operational management, key personnel, physical assets, inventory risk, and R&D execution. Letter-box entities, shell shareholders, and intermediary holding companies without genuine business functions are disregarded; treaty benefits are denied and profits may be reallocated to the jurisdiction where the substance actually resides.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Revenue Suppression - Cross-Border Sales Diverted to Director Accounts

Statutory sanction: Article 63 of the Tax Administration and Collection Law requires payment of the underpaid tax plus daily late-payment interest and imposes a monetary penalty of 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax; Criminal Law Article 201 provides for imprisonment up to 3 years where the evaded amount is relatively large and up to 7 years where the amount is huge, with fines and forfeiture; Article 205 on false VAT invoices carries imprisonment up to life imprisonment in especially serious cases.

Director personal liability: Courts pierce the corporate veil under Article 20 (revised as Article 23 in the 2023 PRC Company Law) where the director commingles WFOE funds with personal assets or uses the company as an instrument of fraud, making the director jointly and severally liable for the WFOE's tax liabilities, penalties, and surcharges. The director as legal representative is placed on the STA joint dishonest taxpayer blacklist, triggering an exit-bar/travel-ban preventing departure from China, restrictions on aircraft and high-speed rail travel, and disqualification from acting as a company director; criminal conviction also attaches personal imprisonment.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation and Failure to Maintain Contemporaneous Documentation

Statutory sanction: Under EIT Law Articles 41, 44, and 48, the tax authority may make a primary transfer-pricing adjustment to the arm's-length amount, impose special-interest at the benchmark loan rate plus 5 percentage points, and require the WFOE to pay the resulting additional CIT; Article 63 of the Tax Administration and Collection Law adds a 50%-500% penalty if the transfer-pricing misstatement is intentional or fraudulent; failure to submit related-party reporting or contemporaneous documents attracts administrative fines under Chapter 6 of the Tax Collection Law and an extended inspection horizon.

Director personal liability: A director who signs false transfer-pricing certificates, knowingly approves round-tripping arrangements, or commingles the shifted profit with personal assets will have the corporate veil pierced and be held personally liable for the WFOE's adjusted tax debt, interest, and penalties. The director is also exposed to a travel ban and blacklisting through the joint punishment mechanism, and, if the scheme involved fraudulent concealment or forged contracts, criminal liability under Articles 201, 204, or 205 of the Criminal Law, resulting in imprisonment and fines.
Shell Company Structure and Treaty-Shopping Abuse - Non-Resident Beneficial Ownership Failure

Statutory sanction: The tax authority may invoke the General Anti-Avoidance Rule under EIT Law Article 47, disregard the intermediary structure, deny the treaty benefit, recharacterize the transactions, and assess the full 10% statutory withholding tax plus interest and penalties; under SAT Circular 35/2015 and the Principal Purpose Test, the beneficial-owner claim is rejected and the WFOE is treated as a non-compliant withholding agent; Article 63 of the Tax Administration and Collection Law permits a penalty of 50% to 500% of the under-withheld tax, and Articles 54-56 authorize tax authority reconstruction of income over the extended statute of limitations for concealment.

Director personal liability: Directors who designed or implemented the treaty-shopping shell structure face piercing of the corporate veil under PRC Company Law because the separate legal personality is abused to obtain unlawful tax benefits; they are jointly and severally liable for the additional tax, interest, and penalties. The legal representative is blacklisted by Chinese tax authorities, restricted from exiting China, and barred from serving as a legal representative of other enterprises; where false documents and sham contracts were knowingly used, directors may be criminally prosecuted for fraud or tax evasion, resulting in personal fines, confiscation, and imprisonment terms of up to seven years.
