Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

For a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise applying for a Class III Medical Device Manufacturing License under ISIC 3250 (coronary drug-eluting stents), a virtual office, commercial mailbox, or shared-service registered address is categorically rejected by the National Medical Products Administration (NMPA) and local municipal market supervision authorities. The WFOE must maintain a physical registered office with demonstrable operational presence, including a dedicated production facility, warehousing area, quality-control laboratory, and accessible administrative premises. The local tax authority (State Taxation Administration) will also treat a virtual address as a red flag for shell-company classification, triggering mandatory on-site assessment and denial of tax registration. Additionally, most commercial banks in China reject virtual-office addresses for opening a standard corporate settlement account and require the bank officer to conduct a physical visit to the registered office as part of the know-your-client (KYC) procedure for the medical-device sector. Therefore, the company must execute an unconditional lease agreement for a physical office and factory space for a minimum term of one year, with the lease notarized and registered with the local housing management authority, and the registered address must match the actual place of business.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA) – electronic portal: Hubei/Nationwide e-Tax Bureau (电子税务局; official English: 'e-Tax Bureau' or 'Online Tax Service Platform'), accessible via https://etax.chinatax.gov.cn for the national unified entry; local STA branch in the registered place of the WFOE handles TIN issuance.

E-government filing portal

A foreign non-resident entity establishing a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE) must first obtain a business license from the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR), which generates a Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) of 18 alphanumeric characters that serves as the primary Tax Identification Number (TIN) for all tax purposes. After incorporation, the WFOE must complete tax registration at the local competent tax authority within 30 days of the date of the business license, by submitting the SAMR business license, the Foreign Investor's Identity Certificate (notarized and apostilled or legalized as applicable), the Articles of Association, the Lease/Rental Agreement for the registered office, the Legal Representative's identity documents, and the completed Tax Registration Form. The local STA office will verify the USCC and issue the taxpayer status confirmation; no separate TIN certificate is issued because the USCC is the TIN. For a foreign non-resident enterprise without a permanent establishment that needs to pay VAT on imported services or withholding tax, the foreign entity may register directly through 'tax identification number assignment for non-resident enterprises' under the STA's non-resident taxpayer registration procedure, which issues a 'Taxpayer Identification Number for Non-Resident Enterprise' (non-resident TIN) in the format of the country code plus a registration number, and must be applied through the withholding agent or directly at the competent tax authority where the withholding obligation arises. The WFOE must also complete VAT general taxpayer registration if annual sales exceed RMB 5 million, or voluntarily as a small-scale taxpayer, and obtain a VAT registration profile with access to the VAT invoice system.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

China's enterprise income tax law does not provide a check-the-box pass-through election for a WFOE limited liability company; the WFOE is a resident enterprise subject to entity-level taxation on worldwide income at 25% standard rate. However, for indirect tax, a small-scale taxpayer may elect to be treated as a general VAT taxpayer once annual taxable turnover is below RMB 5 million, subject to STA approval. No hybrid mismatch elections are available under domestic law; the STA applies specific anti-avoidance rules under Circular 698 and general anti-avoidance principles. The WFOE may elect the 'deductible input VAT method' via general taxpayer status; export sales are zero-rated, and input VAT allocation is apportioned per period. No other statutory tax classification elections exist, unless the entity qualifies as a foreign-invested R&D center under specific preferential policies; otherwise, state 'None' for hybrid mismatch elections and note only the small-scale-to-general VAT taxpayer election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory VAT registration for general taxpayer status is an annual taxable sales turnover threshold of RMB 5,000,000 (five million Renminbi) for entities; below this threshold, the entity is classified as a 'small-scale taxpayer' (with a reduced VAT rate of 1% currently, or 3% standard) and may voluntarily apply for general taxpayer status. For non-resident enterprises supplying taxable services or goods within China, there is no turnover threshold for VAT registration; they must appoint a withholding agent or self-register with the STA for each VAT obligation.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under Article 22 of the VAT Law and the State Council's VAT export rebate regulations, goods exported from China are zero-rated (0% VAT) for registered general VAT taxpayers, provided the goods are actually shipped out of China's customs territory and the sales proceeds are received from an overseas buyer in foreign currency (with exceptions for RMB settlement under approved offshore trade schemes). The WFOE manufacturing Class III medical devices (coronary drug-eluting stents) must hold an export license (if required), be registered with China Customs (Customs Registration Number / 海关进出口货物收发货人备案), and declare the goods under 'General Trade' or 'Processing with Imported Materials' regime. Zero-rating also applies to qualifying cross-border digital services and offshore services provided to overseas entities, but for physical medical device exports, the primary criterion is physical export evidenced by a customs export declaration. The entity must be a VAT general taxpayer; small-scale taxpayers exporting goods may only obtain a VAT exemption, not zero-rating with credit/refund, unless they elect general taxpayer status. The export must be completed within the statutory period; documents must evidence delivery outside China (CFR/CIF/FOB incoterms acceptable) and payment from an overseas account.

  • Customs Export Declaration (出口货物报关单) – officially the 'Export Customs Declaration Form' generated by China Customs 'Single Window' system, bearing the twenty-digit customs declaration number and the new Customs Export Bill of Entry serial number
  • Commercial Invoice (商业发票) issued by the WFOE to the overseas buyer
  • Packing List (装箱单) matching the customs declaration
  • Bill of Lading (提单) or Air Waybill (空运单) evidencing physical shipment out of China, issued by the international carrier
  • Letter of Credit (信用证) or Swift wire transfer confirmation, or bank's Foreign Exchange Receipt Confirmation (出口收汇核销单 / Bank's Foreign Exchange Settlement Receipt) – specifically the 'Inward Remittance Certificate' or 'Export Collection Receipt' from the Chinese bank showing repatriation of the foreign currency
  • Export Contract (出口合同) between the WFOE and the overseas buyer, stating incoterms and payment terms
  • Inspection Certificate (出境货物检验检疫证书) for Class III medical devices issued by China Customs (if required under the medical device export regulations) and the Medical Device Export Sales Certificate (医疗器械出口销售证明) from the National Medical Products Administration (NMPA)
  • Customs declaration for export of medical devices under HS code 9021 or 9018 – the exact HS code for drug-eluting coronary stents is 9021.39 or 9021.90; the WFOE must classify precisely to match the export rebate rate
  • China Customs Export Consignment Declaration (运抵报告) and the 'Export Goods Verification and Rebate Declaration' (出口货物退(免)税申报表) submitted through the e-Tax Bureau export rebate module

China mandates the use of the nationwide 'Electronic Invoice Service System' (电子发票服务平台) under the fully digitalized electronic invoice (全面数字化电子发票, referred to as 'Digital Invoices' or 'Ce Hu Piao') reform. Registered general VAT taxpayers must issue invoices through the STA-approved electronic invoice platform using the enterprise's biometric tax control device or the cloud-based e-invoice system. Real-time fiscal clearance is achieved via the STA's 'Golden Tax Phase IV' (金税四期) system, which automatically cross-validates each invoice against transaction data, inventory, logistics, and bank settlement details. The system applies an official anti-fraud digital stamping mechanism: every digital invoice is assigned a unique e-invoice number and an encrypted QR code, verified by the STA's central clearinghouse. There is a mandatory requirement to upload invoice data in near real-time, including to the special VAT invoice verification platform (增值税发票综合服务平台) for input credit reconciliation. For export transactions, the export declaration data is matched with the VAT invoice and export rebate (exemption/zero-rating) system; no paper invoice is required for export unless the customer requests a commercial invoice for customs purposes.

Export zero-rating claims are subject to '国家税务总局出口退税' (Export Tax Rebate) management under the 'Administrative Measures for the Exemption, Credit, and Refund of VAT on Exported Goods' (国家税务总局公告). The competent tax authority may conduct a post-shipment audit 'to inspect the authenticity and consistency of the export declaration, logistics, payment, and invoice data. Penalties for fraudulent or unverified export zero-rating include: (1) denial and clawback of the VAT refund/exemption claimed, with recovery of the difference between the zero rate and the standard 13% VAT rate; (2) late payment interest from the date of refund issuance; (3) an administrative fine of 50% to 5 times the tax evaded under the PRC Tax Collection Administration Law; (4) 5-year suspension or revocation of the VAT general taxpayer export rebate qualification; (5) inclusion in the tax credit blacklist (D-rating) with severe consequences; (6) criminal liability for smuggling or export fraud under Criminal Law Articles 204 (fraudulent export tax refund) and 201-203, with prison sentences up to life if the fraudulent amount is extremely large. The statute of limitations for tax clawbacks is generally 3 years for under-reporting due to negligence, and up to 10 years or unlimited for fraud or wilful evasion. In addition, if customs documents are found to be forged or manipulated, China Customs may impose separate penalties under the Customs Law, and the NMPA may revoke the medical device export sales certificate, halting all future export eligibility.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a managed floating RMB system with clearly separated current-account and capital-account convertibility rules. Current-account transactions, including trade in goods, trade in services and after-tax dividend remittances, are broadly convertible at onshore market rates after documentary and tax checks. Capital-account transactions such as foreign direct investment injections, shareholder loans, cross-border financing and portfolio flows remain highly regulated and must be routed through SAFE registration and designated commercial banks. A Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE) in the Class III medical device sector may receive foreign-currency capital through a SAFE-registered Foreign Exchange Capital Account, convert those funds to RMB only for approved operating needs, and remit profits abroad only after the annual audit, corporate income tax settlement, capital verification, and bank-level verification of prior FDI registration are completed. In practice, the foreign parent must allow extra lead time for SAFE post-customs monitoring, tax clearance certificates and bank compliance reviews. Although there is no blanket mandatory surrender ratio for export proceeds, all export receipts must be repatriated to China within the statutory trade monitoring cycle and reconciled through SAFE's electronic system. Treasury planning must therefore integrate monthly tax filing, transaction-purpose screening, conversion buffers and clear documentation trails for every cross-border flow.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export proceeds from the WFOE's trade in goods must be repatriated to mainland China within 90 days of the export declaration date and verified under SAFE's trade-in-goods monitoring framework. There is no fixed mandatory conversion ratio for ordinary current-account export receipts; the exporter may retain foreign currency in a domestic foreign-exchange settlement account or sell it to a designated bank at market rates. SAFE may mandate enhanced settlement review for high-risk, mispriced or sanctions-adjacent transactions, and all proceeds must be traceable to customs declarations, invoices and shipping documentation.

Central bank supervision: The People's Bank of China (PBOC) as the central monetary authority, with delegated foreign-exchange administration powers to the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low as a primary banking layer. Offshore fintech EMIs such as digital challenger banks and foreign e-money institutions are not licensed as PRC commercial banks, cannot access CNAPS/CIPS membership rails, cannot hold corporate RMB deposits, cannot open a domestic basic settlement account and cannot accept SAFE-registered FDI capital. They may only be used as ancillary payment tools for lower-value, fully operated cross-border disbursements through licensed local payment partners, but they are legally barred from servicing the regulated capital, tax, customs and FX liquidity needs of a WFOE. Inbound registered capital must be sourced only from the direct foreign investor appearing in the SAFE FDI registration. The parent treasury wires funds via SWIFT MT103 to the WFOE's designated Foreign Exchange Capital Account at a Tier-1 Chinese bank, referencing the SAFE FDI registration number and the approved transaction purpose code for foreign direct investment capital injection. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File (contemporaneous documentation) must be prepared where annual related-party transactions exceed RMB 100 million for tangible property, intangible property, or financial-asset transfers, or RMB 40 million for related-party service transactions; Master File must be prepared where aggregate annual related-party transactions exceed RMB 1 billion (STA Announcement 2016 No. 42).

Economic substance scope: China applies an overarching substance-over-form GAAR under CIT Law Article 47 and the Special Tax Adjustment rules: arrangements with no bona fide commercial purpose may be recharacterised, with interest surcharged at the benchmark rate plus 5 percentage points (Article 48). For medical-device manufacturing, the Economic Substance Regulation-style analysis scrutinises whether Core Income-Generating Activities (CIGA) — raw polymer compounding, laser micro-machining, sterility validation, regulatory master-file ownership, and stent clinical or regulatory risk retention — are genuinely performed in mainland China by the WFOE. A WFOE that outsources all CIGA to foreign affiliates fails the enterprise-level substance standard, risking recharacterisation of reported profits, denial of treaty benefits, and a 10-year extension of the special tax investigation limitation period.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Corporate Income Tax Return

Statutory sanction: Tax Collection Administration Law Article 63: recovery of the underpaid tax, daily late-payment interest at 0.05%, and an administrative fine of 50%-500% of the evaded tax; Article 64: 50%-300% penalty for inaccurate understatement. Where the evaded amount exceeds RMB 50,000 and represents more than 10% of the tax payable, PRC Criminal Law Article 201 imposes up to 7 years' imprisonment plus a fine; Article 211 extends criminal liability to the unit's directly responsible managers.

Director personal liability: Piercing the corporate veil: courts may hold the legal representative and directors personally liable for civil restitution of the underpaid tax and fines where corporate assets are stripped, transferred abroad, or insufficient; the Dishonest Taxpayer List imposes exit and travel bans, asset freezes, and director disqualification; the legal representative and directly responsible officers face criminal detention or fixed-term imprisonment of up to 7 years under Criminal Law Articles 201 and 211.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation / Cross-Border Profit Stripping

Statutory sanction: CIT Law Article 41 (arm's-length principle), Article 46 (thin capitalisation), and Article 48 (special tax adjustment interest: RMB benchmark lending rate plus 5 percentage points on the adjusted amount); STA Announcement 2016 No. 42 defines documentation penalties; failure to provide documentation can trigger recharacterisation and a 10-year retroactive adjustment period. Falsified TP documentation raising evasion intent exposes the company to TCAL Article 63 penalties of 50%-500% of the adjusted tax.

Director personal liability: Where the WFOE is stripped of assets or the adjusted tax cannot be collected, directors who signed the false TP master file or approved intercompany prices face personal civil liability for the adjusted tax under PRC Company Law Articles 149 and 152; travel bans and entry restrictions are imposed on the legal representative through the tax dishonesty blacklisting mechanism; criminal prosecution for intentional tax evasion under Criminal Law Article 201 may attach to the finance director and legal representative, with imprisonment of 3-7 years if the evasion scale is huge.
Shell Company / DTA Relief Abuse (Beneficial Ownership Failure)

Statutory sanction: CIT Law Article 47 (GAAR recharacterisation), Article 48 (interest with 5-percentage-point uplift), and STA Announcement 2018 No. 9: denial of treaty benefits and exclusion of the intermediate from treaty protection; the WFOE must then withhold the full statutory 10% plus late-payment interest; the circular transaction may be annulled and the underlying tax evasion prosecuted under Criminal Law Article 201; financial penalties of 50%-500% of the under-withheld tax apply under TCAL Article 63.

Director personal liability: Directors who executed the shell-company chain and signed the beneficial-ownership declarations are exposed to civil restitution to the tax authority, blacklisting and exit bans alongside the corporate debtor, and criminal liability for aiding tax evasion — imprisonment up to 7 years plus asset confiscation under Criminal Law Articles 25-27 (joint crime) and 201; if funds were laundered through cross-border payments to conceal the conduit conduct, AML liability under Criminal Law Article 191 imposes up to 10 years' imprisonment for money laundering.
