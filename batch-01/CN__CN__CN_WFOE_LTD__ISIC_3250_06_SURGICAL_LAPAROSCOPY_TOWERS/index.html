Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum registered capital is imposed on a wholly foreign-owned enterprise limited liability company under current PRC law. The registered capital stated in the Articles of Association must be validly subscribed by the sole foreign shareholder and fully contributed within five years from issuance of the business license as required by the Company Law. No mandatory escrow account, blocked account, or compulsory capital verification is required at the formation stage. However, foreign-currency capital remittances must be received through a SAFE-regulated foreign exchange capital account after incorporation, and released funds must be applied solely to lawful operating purposes consistent with the approved business scope of the WFOE.

Funding & unlock path: The manufacturing site must obtain fire-protection design review and fire-protection completion acceptance or record from the local fire rescue authority, including Civil Defense fire permits where the premises are classified as major fire-risk industrial use; pass structural load certification for heavy laparoscopy tower assembly racks and warehouse storage; complete occupational hazard assessment and workplace safety inspection under the Work Safety Law; install fire alarms, hydrants, emergency lighting, smoke control, and explosion-proof electrical equipment where required; register special equipment such as air compressors, sterilizers, pressure vessels, and electrical installations with the local market regulator; and maintain cleanroom or controlled-environment validation records required by medical device quality management practice.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; online portal: Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax) at etax.chinatax.gov.cn

E-government filing portal

For a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE), the statutory tax identifier is the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which is issued on the business license by the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) upon incorporation. The WFOE must file tax registration with the local STA office through the Electronic Tax Bureau within 30 days of obtaining the business license, authenticate the legal representative's identity, confirm the VAT taxpayer status classification, and register for enterprise income tax, payroll withholding, stamp duty, and urban maintenance and construction tax. A foreign non-resident parent company without a Chinese permanent establishment does not require a separate TIN for ordinary export manufacturing operations; if the parent derives China-source income or establishes a PE, it must register for tax through the non-resident withholding registration procedure and obtain a taxpayer identification code from the STA.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None for entity classification: China does not operate a check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election under Corporate Income Tax; a WFOE is mandatorily taxed at entity level as a Chinese tax resident enterprise. Indirect tax elections are limited to VAT registration status: an entity below the general VAT taxpayer threshold may elect general VAT taxpayer status to claim input VAT credits, while small-scale taxpayers may elect simplified 3% levy collection on taxable sales.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

All WFOEs must complete tax registration upon establishment. For VAT general taxpayer status, mandatory registration is triggered when annual VAT taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000; entities below that threshold may register as small-scale taxpayers. A monthly sales exemption of up to RMB 100,000 applies to small-scale taxpayers under current national policy.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exported goods manufactured by the WFOE qualify for zero-rate VAT (0%) with input VAT refund if they physically leave Chinese customs territory under a formal customs export declaration, are sold under a genuine export contract to an overseas buyer, and are not consumed or resold within China. The exporter must subsequently collect and repatriate the export proceeds through a designated bank in a convertible foreign currency within the statutory period and submit an export VAT refund declaration through the STA electronic tax system.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Customs Goods Declaration / Export Manifest) issued via the China International Trade Single Window
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export shipment
  • Commercial Invoice
  • Packing List
  • Export Contract and Purchase Order
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or bank export-proceeds receipt confirming foreign exchange repatriation and conversion
  • Export VAT refund declaration and matching VAT tax invoice/export invoice
  • Logistics records and customs release/road-crossing evidence from the Single Window

VAT general taxpayers must issue tax invoices exclusively through STA-approved electronic invoice systems. The STA has implemented fully digitalized electronic invoices on the national E-Invoice Service Platform, requiring real-time online issuance and transmission of invoice data from the taxpayer's U-Key or digital identity to the STA centralized system. Each invoice receives a unique invoice number and an electronic signature/verification code generated by the tax authority, enabling anti-fraud digital stamping and immediate automated cross-checking against declaration returns, customs export data, and bank foreign-exchange receipts under the Golden Tax System IV.

Claiming 0% VAT export treatment without complete supporting documentation subjects the exporter to disallowance of the refund, retrospective clawback of any VAT refund paid, recovery of unpaid VAT, and imposition of late-payment surcharge and fines. Under the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection and the Criminal Law, fraudulent export VAT refunds are punishable by recovery of the refunded tax, a penalty of 50% to 500% of the evaded tax, and criminal liability including imprisonment for up to life sentence for particularly large fraud schemes. The STA, Customs, Public Security, and State Administration of Foreign Exchange jointly monitor export refund claims, and discovered misconduct may result in blacklisting, suspension of export refund qualification, revocation of Customs AEO status, and enhanced retrospective audits.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Under the Foreign Exchange Administration Regulations and SAFE rules, the RMB is fully convertible on current-account items for a WFOE, but capital-account transactions are subject to registration and verified-bank administration. The WFOE can receive export proceeds from overseas customers in USD, EUR, or other trade currencies without a forced surrender ratio; these funds must be repatriated into a domestic designated foreign-exchange account and reported through the SAFE goods-trade monitoring system. After arrival, the company may retain the foreign currency or voluntarily convert it to RMB at the bank spot rate. The more demanding layer is the capital account: foreign direct investment registered capital, external debt, cross-border guarantees, and offshore treasury arrangements each require an FDI foreign-exchange registration, a specific capital-project account, and a verified business rationale before the receiving bank clears the funds. Profit remittance is treated as current-account investment income once the annual after-tax profit has been audited and all applicable corporate income tax, withholding tax for non-resident shareholders, and other statutory taxes have been settled. In practice, the main hurdles are not conversion ceilings but documentation: a certified capital-verification report, current SAFE registration or amendment form, audited annual financial statements, a board resolution declaring the dividend, and a foreign-exchange payment application with the correct purpose code. Any capital-account funds converted into RMB must be used within the approved business scope; they cannot be diverted to entrusted loans, unapproved equity investment, prohibited real-estate purchases, or RMB wealth-management products.

Export proceeds surrender rules

SAFE imposes no mandatory conversion ratio for export proceeds; the formal surrender ratio is 0%. The WFOE must still repatriate goods-trade foreign-exchange proceeds to a domestic designated bank with a foreign-exchange business license. The bank records the expected collection date in the SAFE goods-trade monitoring system based on the underlying export contract and customs declaration. If the proceeds are not collected by the expected date, and especially if more than 90 days have passed since the customs declaration, the WFOE must file an overdue collection explanation and, where necessary, apply for an extension or adjustment to the trade-finance record before the bank can continue clearing the related current-account receipts. The exporter retains the right to keep the foreign currency in its goods-trade settlement account or voluntarily convert it to RMB at the bank spot rate. Every conversion is supported by the export transaction file and is reported to SAFE.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE), the foreign exchange administration arm of the People's Bank of China (PBOC).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

In mainland China, an offshore digital EMI or virtual-bank platform cannot legally hold the WFOE operating account, accept export collections, or provide the statutory clearing link to SAFE. PBOC and CBIRC restrict deposit-taking and payment services to licensed domestic institutions, and SAFE foreign-exchange reporting requires settlement through a domestic bank with a foreign-exchange business license. An offshore EMI may only be used by an overseas parent or a non-mainland treasury entity; for the Chinese WFOE, it is not a viable substitute for any capital-account, trade, or tax-clearing function. Feasibility rating is 0 out of 10 as a primary banking rail and 2 out of 10 only for incidental overseas expense cards outside the Chinese legal entity.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The overseas shareholder wires from its own bank through SWIFT MT103 to the WFOE FDI capital project foreign-exchange account at a domestic tier-1 bank. The beneficiary account must be the one recorded under the SAFE FDI foreign-exchange registration. The remittance instruction must carry the SAFE-recognized capital-account purpose code for foreign direct investment capital injection; a current-account goods-trade code must never be used. The receiving bank will require the business license, FDI registration form, board resolution, and investment agreement before crediting. After arrival, the bank allows voluntary settlement into RMB under the willing-settlement rule, but the converted RMB enters a monitored capital-project settlement account and must be used within the registered business scope. For capital verification, a mainland certified public accountant issues an audited capital-verification report comparing the received amount with the registered capital, the WFOE updates the SAFE FDI registration if the paid-in amount changes, and the bank files the transaction with the SAFE direct-investment information system. The WFOE must not use the capital funds for unapproved intercompany lending, entrusted loans, wealth-management purchases, or offshore investments without a separate SAFE approval.For imports of raw materials, precision components, and manufacturing equipment, the WFOE may use three compliant clearing rails. First, SWIFT MT103 telegraphic transfer from its goods-trade foreign-exchange settlement account for open-account payments, supported by the import contract, commercial invoice, customs declaration, and packing list. Second, an irrevocable commercial letter of credit issued by a tier-1 Chinese bank, usually through SWIFT MT700 with amendments via MT707, governed by UCP600, with sight or usance tenor matched to the shipping documents and payable against a compliant documentary presentation. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: In mainland China, an offshore digital EMI or virtual-bank platform cannot legally hold the WFOE operating account, accept export collections, or provide the statutory clearing link to SAFE. PBOC and CBIRC restrict deposit-taking and payment services to licensed domestic institutions, and SAFE foreign-exchange reporting requires settlement through a domestic bank with a foreign-exchange business license. An offshore EMI may only be used by an overseas parent or a non-mainland treasury entity; for the Chinese WFOE, it is not a viable substitute for any capital-account, trade, or tax-clearing function. Feasibility rating is 0 out of 10 as a primary banking rail and 2 out of 10 only for incidental overseas expense cards outside the Chinese legal entity.
