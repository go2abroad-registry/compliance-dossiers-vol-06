Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; online portal: STA Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax) platform, accessible via chinatax.gov.cn; for cross-border goods, competent authority also includes the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (GACC) for export declaration and export tax rebate (exemption) supervision.

E-government filing portal

The WFOE is a PRC resident enterprise, so its statutory tax identification number is the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) shown on the business license issued by the SAMR. Procedure: (1) complete name pre-approval and company incorporation with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR), obtaining the business license with the USCC; (2) within 30 days after business license issuance, log into the STA e-Tax platform to complete one-time tax registration information reporting, confirming taxpayer type, tax category, accounting basis, legal representative identity, and place of business; (3) the USCC is thereafter the taxpayer identification number for enterprise income tax, VAT, and other taxes; (4) if forecast annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000, apply for general VAT taxpayer status; if below, the WFOE may remain a small-scale VAT taxpayer; (5) complete export tax refund (exemption) registration with the STA and export customs registration with GACC; (6) open a foreign currency bank account and complete trade-in-goods forex registration with the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE). A foreign non-resident parent entity without a Chinese permanent establishment is not issued a separate Chinese USCC/TIN; the WFOE as withholding agent records the non-resident's home-country TIN and certificate of residence on the withholding return, and may apply for treaty relief on an entitlement basis.

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Annual taxable sales exceeding RMB 5,000,000 triggers mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration; below this threshold, the WFOE may be registered as a small-scale VAT taxpayer, with a temporary monthly sales exemption of RMB 100,000 for small-scale taxpayers.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods are eligible for the export VAT zero-rating treatment when they are physically exported outside the customs territory of the People's Republic of China, title passes to an overseas purchaser, the exporter completes a customs export declaration with GACC, the sale is made under commercial export terms, and the export proceeds are collected in foreign currency within the statutory foreign exchange repatriation deadline. The WFOE must be a registered VAT general taxpayer with export tax refund (exemption) status; the exported dialysis hemofilter products must be manufactured under a valid medical device production license and, where required, be covered by the applicable Chinese export registration or certification. Qualifying offshore digital services are not in scope; the zero-rating described applies to merchandise exports.

  • Customs Export Declaration (China Customs Goods Declaration; export Bill of Entry equivalent)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical exit from the PRC customs territory
  • Commercial invoice and export packing list
  • Sales contract or purchase order with the overseas buyer
  • Export proceeds collection confirmation issued by a designated SAFE bank (functional equivalent of an Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC))
  • Export VAT invoice and export tax refund (exemption) declaration data files
  • Medical device export certification or Certificate of Free Sale where required by the importing jurisdiction

Under the Golden Tax Phase IV system, taxpayers must issue invoices through the STA's Fully Digitalized Electronic Invoice platform or through tax control equipment recognized by the STA. Invoices are transmitted to the STA in near real time, and the anti-counterfeit public inspection code and QR code form the digital fiscal verification mechanism; no separate physical fiscal stamp is required for fully digitalized e-invoices. For high-volume exporters, STA and GACC data matching, together with SAFE foreign exchange settlement data, forms the electronic clearance and anti-fraud loop; taxpayers with large invoicing volumes are expected to integrate ERP invoicing through certified e-invoice service providers using API connectivity.

If the STA, GACC, or SAFE determines that an export did not occur, the goods remained in China, the invoice was falsified, or the foreign currency proceeds were not repatriated within the required period, the claimed VAT zero-rating is reversed. The refunded input VAT is clawed back with late-payment interest assessed from the date of the refund, and the taxpayer may be subject to an administrative penalty of 50% to 500% of the tax underpaid or fraudulently obtained under PRC Tax Administration Law. In serious cases involving forged special VAT invoices, fictitious exports, or fraudulent export tax refund claims, criminal liability may arise under the PRC Criminal Law for false issuance of VAT invoices or export tax refund fraud; retrospective recovery for fraudulent claims is not subject to a fixed statute of limitations.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China maintains a managed floating exchange-rate regime with the RMB considered convertible on the current account for trade, services, wages, and ordinary profit remittances, while capital account transactions remain subject to a permission-based framework administered by SAFE. For a WFOE manufacturing disposable dialysis hemofilters, ordinary trade-related foreign exchange earnings may be retained in a domestic current account foreign exchange account or sold to a licensed bank at market rates without mandatory surrender. However, every cross-border settlement must be routed through a designated bank that records the underlying transaction and performs electronic matching against customs and tax verification systems. Profit remittance to a foreign parent is permitted only after completion of the annual statutory audit, filing of corporate income tax returns, withholding of dividend tax, and submission of the board distribution resolution and audited balance sheet to the paying bank. Capital account flows, including foreign direct investment share capital, foreign debt, equity transfers, and exit sale proceeds, require SAFE registration or approval, a separate capital account, and use of purpose-specific payment codes. Practical convertibility hurdles include prolonged documentation checks by the bank, restrictions on converted RMB usage, sanctions and negative-news screening on the entire ownership chain, and the requirement to match each remittance against a genuine commercial or investment rationale.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Statutory surrender is effectively 0%; there is no mandatory conversion ratio, so the WFOE may retain 100% of export proceeds in foreign currency or convert them to RMB at the onshore spot rate through a licensed bank. However, all export proceeds from dialysis hemofilter shipments must be repatriated to a domestic foreign exchange current account and verified through SAFE's goods trade monitoring system, generally within a 180-day collection and verification window from customs export declaration, unless SAFE approves an extension for documented exceptional circumstances.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) acting through the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low and generally not legally viable as the primary treasury rail for a Chinese WFOE. Foreign fintech EMIs cannot open a CNAPS-based RMB account onshore, cannot complete SAFE FDI registration or capital account filing, cannot issue commercial LCs under PRC banking law, and are not permitted to provide settlement and sales foreign exchange services to onshore corporate entities without a domestic banking license. An offshore EMI may only function as an ancillary collection tool in the name of the overseas parent, aggregating third-party receivables before making a single MT103 transfer to the WFOE's onshore Tier-1 bank account. The core operating account, capital account, FX derivative line, and trade finance facility must sit with a licensed PRC domestic bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The foreign parent must first complete SAFE foreign exchange establishment and FDI registration at the local SAFE branch through a designated Tier-1 bank, then open an FDI exchange settlement account or RMB FDI settlement account in the WFOE's name. The remittance should be instructed from the offshore parent's bank to the Chinese bank via SWIFT MT103, with the beneficiary field showing the WFOE and the remittance information field referencing the SAFE-approved capital contribution purpose code. After funds are received, the Chinese bank performs receipt verification and the WFOE engages a PRC certified public accountant to issue a capital verification report. Converted RMB from foreign capital must be held in a separate settlement account and used only for the WFOE's registered business scope, domestic salaries, suppliers, and operating lease expenditures; it cannot be used for entrusted loans, unauthorized financial investment, or real estate purchases unrelated to production.For import of raw materials such as polyethersulfone resin, membrane-forming solvents, and production machinery, the WFOE should use SWIFT MT103 for approved advance or balance payments, but must attach the import contract, commercial invoice, customs declaration, and transport documents for SAFE verification. For large or risk-sensitive trade flows, irrevocable commercial letters of credit and documentary collections (D/P or D/A) are preferred because the domestic bank can negotiate, confirm, and match documents against customs records. For export sales of disposable dialysis hemofilters, the clearing rail is normally an LC advised through Bank of China or ICBC, or a collection with full-set shipping documents; all export proceeds must be credited to the onshore export receivable account and matched with the SAFE goods trade monitoring system.Onshore CNY/USD and CNY/EUR deliverable forward contracts with a licensed domestic bank, available only when supported by verified current account trade exposure or a registered capital account exposure.Onshore RMB/USD cross-currency swaps for long-term hedging of foreign-currency liabilities or FDI-related cash-flow structures, subject to SAFE registration and the approval framework for the underlying transaction.Offshore non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) on CNH are not directly permissible for the onshore WFOE as a contract party; only the non-resident parent or offshore treasury may execute CNH NDFs, while the WFOE must use deliverable onshore forwards and swaps for PRC regulatory compliance.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low and generally not legally viable as the primary treasury rail for a Chinese WFOE. Foreign fintech EMIs cannot open a CNAPS-based RMB account onshore, cannot complete SAFE FDI registration or capital account filing, cannot issue commercial LCs under PRC banking law, and are not permitted to provide settlement and sales foreign exchange services to onshore corporate entities without a domestic banking license. An offshore EMI may only function as an ancillary collection tool in the name of the overseas parent, aggregating third-party receivables before making a single MT103 transfer to the WFOE's onshore Tier-1 bank account. The core operating account, capital account, FX derivative line, and trade finance facility must sit with a licensed PRC domestic bank.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: RMB 100 million of annual related-party transactions for tangible assets, intangible assets, financial services, or interest; RMB 40 million for other related-party transactions, per SAT Announcement 2016 No. 42. Related-party transactions below these thresholds still require the Annual Related Party Transaction Report; a Master File may be required when the ultimate parent group exceeds the consolidated revenue thresholds in SAT Announcement 2016 No. 42.

Economic substance scope: China applies substance-over-form and General Anti-Avoidance Rules under CIT Law Article 47 and Implementation Regulations Article 120. A WFOE manufacturing PES dialysis hemofilters must demonstrate Core Income-Generating Activities in China: physical factory premises, controlled hollow-fiber extrusion, potting, sterilization, quality release, inventory risk, purchasing decisions, supplier approval, and product-liability risk. If the WFOE is a mere contract processor without real decision-making capacity, tax authorities may reattribute profit to the foreign principal, deny treaty benefits to dividend recipients, and impose transfer pricing penalties plus 50%-500% of underpaid tax for concealment.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Fraudulent corporate income tax filing and VAT invoice fraud

Statutory sanction: TCAL Article 63 imposes a penalty of 50%-500% of the unpaid tax plus a daily 0.05% late-payment surcharge; VAT invoice fraud may separately trigger criminal liability under Criminal Law Articles 205 and 206 with fines and imprisonment; the company is blacklisted as a serious dishonest taxpayer.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign or direct fraudulent returns may have the corporate veil pierced; personal assets can be attached if corporate funds are commingled with personal funds or diverted to personal benefit. The legal representative and responsible director face an exit ban under TCAL Article 44. Criminal conviction under Criminal Law Article 201 carries imprisonment up to seven years, fines, and disqualification from corporate management.
Transfer pricing manipulation and economic substance failure

Statutory sanction: CIT Law Articles 41, 44, and 48 permit the tax authority to adjust to arm's-length results, impose daily interest at 0.05% from the underpayment date, and levy a penalty of 50%-500% of the underpaid tax for evasion; the statute of limitation is extended and the taxpayer is publicly classified as an abnormal tax credit entity.

Director personal liability: Directors who approved non-commercial intercompany pricing may be personally liable for the resulting corporate tax debt if they received diverted profits or commingled assets with the company. Personal assets may be seized, exit bans imposed, and criminal liability pursued under Criminal Law Article 201 if false transfer pricing documentation is used to conceal taxable income, with imprisonment up to seven years.
Illicit profit repatriation and withholding tax evasion

Statutory sanction: Thin capitalization rules under CIT Law Article 46 disallow excess interest and recharacterize it as a dividend subject to 10% WHT; failure to withhold triggers a 50%-300% penalty under TCAL Article 69; treaty abuse and shell-company arrangements may be denied benefits under China's GAAR, BEPS principal-purpose test, and SAT anti-abuse announcements.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly approved the false royalty scheme are jointly and severally obligated to ensure withholding, and can be named as respondents in tax enforcement. If corporate assets are transferred abroad to frustrate collection, courts may pierce the veil and enforce against directors' personal and family property; the legal representative is blacklisted, an exit ban is imposed, and willful concealment of taxable income under Criminal Law Article 201 carries imprisonment up to seven years.
