Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None; there is no statutory minimum paid-up capital under Chinese Company Law for a WFOE. However, for the manufacturing of sterile examination nitrile gloves under ISIC 3250, the relevant medical device licensure authority may expect paid-in capital sufficient to cover the production facility build-out, cleanroom validation, and clinical equivalence testing. In practice, a minimum paid-up capital of RMB 1,000,000 is recommended or may be required by the local medical device regulator to demonstrate operational capacity. Capital contributions may be made in cash, equipment, or technology, and must be fully paid within the time limit stated in the AoA, generally within 30 years for WFOEs but subject to any negative list restriction. No blocked escrow account requirement applies, but paid-in capital must be injected through the WFOE's foreign exchange capital account and verified by the designated receiving bank.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety Certification: obtain a fire inspection and acceptance certificate or a fire safety compliance opinion from the local Emergency Management Bureau/Fire & Rescue Department upon completion of new construction or major remodeling; maintain automatic fire alarm systems, fire extinguishers, emergency lighting, exit signs, and approved evacuation routes for dipping-line industrial buildings. Structural load inspections: certified structural engineer or construction quality inspection unit's report demonstrating that building floors can support the dipping line, drying ovens, tanks, and chemical storage; in provinces requiring it, obtain a building inspection report from a qualifying civil engineering lab. Occupational safety clearance: obtain registration under the Work Safety Law, prepare a workplace OHS risk assessment, implement personal protective equipment, chemical safety data sheets, nitrile glove dipping SOPs, nitrile dust control, ergonomic controls, and file a hazard notification with the local Emergency Management Bureau; for controlled chemicals such as curing agents and accelerators, obtain a dangerous chemical use permit or record-filing under the Regulation on Safety Management of Hazardous Chemicals; for sterile medical gloves, obtain the NMPA GMP compliance certificate or self-inspection declaration, and sterile product release testing must be performed in a licensed microbiology laboratory.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; electronic tax administration is performed through the official Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax) platform under each provincial-level STA office.

E-government filing portal

For a WFOE: (1) incorporate as a limited liability company with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) and receive the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), an 18-character identifier that is the legally recognized taxpayer identification number for tax purposes; (2) complete integrated tax registration through the multi-item linkage (one-stop) registration, whereby SAMR registration data is automatically transmitted to the State Taxation Administration and tax authorities open taxpayer files for Corporate Income Tax (CIT), VAT, payroll withholding, stamp tax, and local surcharges; (3) after the business license is issued, log into the Electronic Tax Bureau using the USCC, set up the digital tax password/certificate, and complete any supplemental registrations (e.g., export tax refund filing, general taxpayer status election, bank/financial account reporting) within the statutory registration period; (4) for a foreign non-resident enterprise that does not establish a WFOE but has China-source income, the payer applies withholding registration and obtains a tax identification number for non-resident enterprises (or records the non-resident's home-country TIN) in accordance with the STA's non-resident enterprise tax registration procedures; (5) after obtaining the USCC, the taxpayer must report changes, annual filing, and invoice issuance through the Electronic Tax Bureau.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation - resident WFOE limited liability company taxed on worldwide income; no pass-through treatment is available; territorial-zone exemptions are not assumed for a standard WFOE outside a special tax-exempt zone. — For a WFOE: (1) incorporate as a limited liability company with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) and receive the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), an 18-character identifier that is the legally recognized taxpayer identification number for tax purposes; (2) complete integrated tax registration through the multi-item linkage (one-stop) registration, whereby SAMR registration data is automatically transmitted to the State Taxation Administration and tax authorities open taxpayer files for Corporate Income Tax (CIT), VAT, payroll withholding, stamp tax, and local surcharges; (3) after the business license is issued, log into the Electronic Tax Bureau using the USCC, set up the digital tax password/certificate, and complete any supplemental registrations (e.g., export tax refund filing, general taxpayer status election, bank/financial account reporting) within the statutory registration period; (4) for a foreign non-resident enterprise that does not establish a WFOE but has China-source income, the payer applies withholding registration and obtains a tax identification number for non-resident enterprises (or records the non-resident's home-country TIN) in accordance with the STA's non-resident enterprise tax registration procedures; (5) after obtaining the USCC, the taxpayer must report changes, annual filing, and invoice issuance through the Electronic Tax Bureau.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation - resident WFOE limited liability company taxed on worldwide income; no pass-through treatment is available; territorial-zone exemptions are not assumed for a standard WFOE outside a special tax-exempt zone.

Available election forms

None. China does not provide a U.S.-style check-the-box classification or hybrid-mismatch entity election. The WFOE is automatically a resident enterprise for Corporate Income Tax. Available VAT elections are limited to small-scale taxpayer status (annual taxable sales at or below RMB 5,000,000) versus general taxpayer status, and the filing of export VAT refund/exemption preference, neither of which changes the entity-level CIT classification.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No turnover threshold for tax registration: every WFOE must register for VAT from the date its business license is issued. A simplified 'small-scale taxpayer' regime is available when annual taxable sales are at or below RMB 5,000,000; a general taxpayer registration/election is mandatory when annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export of goods by a VAT-registered WFOE qualifies for 0% VAT if the goods are actually exported from China customs territory and declared to customs under the export goods procedure; the exporter must have valid foreign-trade operator qualification and a customs export registration code; the sale must be a genuine cross-border transaction with ownership and risk transferred to the overseas buyer; and the exporter must collect the foreign exchange proceeds and retain the full export documentation bundle. Qualifying offshore services consumed abroad may also be zero-rated under the cross-border service zero-rating rules, but the current scope covers exported physical sterile nitrile gloves.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Export Goods Declaration) as cleared and stamped/electronically verified by the General Administration of Customs
  • Bill of Lading or equivalent multimodal/air transport document evidencing carriage of the gloves to the foreign destination
  • Commercial export invoice and signed export sales contract
  • Packing list and related loading/weight documents
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or bank-issued foreign exchange receipt record proving repatriation of export proceeds
  • VAT export invoice or ordinary commercial invoice issued for the export sale
  • Foreign Trade Operator registration record and customs export registration code
  • Medical device export qualification/sales certificate for the sterile examination gloves, if required

VAT invoices must be issued through the STA's invoice control system. Under the nationwide Fully Digitalized Electronic Invoice (FDEI) pilot, electronic invoices are generated on the official Electronic Tax Bureau platform, assigned anti-fraud digital stamps and unique codes, and cleared by the tax authority in near-real-time through the electronic invoice service platform. Paper VAT special invoices and VAT ordinary invoices remain lawful only when issued through the integrated invoice management system and reported digitally to the STA; all invoice red-letter/credit reversals must be processed through the same electronic platform.

Export zero-rating is subject to risk-based post-clearance review by the tax authority. If the exporter fails to repatriate export proceeds within the statutory period, cannot substantiate the customs declaration, or is found to have obtained a VAT refund through false documentation, the tax authority will disallow the 0% rate, require repayment of the refunded VAT, and impose daily late-payment interest at 0.05% of the overdue amount. Administrative penalties range from 0.5 to 5 times the evaded tax under the Tax Collection Administration Law. Intentional fraudulent export tax refund cases are referred to criminal enforcement under the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, with possible imprisonment, fines, forfeiture of refunds, suspension of export rebate qualifications, and downgrading of customs credit status.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Chinese onshore FX regime follows a controlled current account convertibility model for a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise. Routine export settlement, import payments, operational expenses and trade-related working capital movements are generally executable through SAFE-designated tier-1 banks without a separate central bank approval per transaction, but every movement is subject to real-business background verification and the matching of foreign exchange receipts to customs declarations, invoices and shipping documents. Capital account items remain strictly controlled: registered capital injection, equity transfer proceeds, cross-border loans, disposal of owner-occupied real estate and dividend repatriation all require SAFE registration and subsequent bank-level verification. A WFOE manufacturing sterile nitrile gloves must complete a Foreign Direct Investment base registration before any inbound capital enters, and the shareholder's registered capital arrives in an onshore foreign exchange capital account where it must be used exclusively for the stated business purposes, never silently recycled into securities or unauthorized intercompany lending. Profit remittance is permitted only after a statutory audit, full tax settlement, corporate income tax payment and withholding tax payment on the distributing entity, board approval and submission of the audited financial statements to the remitting bank. Export proceeds no longer face a mandatory surrender ratio, but they must be repatriated into a Chinese bank account and verified on the trade monitoring platform within the prescribed window. The framework is operationally stable for a genuine medical PPE exporter with complete documentation.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export foreign currency receipts must be repatriated into the WFOE's designated domestic receiving account within 180 calendar days from the date of export customs declaration under the export trade foreign exchange settlement and verification regime. There is no mandatory conversion ratio to RMB after SAFE abolished the compulsory foreign exchange surrender requirement in 2012, so 100% of proceeds may be retained in the foreign-currency account or voluntarily converted into RMB at commercial rates. For each export batch, the WFOE must submit the invoice, customs declaration, bill of lading, packing list and payment receipt into the trade monitoring platform within 15 business days after receipt of funds; failure to repatriate or verify triggers a rate-based penalty, downgrading of trade facilitation status and restriction on future export settlement.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) through the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The foreign shareholder remits registered capital via SWIFT MT103 to the WFOE's onshore foreign exchange capital account at a Chinese tier-1 bank using the legally permitted foreign direct investment purpose code. Every inbound capital remittance must be lodged under the still-valid SAFE Foreign Investment Registration Certificate and the bank must submit the settlement into SAFE's digital monitoring platform on the same service day. After crediting, the WFOE must compile the capital verification file using the bank settlement voucher, shareholder remittance instruction and underlying investment agreement; a licensed Chinese certified public accountant then issues a capital verification report. For operating RMB needs, the WFOE applies to convert foreign currency at the receiving bank and must preserve the conversion voucher for later tax and audit reviews. HSBC or any standard overseas remitter should include the beneficiary bank's SWIFT code, the WFOE's SAFE registration number and a remittance reference citing the registered capital purpose code to ensure smooth inbound clearance and customs-verification matching.Outbound trade settlements are executed through established clearing rails: SWIFT MT103 is used for open-account post-shipment payments and small-value working capital transfers to overseas suppliers; Commercial Letters of Credit are issued through the WFOE's Chinese bank as the issuing bank for large raw-material imports and machinery purchases, with advising or confirmation at the beneficiary's bank; Documentary Collections are used for low-risk one-off transactions where the seller releases documents against payment or acceptance. Every outbound export or import payment must match the customs declaration, invoice, bill of lading and foreign exchange purpose code on the trade monitoring platform, and the bank will verify the underlying contract before releasing US dollars, euros or cross-border RMB. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low. Offshore digital EMI platforms such as Revolut Business, Wise Business, Airwallex and similar global neo-banks cannot legally serve a mainland WFOE as its primary settlement bank because they do not hold a PBOC-issued onshore payment institution license and are outside the SAFE foreign exchange verification framework. They may be used as ancillary collection gateways for overseas e-commerce customers, but they cannot perform SAFe registration, export declaration matching, capital verification or import settlement. The WFOE must clear all cross-border flows through a licensed onshore Chinese commercial bank; any fintech service must route back into the licensed bank and cannot replace it for corporate treasury operations.
