Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies for a WFOE engaged in dental clear aligner sheet thermoforming, trimming and packaging. The registered capital is set by the shareholders in the Articles of Association, contribution amounts and method are scheduled in the Articles, and there is no blocked escrow account or mandatory deposit certificate requirement. Paid-in contributions must be correctly recorded in the company financial records and disclosed through the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System after the business license is obtained.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety: obtain fire protection design review and final acceptance opinion from the Housing and Urban-Rural Development Department for the factory premises, or fire protection acceptance filing where no structural alteration is made. Occupational clearance: file the occupational disease hazard project with the local Health Commission, implement the Three Simultaneous system for workplace safety facilities, provide noise, heat and dust controls around trimming equipment, prepare emergency response plans, and obtain all fire safety acceptance documents before production starts.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; National Electronic Tax Bureau (the STA unified electronic tax administration portal) is the online portal for tax registration, filing, invoicing, and refund administration.

E-government filing portal

For a WFOE: 1) Incorporate a limited liability company with the Market Regulation Administration; the issued business license carries the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which constitutes the entity's taxpayer identification number (TIN) in China. 2) Complete tax registration through the National Electronic Tax Bureau within 30 days after business license issuance by activating the taxpayer e-file and confirming tax type and filing frequency; no separate TIN application is required for a domestic resident enterprise. 3) Apply for VAT general taxpayer status, in particular to support export zero-rating and export VAT refund. For a foreign non-resident entity acting without a domestic establishment, the STA will issue a Non-Resident Taxpayer Identification Number pursuant to a withholding registration filed by the payer or its agent before the first taxable transaction.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation (PRC resident enterprise; the WFOE is taxed as a resident enterprise on its worldwide income and is not eligible for pass-through treatment). — For a WFOE: 1) Incorporate a limited liability company with the Market Regulation Administration; the issued business license carries the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which constitutes the entity's taxpayer identification number (TIN) in China. 2) Complete tax registration through the National Electronic Tax Bureau within 30 days after business license issuance by activating the taxpayer e-file and confirming tax type and filing frequency; no separate TIN application is required for a domestic resident enterprise. 3) Apply for VAT general taxpayer status, in particular to support export zero-rating and export VAT refund. For a foreign non-resident entity acting without a domestic establishment, the STA will issue a Non-Resident Taxpayer Identification Number pursuant to a withholding registration filed by the payer or its agent before the first taxable transaction.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation (PRC resident enterprise; the WFOE is taxed as a resident enterprise on its worldwide income and is not eligible for pass-through treatment).

Available election forms

None (PRC corporate income tax law does not provide a check-the-box entity classification election or a hybrid mismatch election; the WFOE is always taxed as a resident limited liability company. Separate preferential tax elections, such as High and New Technology Enterprise status, may be available but do not alter entity classification).

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 (annual VAT-taxable sales): a taxpayer with annual taxable sales above RMB 5,000,000 is required to register as a general VAT taxpayer; a taxpayer at or below this threshold may remain a small-scale VAT taxpayer. Basic tax registration is nevertheless mandatory for all business entities upon business license issuance, regardless of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The export of self-produced dental clear aligner sheets qualifies for the zero percent VAT rate only where: (a) the goods actually leave the PRC customs territory and the export customs declaration names the WFOE as exporter; (b) the WFOE holds valid VAT general taxpayer status; (c) a genuine sale to an overseas buyer is evidenced by contract, invoice and payment; (d) the export proceeds are collected in foreign exchange and repatriated to China; and (e) the export VAT rebate (exemption) declaration is filed through the STA electronic channel within the statutory filing period. Offshore digital services are not within this manufacturing scope.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Export Goods Declaration Form issued by the General Administration of Customs with the customs declaration identification number)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill showing the WFOE as shipper
  • Commercial Invoice and packing list
  • Export sales contract or order confirmation
  • Bank foreign-exchange collection record (incoming remittance credit advice or export proceeds collection record reported under SAFE)
  • VAT export invoice and export tax rebate (exemption) electronic declaration data

Mandatory issuance of digitally controlled invoices through the STA's unified Fully Digitalized Electronic Invoice platform; VAT special invoices are supported by the Golden Tax System with tax-control data encryption and unique invoice numbers. Invoice data is transmitted to the STA in near-real time and matched against VAT returns and customs/export data, with API-based integration available for high-volume issuers and anti-fraud validation performed within the STA electronic invoice service platform.

The STA cross-matches VAT export-rebate filings against customs export declarations, bank foreign-exchange collection records, contracts, and invoice data under the Golden Tax framework. If a zero-rated export cannot be substantiated, or export proceeds are not repatriated within the required 180-day window, the transaction may be recharacterized as a domestic sale, the 13% output VAT may be assessed, any export VAT refund already paid will be clawed back, a daily late-payment surcharge of 0.05% of the underpaid tax will accrue, and an administrative penalty of between 50% and five times the tax evaded or refunded may be imposed. Fraudulent export-rebate arrangements involving intent may also trigger criminal liability under PRC law.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a managed floating exchange rate regime with strict separation of current account and capital account foreign exchange transactions. For a WFOE in the dental clear aligner sector, current account items such as export proceeds, royalty payments, and intra-trade service fees are generally fully convertible once verified by a designated foreign exchange bank against authentic underlying documents. SAFE has abolished the former mandatory surrender system, permitting export foreign receipts to be held in foreign currency settlement accounts or converted to CNY according to treasury policy; however, every receipt must be reflected in the SAFE monitoring system with a true trade background. Profit remittances by WFOEs are allowed after audited financial statements, tax clearance certificates, and board dividend resolutions are furnished, but the slower supervisory review process creates timing uncertainty. Capital account items, including equity injection from the parent, cross-border loans, and capital increases, require SAFE registration before funds arrive, and cannot be freely swept across the border. Fund conversion from capital account to RMB is subject to the Negative List, and proceeds from capital contributions must be used only for actual operating expenditures, not for equity investment, entrustment loans, or securities holdings. Therefore, liquidity planning must separate complied current account flows from registered capital account waterlines, with strict documentary trail maintenance for every transaction.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export receipts from dental aligner shipments must be repatriated to China without delay after receipt; SAFE permits retention in a current account FX settlement account, and conversion to CNY is freely allowed through a designated bank against the export customs declaration, commercial invoice, and packing list. There is no statutory mandatory surrender ratio, meaning 0% must be forcibly converted; however, 100% of the proceeds must be collected or credited to China within the statutory repatriation period, normally within 30 days from the date of export customs declaration unless extended by SAFE under genuine commercial terms. The exporter must perform trade document verification in the SAFE digital system; at least 30% random export value sampling is applied annually, with full audit required if mismatches are detected.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBoC) and State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Legal feasibility is low for using an offshore EMI as the primary clearing bank. Offshore digital banks and EMIs with no Chinese licensed banking or SAFE foreign exchange business qualification cannot open CNY-denominated corporate accounts or clear capital account funds for a mainland WFOE. They may offer supplementary multi-currency treasury interfaces and improve visibility of inbound remittances, but the legally required payment route for export proceeds and capital contributions is via a SAFE-approved designated domestic bank using the Foreign Exchange Account registration. All cross-border settlement flows must be submitted to the SAFE monitoring system with true trade documents; EMIs that cannot connect to the China Foreign Exchange Monitoring System do not satisfy statutory compliance. Feasibility rating: Medium as an information layer only; Low as a bank or clearing rail.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Foreign direct investment proceeds must be routed via a SWIFT MT103 message from the overseas parent bank to the WFOE's capital account at a domestic designated SAFE-licensed bank. Before transfer, the WFOE must complete SAFE foreign direct investment registration, obtain an FDI Business Registration Certificate, and open a Capital Account under the Unified Social Credit Code. The MT103 message must contain the purpose code and remittance remark identifying the transfer as a shareholder equity injection or capital increase, and the receiving bank must complete capital verification using audited financial statements and the capital contribution schedule. After arrival, the capital must be converted to CNY through the bank's foreign exchange settlement system in compliance with the Negative List and earmarked use rules; the capital verification report is issued after fund arrival.Outbound trade payments for imported materials and services should use SWIFT MT103 for non-LC transactions, with supporting contract, invoice, bill of lading and customs declaration. For larger procurement, irrevocable commercial letters of credit and documentary collections provide settlement security, but the WFOE must ensure the underlying trade is described correctly and the receiving bank is a permitted counterparty. Direct cross-border payments require bank verification of the electronic customs and tax data exchange; where this linkage is unavailable, manual declaration through the bank is needed. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: A Local File is required when annual related-party tangible property transfers exceed CNY 200 million, financial asset transfers exceed CNY 100 million, intangible asset transfers exceed CNY 10 million, or other related-party transactions exceed CNY 40 million. A Master File is required when aggregate annual related-party transactions exceed CNY 1 billion. Related-party transaction reporting schedules must accompany the annual CIT return.

Economic substance scope: China does not apply offshore Economic Substance Regulations, but applies a statutory substance-over-form principle and the General Anti-Avoidance Rule under Enterprise Income Tax Law Article 47. In transfer pricing audits, Core Income-Generating Activities analysis determines where the key functions, risks, assets, and strategic decisions of the aligner R&D, thermoforming, distribution, and financing arrangements are actually located. A WFOE must maintain substantive employees, premises, equipment, decision-making, and accounting records in China; an offshore entity claiming royalties or profits without economic substance will be recharacterized.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion via Fabricated Royalty and Service Fee Payments

Statutory sanction: Under the Tax Administration and Collection Law Article 63, the tax authority recovers the underpaid tax and late interest and imposes a fine of 50% to 500% of the unpaid amount. The Enterprise Income Tax Law permits a transfer pricing adjustment and re-assessment of withholding tax. Where the evasion amount is large, Criminal Law Article 201 applies, carrying imprisonment of up to seven years and criminal fines.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly approve fabricated royalty and service fee payments are treated as directly responsible persons and face criminal prosecution. Chinese courts may pierce the corporate veil under Company Law principles and hold shareholders or directors personally liable for the company's tax debts, with civil asset restitution, asset freezes, travel bans, and imprisonment for criminal evasion.
Shell Entity and Artificial Profit Shifting

Statutory sanction: The General Anti-Avoidance Rule under Enterprise Income Tax Law Article 47 allows the tax authority to recharacterize the arrangement, disregard the shell, impute taxable income on a deemed basis, and assess 25% CIT plus daily late surcharge and penalties. The company may also be placed on the tax non-compliance blacklist and made subject to mandatory special tax audit and cross-border anti-avoidance investigation.

Director personal liability: Under the PRC Company Law doctrine of disregarding the corporate veil, directors and controlling persons may be held personally liable for corporate tax obligations where they have abused limited liability to evade taxes. Directors face civil recovery of diverted assets, exit-control blacklisting, seizure of personal property, and criminal liability where their conduct constitutes tax evasion.
Customs and AML Fraud through False Classification and Invoice Manipulation

Statutory sanction: The Customs Law and Anti-Money Laundering Law authorize confiscation of goods, fines up to three times the evaded customs duties, license revocation, and civil AML penalties. Smuggling offenses under Criminal Law Article 153 may result in imprisonment of up to ten years or more for serious cases; money laundering under Criminal Law Article 191 carries separate imprisonment and fines.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false customs declarations, direct hidden offshore payment-rail accounts, or intentionally conceal mainland-source revenue face personal criminal prosecution for smuggling and money laundering. The corporate veil may be pierced to collect evaded taxes and customs duties from personal assets. Directors are also subject to travel bans, asset freezes, immigration restrictions, and custodial criminal sentences.
