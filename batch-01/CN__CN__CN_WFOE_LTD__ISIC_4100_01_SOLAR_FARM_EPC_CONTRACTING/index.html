Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

Under the PRC Company Law (2023), a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise limited liability company has no statutory minimum paid-up capital and no blocked escrow account is required; the Articles of Association must specify the capital amount, contribution form, and contribution deadline. For a solar-farm EPC contracting WFOE, registered capital should be aligned with the construction qualification grading and tender creditworthiness, with a commercially prudent minimum of Renminbi 10,000,000 for a class-III electric power engineering general contracting qualification. Foreign capital contributions must be remitted through a SAFE-registered foreign exchange capital account and settled under the FDI foreign exchange registration procedures; the contribution commitment is published in the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System.

Funding & unlock path: The WFOE and its subcontractors must obtain, before physical works commence: (1) fire protection design review and fire completion acceptance certificates for permanent structures, inverters, transformer stations, and temporary site welfare facilities, issued by the local Fire and Rescue Division under the Fire Protection Law of the PRC; (2) a civil defense fire permit for the construction site, laydown yard, and temporary offices, including confirmation of access roads, fire hydrants, extinguisher placement, and emergency evacuation plans; (3) structural load and foundation inspection certificates for crane pads, piling equipment, and PV racking foundations under the Building Law and MOHURD engineering-quality regulations; (4) a work-safety pre-assessment and the construction enterprise work safety production license under the Work Safety Law; (5) filing of the emergency response plan with the local emergency management bureau; and (6) occupational hazard assessment and occupational disease prevention registration for workers exposed to dust, noise, welding fume, and high-voltage electromagnetic fields under the Law on the Prevention and Control of Occupational Diseases and its implementing regulations.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA); electronic portals: e-Tax Bureau (Electronic Tax Administration) for tax registration and filing, the STA fully digitalized electronic invoice platform for VAT invoicing, and the China International Trade Single Window for export customs clearance.

E-government filing portal

The WFOE is incorporated with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) and receives a business license bearing the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which serves as the statutory Tax Identification Number (TIN) for corporate income tax, VAT, and all PRC tax filings. SAMR registration data is automatically exchanged with the tax authority; within 30 days of incorporation the WFOE must complete supplementary tax registration through the e-Tax Bureau portal, confirming legal representative, registered address, ISIC 4100 business scope, bank accounts, accounting system, invoice quota, VAT taxpayer status (general or small-scale), payroll withholding registration, and stamp duty registration. For a foreign non-resident enterprise with no establishment in China, no standalone PRC VAT registration is required; the domestic payer obtains a non-resident tax withholding record in the e-Tax Bureau and executes VAT and income tax withholding on payments subject to PRC tax.

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None. The PRC does not provide check-the-box entity classification or hybrid mismatch elections; a WFOE is mandatorily a resident enterprise taxed at entity level on worldwide income. VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 annual taxable turnover (mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration threshold); basic tax registration is compulsory for all entities from incorporation regardless of turnover. Below RMB 5,000,000, the WFOE may be a small-scale VAT taxpayer and may voluntarily elect general taxpayer status.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods: exported solar equipment and materials are zero-rated if actually declared to China Customs, exported under a valid export declaration, and supported by receipt of foreign exchange; input VAT on the goods and attributable input services is recoverable through the export VAT rebate. Services: cross-border taxable services, including offshore engineering design, remote technical consultancy, and other services consumed wholly outside China and supplied to a non-resident customer under Caishui [2016] No. 36, may qualify for 0% VAT if the recipient is outside China, the service is not used in China, and payment is received from overseas. Domestic on-site EPC construction at a Chinese project site is a domestic taxable supply at 9% for construction services or 13% for separately supplied equipment and cannot be zero-rated.

  • China Customs Export Declaration (Export Goods Declaration Form) with a customs assignment number, lodged through the China International Trade Single Window
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export movement of the goods
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or bank foreign-exchange receipt voucher confirming export proceeds were repatriated to the WFOE's China account
  • Export sales contract and commercial invoice stating the goods, Incoterms, value, and 0% VAT treatment
  • Packing list and customs commodity inspection or customs valuation documents, where applicable
  • Zero-rated export sales invoice (ordinary VAT invoice or fully digitalized electronic invoice) clearly marked 0% and linked to the export declaration

VAT invoices must be issued through the STA electronic invoice service platform under Golden Tax System Phase IV. Fully digitalized electronic invoices (FDEI) are transmitted to the STA in real time, carry a unique STA-issued anti-counterfeit invoice number and encryption/verification code, and do not require a physical tax control device. General taxpayers must perform periodic VAT reconciliation through the e-Tax Bureau and verify all special VAT invoices against the national invoice verification platform. For the EPC supply, invoices must separate equipment sales at 13%, construction/installation services at 9%, and independently contracted design services at 6%; failure to separate may cause the entire supply to be recharacterized at the highest applicable rate.

Unverified or fraudulent zero-rating is clawed back retroactively: output VAT at the applicable rate (13% for goods or 9% for construction services) is reassessed from the date of export or invoice, a late-payment surcharge of 0.05% per day accrues from the due date, and a tax penalty of 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax may be imposed under the Law on Administration of Tax Collection and its implementing rules. In cases of fraudulent export tax refund, the refund is recovered without limitation period, the taxpayer loses export rebate status, administrative disqualification and blacklisting apply, and criminal liability for fraudulent export refund under the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China may be pursued. Failure to repatriate export proceeds within 180 days also triggers SAFE foreign-exchange penalties and may invalidate the zero-rating evidence.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China maintains a managed floating exchange rate and a bifurcated convertibility system. Current account transactions (trade in goods and services) are fully convertible in practice: banks verify documentation such as contracts, invoices and customs declarations, then settle automatically. Capital account transactions remain closely monitored. Foreign direct investment enters via a two-step SAFE registration (FDI foreign exchange registration and later equity change/FDI reinvestment registration). Invested foreign exchange capital is held in a dedicated capital account; each use requires a payment-into-account review against the registered business scope. Repatriation of stated capital before liquidation is prohibited; liquidation proceeds can be remitted after tax clearance. Profit remittance is open but subject to bank verification of the audited financial statements, tax payment certificates, and a one-off registration. Dividends to parent are treated as current account payments and do not require a separate SAFE approval, but the bank must maintain the dividend drawer copy and the source enterprise's foreign exchange annual report. The CNY exchange rate follows a crawling peg with reference to a basket; the PBOC daily fixing plus a trading band makes full market-determined convertibility unavailable. Enterprises cannot freely arbitrage between onshore and offshore RMB; transactions must have genuine underlying business. Overall, the territory is non-convertible on the capital account and significantly but administratively convertible on the current account.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no mandatory conversion ratio under the current SAFE measures; all export foreign exchange receipts must be repatriated to mainland China and deposited into a current account foreign exchange account or settled into RMB at the bank's daily spot rate. The statutory timeframe for collection is 90 days from the date of export declaration; companies expecting payment beyond 90 days may file a deferred collection registration at the bank before the expiry. Failure to collect within the period without a valid registration triggers a trade credibility classification downgrade and may restrict future payment settlement.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC); the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) acts as the delegated enforcement agency for all currency and cross-border payment rules.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Offshore digital EMIs (e.g., Airwallex, OFX) are legally barred from acting as the primary clearing bank for a mainland WFOE under the People's Bank of China's licensing framework. The State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) licensing regime requires all capital account inflows, FX conversion, and current account settlement to pass through a licensed domestic commercial bank. Unlicensed fintech platforms may be engaged only for ancillary services such as cloud-hosted treasury dashboards or tariff collection, but they cannot legally hold corporate funds as a deposit-taking institution. Consequently, the viability of using an EMI as the main treasury rail is low and would expose the WFOE to regulatory non-compliance, frozen funds, and inability to complete SAFE online clearance.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Defense evidence: Provide a full corporate ownership cascade from the UBO to the WFOE, certified Chinese translation of the parent registry extract, identity and proof-of-residence for every natural person holding 10% or more, a notarized statement of no PEP status, and a global sanctions screening report (OFAC, EU, UN, and Chinese MOFCOM) for each UBO.

Defense evidence: Deliver 12 months of bank statements for the overseas investor, showing accumulated balances, a signed undertaking on the commercial substance, tax-paid proofs or employment income records for individual investors, and a formal board resolution approving the injection amount and purpose.

Root cause: EPC milestone payments show circularity or no underlying construction

Defense evidence: Provide the full set of executed EPC contracts with schedules, engineering drawings and BOM, subcontractor agreements, evidenced purchase invoices, project site photographs, progress certification by a licensed engineer, and a comparison of the invoiced amount against the contracted milestone plan.

Root cause: Materials or counterparties appear on sanctions or convicted lists

Defense evidence: Deliver a KYC pack for all suppliers and subcontractors, including legal address, ownership, registry, and an independent external trade-compliance declaration; commit to screening transactions against the consolidated Chinese and international sanctions lists and include contractual clauses allowing audit and termination for any sanctioned-party reference.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The initial foreign capital contribution must be wired from the foreign shareholder's own bank account to the WFOE's capital FX account at a licensed mainland branch, after the SAFE Foreign-Invested Enterprise Foreign Exchange Registration has been completed online through the bank. The wire must carry the SAFE-compliant purpose code for capital injection to establish a foreign-invested enterprise and the beneficiary ID field must match the SAFE registration number. Upon receipt, the bank credits the payment-funds-to-be-verified ledger; the WFOE must provide a CPA capital verification report, a board resolution on fund usage, and a detailed project procurement plan before converting to RMB. Each capital disbursement must be submitted through the bank's foreign exchange monitoring system and receipts/reports must be filed.Liquidity clearing for procurement and subcontracting should use SWIFT MT103 messages over the domestic bank's international clearing justification platform for direct overseas payments. Chinese banks also offer RMB cross-border clearing via CNAPS and the Cross-Border Interbank Payment System (CIPS) for Chinese counter-contracts. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Offshore digital EMIs (e.g., Airwallex, OFX) are legally barred from acting as the primary clearing bank for a mainland WFOE under the People's Bank of China's licensing framework. The State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) licensing regime requires all capital account inflows, FX conversion, and current account settlement to pass through a licensed domestic commercial bank. Unlicensed fintech platforms may be engaged only for ancillary services such as cloud-hosted treasury dashboards or tariff collection, but they cannot legally hold corporate funds as a deposit-taking institution. Consequently, the viability of using an EMI as the main treasury rail is low and would expose the WFOE to regulatory non-compliance, frozen funds, and inability to complete SAFE online clearance.
