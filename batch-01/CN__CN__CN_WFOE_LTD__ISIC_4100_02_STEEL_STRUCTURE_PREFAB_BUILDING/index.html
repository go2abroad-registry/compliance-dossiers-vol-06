Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A pure virtual office or commercial mail-drop address is not permissible for SAMR registration, tax confirmation, or corporate bank account opening in China. The WFOE must maintain a physical business place in mainland China evidenced by a lease agreement, title certificate, or sublease with valid landlord consent. For a steel structure prefab EPC company, the registered office may be an administrative head office, but a project site office alone is insufficient for company registration. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; online services are handled through the Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax) portal operated by STA and its provincial/municipal tax service bureaus; customs procedures for export clearance are administered by the General Administration of Customs of China.

E-government filing portal

The foreign investor first incorporates the WFOE with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR). The SAMR business license contains the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which is automatically recognized as the taxpayer identification number (TIN) for corporate income tax, VAT, and withholding tax purposes. Tax registration is completed through the one-stop business registration process, after which the taxpayer obtains e-Tax login credentials and confirms tax registration details with the local STA tax service bureau. For a non-resident enterprise that has no establishment in China but derives China-source income, the non-resident taxpayer must register with the STA for tax withholding purposes and obtain a non-resident TIN via the e-Tax portal or through its withholding agent; treaty relief, where applicable, is claimed through the standard non-resident tax treaty procedure.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None. China does not provide check-the-box or hybrid mismatch entity classification elections. A WFOE is classified as a Chinese resident enterprise under the Corporate Income Tax Law and is taxed at entity level on worldwide income; VAT general taxpayer or small-scale taxpayer status is an elective administrative registration, not an entity classification election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Annual taxable turnover exceeding RMB 5,000,000 triggers mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration in China; entities with turnover at or below RMB 5,000,000 may retain small-scale taxpayer status unless they elect general taxpayer status. Export zero-rating and input VAT credit refunds require general taxpayer status.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export sales of fabricated steel structure goods and components by a general VAT taxpayer qualify for 0% VAT zero-rating with entitlement to refund of attributable input VAT where: the goods are physically exported from China under an export customs declaration; the sale is made to an overseas customer under a genuine cross-border contract; the proceeds are received in foreign currency and repatriated to China; and the exporter retains complete customs, freight, and banking evidence. Cross-border construction services performed wholly outside China may also qualify for zero-rating or VAT exemption under the cross-border taxable services rules. Domestic on-site assembly, engineering design, and EPC services rendered inside China remain taxable at the applicable domestic rates, including 13% for goods and 9% for construction services.

China requires VAT invoices to be issued through the STA electronic invoice service platform using Fully Digitalized Electronic Invoice (FDEI) technology. General taxpayers must transmit invoice data in real time to the STA platform; each invoice receives a unique fiscal number, digital stamp, and anti-fraud validation. Enterprises may integrate their ERP systems with the e-invoice platform through approved APIs or portal-based workflows. Export sales must be supported by compliant zero-rated export invoices recorded in the same e-invoice system; the platform also performs automated cross-checks against customs export data.

Export zero-rating claims are subject to STA post-export verification and data matching with customs and foreign exchange records. If the export is found to be fictitious, goods fail to depart China, or foreign exchange proceeds are not repatriated within the statutory 180-day period, the STA will claw back any VAT exemption or refund, charge late payment interest, and impose penalties ranging from 50 percent to five times the tax under the Tax Administration Law. Customs penalties for false export declarations and smuggling liability under the Customs Law may also apply; deliberate fraudulent export refund claims are subject to criminal prosecution under the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China maintains a managed floating exchange-rate regime under the PBOC/SAFE, with RMB fully convertible for genuine current-account transactions (goods trade, services, profit remittance) and restricted capital-account convertibility. A WFOE's registered capital injection is a capital-account flow and must be pre-filed with SAFE under the FDI foreign-exchange registration system; funds can be received only into an FDI capital account at a licensed domestic bank and each conversion from foreign currency to RMB is reviewed against the FDI business registration code. Shareholder loans require foreign-debt registration and macro-prudential limits. Profit remittance to the overseas parent is treated as a current-account dividend flow, but is permitted only after annual audit, corporate income tax final settlement, and submission of board resolution, audited financial statements and tax clearance certificate to the remitting bank. Practical operating hurdles include strict monitoring of capital-account funds against the approved business purpose, source-of-funds evidence for every remittance, and the fact that domestic RMB funds cannot be used for unauthorised offshore equity investment. Export revenues from steel structure EPC may be retained in foreign currency or converted to RMB; all FX transactions must be executed at the onshore market rate through a designated domestic bank. Overall, the current account is open and functional, while capital-account transactions require granular pre-registration and documentary banking controls.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE's goods-trade monitoring, export proceeds must be repatriated to China and credited to the WFOE's domestic foreign-currency current account within the standard 90-day export collection window measured from the customs export declaration date; if payment terms extend beyond 90 days, the WFOE must file a deferred-receipt registration through its bank in advance. There is no mandatory conversion or surrender ratio in the current legal framework; the WFOE may retain 100% of its eligible export foreign-exchange proceeds in the domestic account or convert them to RMB at a designated bank. Advance receipts received more than 30 days before the export declaration also require a trade-credit report to the bank/SAFE monitoring system.

Central bank supervision: The People's Bank of China (PBOC), acting through the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Offshore EMIs such as Airwallex, Payoneer and Revolut Business are not licensed by PBOC/SAFE as Chinese banks and are legally barred from servicing an onshore corporate account in the same manner as a licensed domestic bank. They may only operate as payment service providers through local licensed partners under SAFE's cross-border payment pilot, and even then they are viable only for auxiliary e-commerce collection or small working-capital flows, not for statutory FDI capital, settlement of EPC trade, or foreign debt. Therefore, as the primary treasury and clearing rail, their viability is Low; they should not be used as a substitute for a Tier-1 domestic bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The shareholder remits registered capital or capital reserve from its overseas bank via SWIFT MT103/MT202 to the WFOE's FDI capital account held at a Tier-1 domestic bank. The payment must identify the SAFE FDI business registration code and use the correct SAFE foreign-exchange transaction purpose code for inbound direct investment / equity contribution. The receiving bank verifies the registration code against SAFE's Foreign Direct Investment system, performs source-of-funds checks on the remitting party, and then either retains the foreign currency or converts it to RMB under the capital settlement rules. Within 60 to 90 days after injection, a licensed PRC CPA must issue a capital verification report, which is required for updating the corporate registry and later dividend distribution. The WFOE must not use capital-account funds for working capital outside the approved business scope unless such use is permissible under the SAFE capital-account settlement regulations.For outbound EPC and steel-structure trade settlements, the WFOE should use SWIFT MT103/MT202 wire transfers for TT payments, Commercial Letters of Credit for large or documentary import/export contracts, and Documentary Collections for lower-risk open-account transactions. Each disbursement must be supported by the underlying contract, commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading or air waybill, customs declaration, and import/export verification records. The bank will perform trade-in-goods authenticity checks and match the payment with SAFE's monitoring system. For cross-border services such as overseas engineering design, a tax payment certificate and service-trade purpose code are required. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File / contemporaneous documentation must be prepared if any of the following prior-year related-party transaction thresholds are exceeded (RMB): tangible property transfer > RMB 200 million; intangible property transfer > RMB 100 million; financial asset transfers > RMB 100 million; related-party services > RMB 40 million. Master File is required when the aggregate related-party transaction amount exceeds RMB 1 billion. CbC Report is required when the ultimate parent group's consolidated revenue exceeds RMB 5.5 billion.

Economic substance scope: China does not have a formal Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) filing regime similar to offshore finance centres, but the substance-over-form principle is embedded in CIT Law Art. 47 GAAR and SAT's beneficial ownership rules. For a WFOE manufacturing steel prefabs, the Core Income-Generating Activities (CIGA) such as procurement, production, engineering decisions, risk management and credit risk must be performed and controlled in China; if key activities are located offshore, tax authorities may reallocate profits, deny treaty benefits or apply a deemed PE. The enterprise should therefore maintain board minutes, employment records, office/factory premises, and decision logs that demonstrate actual CIGA in mainland China.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and False Invoicing

Statutory sanction: The tax authority will recover the underpaid taxes, charge a daily late surcharge of 0.05%, and impose a fine from 50% to 500% of the underpaid amount under Tax Collection Law Art. 63. Criminal Law Art. 201 punishes fraudulent tax evasion with up to 3 years' imprisonment if the tax evaded is relatively large, and 3 to 7 years if the amount is enormous and the evasion ratio is above 30%, plus a fine and criminal confiscation of assets.

Director personal liability: When a director directs, participates in, or knowingly benefits from the fraud, the corporate veil is pierced under Company Law Art. 20; the director is jointly and severally liable for the tax and penalties, individual bank accounts and real property may be seized, and the person is placed on the tax exit-control blacklist (travel ban) under Tax Collection Law Art. 44. A director can also be criminally prosecuted as the principal offender, risking imprisonment and personal asset confiscation.
Shell Company / Treaty Abuse and Conduit Arrangements

Statutory sanction: The General Anti-Abuse Rule (CIT Law Art. 47; Implementation Regulations Arts. 120-123) authorizes tax authorities to recharacterize the arrangement; the denied treaty benefit results in re-assessed 10% or 20% withholding tax, plus late interest, and a 50%-500% penalty for under-withholding. The OECD MLI Principal Purpose Test is read into China-ratified DTAs and used to deny treaty relief if obtaining the benefit is one of the main purposes.

Director personal liability: A director who structures or participates in the conduit arrangement may be assessed personally as a responsible officer for the unpaid withholding tax and penalties when the WFOE cannot satisfy the debt; personal assets can be reached through legal authorization and asset tracing; criminal liability may arise under Criminal Law Art. 201 if the WFOE intentionally fails to withhold taxes; the director can also be blacklisted or denied exit from China under tax security control measures.
Transfer Pricing Profit Stripping

Statutory sanction: CIT Law Arts. 41 and 48 empower the tax authority to make a special tax adjustment using the arm's length principle; the adjustment reattributes profit to the Chinese WFOE and imposes a compensatory interest surcharge equal to the RMB benchmark lending rate plus 5 percentage points for the adjusted year, in addition to late payment charges. If the mispricing is found to be fraudulent and not merely a pricing difference, Tax Collection Law Art. 63 penalties up to 500% of the underpaid tax are applied.

Director personal liability: Directors who personally approve or sign off false transfer pricing documentation or arrange sham loan/fee structures with no commercial justification can be held personally liable for the resulting tax underpayment, including the adjustment and interest; the tax authority may seek civil restitution from the director's personal fortune, impose exit restrictions (travel bans) and refer the case to criminal prosecution for evasion where the deliberate mispricing is proven. The criminal threshold is met when the suppressed profit exceeds specified monetary and tax amounts, yielding imprisonment of up to 7 years plus fines.
