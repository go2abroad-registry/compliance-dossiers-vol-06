Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No fixed statutory minimum paid-in capital is imposed on a WFOE under current PRC Company Law; the subscribed capital, contribution form, and payment schedule are set forth in the Articles of Association and must not exceed a five-year payment period. There is no blocked escrow account requirement for ordinary paid-in capital at the company registration stage. However, for a data center mechanical and electrical EPC enterprise, the MOHURD construction qualification regime imposes minimum net asset, paid-in capital, technical personnel, and performance thresholds; therefore, the equity subscribers should preplan capital remittance and bank verification documents to support the later construction qualification application.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA), operating through provincial and municipal Tax Service Bureaus; online tax-administration access is through the National Unified Electronic Taxation Service Platform (Electronic Tax Bureau) linked from the STA official portal at www.chinatax.gov.cn, and VAT invoice/export-rebate functions are administered on the STA VAT Invoice Comprehensive Service Platform.

E-government filing portal

A Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE) is first registered as a limited liability company with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR), which issues the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC). The USCC is the PRC taxpayer identification number (TIN) and VAT taxpayer number. Within 30 days after receiving the business license, the WFOE must complete tax registration through the Electronic Tax Bureau, file its basic tax information, declare its VAT taxpayer classification, select a general tax accounting method, register its legal representative and financial officer, and activate the tax-control invoice system if applying for general VAT taxpayer status. For a non-resident enterprise that has no establishment or place of business in mainland China, the non-resident enterprise must be registered for PRC tax purposes with the competent STA office at the location where the withholding agent is located or, in the absence of a withholding agent, by direct registration in the relevant provincial Electronic Tax Bureau to obtain a Non-Resident Taxpayer Identification Number; the non-resident must also provide its certificate of tax residence for treaty relief where available.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None; PRC tax law does not provide a check-the-box pass-through election for a WFOE or a hybrid mismatch election in the entity classification sense. The WFOE is taxed at entity level on its global income under corporate income tax law. The only relevant statutory election is VAT classification: a taxpayer whose annual taxable sales do not exceed RMB 5,000,000 may elect to register as a general VAT taxpayer if its accounting is sound, and that election, once made, remains in force for at least 180 days under the STA's general taxpayer administration rules.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

China requires all entities with taxable activities to complete tax registration after incorporation, regardless of turnover. As a separate mandatory threshold, an entity with annual taxable VAT sales exceeding RMB 5,000,000 must register as a VAT general taxpayer; an entity below that threshold may remain a small-scale taxpayer or voluntarily register as a general VAT taxpayer if eligible. For data-center EPC services, the applicable domestic VAT rates are 9% for construction/installation services and 13% for independently supplied tangible goods, if the supplies are separated; where a mixed supply is not separated, the higher rate applies.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods qualify for 0% VAT when they leave PRC customs territory under a valid export customs declaration and are sold to an overseas purchaser; the exporter must be a VAT general taxpayer, must have legally acquired or produced the goods, must account for the export sale separately, and must collect the export proceeds through designated banks in the invoiced currency, with inward remittance or verified cross-border RMB settlement. Zero-rating is obtained through the export tax rebate mechanism: output VAT is charged at 0%, and the related input VAT is refunded after examination. Qualifying cross-border services, including engineering design, installation, commissioning, maintenance and electronic-data-related services performed for recipients outside mainland China and consumed wholly outside China, may be zero-rated or VAT-exempt if they appear in the STA's cross-border taxable services catalogue, are performed offshore, are settled in the agreed currency, and are evidenced by a cross-border service contract and the overseas recipient's non-resident status.

Invoices must be created using the STA-approved electronic invoice systems, including the fully digitized electronic invoice (digital invoice) platform and the tax-control UKey infrastructure. Issued invoices are transmitted in real time to the STA invoice database, and the VAT credit is recognized only after the buyer performs invoice verification and certification. Anti-fraud measures include the unified nationwide invoice code, digital tax-control signatures, automatic comparison of invoice, customs, and foreign-exchange data, and mandatory red-letter reversal procedures for cancelled or amended invoices.

The STA may launch an export tax rebate audit by cross-matching the export declaration, logistics, customs clearance, bank foreign-exchange receipt, and VAT invoice data. If the zero-rating application cannot be substantiated, the 0% rate is revoked, the transaction is re-characterized as domestic taxable supply, output VAT is reimposed at 13% or the applicable rate, input VAT credits are denied, late-payment interest runs from the original filing date, and a tax penalty of 50% to five times the underpaid or rebate amount may be imposed under the Law on Tax Administration and Tax Collection. Fraudulent export-tax-rebate claims carry mandatory clawback, a penalty of one to five times the fraudulently obtained rebate, and criminal liability for export tax rebate fraud under PRC criminal law and the relevant judicial interpretation.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Mainland China operates a managed floating exchange-rate regime with strict current-account convertibility and tightly controlled capital-account convertibility. Current-account receipts and payments for trade in goods and services are generally convertible through licensed designated foreign-exchange banks, but every transaction must pass authenticity verification and be backed by commercial documents such as contracts, invoices, customs declarations, and tax vouchers. Capital-account transactions remain heavily regulated: foreign direct investment into a WFOE requires SAFE registration, a dedicated FDI capital account, and post-registration reporting. Profit repatriation is treated as a current-account outflow once distributable profits are verified, so it requires audited financial statements, tax payment certificates, and a completed annual SAFE foreign-investment reporting cycle. The central hurdles for a data-center EPC treasury are (i) demonstrating that each foreign-currency inflow matches the approved business scope, (ii) proving that any payments to overseas equipment suppliers are commercially genuine under the master EPC contract, and (iii) complying with the prohibition on using capital-account settlement proceeds for purposes beyond normal operational scope such as securities investment, real-estate speculation, or unrelated shareholder debt repayment. In practice the RMB is convertible for operating liquidity, but conversion agility is limited by real-demand underwriting and post-transaction verification, so treasury operations must be planned with documentary lead times and conservative liquidity buffers.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For goods-export proceeds, SAFE requires repatriation to a licensed bank's foreign-exchange settlement account within 90 days of the export customs clearance date unless an extended deferred-payment timetable is approved prior to shipment. Once received onshore, the proceeds must be either settled into CNY or retained in a permitted foreign-exchange settling account within 5 working days. The mandatory conversion ratio is currently zero, meaning the WFOE may lawfully retain 100% of its export proceeds in foreign currency, although every inflow and outflow must be reported through the ASOne trade-monitoring system and matched to the underlying customs declaration.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) through the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE), with enforcement delegated to SAFE branch offices and the ASOne national trade-monitoring system

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Offshore fintech EMI platforms are effectively barred from providing primary corporate banking services to a Mainland-China incorporated WFOE. The PBOC and SAFE recognize only locally licensed commercial banks as providers of foreign-exchange settlement, CNY clearing, and capital-account custody. A foreign EMI cannot hold an SAFE foreign-exchange desigination, cannot open a Capital Escrow Account on behalf of the WFOE, and cannot participate in CIPS or CNAPS as a clearing member. The viability of an offshore digital EMI for this entity is therefore low; it may be used only as a non-executing treasury dashboard or a front-end API that relies on a regulated local bank as the true execution layer. Any independent EMI solution that advertises CNY conversion or onshore settlement for a Chinese legal entity is legally non-viable and would create AML and licensing exposure.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The compliant routing for shareholder equity injection is: (1) open an SAFE-registered FDI capital account at a tier-1 domestic commercial bank; (2) submit the foreign-investment registration confirmation obtained from SAFE; (3) instruct the overseas shareholder to remit via SWIFT MT103 using the standard SAFE international balance-of-payments purpose code for FDI equity contribution (typically 6221 in bank systems); (4) the receiving bank checks the remitter identity, UBO, and purpose code against the SAFE backup file before crediting the capital account; (5) after crediting, the WFOE may settle the foreign currency into CNY through the capital settlement mechanism, but the CNY proceeds may be used only for business purposes approved by SAFE, including project expenses, equipment purchases, wages, or subcontractor payments. A statutory capital verification report from a licensed accounting firm must be produced after the injection is completed to perfect the WFOE formation record.For disbursements to overseas equipment suppliers and engineering subcontractors, the clearing rails are: (a) SWIFT MT103 direct corporate wire after presentation of the EPC contract, invoice, customs declaration, and tax-payment slips; (b) commercial letter of credit issued by the local bank on a cash-collateralized or corporate-guarantee basis, settled through SWIFT MT700/MT799 messages for electromechanical and cooling-system imports; (c) documentary collections (D/P or D/A) for lower-value and burst-supply consignments; and (d) domestic subcontractor settlements in CNY through CNAPS RTGS using the WFOE's Basic Operational Currency Account. Where the receiving domestic party is a Chinese resident, the bank can also use CIPS for the CNY leg, reducing reliance on the USD-correspondent chain. All outbound FX payments must be matched to the correct SAFE BOP purpose code, typically under the goods-trade or project-engineering-service category, and the documentary audit trail must be retained for at least five years.Onshore deliverable CNY forward contracts (yuan-settlement forward agreements) executed with a licensed tier-1 domestic commercial bank and supported by real underlying EPC project cash-flow documentsOnshore CNY foreign-currency swaps matching known receivable and payable maturity dates, subject to SAFE real-demand verification and bank internal limit approvalPlain-vanilla CNY call and put options with the tier-1 bank for hedging invoice-level foreign-exchange exposure as long as the options are terminal-delivery only and not speculativeCross-currency interest-rate swaps (CCIRS) used to hedge long-term foreign-currency project obligations where the underlying loan or financing structure has obtained prior SAFE registrationNon-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are not permissible for mainland-incorporated WFOEs due to the SAFE real-demand principle; any offshore NDF overlay arranged by the parent must be wound down and replaced by an onshore deliverable hedge

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Offshore fintech EMI platforms are effectively barred from providing primary corporate banking services to a Mainland-China incorporated WFOE. The PBOC and SAFE recognize only locally licensed commercial banks as providers of foreign-exchange settlement, CNY clearing, and capital-account custody. A foreign EMI cannot hold an SAFE foreign-exchange desigination, cannot open a Capital Escrow Account on behalf of the WFOE, and cannot participate in CIPS or CNAPS as a clearing member. The viability of an offshore digital EMI for this entity is therefore low; it may be used only as a non-executing treasury dashboard or a front-end API that relies on a regulated local bank as the true execution layer. Any independent EMI solution that advertises CNY conversion or onshore settlement for a Chinese legal entity is legally non-viable and would create AML and licensing exposure.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: RMB: a Chinese WFOE must contemporaneously prepare a Local File and submit the annual related-party transaction declaration when total related-party transactions exceed RMB 200 million, with per-category thresholds of RMB 100 million for tangible asset and financial asset transfers and RMB 40 million for intangible property transfers and related-party services. A Master File is mandatory when the multinational group has consolidated annual revenue above RMB 1 billion and has controlled non-resident entities or related-party transactions meeting specific criteria, and all documentation must be produced in Chinese within 15 days of a request by the State Taxation Administration.

Economic substance scope: Mainland China applies EIT Law Article 47 and the substance-over-form doctrine, requiring a WFOE to have genuine business purpose, actual employees, premises, decision-making authority, assets, and risk-bearing capacity in China. The State Taxation Administration uses the Beneficial Ownership standard of Announcement 9/2018 to deny treaty benefits to letterbox companies that lack discretionary control, employees, bank accounts and substantive operations. Although China Mainland does not administer offshore ESR/CIGA return filings, OECD BEPS Action 5 transparency, country-by-country reporting, and the authorized OECD approach to permanent establishment apply. A datacenter EPC WFOE must align its functional and risk profile with the intercompany contractual allocation; otherwise the tax authority may recharacterize income, attribute profit to a PRC permanent establishment, deny treaty relief, and impose transfer pricing adjustments.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

False Invoicing and Fraudulent VAT Credit in EPC Subcontracting

Statutory sanction: Under Criminal Law Articles 205 and 210, issuing or obtaining false special VAT invoices triggers confiscation, fines up to RMB 500,000 and imprisonment up to life. The tax authority recovers underpaid EIT, VAT, surcharges, a daily 0.05% late payment surcharge, and imposes a 50%-500% penalty under Tax Collection Administration Law Article 63. The entity is placed on the serious tax violation blacklist and loses access to customs facilitation.

Director personal liability: The legal representative, CFO, finance officer, or any person directly responsible who signs or directs the false invoicing can be prosecuted personally. Criminal fines and restitution of unpaid tax may be enforced from personal assets. The responsible person is subject to a travel ban and exit restriction under Tax Collection Administration Law Article 44 until all tax, interest, penalties and a guarantee are settled. The court may pierce the corporate veil if company and personal assets are commingled or the WFOE is used solely as a criminal instrument, leading to personal civil liability for the entire tax loss and imprisonment up to life for serious VAT invoice fraud.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation Through Offshore Management, Royalty, and Financing Charges

Statutory sanction: Under EIT Law Articles 41, 46, 48 and 114 of the Detailed Implementation Rules, the tax authority may adjust the taxable income to an arm's length level, disallow interest beyond the thin capitalization ratio, recharacterize disguised interest or royalty distributions as dividends, impose the 10% WHT on deemed dividend distributions, and assess the 0.05% per-day surcharge. Failure to provide contemporaneous documentation may increase the interest rate applied to the adjustment and separately incur penalties for non-filing of the related-party transaction declaration.

Director personal liability: Directors and the legal representative may be civilly liable to compensate the WFOE for the adjusted tax, interest, and penalties if their negligence breached fiduciary duties under the PRC Company Law. A travel ban may be issued against the legal representative for the full amount of the adjusted tax, interest and fines. If the structure concealed substantial taxable income and the underpaid tax exceeds the criminal evasion threshold with over 10% of the tax due, the directly responsible officers face criminal liability under Criminal Law Article 201, with imprisonment up to seven years for the most serious cases.
Shell Entity Abuse, Treaty Shopping, and Unpaid Dividend or Interest Withholding Tax

Statutory sanction: The competent tax authority may disregard the corporate personality under the general anti-avoidance rule in EIT Law Article 47 and SAT Announcement 9/2018, deny DTA benefits, and recharacterize the offshore conduit as a tax resident of China or a permanent establishment. The full 10% withholding tax is assessed on the gross remittance, with a 0.05% per-day late surcharge and a withholding penalty of up to 100% of the unpaid WHT under Tax Collection Administration Law Article 69. The WFOE is added to the serious dishonesty tax blacklist, subject to public notice, tightened tax administration, and cross-border compliance restrictions.

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally ordered to make restitution of the unpaid withholding tax, interest and penalties if the WFOE lacks assets and the directors caused the distribution without withholding. Under PRC Company Law, the limited liability veil is pierced when the shareholder or director abuses the corporate form to the detriment of state revenue or creditors. The legal representative, tax officer and controlling directors are jointly and severally liable where collusion is proven. The responsible person is blacklisted and prevented from leaving China until all tax, interest, fines and security are paid. In severe cases involving intentional concealment through a cross-border conduit structure, criminal liability under Criminal Law Article 201 may result in imprisonment of three to seven years.
