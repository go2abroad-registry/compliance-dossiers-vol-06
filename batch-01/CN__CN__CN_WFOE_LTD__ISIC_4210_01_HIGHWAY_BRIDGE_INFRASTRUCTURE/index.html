Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA), operating the national electronic tax filing platform and the China International Trade Single Window for coordinated customs-and-tax data exchange.

E-government filing portal

For a WFOE, the entity is first registered with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) and receives a business license bearing the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which automatically serves as the Tax Identification Number (TIN) and VAT registration identifier. The mandatory tax registration is synchronized through the multi-disciplinary registration procedure with the local STA; the WFOE must complete activation of its electronic taxpayer account and may elect general VAT taxpayer status before reaching the turnover threshold. For a foreign non-resident enterprise without a permanent establishment in China, the domestic withholding agent or the non-resident's tax representative must file a non-resident enterprise tax registration with the STA office at the place where the income is sourced; the STA then issues a non-resident tax registration code linked to the contract and withholding obligations.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None. PRC law does not provide a check-the-box or pass-through election for a WFOE; the entity is a resident taxpayer subject to entity-level Corporate Income Tax and VAT. The only relevant elections are limited VAT-status elections between small-scale taxpayer and general taxpayer, and no hybrid mismatch elections are available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory VAT general-taxpayer registration is triggered when cumulative annual taxable turnover exceeds RMB 5,000,000 (five million yuan); a newly formed WFOE may register voluntarily before reaching that threshold. Construction services generally attract the reduced VAT rate of 9 percent.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For goods, zero-rating applies where the WFOE exports tangible goods outside the customs territory of China under a valid customs export declaration and receives the sale proceeds from the overseas buyer in a designated foreign-exchange account. For infrastructure services under an EPC contract, VAT zero-rating or exemption is available for construction and installation services that are physically rendered wholly outside China to an overseas project owner; any domestic pre-construction design, procurement, or mobilization performed within China remains taxable at the applicable VAT rates. The 0 percent rate is substantiated by export documentation, foreign-exchange repatriation, customs clearance of project-dedicated equipment, and offshore project completion evidence.

  • Chinese Customs Export Goods Declaration Form bearing customs release endorsement and export license references where applicable
  • Original Bill of Lading, multimodal transport document, or air waybill evidencing physical movement from China to an overseas destination
  • Designated-bank export proceeds receipt voucher evidencing foreign-currency collection from the non-resident buyer and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange trade-surveillance record
  • Export sales contract, commercial invoice, packing list, and letter of credit or collection documents linking the transaction to the overseas counterparty
  • Offshore project completion certificate and site acceptance minutes for EPC infrastructure services delivered outside China

China requires VAT invoices to be issued through STA-approved tax-control systems. The Fully Digitalized Electronic Invoice (FDEI) system is now the national standard and operates with real-time reporting to STA, digital anti-fraud stamps, and mandatory taxpayer identification embedded in each invoice. Enterprise resource-planning systems must integrate with the STA electronic invoice service platform through certified application programming interfaces (APIs) or use the official browser-based portal; physical invoices are replaced by digitally cleared e-invoices without separate pre-printed fiscal forms.

STA and China Customs conduct post-clearance review of export declarations, bills of lading, and bank receipt records. Failure to repatriate foreign exchange within the statutory period, incomplete documentation, manipulated customs values, or fabricated export evidence invalidates the 0 percent VAT treatment. The tax authority will claw back all VAT refunds or exemptions already granted, assess overdue interest, and may impose penalties generally ranging from 50 percent to five times the tax amount involved. Serious fraud using forged export documents or false declarations is subject to Customs Law fines and criminal liability for tax evasion, including imprisonment and confiscation of proceeds.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a managed floating exchange-rate regime in which the RMB is convertible under the current account for bona fide trade and service transactions, while capital-account transactions are subject to graduated SAFE registration, quota, and real-identity controls. A WFOE established for highway and bridge EPC contracting may freely convert current-account receipts through designated domestic banks after satisfying documentary authenticity checks under the SAFE six-point verification framework. By contrast, inbound foreign direct investment (FDI) equity injections must first complete the Foreign Direct Investment registration and be reported in the Direct Investment Statistical System before they can be converted into RMB. Profit remittance to the non-resident shareholder requires audited financial statements, an audit committee resolution, the shareholders dividend resolution, a tax-paid certificate, and proof that the underlying earnings arise from completed EPC infrastructure contracts. No blanket surrender requirement applies to export or project proceeds; a WFOE may retain verified foreign-currency earnings in approved accounts or settle them voluntarily. However, the repatriation requirement remains: infrastructure and construction services proceeds earned from cross-border projects must be brought into the domestic foreign-currency account within the relevant statutory time window, generally 180 days from the completion or certified date or from the invoice due date when project billing cycles apply. Conversion of capital-account injections is restricted to the actual business purpose and cannot be used for equity or capital-market speculation, short-term structured deposits, or prohibited negative-list activities. Weekly and monthly reporting and bank reconciliation requirements impose strict liquidity monitoring and mandatory audit trail maintenance. This convertibility architecture makes the WFOE treasury model heavily dependent on licensed Chinese commercial banks for FX execution and on SAFE-approved documentation for every cross-border capital flow.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Mandatory surrender of export or construction-related foreign-currency proceeds is no longer applied in mainland China; the mandatory conversion ratio is 0% and the WFOE may retain 100% of verified current-account foreign-currency earnings in a domestic foreign-currency account or voluntarily convert them to RMB at prevailing market rates. Repatriation is nevertheless obligatory. For goods-trade exports, proceeds must be repatriated into the domestic account within 180 days from export customs declaration, subject to bank matching with the SAFE electronic customs data system. For cross-border infrastructure EPC and construction services, the foreign-currency receipts must be credited to the WFOE domestic account within the invoice or contract payment due period, generally not exceeding 180 days after issuance of the certified progress certificate or completion certificate, unless SAFE has approved a specific deferred-payment arrangement. All repatriated proceeds must be supported by contracts, invoices, project completion certificates, and customs or service trade declarations, and the receiving bank must verify the authenticity before any conversion, transfer, or offset is permitted.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) with State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) as the delegated foreign exchange administration

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low and legally not viable as a primary banking solution. Offshore digital EMIs, neobanks, and cross-border payment aggregators do not hold a mainland Chinese banking license, cannot access the People's Bank of China payment and clearing systems, and cannot perform the statutory SAFE verification for cross-border receipts and payments. A China-regulated WFOE must maintain its real treasury, capital, and foreign-currency settlement accounts in a licensed Chinese commercial bank. A fintech EMI may be used only as an ancillary front-end payment technology platform if it connects to licensed Chinese banks and if the WFOE remains the legal account holder at the local receiving institution.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The foreign shareholder must execute an SWIFT MT103 transfer from its named bank to the WFOE's dedicated FDI capital settlement account at the Chinese receiving bank, ensuring the remitter, amount, and purpose code match the SAFE Foreign Direct Investment registration. The payment must be tagged in field 70 with the relevant FDI equity injection purpose code and a clear reference to the highway and bridge infrastructure project. Upon receipt, the domestic bank will check the electronic registration, perform authenticity and anti-money-laundering verification, and only then permit conversion to RMB. The converted RMB must enter the WFOE's RMB basic account and be deployed solely for permitted EPC operating expenditures and infrastructure project costs. A registered Chinese CPA must issue a capital verification report after each tranche, confirming the contribution is fully paid and compliant with the approved shareholding structure.Outbound settlements for imported construction materials, bridge structural steel, cement, geotechnical products, heavy machinery, and third-party subcontractors should be cleared primarily through SWIFT MT103 interbank transfers, commercial letters of credit issued via SWIFT MT700 series for high-value equipment purchases, and documentary collections for trusted counterparties with stable trade histories. Each disbursement requires the presentation of invoices, EPC contracts, import customs declarations, receipt confirmations, and, where applicable, inspection certificates to the domestic Chinese bank. For payments to subcontractors outside China, the WFOE must provide the applicable SAFE service trade or goods trade transaction code, proof that the liability is genuine and incurred in the ordinary course of the infrastructure project, and a signed contract establishing the payment milestones. All outbound wires must be consistent with the WFOE's registered business scope and with the project cash waterfall, and any variance above the bank's threshold requires escalating compliance review.Onshore RMB deliverable forward contracts against USD or other major currencies, executed with SAFE-qualified Chinese commercial banks, supported by documented underlying EPC contract exposures and subject to the bank's FX position limits and macro-prudential adjustment parameters.Cross-currency interest rate swaps for long-dated infrastructure receivables, allowing the WFOE to convert fixed foreign-currency project revenue into floating RMB obligations or vice versa, provided the notional is tied to verified EPC receivables and the bank confirms eligibility under SAFE derivatives rules.FX swaps for short-term liquidity management of foreign-currency project proceeds, enabling simultaneous spot sale and forward purchase of RMB while preserving settlement capability for upcoming supplier payments and capital expenditure.Offshore non-deliverable forwards are not permissible for the China-regulated WFOE; a non-deliverable forward may be used only by the non-resident parent or offshore SPV as a synthetic hedge, and the WFOE must never book an NDF without the receipt of a permissible onshore deliverable underlying exposure.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low and legally not viable as a primary banking solution. Offshore digital EMIs, neobanks, and cross-border payment aggregators do not hold a mainland Chinese banking license, cannot access the People's Bank of China payment and clearing systems, and cannot perform the statutory SAFE verification for cross-border receipts and payments. A China-regulated WFOE must maintain its real treasury, capital, and foreign-currency settlement accounts in a licensed Chinese commercial bank. A fintech EMI may be used only as an ancillary front-end payment technology platform if it connects to licensed Chinese banks and if the WFOE remains the legal account holder at the local receiving institution.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% on outbound dividends paid by a Chinese resident enterprise to a non-resident enterprise without a permanent establishment in China, applied by the WFOE as statutory withholding agent; reduced to 0% only where the recipient is a Chinese tax resident, which does not apply to a foreign parent.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Quarterly provisional CIT payments are due within 15 days after the end of each quarter. The annual CIT return and final payment are due within 5 months after the calendar or fiscal year-end, i.e. by 31 May for a calendar-year WFOE.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: RMB 200,000,000 annual related-party tangible-asset transactions, RMB 100,000,000 annual financial-asset or intangible-asset transactions, or RMB 40,000,000 annual other related-party transactions trigger contemporaneous Local File preparation; Master File is mandatory for the group if the SAT aggregate related-party transaction or enterprise revenue threshold in Public Notice 2016 No. 42 is exceeded.

Economic substance scope: China applies the judicial and administrative substance-over-form doctrine through CIT Law Article 47, implementing SAT Public Notice 2016 No. 42 and 2017 No. 6 for special tax investigations. The WFOE must demonstrate that it exercises real functions, employs qualified personnel, owns assets, and bears risk in project execution; foreign parent entities claiming treaty benefits must satisfy Core Income-Generating Activities analysis under the BEPS-related substance doctrine, and any purely cash-box intermediary cannot conclude contracts or receive dividends at treaty rate.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Transfer-Pricing Base Erosion

Statutory sanction: Special Tax Adjustment under CIT Law Article 41 and Article 114; the tax authority re-characterizes income, denies deductions, applies an imputed interest charge, and imposes a penalty of 50% to 100% of the tax underpaid in serious cases; late-payment surcharge accrues daily at 0.05%.

Director personal liability: The SAT may blacklist the legal representative and responsible director, banning their departure from China until tax is settled; directors may be required to restore the WFOE's assets on civil liability under PRC Company Law for abuse of corporate separate personality; if the scheme intentionally evades tax, Criminal Law Article 201 can result in up to 7 years' imprisonment and fines.
Tax Evasion and False VAT Invoicing

Statutory sanction: Tax Administration Law Article 63 imposes a penalty of 50% to 500% of the evaded tax and a late-payment surcharge; Criminal Law Article 205 punishes false issuance of special VAT invoices with up to life imprisonment and fines, with unit-crime liability against the enterprise; the invoice amount threshold is met under any substantial fraud.

Director personal liability: The director and financial controller are personally liable for restitution of tax revenue; a court may pierce the WFOE's limited liability and order the directors' personal assets to satisfy the evaded tax; a conviction under Article 205 carries a fixed-term or life imprisonment for the responsible person, plus disqualification from company management and an exit or blacklisting order.
Shell Company / Treaty Abuse / AML

Statutory sanction: The SAT invokes the General Anti-Avoidance Rule under CIT Law Article 47 and Article 48, re-characterizes the arrangement, denies treaty benefits, imposes a 10% or full 25% taxation adjustment plus a 50% to 500% penalty, and the financial regulator imposes AML fines under the PRC Anti-Money Laundering Law; repeated non-filing triggers cancellation of the offshore parent's beneficial-ownership certificate.

Director personal liability: Directors are named in the tax blacklist and barred from leaving China; civil liability attaches to their personal assets for fraudulent conveyance under PRC Company Law; criminal banking-license or AML violations carry up to 3 years' imprisonment and up to 7 years for aggravated cases, while tax evasion via treaty abuse may expose directors to up to 7 years under Criminal Law Article 201.
