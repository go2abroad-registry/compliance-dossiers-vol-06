Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; official online filing portal: Electronic Tax Bureau (eTax) web platform, supplemented by the National Electronic Invoice Service Platform.

E-government filing portal

For a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE) formed as a limited liability company in mainland China, the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) printed by the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) on the business license is the statutory Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN), replacing the old separate tax registration certificate. The WFOE must first complete incorporation with SAMR; within 30 days of receipt of the business license it must complete taxpayer registration, taxpayer type determination, bank account filing, accounting system filing, legal representative and financial officer records, and tax collection district assignment through the Electronic Tax Bureau. It must also register for VAT, determine small-scale versus general VAT taxpayer status, and enroll in the nationwide electronic invoice system as a tax-control invoice issuer. A foreign non-resident enterprise without a Chinese permanent establishment that performs taxable activities in China or receives China-source income must apply for a non-resident enterprise taxpayer identification number through the Electronic Tax Bureau, or must be registered by its Chinese payer/withholding agent at the place where the income arises, under the temporary taxpayer registration procedure.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation. The WFOE is a resident enterprise under PRC tax law, because it is incorporated under PRC law and its place of effective management is in China; it is taxed on worldwide income under the Enterprise Income Tax Law at the 25% corporate income tax rate and cannot be classified as a pass-through entity.

Available election forms

None; China does not have U.S.-style check-the-box elections or hybrid mismatch entity classifications. The only relevant elections are VAT-status elections: a newly established entity may elect to be a small-scale VAT taxpayer below the registration threshold, or may voluntarily register as a general VAT taxpayer if statutory conditions are met, enabling input-VAT crediting and export zero-rating.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

9%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

CNY 5,000,000 of annual taxable turnover for mandatory registration as a general VAT taxpayer; construction services such as airport runway asphalt paving are generally subject to 9% VAT when the site is in mainland China.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A Chinese WFOE that is registered as a general VAT taxpayer may zero-rate qualifying exports of goods and qualifying cross-border services. Under the VAT reform framework (including Ministry of Finance and STA circulars implementing zero-rating and exemption for cross-border taxable services), construction and project-management services physically performed outside mainland China for an overseas customer, including airport runway asphalt paving works, are eligible for 0% VAT if the services are not consumed in China, the WFOE maintains separate accounting for zero-rated turnover, and a valid cross-border service contract is retained. Goods physically exported from China are zero-rated upon customs export clearance. Services performed inside mainland China, including domestic airport runway paving, are domestic taxable services subject to 9% VAT and do not qualify for zero-rating.

  • Customs export declaration (bill of entry) as electronically accepted, inspected and cleared by China Customs, evidencing the export of goods and equipment for the asphalt paving project
  • Bill of lading, airway bill, or multimodal transport waybill issued by the carrier showing the exported goods left mainland China and identifying the overseas destination
  • Commercial contract, detailed sales invoice, packing list, and, for works, construction contract with the overseas airport/employer
  • Inward remittance certificate (FIRC) or bank foreign-exchange receipt statement verifying that the export proceeds have been received from the overseas buyer in accordance with SAFE foreign exchange collection verification requirements
  • Payment reconciliation and customs/foreign-exchange matching report showing the link between the customs-cleared goods and the repatriated foreign currency
  • Evidence of overseas project performance for zero-rated construction services, including the foreign worksite certification, employer acceptance certificate, and proof that no taxable service was supplied in China

The WFOE must issue VAT invoices exclusively through the STA's National Electronic Invoice Service Platform or through licensed tax-control digital issuance devices connected to the STA's invoice clearance/checking system. Fully digitalized electronic invoices are transmitted to the tax authorities in near real time with encrypted tax-control coding and anti-counterfeiting digital stamping. Special VAT invoices, where input-credit entitlement is claimed by the buyer, must be uploaded and verified in the national VAT invoice system before an input-VAT credit can be recognized. Invoices for construction services must specify the construction service tax classification, project details, rate, amount, and tax output; cross-regional construction activities require project location reporting for inter-jurisdictional VAT allocation.

The STA conducts post-clearance audit and cross-matching among customs declaration data, VAT invoice data, and SAFE foreign exchange receipt data. If the claimed export zero-rating or refund lacks the required customs, transport, foreign-exchange, or offshore project-consumption documentation, or is found to be fraudulent, the zero-rated basis is reversed and the output VAT on the underlying consideration is recaptured at the domestic rate, together with surcharges, late-payment interest at 0.05% per day, and an administrative penalty of 50% to 300% of the underpaid tax under Articles 63 and 64 of the PRC Tax Administration and Collection Law. Cases involving forged export declarations or fabricated offshore construction contracts constitute criminal export-fraud under Article 204 of the PRC Criminal Law and may result in imprisonment, criminal fines, and permanent disqualification from export tax refund treatment, in addition to extraterritorial anti-corruption and AML review for the entity and its shareholders.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The RMB is convertible on the current account for permitted trade in goods and services, including cross-border construction and engineering services, provided the underlying transaction is genuine and documented. The capital account, by contrast, remains subject to a registration-and-approval regime under SAFE. A WFOE's inbound equity capital must be received in accordance with the foreign investment record-filing, converted through a bank-designated capital settlement account, and verified by a certified public accountant before the funds are deemed paid-in. Repatriation of profit to the offshore shareholder requires audited financial statements, an enterprise income tax clearance and withholding tax payment, a board resolution approving distribution, and a SAFE-confirmed foreign exchange bank declaration. For outbound capital account payments such as foreign loans, guarantees, or shareholder advances, separate SAFE registrations and quota approvals are triggered. The system therefore is one of managed convertibility: daily operational liquidity in RMB is free, while cross-border principal movements, repatriation, and netting structures are constrained by documentary proof, real-need principles, anti-reversal controls, and mandatory transaction coding under the Trade and Cross-Border Payment and Receipt System. This creates latency and requires treasury to maintain local RMB liquidity buffers.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For an engineering WFOE, overseas contract proceeds from runway paving and related consulting services must be repatriated into China within the statutory period generally 180 days from the date of customs declaration or project completion recognition, unless SAFE agrees to a longer period for long-cycle construction. There is no fixed mandatory conversion ratio: after repatriation to the designated foreign exchange account, the enterprise may retain 100% in approved foreign currency or convert into RMB at market rates through lawful bank channels. All receipts must be truthfully declared under the appropriate current-account trade/service code, supported by the external construction contract, invoice, completion certification, and bank instruction evidence.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) as the central monetary authority, with operational foreign exchange administration delegated to the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) and its local branches.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Offshore digital electronic money institutions and non-bank EMIs have virtually no standalone viability for a mainland WFOE as the primary treasury vehicle. PBOC and SAFE require onshore licensed commercial banks to open settlement accounts, monitor FX transaction codes, implement capital registration, and issue the documentary evidence required for auditing and outward remittance. An offshore EMI cannot perform tax withholding, double-taxation treaty statements, SAFE reporting, capital verification, or RMB netting. EMIs are feasible only as a supplemental payment card/wallet channel through a licensed onshore bank, never as the bank of record for cross-border capital or EBITDA repatriation.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The foreign shareholder transmits the equity capital via SWIFT MT103 or equivalent wire directly into the WFOE's FDI capital settlement account at an onshore bank. The remittance instruction must reference the approved FDI enterprise registration code and clearly state the purpose as 'investments in China by non-resident' under the SAFE balance-of-payments declaration system. The receiving bank will pre-check that the remitter is the registered shareholder, that the amount is within the approved capital quota, and that no prohibited goods/services are referenced. After receipt, the bank posts the funds as foreign currency or converts to RMB, and the WFOE engages a licensed CPA firm to issue a capital verification report. The report and bank receipt are filed with the market regulation authority and become the basis for later profit repatriation.For imported polymer-modified bitumen, emulsifiers, aggregates, specialized machinery, and sub-consultant services, the preferred rail is a SWIFT MT103, subject to ICC rules and the bank's post-verification of purchase invoices, bill of lading/airway bill, construction progress certificate, and tax invoice. For large staged runway construction contracts with foreign EPC partners, a commercial letter of credit under UCP600 should be issued, payable against an engineer's certificate and shipping documents. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Offshore digital electronic money institutions and non-bank EMIs have virtually no standalone viability for a mainland WFOE as the primary treasury vehicle. PBOC and SAFE require onshore licensed commercial banks to open settlement accounts, monitor FX transaction codes, implement capital registration, and issue the documentary evidence required for auditing and outward remittance. An offshore EMI cannot perform tax withholding, double-taxation treaty statements, SAFE reporting, capital verification, or RMB netting. EMIs are feasible only as a supplemental payment card/wallet channel through a licensed onshore bank, never as the bank of record for cross-border capital or EBITDA repatriation.
