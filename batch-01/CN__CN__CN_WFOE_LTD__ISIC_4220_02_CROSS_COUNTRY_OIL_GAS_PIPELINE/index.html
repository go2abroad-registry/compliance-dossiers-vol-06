Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No mandatory minimum registered capital or paid-up capital rule applies to a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise limited liability company under the Company Law of the People's Republic of China. Shareholders may set the subscribed capital amount and contribution schedule in the Articles of Association, with full paid-in contribution required within five years after establishment. No blocked escrow capital account is required by the company registry. However, for ISIC 4220 cross-country oil and gas pipeline EPC contracting, the MOHURD construction enterprise qualification system generally applies a minimum registered capital/net capital benchmark and requires paid-in capital attestation for qualification grades; commercial project tenders may separately require bid bonds or performance security.

Funding & unlock path: Construction Fire Design Review and Fire Acceptance Certificate under the Fire Protection Law and the Regulations on Construction Fire Safety Review and Acceptance; Structural load and hydrostatic pressure test certificates for pipeline sections and above-ground installations; Special Equipment Use Registration with SAMR; Occupational health and safety pre-evaluation and completion evaluation report; Major Hazard Source filing and notification to the Emergency Management Bureau; Workplace safety production condition clearance for the construction site under the Work Safety Law of the People's Republic of China.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation - the WFOE is a PRC resident enterprise taxed as a separate juridical entity on worldwide income under the Enterprise Income Tax Law; no pass-through treatment is available in China.

Available election forms

None for US-style check-the-box or hybrid mismatch elections. Available statutory election: a taxpayer may elect simplified small-scale VAT taxpayer treatment until annual taxable turnover exceeds RMB 5,000,000, and may voluntarily elect general VAT taxpayer status before that threshold once formalized. Non-resident contractors have no election to be treated as resident enterprises for Chinese tax purposes.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration is triggered by annual taxable turnover above RMB 5,000,000. All WFOEs must perform tax registration within 30 days after incorporation irrespective of turnover; below RMB 5,000,000 annual turnover the taxpayer may remain a small-scale VAT taxpayer, while small-scale taxpayers must register as general VAT taxpayers once the threshold is crossed.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

General VAT taxpayers may apply 0% VAT to exported goods when the goods are physically exported from China under customs supervision, legal export restrictions are satisfied, and the exporter collects a sales price in convertible foreign exchange or RMB under approved cross-border mechanisms. Cross-border services, including qualifying offshore pipeline engineering and construction services supplied to a non-resident customer and consumed entirely outside China, are eligible for zero-rating under the cross-border services VAT concession; however, construction and installation services performed on real property located within China remain subject to VAT at 9%. The export claim must be evidenced by matching customs declaration data, invoice data, bank foreign exchange receipt data, and, where applicable, a written contract proving the service is rendered and enjoyed wholly outside China.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Customs Bill of Entry / Export Manifest)
  • Original Bill of Lading or Air Waybill
  • Export contract signed by the WFOE and the offshore customer
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Foreign Exchange Repatriation Certificate / Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) issued by a designated foreign exchange bank or the electronic SAFE export FX verification record
  • Export licenses or regulatory permits for oil and gas pipeline equipment and materials where controlled

China mandates digital VAT invoicing through the STA-controlled electronic invoice platform. General VAT taxpayers must issue Special VAT Invoices using the national tax invoice system; invoice data is transmitted in real time to the STA's Golden Tax System for validation, data matching, and anti-fraud verification. The current statutory e-invoicing standard is the Fully Digitalized Electronic Invoice (FDEI), which carries an anti-fraud invoice number, is filed with the STA in real time, and does not require physical stamping. Taxpayers must use certified electronic invoicing APIs or cloud-based tax control systems, reconcile the three-stream consistency of contract, invoice, and payment, and retain electronic customs and foreign exchange data to support zero-rated export sales.

The STA, Customs, SAFE, and banks exchange electronic data to verify that exported goods leave China, invoices match customs declarations, and sales proceeds are repatriated. If zero-rating cannot be substantiated, the taxpayer is denied the 0% rate, the output VAT is clawed back at the applicable rate (13% for goods or 9% for domestic construction services), late-payment interest accrues, and administrative penalties are imposed at 50%-500% of the unpaid or falsely refunded tax depending on severity. Deliberate fabrication of export declarations or foreign exchange receipts constitutes export tax fraud under Article 204 of the Criminal Law of the PRC, carrying fines, confiscation, and imprisonment, and may also trigger customs penalties under the Customs Law and deregistration of the export refund qualification.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

During the current account window, RMB is convertible for trade in goods and services, but every inward and outward current account payment must be reviewed against the goods trade monitoring system and the payment-to-settlement matching database maintained by SAFE. A WFOE used as the operational vehicle for cross-country pipeline EPC will enjoy full convertibility for current account items only after submitting the underlying contract, form receipts, invoices, customs declarations and customs valuation certificates. Capital account convertibility remains restricted. The initial equity injection must enter through a capital account bank confirmation letter, be declared as 'FDI establishment', and be settled to RMB only after a domestic certified public accountant issues a capital verification report. Profit remittance is lawful after CIT final settlement and SAFE current account review, but banks may require the tax clearance record, audit report and a corresponding profit remittance declaration. Surrender requirements apply mainly to export proceeds from cross-border pipeline construction materials and services; these amounts must be repatriated to China within the statutory timeline before conversion to RMB.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Statutory timeline: 90 days from the later of the export customs declaration date or, for cross-border services, the dated invoice/milestone certificate. Mandatory conversion ratio: 100% of export FX proceeds must be repatriated and settled to CNY unless the WFOE holds a valid SAFE-issued current account foreign exchange retention quota. Retention quotas, where granted, are limited to 50% of the prior annual verified current-account FX inflows and are renewable. For pipeline EPC material exports (linepipe, valves, welding sheds), the surrender obligation attaches to the full amount declared in the customs electronic record; any excess retained funds above the quota will be automatically converted by the designated bank at the China foreign exchange market central parity.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China, operating under the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Very Low; legally non-viable as an account provider. Foreign EMIs are generally barred from opening settlement or custodian accounts for mainland WFOEs because China's licensing system reserves account-keeping to domestic banks and licensed payment institutions. The WFOE may receive remittances from overseas EMI customer accounts as a sending network, but the ultimate clearing and receiving must be through a Chinese tier-1 commercial bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The shareholder's remittance must be sent via SWIFT MT103 from the shareholder's home bank to the WFOE's designated FDI capital account. Purpose code should indicate 'FDI capital contribution' with description 'paid-in equity capital for WFOE formation'; the receiving bank must validate the SAFE FDI registration number and remitter identity before crediting. After crediting, the branch issues a capital account inflow notice to the relevant commerce and tax authorities; settlement to RMB is permitted only after the bank's operational review and CPA capital verification report. For project funding beyond equity, an intercompany shareholder loan must be registered with SAFE and can be introduced only under the macro-prudential foreign debt ceiling.For cross-border upstream subcontractors and linepipe suppliers, the preferred clearing rail is an irrevocable commercial letter of credit issued at the WFOE's Chinese bank and advised through the SWIFT network. Direct operational payments should use SWIFT MT103 carrying the value date, underlying project contract, and harmonized system customs code. Documentary collections against full original bills of lading are acceptable for materials where the WFOE has a documented supply contract and no credit exposure. All outward remittances require submission of the original customs declaration, VAT invoice, tax invoice, and a valid service trade self-declaration where applicable.Deliverable CNY/USD forward contracts with a tenor matched to the underlying pipeline receivable/payable cash flow, executed through a SAFE-designated commercial bank with real transaction documentation.FX options (European style) for contingent EPC bid or milestone cash flows, priced and settled with a domestic bank and subject to a valid risk-management rationale, not speculative leverage.Cross-currency interest rate swaps and currency basis swaps for project bridge loans or foreign-currency finance, limited to the notional of registered foreign debt and requiring annual SAFE hedging declaration.Offshore non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are not permissible as a direct onshore instrument; an onshore WFOE can hedge only through deliverable onshore derivatives, with all losses and gains settled in CNY.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Very Low; legally non-viable as an account provider. Foreign EMIs are generally barred from opening settlement or custodian accounts for mainland WFOEs because China's licensing system reserves account-keeping to domestic banks and licensed payment institutions. The WFOE may receive remittances from overseas EMI customer accounts as a sending network, but the ultimate clearing and receiving must be through a Chinese tier-1 commercial bank.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File threshold: related-party tangible asset transfers of RMB 200 million or more; intangible property transfers or licenses of RMB 100 million or more; financial asset transfers of RMB 100 million or more; other related-party transactions of RMB 40 million or more. Master File threshold: aggregate related-party transactions exceeding RMB 1 billion in a single year, or where the group has a country-by-country reporting obligation. Country-by-Country Report threshold: group consolidated revenue of RMB 5.5 billion or more in the prior fiscal year.

Economic substance scope: China applies the economic substance doctrine through the EIT Law Article 47 General Anti-Avoidance Rule, the Special Tax Adjustment Rules, and the beneficial-ownership framework in Public Notice 2018 No. 9. A WFOE that serves as an EPC contractor for cross-country oil and gas pipelines must demonstrate real operational substance in China: actual staff, offices, project management, risk control, and core income-generating activities. Corporate entities interposed solely to exploit treaty benefits or shift profits to low-tax jurisdictions will be recharacterized, with adjustments, interest and penalties.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Transfer Pricing Manipulation through Offshore Service Fees

Statutory sanction: EIT Law Articles 41 and 44; Implementing Regulation Articles 109-123; State Taxation Administration Public Notice 2017 No. 6. The tax authority may re-determine taxable income using the TNMM or profit split method, impose interest from the tax year-end, assess a penalty of 50%-500% of the underpaid tax under Tax Collection Administration Law Article 63, and open a special tax investigation look-back period of up to ten years.

Director personal liability: Where the WFOE and its foreign parent are abused as mere instrumentalities, courts may pierce the corporate veil under Company Law Article 20 and make shareholders jointly liable. Directors who signed false intercompany agreements or payment vouchers may face personal civil restitution and joint liability for the unpaid corporate tax. The legal representative can be blacklisted by the tax authority, which triggers a travel ban and prevents removal from China under the Exit-Entry Administration Law, and criminal prosecution under Criminal Law Article 201 can lead to imprisonment of up to seven years for large-scale evasion.
Shell Company Treaty Relief Abuse / Beneficial Ownership Failure

Statutory sanction: EIT Law Article 47; State Taxation Administration Public Notice 2018 No. 9 on Beneficial Ownership. Treaty relief is denied, the statutory 10% withholding tax is collected, plus interest and a penalty of 50%-500% under Tax Collection Administration Law Article 63; the amount may also be recharacterized and subjected to general anti-avoidance adjustments under the Special Tax Adjustment Rules.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly approve false beneficial-ownership declarations or instruct the WFOE not to withhold are personally liable for the unwithheld tax under Article 69 of the Tax Collection Administration Law. The tax authority may freeze personal bank accounts, blacklist the legal representative, impose departure restrictions, and refer the case for criminal prosecution under Criminal Law Article 201, with civil restitution of diverted profits and imprisonment up to seven years.
False Contract Splitting and Fraudulent Invoicing

Statutory sanction: Tax Collection Administration Law Article 63 imposes a penalty of 50%-500% of the underpaid tax and collection of unpaid tax and late-payment surcharges. Criminal Law Article 201 applies to tax evasion; false invoicing for tax evasion may also be prosecuted under Criminal Law Article 205. For extremely large fraudulent amounts, imprisonment of three to ten years and substantial fines are available, in addition to administrative blacklisting and revocation of licenses.

Director personal liability: Courts may pierce the corporate veil under Company Law Article 20 if the WFOE was used to defraud creditors or tax authorities, making directors jointly and severally liable for the company's tax debts. Directors who instructed, concealed or signed false returns face personal asset seizure, entry-exit travel bans and blacklisting in the State Taxation Administration's credit system. On criminal conviction for tax evasion or fraudulent invoicing, directors may be sentenced to imprisonment up to seven years for tax evasion or up to ten years under the false special VAT invoice route, with personal fines and civil restitution of all wrongfully diverted assets.
