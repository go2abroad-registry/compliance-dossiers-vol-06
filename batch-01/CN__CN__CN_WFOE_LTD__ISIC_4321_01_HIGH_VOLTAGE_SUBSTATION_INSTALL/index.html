Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No minimum registered capital and no blocked escrow-account requirement apply to a standard Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise under current PRC Company Law and Foreign Investment Law. The Company Law imposes a five-year statutory deadline for shareholders to pay their subscribed capital in full unless applicable sectoral licensing rules require a shorter schedule. For the high-voltage substation installation business, the National Energy Administration permit system may test financial capability through subscribed and paid-in capital, audited net assets, and bonding capacity, so the registered capital must be rationally set at a level sufficient to secure the targeted Electric Power Installation (Maintenance, Testing) Facility Permit class and the relevant construction enterprise qualification. Capital may be contributed in cash or permitted non-cash assets, recorded in the registry, and is subject to stamp duty when paid in; no capital verification certificate is mandatory at the incorporation stage.

Funding & unlock path: Obtain the Fire Protection Design Review Opinion and Final Fire Acceptance Certificate from the local Fire and Rescue Division, covering transformer bay oil containment, GIS compartment fire detection and suppression, cable trench fire barriers, and emergency egress. Secure a structural load inspection certificate from a licensed structural engineer for the GIS hall, substation foundations, bus duct supports, and any underground cable vaults. If an underground basement or civil air-defense-protected zone is within the project footprint, obtain the civil air-defense review and approval from the local underground space authority; if none applies, confirm the exemption in writing. Under the Work Safety Law, obtain the Work Safety Production License and register workplace safety files with the Emergency Management Bureau, including high-voltage permit-to-work procedures, live-line operation rules, confined-space entry permits, and emergency rescue arrangements for electrical trauma and SF6 exposure.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation: The CN_WFOE_LTD is a Chinese resident limited-liability enterprise taxed on worldwide income at the statutory 25% corporate income tax rate; it is not a pass-through entity and no partner or shareholder flow-through treatment is available.

Available election forms

None for US-style check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch elections; China does not provide an entity-classification election for a WFOE. The only applicable election is the value-added tax status election between Small-scale VAT taxpayer and General VAT taxpayer, and the availability of simplified VAT collection for qualifying construction/installation services under the VAT implementing rules.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

9%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 0 for basic VAT registration; every taxable enterprise must be VAT registered at creation. Mandatory General VAT taxpayer registration arises at RMB 5,000,000 of annual taxable sales; below that threshold, the WFOE may elect Small-scale taxpayer treatment.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For goods, 0% VAT applies when the goods are physically exported from China under customs supervision, are declared on a valid customs export declaration, and the export proceeds are collected through a designated foreign-exchange bank within the legally prescribed period. For cross-border services, zero-rating or VAT exemption requires the services to be supplied to an overseas party and consumed outside China under the STA cross-border taxable services treatment; installation of a high-voltage substation qualifies only if the service itself is performed abroad or is directly associated with exported goods and the place-of-supply/consumption test is satisfied. A contract with an overseas customer alone is not sufficient; customs clearance, foreign-exchange receipt, and performance evidence are mandatory.

  • Customs Export Declaration Form (customs release record of the export goods declaration)
  • Export Sales Contract and Commercial Invoice
  • Ocean Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, or Railway Consignment Note evidencing cross-border shipment
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / Foreign Exchange Collection Proof issued by a designated foreign-exchange bank
  • Export VAT refund/zero-rating declaration data matched to the STA Golden Tax System and customs import/export electronic data
  • For cross-border installation services: service contract, evidence that the installation site and consumption are outside China, and foreign-currency payment and remittance documentation

Invoicing must be processed through the STA-controlled Golden Tax System and the VAT Invoice Inspection/Verification Platform. VAT invoices are generated through the Electronic Tax Bureau, fully digitalised electronic invoices carry a tax-authority-controlled invoice number and anti-fraud tax-control digital mark, and invoice data is transmitted to the STA in real time at issuance. Taxpayers must reconcile invoice data with each monthly/quarterly VAT return before the relevant filing deadline; abnormal invoices without tax-control clearance are blocked and cannot be used for input VAT credit.

The STA may re-audit any zero-rated export through data matching of customs declarations, foreign-exchange collection, logistics records, and VAT invoice files. If the export is not completed, foreign exchange is not collected within 180 days, required documents are absent, or the declaration is found false or fraudulent, the zero rate is reversed and VAT is assessed at the applicable positive rate (9% for construction/installation services or 13% for goods) plus late-payment interest. Under the Tax Collection and Administration Law, administrative fines range from 50% to 5 times the underpaid tax; fraudulent export-rebate claims can suspend the WFOE's VAT refund/zero-rating rights and expose the entity and responsible persons to criminal liability for fraudulent export tax refund under the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China maintains a managed floating exchange rate regime with strict capital account controls, while current account transactions are largely fully convertible. For a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE) in the electrical substation installation sector, foreign direct investment equity injections must be routed through a SAFE-registered capital account, and RMB settlement for current account operating expenses such as procurement of switchgear components, payroll, and subcontractor services enjoys free convertibility after verified authenticity documentation is submitted via the bank's integrated foreign exchange system. Profit remittance and capital repatriation, however, are categorized as capital account items. Dividends can be converted only after annual audit, tax clearance, and board resolution, and the bank will require evidence of the underlying audited retained earnings, tax payment vouchers, and a SAFE-derived remittance approval or registration code. Additionally, all foreign currency receipts related to export proceeds, if any, must be repatriated to China and either retained in designated foreign exchange settlement accounts or converted into RMB through automatic settlement mechanisms; there is no statutory surrender percentage since the 2012 reform, but every transaction must be fully supported by trade contracts, invoices, customs declarations, and logistics documentation. The practical hurdle for profit remittance from an electrical contractor WFOE is the need to reconcile project milestone invoices with actual service completion certificates, as banks and SAFE will scrutinize the commensurate fee for installation and maintenance services, transfer pricing margins, and any intra-group cross-border payments that could mask capital flight. Overall, the currency convertibility environment is viable for genuine trade and investment, but strict regulatory filing sequences and documentary audits necessitate a disciplined treasury operation with a designated SAFE liaison.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE Circular 1 (2020) amendments, export proceeds must be repatriated to China within 180 days from the date of shipment or completion of installation, but there is no mandatory conversion ratio; enterprises may deposit the foreign currency in an export proceeds settlement account with a domestic bank, convert on a voluntary basis, or retain in foreign exchange, subject to the condition that all funds are traceable to legitimate export contracts, customs declarations, and logistics evidence. The enterprise must file an export receipt record with SAFE through its business bank within the stipulated timeline, and any overdue amount without justified force majeure or contract delay is subject to foreign exchange violation penalties.

Strike-off trigger

Mandatory Audit Threshold Small Company Audit Exemption Regime

Turnover: PRC Company Law Article 164 and Accounting Law Article 9 mandate an independent certified CPA audit for every registered Chinese company, including a WFOE, regardless of size; therefore there is no balance-sheet, turnover, or headcount exemption from the statutory audit itself. For enterprises in the construction/electrical-contracting sector (ISIC 4321/NAICS 238210), additional quantified supervisory triggers are used by regulators to classify the company as a medium or large enterprise: (i) annual construction turnover equal to or exceeding RMB 40 million; (ii) total assets at the balance-sheet date equal to or exceeding RMB 50 million; or (iii) average annual headcount of 300 or more. Once any of those thresholds is satisfied, the enterprise must apply full PRC Accounting Standards (CAS) and present audited financial statements in any construction-qualification renewal, major project tender, credit facility, or foreign-exchange settlement procedure, and the audit report must be issued by a PRC-certified CPA firm registered with the Ministry of Finance and used to corroborate the annual corporate information report.

Quarterly advance CIT returns are due within 15 days after each quarter-end; annual return is due within 5 months after the fiscal year-end (May 31 for calendar-year filers); final balance payment is due by May 31; books and vouchers must be preserved for 10 years.

Box 1 / Line 1 Operating revenue (营业收入). Report all revenue from 500kV GIS substation installation contracts, including milestone progress payments, variation orders, and foreign-currency settlements translated at the applicable monthly or periodic average central parity rate; exclude VAT output tax collected from customers; intercompany services to foreign affiliates must be included at arm's length amounts. Any un-invoiced accrued revenue must still be declared if the project has reached the agreed completion stage. Late filing attracts a penalty of up to RMB 2,000, increased to RMB 10,000 for serious cases under TCAL Art. 62; late payment accrues a surcharge of 0.05% per day on the unpaid tax; understating taxable income or evading tax attracts a penalty of 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax and interest. This is an accounting result for operation; no reduction is permitted on this line for planned foreign shareholder distributions. Box 13 / Line 13 Total accounting profit. Must equal audited financial statements before tax adjustments. If the audited profit differs from the filed return, all differences must be itemized in the tax adjustment schedules and explained in the tax filing cover letter. Foreign investors cannot treat accounting profit as automatically distributable without a board resolution and the required withholding. Box 14 / Line 14 Taxable income after tax adjustments and loss carryforward. Add disallowances from Schedule A105000: excessive entertainment, advertising, related-party interest, depreciation differences, asset impairment reversals, and transfer pricing adjustments. Deductible tax losses expire after 5 years under the general rule; HNTE and science-technology SME categories may carry forward 10 years. If transfer pricing documentation was required and not timely prepared, the tax authority may impute profit upward. Box 15 / Line 15 Taxable income and applicable tax rate. Apply 25% statutory rate to taxable income, or 15% if HNTE/encouraged-industry qualification is held. A Chinese WFOE is a Chinese tax resident enterprise and is taxed on worldwide income; the offshore parent's residence does not reduce the WFOE's rate. Quarterly prepayments are credited here, and a minimum taxable-income amount cannot be manipulated by intercompany invoice timing. Box 16 / Line 16 Final tax due or refund after credits and prepayments. Compute annual tax payable, subtract quarterly advance payments, and claim foreign tax credits only for qualifying foreign-source income with a supporting foreign tax certificate; pay any balance by May 31. Overpayment can be applied or refunded administratively. For non-resident tax declarations, the WFOE as withholding agent must withhold 10% on actual dividend remittance to the foreign parent and file the separate withholding return; treaty relief requires the foreign parent's Tax Residency Certificate and beneficial ownership dossier before the reduced rate is applied, otherwise the tax authority will collect the full statutory withholding plus penalties.
