Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA); the electronic filing and compliance portal is the STA's integrated Electronic Tax Administration (e-tax platform), accessed uniformly by taxpayers through provincial-level Electronic Tax Bureaus.

E-government filing portal

For a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE) limited liability company, the foreign shareholder first registers the company with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR). The issued business license contains a Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which is automatically recognised as the taxpayer identification number (TIN) and VAT registration number by the State Taxation Administration; no separate TIN application is required. Within 30 days after the business license is obtained, the WFOE must complete post-establishment tax registration through the Electronic Tax Administration, including confirmation of the legal representative, registered address, tax classification (general VAT taxpayer or small-scale VAT taxpayer), and competent tax authority. The company then registers for VAT invoice issuance and enables fully digitalised electronic invoicing. A foreign non-resident enterprise without a permanent establishment in China that earns China-source income must separately register in the non-resident enterprise withholding tax and contractor case regime at the competent tax authority of the source payer or paying agent, and will receive a tax reference number for the withholding and declaration process.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

9%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 (CNY five million) in average annual taxable sales is the threshold requiring registration as a general VAT taxpayer; all incorporated businesses with taxable operations, including the WFOE, must complete tax registration regardless of turnover, and small-scale taxpayers below the threshold may elect general taxpayer status on a voluntary basis.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For exported goods, zero-rating eligibility requires the WFOE to be a VAT general taxpayer, to clear the goods through PRC customs under an export declaration, to sell to an overseas buyer, and to repatriate the foreign exchange proceeds to a PRC bank account in compliance with State Administration of Foreign Exchange rules. The export sale is reported at 0% VAT and the related domestic input VAT is claimable through the export tax rebate (exemption) mechanism. For cross-border taxable services, the services must fall within the categories listed in the cross-border taxable services zero-rate and exemption provisions (Caishui [2016] No. 36), be provided to a customer outside China, and be performed wholly outside China or otherwise qualify as exported services; for construction and installation services, onshore cryogenic pipelaying at a domestic LNG receiving terminal is not an export zero-rated supply and is taxable at 9% VAT, whereas offshore or exported construction services meeting the statutory conditions may be eligible for zero-rating. Qualifying offshore digital services, where applicable, must likewise be supplied to a non-resident recipient and fully consumed outside China, supported by a service contract, place-of-supply evidence, and foreign currency receipt documentation.

  • PRC Customs Export Goods Declaration Form (export declaration number generated in the China International Trade Single Window)
  • Original export sales contract or purchase order between the WFOE and the overseas buyer
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Bill of lading, multimodal bill of lading, or air waybill showing departure from PRC customs territory and arrival at the foreign destination
  • Bank inward remittance receipt or SAFE-accepted foreign exchange receipt or declaration record evidencing repatriation of export proceeds
  • Export VAT rebate declaration and supporting electronic tax authority acceptance record (when claiming refund)

Under the STA's Golden Tax Phase IV framework, VAT invoices must be issued and transmitted through the official e-tax and e-invoice service platform. The current fully digitalised electronic invoice (shu dian piao) regime requires the electronic issuance of invoices using STA-assigned invoice numbers and cryptographic signatures; real-time invoice data, including commercial content, tax amounts, counterparty information, and red-flush transactions, is reported to the STA through the electronic fiscal clearance system. High-volume taxpayers are integrated via system-to-system API clearance with the e-tax platform. Offline or non-approved invoicing, manual invoice splitting, and invoice reuse are monitored through mandatory digital stamping and anti-counterfeiting QR verification, and the recipient's tax digital account is automatically updated.

Export zero-rating and rebate claims are jointly reviewed by the STA and customs through the single-window and after-sale audit channels, with routine consistency checks against customs declarations, freight documents, Bank of China or SAFE settlement records, and the foreign exchange receipt ledger. If a zero-rated export claim is found to be false, unsupported by actual export, lacking foreign exchange repatriation, or designed to defraud the VAT rebate, the tax authority will claw back the refunded VAT, impose late-payment surcharge at 0.05% per day, and levy a tax evasion penalty of 50% to five times the underpaid tax under the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Tax Levy and Tax Collection. Fraudulent export tax refund may also constitute the crime of defrauding export tax refunds under the Criminal Law, carrying fines and imprisonment; in cases involving especially large amounts, penalties may include life imprisonment and confiscation of property. The exporter may additionally be disqualified from export rebate treatment for up to three years and may face customs administrative penalties for inaccurate declarations.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a managed floating exchange rate regime in which the RMB is convertible on current account but subject to strict registration, verification and real-trade-backing requirements on capital account. Current account items such as trade in goods, services and profit remittances may in principle be settled through designated banks after documentary compliance, while capital account items including FDI equity injection, shareholder loans, and repatriation of capital require SAFE registration or approval and must be channeled through specific capital accounts. The WFOE's LNG receiving terminal piping business will therefore face two layers of convertibility: first, the conversion of injected USD/EUR working capital into RMB via a capital account settlement procedure under the bank's supervision; second, the conversion of RMB operating profits back into foreign currency for dividend remittance to overseas shareholders. Profit remittance is generally permitted after audited financial statements confirm distributable profits and tax settlement (including withholding tax) has been completed, but banks may require the sponsor to provide prior SAFE registration, board dividend resolutions, and proof that no outstanding foreign debt or tax liabilities exist. Capital account transactions remain highly regulated: any change in business scope, transfer of equity, or increase/decrease of capital must be re-filed with SAFE before liquidation or subsequent cross-border transfers. In practice, this creates a controlled but operationally manageable environment for a project company with a clear EPC contract and audited books.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no longer a fixed mandatory surrender ratio for current account exports in mainland China. Export proceeds must be repatriated into China and collected through a licensed bank within 180 days from the date of export customs declaration (or the contractual collection date if earlier), then may be retained as foreign currency or sold to the bank at the market rate. All proceeds must be reported under the trade foreign exchange monitoring system and matched to customs data; exceptional extensions require SAFE approval.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) acting through the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Not viable as a primary banking rail. Foreign fintech EMIs and offshore digital banks are not licensed to maintain domestic RMB or FX settlement accounts for Chinese resident legal entities, and mainland law prohibits non-licensed institutions from engaging in foreign exchange settlement and sales or cross-border payment clearing. An offshore EMI can only be used as an auxiliary treasury service if it operates through a licensed Chinese partner bank and does not replace the WFOE's local corporate account; for statutory purposes the WFOE must continue to settle through a domestic Tier-1 bank under SAFE supervision.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The foreign parent must first complete SAFE FDI registration and obtain the FDI external debt/investment code, then remit the capital contribution through the SWIFT network to the WFOE's designated foreign exchange capital account at a domestic bank using the SAFE cross-border income declaration purpose code for 'Foreign Direct Investment – Paid-in Capital' and the narrative 'FDI capital injection for CN_WFOE_LTD'. The receiving bank will convert the foreign currency into RMB at the prevailing market rate under the capital account settlement rules, apply the RMB funds only to the approved business purpose, and issue the necessary settlement details to support the subsequent statutory capital verification and tax filings.Trade settlements for the LNG piping project will be cleared predominantly via SWIFT MT103 customer credits for direct supplier wires, SWIFT MT202/MT202 COV for interbank cover payments, and commercial letters of credit (MT700/MT707) opened by the WFOE or its parent through the Tier-1 bank for imported cryogenic pipe and valving. Documentary collections (D/P, D/A) may be used for contract balances where both parties have acceptable credit profiles. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: RMB 200,000,000 in annual related-party tangible asset transfers; RMB 100,000,000 in related-party intangible asset transfers; RMB 100,000,000 in related-party financial asset transfers; or RMB 40,000,000 in other related-party transactions (including services, royalties, interest, and leasing). A Master File is required where the worldwide group's combined revenue exceeds RMB 1 billion and the local threshold test is met, and Country-by-Country reporting is required where group consolidated revenue exceeds RMB 5.5 billion.

Economic substance scope: Although Mainland China does not apply Caribbean-style Economic Substance Regulations, the Chinese GAAR and anti-avoidance jurisprudence require a WFOE to demonstrate real economic substance, including actual decision-making in China, local employees, physical premises, and risk-bearing capacity. A WFOE receiving dividends or enjoying treaty benefits will be denied treaty protection if it is a pure shell, fails the beneficial ownership test, or has no meaningful CIGA in China. The SAT may recharacterize a thinly capitalized or conduit arrangement under EIT Law Articles 47 and 48, and may disallow deductions where the arrangement lacks a bona fide business purpose.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion by False Invoicing or Under-Declaration of Revenue

Statutory sanction: Criminal Law Article 201: fine of 50% to 500% of the evaded tax; unpaid tax recovery; demerit consequences. Tax Collection and Administration Law Article 63 imposes fines of 50% to 500% of unpaid tax and criminal referral where tax evasion exceeds the statutory threshold.

Director personal liability: Directors who initiate or approve the evasion may be jointly and severally liable for unpaid taxes and civil damages; they may face travel restrictions under the 'serious untrustworthy taxpayer' blacklisting regime and imprisonment up to 7 years plus a criminal fine, with personal bank accounts and assets frozen for restitution.
Transfer Pricing Profit Stripping and Internal Pricing Manipulation

Statutory sanction: EIT Law Article 41 and the Special Tax Adjustments Implementation Measures permit the SAT to make a transfer pricing adjustment and impose a daily interest surcharge on the tax underpaid for the period of adjustment; penalties may be mitigated only if the taxpayer has filed contemporaneous documentation.

Director personal liability: If the pricing manipulation is both false and intentional, the doctrine of piercing the corporate veil under Civil Law and Company Law holds directors personally liable for civil restitution to creditor and tax authorities; in aggravated cases with criminal intent, directors are personally prosecuted under Criminal Law Article 201 for tax evasion and are subject to personal asset seizure, travel bans, and imprisonment up to 7 years.
Treaty Abuse through a Shell/Conduit Foreign Parent

Statutory sanction: EIT Law Article 123 and the GAAR rules allow the SAT to ignore the conduit entity, deny treaty benefits, re-impose the 10% statutory WHT, and subject the company to late interest. A pattern of treaty shopping may also invoke anti-money laundering reporting and penalties under the Corporate Income Tax Law implementation rules.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly execute a conduit arrangement for tax avoidance may be personally ordered to cover the recovered tax and civil damages; they face administrative blacklisting, travel bans, and if the arrangement is disguised by false documents, criminal liability for tax fraud or money laundering, leading to imprisonment of up to 7 years and confiscation of personal assets.
