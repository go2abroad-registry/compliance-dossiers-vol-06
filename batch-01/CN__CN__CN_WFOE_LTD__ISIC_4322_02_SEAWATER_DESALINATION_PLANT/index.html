Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum registered capital is imposed by the PRC Company Law for a WFOE LLC engaged in desalination EPC works; the shareholders freely determine the registered capital in the Articles of Association, and capital may be paid in installments within five years as agreed. No escrow or blocked capital account is mandated by Chinese company law for this industry, except that any project financing escrow account required by lenders or SWIFT project payment rails must be opened under a separately executed escrow agreement with a qualified commercial bank.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification in the form of fire protection design review and fire facilities completion acceptance by the local Fire and Rescue Department; structural load inspection certificate from a qualified engineering inspector for all buildings, seawater intake structures, and pipe supports; workplace safety clearance by the Emergency Management Bureau covering occupational hazard evaluation, chemical and pressure equipment registration, confined-space entry protocols, electrical safety inspection, and emergency rescue facilities. Any underground civil defense facility, if incorporated into the plant, requires a concurrence certificate from the local civil defense / people air defense authority before occupancy.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA), acting through provincial and municipal tax service bureaus; primary online portal is the National Electronic Tax Service Platform (New Electronic Tax Service Platform), supplemented by the VAT Invoice Comprehensive Service Platform for invoice verification and export tax refund filing.

E-government filing portal

For the newly established Chinese WFOE, the TIN/VAT registration procedure is: (1) File for market entity registration with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) through the one-window enterprise registration system; the foreign investor must provide notarised and legalised incorporation documents, the legal representative's identity proof, articles of association, and business-scope description covering ISIC 4322 desalination EPC works. No minimum registered capital is required. (2) SAMR issues a business license with an 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which is automatically transmitted to the tax authority under the multi-license integration regime; the USCC is simultaneously the statutory Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) and VAT registration number for the WFOE. (3) Activate the tax registration through the electronic tax service platform, complete real-name authentication of the legal representative and financial officer, and file the financial accounting system and tax-filing responsible person. (4) If expected annual VAT taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000, apply for general VAT taxpayer status; otherwise the entity remains a small-scale VAT taxpayer until the statutory threshold is met. (5) Apply for invoice quotas and activate fully digitalised electronic invoice issuance. A pure non-resident foreign enterprise without a permanent establishment in China does not obtain a Chinese VAT registration; its Chinese counterparty or payer performs statutory withholding where applicable.

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export of tangible goods from mainland China qualifies for zero-rating (VAT exemption with input-VAT refund/rebate) when the exporter is a VAT general taxpayer, the goods are actually declared to and cleared by Chinese Customs for export, title and risk transfer to the overseas buyer, and export proceeds are received through a bank in accordance with State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) rules. For an offshore seawater desalination EPC contract, cross-border services and exported equipment and materials may be zero-rated only where the services are provided to a non-resident recipient and the relevant service is performed outside mainland China; services consumed or performed inside China remain taxable at the applicable rate (9% for construction services and 13% for goods, as applicable). The WFOE must maintain a valid EPC contract, overseas end-user evidence, customs declarations, logistics documents, export invoices, payment matching records, and SAFE foreign-exchange receipts to substantiate the zero-rate claim.

  • Customs Export Declaration prepared through the China International Trade Single Window for the exported desalination equipment and materials
  • Commercial invoice issued by the WFOE to the overseas purchaser
  • Detailed packing list
  • Bill of Lading for sea freight or air waybill / multimodal transport document evidencing physical departure from mainland China
  • Export contract or EPC works contract with the overseas project company
  • Foreign-exchange receipt voucher (bank inward remittance advice or cross-border receipt and payment declaration) issued by a SAFE-designated bank evidencing repatriation of export proceeds

China operates a nationwide fully digitalised electronic invoice (FDEI / all-electric invoice) system on the National Unified Electronic Invoice Service Platform. Taxpayers issue invoices in near real time through the electronic tax platform; the tax authority applies automated tax-control validation, digital stamping, invoice-number verification, and real-time clearance/risk screening. Traditional VAT special invoices are issued using tax-control UKey equipment and must be uploaded to the VAT Invoice Comprehensive Service Platform; buyers must complete invoice verification/confirmation within 360 days from issuance to claim input VAT credits. Anti-fraud controls include encrypted QR codes, national invoice numbers, digital fiscal stamps, invoice-quota management, and automated reconciliation between invoicing data, VAT returns, and customs declaration data.

The tax authority performs automated cross-matching of customs export declarations, export invoices, logistics documents, VAT tax returns, and bank foreign-exchange receipts. Zero-rating is disallowed if the tax authority finds no genuine export, no overseas qualifying recipient, incomplete documentation, or failure to receive export proceeds within the applicable foreign-exchange/tax-rebate deadlines; the entity is then required to pay VAT at the standard rate plus late-payment surcharge of 0.05% per day. Under the Tax Collection Administration Law, fraudulent export tax refunds or false zero-rating declarations can trigger recovery of the entire refunded amount and an administrative penalty of 50% to 5 times the tax evaded or refunded, and serious cases may be referred for criminal liability for tax-refund fraud. Export tax refund claims are due tax-year based under the statutory export tax refund management regime; failing to secure timely foreign-exchange evidence can cause retrospective clawback of input VAT already refunded.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a managed floating exchange rate regime with RMB current-account convertibility and capital-account convertibility subject to administrative control. Under the Foreign Exchange Control Regulations (1996, revised 2008) and the Measures for Administration of Foreign Exchange in Direct Investment, a WFOE is treated as a resident enterprise after incorporation. Inbound equity capital injection from the foreign parent is a capital-account transaction requiring SAFE registration and approval; the funds must enter a designated FDI capital account and, once verified, can be converted to RMB for permitted business use under the negative-list system. Current-account receipts, including revenue from the desalination EPC works, are freely convertible but must be received into a domestic foreign-exchange account and verified through the As-One goods-trade monitoring system. Profit remittance to the overseas parent is classified as a current-account item but requires an audited annual financial statement, corporate income tax final settlement proof, and a board resolution declaring dividends. There is no mandatory surrender of export proceeds in the current policy, but all export receipts must be repatriated to China within the statutory verification cycle. Capital-account outflows, such as repayment of foreign shareholder loans, require additional SAFE registration and must be matched to genuine project cash flows.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE foreign exchange settlement policy, there is no mandated conversion ratio for export foreign-currency receipts; the statutory obligation is repatriation and verification. Export proceeds from goods trade must be received into a domestic designated foreign-exchange account within 180 days from the date of Customs export declaration (unless a longer term is pre-registered with SAFE). Once received, the enterprise may retain the foreign currency or sell it to a domestic bank at the market rate. For desalination EPC equipment exports and related services, the underlying contract and Customs declarations must be uploaded to the As-One system for verification; non-repatriation or late repatriation triggers corrective penalties.

Central bank supervision: The People's Bank of China (PBOC), with delegated regulatory execution by the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity capital must be remitted via SWIFT MT103 from the offshore parent to the WFOE's dedicated FDI capital account at a SAFE-designated domestic branch. The wire instruction must cite the SAFE FDI Registration Certificate number and the purpose code 'FDI equity investment' (use the standard code assigned during registration). Upon receipt, the bank reports the transaction to the SAFE cross-border payment reporting system; the company then obtains a capital verification report from a licensed Chinese CPA within six months of the contribution. RMB conversion is executed in tranches under the 'payment after verification' regime, meaning the bank inspects each disbursement's underlying genuine documents (EPC contract, supplier invoices, payroll, lease agreements) before converting. Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File mandatory where related-party tangible asset transactions exceed RMB 100 million, intangible asset or financial asset related-party transactions exceed RMB 100 million, or other related-party transactions exceed RMB 40 million in the preceding fiscal year; Master File is statutory where the Chinese entity's total related-party transactions reach RMB 1 billion, or where the multinational group fails the relevant turnover or documentation threshold under SAT Announcement 2016 No. 42.

Economic substance scope: China has not enacted the Gulf-style Economic Substance Regulations, but the OECD-endorsed substance concept is embedded in Chinese tax law through the beneficial ownership doctrine, the non-resident treaty shopping rules, the general anti-avoidance rule under Article 47 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, and the place of effective management analysis. Core Income-Generating Activities (CIGA) for the seawater desalination EPC entity include engineering design, procurement, mobilization of construction crews, site management, QA/QC supervision, and financing or risk management decisions; the WFOE must prove board meetings, employee headcount, cost origination, decision-making, and project contracts are actually performed in China, otherwise a temporary or deemed agency/PE can create non-resident corporate income tax exposure, treaty denial, and 10% withholding on recharacterized China-source income.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Intentional False Filing in the Annual Corporate Income Tax Return

Statutory sanction: Article 63 of the Tax Collection and Administration Law: recovery of the underpaid tax and late-payment surcharge plus a penalty of 50% to five times the evaded amount. Article 201 of the Criminal Law: criminal tax evasion liability for evaded tax above RMB 50,000 and more than 10% of the tax payable, leading to imprisonment of up to seven years, a fine, and public registration of serious dishonest tax conduct.

Director personal liability: A court may pierce the WFOE corporate veil where the directors or parent treat the entity as an alter ego, commingle funds, and use the corporate form to evade taxes. The legal representative, finance director, and beneficial owner may face joint civil liability, seizure of personal assets, travel bans and exit restriction, blacklisting under the serious tax violation publicity rules, disqualification from acting as a director, and criminal imprisonment where the director personally caused the false filing.
WHT Non-Compliance on Outbound Dividends, Interest, and Royalties

Statutory sanction: Article 69 of the Tax Collection and Administration Law: withholding agent shall be ordered to recover the unrecovered tax and is liable for a penalty of 50% to three times the tax that was not withheld. Article 32 additionally imposes a 0.05% daily surcharge on the late tax payment. A false treaty-relief form may be treated as fraudulent tax avoidance.

Director personal liability: A director who individually orders or approves an unlawful outward remittance may be jointly and severally liable to restore the corporate funds and satisfy the withheld taxes; directors can be subject to exit-control penalties and a departure ban while the entity remains listed as a serious tax violator. If the unreported withholding is part of a scheme to conceal related-party profit, the director may be criminally charged as the person directly in charge of the tax evasion offence.
Transfer Pricing Profit Shifting and Shell-Entity Substance Deficiency

Statutory sanction: The tax authority may adjust taxable income under Articles 41 and 44 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law and impose special tax adjustment interest at the Chinese benchmark lending rate plus five percentage points from the first day of the tax payment default. Failure to provide the required contemporaneous documentation permits the tax authority to use estimated assessment and to impose enforcement penalties under the Tax Collection and Administration Law, including upward adjustment, interest, and fines for non-compliance with documentation orders.

Director personal liability: Chinese courts may pierce the corporate veil and hold the non-resident shareholder or controlling directors liable for unpaid corporate tax and project liabilities when the WFOE is only a shell and the foreign parent exercises complete economic control. The WFOE directors may face civil restitution, joint liability for the special adjustment, travel restrictions, and potential criminal prosecution for tax evasion as persons actually controlling the entity if deliberate profit stripping and documentation concealment are proven.
