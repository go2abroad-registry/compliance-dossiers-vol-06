Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

For an ISIC 4329 refractory brick masonry WFOE intending to contract with industrial kiln operators and to open bank accounts in China, a pure virtual office or commercial mailbox is rejected by the Municipal Market Supervision Administration because the registered address must be a lawful, usable physical premises that matches the business scope and is suitable for hosting personnel, equipment storage, and practical management work. Some provinces allow 'address hosting' or 'clustering registration' for technology or consulting firms under pilot policies, but the industrial boiler refractory lining contracting industry is not eligible unless the WFOE obtains a valid construction enterprise qualification (which itself requires a physical office, safety-management personnel, and tooling). Banks are also required under PBOC AML rules to verify the registered office through on-site inspection or third-party data; a virtual mailbox without a lease contract, property ownership certificate, or a formal 'place of business' certificate from the local sub-district office will cause account opening to be refused and the taxpayer registration to be classified as 'abnormal'. Therefore, the WFOE must execute a real property lease for at least 12 months, register the lease with the local housing administration, and obtain a rent invoice before applying for the tax registration and bank account.

Competent tax authority

E-government filing portal

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation, meaning the WFOE is a Chinese resident enterprise taxed on worldwide income under the Enterprise Income Tax Law; no pass-through classification is available.

Available election forms

None; China does not provide check-the-box or hybrid mismatch elections under domestic tax law. A WFOE is mandatorily taxed at entity level, although a VAT taxpayer with annual taxable turnover below RMB 5,000,000 may voluntarily elect general VAT taxpayer status if it meets the STA accounting and invoice-control requirements.

9%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Annual taxable sales exceeding RMB 5,000,000 triggers mandatory registration as a general VAT taxpayer; WFOEs are automatically tax-registered upon incorporation regardless of turnover, and small-scale taxpayer status applies below RMB 5,000,000 with a simplified levy rate unless general taxpayer status is elected.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

VAT zero-rating for exported goods is available to a PRC VAT general taxpayer when the refractory materials or prefabricated modules are actually shipped out of PRC customs territory, covered by a valid export customs declaration, sold to an overseas customer, and the export proceeds are repatriated to China within the prescribed foreign-exchange period. On-site refractory lining installation performed inside China for a kiln or boiler is a domestic taxable construction service subjectto 9% VAT; only separately exported refractory goods and physically exported movable refractory units can qualify for export zero-rating. Qualifying cross-border services performed and consumed entirely outside China may also be zero-rated under STA rules on exported services.

  • Customs Export Declaration (the PRC export goods declaration equivalent of a Bill of Entry), as cleared by the General Administration of Customs with the export declaration number and customs valuation
  • Bill of Lading or equivalent multimodal transport document, air waybill, or international cargo receipt evidencing physical dispatch across the PRC customs border
  • Commercial invoice, packing list, and signed export sales contract between the WFOE and the overseas buyer
  • Export VAT invoice (Special VAT Invoice or electronic invoice where input VAT refund is claimed) issued in accordance with STA invoice-filing rules
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or the exporting bank's foreign-exchange collection verification record, together with SAFE interoperable electronic data, evidencing repatriation of export proceeds
  • Certificate of origin, export licence, or restricted-goods permit where refractory materials are subject to export control requirements

All VAT invoices must be issued through the STA-authorised tax-control system or the electronic invoice service platform under the Golden Tax Phase IV architecture. Invoice data is transmitted to the STA in near real time; the fully digitalized electronic invoice (FDEI) regime applies a national anti-counterfeiting digital stamp and QR code. Special VAT invoices must be authenticated and cleared in the National VAT Invoice Management System before input VAT credit can be claimed; API-based automated invoice issuance and reporting is required for enterprises using integrated accounting systems.

The STA may conduct post-export document verification and on-site audits of export zero-rating claims. If the mandatory customs and foreign-exchange repatriation bundle is incomplete, false, forged, or if export proceeds are not received within the statutory deadline, the export sale will be recharacterised as a domestic taxable supply, the zero-rating benefit will be clawed back, and input VAT refunds already released must be repaid with late-payment interest. Administrative penalties range from 50% to 5 times the tax or refund amount involved for deceptive export declarations, export tax-rebate status may be suspended or revoked, and criminal liability for export tax refund fraud may be pursued under the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a managed floating exchange rate regime with current-account convertibility largely liberalised while capital-account convertibility remains carefully controlled. A Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE) engaged in refractory masonry contracting can freely convert RMB for current-account transactions such as trade settlements, service fees, salaries and operational expenses under the 'real transaction background' principle. However, capital-account flows, including foreign direct investment injection, foreign debt, equity transfers and profit remittances, require specific registration with SAFE or its delegated banks. Export proceeds from refractory lining services or imported materials must be repatriated to China unless SAFE approval permits offshore retention. Profit remittance is permissible once annual audited financial statements show distributable after-tax profits, and the WFOE has completed corporate income tax settlement and paid any applicable withholding tax. Dividends are then routed through the bank within the recorded capital-account quota, subject to submission of tax payment certificates, audit reports and a board resolution declaring dividends. Strict documentary checks and a requirement that the commercial substance of each transaction be supported by contracts, customs declarations, service vouchers and bank settlement records materially constrain any attempt to shift funds prematurely or speculate in cross-border flows. Foreign exchange positions are not freely convertible for speculative hedging unless the WFOE demonstrates genuine risk exposure in goods or services. Enterprises must comply with SAFE's annual reporting and business registration filing requirements, and any capital injection must be verified by a certified public accountant under the Foreign Investment Law.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export proceeds for refractory materials and construction services must be repatriated to China within 180 days from the date of customs declaration or service completion, unless SAFE has approved an extended retention period. There is no mandatory conversion ratio under the current post-2012 reform framework; WFOEs may retain export receipts in a foreign-currency current-account deposit account or voluntarily settle into RMB. However, every receipt must be tied to an underlying customs record, service invoice, contract or documentary collection instrument, and banks will file the receipt details with SAFE through the cross-border foreign exchange monitoring system.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) and State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File thresholds in RMB: tangible property transactions equal or exceed RMB 200 million; financial asset transfers equal or exceed RMB 100 million; intangible asset transactions equal or exceed RMB 100 million; other related-party transactions equal or exceed RMB 40 million. Master File is required when total annual related-party transactions equal or exceed RMB 1 billion. Country-by-Country Report is required when the consolidated group revenue for the preceding year equals or exceeds RMB 5.5 billion.

Economic substance scope: Mainland China does not operate Gulf-style Economic Substance Regulations, but economic substance is central to Chinese tax residence, permanent establishment determination, beneficial ownership analysis, and treaty benefit entitlement. The place of effective management, core income-generating activities, employee functions, premises, asset ownership, and risk assumption are all examined when the SAT addresses cross-border arrangements. A WFOE or offshore intermediate company with no substantive operational activity is vulnerable to recharacterization, denial of treaty benefits, GAAR adjustments, and administrative penalties.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Fraud Through False Special VAT Invoices

Statutory sanction: PRC Criminal Law Article 205 imposes criminal liability for issuing false special VAT invoices, including unit fines, confiscation of property, and imprisonment of directly responsible persons, potentially up to life imprisonment. Under the Tax Administration Law Article 63, tax evasion results in a penalty of 50% to 500% of the unpaid tax plus daily late surcharge.

Director personal liability: Directors, the CFO, and the legal representative who organize, approve, or conceal the false invoice scheme are personally subject to criminal prosecution, imprisonment, fines, civil asset restitution, travel bans, and tax-related blacklisting. The corporate form will not shield them where their personal conduct caused the fraudulent filing.
Corporate Income Tax Evasion and Cross-Border Profit Shifting

Statutory sanction: Enterprise Income Tax Law Articles 41 and 47 authorize the SAT to make transfer pricing and general anti-avoidance adjustments, recharacterize the transactions, and impose ordinary tax, interest, and penalties. Under Tax Administration Law Article 63, intentional understatement attracts 50% to 500% of unpaid tax. Criminal Law Article 201 provides imprisonment of up to 3 years for large evasion and 3 to 7 years for huge evasion.

Director personal liability: Directors, de facto controllers, and senior managers who directed the concealment or falsification may be named as directly responsible persons, subject to criminal imprisonment, personal fines, seizure of personal assets, exit bans, and blacklisting. The court may pierce the corporate veil and hold directors jointly liable for the WFOE's unpaid taxes where corporate assets were diverted or the entity was used as a mere instrument of fraud.
Shell Company Abuse, Treaty Shopping, and Absence of Beneficial Ownership

Statutory sanction: Under the General Anti-Avoidance Rule in Enterprise Income Tax Law Article 47 and Implementation Regulations Article 120, the SAT may disregard the abusive arrangement, deny treaty benefits under the Principal Purpose Test, and impose a special tax adjustment with interest and penalties up to 500% of the additional tax. The offshore shell may be blacklisted and its treaty benefits retroactively denied.

Director personal liability: If directors allow the business to operate without substance while using the corporate veil to evade tax or conceal ownership, they can face civil liability for restitution of diverted revenue, joint and several liability for tax debts, blacklisting, travel bans, and criminal prosecution for tax evasion or money laundering where funds are routed through shell accounts.
