Competent corporate registry

State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR), acting through the local municipal or district Market Supervision Administration of the registered office; in special economic zones or free trade zones, the integrated FTZ administrative service window will process the SAMR filing and issue the business licence.

Governing statute

Company Law of the People's Republic of China (2023 Revision), effective 1 July 2024, Articles 4, 23, 26, 27, 29, 33, 35, 47, 54, 58, 66 and 96; Foreign Investment Law of the People's Republic of China (2019), Articles 6, 12 and 29; Administrative Regulation on Market Entity Registration (State Council Order No. 746); Provisions on Foreign Investment Information Reporting (MOFCOM Order No. 62 of 2020).

Formation / constitutional filings

Application for Market Entity Registration (Company Registration), accompanied by the Articles of Association and the Foreign Investment Information Report; the SAMR business licence bearing the Unified Social Credit Code constitutes the certificate of incorporation.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to a general WFOE under the 2023 Company Law; however, Article 47 requires all shareholders to pay the subscribed capital in full within five years from incorporation or within a shorter period specified in the Articles of Association. No blocked escrow account is required by SAMR. Because the WFOE will apply for a construction enterprise qualification for curtain wall or specialized façade work, the capital subscription should be aligned with the applicable MOHURD qualification matrix, which may require a minimum registered capital of RMB 10 million to RMB 20 million and net-asset verification depending on the qualification grade. The capital account must be registered with the State Administration of Foreign Exchange through the receiving bank before foreign currency contributions are remitted.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; online portal: Electronic Tax Service Platform (E-Tax Bureau) accessible at etax.chinatax.gov.cn.

E-government filing portal

1. Within 30 days of obtaining the SAMR business license, the WFOE must complete tax registration and tax identity enrollment at its competent local STA office through the Electronic Tax Service Platform; the system links the USCC to the VAT, Corporate Income Tax, Individual Income Tax, and non-resident withholding registration modules. 3. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None. The People's Republic of China does not provide check-the-box entity classification or hybrid mismatch elections. A China-incorporated WFOE is automatically a Chinese tax resident enterprise and is taxed at entity level under the Corporate Income Tax Law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

All PRC-established entities, including WFOEs, must complete VAT registration within 30 days of the SAMR business license; there is no turnover threshold for basic tax registration. For VAT status classification, annual taxable sales exceeding RMB 5,000,000 mandate general VAT taxpayer registration; enterprises with annual sales at or below RMB 5,000,000 may remain small-scale taxpayers unless they voluntarily apply for general taxpayer status to claim input VAT credits and export refunds.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export of goods: a VAT general taxpayer may apply zero-rating for goods that are actually declared to China Customs, physically exported from the customs territory, supported by genuine export sale documentation, and for which export proceeds are collected in foreign exchange within the statutory period. Qualifying cross-border supply of services: zero-rating or exemption applies to designated offshore services such as design, research, IT, and consulting supplied by a Chinese VAT taxpayer to a non-resident recipient where the services are performed entirely outside China or consumed wholly offshore and the statutory service-export documentation is filed; however, on-site rope-access facade and structural-glass-replacement services physically performed on real estate located in China are domestic construction services subject to VAT at 9% and are not zero-rated. For services rendered on buildings located outside China, the performance-site test must be satisfied through evidence that personnel, equipment, and work-site operations are wholly outside China.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Export Goods Customs Declaration Form) accepted and released by China Customs
  • Ocean or air transport evidence, namely a Bill of Lading or Air Waybill showing actual export movement
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List matching the customs declaration
  • Export sales contract and, where applicable, certificate of origin or export license
  • VAT special invoice for the exported goods (or export invoice) reflecting the zero-rated supply
  • Bank-issued Export Proceeds Collection Confirmation / Inward Remittance Certificate evidencing foreign exchange repatriation within the statutory period

China operates a mandatory national electronic invoicing and anti-fraud clearance system. VAT invoices must be issued through STA-approved tax-control digital equipment or through the fully digitalized electronic invoice system on the Electronic Tax Service Platform; invoice data, including the unique invoice code and cryptographic QR stamp, is transmitted in real time to the STA Golden Tax System for validation, matching with customs and foreign exchange data, and anti-fraud analytics. Fully digitalized e-invoices require no physical stamping or paper filing; reconciliation, verification, and export-rebate matching are performed electronically.

Claiming 0% VAT on exports without satisfying the Customs, logistics, contract, and foreign-exchange repatriation requirements is treated as an unauthorised export-tax-refund claim. The STA may retrospectively open a three-year or longer audit window in civil tax-evasion cases and perform bulk data triangulation between Customs export releases, logistics tracking, bank FX receipts, and invoice clearance. If zero-rating is denied, the supply is recharacterized as a domestic taxable supply: the exporter must pay output VAT at the applicable rate (9% for construction services or 13% for goods), repay any input VAT refund or export rebate previously received, and pay a late-payment surcharge at 0.05% per day. Administrative penalties range from 50% to 500% of the tax evaded; intentionally fraudulent export-refund claims may additionally constitute criminal liability under Article 204 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, with penalties including imprisonment up to life imprisonment and confiscation of property.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China maintains current-account convertibility and closed, approval-based capital-account convertibility. For the registered WFOE, foreign exchange receipts under genuine trade and service contracts may be settled at a SAFE-designated domestic bank as long as supporting documentation is presented. Cross-border capital injection for registered capital is credible only after SAFE's outward-remittance registration and bank-level verification; capital cannot be casually re-deployed to equity investments or real estate outside the scope. Current-account outflows, including service fees and import payments, must be underpinned by contracts, invoices, tax declarations, and valid business licences; profit remittance is permitted only after annual audit, tax clearance, and legal-reserve accrual in compliance with the WFOE Law. Surplus funds under capital account face tight restrictions, and any change in currency denomination or settlement basis may trigger fresh SAFE filings. Long-term convertibility hinges on the project's economic authenticity, the bank's compliance rating, and the absence of adverse media; hence the banking relationship must be structured with full evidentiary transparency.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE's export collection verification rules for goods trade, export proceeds must be repatriated to China through a designated bank within 180 days of the export clearance date; the mandatory surrender ratio has been abolished since 2012, so the statutory conversion ratio is zero percent. Receipts must be recorded in the SAFE monitoring system and either retained in an eligible foreign-exchange account or settled into RMB at the bank's spot rate against a complete customs and commercial document set. For this WFOE's cross-border construction services, high-altitude facade proceeds are current-account service receipts and must be declared under service trade with signed statements of work, invoices, and bank records; no goods-customs timeline applies unless construction materials are physically exported.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Low. Offshore digital EMIs such as Revolut, Wise, or Airwallex do not hold a PBOC banking licence or a SAFE cross-border FX settlement licence and are therefore legally barred from servicing a mainland WFOE as a local clearing or account-holding institution. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The parent must route a SWIFT MT103 wire to the WFOE's SAFE-registered Foreign Exchange Capital Account at the nominated Tier-1 bank, using the SAFE purpose code for foreign direct investment capital injection and quoting the WFOE's unified social credit code in the remittance field. The receiving bank, after checking the SAFE registration record, will acknowledge the capital inflow and permit capital settlement into RMB; this triggers a capital verification report by a licensed Chinese CPA, related industrial and commercial filings, and tax registration. No part of the capital can be redirected to securities, entrusted loans, or real estate beyond the approved business scope.Progress payments for labour and small material purchases are cleared through SWIFT MT103 wires executed by the domestic bank's international clearing channel; medium-value facade procurement is settled by Sight Commercial Letters of Credit opened at the WFOE's nominated bank; documentary collections are used for invoice-backed disbursements when the counterparty accepts deferred payment. RMB-denominated cross-border settlements must be cleared through CIPS (Cross-Border Interbank Payment System) while USD and EUR flows use correspondent bank rails; every outward payment must be matched to an inward guarantee or underlying project invoice and screened by the bank's sanctions and anti-money-laundering filter.RMB/USD spot and forward contracts with genuine trade-background support and SAFE registration at a licensed onshore bank.Foreign exchange swaps and currency swaps matching the tenor of the WFOE's future facade revenue or supplier payments, subject to CFETS/Shanghai Clearing House rules.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are not permitted onshore; offshore NDF access cannot be booked in the China banking book.Long-dated cross-currency swaps for contracted capital expenditure are permissible only with SAFE special approval and a maturity profile aligned to the underlying cash flows.

Annual return and self-assessment must be filed within five months after the close of the calendar tax year, i.e. on or before May 31 of the following year. Quarterly corporate income tax prepayments are due within 15 days after the end of each quarter.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: RMB 200,000,000 for mandatory Local File (annual related-party tangible goods transactions); RMB 100,000,000 for financial assets; RMB 50,000,000 for intangible assets and financial loans; Master File is applicable when total annual related-party transactions exceed RMB 1,000,000,000; Country-by-Country notification is triggered where the ultimate parent's consolidated revenue is above RMB 5,500,000,000.

Economic substance scope: China does not apply a separate Economic Substance Regulations framework, but the General Anti-Avoidance Rule under Article 47 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law and the substance-over-form principle recharacterize arrangements lacking commercial purpose. A WFOE performing rope-access facade services must demonstrate core income-generating activities in China: contracts, skilled employees, site supervision, decision-making, equipment, bank accounts and actual billing. A shell or conduit arrangement created solely to obtain treaty benefits or strip profits offshore will be disregarded and reassessed.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and False Declaration

Statutory sanction: Article 63 of the Tax Collection Administration Law imposes recovery of additional tax, a financial penalty of 50% to 500% of the tax underpaid, plus 0.05% daily late-payment interest. Article 201 of the Criminal Law imposes criminal fines and imprisonment of up to seven years where the evaded amount is significant and exceeds 10% of payable tax.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced when the WFOE is used as an instrument of fraud. Directors and directly responsible persons are jointly and severally liable for civil restitution of tax underpaid, their personal assets may be attached, they may be placed on the tax blacklist and exit-banned, and they risk criminal prosecution with up to seven years' imprisonment.
Transfer Pricing Abuse and Thin Capitalization

Statutory sanction: Articles 41-46 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law and the special tax adjustment rules require transfer pricing adjustment with interest computed on the adjusted tax. Excess interest under the 2:1 debt-to-equity ratio for general enterprises is non-deductible and recharacterized as a deemed dividend subject to 10% withholding tax. Late-payment surcharges and fines up to five times the underpaid tax apply.

Director personal liability: Directors who approve or implement non-arm's-length related-party transactions can be personally assessed where the corporate form is used to evade tax. They face civil recovery, travel bans, blacklisting, and if they conceal assets or use the company to evade taxation, criminal liability under Articles 201 and 203 of the Criminal Law with imprisonment up to seven years.
Shell Company and Treaty Shopping Abusive Structure

Statutory sanction: Article 47 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law and the General Anti-Avoidance Rule allow the tax authority to disregard the shell structure, deny treaty benefits, recharacterize the income as Chinese-source, and impose full 10% (or higher) withholding tax, with late interest and penalties under Articles 48 and 49 of the Tax Collection Administration Law.

Director personal liability: Where the WFOE is merely a sham and the directors misuse limited liability to deceive tax authorities, the personal civil liability of directors attaches to restored taxes, interest and fines. Directors face exit bans, seizure of personal assets, blacklisting, and criminal prosecution for fraudulent tax avoidance with imprisonment potential under Article 201 of the Criminal Law.
