A pure virtual office or commercial mailbox is not acceptable as the sole registered office of a WFOE importing specialty green coffee. Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum registered capital applies to a wholly foreign-owned limited liability company engaging in wholesale distribution of specialty green coffee beans under ISIC 4631. The registered capital shall be determined by the sole shareholder in the Articles of Association and may be subscribed within five years in accordance with the 2023 Company Law. Capital contributions may be made in cash or in kind, must be recorded in the company's capital verification ledger, and foreign currency subscriptions must be converted into RMB and paid into a domestic capital contribution bank account. There is no requirement for an escrow or blocked capital account for this non-licensed wholesale distribution activity.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification is required for any leased warehouse handling commercial volumes of green coffee beans: the facility must obtain a Fire Safety Inspection Certificate from the local Fire and Rescue Department under the Fire Protection Law of the People's Republic of China, including verification of fire separation, portable extinguishers, emergency lighting, and egress routes. The Company must also obtain forklift annual inspection and operator certification, electrical safety inspection, and a work-safety filing with the local Emergency Management Bureau under the Work Safety Law. No civil defense construction permit or special hazardous-chemical civil defense clearance is applicable because green coffee beans are not dangerous goods and the warehouse is not an engineered civil-defense protected structure.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum registered capital applies to a wholly foreign-owned limited liability company engaging in wholesale distribution of specialty green coffee beans under ISIC 4631. Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA), People's Republic of China; administrative access through the National Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax) online portal at the taxpayer's local STA competent bureau jurisdiction.

E-government filing portal

Upon incorporation before the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR), the WFOE obtains an 18-character Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) printed on the Business License. The USCC functions as the statutory Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) and VAT registration number. Tax registration data is automatically synchronized from SAMR to the STA; the WFOE must complete supplementary taxpayer registration details and confirm banking, accounting, and chief tax officer information in the Electronic Tax Bureau. A foreign parent does not require a separate Chinese TIN for the WFOE's operational VAT registration. If a foreign enterprise directly supplies taxable services to Chinese customers without an establishment, it must register under the Non-Resident Enterprise Tax Identification Number protocol or rely on a Chinese withholding agent; however, the WFOE itself is a Chinese resident enterprise taxed at entity level.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None. China does not provide check-the-box pass-through elections or hybrid mismatch elections for WFOEs. A WFOE is taxable as a resident enterprise on worldwide income, and VAT registration status (general taxpayer vs small-scale taxpayer) is determined by turnover thresholds rather than an elective entity classification.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No exemption threshold for business registration; any entity engaged in taxable commercial activity in China must obtain VAT registration. Mandatory general taxpayer registration is required when annual taxable sales exceed CNY 5,000,000; below this threshold the taxpayer is generally classified as a small-scale taxpayer under the simplified VAT method.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to physical exports of goods from China Customs tariff territory. The qualifying export must be evidenced by a formal Customs export declaration in the name of the WFOE, the goods must cross the national border, the WFOE must receive the export sales consideration in foreign currency from a genuine overseas buyer, and the associated VAT invoice must be issued and reported. For a WFOE exporting specialty green coffee beans, no domestic VAT is levied on export turnover, and input VAT on purchases and processing is refundable under the Export VAT Refund (export rebate) regime. Domestic sales of green coffee beans remain subject to the 9% agricultural products reduced VAT rate.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bill of Entry)
  • Bill of Lading
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC)

China has adopted fully digital electronic invoices nationwide through the State Taxation Administration Electronic Tax Bureau. Registered taxpayers must issue VAT invoices through the official e-invoice service platform; invoice issuance data is transmitted in real time to STA. The platform applies cryptographic anti-counterfeiting, a unique invoice code, and online verification through the national VAT invoice verification system. There is no pre-issue fiscal clearance stamping model, but non-compliant or fraudulent invoice data triggers automated anti-fraud risk control. In pilot regimes, the fully digitalised e-invoice is mandatory, replacing traditional paper invoices and physical seals.

If the foreign exchange proceeds are not received and verified within the statutory deadline, or if the supporting documentation is fraudulent or incomplete, the exported goods are treated as domestic sales and the VAT differences at the applicable rate are collected together with a daily late-payment surcharge. The STA may disallow the export refund, claw back any refunded VAT, and impose a penalty from 50% to 500% of the tax involved under the Tax Collection and Administration Law where fraudulent export refund is established. Serious cases involving forged Customs declarations, false VAT invoices, or circular remittances are referred to public security and customs anti-smuggling authorities for criminal prosecution.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Under China's legal framework, the RMB is not fully freely convertible for capital-account purposes, while current-account transactions for bona fide trade and services are generally convertible on a real-needs basis. A WFOE may purchase or sell foreign exchange through domestic designated banks after presenting authentic underlying transaction documents such as trade contracts, commercial invoices, customs declarations, and transport documents. Capital-account transactions, including inbound paid-in equity injections, shareholder loans, and cross-border guarantees, require prior SAFE registration or bank-level verification under the FDI system, and the converted RMB must be used for actual business purposes rather than financial arbitrage. Profit and dividend remittance to foreign shareholders is permitted only after annual audit, tax clearance, and compliance with the SAFE electronic verification platform. Goods export proceeds are subject to mandatory repatriation to China and must be either retained in a domestic foreign exchange account or settled into RMB, but there is currently no forced surrender ratio. For a specialty green coffee importer-distributor, the commercial model relies heavily on documentary trade transactions, so the treasury must maintain precise invoice-to-shipment-to-payment matching to secure the bank and SAFE approvals for each recurring cross-border payment.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export proceeds must be repatriated via a domestic designated bank and cleared through the SAFE trade monitoring system within 180 days from the date of export unless an extension is approved by the bank under SAFE rules; there is no mandatory conversion ratio, and the WFOE may retain the FX in a domestic foreign currency account or convert it to RMB at market rate subject to matched customs and invoice data.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Offshore digital EMI providers are not viable as the primary banking or clearing rail for a PRC-incorporated WFOE. They lack domestic RMB clearing capability, do not report to SAFE, cannot service FDI capital accounts, and are generally not licensed to open Chinese domestic settlement accounts. They may only be used as auxiliary non-PRC expense sub-accounts, and even then compliance risk and cross-border transfer restrictions make them insufficient for statutory tax, payroll, trade, or capital-verification purposes.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity capital must be routed by SWIFT wire to the WFOE FDI capital-account after SAFE FDI registration has been confirmed by the receiving domestic bank. The remittance must carry the correct SAFE-recognized payment purpose code for foreign direct investment, state the investor name exactly as registered, and avoid third-party payment or netting. The receiving bank will record the contract in the SAFE system, settle the capital into RMB only on actual-use principle, and the WFOE must appoint a certified PRC CPA to issue a capital verification report evidencing the paid-in capital before operational disbursement of those funds.Outbound payments for specialty green coffee inventory can be executed through SWIFT MT103 telegraphic transfers against matched trade documents (purchase contract, commercial invoice, bill of lading, packing list, quality certificate, and customs declaration) and are processed via the SAFE trade monitoring platform. Annual Enterprise Information Publicity Report (annual business report under the Enterprise Information Publicity Interim Regulations), filed through the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System Annually on or before 30 June, with the filing window opening on 1 January for the preceding calendar year $0 USD Late filing triggers automatic inclusion in the Enterprise Business Exception List; after three consecutive years without correction, the enterprise is placed on the Serious Illegal and Untrustworthy Enterprise List, resulting in market access bans, government procurement exclusions, bank and credit restrictions, and ultimately potential revocation of the business license after persistent non-compliance.
Annual Foreign Investment Comprehensive Information Report (Foreign Investment Information Annual Report) Annually from 1 January through 30 June for the preceding calendar year, aligned with the SAMR annual report window $0 USD Failure to submit or delayed submission draws a rectification order and fines up to RMB 100,000; intentionally false or materially omitted information draws fines up to RMB 500,000; violations are shared with the national credit-information system and may block foreign-exchange or cross-border payment transaction processing.
Annual Enterprise Income Tax Reconciliation Return (annual corporate income tax filing and settlement) Annually on or before 31 May following the close of the calendar tax year $0 USD Late filing attracts a threshold-based administrative penalty of up to RMB 2,000 generally and RMB 2,000 to 10,000 in serious cases; overdue tax accrues a daily surcharge of 0.05% of the underpaid tax; fraudulent or evasive understatement may be penalized at 50% to 500% of the tax evaded, with compulsory collection, tax liens, asset seizure, exit restrictions and criminal referral in extreme cases.

Strike-off trigger

Mandatory Audit Threshold Mandatory Audit for All Entities

Turnover: Mandatory audit is not threshold-based. Every PRC limited liability company, including a wholly foreign-owned enterprise, must engage a PRC-licensed CPA firm to audit its annual financial statements under the PRC Company Law and Accounting Law. There is no balance-sheet, turnover, or headcount safe harbor for a WFOE engaging in green-coffee wholesale distribution; even a single-shareholder, low-asset, low-headcount WFOE remains subject to the annual audit. The audit must be performed under PRC Standards on Auditing and express an opinion on whether the financial statements prepared in accordance with PRC Accounting Standards for Business Enterprises give a true and fair view.
