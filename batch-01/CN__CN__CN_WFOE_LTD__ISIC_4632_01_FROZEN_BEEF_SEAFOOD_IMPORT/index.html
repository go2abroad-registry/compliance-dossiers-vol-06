Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-up capital and no blocked escrow account is required at the national level; however, under the revised Companies Law, all subscribed capital of a limited liability company must be fully paid within five years after establishment unless the Articles of Association impose a shorter term. For a WFOE engaged in importation of frozen beef and seafood, shareholders should maintain a capital level consistent with operational scale, customs bond obligations, and commercial credit requirements.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; General Administration of Customs (GACC) collects import VAT at inbound clearance; official electronic portal is the STA e-Tax platform.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the WFOE as a limited liability foreign-invested enterprise with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR); the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) printed on the business license is the statutory Tax Identification Number. Step 2: Complete tax registration through the SAMR one-stop data-sharing process; no separate TIN is issued. Step 3: Activate the e-Tax account on the STA electronic tax service platform and bind the legal representative and financial officer identities. Step 4: Apply for VAT general taxpayer status either mandatorily (annual taxable sales exceed CNY 5,000,000) or voluntarily to recover input VAT on imported frozen beef and seafood. Step 5: For a foreign non-resident enterprise without a WFOE entity, register for non-resident tax obligations through the e-Tax platform or through a withholding agent; import VAT on goods is collected by Customs and is not obtained through a domestic VAT registration.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None for entity classification. China does not administer a check-the-box regime or hybrid mismatch election for WFOEs; a WFOE is a resident enterprise taxed at entity level under Corporate Income Tax. A small-scale VAT taxpayer may voluntarily elect general VAT taxpayer status if it meets the statutory conditions.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

CNY 0 for basic tax registration because every WFOE must be registered upon incorporation; mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration triggers when annual taxable sales exceed CNY 5,000,000.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Imported frozen beef and seafood are subject to import VAT at clearance and are not zero-rated. If the WFOE subsequently exports goods from China, 0% VAT zero-rating applies where the exporter is a VAT general taxpayer, the buyer is a non-resident, the goods are physically exported through the GACC, the Customs export declaration matches the invoice and logistics records, and the export proceeds are repatriated to China within 180 days. Qualifying offshore services may also be zero-rated under the STA cross-border services catalogue when provided to overseas customers and consumed wholly outside China.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Export Goods Declaration) submitted through the GACC Single Window electronic customs system
  • Bill of Lading, sea waybill, or multimodal transport document evidencing physical departure from China customs territory
  • Foreign exchange receipt certificate and bank export proceeds collection record evidencing repatriation of the export price to China
  • Commercial invoice, export contract or purchase order, and packing list matching the Customs declaration data
  • Export VAT refund declaration and the STA-to-Customs electronic cross-check record used for zero-rating substantiation

China mandates real-time issuance of VAT invoices through the STA fully digitalized electronic invoice platform operated under the Golden Tax Project Phase IV. VAT general taxpayers must issue Special VAT Invoices through the e-Tax platform; each invoice is transmitted by API to the STA, bears an electronic tax control number and QR code, and must be verified on the VAT Invoice Comprehensive Service Platform before the buyer may claim input VAT credit. Anti-fraud control is effected through digital tax control, automated data matching, and transaction reconciliation rather than a physical fiscal stamp. Import VAT is evidenced by a Customs import VAT payment voucher, not an e-invoice.

The STA and GACC jointly cross-check export declarations, VAT invoices, logistics documents, and bank foreign exchange receipts. If zero-rating or refund is claimed without verifiable export or proceeds collection, the STA will claw back the refunded VAT and input credits, assess overdue interest, and impose administrative penalties under the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Tax Collection, generally from 50% up to five times the tax involved for fraudulent claims. Intentional documentation fraud may lead to criminal prosecution for export tax fraud, and false customs declarations may separately attract penalties under customs law.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China maintains a managed floating exchange rate regime calibrated to a basket of currencies. The renminbi is fully convertible for current account balance-of-payments items, allowing trade in goods (including deep-frozen beef cuts and pelagic fish), services, and remittance of legally declared operating profits by WFOEs after verification of corporate income tax, audit, and foreign exchange declaration. However, capital account transactions remain subject to a comprehensive registration-to-approval framework: equity injections must be booked in the SAFE FDI registration system; foreign currency debt (if any) must be filed via the SAFE cross-border financing macroprudential module; and outbound investment proceeds and dividends require proof of the underlying paid-in capital, audited financial statements, and tax settlement certificates. Effective conversion of foreign currency into RMB for domestic working capital is unrestricted once the trade-related documentary goods flow is verified using customs declarations, bills of lading, and commercial invoices. Profit remittance (dividend payout) is permitted only to the extent that a WFOE is not in a capital/liability mismatch and has received a tax clearance certificate from the State Taxation Administration. Overall, current account convertibility is liberalised but capital account flows remain tightly controlled and monitored on a transaction-by-transaction basis.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the current SAFE framework, mandatory surrender of export proceeds to the state has been eliminated. Every WFOE and domestic importer-exporter must repatriate export proceeds into a designated foreign exchange account at a licensed bank within 180 days of the actual customs export date (with possible extension under force majeure or official SAFE approval). There is no fixed statutory conversion ratio; the exporter may choose to retain US dollars or other collectible currencies in a foreign exchange settlement account or convert them to RMB at the prevailing onshore market rate. Nonetheless, the receipts-verification requirement demands that each remittance be matched against the Electronic Export Data Exchange System, using the customs export declaration number and invoice, with an export reconciliation report to SAFE quarterly. A documented prior shipment, timely collection of foreign exchange, and non-cross-currency abuse are monitored through the Trade Monitoring Notice. Failure to repatriate or verify within the timeline triggers automatic posting in the foreign exchange abandonment risk list and subsequent suspension of trade facilitation for the entity.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China, operating under the People's Bank of China (PBOC) as the central administrative authority for all cross-border capital and foreign exchange transactions.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Unviable under current Chinese regulations. Under the PBOC and China Banking and Insurance Regulatory Commission framework, no fintech electronic money institution (EMI) may open or maintain multi-currency settlement accounts for a China-resident entity. Licensed commercial banks under SAFE are the sole legal custodians of foreign exchange and RMB current accounts. Global digital EMIs such as Revolut Business or Wise Business may only serve as settlement technology participants via a Chinese-licensed partner bank; they cannot issue account numbers or clear trade documents on behalf of a WFOE. A WFOE must use the domestic correspondent bank network for all cross-border payment messages and documentary trade instruments.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The offshore parent should wire the USD or EUR capital injection against the WFOE business licence and SAFE capital account notice using a SWIFT MT103 with purpose code Foreign Direct Investment - Share Capital, remitting directly to the designated Foreign Exchange Capital Account opened with the Chinese licensed commercial bank in the name of the WFOE. The receiving bank will post a capital account notification in the SAFE direct investment registration system and issue a foreign exchange coordination confirmation to the client. A certified public accountant (CPA) must then issue a capital verification report confirming that the funds have been lawfully landed before the WFOE can deploy the working capital. Additional fines may be imposed for refusing to correct or for publishing false information; credit restrictions apply to government procurement, financing, tax rebates, and customs clearance.

Turnover: No balance-sheet, turnover, or headcount threshold exempts a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise from statutory audit in mainland China. The PRC Company Law and PRC Accounting Law require every company to prepare annual financial reports and have them audited by a licensed PRC accounting firm. A WFOE must obtain a certified CPA audit report for each fiscal year, regardless of zero revenue, single shareholder, or small workforce. The independent auditor must issue an opinion that the financial statements are presented fairly in accordance with PRC accounting standards. Foreign parent IFRS consolidation is not a substitute for the local statutory audit.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File required where annual related-party transactions exceed RMB 200 million for tangible property, RMB 100 million for intangible property, RMB 100 million for financial assets, or RMB 40 million for other transactions. Master File is required for MNE groups with annual consolidated revenue exceeding RMB 5 billion and cross-border related-party transactions meeting the Local File threshold.

Economic substance scope: Mainland China does not apply a formal Economic Substance Regulations regime of the Gulf type, but the Enterprise Income Tax Law Article 47 incorporates a general anti-abuse rule and substance-over-form analysis. For treaty entitlement, the WFOE and its non-resident shareholder must prove that Core Income-Generating Activities, including procurement decisions, cold-chain management, inventory risk, invoicing and financing, are performed by real employees with decision-making authority in their respective jurisdictions. A shell vehicle without economic substance is denied treaty benefits and distributions may be recharacterized as taxable dividends.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Corporate Income Tax Filing

Statutory sanction: Tax Collection Administration Law Article 63 orders payment of the evaded tax and late fee, with a fine of 50% to 5 times the evaded amount. Criminal Law Article 201 applies where the evaded amount exceeds RMB 50,000 and exceeds 10% of the tax payable, with imprisonment up to 3 years, or up to 7 years for enormous amounts.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil may be pierced where a director personally directs the evasion, commingles personal and corporate assets or operates the WFOE as a mere instrumentality. Directors can be jointly and severally liable for unpaid tax, placed on the tax-related dishonesty blacklist, banned from exiting China, barred from high consumption, and criminally prosecuted as directly responsible officers with imprisonment up to 7 years.
Shell Enterprise and Double Tax Treaty Abuse

Statutory sanction: Enterprise Income Tax Law Article 47 permits the tax authority to apply the general anti-abuse rule, recharacterize the structure, disregard the shell, deny treaty benefits and reimpose 10% withholding tax on distributions, plus interest at the benchmark rate plus 5 percentage points. False treaty-benefit filings may trigger fines under Tax Collection Administration Law Articles 63 and 64.

Director personal liability: Directors who cause the artificial structuring may be jointly and severally liable for the unpaid withholding tax and interest. Directors responsible for forged Tax Residency Certificates or fabricated board resolutions face criminal prosecution for fraud and forgery, civil monetary restitution, travel bans and blacklisting, with imprisonment up to 7 years in aggravated cases.
Transfer Pricing and Off-Market Import Pricing

Statutory sanction: The tax authority adjusts the transfer price under Enterprise Income Tax Law Article 41 and imposes interest on the underpaid tax from the filing deadline at the benchmark RMB lending rate plus 5 percentage points. Failure to maintain documentation may trigger administrative fines, and fraudulent documentation may lead to penalties of 50% to 5 times the tax underpaid under Tax Collection Administration Law Article 63.

Director personal liability: Where transfer pricing is a device for deliberate tax evasion and the director directs the falsification of accounts or customs documents, the corporate veil may be pierced. Directors may be personally assessed for company tax debts, placed on the tax dishonesty blacklist with exit restrictions, prosecuted for customs fraud and subject to criminal imprisonment up to 7 years under Criminal Law Article 201, with additional liability for false VAT invoice offences under Criminal Law Articles 205 and 208.
