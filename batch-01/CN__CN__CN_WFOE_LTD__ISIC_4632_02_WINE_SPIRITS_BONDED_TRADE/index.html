Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) through the provincial or municipal Market Supervision Administration at the place of incorporation, operating the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System and the online Foreign-Invested Enterprise Registration Platform.

Governing statute

Company Law of the People's Republic of China (2023 Revision), effective 1 July 2024, particularly Articles 23 (establishment of limited liability company), 24 (articles of association), 46 (board of directors), 47 (five-year paid-in capital obligation), and 59 (registration); Foreign Investment Law of the People's Republic of China (2019), Article 31; and the Regulations on the Registration Administration of Market Entities (State Council Decree No. 746, as amended), dealing with the establishment registration of foreign-invested enterprises.

Formation / constitutional filings

Application for Registration of Establishment of a Foreign-Invested Enterprise (foreign-invested enterprise establishment registration application) together with the Articles of Association, the list of directors and supervisors, and the board resolution approving formation and governance.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 27 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None stated under Chinese national law for ordinary limited liability companies, as the Company Law 2023 revision abolishes minimum registered capital. However, Article 47 requires each shareholder to pay up subscribed capital within five years from the date of establishment. For a bonded wine and spirits wholesale WFOE, the registered capital must be a credible amount sufficient to evidence financial capacity for the bonded trade and to satisfy the responsible bank's anti-money laundering and capital verification expectations. There is no statutory blocked escrow account requirement, but the foreign investor's first capital contribution must enter a designated capital collection account in China before the enterprise can lawfully commence distribution activities.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety Acceptance Certificate (消防验收) for the warehouse building and racking layout, comprising structural load verification for multi-tier racks (design uniform live load not less than 2.5 kN/m²), an automatic fire alarm system linked to a remote monitoring center, a high-level sprinkler or foam suppression system rated for ordinary combustible liquid storage, and a Civil Defense clearance permitting six-monthly fire safety inspections. Occupational safety approval under the Work Safety Law, including confined-space entry permits, fork-lift operator certificates, chemical hazard labeling for high-proof spirits, and an emergency spill response plan approved by the district safety regulator.

Paid-in capital rule: None stated under Chinese national law for ordinary limited liability companies, as the Company Law 2023 revision abolishes minimum registered capital. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA), acting through the official online taxpayer service portal known as the Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax), with provincial-level e-Tax instances used for filing, registration, invoice, and export-rebate administration.

E-government filing portal

For the target CN WFOE, the foreign-invested entity is a Chinese resident limited liability company. Step 1: Complete incorporation with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) and receive the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which is the statutory Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) for Chinese enterprises. Step 2: Within 30 days of issuance of the business license, complete tax registration through the e-Tax portal by confirming or supplementing tax-specific data, including the registered address, bank account details, legal representative and financial officer identity, accounting standards, industry classification, business scope, and the designated tax authority. Step 3: Request automatic synchronization of the SAMR business license into the tax system; no separate paper TIN certificate is issued because the USCC is the TIN. Step 4: File and complete tax type registration for Value Added Tax (VAT), Corporate Income Tax (CIT), stamp duty, urban maintenance and construction tax, educational surcharges, and any local levy, and activate the relevant tax reporting schedules. Step 5: Elect or confirm VAT taxpayer classification: small-scale taxpayer or general VAT taxpayer; an entity with annual taxable sales above CNY 5,000,000 must be registered as a general VAT taxpayer, and a new WFOE may voluntarily elect general taxpayer status. Step 6: Activate electronic invoice issuance through the e-Tax fully digitalized electronic invoice service platform and obtain the corporate tax-control digital certificate. Step 7: For any non-resident parent or related foreign enterprise without a permanent establishment in China, no separate Chinese TIN is required; if withholding obligations arise, the WFOE as payer reports the non-resident income through e-Tax using the non-resident country-specific taxpayer reference number under the relevant double tax treaty.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None (no check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election exists in the PRC). The WFOE is per se a resident enterprise taxed at entity level under CIT. Available statutory elections relate to VAT: an eligible entity may elect small-scale taxpayer status (simplified levy, no input VAT credit) or general VAT taxpayer status (standard rate with input VAT credit; mandatory when annual assessable sales exceed CNY 5,000,000). General VAT taxpayers may elect to issue VAT Special Invoices for deductible output supplies. There is no consolidated CIT filing or group relief election for separate legal entities in the PRC.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

All Chinese business entities are required to register for VAT upon incorporation; there is no general turnover exemption from registration. An annual VAT-taxable sales threshold of CNY 5,000,000 triggers mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration. A small-scale taxpayer with monthly sales not exceeding CNY 100,000 may qualify for the monthly VAT exemption, provided invoices are issued in compliance with STA rules. For the bonded warehouse wholesale of wine and spirits, the entity must also be registered with Customs and licensed as a bonded warehouse operator or user.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A sale of wine or spirits by the bonded warehouse WFOE qualifies for the PRC VAT zero-rating only when the goods are physically exported from China and actually leave customs supervision to a foreign destination, or are supplied to an overseas buyer in an export processing zone under customs supervision. The supply must not be deemed a domestic sale; bonded goods released into the domestic market are subject to normal import VAT, customs duty, and consumption tax. For qualifying exports, the VAT output is zero-rated and the related input VAT previously incurred may be refunded through the export tax rebate procedure; exported taxable consumer goods also receive consumption tax exemption or rebate. The zero-rating claim requires the exporter to hold the statutory export documentary set, prove foreign-exchange collection, and file an export tax refund declaration through the e-Tax and customs information systems.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bill of Entry) with customs export endorsement and export rebate proof
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill showing physical export and destination outside China
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or designated-bank export proceeds collection receipt evidencing foreign exchange repatriation
  • Commercial invoice and packing list for the exported wine or spirits
  • Bonded warehouse outbound or transfer order and customs supervision release record
  • Export tax rebate declaration filed through the e-Tax platform with documentary retention for statutory audit
  • Sales contract or purchase order with overseas buyer
  • Customs value declaration and commodity HS classification matching the ISIC 4632 and NAICS 424820 wine and spirits wholesale scope

China operates a fully digitalized electronic invoice system under the e-Tax platform. All VAT invoices, including Normal VAT Invoices and VAT Special Invoices, must be issued through the STA-authorized electronic invoice service and must carry the STA-issued unique electronic invoice number and tax-control digital stamp or signature. Invoice data is transmitted in real time to the STA for fiscal clearance, anti-fraud control, and tax authority matching. Invoices must be classified using the official national goods-and-services tax classification code and must be itemized for wine and spirits with the applicable tax categories, quantities, unit prices, VAT rate, and excise tax description. The issuance of VAT Special Invoices is restricted to registered general VAT taxpayers and requires prior VAT general taxpayer status. Customs declarations and export rebate vouchers are cross-checked electronically between the customs authority and STA systems.

Export zero-rating is subject to joint audit by the STA, customs, and foreign-exchange authorities. Any false, overstated, or unverifiable export claim will be disallowed, the refunded VAT and consumption tax clawed back, and a daily late-payment surcharge will accrue from the original refund date. Under the Tax Collection Administration Law of the PRC, tax evasion or false export documentation exposes the WFOE to an administrative penalty of 50% up to 500% of the unpaid tax, asset seizure, and enforced collection. Customs Law penalties may also apply for inaccurate or fraudulent export declarations. Fraudulently obtaining export tax refunds is a criminal offense under the Criminal Law of the PRC and may result in criminal liability for the entity and responsible persons, export refund suspension, tax credit downgrade, enhanced audit monitoring, and public blacklisting.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China maintains a managed float and an administrative foreign exchange control regime. For current account items, the renminbi is broadly convertible: trade receipts and payments can be settled once the underlying transaction has been verified by a SAFE-designated bank against contracts, customs declarations, invoices, and transport documents. Profit remittances by a WFOE are permitted if funded from after-tax distributable profits, supported by audited financial statements, tax clearance, board resolution, and capital account verification. However, capital account items such as FDI paid-in capital, inbound loans, and portfolio flows remain subject to case-by-case SAFE registration, negative list restrictions, and custody requirements; liquidation proceeds require separate approval. Export proceeds are no longer subject to a fixed mandatory conversion percentage, but funds must be repatriated to China through compliant channels and bank-reported to SAFE. A WFOE may retain foreign currency in designated accounts or convert to RMB, yet speculative conversion is prohibited. Bonded wine traders must maintain full cargo documentation because SAFE audits compare payment flows, customs warehousing movements, and invoices; therefore currency planning must build in 2-5 business day settlement lead times and precise purpose codes.

Export proceeds surrender rules

All export proceeds must be repatriated to mainland bank accounts no later than 90 days from the date of customs export declaration or as otherwise evidenced by the bill of lading or air waybill; for goods sold later from a bonded warehouse, the foreign exchange receipt must be repatriated within 180 days of title transfer or warehouse transfer unless a formal deferred collection filing has been submitted to SAFE. There is no statutory mandatory conversion ratio; an exporter may retain the foreign currency in a SAFE-approved foreign exchange settlement account or sell it to a licensed domestic bank. Failure to repatriate within the statutory period requires submission of a delayed receipt reason letter, bank statements, customs records, and a formal record-filing application to SAFE.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China, operating under the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Rating: Very Low for serving as the primary treasury rail. Offshore fintech EMIs such as Revolut and Wise are not licensed by PBOC or SAFE to open onshore RMB accounts, maintain SAFE verification codes, or carry out export proceeds repatriation and capital account settlements. They may be used by the overseas parent for pre-export invoice reconciliation or ancillary employee expense cards, but they cannot replace a tier-1 PRC bank for bonded warehouse trade because customs, tax, and FX supervision requires the local bank to synchronise electronic customs declarations, the SAFE monitoring system, and tax bureau payment vouchers. At best, an offshore digital EMI can act as a secondary information channel for FX quotation or as an offshore liquidity pocket, provided China's data localisation and cross-border payment rules are observed; no bonded trade funds should be routed through an unlicensed virtual account.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Route inbound FDI funds from the overseas parent through a licensed tier-1 Chinese bank by SWIFT MT103 or MT202 with the remittance purpose designated as direct investment equity contribution to the WFOE. The bank will verify and generate the SAFE FDI foreign exchange registration record and capital account payment code after receiving the business licence, articles of association, foreign investment information report, board resolution on capital call, and legal representative identification. Funds must land in a dedicated foreign currency capital account or RMB capital settlement account, undergo the bank's foreign exchange capital verification procedure, and be booked into paid-in capital. The WFOE must not deploy the capital for speculative FX trading, intra-group lending outside approved scope, or purchases outside its business scope; subsequent disbursements to the bonded warehouse must be linked to customs declarations, warehouse receipts, and underlying trade contracts.Use SWIFT MT103 for direct supplier remittances, commercial documentary collections for high-value or title-conditional settlements, and commercial letters of credit for confirmed vintage purchases. For imports into the bonded warehouse, funds may be remitted only after the bank verifies the import contract, customs declaration, bill of lading or airway bill, commercial invoice, and insurance documents. When bonded wine and spirits are withdrawn for domestic sale from the bonded warehouse, settlement is effected in RMB with customs duty and value-added tax collected at the point of release. For transfers between bonded warehouses, use customs bonded transfer documentation; such movement is not treated as a cross-border payment unless title passes to an overseas party. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation (Local File) is mandatory when annual related-party tangible asset purchases/sales exceed RMB 200 million, or related-party financial asset transfers exceed RMB 100 million, or related-party intangible asset transactions or service transactions exceed RMB 40 million; an annual reporting threshold for related-party transactions also requires Schedule A105050/A100000 disclosures; Master File is required when the multinational group consolidated revenue exceeds RMB 10 billion; a bonded wine wholesale WFOE transacting with offshore affiliates must prepare both Master File and Local File without relying on the de minimis exemption because cross-border transaction values almost always exceed these thresholds

Economic substance scope: China does not operate a statutory offshore Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) regime akin to zero-tax islands, but the Core Income-Generating Activities (CIGA) concept is applied through China's Enterprise Income Tax Law Article 47, the General Anti-Avoidance Rules, the Special Tax Adjustment Measures, and the beneficial ownership test under DTA claims: an entity without employees, physical premises, real decision-making, financial capacity, and risk control will be considered a shell; the SAT may disregard its separate existence, allocate profits to the Chinese WFOE, adjust all related-party transactions, impose interest and penalties, and deny treaty benefits; WFOEs involved in bonded trade must therefore show actual warehousing, inventory management, customs operations, sales procurement staff, and board governance in China, while any non-resident shareholder claiming relief must prove economic substance in its own residence jurisdiction.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Corporate Income Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Return Filing

Statutory sanction: Tax Collection Administration Law Article 63 authorizes recovery of the underpaid tax and late-payment interest at 0.05% per day plus an administrative fine of 50% to 500% of the underpaid amount; Criminal Law Article 201 criminalizes tax fraud where the evaded amount exceeds RMB 50,000 and is greater than 10% of the tax due, providing graduated terms of up to 7 years' imprisonment, confiscation, and criminal fines.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil may be pierced under PRC Company Law Article 20: where a director or shareholder abuses the independence of the limited liability entity to evade taxes and cause serious loss to the State, the court may hold directors and shareholders jointly and severally liable for the unpaid tax; tax authorities may obtain court orders to freeze personal bank accounts, seize personal assets, restrict departures under Exit-Entry Administration Law Article 12, place the individual on the national serious tax dishonesty blacklist, and criminal conviction results in imprisonment of up to 7 years.
Transfer Pricing Abuse / Shell Company / Thin Capitalization

Statutory sanction: Enterprise Income Tax Law Articles 41, 46 and 47, together with the SAT Special Tax Adjustment Measures, authorize transfer pricing adjustments, imposition of compensatory interest in addition to tax, disallowance of excess interest under thin capitalization ratios (debt-to-equity generally 2:1 for non-financial entities), and recharacterization of disguised distributions; if the pricing is fraudulent rather than a good-faith adjustment, Tax Collection Administration Law Article 63 applies with a fine of 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly orchestrate sham transactions are treated as abusers of corporate personality under Company Law Article 20 and may be ordered to compensate the tax shortfall from personal assets; the SAT may apply substance-over-form to pierce the WFOE and its offshore shell chain, requiring directors to produce missing books or face contempt and asset seizure; blacklisting, exit bans, and concurrent criminal tax evasion charges may lead to substantial personal fines and imprisonment.
Bonded Warehouse Duty Evasion and Money Laundering (Customs / AML)

Statutory sanction: Customs Law Article 82 requires confiscation of the smuggled goods and unlawful gains; a fine of up to 5 times the value of the smuggled goods or the evaded duty may be imposed depending on severity; Criminal Law Article 153 (smuggling ordinary goods) imposes fixed-term imprisonment from 3 years up to life in serious cases, with fines and confiscation of property; Criminal Law Article 191 (money laundering) additionally penalizes the concealment of smuggling proceeds with imprisonment of up to 10 years, criminal fines, and confiscation.

Director personal liability: The director who signed false bonded warehouse records or authorized the illegal diversion is personally responsible: customs authorities may seize personal property and personally recover the unpaid duties and fines when the corporate structure is used as an instrumentality of smuggling; the exit-exit restriction and blacklisting mechanisms deny the director departure from China; criminal conviction under Article 153 or Article 191 can lead to life imprisonment for large-scale bonded spirits smuggling, plus civil asset restitution for the full customs duty and taxes evaded.
