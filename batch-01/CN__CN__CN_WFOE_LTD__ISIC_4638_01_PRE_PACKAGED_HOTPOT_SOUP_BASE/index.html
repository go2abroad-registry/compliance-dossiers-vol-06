No statutory minimum registered capital applies to a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise under the Company Law, unless the industry falls within the Foreign Investment Negative List. Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

E-government filing portal

For the CN_WFOE_LTD, the WFOE is the resident taxpayer. Stage 1: the foreign investors complete the Foreign Investment Information Report through the Ministry of Commerce portal, then obtain the business licence from the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) with a Unified Social Credit Code (USCC). Stage 2: under the 'one licence, one code' reform, SAMR transmits the formation data to the STA and the tax record is automatically created; the USCC is simultaneously the statutory Tax Identification Number (TIN/VAT ID), replacing any separate tax-registration certificate. Stage 3: within 30 days of issue, the WFOE's legal representative, using real-name authentication, logs into the e-Tax portal and finalises the supplementary tax record (shareholders, ultimate beneficial owner, accounting policy, bank account, and payroll/withholding profiles), which activates the taxpayer account. Stage 4: the WFOE applies for small-scale or general VAT taxpayer treatment if its annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000, or elects general taxpayer status for input-VAT deduction. Stage 5: if exporting, the WFOE completes the GACC customs-registration code process and the SAFE trade-in-goods foreign-exchange registration. A non-resident foreign corporate shareholder without a PRC establishment does not obtain a Chinese TIN solely for holding the WFOE; if the WFOE pays PRC-source dividends/interest/royalties, the WFOE obtains the investor's reference code through the withholding registration function.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

The WFOE's legal-entity tax registration is mandatory upon business licensing, with no de minimis turnover exemption. The qualifying regime threshold is RMB 5,000,000 of annual taxable sales: once exceeded, the WFOE must be registered as a general VAT taxpayer and apply the standard 13% rate to sales of taxable goods such as pre-packaged hotpot soup base; below RMB 5,000,000 the WFOE may remain a small-scale taxpayer (current levy rate 3%, with temporary reductions) or voluntarily elect general taxpayer status.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The WFOE general VAT taxpayer may claim the PRC zero export VAT rate on the pre-packaged hotpot soup base only where the goods are declared to and released by GACC for physical export outside the customs territory, sold by and invoiced in the name of the WFOE to an overseas buyer, and the export proceeds are received in foreign currency through the designated banking system. If the goods are supplied to a foreign party without actual customs exportation, no zero-rating/refund applies. Qualifying offshore services/digital supplies remain subject to the statutory cross-border VAT zero-rating catalogue and require documentary proof of performance outside China for a non-resident customer.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Cargo Export Declaration) with GACC release/clearance result code
  • Commercial invoice issued by the WFOE
  • Packing list
  • Bill of Lading (B/L) or equivalent air waybill/combined transport document
  • Export sales contract and purchase order
  • Bank foreign-exchange receipt voucher (bank settlement memo, SWIFT credit advice, or equivalent inward remittance certificate) evidencing receipt of export proceeds
  • Export VAT rebate/exemption declaration form submitted through the e-Tax platform when refund is claimed
  • SAFE foreign-exchange monitoring records reconciling goods description, customs date, invoice amount, and payment receipt

Under the Golden Tax Project IV and the all-electronic invoice framework, the WFOE must issue compliant VAT invoices through the national e-invoice service platform. The platform validates each invoice, assigns a State-controlled invoice code/serial number, applies an encrypted anti-counterfeit stamp, and uploads the invoice data to the STA in near real time through the enterprise's tax control device (tax U-Key) or digital electronic invoice key. At issuance, all invoices (paper special/ordinary VAT invoices during the transition, and digital electronic invoices in pilot areas) are transmitted to the tax authority's clearance nodes. The WFOE must report issued invoices on the monthly e-filing, reconcile delivered/issued invoice data, obtain electronic approval for red-letter credit notes, and, if using enterprise ERP/billing systems, connect to the official e-invoice API where required by provincial tax authorities.

The STA, GACC, and SAFE jointly cross-check customs declaration data, e-invoice records, transport documents, and bank receipts. Where zero-rating or export VAT refund is claimed on goods not actually exported, with misrepresented or false customs declarations, without genuine foreign-exchange repatriation, or beyond the 180-day repatriation deadline unless an approved extension exists, the tax authority will reverse the zero-rating, claw back all VAT refunds paid, impose daily late-payment surcharges of 0.05% of the unpaid tax, and levy penalties of 0.5 to 5 times the evaded tax under the Law on Tax Collection and Administration. Intentional fraudulent export refunds will also be transferred for criminal investigation under the Criminal Law's false-export tax-refund offence, punishable by fines, confiscation, and imprisonment up to life imprisonment; the WFOE's export tax-rebate status and Customs credit rating may be suspended or downgraded.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The People's Republic of China operates a managed floating exchange rate regime with strict current account convertibility but significant capital account controls. The RMB is convertible for trade in goods and services, and foreign exchange received from export proceeds may be retained or settled under SAFE's Qualified Foreign Exchange Settlement system, but the balance must be declared and reconciled through the Foreign Exchange Administration Platform. Profit remittance by a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE) requires audited financial statements, tax clearance certificates, and proof of capital verification through the SAFE registration system. While current account items such as trade payments and dividends from lawful profits are generally permitted, capital account items such as foreign debt injection or equity capital increases require separate SAFE registration and may face waiting periods or quota limits. The WFOE must convert registered paid-in capital into RMB at a domestic bank through the capital account system, and outbound remittances of funds not representing true trade or lawfully declared dividends will be blocked. Surrender of export proceeds is no longer mandatory under the 2019 SAFE reforms; exporters may retain foreign currency in licensed bank accounts or settle on a voluntary basis, but all receipts above USD 500,000 must be electronically reported within 30 days through the SAFE digital platform. In practice, banks apply post-verification systems, and any mismatch between customs declarations, invoices, and logistics documents will trigger a foreign exchange adjudication review. The Central Bank implements macro-prudential cross-border financing parameters for WFOEs, including the discretion to adjust upper limits on net assets and to enforce counter-cyclical factors, meaning external borrowing by the WFOE must pass a Foreign Debt Macro-Prudential Regulation registration before funds can be brought onshore. The de facto non-convertibility of the capital account and the paper-intensive nature of dividend repatriation require treasury teams to maintain documentary discipline across every inbound and outbound payment.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Since the SAFE Circular 28 Fa (2019) reform, there is no mandatory conversion ratio or statutory surrender timeline for current-account export receipts. Exporters may retain FX proceeds in a domestic foreign currency account or voluntarily settle into RMB; however, all export-related FX receipts must be reported to SAFE through the bank within 30 days of receipt using bank transaction reporting codes 101010 (export trade in goods). Receipts above USD 500,000, and all receipts from sensitive jurisdictions, require supporting customs declaration records, commercial invoices, bills of lading, and sales contracts. The bank must verify that the receiving entity is the actual exporter under the customs registration number. If the WFOE receives payment into an offshore account instead of repatriating, the transaction must be disclosed under the offshore retained proceeds filing requirement, and the retained amount may trigger capital verification adjustments.

Central bank supervision: The State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Not viable as a primary banking rail. The PRC regulators require full bank account opening through a licensed domestic bank branch, and the WFOE must have a physical registered business address and a Chinese business license. Offshore fintech EMIs such as Airwallex, Payoneer, or Currenxie cannot be used as the principal settlement channel because the SAFE system and People's Bank of China settlement platforms require domestic bank participation for capital injection, customs reconciliation, export proceeds verification, and tax refund matching. Foreign EMIs may only serve as auxiliary collection tools for certain cross-border e-commerce receivables, but even then the funds must be routed back to a domestic bank account and declared under the cross-border e-commerce revenue code. For this entity, which is exporting pre-packaged hotpot soup base prepared for bulk wholesale, the corporate treasury must rely on state-owned banks to obtain the necessary SAFE registration, customs linkage, and licensing verification. No fintech EMI can provide a compliant capital verification report or meet the Foreign Investment Disbursement-related documentation requirements of SAFE.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: The WFOE must prepare contemporaneous Local File documentation when the prior-year related-party transaction amount exceeds: RMB 200,000,000 for tangible goods, RMB 100,000,000 for intangible property transfers or usage, RMB 100,000,000 for financial asset transfers, and RMB 40,000,000 for related-party service receipts or provision; a Chinese Master File and Country-by-Country Report are required for MNE groups with consolidated group revenue of at least RMB 5,500,000,000 in the relevant fiscal year, and an annual related-party transaction report must be filed with the CIT annual return even below those thresholds.

Economic substance scope: Mainland China does not maintain a Gulf-style Economic Substance Regulations statute, but the economic substance doctrine is applied through the General Anti-Avoidance Rule under CIT Law Article 47, the beneficial-ownership test for treaty relief, the BEPS-aligned functional and risk analysis, and the judicial substance-over-form principle. The WFOE must demonstrate employees, physical premises, procurement authority, inventory risk, credit risk and management decisions in China; if the wholly foreign-owned enterprise is a non-substantive conduit through which hotpot soup base export profits are routed offshore, the STA may reallocate the profit to the value-creation jurisdiction, disallow treaty benefits and impose special-tax-adjustment interest. In addition, China's controlled foreign corporation rules apply to low-taxed offshore subsidiaries that accumulate passive income without real economic substance, requiring current-year attribution of distributable income to the Chinese investor.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Cross-border transfer pricing manipulation and profit stripping through an offshore distributor

Statutory sanction: The State Taxation Administration may re-determine taxable income under CIT Law Articles 41 and 48, impose special tax adjustment interest at the benchmark lending rate plus five percentage points under the Implementing Regulations, impose a fine of 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax under Article 64 of the Law on Tax Administration and Collection, and extend the assessment period to 10 years for non-disclosed related-party transactions.

Director personal liability: A director who signs false related-party disclosures or directs the transfer-pricing manipulation can be pierced through under PRC Company Law where the corporate form is abused to harm the State's tax claim; the director may be jointly and severally liable to restore the underpaid tax, placed on the serious tax non-compliant list, subjected to exit control and travel bans, and criminally convicted for tax evasion under Criminal Law Article 201, with imprisonment up to three years where evasion exceeds RMB 50,000 and ten percent of disclosed tax and up to seven years in aggravated cases.
Fraudulent VAT export refund through false export declarations and false special fapiao

Statutory sanction: The tax authority claws back the refunded VAT, imposes a penalty of 50% to 500% of the fraudulently obtained refund under Law on Tax Administration and Collection Article 63, cancels export tax refund qualifications and customs registration, and refers the case to criminal authorities. Under Criminal Law Article 205, issuing false special VAT fapiao or using false fapiao to deceitfully obtain export tax refunds is punishable by custodial sentences of up to life imprisonment and asset confiscation.

Director personal liability: The legal representative, financial controller and responsible directors are treated as directly responsible persons under Criminal Law Articles 205 and 211; they face criminal imprisonment, personal fines and confiscation of property. They are also exposed to exit bans and blacklisting by tax and customs authorities, deprivation from serving as directors or legal representatives of new companies, and civil restitution claims by the State for the fraudulent refund amount; the corporate veil will be pierced where the directors diverted refund money for personal use.
Treaty abuse, shell shareholding and false beneficial ownership by a zero-substance nominee

Statutory sanction: The tax authority will deny the treaty benefit and require payment of the full 10% statutory withholding tax on all dividend distributions, together with late-payment surcharge and interest; the withholding agent that failed to withhold is liable for a fine of 50% to 300% of the amount not withheld under Article 69 of the Law on Tax Administration and Collection; the offshore structure may also be recharacterized under CIT Law Article 47 as an arrangement lacking reasonable commercial purpose, subjecting the WFOE to additional tax on the deemed reallocation.

Director personal liability: A director who signs a false beneficial-ownership declaration or knowingly causes the WFOE to remit dividends through a paper shell can be personally jointly liable for the unpaid withholding tax under the corporate veil-piercing doctrine; the director may be blacklisted as a major tax non-compliant responsible person, banned from overseas travel, prohibited from assuming new legal representative roles, and prosecuted criminally for tax evasion where the avoided withholding exceeds the criminal threshold, with the court empowered to impose imprisonment and restitution out of personal assets.
