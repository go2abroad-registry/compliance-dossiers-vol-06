Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-up capital and no blocked escrow account requirement apply to a general trading/wholesale WFOE under the current PRC Company Law. The registered capital is subscribed under the capital contribution schedule in the Articles of Association and may be paid in RMB or permitted foreign currency through a licensed bank capital account. The paid-in capital must not be withdrawn or used as collateral before the company satisfies its lawful creditor protection obligations. For second-hand construction machinery wholesale, no additional statutory deposit or escrow rule applies, but the capital contribution period must be expressly stated in the Articles.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA); mandatory online access through the nationwide Electronic Tax Administration (E-Tax) platform and provincial-level E-Tax Bureau portals operated under the STA's unified digital tax system.

E-government filing portal

For a foreign investor establishing a Chinese WFOE, the statutory procedure is: (1) complete incorporation with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) and obtain the Business License showing the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which legally serves as both the corporate TIN and the VAT ID; (2) allow the automatic SAMR-STA data synchronization to create the tax profile, then activate tax registration on the E-Tax portal; (3) file the taxpayer profile, legal representative, responsible finance officer, accounting system, business premises, and bank account information; (4) determine VAT status as Small-Scale Taxpayer or General VAT Taxpayer and apply for electronic invoice issuance quotas; (5) for export activities, complete the customs import/export enterprise filing, obtain the Customs Declaration operating code, and make the export tax rebate (exemption) filing with the competent STA tax office. A pure foreign non-resident seller without a Chinese establishment is generally not required to register for VAT on the export of goods where title passes offshore, but the WFOE is a Chinese resident legal entity and must carry full tax registration from incorporation.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation: the WFOE is a Chinese resident limited liability company subjected to Enterprise Income Tax at the 25% headline CIT rate on worldwide income; no pass-through regime applies, and dividend distributions to foreign shareholders are subject to 10% withholding tax unless reduced under an applicable tax treaty.

Available election forms

None for corporate income tax classification; China does not provide check-the-box elections or hybrid mismatch elections for a WFOE. The only relevant election is VAT status: a taxpayer whose annual taxable sales are below the RMB 5,000,000 threshold may elect voluntarily to become a General VAT Taxpayer; taxpayers above the threshold are mandatorily General VAT Taxpayers.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

CNY 5,000,000 (RMB 5 million) annual taxable turnover is the mandatory economic threshold above which a taxpayer must register as a General VAT Taxpayer; below the threshold, Small-Scale Taxpayer status applies, and monthly taxable sales up to CNY 100,000 (quarterly CNY 300,000) are exempt from VAT for small-scale taxpayers.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods exported from China qualify for a 0% VAT rate and output VAT exemption only where the goods are physically loaded and cleared by Chinese Customs under an export declaration, the seller is a VAT-registered taxpayer holding a valid export rebate filing, the export proceeds are collected from the overseas buyer through banking channels and verified under the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) trade monitoring system, and the entire purchase-to-export invoice and documentation chain is retained. For used construction excavators, the WFOE must show traceable input VAT evidence, include model and serial number details consistent with the export declaration, and satisfy any applicable Customs control or used mechanical machinery export requirements; failure to substantiate the actual export or repatriation of proceeds disqualifies the 0% rate and the associated VAT rebate.

  • Customs Export Goods Declaration (China Customs export declaration form with the 18-digit declaration number and 'Export' trade regime code)
  • Original transport document: Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, or multimodal cargo receipt proving discharge from a Chinese customs port
  • Commercial invoice and detailed packing list describing the used construction excavator, serial numbers, model, and declared customs value
  • Export sales contract or purchase order between the WFOE and the overseas buyer
  • VAT special invoice or validated VAT purchase invoice evidencing the input VAT chain for the used equipment
  • Export invoice issued to the overseas buyer in a foreign settlement currency with full commercial terms
  • Bank receipt or SWIFT MT103 confirming export proceeds from the overseas buyer, supported by the SAFE trade monitoring collection verification record
  • Export tax rebate/exemption declaration package prepared on the STA E-Tax portal, including the Export Goods VAT Rebate declaration and its supporting ledgers

China operates the Golden Tax Project IV fiscal supervision system with near-real-time invoice data transmission. All commercial VAT invoices must be issued through the STA-controlled Electronic Tax Bureau platform or integrated API-based billing systems; fully digitalized electronic invoices (FDEI) are cryptographically signed with tax-control codes and carry unique national invoice sequence numbers. VAT special invoices must pass authority verification and reconciliation before input VAT credits or export VAT rebates can be claimed; paper invoices, where still permitted, require digital anti-fraud stamping and tax authority code allocation.

Under the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Tax Collection and the Administrative Measures for Export Tax Refund (Exemption), tax authorities may suspend, refuse, or claw back any export zero-rating or VAT refund where the customs, transport, input invoice, or FX collection documentation is missing, inconsistent, or artificial. The clawback includes the full refunded tax plus late-payment interest of 0.05% per day and an administrative penalty ranging from 50% to 500% of the evaded or fraudulently refunded tax. If export proceeds are not received within 180 days without an approved exception, the export VAT zero-rating is reversed, VAT becomes assessable as a domestic taxable supply, and additional late surcharges apply. Knowingly submitting forged customs declarations, bills of lading, or remittance evidence constitutes tax fraud under Article 204 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, punishable by criminal detention, fixed-term imprisonment, life imprisonment, fines, and confiscation of property; false Customs declarations may additionally trigger Customs Law penalties including fines, seizure of goods, and suspension of import/export qualification.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a managed floating exchange rate regime with current-account RMB convertibility and strict capital-account controls administered by SAFE. A WFOE may convert foreign-currency investment for operating purposes under an FDI registration, but free convertibility is not available for speculative or unapproved balance-of-payment items. Current payments such as trade settlements, service fees, dividends, and royalties are generally permitted once the underlying transaction is verified against the authenticated commercial documents, customs declarations, invoices, and tax compliance proof. Profit remittance requires an annual audit or tax filing record, a foreign-exchange payment application, and the absence of unresolved tax assessments; since 2017, banks independently verify the tax status through the tax department interface before releasing dividends. Capital reductions, exit proceeds, and loan repayments require SAFE special approval or filing. For export receipts, no forced conversion is required, however the proceeds must be received into a licensed foreign-exchange settlement account and reconcile with the customs trade data within the regulatory window. Any conversion from foreign exchange into CNY must be channeled through a designated bank, and the CNY cannot be freely converted back in excess of the approved capital account quota. The overall assessment is that liquidity is controllable but operationally layered with documentary compliance.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export proceeds must enter China through a designated SAFE-monitored bank account and be declared to the SAFE trade monitoring system within 30 days after settlement. The mandatory conversion ratio is 0% — enterprises may retain export foreign currency in settlement accounts indefinitely; however, nothing may remain unreported, and conversion from foreign exchange to RMB must be supported by customs declaration, contract, invoice, and transport documents.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Offshore digital EMIs such as Revolut, Wise, or Airwallex cannot act as a KYC-passed clearing bank for a mainland WFOE. They lack PBoC/SAFE licenses to process FX capital flows, cannot issue domestic CNY account numbers, and are legally barred from servicing entities domiciled in the Chinese customs and SAFE surveillance net. Viability is Low. Such EMIs may only support non-China ancillary subsidiaries; the mainland entity must use a local tier-1 bank for all statutory FX and RMB settlement.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% standard China Enterprise Income Tax rate under Article 4 of the PRC Enterprise Income Tax Law

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% statutory withholding tax on dividends paid by a Chinese resident WFOE to a non-resident enterprise shareholder under PRC Enterprise Income Tax Law Articles 3 and 37, subject to reduction under an applicable bilateral Double Tax Treaty.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File documentation must be prepared when total annual related-party transactions exceed RMB 40 million; Master File documentation must be prepared when aggregate annual related-party transactions exceed RMB 1 billion.

Economic substance scope: Although China does not label its regime as Economic Substance Regulations, the PRC Enterprise Income Tax Law contains a general anti-avoidance rule, substance-over-form doctrine, and the OECD BEPS Principal Purpose Test. The WFOE must demonstrate core income-generating activities in China, including source and procurement of used excavators, pre-shipment inspection, storage, negotiation, export documentation, credit risk assumption, and logistics management. A company without these activities will be treated as a shell and face reallocation of profit to foreign principal, deemed permanent establishment, or denial of treaty benefits.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion - Deliberate Omission of Export Revenue

Statutory sanction: PRC Tax Administration Law Article 63 imposes recovery of underpaid tax plus a fine of 50% to 500% of the unpaid amount; Article 32 imposes a 0.05% daily late-payment surcharge; Criminal Law Article 201 imposes imprisonment up to 7 years and criminal fines for willful concealment of taxable income above the statutory threshold.

Director personal liability: Directors who authorize false accounts or offshore diversion may face piercing of the corporate veil under PRC Company Law, become jointly and severally liable for the company's tax arrears, and suffer freezing and confiscation of personal assets. Residential and travel restrictions are imposed through the tax blacklisting and frontier-exit control system, and directors with criminal exposure may be detained and imprisoned.
Transfer Pricing Abuse and Thin Capitalization

Statutory sanction: PRC Enterprise Income Tax Law Article 41 allows recharacterization and adjustment of non-arm's length transactions; Article 44 permits the tax authority to assess profit using available information when documentation is absent; Article 46 disallows related-party interest exceeding the 2:1 debt-to-equity ratio; Tax Administration Law Article 62 imposes a fine of up to RMB 10,000 for filing failures, and special tax adjustment interest of 5 percentage points above the benchmark loan rate is applied.

Director personal liability: Directors who deliberately sign uncommercial intercompany agreements, transfer assets to related parties, or participate in a fraudulent scheme to reduce taxable income can be held personally liable for the restored tax debt and related interest. Courts and tax authorities may pierce the corporate veil, lift corporate personality, trace distributions into directors' personal accounts, impose exit bans under the tax blacklist system, and consolidate civil liability with criminal charges for fraud.
AML / Shell Company / Economic Substance Abuse

Statutory sanction: PRC Anti-Money Laundering Law Articles 27 and 32 impose fines up to RMB 5 million, suspension of business, and revocation of licenses for concealment of beneficial ownership or violation of customer due diligence obligations; State Administration of Foreign Exchange Regulations impose a fine of 30% to 100% of unlawful foreign exchange proceeds; Criminal Law Article 191 imposes up to 10 years imprisonment and fines for money laundering.

Director personal liability: Directors who place this shell through repeated formations, make false declarations on the actual controller register, or commingle corporate and personal funds may see the corporate veil pierced, making them personally liable for all tax arrears and third-party claims. They also face criminal detention, personal fines, seizure of personal assets, immediate exit controls, and permanent prohibition from acting as company directors in China.
