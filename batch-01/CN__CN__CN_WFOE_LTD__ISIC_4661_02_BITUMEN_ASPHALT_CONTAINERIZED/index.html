Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum registered capital applies to a wholesale bitumen trading WFOE under the current PRC Company Law. The shareholders agree the registered capital and contribution schedule in the Articles of Association. Contributions may be paid in cash or in kind and must be fully paid within five years from incorporation unless the articles prescribe an earlier date. No blocked escrow account or capital verification account is required by law for an ordinary trading WFOE.

Funding & unlock path: As an office-trading entity, no civil defense or fire safety permit is required for the registered office beyond ordinary building compliance with the PRC Fire Protection Law and building code. If the company leases a warehouse or tank terminal for containerized bitumen, the lessor must provide a Fire Protection Acceptance Certificate for the building, and the lessee must obtain a Fire Safety Inspection Certificate before the facility is placed into service from the local Fire and Rescue Service. Structural load inspection by a qualified engineering inspection agency is required for stacking loaded containers, pallets, or tank racks. Occupational safety approval must follow the National Work Safety Law, including workplace risk assessment, emergency evacuation signage, PPE provision, spill containment, and annual safety training records. If a later activity introduces cutback bitumen, solvents, or other listed dangerous chemicals, the Emergency Management Bureau will require a separate hazardous chemicals operating permit.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China, operating through local tax service offices and the national Electronic Tax Bureau online taxpayer portal

E-government filing portal

For a WFOE, tax registration is merged into business registration under the multi-license integration reform. (1) The founding foreign investors register the limited liability WFOE with the Administration for Market Regulation and receive an 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) business license; the USCC is the statutory taxpayer and VAT registration identity, and no separate TIN certificate is issued. (2) Within 30 calendar days of license issuance, complete the supplementary tax registration data online in the Electronic Tax Bureau portal, including legal representative, responsible person, controllers, accounting protocol, industry classification, registered address, actual place of business, bank account details, and expected tax types. (3) The local tax authority activates the resident enterprise tax account and determines the initial tax registers, including whether VAT status is small-scale taxpayer or general VAT taxpayer and which filings apply. (4) For a foreign non-resident enterprise without an incorporated Chinese vehicle, a Chinese payer withholds the relevant taxes and handles non-resident tax registration through the e-tax withholding system; the non-resident enterprise obtains a Tax Identification Number or treaty identification only if required by the competent authority for the specific income payment.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None. China does not maintain a check-the-box or hybrid mismatch entity election. A WFOE is compulsorily classified as a resident enterprise taxpayer taxed on worldwide income. The only practical status election is the VAT taxpayer-status election: an active seller with annual taxable sales under the general VAT registration threshold may elect to register as a general VAT taxpayer to claim input VAT credits and export refunds.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

CNY 5,000,000 of annual taxable sales in the mainland VAT jurisdiction is the compulsory general VAT taxpayer registration threshold; every WFOE must complete basic tax registration upon license issuance irrespective of turnover, and sellers below that threshold are ordinarily classified as small-scale VAT taxpayers.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A general VAT taxpayer may claim the 0% export VAT rate for containerized bitumen/asphalt when the goods are actually sold to a non-resident overseas purchaser, physically exported from a Chinese customs office under an export customs declaration, transported outside Chinese customs territory, and supported by foreign-exchange receipt and export document evidence. The transaction must not fall into the VAT-exemption-without-refund category, and all export refund records must be submitted through the export tax refund system. For qualifying offshore digital services, zero-rating additionally requires the service to be supplied to a non-resident recipient and consumed wholly outside China.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bill of Entry) filed by the exporter or licensed customs broker through China's International Trade Single Window
  • Bill of Lading (or equivalent multimodal transport document) showing shipment from a Chinese port to the overseas destination
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) and/or bank foreign-exchange receipt evidencing receipt of the export sale proceeds in the exporter's domestic foreign-exchange account

VAT invoices must be issued through the STA-controlled electronic invoicing environment. Traditional paper VAT special and ordinary invoices are generated with approved tax-control/anti-counterfeit equipment or the e-invoice platform, and invoice data is transmitted in real time to the national Golden Tax administrative system. The newer Full Digital Electronic Invoice operates as structured data, is filed automatically into the national e-invoice platform, carries a unique digital invoice number, and does not require a physical seal. Red-letter credit-note invoices must be validated electronically before issuance, and all invoices are cross-checked in the national invoice verification database, which functions as the anti-fraud digital stamping/clearance control.

The STA export tax refund system conducts automated and manual risk control by matching customs declarations, transport documents, invoice data, foreign-exchange receipts and related-party indicators. If zero-rating or the resulting refund is unsubstantiated or fraudulent, the refunded VAT is clawed back together with late-payment surcharge, and administrative penalties of 0.5 to 5 times the assessed tax amount may be imposed under the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection. Cases involving false export declarations, forged documents or wholly fictitious export transactions are transferred to customs or tax police and may constitute the criminal offense of false export-tax refund fraud under the Criminal Law, exposing the entity to suspension of refund entitlement, increased audit scrutiny, severe imprisonment penalties, and confiscation of property.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a managed floating exchange rate regime with explicit differentiation between current-account and capital-account convertibility. Current account receipts have been substantially liberalized under SAFE, and banks can settle export proceeds into RMB automatically after verifying underlying trade documents. The RMB is not freely convertible for capital-account transactions. A Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE) requires SAFE registration for its foreign exchange capital account, and every inward injection must be reported and verified against the approved capital contribution plan. Surplus capital and dividends can only be remitted after audit, tax clearance, and SAFE/AML compliance checks. Profit repatriation is possible but requires evidence of payment of withholding tax, audited financial statements, and board/shareholder resolutions. Long-term debt, equity injection, and liquidation distributions are subject to tight quota, reporting, and real-background validation requirements. The authorities also monitor round-tripping, mismatches between trade flows and cash flows, and transfer pricing deviations. Therefore the treasury function must regard onshore and cross-border liquidity as distinct pools and must document every movement with a concrete trade or capital justification.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the current goods trade foreign exchange administration rules, export receipts must be repatriated through a domestic designated bank within 180 days of customs declaration or earlier if the underlying contract specifies a shorter term. There is no mandatory conversion ratio currently imposed; the WFOE may retain receipts in a qualified foreign exchange settlement account or convert to RMB. Any deferred payment period exceeding 180 days requires a prior SAFE filing and supporting trade documentation.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) and State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low. Chinese regulation requires all foreign exchange remittances and RMB/FX conversion to be handled by licensed financial institutions under SAFE supervision. Offshore fintech EMIs such as digital wallet or card providers do not possess a mainland commercial banking license and cannot open onshore native currency accounts for the WFOE. They may act only as supplementary collection tools in other jurisdictions, but using them as the primary treasury rail would create a regulatory violation, block capital verification, and prevent compliant profit repatriation.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File is mandatory when annual related-party transactions exceed any one of the following RMB thresholds: RMB 400 million for tangible property transactions, RMB 200 million for intangible property transactions, RMB 100 million for financial transactions, and RMB 100 million for other related-party transactions. Master File is triggered when the consolidated group revenue of the ultimate parent exceeds RMB 5.5 billion and the Chinese entity is itself obliged to file a Local File.

Economic substance scope: China does not administer offshore Economic Substance Regulations of the UAE type; however, the State Taxation Administration applies the General Anti-Abuse Rule (CITA Article 47) and the Limitation-on-Benefits clause in tax treaties to deny treaty benefits or incentive rates where the WFOE lacks commercial substance. The Chinese entity must prove that Core Income-Generating Activities (CIGA) such as procurement, inventory management, logistics decision-making, risk assumption, container contracting, invoicing and treasury control are exercised in China with adequate staff, office and documentation.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Understatement

Statutory sanction: CITA Article 50 and Tax Collection and Administration Law Article 63 impose confiscation of illegal gains, a penalty of 50% to 500% of the tax underpaid, daily late-payment surcharge at 0.05%, and, under Criminal Law Article 201, a criminal fine plus imprisonment of up to 7 years for those directly responsible.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced where directors participate in arranging the false understatement; directors become jointly and severally liable for the unpaid corporate tax, penalties and surcharges. They may be subjected to a departure prohibition order (travel blacklisting) under TCAL Article 12 where the unpaid tax exceeds RMB 10,000, and face criminal imprisonment if convicted of tax evasion. Civil asset restitution can reach the director's personal assets to the extent the underpaid liability was used to fund shareholder distributions or was shifted through a sham offshore entity.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance and Treaty Abuse

Statutory sanction: The tax authority may invoke CITA Article 47 general anti-abuse rules to recharacterize the entire structure, deny the treaty claim, add the full 25% tax with a 15% non-deductible interest surcharge on the historical adjustment, and impose penalties under TCAL Article 44 at 50% to 300% of the additional tax.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign treaty benefit declarations knowing the WFOE has no commercial substance are personally liable for any wrongful treaty relief obtained; they can be subject to civil restitution equal to the tax deficiency plus interest. Criminal liability may attach under Criminal Law Article 201 for intentional misrepresentation, and the responsible director may be placed on China's tax departure blacklist, preventing travel and asset exit.
Transfer Pricing Misdeclaration and Base Erosion

Statutory sanction: CITA Article 41 authorizes the tax authority to apply the arm's length principle and make transfer pricing adjustments. SAT Announcement [2016] No. 42 imposes a 15% non-deductible interest surcharge on contemporaneous documentation deficiencies, penalties for filing failures up to RMB 10,000, and the tax bureau may apply a penalty of 50% to 300% of the underpaid tax for negligent or intentional mispricing.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly sign fraudulent intercompany contracts or who approve fictitious container and trucking fee arrangements are personally liable for the resulting tax deficiency under civil tort law and corporate law. Deliberate TP manipulation that creates fake invoices can trigger Criminal Law Article 205, exposing the responsible officers and directors to criminal fines and detention of 3 to 10 years. The court may lift the corporate veil and order the director's personal assets to be frozen to satisfy the group's unpaid tax liabilities.
