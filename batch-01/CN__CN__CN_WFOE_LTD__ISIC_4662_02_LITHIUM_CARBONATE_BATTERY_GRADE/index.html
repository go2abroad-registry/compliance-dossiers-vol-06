Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; online electronic portal: National Electronic Tax System (e-Tax) at etax.chinatax.gov.cn

E-government filing portal

For a foreign non-resident enterprise forming a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE), the WFOE is incorporated in China and therefore obtains a Chinese resident taxpayer identity. The statutory Tax Identification Number (TIN) / VAT identity is the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), automatically generated at registration and synchronized by the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) to the State Taxation Administration (STA). The mandatory procedure is: (1) register the WFOE with SAMR and obtain the Business License; (2) complete the supplementary tax registration information confirmation through the e-Tax portal with the competent local STA office within 30 days of the Business License; (3) use the USCC as the TIN/VAT ID and download the electronic tax registration certificate; (4) activate VAT taxpayer status by determining whether the entity is a small-scale or general VAT taxpayer; (5) open the VAT invoice-issuing account and select the invoice mode (fully digitalized e-invoice or special VAT invoice via tax-control equipment); and (6) register employer withholding, customs, and foreign exchange profiles with the relevant authorities. A foreign non-resident parent company without a permanent establishment in China does not obtain a formation TIN; where it derives China-source income, the Chinese payer/withholding agent withholds tax, and the non-resident can register or file for treaty relief through the e-Tax non-resident service module.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None – China does not operate a check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election framework. A WFOE is mandatorily treated as a Chinese resident enterprise taxable at entity level on worldwide income. The only available status election is under VAT: an enterprise below the general taxpayer threshold may voluntarily register as a general VAT taxpayer, but no entity classification election exists for corporate income tax purposes.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

There is no turnover-based exemption from VAT registration for a WFOE; every enterprise must register as a VAT taxpayer upon incorporation. Mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration applies when taxable turnover exceeds CNY 5,000,000 in any rolling 12 consecutive months; below that threshold small-scale taxpayer status (simplified levy) applies.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export of physical goods (including battery-grade lithium carbonate powder) is zero-rated under China's VAT export tax refund (exemption) regime. Statutory eligibility requires: (1) the goods are physically exported from the China Customs territory under a valid Customs Export Declaration; (2) the WFOE is the registered exporter and a VAT general taxpayer; (3) the goods were previously purchased with input VAT that is identifiable and traced through the e-invoice system; (4) ownership and risk pass to an overseas buyer; (5) the sale consideration is expressed and collected in a permitted foreign currency; and (6) the export proceeds are repatriated into China within the statutory foreign-exchange collection period. Qualifying cross-border services may also be zero-rated if supplied to overseas recipients, paid in foreign currency, and listed in the STA cross-border services zero-rating catalogue.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bill of Entry) evidencing physical exit from China customs territory
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill or equivalent transport document issued by the carrier
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) / bank foreign exchange receipt voucher evidencing collection from the overseas buyer
  • Export Proceeds Verification record submitted through the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) monitoring system
  • Export Contract / Sales Agreement with the overseas buyer
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Any customs-required inspection or quality certificate for the lithium-carbonate consignment

China mandates STA-approved electronic invoicing through the national e-invoice (fully digitalized electronic invoice) platform and the Golden Tax system. Invoice data is transmitted to the STA in real time, encrypted with a unique tax-control code and anti-fraud digital signature, and cleared through the national invoice verification system before the buyer may legally claim an input VAT credit. Special VAT invoices for domestic sales require tax-control equipment/software; fully digitalized e-invoices carry an embedded statutory electronic stamp and QR code and require no physical tax seal.

Export zero-rating claims are jointly scrutinized by the STA and Customs risk-control systems. If the goods are not actually exported, the Customs declaration is false or inconsistent with the bill of lading, or foreign exchange proceeds are not repatriated within the applicable 180-day period, the refund is denied and any zero-rating already granted is retrospectively clawed back with statutory late-payment interest. Fraudulent or materially false export-refund claims expose the WFOE to administrative fines of up to five times the tax defrauded, suspension or revocation of export-tax-refund qualifications, publication of non-compliant taxpayers, and referral for criminal prosecution for tax evasion/fraud under the Criminal Law. The STA assessment/recovery window is five years for ordinary adjustments, but in cases of deliberate fraud recovery is not time-barred.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Chinese renminbi (CNY) operates under a managed floating exchange rate regime anchored to a trade-weighted basket of currencies. Mainland China has achieved full current-account convertibility for trade and ordinary business transactions, yet the capital account remains subject to a comprehensive registration, approval, and quota-based control architecture administered by SAFE. A WFOE can receive foreign equity capital through a dedicated foreign exchange capital account only after completing FDI foreign exchange registration with the local SAFEbranch and filing it with the designated custodian bank. The contribution must be economically justified, consistent with the registered business scope, and verified by a licensed CPA after remittance. Dividend repatriation from the WFOE to its foreign parent is permissible once the entity has completed annual audits, filed withholding tax returns, obtained the certificate of tax paid, and presented board resolutions evidencing distributable profits. Profit remittance to non-resident shareholders generally requires a real economic substance review, tax clearance certificates, and a bank-level KYC investigation into the recipient's beneficial ownership. The Chinese regulatory framework does not recognize offshore fintech EMIs as qualified clearing or settlement institutions for onshore RMB conversion or foreign exchange transactions; any entity seeking to operate a domestic corporate treasury must engage a licensed onshore bank. Current-account payments, such as trade disbursements for imported lithium carbonate, are liberally permitted provided the underlying contract, customs declaration, shipping documents, invoice, and logistics evidence are presented to the bank. However, a false trade declaration, mismatch between invoice value and customs valuation, or evidence of round-tripping capital through trade invoices could trigger anti-money laundering inquiry, SAFE investigation, and asset freeze protocols.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under current SAFE regulations, there is no fixed mandatory conversion ratio for export foreign exchange receipts. Export proceeds must, however, be repatriated to China and credited to a domestic foreign currency settlement account within 180 days of the date of export declaration unless an extension is approved by the local SAFE branch. The WFOE may retain such proceeds in foreign currency or voluntarily convert them into RMB within its bank-assigned annual foreign exchange settlement quota, subject to authenticity and T+2 matching verification plus trade documentation review.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China, operating under the mandate and policy framework of the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Not viable in Mainland China. The PBOC and SAFE do not authorize offshore digital EMI institutions to open onshore RMB accounts, provide CNY foreign exchange conversion, or execute SAFE-regulated capital account transactions. Foreign fintech EMIs also cannot connect directly to the China Payment and Clearing Association's interbank clearing system for corporate treasury operations. A WFOE in the lithium carbonate trade must use a licensed domestic commercial bank for all onshore clearing, FX conversion, and capital verification procedures. EMIs may be useful only as ancillary payment tools outside China, but they cannot replace the mandatory PRC banking rails.

Defense evidence: Provide a completion certificate from the Chinese corporate registry identifying all shareholders above the WFOE; a full organizational chart through the ultimate individual shareholders; certified passport or national ID copies of each natural person; audited group financial statements; and a sworn statement explaining source of wealth and source of funds for the capital injection.
Trade-based money laundering and transaction-invoice value manipulation in lithium carbonate commodity flows

Root cause: Trade-based money laundering and transaction-invoice value manipulation in lithium carbonate commodity flows

Defense evidence: Submit the original purchase contract, two or more independent third-party commodity price indices, sales invoices, packing list, bill of lading, customs declaration forms, cargo insurance certificate, and a licensed customs broker's declaration summary showing that the invoiced unit prices are within the tolerance band of internationally accepted battery-grade lithium carbonate prices.
Sanctions or political exposure risk on end customers, freight forwarders, and upstream lithium concentrate suppliers

Root cause: Sanctions or political exposure risk on end customers, freight forwarders, and upstream lithium concentrate suppliers

Defense evidence: Produce a completed sanctions screening report from an independent provider covering all counterparties, beneficial owners, shipping lines, and freight forwarders; explicit compliance certificates from each supplier and customer; end-user representation letters; and an internal trade compliance manual showing that no entity in the chain appears on the OFAC, UN, EU, or SAFE restricted lists.

Root cause: Unclear provenance of injected equity capital and possible round-tripping from related party transactions

Defense evidence: Deliver the audited balance sheet of the foreign parent, bank statements covering the remitting account for at least 12 months, board resolution authorizing the contribution, share allotment registry, and a notarized corporate undertaking that the injected funds are not derived from illegal activities or from offshore proceeds of PRC origin.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The inbound remittance must be routed via SWIFT MT103 to the WFOE's dedicated Foreign Exchange Capital Account at an approved onshore bank. Before remittance, the WFOE must complete the FDI foreign exchange registration with SAFE and obtain a Foreign Exchange Business Registration Form bearing a unique code. The remitter's message must include the SAFE registration code, the intended purpose code for direct investment equity injection, and a clear reference to 'Equity Contribution under FDI Registration Number [XXXX]'. Upon receipt, the bank verifies the remittance against the filed capital schedule, holds the funds in an escrow-like manner until CPA capital verification, and then releases the funds to a general RMB or foreign currency settlement account after issuance of the capital verification report and an intragroup borrowing or disbursement justification. The bank will also require the WFOE to produce a receipt of tax registration and, post-injection, a filing with SAFE indicating the actual utilization date and amount.Trade settlements for lithium carbonate imports from outside China should be cleared using SWIFT MT103 payment instructions for ordinary current-account transactions, irrevocable documentary commercial letters of credit (L/C) for high-value supplier relationships, and documentary collections for smaller or repeat purchases. For every outward remittance, the WFOE must provide bank requested documents including the signed commercial contract, invoice, bill of lading or airway bill, packing list, customs declaration, and a calibration certificate from an independent laboratory confirming the battery-grade specification. When the final consignee is located in a restricted area, the bank may require a Commodity Due Diligence Report and a denial-of-shipment certificate. The outbound route must also satisfy SAFE's authentic transaction verification and the PBOC's cross-border RMB reporting thresholds if the settlement is in CNY under a RMB settle agreement.Identifiable and documented corporate spot-forward contracts for hedging trade receivables or payables denominated in USD, EUR, or JPY with a qualified domestic bank, provided the underlying export/import transaction has been verified by the bank.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) prohibited onshore; offshore CNH NDFs may be used by the foreign parent only as a synthetic hedge, not by the Chinese WFOE directly.Cross-currency swaps permitted strictly for current-account trade flows where the WFOE has a verifiable foreign currency liability or receivable and the swap is approved by the bank's treasury desk under SAFE margin and registration rules.Plain-vanilla CNY foreign exchange options that reduce, not amplify, the WFOE's long-term exposure to USD/CNY volatility, subject to a real trade-background test and two-way settlement documentation.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Not viable in Mainland China. The PBOC and SAFE do not authorize offshore digital EMI institutions to open onshore RMB accounts, provide CNY foreign exchange conversion, or execute SAFE-regulated capital account transactions. Foreign fintech EMIs also cannot connect directly to the China Payment and Clearing Association's interbank clearing system for corporate treasury operations. A WFOE in the lithium carbonate trade must use a licensed domestic commercial bank for all onshore clearing, FX conversion, and capital verification procedures. EMIs may be useful only as ancillary payment tools outside China, but they cannot replace the mandatory PRC banking rails.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: RMB 40,000,000 of annual related-party service fees, interest, or other transactions; RMB 100,000,000 for transfers of intangibles or financial assets; RMB 200,000,000 for tangible goods transactions - crossing any threshold triggers mandatory Local File preparation; Master File is required when aggregate related-party transactions exceed RMB 1,000,000,000 or when designated by the STA; Country-by-Country reporting applies to MNEs with consolidated group revenue above RMB 5,500,000,000.

Economic substance scope: China does not operate a separate Economic Substance Regulations regime as seen in other jurisdictions, but the Enterprise Income Tax Law's General Anti-Avoidance Rule and the Special Tax Adjustment provisions impose a substance-over-form test: a non-resident claiming treaty benefits or intercompany pricing must demonstrate that its Core Income-Generating Activities are performed in its claimed jurisdiction, with real personnel, offices, and decision-making functions. The Beneficial Ownership circular of 2018 requires substantive economic activity for treaty relief, and Chinese tax authorities may challenge mailbox companies and back-to-back arrangements both prospectively and retroactively.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion (Fraudulent Trade Mis-Invoicing)

Statutory sanction: Article 63 of the Law on Administration of Tax Collection imposes confiscation of illegal gains and a penalty of 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax; customs valuation penalties apply under the Customs Law; Article 201 of the Criminal Law authorizes imprisonment up to 3 years, or 3-7 years where the underpaid tax exceeds RMB 100,000 and exceeds 10% of the tax due, together with criminal fines; the STA also blacklists the entity under the Joint Punishment of Dishonest Taxpayers measures, with public disclosure and blocking of export tax rebates.

Director personal liability: Piercing the corporate veil: directors who ordered or approved the false invoices are jointly and severally liable for the short-paid tax, penalties, and the daily 0.05% surcharge out of personal assets; personal travel bans and exit restrictions apply to directors named on the tax dishonesty blacklist; the chief financial officer and chairman risk criminal detention, imprisonment of 3-7 years, personal fines, and civil restitution of the full evaded tax.
Abusive Treaty Shopping / Shell Non-Resident Parent / False Beneficial Ownership

Statutory sanction: The General Anti-Avoidance Rule and Special Tax Adjustment provisions (Articles 120-125 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law and Article 47 of the Tax Collection Law) allow denial of treaty benefits, recharacterization of the transaction, additional Enterprise Income Tax and withholding tax, interest at the statutory overdue rate, and a penalty of up to 50%-300% of the adjusted tax; the tax authorities may disregard the intermediate special purpose vehicle and deem the dividend paid to the ultimate parent, applying the 10% statutory withholding differential.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign beneficial-ownership declarations known to be false are personally liable for the withholding tax difference plus penalties; personal bank accounts may be frozen and assets seized; travel bans and personal credit blacklisting apply; criminal liability for tax fraud arises if the treaty abuse conceals taxable income, carrying imprisonment up to 7 years and personal fines; joint and several civil restitution of the entire under-withheld tax is imposed on the responsible directors.
AML Violations / Trade-Based Money Laundering Using Fraudulent Payment Rails

Statutory sanction: The Anti-Money Laundering Law (2025 amendments) imposes fines up to RMB 5,000,000 on institutions and RMB 500,000 on responsible officers, with suspension of operations for egregious cases; Article 191 of the Criminal Law treats trade-based money laundering as a separate offense carrying 5-10 years imprisonment plus confiscation of proceeds; customs fraud penalties reach 10%-50% of the duty or tax evaded; the PBOC and STA share suspicious-transaction reports, and false Letter of Credit documents trigger banking blacklisting of the WFOE.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners with control over the payment rails are personally subject to criminal fines and imprisonment of 5-10 years; personal assets may be confiscated as instrumentalities of money laundering; the corporate veil is pierced to impose joint and several civil liability on directors for the repatriated funds; travel bans, exit restrictions, and banking blacklisting apply; directors may also be banned from serving as senior officers of any Chinese entity and face civil restitution actions by customs and tax authorities.
