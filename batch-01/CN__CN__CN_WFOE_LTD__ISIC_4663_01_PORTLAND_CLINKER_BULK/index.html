Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None for a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise engaged in ISIC 4663 wholesale distribution of construction materials. The registered capital is determined by the Articles of Association and contributed by the shareholder within the contribution period stated in the AoA, subject to the maximum five-year capital payment period under Article 47 of the Company Law. There is no government-mandated blocked escrow account requirement for this business scope; capital may be paid in RMB or convertible foreign currency into the company's capital contribution account at a domestic designated bank, and the contribution schedule must be filed with SAMR.

Funding & unlock path: The warehouse or office premises must obtain fire protection design review and fire safety acceptance or recordation certification from the local Fire and Rescue Brigade before occupancy, including installation of fire extinguishers, emergency lighting, exit signs, smoke alarms and an approved evacuation plan. Structural load compliance is evidenced by the building property certificate and the construction completion inspection certificate; no separate structural load inspection is required for ordinary office or palletized bagged clinker storage. The company must also establish workplace safety clearance under the Law on Work Safety and the Law on Prevention and Control of Occupational Diseases, including a dust exposure assessment where cement dust is handled, provision of dust masks and protective equipment, and registration of workers in the occupational hazard monitoring system. If the company operates only through third-party warehouses and does not physically handle goods, the civil defense fire permit and occupational hazard filing are Not Applicable except for the general office fire safety certificate.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA); the principal online compliance portal is the national Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax) system, accessible at https://etax.chinatax.gov.cn, supplemented by the local e-tax service portal designated by the municipal STA office of registration.

E-government filing portal

Upon incorporation with the Administration for Market Regulation (AMR), the WFOE is automatically assigned a Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which serves as the statutory taxpayer identification number for both Enterprise Income Tax and VAT. The WFOE must complete tax registration with the competent STA office within 30 days of issuance of the business license. The registration is effected through the e-Tax portal by filing the required tax registration data, uploading the electronic business license, corporate charter, legal representative identity proof, and financial/accounting system statement. The STA activates the USCC for all tax filings after verification, and no separate paper certificate is required beyond the Tax Registration Confirmation. A foreign non-resident enterprise without a Chinese resident establishment that derives PRC-sourced income or carries on a permanent establishment must complete non-resident tax registration with the STA to obtain a temporary tax ID or be subjected to statutory withholding by its paying counterpart; no minimum capital requirement applies to the WFOE formation.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export sales of Portland clinker in bulk by the WFOE are eligible for zero-rating under the PRC VAT export rebate system if the goods are physically exported from PRC customs territory, the exporter is a general VAT taxpayer, the sale is bona fide, the goods are on the permitted export product list, and the foreign exchange proceeds are repatriated in accordance with SAFE rules. The zero rate eliminates the output VAT on the export sale while preserving the WFOE’s right to a refund or offset of input VAT credited to the export product chain. Qualifying offshore digital services, if ever in scope, require proof of delivery to recipients outside China and final consumption tests as described in the VAT circular.

  • Export Customs Declaration bearing the customs release number and approved export date
  • Bill of Lading or sea waybill evidencing cargo shipment beyond the PRC customs boundary
  • Commercial invoice, packing list, and export sales contract
  • Bank receipt of foreign exchange settlement evidencing inward remittance (SAFE-certified)
  • Export foreign exchange receipt verification record from the SAFE export monitoring system
  • VAT ordinary invoice or VAT special invoice for export sale
  • Export license or other statutory permit, if the specific clinker shipment is subject to export controls

The WFOE must issue VAT invoices through the national VAT invoice management system integrated with the e-Tax platform in near real time. The Golden Tax Phase IV system requires digital upload of all invoice data, including issuer, purchaser, item, amount, tax and invoice code. Full digital electronic invoices (e-invoices) are the statutory norm for general VAT taxpayers; approved ERP APIs allow system-to-system fiscal clearance. Each invoice carries a unique invoice code and serial number, and anti-fraud algorithmic monitoring is mandatory. Any remaining paper invoice usage is being phased out under the national fully-digitalized e-invoice framework. Export transactions must be linked to electronic customs clearance data to substantiate zero-rated VAT treatment.

False or unsupported claims of export zero-rating expose the WFOE to joint audit by the STA and Customs. Penalties include confiscation of the VAT refunded, a fine of one to five times the amount defrauded, late-payment surcharge, suspension of export tax refund entitlement, downgrade of taxpayer credit status, customs blacklisting, and possible criminal prosecution for the crime of defrauding export tax refunds. Retrospective clawback can cover a ten-year period in cases of serious fraud; any VAT refunded on the basis of incomplete, false, or extraneously defective customs or foreign exchange documentation must be repaid with interest, and the sale may be reclassified as a domestic taxable sale, requiring output VAT at the standard rate in addition to penalties.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

As a component of China's strictly regulated financial system, the Renminbi enjoys current-account convertibility for documented bona fide trade and travel transactions, but remains subject to comprehensive capital-account convertibility controls. SAFE administers the Foreign Exchange Registration System, under which FDI inflows, by way of registered capital contributions, require a Foreign Exchange Registration Notice and are routed through a Foreign Exchange Capital Account that is earmarked for approved legitimate business purposes. Outbound trade settlements must be supported by the Customs declaration, commercial invoices, and transport documents, which the banks verify under the principle of external truthfulness and prudence. Profit remittance by WFOEs is possible through the annual audit and tax clearance process, but foreign shareholders must present the audited financial statements, corporate income tax filing completion certificate, and a board resolution on dividend distribution. Dividend remittances do not require separate SAFE approval once the bank verifies these documents, yet any principal repayment of shareholder loans or repatriation of capital will trigger capital-account safeguards, requiring prior registration and matching verification. Surrender of export proceeds has been significantly liberalized since 2012, with no mandatory conversion; enterprises may retain designated percentages in foreign currency accounts at domestic banks, provided all receipts are repatriated for settlement or retained with a compliant receipt declaration. Nonetheless, under current trade-facilitation reforms, banks apply a macro-prudential assessment and use a whitelist of credible enterprises to monitor abnormal cross-border flows, and any mismatch between declared trade and actual payments may trigger a SAFE onsite inspection. The practical result is that the WFOE needs disciplined documentary support for every CNY cross-border transfer, but standard import/export, service, and dividend transactions can flow within statutory timelines if files are complete.

Export proceeds surrender rules

China has no general mandatory surrender of export proceeds. Under SAFE regulations, a resident exporter is required to repatriate export proceeds to mainland China through designated banks or retain them in a domestic foreign currency account or approved offshore account within 90 days from the date of export customs declaration, unless otherwise approved for longer offshore retention. Conversion into RMB is at the exporter's election on a transaction-by-transaction basis, but the bank must verify the corresponding Customs export declaration and the foreign exchange receipt collection payment system entry before completing a settlement.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Wise Fit: High

Low to not viable in mainland China. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: In CNY, a Local File is mandatory when related-party transactions in a fiscal year reach: tangible asset transfers RMB 200 million, intangible asset transfers RMB 100 million, financial asset transfers RMB 100 million, or other related-party transactions RMB 40 million. A Master File is mandatory when aggregate related-party transactions in one year exceed RMB 1 billion. Related-party transaction reporting schedules must accompany the annual corporate income tax filing.

Economic substance scope: Economic Substance Regulations in the strict free-zone form are not enacted in mainland China. Instead, the State Taxation Administration applies tax-residency and substance-over-form principles through the General Anti-Avoidance Rule, special tax adjustment rules, and the Principal Purpose Test under double tax treaties. A WFOE trading Portland clinker must evidence real personnel, premises, decision-making, invoices, storage, freight contracts, and bank flows in China. The Core Income-Generating Activities of wholesale distribution must be performed by the China entity. If management and control are shifted solely to obtain a treaty benefit, the tax authority may recharacterize the arrangement, reattribute profits to the Chinese entity, deny treaty relief, or impose special tax adjustment interest and penalties.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Transfer Pricing / Profit Stripping

Statutory sanction: The State Taxation Administration is authorized under Articles 41 and 48 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law to make special tax adjustments, reprice the intercompany transaction, impose interest at the benchmark lending rate plus five percentage points, and assess penalties of 50% to 500% of underpaid tax under Article 63 of the Tax Collection and Administration Law if the mispricing is fraudulent.

Director personal liability: Chinese courts may pierce the corporate veil under Article 20 of the former Company Law and Article 23 of the revised Company Law if shareholders abuse limited liability and strip the WFOE of assets. Directors who knowingly sign false transfer pricing documentation can be ordered to personally restore unpaid tax and damages to creditors. In serious tax evasion cases, the legal representative and chief financial officer risk criminal prosecution under Article 201 of the Criminal Law, with imprisonment for up to seven years for particularly large amounts, plus fines, travel bans, asset freezes, and publication on the serious tax dishonesty list.
Fictitious Invoicing and VAT Fraud

Statutory sanction: Issuing false VAT special invoices violates Article 205 of the Criminal Law. Sanctions include fixed-term imprisonment of ten years to life imprisonment for severe amounts, confiscation of property, and monetary fines. Administratively, Article 63 of the Tax Collection and Administration Law imposes a 50% to 500% penalty, denial of input VAT credits, and suspension of VAT invoice quotas or invoice system access.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil will be pierced because false invoices are instruments of crime and not an independent corporate act. The director who procures or authorizes the false invoices is jointly and severally liable with the company for unpaid tax, social insurance, and trade creditors. Criminal liability attaches to the directly responsible person and can include asset freezing, border travel bans, and imprisonment. Director indemnity insurance and company indemnities cannot cover criminal fines.
Withholding Tax Avoidance and Treaty Abuse

Statutory sanction: The State Taxation Administration will disregard the abusive arrangement under Article 47 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law and the Principal Purpose Test under the Multilateral Convention. The mischaracterized payment is recast as a dividend, the statutory 10% withholding tax is collected, interest runs from the original withholding date, and penalties of 50% to 300% of the unwithheld tax are imposed under Article 69 of the Tax Collection and Administration Law. The conduit entity's treaty relief claim is rejected and the taxpayer is named on the public serious tax violation list.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign sham contracts and service agreements without economic substance face civil liability for the WFOE's unpaid withholding because their conduct constitutes abuse of juridical personality. Under the Company Law veil-piercing doctrine, directors and shareholders may be jointly and severally liable to the tax authority. Criminal liability may attach if false documents cause a tax loss exceeding RMB 100,000; directors may be placed on the exit-ban list, detained at the border, and sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment of up to seven years under Article 201 of the Criminal Law for evading the collection of tax.
