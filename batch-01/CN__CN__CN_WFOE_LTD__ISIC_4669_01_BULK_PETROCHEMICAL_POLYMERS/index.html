Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety: the registered office and any associated warehouse must obtain the Fire Safety Acceptance Certificate or, for facilities below the statutory threshold, a filing (recordation) of fire safety self-inspection in accordance with the Fire Protection Law of the PRC (2019 amendment). Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; electronic filing and registration are performed through the STA Electronic Tax Bureau e-Tax portal (https://etax.chinatax.gov.cn), with the central public information portal at https://www.chinatax.gov.cn.

E-government filing portal

The WFOE is incorporated in mainland China as a Chinese resident legal person, so it does not use a foreign non-resident tax registration path. It obtains its statutory Tax Identification Number through the registration integration procedure: (1) the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) issues the business license with an 18-character Uniform Social Credit Code (USCC); (2) the USCC is automatically transmitted to the STA as the unified taxpayer identifier for VAT, Enterprise Income Tax, stamp duty and all other tax filings; (3) if automatic tax registration is not completed, the legal representative must complete tax registration through the STA Electronic Tax Bureau within 30 days of business license issuance, providing the business license, articles of association, legal representative identity document, registered-address proof, bank account information and accounting records; (4) a separate TIN/VAT ID number is not issued because the USCC is the single tax code. For a foreign non-resident entity with China-source income but no registered Chinese establishment, the foreign entity or its Chinese withholding agent completes non-resident tax information registration with the local STA office, supplying the home-country tax identification number and a certificate of tax residence; the STA then assigns a non-resident taxpayer master-file code for withholding, VAT and Enterprise Income Tax purposes.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation (resident WFOE taxed on worldwide income; no territorial exemption and no tax-exempt-zone classification)

Available election forms

None; China has no check-the-box, LLC pass-through, or hybrid mismatch elections for income tax. Only a VAT registration classification election exists: a below-threshold WFOE may voluntarily register as a general VAT taxpayer to claim input credits; otherwise it remains a small-scale taxpayer.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Basic tax registration is mandatory from the date of establishment with no turnover threshold; mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration is triggered when annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000, while businesses at or below that threshold are small-scale taxpayers subject to the 3% levy rate (unless the general taxpayer election is exercised).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The export of qualifying goods, including virgin polyethylene and polypropylene granules, is eligible for 0% VAT export zero-rating only if the goods are physically exported from mainland China under a valid customs export declaration, the sale is a genuine transfer of title to a non-resident overseas buyer at arm's length, the complete export documentation set is internally consistent, and the export proceeds are received through a Chinese-licensed bank within the statutory foreign-exchange repatriation deadline. The WFOE may then apply the export exemption-and-refund method to recover attributable input VAT on the exported petrochemical polymers.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bill of Entry) issued by China Customs after actual export clearance
  • Bill of Lading, sea waybill, air waybill or international consignment note evidencing physical movement of the goods out of mainland China
  • Commercial invoice and detailed packing list conforming exactly to the export declaration
  • Sales contract and export order specifying Incoterms, overseas buyer identity and payment terms
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or Chinese bank-issued export foreign-exchange receipt voucher recorded in the SAFE Service System for cross-border current-account receipts
  • VAT invoice, where applicable, issued through the STA e-invoice platform with the digital tax-control stamp

All VAT invoices, including special and ordinary VAT invoices, must be issued through the STA-authorized Electronic Invoice Service Platform using the full digital electronic invoice (FDEI) mechanism integrated with the Golden Tax System; invoice data is transmitted to the tax authority in real time, each invoice receives an automatic digital tax-control stamp and unique invoice code, and no manual or offline billing is permitted outside the approved electronic clearance process.

The STA will audit the export file for document consistency, physical exit, counterparty substance, transfer-pricing integrity and timely repatriation of foreign exchange. Missing, inconsistent, or forged customs declarations, bills of lading, payment records, or FIRC-equivalent banking evidence will cause the export zero-rating to be withdrawn; the transaction will be recharacterized as a domestic taxable sale with 13% VAT assessed on the full export price, previously refunded input VAT will be clawed back with daily late-payment interest, and penalties ranging from 50% to five times the tax involved may be imposed in fraudulent or grossly negligent cases, with referral to criminal authorities for export VAT fraud under the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China maintains a bifurcated foreign exchange system. Current account items are generally freely convertible, permitting trade-related payments and income remittances after simple documentary verification; however, capital account items were historically subject to strict quota-and-approval controls. For a WFOE engaged in wholesale petrochemical polymer distribution, operating profit and dividend remittance are classified under the current account and can be converted into USD/EUR at a licensed bank, provided the bank receives audited financial statements, tax payment certificates on profit remittance, and board resolution declaring the distribution. The capital account side remains controlled in respect of equity injection, external debt, and cross-border guarantees. All capital inflows under the FDI framework must be lodged into a designated capital foreign exchange settlement account and are subject to negative-list review and SAFE's payment-purpose checking. Repatriation of the original paid-in capital is possible only after lawful dissolution and a SAFE tax clawback certificate. Thus, convertibility is high for operational trade flows but not fully free for financial transactions; treasury planning must preserve a permanent documentary chain.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE's 2020 Measures for Administration of Trade in Goods FX, exporters must repatriate 100% of contract export proceeds to China and account for them through the Trade Monitoring System no later than 180 days after the customs export declaration date. There is no statutory mandatory conversion ratio; the exporter may voluntarily sell foreign exchange or retain the proceeds in a non-resident/foreign-currency settlement account until ready for CNY conversion. However, the target WFOE must comply with the 'collection verification' matching of Customs export data to bank receipts to avoid administrative penalties.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) and State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Viability is Low for all offshore digital EMIs. The People's Bank of China and SAFE only recognise licensed banks and bank-like financial institutions for opening corporate CNY and foreign currency accounts; standalone EMIs are not granted a banking licence in China. Foreign fintech EMIs may collaborate with a local bank but cannot provide settlement finality or hold the WFOE's funds independently. Domestic fintechs such as Alipay and WeChat Pay are payment tools, not account-holding institutions, and cannot satisfy SAFE's account host responsibility for corporate treasury. Therefore, the WFOE must rely on a tier-1 bank rail, using EMIs solely as payment initiation interfaces if the bank permits the API connection.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

FDI capital injection must utilise the approved SAFE foreign exchange settlement account under the bank's direct remittance rail. The remitter should wire via SWIFT MT103 with purpose code accurately described as 'FDI paid-in capital contribution'; the receiving bank then files capital account information through the SAFE digital system. The investor must complete the FDI Admittance Form and obtain a capital verification confirmation from a Chinese certified public accountant. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Under SAT Public Notice [2016] No. 42, a Local File must be maintained by a Chinese controlled taxpayer in any fiscal year where related-party tangible property transfers reach or exceed RMB 200 million; related-party intangible property transfers or financial-asset transfers reach or exceed RMB 100 million; or other related-party transactions reach or exceed RMB 40 million. A Master File may also be required if the global ultimate parent prepares one or if the Chinese enterprise meets statutory turnover/related-party transaction thresholds.

Economic substance scope: China does not impose a separate Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) filing similar to some offshore financial centers; however, substantive economic activities are embedded in the general anti-avoidance rule under CITA Article 47 and in treaty benefit entitlement. A WFOE distributing bulk petrochemical polymers must demonstrate that it performs real functions (e.g. procurement negotiation, inventory risk, logistics, credit risk, and Chinese market development), owns or controls relevant assets, and employs qualified personnel in China. Lacking CIGA in China would expose intercompany cross-border flows to recharacterization, denial of treaty benefits, and penalty.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Tax Return

Statutory sanction: Tax Collection Administration Law Article 63: monetary penalty of 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax, plus daily late-payment interest. Criminal Law Article 201: criminal tax evasion conviction attracts a fine and imprisonment of up to 7 years where the evaded amount is substantial.

Director personal liability: Courts and tax authorities may pierce the corporate veil where the WFOE is a mere facade or assets are commingled with shareholder personal assets. Directors who ordered, approved, or participated in the evasion face personal civil liability to the tax authority and to creditors; the legal representative may be placed on the exit-ban (blacklisting) system preventing overseas travel until taxes are settled. Senior officers face criminal detention or imprisonment and are disqualified from acting as directors.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation / Thin Capitalization

Statutory sanction: CITA Articles 41 and 46 authorize tax authorities to make a transfer-pricing adjustment to taxable income. The excessive-interest expense is disallowed and may be recharacterized as a deemed dividend subject to 10% withholding tax. Sanctions under Tax Collection Administration Law apply for underpayment of tax, including interest and penalties up to 50% to 500% of unpaid tax.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign transfer-pricing documentation or knowingly approve non-arm's length transactions can be held jointly and severally liable to the company and tax authority for resulting tax shortfalls where bad faith or misconduct is proved. Exit controls, asset freezes, and blacklisting may attach to the legal representative. In serious cases involving forged intercompany agreements, criminal fraud or tax evasion charges can extend to individual directors.
Treaty Shopping / Shell Company Substance Abuse

Statutory sanction: China's GAAR under CITA Article 47 empowers the tax authority to disregard the conduit entity and recharacterize the transaction. Treaty benefits are denied; the statutory 10% WHT is re-imposed with daily late penalties and potential underpayment penalties. For AML purposes, predicate tax evasion may trigger Financial Intelligence Unit reporting and asset seizure.

Director personal liability: Directors who establish or maintain a sham conduit with no economic substance expose themselves to civil claims for restitution and to criminal prosecution for evasion or defrauding the tax authority. The legal representative of the WFOE may be placed on the tax-involved person blacklist, resulting in travel bans, credit restriction, and prohibition on high consumption. If the conduit's funds are diverted to personal accounts, directors may face personal civil asset tracing, forfeiture, and imprisonment.
