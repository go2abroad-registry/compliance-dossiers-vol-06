Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-up capital is imposed for a WFOE limited liability company under Chinese law; registered capital is subject to the shareholders' subscription stated in the articles of association. The Foreign Investment Law and SAMR registration rules do not require a blocked escrow account. However, when the WFOE imports or physically distributes fertilizers under ISIC 4669, commercial banks and the counterparty may request a capital adequacy or cash margin covenant in the underlying letters of credit and freight charter contracts. No escrow capitalization rule applies at the state incorporation level.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification: the warehouse premises must obtain a Fire Protection Acceptance Certificate or a Fire Safety Filing Completion Certificate under the Fire Protection Law; Civil Defense clearance is required if the site is located within a civil defense protected zone; occupational clearance: a workplace safety filing and a structural load inspection are required for any racking or bagged storage area, and if the stored fertilizer is classified as a major hazard source under the Regulation on Safety Management of Dangerous Chemicals (State Council Decree No. 591), a Safety Production License notification and an emergency response plan shall be filed with the local Emergency Management Bureau. Prilled urea and potassium chloride are generally non-hazardous and non-explosive, but the facility must still obtain a Civil Defense Fire Permit and a building structural safety inspection before warehouse operations commence.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; electronic filing through the e-Tax Bureau and Golden Tax Phase IV invoice management system

E-government filing portal

For the WFOE, incorporation with the State Administration for Market Regulation yields the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which is also the enterprise taxpayer identification number and VAT registration number. Within 30 calendar days of business license issuance, the WFOE must complete tax registration confirmation through the competent STA office via the e-Tax Bureau, submitting the business license, articles of association, legal representative identity, registered address proof, bank account opening certificate, and investor information. The tax authority then activates corporate income tax, value-added tax, stamp duty, and surcharge registrations, sets the filing period, determines general or small-scale VAT taxpayer status, and configures electronic invoicing quotas. A foreign non-resident entity without a Chinese establishment must separately obtain a non-resident tax identification number through withholding registration, but the Chinese-incorporated WFOE uses the USCC as its tax ID.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation (the WFOE is a Chinese tax resident enterprise taxed on worldwide income; no pass-through treatment)

Available election forms

None for check-the-box entity classification or hybrid mismatch elections. Administrative elections available through the e-Tax Bureau include voluntary registration as a general VAT taxpayer before exceeding the statutory threshold and the simplified small-scale taxpayer method.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

All enterprises must complete tax registration upon establishment regardless of turnover; for VAT classification, annual taxable turnover exceeding RMB 5,000,000 triggers mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration, while turnover at or below that amount may remain small-scale unless voluntary general status is elected.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The export of urea and potassium chloride goods to non-resident buyers qualifies for the zero-rated VAT export treatment where the goods are physically removed from China's customs territory under an export declaration, the exporter is a registered VAT taxpayer, the export sale is supported by an export invoice, the goods and values match the customs declaration, and the foreign currency proceeds are received and repatriated through approved banking channels within the statutory period; although the domestic supply of fertilizer may fall in the 9% reduced VAT category, exported goods are zero-rated with entitlement to refund of attributable input VAT.

  • Customs Export Goods Declaration (export tax rebate special copy)
  • Commercial invoice (export invoice)
  • Packing list
  • Bill of lading or multimodal transport document
  • Export sales contract or order
  • Bank foreign exchange collection voucher / export proceeds receipt
  • VAT special invoice(s) for the purchased fertilizer goods (input VAT certificate)

Invoicing is administered under Golden Tax Phase IV: special VAT invoices and fully digital electronic invoices must be transmitted to the State Taxation Administration in real time, with serialized digital stamping, API-based data exchange from enterprise systems, and monthly reconciliation of issued invoices against VAT returns; no offline invoice issuance is permitted for fully digital e-invoices, and transition-period traditional invoices remain subject to strict inventory reporting.

The State Taxation Administration and General Administration of Customs jointly cross-check export declarations, VAT invoices, transport documents, and SAFE foreign exchange monitoring. Failure to substantiate physical export or failure to repatriate export proceeds within the prescribed period triggers recovery of the entire refund, late payment surcharge, a fine of 100% to 500% of the fraudulently obtained refund under Article 66 of the Tax Collection Administration Law, suspension of export tax refund rights, and referral for criminal liability for defrauding export tax refunds under the Criminal Law, which may carry imprisonment up to life and property confiscation.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates an RMB managed floating exchange rate system with bifurcated current account and capital account convertibility. Current account flows, especially trade, are de jure convertible but must pass authenticity verification before settlement. Capital account transactions remain controlled: a foreign-owned limited company cannot convert foreign capital contributions freely until the bank confirms the FDI registration, the capital injection is creditable, and the use falls within the negative list. Once SAFE registration and capital verification are complete, the WFOE may settle foreign exchange into RMB on a payment-based or willful settlement basis. Dividend repatriation is treated as current account after reserve allocation and corporate income tax filing, requiring audited financial statements, tax settlement certificates, and board-approved profit distribution resolution. Surrender is no longer a fixed compulsory sale requirement; export proceeds must be repatriated to a domestic foreign exchange account within the statutory period. The practical obstacles are documentary: banks require underlying contracts, customs declarations, transport records, and VAT/receipt verification before allowing any conversion or outward remittance.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the SAFE Goods Trade Administration, export proceeds must be repatriated into a domestic FX account within 90 days after export customs declaration unless registered as a delayed collection; there is no mandatory conversion ratio. Enterprises can retain 100% of export receipts in foreign currency or sell to a bank at market rate; both operations are subject to matching transaction records in the trade monitoring system. Advance receipts and deferred receipts must be reported separately through the designated bank.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE), an administrative authority under the People's Bank of China (PBOC), People's Republic of China

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Foreign digital EMIs are not licensed by the National Financial Regulatory Administration to open onshore RMB deposit accounts or provide payment and clearing services directly to PRC legal entities. A WFOE must maintain its primary operational and capital accounts with a licensed domestic bank; offshore EMI e-wallets may serve only as ancillary collection or payout accessories and cannot clear SAFE-governed trade or FDI flows. Viability rating: LOW and not viable as primary or sole treasury provider.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Before inbound remittance, the WFOE must complete SAFE FDI registration at the onshore bank and obtain the FDI Credentials. The non-resident shareholder remits the equity contribution from its own bank account via SWIFT MT103 to the WFOE designated FDI capital account at the Chinese bank. The remittance instruction should include the equity contribution purpose, the SAFE FDI registration code, and the ISIC 4669 fertilizer wholesale business scope. The receiving bank classifies the transaction as direct investment equity capital, and the WFOE then arranges a capital verification report by a Chinese certified public accountant. Under the payment-based settlement reform, capital funds can be used for self-operations after settlement but cannot be used for on-lending, securities investment, or real estate acquisition outside the permitted scope.Outbound trade disbursements must be processed through a domestic SAFE-supervised bank under the goods trade foreign exchange payment system. For open account transactions, use SWIFT MT103 with contract, invoice, customs declaration number, transport documents, and trade authenticity verification. For documentary credit transactions, use commercial letters of credit with MT700 and MT707 messages. For lower-risk or relationship-based transactions, use documentary collections such as documents against payment and documents against acceptance. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File contemporaneous documentation is required when the total annual amount of related-party transactions reaches RMB 100 million; Master File documentation is required when the ultimate parent's worldwide consolidated revenue for the preceding fiscal year reaches RMB 2 billion and the Chinese WFOE is a member of the multinational group. Annual related-party transaction schedules must be filed with the CIT return when tangible-asset related-party transactions reach RMB 200 million, intangible or financial-asset related-party transactions reach RMB 100 million, or other related-party transactions reach RMB 40 million.

Economic substance scope: China does not maintain a standalone Economic Substance Regulations statute in the style of UAE-free-zone codes, but the PRC tax system applies substance-over-form, general anti-avoidance rules under Article 47 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, and the OECD BEPS Action 6 framework. A Chinese WFOE must demonstrate real premises, employees, local decision-making, inventory management, contract negotiation, invoicing, logistics coordination and risk-bearing in China for its wholesale fertilizer commodity trading. For treaty relief, the non-resident recipient must perform its core income-generating activities in the treaty jurisdiction; a letter-box company with no CIGA will be denied beneficial ownership and treaty benefits.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Transfer Pricing Manipulation and False Related-Party Reporting

Statutory sanction: Article 41 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law authorizes the tax authority to make transfer-pricing adjustments and re-determine taxable income; Article 63 of the Tax Collection and Administration Law imposes a penalty of 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax plus daily late-payment surcharge at 0.05%; failure to file required related-party documentation can be penalized separately under the TCAL.

Director personal liability: If directors knowingly executed sham contracts, fabricated invoices, or directed the artificial pricing structure, the court may pierce the corporate veil under the PRC Company Law and hold the directors and controlling shareholders personally liable for the corporate tax debt. Directors may also be blacklisted as seriously dishonest taxpayers, subjected to travel bans, denied exit from China, and face criminal prosecution under Article 201 of the Criminal Law with potential imprisonment up to 7 years plus fines for substantial tax evasion.
Failure to Withhold and Remit Outbound Dividends, Interest, and Royalties

Statutory sanction: Article 69 of the Tax Collection and Administration Law provides that a withholding agent that fails to withhold or remit is ordered to withhold and remit within a prescribed time, with enforced collection and an administrative fine of 50% to 300% of the unpaid withholding tax; where the tax was withheld but not remitted, Article 68 applies with daily surcharges and further penalties.

Director personal liability: The legal representative and responsible directors may be placed on the tax dishonesty blacklist and restricted from leaving China under TCAL exit controls. If directors dissipated company assets to avoid the remittance obligation, they may be jointly and severally liable for the unpaid withholding tax under veil-piercing principles. Wilful failure to remit withheld tax is punishable under the Criminal Law as tax evasion, with fines and imprisonment up to 7 years for egregious cases.
Conduit Treaty Shopping and Absence of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Beneficial ownership is denied under State Taxation Administration Bulletin 2018 No. 9, the reduced DTA rate is revoked, the statutory 10% withholding is collected, and Article 47 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law applies as a general anti-avoidance rule to recharacterize the arrangement. Article 63 of the TCAL may additionally impose a penalty of 50% to 500% of the tax under-collected, with daily surcharges.

Director personal liability: Directors who signed false beneficial-ownership declarations, generated fake board minutes, or caused the WFOE to remit dividends without proper substance documentation can be held civilly liable for restitution of the unpaid tax. The blacklist regime permits exit bans and severe credit restrictions; if forged tax residency certificates or fabricated official documents are used, the responsible directors face criminal liability under the PRC Criminal Law for forgery and fraud, including substantial fines and imprisonment up to 10 years for serious official-document forgery.
