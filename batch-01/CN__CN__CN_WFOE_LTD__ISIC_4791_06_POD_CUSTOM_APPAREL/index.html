Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A pure virtual office, commercial mailbox, or registered-agent-only address is generally rejected by Chinese municipal tax authorities and commercial banks when establishing a WFOE for retail e-commerce and print-on-demand operations. The WFOE must file a valid lease contract or property ownership certificate for a physical business premise, and tax registration and bank account opening inspections require the actual existence of office space, signage, and operating personnel. Some pilot free-trade zones allow an address service, but banks still apply de-risking and require evidence of day-to-day management presence, making a shared address high-risk. For this industry, a virtual office is not approved unless lawfully provided by a qualified address service provider in a pilot FTZ and supported by real employee seats and lease invoices.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None under PRC statutory law: a wholly foreign-owned limited liability company may adopt a declared registered capital without a minimum paid-up amount, and no escrow or blocked capital verification account is required. The sole shareholder must contribute the full subscribed capital within the contribution period stated in the articles of association, not to exceed five years under the 2023 Company Law. Contributions may be made in cash, equipment, intellectual property, equity, or land-use rights at fair value. For this low-risk e-commerce and fulfillment activity, the foreign shareholder may subscribe for capital with a deferred payment schedule, but the registered amount will be published and may be relied on by creditors; any reduction, increase, or extension of contribution time must be resolved by the shareholder and filed with the market supervision authority.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China (PRC); online electronic filing portal: the National Electronic Tax Bureau e-tax service platform operated by STA and its provincial tax bureaus (accessible from www.chinatax.gov.cn and the provincial e-tax gateways).

E-government filing portal

After the WFOE is incorporated, the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) issues an 18-digit Uniform Social Credit Code (USCC), which automatically serves as the taxpayer identification number (TIN) and VAT registration number. No separate TIN application is required: tax registration is integrated into the business license. Within 30 days after issuance of the USCC, the WFOE must complete supplementary tax status registration in the electronic tax bureau including VAT taxpayer status, legal representative identity, equity structure, industry classification ISIC 4791, and banking details. The foreign parent company must provide notarised and legalised registration, a board resolution, and evidence of capital contribution to the SAMR during incorporation. For a foreign non-resident entity without a Chinese permanent establishment, the procedure for obtaining a Chinese TIN consists of filing a non-resident enterprise tax registration with the competent STA office, submitting the home-country certificate of incorporation, a tax residence certificate, the appointment of the Chinese withholding agent or payer, and a description of the Chinese-source income; STA then issues a non-resident taxpayer identification number used solely for withholding and declaration purposes. A WFOE is, by default, a PRC resident taxpayer subject to entity-level taxation rather than a non-resident TIN holder.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None. The PRC tax system does not provide check-the-box, classification, or hybrid mismatch elections for a WFOE. A wholly foreign-owned limited liability company is compulsorily treated as a PRC resident enterprise for enterprise income tax purposes. No entity-level pass-through or transparent status is available. The small-scale VAT taxpayer status is an administrative indirect tax registration category, not an entity classification election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

All incorporated WFOEs must complete VAT registration together with tax registration immediately upon receipt of the USCC; there is no turnover threshold for basic VAT registration. For mandatory VAT general taxpayer classification, however, any enterprise whose taxable domestic sales exceed RMB 5,000,000 in a rolling 12-month period must be registered as a general VAT taxpayer. Export-oriented businesses may, and in practice must, apply for general VAT taxpayer registration regardless of turnover in order to claim 0% export VAT treatment and input VAT refunds.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under the PRC VAT law, exported goods are zero-rated for VAT where: (i) the goods physically leave the customs territory of the PRC under a valid customs export declaration; (ii) the exporter is a VAT general taxpayer properly registered with the General Administration of Customs (GACC) as a customs exporter; (iii) the export value is supported by commercial, transport, and customs documentation; and (iv) the export-related foreign exchange proceeds are repatriated and recorded through licensed PRC banks in accordance with the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) trade verification rules. Print-on-Demand custom apparel qualifies only if the completed garments are physically dispatched to an overseas buyer or destination outside mainland China; any portion sold for consumption in China remains subject to VAT at the standard rate. Qualifying cross-border services may also be zero-rated only if the service is within the published STA scope for zero-rated offshore services, is supplied to a non-resident overseas customer, is consumed entirely offshore, and is not connected with goods or services for use in China. Remote digital services rendered to consumers located within China are not zero-rated.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Export Goods Declaration) filed through the China International Trade Single Window and accepted by the General Administration of Customs (GACC)
  • Commercial invoice evidencing the export value, HS code, buyer, consignee, and description of the POD apparel
  • Packing list reconciled with the export declaration and commercial invoice
  • Ocean Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, or express courier proof-of-export signed by the carrier
  • Export foreign exchange collection record or bank remittance certificate issued by a licensed PRC bank and verified in the SAFE cross-border trade system (the functional equivalent of an Inward Remittance Certificate, FIRC)

The PRC mandates issuance of Fully Digitalized Electronic Invoices (DEI) through STA's Electronic Tax Bureau platform. All newly formed enterprises, including WFOEs, are incorporated into the national electronic invoicing system and must transmit invoice data to the STA in real time at the moment of invoice issuance using STA-approved APIs or an STA-certified electronic invoice service provider. Each DEI carries a secure QR code and cryptographic digital signature generated by STA's central invoice issuance engine, with anti-fraud digital stamping replacing physical invoice sealing. For general VAT taxpayers, special VAT invoices remain subject to tax-control system matching and verification. Commodity and service tax classification codes are compulsory; e-commerce and Print-on-Demand platforms must ensure that every domestic sale is electronically invoiced at the applicable VAT rate, and that export sales are separately evidenced with customs and logistics proof for zero-rating.

Tax authorities will cross-check the customs export declaration, transport records, foreign exchange collection records, and VAT filing data. For any claimed zero-rating or VAT rebate found to be unsupported, falsified, or not backed by actual physical export and repatriated proceeds, the STA will claw back the VAT benefit, add late payment surcharge interest, and impose an administrative penalty ranging from one to five times the fraudulently obtained refund under the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection. If the conduct is intentional and material, it may constitute the criminal offense of fraudulent export tax refunds under the PRC Criminal Law. The exporter may also be disqualified from export VAT rebate treatment, have its VAT general taxpayer status reclassified, incur customs administrative penalties for false export declarations, and be subject to SAFE fines for export-proceeds non-repatriation or false collection records.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a managed floating exchange rate system with current-account convertibility and a regulated capital account. All bona fide trade- and service-related foreign currencies may be settled into RMB at designated foreign-exchange banks upon presentation of contracts, customs declarations, invoices and SAFE-monitored trade records. Capital-account transactions, however, remain strictly controlled: FDI subscriptions must obtain SAFE foreign-exchange registration and be remitted into a dedicated FDI capital account; subsequent conversion to RMB requires authenticity verification and is designated to permitted business uses. There is no statutory mandatory surrender ratio for current-account export receipts, but proceeds must be repatriated to China and either retained in foreign currency or settled through a domestic bank. Profit remittance by a WFOE is legally permissible after completion of annual audit, corporate income tax final settlement, and formal bank verification of audited financial statements and profit distribution resolution; practical hurdles include delayed SAFE registration updates, incomplete customs/tax documentation, and strict ultimate-beneficial-owner due diligence.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE goods-trade monitoring rules, export proceeds must be repatriated to China and enter a designated foreign-exchange bank within the trade contract period and, in any event, within the SAFE monitoring window normally not exceeding 90 days from the customs export declaration unless an approved extension or deferred payment arrangement is reported. No fixed mandatory conversion ratio applies; the exporter may retain the proceeds in a current-account foreign-currency settlement account or convert 100% to RMB at the bank's prevailing on-demand rate. For advance receipts exceeding USD 500,000 and more than 30 days before shipment, and for deferred receipts beyond 90 days after export, the exporter must file a supplementary report through the bank for SAFE balance-of-payments monitoring.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange of the People's Republic of China (SAFE), operating under the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Low viability as a standalone clearing solution. Financial technology EMIs are not licensed deposit-taking institutions in China and may not clear RMB or hold customer funds in domestic bank accounts. A WFOE cannot rely on an offshore EMI for statutory account opening, FDI capital injection, or export proceeds collection. Such providers may act only as a software or acquiring layer integrated with a licensed domestic bank or a cross-border payment institution holding a PBOC non-bank payment license; all funds must ultimately enter or leave through SAFE-supervised bank rails. Stripe and PayPal therefore require local integration: PayPal through its licensed China subsidiary and domestic bank settlement, and Stripe through a licensed cross-border payment facilitator or a Hong Kong affiliate, neither of which replaces a mainland Tier-1 commercial bank treasury relationship.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Foreign investor executes board resolutions and a capital contribution agreement, then obtains SAFE FDI foreign-exchange registration through the selected Tier-1 bank. The WFOE opens a dedicated FDI capital account at the bank, and the overseas parent remits equity funds via SWIFT MT103 using the SAFE transaction classification code for direct-investment equity capital, referencing the FDI registration contract number. Upon receipt, the bank performs authenticity verification and registers the inflow in SAFE's capital project monitoring system. After conversion to RMB in a capital settlement account, a Chinese certified public accountant issues a Capital Verification Report. Settled capital may only be used for current operating expenditures such as inventory procurement, salaries, rent, marketing, and approved business investments; it cannot be used for entrusted loans, equity investment outside the approved scope, or speculative financial products without separate SAFE approval.For raw-material and supplier payments, the WFOE uses either RMB or foreign-currency clearing through its SAFE-designated domestic bank. Open-account settlement is executed via SWIFT MT103 with the applicable current-account trade purpose code and is supported by import contracts, commercial invoices, purchase orders, and customs import declarations where required. For high-value or credit-supported purchases, the bank issues an irrevocable Commercial Letter of Credit via SWIFT MT700, with negotiation through correspondent banks under UCP600. Documentary Collections (D/P or D/A) may be used for lower-value or counterparty-controlled shipments, with the collecting bank releasing documents against payment or acceptance. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low viability as a standalone clearing solution. Financial technology EMIs are not licensed deposit-taking institutions in China and may not clear RMB or hold customer funds in domestic bank accounts. A WFOE cannot rely on an offshore EMI for statutory account opening, FDI capital injection, or export proceeds collection. Such providers may act only as a software or acquiring layer integrated with a licensed domestic bank or a cross-border payment institution holding a PBOC non-bank payment license; all funds must ultimately enter or leave through SAFE-supervised bank rails. Stripe and PayPal therefore require local integration: PayPal through its licensed China subsidiary and domestic bank settlement, and Stripe through a licensed cross-border payment facilitator or a Hong Kong affiliate, neither of which replaces a mainland Tier-1 commercial bank treasury relationship.
