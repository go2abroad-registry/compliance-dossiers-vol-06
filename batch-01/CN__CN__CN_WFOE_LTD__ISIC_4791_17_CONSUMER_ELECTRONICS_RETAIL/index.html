None. For a permitted-sector WFOE, there is no statutory minimum paid-up registered capital and no escrow or blocked capital account requirement under the Company Law and Foreign Investment Law. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA); electronic filing, tax registration, VAT invoice issuance and export refund administration are conducted through the STA-managed Electronic Taxation Platform (Electronic Tax Bureau) integrated with the Golden Tax System Phase IV.

E-government filing portal

For a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE), the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) printed on the business license issued by the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) is the statutory taxpayer identification number (TIN) and VAT identification number; there is no separate VAT registration number. The WFOE must first complete SAMR registration and obtain the business license with the USCC. Thereafter, within 30 calendar days of business license issuance, the legal representative and financial officer must complete tax registration information supplementation through the STA Electronic Tax Bureau, filing the entity's registered address, business scope, banking details, investor information, and responsible personnel. The tax authority then classifies the taxpayer for VAT purposes as either a small-scale taxpayer or a general VAT taxpayer. If annual VAT taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000, or if the entity voluntarily applies with eligible conditions, the WFOE must obtain general VAT taxpayer status through the Electronic Tax Bureau. The same channel is used to activate the fully digital electronic invoice account, apply for invoice types and quotas, and submit monthly or quarterly VAT returns. As a China-resident enterprise, the WFOE does not need a separate non-resident tax registration; however, any non-resident enterprise with Chinese-source income not effectively connected to the WFOE must obtain a non-resident TIN through the payer's withholding procedures.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None. China does not provide U.S.-style check-the-box classification elections or hybrid mismatch elections. A WFOE is mandatorily treated as a resident enterprise subject to entity-level corporate income tax on worldwide income; for VAT, a small-scale taxpayer may voluntarily elect general taxpayer status if the statutory conditions are met, but this is a VAT collection-status election rather than an entity classification election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No monetary turnover threshold is required for basic tax/VAT registration because every enterprise with a SAMR business license must register for VAT after incorporation. For mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration, the statutory threshold is annual VAT taxable sales exceeding RMB 5,000,000; taxpayers below this threshold remain small-scale VAT taxpayers unless they voluntarily elect general taxpayer status.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rated VAT treatment is available for goods physically exported from China if the WFOE is a general VAT taxpayer, the goods are sold to a non-resident buyer, a valid Customs Export Declaration is completed, the goods actually leave China customs territory, and the export proceeds are repatriated to China and receipted through banking channels monitored by the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE). For qualifying cross-border services, zero-rating or VAT exemption may apply where the recipient is an overseas enterprise and the service is rendered and wholly consumed outside China, evidenced by the underlying contract, delivery records, and banking settlement proof. The WFOE must maintain complete entity-level records demonstrating the export transaction is genuine and that the overseas buyer actually paid and received the goods or services.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Export Customs Clearance Form with customs release records)
  • Bill of Lading or equivalent transport document (ocean bill of lading, multimodal transport document, air waybill, or courier receipt)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or equivalent bank foreign-exchange receipt voucher issued by the settlement bank
  • Commercial invoice and detailed packing list
  • SAFE verification records and foreign exchange account statements evidencing repatriation of export proceeds

All VAT invoices must be issued through the STA-monitored full digital electronic invoice platform integrated with the Golden Tax System Phase IV. VAT special invoices carry a tax-control digital encryption and anti-counterfeit stamp, and issuance data must be transmitted to the tax authority in real time at the moment of issuance. Invoice quota allocation, voiding, red-letter credit notes, and monthly aggregate invoice reporting are all managed electronically through the Electronic Tax Bureau; taxpayers must reconcile sales, purchasing inputs, inventory movement, customs declarations, and VAT invoice data for automated cross-checking by the STA.

The STA performs automated cross-agency data matching under Golden Tax System Phase IV, reconciling customs export declarations, logistics manifests, VAT special invoices, and SAFE foreign-exchange receipts. Where export zero-rating or refund is claimed without a valid customs release, genuine physical shipment, or timely and verified FX repatriation, the refund is denied; if already paid, the refund is clawed back with statutory late-payment interest. Under the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection and the Regulations on Administration of Export Tax Refunds, underpayment, misdeclaration, or fraudulent refund claims may attract an administrative fine of 50% to five times the tax or refund involved, revocation of export tax refund qualification, enterprise credit downgrade, and enhanced audit monitoring. Fraudulent obtaining of export tax refunds may also constitute the crime of fraudulently obtaining export tax rebates under the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, exposing responsible persons to criminal fines and imprisonment.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The People's Republic of China operates a managed floating exchange rate regime. Current account transactions are formally convertible under Article VIII of the IMF Articles of Agreement, but every remittance must be supported by genuine trade or service background and is screened by the local settlement bank. Export receipts are repatriated into a domestic foreign-currency current account and may be retained or voluntarily converted into RMB; there is no mandatory surrender ratio. Capital account transactions, by contrast, are not freely convertible. A WFOE must complete SAFE foreign direct investment registration, open a designated foreign-exchange capital account, and ensure that the inbound wire is labelled with the correct FDI purpose code. Once settled into RMB, the funds are confined by the Foreign Investment Negative List and cannot be used for equity investment, securities, regulated real estate, or the repayment of unregistered offshore borrowing. Profit remittance to the offshore parent is technically a current account payment, but the bank requires audited financial statements, corporate income tax settlement documents, the dividend declaration resolution, board approval minutes, and a written certificate from the CFO confirming distributable retained earnings. In practice, disbursement can be delayed by the serial compliance review of source documents and by SAFE's automatic trade monitoring system flags.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export proceeds must be repatriated to an onshore current-account foreign-currency bank account through licensed banking channels and matched to the customs export declaration. There is no mandatory conversion ratio; the WFOE may retain 100% of export foreign exchange receipts in its foreign-currency account or voluntarily convert to RMB. SAFE's goods-trade monitoring regime expects export receipts to be cleared within 180 days from the date of customs declaration; overdue receipts that are not explained trigger a bank compliance re-review and may lead to a B/C monitoring classification, which raises the documentation burden on all subsequent current account payments.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC); foreign exchange supervision and enforcement are delegated to the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) and its provincial and local branches.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low feasibility rating. Offshore digital EMIs such as Wise, Revolut, or Airwallex cannot act as the principal banking rail for a mainland Chinese WFOE. They do not hold a Chinese commercial banking licence and are not authorised by PBOC and SAFE to maintain RMB capital accounts, FDI settlement accounts, or trade-related FX accounts for resident entities. They may be used at the edge of treasury operations as an external payment aggregator for collecting overseas marketplace receivables, but every compliant treasury flow must ultimately settle through a licensed onshore domestic bank. Relying on an EMI as the primary clearing rail would violate the PRC Foreign Exchange Administration Regulations and expose the WFOE to regulatory sanctions.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The offshore parent should initiate a SWIFT MT103, or MT202 for high-value consolidated funding, to the WFOE's SAFE-designated bank, using the FDI purpose code for capital contribution and clearly stating the WFOE name in the beneficiary remittance information. The receiving bank will verify SAFE FDI registration, open or replenish the foreign-exchange capital account, and book the receipt. A PRC CPA must then issue a Capital Contribution Verification Report after the funds are credited. The injection may be retained in USD or EUR or voluntarily settled into RMB through a post-settlement capital account; every subsequent RMB disbursement must be screened for negative-list and prohibited-use categories. No loan or quasi-capital injection should be mixed into the same wire; separate current account and capital account rails must be used.For settlement of imported consumer electronics components and finished goods, the WFOE may use an irrevocable Commercial Letter of Credit for high-value confirmed purchase orders, Documentary Collections at D/P or D/A terms for established suppliers, and SWIFT MT103 direct transfers for small-dollar fully documented repeat invoices. Each outbound payment must be matched to the trade contract, commercial invoice, packing list, customs declaration and, where applicable, the import goods verification form. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low feasibility rating. Offshore digital EMIs such as Wise, Revolut, or Airwallex cannot act as the principal banking rail for a mainland Chinese WFOE. They do not hold a Chinese commercial banking licence and are not authorised by PBOC and SAFE to maintain RMB capital accounts, FDI settlement accounts, or trade-related FX accounts for resident entities. They may be used at the edge of treasury operations as an external payment aggregator for collecting overseas marketplace receivables, but every compliant treasury flow must ultimately settle through a licensed onshore domestic bank. Relying on an EMI as the primary clearing rail would violate the PRC Foreign Exchange Administration Regulations and expose the WFOE to regulatory sanctions.
