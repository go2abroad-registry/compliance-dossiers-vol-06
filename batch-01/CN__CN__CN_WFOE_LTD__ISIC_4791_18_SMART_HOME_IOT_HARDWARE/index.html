Competent corporate registry

State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) and the municipal/district Administration for Market Regulation where the registered office is located; for foreign exchange aspects, the local branch of the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) and the designated commercial bank.

Governing statute

Formation / constitutional filings

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None. There is no statutory minimum paid-up capital for a WFOE engaged in retailing smart-home IoT hardware outside the Foreign Investment Negative List. The shareholder must subscribe a reasonable registered capital, and under Article 47 of the 2023 Company Law the full subscribed capital must be paid within five years from incorporation, or a shorter period fixed in the Articles of Association. Foreign exchange remittances must be received in a SAFE-registered foreign currency capital account before settlement; no blocked escrow account is required. Capital may be in cash or in-kind in accordance with the Articles, but contribution records and capital verification accounts must be maintained for regulatory review.

Funding & unlock path: Not Applicable at formation for a pure retail office or showroom. Before physical occupancy, the company must complete the building fire-safety inspection and obtain the local fire and rescue brigade's pre-use fire safety inspection opinion. If a warehouse or assembly area is added later, the company must obtain a renewed fire-safety acceptance and put-into-use inspection, install fire suppression equipment, maintain evacuation routes, and pass the annual fire-safety and work-safety inspection. The workplace must also comply with the Work Safety Law and occupational health requirements for employees. A separate civil-defense or air-raid shelter approval is not required for this ordinary commercial retail use.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA), through the National Electronic Tax Administration platform (Electronic Tax Bureau) and the national value-added tax electronic invoice service platform

E-government filing portal

A foreign non-resident entity investing in China first incorporates the WFOE with the State Administration for Market Regulation; the unified social credit code (USCC) is automatically issued and acts as the taxpayer identification number (TIN/VAT ID). The system performs integrated tax registration with the local STA office at incorporation, with no separate TIN application step. Non-resident suppliers or recipients that are not registered WFOEs but require withholding from Chinese sources must obtain a non-resident tax registration record through the local STA's withholding registration procedure, and every electronic tax filing is submitted via the Electronic Tax Bureau after bank account and accounting system registration.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A foreign non-resident entity investing in China first incorporates the WFOE with the State Administration for Market Regulation; the unified social credit code (USCC) is automatically issued and acts as the taxpayer identification number (TIN/VAT ID). The system performs integrated tax registration with the local STA office at incorporation, with no separate TIN application step. Non-resident suppliers or recipients that are not registered WFOEs but require withholding from Chinese sources must obtain a non-resident tax registration record through the local STA's withholding registration procedure, and every electronic tax filing is submitted via the Electronic Tax Bureau after bank account and accounting system registration.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None. The PRC Enterprise Income Tax Law taxes a WFOE as a resident enterprise on worldwide income at entity level; there is no check-the-box election for foreign-owned domestic companies. Hybrid mismatch adjustments are administrative and mandatory, not elective.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

All incorporated WFOEs are required to register for VAT upon tax registration. General taxpayer VAT registration becomes compulsory when annual taxable turnover exceeds RMB 5,000,000; below this threshold a small-scale taxpayer status with a 3% collection rate is available, but exports are zero-rated only to registered export taxpayers.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

VAT zero-rating applies to tangible IoT smart-home hardware physically exported from China if the goods are declared for export under customs supervision, delivered outside the PRC customs territory, the exporter is the registered VAT taxpayer, export proceeds are collected in foreign exchange from the overseas buyer, and the input VAT is allocated to the exported goods. Cross-border services supplied by the exporter to offshore customers and consumed wholly outside China likewise qualify for zero-rating or exemption under STA circulars if the service contract, delivery record and overseas consumption evidence are retained.

  • Customs Export Declaration containing commodity code, quantity, FOB value, destination and exporter name
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing physical departure from Chinese customs territory
  • Commercial export invoice, packing list and sales contract matched to the customs declaration
  • Bank foreign-exchange collection voucher or export settlement certificate proving inward remittance from the overseas buyer within the statutory period
  • Export tax refund declaration and VAT filing records reconciling the zero-rated export turnover and input VAT

Fully digitalised electronic invoices (F-E-invoices) are mandatory and must be issued through the national electronic invoice service platform under the Golden Tax Phase IV system. Invoice data is transmitted in real-time to STA for automated clearance, uniqueness verification, ledger logging, and anti-fraud analysis; each F-E-invoice carries a unique invoice code, QR code for verification, and machine-readable digital payload. No physical fiscal stamp is required for F-E-invoices, but pre-printed or paper special VAT invoices remain subject to the legacy control system and must be reported during the statutory tax return period.

Export VAT refund and zero-rating claims are subject to post-export verification and risk-rating by STA. If documentation is false, incomplete, or foreign exchange is not timely repatriated, the zero-rating is revoked, refunded input VAT is clawed back with late-payment surcharge, administrative penalties of up to five times the tax refund wrongly claimed may be imposed, export-refund eligibility can be suspended for up to three years, and criminal liability for customs document forgery or export-tax fraud may apply.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The current account has achieved a high degree of convertibility under People's Bank of China/SAFE rules: import payments, trade-related service fees, and regular profit remittances may be purchased or paid through domestic banks upon production of genuine transactional documents. The capital account remains materially restricted. WFOE registered capital injections require a SAFE foreign exchange business registration and must be routed through a designated capital account. Subsequent changes to share capital, shareholder loans, or cross-border guarantees require additional SAFE filings. Surplus profits declared by the audited WFOE may be remitted abroad only after payment of enterprise income tax, completion of foreign exchange registration, verification of board dividend resolution, and confirmation that prior year losses have been fully offset. Dividends must be remitted from the current-account foreign exchange settlement of after-tax profits; banks will conduct post-verification against the annual audit. Retained foreign currency export proceeds may be held voluntarily, but conversion to CNY, if any, is executed at actual transaction banks without mandatory surrender ratio.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export proceeds must be repatriated to China and credited to the exporter's foreign exchange current account within 180 days after the export customs declaration date, unless SAFE has approved an extension. There is no mandatory conversion ratio under the voluntary settlement regime; the WFOE may retain the foreign currency indefinitely in a current-account forex account or convert it into CNY at the prevailing market rate through a designated bank. All conversion transactions must be connected to genuine commercial receipts and reported under the bank's trade-finance monitoring system.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC), with delegated administration by the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) and SAFE local branches.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Viability: Low as a primary onshore banking rail and High only as a supplementary offshore merchant-of-record/acquiring layer. A mainland WFOE cannot use offshore EMI IBANs as its registered capital account, tax refund collection account, or customs settlement account because digital EMIs lack a PBOC payment institution licence, do not participate in CNAPS/CFXPS, and are outside SAFE cross-border reporting. Stripe and Shopify Payments are useful for card-not-present sales in smart-home IoT retail, but their settlement to mainland China requires a domestic bank receiving account or an approved cross-border receiving service. The legal structure should therefore place the EMI exposure in an overseas sales vehicle or merchant-of-record while keeping all regulatory capital and trade payments inside tier-1 Chinese banks.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound registered capital must be sent by SWIFT MT103 from the overseas shareholder's banking account to the WFOE's dedicated SAFE capital account at the onshore bank. The remittance must include the SAFE FDI control number and the correct capital-project purpose code for foreign-invested enterprise equity injection. On credit, the bank verifies the remitter, amount, and underlying FDI registration before releasing funds into the settlement account; funds must be used on a payment basis for approved operating expenses. A licensed Chinese CPA then performs capital verification and issues the capital verification report, which becomes the precondition for subsequent annual SAFE inspection and profit repatriation.For importing IoT hardware components, use SWIFT MT103 for small open-account payments to foreign suppliers, accompanied by trade contract, commercial invoice, bill of lading/airway bill, and import customs declaration. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: State Taxation Administration Bulletin No. 42 of 2016 requires contemporaneous Local File documentation when annual related-party transaction amounts exceed RMB 200 million for tangible property purchases or sales, RMB 100 million for intangible property transfers or financial asset transfers, RMB 40 million for related-party provision of services, or other statutory thresholds as announced. Master File preparation may be required when annual related-party revenue is RMB 1 billion or more and the taxpayer has related parties in two or more foreign jurisdictions, subject to the prescribed conditions. Annual related-party transaction reporting is mandatory whenever any related-party transaction exists.

Economic substance scope: Mainland China does not operate a stand-alone Economic Substance Regulations statute, but substance-over-form is enforced through the general anti-avoidance rule in Article 47 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, the Special Tax Adjustment Measures, and the Beneficial Owner doctrine under tax treaties. A Chinese WFOE engaged in smart-home IoT retail must demonstrate that it performs the core income-generating activities in China: employment of managers and technical staff, control of inventory and logistics, acceptance and management of warranty risk, negotiation with customers and suppliers, and independent decision-making. A vehicle that merely holds bank accounts and passes invoices between a foreign principal and Chinese customers may be recharacterized as a shell, with profits reallocated and treaty benefits denied.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and False Invoicing

Statutory sanction: Article 32 of the Tax Administration Law imposes a daily late-payment surcharge of 0.05%. Article 63 imposes an administrative penalty of 50% to 500% of the tax evaded, plus recovery of the entire underpaid tax. PRC Criminal Law Article 201 criminalizes tax evasion with fines and imprisonment of up to seven years for serious amounts; Article 205 criminalizes false issuance of VAT invoices with severe imprisonment and fines. The taxpayer will also be listed as a serious untrustworthy entity.

Director personal liability: A director or legal representative who orders or participates in the false reporting can be pierced under PRC Company Law Article 20 when the corporate form has been abused. The director is then personally and jointly liable for civil restitution of the evaded tax, interest and penalties. The individual may also be blacklisted by the tax authorities, triggering exit-control and travel bans, and may be criminally sentenced for tax evasion as a directly responsible person.
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance and Inadequate Documentation

Statutory sanction: Article 41 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law empowers tax authorities to make an arm's length re-determination and to adjust taxable income. Under State Taxation Administration Bulletin No. 42 of 2016, the adjustment carries an interest surcharge calculated at the benchmark loan rate plus five percentage points, accruing from the due date to the date of payment. Article 70 of the Tax Administration Law allows fines for failure to provide related-party documentation; Article 63 may be invoked if non-compliance is fraudulent, giving rise to a penalty equal to 50% to 500% of the tax deficiency.

Director personal liability: Directors that knowingly sign false related-party declarations or approve fabricated intercompany contracts expose themselves to civil liability under the abuse of corporate independence doctrine in PRC Company Law Article 20, with personal assets available to satisfy the tax claim. If the transfer pricing manipulation is determined to have been intentional and fraudulent, the director is criminally liable under PRC Criminal Law Article 201, is subject to travel blacklisting and may be barred from leaving China pending resolution of the tax debt.
Withholding Tax Evasion and Double Tax Agreement Abuse Through Shell Conduit

Statutory sanction: Article 69 of the Tax Administration Law imposes a penalty of 50% to three times the amount that should have been withheld; Article 32 adds the daily late-payment surcharge. Article 47 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law contains the general anti-avoidance rule under which the tax authority can disregard the conduit and recharacterize the true owner, denying treaty benefits and reassessing tax, interest and penalties. Criminal liability under PRC Criminal Law Articles 201 and 205 may apply if forged documents are used for the sham arrangement.

Director personal liability: Directors who resolve to declare dividends or authorize royalty payments while knowingly failing to withhold or while fabricating beneficial-owner documents can be held personally liable for the unremitted withholding tax and penalties as persons in control of the tax withholding process. The corporate veil will be pierced where the WFOE is treated as an extension of the foreign shareholder. Responsible individuals face travel bans and exit-control blacklisting, civil asset seizure, and criminal imprisonment for tax-evasion or document-fraud offenses.
