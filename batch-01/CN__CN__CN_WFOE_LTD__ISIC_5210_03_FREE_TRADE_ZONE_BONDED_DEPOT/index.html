Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR), acting through the local Municipal Market Supervision Administration and the designated Free Trade Zone Company Registration Service Window.

Governing statute

Company Law of the People's Republic of China (2023 Revision, effective 1 July 2024), and the PRC Foreign Investment Law (2019; effective 1 January 2020), together with the Regulations on the Administration of Company Registration of the State Council. The relevant corporate governance provisions include Article 24 on limited liability companies, Article 47 on the capital contribution time limit, Article 55 and Article 56 on the board of directors for limited liability companies, and the applicable provisions of the Interim Measures for the Administration of Company Registration and the Special Administrative Measures for Foreign Investment Access Pilot Free Trade Zones.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-up capital is imposed for a bonded warehouse logistics WFOE. The shareholders must subscribe the registered capital stated in the Articles of Association, and under the PRC Company Law (2023 Revision) the subscribed capital must be fully paid within five years from the date of establishment unless a shorter period is specified in the Articles. No blocked escrow account is required; however, foreign exchange capital contributions must be remitted through the statutory cross-border capital account and registered with the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the applicable FTZ facilitation rules.

Funding & unlock path: The bonded warehouse facility must obtain the Building Fire Acceptance Opinion and the Fire Safety Certificate for Public Assembly and Storage Premises from the Fire Rescue Department, confirming functional automatic fire alarm systems, sufficient fire hydrants and extinguishers, emergency lighting, smoke extraction, and compliant evacuation routes. A structural load inspection certificate from a qualified structural engineer must demonstrate that the racking system and bonded goods stack loads cannot exceed the building floor load-capacity, and the warehouse must maintain a Civil Defense and Emergency Evacuation Plan approved by the Free Trade Zone administrative committee. The company must also obtain an occupational health and safety clearance under the Work Safety Law of the People's Republic of China, including documented forklift operator certification, personal protective equipment protocols, liquid spill containment procedures, hazardous goods segregation, and a comprehensive accident emergency response plan filed with the local workplace safety authority before commencing bonded storage operations.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital is imposed for a bonded warehouse logistics WFOE. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA); online portal: Electronic Tax Bureau (e-tax bureau) / National Integrated Online Tax Service Platform. Customs and bonded depot clearing are coordinated through the General Administration of Customs (GACC) and the China International Trade Single Window.

E-government filing portal

Foreign investors incorporate the WFOE as a limited liability company with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR). After name registration and approval against the Foreign Investment Negative List, SAMR issues the WFOE Business License bearing the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC); the USCC is the PRC taxpayer identification number (TIN) and VAT registration identifier. SAMR transmits the incorporation record to STA under the Multi-Certificate Integration / One License One Code system, so no separate tax registration certificate is issued. Within 30 days of license issuance, the legal representative files the tax registration information report and completes the taxpayer profile in the Electronic Tax Bureau, including registered address, business scope, banking account, accounting method, and bonded warehouse site information. The WFOE then elects its VAT status: if annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5 million, registration as a VAT general taxpayer is mandatory; if below that threshold, it may elect small-scale or general VAT taxpayer status. It then obtains fiscal invoicing capability through the Tax UKey and/or the fully digitalised electronic invoice account administered by STA. For bonded warehouse operations, the WFOE must separately register with GACC to obtain a customs registration code, apply for Bonded Warehouse Registration and customs supervised warehouse approval, and establish the bonded warehouse electronic account through the China International Trade Single Window.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No pass-through, check-the-box, or hybrid mismatch election exists in PRC tax law; a WFOE is always treated as a resident enterprise subject to entity-level corporate income tax. The available elections are: (i) VAT taxpayer status election between small-scale taxpayer and VAT general taxpayer, subject to the RMB 5 million annual taxable turnover threshold; and (ii) simplified VAT calculation election for specific listed service transactions at reduced collection rates when lawfully available. Hybrid mismatch election is not available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 (annual taxable sales excluding VAT) is the statutory threshold for mandatory VAT general taxpayer registration; all business entities must complete tax registration upon incorporation irrespective of turnover. Small-scale taxpayer monthly sales below the applicable VAT exemption threshold are exempt from filing that period.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods: a 0% VAT rate applies to goods sold and physically exported from mainland China to a destination outside the PRC customs territory, including re-export of bonded goods from a customs free trade zone or bonded depot. The exporter or taxpayer claiming zero-rating must hold a valid Customs Export Declaration evidencing an export or bonded re-export procedure, international transport documents, and evidence that the sale proceeds have been repatriated to China in foreign exchange within the prescribed period. Services: only cross-border taxable services wholly consumed outside China and expressly listed by STA qualify for zero-rating or exemption; bonded warehouse storage and logistics services physically performed inside the PRC customs territory remain taxable at 6% VAT regardless of the foreign identity of the customer because the place of supply is in China. The bonded zone is a customs supervision and deferred-duty arrangement, not a VAT-exemption zone.

  • Customs Export Declaration (China Customs export goods declaration filed through the China International Trade Single Window, showing the export or bonded re-export customs procedure)
  • Bill of Lading or air waybill or equivalent international transport document evidencing physical departure of the goods from China
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / bank foreign exchange receipt confirmation issued by a SAFE-authorized bank, evidencing that the overseas buyer's payment for the exported goods has been repatriated to the WFOE's bank account in China
  • Export commercial invoice and packing list matching the customs declaration value, quantity, and marks
  • Bonded warehouse entry-exit ledger and GACC supervision records if the goods are re-exported from the bonded depot

China operates real-time invoice clearance through the STA Electronic Invoice Service Platform for fully digitalised electronic invoices (full-electronic invoices), with centrally generated invoice numbers, tax-control encryption, QR anti-fraud stamping, and real-time transmission of issuance data. Taxpayers using traditional fiscal invoicing issue VAT invoices through the VAT Invoice Management System and upload invoice data to STA in real-time; offline issuance is not permitted. Bonded warehouse imports, exports, re-exports, and entry-exit records are filed electronically through the China International Trade Single Window under GACC supervision; no manual paper stamping is acceptable for these statutory records.

The STA, GACC, and State Administration of Foreign Exchange jointly scrutinise export declarations, logistics data, bonded warehouse records, and foreign exchange receipts. False declarations or documentation to obtain zero-rated treatment or export tax rebates are treated as fraudulent tax refunds under the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection and the Criminal Law. Penalties include recovery of the unpaid or fraudulently refunded VAT, fines from 50% to 500% of the tax involved, suspension or forfeiture of export tax rebate rights, downgrade of the enterprise's SAFE trade-in-goods classification, cancellation of customs bonded warehouse registration, personal liability for responsible officers, and criminal prosecution for defrauding the State of export tax rebates. Retroactive recovery is not time-limited for fraudulent schemes, and the tax authorities may reopen previously cleared export filings and reassess VAT, customs duties, and penalties.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a managed float for the Renminbi under a statutory regime that combines current account convertibility with capital account administrative controls. Since China accepted IMF Article VIII obligations, legitimate trade and service payments may generally be converted and remitted on the basis of supporting documents, while the Renminbi remains restricted in capital account transactions. A Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise in a bonded free trade zone must complete SAFE foreign direct investment registration or a delegated-bank filing, open a foreign exchange capital account, and apply funds only to approved business purposes. Capital contributions must be reported to SAFE, verified by a Chinese certified public accountant, and matched against the investment purpose. Profit distribution to foreign shareholders is recognized as a current account item only after the annual statutory audit, tax clearance, and SAFE filing; dividends may then be remitted abroad subject to board resolutions, audited financial statements, and a bank authenticity review. The old mandatory foreign exchange surrender regime has been replaced by a repatriation and verification regime: goods trade export proceeds must be collected in China and reconciled with customs declarations, but the exporter may retain the currency in a foreign currency account or sell it to the bank for Renminbi at market rates. There is no fixed conversion ratio. Bonded depot operators must also reconcile warehouse receipts, customs entry and exit records, and payment flows because the goods remain under customs supervision until re-exportation, making movement between bonded and non-bonded accounts a significant compliance risk.

Export proceeds surrender rules

The mandatory pre-reform foreign exchange surrender system has been abolished. Under SAFE goods trade administration, export proceeds must be repatriated to a domestic designated foreign currency account and reconciled with customs export data, generally within 90 days of the export declaration unless a deferred receipt registration is approved by SAFE through the trade monitoring system. There is no mandatory conversion ratio; the exporter may retain the foreign currency or convert it to Renminbi at prevailing market rates. The bank will require an electronic customs declaration, invoice, contract, and the SAFE goods trade verification code before accepting settlement or transfer.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Offshore financial technology EMIs, including global digital neobanks and cross-border payment platforms, are not licensed as deposit-taking banks in mainland China and cannot lawfully open primary deposit or treasury settlement accounts for a domestic WFOE. PBOC and SAFE require RMB and foreign currency settlement files to be handled by SAFE-designated banking institutions or licensed payment institutions with cross-border payment qualifications; foreign digital EMIs generally fall outside the approved account infrastructure for a Chinese corporate treasury. For a bonded warehouse operator, such EMIs are not viable for capital verification, statutory FX filings, or operational clearing and should only be considered as auxiliary collection tools through a licensed local payment partner or a designated bank cross-border product.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Ultimate beneficial ownership transiting through offshore holding companies in opaque jurisdictions with no clear economic purpose.

Root cause: Ultimate beneficial ownership transiting through offshore holding companies in opaque jurisdictions with no clear economic purpose.

Defense evidence: Full corporate ownership chart from the WFOE to every natural person; certificates of incorporation, memorandum and articles, register of directors and shareholders for each intermediate entity; passport copies of ultimate individual owners; six months of parent-company bank statements; notarized declarations of source of wealth signed by each UBO; and an external legal opinion introducing and validating the ownership chain.
Bonded warehouse license and customs registration inconsistent with declared trading activity or missing physical substance.

Root cause: Bonded warehouse license and customs registration inconsistent with declared trading activity or missing physical substance.

Defense evidence: Valid customs clearance registration certificate for the free trade zone; bonded warehouse license or zone operating permit; lease or ownership proof for the bonded depot; warehouse management system screenshots with two years of storage, receipt, and issue ledgers; employee social insurance contribution schedules; photographs or video of the facility; and a board-approved business plan showing projected trade flows, counterparties, inventory turnover, and bonded logistics capability.
Unsettled proceeds or recurring late collection of export earnings creating an adverse flag under SAFE goods trade monitoring.

Root cause: Unsettled proceeds or recurring late collection of export earnings creating an adverse flag under SAFE goods trade monitoring.

Defense evidence: Executed sales contracts and purchase orders; commercial invoices, packing lists, and bill of lading or air waybill evidence; customs export declarations; bank MT103 credit advices; a certified accounts receivable aging report; warehouse dispatch records; and a written reconciliation letter prepared by the external auditor specifically explaining each overdue collection event.
Cross-border capital injection with no evidential tie to the registered investment amount or funds sourced from non-bank lending channels.

Defense evidence: SAFE FDI registration receipt; capital contribution schedule and board resolution; SAFE-approved capital account details; inward SWIFT MT103 payment where the remitter exactly matches the FDI shareholder; source-of-funds evidence including audited investor financial statements, tax returns, and solvency letters; and a post-remittance capital verification report issued by a PRC certified public accountant before funds are deployed.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity injection must be structured as a SWIFT MT103 wire from the overseas corporate shareholder to the WFOE's SAFE-registered Foreign Exchange Capital Account at a designated Chinese bank. The remittance purpose must be declared as Foreign Direct Investment Equity Contribution, and the bank will file the SAFE direct investment declaration code on the inward transfer. The WFOE must hold the FDI registration receipt, complete the foreign exchange business registration, and, after remittance, instruct the bank to convert into RMB or retain the foreign currency within the approved business scope. A PRC certified public accountant then issues the capital verification report. For bonded warehouse operations, capital account funds must be segregated from bonded logistics expenses, customs deposits, and ordinary operating flows; transfers from the capital account to general RMB accounts must satisfy the real-use and authenticity review requirements.Outbound trade disbursements are cleared through SWIFT MT103 for cross-border wire transfers in USD, EUR, or CNY, through CIPS for RMB cross-border settlements, and through domestic clearing for free trade zone transactions involving Chinese counterparties. For bulk trade settlements, banks may issue Commercial Letters of Credit, sight or usance, backed by warehouse receipts and customs declarations, and may handle documentary collections on a documents against payment or documents against acceptance basis. Every outbound payment requires the trade contract, invoice, transport and customs documents, and the SAFE goods trade verification code. Mandatory Audit Threshold Mandatory Audit for All Entities

Turnover: For this WFOE, the statutory audit is triggered categorically by foreign ownership: the entity is 100 percent foreign-invested and therefore has no audit exemption based on small size. Under PRC foreign-investment information reporting and the accounting regulatory framework, a wholly foreign-owned enterprise must submit annual financial statements audited by a licensed PRC CPA firm. In the PRC enterprise-size classification applicable to warehousing and logistics activities, mandatory full independent audit is nonetheless reinforced if any two of the following quantitative thresholds are exceeded: total assets of RMB 50 million, annual operating revenue of RMB 100 million, or 300 full-time employees. Separate audited-financial-statement obligations arise if the WFOE holds AEO customs certification, holds a bonded-warehouse operating license, seeks government procurement rights, applies for high-tech enterprise status, or uses bank credit facilities secured by the bonded warehouse assets.
