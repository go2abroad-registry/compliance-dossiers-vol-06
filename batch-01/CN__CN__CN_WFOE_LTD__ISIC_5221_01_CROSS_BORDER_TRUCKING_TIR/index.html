Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A virtual office or commercial mailbox is generally not permissible as the sole registered office for a WFOE engaged in registered international road freight under ISIC 5221. The Regulations on the Administration of Market Entity Registration require a fixed production or business address to be verified by the local market supervision authority. For a road transport enterprise, the Ministry of Transport regulations require the applicant to have a fixed business premises with adequate parking, dispatch, and safety management facilities; however, a separate operational depot can be leased at a different location, while the registered address must still be a physical site documented by a valid lease agreement, property ownership certificate, or a notarized landlord permission letter. A pure virtual office service that provides only a registered address without any actual employee presence is rejected by the State Taxation Administration for general taxpayer VAT registration, by the local tax bureau for issuing professional transport VAT invoices, by the Ministry of Transport for the Road Transportation Business License (道路运输经营许可证), and by banks for business settlement account opening under PBOC Account Opening Requirements. Some free-trade zones and collective industrial parks may offer 'clustered registration' services, but those are acceptable only if the cluster provider maintains a physical leased space, management personnel, and the traffic authority accepts that address for vehicle licenses. The WFOE must, in all cases, maintain a physical presence in the form of leased office space, at least one resident registered manager, and the transport operation center's dispatch records.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-up capital for a WFOE limited liability company; registered capital is a subscribed amount under the governing Articles of Association and must be paid down within five years from incorporation per Company Law Article 47. There is no blocked escrow account requirement under general PRC incorporation law. The foreign investor must comply with the Foreign Investment Negative List, and the provincial transport department may require evidence of fleet ownership or lease and operational financial capacity for road freight licensing; that requirement is not a paid-up capital or escrow condition.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety Acceptance Filing or Fire Protection Acceptance Certificate under the PRC Fire Protection Law for any depot, garage, warehouse or office premise; Civil Defense Fire Clearance, including inspection of fire extinguishers, evacuation routes, alarms and emergency lighting; structural load verification for parking decks or warehouses; workplace occupational hazard assessment and occupational health checks for drivers and mechanics; road transport safety management system with a designated safety officer; periodic technical inspection of trucks; and driver qualification verification before dispatch. If no physical depot is maintained beyond the registered office, the fire clearance applies to that office lease only.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; online filing is performed via the National Electronic Tax Bureau portal at etax.chinatax.gov.cn, with local STA offices executing registration and field inspections.

E-government filing portal

For a foreign non-resident entity establishing a WFOE, the following exhaustive procedure applies. Step 1: complete the foreign-invested enterprise incorporation with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) and obtain the 18-character Unified Social Credit Code (USCC). Step 2: within 30 days of issuance of the business licence, log into the National Electronic Tax Bureau and complete the taxpayer registration, during which the USCC is designated as the statutory taxpayer identification number (TIN); no separate VAT identification number is issued. Step 3: upload the business licence, foreign investment information report, articles of association, shareholder identity documents, director identification, registered address proof, and the industry licence required for bonded road freight. Step 4: select the VAT payer status by electing either small-scale taxpayer status or general taxpayer status; the latter is compulsory once annual taxable turnover exceeds RMB 5,000,000. Step 5: activate the electronic invoice capability using the digital Tax UKey and register the bank accounts used for export foreign exchange receipts. For a foreign non-resident enterprise that does not create a permanent establishment in China and is not the WFOE itself, the Chinese counterparty or withholding agent must complete non-resident withholding registration with the STA; the non-resident enterprise does not receive a full PRC TIN unless it acquires real estate or is otherwise subject to direct tax assessment.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None. The People's Republic of China does not provide check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch flow-through elections in the domestic income tax system. A WFOE is a resident enterprise and is inevitably subject to entity-level corporate income taxation on its worldwide income. No pass-through designation or territorial exemption election is available for a standard WFOE engaged in bonded freight transport.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

9%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 in annual taxable turnover. Every WFOE must complete tax registration within 30 days of incorporation regardless of turnover; cross-border trucking turnover below this threshold permits small-scale taxpayer status, while turnover above it triggers compulsory general VAT taxpayer registration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Cross-border bonded highway transport services supplied between China and a foreign customs territory under the TIR Carnet regime qualify for the 0% VAT export rate when the transport operator is a registered general VAT taxpayer and the service constitutes international transportation. The zero rating applies only to the cross-border leg; domestic pre-carriage and post-carriage remain taxable at 9%. Eligibility requires a genuine TIR transport operation with a valid TIR Carnet, cargo placed under customs transit, and evidence that the service is exported from China. Input VAT attributable to the cross-border leg may be credited, and excess input VAT refunded via the STA export refund procedure.

  • People's Republic of China Customs Export Goods Declaration
  • TIR Carnet with all validated and uncancelled vouchers
  • International Road Waybill (CMR consignment note or equivalent TIR transport document)
  • Commercial invoice and packing list issued by the WFOE
  • Bank export proceeds verification slip or foreign exchange income settlement certificate, serving as the Inward Remittance Certificate equivalent

Golden Tax Phase IV governance requires all VAT invoices to be issued through the STA-controlled electronic invoice service platform using the digital Tax UKey. Special VAT invoices and fully digitalised electronic invoices are cleared in near-real time, with mandatory API/EDI integration for high-volume freight operators. Anti-fraud protection is enforced through cryptographic digital stamping, dynamic invoice codes, and automatic reconciliation of invoice data with customs export records and foreign exchange repatriation records.

Fraudulent, false, or unsubstantiated export zero-rating claims trigger retrospective revocation of the zero rate, recovery of input VAT refunded, collection of underpaid VAT with daily late payment surcharges, and administrative fines of 50% to five times the tax underpaid. Serious cases involving forged TIR Carnets, false customs declarations, fictitious waybills, or failure to repatriate export proceeds are referred for criminal prosecution under the PRC Criminal Law. STA anti-fraud systems automatically match zero-rated sales against customs clearance records, TIR Carnet validation data, and SAFE foreign exchange receipts. Records must be retained for the statutory retention period, and the tax authorities are not time-barred from recovering tax in cases of deliberate fraud.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The RMB is fully convertible for current account items, including freight charges, fuel payments and operational disbursements of an ISIC 5221 TIR logistics entity, provided such payments are supported by genuine commercial documents and reported to the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) through the corporate bank's monitoring platform. Capital account items remain strictly regulated: FDI equity contributions must be registered under SAFE's direct investment information system, held in a designated foreign currency capital account, and utilized only within the permitted investment scope as recorded in the WFOE's establishment certificate and business scope. Profit repatriation is lawful after the annual statutory audit is completed, corporate income tax is settled, and the bank verifies the dividend resolution and the foreign investor's effective tax payment record. There is no blanket surrender requirement for export proceeds; freight collections may be retained in foreign currency or converted to RMB at the same bank. For hedging, onshore banks may provide forward, swap and option products only when the WFOE proves an underlying current or capital account exposure, and offshore non-deliverable forwards are not ordinarily accessible to mainland enterprises.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the SAFE trade-in-goods administration, export-related foreign currency receipts, including cross-border road transport service income, must be repatriated to a mainland licensed bank account within 90 days after customs declaration, unless an extension is registered for late payment. There is no mandatory conversion ratio; the beneficiary may retain 100% of the proceeds in foreign currency or sell them at the prevailing interbank rate. The exporting WFOE must submit remittance details under the correct revenue purpose code and complete monthly reconciliation with SAFE's trade data verification rules.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Very low viability. Mainland China does not allow an offshore electronic money institution (EMI) or overseas fintech payment company to act as the primary clearing bank for a domestic WFOE. Any institution performing payment, deposit-taking, or FX conversion services for a Chinese entity must hold a PBOC payment license or be an approved domestic bank. As a result, all cross-border treasury and liquidity flows for this TIR trucking entity should be routed through a Chinese tier-1 commercial bank; EMIs may only provide auxiliary invoicing or data services and cannot replace the local banking rails.

Strike-off trigger

Mandatory Audit Threshold Mandatory Audit for All Entities

Turnover: Under PRC Company Law every limited liability company, including a wholly foreign-owned enterprise, must prepare annual financial reports and have them audited by a certified public accountant registered in China at the end of each fiscal year, regardless of turnover or headcount. In practice the legal mandate applies without a minimum revenue threshold; even a company with zero revenue or dormant status must produce a statutory audit unless it has obtained formal deregistration. Quantitative administrative classification triggers under current Chinese statistical and accounting supervision rules are as follows: small enterprises generally have assets below RMB 30 million or annual revenue below RMB 50 million or headcount below 300; mid-sized enterprises exceed those bases but stay below assets of RMB 500 million, annual revenue of RMB 500 million, and headcount of 1,000; large enterprises meet or exceed RMB 500 million in assets, RMB 500 million in annual revenue, and 1,000 employees. Those larger enterprises are subject to stricter mandatory audit, disclosure, and consolidated-reporting obligations. Banks, insurance companies, listed groups, and high-risk licensed operators face a mandatory audit irrespective of size. No size-based exemption exists for a WFOE in the normal operating course; the only relief is through the dormant-company or simplified deregistration route, which is narrowly construed by the market regulation authority.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File thresholds: related-party tangible asset transfers exceeding RMB 200 million, and related-party service, intangible-asset, or financial-asset transfers exceeding RMB 100 million in a tax year; Master File is required where aggregate cross-border related-party transactions exceed RMB 1 billion; annual related-party transaction reporting forms must be filed with the annual CIT return whenever related-party transactions exist.

Economic substance scope: China does not operate a separate Gulf-style Economic Substance Regulations statute, but the EIT Law GAAR and DTA jurisprudence impose an equivalent economic-substance and beneficial-ownership requirement. For a TIR cross-border trucking WFOE, Core Income-Generating Activities include China-based vehicle ownership or lease, driver employment and control, route dispatch, TIR carnet and customs bond management, maintenance, invoicing, collection, and assumption of transit risk. A WFOE that records profit while all substantive decisions and operations occur offshore may be recharacterized, adjusted, and denied treaty benefits.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Documentary Conduct

Statutory sanction: Tax Collection Administration Law Article 63 authorizes recovery of the underpaid tax, daily overdue interest of 0.05%, and a fine of 50% to 500% of the evaded amount; Criminal Law Article 201 imposes criminal liability for tax evasion with imprisonment of up to 3 years for ordinary evasion and 3 to 7 years for large evasion, plus fines; false issuance or use of false special VAT invoices may trigger Criminal Law Article 205 with penalties that can reach life imprisonment.

Director personal liability: Under PRC Company Law Article 23 (2023 revision), the corporate veil may be pierced when shareholders or directors abuse the limited liability entity to evade tax debts, leading to joint and several personal liability over personal assets; the legal representative is subject to exit-control travel bans under TCAL Article 44, inclusion in the national tax dishonesty blacklist, asset seizure, and criminal imprisonment for directly responsible persons.
Withholding Tax Non-Compliance and DTA Abuse on Profit Repatriation

Statutory sanction: TCAL Article 69 imposes a fine of 50% to 300% of the unwithheld tax; TCAL Article 32 adds daily late-payment interest of 0.05%; EIT Law Articles 37 and 38 set out the withholding obligation; treaty benefits are denied where the beneficial ownership or residence requirement is not met and the underpaid withholding tax may be assessed against the non-resident shareholder.

Director personal liability: Directors and financial officers who direct or execute a scheme to avoid withholding may be treated as directly responsible persons for criminal tax evasion; the legal representative and CFO can be blocked from leaving China until the tax liability is settled; if the WFOE's assets are diverted to shareholders before collection, courts can pierce the veil and impose personal restitution, personal fines, and imprisonment of up to 7 years.
Transfer Pricing Mispricing and Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: EIT Law Article 41 authorizes reallocation of profits on an arm's-length basis; Article 48 imposes interest on the underpaid tax arising from special tax adjustments; TCAL Article 62 imposes fines for failure to file related-party transaction reports; SAT Announcement 2016 No. 42 allows the tax authority to use available comparables and assess a reasonable profit where documentation is absent; criminal liability can arise if false documents are used to conceal taxable income.

Director personal liability: Directors and CFOs who knowingly sign false related-party filings face personal fines and imprisonment for tax evasion; tax authorities may impose joint liability on directors who receive disguised distributions; the corporate veil can be pierced to reach personal assets for adjusted tax, interest, and penalties; the legal representative faces exit bans, asset freezes, blacklisting, and criminal prosecution in egregious cases.
