Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None. The PRC Company Law and Foreign Investment Law impose no statutory minimum paid-up capital and no blocked escrow account condition for a wholly foreign-owned enterprise limited liability company. Registered capital is fixed in the Articles of Association and subscribed by the foreign shareholder according to the contribution schedule recorded with SAMR. For the certified GDP pharmaceutical freight-validation activity, the principal formation funding requirements are operational: the board must ensure sufficient capital for licensed premises, temperature-controlled freight equipment, validation tools, quality-management personnel, and working capital before services commence. No capital escrow is required at formation. If an international freight-forwarding recordal is subsequently sought, the local commerce bureau may apply historical minimum registered capital thresholds, and the company should verify the applicable MOFCOM filing requirements.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of China; online portal: the Electronic Taxpayer (e-Tax) Bureau platform available on the STA official website.

E-government filing portal

A foreign non-resident enterprise establishing a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE) in China must first complete registration with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) to obtain the legal entity business license. Within 30 days thereafter, the entity must complete tax registration with the local State Taxation Administration office through the Electronic Taxpayer (e-Tax) Bureau, submitting the business license, seat/lease agreement, articles of association, shareholder identification documents and bank account opening certificate. The system assigns a unified 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) used as the de facto Tax Identification Number (TIN) and VAT registration number. A separate VAT general taxpayer registration must be made if annual taxable turnover exceeds RMB 5 million or is elected voluntarily. The entire process is paperless via the e-Tax portal.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No monetary threshold for tax registration – every WFOE must register. For standard (general) VAT taxpayer status, the mandatory threshold is annual taxable turnover of RMB 5,000,000. Below this threshold, an entity may elect general taxpayer status or remain a small-scale taxpayer subject to special simplified rules.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For goods: Export sales of goods are zero-rated under Article 24 of the VAT Law, provided the goods physically leave China customs territory and the exporter files an export declaration. The exporter must be a general VAT taxpayer, have completed export tax rebate (exemption and refund) filing, and settle export proceeds in foreign exchange in accordance with SAFE regulations. For services: Cross-border sales of qualifying services, including transportation services and certain logistics auxiliary services related to export goods, may apply zero-rate or VAT exemption only if the buyer is an overseas entity, the services are consumed entirely outside China, and documentary evidence certifies the offshore nature. Specifically, international freight forwarding and export-loading services performed for cargo exported from China can enjoy VAT zero-rating or exemption under STA rules when the contract, invoice, customs declaration and foreign exchange collection documents are consistent. For GDP pharma logistics services, the exemption/zero-rating also requires the drug transportation temperature-record logs and chain-of-custody dossier to prove the service relates to physically exported goods.

  • Customs Export Declaration (export goods customs declaration form) duly accepted by China Customs
  • Bill of Lading (B/L) or Airway Bill (AWB) evidencing international carriage of the exported goods
  • Foreign Exchange Collection Verification – current system uses the SAFE Trade Foreign Exchange Monitoring System registration and bank receipt / Foreign Exchange Inward Remittance Advice evidencing actual receipt of export proceeds
  • Official VAT special invoice for the export sale, if issued
  • Export contract / commercial invoice and packing list
  • For GDP pharma cargo: the Good Distribution Practice (GDP) temperature excursion report / chain-of-custody documentation

China has mandated fully digital electronic invoices issued via the nationwide Electronic Invoice Platform operated by the STA. Official VAT invoices (special and ordinary VAT fapiao) must be digitally encrypted and transmitted in real-time to the STA database. The system applies digital fingerprints, automated data-pooling and artificial-intelligence-based risk screening (Golden Tax Phase IV). For B2B transactions, buyers and sellers perform real-time reconciliation through the VAT invoice public service platform. No additional physical stamping is required after digitisation; every digital invoice is embedded with a unique invoice code and verification failsafe.

China implements a strict export tax rebate audit system. Tax authorities verify on a risk-based sample that export declarations, invoice details, customs clearance data, FX receipt information and logistics records match (the 'three-reconciliation' / business-flow, fund-flow and logistics-flow matching). Failure to collect export proceeds within the statutory period or providing false documents triggers restoration of VAT for the relevant export sale plus late payment interest (0.05% daily). Penalties under the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection and the Criminal Law include: forfeiture of unpaid or underpaid tax, a fine of 50% to 500% of the evaded amount, and criminal liability for fraudulent export tax refunds (crime of fraudulent export tax refund) with sentences up to life imprisonment and confiscation of property. Additionally, tax agents or logistics operators that participate in fraud may face licence revocation and inclusion in the national tax integrity blacklist.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a managed floating renminbi regime and has accepted Article VIII obligations, making the current account generally convertible while the capital account remains regulated. For the WFOE, ordinary trade-related receipts and payments can be made through banks under SAFE's total-amount verification framework after submission of genuine transaction documents; no sovereign approval is needed for current account flows. Capital account transactions, including FDI capital injections, shareholder loans, and outward remittances on liquidation, require SAFE registration or approval. The FDI capital contribution is monitored through the SAFE Digital Foreign Exchange Administration platform, and funds must be deployed for lawful business purposes consistent with the approved business scope. Profit remittance is permissible only after annual statutory audit, tax clearance, and completion of dividend distribution; the bank will review corporate resolutions, audited financial statements, and tax withholding certificates. Although export proceeds must be repatriated to China, there is no mandatory conversion ratio; the exporter may retain proceeds in a foreign exchange settlement account or convert voluntarily at market rates. Any avoidance of verification through inflated invoices or mismatched logistics is treated as a serious AML violation.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the SAFE goods-trade foreign exchange administration framework, export proceeds from goods must be repatriated to a domestic foreign exchange settlement account or settled to renminbi, with no mandatory conversion percentage currently imposed. The banking compliance baseline requires collection within 90 days of the customs export declaration; receipts beyond that window must be reported to SAFE as late or delayed collection exceptions with an explanation supported by the sales contract, invoice, customs declaration, and correspondence. A-class enterprises may use a total-amount verification scheme with a lower documentation burden, but full supporting records must remain available for post-verification. Mandatory conversion ratio: 0%; voluntary settlement or retention in foreign exchange is permitted. Offshore retention of export proceeds is not generally allowed without SAFE approval for special treasury centralization arrangements. The WFOE must ensure the export payment corresponds to the actual goods description, value, quantity, and customs data, as the verification system will match the bank receipt against China Customs records.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) and State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low as a primary banking rail. Offshore digital EMI platforms do not hold PBOC payment-institution licenses and are legally barred from opening domestic RMB accounts or receiving SAFE capital account registrations for a Mainland China WFOE. They can support the group treasury outside China only as ancillary technology, and any cross-border flow must clear through a licensed domestic bank or a PBOC-licensed non-bank payment institution using a sponsor bank arrangement. The WFOE should not rely on Airwallex, Payoneer, Revolut, or similar EMIs for FDI capital, export proceeds, or Chinese tax declarations; instead, it should source onshore account infrastructure from a Tier-1 commercial bank and use EMIs only where they act as a front-end interface to that bank's correspondent network.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Opacity in the ultimate beneficial ownership chain, especially an offshore intermediate holding company in a low-tax jurisdiction.

Root cause: Opacity in the ultimate beneficial ownership chain, especially an offshore intermediate holding company in a low-tax jurisdiction.

Defense evidence: Full ownership chain from the foreign parent to the WFOE, including certificates of incorporation and good standing; register of directors and shareholders; notarized declaration and passport copies of every underlying individual UBO; source of wealth and source of funds evidence; bank reference letters from the parent's bankers; group organogram; MOFCOM foreign investment information filing; and the SAFE FDI registration certificate.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The foreign shareholder must route the FDI capital contribution through the WFOE's SAFE-registered FDI foreign exchange capital account at a domestic Tier-1 bank. The remittance instruction should use SWIFT MT103 with the correct SAFE capital account reference, the Chinese entity's registration name, and an explicit purpose code corresponding to direct investment paid-in capital. Before the transfer, the WFOE must complete the Foreign Investment Information Report with MOFCOM and obtain the SAFE FDI foreign exchange registration voucher from the local SAFE branch. Funds may be sent in USD, EUR, or RMB; RMB injections must be reported to PBOC's RMB cross-border payment information management system (RCPMIS) and may be routed through CIPS. After receipt, the bank verifies the funds against the registration and the actual paid-in equity; a Chinese CPA then issues a capital verification report if required by the bank, the shareholders' agreement, or local administration. The proceeds must be reserved for lawful operational use in the approved business scope; they cannot be diverted to equity or securities investment, non-business real estate, or entrusted loans in China without separate approval.Operational cross-border payments for freight, logistics, warehousing, and clinical trial services should be processed by the main onshore clearing bank using SWIFT MT103 for bank-to-bank transfers, or CIPS for RMB payments. For goods-related purchases requiring settlement terms, the WFOE can open an irrevocable sight commercial letter of credit via MT700 with a confirming bank overseas, and documentary collections may be used with a negotiating bank through the corresponding SWIFT collection messages. All service-trade outward payments must be supported by contract, commercial invoice, and, where applicable, Chinese tax withholding certificates for VAT and corporate income tax; banks will conduct a real-demand review and match the remittance against SAFE's service trade filing regime. For goods trade, the payment must be supported by the electronic customs declaration, import documents, and the bank's goods-trade foreign exchange payment filing. The WFOE should maintain a central treasury calendar to manage settlement dates, account balances, and tax payment deadlines in both USD and RMB.Onshore FX forward contracts for genuine current-account exposure, executed under an FX settlement and trading agreement with a licensed Chinese bank, with SAFE real-demand filing.FX options, zero-cost collars, and risk-reversal combinations on RMB/USD, RMB/EUR, or other allowable currency pairs, limited to hedging documented trade and logistics revenue and payables.Cross-currency swaps and FX swaps for funding and capital account liabilities such as shareholder loans, provided the external debt is registered under SAFE's macroprudential foreign debt framework.Onshore NDFs are not available for domestic entities; offshore NDFs are outside the permitted hedge boundary for a Mainland China WFOE because RMB hedging must be contracted onshore with supporting real economic exposure.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% standard Enterprise Income Tax (EIT) rate under PRC EIT Law Article 4

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% of gross dividends paid by a Chinese-resident enterprise to its non-resident corporate shareholder, under EIT Law Articles 3 and 37.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: In RMB: Master File must be prepared if total related-party revenue in the previous year exceeds RMB 2 billion; Local File must be prepared if related-party transactions exceed any of the following thresholds: RMB 400 million in tangible goods transfers, RMB 100 million in intangible property transfers, RMB 100 million in related-party services, or RMB 100 million in financial asset transfers. Country-by-Country Report must be filed if the consolidated group revenue of the ultimate parent exceeds RMB 5.5 billion.

Economic substance scope: China does not impose Gulf-style Economic Substance Regulations in literal form; however, EIT Law anti-avoidance rules and DTA beneficial ownership doctrine require substantive entities. For treaty relief, the beneficial owner must exercise real decision-making over assets and risks in its residence jurisdiction. For domestic tax, a WFOE without substance may have payments recharacterized, lose treaty benefits, or be consolidated under CFC provisions when offshore low-taxed enterprises lack core income-generating activities. A GDP-pharma freight WFOE must maintain physical premises, qualified personnel, cold-chain infrastructure, contract files, licenses and board minutes in China to prove economic substance.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion through false VAT invoices and inflated costs

Statutory sanction: Tax Administration Law Article 63: penalty of 50% to 5 times the underpaid or evaded tax plus daily 0.05% late-payment interest and forfeited deductions; Criminal Law Article 205: for issuing or obtaining false special VAT invoices, fines, confiscation, and imprisonment up to life for extremely serious amounts; Criminal Law Article 201: up to 7 years imprisonment for tax evasion.

Director personal liability: Courts may pierce the WFOE corporate veil because fraud caused corporate insolvency, making directors jointly and severally liable for unpaid tax, VAT criminal fines, and civil restitution; the legal representative and responsible director are placed on the tax authority exit-control blacklist and denied departure from China until the debt is settled; criminal conviction results in imprisonment, personal fines, confiscation, and full restitution.
Withholding tax default and DTA abuse on profit repatriation

Statutory sanction: EIT Law Articles 3, 37 and 38; Tax Administration Law Article 69: withholding agent penalty of 50% to 3 times the unwithheld tax and recovery of the entire tax from the payer; EIT Law Article 47 GAAR permits recharacterization of the conduit structure, denial of treaty benefits, and upward taxable adjustment with interest; criminal proceedings may follow for deliberate evasion under Criminal Law Article 201.

Director personal liability: Personal travel bans and exit-control blacklisting apply when legal representatives have uncollected tax liabilities; directors who knowingly participated in the sham structure face personal civil liability for the WHT shortfall, damage claims by co-shareholders, and criminal exposure as co-principals under Criminal Law unit-crime rules, including imprisonment and restitution.
Transfer pricing documentation failure and artificial profit shifting to related offshore service provider

Statutory sanction: EIT Law Articles 41-48: tax authority may adjust taxable income, disallow excess related-party deductions, apply the 2:1 thin-capitalization limitation, charge late-payment interest on tax adjustments, and impose 50%-5x penalties under Article 63 if intent is established; Article 44 permits tax assessment based on available information and comparable data when TP documentation is absent.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly approved sham transfer pricing policies can be held personally liable for added tax and penalties to the extent corporate assets were diverted to offshore accounts; the legal representative is entered into the serious tax violation blacklisting system and travel restricted; under criminal thresholds, intentional false documentation supporting the sham charges is treated as tax fraud, leading to up to 7 years imprisonment for the responsible officers, restitution, and disqualification from serving as directors.
