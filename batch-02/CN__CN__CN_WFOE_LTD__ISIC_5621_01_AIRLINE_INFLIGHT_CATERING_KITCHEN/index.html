Competent corporate registry

State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) through the local Market Supervision and Administration Bureau (MSAB) of the place of the WFOE registered office, Enterprise Registration Section; filings are made on the nationwide integrated online government service platform and the provincial one-stop enterprise registration portal.

Governing statute

Company Law of the People's Republic of China (2023 Revision, effective 1 July 2024), Articles 4, 10-13, 16-19, 23-32, 46-47, 60-66, 67-78, 82-91, 95-100 and 204-206; Foreign Investment Law of the People's Republic of China (2019), Articles 1-4, 12, 15 and 28; Implementing Regulations of the Foreign Investment Law (2019), Articles 2-5 and 11-22; Interim Measures for the Administration of Market Entity Registration (SAMR Order No. 68, 2021); Regulations on the Administration of Company Registration (2021).

Formation / constitutional filings

Application for Registration of a Foreign-Invested Company (Foreign Investment Enterprise Establishment Registration Dossier), together with the notarized and approved Articles of Association, submitted electronically or in person to the local SAMR enterprise registration window.

Incorporation processing time

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None. The PRC Company Law does not impose a minimum paid-up capital amount for a wholly foreign-owned limited liability company; shareholders fix the registered capital in the Articles of Association and may contribute over a period not exceeding five years from establishment. No blocked escrow account is required for incorporation. For the ISIC 5621 airline catering business, the food licensing and airport authorities may require evidence of operational working capital and fixed-asset investment, which can be shown by company bank deposit certificates or an escrow arrangement, but that is an industry operating condition rather than a statutory capital formation rule.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; electronic portal: the Chinese Electronic Taxpayer (e-Tax) system operated by provincial STA branches under the national STA platform, accessible through the official STA website www.chinatax.gov.cn

E-government filing portal

Step 2: Under the multi-license integration reform, the Administration for Market Regulation shares the registration data with the STA, automatically completing basic tax registration; the USCC becomes the statutory Tax Identification Number (TIN) for the WFOE. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation; the WFOE is a resident enterprise taxpayer subject to PRC Corporate Income Tax on worldwide income, and there is no pass-through treatment.

Available election forms

None for check-the-box or hybrid mismatch elections. Available election: a small-scale VAT taxpayer may voluntarily register as a VAT general taxpayer even if annual taxable turnover is below RMB 5,000,000; once general taxpayer status is selected, reversion to small-scale status is generally unavailable within a rolling 12-month period. Simplified VAT collection applies to qualifying small-scale taxpayers where applicable.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Basic tax registration is mandatory from establishment without a turnover threshold. Compulsory general VAT taxpayer registration applies when annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000 (CNY 5 million); taxpayers below this threshold may remain small-scale VAT taxpayers or elect general status under STA rules.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods physically exported from the Customs Territory of the PRC, including aviation catering supplies loaded onto outbound international flights under customs supervision and consumed after the aircraft leaves PRC territory, are zero-rated for VAT upon completion of a lawful export customs declaration. The claim requires a genuine export sale contract with an overseas airline, actual loading and departure from customs territory, a customs export declaration, and proof that foreign exchange has been received from the non-PRC airline or a properly designated payer. Services rendered wholly abroad and qualifying international transportation-related services are zero-rated under applicable STA service-export measures; domestic inflight catering on domestic flights remains domestically taxable and cannot be zero-rated.

  • Customs Export Declaration (export tax-rebate copy) containing the customs release seal and export declaration number
  • Master and House Air Waybill or equivalent transport document evidencing carriage on the outbound international flight
  • Export sales contract, commercial invoice, and packing list evidencing the airline as buyer and specifying the inflight catering goods
  • Bank Inward Remittance Certificate or foreign-exchange collection proof issued by a PRC-designated bank, evidencing repatriation of the export proceeds from the overseas airline
  • Bonded warehouse transfer or logistics supervision paperwork and the aircraft departure manifest demonstrating physical departure of the catering supplies from PRC customs territory

VAT invoices, both special and ordinary, must be issued through the national VAT invoice management system and the fully digital electronic invoice (FDEI) platform operated by the State Taxation Administration. Invoice data is transmitted to STA in near real time; each invoice receives a unique validated invoice number, anti-counterfeit QR coding, and digital seal. For bonded aviation catering and export supplies, the e-invoice data, customs bonded warehouse records, export customs declaration data, and the departure manifest must reconcile. There is no separate export tax stamp; the controlling clearance evidence is the customs export declaration.

Customs, tax, and foreign-exchange authorities jointly audit export VAT zero-rating and refund claims using risk classification and document matching. If the zero-rated export treatment is unsupported, falsified, or the required foreign-exchange repatriation is not completed within the statutory deadline, the export VAT refund is reversed and collected back with late-payment surcharge. Under the Law of the PRC on the Administration of Tax Levying and Collection, fraudulent export tax refunds can attract fines of one to five times the refund amount, potential suspension of future export refund handling, and downgrade of the exporter's tax credit classification. Under Article 204 of the Criminal Law of the PRC, obtaining export VAT refund by fraudulent means is a criminal offense, with penalties from five years up to life imprisonment for especially large amounts, plus fines and confiscation of property.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The renminbi (CNY) is convertible on the current account under IMF Article VIII, but the capital account remains controlled and non-convertible without specific regulatory approval. SAFE administers a registration-based regime for foreign direct investment, and a WFOE must obtain an FDI registration certificate before receiving inbound equity or loan proceeds. Funds are disbursed into a domestic Renminbi or foreign exchange settlement account, and cross-border wires are tracked against legally verified transactional documents. For genuine trade and service payments, current-account convertibility permits relatively smooth remittance, but profit repatriation as dividends requires annual statutory audit, tax clearance, and an enterprise income tax filing. All outbound remittances, including dividends, royalties, and service fees, must be supported by original contracts, invoices, and proof of contract performance. In practice, banks conduct per-transaction compliance review; therefore, speed depends on the quality and authenticity of documentation. Offshore CNY (CNH) markets exist but onshore corporate hedging and conversion must be executed through a domestic bank under SAFE rules.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export proceeds must be received into the enterprise's FX settlement account promptly and reported to SAFE via the Goods Trade Monitoring Platform. The governing rule requires that export foreign exchange be collected within the contractually agreed payment term, and the bank monitors overdue collection alerts. No mandatory surrender ratio is applicable; proceeds may be retained in FX, converted to CNY, or used for approved permissible payments, but any retention requires a balance-of-payments declaration and reconciliation with the customs export declaration.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE), operating under the People's Bank of China (PBoC)

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital proceeds from the foreign shareholder must first be received into a designated capital account (FDI foreign exchange settlement account) with the selected Tier-1 bank. The funds are released only after the enterprise has completed an FDI Registration Record with SAFE and the bank has verified the uniqueness of the payment classification code (typically code 622, 621, or the applicable ASIP transaction code). A licensed CPA firm then issues a capital verification report confirming that the funds have been actually subscribed in accordance with the company's Articles of Association. If received in a general operational account, the proceeds cannot be used until the foreign exchange business record has been completed and the bank has confirmed the matching purpose code, funding amount, and entity details; anything outside the registered trap will be rejected.For international settlements related to imported food ingredients, aviation equipment leases, or airport fuel and service charges, the WFOE should use SWIFT MT103 for direct wire payments of operating expenses. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low. Cross-border fintech EMIs (e.g., Revolut, Airwallex, Wise) are not licensed as deposit-taking institutions in mainland China; they lack the dedicated SAFE/FTZ account infrastructure and cannot serve as primary treasury or clearing bank for a WFOE's statutory foreign exchange registration, capital account monitoring, or trade settlement. At most, they may function as a secondary remittance conduit in limited scenarios where a regulated domestic bank acts as the settling sponsor; however, SAFE licensing and BOP/reporting requirements make this an impractical and legally fragile choice for the in-flight catering treasury architecture.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: RMB 200,000,000 for annual related-party tangible asset transactions; RMB 100,000,000 for intangible asset transactions; RMB 100,000,000 for financial asset transactions; RMB 40,000,000 for other related-party transactions. A master file and Country-by-Country Report are required when the ultimate parent group's consolidated revenue exceeds RMB 5,500,000,000. These thresholds trigger the mandatory contemporaneous Local File and, where applicable, Master File preparation before the annual CIT return date.

Economic substance scope: The WFOE must demonstrate actual economic substance in China. The EIT law has no separate annual Economic Substance Regulations filing like Cayman or BVI, but tax authorities apply Article 47 GAAR, the place-of-effective-management rule, and the beneficial ownership doctrine. For an airline inflight catering kitchen, core income-generating activities (CIGA) include meal production, food safety certification, inventory procurement in the bonded zone, airport ramp delivery, and contract management with airlines. If these activities are not in China, the STA can disregard the WFOE's corporate form and tax the profits where the de facto management and CIGA are really located. A WFOE that is an empty facade with no kitchen, no physical presence, and no operational control will be treated as a paper entity and subject to special tax investigation.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Corporate Income Tax Filing

Statutory sanction: Under PRC Tax Collection Administration Law Article 63, the WFOE must repay the evaded tax, pay a penalty of 50% to 500% of the underpaid amount, and pay a 0.05% daily late surcharge. Under Criminal Law Article 201, tax evasion of RMB 50,000 or more and more than 10% of tax payable is a criminal offence; the legal consequences are up to 3 years' imprisonment plus a fine, increasing to 3-7 years for a huge amount.

Director personal liability: A court will pierce the corporate veil where directors have used the WFOE as a mere instrument to evade taxes or where corporate and personal funds are mixed. The legal representative, finance director, and tax preparer who signed the false return face joint civil restitution of the tax loss; they may be blacklisted as serious tax untrustworthy persons and restricted from exit from China; on criminal conviction the responsible director receives a prison term and personal property is confiscated to satisfy the fine.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance and Artificial Profit Allocation

Statutory sanction: Under EIT Law Article 47 (GAAR), the tax authority may disregard the transaction and recharacterize the income; Article 48 applies an added special tax adjustment interest on the reassessed tax, and Article 36 of the Tax Collection Law permits adjustment of related-party transactions. The entity will be disqualified from any preferential rate, its invoices may be invalidated, and a special tax adjustment notice may be issued for up to 10 years.

Director personal liability: If the WFOE's assets are insufficient and the sole shareholder or directors admit the shell arrangement, personal liability can be imposed under Company Law veil-piercing provisions. Directors may be barred from serving as directors, restricted from high-consumption travel and overseas exits, and, if the shell arrangement was used to obtain tax refunds or defraud aviation clients, criminal liability for fraud or tax evasion can lead to imprisonment.
Unlawful Profit Repatriation, Withholding Tax Failure and AML Exposure

Statutory sanction: Tax Collection Administration Law Article 69: the withholding agent that fails to withhold tax must pay a penalty of 50% to 300% of the un-withheld tax and remains liable for the tax if the non-resident cannot be collected. Under Foreign Exchange Administration Regulations, unauthorized cross-border capital flows and misrepresentation can be penalized with civil fines, and criminal money laundering charges under Criminal Law Article 191 may apply if profits are transferred via shell accounts to conceal their unlawful or untaxed origin.

Director personal liability: Directors who order or participate in unlawful overseas transfers may be personally ordered to repatriate the funds and pay restitution to the tax authority; the legal representative can be prevented from leaving China until the tax defect is cured under Tax Collection Law Article 44. If the scheme is criminal, the responsible directors face up to 10 years' imprisonment for money laundering and asset forfeiture of personally controlled accounts. Tax blacklisting imposes travel restrictions on outbound flights and high-speed rail within China.
