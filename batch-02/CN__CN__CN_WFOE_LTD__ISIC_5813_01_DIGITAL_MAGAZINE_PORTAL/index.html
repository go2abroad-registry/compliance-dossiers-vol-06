Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum registered capital and no blocked escrow account requirement apply to this WFOE limited liability company formed for digital magazine publishing and sponsored media placements. Under Article 47 of the Company Law (2023 Revision), the registered capital is set in the Articles of Association and must be fully paid within five years from establishment unless a shorter period is prescribed by an applicable licensing authority. Paid-in capital may be contributed after the basic bank account is opened; it is not held in a court- or government-blocked escrow account. The Company must accurately disclose paid-in capital in the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System and must ensure that shareholders do not unlawfully withdraw capital under Article 53.

Funding & unlock path: Not Applicable for manufacturing or warehousing fire permits, civil defense certificates or structural load inspections; however, the general office space must satisfy ordinary Fire Law requirements, the lessor's occupancy safety requirements, and basic workplace safety and emergency evacuation protocols under the Work Safety Law of the People's Republic of China.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA) through its provincial and municipal Offices of the State Taxation Administration; online access is via the nation-wide integrated 'Electronic Taxpayer Service Station' (Electronic Tax Bureau) operated by the STA.

E-government filing portal

After the WFOE is incorporated with the State Administration for Market Regulation, the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) automatically constitutes the statutory Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) and VAT registration number. Taxpayer registration must be activated within 30 days of issuance of the business license through the STA Electronic Tax Bureau: the WFOE must bind the legal representative, financial officer and accountant, file the authorized bank account details, elect the taxpayer status (general VAT taxpayer or small-scale VAT taxpayer), confirm the tax filing periods and accounting standards, and complete the VAT, Enterprise Income Tax and Individual Income Tax registration profiles. The WFOE, being a PRC resident legal entity, does not undergo a separate foreign non-resident registration; the foreign parent entity is not required to obtain a Chinese TIN solely for ownership of the WFOE. If a foreign non-resident entity has China-source income without a fixed place of business, the local STA will issue a non-resident TIN to the withholding agent before the first payment, but that is not applicable to the WFOE's own tax ID.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — the WFOE is a PRC resident enterprise under the Enterprise Income Tax Law and is taxed at the entity level on its worldwide income; it is not a pass-through entity.

Available election forms

None. The PRC does not provide 'check-the-box' entity classification elections for limited liability companies. A WFOE is mandatorily a resident enterprise. The hybrid mismatch and double-resident concerns are addressed through General Anti-Avoidance Rules (GAAR), the tax residence certificate procedure for treaty benefits, and the PRC special tax adjustment provisions. A small-scale VAT taxpayer may elect to become a general VAT taxpayer if its annual taxable sales are below the statutory threshold and its accounting system is adequate, but this is not an entity classification election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 of annual taxable sales. All newly incorporated WFOEs must complete tax registration within 30 days regardless of turnover. Annual taxable sales exceeding RMB 5,000,000 trigger mandatory registration as a general VAT taxpayer; below that threshold the entity may remain a small-scale VAT taxpayer, but registration itself is mandatory from commencement of business.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For physical exports, a 0% VAT rate applies if the goods are duly declared to customs, physically leave Mainland China, are sold on arm's-length terms to an overseas buyer, and the export proceeds are repatriated to China. For qualifying cross-border digital services, the WFOE may apply the zero-rating or VAT exemption only if the service is supplied to a non-resident enterprise or individual, the service is wholly consumed outside Mainland China, no establishment or permanent place of the foreign recipient within China is involved, the consideration is paid from outside China in foreign currency, and the cross-border taxable activity zero-rating/exemption record has been filed through the Electronic Tax Bureau. For the digital magazine portal, overseas digital subscriptions and sponsored media placements can qualify only when the economic use and consumption of the content or advertisement occurs entirely outside China; domestic digital magazine sales and domestic advertising placements remain taxable at the applicable VAT rate.

  • Customs Export Goods Declaration issued by the General Administration of Customs (for physical export of printed magazines, promotional goods, or other tangible merchandise)
  • Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, or multimodal transport document evidencing shipment from Chinese customs territory
  • Commercial export invoice and packing list
  • Export sales contract signed with the overseas buyer
  • Bank foreign exchange inward remittance receipt / Foreign Exchange Income Declaration Form evidencing export proceeds repatriation
  • Zero-rated VAT export invoice generated through the STA e-invoice platform
  • For pure digital exports: cross-border digital service contract, overseas recipient self-declaration of foreign consumption, payment collection evidence from abroad, and the STA cross-border taxable activity zero-rating/exemption filing record

All VAT invoices must be issued through the STA VAT invoice management system under the Golden Tax system. Anti-counterfeiting tax control digital stamping, unique invoice codes, QR codes and real-time transmission to the STA are mandatory. Fully digital electronic invoices are required for qualifying taxpayers by the national e-invoice pilot; high-volume issuers may integrate their ERP systems with the STA e-invoice platform through the approved API and tax UKey methods. VAT special invoices are required for a general taxpayer to claim input VAT credit; each special invoice must be logged and certified in the STA invoice confirmation platform. No private-sector or third-party fiscal clearance stamp is used; the STA's e-invoice platform performs central real-time clearance and data validation.

The STA and the General Administration of Customs jointly review export zero-rating claims. If the required customs, logistics, contract, and foreign exchange repatriation evidence is missing, false, or not completed within the 180-day repatriation deadline, the zero-rated output VAT is reassessed as domestic taxable sales, any refunded input VAT is clawed back, and late-payment interest of 0.05% per day on the underpaid tax is imposed. Fraudulent claims are treated as tax evasion or export VAT refund fraud: administrative penalties range from 50% to five times the evaded tax, suspension of export VAT refund treatment, and confiscation of the refunded amount. Criminal liability for fraudulent export refunds can arise under Article 204 of the Criminal Law of the PRC, with penalties up to imprisonment and criminal fines. The recovery period is generally three years for computational errors, five years for gross negligence or non-disclosure, and no time limit for fraudulent or evasive acts.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a managed float with dual-tier current and capital account convertibility. Current account transactions are fully or largely convertible under SAFE verification: digital magazine subscription income, advertising receipts from overseas ad networks, and payments for content licensing can be collected with no quantitative conversion cap once the underlying trade is genuine and documented. Capital account items, including FDI equity injections, remain registered and controlled: the WFOE must complete foreign exchange registration at the receiving bank before any cross-border capital inflow, and each RMB conversion and payment out of the capital account must comply with the negative-list and operational-scope review. Profit remittances to the foreign parent are lawful after annual statutory audit, enterprise income tax settlement, and tax certificate issuance, but the bank will verify that the prior-year registered capital has been fully paid and the business is not in a restricted sector. No blanket surrender requirement exists today, but export/service receipts generally must be repatriated to a designated domestic foreign exchange account and matched with the SAFE monitoring system before conversion. Treasury planning must therefore separate current-account service receipts from capital-account equity flows and maintain a full audit trail for every cross-border credit and debit.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For goods exports, export proceeds must be repatriated within the SAFE-prescribed trade-receipt period, typically 180 days from the export customs declaration date, by placing the foreign currency into a domestic designated foreign exchange settlement account under the WFOE's export verification record. There is no statutory percentage of export receipts that must be force-converted into RMB; the exporter may retain 100% in foreign exchange or convert voluntarily based on operational needs. For service-trade export revenue of the digital magazine portal, including ad-network payouts and online subscription collections, the WFOE must receive the funds through a designated bank under the matching service contract and the bank will submit current account data to SAFE; again, no mandatory conversion ratio applies. Timely reporting and authenticity documents are the binding statutory requirements.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE), operating under the People's Bank of China (PBoC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low to near-zero viability as a primary corporate banking rail. The PBoC licensing framework does not authorize foreign electronic money institutions such as Airwallex, Payoneer, or Currencycloud to open domestic RMB settlement accounts, receive FDI capital injections, or clear SAFE-regulated transactions on behalf of a mainland WFOE. They may only function as an ancillary global collection node for offshore PSP revenue; the resulting funds must still be swept by SWIFT into the onshore Tier-1 bank account under the WFOE's own name, with each sweep reported under the applicable SAFE current-account purpose code. Legal responsibility for tax, foreign-exchange reporting, AML declarations, and KYC remains entirely with the WFOE and its directors. Therefore, the treasury architecture must not be designed around an EMI as a bank substitute.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound FDI capital injection must flow from the direct foreign parent to the WFOE's SAFE-registered Foreign Exchange Capital Account at its designated Tier-1 bank. Before remittance, the receiving bank completes the FDI foreign-exchange registration under SAFE delegated authority. The parent then sends a SWIFT MT103 instruction with the WFOE as the sole beneficiary and the remittance purpose stated as equity investment into the WFOE, matching the SAFE capital-account transaction purpose code selected by the receiving bank. The equity funds must be segregated from service-trade receivables and other operational cash flows. After funds arrive, the bank either retains the foreign currency or converts it under the FDI capital-settlement system, and a registered Chinese CPA issues a capital-verification certificate after confirming receipt and booking equity. All converted RMB payments must remain within the WFOE's approved business scope and negative-list compliance framework, with ongoing reporting to SAFE.Outbound settlements for content licensing, software-as-a-service, design, advertising-tech costs, and overseas vendor fees are processed under current-account service-trade rules. The preferred clearing rail is a SWIFT MT103 transfer through the relationship bank, supported by the signed contract, commercial invoice, tax payment certificates, and, where required, the service-trade registration declaration. For physical printed magazine distribution or any goods-related media logistics, the bank may structure a Commercial Letter of Credit at sight or usance, or a Documentary Collection under D/P or D/A terms, with the full transport package including commercial invoice, bill of lading, packing list, insurance certificate, and customs declaration. Every outbound disbursement must be reconciled with the original underlying transaction evidence to avoid SAFE penalties and bank compliance escalation.Onshore deliverable RMB foreign-exchange forward contracts (USD/CNY, EUR/CNY) booked with a licensed domestic bank on the basis of genuine current or capital account exposure; speculative uncovered positions are prohibited.Cross-currency interest rate swaps are permissible only where the WFOE has a matched foreign-currency liability or long-term capital commitment, the counterparty bank holds a valid derivatives licence, and SAFE derivatives-reporting rules are observed.Currency options and FX swaps are acceptable for hedging the digital magazine's actual subscription and advertising income volatility when the counterparty bank is a SAFE-licensed market maker and the notional amount is within the declared underlying exposure.Offshore non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are not legal onshore for a mainland WFOE; the foreign parent may use NDF or CNH forwards outside China for group-level hedging, but no domestic RMB derivative or SAFE-regulated flow may be created to circumvent current-account or capital-account controls.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low to near-zero viability as a primary corporate banking rail. The PBoC licensing framework does not authorize foreign electronic money institutions such as Airwallex, Payoneer, or Currencycloud to open domestic RMB settlement accounts, receive FDI capital injections, or clear SAFE-regulated transactions on behalf of a mainland WFOE. They may only function as an ancillary global collection node for offshore PSP revenue; the resulting funds must still be swept by SWIFT into the onshore Tier-1 bank account under the WFOE's own name, with each sweep reported under the applicable SAFE current-account purpose code. Legal responsibility for tax, foreign-exchange reporting, AML declarations, and KYC remains entirely with the WFOE and its directors. Therefore, the treasury architecture must not be designed around an EMI as a bank substitute.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Under SAT Public Notice [2016] No. 42, the WFOE must prepare a Local File in a jurisdiction-compliant Chinese and English version where annual related-party tangible asset transfers exceed RMB 200 million, intangible-asset or financial-asset transfers exceed RMB 100 million, or other related-party transactions exceed RMB 40 million; the Master File is required once group-wide related-party transactions exceed RMB 1 billion, and Country-by-Country Reporting is separately due for multinational groups meeting the revenue threshold.

Economic substance scope: China applies economic substance review through the general anti-avoidance rule, beneficial ownership rules, and the substance-over-form doctrine rather than a separate Economic Substance Regulation. The WFOE must perform core income-generating activities in China: editorial production, advertising inventory management, content moderation, user subscription collection, data hosting and risk management; employees, office premises, budgets and decisions must be commensurate with the digital publishing activities. A WFOE that is a pure flow-through mailbox entity may be denied treaty benefits, have its profits recharacterised as non-resident income, and face deemed dividend withholding.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Transfer Pricing Manipulation and Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: The tax authority may invoke Enterprise Income Tax Law Article 47 and Tax Collection Law Article 63 to re-determine taxable income, deny deductions, impose a penalty of 50% to 500% of the unpaid tax, add the 0.05% daily late-payment surcharge, and refer the case for criminal prosecution under Criminal Law Article 201 if the unpaid tax exceeds RMB 50,000 and exceeds 10% of the tax due.

Director personal liability: Directors who signed or directed the false filing may be personally liable to restore diverted corporate assets under the corporate veil-piercing doctrine of Company Law Article 20; they may be placed on the tax blacklist with travel-ban restrictions and exit-control measures through the tax authority and immigration courts, and criminal courts may sentence the responsible directors to imprisonment of up to seven years for aggravated tax evasion, with fines and confiscation of diverted assets.
Shell Company and Treaty Abuse

Statutory sanction: The tax authorities may deny the DTA claim, deem the WFOE to lack reasonableness, apply the enterprise income tax general anti-avoidance adjustment under EIT Law Article 47, reclassify the revenue as paid to a non-resident, impose 10% withholding tax on the deemed dividend or service payment, and add interest and penalty of up to 500% of the evaded tax under Tax Collection Law Article 63. The entity may also be administratively deregistered or listed as a non-operational shell in the credit supervision system.

Director personal liability: Once the shell is disregarded, creditors and tax authorities may pierce the corporate veil and hold directors jointly liable for the WFOE's unpaid tax and restitution; directors will be blacklisted and their exit from China blocked until the tax debt is settled or security is provided. If the shell-misuse scheme is fraudulent, directors also face criminal fraud or tax evasion liability with imprisonment, asset freezing, and disqualification from serving as directors in any PRC company.
Failure to Withhold on Outbound Royalties and Interest

Statutory sanction: Under Tax Collection Law Article 69, the WFOE as withholding agent is liable for a fine of 50% to 300% of the amount that should have been withheld; the tax authority may also recover the unpaid tax from the WFOE and disallow the related expense deduction, plus late-payment surcharges. If false invoices or fraudulent accounting records are used, Article 63 penalties and criminal referral under Article 201 apply.

Director personal liability: Directors and the finance officer who knowingly directed or permitted the non-withholding are personally exposed to civil recovery of the withheld tax if the WFOE cannot pay; they will be listed on the serious-tax-violation blacklist with travel-ban consequences, and in aggravated cases criminal liability attaches for tax evasion or fraud, resulting in imprisonment, fines, personal asset execution, and prohibition from re-entering mainland China until the liabilities are discharged.
