None. Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA); online administration through the China Electronic Tax Bureau / Electronic Taxpayer Service Platform, integrated with the nationwide Golden Tax System (Phase III and IV).

E-government filing portal

A wholly foreign-owned enterprise (WFOE) is first registered with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR). The SAMR business license carries the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which the STA recognizes as the taxpayer identification number for corporate income tax, VAT and all other Chinese taxes. Within 30 days of license issuance the WFOE must complete integrated tax registration through the Electronic Tax Bureau; the portal automatically pulls the USCC and establishes the VAT taxpayer profile, withholding registration and periodic filing obligations. For a foreign non-resident enterprise without a Chinese permanent establishment and with China-source income, a China Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) is obtained by applying directly to the competent STA office or through the withholding agent in China; this is used for withholding and on-line filing. The WFOE is taxed at entity level, and foreign shareholders do not receive a Chinese TIN solely by reason of their equity ownership.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation (a WFOE limited liability company is a Chinese resident enterprise subject to corporate income tax on worldwide income at the statutory 25% rate; no pass-through or partnership treatment is permitted)

Available election forms

None. The People's Republic of China does not provide check-the-box or US-style entity classification elections; a WFOE limited liability company is mandatorily taxed at entity level under the Corporate Income Tax Law. No hybrid mismatch election is available, although the STA may apply general anti-avoidance rules to hybrid arrangements.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Every WFOE must complete tax registration upon business license issuance regardless of turnover. For VAT general taxpayer classification, mandatory registration arises when annual VAT taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000; below this threshold the entity remains a small-scale VAT taxpayer unless it voluntarily elects general taxpayer status with satisfactory accounting records. The small-scale taxpayer monthly VAT exemption of RMB 100,000 does not remove the obligation to register or file VAT returns.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A VAT zero rate may be claimed where the WFOE exports physical goods through a customs clearance, or supplies qualifying cross-border services consumed wholly outside China, including software services and specified value-added telecommunications/digital services provided to overseas businesses or individuals. The WFOE must demonstrate through contract terms, place-of-supply rules, billing address, IP logs and bank settlement evidence that the recipient and consumption are outside the customs territory of China. For app-store MMORPG revenue, zero-rating requires platform settlement statements showing Apple/Google payouts funded by non-China end users and evidence that the digital game supply is not consumed in China; failure to substantiate the foreign consumption chain makes the revenue taxable at the applicable VAT rate.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bill of Entry) with a valid export customs clearance number
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export (where goods are exported)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (IRC) or a bank-issued foreign exchange collection record demonstrating repatriation of export proceeds to the WFOE's Chinese bank account, with cross-border RMB settlement accepted if SAFE-approved
  • Export sales contract or binding purchase order
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Apple/Google platform settlement statements identifying overseas end-user transactions for digital MMORPG supplies
  • Zero-rated VAT invoice and the electronic invoice serial number reported to the STA

VAT invoices must be issued through the STA Golden Tax System using the Electronic Tax Bureau. Under the nationwide full-digital electronic invoice (FDEI) reform, VAT special invoices and ordinary invoices are generated electronically with a unique tax-invoice code, digital signature and electronic stamp; invoice data is uploaded to the STA in near-real-time before VAT returns can be filed. Special VAT invoices used for B2B input credit require the buyer tax identity and are authenticated and matched in the electronic invoicing and clearance module. Anti-fraud controls include mandatory red-letter invoice reversal, automatic risk monitoring, upload validation, and prohibition of manual paper-only issuance except for statutory exceptions.

Zero-rated exports are subject to documentary and monetary scrutiny by the STA and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange. If an export claim is unsubstantiated or foreign exchange is not repatriated within the 180-day statutory deadline without a valid approved extension, the VAT zero rate is retrospectively reversed and output VAT is assessed at the applicable rate (13% for goods or 6% for services), together with daily late-payment interest of 0.05% on the outstanding tax. Fraudulent export refund claims are penalized under the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection by recovery of refunded tax, administrative fines of one to five times the tax involved, suspension or revocation of export tax refund qualification and VAT special invoice issuance rights, and criminal liability under the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, including imprisonment and asset confiscation.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China maintains a managed floating RMB regime with asymmetrical convertibility: current account transactions are generally convertible onshore after authenticity verification by a designated bank, while capital account transactions require separate SAFE registration and case-by-case approval. This WFOE's FDI capital contribution must first register with SAFE via the receiving bank and can only be settled into RMB on a restricted payment-use basis. RMB is not a fully floating hard currency; offshore convertibility is limited, so the WFOE cannot treat its capital or operating pools as freely fungible across borders. Export proceeds from global gaming platforms are classified as service trade receipts: they must be repatriated to domestic designated FX settlement accounts and processed under the service-trade verification system. Monthly Apple and Google Play payouts constitute receivable events and carry the same documentary burden as any commercial invoice. Profit remittances abroad require the annual statutory audit, tax settlement certification, and a board resolution, and are subject to applicable PRC profit-remittance rules. A no-surrender policy on export proceeds means FX can be retained in the account, but the bank's compliance committee will scrutinise each remittance against the declaration data, the technical SLAs, the app-store settlement certificate and the local tax filing status. Banks also enforce the monitoring of substantial use rule under the PBOC on cross-border capital flows, so a realistic treasury blueprint must treat the WFOE's capital account, current-account operational inflows, and monthly intercompany SDK charges as separate verified cycles.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export proceeds are not subject to mandatory conversion; however, the WFOE must repatriate the full amount of its Apple/Google service-trade receipts to a domestic designated FX bank within the contractual invoice/payout window, which for online software publishing generally aligns with the platform settlement cycles. SAFE requires the underlying collection to be verified against the submitted digital transaction reports; goods exports are subject to a 90-day repatriation timeline, and service exports must be completed within the agreed contract terms of the online store agreement. The entity may retain up to 100% of its export receipts in foreign currency, or convert 100% at the spot/forward rate, with no statutory minimum conversion ratio.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

LOW - STRUCTURALLY UNVIABLE. Offshore fintech EMIs such as Revolut, Airwallex Business, or Wise are not licensed by the PBOC or SAFE to provide onshore RMB clearing, maintain domestic Chinese bank accounts, or act as the WFOE's authorised foreign exchange settlement bank. The China-specific KYC/account opening framework requires every corporate account to be opened with a licensed bank branch inside the country. Although an EMI may be useful for the WFOE's offshore subsidiaries or for receiving Apple/Google payouts into a Hong Kong/USD corporate account before repatriation, the onshore WFOE cannot legally use such EMIs as its primary clearing rail or treasury platform. Doing so would violate the capital-flow monitoring rules, expose the entity to tax non-compliance, and trigger automatic AML escalation. Therefore, the treasury blueprint relies exclusively on Tier-1 domestic banks for the WFOE's operational liquidity, and EMIs are limited to occasional expense handling for overseas trade shows or contractor payments in jurisdictions where the EMI is duly licensed.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File / contemporaneous documentation is mandatory in China when a specific related-party transaction category exceeds: CNY 200 million for tangible asset transfer, CNY 100 million for intangible asset transfer or financial asset transfer, and CNY 40 million for other transactions (services, commissions, expense sharing). Master File preparation is required when the MNE group's aggregate related-party transactions exceed CNY 1 billion (or where the group crosses the CNY 5 billion annual revenue threshold for Country-by-Country Reporting), with electronic CbCR filing and notification mandated separately.

Economic substance scope: China applies an economic-substance and substance-over-form doctrine in the context of treaty benefits, anti-avoidance (EIT Law Art. 47), and transfer-pricing analysis. The WFOE must have real employees, physical office, hardware/software infrastructure, decision-making authority over the Chinese MMORPG operation, and management of material risks and assets. The Core Income-Generating Activities (CIGA) of game publishing, user acquisition, localisation, community management, payment processing integration and data localisation should be performed in China; if those functions are merely contracted to an offshore parent or a mailbox Hong Kong affiliate, the Chinese tax authority can deny treaty relief, recharacterize the arrangement as tax avoidance, and impose adjustments, penalties, and criminal referral.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Revenue Reporting

Statutory sanction: PRC Criminal Law Article 201: tax evasion exceeding RMB 50,000 and more than 10% of the tax payable carries fines of 1 to 5 times evaded tax and up to 7 years imprisonment for significant evasion. TCAL Article 63 imposes a civil penalty of 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax; late-payment interest at 0.05% daily applies to the underpayment.

Director personal liability: Where the corporate veil is pierced (PRC Company Law Art. 20), directors, CFOs and actual controllers can be held personally liable for the company's tax debts; personal bank accounts, shares, and assets may be frozen/seizured. Courts may order direct criminal imprisonment for the responsible individuals, impose exit bans/blacklisting under the Serious Tax Violation List, and confiscate personal gains derived from the fraudulent scheme.
Cross-Border Withholding Tax and DTA Abuse

Statutory sanction: PRC Enterprise Income Tax Law Article 37 mandates withholding; TCAL Article 69 punishes a withholding agent's failure to withhold or remit with a penalty of 50% to 300% of the unwithheld/unremitted tax. Using forged tax documents triggers Criminal Law Article 205 (forgery/counterfeiting of tax instruments) with imprisonment up to 10 years. The General Anti-Avoidance Rule (EIT Law Art. 47) allows recharacterization of the abusive structure and adjustment of taxable income.

Director personal liability: The director may be personally liable as the withholding agent and for unjust enrichment; personal assets can be seized to satisfy unwithheld tax and penalties. Criminal liability for forgery and tax evasion can attach to individuals who designed or executed the conduit arrangement. The responsible individuals face removal from the country, travel bans, exit restriction under the Exit-Entry Administration Law, disqualification from serving as directors or senior managers, and joint-and-several civil liability to creditors and the tax bureau.
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance, Thin Capitalisation and Sham Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: EIT Law Articles 41-48 and 121 permit the tax authority to re-determine profits at arm's length; interest runs on the transferred net profit; failure to file documentation can result in penalties under SAT Public Notice [2016] No. 42 and TCAL Art. 63 if evasion is found (50%-500% penalty). Under the Administrative Measures for Special Tax Adjustments, non-documentation may result in a specific documentation penalty up to RMB 10,000, but the most dangerous consequence is mandatory anti-avoidance audit and criminal referral.

Director personal liability: Where the Chinese company is exposed as a shell or sham without independent management and substance, the company law veil-piercing doctrine allows the court to impose personal liability on directors for unpaid corporate taxes and restitution; the directors' own salaries, dividends, and transferred assets are clawed back. In serious cases involving a deliberate scheme to evade tax via transfer-pricing manipulation, the individuals risk incarceration for tax evasion (Criminal Law Art. 201), travel blacklist status, asset freezes, and bar from future company formation in China.
