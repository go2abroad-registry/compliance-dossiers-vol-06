Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum registered capital or minimum paid-up capital is imposed for an ordinary wholly foreign-owned enterprise engaged in digital media production and online distribution. Pursuant to Article 47 of the PRC Company Law (2023 Revision), shareholders must make full payment of the subscribed registered capital within five (5) years after establishment, unless a shorter period is specified in the Articles of Association. No blocked escrow account is required by SAMR; however, capital contributions must be remitted through a dedicated bank capital contribution account and foreign exchange receipt and settlement must comply with SAFE regulations on foreign direct investment, including lawful use of settled funds for the company's business operations.

Funding & unlock path: Not Applicable - no civil defense fire permit or industrial fire safety license is required for a digital media company with no physical manufacturing facility. If a physical office is leased, the lessor must ensure the premises have passed fire safety inspection or fire protection acceptance as required by the PRC Fire Protection Law, and the company must comply with workplace safety registration and routine occupational safety obligations under the PRC Work Safety Law for employees.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) and the in-charge local tax authority of the People's Republic of China; online electronic portal is the STA Electronic Tax Bureau.

E-government filing portal

For a foreign investor establishing a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE), the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) issues an 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) upon business registration, and tax registration is automatically completed under the integrated multi-certificate reform; the USCC functions as the statutory TIN and VAT registration number. Within 30 days after formation, the WFOE must complete supplementary tax registration with the in-charge tax authority through the Electronic Tax Bureau, file bank account details, legal representative and tax accounting information, and apply for digital electronic invoice quota and issuance eligibility. A foreign non-resident enterprise without a permanent establishment in China must obtain a PRC TIN by filing non-resident tax registration with the in-charge tax authority or through its withholding agent, supported by foreign incorporation documents, board resolutions, identity documents, and power of attorney. The WFOE must also complete customs registration for import/export, export tax rebate filing if applicable, and SAFE foreign-exchange registration through a designated bank.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation; the WFOE is a PRC resident enterprise taxed under the Enterprise Income Tax Law on worldwide income at the statutory 25% rate, with no pass-through or fiscal-transparency eligibility.

Available election forms

None; PRC law does not provide check-the-box or hybrid mismatch elections. The only relevant statutory elections are voluntary general VAT taxpayer registration below the RMB 5,000,000 annual turnover threshold, simplified VAT levy treatment for eligible small-scale taxpayers, and Enterprise Income Tax preferential elections such as High and New Technology Enterprise status, small low-profit enterprise reduced rates, and accelerated depreciation where statutory conditions are met.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 annual taxable sales for mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration; below this threshold small-scale taxpayer simplified treatment applies. The current small-scale taxpayer exemption is RMB 100,000 monthly or RMB 300,000 quarterly taxable sales. There is no separate registration threshold for a newly formed WFOE; tax and VAT registration is mandatory upon incorporation.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Physical goods exported from mainland China qualify for 0% VAT when they are actually declared to China Customs, physically leave China, and the exporter collects and repatriates the foreign sales proceeds within statutory time limits. Cross-border supply of vertical micro-drama production and distribution services qualifies for the 0% VAT rate under Caishui [2016] No. 36 Annex 4 if the customer is an overseas entity or individual, the service is wholly consumed outside mainland China, the payment is received from outside China, and the taxpayer files the statutorily prescribed cross-border service zero-rating and exemption administrative documentation with the STA. The WFOE must separately substantiate platform-based sales through Apple IAP, Google Play Billing, and Stripe with contractual documents, platform settlement reports, and evidence that the digital content or licensing service was conclusively consumed outside mainland China.

  • Customs Export Goods Declaration Form (Export Rebate Special Copy) filed through the China International Trade Single Window
  • Original negotiable Bill of Lading, sea/air waybill, or courier receipt evidencing physical departure from mainland China
  • Export commercial invoice and packing list
  • Export sales contract or purchase order with the overseas buyer
  • Export Proceeds Collection Certificate issued by a PRC designated foreign-exchange bank (functional equivalent of an Inward Remittance Certificate/FIRC)
  • VAT special invoices or procurement invoices supporting the input VAT attributed to the exported goods or services where a refund is claimed
  • Cross-border service agreement, platform settlement reports from Apple/Google/Stripe, and proof that the micro-drama streaming or licensing service was wholly consumed outside mainland China

Fully digitalised electronic invoices (FDEIs) must be issued through the STA Electronic Tax Bureau Digital Electronic Invoice Service Platform under the Golden Tax IV System. Issuance is transmitted and cleared in real time via the national invoice control system and API-based integration with approved issuer systems. Each FDEI carries a centrally generated unique 20-digit invoice number, tax QR code, and anti-fraud digital tax-control seal. Red-letter reversal invoices, invoice quota changes, and tax digital account reconciliations must be processed through the platform before any zero-rated export refund claim is filed.

If export zero-rating is claimed without valid customs declarations, foreign-exchange repatriation evidence, or offshore-consumption substantiation, the tax authority will deny or claw back the zero-rated treatment, re-levy VAT at the applicable rate, recover any input VAT refunded, impose late-payment surcharges of 0.05% per day, and impose fines ranging from 50% to five times the underpaid or fraudulently refunded tax under Articles 63 and 66 of the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection. Criminal liability for fraudulent export tax rebates may arise under Article 204 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, and the entity may be published on the tax authorities' serious tax violation credit blacklist.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China maintains de jure current account convertibility under IMF Article VIII, but the capital account remains tightly administered. SAFE and the PBOC exercise item-by-item vetting of every inward and outward capital flow. Export and service receipt proceeds are freely retained or converted on a genuine-trade basis, yet each conversion requires bank verification of underlying trade documents. Capital contributions, shareholder loans, cross-border guarantees and dividend repatriation require prior foreign exchange registration, a designated capital account, and purpose-specific use checks. In practice, the WFOE can freely pay operating expenses such as wages, rent and production services with its converted RMB, but cannot convert surplus RMB into foreign currency without a documented real need. Profit remittance to the offshore shareholder demands an audited statutory annual report, a cleared annual corporate income tax return, a tax clearance letter, a board resolution, and proof that the declared profit corresponds to accumulated after-tax retained earnings. The bank then verifies the distribution is not secretly a disguised investment or remittance of illegal funds. Thus, while current trade flows are functionally convertible, capital flows remain heavily regulated and can be stalled by missing compliance documentation.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE's Goods Trade Foreign Exchange Inspection and Administration measures, export proceeds must be repatriated onshore within 90 days from the date of customs export declaration. There is no statutory mandatory conversion ratio; the WFOE may keep the proceeds in a domestic foreign exchange settlement account or sell them to the commercial bank on a voluntary basis, provided the transaction has a genuine trade background and is reported through the bank's digital cross-border payment platform. Any foreign-exchange income that stays outside China beyond the 90-day limit requires express SAFE approval under the centralised operation and offshore account pilot policies. Banks apply a dual verification against export customs data and the enterprise's trade credit ratings, and suspend collection if mismatches or delayed surrenders occur.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the People's Bank of China (PBOC) and the State Council

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Offshore digital EMIs such as Wise, Payoneer and Revolut have no licence to operate as banks or payment institutions in mainland China, and they are legally barred from opening local clearing accounts, issuing omnibus accounts or settling RMB transactions for a PRC-domiciled WFOE. Therefore, any attempt to use such an EMI for onshore treasury, payroll, supplier payments or VAT settlement is non-compliant. A WFOE may, however, use an overseas holding or foreign subsidiary to collect App Store, Google Play and Stripe revenues abroad, and that offshore entity may lawfully bank with an EMI if the EMI holds the appropriate licence in its jurisdiction. The onshore WFOE would then receive the funds as foreign exchange inflows under an FDI or services-trade payment, with all necessary SAFE registration and supporting contracts. Viability rating for the onshore entity is Low; for the offshore collection vehicle it is conditional and Medium only with proper licensing and transfer-pricing implementation.

Unclear source of funds and suspected round-tripping or illegal transfer pricing

Root cause: Unclear source of funds and suspected round-tripping or illegal transfer pricing

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital for the WFOE will be routed by SWIFT MT103/202 from the foreign parent. The payment must cite the correct SAFE purpose code for FDI capital contribution (e.g., 6210) and the beneficiary bank account authorised as a foreign exchange capital account. The bank will match the remittance to the registered FDI quota, issue a receipt voucher, and after the capital verification report by a PRC CPA, the enterprise can settle into RMB under the 'payment for actual use' mechanism. Each settlement requires proof of use such as employment contracts, payroll records, lease invoices, procurement contracts or tax vouchers; retained foreign currency may remain in the FX capital account. SAFE monitors fund flows through the 'FDI Compliance and Verification System', and any deviation from the business scope must be instructed in writing before execution. For emergency liquidity, the shareholder can also inject via a temporary current-account inbound remittance, but that will be classified as trade and services proceeds and must strictly follow current-account verification.Outbound treasury disbursements are cleared on three primary rails. First, SWIFT MT103 messages are used for direct payments of royalties, production advances, tax remittances and intercompany service fees, with each instruction supported by the corresponding contract and invoice and, where threshold applies, the tax-filing certificate. Second, commercial letters of credit (documentary credits) are recommended for milestone-based distribution or licensing deals where the beneficiary requires a bank's undertaking; the WFOE will open a sight or usance LC through its Tier-1 bank with documents such as commercial invoices, bills of lading or delivery certificates, and certificates of content acceptance. Third, documentary collections (D/P or D/A) apply for transactions between the WFOE and trusted counterparties where no bank payment undertaking is needed, with documents routed through the remitting bank. Every outward payment in foreign currency must be reported under China's International Income and Payments statistics (BOP) and must clear the bank's 'authenticity of trade background' check; any abnormal crossing, prepayment above contract value, or settlement to an unlisted party will be reported to SAFE as a suspicious transaction.Forward contracts for foreign exchange settlement and sale under genuine trade backgrounds, matching the maturity and amount of underlying receivables/payablesCurrency swap transactions between RMB and USD/other major currencies for duration/interest conversion on existing debt or cash-flow positionsCombinations of ordinary European-style call and put options (collar, straddle and risk reversal structures) traded in the interbank market via licensed banksNon-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are not permissible as onshore hedging instruments; both the WFOE and its bank are prohibited from executing NDFs inside mainland China, and any forward-contract mismatch to a purely speculative exposure would constitute an illegal derivative deal

Mandatory Audit Threshold Small Company Audit Exemption Regime

Turnover: Under the PRC Company Law, every limited liability company, including a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise, must prepare annual financial reports at each fiscal year-end and have the reports audited by an independent accounting firm. The obligation is universal; there is no quantitative balance-sheet, turnover, or headcount exemption, and the WFOE remains audit-mandated even if it qualifies as a small low-profit enterprise. The regulatory threshold is therefore zero turnover and zero assets: any registered WFOE, irrespective of size, is legally required to obtain a certified CPA audit. Simplified small-enterprise accounting presentation may be permitted, but it does not remove the independent audit duty.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% on the gross amount of dividends distributed by the WFOE to a non-resident enterprise shareholder, withheld at the time of distribution under Article 37 and Article 3 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law; dividends distributed to Chinese tax-resident corporate shareholders are exempt, but distributions to foreign individuals are not covered by this corporate WHT regime and may be subject to Individual Income Tax.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: A Local File is required when annual related-party transactions exceed the following aggregate thresholds: RMB 200 million for tangible asset transfers; RMB 100 million for financial asset transfers; RMB 40 million for intangible asset transfers, including copyrights and software; and RMB 40 million for related-party services, including management and marketing services. A Master File is required when the WFOE is a member of an MNE group whose previous-year consolidated revenue exceeded RMB 2 billion and whose related-party transactions include cross-border transactions. The Annual Related Party Transaction Report must be submitted together with the annual CIT return regardless of these thresholds whenever related-party transactions occur.

Economic substance scope: China does not apply the offshore Economic Substance Regulations model as used in British Crown Dependencies, but Chinese tax authorities rigorously apply the substance-over-form doctrine, the beneficial-ownership doctrine, general anti-avoidance rules under Article 47 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, and special tax adjustment rules to deny treaty benefits and recharacterise arrangements where the WFOE lacks people, premises, functions and risks. Core Income-Generating Activities for a vertical micro-drama WFOE include content acquisition and commissioning, production management, platform contract negotiation, user acquisition and monetisation strategy, royalty contracting, treasury management, and strategic risk assumption; these functions must be performed and directed in mainland China. A letterbox WFOE with no economic substance that merely holds legal title and remits profits will be treated as a taxable entity still liable to CIT on domestic-source income, but with deductions denied to the extent related-party payments lack commercial rationality.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion through aggressive transfer pricing and royalty stripping

Statutory sanction: Chinese tax authorities may make a special tax adjustment under Articles 41, 46 and 47 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, recasting the excessive royalty and service fees as dividends or taxable income; the WFOE must pay the additional CIT plus interest at the relevant benchmark lending rate and a penalty of 50% to 5 times the underpaid tax under Article 63 of the Tax Collection Administration Law. Failure to keep and provide transfer pricing documentation also attracts fixed penalties and may shift the burden of proof against the taxpayer.

Director personal liability: If the WFOE is found to be a sham used to evade tax, courts may pierce the corporate veil under Chinese Company Law where the shareholder or directors abuse the company's independent legal personality and limited liability to evade debts; the responsible shareholders and directors become jointly and severally liable for the unpaid corporate tax, interest and penalties. Directors who knowingly signed false related-party agreements and manipulated invoices may be placed under exit control through the Joint Punishment for Tax Dishonesty Memorandum, denied outward travel, restricted on high-consumption, and criminally prosecuted under Criminal Law Article 201, carrying fines and imprisonment up to 7 years for large-scale evasion.
Failure to withhold and remit withholding tax on profit repatriation

Statutory sanction: Under Article 69 of the Tax Collection Administration Law, a withholding agent that fails to withhold or fails to remit withheld tax must pay the unwithheld tax and is subject to a penalty of 50% to 3 times the unwithheld amount. The tax authority may block foreign exchange settlement and bank remittance under its collection powers, add daily 0.05% late-payment surcharges from the due date, and pursue the tax from the withholding agent before the taxpayer where the obligation was not fulfilled.

Director personal liability: Directors and legal representatives of the WFOE can be personally blacklisted for tax debts, with travel bans preventing exit from China until the outstanding WHT is settled. Where the director approved the overseas diversion of corporate funds to avoid withholding, the director may be personally liable for unjust enrichment and breach of fiduciary duty in a creditor suit; if the diversion is accompanied by intentionally false tax returns, criminal tax evasion liability under Criminal Law Article 201 may attach, and the withdrawal of funds into personal accounts also exposes the director to misappropriation or fraud liability requiring asset restitution.
Shell-company structure, sham economic substance and treaty shopping

Statutory sanction: The Chinese tax authority may invoke the General Anti-Avoidance Rule under Article 47 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law and the Beneficial Ownership rules under the treaty benefit regulations, deny the treaty rate, and reassess WHT at the statutory 10% plus interest and penalties. False VAT invoices are punishable under Criminal Law Article 205 with fines and imprisonment ranging from ordinary cases up to 10 years, and life imprisonment for huge amounts; the Tax Collection Administration Law penalty of 50% to 5 times the evaded tax will also apply. The company registration itself may be revoked under foreign investment and company registration rules if the business activities are found to be unlawful.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil may be pierced under Chinese Company Law and Civil Code principles if the shell company was used to commit fraud against tax creditors or commercial counterparties; directors who orchestrated the shell structure will be jointly liable for the unpaid corporate tax, VAT, penalties and third-party claims. In serious cases, the legal representative must pay restitution from personal assets; travel restrictions are mandatory once the entity is blacklisted; and criminal prosecution for issuing false special VAT invoices and evading tax may result in imprisonment of up to 7 years for tax evasion, up to life imprisonment for massive false invoicing, and a permanent entry restriction on public market and government procurement for the responsible individuals.
