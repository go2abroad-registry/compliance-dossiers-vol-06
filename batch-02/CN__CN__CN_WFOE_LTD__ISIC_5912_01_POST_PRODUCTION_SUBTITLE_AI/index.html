Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; all filings, VAT invoice issuance, and zero-rating/refund applications are executed through the nationwide Electronic Tax Bureau (etax.chinatax.gov.cn) and the STA Electronic Invoice Service Platform.

E-government filing portal

The WFOE obtains its Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) from the local Administration for Market Regulation at incorporation. Under the multi-license integration system, the USCC is the only statutory tax identification number; no separate VAT registration certificate is issued. The incorporation record is automatically transmitted to the STA to create the electronic tax file. Within the legally prescribed post-incorporation period, the company must complete its taxpayer classification (small-scale or general VAT taxpayer), register its financial accounting and cost-reporting policies, file its bank deposit account details, and activate fully digital e-invoice issuance capability in the Electronic Tax Bureau. If a foreign non-resident counterparty that has no Chinese establishment is engaged, that counterparty must provide its foreign TIN and, where seeking treaty benefits or performing withholding-related obligations, must separately obtain a Chinese non-resident tax identification number from the competent STA office before the relevant payment or refund claim. For the WFOE itself, no additional TIN application is required; the USCC appearing on the business license is the definitive TIN used for all VAT, customs, EIT, and withholding activities.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For the AI subtitle transcription and voice-cloning studio, 0% VAT is claimable only if the service is correctly characterised and contracted as a cross-border software service, information-system service, R&D/technical service, or another category expressly listed for zero-rating in Cai Shui [2016] No. 36 Annex 4, where the recipient is a non-resident enterprise and the service is consumed entirely outside mainland China. Services that fall under post-production broadcasting, film, television programme production, or cultural-creative services will generally qualify for exemption rather than zero-rating, meaning input VAT cannot be refunded. The WFOE must establish all five elements: foreign recipient, foreign consumption, foreign currency consideration, successful repatriation of proceeds, and delivery or production evidence.

  • For physical goods exported by the WFOE: PRC Customs Export Goods Declaration (Export Bill of Entry), the signed Bill of Lading or airway bill, and the bank's export collection/foreign exchange receipt voucher.
  • For cross-border services: the executed cross-border service contract specifying price, intellectual property ownership, service scope and delivery terms; the issued Digital E-Invoice marked at 0% or exempt; a foreign exchange inward remittance voucher from a PRC-designated bank (foreign exchange collection receipt); proof of the overseas recipient's non-resident status using its foreign tax registration number and registered address; and objective evidence of offshore consumption such as project locus records, IP logs, overseas screen deliverables, or client sign-off.

The STA mandates fully digital electronic invoices (Digital E-Invoices) issued via the Electronic Invoice Service Platform, integrated with the Golden Tax Phase IV system. Every invoice must be transmitted to and cleared by the STA at issuance, and the system generates a unique tax control code and digital stamp for anti-counterfeiting. Traditional tax-control equipment is being phased into this unified platform; where still used, the VAT invoice management system must be reconciled with the VAT return before monthly filing. Real-time data exchange via the STA standard data interface is available for ERP integration and makes invoice, contract, and settlement data available to the audit system.

The STA and Customs jointly apply Golden Tax Phase IV audit analytics to export zero-rating claims. If supporting documentation is missing, inconsistent, or fraudulent, the zero-rated output VAT is reactivated, the corresponding input VAT refund is clawed back, overdue interest is charged, and, under Article 63 of the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection and Tax Levy, an administrative penalty of 50% to 500% of the evaded tax may be imposed. Fraudulent export VAT refund claims are also subject to criminal liability under the Criminal Law's export-rebate fraud provisions; principals and responsible officers may face imprisonment and additional fines.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Mainland China operates a managed floating exchange-rate regime with cautious capital-account liberalization. The RMB is fully convertible on trade-related current operations, including software services, royalties, and audio post-production disbursements, only after banks verify the underlying transaction documents. The capital account remains restricted: foreign direct investment into a WFOE requires SAFE registration evidenced by an FDI code, but once registered, inward equity remittances may settle into RMB through the designated bank. Profit repatriation is permitted annually after audited financial statements and corporate income-tax final settlement; the bank reviews the dividend resolution, audited profits, tax payment certificates, and a foreign-exchange declaration before purchasing FX. Surrender requirements no longer mandate forced conversion; exporters retain proceeds in FX accounts or convert at will. Remittances for artificial-intelligence subtitling and voice-cloning services are subject to service-trade verification, including contracts and invoices, to ensure current-account authenticity. The overall assessment is that current-account proceeds are effectively convertible through banks under a real-need principle, while capital flows are scheme-based and monitored by SAFE's digital system, making silent quota avoidance and round-tripping high-risk. For an AI subtitle and dubbing studio, contract granularity, invoice consistency, and IP licensing documentation are the decisive convertibility gateways.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Goods and services exporters are required to repatriate foreign-currency receipts to a licensed domestic bank or hold them in an approved offshore account. SAFE trade monitoring requires declaration of any collection period beyond 90 days from the export reporting reference as deferred collection; actual receipt is driven by the commercial payment terms, but the bank must be able to match the invoice and the underlying sale. Mandatory conversion ratio: 0%; there is no forced surrender of export proceeds. The exporter may retain USD or other foreign currency in a current-account FX account or settle into RMB at market rate. All settlements require matching contract, invoice, customs or service transaction records, and if any mismatch appears, the bank will suspend settlement and request a written explanation.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC), as the central bank, with foreign-exchange regulatory power delegated to the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low. PRC banking regulations reserve domestic RMB deposit-taking, foreign-exchange settlement, and SAFE-registered capital-account processes to licensed banks. Cross-border digital EMI providers such as Wise and Stripe do not hold a mainland China banking or payment-institution license capable of servicing a WFOE as a domestic operational account provider. They cannot legally open a CNY settlement account, execute an FDI capital injection narrative, or file SAFE reporting on behalf of a mainland entity. Stripe and Wise may only be used by an affiliated offshore holding company to collect overseas card receipts, but those funds must be repatriated to the mainland WFOE through a licensed bank channel. They are not a substitute for tier-1 commercial bank onboarding.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity injections must travel from the overseas shareholder's own bank account to the WFOE's SAFE-registered FDI capital account in mainland China using SWIFT MT103. The remittance instruction must state the SAFE FDI registration code and use the BOP receipt transaction code for direct-investment equity injection, commonly 621011, with confirmation from the receiving bank at time of placement. No capital contribution may be received on a personal account or routed through Wise or Stripe. The WFOE must first complete the FDI basic-information registration through its designated tier-1 bank. After receipt, a licensed certified public accountant issues a capital verification report confirming the contribution against the shareholder resolution and updated Articles of Association. The foreign currency may then be settled into RMB at the bank's spot rate within the capital settlement policy, and the settlement proceeds must be placed in a RMB capital account and used only for lawful operating and investment purposes. The bank will file the maintenance record and later cross-check it with annual SAFE reporting, dividend remittance, and disposal of the WFOE equity.For outbound subtitle transcription, audio dubbing, voice-cloning, and post-production service payments, the primary clearing rail is SWIFT MT103 for service-trade remittances. The WFOE must submit a service-trade foreign-exchange payment application, the signed service agreement, a commercial invoice, the relevant tax withholding or corporate income-tax payment certificate, and a calculation of royalties or licence fees. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File (financial-year related party transactions) must be retained if any of the following RMB thresholds are met: tangible asset ownership transfer amount > RMB 200 million (excluding toll/processing transactions); financial asset transfer amount > RMB 100 million; intangible asset transfer amount > RMB 100 million; or total other related-party transaction amount > RMB 40 million. Master File is required when total annual related-party transactions exceed RMB 1 billion. Contemporaneous documentation must be prepared by the filing due date and submitted within 30 days upon tax authority request.

Economic substance scope: China applies economic substance doctrines through Article 47 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law (General Anti-Avoidance Rule) and implementing rules that disregard entities without substantive business activities, purposeful arrangements, or that contradict the commercial substance of a transaction. For a WFOE engaged in AI voice cloning and subtitling, the Core Income-Generating Activities (CIGA) must be performed in China: employees with technical qualifications, decision-making for service delivery, maintenance of own hardware/software, and assumption of economic risk. Although China does not use the EU-style Economic Substance Regulations acronym, the tax authorities will deny treaty benefits or reallocate income to the extent that the WFOE lacks real operational substance, particularly when foreign shareholders use China as a pure booking or invoicing platform.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Transfer Pricing Manipulation and Base Erosion

Statutory sanction: Enterprise Income Tax Law Article 41 permits tax authority re-determination of taxable income under the arm's length principle; Article 114 of the Implementing Regulation imposes a 10% interest surcharge (statutory benchmark rate plus 5%) on underpaid tax for special tax adjustments. The Taxation Administration Law Article 63 treats this as tax evasion if intent is shown, imposing fines of 50% to 500% of the evaded tax. Criminal liability can arise under Criminal Law Article 201 for evading tax over RMB 50,000 and more than 10% of the tax due.

Director personal liability: Directors who intentionally instruct or knowingly facilitate the artificial royalty arrangement may be personally liable as co-perpetrators of tax evasion, subject to criminal fines and imprisonment up to seven years. Civilly, directors may be ordered to return unlawful distributions and compensate corporate and state losses. Under the Exit Control Rules, a director or legal representative can be blacklisted and banned from exiting China until the tax debt is settled, and personal assets may be frozen.
Failure to Withhold Non-Resident Income Tax

Statutory sanction: Taxation Administration Law Article 69 states that the withholding agent that fails to withhold shall be fined 50% to 300% of the amount that should have been withheld, and the tax bureau will recover the tax directly from the non-resident payer. Criminal penalties may apply under Criminal Law Article 210 (forging or falsely issuing withholding documents) or as accessory to evasion where the failure is part of a scheme to evade tax.

Director personal liability: Directors face penalties as individuals responsible for the omission, including personal fines up to RMB 100,000, and if the unpaid tax exceeds statutory thresholds, imprisonment for up to seven years for tax evasion (Criminal Law Art. 201). Travel bans and judicial attachment of personal assets can be imposed where the director is deemed the actual controller responsible for the offshore payment instructions. If the WFOE is bankrupt, the director may be held civilly liable for the uncollected tax under the principle of piercing the corporate veil.
Fictitious R&D and False Invoicing for AI Tax Incentives

Statutory sanction: Criminal Law Article 205 (false issuance of special VAT invoices) and Article 205-1 (false issuance of ordinary invoices) impose fines on both the entity and directly responsible directors. False issuance of ordinary invoices causing tax loss can lead to imprisonment of up to seven years and criminal fines. Under the Taxation Administration Law Article 63, the false R&D deduction constitutes tax fraud, subject to fines of 50% to 500% of the evaded tax. Additionally, the Enterprise Income Tax Law disallows the deduction and imposes late payment interest.

Director personal liability: Directors who authorize the false invoicing or knowingly supervise the fake R&D scheme face personal criminal liability under Criminal Law Article 205, with sentences of up to life imprisonment for particularly large amounts. They may be permanently disqualified from acting as directors, face confiscation of personal property, and be subject to civil restitution to the tax authority. Travel bans (coastal control) and passport cancellation are immediate administrative measures, and offshore assets of the directors may be reached through mutual legal assistance frameworks.
