Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum registered capital applies to a wholly foreign-owned enterprise (WFOE) limited liability company under Chinese law. The shareholders must determine a reasonable registered capital in the Articles of Association. Under Article 47 of the 2023 Company Law, each shareholder must contribute subscribed capital in full within five years from incorporation unless the Articles of Association specify a shorter period. No government-controlled blocked escrow account is required. The registered capital must be remitted into a designated foreign exchange capital account opened with a bank authorized by the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE), and all inward remittances will be subject to foreign exchange receipt verification procedures. For this Generative AI and LLM API platform, the registered capital should be sufficient to cover office lease, staff wages, computing infrastructure, cloud hosting, legal compliance, and working capital requirements.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA); online electronic filing and registration via the China Electronic Taxpayer eTax portal (eTax/e-Chinatax) at the competent provincial-level STA access point for the WFOE registered address

E-government filing portal

1) The foreign parent incorporates a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE) under Chinese Company Law through the local Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR), obtaining a Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which is the statutory Tax Identification Number (TIN) for tax purposes. 2) Within 30 calendar days after the business license is issued, the WFOE must complete tax registration through the STA eTax portal by submitting the business license, articles of association, legal representative identification, registered address proof, investor details and bank account information. 3) The STA issues a tax registration record and the WFOE is assigned the USCC-based taxpayer identification number, which is used for VAT, Corporate Income Tax, withholding and all other filings. 4) If projected annual taxable turnover will be at or above RMB 5,000,000, the entity must apply for VAT general taxpayer status to issue standard VAT invoices and claim input VAT credits. 5) If a foreign non-resident enterprise earns China-source income without a permanent establishment, it must complete non-resident enterprise tax registration via the withholding agent or directly through STA eTax to obtain a TIN and fulfil CIT and VAT withholding obligations.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

China does not operate a US-style check-the-box or hybrid mismatch pass-through election. The WFOE is mandatorily an entity-level Corporate Income Tax taxpayer resident in Mainland China. Available statutory elections include the VAT general taxpayer registration election, the small-scale taxpayer simplified-levy election when below the RMB 5,000,000 threshold, and software enterprise preferential tax treatment elections under the State Council software enterprise rules if the WFOE separately qualifies. No pass-through election exists.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

6%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Tax registration and VAT registration are compulsory for every WFOE immediately upon issuance of the business license with no economic turnover threshold; the mandatory general (standard) VAT taxpayer registration threshold is RMB 5,000,000 of annual assessable taxable turnover, below which the small-scale taxpayer regime applies

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under Chinese VAT law, a WFOE may apply the 0% VAT zero-rating to exported software and information technology services, including AI/LLM API inference services, where: (i) the service is supplied to an overseas customer located outside Mainland China; (ii) the service is wholly consumed outside Mainland China with no onshore end-user or domestic use; (iii) the transaction is entered in the STA export tax refund system; and (iv) the export is documented by contract, commercial invoice, foreign exchange collection proof and service delivery evidence. Pure digital API services do not require physical customs export declarations; zero-rating is substantiated through service-export documentation and foreign exchange repatriation records, while any physical goods or media shipped as part of the export must additionally have a Customs Export Declaration.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bill of Entry) for any physical goods or media exported in support of the AI API transaction
  • Bill of Lading or airway bill for physical exports
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) / SAFE-recognized bank foreign-exchange settlement receipt evidencing collection of export proceeds from overseas
  • Signed cross-border service contract or purchase order identifying the AI/LLM API service, the non-resident purchaser, and the fee schedule
  • Commercial invoice issued through the STA e-invoice platform at the 0% VAT rate
  • Service delivery and consumption evidence such as API token usage logs, server access records or third-party platform statements proving offshore consumption

All VAT invoices must be issued through the STA national fully digitalized electronic invoice platform (Electronic Invoice Service Platform), with real-time data transmission, API-based transaction reporting, unique digital invoice numbers and QR codes; no physical self-printing or manual tax stamping is permitted. The Golden Tax Phase IV system continuously matches invoice data against customs, treasury, exchange and enterprise bank records, and every zero-rated export invoice must be filed in the STA export tax refund declaration system with the supporting export documentation attached. Invoices issued outside the approved platform are invalid for input VAT deduction and export zero-rated refund purposes.

STA, customs and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange jointly audit export zero-rating files. If supporting documentation is missing, internally inconsistent or fraudulent, the zero-rating or export refund is denied, the output VAT is clawed back, late payment surcharge accrues, and administrative penalties of 50% to 500% of the evaded or refunded tax are imposed under the Tax Administration and Collection Law. Cases involving forged FIRCs, false Customs declarations or fictitious export contracts may constitute fraudulent export tax refund crimes under the Criminal Law, carrying criminal fines and imprisonment up to life imprisonment.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a managed floating exchange-rate system with strict separation between current-account and capital-account convertibility. Under the Foreign Exchange Administration Regulations, SAFE and PBoC jointly supervise RMB convertibility. Genuine current-account transactions of a WFOE engaged in AI API and software-export services, including token usage fees, subscription revenue, and SaaS/API royalties from overseas clients, are generally convertible once the transaction is supported by a signed economic contract, invoice, and payment record. Capital-account items, however, require case-by-case registration: FDI equity injections must be recorded through the FDI Business Registration system; the foreign exchange capital settlement account can convert the injected foreign currency into RMB, but disbursements from that account are subject to the 'four-by-four' deployment review and the negative-list use restrictions. Profit remittance to the foreign parent requires audited financial statements, tax clearance certificates, mandatory statutory reserve contributions, and an SAFE outbound remittance declaration. Dividend repatriation is lawful only when retained earnings exist after corporate income tax and reserve requirements are satisfied. The authorities encourage RMB settlement but USD is still accepted for bona fide trade and service receipts. In practice, the WFOE must avoid any mismatch between the declared purpose code and the actual underlying business, maintain a documented trail from issuance contract to token consumption, and ensure that any cryptocurrency/USDC payment is converted offshore and never introduced into the onshore banking perimeter as a direct settlement token. Accordingly, the treasury blueprint should maintain separate onshore CNY operating liquidity and offshore USD receipts, with each cross-border flow anchored to a verifiable commercial event.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export proceeds, including AI API fees from overseas customers, must be repatriated to China through a designated foreign exchange bank and reported to SAFE. The statutory requirement is repatriation and verification, not a fixed mandatory surrender percentage; after the 2012 SAFE reforms the WFOE may retain foreign exchange or settle it to CNY based on its treasury need. For goods exports the standard collection period is 90 days after customs declaration; for services the collection period should follow the contract and, in practice, be completed within 180 days from invoice maturity. Any delayed collection beyond the normal trade period requires a deferred-receipt registration or an application to the local SAFE branch. The mandatory conversion ratio is 0% because there is no fixed quota; however, the foreign exchange received must be either retained in a compliant current-account receipt account or settled through the bank on a transaction-specific basis, and any surplus not substantiated by the underlying export/service contract must be reversed.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBoC) and State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Offshore fintech EMIs have very low viability as the primary banking layer for an onshore Chinese WFOE. Non-bank payment institutions providing account, clearing, and payment services in China must obtain a PBoC Payment Business License; no foreign EMI holds that license. Therefore Revolut Business, Wise, Airwallex, and similar offshore digital entities cannot open onshore RMB bank accounts, issue a domestic settlement account, or produce the SAFE capital verification documentation. They may only be used as auxiliary offshore collection tools for the foreign parent receiving USD before making a regulated MT103 equity injection. All onshore treasury duties—FDI registration, capital verification, CNY settlement, trade payments, tax remittance, and documentary evidence—must sit with a licensed PRC commercial bank. Any attempt to route WFOE operating revenue through an offshore EMI to bypass SAFE will trigger an AML red flag and legal exposure.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The foreign parent must execute the capital contribution through a regulated banking channel, using SWIFT MT103, with the remittance purpose code corresponding to equity investment in a foreign-invested enterprise. The funds must land in the WFOE's designated foreign-currency capital settlement account at a licensed PRC commercial bank. After receipt, the WFOE must complete the SAFE FDI registration or update, present the FDI Business Registration Form, and instruct the bank to settle the foreign currency into RMB. The bank will register the capital settlement in the SAFE foreign exchange platform, and a qualified PRC CPA must issue a capital verification report. Disbursements from the settled capital account must comply with the negative-list use restrictions and be supported by invoices, contracts, and payroll/tax documents. No personal wallet, offshore EMI, or third-party collection account may be used as the inbound route for registered capital.For AI API service disbursements to overseas cloud providers, model vendors, license holders, and independent contractors, the standard clearing rail is a current-account SWIFT MT103 remittance supported by a service agreement, invoice, payment schedule, and SAFE purpose code. For any physical-goods import incidental to the WFOE operations, a commercial letter of credit (MT700) or documentary collection is permissible, but the bank will require transport documents, customs declarations, and matching invoices. The WFOE must not use personal cards or crypto rails for corporate trade settlements; all outbound payments require the corporate seal, the mandated bank reviewer sign-off, and proof of tax withholding if the payment includes royalty or service-royalty components.Onshore deliverable forward contracts (CNY/USD) for genuine current-account exposure are permissible, with SAFE real-demand confirmation through a designated PRC bank and no speculative gapping.Cross-currency interest rate swaps are permitted only for hedging confirmed offshore liabilities or FX debt positions, require the bank’s internal product eligibility and SAFE filing, and must be matched to an existing notional.Offshore non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are not lawful for the onshore WFOE treasury; only the offshore parent may execute NDFs as a hedge, and those contracts cannot settle against the WFOE’s onshore account.FX option collars and risk reversals are permitted for hedging real trade or service exposure, but each transaction must be supported by an underlying contract, invoice, and SAFE documentary check.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Offshore fintech EMIs have very low viability as the primary banking layer for an onshore Chinese WFOE. Non-bank payment institutions providing account, clearing, and payment services in China must obtain a PBoC Payment Business License; no foreign EMI holds that license. Therefore Revolut Business, Wise, Airwallex, and similar offshore digital entities cannot open onshore RMB bank accounts, issue a domestic settlement account, or produce the SAFE capital verification documentation. They may only be used as auxiliary offshore collection tools for the foreign parent receiving USD before making a regulated MT103 equity injection. All onshore treasury duties—FDI registration, capital verification, CNY settlement, trade payments, tax remittance, and documentary evidence—must sit with a licensed PRC commercial bank. Any attempt to route WFOE operating revenue through an offshore EMI to bypass SAFE will trigger an AML red flag and legal exposure.

Mandatory Audit Threshold Small Company Audit Exemption Regime

Turnover: There are no quantitative balance-sheet, turnover, or headcount thresholds that exempt a China WFOE from the statutory audit. Article 164 of the PRC Company Law and the PRC Accounting Law require every company, including a WFOE LLC, to prepare annual financial statements and have them audited by an independent certified public accounting firm licensed in China. The Foreign Investment Law and the Foreign Investment Information Reporting Measures further require the WFOE to maintain audited financial statements as the basis for the annual foreign-investment report. If the enterprise qualifies under the Ministry of Finance Small Enterprise Accounting Standard, simplified accounting may be used, but the audit obligation remains. For an AI/LLM API platform with revenue from Stripe and USDC, the audit must independently test revenue recognition from API token usage, digital-asset receipts, foreign-exchange conversion, VAT treatment, and intercompany transactions; the auditor must be a Chinese CPA firm with no disqualifying relationship. The absence of balance-sheet or headcount safe harbor means a dormant WFOE is still required to incur the audit.
