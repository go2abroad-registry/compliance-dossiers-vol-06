Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None. No statutory minimum paid-up capital is imposed on a general WFOE limited liability company under the PRC Company Law or Foreign Investment Law. No escrow-blocked capital account is mandated. The articles of association must state the total registered capital, share class, number of shares, par value, subscription amount and contribution schedule. The sole foreign shareholder may contribute in installments, but all subscribed capital must be paid in full within five years from the date of issuance of the Business License. Paid-in capital for non-RMB contributions must be remitted through a bank with State Administration of Foreign Exchange capital-account qualification, deposited into the company RMB capital account, and used only for lawful business operations. No statutory capital verification report is required for ordinary software WFOEs unless required by the bank, the licensed industry, or a special regulatory condition.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China, administered through provincial and municipal tax bureaus; the national electronic online portal is the Electronic Tax Administration (E-Tax) platform used for filing, registration, VAT return submission, and invoice authorization.

E-government filing portal

For the WFOE, the tax identification number is the Unified Social Credit Code printed on the business license issued by the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR). The WFOE must complete statutory tax registration within 30 days after incorporation through the E-Tax portal by submitting the authenticated business license, articles of association, board resolution, legal representative identification, registered address proof, accounting system documentation, and bank settlement account details. The E-Tax system automatically creates the permanent taxpayer file and VAT registration under the same Unified Social Credit Code. The entity then elects small-scale taxpayer or general taxpayer VAT status, applies for electronic invoice quota, and obtains the tax UKey and digital invoicing certificate. A foreign non-resident entity without a permanent establishment that needs a TIN/VAT ID for Chinese-source taxable supplies must apply at the competent tax office of its withholding agent or directly through E-Tax using its original overseas registration certificate, a board resolution appointing a fiscal representative, local contact person details, and the relevant cross-border contract; the STA then issues a separate non-resident taxpayer identification number.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation; the WFOE is a Chinese resident enterprise taxed on worldwide income with no pass-through entitlement, while non-resident entities are subject to territorial-source taxation on Chinese-derived income.

Available election forms

None. Chinese law does not provide a check-the-box pass-through election or hybrid mismatch election for a limited-liability WFOE. The only administrative elections are VAT status election between small-scale taxpayer and general taxpayer within statutory turnover limits and elective simplified tax collection methods only where specifically authorised by local STA.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No turnover-based VAT registration exemption for a limited-liability WFOE; VAT registration is mandatory within 30 days of incorporation regardless of turnover. For small-scale taxpayer classification, annual taxable sales not exceeding RMB 5,000,000 may remain small-scale; once annual VAT-taxable turnover exceeds RMB 5,000,000, registration as a general VAT taxpayer is mandatory.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exported goods qualify for the 0% VAT export rate when they are physically exported from China, supported by a valid customs export declaration, genuine cross-border sales contract, commercial invoice, payment in foreign currency, and proof that the goods left China through a regulated customs port. Qualifying cross-border taxable services, including AI software and digital avatar video generation services supplied by a Chinese resident to a non-resident customer, qualify for 0% VAT when the service is wholly consumed outside China, the recipient is outside China, the payment is received in foreign currency, and the contract, service delivery logs, IP address evidence, and overseas customer identity documentation are retained. The zero-rated claim must be supported on the VAT return and all substantive evidence must be kept for STA inspection.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bill of Entry)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or bank inward foreign-currency collection certificate
  • Export sales contract and commercial invoice
  • Packing list and customs inspection/export verification records
  • For zero-rated cross-border software/SaaS services: service agreement, foreign-currency receipt evidence, service delivery and usage logs, overseas recipient identification, and IP/geolocation records proving foreign consumption

China operates mandatory real-time fiscal electronic invoicing through the Golden Tax Phase IV system and the national Fully-Digitalised Electronic Invoice platform. Taxpayers must issue VAT invoices through STA-certified systems; invoice data are transmitted to the STA in near real time via API. Each electronic invoice bears a unique tax control number, digital signature, and anti-fraud QR code. Physical stamping is not required for fully digitalised e-invoices, and no unofficial invoice can support input VAT credit or export refund substantiation.

Export zero-rating and export VAT refund claims are subject to ex-post STA audit and customs-treasury reconciliation. False, inflated, or unsubstantiated zero-rating claims are retrospectively disallowed, the refunded VAT is clawed back with statutory overdue interest, and penalties of 50% to 500% of the evaded or refunded tax may be imposed. Serious violations can result in cancellation of export tax rebate qualification, inclusion in the tax dishonesty blacklist, restrictions on outbound customs clearance, and criminal liability for fraudulent export VAT refund under Chinese tax and criminal law. Failure to repatriate export proceeds within the statutory foreign-exchange deadline or submission of false FIRC evidence may also trigger State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) monetary penalties and administrative sanctions.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China maintains a managed floating exchange rate regime with RMB convertible for current account transactions (trade in goods and services, profit remittances) but tightly supervised for capital account flows. A China WFOE may open RMB basic deposit accounts and foreign exchange capital accounts with designated domestic banks. Capital injections from a foreign parent require SAFE/FDI registration, a bank-level foreign capital account, and post-arrival capital verification; capital funds cannot be used for unapproved equity investment, offshore lending, or non-business expenditures. Profits can be remitted abroad as current account dividends after a statutory annual audit, final tax settlement and tax clearance, board resolution, and bank review of audited financial statements. For the AI avatar video generation SaaS business, receivables from overseas software subscriptions are cross-border service trade proceeds; they must be repatriated into China through the domestic banking system and supported by contracts, invoices, and platform reconciliation reports. SAFE does not impose mandatory foreign exchange surrender quotas on export proceeds; the WFOE may retain eligible foreign currency receipts in a foreign exchange settlement account or voluntarily convert them to RMB. However, all cross-border payments must be cleared through CIPS or SWIFT rails via SAFE-licensed banks, and any offshore fintech EMI arrangement lacks legal CNY settlement capacity for a mainland WFOE.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE rules, export-related foreign exchange proceeds, including software and IP licensing income, must be repatriated to a domestic designated foreign exchange account within 180 days of the underlying transaction or customs declaration. No mandatory conversion ratio applies; after repatriation, the WFOE may retain 100% of the proceeds in a foreign exchange settlement account or sell them to a designated bank at the market rate. Each receipt requires a cross-border income declaration, underlying contract/invoice, and where the amount exceeds the bank's delegated threshold, SAFE-compliant service trade documentation.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBoC) / State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Direct viability is low because offshore digital EMIs are effectively prohibited from acting as the primary clearing bank for a mainland China WFOE. A foreign fintech EMI lacks a PBOC banking license, cannot hold a Chinese legal-person settlement account, and cannot directly participate in local CNY clearing via CIPS. Stripe and Paddle may continue to serve as merchant acquirers for overseas-facing collection only if the WFOE uses a licensed Chinese cross-border payment aggregator, or if the contracting counterparty is an offshore affiliate and proceeds are remitted to the WFOE through standard SWIFT/SAFE service-trade wiring. For local payroll, VAT payment, FX settlement, and compliance with SAFE, the WFOE must maintain a domestic Tier-1 bank relationship; any offshore receipt parking arrangement that avoids repatriation would violate current-account rules.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The foreign parent remits via SWIFT MT103 to the WFOE's FDI foreign exchange capital account at a designated domestic bank, using the SAFE direct investment purpose classification for inbound equity capital. The remittance must identify the Chinese beneficiary name and account number, and the receiving bank will require the FDI registration code and underlying capital contribution contract before applying credit. A licensed local CPA then issues the capital verification report certifying that the contributed capital is fully paid and compliant. Domestic RMB disbursements are cleared through the PBoC clearing system, while cross-border RMB remittances can be executed via CIPS to a counterparty's Chinese correspondent bank. Documentary collections are acceptable only where a physical technology product or media asset is involved, not for recurring avatar subscription revenues.Real-demand trade-backed RMB/FX deliverable forwards with a licensed Chinese bank on current-account receivables and payables, including international SaaS subscription revenue.Cross-currency swaps and FX options with a domestic bank for hedging long-term RMB/USD balance-sheet exposure arising from cloud infrastructure and overseas payroll, subject to the non-speculative real-demand principle.No onshore CNY non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are permitted for mainland WFOEs; offshore CNH NDFs are available only to non-resident counterparties outside China and cannot be booked onshore.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Direct viability is low because offshore digital EMIs are effectively prohibited from acting as the primary clearing bank for a mainland China WFOE. A foreign fintech EMI lacks a PBOC banking license, cannot hold a Chinese legal-person settlement account, and cannot directly participate in local CNY clearing via CIPS. Stripe and Paddle may continue to serve as merchant acquirers for overseas-facing collection only if the WFOE uses a licensed Chinese cross-border payment aggregator, or if the contracting counterparty is an offshore affiliate and proceeds are remitted to the WFOE through standard SWIFT/SAFE service-trade wiring. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File: prepare and maintain contemporaneous documentation when annual related-party transactions exceed RMB 100 million; Master File: prepare when aggregate related-party transactions exceed RMB 1 billion; CbC Report: file when the consolidated group revenue of the ultimate parent in the previous fiscal year reaches RMB 5.5 billion and China is the filing jurisdiction for group reporting.

Economic substance scope: China applies the GAAR and substance-over-form doctrine under Enterprise Income Tax Law Article 47 and the Special Tax Adjustment Implementation Measures. A WFOE with no employees, no physical office, no board minutes, no control over its AI intangibles and no core income-generating activities in China is exposed to recharacterisation as a shell: its tax residency and treaty entitlements may be ignored, income may be attributed to the true beneficial owner, and its related-party transactions may be adjusted. Although China has no standalone Economic Substance Regulation, the same substance analysis is embedded in the beneficial ownership test for treaty relief, the non-resident enterprise PE threshold, the CFC rules and the core income-generating activities assessment.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion via False R&D Super-Deduction

Statutory sanction: Under the Tax Administration Law Article 63, the tax authorities recover the underpaid tax, charge a daily surcharge of 0.05%, and impose a fine of 50% to 500% of the underpaid amount. Where the amount is substantial and the evasion ratio meets the criminal threshold, the taxpayer and responsible persons are referred to the Public Security Organs under Criminal Law Article 201, exposing the company to fines and the responsible officers to imprisonment.

Director personal liability: Directors, CFOs and tax preparers who knowingly signed or directed fake R&D reporting may be civilly liable to restore the corporate tax shortfall, have personal bank accounts and assets frozen, be added to the tax dishonesty blacklist, be prohibited from leaving mainland China, and upon criminal conviction be sentenced to up to three years' imprisonment for ordinary tax evasion, rising to three to seven years where the amount is enormous and the proportion of tax evaded is high.
Shell Entity and Treaty Benefit Abuse

Statutory sanction: The tax authority may apply the General Anti-Avoidance Rule under EIT Law Article 47 and the Special Tax Adjustment Measures to disregard the intermediary, deny the treaty benefit, reassess the dividend as an immediate outflow subject to the 10% statutory withholding tax, assess late surcharge, and impose a fine under Tax Administration Law Article 63 of 50% to 500% of the underpaid withholding tax.

Director personal liability: Directors who caused the WFOE to under-withhold or who certified a shell entity's beneficial ownership may be personally jointly liable for the deficient withholding tax and penalties; they may be subjected to travel bans and asset seizure, and where the false treaty claim was knowingly executed, criminal liability may attach as tax evasion, with imprisonment from three to seven years for large amounts.
Transfer Pricing Documentation Failure and Artificial Intercompany Pricing

Statutory sanction: Under the Special Tax Adjustment Implementation Measures and Tax Administration Law Article 62, failure to report related-party transactions or maintain documentation triggers a fine of up to RMB 10,000, or RMB 50,000 in serious cases, and the tax authority may disregard the annotated pricing, make an upward adjustment of taxable income, apply interest at 5 percentage points over the benchmark lending rate for the special tax adjustment period, and impose penalties if the underpayment is attributed to intentional under-filing.

Director personal liability: Directors who approved the transfer pricing policy, signed related-party contracts without substance review, or failed to ensure documentation preparation can be held civilly liable for the adjusted tax, interest and administrative penalties; additionally, the responsible legal representative may be barred from exiting China while tax liability remains unsettled, and in cases of deliberate concealment with forged documentation, criminal exposure arises under Criminal Law Article 201 for tax evasion.
