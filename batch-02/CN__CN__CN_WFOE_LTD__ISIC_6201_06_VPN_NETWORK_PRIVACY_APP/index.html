None. The PRC Company Law does not impose a minimum paid-up capital threshold for a general technology-service WFOE. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA), acting through local competent tax bureaus; online taxpayer portal: China Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax); electronic invoice issuance is managed on the National Electronic Invoice Service Platform.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Foreign investor notarizes, apostilles and submits incorporation documents to the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) to establish the Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE). Under the unified social credit reform, the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCI) on the SAMR-issued Business License is automatically the statutory Taxpayer Identification Number and the VAT registration number. Step 2: Within 30 days of Business License issuance, the WFOE must log into China Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax) and complete tax information confirmation, including VAT registration category (general taxpayer or small-scale taxpayer), corporate income tax registration, stamp duty registration, urban maintenance and construction tax registration, educational surcharge registration, and legal representative digital certificate binding. Step 3: A foreign non-resident enterprise without a Chinese establishment that makes taxable supplies into China must either register directly with the competent local STA office or appoint a Chinese withholding agent; the STA issues a separate Non-Resident Enterprise Tax Code / withholding registration number, and each cross-border contract must be filed through the STA's non-resident enterprise contract registration module. Step 4: After VAT registration, the WFOE must activate electronic invoice issuance by obtaining a tax U-Key and digital certificate from the local tax bureau, then register the invoicing terminal on the National Electronic Invoice Service Platform. No separate VAT ID is issued because the USCI is the sole statutory taxpayer code for VAT, Enterprise Income Tax, customs and foreign exchange reporting.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation - PRC WFOE is a Chinese resident limited liability enterprise taxed under the Enterprise Income Tax Law of the People's Republic of China on its worldwide income; no pass-through treatment is available; dividends paid to foreign shareholders are subject to withholding tax after entity-level Enterprise Income Tax.

Available election forms

None - the PRC tax system does not provide check-the-box elections, hybrid mismatch elections, or entity classification elections for a domestic WFOE. A WFOE is mandatorily taxed as a resident enterprise at entity level. The only administrative elections available are the VAT general taxpayer versus small-scale taxpayer status election and accounting method elections under the Enterprise Income Tax Law, but these do not alter entity tax classification.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration is triggered when annual aggregate taxable turnover exceeds RMB 5,000,000 (approximately USD 685,000); however, all taxable enterprises, including non-resident digital suppliers, must register for VAT upon commencement of taxable activity. Domestic software, network security and software-as-a-service supplies are generally taxed at the 6% VAT rate for modern services, while cross-border qualifying supplies are zero-rated at 0%. Small-scale taxpayers below the RMB 5,000,000 threshold may apply a simplified VAT levy at 3% or the applicable reduced rate.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is available for exported goods that have physically left the PRC customs territory and for qualifying cross-border services supplied to overseas recipients, wholly consumed outside China, paid for in foreign currency, and repatriated through the PRC banking system. For the VPN network privacy SaaS activity, the supply qualifies for 0% VAT when the customer is located outside the PRC, the service is delivered and consumed outside Chinese territory, no domestic Chinese users are serviced, and the consideration is settled through overseas payment rails such as Stripe or Paymentwall and subsequently repatriated to China in accordance with State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) rules. The taxpayer reports the transaction as zero-rated export service revenue on the VAT return and maintains contemporaneous documentary evidence to substantiate the cross-border consumption.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Export Goods Declaration Form) confirmed by the General Administration of Customs of the PRC for goods export; for pure digital services, a customs declaration is not required but the service export must be documented by a cross-border service contract and service delivery records.
  • Bill of Lading, sea waybill or airway bill showing the WFOE as shipper and the overseas customer as consignee for physical goods exports.
  • Original export commercial invoice, packing list, and sales contract or purchase order.
  • Bank Foreign Exchange Collection Receipt / Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) issued by a bank inside the SAFE monitoring system showing actual repatriation of export or cross-border service proceeds to the PRC.
  • Export VAT rebate declaration and e-Tax generated export-service zero-rating files filed with the State Taxation Administration, including the export goods declaration cross-reference, FX repatriation evidence, and digital service transaction logs.

China uses the National VAT Invoice System under the Golden Tax Phase IV, operated through the National Electronic Invoice Service Platform. Every VAT invoice, including full-digital electronic invoices, must be issued through tax-approved hardware such as the Tax U-Key or through an approved e-invoice API integrated with the taxpayer's ERP or SaaS billing system. Invoice data is transmitted to the STA in real time at the moment of issuance. Each invoice must contain a unique invoice code, unique invoice number, QR anti-counterfeit code, payer Unified Social Credit Code, taxable amount, VAT rate and VAT amount. Special VAT Invoices for general taxpayers are subject to centralized cross-checking between the purchaser's and seller's invoice records. Digital stamping is implemented through the electronic signatures of the legal representative and finance officer; any unauthorised alteration invalidates the invoice for input VAT credit and export rebate purposes.

If the STA audit determines that zero-rating was not adequately substantiated, the taxpayer is retrospectively denied the 0% rate, output VAT is assessed at the domestic rate applicable to the supply (6% for the VPN SaaS service; 13% for tangible goods), late payment surcharge accrues at 0.05% per day, and administrative penalties ranging from 50% to 500% of the VAT evaded are imposed under the Law on Administration of Tax Levy and Collection of the People's Republic of China. Fraudulent export VAT zero-rating or export rebate claims trigger full clawback of refunded VAT plus interest, downgrade of the taxpayer's tax credit rating to D, suspension of export tax rebate rights, and criminal referral. Serious cases constitute the criminal offence of fraudulent export tax rebate under the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, punishable by fines and imprisonment up to life imprisonment. The statutory record retention period is generally five years, but fraud investigations may extend the look-back period to ten years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a managed current-account-convertible regime and a strict capital-account-convertibility framework. The Renminbi is convertible for genuine current-account transactions such as trade in services, royalties, dividends, and interest, provided the paying bank verifies the underlying documents. Capital-account activities, including FDI equity injection, external debt borrowing, acquisitions, and offshore lending, require prior SAFE registration and are monitored through designated bank accounts. Profit remittance from a wholly foreign-owned enterprise is legally treated as a current-account item but is subject to a pre-clearance gate: the company must have completed its annual statutory audit, filed both income tax and VAT returns, obtained a tax clearance certificate, passed transfer-pricing review if applicable, and prepared a board resolution approving the dividend distribution; the remitting bank then checks these documents plus the SAFE registration record. There is no unrestricted currency exchange: all FX purchase/sale must be grounded in real trade or investment demands, and speculative conversions are prohibited. Export proceeds must be repatriated to China, but the exporter may retain them in a foreign-currency account or settle them into RMB at its discretion under SAFE trade-monitoring rules. This controlled architecture means the WFOE cannot rely on unlicensed offshore fintech payment providers as its primary treasury clearing rail.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the SAFE Measures for the Administration of Foreign Exchange in Trade in Goods, goods-trade export proceeds must be repatriated into China within the general 180-day statutory window counted from the export customs declaration date, unless a pre-approved exception or extension applies. There is no fixed percentage mandatory conversion ratio: the WFOE may retain the full export receipt in a designated foreign-currency settlement account or choose to convert all or part into RMB at an onshore designated bank. Every collection event must be reconciled with the customs declaration data in SAFE's trade monitoring system; if the receipt exceeds the reported value, lacks a recall period, or arrives after the statutory window, the bank must reject or suspend settlement. The full economic value of the export proceeds must enter China; any unrepatriated balance is considered illegal evasion and subject to administrative penalties and rating downgrades within the banking system.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) and State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Low as a primary clearing rail. Offshore digital EMIs such as Stripe and Paymentwall are not licensed to open onshore RMB operating accounts or to execute local CNY clearing on behalf of a China-registered WFOE. They may function only as an offshore front-end subscription capture layer for overseas users, but all treasury liquidity must be repatriated through a licensed domestic bank or an approved cross-border payment institution that reports to SAFE. Since the WFOE's export/service revenue must be received in China within the statutory SAFE framework, retaining funds in an unlicensed EMI wallet would create a surrender and repatriation violation. Therefore, the blueprint requires an onshore Tier-1 domestic bank account as the principal reconciliation and FX settlement point.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The ultimate parent must wire the equity capital from its named corporate bank account using a SWIFT MT103 to the WFOE's SAFE-registered Foreign Exchange Capital Account held at Bank of China or ICBC. The payment instruction must carry the relevant purpose code for foreign direct investment and the WFOE's FDI registration code issued by SAFE. The receiving bank will verify against the SAFE system, check the BO/KYC profile, and credit the foreign-currency amount. Under the reform-measure 'payment for verification' model, the bank may convert the funds into RMB and release them only when the WFOE presents the underlying business contracts and intended-use schedule for the declared capital purpose. A licensed Chinese CPA must subsequently issue the capital verification report, and SAFE will publish the incremental capital registration record.For operational expenses, intercompany royalties, and software licensing fees, settlement will occur through SWIFT MT103 wire transfers from the WFOE's designated current-account foreign-currency settlement account or via onshore purchase of FX at the bank's counter, using the appropriate SAFE service-trade purpose code and a supporting contract, invoice, and tax certificate. For any physical network gateway hardware exported, standard Merchandise Trade clearing rails apply: commercial letters of credit and documentary collections routed through the bank's trade finance department, with customs declaration data linked to the FX settlement record. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low as a primary clearing rail. Offshore digital EMIs such as Stripe and Paymentwall are not licensed to open onshore RMB operating accounts or to execute local CNY clearing on behalf of a China-registered WFOE. They may function only as an offshore front-end subscription capture layer for overseas users, but all treasury liquidity must be repatriated through a licensed domestic bank or an approved cross-border payment institution that reports to SAFE. Since the WFOE's export/service revenue must be received in China within the statutory SAFE framework, retaining funds in an unlicensed EMI wallet would create a surrender and repatriation violation. Therefore, the blueprint requires an onshore Tier-1 domestic bank account as the principal reconciliation and FX settlement point.
