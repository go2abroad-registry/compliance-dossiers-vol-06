Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A virtual office, co-working mailbox, or commercial mail-forwarding address is not permissible as the sole registered office for a WFOE operating a fintech software application. The PRC Company Law and SAMR registration rules require a fixed business premises located within the registered administrative district, evidenced by a valid lease or occupancy certificate. District tax authorities and PBOC AML supervisors closely audit the alignment between the registered address and actual economic operations. Free-trade-zone address-sharing pilots still demand an executed lease and a physical company presence; they are not equivalent to a virtual office. PRC commercial banks, Stripe, and Apple IAP onboarding reject business addresses that cannot be independently confirmed as a physical operational location. Accordingly, a conventional physical office lease with a minimum 12-month term is mandatory for incorporation and bank account opening.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None. For an ordinary technology WFOE under ISIC 6201 there is no statutory minimum registered capital and no blocked escrow account is required by SAMR. Under the 2023 PRC Company Law, shareholders must actually pay their subscribed capital within five years after incorporation or within such shorter period stated in the Articles of Association, and the bank must receive the capital before it can be used. Capital must be remitted through the foreign exchange capital account and settled only for genuine business purposes. Excessively high registered capital may trigger foreign exchange, tax thin capitalization, sanctions screening, and KYC verification; the directors should therefore fix capital at an amount justified by the business plan.

Funding & unlock path: Not Applicable – no fire safety certificate, no structural load inspection, and no occupational safety license is required for a software company occupying standard Grade-A office space. The office lease must nevertheless comply with fire protection laws and the landlord must hold a current fire inspection certificate. If the Company later installs high-availability server equipment, it must obtain building load verification and fire-fighting acceptance documentation from the local fire and rescue authority.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; online registration and filing are processed through the STA Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax) portal.

E-government filing portal

For a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE) incorporated in mainland China, the tax identification number is the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) automatically assigned on issuance of the business license by the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR). The WFOE must complete tax registration through the Electronic Tax Bureau within 30 days of incorporation. A foreign non-resident enterprise with no registered establishment in China must apply for non-resident tax registration with the competent State Taxation Administration office within 30 days after the occurrence of a Chinese tax obligation, submitting its home-country incorporation certificate, proof of beneficial ownership, board resolution, and the China non-resident tax registration application; the STA will then issue a separate non-resident taxpayer identification number.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rated VAT may be claimed for qualifying cross-border provision of software services and information technology services where the service recipient is located outside mainland China and the service is wholly consumed outside China. The exporter must provide the cross-border service contract, invoices, evidence of overseas payment, and proof of offshore service performance/consumption, and must file through the STA Electronic Tax Bureau to confirm zero-rating treatment. For tangible goods or media, the exporter must satisfy customs export procedures.

  • Customs Export Declaration Form (for physical goods or tangible media; not applicable to pure cloud software services)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill for any physical deliverables
  • Cross-border service agreement or sales contract
  • Commercial invoice and digital e-invoice issued through the STA e-invoice platform
  • Bank receipt of foreign exchange or SAFE-monitored cross-border settlement certificate evidencing inbound payment repatriation from the overseas contracting party
  • Export foreign exchange collection verification record where goods are physically exported

China mandates full digital electronic invoicing through the State Taxation Administration's Digital Electronic Invoice System. VAT-registered taxpayers must issue standardized digital e-invoices with fiscal digital signatures through the STA-approved electronic invoice service platform, transmitting invoice data to the tax authority in real time. High-volume B2B/B2C taxpayers must use API-based integration; the Golden Tax System Phase IV performs automated anti-fraud matching, invoice authenticity verification, consumption monitoring, and risk alerts. No physical tax control device is required for fully digitalized e-invoices.

Tax authorities will retrospectively claw back VAT zero-rating benefits, assess late-payment surcharges, and impose penalties of 50% to five times the tax involved if documentation is false, incomplete, or fails to substantiate offshore consumption or payment repatriation. Under Article 63 of the PRC Tax Collection Administration Law, fraudulent zero-rating or fraudulent export refund claims may trigger criminal liability, and under PRC Criminal Law exports tax fraud offences can result in imprisonment and additional monetary penalties.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a managed floating exchange-rate system and maintains current-account RMB convertibility for legitimate trade and services transactions, consistent with its IMF Article VIII obligations. The capital account, however, remains regulated: every inflow and outflow linked to foreign direct investment, offshore indirect financing, and portfolio exposure must be anchored by an SAFE registration, a designated bank account structure, and a valid transaction purpose code. A WFOE developing financial portfolio-tracking cloud software may therefore freely convert RMB to foreign currency for current-account payments such as software licensing, cloud infrastructure fees, and after-tax profit dividends, provided the underlying contract, invoice, tax settlement certificate, and bank record are complete. In practice, banks will review the economic substance of each payment, apply a suspicious transaction reporting overlay, and verify that the WFOE has passed SAFE's investment-entity compliance screening. Profit remittances require an annual audit, a corporate income tax final settlement certificate, a dividend resolution, and an FDI registration confirmation; purchases of foreign currency for dividend distribution are handled by the settling bank under the current-account conversion category. For injected capital, the WFOE must open an FDI capital settlement account, obtain the SAFE-issued foreign-exchange registration code, and convert the funds to RMB only following the bank's verification of the declared business use. This dual-track convertibility gives the WFOE operational liquidity and FX freedom while carefully restricting capital-account mobility.

Export proceeds surrender rules

SAFE does not currently prescribe a statutory fixed percentage for mandatory conversion of export or service-trade foreign-exchange receipts. Under the total-verification trade administration regime introduced by SAFE, 100% of cross-border software and services revenue must be repatriated to a domestic bank account and reported through the bank's service-trade transaction system; the WFOE may then choose to retain the foreign currency in an approved foreign-exchange settlement account within the bank-assigned quota or convert the full amount to RMB at spot. The usual statutory timeline for software/service-trade proceeds is receipt within 30 days from the contractual due date and in no event later than 90 days from the invoice or completion date. Retention beyond the quota, incomplete documentation, or failure to show a matching contract and tax invoice will trigger SAFE investigation and possible suspension of the current-account conversion facility.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC), with delegated foreign-exchange and balance-of-payments enforcement powers granted to the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound equity capital must be remitted by SWIFT MT103 or MT202 from the overseas investor's bank to the WFOE's FDI Foreign Exchange Capital Settlement Account maintained at a licensed Tier-1 Chinese bank. The remittance must carry the correct SAFE transaction purpose code for FDI equity injection, the WFOE's SAFE foreign-exchange registration code, and the investor's name, and must match the capital contribution schedule recorded in the FDI registration certificate. Upon receipt, the bank verifies the SAFE registration, completes the capital transfer registration, and the WFOE obtains a capital verification report from a Chinese certified public accountant. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low and legally restricted. Offshore digital EMI platforms such as Revolut Business, Airwallex, Payoneer, and similar services do not hold PBOC banking licences and are not authorised to open domestic RMB clearing accounts or provide Chinese local bank routing for a mainland WFOE. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation is mandatory when in a single fiscal year the WFOE's related-party service transactions (including cross-border SaaS, cloud, management, and technical services) equal or exceed CNY 200,000,000; related-party tangible property transfers equal or exceed CNY 200,000,000; related-party intangible property transfers equal or exceed CNY 100,000,000; or related-party financial asset transfers equal or exceed CNY 100,000,000. A Master File is required when the multinational group's consolidated annual revenue equals or exceeds CNY 2,000,000,000 and cross-border related-party transactions exist.

Economic substance scope: Although mainland China does not apply a UAE-style Economic Substance Regulations proxy, Chinese tax law adopts a substance-over-form doctrine via General Anti-Avoidance Rules under Enterprise Income Tax Law Article 47, and through the anti-treaty-shopping beneficial ownership test under the STA tax treaty administration rules. A WFOE engaging in financial portfolio tracking services must perform its Core Income-Generating Activities in China, including strategic decision-making, R&D management, software maintenance, client contracting, risk management, and revenue booking; offshore shell entities without these functions, assets, and risks will be disregarded and the profits will be reattributed to the Chinese enterprise.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Transfer Pricing Manipulation and Offshore Profit Shifting

Statutory sanction: Enterprise Income Tax Law Article 41 mandates an arm's length adjustment; Article 47 allows GAAR recharacterization; implementing regulations allow the STA to increase taxable income, deny the deductions, charge interest, and impose a penalty of 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax under the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection Article 63.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false intercompany agreements or cause fraudulent invoicing may have the corporate veil pierced for restitution of the diverted assets, may be subjected to asset preservation and exit-control measures under LATC Article 44, and may be charged with criminal tax evasion under Criminal Law Article 201, resulting in fines and imprisonment of up to seven years for amounts exceeding the criminal threshold.
Treaty Shopping and Non-Beneficial Ownership Withholding Reduction

Statutory sanction: The STA will deny treaty benefits under the beneficial ownership principle and Public Notice [2019] No. 35, impose the statutory 10% dividend withholding, add a daily 0.05% late payment surcharge, and penalize the WFOE as withholding agent under LATC Article 69 in an amount ranging from 50% to 300% of the tax that should have been withheld.

Director personal liability: Directors who approve or execute the dividend payment knowing that the DTA claim is fraudulent are jointly responsible for the unpaid withholding tax; personal travel bans and blacklisting may be imposed to secure collection, and the directors may be prosecuted under Criminal Law Article 201 or General Fraud Provisions if the false tax treaty claim is proved, with imprisonment up to seven years.
Wilful Failure to Withhold on Outbound Dividends, Interest, and Royalties

Statutory sanction: Under the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection Article 69, a withholding agent that fails to withhold is liable for a penalty of 50% to 300% of the amount not withheld, together with the daily 0.05% surcharge; under Article 63, deliberate underdeclaration may be reclassified as tax evasion and penalized at 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax.

Director personal liability: Directors cannot hide behind the limited liability entity where they directed the unlawful non-withholding; civil restitution and asset freezing apply, exit-restraint and blacklisting prevent departure from China, and criminal prosecution under Criminal Law Article 201 may lead to detention, fines, and imprisonment of up to seven years for the directly responsible officers.
