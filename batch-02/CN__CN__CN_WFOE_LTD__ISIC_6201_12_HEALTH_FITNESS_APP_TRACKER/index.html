Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-up capital and no blocked escrow capital account is required for a WFOE engaged solely in ISIC 6201 computer programming and digital health/fitness app tracker services. The sole foreign shareholder shall fix the registered capital in the Articles of Association; the subscribed capital must be fully paid in within five years from the date of establishment unless the Articles of Association provide a shorter period, and every contribution must be remitted to a domestic capital account opened through a designated foreign exchange bank. Withdrawal or reduction of capital before lawful deregistration is prohibited; paid-in capital amounts must be reported in the annual investor investment information report to SAMR and the Ministry of Commerce.

Funding & unlock path: Not Applicable - a Civil Defense fire permit is not triggered for an office software company. The registered office must nevertheless be located in a building that has obtained the fire safety acceptance certificate required under the Fire Protection Law of the People's Republic of China, and the company must maintain fire extinguishers, emergency exits, evacuation lighting, no-smoking signage, and an emergency plan. The employer must fulfil the Work Safety Law obligations applicable to staffing and office premises, arrange occupational safety training for employees, and register any workplace injury in accordance with the Social Insurance Law; no special civil defense or explosive license is applicable.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA) and its unified State Taxation Administration Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax Bureau) online portal, together with the China International Trade Single Window for customs/export data filings.

E-government filing portal

After the WFOE is incorporated by the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR), the 18-character Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) printed on the business license is automatically the statutory Tax Identification Number (TIN) and VAT identification number for the WFOE; SAMR transmits the registration data to the STA. Within 30 calendar days after the business license issue date, the WFOE must complete supplementary tax registration through the Electronic Tax Bureau by confirming registered address, legal representative, finance personnel, bank account details, accounting rules and expected taxable business scope. The competent tax authority then completes the taxpayer profile, assigns VAT filing frequency and either leaves the WFOE as a small-scale VAT taxpayer or approves general VAT taxpayer status if election or mandatory threshold applies. A foreign non-resident shareholder is not issued a separate PRC VAT/TIN merely for holding equity; if the foreign shareholder has separate PRC-source taxable income, the local STA office opens a non-resident tax file number for withholding assessment upon submission of the prescribed foreign-entity documents.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 annual taxable sales (mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration threshold); VAT registration itself is triggered by incorporation and automatic USCC taxpayer identification, so no separate monetary registration threshold applies to a newly established WFOE.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For goods, 0% VAT applies when the WFOE files a customs export declaration, title/risk transfers to the overseas buyer, the goods physically leave the PRC customs territory, and export proceeds are repatriated. For qualifying cross-border digital services, including software services and information technology services consumed wholly outside the PRC and supplied to an overseas recipient, the 0% rate is available under Caishui [2016] No. 36 and subsequent STA implementing announcements, provided the service is not used within China, payment is received from outside the PRC in foreign exchange, and contemporaneous delivery/use evidence is maintained. Because the fitness tracking app is a digital health app, the WFOE should evidence offshore consumption through app-store download/geolocation data, foreign-customer contracts and usage analytics, and an inward foreign exchange receipt from Apple, Google or other overseas platform operators. The export rebate mechanism is implemented through the exempt-credit-refund method for general VAT taxpayers; zero-rating is not automatic and must be supported by the documentation bundle below.

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronic export declaration filed through the China International Trade Single Window, bearing the 18-digit export declaration number) for physical goods; for digital/software services, the corresponding export service registration and declaration record
  • Bill of Lading, air waybill or multimodal transport document evidencing physical export of goods; for the digital health app, server/delivery logs, app-store download reports and overseas user access records evidencing offshore supply
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or SAFE-designated bank export proceeds collection/verification voucher evidencing repatriation of the foreign currency receipt
  • VAT zero-rated invoice issued in the FDEI system, commercial invoice/contract, software license or service handover certificate, and if applicable platform sales reports from Apple IAP and Google Play Billing identifying offshore end-user consumption

China has mandated the fully digitalized electronic invoice (FDEI) system under the Golden Tax Phase IV architecture; taxpayers issue VAT invoices through the official e-invoice service platform or an STA-certified API mediation platform. Each FDEI receives a unique 20-digit invoice number and a cryptographic digital signature/verification code and is transmitted in real time to the national invoice database for fiscal clearance before it is legally issuable to the customer. Anti-fraud protection relies on national invoice authentication, red-letter offsetting invoice control, prohibition of duplicate invoice numbers, and automated cross-matching against customs, FX receipt and VAT filing data; paper-only or non-cleared private invoices do not satisfy the statutory invoicing mandate.

Export zero-rating and refund claims are subject to automated cross-matching among STA invoice data, customs export declarations, foreign exchange collection records and bank remittance evidence under the Golden Tax Phase IV risk-control system. If unverified, fraudulent or overstated export supplies are discovered, the STA may disallow the 0% treatment, claw back the full refunded VAT amount, assess daily late-payment interest and impose an administrative penalty of up to five times the tax fraudulently obtained. The taxpayer may also lose export-refund qualification and be named a serious untrustworthy taxpayer, with consequences for invoicing limits and continued cross-border operations. Cases involving intentional export-tax-refund fraud are referred under Article 204 of the Criminal Law and can result in terms of imprisonment, criminal fines and confiscation of property in addition to full civil restitution.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

RMB is fully current-account convertible for legitimate trade and service transactions, but capital-account transactions remain strictly regulated. A China-incorporated WFOE is treated as a resident enterprise and may receive FDI equity injections, convert proceeds through SAFE-registered banks, and make operational payments only within the negative-list framework. Dividend remittances to overseas shareholders are treated as current-account items and can be paid after annual audit, tax clearance, and board distribution resolution, provided the bank verifies there is no unreconciled capital or tax liability. Export of digital app telemetry services is classified as cross-border service trade and must be supported by authentic service agreements and settlement reports. Inbound FDI capital is subject to a real-need review; funds cannot be used for lending, securities investment, or purchase of unapproved real estate. The foreign investment negative list is periodically updated; a WFOE in the digital health software sector is eligible for foreign investment unless its activities cross into prohibited data services, gambling, or clinical medical practice. The overall liquidity posture is therefore open but evidence-driven: the WFOE can move operating cash and after-tax profits freely, while capital injections and debt financing are subject to registration, restricted use, and designated-bank channel requirements.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE Notice on Trade in Goods Foreign Exchange Administration, the WFOE must ensure export proceeds are repatriated into China within 90 days of shipment or service completion date. There is no fixed mandatory conversion ratio for genuine export receipts; the company may retain them in a designated foreign exchange account or voluntarily convert into RMB at market rates. For advance payments, the corresponding export or service acceptance must occur within 365 days; otherwise the advance must be classified as cross-border financing and obtain SAFE pre-registration. All export receipts must be declared through the Trade Monitoring System with the bank checking electronic customs, invoice, and contract records. Digital health app receipts may be treated as service trade proceeds and the supporting subcontracts and platform settlement reports must evidence the commercial reality.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE), the administrative arm delegated by the People's Bank of China (PBOC), exercising China's foreign exchange control powers.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Legal viability is very low for any offshore digital EMI serving as the WFOE's principal onshore bank. China does not issue EMI-style licenses; access to the domestic RMB clearing system requires a banking license or a central-bank payment-institution license. Foreign fintech platforms such as Wise, Revolut Business, or Airwallex cannot open mainland-based operating or capital accounts for a WFOE, cannot process SAFE-registered FDI settlements, and cannot issue CNY trade receipts acceptable for VAT and tax invoice purposes. At most, such platforms can be used as auxiliary offshore collection and payout gateways for overseas revenue, provided the WFOE fully discloses them to its resident bank and all settlement is ultimately routed through the domestic designated bank for tax and SAFE declaration. Recommending an offshore EMI as the principal treasury rail would violate PBOC and SAFE bank licensing requirements.

Root cause: Ultimate beneficial owner (UBO) opacity caused by layered offshore holding vehicles and nominee arrangements.

Defense evidence: Certified group structure chart from the ultimate shareholders through each intermediary entity; notarized certificates of incorporation and good standing for all holding vehicles; register of directors and shareholders; signed UBO declaration with passport copies and proof of residential address; source-of-wealth report showing legitimate investor income supported by three years of audited accounts or bank statements; board resolution of the WFOE confirming no nominee equity, no bearer shares, and no bearer warrants.
Mismatch between the remitting party and the registered shareholder in the inbound FDI capital injection.

Root cause: Mismatch between the remitting party and the registered shareholder in the inbound FDI capital injection.

Defense evidence: SAFE FDI registration confirmation with the receiving bank; original SWIFT MT103 or equivalent wire message showing the remitter name is identical to the direct registered shareholder; legal opinion from PRC-qualified counsel confirming the contribution is fully documented; the contributing shareholder's latest audited balance sheet and financial capacity calculation; capital commitment agreement; board resolution of the shareholder authorizing the remittance; certificate of the shareholder's bank signatories.
Digital health, biometric telemetry, and cross-border data flows could touch regulated personal information and trigger PIPL compliance red flags.

Root cause: Digital health, biometric telemetry, and cross-border data flows could touch regulated personal information and trigger PIPL compliance red flags.

Defense evidence: Data flow map demonstrating that personal health and fitness data of Chinese users is stored on servers in mainland China; PIPL-compliant privacy policy and separate consent wording for biometric and health data; data protection impact assessment and cross-border transfer security assessment records; evidence of compliance with critical information infrastructure protection requirements; legal opinion that the app does not engage in prohibited remote medical diagnostics; Chinese app store and ICP registration certificates if the app is distributed domestically.
Apple IAP and Google Play Billing revenue settlements create a tri-party commercial structure that may raise trade genuineness concerns under service trade foreign exchange monitoring.

Root cause: Apple IAP and Google Play Billing revenue settlements create a tri-party commercial structure that may raise trade genuineness concerns under service trade foreign exchange monitoring.

Defense evidence: Apple Developer Program agreement and Google Play Developer distribution agreement; monthly financial reports from App Store Connect and Google Play Console; reconciliation ledgers showing net settlements after platform fees; tax deduction and withholding receipts for any overseas platform service charges; service description and pricing catalog for the health fitness app; treasury officer letter explaining app-store commissions as platform fees separate from the WFOE's exported subscription services; confirmation that settlement uses standard cross-border payment rails with no cryptocurrency, virtual currency, or off-book e-wallet channel.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The only compliant route is a SWIFT MT103 wire from the direct overseas shareholder's corporate bank account to the WFOE's FDI capital foreign exchange account at a designated local bank. Before remittance, the WFOE must complete FDI foreign exchange registration at the receiving bank through the SAFE information system and obtain a registration reference to quote in the remittance instruction. The payment purpose code must be SAFE's inbound direct investment equity capital classification. The bank will enforce negative-list screening and verify that the capital is used for operating expenses only; conversion to RMB is made under the payment-for-use rule rather than free conversion. After settlement, the RMB funds are credited to the WFOE's basic deposit account. A licensed CPA in China should issue a capital verification report to substantiate the statutory capital contribution; the company may decide the paid-in schedule under its articles if no minimum capital is required, but any amount recorded in the corporate registry must be fully verified.Operational trade and service disbursements should be executed through SWIFT MT103 messages via the tier-1 bank current account for cloud hosting, software licensing, marketing, professional fees, and app-store commissions. The payment instruction must include the correct SAFE service trade purpose code and be backed by a contract, commercial invoice, and the resident tax certificate for cross-border withholding where applicable. For physical hardware purchases such as white-label fitness tracker devices, an irrevocable commercial letter of credit or documentary collection can be used on the goods-trade side; the bank will need transport documents and customs declarations to file in the Trade Monitoring System. Payments to Apple and Google platform entities should be accompanied by platform fee notes or invoices, reconciliation statements, and the relevant double-taxation treaty documentation to prevent transfer pricing challenges. All outbound disbursements are cleared only after the bank has screened the counterparty against sanctions and politically exposed persons lists.Plain-vanilla onshore CNY/USD forward contracts documented as forward settlement and sale through a designated domestic bank, maturities generally up to 12 months, supported by forecast account payables and receivables and underlying trade or service documents.CNY/USD cross-currency swaps and currency interest rate swaps used solely to align the currency basis between RMB operating costs and USD-denominated revenue, with the notional not exceeding projected cash flow and no speculative leverage.European-style FX options and risk reversals linked to physical sale or purchase of USD against RMB, subject to the bank's real-need verification and with no naked position if the underlying exposure disappears.Offshore non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are not permitted for a mainland WFOE to hedge its onshore RMB exposure; NDF usage is reserved for offshore holding entities and cannot be booked as a hedging instrument in the WFOE treasury without a separate SAFE-approved cross-border programme.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Legal viability is very low for any offshore digital EMI serving as the WFOE's principal onshore bank. China does not issue EMI-style licenses; access to the domestic RMB clearing system requires a banking license or a central-bank payment-institution license. Foreign fintech platforms such as Wise, Revolut Business, or Airwallex cannot open mainland-based operating or capital accounts for a WFOE, cannot process SAFE-registered FDI settlements, and cannot issue CNY trade receipts acceptable for VAT and tax invoice purposes. At most, such platforms can be used as auxiliary offshore collection and payout gateways for overseas revenue, provided the WFOE fully discloses them to its resident bank and all settlement is ultimately routed through the domestic designated bank for tax and SAFE declaration. Recommending an offshore EMI as the principal treasury rail would violate PBOC and SAFE bank licensing requirements.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File (Contemporaneous Transfer Pricing Documentation) is mandatory when annual related-party transactions of tangible assets exceed RMB 200 million, or intangible transfers, financial asset transfers, or service transactions each exceed RMB 100 million. A Master File is required when the multinational group's consolidated annual revenue exceeds RMB 2 billion.

Economic substance scope: China has not enacted an OECD/tax-haven-style Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) statute, but the Enterprise Income Tax Law, Article 47, codifies a General Anti-Avoidance Rule (GAAR) requiring substance over form. The State Taxation Administration applies an anti-conduit doctrine and beneficial-ownership analysis to deny treaty benefits to shell entities. A Chinese WFOE must demonstrate that core income-generating activities (CIGA) such as product development, biometric data processing, contractual execution, user on-boarding, and risk-taking functions occur in mainland China. Merely locating servers or cloud infrastructure in China is not sufficient to create allocable taxable income; the analysis will look to personnel, office premises, decision-making, and authoritative governance. Any profit allocation removing these functions from China, even through a foreign parent contract result, will be challenged and adjusted.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Transfer Pricing Manipulation

Statutory sanction: Under Enterprise Income Tax Law Articles 41 and 48, the Chinese tax authority may re-determine the taxable income, disallow deductions, and make retroactive adjustments for up to ten years (five years for ordinary TP); a penalty of 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax may be imposed under Tax Collection Law Article 63, plus daily interest of 0.05%.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly approve the artificial transfer-pricing policy will be jointly and severally liable for the resulting corporate tax shortfall under Chinese civil law principles of bad-faith management. Travel restrictions (exit blacklisting) under Tax Collection Law Article 44 may be issued, preventing the director from leaving mainland China until the debt is settled. If the evasion exceeds RMB 50,000 and 10% of tax due, criminal prosecution under Criminal Law Article 203 exposes the director to imprisonment for up to seven years.
False Invoicing and Fraudulent Revenue Understatement

Statutory sanction: Tax Collection Law Article 63 imposes payment of the additional tax plus a penalty of 50% to 500% of the underpaid amount. Where false VAT invoices are used, Criminal Law Article 205 provides for severe penalties, including up to fifteen years of imprisonment for aggravated false-invoicing offences. The tax authority may also disallow all related input tax deductions and impose daily late-payment interest.

Director personal liability: Directors who participate in or issue instructions regarding false records are personally liable for civil restitution of the company's losses and tax liabilities. The director's personal bank accounts and assets may be frozen; an exit ban travel restriction applies while the case is under investigation. Criminal liability may lead to a fixed-term imprisonment of up to seven years for tax evasion, potentially increased to ten years or more if the false-invoicing is deemed especially serious, and the director may be deprived of political rights and barred from serving as a company director.
Treaty Abuse / Shell Company Substance Deficiency

Statutory sanction: The Chinese tax authority will deny treaty relief under the beneficial-ownership test and the GAAR of Enterprise Income Tax Law Article 47. The WFOE must then withhold the full 10% statutory rate, plus late interest from the dividend date, and a penalty of 50% to 500% may be assessed for the false treaty-benefit claim. Public notices on anti-abuse (e.g., STA Public Notice on Beneficial Ownership) may further levy a separate penalty for intentionally misleading documentation.

Director personal liability: Directors who structured the Hong Kong conduit with knowledge of its sham nature are personally liable for the additional withholding tax and penalties. The directors may be blacklisted as 'serious tax violators' and banned from cross-border movements. Where the misuse is found to be tax evasion with a criminal element, the director faces up to seven years' imprisonment; the court can also order asset tracing and restitution using the 'piercing the corporate veil' doctrine, rendering the director's personal property reachable for the unpaid corporate tax and criminal fines.
