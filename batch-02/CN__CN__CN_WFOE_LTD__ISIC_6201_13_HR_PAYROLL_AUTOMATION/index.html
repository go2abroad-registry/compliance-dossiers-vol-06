Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

Virtual offices and commercial mailboxes are generally rejected for WFOE establishment and are not accepted by SAMR, tax authorities, or banks for the payroll/HR SaaS industry. A WFOE must maintain a physical business address evidenced by a lease agreement and a valid property ownership certificate or real estate registration certificate. The address must be the actual place of operations and must be shown on the business license; the tax authority and bank will require the same address for invoice registration and RMB/Foreign Currency bank account opening. Some Free Trade Zones and government-designated incubators allow licensed address hosting arrangements, but the arrangement must be confirmed in writing by the local SAMR, the tax authority, and the settlement bank, and the company must maintain a separate actual office for payroll data operations and staff compliance. For this reason, a pure virtual office is not permissible without express regulatory acceptance and confirmed bank/tax approval.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China, via the Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax@China) online portal at https://etax.chinatax.gov.cn

E-government filing portal

A foreign non-resident entity establishing a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE) must first obtain a business license from the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR). Following incorporation, the WFOE must register for tax identification with the local STA office within 30 days of the business license issuance. The process involves submitting the business license, articles of association, legal representative identification, and proof of registered address. The STA issues a unified social credit code (USCC) which serves as the Tax Identification Number (TIN) for all tax purposes, including VAT. For non-resident entities without a permanent establishment, a separate tax registration for withholding purposes is required via the 'Non-Resident Enterprise Tax Registration' procedure, obtaining a temporary tax ID for filing and payment obligations.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

For general VAT taxpayers, mandatory registration is required when annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5 million (approximately USD 700,000). Below this threshold, entities may register as small-scale taxpayers with a reduced rate of 3% (or 1% under certain temporary policies), but WFOEs engaged in cross-border services typically opt for general taxpayer status to claim input VAT credits and apply zero-rating on exports.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating (0% VAT) applies to exported goods and certain offshore services, including software and payroll automation services provided to non-resident clients, provided the services are consumed entirely outside China. The WFOE must be registered as a general VAT taxpayer and must maintain separate accounting for export sales. The service contract must clearly state the cross-border nature, and the recipient must be a non-resident enterprise without a Chinese establishment. For goods, the physical export must be completed through a Chinese customs port.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bill of Entry) - Form 1 (出口货物报关单)
  • Bill of Lading (B/L) or Airway Bill (AWB) evidencing physical shipment
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or Foreign Exchange Receipt Confirmation (银行收汇凭证) proving foreign currency repatriation
  • Export Contract and Commercial Invoice
  • VAT Invoice (special VAT invoice for export) issued under the ETRS
  • Customs Export Goods Verification Sheet (出口收汇核销单) - where applicable

China mandates the use of the 'Golden Tax System Phase IV' (GTS IV) for all VAT invoices. General VAT taxpayers must issue invoices through the official 'VAT Invoice Management System' (VIMS) which integrates with the STA's real-time fiscal clearance platform. All invoices must be digitally stamped with a unique anti-fraud QR code and transmitted to the STA's central database via API within 24 hours of issuance. The system enforces real-time reconciliation of invoice data, and any mismatch triggers automatic audit flags. For export transactions, the 'Export Tax Rebate System' (ETRS) requires electronic submission of export declarations and customs data to substantiate zero-rating claims.

The STA and the General Administration of Customs (GACC) conduct joint audits on export zero-rating claims. Any false declaration, forged documentation, or failure to repatriate foreign exchange within the 180-day deadline results in immediate clawback of the zero-rated benefit, plus a penalty of 50% to 500% of the tax evaded under Article 63 of the Tax Collection and Administration Law. Additionally, the entity may face suspension of export tax rebate rights for up to 3 years, criminal liability for fraud, and inclusion in the national tax integrity blacklist.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The renminbi (RMB) is fully convertible for current account transactions, including trade in goods and services, remittance of investment returns, and reasonable non-trade foreign exchange payments, subject to the mandatory presentation of authentic transactional documentation and a 'real trade background' verification by designated banks. On the capital account, the RMB remains strictly non-convertible: every cross-border capital flow, including foreign direct investment equity injections, shareholder loans, buy-back and exit proceeds, requires prior SAFE registration or case-by-case approval. A WFOE incorporated under Chinese law is treated as a domestic resident enterprise for foreign exchange purposes, meaning its entire financial balance sheet is within SAFE's monitoring regime. All foreign currency capital contributions must be routed into a designated capital settlement account; conversion into RMB for working capital is permissible only for approved business purposes. Profit remittances to offshore shareholders are permitted annually after statutory audit, but require no arrears in tax, proof of tax clearance, an audit report, and a SAFE-verified remittance application. Additional compliance considerations include the prohibition on using converted RMB capital for domestic equity investments, entrusted loans, or purchase of real estate beyond operational needs. Since 2020, pilot Free Trade Zone (FTZ) policies allow more liberalized cross-border capital pooling and a negative-list approach, but the default national regime remains administratively intensive. Payment settlements for payroll automation software subscriptions, if structured as cross-border service fees, are treated as current account items and thus more rapidly processable, but the underlying contract, invoices, and service authenticity must survive bank and SAFE review to avoid being retroactively re-characterized as a capital-account transaction.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE's Circular on Regulating Goods Trade Foreign Exchange Administration (Huifa [2019] No. 28), export proceeds from Chinese goods and technology sales must be repatriated into a domestic foreign currency settlement account within 180 calendar days from the date of export declaration. There is no mandatory conversion ratio; enterprises may retain the foreign currency in their settlement account or voluntarily convert to RMB at prevailing market rates through a designated bank. However, all repatriated proceeds must be supported by the corresponding customs declaration, commercial invoices, bills of lading, and proof of trade receipts for the authenticity review system. Failure to repatriate within the 180-day window subjects the enterprise to SAFE penalty, inclusion on the 'Prospective Monitoring Enterprise' list, and forced settlement of the unrepatriated amount. For WFOEs engaged in cross-border payroll automation, software licensing and SaaS service income are classified as service trade proceeds, which have a 90-day repatriation timeline under current service trade foreign exchange control guidelines, unless exempted under a special FTZ or cross-border e-commerce pilot policy. The same documentation standard applies: signed agreements, issued invoices, bank receipts, and an internal international balance of payments declaration (via the bank's IOP system).

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) and State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Fintech-enabled electronic money institutions (EMIs) such as Wise, Revolut, Airwallex, Currenxie, and Payoneer are not licensed as banking institutions in mainland China and thus cannot operate domestic current accounts, accept RMB deposits, or provide RMB clearing services directly for a WFOE. The People's Bank of China regulates payment services under the Administrative Measures for Non-Financial Institution Payment Services, which restricts deposit-taking and foreign exchange settlement to licensed banks. Offshore EMIs may be used solely as ancillary rails for cross-border receipts and disbursements in non-RMB currencies, but only after the WFOE's domiciled bank approves each outward/inward transit; all foreign exchange transactions must ultimately pass through a SAFE-authorized domestic bank for conversion and filing. The onboarding viability for a mainland WFOE with an offshore EMI is rated Low, legally constrained by the P.R.C. Foreign Exchange Control Regulations (2008) and the BOP Declaration requirements, which mandate that all cross-border trade and investment transactions be declared via the domestic banking system. Using an offshore EMI as the primary treasury account exposes the WFOE to fines, account freezing, and adverse compliance records. Hence, the treasury blueprint must prioritize relationships with Tier-1 domestic banks, with EMIs limited to holding small working capital pools outside the P.R.C. for paying foreign contractors, subject to internal treasury policy and transfer-pricing considerations.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Fintech-enabled electronic money institutions (EMIs) such as Wise, Revolut, Airwallex, Currenxie, and Payoneer are not licensed as banking institutions in mainland China and thus cannot operate domestic current accounts, accept RMB deposits, or provide RMB clearing services directly for a WFOE. The People's Bank of China regulates payment services under the Administrative Measures for Non-Financial Institution Payment Services, which restricts deposit-taking and foreign exchange settlement to licensed banks. Offshore EMIs may be used solely as ancillary rails for cross-border receipts and disbursements in non-RMB currencies, but only after the WFOE's domiciled bank approves each outward/inward transit; all foreign exchange transactions must ultimately pass through a SAFE-authorized domestic bank for conversion and filing. The onboarding viability for a mainland WFOE with an offshore EMI is rated Low, legally constrained by the P.R.C. Foreign Exchange Control Regulations (2008) and the BOP Declaration requirements, which mandate that all cross-border trade and investment transactions be declared via the domestic banking system. Using an offshore EMI as the primary treasury account exposes the WFOE to fines, account freezing, and adverse compliance records. Hence, the treasury blueprint must prioritize relationships with Tier-1 domestic banks, with EMIs limited to holding small working capital pools outside the P.R.C. for paying foreign contractors, subject to internal treasury policy and transfer-pricing considerations.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Contemporaneous Local File is mandatory when related-party tangible asset transfers exceed RMB 200 million, financial asset transfers exceed RMB 100 million, intangible asset transfers exceed RMB 100 million, or other aggregate related-party transactions such as intra-group services exceed RMB 40 million in a tax year. Master File preparation is triggered for multinational groups where cross-border related-party transactions exceed RMB 1 billion or the ultimate parent group satisfies the consolidated revenue threshold under SAT Announcement 2016 No. 42.

Economic substance scope: China does not apply a separate named Economic Substance Regulations regime comparable to offshore jurisdictions, but substance-over-form permeates the Enterprise Income Tax Law, the General Anti-Avoidance Rule under Article 47, the Beneficial Ownership tests in SAT Announcement 2018 No. 9, and the treaty abuse provisions under the Multilateral Instrument. For a payroll automation SaaS WFOE, Core Income-Generating Activities include software development, payroll computation, payment processing, data hosting, client contracting, compliance monitoring and risk assumption. These functions must be actually performed with employees, physical assets and decision-making capacity in China; an invoicing-only entity that receives service fees while foreign affiliates perform and control the real functions will be recharacterized.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Transfer Pricing Documentation Failure and Artificial Profit Shifting

Statutory sanction: The State Taxation Administration may make a deemed taxable income adjustment under Article 41 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law and impose interest equal to the RMB benchmark lending rate plus 5 percentage points under Article 48 in special tax adjustments. Under Articles 62 and 63 of the Tax Collection and Administration Law, failure to keep or provide documentation attracts fines up to RMB 20,000, while false under-declaration of taxable income attracts a penalty of 50%-500% of the underpaid tax.

Director personal liability: The legal representative, chief financial officer and actual controller may be blacklisted and prohibited from leaving China while tax arrears and penalties remain unpaid under the tax exit restriction procedure. If the directors signed fraudulent returns or concealed transfer pricing facts, Criminal Law Article 201 exposes them to imprisonment of up to 7 years. Courts may pierce the corporate veil where directors abused the WFOE separate personality to syphon assets and evade tax.
Treaty Shopping and Shell Company Beneficial Ownership Abuse

Statutory sanction: The claimed treaty benefit is denied under SAT Announcement 2018 No. 9 and SAT Announcement 2015 No. 60, and the WFOE is directed to withhold the full 10% statutory WHT on the outbound payment. The tax authority may also apply Article 47 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law to recharacterize the conduit arrangement, impose a 0.05% per-day late-payment surcharge and a 50%-300% penalty under the Tax Collection and Administration Law for failure to withhold.

Director personal liability: Directors who certify false beneficial ownership declarations or knowingly implement a conduit arrangement are personally exposed to civil recovery of under-withheld tax and interest. The responsible persons may be placed on the tax blacklist and banned from exiting China. Criminal exposure arises where false treaty certificates or forged board resolutions are used, leading to penalties and imprisonment for tax fraud or document forgery.
Unlawful Profit Repatriation and Withholding Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Under Article 69 of the Tax Collection and Administration Law, the withholding agent that fails to withhold is fined 50%-300% of the uncollected tax, and the tax itself is recovered from the non-resident beneficiary. If the withholding agent collects the tax but fails to remit it, Article 68 imposes a late-payment surcharge and a penalty of 50%-500% of the unpaid remitted tax. In addition, the disguised distribution is adjusted and reclassified as a dividend by the tax authority.

Director personal liability: The legal representative and the director who declared or approved the dividend can be jointly and severally liable for the unpaid withholding tax if they used their control to strip the company of distributable assets. The actual controller or responsible person may be subject to a travel ban, property seizure and transfer of the tax debt to shareholders through veil-piercing under the PRC Company Law. If the scheme is found to be intentional tax evasion by the responsible officer rather than mere negligence, Criminal Law Article 201 carries imprisonment up to 3 years initially and 3-7 years for egregious evasion, plus criminal fines on the entity.
