After the State Administration for Market Regulation issues the WFOE business licence containing the Unified Social Credit Code, the company must complete tax registration with the competent STA bureau within 30 days of establishment. Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Under PRC law, current account items are freely convertible in the sense that banks can process genuine trade and service payments after verifying contracts, invoices and customs documents; no prior SAFE approval is required for ordinary software exports or royalty payments. Capital account transactions, however, remain controlled: the WFOE must obtain SAFE foreign exchange registration for FDI, open a dedicated capital account, and every inbound capital contribution must be supported by a capital verification report before local utilisation. Profit remittance to the foreign shareholder is lawful once the WFOE has passed the annual audit, paid enterprise income tax, had a board resolution on dividend distribution and settled any withholding tax; SAFE and the bank will then release foreign currency at the prevailing market rate. Export earnings must be repatriated to a domestic bank within 180 days of customs declaration, but there is no mandatory surrender percentage; the enterprise can hold the foreign currency in its export proceeds account or sell it to the bank. The PBOC prohibits financial institutions from clearing or providing conversion services for crypto asset transactions, so USDC or stablecoin settlement is not a lawful treasury rail. In practice, the entity must keep a single documentary audit trail from underlying contracts, through SAFE systems, to bank settlements.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export receipts must be brought back into China and credited to a current-account foreign exchange bank account within 180 days from the date of export customs declaration, or within 180 days from the date stipulated in the export contract for open-account terms when the due date falls later. No minimum percentage must be converted; repatriated currency may be retained, settled, or swapped with the bank. Before payment, the bank checks the transaction against the Customs and Tax declaration and the SAFE monitoring list; any overdue collection beyond 180 days requires a written SAFE delayed-receipt filing with buyer correspondence, aging schedule, and legal opinions.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China, operating under the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Unavailable and legally prohibited for mainland CNY operations. Non-bank payment institutions licensed in China are closed-loop; foreign EMIs such as Stripe, Airwallex or Payoneer may offer cross-border collection from overseas, but they cannot open a local RMB settlement account or act as clearing bank for WFOE receipts inside China. Stripe does not support mainland China-domiciled merchants for domestic settlement; using a Hong Kong entity or offshore EMI creates unrepatriated receivables and breaks the SAFE audit trail. The PBoC and multiple Chinese regulators have declared crypto-asset related financial services illegal, so USDC or other stablecoin clearing rails cannot be used for treasury purposes. The only viable path is a licensed domestic commercial bank acting as custodian of both the foreign-currency capital account and the RMB operating account, with SWIFT, CIPS, and SAFE message flows.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Foreign investor telegraphically transfers registered capital from its corporate account to the WFOE's SAFE-coded capital account at the designated commercial bank, using SWIFT MT103 with the SAFE registration code in the narrative and remittance purpose stated as FDI capital contribution. The bank verifies the capital account credit and records it with SAFE; within the statutory period the WFOE engages a licensed PRC CPA to issue a capital verification report, after which funds may be transferred to an RMB general deposit account for operational use. Any use beyond the registered business scope, or for equity investment, requires a separate SAFE filing. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Unavailable and legally prohibited for mainland CNY operations. Non-bank payment institutions licensed in China are closed-loop; foreign EMIs such as Stripe, Airwallex or Payoneer may offer cross-border collection from overseas, but they cannot open a local RMB settlement account or act as clearing bank for WFOE receipts inside China. Stripe does not support mainland China-domiciled merchants for domestic settlement; using a Hong Kong entity or offshore EMI creates unrepatriated receivables and breaks the SAFE audit trail. The PBoC and multiple Chinese regulators have declared crypto-asset related financial services illegal, so USDC or other stablecoin clearing rails cannot be used for treasury purposes. The only viable path is a licensed domestic commercial bank acting as custodian of both the foreign-currency capital account and the RMB operating account, with SWIFT, CIPS, and SAFE message flows.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File or contemporaneous documentation must be prepared if annual related-party transactions exceed CNY 200,000,000 for tangible property transfers, CNY 100,000,000 for intangible property transfers, CNY 100,000,000 for financial asset transfers, or CNY 100,000,000 for related-party service provision. Master File must be prepared when aggregate annual related-party transactions reach CNY 1,000,000,000 or another statutory condition applies. Documentation must be produced within 30 days of a tax-authority demand and maintained according to SAT Announcement 2016 No. 42.

Economic substance scope: A WFOE must maintain real business substance through premises, employees, local decision-making, operational functions and risk assumption. China's GAAR under EIT Law Art. 47 permits recharacterization of transactions lacking commercial purpose. Where a BVI, Cayman or Hong Kong intermediate merely collects China-source API revenue with no Core Income-Generating Activities, it may lack economic substance in its claimed tax residence. Although Economic Substance Regulations in offshore jurisdictions are foreign statutes, Chinese tax authorities will apply domestic anti-avoidance, treaty abuse and beneficial-ownership doctrines to displace the structure.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion by False Cross-Border Service Payments and Falsified Invoicing

Statutory sanction: Under Tax Collection Administration Law Arts. 63-66, unpaid tax is collected with a daily late-payment surcharge of 0.05% and a fine of 50% to 500% of the evaded tax. Falsely issuing VAT special invoices is punished under Criminal Law Art. 205 with imprisonment up to life where the amount is particularly large.

Director personal liability: Under Company Law Art. 23, abuse of limited liability can lead to piercing the corporate veil. Directors and actual controllers may be jointly and severally liable for unpaid corporate tax and civil restitution. The legal representative may be blacklisted, exit-banned and exposed to imprisonment under Criminal Law Art. 201 for up to 7 years where intentional tax evasion reaches the criminal threshold.
Shell Company and Treaty Abuse Claiming DTA Benefits

Statutory sanction: The Chinese tax authority may apply the GAAR under EIT Law Art. 47 and the Special Tax Adjustment Measures, deny treaty benefits, recapture 10% withholding, impose special interest surcharges and issue fines of 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax.

Director personal liability: Directors and controlling shareholders can be held civilly liable for the WFOE's unpaid tax if the corporate form is abused. Travel bans, tax blacklisting and personal asset recovery are available under tax enforcement powers. A director who intentionally uses a shell to conceal taxable profits can face criminal tax-evasion charges carrying imprisonment up to 7 years.
Failure to Prepare and File Transfer-Pricing Documentation and Thin-Capitalization Violations

Statutory sanction: The tax authority may reprice intercompany transactions, deny interest deductions exceeding the thin-capitalization 2:1 debt-to-equity limit under EIT Law Art. 46, and treat excess payments as deemed dividends subject to 10% WHT. TP adjustments carry interest surcharges of 5 percentage points above benchmark rate, and a false annual return produces a RMB 2,000-10,000 late filing fine plus 50%-500% underpayment penalty.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false tax returns or knowingly control concealment are exposed to joint civil liability for the resulting tax shortfall. Legal representatives may face exit restrictions and tax blacklisting. Where related-party manipulation is deliberate and large-scale, criminal prosecution under Criminal Law Art. 201 can result in imprisonment up to 7 years and asset confiscation.
