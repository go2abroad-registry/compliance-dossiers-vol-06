Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A virtual office or commercial mailbox is not permissible as the registered address for a WFOE engaged in data center colocation and bare-metal server hosting. SAMR registration requires a genuine physical lease agreement, landlord ownership documentation, and a site inspection report demonstrating that the actual servers, cooling, power, and network equipment will be installed at the registered address. Local tax authorities require the registered address to be the actual place of business for VAT, corporate income tax, and invoice issuance; banks require physical presence for KYC and corporate settlement account opening. A virtual office will likely be rejected by banks and tax bureaus, and may be treated as false registration under market entity registration regulations, exposing the WFOE to fines, account freezing, and loss of qualified business status.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None. The PRC Company Law does not require a minimum paid-up registered capital for a general WFOE limited liability company. The registered capital amount and the contribution schedule are recorded in the articles of association. Capital contributions must be in the subscribing shareholder's own name, denominated in RMB or a convertible currency, remitted into a dedicated capital contribution bank account opened in the company's name at an authorised bank in China, and verified by the receiving bank. There is no statutory requirement for an escrow or blocked account at the stage of incorporation, but once a capital account is opened, funds cannot be withdrawn except for lawful operational purposes and must be reported to the State Administration of Foreign Exchange through the bank.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA); online portal: National Electronic Tax Service Platform (e-Tax, https://etax.chinatax.gov.cn)

E-government filing portal

For the WFOE, after obtaining a business license from the Administration for Market Regulation, the enterprise automatically receives a Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) which functions as the tax identification number (TIN). Within 30 days of incorporation, the entity must complete tax registration through the e-Tax portal, uploading the business license, corporate articles, legal representative identity documents, and bank account information. The system will assign VAT general/small-scale taxpayer status based on the projected annual turnover. For a foreign non-resident entity without a permanent establishment in China, apply for tax registration at the local STA bureau where the payer is located or through the non-resident tax code procedure under the applicable double tax treaty, submitting the certificate of residence and income tax withholding registration forms.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export of goods is zero-rated upon submission of the export customs declaration and proof of foreign exchange settlement. For services, zero-rating applies only to offshore services that are fully consumed outside China (post-2018 VAT reform). Data center colocation leasing is generally a domestic service subject to standard VAT rates; however, if the contract is with an offshore non-resident enterprise and the server equipment is located in a comprehensive bonded zone or the service is physically delivered abroad, it may qualify for zero-rating. The statutory tests are: (1) service is performed outside China; (2) both service recipient and usage are located outside China; (3) payment is in foreign currency and repatriated; and (4) no domestic taxable consumption occurs.

  • Customs Export Declaration Form (export customs declaration record with customs seal)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (showing the goods are physically leaving China)
  • Foreign Exchange Settlement Certificate (Inward Remittance Certificate / export foreign exchange verification form)

China mandates the issuance of all VAT invoices through the official electronic invoice system (e-Fapiao) integrated with the National e-Tax system. Special VAT invoices require cryptographic tax-control equipment or the fully digitalized e-invoice platform with real-time data reporting to the STA. There is no optional offline issuance; invoice data is cleared automatically and matched to VAT returns. Anti-fraud measures include digital signatures, a national tax-control chip, and strict invoice quota controls.

Under China's export tax refund administration, any false or unsubstantiated zero-rating claim is deemed tax fraud. The tax authority may claw back the entire VAT refund, impose a penalty ranging from 50% to 5 times the refund amount, suspend export tax refund eligibility, and transfer criminal liability to the Public Security Bureau for prosecuted cases. The statutory audit statute of limitations is 10 years for cases involving fraudulent refund or tax evasion, and the company will be downgraded on the tax credit rating scale, restricting invoice quotas and export filing.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China maintains separate current-account and capital-account convertibility regimes. Current-account transactions, including trade in goods and services, dividends, royalties and operating remittances, are generally convertible subject to authentic documentation and SAFE reporting; banks operate on a verify-before-process basis using the Trade Monitoring System. Export proceeds are no longer subject to mandatory conversion quotas; a WFOE may retain foreign currency in its domestic foreign exchange settlement account or sell it to a licensed bank at the prevailing market rate, provided that the export documentary trail, customs declarations and receipt records are complete. Capital-account transactions, including equity injection by the overseas parent, foreign debt and liquidation or profit distribution, require SAFE registration, dedicated capital accounts, and compliance with the negative list for foreign investment. The principal profit remittance hurdle is not exchange-control approval but proof of distributable profits: audited statutory financial statements, tax clearance, shareholder resolution and bank-level distribution reporting. The RMB exchange rate is managed with reference to a basket of currencies, and onshore spot transactions observe daily fluctuation bands. Surrender requirements have been liberalized; the PBC and SAFE may still impose macro-prudential adjustment factors on cross-border financing and short-term foreign debt, but ordinary trade proceeds are not forcibly converted.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no fixed mandatory conversion ratio for export foreign currency receipts in China today. Under the goods trade foreign exchange administration framework, an A-class enterprise must repatriate export proceeds into its domestic foreign exchange settlement account within 180 days from the customs export declaration date, or within the extended period accepted by SAFE for valid commercial reasons, after which the funds may either be retained in foreign currency or converted into RMB at any licensed bank. B-class and C-class enterprises are subject to stricter bank verification, documentary reconciliation and, where required, SAFE confirmation before conversion or repatriation.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBC); State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low as a primary account-holding rail. Foreign digital EMI providers such as Revolut, Airwallex and Currencycloud are not licensed by the PBC or SAFE as deposit-taking institutions and cannot legally maintain a settlement or clearing account in the name of a PRC WFOE. They may be used only as auxiliary cross-border payment gateways through a licensed Chinese payment institution or bank, and even then their services are more relevant to e-commerce receipts than to the monthly ACH/SWIFT treasury operations of a data center colocation business. The WFOE must maintain its core settlement and capital accounts at a PRC commercial bank while using correspondent banking corridors for foreign-currency flows.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The overseas parent must first complete SAFE foreign direct investment (FDI) basic information registration through the receiving PRC bank. The WFOE opens a capital-account-based FDI settlement account at the chosen tier-1 bank. The parent then remits the registered capital via SWIFT MT103 with the beneficiary defined as the WFOE, the beneficiary bank account tagged to the SAFE capital account, and the payment purpose field referencing the FDI registration code and the purpose label 'Registered Capital / FDI Capital Injection'. The receiving bank validates the transaction against the capital account policy, ensures the underlying BOP code is a capital-account direct investment code rather than a current-account trade code, and records the receipt. After the funds clear, a PRC-registered accounting firm issues the capital verification report confirming paid-in capital. Subsequent RMB settlement of the foreign-currency capital is allowed for bona fide operating expenditures under the FDI capital settlement negative list, which prohibits use for prohibited investments, non-self-use real estate, and unauthorised equity investment.For monthly rental collections and ordinary domestic vendor disbursements, the WFOE can use China's ACH-style small-amount bulk payment system with approved batch files. For overseas tenants or cross-border working-capital vendor payments, the primary rail is SWIFT MT103 backed by invoices, purchase orders and customs declarations. For high-value imports of server equipment, precision cooling systems and electrical infrastructure, an irrevocable commercial letter of credit (MT700) issued by the PRC bank is the preferred settlement rail because it protects both the WFOE and the equipment vendor. For standardized machinery or repeated component purchases, documentary collections (CAD/DP) can be used where shipping title documents are transferred through banking channels. All outbound trade payments must be reported under the correct BOP transaction code, reconciled with import verification records and cleared through either the CIPS network for RMB or the SWIFT correspondent banking system for USD, EUR and other convertible currencies.Onshore deliverable CNY/USD forward contracts with genuine trade or capital exposure, supported by SAFE-validated documentary evidence and bank ISDA documentationOnshore FX swaps and rolling forwards used to align the timing of foreign-currency capital injection, monthly RMB lease collections and scheduled overseas capex disbursementsCross-currency interest rate swaps matched to long-term data center financing where the notional and tenor cover only the underlying loan or lease liabilityNon-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are not a permissible instrument for the onshore WFOE; any NDF-based hedge must be executed by the non-resident parent outside China and cannot involve conversion or settlement through the WFOE's onshore capital account

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% on dividends distributed by the WFOE to a non-resident enterprise shareholder where the dividends are paid from post-CIT profits, unless the applicable Double Tax Agreement (DTA) provides a reduced rate.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Provisional CIT returns and quarterly payments are due within 15 days after the end of each quarter. The annual return must be filed and the final settlement completed within 5 months after the fiscal year-end, i.e., by May 31 for a calendar-year WFOE.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File contemporaneous documentation is mandatory if annual related-party tangible asset transactions equal or exceed RMB 200 million; if intangible property transfer/licensing reaches RMB 100 million; if financial asset transactions reach RMB 100 million; or if any other related-party transactions reach RMB 40 million. A Master File is required when the total annual related-party transactions exceed RMB 1 billion, or under the SAT's special adjustment rules where the enterprise's related-party transaction amount is material relative to its scale.

Economic substance scope: The PRC Enterprise Income Tax Law applies a substance-over-form doctrine; the WFOE must demonstrate that its core income-generating activities (CIGA) are performed in China, including active oversight of data center rack capacity, client onboarding, power and network operation, troubleshooting, billing, and contract management. For DTA and anti-abuse purposes, a WFOE that is merely a booking shell, with directors in the parent state and all operational decisions made offshore, may be disregarded under the General Anti-Avoidance Rule (Article 47-48 of the EIT Law) and SAT guidelines. To claim the reduced dividend withholding rate, the foreign ultimate parent must separately have real economic substance as owner of the shares, not merely hold a nominee or conduit interest. The data center colocation business itself must also maintain physical presence, utility contracts, employment records, insurance policies, and board meetings in China.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Bookkeeping of Data Center Revenue

Statutory sanction: Under Article 63 of the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection (TCAL), tax authorities shall recover the underpaid CIT and impose a penalty of 50% to five times the tax underpaid, in addition to daily late surcharges of 0.05%. If the underpayment is deliberately concealed, it constitutes criminal tax evasion under PRC Criminal Law Article 201, exposing the entity to fines and imprisonment of up to seven years for those criminally liable.

Director personal liability: Directors, general managers and actual controlling persons may be personally liable where the corporate veil is pierced because the WFOE is a mere instrument or was used fraudulently; company assets, personal assets and restitution for unpaid taxes and interest may be reached. The legal representative may be placed on the tax authority's non-payer blacklist, which triggers travel restrictions, exit bans by border authorities, and disqualification from serving as senior management in any other Chinese entity.
Transfer Pricing and Economic Substance Abuse (Shell Structure)

Statutory sanction: Under Article 41 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law and the General Anti-Avoidance Rule in Articles 47 and 48, the tax authority may re-characterize the transactions, disregard the artificial structure, adjust taxable income, and assess additional CIT plus special tax adjustment interest. Where the arrangement is accompanied by false invoices or deliberate concealment, TCAL Article 63 imposes a 50% to five-times penalty and Criminal Law Article 201 may apply. The failure to prepare Local/Master File documentation when the threshold is met is separately penalized under TCAL and may be treated as non-compliance with the arm's length standard.

Director personal liability: A finding of sham status permits tax authorities and courts to pierce the corporate veil and recover underpaid tax from shareholders and directors who participated in the artificial structure; civil asset restitution from personally held property is available. The legal representative and relevant directors are also subject to the joint disciplinary blacklist, travel bans on leaving Chinese mainland, and potential criminal liability for evasion where the TP abuse was knowingly concealed.
Withholding Tax Evasion and DTA Benefit Fraud on Outbound Dividends, Interest and Royalties

Statutory sanction: Under Article 69 of TCAL, a withholding agent that fails to withhold and remit tax is liable to recover the tax and to a penalty of 50% to three times the tax not withheld; late surcharges continue to accrue. The enterprise may also be denied deduction of the underlying interest or royalty, and the non-resident's treaty relief claim may be retrospectively denied with full tax and interest assessed. Where the WFOE deliberately uses layered offshore entities and false documentation to evade WHT, the conduct is a criminal offense with imprisonment up to seven years under Criminal Law Article 201, and the movement of the funds abroad may separately trigger money-laundering liability under Criminal Law Article 191.

Director personal liability: Directors who authorize the unwithheld distribution or sign false beneficial ownership declarations can be held personally liable after piercing of the corporate veil, especially where the distribution renders the WFOE insolvent or the restructuring existed to strip assets. Personal civil restitution covers unpaid WHT, penalties, surcharges and interest; the relevant individuals are Blacklisted by the tax and foreign exchange authorities, restrained from traveling outside China, and face imprisonment for tax fraud or laundering the repatriated profits if criminal fraud is established.
