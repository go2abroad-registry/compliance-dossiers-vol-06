Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

For a WFOE engaging in single-family-office and private investment holding activities, a virtual office, commercial mailbox, or bare registered address is not permissible and will be rejected by the local SAMR, the tax authority, and the commercial bank. PRC company law requires an identifiable and usable domicile, and bank account onboarding requires proof of a physical lease, property ownership documentation or landlord consent, an entrance sign, and the actual presence of management or personnel. Virtual office arrangements are treated as evidence of shell-company risk and will trigger enhanced UBO disclosure, a likely refusal to issue VAT invoices, and a probable administrative penalty for falsely declaring the registered address. If an executive business center is used, it must be a licensed shared-office provider with a genuine service agreement, dedicated desk or meeting-room space, reception facilities, and the WFOE must maintain its books, records, corporate seals, and at least one responsible employee at that address.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum registered capital is prescribed for a WFOE used as a passive Single Family Office holding vehicle. The sole shareholder must subscribe for the full registered capital in the Articles of Association, and under Article 47 of the Company Law all capital contributions must be paid in full within five years from incorporation unless a shorter period is required by administrative regulation. There is no blocked escrow-account requirement at state level; however, inbound foreign-currency capital contributions must be routed through a SAFE-registered capital account after foreign exchange registration is completed and cannot be made directly through ordinary settlement accounts.

Funding & unlock path: Not Applicable as a manufacturing or warehousing permit. As an office-only tenant, the WFOE must obtain or confirm the landlord's building fire safety acceptance certificate, maintain basic fire extinguishers and emergency signage, and register the business office with local fire rescue authorities if required; no separate industrial Civil Defense fire license is triggered.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China, administered by provincial and municipal tax service bureaux; electronic compliance is transacted through the China Electronic Taxpayer (e-Taxation) platform operated by STA and its regional portals.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: After incorporation, the WFOE receives an 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) from the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR); this USCC is the statutory tax identification number for all PRC resident legal persons. Step 2: Within 30 days of obtaining the business license, the company must complete tax registration on the China Electronic Taxpayer platform by filing entity information, registered address, legal representative, registered capital, bank account details, accounting system, and intended tax types. Step 3: The local competent tax authority confirms registration and assigns tax type statuses for Corporate Income Tax (CIT), Value-Added Tax (VAT), stamp duty, urban maintenance and construction tax, education surcharges, and Individual Income Tax withholding for employees. Step 4: For a foreign non-resident enterprise that has not created a WFOE but derives PRC-source income, the non-resident enterprise obtains a non-resident tax identification number from the competent SAT office or through a qualified PRC withholding agent using the same electronic taxpayer platform; the application must include legal existence evidence, foreign tax residence certificate, contract or income source documents, and completed non-resident tax registration forms in the STA system.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None. The PRC does not maintain a check-the-box or equivalent entity classification election. A WFOE is automatically a PRC tax resident enterprise and is taxable at entity level on its worldwide income; no pass-through or transparent election is available for a Chinese limited liability company. Hybrid mismatch elections are not part of the PRC domestic law framework; any perceived hybrid outcomes are addressed by the general anti-abuse rule, transfer pricing rules, and controlled foreign corporation rules during audit.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 annual VAT taxable sales is the statutory threshold for mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration. A foreign-invested WFOE must complete tax registration regardless of turnover, but below this threshold it may remain a small-scale taxpayer. The WFOE's family office and investment management services, where taxable, generally fall under the VAT category of modern services at 6%; the 13% standard headline rate applies to sales of goods. Passive investment holding and dividend or interest income do not attract VAT until separately charged as a management fee.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

PRC VAT general taxpayers may claim the 0% export VAT rate only when goods are properly declared to Customs and physically exported from PRC customs territory, or when a supply is a cross-border taxable service or intangible asset designated under the VAT zero-rating rules and supplied to a non-resident recipient with the service wholly consumed outside the PRC. For a WFOE single family office, passive family asset custody and private investment holding are not automatically zero-rated; the tax authority must confirm eligibility for any designated offshore service with a written qualification determination, supported by a cross-border service contract, invoice, bank foreign-exchange receipt, and proof that the overseas family or entity is the beneficial consumer outside China.

  • China Customs Export Goods Declaration (Export Customs Declaration Form) bearing an accepted export declaration number; the Indian-style 'Bill of Entry' designation is not a PRC statutory document name.
  • Ocean Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, or rail/road multimodal transport document evidencing actual physical exit from the PRC customs territory.
  • Commercial export invoice, packing list, and signed export sales contract reflecting the overseas buyer and agreed payment terms.
  • Bank foreign-exchange receipt or SAFE-designated bank export proceeds collection confirmation; in PRC practice the 'Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC)' nomenclature is not used, and the required evidence is the bank-issued inward foreign exchange income declaration or cross-border RMB settlement receipt.
  • For cross-border services, the cross-border service agreement, service export invoice, and Trade in Services Foreign Exchange Income and Expense Declaration filed through the SAFE reporting channel, together with any SAT approval notice confirming zero-rating eligibility.

PRC VAT invoices are governed by the STA Golden Tax Phase IV system and the nationwide fully digitalized electronic invoice framework. All VAT special invoices must be issued through the STA-approved invoice management system or the China Electronic Taxpayer platform. Each e-invoice carries a unique invoice code and digital anti-counterfeit signature; issuance data is transmitted to SAT and cross-matched with VAT returns, Customs declarations, and bank foreign-exchange data. There is no physical tax-office pre-clearance stamp for each invoice, but the system blocks issuance to non-qualified sellers, applies the correct tax classification code, and suspends input tax credits on abnormal or unmatched invoices until verification.

The STA, General Administration of Customs, and SAFE perform automated tripartite data matching for every export zero-rating claim. If the export proceeds are not received or waived through an approved exemption within the 180-day foreign-exchange collection period, the claiming enterprise is recharacterized as making a domestic taxable sale; the previously refunded VAT and input credits are clawed back, the applicable VAT plus urban maintenance and construction tax and education surcharges must be paid, and late payment surcharge accrues at 0.05% per day. Where export refund fraud is established under Article 66 of the PRC Tax Collection Administration Law, the tax authority recovers the fraudulently obtained refund and imposes a fine of one to five times the amount, and may suspend the enterprise's export refund processing. If the amount crosses criminal thresholds, the responsible individuals may face criminal liability under Article 204 of the Criminal Law, with escalating imprisonment and fines or asset confiscation.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Chinese yuan (RMB) is not freely convertible on the capital account, while current account transactions for trade in goods and services are generally convertible under SAFE verification. Capital account transactions, including foreign direct investment (FDI), external borrowings, and outbound investments, require prior regulatory approval, registration, or filing with SAFE. A WFOE engaged in single-family office investment holding must secure an FDI registration certificate and a SAFE registration for its capital account. Profit remittances are permitted for genuine historical earnings provided the enterprise has completed annual compliance filings, audited financial statements, tax clearance, and verified the source of the distributable profits. Dividends can be remitted abroad in RMB-converted foreign currency, but each remittance requires supporting documentation including board resolutions, interim audit reports, and tax payment proof. Capital injection funds must be lodged in a designated capital escrow account (FDI settlement account), and the use of these funds is strictly limited to the approved business scope and cannot be transferred into onshore securities markets without special clearance. The corporation must demonstrate legitimate investor identities, transparent upstream funding sources, and adherence to the negative list for foreign investment. Since this entity is in a restricted category of private investment holding, it is not eligible for automatic current account convertibility and must maintain a robust, audit-verified trail of each cross-border flow to satisfy both PBOC and SAFE requirements.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export proceeds must be repatriated into China through designated foreign currency accounts or domestic settlement accounts within a monitoring period generally aligned with the standard 180-day trade settlement cycle. For current account receipts, the enterprise has the option to retain the foreign currency in an offshore or onshore foreign exchange settlement account, but all inflows must be reported under the SAFE trade monitoring system. Since 2012, mandatory surrender obligations have been abolished, but the enterprise must complete a bank-assisted receipt verification process, and any excess holding beyond normal operational needs may trigger SAFE inquiry. For the WFOE single family office, if export operations are not the core business, the actual trading activities will be zero, said rule applies only to export-heavy subsidiary operations.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) and State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low viability with legal constraints. Foreign fintech electronic money institutions (EMIs) are not licensed to open domestic bank accounts or provide deposit-taking services for entities in mainland China. Non-bank payment institutions must hold a local payment business permit, which is granted only to qualified Chinese companies, and they cannot operate as a full-scale commercial bank. For cross-border settlements, a WFOE must rely on domestic tier-1 banks for all RMB and FX clearing. Offshore EMIs can only act as ancillary prepaid tool providers for certain employee expense scenarios, not as the primary treasury clearing rail. Any attempt to route capital injections or profit dividends through an EMI will be rejected by SAFE and may trigger anti-money laundering sanctions.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Ultimate beneficial owner (UBO) opacity for a single-family office with multi-jurisdictional asset holding layers

Root cause: Ultimate beneficial owner (UBO) opacity for a single-family office with multi-jurisdictional asset holding layers

Defense evidence: A complete corporate org chart from the WFOE up to the ultimate individual beneficiaries, including certified certificates of incorporation, memorandum and articles of association, shareholder registers, register of directors, and notarized identity documents of all natural persons holding 10% or more equity or effective control. Provide third-party legal opinion from a reputable international law firm confirming the chain of ownership and absence of anonymous shell entities.
Source of wealth and source of funds for high-net-worth family assets to be injected as FDI capital

Root cause: Source of wealth and source of funds for high-net-worth family assets to be injected as FDI capital

Defense evidence: Audited financial statements from the originating family trust or holding vehicle for the past three years, bank statements showing the historical accumulation of funds, sale agreements or inheritance certificates for any major asset sales, tax payment receipts, and a professionally prepared source-of-wealth report. All documents must be certified by a CPA and, if sourced from a foreign jurisdiction, legalized or apostilled and translated into Chinese by a sworn translator.
Adverse media or politically exposed person (PEP) linkage associated with the family office founder

Root cause: Adverse media or politically exposed person (PEP) linkage associated with the family office founder

Defense evidence: A full adverse media search report from a recognized investigation vendor (e.g., Dow Jones, Refinitiv) covering at least a five-year lookback period, an enhanced PEP screening report, a written explanation letter from the client addressing any hits, and, if applicable, evidence of termination of any questionable business relationships. For any residual PEP risk, a board resolution appointing a designated local AML compliance officer and implementing enhanced transaction monitoring will be required.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

FDI capital injection must be routed from the overseas parent or family trust bank account via international SWIFT MT103 payment into the WFOE's designated capital escrow account opened with a tier-1 Chinese bank. The remitter must exactly identify the WFOE and the SAFE FDI registration number, and the payment purpose code must be 'Investment - New Paid-in Capital' (code typically 91001). The bank will perform a capital verification and coordinate with the local Administration for Market Regulation to ensure the capital is registered. All foreign currency received in the capital account must be converted to RMB through the designated bank's FX system, with a capital application form submitted to SAFE and monitored against the approved capital amount. If the injection includes intellectual property or other non-cash assets, an asset appraisal report from a licensed Chinese appraiser and a supplementary verification through SAFE are mandatory.Approved operational trade settlements should be executed using SWIFT MT103 messages for supplier payments under a verified commercial invoice and import customs declaration. For larger and riskier transactions, the WFOE may open an import Letter of Credit (L/C) with its tier-1 bank, providing standardized terms including INCOTERMS and required transport documents. Documentary collections are acceptable where the counterparty risk is low and the underlying trade contract is clearly documented. All outbound payments need a tax declaration confirmation from the local tax bureau, and for service imports, the relevant tax registration certificate must be shown to prevent money laundering. For family office external management and professional fees, payments must be routed through the basic operational account with careful purpose descriptions consistent with the approved business license.Onshore deliverable forward foreign exchange contracts (FX forwards) with domestic authorized banks to hedge actual trade or investment exposure. These must be linked to a genuine underlying transaction approved by SAFE.Foreign currency swap agreements (FX swaps) for managing liquidity mismatches between RMB and foreign currency positions, strictly for trade financing or capital account settlement needs, subject to SAFE documentation.Cross-currency interest rate swaps (CCIRS) are permissible only for hedging medium-term foreign currency loan exposures and must have a maturity-matched debt service obligation.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are generally prohibited onshore for Chinese entities, so treasury operations should not execute NDF contracts; any hedging of RMB must be done via deliverable forwards at a locally licensed bank.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low viability with legal constraints. Foreign fintech electronic money institutions (EMIs) are not licensed to open domestic bank accounts or provide deposit-taking services for entities in mainland China. Non-bank payment institutions must hold a local payment business permit, which is granted only to qualified Chinese companies, and they cannot operate as a full-scale commercial bank. For cross-border settlements, a WFOE must rely on domestic tier-1 banks for all RMB and FX clearing. Offshore EMIs can only act as ancillary prepaid tool providers for certain employee expense scenarios, not as the primary treasury clearing rail. Any attempt to route capital injections or profit dividends through an EMI will be rejected by SAFE and may trigger anti-money laundering sanctions.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File (contemporaneous documentation) is mandatory when the aggregate value of related-party transactions in one of the following categories exceeds: (a) tangible asset transfers (excluding within-China transactions) RMB 200 million; (b) intangible asset transfers RMB 100 million; (c) financial asset transfers RMB 100 million; (d) any other related-party transactions RMB 40 million. Master File is required when the group's consolidated turnover exceeds RMB 5 billion and the group begins cross-border related-party transactions within China.

Economic substance scope: Although China does not have an Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) regime analogous to the UAE, general anti-avoidance rules under Article 47 of the CIT Law and Article 120 of the Implementation Regulations apply. Transactions lacking commercial substance and with core income-generating activities (CIGA) located outside China may be recharacterised. A Chinese WFOE holding passive family assets must demonstrate real management and decision-making in China (board meetings, office premises, local employees) to preserve its tax domicile and avoid being treated as a shell conduit subject to 10% WHT on outbound payments under the anti-abuse doctrine.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Declarations

Statutory sanction: Article 63 of the Tax Collection Administration Law (PRC): penalty of 50% up to 500% of the underpaid tax. Article 201 of the Criminal Law: fines and imprisonment of up to 7 years where the amount evaded exceeds RMB 100,000 or constitutes more than 10% of the tax payable.

Director personal liability: Civil: court-ordered restitution of the evaded tax and penalties, personal asset freezing/attachment, and inclusion in the State Taxation Administration's blacklist triggering a high-level travel ban (exit restriction). Criminal: the legal representative and accountant in charge may be personally prosecuted with up to 7 years imprisonment for tax fraud.
Shell Company / Treaty Shopping / Conduit Abuse

Statutory sanction: Article 47 CIT Law (GAAR) and Article 120 Implementation Regulations: tax authority may recharacterise the payment and disallow treaty relief. Retrospective re-collection of 10% WHT on all outbound payments, plus interest at the lending benchmark rate; additional penalty up to 50% of the re-collected tax under Article 63 (penalty for underpayment).

Director personal liability: Civil: directors who knowingly implemented the conduit arrangement are jointly and severally liable for the additional tax and penalties. Travel restriction (clearance to depart) will be imposed until the outstanding tax is settled. Criminal: if the recharacterisation leads to a determined underpayment exceeding RMB 100,000, directors may be prosecuted for tax evasion under Article 201 with up to 7 years imprisonment.
AML / Undisclosed Offshore Accounts and Unreported Foreign Assets

Statutory sanction: Article 10 of the Anti-Money Laundering Law (PRC): penalties of RMB 2 million - 10 million for institutions and up to RMB 1 million for liable individuals; confiscation of illegal gains. Foreign exchange control violations under SAFE Regulations attract fines of up to 30% of illegal FX transactions. For tax laundering, Article 191 of the Criminal Law applies.

Director personal liability: Civil: all concealed assets are restituted, bank accounts frozen, and the company and directors are blacklisted across the tax and banking systems; travel ban issued under the Exit-Entry Administration Law. Criminal: directors may receive 5-10 years imprisonment for money laundering or 3-7 years for evasion of foreign-exchange controls, plus personal fines equal to twice the amount laundered.
