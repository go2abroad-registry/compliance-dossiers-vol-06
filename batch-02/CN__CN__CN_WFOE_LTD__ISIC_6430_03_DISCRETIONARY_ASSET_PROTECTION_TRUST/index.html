Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A virtual office or commercial mailbox is generally insufficient for the establishment of a WFOE in China. Under the PRC Company Law and market registration rules, the registered office must be the actual principal place of business; SAMR and local tax authorities may reject a virtual office or mail-forwarding address, and the public security bureau requires a physical plaque-bearing location for the company seal and registration. For trust and fiduciary administration, PBOC and the National Financial Regulatory Administration apply the highest substantive presence standard: written policies, meeting minutes, risk records, trust account records and sealed agreements must be physically accessible at the registered office; a virtual office that lacks staff, fixed equipment, original records and the capability to receive regulatory inspectors will be deemed non-compliant. The WFOE must lease or own a physical office within the registered administrative region before filing incorporation.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

The PRC Company Law does not impose a statutory minimum paid-up capital for a WFOE limited liability company. The registered capital must be subscribed in the Articles of Association, and the sole shareholder must remit the subscribed amount into the company's capital verification account in accordance with the contribution schedule in the Articles (usually within 120 days of issuance of the business license). There is no mandatory escrow account under general corporate law. However, because the ISIC 6430 scope is fiduciary administration of trusts, the competent financial regulator requires the WFOE to maintain a minimum regulatory operating capital of RMB 10 million, of which at least RMB 2 million must be actually paid-in before the financial fiduciary license is issued, held in a dedicated bank escrow account subject to regulatory release.

Funding & unlock path: The WFOE must obtain a Civil Defense Fire Safety Acceptance Certificate (建设工程消防验收备案) referring to the specific leased office floor and the building's first-responder survey, confirming compliance with fire safety standards under the Fire Protection Law of the PRC. A structural load inspection is not required because no warehouse or production machinery is installed, but the lessor's building safety certificate must be retained on file. The company must further complete a workplace safety registration with the local Emergency Management Bureau, implement a fire evacuation plan, obtain a work-safety permit for all occupied rooms, and conduct an annual occupational health check of employees who handle physical trust files. The Civil Defense (air-raid shelter) clearance is Not Applicable to a pure administrative office unless the building's basement is used as a document vault, in which case the local civil-air-defense authority must issue a storage-use permit.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA); official online portal: STA Electronic Tax Service Platform (electronic tax bureau) accessible via chinatax.gov.cn and local provincial e-tax online service nodes.

E-government filing portal

For a WFOE, the foreign investor first incorporates the limited liability company with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) and obtains a business license bearing the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which the tax system recognizes as the taxpayer identification number (TIN/VAT identity). Within 30 days of the business license issuance, and using the SAMR-STA one-stop registration service, the entity completes tax registration with the STA; the tax authority activates the taxpayer file, determines small-scale or general VAT taxpayer status, and allocates an electronic tax number. The foreign parent does not need a separate PRC tax ID unless it carries on a taxable presence in China without an establishment; in that case the non-resident enterprise must register, or be registered through withholding by the WFOE, under the PRC Law on Administration of Tax Collection and applicable VAT withholding rules. For trust administration, hold all statutory licenses such as a financial or trust license from the National Financial Regulatory Administration, and register the trust account with the tax authority for stamp duty and VAT purposes as applicable.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None; PRC tax classification is statutory rather than elective. There is no check-the-box regime for a WFOE. A taxpayer may voluntarily elect general VAT taxpayer status when below the mandatory registration threshold, and may choose simplified small-scale taxpayer taxation if it meets the statutory conditions; foreign investors may use applicable double tax treaty provisions to determine permanent establishment status and must apply the CFC and GAAR rules for offshore asset protection trust structures.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

6%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 cumulative taxable turnover in any consecutive 12-month period is the mandatory threshold for general VAT taxpayer registration; all WFOEs must complete tax registration within 30 days of issuance of the business license regardless of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods are zero-rated when the goods are actually exported from China through customs, the export declaration is completed, foreign currency proceeds are received and repatriated, and the export VAT refund is filed with the STA. Cross-border services, including fiduciary services supplied to non-resident trustees or beneficiaries and consumed entirely outside China, may be zero-rated or VAT-exempt where the recipient is a non-resident enterprise, the services are not used within China, and all required cross-border service documentation is retained.

  • Customs Export Declaration Form (electronic customs declaration from China Customs)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Export Contract
  • Bank-issued Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) / foreign exchange collection confirmation
  • VAT special invoice or export invoice
  • Foreign exchange receipt verification record

China requires electronic VAT invoicing through the STA Electronic Invoice Service Platform and the VAT Invoice Management System. All-digital electronic invoices are uploaded to the tax authority in real time at issuance; taxpayers must implement API integration or use the official web platform. The system applies encrypted invoice codes and tax-control digital signatures to prevent fraud; unsupported legacy paper invoicing is being eliminated.

The STA and customs jointly audit export refund and zero-rating claims. If documentation is false, incomplete, or not retained, zero-rating is clawed back, the VAT refund must be repaid with a daily late-payment surcharge of 0.05% of the unpaid tax, and administrative penalties under the PRC Law on Administration of Tax Collection may range from 50% to five times the evaded tax. Fraudulent export VAT refund or zero-rating claims may constitute criminal tax fraud under the PRC Criminal Law, exposing directors and responsible officers to imprisonment, fines, and confiscation.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a hybrid convertibility regime. Current account transactions under trade in goods and services are generally convertible for foreign-invested enterprises such as a wholly foreign-owned enterprise (WFOE), provided the underlying transaction is genuine and supported by VAT invoices, customs declarations, and contract documents submitted to the remitting bank. Capital account items remain subject to SAFE's registration and approval system. A WFOE receiving inbound FDI equity capital must first complete SAFE's FDI foreign exchange registration through the local administration or an approved bank, open a foreign exchange capital account, and may use funds only for approved business purposes within the Chinese domestic regulatory perimeter. Profit remittance by a WFOE to its overseas shareholders after tax is treated as a current account distribution and requires submission of audited financial statements, tax clearance certificates, the board's profit distribution resolution, and evidence that the distributed amount is within cumulative after-tax retained earnings. The effective hurdle is the factual genuineness review of each FX transaction, not an absolute quota. Export proceeds must be repatriated and are subject to the trade monitoring system; while there is no mandatory conversion ratio for ordinary exporters, the settlement of export proceeds into RMB must match the declared trade commodity codes and the enterprise's operational scale. The practical outcome is that working liquidity inside China is normally held in RMB, with foreign currency balances kept only within approved capital accounts and settlement timing aligned with actual commercial need.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export foreign currency receipts from cross-border goods transactions must be brought onshore within 180 days from the date of receipt (or within the SAFE-approved extended period in justified hardship cases) and must be reported into the trade digital linkage system of SAFE. There is no mandatory conversion ratio, meaning the default mandatory conversion ratio is 0% and the exporter may either retain 100% of eligible receipts in a foreign exchange settlement account or voluntarily convert any portion into RMB. The conversion must be supported by the export customs declaration, invoice, and transport documents, and any significant accumulation without corresponding trade volume will trigger SAFE inspection and business suspension risk.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) as the monetary authority, with the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) as the delegated foreign exchange control agency responsible for registration, supervision, and enforcement of cross-border capital flows

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low viability and legally prohibited for direct onshore use by the WFOE; offshore fintech EMI accounts cannot lawfully maintain RMB balances, perform FX settlement, or clear trade payments for a mainland entity. The correct treasury route is exclusive reliance on domestic tier-1 commercial banks operating under a PBOC banking license and SAFE FX settlement and sale qualification.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound injection uses the SWIFT MT103 wire protocol from the overseas shareholder or trust vehicle to the WFOE's designated SAFE-registered foreign exchange capital account. The remittance instruction must reflect FDI equity injection using SAFE balance-of-payments transaction code 622011 for greenfield establishment or 622012 for capital increase, and must quote the WFOE's SAFE FDI registration number in the narrative field. The receiving bank verifies the registered capital baseline, exchanges the foreign currency into RMB only after licensed CPA capital verification, and then releases the RMB funds for disbursement in accordance with the approved business scope.Outbound disbursements from the WFOE should be cleared through bank-originated SWIFT MT103 for direct fee payments, consultant invoices and eligible trust distributions; through irrevocable commercial letters of credit (L/C) for goods-based trade where documentary compliance under UCP 600 is required; and through documentary collections (D/P and D/A) only for established low-risk trading counterparties where the WFOE holds signed contracts, transport evidence and customs records. All outbound remittances must be supported by declared trade purpose codes, underlying agreements, tax payment certificates where relevant, and must pass through the reporting bank's post-transaction monitoring.Vanilla onshore foreign exchange forward contracts with a real underlying trade or current-account exposureCross-currency interest rate swaps with SAFE-approved banks for long-term project-level exposureVanilla FX options and option combination strategies under the bank's foreign exchange settlement and sale qualificationNon-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are not permitted onshore for the WFOE and may only be executed by an offshore affiliate outside mainland China under genuinely separate treasury mandates

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low viability and legally prohibited for direct onshore use by the WFOE; offshore fintech EMI accounts cannot lawfully maintain RMB balances, perform FX settlement, or clear trade payments for a mainland entity. The correct treasury route is exclusive reliance on domestic tier-1 commercial banks operating under a PBOC banking license and SAFE FX settlement and sale qualification.
