Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

Virtual office and commercial mailbox arrangements are not acceptable as the sole registered address for a China WFOE. The Company Law, tax registration practice and bank account opening rules require a physical business premises that is actually controlled and used by the WFOE, evidenced by a lease contract, title certificate or occupancy certificate and tax invoice for rent. Tax authorities and banks will reject a pure virtual office, registered-agent-only mailbox or PO Box for enterprise income tax registration, VAT general taxpayer status, payroll social insurance registration and corporate banking, particularly for a company administering an ESOP incentive trust with payroll wires and equity transfers. A serviced office is acceptable only if it is a rentable physical unit with measurable premises and access control; a virtual address is otherwise rejected.

Competent corporate registry

State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) through the local Administration for Market Regulation (Market Supervision Administration) of the district or city where the registered office is located; filings are made on the SAMR National Enterprise Registration Information System.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum registered capital applies to a WFOE whose only business scope is ISIC 6430 administration of employee share incentive plans (ESOP trust administration). No blocked escrow capital account is mandated by general Chinese company registration law. However, under the new Company Law, each shareholder's subscribed capital must be paid in full within five years after establishment; if the foreign shareholder injects foreign currency, the contribution must be received through a designated foreign exchange bank in China and must be reported under the Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) statistics and SAFE procedures.

Funding & unlock path: Not Applicable; no Civil Defense fire permit, structural load inspection, or hazardous-process occupational clearance is required for the administrative office scope under ISIC 6430. The WFOE remains subject to the general fire prevention obligations of the Fire Prevention Law and to the workplace safety duties of the Work Safety Law, including maintaining accessible exits and portable fire extinguishers in the leased office premises.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; online registration and compliance filings are performed through the national Electronic Tax Bureau (E-Tax Bureau) portal and the VAT Invoice Comprehensive Service Platform.

E-government filing portal

The WFOE is a PRC resident entity. Immediately after the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) issues the business license, the 18-character Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) is automatically adopted as the taxpayer identification number (TIN) and VAT registration number. The legal representative or authorized agent must, within 30 days of business registration, complete tax registration via the E-Tax Bureau by submitting the business license, articles of association, registered address proof, bank account opening certificate, and identity document. The STA will confirm the taxpayer type (small-scale or general VAT taxpayer), register corporate income tax, VAT, withholding obligations and electronic invoice quotas, and issue the electronic tax registration confirmation. For a foreign non-resident entity that has no permanent establishment in China but earns Chinese-source income, the non-resident entity or its withholding agent must open a separate non-resident enterprise tax registration file with the STA, supported by the cross-border service contract and the entity's home-country TIN, solely for PRC withholding tax administration.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation - the WFOE is a PRC resident enterprise taxed on worldwide income at 25% under the Enterprise Income Tax Law; profits distributed to foreign shareholders are subject to withholding tax at 10% or a treaty-reduced rate; pass-through taxation is unavailable.

Available election forms

None. China does not operate a check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election regime. The only relevant statutory election is VAT-related: an enterprise with annual taxable sales below RMB 5,000,000 may elect to register as a general VAT taxpayer in order to claim input VAT credits; once the threshold is exceeded, general taxpayer registration is compulsory.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VAT registration is mandatory immediately upon establishment regardless of turnover. Compulsory general taxpayer registration arises when annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000. A small-scale taxpayer may remain on simplified levy and may qualify for monthly exemption if taxable sales do not exceed RMB 100,000 per month or RMB 300,000 per quarter, subject to current STA policy.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For goods, 0% VAT is available only for physically exported goods declared to China Customs by a VAT-registered exporter; the exporter must complete the electronic customs export declaration and collect export proceeds before the export tax rebate claim is accepted. For services, zero-rating or exemption is allowed only for cross-border taxable services enumerated in STA Notice Cai Shui (2016) No.36 and subsequent official announcements. Trust administration services for employee share plans are not automatically zero-rated; they are subject to 6% VAT unless the service is supplied to an overseas service recipient, consumed entirely outside China, and confirmed by STA filing as an eligible offshore service or offshore digital service. The WFOE must maintain a cross-border contract, evidence that the beneficiary is foreign, foreign-currency payment records, and a formal zero-rating confirmation from the tax authority.

  • China Customs export goods declaration (the electronic Export Goods Declaration)
  • Commercial invoice / export invoice
  • Packing list
  • Bill of Lading (sea) or Master Air Waybill (air)
  • Bank-issued export proceeds receipt / foreign exchange settlement confirmation (the Chinese statutory equivalent of an inward remittance certificate / FIRC)
  • Service trade foreign exchange declaration and cross-border service contract for qualifying offshore digital services

The WFOE must issue Full-Digitalized Electronic Invoices (digital e-invoices) through the STA E-Tax Bureau electronic invoice platform. General taxpayers must issue Special VAT Invoices with invoice data transmitted to the STA in near real time using the platform API or tax certificate UKey; each invoice carries a unique anti-counterfeit invoice code, is automatically cleared and matched against the VAT Invoice Comprehensive Service Platform, and paper stamping is not required.

STA and Customs audits trace the entire chain from customs declaration and logistics to foreign exchange receipt and re-invoicing. If the zero-rated export treatment is not substantiated, the taxpayer must reverse the exemption, pay VAT at the applicable domestic rate (6% for services or 13% for goods), and pay a late-payment surcharge of 0.05% per day from the original due date. Fraudulent zero-rating or export rebate claims are treated as tax evasion and export tax fraud: administrative fines range from 50% to five times the tax evaded, the enterprise may lose its export rebate qualification, and responsible officers are subject to criminal liability under the Criminal Law, including imprisonment up to life for serious cases.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a managed floating exchange-rate regime under PBOC/SAFE. Current-account items - goods trade, service fees, wages, investment income and after-tax dividends - are generally convertible for onshore enterprises after the designated bank has verified the authenticity of the underlying contract, invoice, tax clearance and payment instruction. Capital-account items - inbound equity capital, cross-border loans, securities investment and trust transactions - remain tightly controlled. A WFOE established as an ESOP administration trust will therefore need a specific SAFE registration for each dollar of foreign direct investment capital contribution; funds held in the capital foreign-exchange account cannot be freely converted into RMB until a genuine operating purpose is proven and must not be placed into securities, real estate or shadow-banking products. Profit remission by the foreign parent requires annual audited financial statements, full corporate income tax settlement, proof that dividend withholding tax has been paid including treaty eligibility certificates where applicable, and an investor resolution. The bank will additionally screen for anti-round-tripping mismatches and require evidence that the offshore subscriber has not recycled inbound funds through onshore shell or fictitious trade channels.

Export proceeds surrender rules

China eliminated mandatory foreign-exchange surrender. The mandatory conversion ratio is 0%; exporters may choose to retain 100% of receipts in foreign currency in a current-account settlement account or convert voluntarily through a bank. Goods-export proceeds must generally be received through the domestic banking system and reported to SAFE's Trade Monitoring System. Export collection should not be delayed beyond 30 days from the date of export or the due date stated in the contract; any delay longer than 30 days requires an explanation and documentation establishing the underlying shipment, payment terms and customer relationship. Advance receipts for goods exports should normally be followed by customs declaration within 30 days absent a justified extension.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE), with the People's Bank of China (PBOC) as the monetary authority and joint regulator of RMB clearing and cross-border capital flows

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low - Permanent legal constraint. Onshore RMB clearing is reserved to licensed banks and licensed non-bank payment institutions in China. Offshore EMIs such as Airwallex, Wise, Revolut and Currenxie cannot open a domestic CNY account in the WFOE's name, cannot join CIPS as a domestic member, and cannot clear inbound FDI capital in RMB. Their internal ledgers do not satisfy SAFE balance-of-payments reporting and cannot be used to prove capital contribution. They may be used only as upstream collection or downstream payout service providers in non-China legs, and only with full corresponding-bank transparency; the primary onshore treasury must sit at a licensed Chinese commercial bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound registered capital contribution from the overseas shareholder or ESOP trust must be routed as direct investment through a bank designated by SAFE and into an onshore FDI Capital Account. The WFOE should first complete the SAFE Foreign-Invested Enterprise FDI basic information registration and obtain the FDI Business Registration Certificate. Wire transfers should use SWIFT MT103 for foreign currency or cross-border RMB via CIPS/MT103, with all remitter and beneficiary identifiers, a board resolution authorizing the capital call, and the payment purpose indicated in the remittance instructions as equity investment and registered capital contribution to a Chinese WFOE. The booking bank will record the transaction under the SAFE balance-of-payments standard classification as foreign direct investment equity; no offshore EMI may be used as an intermediate. The payment file must include the underlying ESOP or service contract, tax withholding certificate, board resolution and the appropriate SAFE payment purpose code for the transaction. For physical-goods trade that may be incidental to the WFOE's treasury operations, use domestic RMB clearing through CIPS or SWIFT MT103 for foreign currency; commercial letters of credit and documentary collections under UCP600 and URC522 are reserved for actual trade transactions with shipping and customs documentation. For pure financial ESOP flows, letters of credit and collections are not necessary and should be avoided. All outbound payments must be executed from a bank-supervised settlement account after authenticity verification.Onshore deliverable CNY/USD and CNY/HKD forward contracts executed with a Tier-1 Chinese bank under SAFE's real-demand principle; the hedge must be tied to an underlying current-account obligation or approved capital-flow event and cannot generate speculative gain.FX swaps comprising spot and forward combinations are permissible for short-term treasury liquidity management between RMB and foreign currency, provided the transaction is booked at a licensed domestic bank and reported in the bank's SAFE reporting returns.Cross-currency interest-rate swaps are permitted only when the WFOE has SAFE-registered external debt or another approved capital-account purpose; the swap notional must not exceed the approved debt service and the hedge must remain matched to actual foreign-currency exposure. Non-deliverable forwards are not permissible onshore for the Chinese entity; only an offshore parent may transact NDFs outside China without onshore RMB settlement.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low - Permanent legal constraint. Onshore RMB clearing is reserved to licensed banks and licensed non-bank payment institutions in China. Offshore EMIs such as Airwallex, Wise, Revolut and Currenxie cannot open a domestic CNY account in the WFOE's name, cannot join CIPS as a domestic member, and cannot clear inbound FDI capital in RMB. Their internal ledgers do not satisfy SAFE balance-of-payments reporting and cannot be used to prove capital contribution. They may be used only as upstream collection or downstream payout service providers in non-China legs, and only with full corresponding-bank transparency; the primary onshore treasury must sit at a licensed Chinese commercial bank.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% statutory withholding tax on dividends paid by a Chinese WFOE to non-resident enterprise shareholders without a Chinese establishment/place of business. Dividend distributions to qualified Chinese resident corporate shareholders are exempt. Under bilateral DTA, the dividend rate may be reduced, commonly to 5% under the China-Hong Kong DTA where the beneficial owner holds at least 25% of the paying company's capital, or cannot be reduced below 5% in certain treaties.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File is mandatory where related-party transactions exceed: RMB 200 million for tangible asset transfers; RMB 100 million for intangible asset transfers; RMB 100 million for financial asset transfers; or RMB 40 million for related-party service transactions in the fiscal year. Master File is mandatory where the total annual related-party transaction amount reaches RMB 1 billion. Country-by-Country Reporting is triggered where the ultimate parent's consolidated group revenue is at least RMB 5.5 billion.

Economic substance scope: China applies substance-over-form principles and General Anti-Avoidance Rules under CIT Law Article 47 and SAT Bulletin 2017 No. 6. A WFOE operating an employee stock ownership trust must demonstrate real local commercial activity, local managers, payroll, office premises, board decisions, and risk control functions. The foreign ESOP trust claiming dividend, interest, or royalty benefits under a DTA must prove Beneficial Owner status and substantive CIGA in its residence jurisdiction. A shell, letterbox, or conduit structure with no economic substance may be recharacterized, the treaty benefit denied, the underlying transaction adjusted, and the WFOE's related-party deductions disallowed.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Corporate Income Tax Return

Statutory sanction: Tax Administration Law Article 63 requires recovery of the unpaid tax, overdue interest, and a penalty of 50% to 500% of the underpaid amount. Criminal Law Article 201 imposes up to 3 years imprisonment or criminal detention for evasion exceeding RMB 50,000 and greater than 10% of the payable tax; for extremely large evasion amounts, imprisonment of 3 to 7 years applies with fines.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly instruct or participate in the false filing are jointly and severally liable for restitution of the unpaid tax; the court may pierce the corporate veil to reach personal assets, freeze bank accounts, impose travel bans and tax blacklisting, and after criminal conviction confiscate personal property and imprison the director for up to 7 years.
Transfer Pricing Mispricing and Documentation Failure

Statutory sanction: CIT Law Articles 41, 44, and related SAT announcements allow the tax authority to re-determine taxable income by deemed income methods, adjust the WFOE's tax base, impose TP adjustment interest calculated from the first taxable year, and add daily late-payment interest of 0.05% on underpaid tax. Where intentional fraud is proven, Article 63 penalties of 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax apply.

Director personal liability: Directors who cause the related-party pricing distortion may be personally liable for the adjusted tax and interest; personal assets may be attached if the arrangement is found to be a sham dividend-stripping scheme; tax-exit clearance is blocked; the director may be placed on the tax non-compliant blacklist with travel restrictions and may face criminal prosecution for tax evasion if intent is established.
Shell Company / Treaty Abuse / Beneficial Ownership Failure

Statutory sanction: CIT Law Article 47 and SAT Bulletin 2018 No. 9 empower the tax authority to disregard the conduit company, deny treaty benefits, recharacterize the recipient, and impose the 10% Chinese withholding tax on the entire payment plus late interest. If the arrangement is ruled fraudulent, Tax Administration Law Article 63 imposes a fine up to 500% of the underpaid tax and referral to criminal authorities.

Director personal liability: Directors serving as withholding agents who knowingly remit dividends or royalties without proper withholding become personally liable for the unwithheld WHT; personal assets can be used for restitution; travel bans and blacklisting under China's tax enforcement and social credit collective punishment measures apply; if convicted of tax evasion through sham arrangements, the director faces imprisonment up to 7 years and asset confiscation.
