Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A virtual office or commercial mailbox is not permissible for a China WFOE operating as a special purpose acquisition and holding vehicle. The State Administration for Market Regulation and local tax authorities require the registered address to be an actual physical site within the registered jurisdiction, with a lease or ownership certificate, a visible plaque, and the capacity to receive official legal and tax correspondence. Corporate banks additionally reject virtual offices during account opening because they fail the know-your-customer visit and physical presence requirements. A shell or blank-check entity without a real office faces a high probability of having its registration challenged by the SAMR and the tax bureau as a fictitious address, which may lead to freezing of tax status and inability to open bank accounts.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None for an ordinary WFOE limited liability company; the PRC Company Law (2023 Revision) has abolished the statutory minimum paid-up capital threshold for non-licensed industries. The articles of association must state the subscribed registered capital and the contribution schedule, and the sole shareholder must complete the paid-in contribution within the statutory five-year period from establishment unless a shorter schedule is agreed in the SPAC sponsor agreement. There is no mandatory blocked escrow account as a Companies Act incorporation condition; however, for SPAC operations, a segregated trust or escrow account must be established with a licensed PRC commercial bank or qualified custodian in accordance with the escrow agreement and the relevant securities listing or public-market financing requirements before any public-market funds are deposited.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None. China does not operate a check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election system for a WFOE; the entity is always taxed at entity level under the Enterprise Income Tax Law. The only elective feature relevant for indirect tax is the choice between small-scale VAT taxpayer treatment and general VAT taxpayer treatment, which is available only where turnover remains below the RMB 5,000,000 general taxpayer threshold.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

There is no turnover threshold for the WFOE to become VAT-registered: incorporation automatically creates tax registration obligations. Mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration applies once annual taxable turnover exceeds RMB 5,000,000; below that amount small-scale VAT taxpayer treatment may apply. Financial services supplied by the SPAC vehicle are generally taxable at 6% under the services rate while the general headline VAT rate for goods is 13%.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods zero-rating applies when tangible goods are physically exported from China under a customs export declaration, the sale is made to a non-resident buyer, and export foreign exchange proceeds are repatriated to China within the statutory collection period. Cross-border services, including offshore digital services, qualify for 0% VAT where the service recipient is outside China, the service is wholly consumed outside China, and contemporaneous contracts, performance evidence and foreign currency settlement records are retained. A SPAC-related financial service is zero-rated only where the entire transaction counterparty, underlying economic activity and consumption occur outside China and the STA recognises the offshore supply under cross-border service tax rules.

  • China Customs Export Declaration (Customs Bill of Export) lodged through the China International Trade Single Window
  • Ocean Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export from China and arrival in the overseas destination
  • Commercial invoice, packing list and export sales contract signed by the WFOE
  • Bank-issued foreign exchange receipt voucher and SAFE trade-in-foreign-exchange declaration record evidencing repatriation of export proceeds

Golden Tax Phase IV requires all VAT invoices to be issued through the STA national electronic invoice service platform with real-time fiscal clearance. Each invoice must carry a unique digital invoice code and an anti-fraud cryptographic digital stamp generated by the platform. WFOEs must integrate their billing/ERP systems with the STA e-invoice API for immediate transmission of invoice issuance, cancellation, red-flag reversal and exception reporting; fully-digitised electronic invoices are the national standard and legacy offline tax-control invoices are being phased out.

The STA Golden Tax IV system reconciles customs export declarations, VAT invoice data, transport documents, bank receipts and SAFE foreign exchange records. If zero-rating is unsubstantiated, STA retroactively reverses the 0% VAT rate, assesses output VAT at the domestic rate, claws back any VAT refund already paid, imposes daily late-payment surcharge and administrative fines for tax maladministration under the Law on Administration of Tax Collection, and refers deliberate cases for criminal prosecution under the Criminal Law provisions on fraudulent export tax rebates. Liability may extend to the WFOE's legal representative, finance officer and beneficial shareholders where fraud or false documentation is proven.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Chinese Mainland maintains a split current and capital account FX regime. Current account receipts and payments, including trade-related settlements and service fees, are fully convertible and may be effected through designated domestic banks without SAFE pre-approval, provided the transaction is genuine and supported by customary underlying documentation. Capital account items, however, remain heavily controlled; all inbound equity investment into a WFOE, outbound acquisition disbursements, and external borrowing must be registered with SAFE under the FDI protocol and, where relevant, under PBOC foreign debt rules. Registered capital of a SPAC-oriented WFOE may be denominated in RMB or foreign currency; once foreign currency capital is received, it must be settled into RMB through the designated bank's capital account system and monitored for compliance with the negative list governing settlement use. Profit remittance to offshore investors is permitted after annual statutory audit, tax clearance, and confirmation of registered capital paid-in; remittance is processed through current account channels but banks require audit reports, tax certificates, board resolutions, and foreign exchange registration certificates. Access to onshore FX markets for hedging is limited to real exposures evidenced by contracts or a valid underlying investment file, and speculative derivatives are prohibited. The overall regime is therefore workable for a genuine SPAC vehicle but demands documentary discipline, pre-registration with SAFE, and strict sponsorship of every capital step.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Goods-trade export proceeds must be repatriated into China within 180 days from the date of customs declaration. No fixed statutory percentage of export proceeds is required to be mandatorily converted into RMB; the surrender ratio is effectively zero after recent liberalisation, but any export receipts retained in foreign currency must be held in an approved current-account FX account at a designated domestic bank and must be supported by trade-invoice and shipping documentation. The enterprise is also required to perform foreign exchange receipt and payment declaration through the bank's reporting system before settlement.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Viability rating: Low; effectively prohibited. Offshore fintech EMIs and digital challenger banks are not viable as primary banking rails for a Mainland WFOE. They are legally barred from holding a PBOC banking licence or CNAPS settlement membership, do not have access to SAFE foreign exchange registration, and cannot provide a lawful RMB clearing path for registered capital or trade disbursements. Such EMIs can only act as auxiliary tooling for offshore parent-company balances in permitted jurisdictions; the SPAC vehicle's onshore operating, capital, and escrow accounts must remain at a licensed Chinese commercial bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity capital must arrive via SWIFT MT103 from the registered offshore shareholder or the SPAC trust custodian holding IPO proceeds, with the remittance field indicating the SAFE FDI registration number and the transaction purpose code for direct investment capital contribution. The receiving Tier-1 bank will validate the remittance against the capital contribution list filed under the FDI registration, perform the foreign exchange receipt declaration, and then settle into RMB through the bank's capital account supervision system; the settlement must be applied exclusively to the approved business scope and must not be used for prohibited negative-list activities such as entrusted lending, real-estate speculation, or investment into securities without an additional licence. After settlement, a Chinese registered accountant must issue a capital verification report to evidence paid-in capital and complete the market-regulator filing.Operational, advisory, and acquisition disbursements from the WFOE should be settled through SWIFT MT103 for direct corporate payments; commercial letters of credit via MT700 where the target transaction needs upstream documentary protection; and documentary collections for lower-risk trade-based settlements. For RMB-denominated payments within China's cross-border schemes, the bank may route through CIPS or the RMB clearing bank with an accompanying payment purpose code; all outbound transfers require a completed SAFE payment declaration, the underlying invoice or acquisition SPA copy, and where applicable the post-verification record from the bank's FX system.Onshore deliverable RMB/FX forward contracts linked to verified trade, capital, or foreign-currency debt obligations, executed under a SAFE-registered master agreement with a domestic Tier-1 bank.Offshore NDFs are only permissible in the overseas parent or sponsor entity outside the WFOE, with no onshore settlement or delivery into the Mainland RMB system; the WFOE itself cannot use NDFs as a direct hedge.Cross-currency swaps on registered external loans or SPAC-related debt instruments, limited to hedging principal and interest exposures and prohibited for speculative rebooking.FX options used by the WFOE on a buyer-only or hedging basis when the underlying transaction has been evidenced and authorised by the bank.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Viability rating: Low; effectively prohibited. Offshore fintech EMIs and digital challenger banks are not viable as primary banking rails for a Mainland WFOE. They are legally barred from holding a PBOC banking licence or CNAPS settlement membership, do not have access to SAFE foreign exchange registration, and cannot provide a lawful RMB clearing path for registered capital or trade disbursements. Such EMIs can only act as auxiliary tooling for offshore parent-company balances in permitted jurisdictions; the SPAC vehicle's onshore operating, capital, and escrow accounts must remain at a licensed Chinese commercial bank.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% statutory withholding tax on dividends paid by a PRC-resident WFOE to a non-resident shareholder under Article 37 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law (EIT Law) and Caishui [2008] No. 1. Treaty relief: for an investor resident in a treaty jurisdiction, and holding at least 25% of the WFOE's shares for at least 12 months, the withholding rate is typically reduced to 5% (e.g., under the China-Hong Kong DTA, China-UK DTA, China-Singapore DTA). Some treaties provide 10% for portfolio investors; certain DTA articles allow exemption on dividends paid from income subject to full CIT.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: RMB. Local transfer pricing documentation (Local File) must be prepared and maintained if, in the fiscal year, any of the following thresholds is exceeded: (a) aggregate related-party tangible asset purchases/sales > RMB 200 million; (b) aggregate related-party intangible property transfers > RMB 100 million; (c) aggregate intercompany financing including loans, guarantees, fund deposits > RMB 100 million; (d) aggregate other related-party transactions (services, cost-sharing) > RMB 40 million. Master File (Group File) is mandatory when the group's aggregate annual related-party transactions exceed RMB 1 billion or the WFOE is the party to cross-border related-party transactions exceeding RMB 100 million and belongs to an MNEG filing the group file. Country-by-Country Reporting thresholds follow BEPS action 13 for groups with consolidated revenue above RMB 5.5 billion.

Economic substance scope: China does not maintain a standalone 'Economic Substance Regulations' (ESR) statute as in the UAE. However, the Economic Substance Doctrine is enforceable through: (i) the general anti-avoidance rule in EIT Law Art. 47, enabling tax authorities to re-characterize arrangements with no purpose other than tax avoidance; (ii) the 2018 Beneficial Ownership Standard (SAT Public Notice [2018] No. 9) requiring treaty claimants to show substantive business activity, (iii) the 'substance over form' doctrine applied in transfer pricing audits, and (iv) anti-money-laundering verification of 'beneficial owner' for all SPAC escrow and trust payments. For a SPAC WFOE, Core Income-Generating Activities (CIGA) include the management and oversight of the escrow/trust fund, the due diligence and negotiation for target acquisition, board approval of acquisition agreements, investment decisions regarding the listing proceeds, and ongoing Compliance Officer functions. Failure to perform CIGA in China, with only nominal staff and a mailbox address, will cause treaty benefits to be denied, the trust profits to be re-sourceable to China, and the WFOE to be considered a transparent shell for CIT and AML purposes.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Shell Company with No Economic Substance and False Beneficial Ownership Claim

Statutory sanction: EIT Law Article 47 (GAAR) allows the tax authority to recharacterize the inserted Hong Kong entity as fiscally transparent, deny treaty benefits, and impute all trust investment income and sponsor fees to the Chinese WFOE. The recharacterization imposes tax at 25% ordinary rate plus late interest and credits; under Tax Collection Administration Law Art. 63, a penalty of 50%-5x the additional tax found due may be imposed. Off-shore resale through a shell may also trigger Art. 26 of the Tax Law on the Administration of Tax Collection requiring payment before relief.

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally and severally liable for the WFOE's unpaid CIT if the company is used to defraud creditors and tax; courts will pierce the corporate veil under Civil Law Principles (Art. 20 of the Civil Code) when abuse of legal personality is proven. Directors have been blacklisted (entry and exit prohibition) under TCAL Article 44, their personal bank accounts attached, and if they execute fabricated shareholder resolutions, they face up to 7 years' imprisonment for tax evasion under Criminal Law Art. 201. Personal fines up to RMB 500,000 can be imposed in serious fraud cases.
Criminal Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Transfer Pricing

Statutory sanction: Tax Collection Administration Law Art. 63: mandatory penalty of 50%-5x of the underpaid tax, plus daily 0.05% late surcharge. The Special Tax Adjustment framework under EIT Law Art. 41 opens audit exposure for 10 years (if thargeted) and requires interest assessed on the adjustment. Criminal Law Art. 201: where the evaded amount exceeds RMB 100,000 and is more than 10% of the tax that should be paid, the company's legal representative and other directly responsible supervisors can be convicted of tax evasion, punishable by up to 3 years' imprisonment and fines (for minor amounts) up to 7 years' imprisonment and fine for major amounts.

Director personal liability: Directors and CFOs who signed false tax returns may be individually criminally prosecuted under Art. 201, notwithstanding company liquidation, with imprisonment of 3-7 years depending on the amount. The tax authority can attach personal assets for the portion of tax unpaid if the company is dissolved without paying; travel bans and 'Serious Tax Dishonesty' blacklisting are published. Civil restoration of the tax underpaid is a precondition to suspension or reduction of criminal sentence.
Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Breach in SPAC Escrow Fund Flows

Statutory sanction: PRC Anti-Money Laundering Law (revised 2022), Articles 48-49 impose administrative fines: for institutions up to RMB 10 million (or 5% of the transaction amount) for serious failure in AML obligations; direct responsible persons face personal fines up to RMB 200,000. The State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) can impose penalties of 0.5%-1x illegal outward remittance and order repayment. Criminal Law Art. 191 (Money Laundering), as amended, criminalizes the concealment of proceeds from tax evasion, fraud, securities crime, or corruption: imprisonment for 5-10 years plus a fine for individuals; asset confiscation of the criminally derived proceeds and any laundering vehicles.

Director personal liability: Directors of the WFOE are statutorily liable for AML compliance. Failure to appoint an AML compliance officer and program can give rise to personal fines of RMB 50,000-200,000 per infraction. Directors who personally authorize or execute the layering of escrow funds through multiple non-resident accounts commit a predicate offence to money laundering and face 5-10 years' imprisonment, revocation of the business license of the SPAC, and civil restitution for the full value of tainted proceeds. The travel ban applies and the tax bureau will coordinate with the public security and customs agencies to blacklist and extradite the principals.
