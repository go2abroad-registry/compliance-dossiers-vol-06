Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No minimum paid-in capital and no blocked escrow account requirement under the PRC Company Law. A WFOE must state a registered capital amount in its Articles of Association. Under Article 47 of the 2023 Company Law, shareholders of a limited liability company must fully pay their subscribed contributions within five years from the date of establishment unless an industry-specific law imposes a shorter period. If the REIT management activities require a financial regulatory license, the China Securities Regulatory Commission or local financial regulatory bureau may impose an independent minimum paid-in capital, net capital, or escrow condition, and that condition is then mandatory.

Funding & unlock path: Not Applicable for a pure office-based REIT vehicle; no Civil Defense fire permit is required for non-production office premises beyond the building owner's fire safety inspection and occupancy certificate. The WFOE must, however, maintain workplace fire safety compliance, conduct annual fire drills, and register occupational health and safety procedures under the Work Safety Law. A local fire authority civil-defense clearance and structural load inspection will apply only if the entity later constructs or acquires physical storage, warehouse, or redevelopment facilities.

Paid-in capital rule: No minimum paid-in capital and no blocked escrow account requirement under the PRC Company Law. A WFOE must state a registered capital amount in its Articles of Association. E-government filing portal

For a foreign-invested Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE), the statutory TIN is obtained as follows: (1) Register the entity with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) or its local counterpart and obtain the Business License. (2) Within 30 days after Business License issuance, apply for tax registration with the competent tax authority by submitting the standard tax registration application form prescribed by the STA, the Business License, Articles of Association, investment contract, and identification of the legal representative. (3) The tax authority assigns a Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) which constitutes the enterprise's taxpayer identification number (TIN), used for all CIT, VAT, and other filings. (4) If the entity qualifies as a general VAT taxpayer, complete the VAT general taxpayer registration. (5) A foreign non-resident enterprise without a permanent establishment in China may apply for a non-resident taxpayer identification number through the same competent tax authority when it needs to self-file or claim treaty benefits.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export of goods from mainland China is eligible for VAT zero-rating, provided the goods are actually exported and customs clearance records are maintained. Qualifying cross-border services, including software development, technical consulting, and other designated modern services provided to overseas customers, are also zero-rated. The exporter must be a VAT general taxpayer, the export proceeds must be received in foreign currency within the prescribed repatriation period, and the service must be used entirely outside China. Documentation must prove the flow of goods or services and the corresponding foreign-exchange receipt.

  • Customs Export Declaration (release certificate) issued by China Customs
  • Bill of Lading (or Air Waybill) issued by the carrier
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (bank receipt confirming export proceeds in foreign currency)

The State Taxation Administration operates a nationwide electronic invoicing system under the Golden Tax Phase IV framework. All VAT invoices must be issued through the official e-Invoice platform, which employs API-based integration with enterprise accounting systems. Invoices are assigned unique digital codes and transmitted in real time to the STA central database. Mandatory digital encryption and stamping apply to each invoice to prevent forgery. Full digital invoices (without physical paper) are used for most transactions. Real-time fiscal clearance is achieved through automatic database matching of invoice data with payment reconciliations.

Any false or inaccurate zero-rating claim will result in the denial or recovery of the VAT refund (clawback) together with interest for late payment. The tax authority may impose administrative fines of one to five times the amount of the fraudulently obtained refund. If the conduct constitutes criminal tax fraud (Article 204 of the Criminal Law), imprisonment and fines are applicable. Additionally, the exporter's credit rating may be downgraded, and future export refund privileges may be suspended.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China maintains a regulated, asymmetric convertibility framework: current account transactions are generally convertible after authenticity and compliance checks by designated commercial banks, while capital account transactions remain subject to PBOC and SAFE registration, bank verification, or approval. A Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise established as a REIT must complete foreign exchange registration for its foreign direct investment through a domestic settlement bank before it can receive inbound equity capital from offshore shareholders. Foreign-currency capital contributions must first enter a dedicated foreign exchange capital account and cannot be credited directly to a general operational RMB account. Conversion of such capital into RMB is permitted under the bank-based capital account settlement regime, but the resulting RMB funds must be deployed strictly in line with the approved business scope, the negative list for foreign investment, and the project purpose described in the application. Profit remittances and dividend distributions to foreign investors are treated as current account outflows and are permitted only after the WFOE has completed the annual statutory audit, paid or settled applicable taxes, and provided the bank with the prior-year financial statements, dividend resolution, and tax clearance evidence. These controls make treasury operations slower and more document-intensive than in fully liberalized jurisdictions, but they are manageable for a licensed, audited WFOE with a clear tax identity and a fully qualified domestic banking relationship.

Export proceeds surrender rules

China abolished mandatory export proceeds surrender for goods trade. Under the current SAFE framework, export-related foreign currency receipts must be repatriated to a domestic bank settlement account by the documented contract due date and generally within 180 days after the export customs declaration. The exporter may retain eligible export proceeds in a foreign exchange settlement account or voluntarily convert them to RMB at market rates; there is no statutory fixed percentage conversion ratio. For a WFOE structured as a real estate investment trust, export trading revenue is typically absent, so the relevant obligation applies only to ancillary cross-border goods or services revenue, which must be declared through the bank's trade monitoring system with supporting customs declarations, invoices, contracts, and logistics evidence.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE), operating under the supervision of the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low / Not Viable as a primary banking rail. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity capital must be sent via SWIFT MT103 or equivalent bank wire from the foreign investor's account to the WFOE's dedicated foreign exchange capital account at a licensed domestic bank. The remittance must clearly reference the registered FDI code and use the SAFE-designated foreign exchange declaration purpose code for foreign direct investment equity injection rather than a current account trade remittance code. After receipt, the banker must verify the authenticity of the investment contract, shareholder resolution, and FDI registration; the WFOE then applies for conversion of the foreign currency capital into RMB under the bank-based settlement regime. Capital verification must be completed by a Chinese certified public accountant after the funds are credited, and the verification report must be filed with the company registration authority and retained by the bank for subsequent payment-use monitoring. Converted RMB may then be released for permitted real property acquisition, construction, and operating expenses only in accordance with the approved foreign investment negative list.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: Low / Not Viable as a primary banking rail. China does not permit unlicensed offshore digital EMIs or foreign payment fintechs to open onshore corporate bank accounts, hold client funds, perform capital verification, or issue clearing channels for a domestic WFOE. Offshore EMIs such as Wise, Payoneer, or Airwallex can only provide auxiliary cross-border collection or disbursement support through Chinese-licensed non-bank payment institutions, but they cannot act as the legal settlement bank for equity injection, FX conversion, profit remittance, or regulatory declarations. The WFOE must keep its principal treasury accounts with a licensed domestic commercial bank; any EMI solution is restricted to tenant rent collection or low-value ancillary payment attachments and may not replace the domestic bank relationship.

Turnover: Under PRC Company Law, every company, including a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise, must prepare annual financial accounting reports and have them audited by an accounting firm licensed in China; there is no minimum balance-sheet, turnover, or headcount exemption for an active WFOE. As a practical quantitative floor, any WFOE with total assets greater than zero, any operating revenue, or any employees is subject to mandatory statutory audit if it is a reporting foreign-invested enterprise.
