Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA); electronic tax filing portal: STA Electronic Tax Bureau available through chinatax.gov.cn, with all VAT, corporate income tax, and stamp duty filings performed online.

E-government filing portal

For a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE), the foreign-invested entity first registers with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) and obtains the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), an 18-character identifier that functions as the PRC taxpayer identification number. The SAMR registration information is automatically exchanged with the tax administration; the entity must then complete or confirm its tax registration with the local competent STA bureau, normally through the e-Tax Bureau, within 30 days of license issuance. The WFOE must file its articles of association, registered address proof, legal representative identity documentation, banking account confirmation, and expected business scope in the tax registration. The competent tax authority determines the relevant taxes and levies, including corporate income tax, VAT, stamp duty, urban maintenance and construction tax, and education surcharges, and it then formalizes the taxpayer account under the USCC. If the expected annual taxable turnover exceeds CNY 5,000,000, the WFOE must apply for general VAT taxpayer status; a non-resident enterprise without a permanent establishment in China does not obtain a Chinese USCC and instead registers as a non-resident taxpayer through its withholding agent so that China-source income taxes can be withheld at the statutory rates.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

6%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

All business entities with taxable supplies must complete tax registration; mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration is triggered when annual taxable turnover exceeds CNY 5,000,000 (RMB 5 million) over any continuous 12-month period. Entities below the threshold may elect general taxpayer status if their accounting is sound; small-scale taxpayer treatment applies below the threshold, with a reduced VAT levy rate for eligible small-scale suppliers.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For goods, 0% VAT export treatment applies if the exporter is a registered VAT general taxpayer, the goods are actually declared for export and leave China customs territory, the export contract is genuine, and the corresponding foreign currency proceeds are repatriated into China within the statutory period. For services, cross-border taxable services are zero-rated or exempt only when they fall within the specific services listed in the Ministry of Finance and STA cross-border taxable services zero-rating and VAT exemption catalogue and when the services are consumed entirely outside China by a non-resident recipient with no domestic consumer nexus; a P2P micro-lending platform must therefore obtain confirmatory tax-treatment advice before applying zero rating to offshore loan facilitation fees, because financial services are not broadly zero-rated. Qualifying exporters must retain all evidence supporting actual export, foreign consumption, payment from outside China, and the proper invoice chain.

  • Customs export declaration for goods (Export Goods Customs Declaration in the export tax-refund channel)
  • Bill of Lading, air waybill, or combined transport document evidencing physical departure from China customs territory
  • Commercial invoice, packing list, and export sales contract
  • Bank foreign-exchange income declaration or inward remittance voucher evidencing receipt of export proceeds from the foreign buyer

VAT invoices must be issued through the State Taxation Administration's Unified Electronic Invoice Service Platform; all fully digitalized electronic invoices must be created by the taxpayer through the e-Tax Bureau platform and are transmitted to the STA tax system in near real time with a tax-control code and anti-counterfeit electronic stamp. The STA Golden Tax Phase IV system automatically reconciles invoice issuance, VAT returns, payment, and input credit claims using application programming interface (API) data exchange for large taxpayers. No VAT special invoice may be issued without an STA-issued tax-control registration and digital fiscal stamp; failure to route invoices through the authorized electronic invoice system renders input VAT deductions unavailable.

If the export zero-rating claim is not fully substantiated, the STA will claw back the refunded output VAT, assess output VAT and input VAT credit adjustments, impose a late-payment surcharge of 0.05% per day on the underpaid tax, and, in fraud or evasion cases, levy an administrative penalty of 50% to 5 times the evaded tax. Wholly fictitious export declarations, forged customs documents, or false remittance vouchers may be referred to judicial authorities for criminal liability under the PRC Criminal Law for fraudulent export tax refunds; the entity may also be named in the STA major tax violation publicity list and disqualified from export tax refund and zero-rating privileges.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The People's Republic of China operates an administratively supervised managed float with a two-track convertibility framework. Current-account receipts and payments are generally convertible and settlement and sale of trade- and service-related foreign exchange is deregulated, but capital-account transactions such as direct investment injections, foreign debt, cross-border security and repatriation of equity capital require pre-registration or approval with SAFE and the transactional bank. For a wholly foreign-owned enterprise (WFOE), share capital and shareholder loans brought in from overseas must pass through a foreign exchange capital account after an FDI foreign exchange registration; funds are credited subject to real-use supervision and the SAFE positive-list approach. Profit remittances are permitted after affirmative tax clearance, statutory auditing by a PRC CPA, and declaration through the bank under a current-account distributed-profit item; in practice, the treasury must demonstrate that the underlying earnings derive from a licensed micro-lending operation and that all withholding taxes on interest and income have been fully settled. Settlement of RMB into operational cash or lending disbursements is allowed for working capital, rent, payroll, software licensing and approved digital lending infrastructure, but cannot be diverted to securities investment, wealth management, real estate or unrelated transactions. The RMB exchange rate remains a controlled floating rate set against a basket; derivative hedges are permitted only with genuine exposure. Therefore, the WFOE must design a fully documented daily liquidity plan that separates foreign-currency capital, RMB consumer-loan disbursement accounts, and tax and escrow accounts to satisfy SAFE spot checks and bank compliance reviews.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Statutory framework: export foreign-currency proceeds must be repatriated to a domestic bank account within 180 days from the date of export customs declaration, unless a longer credit term is approved and declared via the Trade Monitoring System; the mandatory conversion or surrender ratio is 0%. The exporter may maintain 100% of collected proceeds in a foreign-currency settlement account or voluntarily convert to RMB at the market rate. All receipts, regardless of amount, must be declared to the receiving bank through SAFE's cross-border receipt and verification platform and matched against the electronic customs declaration form. In practice, service-trade and high-tech export proceeds forming the treasury inflow for the WFOE must be supported by invoices, service contracts and an internal certificate of compliance for SAFE's trade verification system.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE), the exchange-control arm of the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Cross-border fintech EMI players such as Airwallex, Currenxie and Payoneer are not licensed as domestic commercial banks in China and cannot lawfully open primary corporate accounts for PRC-incorporated WFOEs under the PBOC banking account rules. Their ancillary acceptance can only be used for inbound e-commerce collection in foreign currency and is not suitable as the regulated lending cash-flow spine. A high-risk micro-loan platform's bank account, loan disbursement custody account and consumer repayment collection mandates must be held at a SAFE-designated onshore bank because SAFE transaction-verification, AML customer due diligence and capital-account supervision require a licensed PRC banking counterparty. Any attempt to use EMI omnibus rails for equity capital settlement, consumer lending payouts, or profit remittance would trigger regulatory violation and immediate account freezing; therefore, the legal viability of EMI onboarding is Low and the recommended route is a domestic tier-one commercial bank account opened directly by the WFOE.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound equity contributions must be pre-registered through the FDI foreign exchange registration system at the receiving bank, with shareholder and WFOE details recorded in SAFE's database. The remitting bank must transmit a SWIFT MT103 using the purpose code classified by SAFE as direct investment or equity capital contribution, explicitly identifying the WFOE and the foreign investor. RMB-denominated capital injections should use CIPS with the instruction RMB FDI Equity Contribution and reference the foreign investment registration certificate. Immediately after receipt, funds stay in the foreign-currency capital account or RMB capital account; the bank will not permit unauthorized disbursement before the PRC-certified CPA completes a capital verification report and the bank has verified the foreign exchange registration. Once verified, funds can be settled into RMB for payroll, office lease, IT infrastructure, cloud services and consumer-lending loan disbursement, each payment requiring a purpose-linked invoice or receipt and SAFE-compliant real-use evidence. Repatriation of surplus capital or dividends requires prior SAFE or retail bank filing, tax settlement and audited financial statements; the wire must be processed through the same bank using a current-account or distributed-profit purpose code with proof of the foreign investor's bank account.For pure service-trade disbursements such as software licensing, IT hosting, data services and consultancy, use SWIFT MT103 with the service-trade payment purpose code and matching contract, invoice, withholding tax certificate and bank-transaction verification. For physical imports necessary to operate the lending technology, a commercial letter of credit (L/C at sight) or documentary collection (D/P or D/A) may be issued through the same onshore bank, but the WFOE's core loan-outbound flows must never travel through an L/C because lending is not a merchandise trade. Outbound disbursements for licensed micro-loan originations remain domestic RMB rails via bank transfer or the licensed borrower wallet, not SWIFT. All cross-border outbound payments must be reconciled in the SAFE trade-monitoring platform; any transfer made before documentation is available will be treated as non-compliant and automatically blocked.Onshore deliverable RMB/FX forward contracts in the interbank or bank-customer market with a genuine current-account or capital-account exposure and 100% underlying documentationCross-currency swaps for matched principal and interest on approved foreign-debt or shareholder-loan balances only after the debt has been registered with SAFE and within the foreign-debt quotaBank-intermediated FX options including collars where notional exposure is asymmetric but strictly limited to the audited forecast transaction flowOffshore non-deliverable forwards and any speculative NDF overlay are not permissible for a PRC domestic entity; if a hedge is needed, it must be settled deliverably onshore with banks holding a valid derivative license

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Cross-border fintech EMI players such as Airwallex, Currenxie and Payoneer are not licensed as domestic commercial banks in China and cannot lawfully open primary corporate accounts for PRC-incorporated WFOEs under the PBOC banking account rules. Their ancillary acceptance can only be used for inbound e-commerce collection in foreign currency and is not suitable as the regulated lending cash-flow spine. A high-risk micro-loan platform's bank account, loan disbursement custody account and consumer repayment collection mandates must be held at a SAFE-designated onshore bank because SAFE transaction-verification, AML customer due diligence and capital-account supervision require a licensed PRC banking counterparty. Any attempt to use EMI omnibus rails for equity capital settlement, consumer lending payouts, or profit remittance would trigger regulatory violation and immediate account freezing; therefore, the legal viability of EMI onboarding is Low and the recommended route is a domestic tier-one commercial bank account opened directly by the WFOE.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File threshold: intercompany tangible property transfers in the prior year exceeding RMB 200 million; intangible property transfers exceeding RMB 100 million; financial transfers (loans, guarantees) exceeding RMB 100 million; services exceeding RMB 40 million; other transactions exceeding RMB 20 million. Master File is mandatory if total related-party revenue (turnover) in the prior year exceeds RMB 1 billion. These thresholds are per State Taxation Administration Announcement No. 42 of 2016.

Economic substance scope: China applies a substance-over-form doctrine under Article 47 of EITL and the General Anti-Avoidance Rule (GAAR) under Article 120 of the Implementation Regulations. Tax authorities may assess whether the WFOE's personnel, offices, server logistics and decision-making functions in China constitute core income-generating activities (CIGA) applying the OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines. If the platform merely passes risks to an offshore parent without real control, or if contractual allocation of profits is inconsistent with actual contributions, the tax bureau may re-characterize the transaction, adjust the taxable profit, and levy a 5% interest rate marker on the assessed deficiency. Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) as known in offshore centres do not formally apply in China, but CIGA analysis in the 2024 Special Tax Adjustment Work Guidelines effectively enforces the same principle.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion Through Artificial Revenue Suppression

Statutory sanction: Article 63 of the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection: the tax authority will recover the underpaid tax and impose a fine of 50% to 500% of the evaded tax amount. If the evaded tax exceeds RMB 50,000 and exceeds 10% of the amount legally payable, Article 203 of the Criminal Law may result in a fine and imprisonment for up to 7 years for the responsible directors.

Director personal liability: Under Article 20 of the Company Law and Article 14 of the Tax Collection Administration Law, directors who knowingly approved the false accounting face joint and several civil liability to restore all evaded taxes plus interest, and may be subjected to border exit controls (blacklisting) and 15–365 days of administrative detention, with criminal imprisonment of 3–7 years for fraud involving public funds or aggravated circumstances.
Unlicensed Deposit Taking / Public Lending without Financial License

Statutory sanction: Article 176 of the Criminal Law (Illegal Absorption of Public Deposits) imposes fines of RMB 20,000–200,000 for the entity and imprisonment of 3–10 years for the 'directly responsible managers'. Under the Measures for the Administration of Internet Lending Information Intermediary Institutions (2016), unlicensed platforms are shut down and their bank accounts frozed.

Director personal liability: Corporate veil-piercing is specifically applied: directors, legal representatives and actual controllers are jointly and severally liable to return each investor's principal and interest if they have used investor funds for personal expenditures or to disguise the P2P loan book. Courts order travel bans (City Exit Notices) and Chinese personal ID restriction measures; criminal detention cannot be bailed if the raised funds exceed RMB 1 million.
Transfer Pricing Mispricing of the Core Lending Algorithm

Statutory sanction: Special Tax Adjustment under Article 41 EITL and Article 121 Implementation Regulation: the tax authority re-determines the arm's length price, adds back the excess royalty, imposes a 5% interest surcharge on the underpaid tax, and assesses a 50%–200% penalty for false pricing. Under Article 44 of the EITL, failure to file contemporaneous TP documentation triggers an additional penalty of RMB 100,000 to 1,000,000.

Director personal liability: The director who chairs the transfer pricing committee cannot disclaim liability by signing a separate 'service agreement'; under the Company Law's duty of care, if the inflated royalty caused the WFOE to violate its minimum registered capital obligations and default on creditors, the director must compensate the WFOE's loss from personal assets. The State Taxation Administration may blacklist the director's name under the 'Joint Punishment for Major Tax Overdue' system, restricting high-consumption travel, airline use and property purchase, and in the worst case initiate criminal proceedings for fraudulent avoidance under Article 201 Criminal Law (imprisonment up to 3 years for gross underpayment).
