Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum registered capital applies to a general wholly foreign-owned enterprise limited liability company engaged in management consulting or AEO supply-chain audit services. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA); online filing and registration are performed through the Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax) platform administered by the local competent tax office of the WFOE's registered address.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Upon incorporation under the Foreign Investment Law, the WFOE obtains a Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) from the Administration for Market Regulation (AMR); the USCC is the statutory Tax Identification Number (TIN) for enterprise income tax, VAT, and other national taxes. Step 2: Within 30 days after issuance of the business license, the WFOE must complete tax registration by activating the Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax) account with the local competent tax authority, using legal representative real-name authentication and the entity's USCC. Step 3: The WFOE submits digital copies of the business license, articles of association, legal representative identification, office lease proof, bank account information, and financial/accounting system details. Step 4: The competent tax authority opens the taxpayer profile, determines VAT taxpayer status (general VAT taxpayer or small-scale VAT taxpayer), sets the filing and payment cycle, and activates the Digitalized Electronic Invoice (DEI) quota. Step 5: For a foreign non-resident enterprise with no Chinese establishment, the Chinese resident payer or withholding agent must complete withholding tax registration through the e-Tax platform and file the non-resident tax withholding record; the non-resident may also apply for treaty relief using a Certificate of Tax Residency from its resident jurisdiction.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None for US-style check-the-box or partnership pass-through elections. A WFOE is treated as a Chinese resident enterprise taxable on worldwide income at the statutory enterprise income tax rate. No hybrid mismatch election exists under Chinese domestic law. In VAT, a taxpayer with annual taxable turnover at or below RMB 5,000,000 may elect to register as a general VAT taxpayer if certain accounting and internal control conditions are met; taxpayers above the threshold are mandatorily registered as general VAT taxpayers.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

6%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 annual taxable turnover is the mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration threshold; all entities with taxable supplies must complete tax registration and VAT status determination irrespective of turnover, and small-scale VAT taxpayer status applies below the threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For goods, the export must be physically shipped from China, declared to the General Administration of Customs (GACC) as an export clearance, and the export proceeds must be repatriated to a Chinese designated bank; the VAT rate on qualifying exported goods is 0% and attributable input VAT is rebatable. For services, zero-rating applies only to STA-listed zero-rated cross-border services such as international transportation, certain technology/design/research and development services, and recognized offshore service outsourcing arrangements, where the recipient is a non-resident enterprise, the service is consumed entirely outside China, and the service supply is supported by contract, invoice, performance evidence, and overseas payment receipts. A management consulting and AEO supply-chain audit service that is not specifically covered by the zero-rated service catalogue is generally VAT-exempt rather than zero-rated; exempt cross-border services do not generate input VAT credit recovery.

  • Customs Export Goods Declaration with GACC electronic clearance/release status and, where applicable, the WFOE's AEO registration identifier
  • Commercial invoice, packing list, and underlying export sales contract or purchase order
  • Bill of Lading, air waybill, or other international transport document proving physical export from China
  • Bank foreign exchange collection and settlement record, including the SAFE-backed trade in goods receipts/payments record evidencing receipt from the overseas buyer
  • Digitalized Electronic Invoice (DEI) issued for the export supply, with export transaction marking where required
  • For qualifying zero-rated services: executed service contract, service completion/acceptance certificate, certificate of tax residence of the overseas recipient, and documentary evidence that the service was performed or delivered outside China

All VAT invoices must be issued through the Electronic Tax Bureau's Digitalized Electronic Invoice (DEI) platform and transmitted in real time to the STA for validation. The system assigns each invoice a unique 20-digit electronic invoice code and an anti-counterfeiting verification code, replacing legacy physical tax-control devices. Input VAT credit is allowed only for invoices validated and available on the STA national invoice service platform. Large-scale taxpayers may integrate with the platform through endorsed APIs for automated issuance and inventory-invoice reconciliation. There is no private offline fiscal stamping; digital fiscal authentication is mandatory before an invoice is legally effective for VAT credit.

Failure to substantiate export zero-rating results in retroactive assessment of output VAT at the applicable domestic rate (6% for consulting and audit-type services or 13% for goods) plus a late-payment surcharge of 0.05% per day from the original due date. The STA may claw back input VAT credits previously claimed, and under Article 63 of the PRC Tax Administration Law, false or unverified documentation may trigger a penalty of 50% to 5 times the tax underpaid. Fraudulent export VAT refund or zero-rating claims, including forged customs declarations, false bills of lading, or fake foreign-exchange records, are recoverable with a penalty of 1 to 5 times the amount defrauded under Article 66; serious cases are transferred for criminal prosecution. The STA and GACC operate joint export-refund audit, big-data risk screening, and AEO compliance monitoring, and a violation may result in suspension or revocation of AEO certification in addition to tax and customs penalties.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Renminbi is operationally convertible on the current account for trade in goods, cross-border services, employee compensation, and legitimate business operating expenses, subject to bank verification of genuine underlying transactions and reporting through SAFE's monitoring systems. The capital account remains heavily controlled and not freely convertible. A Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise in mainland China must register its capital structure and foreign investment information before opening a foreign exchange capital account; once funds are remitted for registered capital, the receiving bank requires an underlying FDI registration, a legitimate RMB settlement purpose consistent with the national negative list, and a clear audit trail to the foreign shareholder. Profit remittance to the offshore parent is permitted but demands a statutory annual audit, tax clearance, board resolution, proof that all Chinese taxes have been settled, and a bank-facilitated SAFE payment reporting. Any attempt to use the entity for unauthorized cross-border lending, disguised equity transfers, or round-tripping is treated as illegal capital movement and can invalidate the treasury arrangement. In practice, the authorities encourage efficient operational convertibility for ordinary service and goods transactions but require complete harmonization of the bank ledger, tax filings, customs declarations, and service contracts. There is no meaningful convertibility right for speculative or capital-account purposes outside a licensed bank channel.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no mandatory conversion ratio applicable to export proceeds as of the current SAFE regime; the historical mandatory surrender requirement was abolished to permit exporters to retain or settle foreign exchange in a verified foreign exchange account. However, repatriation is compulsory: export foreign currency receipts must be brought back to mainland China within 180 days of the export customs declaration date unless a deferred collection registration is approved in advance with supporting documentation. The receiving bank must reconcile the incoming wire with the export invoice, customs declaration, bill of lading, and the Trade in Goods Foreign Exchange Monitoring System record. The exporter may convert the proceeds to RMB at the bank counter rate or retain them in a foreign exchange settlement account within its legitimate operational quota. Any irreconcilable shortfall, over-retention, or late repatriation requires a formal written explanation to SAFE and may trigger trade-based money laundering review.

Central bank supervision: The People's Bank of China (PBC) as the central monetary authority, together with the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) and its provincial branches, is the exclusive regulatory authority for foreign exchange control, cross-border capital flows, and banking KYC compliance for mainland-incorporated entities.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Offshore fintech payment institutions and EMIs such as Wise, Payoneer, and Currencycloud are not licensed to provide primary domestic RMB or foreign exchange accounts to a mainland-incorporated WFOE under PBC regulation on non-bank payment institutions and bank card clearing operations. They also cannot serve as a statutory funnel for capital injection, SAFE-monitored export verification, dividend remittance, or the mandatory physical KYC account-opening process. Only licensed Chinese commercial banks can act as the principal treasury and clearing channel for the WFOE. Such EMIs may be used as auxiliary collection tools for selected overseas client receivables where the entry is routed directly into a registered SAFE trade account at a domestic partner bank, but they cannot be the primary liquidity rail. Viability rating: Low for primary treasury use; limited to auxiliary cross-border collection in fully licensed bank partnership mode.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital must be remitted by the registered offshore shareholder directly through an SWIFT MT103 or a CIPS cross-border RMB payment into the WFOE's foreign exchange capital account at a designated tier-1 commercial bank. Before remittance, the WFOE must complete the Foreign Investment Information Report with the local market regulation authority and SAFE registration with the bank. The bank will classify the inflow as FDI Capital Injection, verify that the remitter is the shareholder recorded in the equity registration, and require a board resolution, subscription agreement, and anti-money-laundering source-of-funds dossier. Upon receipt, the bank settles the capital into RMB under the negative-list review and releases it only for the approved operational scope. A capital verification certificate or bank confirmation is issued for the statutory audit and commercial registry capital contribution filing. Any inflow from an unregistered third party or from an unregistered nominee will be rejected.For service-trade payments to overseas sub-auditors, software vendors, licensing counterparties, freight forwarders, and security equipment suppliers, the standard rail is SWIFT MT103 with the correct SAFE service-trade purpose code, accompanied by the commercial contract, invoice, tax registration certificate, and cross-border service payment filing evidence. For physical imports of security audit equipment such as surveillance systems, detection scanners, or customs-bonded verification hardware, the preferred clearing rail is a documentary sight letter of credit opened through the tier-1 bank against a commercial invoice, bill of lading, and clean transport document. Documentary collections are acceptable for lower-risk repeat purchases with long-standing suppliers. Cross-border RMB clearing for service and goods should be routed through CIPS to shorten settlement time and provide natural alignment with SAFE monitoring. All outbound payments above the facilitation threshold must receive the bank's genuine-transaction review; the AEO audit service itself is not an export of goods, so no commodity LC is required except where the WFOE lawfully re-sells hardware.Onshore Renminbi foreign exchange forward contracts, both fixed-date and window forwards, with a genuine underlying commercial exposure in a current account or capital account transaction and a bank-filed real-demand transaction record.Onshore FX swaps and cross-currency swaps for hedging the WFOE's foreign-currency working capital, loan repayment, or dividend exposure, provided the bank has the underlying exposure documentation and the transaction is booked through a licensed Chinese bank.Onshore RMB options, including structured options with no speculative build, limited to a maximum loss of the premium and fully supported by documentary evidence of the underlying exposure.Offshore non-deliverable forwards are excluded from the standard treasury mandate; an onshore WFOE may not use an NDF as a direct hedge because it falls outside the onshore real-demand principle and requires special no-arbitrage compliance approval through a licensed bank's offshore booking structure.

Such EMIs may be used as auxiliary collection tools for selected overseas client receivables where the entry is routed directly into a registered SAFE trade account at a domestic partner bank, but they cannot be the primary liquidity rail. Viability rating: Low for primary treasury use; limited to auxiliary cross-border collection in fully licensed bank partnership mode.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: The WFOE must prepare a Local File if annual related-party transactions exceed RMB 40 million for services or other transactions, RMB 200 million for tangible property transfers, RMB 100 million for intangible property transfers, or RMB 100 million for financial-asset transfers. A Master File is mandatory if aggregate related-party transactions in the prior year exceeded RMB 1 billion, and a Country-by-Country Report is required when the MNE group's consolidated revenue reaches RMB 5.5 billion.

Economic substance scope: China does not operate a GCC-style Economic Substance Regulation, but economic substance is enforced through the General Anti-Avoidance Rule under CIT Law Article 47 and Implementation Regulation Article 120. For treaty relief and transfer pricing, the WFOE must demonstrate Core Income-Generating Activities in China, including actual employees performing AEO audits, office or operational premises, business decision-making, risk management, bank accounts, and financial records. A WFOE without substantive activity is vulnerable to recharacterisation of intercompany transactions, denial of treaty benefits, and deemed distributions subject to 10% WHT.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and False Accounting

Statutory sanction: PRC Tax Collection Law Article 63 requires payment of the evaded tax plus a penalty of 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax and daily late-payment surcharge of 0.05%; CIT Law Article 41 authorises transfer-pricing adjustments; false invoice activity may trigger Criminal Law Article 205 imposing fines and imprisonment.

Director personal liability: Directors who direct or approve the fraudulent filings may have the corporate veil pierced under the PRC Company Law and be held personally liable for the unpaid tax and civil restitution; they may be blacklisted as seriously dishonest taxpayers, subjected to exit and travel bans, and face criminal liability under Criminal Law Article 201 with imprisonment up to 3 years for relatively large evasion and 3 to 7 years for aggravated evasion.
Treaty Abuse and Beneficial Ownership Fraud

Statutory sanction: The tax authority will deny treaty relief under SAT Bulletin 2015 No.60, reapply the 10% statutory WHT under CIT Law Article 37, impose the 0.05% daily late surcharge, and assess a penalty under Tax Collection Law Article 69 of 50% to 300% of the unwithheld tax. The arrangement may also be recharacterised under the General Anti-Avoidance Rule.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false beneficial-ownership declarations or establish the conduit structure to abuse the treaty can be jointly and severally liable for the under-withheld tax; they may be named in the tax dishonesty blacklist, barred from leaving China during the tax investigation, and criminally prosecuted under Criminal Law Article 201 if the conduit arrangement amounts to intentional tax evasion.
Customs AEO Fraud and Export Declaration Misstatement

Statutory sanction: The PRC Customs Law imposes confiscation of illegal proceeds, fines of up to 5 times the value of the goods or up to RMB 500,000 for false declarations, revocation of AEO certification and customs registration, and withdrawal of customs facilitation privileges; criminal smuggling and tax-refund fraud under Criminal Law Articles 153 and 204 can lead to imprisonment up to life in severe cases.

Director personal liability: Directors and legal representatives of the WFOE face personal liability for civil imposition and asset restitution where the corporate entity is used as an instrument of customs fraud; they may be blacklisted by the customs authority, subjected to travel and exit restrictions, and prosecuted criminally for smuggling or fraudulent export tax refunds, with imprisonment terms ranging from fixed terms to life imprisonment for enormous amounts.
