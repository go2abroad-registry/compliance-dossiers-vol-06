Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None: under the PRC Company Law and the 2014 capital registration reform, a standard WFOE limited liability company has no statutory minimum paid-up capital and no blocked escrow account requirement. The shareholders must set the subscribed registered capital in the Articles of Association and must make full contribution within five years after incorporation under the 2023 Amendment. Capital may be subscribed in cash or in permitted non-monetary property, and the actual contribution must be recorded in the company's financial books and in the register of shareholders, but no mandatory capital verification report is required for the ordinary digital-services WFOE. If the foreign investor later needs to remit equity capital into China, the full amount must be registered with the local SAFE through the operating bank.

Funding & unlock path: Not Applicable at the industrial licensing level; however, the leased office premises must comply with the Fire Protection Law of the People's Republic of China by maintaining fire-control equipment, evacuation signage, and a fire safety management protocol. If the office is used as a public gathering or large open-plan workplace, the local Fire and Rescue Department may conduct a fire inspection. Occupational safety for office workers follows the Law on Prevention and Control of Occupational Diseases and general workplace safety rules, but no Civil Defense industrial fire permit is required because no combustible raw materials, hazardous chemicals, or production machinery will be present.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China, acting through the China Electronic Tax Administration online portal (e-Tax) and the STA Electronic Invoice Service Platform for VAT and e-invoice administration

E-government filing portal

A PRC-incorporated Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE) is a Chinese resident enterprise. Upon incorporation with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR), the Unified Social Credit Code is automatically generated and synchronized to the STA as the sole Taxpayer Identification Number; no separate TIN application or certificate is required. The WFOE must complete the post-incorporation tax registration confirmation through the e-Tax portal within 30 days of issuance of the business license, including bank account filing, accounting system filing, invoice application, and appointment of a tax responsible person. The non-resident is then assigned a Chinese tax identification code for withholding, VAT and income tax compliance.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation: The WFOE is a PRC tax resident subject to Corporate Income Tax on worldwide income at the standard 25% rate; it is not pass-through eligible, and no territorial scope exemption applies to domestic-resident enterprises. Non-resident enterprises without a permanent establishment in China are taxed on China-source income only, but that classification does not apply to the WFOE itself. The WFOE is not located in a statutory tax-exempt zone unless a specific preferential park scheme is separately approved by the STA.

Available election forms

None: China does not operate a check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch entity election regime comparable to other jurisdictions. The WFOE cannot elect pass-through treatment. Limited VAT elections are available, such as the small-scale taxpayer status below the RMB 5 million turnover threshold, and the corporate income tax Small Low-Profit Enterprise preferential rate may self-apply if statutory conditions are met, but these do not alter the fundamental entity-level taxation classification.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 of annual taxable sales is the mandatory threshold above which a taxpayer must be registered as a general VAT taxpayer. All newly incorporated enterprises, including the WFOE, must complete basic tax registration within 30 days of obtaining the business license regardless of turnover. Below RMB 5,000,000, the WFOE may be classified as a small-scale taxpayer and may voluntarily apply for general taxpayer status if it requires full input VAT credits or issues special VAT invoices to customers.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For physical goods, exported goods are eligible for zero-rating and VAT export rebate if the exporter is a general VAT taxpayer, the goods are lawfully exported through customs, a valid Customs Export Declaration exists, the export proceeds are repatriated into China, and the exporter maintains full documentary proof. For cross-border services, the WFOE may claim zero-rating if its ASO, SEO, app localization and digital advertising services qualify under the STA's cross-border taxable services catalogue as services supplied to an overseas recipient, consumed wholly outside China, settled in foreign currency from outside China, and not used inside mainland China. The WFOE must file the cross-border service zero-rating or exemption declaration through the e-Tax portal before claiming the 0% rate and must demonstrate with contracts, invoices, remittance records, and service delivery evidence that the offshore principal is the beneficiary and that the services are not for domestic app-store users. If the services support both domestic and overseas distribution, only the overseas-consumed portion qualifies; the domestic portion remains taxable at 6% VAT.

  • Ocean Bill of Lading or Air Waybill or International Combined Transport Waybill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or bank foreign exchange receipt confirmation evidencing repatriation of the export sale proceeds
  • Commercial invoice and packing list matching customs declaration data

The Golden Tax Project Phase IV and the STA Electronic Invoice Service Platform mandate real-time fiscal clearance for all VAT invoices. Fully digitalized electronic invoices carry a unique invoice number and cryptographic code generated by the STA system, are transmitted to the STA upon issuance, and require no physical tax-control device or manual stamping. Traditional VAT special invoices must be issued through tax-control UKey or compatible authorized invoicing systems. E-commerce and ERP platforms must integrate with STA-approved electronic invoice APIs for bulk issuance, bookkeeping, and reconciliation. Monthly VAT returns filed through the e-Tax portal are pre-populated with invoice data, customs data, and treasury data, enabling automated cross-checking; failure to comply may result in invoice quota restrictions, system suspension, and administrative penalties.

The STA and China Customs jointly audit export zero-rating and export tax rebate claims using cross-agency data matching. If the customs declaration, invoice, Bill of Lading, foreign exchange receipt, or offshore service consumption evidence is missing, inconsistent, or fraudulent, the claimed zero-rating is retrospectively revoked and VAT is assessed at the applicable rate (13% for goods or 6% for services), together with late payment surcharge at 0.05% per day and an administrative penalty of 50% to five times the underpaid tax under the Law of the People's Republic of China on Tax Administration and Fee Collection. Falsely obtaining an export tax rebate or using false export documentation also constitutes a criminal offense under Article 204 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, subject to imprisonment, fines, and confiscation of property in serious cases. Failure to repatriate export proceeds within the statutory deadline may additionally trigger foreign-exchange administrative penalties under the State Administration of Foreign Exchange rules and can disqualify the exporter from future zero-rating treatment.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The RMB is convertible on current account for trade in services, but every receipt or payment must be verified against an underlying contract and processed through a SAFE-designated bank; there is no independent right to move funds without documentary proof. Capital account transactions, including inbound FDI equity, are not freely convertible and require FDI foreign exchange registration before the funds arrive, a dedicated foreign exchange capital account for the WFOE, post-receipt capital verification by a Chinese CPA, and settlement in RMB in tranches linked to real operating expenses. Profit remittance abroad is generally treated as a post-tax current-account item, but banks require audited financial statements, tax clearance, and a board resolution approving the dividend, and the Chinese withholding tax rate for outbound dividends is 10% unless reduced by an applicable double-tax treaty. Additional hurdles include matching foreign clients' names to bank remitters, avoiding so-called transit flows where the WFOE is merely a treasury pass-through for media payments, and collecting clean Apple/Google campaign documentation for every service invoice. Because the entity is an ASO/SEO localization agency, substance is evidenced by local staff, Chinese IP contracts, and access to the parent's app-store platform.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under current regulations, China has no mandatory foreign-exchange surrender requirement for trade or service receipts; the statutory requirement is repatriation and verification. Goods-trade export receipts must be reported through the bank's trade-monitoring system within the agreed trade period, with unusual collection after 90 days automatically flagged; service-related receipts in this agency model are matched to the contractual payment clause and long-dated overdue receivables over 365 days trigger bank compliance review. The mandatory conversion ratio is zero, meaning the WFOE may keep otherwise valid service receipts in a foreign currency account or settle them to RMB through a designated bank at the interbank rate.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) and State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Low as a statutory clearing rail; conditional as a settlement collection rail. Wise B2B and Payoneer are not licensed in China as deposit-taking banks or designated foreign-exchange banks; they cannot open the statutory FDI foreign exchange capital account, cannot perform SAFE FDI registration, cannot verify trade-in-services receipts for current account purposes, and cannot remit dividends on behalf of a mainland WFOE. A mainland WFOE may only maintain legal RMB and foreign-currency operating accounts through a Chinese licensed bank. However, Payoneer or Wise can be used offshore as an ancillary collection wallet for low-value, high-frequency App Store or ASO client receipts originating outside mainland China; those funds must then enter the Chinese bank account through a licensed cross-border payment institution or normal bank remittance, with the underlying service contract and platform statement attached. Chinese bank compliance will treat credits from Payoneer or Wise as higher-risk third-party cross-border e-commerce collections, requiring extensive proof that the EMI is not obscuring the true client or violating SAFE's direct current-account settlement principle. For this WFOE's formation treasury, all capital injections, dividends, and material trade disbursements must be bank-routed; EMI rails are limited to invoiced micro-collections under a documented commercial arrangement.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital must be injected by the registered foreign investor from its own bank account, denominated in USD, HKD, EUR, or another freely convertible currency, into the WFOE's SAFE-registered FDI Foreign Exchange Capital Account. Before the wire is initiated, the account-opening bank must complete the FDI foreign exchange registration in the SAFE system using the WFOE's incorporation documents, articles, shareholder resolution, and foreign investor passport or corporate certification. The SWIFT MT103 should identify the beneficiary bank and account number and include remittance information such as FDI equity contribution under contract number, with no third-party remitter permitted. After funds are credited, the bank conducts authenticity review and executes foreign exchange settlement in tranches into the RMB Basic Deposit Account, supported by the enterprise's stated use for salaries, office rent, marketing expenses, and software subscriptions. A Chinese CPA should then issue a capital verification report, and the entity should update the SAFE FDI record if the injected amount exceeds the registered amount. Any shareholder loan beyond equity must be separately registered as foreign debt under SAFE and is subject to macro-prudential limits. The bank reviews the payment under SAFE's current-account trade-in-services framework and assigns the correct purpose code for marketing or business services; no pre-approval is required for genuine service payments, but the documentary trail must be complete. For recurring digital advertising expenses, the WFOE should pay Apple, Google, and TikTok from its corporate bank account using a direct bank transfer rather than a personal card, and should maintain a separate ledger for media pass-through costs if the commercial model is gross billing. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low as a statutory clearing rail; conditional as a settlement collection rail. Wise B2B and Payoneer are not licensed in China as deposit-taking banks or designated foreign-exchange banks; they cannot open the statutory FDI foreign exchange capital account, cannot perform SAFE FDI registration, cannot verify trade-in-services receipts for current account purposes, and cannot remit dividends on behalf of a mainland WFOE. For this WFOE's formation treasury, all capital injections, dividends, and material trade disbursements must be bank-routed; EMI rails are limited to invoiced micro-collections under a documented commercial arrangement.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: RMB 200 million or more in intercompany tangible-property transactions, RMB 100 million in intercompany intangible-property or financial-asset transactions, or RMB 40 million in other related-party service/royalty/interest transactions, any of which triggers mandatory contemporaneous Local File and related-party transaction reporting; comprehensive annual related-party transaction schedules must be filed regardless of threshold.

Economic substance scope: Although China does not use the term 'Economic Substance Regulations' (ESR), Articles 41 and 47 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law and the Special Tax Adjustments Measures enforce a strict economic-substance standard. The WFOE must prove that its Chinese establishment performs real functions, owns controlling risks, and executes the core income-generating activities (CIGA) for the ASO/SEO localization business. Pure fee-skimmers, letter-box operations, or arrangements where offshore principals make managerial decisions without Chinese personnel are subject to General Anti-Avoidance Rule (GAAR) recharacterization and treaty-benefit denial.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Under Article 63 of the PRC Tax Administration Law, the State Taxation Administration will order repayment of the underpaid CIT, impose a late-payment surcharge of 0.05% per day, and levy an administrative fine of 50% to 500% of the evaded amount. When the evaded tax exceeds 10% of the payable tax and exceeds RMB 50,000, the entity is exposed to criminal prosecution under Article 201 of the PRC Criminal Law.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced where the director personally directs the concealment of offshore-bound revenue. That director faces joint and several civil restitution for the unpaid tax and surcharges, a departure ban (blacklisting) imposed by the tax and immigration authorities, and fixed-term imprisonment of up to 3 years for relatively large evasion, or 3–7 years where the amount is huge.
Shell Company / Sham Substance

Statutory sanction: Under Article 47 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law and the GAAR provisions of the Special Tax Adjustments Measures, the tax authority may re-determine the taxable income using reasonable methods, disallow treaty benefits, and restate profits to the Chinese entity. A sham substance claim may also activate the Company Law doctrine of disregard of corporate personality, leading to reversal of any tax-free intercompany transfers.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly execute the sham WFOE structure are personally liable for the corporate CIT shortfall up to the benefit they caused to be shifted offshore, may be placed on the State Credit Blacklist, and may face criminal liability under the Fraudulent Litigation or Tax Evasion articles where the arrangement is used to conceal diverted profits.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation

Statutory sanction: Under Article 41 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law and the PRC Transfer Pricing Documentation Rules, the tax bureau will make a primary adjustment to the taxable income, impose a 5-year retroactive limitation, add interest on the underpayment, and may levy a penalty of 50% to 500% for false declarations. Failure to file the required contemporaneous documentation triggers a presumption that the audit adjustment is correct.

Director personal liability: The director or financial controller who signs the false CIT return and related-party schedules is considered a directly responsible person and may be ordered to physically appear, produce financial records, or be restrained from leaving China. Where the TP manipulation is combined with tax evasion, the director inherits personal civil liability for restitution, disqualification from serving as a legal representative, and imprisonment under the criminal tax evasion provisions.
