Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A virtual office or commercial mailbox is not permissible for a WFOE registered in China. The Company Law and implementation regulations require a fixed registered address that is the actual business premises. Local market supervision bureaus and tax authorities reject post office boxes for obtaining a tax registration certificate (UCC) and opening a bank settlement account. For an IP patent prosecution agency, the State Intellectual Property Office (CNIPA) licensing criteria impose a physical office requirement for patent agency records. A virtual address may be allowed only under registered address on a temporary basis in certain free trade zones (e.g., one-stop service address), but industry regulators and banks generally require an actual meeting room, lease contract with a commercial property, and site inspection.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None. Under PRC Company Law (2023) and Foreign Investment Law, a WFOE engaged in patent prosecution and international trademark registration services has no statutory minimum paid-up capital and no blocked escrow account requirement. The sole shareholder must subscribe the registered capital as set out in the Articles of Association and must make actual payment according to the contribution timetable stated therein; excess paid-in capital may be withdrawn only through lawful procedures. The registered capital should nevertheless be sufficient to cover office leasing, payroll, professional indemnity, and operating costs.

Funding & unlock path: Office occupancy clearance: the landlord must provide a valid fire-safety inspection or acceptance certificate for the building; the WFOE, as lessee, must maintain fire extinguishers, emergency lighting, an evacuation plan, and fire-safety self-inspection records in accordance with the PRC Fire Control Law. General workplace safety registration under the Work Safety Law and occupational disease prevention obligations apply to all office employees. No civil defense shelter or special structural clearance is required for ISIC 7490.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; online electronic portal: Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax Bureau) operated under the unified STA national e-tax platform and provincial tax service websites.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the WFOE with the local State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR). The business license will bear the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which is simultaneously the legal entity's Tax Identification Number (TIN) and VAT registration number in the PRC tax system. Step 2: Within 30 days after license issuance, complete tax record-filing in the STA e-Tax Bureau, confirming taxpayer status for Enterprise Income Tax, VAT, urban maintenance and construction tax, education surcharges, stamp duty, and payroll/individual income tax withholding. Step 3: Determine VAT status: if projected annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000, general VAT taxpayer registration is mandatory; below that threshold, the WFOE may elect general taxpayer status or use the small-scale taxpayer simplified method. Step 4: Register bank account details in the e-Tax system, complete accounting system filing, and apply for electronic invoice issuance capacity and VAT invoice quotas. Step 5: For a foreign non-resident enterprise without an establishment in China, the PRC payer performs withholding tax registration and the non-resident obtains a separate TIN through the e-Tax Bureau; however, in this payload the WFOE is a Chinese resident entity, so the USCC constitutes its permanent statutory TIN.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None for entity classification. PRC law does not provide check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election mechanisms. A WFOE is automatically a resident enterprise taxed at entity level under the Enterprise Income Tax Law. For VAT only, a taxpayer below the RMB 5,000,000 annual taxable sales threshold may elect small-scale simplified taxation; this is not a corporate classification election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No monetary threshold for basic tax registration because a WFOE is automatically registered as a legal entity. The statutory VAT status threshold is RMB 5,000,000 in annual taxable sales for mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration; below this amount, small-scale taxpayer treatment applies, with an optional small-scale VAT exemption for monthly sales not exceeding RMB 100,000 or quarterly sales not exceeding RMB 300,000.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For goods: 0% VAT applies when goods are actually exported from China, the export is evidenced by a Customs export declaration, the exporter is a registered VAT taxpayer, transport documentation confirms movement out of China, and foreign-currency export proceeds are repatriated through bank channels. For cross-border services: zero-rating or VAT exemption is available only for services expressly listed in the STA cross-border taxable service catalogue, such as international transport, research and development, software design, and certain qualifying digital/information services, provided the service recipient is a non-resident enterprise, the services are consumed outside China, and documentary proof is retained. Patent prosecution and international trademark registration agency services are not automatically zero-rated; they are generally treated as taxable services at 6% unless a separate qualifying cross-border service category is met. If the service does not qualify, output VAT must be charged and paid.

  • Customs Export Goods Declaration Form filed through the Customs Single Window system
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill or equivalent transport document evidencing export movement from China
  • Commercial invoice / export contract or service agreement
  • VAT special invoice or ordinary VAT invoice issued for the export or cross-border supply
  • Bank foreign-exchange collection certificate, settlement/verification document, or foreign-currency bank statement evidencing receipt from an overseas party within the repatriation deadline
  • For cross-border services: engagement letter, service performance documentation, and a statement confirming the non-resident recipient and offshore consumption

China operates the STA VAT invoice management system under the Golden Tax framework. VAT invoices must be issued through the approved e-invoicing system or the e-Tax Bureau in digital form. Full Digital Electronic Invoices are integrated with the STA real-time clearance and validation engine; each invoice carries a unique tax control number and anti-counterfeit QR code, and invoice data is transmitted to the national VAT electronic invoice platform as a condition of validity. ERP and accounting systems may use authorized machine-to-machine interfaces/API-style integration for invoice issuance and data reporting. Third-party manual stamping cannot replace the digital tax-control marking; invoices must be verified through the National VAT Invoice Verification Platform.

The STA and the General Administration of Customs may jointly audit export VAT refund and zero-rating claims. Unverified or fraudulent export zero-rating results in immediate clawback of the input VAT refunded and output VAT underdeclared, plus a daily late-payment surcharge of 0.05% and an administrative penalty of 50% to 500% of the evaded tax under the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection. Fraudulent export rebate claims also trigger criminal liability under Article 204 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, including fines, confiscation, and imprisonment. The look-back period for tax fraud is unlimited; for non-fraudulent underpayments, the general clawback window is 3 to 5 years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China maintains a managed floating exchange-rate regime with RMB freely convertible on current account and strictly controlled, approval-based convertibility on capital account. For a WFOE, ordinary operational payments such as IP fees, patent office charges, salaries and taxes can be settled with the bank under the current-account truthfulness review principle, provided invoices, contracts and customs or tax documents are presented. Capital account flows, including inbound equity contributions, cross-border loans, dividend remittance and liquidation proceeds, require pre-registration or approval by SAFE and are settled through dedicated bank accounts. The WFOE must first complete foreign investment registration with the local commerce bureau and Foreign Exchange Registration for Direct Investment at a designated bank. The bank then grants FDI capital account status and permits conversion only into an RMB settlement-pending account for permitted operating expenditures. Profit remittance to the overseas parent is lawful after annual statutory audit and tax clearance, but requires evidence of paid-in registered capital and proof that all statutory registration obligations are current. Withdrawal of investment or reduction of registered capital requires an additional SAFE liquidation registration and will be subject to bank verification of surplus, tax payments and creditor claims. Trade-related export receipts are not subject to a literal surrender ratio, but all such proceeds must be repatriated and banked domestically. Offshore fintech channels cannot legalise RMB conversion or replace SAFE-registered banking procedures.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the SAFE trade-in-goods monitoring regime, merchandise export receipts must be repatriated to and credited within a domestic current-account foreign exchange bank account of the exporter. Banks perform total-amount screening against customs export data and require matching customs declarations within the monitoring window, with 90 days generally applicable to advanced receipts, deferred payments and prepayment adjustments. No statutory fixed conversion ratio is imposed on export receipts; the enterprise may retain 100% of export foreign currency in its current-account foreign exchange bank account or instruct the bank to convert any amount into RMB at the prevailing market rate. If export proceeds remain unrepatriated more than 180 days after export customs clearance, the exporter must submit a written delayed collection application to the local SAFE branch together with the original trade contract, customs declaration, transport documents and the bank confirmation explaining the commercial cause.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China, operating under the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low and legally blocked as a primary account provider. Offshore digital EMI platforms such as Wise, Revolut and Airwallex are neither licensed nor authorised by the PBOC/SAFE to hold or settle RMB deposits for onshore Chinese entities, and no PRC anti-money laundering regime permits a WFOE to use them as its formal operational account. An offshore EMI can at best serve as an ancillary information reference or facilitate parent-side remittance initiation, but all statutory capital injections, export receipts, tax payments, patent-office disbursements and dividend remittances must move through a SAFE-designated domestic licensed bank. Using an EMI as an offshore treasury buffer without a recognised foreign exchange registration would create an unreported offshore liability and expose the WFOE to account freezing, PBOC fines and adverse KYC findings in any subsequent audit.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low and legally blocked as a primary account provider. Offshore digital EMI platforms such as Wise, Revolut and Airwallex are neither licensed nor authorised by the PBOC/SAFE to hold or settle RMB deposits for onshore Chinese entities, and no PRC anti-money laundering regime permits a WFOE to use them as its formal operational account. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: RMB 100,000,000 for tangible asset transfers, RMB 10,000,000 for intangible property transfers and service transactions, and RMB 1,000,000,000 aggregate related-party transactions for Master File preparation, as revised by SAT Announcement [2016] No. 42; a WFOE must also file the Annual Related Party Transaction Report when any related-party transactions exist, irrespective of the documentation thresholds.

Economic substance scope: China does not administer a separate UAE-style Economic Substance Regulations registration regime, but the statutory General Anti-Avoidance Rules, the special tax adjustment regime, and the treaty beneficial ownership doctrine require the WFOE and any non-resident intermediate holding company to perform substantive Core Income-Generating Activities (CIGA) in the claimed jurisdiction. A patent prosecution WFOE must maintain qualified employees, physical premises, decision-making authority, business risk assumption, and contemporaneous records proving that services and intangible property transfers were actually performed and compensated at arm's length. Entities lacking such substance will be denied treaty benefits and may be recharacterized under Article 47 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Transfer pricing manipulation and tax evasion

Statutory sanction: A special tax adjustment under Article 41 and Article 48 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law imposes upward pricing adjustments, imputed interest at market rates, and an administrative penalty of 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax under Article 63 of the Tax Collection and Administration Law. Repeated or intentional conduct reaching the criminal threshold triggers prosecution under Article 201 of the PRC Criminal Law.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil may be pierced if directors signed or knowingly approved the false invoices and pricing arrangements; directors are jointly and severally liable for civil restitution of the unpaid tax. The legal representative is blacklisted, placed under an exit ban, and prohibited from leaving China until the tax liabilities are settled; fraudulent conduct after the criminal threshold is met carries imprisonment of up to seven years plus fines and confiscation of personal assets.
Shell company and DTA treaty shopping

Statutory sanction: The tax authority will deny treaty benefits on the basis of the OECD Principal Purpose Test and the Chinese beneficial ownership doctrine, recharacterize the intermediary under the General Anti-Avoidance Rule of Article 47 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, and assess the 10% statutory withholding tax on the full dividend stream, plus daily late surcharges and fines of 50% to 500% if the arrangement is characterized as fraudulent tax avoidance.

Director personal liability: Directors who structured, approved, or signed the shareholder resolutions and remittance instructions for the sham holding structure are jointly and severally liable for the additional withholding tax and civil damages. The legal representative is placed on the tax blacklist with an international exit ban; deliberate falsification of treaty benefit claims is criminal fraud under Articles 201 and 264 of the PRC Criminal Law, exposing directors to imprisonment of up to seven years and personal asset forfeiture.
Withholding tax non-compliance and false non-resident tax declaration

Statutory sanction: Under Article 69 of the Tax Collection and Administration Law, the WFOE as withholding agent is fined 50% to 300% of the amount under-withheld and must pay the unpaid tax, plus a daily surcharge of 0.05%; if the false classification is found to be deliberate concealment, Article 63 penalties of 50% to 500% apply and the case can be referred for criminal prosecution under Article 201 of the PRC Criminal Law.

Director personal liability: Directors who approved the remittance instructions, signed the foreign payment agreements, or directed the accounting treatment are personally liable for civil restitution where the WFOE lacks assets to satisfy the reinforced tax assessment; the legal representative is blacklisted and barred from exiting China until liabilities are discharged; knowing participation in the false withholding declaration carries criminal liability with imprisonment of up to seven years and personal fines, extending the pierce of the corporate veil to personal bank accounts and assets.
