No statutory minimum registered capital is imposed on a general Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise under current PRC Company Law, provided the proposed business scope is not subject to the Foreign Investment Negative List. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA); online electronic portal is the unified Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax) platform operated by the STA and accessed through provincial and municipal tax service bureaus.

E-government filing portal

Under Mainland China's multi-credential integration regime, the statutory tax identification number (TIN) for a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise is the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) embedded in the SAMR business license; no separate VAT ID is issued. The foreign-invested WFOE and its non-resident shareholders must proceed as follows. 1. The foreign investor obtains the WFOE business license from the State Administration for Market Regulation using notarized and legalized corporate existence documents, board resolutions, articles of association, registered address proof, and legal representative identity documentation. 2. Upon license issuance, SAMR transfers the registration record electronically to the STA, which automatically creates the WFOE taxpayer account using the USCC as the TIN. 3. The WFOE legal representative and designated tax officer complete supplementary tax registration through the e-Tax platform within 30 calendar days, declaring accounting system details, domestic bank account, parent/shareholder particulars, business scope, VAT taxpayer type, and applicable VAT rate category. 4. The e-Tax account is activated, invoice quotas are set, and the WFOE obtains the tax-control digital certificate and encryption credentials required to issue fully digitalized electronic VAT invoices through the STA National Unified Electronic Invoice Service Platform. 5. Foreign non-resident shareholders that do not carry on a Chinese business are not issued a separate mainland TIN; for China-source income such as dividends, royalties, or service fees, the WFOE withholds and remits the tax and the local STA records the foreign shareholder identity in the withholding filing reference. 6. Any change to legal representative, registered address, capital structure, business scope, or bank account must be reported through the e-Tax platform within 30 days.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation - The WFOE is a Chinese tax resident enterprise taxed at the entity level under the Enterprise Income Tax Law on its worldwide income; no flow-through or partnership treatment is available to the foreign shareholders. For VAT, the WFOE is a separate taxpayer classified as a small-scale VAT taxpayer or a general VAT taxpayer depending on annual taxable sales.

Available election forms

None. Mainland China does not provide a check-the-box entity classification election for a WFOE limited liability company, no elective pass-through status, and no domestic hybrid mismatch election. The STA may recharacterize an arrangement under substance-over-form, controlled-foreign-company, thin capitalization, and general anti-avoidance rules if hybrid structures create unintended tax benefits.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Annual taxable sales exceeding RMB 5,000,000 (or the then-current statutory small-scale taxpayer threshold) in any consecutive 12-month period triggers mandatory registration as a general VAT taxpayer; below the threshold the WFOE may remain a small-scale taxpayer subject to simplified VAT calculation. VAT taxpayer registration is integrated with the business license, so the threshold primarily determines general versus small-scale classification and invoice credit entitlement.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating or exemption for the cross-border supply of 3D game art, sculpting, rigging, and creative asset services is available when the services are supplied to an overseas client for consumption entirely outside Mainland China. The WFOE must evidence that no domestic consumer exists, the contract is in the name of a non-resident principal, the work product is delivered and used offshore, and the consideration is received in a permitted foreign exchange currency through an onshore bank channel. The statutory framework includes Caishui [2016] No. 36 and STA Announcement No. 29 of 2016 governing cross-border taxable services. If the service qualifies as an export zero-rated service, output VAT is charged at 0% and input VAT attributable to that export may be refunded; if it qualifies only as an exempt cross-border service, output VAT is not charged but corresponding input VAT is not refundable. Proceeds collected through digital payment rails such as PayPal must be swept into the WFOE onshore domestic bank account and settled through SAFE-designated foreign exchange procedures before the zero-rating can be substantiated.

  • Customs Export Goods Declaration Form issued by the General Administration of Customs of China for any physical deliverables accompanying the service, such as sample models, hard drives, or art assets.
  • Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, or logistics handover certificate evidencing physical export movement when goods, prototypes, data storage devices, or printed art assets cross the border.
  • Cross-border service agreement or work order showing the overseas client, scope of 3D art production, ownership of intellectual property, delivery terms, and offshore consumption clause.
  • Commercial invoice issued from the e-Tax system, clearly marked as export zero-rated or exempt service, matching the contract currency and receivable amount.
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC), bank inward remittance advice, or foreign exchange settlement receipt from a SAFE-designated bank evidencing actual receipt and conversion of the export proceeds from the non-resident client.
  • Client acceptance certificate, milestone sign-off, handover log, or digital delivery confirmation, supported by the non-resident client business registration or tax residency certificate, proving that the deliverable was transferred to and consumed outside Mainland China.

The WFOE is required to issue fully digitalized electronic VAT invoices through the STA National Unified Electronic Invoice Service Platform. Invoices carry an encrypted digital signature and anti-forgery QR code and are uploaded to the STA Golden Tax system in near real-time at issuance. Large-scale issuers must integrate their ERP systems through the standard STA data-exchange and API protocol; invoice data is automatically reconciled against the VAT return, invoice credit pool, and customer/vendor profiles to detect volume spikes, split-billing, and cross-border mismatches. There is no manual printing of fully digitalized invoices, and the tax authority can block invoice quotas or trigger on-site inspection when anomaly indicators are met.

The STA collaborates with the General Administration of Customs and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange in joint risk reviews using Golden Tax system data, customs export records, and foreign exchange receipt systems. If the zero-rating claim is audited and any document in the substantiation bundle is false, incomplete, or inconsistent, the WFOE must immediately unwind the zero rating and pay output VAT at the applicable domestic rate, generally 6% for its modern services, plus a late-payment surcharge of 0.05% of the tax due per day and an administrative penalty ranging from 50% to 5 times the underpaid tax where the underpayment is characterized as evasion or fraud. In serious cases, false issuance of VAT invoices or fraudulent export-tax-refund claims carries criminal liability under the Criminal Law, cancellation of export-refund facilities, loss of input VAT deductions, inclusion on the tax-integrity blacklist, and retrospective clawbacks. The tax authority may reopen underpaid or false zero-rated transactions for three to five years under the Tax Collection Administration Law, and for fraud or evasion the recovery period is not closed until the tax, interest, and penalties are fully collected.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The RMB is convertible on current account under China's IMF Article VIII undertakings, meaning export service receipts for 3D game art, such as WFOE invoices for sculpting and rigging services, can be received and settled through licenced banks after trade authenticity verification. Capital account items remain restricted. A WFOE's initial capital injection requires SAFE's foreign direct investment registration, a dedicated FDI capital account and a specific approved purpose. Subsequent profit remittances to the overseas parent are classified as current account investment income but remain operationally controlled: they are permitted only after audited financial statements show genuine distributable profits, statutory reserves have been appropriated, enterprise income tax has been settled, tax clearance certificates have been obtained, and the remitting bank and SAFE have verified the board resolution. Dividends cannot be remitted in advance of profits, and settled capital funds cannot be diverted into prohibited uses such as domestic securities, real estate speculative investment, or non-operating loans. The bank will also monitor settlement volumes against the WFOE's declared business scope, payroll, and operating expenses to prevent shell-company operations. In practice, convertibility is therefore readily available for genuine trade and service flows but remains tightly verified and subject to administrative review, particularly for related-party payments and year-end profit repatriation.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For goods and service export proceeds, the WFOE must repatriate all foreign exchange earnings onshore through a licenced Chinese bank, normally within 180 days from the payment date or the commercial maturity schedule agreed in the export contract. There is no fixed mandatory conversion ratio after settlement because SAFE abolished forced surrender; the company may retain the foreign currency in a foreign exchange current-account settlement account or convert it into RMB at the bank's prevailing market rate. However, all receipts must be traceable to real trade and must be reported under SAFE's trade monitoring system using the correct service-trade BOP code. Any transaction without an underlying contract, invoice, or deliverable evidence will be rejected, and after conversion the WFOE must keep sufficient current-account records to demonstrate the flow from foreign client funds to domestic operating accounts.

Central bank supervision: The People's Bank of China (PBoC) as the central bank, with the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) as the designated foreign exchange control regulator

PayPal Fit: High

Viability is very low and legally not feasible as a primary account. Offshore fintech electronic money institutions are not licensed under PRC banking and foreign-exchange regulations to hold a WFOE's corporate RMB account, to operate an FDI capital account, or to file current-account trade data with SAFE. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File is mandatory when annual related-party tangible asset transfers exceed RMB 200 million, or intangible asset transfers exceed RMB 100 million, or financial asset transfers exceed RMB 100 million, or other related-party transactions exceed RMB 40 million. Master File must be prepared when the WFOE belongs to a multinational group whose prior-year consolidated revenue exceeds RMB 5.5 billion or whose total related-party transactions exceed RMB 1 billion.

Economic substance scope: China applies a substance-over-form requirement and the beneficial ownership test for treaty and withholding matters. Although China does not operate a standalone Economic Substance Regulations filing regime, the WFOE must demonstrate core income-generating activities actually performed in China, including operational decision-making, risk-bearing, employee headcount, office premises and bank accounts. If the WFOE is a mere invoice shell with no real activity, the State Taxation Administration may re-characterize income, deny treaty benefits, impute a deemed dividend to the foreign parent and impose special tax adjustment interest.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Shell entity / tax treaty abuse

Statutory sanction: Denial of treaty relief, re-application of 10% statutory withholding tax, re-characterization of the entire income under the General Anti-Avoidance Rule (Enterprise Income Tax Law Article 47 and Article 120), additional tax with interest, and a penalty of 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax under Tax Collection Law Article 63.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly used the shell WFOE to channel profits and misuse treaty relief will face civil asset restitution to the Chinese fisc, joint and several liability for the unpaid tax if corporate assets are stripped, travel bans and blacklisting in the tax integrity system, and criminal prosecution for tax evasion under Criminal Law Article 201 with imprisonment of up to seven years where the evaded amount crosses the criminal threshold.
Transfer pricing manipulation

Statutory sanction: Tax authority may make an adjusted assessment under Enterprise Income Tax Law Article 41 and Article 44, reprice the related-party transactions using the most appropriate arm's-length method, disallow excessive deductions, impose a 5% per annum interest charge on the underpayment, and apply penalties of 50% to 500% of underpaid tax where the behavior constitutes tax evasion; refusal to produce contemporaneous documentation also triggers a penalty under Article 121 of the Tax Collection Law implementing measures.

Director personal liability: Directors who designed or signed the manipulated contracts face personal civil liability for restitution, blacklisting and exit bans under cross-border tax enforcement cooperation, and criminal imprisonment if the underpayment is knowingly concealed through false documentation; courts may pierce the corporate veil to reach personal and family assets when the WFOE is found to be a mere instrumentality of the foreign shareholder.
Disguised profit repatriation / failure to withhold dividends

Statutory sanction: The tax authority re-characterizes the disguised remittance as a dividend and imposes 10% withholding tax on the entire amount, recovers unpaid withholding tax from the WFOE as withholding agent, adds late-payment surcharge at 0.05% per day, and imposes a penalty of 50% to 500% of the unpaid withholding tax under Tax Collection Law Article 63 and Article 69.

Director personal liability: Directors who directed the fake service or loan scheme are personally liable for the un-collected withholding tax if the WFOE cannot pay, face civil asset restitution and freezing of personal bank accounts, are subjected to travel restriction and blacklisting as serious tax violators, and may be sentenced to up to seven years' imprisonment for tax evasion where the disguised dividend scheme intentionally misleads the tax authority.
