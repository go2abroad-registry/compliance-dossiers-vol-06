Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-up capital is imposed for an ISIC 7490 medical device regulatory consulting WFOE. Under the PRC Company Law (2023 Revision), the registered capital declared in the Articles of Association must be fully subscribed within five years from establishment unless a shorter period is specified in the Articles. No escrow or blocked account rule applies to consulting service WFOEs; the state does not require funds to be deposited in an escrow account before registration. Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA). Online electronic portal: National Unified Electronic Tax Service Platform (etax.chinatax.gov.cn), operated by the STA; all tax registration, VAT invoice issuance, filing, export tax refund and non-resident taxpayer procedures are administered through this platform.

E-government filing portal

For a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE): (1) Obtain a Business License from the local Administration for Market Regulation; the registration data is automatically transmitted to the competent tax office; (2) Complete tax registration in the National Unified Electronic Tax Service Platform within 30 days after the Business License date, confirming the taxpayer type as a Chinese resident enterprise; (3) Use the Unified Social Credit Code printed on the Business License as the permanent Tax Identification Number (TIN); no separate paper TIN certificate is issued; (4) Appoint a legal representative, open a bank account in China, and complete Corporate Income Tax, VAT, social insurance and local surcharge registrations through the same platform; (5) Register for VAT as a small-scale taxpayer or apply for General VAT Taxpayer status if estimated annual taxable sales exceed CNY 5,000,000; (6) Apply for invoice type, invoice cap and entitlement to issue Fully Digitalized Electronic Invoices. For a foreign non-resident entity without a permanent establishment that has China-sourced payments, the TIN is obtained through the withholding agent using the Non-Resident Taxpayer Registration module in the same electronic platform, submitting certificate of incorporation, tax residence certificate, and a written explanation of the China-sourced payments.

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None. PRC Corporate Income Tax law does not provide a check-the-box pass-through election or a hybrid mismatch entity election for a WFOE; the entity is mandatorily taxable as a Chinese tax resident enterprise on its worldwide income at the 25% rate. VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

6%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Tax registration is compulsory from establishment without a minimum turnover threshold; every WFOE must register with the competent tax authority within 30 days of Business License issuance. Mandatory General VAT Taxpayer registration applies when annual aggregate taxable sales exceed CNY 5,000,000; taxpayers below this threshold may remain small-scale taxpayers or voluntarily register as General VAT Taxpayers.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods exports: 0% VAT applies only where the goods are physically exported, the WFOE is the exporter named in the Export Goods Customs Declaration, the goods clear Chinese customs, and the export proceeds are received and repatriated in foreign currency within the statutory period. Offshore services: cross-border regulatory consulting, including FDA 510(k) and CE-mark compliance consulting, qualifies for VAT zero-rating or exemption only if the recipient is a non-resident enterprise or individual, the service is performed for and consumed entirely outside China, no domestic entity in China obtains an economic benefit from the service, and the consideration is paid in foreign currency and repatriated to China. The service must be classified in the Catalogue of Cross-Border Taxable Services for VAT Zero-Rating or Exemption (CaiShui [2016] No. 36, Annex 4, and implementing STA announcements). If the classification or offshore-consumption evidence fails, the STA will reclassify the income as domestic taxable revenue subject to 6% VAT.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Export Goods Customs Declaration Form) submitted through the China International Trade Single Window, or the electronic customs clearance record
  • Bill of Lading (or Air Waybill) evidencing physical departure of the goods from Chinese customs territory
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or a bank-issued Foreign Exchange Receipt/Return Certificate proving receipt and repatriation of the export collection in foreign currency
  • Commercial invoice, sales contract and packing list matching the customs declaration
  • For service exports: cross-border service agreement, statement of work, deliverables proving offshore consumption (for example FDA 510(k) submission acknowledgment, CE technical file documentation, regulatory correspondence), and bank receipts for foreign-currency payments

The STA mandates the Fully Digitalized Electronic Invoice (FDEI) system. VAT invoices are created through the National Electronic Invoice Service Platform and transmitted to the tax authority in real time. The platform assigns a unique invoice number and cryptographic digital seal, and buyers must verify the invoice on the national invoice verification system before claiming VAT input credits. Paper invoices issued from legacy tax-control devices remain subject to real-time upload through the VAT Invoice Management System; no private invoice rendering, local fiscal stamping or off-platform clearance is recognized. Special VAT invoices for General VAT Taxpayers are subject to online matching and anti-fraud validation.

The STA, the General Administration of Customs and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange exchange audit data to verify export declarations, VAT refund claims and foreign exchange receipts. Failure to substantiate zero-rating - including missing FIRC, uncollected export proceeds beyond 180 days, false customs declarations, or services actually consumed in China - triggers reversal of the 0% rate, assessment of 6% VAT on the gross revenue, daily late-payment surcharge of 0.05% per day, clawback of any input VAT refunded with interest, administrative penalties of 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax under the Tax Collection Administration Law of the People's Republic of China, and criminal referral for export tax-refund fraud under the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates an IMF Article VIII current-account convertibility regime, meaning trade and service payments, wages, and profit remittances are generally convertible once the bank has verified the underlying transaction documentation. However, the renminbi remains heavily managed and is not freely convertible on the capital account; foreign direct investment, foreign-currency borrowings, outbound investments, and cross-border guarantees all require registration, approval, or filing with SAFE or via delegated commercial banks. Capital inflows such as registered capital are credited to a dedicated foreign-currency capital account and must be registered under the FDI information reporting system before settlement into RMB. Profit repatriation by a WFOE is permitted on a current-account basis, provided the entity has filed annual financial statements, completed corporate income tax settlement, and presents an audited profit-distribution resolution and remittance application to its settlement bank. Historical mandatory export surrender ratios have been abolished; exporters may retain 100% of their proceeds in foreign-currency accounts or convert voluntarily. Nevertheless, the authorities require truthfulness in trade documentation and monitor flows through the Trade Foreign Exchange Monitoring System, imposing substantial penalties for false invoicing or capital-account disguised remittances.

Export proceeds surrender rules

The statutory regime no longer imposes a fixed conversion ratio; the mandatory surrender requirement was fully abolished in 2012. Export proceeds from goods trade must be repatriated to China and received by a domestic bank within the time frame corresponding to the commercial terms, and in practice exporters must complete trade-fx verification within 90 days of the customs declaration date for standard export contracts unless a longer contractual payment period is registered. Enterprises may retain the whole 100% of the foreign currency in their export proceeds settlement account or settle into RMB at their discretion, subject to bank verification and true-matching of customs, contract, invoice, and shipping data.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) as the monetary authority, with the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) as the delegated foreign exchange administrative body.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Legally non-viable as a primary banking rail for a China-domiciled WFOE. Fintech EMIs (e.g., Wise, Payoneer, Revolut) are not licensed by PBOC or SAFE to provide RMB clearing, domestic current accounts, or capital-account settlement services in mainland China. Their utility is confined to ancillary collection of overseas client payments before conversion into explicit operational inflows; however, all RMB-denominated tax payments, capital injection, and profit repatriation must route through a licensed PRC commercial bank under SAFE verification. Any attempt to use an EMI as the operational treasury account would breach PRC banking law and expose the entity to account freezing and sanctions.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital injection must be routed via SWIFT wire from the overseas shareholder's bank to the WFOE's FDI capital account (or settlement account) at a designated PRC bank, with the remittance purpose code 'Investment in operating enterprises' (or equivalent SAFE capital-account purpose code). The WFOE must first complete Foreign Investment (FDI) information registration with SAFE at the local branch through the bank, open a capital account based on the registration certificate, and then the CPA in China issues a capital verification report upon receipt. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Contemporaneous Local File is statutorily required when annual related-party transactions exceed any single threshold under SAT Public Notice [2016] No. 42: RMB 200 million for tangible asset transfers, RMB 100 million for intangible asset transfers, RMB 100 million for financial asset transfers, and RMB 40 million for related-party service fees. Master File is required when the group's annual consolidated revenue exceeds RMB 2 billion and the Chinese entity is part of an MNE group that prepares Country-by-Country reporting or has significant cross-border related-party transactions.

Economic substance scope: China does not apply the Gulf-style Economic Substance Regulations, but applies an explicit substance-over-form doctrine through Article 47 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, the Special Tax Adjustment Measures, and the General Anti-Abuse Rule. For a medical-device regulatory consulting WFOE, the Core Income-Generating Activities (CIGA) include contract negotiation, regulatory strategy formation, FDA submission preparation, CE technical file drafting, project management, risk assumption and client-facing scientific decision-making. The WFOE must have premises, qualified staff, decision-making records and cash-flow controls in China; without real economic substance, tax authorities may deny treaty benefits, disregard shell entities, disregard intercompany allocations, impose withholding back-up, and recharacterise profits as taxable dividends.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion - Concealment of Revenue or Fictitious Costs

Statutory sanction: Under Article 63 of the Tax Collection and Administration Law and Article 63 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, the tax authority recovers the underpaid tax, imposes a daily surcharge of 0.05%, and levies a administrative fine of 50% to 500% of the evaded tax. Under Article 201 of the Criminal Law, if the evaded amount exceeds 10% of taxable income and RMB 50,000, imprisonment is up to 3 years plus fine; if it exceeds 30% and RMB 500,000, imprisonment is 3 to 7 years plus fine.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced under Article 23 of the PRC Company Law where a shareholder or director abuses the WFOE's independent legal personality to evade taxes. Directors who signed false declarations or directed concealment face joint and several civil liability for restitution of the unpaid tax and fines, asset freezes, travel-ban blacklisting under the dishonest judgment debtor system, and criminal imprisonment of up to 7 years for tax evasion.
Fraudulent Invoicing and False Documentary Evidence

Statutory sanction: Issuing or obtaining false special VAT invoices violates Article 205 of the Criminal Law, and issuing false unspecified invoices violates Article 205-1; penalties include fines and imprisonment up to 15 years or life imprisonment in especially serious cases. The false invoices are wholly non-deductible, input VAT credits are denied, and Article 63 penalties of 50% to 500% of evaded tax apply on top.

Director personal liability: Directors, financial controllers and legal representatives who sign or order false documentation cannot hide behind the corporation. Article 23 of the Company Law allows courts to impose joint personal civil liability for all unpaid tax and penalties, and criminal liability extends to personal imprisonment, confiscation of personal assets, restitution to the treasury, and inclusion on international or domestic travel-blacklist enforcement systems.
Shell Company Treaty Abuse and Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Article 47 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law and the Special Tax Adjustment Measures empower the State Taxation Administration to make an adjustment, deny the treaty benefit, recharacterise the transaction, impose interest, and if the adjustment was caused by wilful concealment or false disclosure, apply the 50% to 500% evasion penalty. Fraudulent use of a forged tax residency certificate additionally triggers criminal liability for forged official documents under Article 280 of the Criminal Law.

Director personal liability: Where the sole shareholder or managing director is the real principal of the shell structure, cannot prove independent books, bank accounts and assets, or commits fraudulent treaty declarations, the court pierces the corporate veil and orders the individual to assume joint liability for all unpaid Chinese tax, civil restitution of diverted profits, asset disclosure and judicial freeze, exit-control travel blacklisting, and criminal imprisonment for tax evasion or document forgery.
