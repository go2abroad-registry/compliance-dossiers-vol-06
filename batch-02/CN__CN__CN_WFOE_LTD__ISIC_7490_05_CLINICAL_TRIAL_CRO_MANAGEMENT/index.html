Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; taxpayer-facing electronic portal: China Electronic Taxpayer Service System and the National Electronic Invoice Service Platform.

E-government filing portal

After the SAMR business license is issued, the WFOE must complete taxpayer registration within 30 calendar days through the multi-license integration procedure and the in-charge STA office via the electronic tax bureau. The STA assigns the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) as the statutory Tax Identification Number (TIN). The procedure covers taxpayer registration, VAT general taxpayer qualification (mandatory when annual taxable sales exceed CNY 5,000,000, or voluntary where input VAT credit is required), filing of bank accounts, registration of accounting methods, invoice authorization and quota setting, and activation of the digital tax account. Foreign shareholders and non-resident directors must provide valid passports, business license, articles of association, proof of registered office, and legal representative identity documents before the TIN is activated.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None. China does not permit check-the-box elections, hybrid mismatch elections, or flow-through tax classification for a standard limited-liability WFOE; the entity is taxed as a PRC tax resident under Enterprise Income Tax law and VAT law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

6%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

CNY 5,000,000 of annual taxable sales; exceeding this threshold triggers mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration, while turnover below the threshold permits small-scale taxpayer status with a 3% collection rate.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A PRC VAT taxpayer may apply 0% VAT to qualifying exported services, including cross-border clinical trial data management and CRO services, where the services are consumed entirely outside China, supplied to an overseas enterprise or institution, and the foreign-currency consideration is received from outside the PRC customs territory. The service must fall within the STA cross-border zero-rating catalogue and be evidenced by a cross-border service contract. Physical goods exports are zero-rated upon formal customs export clearance without additional approval.

  • For goods export: customs export declaration form (export customs declaration document) and customs-cleared export entry/manifest
  • Bill of Lading or air waybill proving outward movement from PRC customs territory
  • For cross-border CRO services: signed cross-border service agreement, service completion or acceptance certificate, and VAT invoice issued under the STA zero-rated export invoice rules
  • Bank inward foreign exchange settlement receipt / foreign exchange receipt certificate evidencing receipt of export consideration from the overseas buyer

China mandates issuance and real-time reporting of electronic invoices through the STA National Electronic Invoice Service Platform. Fully digitalized electronic invoices carry a statutory single anti-fraud invoice number and tamper-resistant QR code, are captured in real time in the taxpayer digital account, and are cleared into STA surveillance systems automatically. Traditional tax control equipment and physical stamping are being phased out; B2B and export-related invoices must be issued from STA-approved software and transmitted electronically to the national clearance platform.

Export zero-rating is jointly scrutinized by STA, customs, and SAFE through cross-referenced export declarations, logistics data, electronic invoices, and foreign exchange inflow records. If the substantiating documentation is incomplete, the service is not consumed offshore, or the foreign exchange is not repatriated within the statutory deadline, the 0% rate is revoked and VAT must be assessed at the applicable domestic rate with late payment interest. Fraudulent or unsubstantiated zero-rating claims attract administrative penalties of 0.5 to 5 times the tax underpaid under the Tax Administration Law, inclusion on the STA serious-tax-violation blacklist, customs and foreign exchange disqualification, and criminal liability for false invoicing or tax fraud in serious cases.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Mainland China runs a managed current-account convertibility system with heavily regulated capital-account movements. For the WFOE, current-account revenue from clinical trial CRO service fees, milestone payments, and reimbursement of pass-through costs is broadly convertible: after the bank verifies the underlying service contract, invoice, and trial filing evidence, proceeds may remain in a domestic FX account or be settled into RMB at the spot rate. Capital-account items, including paid-in equity capital from the foreign parent and any subsequent shareholder loans, require SAFE FDI registration or foreign debt registration before remittance. No mandatory surrender ratio is currently imposed on current-account export receipts; however, SAFE trade monitoring compares receipts with customs or service-trade declarations, and any unreceived receivables beyond 180 days must be explained. Profit remittance is permitted only from genuinely audited retained earnings, and banks require proof of income tax filing and withholding, audited financial statements, and a current-account payment declaration. A clinical trial CRO must therefore maintain clean documentary trails from sponsor contracts to NMPA filings to survive FX audit.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For goods exports, SAFE applies a six-month collection benchmark: export proceeds must be repatriated into a licensed Chinese bank within 180 days of customs export declaration, unless an extension has been filed. Service-export proceeds, including CRO milestone payments, must be brought back following the invoice or date of service completion and matched to the declared service revenue within the same reporting cycle. There is no fixed mandatory conversion ratio; enterprises may hold the FX in their current account, convert to RMB at spot, or use it to pay eligible current-account obligations. All receipts above the invoiced quota must be reconciled with trade verification records, and mismatches require supporting contracts and updated FX declarations.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBC); State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) as the applied FX licensing, registration, and enforcement authority.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Very low and not viable for onshore account services. Foreign EMI providers cannot obtain a PBoC banking or payment-institution license to take RMB deposits or open domestic current accounts; they may lawfully engage only in settlement activities outside mainland China. The WFOE must use a licensed onshore Tier-1 bank for its FDI capital account, CNY operating account, tax payments, and FX. An offshore EMI can be used solely by the foreign parent to route sponsor funds to an onshore bank, never as an account of record for the WFOE. Legal constraints include PBoC payment-management rules and SAFE's bank-only settlement framework.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The offshore parent wires registered capital to the WFOE's FDI capital foreign-exchange account at a domestic Tier-1 bank. Before receipt, the WFOE files Foreign Investment Information Reporting with the Chinese market regulator and obtains the FDI registration profile, after which the bank completes SAFE FDI Registration online and opens the capital account. On receipt, the bank posts the transaction as FDI capital injection using SAFE's BOP purpose code for equity capital input; the parent's SWIFT message must contain the WFOE name and account, exact currency, FDI registration reference, purpose field marked as FDI Registered Capital Injection, and a reference to the articles of association capital schedule. The bank then verifies the funds against the FDI reporting record and may convert the capital into RMB at spot if the settlement purpose is allowed by the operating scope and capital-use rules. A licensed CPA in China issues the capital verification report after funds are credited, and paid-in capital is registered with the market regulator.For imports of investigational medicinal products, ancillaries, laboratory equipment, and outsourced analysis, the WFOE presents invoices, NMPA permissions, trial licences, and customs declarations to its bank and purchases FX from its current account. Approved clearing rails include SWIFT MT103 for service fees and milestone sub-contractor payments, commercial letters of credit for high-value clinical supply imports, and documentary collections where the vendor requires documents against acceptance or payment. All outbound service payments must carry a SAFE current-account purpose code consistent with clinical trial testing or management-related services, and cannot be executed until the contract and milestone evidence have passed the bank's compliance screening. Mandatory Audit Threshold Small Company Audit Exemption Regime

Turnover: Under Article 164 of the PRC Company Law, every PRC-incorporated limited liability company, including a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise, must prepare annual financial reports and have the year-end financial statements audited by an accounting firm practicing under the PRC Certified Public Accountants regime. There is no balance-sheet threshold, turnover threshold, or headcount threshold that exempts an ordinary WFOE from the statutory audit obligation. The annual financial report must be prepared within four months after the balance-sheet date, and the statutory audit must be completed in time for the shareholders' meeting to approve the audited financial statements. In addition, a WFOE performing clinical-trial CRO services is subject to pharmaceutical GCP supervisory inspection by the NMPA where audited accounting records support lawful service revenue recognition, intercompany transfer pricing, and regulatory cost substantiation.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File thresholds under SAT Public Notice No. 42 (2016): related-party tangible property transfers exceeding RMB 200 million; intangible property transfers exceeding RMB 100 million; financial asset transfers exceeding RMB 100 million; other related-party transactions exceeding RMB 40 million. Master File preparation is triggered where aggregate related-party transactions reach RMB 1 billion in a tax year or where the multinational group meets Country-by-Country reporting thresholds.

Economic substance scope: China applies economic substance through the general anti-avoidance rule and beneficial ownership jurisprudence rather than a UAE-style ESR label, but the substance demanded is the same: the WFOE must have local personnel, office premises, budget autonomy, decision-making power and actual operational control. For a clinical-trial CRO, Core Income-Generating Activities are protocol negotiation, clinical site selection, investigator management, patient monitoring, safety reporting, data quality review, statistical analysis and regulatory filing performed by employees physically located in China. If these activities are controlled abroad or the WFOE is a paper entity lacking the capability and personnel to perform the contracted work, the tax authority may recharacterize the revenue, deny treaty benefits and impose transfer pricing adjustments.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion through false invoicing and fabricated CRO expenses

Statutory sanction: PRC Tax Collection Administration Law Article 63: recovery of the underpaid tax, daily late-payment interest, and a fine of 50% to five times the unpaid tax. PRC Criminal Law Article 201: the enterprise is fined and the directly responsible directors are subject to criminal detention or imprisonment of up to three years, or three to seven years where the amount is enormous.

Director personal liability: Directors who approve, sign, or direct the false invoicing and fraudulent understatement are jointly and severally liable for the company's unpaid tax and penalties under PRC Company Law Articles 21 and 23; personal assets may be seized, a departure restriction/travel ban may be imposed, and they may be blacklisted on the national tax credit system, which also disqualifies them from serving as directors of other enterprises; criminal imprisonment applies.
Transfer pricing documentation failure and non-arm's length profit stripping

Statutory sanction: PRC EIT Law Articles 41, 42, 44 and SAT Public Notice No. 42 (2016): the tax authority may reallocate income by applying the arm's length standard, impose a special tax adjustment with compounded interest, and assess penalties under the Tax Collection Administration Law. Failure to provide contemporaneous documentation can trigger documentation-related penalties, a credit-rating downgrade, and an administrative penalty on the additional tax payable calculated from 50% to five times the underpaid tax.

Director personal liability: Directors who personally design or execute profit-stripping arrangements, sign false intercompany agreements, or approve TP documentation without factual foundation may be treated as controllers causing tax underpayment; the corporate veil may be pierced for civil recovery of taxes, asset freezes and travel bans may be issued, and directors may face criminal prosecution as accomplices to tax evasion under Criminal Law Article 201.
Shell company and economic substance abuse of double tax treaty relief

Statutory sanction: PRC EIT Law Article 47 (GAAR) and Article 48 allow the tax authority to recharacterize the entire arrangement, deny the claimed treaty benefit, restore the statutory 10% withholding rate, impose a 50% to 5x penalty on the tax shortfall, and apply a 10-year special tax adjustment retroactive period. SAT Announcement 2018 No. 9 requires the non-resident claimant to prove beneficial ownership and substantive business purpose; treaty-shopping structures without these elements are disregarded.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly use a shell WFOE to obtain DTA benefits and distribute profit offshore may be personally liable for unpaid withholding tax as responsible officers; the withholding agent's failure to withhold creates liability under Tax Collection Law Article 69. Directors may face asset restitution orders, departure bans and blacklisting, and if the offshore profit transfers are layered or disguised, criminal liability for tax evasion or money laundering can arise.
