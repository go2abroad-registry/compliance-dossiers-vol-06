Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None. There is no statutory minimum registered capital or blocked escrow account requirement for an ordinary service-sector WFOE engaged in AI data labeling and BPO. The registered capital shall be stated in the Articles of Association and must be fully paid by all shareholders within five years from incorporation under the 2023 Company Law, unless a shorter contribution period is adopted in the Articles. Foreign exchange contributions must be remitted through a designated bank and reported under the State Administration of Foreign Exchange foreign direct investment registration procedure.

Funding & unlock path: Not Applicable as a civil-defense installation or manufacturing facility. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA) and its electronic tax administration portal, the National Electronic Tax Bureau (China e-Tax Bureau), operated through the STA's digital taxpayer services, including the China Tax Service web portal.

E-government filing portal

Following approval by the Ministry of Commerce and local market-regulation authority, the WFOE is incorporated with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR), which issues a business license and an 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC). The USCC is automatically synchronised to the STA electronic tax bureau and constitutes the statutory TIN and VAT identification number; no separate TIN application is required. The WFOE's legal representative and foreign shareholders then provide identity documentation through the National Electronic Tax Bureau to activate the entity's VAT taxpayer file, accounting profile and e-invoice eligibility. If export zero-rating is intended, the WFOE must also obtain customs registration with the General Administration of Customs (GACC) using the same USCC and complete foreign-exchange repatriation reporting arrangements with the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE).

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None. China does not apply check-the-box or hybrid mismatch elections. However, a WFOE may elect to be treated as a general VAT taxpayer once its annual taxable turnover exceeds RMB 5 million or, below that threshold, at its option subject to STA conditions; otherwise it remains a small-scale VAT taxpayer.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

6%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5 million of annual taxable turnover (including services supplied by the WFOE) is the statutory threshold triggering compulsory general VAT taxpayer registration; below RMB 5 million the WFOE is registerable as a small-scale VAT taxpayer, but tax registration is nevertheless mandatory from the first taxable supply in China.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is available for physical exports of goods where the WFOE is a VAT general taxpayer, the goods are exported through GACC customs procedures, the export sale is made under a foreign contract, consideration is charged in foreign currency, and the export proceeds are repatriated into China within the statutory period. For cross-border services such as data labeling, AI training data curation and RLHF BPO, zero-rating or export-deemed exemption applies only where the service is rendered to an overseas recipient and consumed wholly outside China; the WFOE must demonstrate the offshore nature through the export service contract, evidence that no concurrent domestic-use benefit is obtained, and, where applicable, inclusion in the STA catalogue of cross-border zero-rated/exempt services (including offshore outsourcing of IT and business-process services).

  • Export Goods Customs Declaration (Export Goods Declaration Form) as cleared by the General Administration of Customs
  • Customs-approved bill of lading or airway bill evidencing physical departure from PRC customs territory
  • Signed export contract and commercial invoice with the overseas buyer
  • Packing list and customs valuation documents if goods are exported
  • Bank receipt or Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) issued by a PRC bank confirming foreign-exchange receipt from abroad
  • VAT export zero-rating declaration and supporting tax-audit schedule filed on the National Electronic Tax Bureau and, for services, the cross-border service export registration/declaration submitted to STA and SAFE

China operates a national real-time e-invoicing clearance regime through the STA National Electronic Invoice Service Platform. All VAT invoices issued by a WFOE must be generated as all-digital e-invoices (full-digital invoices) in the e-Tax Bureau, transmitted in real time to the tax authorities, and carry a unique invoice number and anti-counterfeiting QR/verification code. Registered enterprises integrate their billing or ERP systems with the STA through certified APIs or e-Tax application interfaces; invoice consumption, input VAT deductions and export VAT zero-rating declarations are validated on a real-time basis. Separate paper fiscal stamping is abolished in favour of the Golden Tax System phase-IV data clearance and the all-digital e-invoice anti-fraud engine.

If zero-rating is claimed with incomplete or inconsistent documents, STA may withdraw zero-rating and raise unpaid output VAT together with input-credit clawback, recalculated at the applicable positive rate (6% for VAT on services or 13% for goods if export status fails). Unpaid tax carries a late-payment surcharge of 0.05% per day from the due date until settlement. Under the PRC Tax Collection and Administration Law, fraudulent zero-rating claims are treated as tax evasion or export-rebate fraud, with administrative penalties of 50% to five times the underpaid/refunded VAT and criminal liability under the PRC Criminal Law for fraudulent export tax rebates, including imprisonment; recovery is not time-limited in cases of intentional false export declarations or forged customs/financial documents.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China maintains a managed floating RMB exchange rate regime underpinned by current-account convertibility formalised within its Foreign Exchange Control Regulations. Revenue generated by the AI data-labeling BPO service exports is treated as current-account service-trade receipts; such receipts are freely convertible into RMB at a designated FX bank after contract, invoice, and transaction background verification. Capital-account items, including FDI equity injections into the WFOE, remain subject to SAFE registration, purpose-bound fund usage, and a separate scheme for capital-account FX positions. Profit remittance is permitted only after the firm documents audited after-tax earnings and withholds the applicable enterprise income tax for the non-resident parent-scope shareholder, evidenced by tax clearance manifests. In practice the convertibility friction appears in non-routine capital flows: cross-border shareholder loans, licence/IP royalties, and deployment of unused equity capital require additional registration or debt-modality approval. The WFOE treasury must therefore maintain a full cash-flow ledger showing that every RMB conversion and every foreign-currency receipt falls within the narrow purpose code declared to SAFE through the commercial bank. Current-account receipts have no compulsory surrender ratio, yet they must be repatriated into the bank account of the Chinese entity rather than left offshore; delaying repatriation exposes the enterprise to verification downgrades and throttled trade facilitation privileges.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Goods-trade export proceeds must be repatriated into the Chinese exporter's account within the operational benchmark of 180 days from customs declaration and may be retained in a foreign-currency account or settled into RMB at a designated bank; no mandatory conversion ratio exists since the post-2007 reform. For service-trade exports such as AI data-labeling fees, the receipt must be credited to the WFOE's domestic bank account on the contractual payment date, and while no statutory surrender percentage applies, every inward remittance must be matched against the underlying contract and verified by the bank under SAFE's current-account monitoring. Enterprises classified as Class 'A' enjoy fast-track clearance; Class 'B' and 'C' classifications impose escalated documentary verification and restrict the WFOE from producing inbound foreign currency without prior compliance approvals.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) and State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) acting jointly as the central foreign exchange regulator package

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

PayPal Fit: High

Low viability as a primary treasury rail. The Chinese WFOE cannot be serviced by Wise or PayPal as a substitutive corporate bank account because these fintech EMIs do not hold a PBOC payment institution licence and are not authorised to receive FDI capital injections, issue RMB accounts, or perform SAFE reporting within mainland China. They may only serve as supplemental micro-disbursement rails for small foreign-currency payments to overseas annotation contractors through Mass PayPal Payout or similar channels. Any attempt to route principal current-account service receipts or equity capital through an offshore EMI would violate SAFE's current-account verification framework and expose the enterprise to administrative penalties and demotion of its FX compliance classification.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The WFOE must first complete the FDI information registration through the designated commercial bank under SAFE. After obtaining the unique SAFE registration code, the treasury opens a foreign-currency capital account. The overseas shareholder then wires the equity amount via SWIFT MT103 using the purpose code 'Direct Investment - Paid-in Capital and Premium' and referencing the SAFE registration number on the remittance advice. Upon receipt, the bank performs a name and remittance-comparison check, converts the strict business-use portion into RMB via a capital-settlement transaction, and credits the remaining quota to a domestic RMB expenditure account for working capital. A local certified public accountant issues the capital verification report; the enterprise may use the funds only for payroll, office leasing, office equipment, cloud infrastructure, and contracted service costs. Any balance reserved for future FX conversion may be placed in a RMB capital deposit or hedged with a forward contract at the same bank.For the AI data-labeling BPO model, outbound disbursements are primarily vendor fees for specialised annotation platforms, overseas cloud servers, international statistics subscriptions, and independent contractor compensation. The compliant clearing rail is SWIFT MT103 customer credit transfer after the bank verifies the service contract, proforma invoice, and completion certificate. Commercial letters of credit and documentary collections are only required if the WFOE later imports physical hardware such as GPU clusters; these instruments would be supported by a sales contract, transport document, and customs declaration. Dividend repatriation to the foreign parent is permitted after the enterprise income tax withholding clearance and SAFE outward-remittance form submission, accompanied by the annual or interim audit report and board-approved dividend resolution. Turnover: Under PRC Company Law Article 164, every WFOE, irrespective of balance-sheet size, turnover, or headcount, must be independently audited by a PRC-registered CPA firm each fiscal year; no size-based exemption exists for the WFOE legal form. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% for dividends and other profit distributions paid by a Chinese resident enterprise to its non-resident shareholder, unless reduced under an applicable Double Tax Agreement. The nominal statutory CIT rate before State Council reduction is 20%, but the effective levy is 10%.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File is mandatory when annual related-party tangible or financial asset transactions exceed RMB 200 million, intangible property transactions exceed RMB 100 million, or related-party service transaction value exceeds RMB 40 million. Master File must be prepared if the MNE group's prior-year consolidated revenue exceeds RMB 2 billion and the group has Chinese related-party transactions.

Economic substance scope: China applies substance-over-form and economic substance principles through the General Anti-Avoidance Rules and the PRC Enterprise Income Tax Law. Although China does not impose Caribbean-style Economic Substance Regulations, treaty benefits and transfer pricing outcomes require the WFOE to show real substance: actual employees, office premises, active decision-making on AI data annotation, RLHF curation, quality control, invoicing, and IT infrastructure. Core Income-Generating Activities (CIGA) for the BPO are performed in mainland China; if the WFOE is a mere shell routing BPO income to an offshore entity for tax benefit, tax authorities may recharacterise the profit, deny treaty relief, and impose sanctions.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Invoicing

Statutory sanction: Tax Collection Administration Law Article 63: additional tax, penalty of 50%-500% of underpaid tax, and possible criminal prosecution under Criminal Law Article 201; unpaid tax plus interest is recoverable from company assets and, in fraudulent cases, from directors' personal assets.

Director personal liability: Directors who directed false invoicing or profit shifting face restitution of evaded tax from personal assets, travel restrictions or blacklisting preventing exit from China under Tax Collection Administration Law Article 44, and imprisonment up to three years for relatively large evasion; imprisonment of three to seven years applies to evasions of a huge amount accounting for more than 30% of the tax due.
Shell Company / Conduit Treaty Abuse

Statutory sanction: Denial of treaty benefits and recharacterisation by tax authorities under the General Anti-Avoidance Rule in the Enterprise Income Tax Law and its Implementing Regulations; re-assessed tax, interest, and a penalty up to 50%-500% of any underpayments. The beneficial ownership test under Announcement 60/2015 and the substance-over-form doctrine nullify the structural tax advantage.

Director personal liability: Courts may pierce the corporate veil where willful evasion is established; directors are individually liable for the shortfall, may be travel-banned and placed on the public joint credit blacklist, and may face criminal tax evasion liability with imprisonment up to seven years for significant amounts.
AML / Foreign Exchange Control Violation in Payment Rails

Statutory sanction: Foreign exchange regulators may impose fines of 30%-100% of the illegal amount under the Foreign Exchange Administration Regulations, tax authorities may disallow deductions and impose withholding penalties for undeclared outbound payments, and anti-money laundering regulators may impose fines on the entity and responsible persons under the PRC Anti-Money Laundering Law.

Director personal liability: Directors face personal fines, blacklisting and bank account controls, travel bans while proceedings are pending, and criminal liability under Criminal Law Article 191 for money laundering with prison terms up to five years for the basic offense and longer for serious circumstances, plus civil asset restitution for amounts concealed.
