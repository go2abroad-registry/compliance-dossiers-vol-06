Stage 02

Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Enterprise Registration and Review Department of the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR), acting through the local municipal-level Administration for Market Regulation at the registered office jurisdiction of the WFOE.

Governing statute

Company Law of the People's Republic of China (Revised 2023, effective 1 July 2024), Articles 23-47 (establishment, registered capital, contribution periods, board of directors, legal representative and registration); Foreign Investment Law of the People's Republic of China (2019), Articles 3, 4, 12 and 18; the Market Entity Registration Administration Regulations of the People's Republic of China (State Council Decree No. 746), Articles 8 and 16-20; and the current Interim Negative List for Foreign Investment Access for digital publishing and content syndication screening.

Formation / constitutional filings

Company Establishment Registration and Filing Application Form, as published by SAMR, together with the Articles of Association, the Name Pre-Approval Notice and the supporting corporate governance appointments.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 20 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None. No statutory minimum registered capital is imposed on a standard wholly foreign-owned limited liability company carrying on non-restricted digital publishing; the registered capital amount is set in the Articles of Association. However, under the 2023 Company Law, shareholders must pay their subscribed capital in full within five years from incorporation, unless a shorter period is stated in the Articles. No blocked escrow or guarantee deposit is required by the corporate registrar; foreign direct investment capital must be wired into a Chinese bank account opened through proper SAFE-institution banking channels after the entity is licensed.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; national electronic portal: State Taxation Administration Electronic Taxpayer Service Portal (e-Tax platform); local STA branch/tax service bureau supervises in-person verification and acceptance.

E-government filing portal

For the CN_WFOE_LTD wholly foreign-owned limited liability company: (1) pre-approve the company name and complete SAMR incorporation under the Foreign Investment Negative List; (2) upon issuance of the Business License by the State Administration for Market Regulation, the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) printed on the license is automatically transmitted to the STA and serves as the statutory Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) for both CIT and VAT; (3) within 30 days after license issuance, complete supplementary tax registration information through the STA e-Tax portal, confirming legal representative, resident address, capital, banking relationships, and actual business scope; (4) elect the appropriate VAT taxpayer type: small-scale taxpayer where annual taxable sales are at or below RMB 5,000,000, or general taxpayer for larger or voluntary-registered businesses; (5) register for All-Electronic Invoice issuance and assign the entity to a tax collection district; (6) open a designated bank account for tax payments and foreign-exchange settlement. A foreign non-resident enterprise without a Chinese establishment may separately complete non-resident taxpayer registration through the e-Tax portal to obtain a non-resident taxpayer file number for Chinese withholding tax and VAT obligations, but the mainland WFOE itself is a resident PRC taxpayer.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation: resident PRC enterprise; CIT levied at the statutory 25% rate on worldwide income; no pass-through eligibility.

Available election forms

None under PRC CIT: a WFOE cannot elect pass-through, check-the-box, or transparent treatment. VAT only: small-scale taxpayer status is available below RMB 5,000,000 annual taxable sales; voluntary VAT general taxpayer registration may be elected; hybrid mismatch allocations involving the foreign parent are governed by PRC GAAR/transfer-pricing rules, not entity elections.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 annual taxable sales for mandatory VAT general-taxpayer registration; every fixed-place business operator is required to complete tax registration upon SAMR issuance of the Unified Social Credit Code, even below the threshold. A domestic digital publishing supply generally falls under the 6% modern-services VAT rate; the 13% headline rate applies to goods and is used as the standard fallback rate for unclassified taxable supplies.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

To claim 0% VAT on cross-border digital webtoon syndication, the WFOE must establish that the supply qualifies as a cross-border taxable service (or, where applicable, exported goods if printed media is physically shipped): (i) the contractual counterparty or consumer is located outside mainland China; (ii) the digital content is delivered by internet and consumed entirely outside the PRC; (iii) the consideration is invoiced to the non-resident platform/subscriber in foreign currency and settled through a designated foreign-exchange bank; (iv) the WFOE keeps a precise jurisdictional breakdown proving that Apple IAP and Google Play Billing revenue attributable to PRC users is separated and treated as domestic taxable supply; and (v) the transaction falls within the STA's zero-rating/exemption definition for cross-border taxable services. Revenue traceable to PRC App Store users or domestic platforms must not be zero-rated and must be declared as taxable domestic turnover.

  • Customs Export Declaration Form (export-goods customs declaration) — mandatory only when printed comic, graphic novel, or physical media editions are shipped out of China; pure digital downloads have no customs event
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill — required solely for physical export cargo; not generated for electronic delivery through Apple IAP / Google Play
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (bank-issued FIRC or electronic foreign-exchange settlement receipt) — mandatory to evidence that offshore consideration entered China through a designated foreign-exchange bank
  • Digital webtoon syndication / licensing agreement with the overseas platform or end-customer, specifying contract value, currency, service scope, and the absence of PRC consumption
  • Apple IAP / Google Play Billing settlement statements showing gross subscriber sales, store commissions, platform tax handling, and net remittance to the WFOE
  • Sales location and IP-country analytics demonstrating final consumer access from outside mainland China and reconciliation with zero-rated revenue

Mandatory issuance through the STA's national e-invoice service platform under the All-Electronic Invoice (e-invoice) standard, integrated with the Golden Tax System IV. Each invoice receives an STA-issued digital invoice number and cryptographic anti-counterfeit mark; issuance data are transmitted to the tax authority in near real time. High-volume taxpayers may use system-to-system API integrations with the STA platform, but all invoices must reside in the official e-invoice database and reconcile with monthly VAT returns and export-documentation vouchers. No offline invoice printing bypass is permitted for e-invoice-registered taxpayers.

In a State Taxation Administration or SAFE audit, the export zero-rating claim will be rejected if the mandatory documentation package is incomplete, the foreign-exchange repatriation exceeds 180 days without an approved extension, or the PRC-consumption split is not credible. Consequences include reassessment of output VAT at the applicable domestic rate (normally 6% for digital services), clawback of any refund already paid, late-payment surcharge at 0.05% per day, administrative penalties from 50% up to 5 times the underpaid VAT/tax for false export declarations, possible suspension of export-tax-preference programs for up to three years, and criminal liability for tax fraud under PRC law if the false zero-rating is intentional and material. SAFE may additionally impose foreign-exchange penalties for unrepatriated export proceeds.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The renminbi (RMB) is fully convertible on the current account, covering trade in goods and services, including digital webtoon licensing and Apple/Google IAP revenue. However, the capital account remains heavily managed by SAFE: all FDI capital contributions, foreign debt borrowings, and equity reallocations require explicit prior registration, and proceeds are monitored against permitted use categories. In practice, a WFOE must open a foreign currency capital account and an RMB basic account, then verify each inbound transfer with capital account information codes in the SAFE Foreign Exchange Account system. Funds received as registered capital can only be deployed for operating expenses, procurement, mandatory payroll, or permitted current items; they cannot be deployed for equity investment, cross-border lending, or on-lending without a separate filing. Profit remittance to offshore shareholders requires a PRC statutory audit report, a certified tax clearance certificate (reflecting full withholding and VAT payments), a board resolution approving the profit distribution, and a bank-level compliance review; SAFE validates the distribution in the Foreign Exchange Control System before remittance. Surrender requirements are moderate: export proceeds from webtoon subscriptions and chapter sales must be repatriated into a domestic foreign exchange settlement account or immediately converted into RMB at market rates. All transactions must be reported through the Goods/Services Trade Reporting System within the statutory 30-day timeline for current account receipts. Overall, the regime demands a disciplined documentary trail for every capital and revenue movement, and any contemplated treasury liquidity must be planned against SAFE verification windows and bank KYC cycles.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE Circular 2013/20 and the Administration of Foreign Exchange Settlement and Sales for Export Proceeds, a WFOE must repatriate export proceeds into mainland China within 90 days from the date of export declaration or within 90 days from the date of payment receipt as evidenced by the bank credit note, whichever is earlier under the applicable reporting mechanism. There is no mandatory conversion ratio; the WFOE may retain export proceeds in a designated foreign currency settlement account or convert them into RMB at the onshore market rate. However, all export receipts must be entered in the export goods/services verification system, and any delay beyond the 90-day window must be supported by and lodged with the local SAFE branch as an exemption. Export proceeds from digital content platforms must also be matched with the underlying commercial contract, platform settlement statement, and applicable service trade tariff code to avoid penalties.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China, operating under the mandate of the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low viability for local CNY clearing. Offshore digital EMIs such as specialized fintech banks, virtual banking platforms, and payment startups do not hold PBOC banking licenses and are legally barred from opening RMB basic accounts, settlement accounts, or capital escrow accounts on behalf of mainland SMEs or WFOEs. They cannot act as the primary cash management or treasury clearing channel for the Chinese entity. Their utility is limited to auxiliary foreign-to-local collection in the overseas jurisdiction (e.g., channeling Apple/Google remittances from foreign entity to the WFOE) but they can neither verify Chinese FX purpose codes nor satisfy SAFE account reporting obligations. Therefore, the treasury blueprint must route all main corporate banking flows through a SAFE-approved Tier-1 domestic commercial bank with local branch registration and a recognized clearing relationship.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Opaque beneficial ownership chain due to offshore holding structure and possible nominee shareholders in the WFOE's parent entity.

Root cause: Opaque beneficial ownership chain due to offshore holding structure and possible nominee shareholders in the WFOE's parent entity.

Defense evidence: Provide a complete corporate registry extract for every holding layer in China (from the WFOE up to the ultimate controller); notarized share certificates for each edge; a signed beneficial owner declaration (Form BO-PLUS) listing all shareholders with more than 10% equity; passport copies of the ultimate direct/indirect owners; and a legal opinion from a Chinese law firm describing the control chain, including any LP/GP arrangements or trust beneficiaries.
Unverified source of funds for the initial capital injection from overseas shareholders into the Chinese bootstrapped entity.

Root cause: Unverified source of funds for the initial capital injection from overseas shareholders into the Chinese bootstrapped entity.

The defense dossier must demonstrate that the payment matches the declared registered capital amount and that no part of the funds is connected to sanctioned entities or unexplained cash deposits.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The inbound capital injection should be routed through SAFE-registered Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) account facilities. The overseas parent must instruct a SWIFT MT103/MT202 payment to the WFOE's foreign currency capital account at the designated Tier-1 bank, with purpose code 'FDI registered capital injection' and the SAFE capital registration number included in the remittance reference field. Upon receipt, the WFOE's treasury team must present the underlying board resolution, signed business contract, and the original SAFE Foreign Exchange Registration Certificate to the bank so that the bank can complete the capital account information verification in the SAFE Capital Justice system. The bank then obtains a foreign exchange settlement voucher, which converts the foreign currency capital into RMB for operational use. Any subsequent injections require a new SAFE filing with an updated scope and amount, and the bank will cross-check shareholder identity against the known beneficial owner list.All outbound trade settlements for webtoon syndication must be executed through the clearing rails accepted by Chinese commercial banks. For royalty, licensing, and subcontract payments to overseas content creators and translation vendors, the primary rail is a SWIFT MT103 wire with the appropriate service-trade purpose code and verified invoice. For high-value capital equipment purchases (e.g., specialized graphics servers or digital distribution platforms), a commercial Letter of Credit (L/C) with a 90-day after-sight clause is encouraged to protect both the WFOE and the foreign seller. For smaller contract services exceeding USD 10,000, documentary collections (DP/DA) may be used where the collecting bank presents signed custody documents to the foreign remitter. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% standard Enterprise Income Tax (EIT) rate for resident Chinese enterprises including a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% on gross dividends paid by a Chinese resident WFOE to a non-resident enterprise investor, imposed under EIT Law Article 3 and Article 37; no surtax on dividend repatriation.
