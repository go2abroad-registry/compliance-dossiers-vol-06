Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

Virtual offices and commercial mailboxes are generally not permissible as the sole registered office for a Hong Kong entity whose ultimate parent is a PRC Foreign-Invested Partnership operating a shipyard. The Inland Revenue Department and Hong Kong banks require substantive physical presence because the business is medium-risk enhanced and requires statutory licensing. A serviced office may be accepted only if it provides a dedicated physical suite, meeting rooms, staffed reception, the entity name conspicuously displayed, and full access to records during business hours. However, even a premium serviced office may be challenged by the Hong Kong Marine Department and shipbuilding license authorities, which require actual operational premises, engineering management, and contracts administration in Hong Kong. Therefore, a virtual office is categorically rejected because it undermines the commercial substance required for bank account opening, maritime letter-of-credit issuance, and tax residence analysis.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-up capital is imposed under the Limited Partnership Ordinance (Cap 37), and no blocked escrow account is statutorily required. The executed Limited Partnership Agreement must specify the capital contribution of each partner; cash subscriptions must be received in cleared funds into a bank account in the name of the partnership before any distribution or management fee may be paid. For shipbuilding contracts, commercial lenders or the shipyard's procurement counterparties may require a project-specific escrow account holding not less than 10 percent of the contract value as a condition of issuing maritime installment letters of credit.

Funding & unlock path: The shipyard must obtain a Fire Services Certificate covering emergency means of escape, fire hydrant coverage, and chemical storage spacing; a Dangerous Goods Licence must be obtained from the Fire Services Department. Buildings Department inspection of covered slips and structural load capacity is required. The Labour Department workplace safety clearance under the Factories and Industrial Undertakings Ordinance (Cap 509) for welding, painting and confined spaces is mandatory. Civil Defense clearance, where required by the Emergency Units, must provide a shelter with 45-minute fire endurance and independent ventilation on the dockyard premises.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital is imposed under the Limited Partnership Ordinance (Cap 37), and no blocked escrow account is statutorily required. Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; electronic portal: National Integrated Tax Service Network (E-Tax) via https://etax.chinatax.gov.cn

E-government filing portal

The Foreign-Invested Partnership (FIP) shall complete the following organically in sequence: (1) Name registration and Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) issuance at the local Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) upon submitting the partnership deed, subscriptions, and foreign investor qualification proof; (2) Within 30 calendar days after issuance of the business license, file the Basic Tax Status Information form with the competent local STA office and request tax registration, producing the USCC, legal entity/branch address, bank account information, and signatory lists; the USCC becomes the de facto TIN for all tax filings; (3) Apply for VAT registration status on the E-Tax portal, electing either small-scale taxpayer or general VAT taxpayer classification; (4) Concurrently activate the Golden Tax Phase IV (GTP-IV) e-invoice system by installing the digital tax control certificate and applying for the official invoice denomination; (5) For cross-border and offshore operations, file the 'Foreign Investment Registration' with the State Administration of Foreign Exchange and obtain an 'Export Enterprise Identifier' in the Customs registration system. Non-resident partners with no Chinese permanent establishment are not separately issued a Chinese TIN; instead, the FIP's USCC is used as the statutory identifier on returns and withholding certificates.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

None for income tax check-the-box; PRC law mandates pass-through treatment of partnerships under the Enterprise Income Tax regime. A mandatory election exists for VAT general taxpayer status: a FIP with annual taxable turnover below RMB 5,000,000 may elect to become a general VAT taxpayer (and thereby claim input VAT credits and export zero-rating), subject to STA acceptance; conversely, a taxpayer above the threshold must register as a general VAT taxpayer. Hybrid mismatch and US-classification elections are not available under PRC law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Basic VAT registration is mandatory for all PRC business entities within 30 days of formation; mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration applies upon annual taxable sales exceeding RMB 5,000,000 (small-scale taxpayer threshold).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For a Chinese VAT general taxpayer exporting self-manufactured deep-sea container vessels, the 0% VAT export rate applies when: (a) the exporter holds a valid export license for the shipbuilding sector and the vessel is actually and irreversibly removed from Chinese customs territory; (b) the vessel sale is an ordinary sale of goods governed by an export contract; (c) the exporter receives the full purchase price in a freely convertible foreign currency and repatriates the proceeds to a Chinese foreign-exchange bank account within the statutory period; (d) the VAT input credits attributable to the exported shipbuilding materials, assembly and engineering costs have been properly declared and can be proven; (e) no export of prohibited or restricted ship-related technology occurs without an export license. Eligible shipbuilding export income is zero-rated, with corresponding input VAT refunded via the Export Tax Refund (exemption and reduction) mechanism. Services that qualify as offshore shipping services in connection therewith may be zero-rated upon supply to a recipient outside the customs territory.

  • Customs Export Declaration Form (Customs Bill of Entry equivalent under PRC Customs Law)
  • Ocean Bill of Lading (or Multimodal Transport Document) endorsed by the carrier
  • Final commercial invoice for the vessel issuance
  • Export Sales Contract or Ship Purchase Agreement
  • Foreign Exchange Receipt Voucher (bank's trade settlement confirmation evidencing inward remittance of export proceeds)
  • Export VAT Refund Declaration Form (export tax reimbursement application with invoice routing and GTP-IV matching data)

All VAT invoices must be issued via the State Taxation Administration's Golden Tax Phase IV (GTP-IV) platform or the fully digitalized electronic invoice (e-invoice) system. For general VAT taxpayers, invoice data and all input/output tax line items require real-time transmission through the E-Tax API interface. Each invoice bears a unique invoice code and a digitally generated anti-counterfeit stamp (QR/watermark) that is verified by the national invoice query and clearance database. Export VAT refunds are cleared through the electronic Export Tax Rebate System, which automatically monitors customs declarations, export proceeds, and time limits. The e-invoice exchange between the FIP, shipyard suppliers, and logistics parties must be reconciled with the customs export declaration to avoid mismatching of the foreign exchange repatriation proof.

The STA performs post-clearance risk screening using cross-system matching of customs declarations, vessel delivery certificates, and bank receipts. Any false zero-rating claim, artificial export recycling, or failure to repatriate foreign exchange within 180 days triggers: immediate zero-rating cancellation and recomputation of VAT at 13%; late-payment surcharge of 0.05% per day; an administrative penalty of between 0.5 and 5 times the amount of tax fraudulently refunded under the Tax Administration and Collection Law, Article 63; and criminal prosecution under PRC Criminal Law Article 204 if the fraudulent refund exceeds the statutory criminal threshold, including imprisonment for up to 10 years plus confiscation of assets. Also, the FIP may be delisted from the export tax refund roster and barred from zero-rate treatment for a period up to 3 years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China maintains a managed and partially convertible exchange-control regime. The current account is convertible for bona fide trade, shipping and operating services, but every payment requires bank verification under SAFE guidelines and, where needed, a tax certificate. The capital account remains heavily regulated; a mainland Foreign-Invested Partnership is required to deposit foreign equity capital into a dedicated SAFE-registered foreign exchange capital account, register the investment through the bank's SAFE system, and submit the underlying joint-venture or partnership agreement before converting funds into RMB. Profit remittance to a Hong Kong or other non-resident partner is legal only after statutory audit, payment of corporate income tax, VAT and other due taxes, and SAFE profit distribution filing; banks examine the dividend availability in the FIP's audited accounts. CNY is not freely convertible on the capital side, and SAFE will treat any large or round-tripped flow as disguised capital movement. In practice, the FIP can access offshore CNH liquidity through Hong Kong treasury nodes, but onshore clearing and settlement must remain at a licensed domestic bank; using parallel fintech origination would not meet SAFE reporting duties.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export proceeds must be repatriated to mainland China within 90 days of the export customs declaration. There is no mandatory conversion ratio for general corporate export receipts; the FIP may retain foreign currency in its SAFE-monitored export settlement account or sell the currency to a licensed bank for RMB. Each receipt must be linked to a matching customs declaration through SAFE's goods-trade monitoring system; A-class filers receive normal processing, while B-class or C-class filers face accelerated documentary review and full original bill verification.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) acting through the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) for mainland China exchange-control administration, with the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) overseeing the offshore CNH clearing and treasury node in Hong Kong SAR.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low viability. Mainland China licensing rules do not permit non-bank fintech EMIs to open foreign exchange accounts or execute current-account and capital-account settlement for a China-domiciled FIP. Cross-border fintech platforms may support invoice presentation, reconciliation and payment status APIs, but all lawful RMB and foreign-currency clearing must route through a licensed commercial bank, CIPS and the global SWIFT network. A Hong Kong-licensed EMI cannot substitute for the mainland SAFE-regulated bank account and cannot be appointed as the primary treasury rail for this entity.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All foreign equity contributions must be wired in USD, EUR or HKD to a SAFE-registered foreign exchange capital account at a tier-one domestic bank. The bank must complete the FDI foreign-exchange registration for the FIP in the SAFE system using the foreign-invested partnership contract and the SAFE transaction purpose code for direct-investment capital injection. Once the capital verification is performed and the SAFE registration record is obtained, the bank may convert the capital into RMB for permitted operating expenditures, always subject to the foreign investment negative list. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Chinese Local File documentation is required if annual related-party tangible-asset transfers exceed RMB 200 million, intangible-asset transfers exceed RMB 10 million, financing/fund transfers exceed RMB 40 million, or service transactions exceed RMB 10 million; Master File is required when the multinational group has consolidated revenue exceeding RMB 1 billion and engaged in related-party transactions in China.

Economic substance scope: China applies substance-over-form under the PRC Enterprise Income Tax Law and the SAT Special Tax Adjustment rules; although China does not use the UAE Economic Substance Regulations, a Hong Kong partner claiming treaty benefits must demonstrate core income-generating activities in Hong Kong, including real staff, premises, decision-making, functions, assets, and risks, otherwise the Chinese tax authority may strike down the Hong Kong vehicle under the General Anti-Abuse Rule, deny treaty relief, and recharacterize the payment as taxable Chinese-source income.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and False Shipyard Financial Statements

Statutory sanction: PRC Tax Collection and Administration Law Article 63 imposes repayment of tax evaded plus a penalty of 50% to five times the underpaid amount; daily late surcharge of 0.05% accrues from the due date; if the criminal threshold is met, PRC Criminal Law Article 201 imposes imprisonment up to seven years and criminal fines.

Director personal liability: Directors and general partners who knowingly sign false returns or cause the FIP to underdeclare income are personally exposed to civil restitution, monetary penalties, travel bans, inclusion on the dishonest taxpayer blacklist, and imprisonment for tax evasion.
Treaty Abuse / Shell Vehicle / Deficient Beneficial Ownership

Statutory sanction: The State Taxation Administration will deny treaty benefits under the PRC General Anti-Abuse Rule and SAT Announcement 2015 No. 60, imposing full 10% withholding, back interest, and a 50% to fivefold tax evasion penalty if the abusive arrangement is treated as a false declaration; serious structures may also be reclassified as foreign-controlled resident enterprises with CIT at 25%.

Director personal liability: Directors and partners cannot shield themselves behind the FIP; personal civil liability extends to unpaid withholding taxes, interest, and penalties, and deliberate treaty fraud with sustained abusive structuring can trigger criminal liability, asset freezes, exit bans, and imprisonment.
AML / Money Laundering / Foreign Exchange Fraud via Escrow and L/C Payment Rails

Statutory sanction: PRC Anti-Money Laundering Law, PRC Foreign Exchange Administration Regulations, and PRC Criminal Law Article 191 authorize seizure, fines equal to the illegal transaction amount, administrative penalties of 5% to 30% of the illegal foreign-exchange amounts, and criminal penalties of up to ten years for money laundering.

Director personal liability: Directors and general partners are personally liable for AML violations, including forfeiture of assets, blacklisting, travel restrictions, and imprisonment; the corporate or partnership veil is pierced where the entity is used as a façade for illicit fund transfers and criminal concealment.
