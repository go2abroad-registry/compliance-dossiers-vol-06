For an entity engaged in double-shot PBT plastic injection of custom mechanical keyboard keycaps, a virtual office or commercial mailbox is not permissible as the principal registered office. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA); electronic filing is effected through the STA Unified Electronic Tax Bureau at etax.chinatax.gov.cn, with the official public portal at chinatax.gov.cn

E-government filing portal

The Foreign-Invested Partnership (FIP) first completes market supervision registration and obtains the 18-character Unified Social Credit Code (USCC). Within 30 calendar days of the business license date, the FIP must submit a tax registration application, partnership agreement, USCC license, investor identity and beneficial ownership documentation, place of management or business address proof, and responsible-person identification to the competent STA office, either online through the e-Tax bureau or in person. The STA then issues a taxpayer identification number identical to the USCC and activates VAT, withholding, e-invoice, customs-tax data exchange, and tax declaration credentials. Bank account information must be filed with the tax authority, and the FIP should complete the registration of its foreign-invested partnership particulars and partner capital contributions for cross-border and treaty compliance.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

No check-the-box entity election exists under PRC enterprise income tax law; partnership-level tax transparency is mandatory. The FIP may, however, elect VAT status as small-scale or general VAT taxpayer, with general taxpayer status compulsory above RMB 5,000,000 of annual taxable sales and advisable for export zero-rating refund claims. Partners may agree on profit-sharing allocation for tax purposes if the allocation has economic substance, and non-resident partners may elect to apply treaty benefits under the China-Hong Kong double taxation arrangement through procedures for treaty relief; no hybrid-mismatch entity election is available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 of annual taxable sales for mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration; small-scale VAT taxpayers enjoy monthly sales exemption up to RMB 100,000 under current policy, but the FIP manufacturing and export business will ordinarily be a general VAT taxpayer and the RMB 100,000 micro-exemption is inapplicable

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A 0% VAT rate applies when the FIP is a general VAT taxpayer with export trading rights, the double-shot PBT keycaps are lawfully cleared by customs and physically exported from mainland China customs territory to an overseas destination, including Hong Kong SAR which is a separate customs and VAT territory from mainland China, title and risk pass to an overseas buyer, the sales proceeds are collected in foreign currency in accordance with SAFE rules, all statutory export documentation is retained, and the export tax rebate declaration is accurately filed. The input VAT on PBT resin, keycap molds, injection molding utilities, purchased machinery, and other creditable inputs may be refunded to the extent of the export rebate limitation and after customs record matching.

  • Customs export declaration with electronic release record
  • Bill of lading or airway bill evidencing physical export
  • Inward remittance certificate or foreign-exchange collection bank receipt for the export proceeds
  • Export sales contract and purchase order
  • Commercial invoice and detailed packing list
  • VAT special invoices or special customs payment forms for input purchases supporting the refund claim
  • Export tax rebate declaration and reconciliation statement filed with the STA

China operates a nationwide fully digital e-invoice (Digitalized Special Value-Added Tax Invoice) system on the STA platform; every VAT invoice must be issued through the tax authority-controlled electronic invoice service in XML or OFD format, carry a unique 20-digit invoice number and cryptographic signature, and be reported to the STA in near real time. No physical fiscal stamping is required, but higher-volume taxpayers must integrate enterprise resource planning systems with STA application programming interfaces; all invoice data is cleared and reconciled in the Golden Tax Phase IV platform together with customs declarations, logistics documents, contracts, and bank foreign-exchange receipts.

Failure to repatriate export proceeds within the 180-day statutory deadline, a material discrepancy between customs declarations and bills of lading, non-existent goods, or false export declarations will invalidate the zero-rating and require recovery of any already refunded VAT; the recovered tax bears late-payment interest accruing daily. Fraudulent export tax refunds may be penalized administratively at 50% to five times the refunded amount, with suspension of export tax rebate qualification for up to three years, and serious cases constitute criminal fraud under Article 204 of the PRC Criminal Law carrying criminal fines and imprisonment up to life imprisonment. Golden Tax Phase IV cross-system risk control automatically flags anomalies in invoice issuance, customs declarations, freight movements, insurance, and foreign-exchange receipts, and the STA may open retrospective audit and clawback reviews without prior notice.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China maintains current-account RMB convertibility for genuine trade, services, and investment income, including the distributable profits of a Foreign-Invested Partnership (CN_FIP). Such transactions are processed by licensed banks under authenticity and conformity verification. Capital-account transactions remain controlled: an inbound FDI capital injection from the Hong Kong partner requires foreign investment information reporting, AMR registration, and SAFE direct-investment FX registration before the funds can be credited. Profit remittance to the Hong Kong partner is treated as current-account investment income and is permissible after bank-level verification. Key remittance hurdles include: audited annual financial statements; a partnership distribution resolution or equivalent governing document; calculation of the distributable profit limited to audited retained earnings; settlement of PRC withholding tax, generally 10% and potentially 5% under the Hong Kong-China tax arrangement if the HK partner establishes treaty beneficial ownership; and SAFE outbound remittance reporting. No remittance may exceed after-tax distributable profits, and unresolved tax arrears, unreconciled FX balances, or unverified trade documents will block the transaction.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE trade-in-goods rules, the CN_FIP must repatriate 100% of export proceeds for goods exported from China to a licensed bank account in China within 90 days from the date of export customs declaration. If receipt is reasonably expected beyond 90 days, the FIP must apply through the bank to register a delayed export receipt before the original due date. There is no compulsory conversion ratio; export proceeds may be retained in a foreign-currency current account or converted into RMB at the prevailing market rate. Each incoming receipt must be reconciled with the corresponding customs declaration through the SAFE trade monitoring and foreign-exchange verification system, and any non-receipt, under-receipt, or over-receipt must be justified to the bank to avoid a compliance exception.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) and State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low as a primary banking and clearing rail; Medium only as a supporting payment-acceptance layer. Offshore digital EMIs, including Hong Kong-licensed payment institutions and global payment facilitators such as Stripe, Shopify Payments, Payoneer, Airwallex, or similar, cannot open or operate onshore capital project foreign-exchange accounts, cannot receive FDI capital contributions, and cannot act as the bank of record for export proceeds in China without a licensed Chinese bank partner. They may be used to collect e-commerce card payments outside China and to initiate settlement to an onshore licensed bank current account, but all CNY clearing and all capital-account flows must be executed by a licensed domestic bank. PBOC payment-institution licensing rules and SAFE foreign-exchange regulations prohibit an offshore EMI from holding client funds in an onshore custody capacity or from processing direct investment capital without a Chinese banking licence.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: HK$10,000,000 aggregate value of controlled transactions with related parties in a year of assessment; above this threshold, a Local File (and if applicable a Master File) is mandatory; HK also requires Master File and CbC reporting for MNE groups with group revenue exceeding approximately HK$680,000,000

Economic substance scope: Hong Kong does not maintain a statute-based Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) regime similar to BVI or Cayman, but the Inland Revenue Ordinance's territorial source principle and anti-abuse rules (ss.61/61A) require an actual economic presence in Hong Kong. A foreign-invested partnership used as a pure invoicing shell with no keycap production, procurement activity, staff, or management functions in HK is vulnerable to recharacterization and additional taxation

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Profits Tax Return

Statutory sanction: Upon summary conviction under IRO s.82(1): fine up to HK$200,000 and imprisonment up to 3 years; additional tax up to three times the tax undercharged (s.82A); interest on unpaid tax may be assessed

Director personal liability: For the partnership context, any managing partner or tax representative who signs a false return is personally liable; courts may issue civil recovery orders, impose travel restrictions preventing departure from Hong Kong, and order imprisonment of the responsible partner
Shell Company / General Anti-Abuse (GAAR)

Statutory sanction: Under IRO ss.61/61A, the Commissioner may disregard the partnership or recharacterize the income; tax assessments may be amended, leading to additional tax of up to 100% of the tax evaded plus penalty tax and interest; criminal sanctions apply if evasion is proven

Director personal liability: Partners and officers of corporate partners can be held personally responsible for the arrangement; the corporate veil is pierceable for abuse, and individual culpable actors face fines up to HK$100,000 and imprisonment up to 3 years
Transfer Pricing Documentation Failure and Improper Related-Party Pricing

Statutory sanction: The IRD can impose transfer pricing adjustments on the partnership's assessable profits, levy additional tax up to 100% of the adjusted amount, and impose penalties for failure to keep records and comply with information notices under IRO s.51; continuous non-compliance may lead to daily penalties

Director personal liability: Partners and finance managers who fail to maintain required TP documentation are jointly liable; the Commissioner may obtain an order preventing them from leaving Hong Kong until records and tax liabilities are settled
Anti-Money Laundering and Use of Payment Rail Proceeds

Statutory sanction: Under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Ordinance (Cap. 615), breaches of reporting and record-keeping duties carry a maximum fine of HK$5,000,000 and imprisonment up to 7 years; underlying fraud charges may coexist

Director personal liability: Directors, partners, and compliance officers are personally exposed to criminal prosecution; personal travel bans, asset confiscation orders, and disqualification orders can be imposed; HK courts have demonstrated willingness to pierce through the partnership for effective AML enforcement
