Stage 03
Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

      
Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

    

    

      
Tax ID Application Infrastructure

      

        
Competent tax authority
State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; official electronic portal: the China Electronic Tax Administration (e-Tax) system. Hong Kong SAR maintains a separate taxation regime under the Inland Revenue Department, but the national tax identity for this China-domiciled Foreign-Invested Partnership is registered with the STA.

        
E-government filing portal
For the Foreign-Invested Partnership (FIP) formed in mainland China, the partnership must complete tax registration with the competent local STA office within 30 calendar days after the Administration for Market Regulation issues the business licence. Tax registration is integrated with business registration, so the partnership automatically receives the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code, which functions as its national Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) and VAT ID. A foreign non-resident partner that needs a separate non-resident TIN must file a written registration application through the e-Tax portal using the official non-resident enterprise registration procedure, accompanied by the partnership agreement, the FIP registration certificate, the foreign partner's Hong Kong incorporation/business registration certificate (where applicable), certified identity documents of the partners, and a power of attorney appointing a resident tax representative. The STA verifies the dossier, issues the non-resident TIN, activates the partnership's e-Tax account, and grants access to electronic VAT invoice issuance and general-taxpayer status where eligible.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

      

        
Default tax classification
Pass_Through_Eligible

        
Available election forms
None for income-tax entity classification: the PRC does not provide check-the-box or hybrid mismatch elections, and the partnership's pass-through status is statutory. The only available elections are the VAT general-taxpayer election for registrants with annual taxable sales below RMB 5,000,000, and the simplified VAT levy method election for small-scale taxpayers.

      

    

    

      
VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

      

        
Standard VAT / GST rate
13%

        
Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)
No de minimis turnover threshold for VAT registration: any entity engaging in taxable sales of goods in China must register for VAT from its first taxable transaction. Compulsory VAT general-taxpayer registration applies once annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000; below that amount, the taxpayer may remain a small-scale taxpayer subject to simplified VAT rates.

      

      
      

        Export 0% zero-rating conditions
        
The FIP may claim 0% VAT on export sales of the cast epoxy-resin polyhedral dice if it is a registered VAT general taxpayer, the goods are physically exported from the PRC customs territory under a direct export customs declaration made in the partnership's name, title and risk pass to the overseas customer, the goods are not prohibited from export, the export sales invoice is issued, the production or purchase inputs are substantiated by special VAT invoices or complete factory costing records, and the export proceeds are collected and repatriated through an authorised bank and reported into the SAFE trade-in-goods foreign-exchange monitoring system. Qualifying offshore digital services are zero-rated only where supplied to a non-resident and consumed outside China; the present goods business follows the physical-export zero-rating route.

Mandatory use of the National Unified Electronic Invoice Platform (the Fully Digitalised Electronic Invoice system) for all VAT-invoicing partnerships. Invoice XML data must be transmitted in real time to the STA through the official e-invoice API. The platform assigns a unique invoice number and QR code, performs automated anti-fraud validation and digital authentication, and retains the invoice in the national invoice database; legacy tax-control physical invoice devices are permitted only on a transitional basis for paper-invoice users.

      
Export zero-rating and any resulting VAT refund are subject to retrospective verification by the STA using customs, banking, and foreign-exchange data. If the goods are not actually exported, the foreign currency is not repatriated within 180 days, or the supporting documentation is false, the STA will reverse the 0% rate, claw back all VAT refunds or credits paid plus late-payment interest, and impose a penalty of 50% up to 500% of the evaded tax under the PRC Tax Administration Law. Fraudulent claims involving forged customs declarations or sham export documents may additionally be prosecuted as export-tax-refund fraud under Article 204 of the Criminal Law, and the partnership and responsible partners may be placed on the serious-tax-offence blacklist.

Stage 04
Banking & Treasury Architecture

      
Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

    

    

      
Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

      

        
Current-account convertibility
The RMB is fully convertible on the current account for bona fide trade, cross-border e-commerce, and service receipts, while the capital account remains controlled. The FIP must repatriate every export and crowdfunding receipt to a domestic bank, retain it in a foreign-currency current account or convert it at a designated bank, and map each receipt to a customs-clearable transaction in the SAFE monitoring system. Crowdfunding advances from Kickstarter and PayPal settlements are treated as advance payments for goods or cross-border trade proceeds only if the FIP can prove shipment of the RPG dice and the absence of third-party collection. Capital contributions from foreign partners require SAFE foreign investment registration, a capital project foreign currency account, a Chinese CPA capital verification report, and post-establishment changes filing; such funds cannot be deployed for prohibited purposes such as unapproved securities investment or entrusted lending. Dividend or profit remittances to non-resident partners are permissible after annual audit, tax settlement under applicable non-resident partnership taxation rules, partnership distribution resolution, and SAFE registration, but banks will reject any distribution if the underlying capital has not been verified or if the exported goods receipts have not been reconciled. The practical hurdle is documentary granularity rather than outright prohibition.

        
Export proceeds surrender rules
Goods-trade export proceeds must be repatriated to a domestic foreign-currency current account within 90 calendar days of the customs export date unless an SAFE-approved deferred collection registration extends the term. The mandatory conversion ratio is 0%; the FIP may retain 100% of eligible export proceeds in foreign currency or sell them to the designated bank at market rates. Each credit must be matched to the SAFE cross-border receipts-and-payments data, the customs declaration, the commercial invoice, and the logistics proof, and any platform-aggregated advance receipt above the SAFE threshold must be registered before settlement to avoid an automatic trade fraud flag.

      

      
Central bank supervision: The People's Bank of China (PBOC) together with the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China, which has delegated authority over goods-trade verification, capital account registration, and cross-border settlement supervision for all mainland-domiciled foreign-invested partnerships; the Hong Kong SAR is a separate no-exchange-control jurisdiction, but it is not the governing FX regulator for the mainland-registered FIP.

    

    

      
Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

      

        
        

          

            Stripe
            Fit: High
          

          
Offshore digital EMI accounts such as Wise, Revolut Business, Payoneer, and Airwallex are not viable as primary banking rails for a mainland-registered FIP. They are not licensed banking institutions in the PRC, cannot receive SAFE registration, cannot support capital verification, and cannot administer RMB basic accounts. PayPal and Stripe likewise act as payment aggregators, not bank alternatives. The FIP must maintain a domestic corporate account with a Designated Foreign Exchange Bank; proceeds from PayPal, Stripe, and Kickstarter should be settled through licensed cross-border payment institutions into that domestic account. Offshore EMI accounts may be used only for paying non-resident suppliers or overseas marketing costs after a lawful outbound remittance has been completed, and such accounts must not be presented as the FIP's primary financial-statement bank in China. Viability rating is low for primary compliance and medium only for narrow ancillary foreign-currency work streams.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

      

        Foreign partners must first complete the SAFE foreign investment information registration for the partnership at the designated bank and obtain the relevant business registration code before any inbound remittance. The remittance should be a SWIFT MT103 from each partner's own overseas account to the FIP's corresponding foreign-currency capital account, with the beneficiary account name identical to the FIP registration name and the remittance purpose code describing a partnership capital contribution by a foreign investor. The bank will verify the transaction against the SAFE system, require the capital contribution declaration, and after receipt the FIP must engage a PRC certified public accountant to issue a capital verification report. If non-resident partners contribute goods, technology, or services instead of cash, separate asset valuation and SAFE registration are required. Converted RMB from the capital account is subject to the negative-list regime: no unapproved securities investment, no entrusted loans, no real-estate purchases outside the business scope, and no non-business investment; all converted RMB must be debited from the RMB capital account to the FIP's ordinary operating account for permitted expenditure.For raw materials, packaging, and logistics paid to overseas suppliers, the FIP should use SWIFT MT103 with a goods-trade purpose code and the underlying invoice and packing list entered into the bank's electronic trade system. For high-value or repeat resin and dice-mold supplier contracts, a Commercial Letter of Credit issued through SWIFT MT700 is prudent to protect the FIP's deposit; for moderate-value shipments, Documentary Collections through SWIFT MT400 and MT202 provide a lower-cost middle path. For payments to Hong Kong suppliers that accept CNY, use cross-border RMB clearing via the Cross-Border Interbank Payment System, which eliminates FX mismatch and is counted as trade settlement for SAFE reporting. All outbound payments must be matched against verified imports or services; PayPal and Stripe payouts should not be used for supplier settlement because they do not generate SAFE-compliant goods-trade evidence.Deliverable onshore forward contracts with a genuine trade background, tenor aligned to invoice maturity, usually not exceeding 12 months without SAFE approval, and limited by bank credit lines.Plain vanilla FX options or option combinations with forward contracts, used for risk reduction only; exotic, barrier, or leveraged options are prohibited for non-bank corporate clients.Cross-currency swaps for foreign-currency debt or trade-related principal and interest commitments, with registered foreign debt quota and SAFE approval where applicable; no speculative swap without an underlying exposure.Non-deliverable forwards are not permissible onshore for the mainland FIP; only offshore CNH NDFs may be used by a separate Hong Kong affiliate for genuine offshore cash-flow exposure, and SAFE will expect no onshore FIP participation in speculative RMB positions.
      

      
E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Offshore digital EMI accounts such as Wise, Revolut Business, Payoneer, and Airwallex are not viable as primary banking rails for a mainland-registered FIP. They are not licensed banking institutions in the PRC, cannot receive SAFE registration, cannot support capital verification, and cannot administer RMB basic accounts. PayPal and Stripe likewise act as payment aggregators, not bank alternatives. The FIP must maintain a domestic corporate account with a Designated Foreign Exchange Bank; proceeds from PayPal, Stripe, and Kickstarter should be settled through licensed cross-border payment institutions into that domestic account. Offshore EMI accounts may be used only for paying non-resident suppliers or overseas marketing costs after a lawful outbound remittance has been completed, and such accounts must not be presented as the FIP's primary financial-statement bank in China. Viability rating is low for primary compliance and medium only for narrow ancillary foreign-currency work streams.
