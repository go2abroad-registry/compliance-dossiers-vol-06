Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Inland Revenue Department (IRD) of the Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region; online electronic portal: eTAX at https://www.etax.gov.hk. Business Registration is under the IRD's Business Registration Office; the Companies Registry administers the limited partnership register.

E-government filing portal

The Foreign-Invested Partnership (FIP) must first execute a partnership agreement specifying all general and limited partners, the Hong Kong resident manager, and the principal place of business in Hong Kong. If structured as a limited partnership, file with the Companies Registry under the Limited Partnerships Fund Ordinance (Cap. 637) to obtain the Certificate of Registration. Next, apply to the Business Registration Office of the Inland Revenue Department within one month of commencement of business for a Business Registration Certificate under the Business Registration Ordinance (Cap. 310). The resulting Business Registration Number (BRN) constitutes the partnership's statutory tax identification number (TIN/VAT ID equivalent) for Hong Kong profits tax and employer obligations. Each non-resident partner without a Hong Kong identity card must separately register with the IRD as an individual taxpayer, furnishing legal name, nationality, overseas tax reference number, and a Hong Kong correspondence address, after which the IRD assigns a Hong Kong tax identification number for assessment of that partner's share of partnership profits. No minimum capital deposit or approval by the foreign exchange authorities is required.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

None. Hong Kong does not offer a check-the-box election or analogous entity classification election. Under the Inland Revenue Ordinance (Cap. 112), a partnership is not itself chargeable to profits tax; the assessable profits are computed at the partnership level and each partner is assessed on their share. There is also no domestic hybrid mismatch election; cross-border entities are assessed according to the source principle and territorial tax rules.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT / GST / Sales and Use Tax in Hong Kong (Hong Kong imposes no general consumption tax; however, specific excise duties exist on a narrow class of dutiable commodities)

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - there is no indirect tax registration threshold as no VAT/GST is levied. The Business Registration Ordinance (Cap. 310) nonetheless mandates registration for every business carried on in Hong Kong within one month of commencement, irrespective of turnover or profit; a nil turnover partnership still requires a Business Registration Certificate.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable as a VAT zero-rating claim because Hong Kong has no VAT/GST regime and hence no 0% export VAT rate and no corresponding refund procedure. For direct tax purposes, the FIP may invoke the offshore profit source doctrine: profits derived from export sales of the silicone fidget products are not chargeable to Hong Kong profits tax if the purchase and resale contracts were made and effected outside Hong Kong, with no profit-producing operations carried out in Hong Kong. To substantiate an offshore claim, the partnership should maintain partner meeting minutes, contract execution files, shipping documents, invoices, and correspondence demonstrating that the goods moved directly from the mainland China manufacturing facility to the overseas buyer and that the value-appreciating steps occurred outside Hong Kong.

  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical dispatch of the goods from mainland China or Hong Kong to the foreign buyer and the port/airport of destination

Not applicable - Hong Kong has no real-time fiscal clearance mandate, no mandatory e-invoicing platform, and no digital stamping or anti-fraud device for ordinary commercial invoices. Trade declarations for goods movement, where required by the Census and Statistics Ordinance (Cap. 430), are lodged electronically via the Government Electronic Trading Services (GETS) for statistical or dutiable purposes, but are not a tax clearance mechanism.

Although unverified export claims cannot trigger a VAT clawback because no VAT zero-rating exists in Hong Kong, the IRD will subject offshore profit claims to rigorous audit scrutiny. If the documentary bundle is absent, inconsistent, or intentionally falsified, the IRD may re-characterise the profits as sourced in Hong Kong and assess profits tax on the partners at the applicable rate (16.5% for a corporation partner and graduated personal rates up to 15% for an individual partner). Additional tax under section 82A of the Inland Revenue Ordinance (Cap. 112) may be imposed up to three times the amount of tax undercharged, and criminal liability for knowingly making false declarations or books can lead to fines, custodial sentences, and orders for retrospective clawback of the tax and interest.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Mainland China runs a managed floating exchange-rate system. Current-account convertibility is broadly available, including trade receipts, service fees, profit remittances and payroll, so the FIP can freely settle export proceeds into RMB through its local bank as long as each transaction is supported by customs and contract documents. Capital-account convertibility is still restricted: the Hong Kong partner's injection is treated as inbound foreign direct investment, requiring a SAFE FDI registration before remittance, a restricted capital account number, permissible-use review, a CPA capital verification report, and compliance with the foreign investment negative list. Foreign exchange capital proceeds can be converted into RMB at the spot rate under the positive-list settlement method, but cannot be deployed for securities, entrusted loans, repayment of domestic RMB loans outside the permitted operating cycle, or prohibited uses. Repatriation of profits to the Hong Kong partner is allowed only after annual audit and tax clearance or tax advance payment, and the bank will remit based on an underlying partner resolution, audited financial statements, and historical balance-of-payments declarations. Hong Kong itself has no exchange controls and offers offshore CNH liquidity, but the mainland FIP must convert at onshore CNY rates through its PRC bank and cannot use Hong Kong CNH liquidity directly as onshore operating capital.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export foreign currency receipts arising from fidget sales must be received into the FIP's designated foreign exchange settlement account in China within 180 days from the date of customs export declaration; if the sales contract allows a longer trade credit period, the FIP must report the expected collection maturity in the SAFE and bank trade monitoring system before shipment. The statutory mandatory conversion ratio is 0% under the current-account voluntary settlement regime: the FIP may keep 100% of export proceeds in foreign currency or sell up to 100% to the bank for CNY at the prevailing onshore market rate. The bank will match each receipt to the customs declaration and the related receipt-disposal record in the Trade Receivables and Payables Monitoring System, and may suspend further payment acceptance if receipts are overdue or unmatched.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China, acting under the People's Bank of China (PBOC); Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) is the separate currency-board supervisor for the Hong Kong clearing node.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Offshore digital fintech EMI providers, including Wise, Airwallex and similar institutions, are legally barred from acting as the primary bank for a PRC Foreign-Invested Partnership because they are not licensed domestic settlement banks in China and cannot perform SAFE FDI registration, capital verification, RMB basic deposit account services, or statutory trade verification. Their viability is Low; they may only serve as ancillary collection agents in licensed cross-border ecommerce settlement rails and must never handle statutory capital contributions or export surrender flows for the mainland FIP.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The capital injection must be initiated as a direct-investment remittance from the Hong Kong partner's bank in HKD, USD or CNH via SWIFT MT103, or via CIPS for CNY, to the FIP's capital escrow account at the selected mainland Tier-1 bank. The remittance instruction must include the SAFE FDI registration enterprise code and the BOPS transaction purpose code corresponding to inbound direct investment capital contribution; the bank will post the funds as capital in transit, not as immediately disposable operating funds. After the contribution is credited and the CPA capital verification report is issued, the FIP may convert the foreign exchange into RMB under the SAFE capital-account settlement module and transfer the RMB to its basic operational account. All capital-account receipts must be used only for the partnership's disclosed manufacturing, raw material, working-capital and permitted operating purposes; platform payment systems cannot replace the SWIFT or CIPS capital transfer rails.For outbound supplier payments for silicone resin and manufacturing inputs, use SWIFT MT103 for regular invoices denominated in USD, HKD or EUR, or CIPS for cross-border RMB if the counterparty accepts CNY. Cross-border ecommerce buyer proceeds may be collected through licensed platform payment aggregators and then forwarded to the FIP's export foreign exchange account, but those aggregators must not be used for capital-account injections or statutory capital verification.Onshore deliverable CNY forward contracts in CNY/USD, CNY/EUR and CNY/HKD for hedging committed export receipts and import payments with genuine underlying current-account exposure and a maximum standard tenor of 12 months.Onshore CNY/USD foreign exchange swaps to manage the timing mismatch between capital settlement and operating liquidity, with documented economic exposure and no speculative leverage.Onshore vanilla currency options or zero-cost collar structures to cap procurement and export currency risk, executed only through a licensed PRC bank and supported by trade or capital-flow documentation.Offshore CNH non-deliverable forwards are permitted only in the Hong Kong treasury node for the Hong Kong partner; the mainland FIP is not allowed to execute NDFs onshore and must use deliverable CNY forwards as the compliant onshore hedging alternative.

Their viability is Low; they may only serve as ancillary collection agents in licensed cross-border ecommerce settlement rails and must never handle statutory capital contributions or export surrender flows for the mainland FIP.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% standard PRC dividend withholding tax; however, FIP profit distributions are not dividends. The non-resident corporate partner's distributive share is directly China-source income taxable at 10% (absent a PE) unless a DTA reduces the rate.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Under SAT Announcement 2016 No.42, Local File is required when annual related-party transactions exceed any of: RMB 200 million for tangible property, RMB 20 million for intangible property, RMB 100 million for financial transactions, or RMB 40 million for other services. Master File is required if aggregate related-party transactions exceed RMB 1 billion. The annual Related Party Transactions Report must be attached whenever any related-party dealings exist.

Economic substance scope: China applies the substance-over-form doctrine, GAAR, and the beneficial ownership test in treaty relief to prevent Hong Kong-shell entities from obtaining 5%/7% rates. The HK partner must maintain dedicated staff, independent decision-making, proper accounting records, and real economic risk for holding and funding the FIP. Hong Kong ESR does not directly cover silicone fidget manufacturing, but it imposes substance requirements for any Hong Kong-incorporated pure holding company before the HKIRD will certify treaty residency.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Filing

Statutory sanction: Administrative penalty of 50% to 5 times the underpaid tax plus daily 0.05% late-payment surcharge; criminal tax evasion penalty of a fine and up to 7 years imprisonment where the evaded amount is large (generally exceeding RMB 100,000).

Director personal liability: PRC FIP general partners and responsible officers are jointly and severally liable for unpaid taxes of the partnership; courts may pierce the partnership veil where fraud, co-mingling, or undercapitalization is proven, and directors may face asset freezes, travel bans, and imprisonment.
Shell Company / Treaty Shopping

Statutory sanction: Denial of DTA benefits, recharacterization to the full 10% non-resident rate or, where a PE exists, the full 25% CIT; additional penalty of 50% to 5 times underpaid tax; public disclosure of the anti-avoidance adjustment.

Director personal liability: Directors or general partners who knowingly orchestrated the sham arrangement are personally liable for connected tax deficiencies under PRC General Anti-Avoidance Rules; Hong Kong directors may be disqualified and subjected to travel restrictions.
Transfer Pricing Documentation Failure

Statutory sanction: Tax authority downward adjustment of expenses, interest and penalties; fine of up to RMB 100,000 for non-provision of documentation; penalty of 50% to 5 times underpaid tax for transfer pricing recharacterization.

Director personal liability: Where directors sign false tax filings or conceal transfer pricing records, they incur personal responsibility for restitution of the FIP's tax deficiency; general partners may become jointly liable with the FIP for the adjusted tax, interest, and penalties.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Non-Disclosure

Statutory sanction: AML penalties of up to RMB 5 million or 20% of the suspicious transaction amount for serious violations; criminal money-laundering conviction carries a fine and up to 10 years imprisonment.

Director personal liability: Directors, general partners, and compliance officers may be disqualified, have assets frozen, and be banned from leaving China pending resolution of AML investigations; individual criminal liability attaches where the director intentionally furthered the suspicious flow.
