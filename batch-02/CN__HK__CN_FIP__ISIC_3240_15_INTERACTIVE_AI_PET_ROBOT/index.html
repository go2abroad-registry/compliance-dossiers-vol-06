Competent corporate registry

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China, administered by the competent local tax authority at the FIP's registered address; official online portal: State Taxation Administration Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax) platform.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Complete the foreign-invested partnership formation and registration with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) to obtain the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which is legally recognised as the taxpayer identification number (TIN) for tax purposes. Step 2: Within 30 days of SAMR registration, complete tax registration through the Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax) platform using the USCC; no separate TIN certificate is issued because the USCC is synchronised automatically to STA records. Step 3: For each non-resident foreign partner, lodge legalised/certified incorporation or identity documents and evidence of tax residence; the FIP must register the non-resident partners in the e-Tax system for withholding and reporting purposes. Step 4: Submit the partnership agreement, capital contribution schedule, and authorised representative letter to the local STA. Step 5: Determine VAT status: if projected annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000, register as a general VAT taxpayer; otherwise the FIP may remain a small-scale VAT taxpayer or elect general status. Step 6: Activate the digital invoice service and obtain the digital tax-control certificate through the e-Tax platform.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

None. PRC law mandates pass-through income tax treatment for partnerships under the Enterprise Income Tax Law and the partnership taxation notice (Cai Shui [2008] No. 159). No check-the-box election and no hybrid mismatch election is available to a Chinese Foreign-Invested Partnership; the only available tax-registration election is between small-scale VAT taxpayer and general VAT taxpayer status.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

All activities subject to VAT trigger registration obligations from commencement; compulsory general VAT taxpayer registration applies when annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000, while small-scale VAT taxpayer status applies below that threshold and may benefit from the RMB 100,000 monthly / RMB 300,000 quarterly sales exemption under current STA preferential rules.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A VAT general taxpayer FIP may apply the 0% export VAT rate to genuine sales of tangible interactive AI pet robot goods that are declared through PRC customs and physically leave the customs territory to an overseas buyer. The export must match the export contract, the commercial invoice, the customs declaration, and the repatriated sales proceeds. Embedded AI/software delivered as an integral part of the hardware is treated as part of the exported good; standalone offshore digital or software services qualify for zero-rating/exemption only if wholly consumed outside China and supported by the STA cross-border service substantiation framework.

  • Export Goods Customs Declaration (PRC export customs declaration record demonstrating physical export)
  • Ocean or air Bill of Lading / international waybill evidencing outbound shipment to the foreign buyer
  • Commercial invoice issued by the FIP
  • Bank receipt or cross-border settlement statement (foreign exchange receivable confirmation / export proceeds receipt record)
  • Foreign exchange contract or purchase order from the overseas buyer
  • Where applicable, cross-border e-commerce export transaction records and platform order documentation

The FIP must issue all VAT invoices through the STA Fully Digitalised Electronic Invoice (digital fapiao) platform, with data transmitted in real time through the Electronic Tax Bureau API. Every invoice is protected by an encrypted anti-fraud tax-control code and digital signature; the Golden Tax System IV monitors issuance, voidance, red-letter invoices, and export-related evidence electronically. No paper-only or non-authorised offline invoicing is permitted for VAT special invoices once digital invoice status is activated.

Unverified or false export zero-rating claims are reversed through the Golden Tax System and audit review, requiring clawback of any VAT refund or credit claimed, plus statutory late payment surcharge. Under the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection, fraudulent export claims are punishable by administrative fines ranging from 50% to 500% of the refunded or underpaid tax and may result in disqualification from the export refund regime. Where the evidence meets criminal thresholds, the Criminal Law provision on fraudulently obtaining export tax refunds applies, carrying fines of one to five times the defrauded amount and custodial penalties depending on the amount and aggravating circumstances.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Hong Kong operates as an offshore special territory with no exchange controls: the HKD is freely convertible with the USD through the linked exchange rate system, and all capital account transactions may pass through authorized banks without FOREX surrender. The mainland Chinese parent/FIP, however, is subject to SAFE's structured current-account and capital-account controls. Trade and service payments are largely current-account convertible, but capital-account transactions including FDI contributions and shareholder loans require SAFE registration and designated capital accounts. Profit remittances for an FIP are permitted once the partner contribution has been verified and the enterprise has completed tax filing (including corporate income tax and WHT on the non-resident partner's distributive profits), and require an official profit distribution resolution plus audit statements. There is no statutory surrender requirement in mainland China today; export proceeds may be retained in FX or converted to CNY at the bank's spot rate. The principal operative friction is the annual SAFE/BOP reporting and negative-list scrutiny, not convertibility.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE's goods-trade administration, a category-A exporter must repatriate export proceeds to its domestic foreign exchange account within 180 days from the date of the customs export declaration, unless the settlement is supported by an approved offshore retention agreement and properly reported through the bank's trade-monitoring system. Mandatory conversion ratios: 0% for category-A enterprises; the FIP may retain 100% of its export proceeds in foreign currency or convert all or part into RMB at the designated bank. Category-B and category-C enterprises face enhanced verification, shorter settlement windows, and documentary dossiers at SAFE/banks; any conversion requirement is applied on a case-by-case basis and never as an arbitrary fixed percentage.

Central bank supervision: Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA); the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region does not maintain a separate foreign exchange administration. For cross-border transactions crossing into mainland China, the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the People's Bank of China (PBOC) is the competent foreign exchange authority.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Rating: Conditional supplementary only; low viability as a primary treasury depository. An offshore fintech EMI is not legally qualified to serve as the principal SAFE-registered bank for the mainland FIP because it cannot hold a SAFE-designated foreign exchange capital account, cannot perform goods-trade verification, and is not a Chinese domestic deposit-taking institution. Such an EMI may lawfully support the structure only for offshore multi-currency payment collection from e-commerce marketplaces (including credit-card and Stripe rails) into a Hong Kong intermediate vehicle, provided it is authorized by the HKMA as a Stored Value Facility or Money Service Operator, and provided the funds are subsequently moved via SWIFT/CIPS into the onshore FIP bank under an accurately declared trade purpose. The EMI must never process RMB-denominated payments on behalf of the mainland entity or represent itself as a substitute for a licensed mainland bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Foreign investors remit from their own overseas bank accounts via SWIFT MT103 to the FIP's dedicated SAFE-registered capital account at a designated mainland bank. The beneficiary name on the wire must exactly match the SAFE foreign-exchange registration name; MT103 field 70 must state the SAFE FDI registration number and the payment purpose as FDI Capital Contribution - Establishment of Foreign-Invested Partnership. The mainland receiving bank verifies the inflow against the FDI registration, assigns the SAFE cross-border direct-investment transaction code, and credits the foreign-currency capital or settles to RMB within the capital account. Use of funds is restricted to the FIP's business operations and requires original expense vouchers; if the partnership agreement provides for paid-in capital, a Chinese certified public accountant must issue a capital verification report before dividends or capital account conversions are made.For raw-material import and component procurement, the FIP should use SWIFT MT103 for high-value direct remittances with commercial invoices, contracts and customs declarations attached. For cross-border purchases of AI chips, sensors, motors, and mechanical parts, an irrevocable commercial documentary letter of credit (MT700) payable at sight or term provides the strongest clearing rail; for smaller repeat shipments, documentary collections (D/P and D/A using MT400/MT412) are acceptable. RMB-denominated cross-border trade should be routed through CIPS (Cross-Border Interbank Payment System) or the CNH clearing rails in Hong Kong using CHATS; all such payments must carry the SAFE filing reference, tax registration number, and matching logistics evidence.Onshore CNY forward settlement and sale contracts (deliverable forwards) with maturity aligned to actual export receivables or import payables.CNY/HKD and CNY/USD currency swaps for managing rollover of forward positions and balance-sheet FX exposure from capital account balances.Offshore CNH deliverable forwards in Hong Kong for natural CNH income exposure.CNY non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) permitted in the offshore Hong Kong market only as an economic hedge against identifiable CNY exposure, never for speculative or uncovered trading.European-style foreign exchange options used for risk forbearance, subject to bank credit approval, premium payment, and documented underlying exposure.

Strike-off trigger

Mandatory Audit Threshold Mandatory Audit for All Entities

Turnover: Under Hong Kong's Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622) there is no balance-sheet, turnover or employee-headcount audit exemption for companies: every non-dormant company must appoint a practising CPA auditor and file audited financial statements. A Foreign-Invested Partnership (limited partnership) is not a company and is therefore not compulsorily audited by the Companies Ordinance. A mandatory independent CPA audit is triggered only when: (1) the Limited Partnership Agreement / fund constitutional document requires US-GAAP/IFRS audited accounts; (2) a licensed sectoral threshold is crossed (e.g. authorised money lender, insurer, securities dealer, travel agent, or other regulated ISIC 3240 supporting business whose licence conditions require audited accounts); (3) IRD demands audited financial statements under Inland Revenue Ordinance ss. 51C, 61A or 82; or (4) a bank/investor covenant with turnover above HK$10 million or total assets above HK$10 million is triggered. The universally cited Hong Kong professional threshold for an SME-style audit is approximately HK$10 million turnover, HK$10 million total assets or 50 employees, although this is a market benchmark, not a statutory exemption.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File mandatory if controlled transactions exceed: HKD 220M (goods), HKD 110M (services), HKD 55M (financing or intangibles), or HKD 22M (other transactions). Master File required if MNE group consolidated revenue reaches HKD 680M.

Economic substance scope: HK has no Economic Substance Regulations; however, section 61A GAAR and territorial-source doctrine require real decision-making and substantive operational activities in HK for profits to qualify as taxable or exempt. From 2023, IRD strips away artificial offshore claims lacking economic substance.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion (Tax Return Fraud)

Statutory sanction: Upon conviction in the District Court: fine up to HKD 50,000 plus 200% penalty on tax undercharged; also 2018 revised provisions impose imprisonment up to 5 years.

Director personal liability: General and limited partners are jointly and severally liable for the outstanding tax and penalties; tax officials may obtain civil restraint orders, freeze partnership assets, impose travel bans, and commit offenders to jail for the tax period.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Tax reassessment of the original profit plus penalty up to 10% of under-assessed tax for incorrect returns; additional 100% for gross negligence under section 82A IRO.

Director personal liability: IRD may disregard the partnership as a sham and assess partners directly; directors/partners sharing profits are personally liable for denied offshore claims, and the court may issue a personal recovery order and travel restriction without bail.
Transfer Pricing Non-disclosure

Statutory sanction: Adjusted arm's length profit; immediate recovery of underpaid tax plus compound interest; penalty of 100% on the tax shortfall for failure to keep sufficient records.

Director personal liability: The managing partner must sign the TP declaration; deliberate non-disclosure exposes the managing partner to personal liability for penalties and director disqualification; foreign partners cannot rely on corporate veil in HK.
AML / Money Laundering through Trade Payments

Statutory sanction: Under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Ordinance (Cap. 615), unlicensed money service operation or wilful non-compliance attracts a fine of HKD 5 million and imprisonment up to 7 years.

Director personal liability: Partners without adequate AML controls are subject to criminal liability and director disqualification; HKMA/Police can impose travel bans, seize partnership assets, and order civil restitution to victims.
