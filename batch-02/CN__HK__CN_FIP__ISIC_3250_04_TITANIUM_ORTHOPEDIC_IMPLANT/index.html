Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China, acting through the local STA branch for the Foreign-Invested Partnership's registered mainland address, with the unified electronic tax bureau platform at https://etax.chinatax.gov.cn; for the Hong Kong SAR component, the Inland Revenue Department (IRD) of the Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region administers profits tax and business registration through the IRD eTAX electronic tax service platform at https://www.ird.gov.hk.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Register the Foreign-Invested Partnership (FIP) with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) under the Foreign Investment Law and the Market Entity Registration Administration Regulations to obtain the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which is the primary PRC taxpayer identification number for VAT, Enterprise Income Tax, customs, and export tax rebate administration. Step 2: Under the integrated market and tax registration reform in China, the SAMR registration data is automatically transmitted to the STA; no separate paper tax registration certificate is issued, but the FIP must access e-Tax China within 30 days to complete taxpayer supplementary information, including industry classification, accounting standards, finance responsible officer, and VAT taxpayer type. Step 3: Apply through the e-Tax China VAT module for general VAT taxpayer status if annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000, or voluntarily elect general taxpayer status if below that threshold and the FIP has adequate accounting records. Step 4: Register with the General Administration of Customs (GACC) of the People's Republic of China through the China International Trade Single Window to obtain the Customs registration code required to lodge export declarations for titanium orthopedic implants. Step 5: For any Hong Kong SAR sales, treasury, or holding function, register with the IRD Business Registration Office within one month of commencing business to obtain the Business Registration Certificate and Business Registration Number; Hong Kong does not impose VAT/GST, so no separate Hong Kong VAT registration exists. Step 6: Non-resident partners are not required to obtain a separate PRC tax identifier solely because of their partnership interest, but a non-resident partner deriving PRC-source income or performing services in China through the FIP may require separate tax registration or a withholding registration through e-Tax China.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

No check-the-box election or hybrid mismatch election exists for income tax purposes under PRC law; the Foreign-Invested Partnership is mandatorily fiscally transparent for Enterprise Income Tax, with each partner taxed on its distributive share under the partner's residence and income classification. For VAT, the FIP may elect general VAT taxpayer status even when annual turnover is below the RMB 5,000,000 compulsory threshold, and such an election is generally irrevocable while the general taxpayer conditions continue to be satisfied. In Hong Kong SAR, partnership profits are also assessed on a pass-through basis under the Inland Revenue Ordinance, with the territorial source principle determining assessable profits; no entity-level election is available in Hong Kong.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 of taxable sales in any consecutive 12-month period, which triggers mandatory registration as a general VAT taxpayer; below RMB 5,000,000 the FIP may remain a small-scale VAT taxpayer unless it voluntarily elects general VAT taxpayer status. There is no turnover threshold for basic tax registration because all market entities must complete tax registration upon SAMR registration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under PRC VAT law, the export of titanium orthopedic implants qualifies for the 0% VAT export rate and eligible input VAT refund where: (1) the FIP is a registered VAT general taxpayer and has completed the export tax rebate registration/filing before the first export; (2) the goods are physically exported from the PRC customs territory, including export to Hong Kong SAR as a separate customs territory; (3) the export transaction is supported by a valid Export Goods Customs Declaration Form released by China Customs; (4) the FIP holds the export contract, commercial invoice, logistics documents, medical device export documentation, and foreign exchange receipt evidence; and (5) the export proceeds are received and repatriated through lawful banking channels and verified within the statutory 180-day foreign exchange repatriation deadline. Zero-rating will be denied if the goods are diverted into domestic sale, the customs declaration is false, or the foreign exchange evidence is absent or inconsistent.

  • Export Goods Customs Declaration Form (customs export declaration/bill of entry equivalent) bearing the release date and export declaration number
  • Negotiable ocean bill of lading or air waybill evidencing shipment from a Chinese port/airport to the overseas consignee
  • Commercial invoice, packing list, and signed export sales contract
  • Bank foreign-exchange receipt record / FIRC-equivalent export proceeds repatriation and settlement confirmation issued by the designated foreign-exchange bank
  • Export tax rebate declaration submitted through the e-Tax China export rebate module
  • Valid PRC medical device registration or market-entry approval and Medical Device Export Sales Certificate for the titanium orthopedic implant under the Regulations on the Supervision and Administration of Medical Devices
  • GACC Customs registration code or electronic registration confirmation evidencing the FIP as exporter of record

PRC VAT invoices must be issued through the STA's nationwide e-invoice platform or the VAT invoice administration system, depending on the taxpayer's approved mode. The FIP is expected to adopt fully digitalized electronic invoices, which are transmitted to STA's centralized database in near-real time. Special VAT invoices for taxable supplies must contain the statutory 20-digit digital invoice number, the goods or services tax classification code for titanium orthopedic implants, and a dynamic anti-counterfeit QR code. Enterprises with ERP systems may integrate via the official e-invoice API; web-based issuance is available for lower-volume taxpayers. There is no private invoice-clearing or third-party fiscal stamping authority; invoice validation, anti-fraud digital stamping, and fiscal reconciliation are controlled centrally by the STA through the Golden Tax III platform and cross-matching with China Customs data. For Hong Kong SAR, no e-invoicing clearance or VAT invoice mandate applies.

The STA and China Customs jointly audit export zero-rating and refund claims. Fraudulent or unsubstantiated exports will be recharacterized as domestic taxable supplies, resulting in clawback of the 0% VAT treatment and re-assessment of VAT at 13%. The taxpayer will be liable for the clawed-back input VAT, daily late-payment surcharges of 0.05% of the overdue amount, and administrative penalties of 50% to five times the amount of tax involved. The STA may suspend or cancel the FIP's export tax rebate filing status and export tax rebate qualification. In cases of proven fraud, the matter is referred for criminal prosecution under the Criminal Law export tax rebate fraud provisions, and tax collection is not time-barred. Partnership partners may also be jointly and severally liable to the extent of the falsely refunded amount, and cross-border bank payment data, customs data, and Golden Tax III matching systems will be used to support retrospective reassessment.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Hong Kong SAR operates a fully convertible current and capital account environment. The HK dollar is managed under a currency board linked to the US dollar in the 7.75 to 7.85 band, but the HKMA imposes no exchange controls and no distinction is made between resident and non-resident FX transactions. The FIP can therefore receive USD export proceeds, retain them in multi-currency accounts, convert to CNH or HKD, and remit distributable profits to non-resident partners without prior FX approval. Profit remittance friction is limited to tax and AML processes. If the FIP maintains a mainland manufacturing base, the PRC limb is different: current-account trade payments are convertible against genuine supporting documents, while capital injections, intercompany loans, and direct cross-border investments require SAFE or NDRC registration and opening of a designated capital account. 10% default PRC withholding tax on China-sourced dividends paid to a non-resident enterprise; where the FIP distributes profits to a Hong Kong corporate partner and the distribution is not effectively connected with a PRC PE, WHT applies at 10%, reducible to 5% under the China-Hong Kong DTA if the Hong Kong company is the beneficial owner and has directly held at least 25% of the capital in the payer.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File must be prepared if any related-party transaction category exceeds the statutory threshold in the prior fiscal year: tangible property transfers RMB 200 million, intangible property transfers RMB 100 million, financial asset transfers RMB 100 million, or other related-party transactions RMB 40 million; Master File is required when the aggregate amount of related-party transactions exceeds RMB 1 billion.

Economic substance scope: Under PRC law, the FIP must have substance in mainland China for its manufacturing, payroll, asset use, and decision-making; the Hong Kong partner must satisfy the China-Hong Kong DTA beneficial-ownership test and Hong Kong's Foreign-Source Income Exemption (FSIE) core income-generating activities (CIGA) requirement before any offshore passive income or treaty benefit is recognized; the PRC General Anti-Avoidance Rule (GAAR) under Enterprise Income Tax Law Article 47 can recharacterize or disregard an entity or arrangement lacking commercial substance.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and False CIT Filing

Statutory sanction: PRC Tax Collection Administration Law Article 63 imposes a penalty of 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax plus daily interest; PRC Criminal Law Article 201 criminalizes tax evasion, providing for criminal fines and imprisonment of up to seven years where the evaded tax is large and exceeds 10% of the tax payable.

Director personal liability: Directors/managing partners who caused or knowingly benefited from the evasion may be personally liable for the unpaid tax, penalties, and restitution of diverted assets; PRC exit-control rules permit travel bans/blacklisting of responsible persons; individual criminal convictions can lead to asset seizure, disqualification from director positions, and imprisonment.
Shell Company and Treaty Abuse

Statutory sanction: Under PRC Enterprise Income Tax Law Article 47 and the General Anti-Avoidance Rule, tax authorities may disregard the Hong Kong entity, recharacterize the transaction, deny treaty relief, and impose additional tax and interest; in Hong Kong, Inland Revenue Ordinance section 82 penalizes false statements and fraudulent claims with monetary penalties of up to 300% of the underassessed tax and criminal prosecution.

Director personal liability: Directors signing false residence or beneficial-ownership declarations face personal refund orders, confiscation of tax savings, travel bans, and extradition-risk blacklisting; Hong Kong law permits imprisonment for aiding false treaty declarations and fraudulent tax evasion; if the Hong Kong company is found to have no substance and is wound up, directors can incur unlimited personal civil liability.
Environmental Crimes and Hazardous Waste Disposal

Statutory sanction: PRC Environmental Protection Law Article 59 authorizes daily iteration fines of RMB 10,000 to RMB 1,000,000 for continuing violations; Article 64 requires full remediation and ecological-damage compensation; PRC Criminal Law Article 338 criminalizes serious environmental pollution, with fines and imprisonment of up to seven years.

Director personal liability: Directors, plant managers, and responsible shareholders who directed, authorized, or tolerated the unlawful disposal can be personally liable for remediation costs and civil damages; PRC authorities may freeze personal assets, impose travel bans and blacklisting, disqualify directors, and pursue criminal imprisonment under Article 338 for environmental pollution crimes.
