Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible; the PRC Foreign-Invested Partnership is transparent for PRC Enterprise Income Tax, with partners taxed directly, while any Hong Kong situs/offshore analysis follows the territorial source principle; the FIP is not an entity-level PRC income taxpayer.

Available election forms

None; neither PRC nor Hong Kong offers a U.S.-style check-the-box election, and no domestic hybrid-mismatch election is available for a Foreign-Invested Partnership. The pass-through classification is statutory and non-elective.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

PRC: mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration when annual taxable turnover exceeds RMB 5,000,000; below this threshold, small-scale taxpayer treatment at the reduced statutory levy rate applies unless general taxpayer status is voluntarily chosen for export refund purposes. Hong Kong: no VAT/GST registration threshold; business registration is required only under the Business Registration Ordinance when carrying on business in Hong Kong.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under PRC VAT law, the Color Doppler ultrasound console and associated medical diagnostic scanner goods qualify for VAT zero rate and refund/exemption of input VAT where: (1) the seller is a PRC VAT general taxpayer; (2) the goods are actually exported from China under a customs export declaration; (3) the export sales contract, invoice, bill of lading, and supporting transport records are consistent; and (4) the export proceeds are received and repatriated in accordance with SAFE foreign-exchange rules within the applicable time limit. The zero-rating/refund claim must be made by the exporting entity and cannot lawfully be used for domestic consumption or goods destined for restricted export.

  • Export Goods Customs Declaration Form released through the China International Trade Single Window
  • Original commercial invoice
  • Packing list
  • Clean onboard ocean bill of lading or air waybill
  • Export sales contract / purchase order
  • Bank inward remittance advice or Letter of Credit/TT wire transfer confirmation, together with the Foreign Exchange Receipt Voucher or SAFE export-collection confirmation
  • VAT special invoice or general VAT invoice applicable to the export sale
  • Export tax refund declaration / export VAT refund application filed through the STA electronic tax platform

PRC: VAT special invoices and fully digital electronic invoices are issued through the STA VAT Invoice Administration System and the State Taxation Administration Electronic Invoice Service Platform; invoice data must be transmitted to the tax authority in real time through approved API-integration channels, with electronic signatures replacing physical tax seals. Golden Tax Phase IV and the China International Trade Single Window cross-check customs export declarations, VAT invoice data, and foreign exchange receipts during export refund processing. Hong Kong: no statutory real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing, or digital-stamping mandate; IRD accepts normal commercial records.

If the customs export declaration, bill of lading, invoice, and foreign-exchange receipt cannot be reconciled by the STA under the Golden Tax Phase IV cross-department audit, the export VAT refund is denied and clawed back together with late-payment surcharge. Under Article 66 of the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection, fraudulent export-tax-refund claims attract recovery of the refunded amount, a fine of up to five times the refund obtained, revocation of the export-tax-refund qualification, and referral for criminal liability under Article 204 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China. Under Article 63, false export declarations or understated export sales may be re-characterized as tax evasion, triggering back taxes, surcharges, penalties, increased penalty multipliers for serious cases, and criminal exposure for the partnership and its responsible partners. For Hong Kong aspects, inaccurate sourcing or profits declarations can lead to additional assessments, penalties, and prosecution under the Inland Revenue Ordinance.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China's Mainland retains a managed floating rate regime with the RMB fully current-account convertible under PBOC and SAFE oversight, while capital account items remain subject to pre-registration, purpose-based supervision, and restricted convertibility. For the Foreign-Invested Partnership operating a phased-array medical ultrasound console export line, ordinary trade receipts are current account items, meaning export settlements, import payments, and service fees can flow through designated banks once authentic trade documents are filed in the goods trade settlement system. However, partner capital injections, debt financing, and cross-currency transactions require a capital account record, a valid foreign investment business registration, and a bank-level FX pre-review. Profit remittances to overseas partners are permitted if the partnership has completed corporate income tax settlement, presents audited financials, and obtains a tax clearance certificate; no mandatory holding period is imposed for genuine earnings. Mandatory surrender requirements were abolished in the 2012 reform, but all export proceeds must be repatriated to a domestic bank account within the statutory collection window and reported in the SAFE monitoring system, preventing arbitrary offshore retention. The Hong Kong jurisdiction element provides treasury flexibility for the offshore parent, but the onshore FIP must route every foreign exchange movement through a licensed mainland bank subject to PBOC liquidity controls and SAFE deal-level verification before any capital operation is cleared.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export proceeds must be collected within 180 days from the customs export declaration date, unless a written extension is granted under SAFE trade facilitation rules. Since 2012 there is no mandatory conversion ratio; the partnership may retain 100% of eligible export foreign exchange in a designated foreign exchange settlement account or convert it into RMB at the prevailing bank spot price. SAFE requires reporting through the Goods Trade Foreign Exchange Monitoring System, with each receipt matched to the original customs document, export contract, and commercial invoice.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) / State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: Low / Very Low. Under PBOC and SAFE rules, autonomous fintech EMIs such as Airwallex, Lianlian, or other digital money institutions cannot open onshore capital accounts, handle FDI capital injections, complete SAFE registrations, or verify export collection authenticity for a Foreign-Invested Partnership. Their limited licensing permits ancillary cross-border e-commerce receivables collection under specific pilots, but for treasury-grade capital clearance and regulatory reporting, the entity must maintain a primary relationship with a SAFE-authorized domestic commercial bank. Hong Kong-based EMIs may support the offshore parent only for pre-funding, invoice chasing, and cross-border float outside the PRC statutory bank framework; they are legally barred from serving as the main clearing rail for onshore capital account flows.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: HKD: Local File/contemporaneous documentation is generally triggered when cross-border controlled non-financial transactions exceed HKD 20 million, controlled financing transactions exceed HKD 10 million, or controlled domestic transactions exceed HKD 60 million; a Master File is expected when group consolidated turnover exceeds HKD 2.8 billion.

Economic substance scope: Hong Kong does not maintain a separate Economic Substance Regulations regime like the BVI/Cayman, but the source rule requires the core income-generating activities (CIGA), i.e., deciding, contracting, logistics, financing and sales support for the medical imaging business, to be performed in HK if profits are to be treated as HK-source and entitled to treaty benefits. A foreign-invested partnership must maintain local office, employees, decision-making records, and bank accounts; otherwise the IRD or foreign tax authorities can recharacterize the structure as a sham and deny treaty relief.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Tax Declaration

Statutory sanction: Inland Revenue Ordinance (Cap.112) section 82A: on conviction, a fine of HK$100,000, an additional penalty of up to three times the tax undercharged, and imprisonment for 3 years; section 82 penalizes incorrect returns with a level 3 fine (HK$10,000) plus threefold tax undercharge.

Director personal liability: Directors or general partners who sign or knowingly submit false returns are personally liable as principals in the offence; the court may order civil restitution of underpaid tax and costs; upon conviction, the director faces possible imprisonment of up to 3 years and automatic disqualification as a director under the Companies Ordinance; travel bans/blacklisting by the IRD may be ancillary in serious fraud cases.
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance / Artificial Intercompany Arrangement

Statutory sanction: IRO sections 50A, 50AA and the transfer pricing rules introduced by the Inland Revenue (Amendment) (No. 6) Ordinance 2022 empower the IRD to compute taxable profits as if transactions had been made at arm's length; failure to comply with a transfer pricing notice carries a fine on conviction at level 4 (HK$25,000) and daily penalties, while the additional tax undercharged may be recovered up to threefold.

Director personal liability: Directors who executed the non-arm's length contracts cannot shelter behind the company/partnership veil where fraud is shown; civil asset recovery can extend to the directors' personal bank accounts and property; a personal liability order may be made in a winding-up, and criminal conviction for tax evasion carries imprisonment; immigration/travel restrictions are routinely imposed when directors are under criminal investigation for TP fraud.
Shell Company Misuse / Beneficial Ownership and AML

Statutory sanction: Failure to maintain the Significant Controllers Register under the Companies Ordinance (Cap.622) is a Level 4 offence with fines up to HK$25,000 plus a daily default fine; breaches of the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Ordinance (Cap.615) customer due diligence and record-keeping duties can lead to a fine of HK$100,000 and imprisonment; on conviction of money laundering, the maximum term is 14 years and a fine of HK$5 million.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil will be pierced where the shell has no economic substance; directors/controllers are liable for civil asset restitution and disgorgement of profits, confiscation orders under the Organized and Serious Crimes Ordinance, cancellation/blacklisting of Hong Kong banking facilities, travel bans/airport watches, and imprisonment of up to 14 years for money laundering if proceeds of tax fraud pass through their accounts.
