Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A virtual office or commercial mailbox is generally permissible for the registered office address under the Limited Partnership Ordinance (Cap. 37), provided it is a physical address in Hong Kong where legal documents can be served. However, for a manufacturing entity engaged in the production of disposable dialysis hemofilters, the Inland Revenue Department and commercial banks will likely reject a virtual office for tax registration and bank account opening due to the requirement for substantive business presence. The Hong Kong Inland Revenue Ordinance (Cap. 112) requires a place of business with actual operations, and the HKMA's AML guidelines mandate that banks verify the physical location of the business. Therefore, a virtual office is not recommended; a physical office or factory premises is required to demonstrate commercial substance and to satisfy the economic substance requirements under the Inland Revenue (Amendment) (No. 6) Ordinance 2021.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None. A Hong Kong foreign-invested partnership has no statutory minimum paid-up capital under Cap. 37 or Cap. 310, and no blocked escrow or designated capital account rule applies. Capital contributions are contractually determined in the partnership deed and must be evidenced by a capital contribution schedule for AML/KYC and bank onboarding purposes.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Services Department approval for fire service installations, means of escape and fire-load compliance under the Fire Services Ordinance (Cap. 502); Dangerous Goods Licences under Cap. 295 for compressed gases, flammable solvents and ethylene oxide; Buildings Department structural load clearances for installation of industrial equipment; Labour Department occupational safety clearance under the Factories and Industrial Undertakings Ordinance (Cap. 59), including hazardous substance control, machine guarding, ventilation and noise control; issuance of a Certificate of Occupier’s Liability Insurance or employer’s compensation policy as a condition of factory operation.

The bank mandate shall include a full copy of this resolution, the partnership deed, the capital schedule, and the beneficial ownership declaration.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Inland Revenue Department (IRD) of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region Government; electronic services via GovHK e-Tax and Hong Kong Trade Single Window for customs/trade declarations.

E-government filing portal

The foreign-invested partnership must first be constituted and, if established as a limited partnership, registered with the Companies Registry under the Limited Partnerships Ordinance (Cap. 37) or the Limited Partnership Funds Ordinance (Cap. 637) where applicable. For tax purposes, the partnership or its authorized tax representative must then apply to the Business Registration Office of the IRD under the Business Registration Ordinance (Cap. 310) within one month of commencing business in Hong Kong. The application must state the partnership's principal place of business, nature of activities, partner details (names, addresses, tax residence and foreign tax IDs), and must attach the partnership deed and certified identity documents for non-resident partners. The IRD issues a Business Registration Certificate bearing the Business Registration Number (BRN), which is the statutory tax identification number for Profits Tax filings, employer returns and all IRD correspondence. No separate VAT ID exists because Hong Kong does not impose general VAT/GST. The BRN must be renewed annually, displayed at the Hong Kong place of business, and all changes must be reported to the IRD.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible; Hong Kong territorial-source principle also applies: the partnership itself is not subject to Profits Tax; each partner is taxed on his or her share of Hong Kong-source profits, while qualifying offshore profits are outside the charge unless receipt or activity in Hong Kong creates source.

Available election forms

None. No check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election is available under Hong Kong law; a partnership can, however, claim offshore-source treatment on the Profits Tax return and must support the claim with contracts, bills of lading and bank remittance evidence.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None (no VAT/GST/sales and use tax; only excise duty on four categories of dutiable commodities under the Dutiable Commodities Ordinance).

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No VAT/GST registration threshold applies; every business in Hong Kong must register for Business Registration under Cap. 310 irrespective of turnover. The BRN fee is nominal and is not a VAT-equivalent charge.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable. Hong Kong has no VAT/GST/sales tax, so there is no 0% export VAT rate, no zero-rating certificate, and no input VAT refund mechanism. Exports of disposable dialysis hemofilters are outside the scope of Hong Kong indirect tax. The seller should rely on the normal commercial invoice; the goods are not subject to customs duty unless they fall within the four dutiable commodity classes (they do not). For Profits Tax purposes, an offshore export profit claim must be substantiated with overseas contracts and evidence that title and risk pass outside Hong Kong.

  • Customs Export Declaration lodged through the Hong Kong Trade Single Window under the Import and Export (Registration) Regulations made under the Import and Export Ordinance (Cap. 60), containing the HS classification, quantity, value and destination of the dialysis hemofilter shipment;
  • Ocean bill of lading, air waybill, or equivalent multimodal transport document evidencing physical export from Hong Kong and delivery to the overseas consignee;
  • Foreign-exchange repatriation evidence in the form of a bank-issued SWIFT MT103/TT credit advice or inward remittance certificate (FIRC) confirming receipt of export proceeds from the overseas buyer.

None. No real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing government approval, API clearance, or anti-fraud digital stamping exists. Commercial invoices are self-issued. Records must be retained for seven years under Section 51C of the Inland Revenue Ordinance. Import/export declarations are filed electronically via the Trade Single Window within fourteen days after importation/exportation.

No VAT clawback applies because no VAT/zero-rating is levied. Substantiation failures are nevertheless critical: an unsupported offshore profits claim can be reversed by the IRD as Hong Kong-source, with additional assessments and penalties. Under the Inland Revenue Ordinance, the penalty may be up to treble the tax undercharged; false export declarations to Hong Kong Customs attract fine and imprisonment under the Import and Export (Registration) Regulations; and failure to retain the required shipping, contract and bank records for seven years undermines the entire export evidence file. The structured 180-day FX documentation deadline is an internal compliance safeguard, not a Hong Kong VAT statute deadline.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The mainland-registered Foreign-Invested Partnership (CN_FIP), notwithstanding its Hong Kong investor jurisdiction, is an onshore PRC entity and therefore sits within China's administered floating exchange-rate and capital-control perimeter. The Renminbi remains not freely convertible on the capital account; the current account is fully convertible subject to genuine-transaction verification. Capital contributions from the Hong Kong partner must be routed through a SAFE-registered capital account, reported under FDI information registration, and may not be borrowed, invested, or transferred offshore without separate approval. Export revenue from dialysis hemofilter sales is current-account income and can be settled into RMB at a designated bank against commercial documents, but the company remains obliged to repatriate the foreign currency into China and cannot park proceeds in offshore accounts. Profit and partnership distributions face de facto convertibility hurdles: the FIP must first complete annual audit, tax payment or withholding, SAFE income verification, and a bank-supplied remittance form; dividends or partnership returns require proof that the underlying capital was lawfully registered and that no outstanding external-debt or foreign-exchange positions exist. Consequently, treasury planning must integrate SAFE registration, purpose-code discipline, and licensed-bank execution before any cross-border movement.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE's Goods Trade Foreign Exchange Administration, export proceeds from the medical-device business must be repatriated into a domestic current-account foreign-exchange bank account within 90 days of the customs export declaration, unless a deferred-payment arrangement was filed with SAFE before the due date; open-account terms longer than 90 days require supporting contracts and an electronic filing. The mandatory conversion ratio is zero: the FIP may keep up to 100 percent of export proceeds as foreign currency within the approved current-account settlement quota or fully settle into RMB at a designated tier-1 bank. Any foreign exchange above the bank-assessed retention ceiling must be converted into RMB promptly. All remittances must be matched against Customs declarations, commercial invoices, bills of lading, and the SAFE receiving-payment verification system.

Central bank supervision: The State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China, discharging delegated authority from the People's Bank of China (PBOC), with the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) as the CNH clearing and currency-board supervisor for Hong Kong SAR

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low, not viable as principal banking or clearing rail. An offshore digital EMI is not a licensed domestic settlement institution under the PBOC Administrative Measures for Payment Services and cannot open a SAFE-registered capital account, conduct FDI registration, receive export proceeds, or settle trade on behalf of a mainland FIP; it is legally barred from servicing the entity as primary bank. At best, such an EMI can support ancillary named-beneficiary payments only after onshore bank clearing has occurred through CIPS or SWIFT; using it earlier would break SAFE verification and impair capital-account compliance.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All Hong Kong investor capital contributions must be remitted by SWIFT MT103 from the named Hong Kong partner's own bank account to the FIP's SAFE-registered foreign-currency capital account at a tier-1 domestic bank. The instruction should state 'Foreign Direct Investment Equity Capital Contribution to CN_FIP' and the receiving bank must book the transaction under the SAFE Balance-of-Payments direct-investment equity-capital code; using a generic 'other remittance' purpose code will suspend clearing. The required pack includes the FDI Foreign Exchange Registration Form, the FIP partnership agreement, a capital contribution schedule, and an anti-funnel declaration. After credit, a licensed Chinese CPA must perform capital verification and file the Capital Verification Report with the Administration for Market Regulation and SAFE; funds may not be moved to the operating account until the capital-account cross-check is completed and a lawful domestic use plan is established.Outward payments for raw materials, polyethersulfone resin, hollow-fiber spinning consumables, filtration equipment, and technical services should use SWIFT MT103 for direct telegraphic-transfer settlement after the bank matches invoice, contract, Customs declaration, and payment order. RMB-denominated trade with Hong Kong and ASEAN counterparties should be settled through CIPS via a CIPS participant bank, preserving SWIFT interoperability. Mandatory Audit Threshold Mandatory Audit for All Entities

Turnover: Under Part 9 of the Hong Kong Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622), every Hong Kong incorporated company is required to appoint a certified public accountant auditor, and there is no small-company audit exemption based on balance-sheet size, turnover, or headcount. A private company is exempt from audit only if it is dormant and has no relevant accounting transactions after filing the necessary dormancy declaration. A Hong Kong foreign-invested partnership, as a pass-through non-corporate entity, is not itself subject to Companies Ordinance audit thresholds; however, an independent certified audit becomes legally or contractually mandatory if the general partner is a Hong Kong company, if the partnership is required to produce audited financial statements under its medical device licence application, or if a licensed lending or guarantee arrangement imposes audited reporting. The Inland Revenue Ordinance separately requires every business to keep sufficient accounting records for seven years and to submit with the BIR52 partnership return a certified profit and loss account, balance sheet, and tax computation for each partner.
