Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA) using the national electronic tax service platform (Electronic Tax Bureau / e-Tax portal); for the Hong Kong SAR segment, the Inland Revenue Department of Hong Kong SAR (IRD) using the eTAX electronic filing portal remains separately competent, but the mainland-registered Foreign-Invested Partnership files its primary registration with the STA.

E-government filing portal

Step 2: File the initial tax registration through the STA Electronic Tax Bureau / e-Tax portal within 30 days of the business licence date, submitting the partnership agreement, foreign investor identity documents, registered address proof, and expected business scope. Step 3: Elect VAT taxpayer status in the e-Tax system; apply for general VAT-taxpayer status if required by scale or to support export zero-rating and input-VAT refunds. Step 4: If a foreign non-resident partner is not separately tax-registered in mainland China, obtain a non-resident counterparty registration / withholding reference number through the e-Tax platform for withholding income tax on China-source income; this is separate from the FIP TIN. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible; under PRC partnership tax rules, the FIP is transparent for Enterprise Income Tax and does not pay entity-level EIT; each corporate or individual partner is taxed on its distributive share, while VAT applies at the FIP level on taxable mainland supplies.

Available election forms

None; PRC domestic law has no check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch elections for a Foreign-Invested Partnership; transparent classification is mandatory, and partners may separately claim foreign tax credits or Mainland-Hong Kong double taxation arrangement relief.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 (five million Chinese Yuan) of annual aggregate taxable sales is the mandatory turnover threshold for general VAT taxpayer registration; the FIP must still obtain its Unified Social Credit Code/TIN upon incorporation irrespective of turnover, and may apply to become a general VAT taxpayer voluntarily to support zero-rated export refunds.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is available where the FIP is a registered general VAT taxpayer and exports dried goji berries (Lycium) or herbal botanicals physically out of mainland China customs territory to an offshore buyer, including goods exported to Hong Kong SAR for re-export or onward international sale, and the export is evidenced by a Chinese Customs Export Declaration, commercial invoice, transport documentation, and foreign-currency payment receipt; the goods must leave mainland customs control and be sold in export trade; offshore services are not in this entity's scope.

  • Chinese Customs Export Declaration (customs clearance declaration for export goods)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical exit from mainland China customs territory
  • Export sales contract and commercial invoice
  • Packing list
  • Bank-generated foreign exchange receipt / foreign exchange income declaration record evidencing repatriation and settlement of the export proceeds
  • VAT invoice for the export sale as issued through the fully digitalised e-invoice system
  • Export tax rebate/zero-rating application filed through the STA Electronic Tax Bureau / e-Tax portal and linked to the China Customs Single Window declaration
  • Certificate of Origin and phytosanitary/health certification for dried goji berries/herbal products where required by the importing jurisdiction
  • Hong Kong import/export documentation (such as Hong Kong Customs import/export declaration or movement permit) where the goods are transshipped through Hong Kong

The FIP must issue all VAT invoices through the State Taxation Administration's fully digitalised electronic invoice service platform under Golden Tax Phase IV; invoice data, sales data, and VAT returns are transmitted to the tax authority in real time; every invoice is digitally stamped with a tax-control seal and anti-counterfeiting QR code before issue; high-volume exporters use mandatory system-to-system integration with the STA platform and China Customs Single Window to match export declarations, VAT invoices, and foreign-exchange receipts.

China Customs and the STA jointly audit export zero-rating and refund claims by cross-matching customs declarations, e-invoices, VAT returns, and foreign-exchange receipts under Golden Tax Phase IV. If export zero-rating is obtained or claimed on the basis of false declarations, circular exports, fabricated transport documents, unverified payments, or failure to repatriate and settle export proceeds within the prescribed 180-day foreign-exchange collection period, the tax authority will disallow the claim, claw back the VAT exemption or refund, impose late-payment surcharges, and apply penalties under the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection and tax evasion rules ranging from 50 percent to 500 percent of the improper tax amount; fraudulent export-rebate conduct may also be prosecuted under the PRC Criminal Law as export tax rebate fraud, with loss of export-rebate status and potential custody/property penalties.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Hong Kong Special Administrative Region operates a fully convertible HKD and imposes no distinction between current-account and capital-account convertibility for treasury transactions; all legitimate FX transactions may be executed at market-determined rates under the Linked Exchange Rate System. For the underlying PRC Foreign-Invested Partnership, current-account items such as cross-border trade payment and service fees are broadly convertible after bank verification, while capital-account inflows such as paid-in capital from the Hong Kong limited partner require Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) registration with SAFE and a designated capital account. Profit distribution and dividend/partnership-profit remittances from the PRC FIP to Hong Kong limited partners require a certified audit report, tax clearance certificate, and bank-level proof that the distributed profits were generated by the registered PRC business. Although the Hong Kong layer faces no surrender or repatriation restrictions, the onshore leg still faces practical hurdles because banks apply substance-over-form safeguards and simultaneous customs and tax matching for dried lycium/herbal wholesale exports. Overall, the route is workable, but the capital account approvals and FX purpose-code integrity must be independently documented to avoid anti-circumvention sanctions under SAFE Notice 2019-28.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the current SAFE foreign-exchange framework, there is no mandatory conversion ratio; the exporter may retain up to 100% of foreign currency receipts in a foreign-exchange settlement account or convert them into RMB at the prevailing market rate. Export proceeds from dried goji berries and herbal botanicals must be repatriated into China through the designated receiving bank and be included in the trade-monitoring system. The bank must complete verification within 90 days after the customs export declaration; if collection occurs more than 90 days after shipment, the exporter must provide a written delay explanation and supporting evidence such as transport documents, buyer payment undertaking, or bank reference. In the Hong Kong treasury layer, no statutory surrender requirement or conversion ratio applies.

Central bank supervision: Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) for the Hong Kong SAR treasury layer, together with the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the People's Bank of China for the mainland China Foreign-Invested Partnership (FIP) layer

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Fintech EMI cross-border viability is Low-to-Medium for the Hong Kong treasury layer and Low for the PRC FIP layer. HKMA-licensed virtual banks and stored value facilities are legally able to provide transaction accounts to Hong Kong-incorporated vehicles, but they generally cannot open a SAFE-registered capital account or render commercial documentary trade services for a mainland Foreign-Invested Partnership. Offshore EMIs such as Airwallex, Currencycloud, and Revolut Business are not licensed deposit-taking institutions in mainland China and are legally barred from acting as the primary treasury rail for the onshore FIP; they may only serve as ancillary collection dashboards for small remote receivables once the primary licensed bank spine is active. The prudent design is to keep all capital account, FX conversion, LC, and documentary collection activity inside Tier-1 licensed banks and use any HKMA-authorised EMI strictly as a downstream e-commerce collection overlay.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The inbound capital injection from the Hong Kong limited partner to the PRC FIP must be executed as a SWIFT MT103 wire into the FIP's designated SAFE-registered capital account at a licensed designated bank. The remittance instruction must state the exact corporate name of the FIP as beneficiary and include the FX transaction purpose code 622011 for paid-in capital of a foreign-invested enterprise. The Hong Kong remitting entity must ensure the SWIFT sender name matches the registered company certificate, and the FIP must complete Foreign Investment Information Reporting through the AMEIZ-style integrated investment management system before the wire arrives. After funds are credited, the receiving bank issues an inbound capital account confirmation and performs capital verification against the partnership's capital contribution schedule, the signed partnership agreement, and the notarised registration certificate. If the capital is retained as foreign currency, it may be deposited into a foreign currency capital account; if RMB is required for operating expenses, the bank will conduct a lawful capital settlement transaction and monitor the funds under SAFE anti-circumvention scrutiny.Outbound trade disbursements for the dried goji berries and herbal botanicals business should use a layered clearing approach. For direct sales to overseas buyers, SWIFT MT103 is the primary rail for settlement of export proceeds identified against a commercial invoice and customs declaration.
