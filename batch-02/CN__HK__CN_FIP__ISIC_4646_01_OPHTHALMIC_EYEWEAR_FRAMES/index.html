Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The People's Republic of China maintains a bifurcated convertibility framework. The current account is fully convertible under IMF Article VIII, allowing free settlement of trade in goods and services, but every transaction passes through bank-side authenticity verification under SAFE's Trade in Goods Foreign Exchange Administration Guidelines and the accompanying list-based management of exempt, verified, and prohibited trade categories. The capital account remains strictly regulated: a Foreign-Invested Partnership (FIP) must complete foreign exchange registration with SAFE through its designated bank, open a dedicated FDI capital account for partner capital injections, and may convert foreign currency into renminbi only under the discretionary settlement policy, with the resulting RMB held in a settlement-pending payment account and deployed solely for genuine registered business purposes. Profit remittance to non-resident partners is permitted on the current account but requires audited financial statements, corporate income tax clearance certificates including withholding tax settlement at the partner level, a partner resolution declaring distribution, and a bank authenticity review, all of which typically complete in five to ten business days. There is no statutory remittance quota, yet the bank must verify that cumulative distributions do not exceed accumulated distributable profits net of required reserves. The renminbi operates under a managed floating exchange rate regime with the daily USD/CNY trading band set at plus or minus two percent around the central parity published by the China Foreign Exchange Trade System.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export proceeds from the wholesale distribution of ophthalmic eyewear frames must be repatriated onshore into the FIP's current-account foreign exchange account; there is no mandatory conversion ratio since the 2012 SAFE reform, allowing the partnership to retain one hundred percent of export receipts in foreign currency. Repatriation should normally occur within 90 days from the export customs declaration date. Advance receipts exceeding 30 days or deferred receipts beyond 90 days under open-account terms trigger obligatory reporting in the SAFE trade monitoring system; failure to report or repatriate downgrades the entity's trade credit classification from A to B or C, which in turn escalates document-to-document verification, increases examination ratios, and may suspend preferential foreign exchange settlement channels.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the People's Bank of China (PBOC), with the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) as the offshore clearing counterparty for the Hong Kong SAR structuring node

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low, with narrow exceptions. Offshore digital EMI platforms such as Wise, Revolut Business, and Airwallex do not hold deposit-taking banking licenses in mainland China and are legally barred from maintaining onshore renminbi or foreign currency accounts for a Chinese-domiciled FIP. They also cannot execute mandatory SAFE trade monitoring filings or issue compliant capital account receipts. A strictly limited alternative exists for e-commerce tail collections: a SAFE-licensed Chinese payment institution (e.g., LianLian Global or PingPong) may settle cross-border platform-pay proceeds, but only after the FIP has established an onshore bank account and registered its trade-in-goods baseline with SAFE. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Partner capital is wired from the Hong Kong treasury node to the FIP's dedicated FDI capital account at the designated onshore bank, citing SAFE transaction code 622011 (Foreign Direct Investment Paid-in Capital) and the entity's foreign exchange registration certificate number in the remittance narrative. The receiving bank verifies the inflow against the registered capital and the filed FDI information, posts the entry into the capital account, and releases funds under the discretionary settlement policy into a settlement-pending payment account in renminbi for genuine business uses such as raw-material procurement from mainland acetate frame suppliers, warehouse leasing, and staff payroll. A certified public accountant may be engaged to issue a capital verification report, which the bank may require before activating the operational current account for trade collections.International disbursements for the eyewear-frame wholesale book run through three rails: (1) SWIFT MT103 telegraphic transfers for urgent supplier payments and small-value top-ups, with each instruction supported by the underlying commercial invoice, purchase order, and customs declaration to satisfy bank authenticity review; (2) documentary collections (documents against payment or acceptance) for medium-value shipments to emerging-market buyers where a letter of credit is over-engineered; and (3) commercial letters of credit, both at-sight and usance, issued by the designated bank for large wholesale orders, with the FIP's export bills negotiated and discounted to optimize working capital. Cross-border renminbi clearing via the Hong Kong offshore CNH pool is available for mainland supplier settlements, with the HKMA's real-time gross settlement system serving as the offshore clearing rails. Turnover: Hong Kong partnerships are not automatically required to produce audited financial statements merely for being a partnership. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% statutory withholding tax on dividends paid to non-resident corporate shareholders; under the China-Hong Kong Double Tax Arrangement, the rate is reduced to 5% when the beneficial owner is a Hong Kong resident enterprise holding at least 25% of the capital of the Chinese payer. For FIP distributions, profits not distributed are not subject to dividend WHT; once distributed to a non-resident partner, the payment is treated as a profit share and may be taxed under the PE rule or, to the extent recharacterized as a dividend, at the 10% statutory rate unless treaty relief is obtained.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Under SAT Bulletin [2016] No. 42, a Local File is mandatory when annual related-party transactions with a domestic or foreign affiliate exceed: (i) RMB 200 million for tangible property transfers; (ii) RMB 100 million for intangible property; or (iii) RMB 40 million for services. A Master File is required when aggregate annual related-party transactions exceed RMB 1 billion or the ultimate parent of the multinational group is obligated to prepare a Master File in its jurisdiction.

Economic substance scope: China's substance-over-form rule under Art. 47 of TCAL and Art. 120 of the CIT Law allows tax authorities to recharacterize artificial arrangements lacking commercial substance. The Hong Kong partner relying on the DTA must demonstrate economic substance through actual office premises, employees, decisions made in Hong Kong, and meaningful management of risk, consistent with IRD Departmental Interpretation Practice Notes (DIPN) No. 60 and the Core Income-Generating Activities (CIGA) analysis. If the Hong Kong company's CIGA are all performed in mainland China, treaty benefits will be denied and the shell structure may be ignored.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion by Fabricated Intercompany Service Agreements

Statutory sanction: TCAL Art. 63 imposes a penalty of 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax, plus restitution and daily surcharge. If the evaded tax exceeds RMB 50,000 and constitutes 10% of the tax payable, Art. 201 of the Criminal Law applies, punishable by imprisonment of up to 7 years and a fine.

Director personal liability: Under the Partnership Enterprise Law Art. 106 and Civil Code Art. 83, the corporate veil may be pierced to hold directors and actual managers civilly liable for the unpaid tax and damages. The involved individuals may be placed on the tax blacklist, face a travel ban, prohibition on high consumption, freezing of personal assets, and criminal imprisonment if they knowingly directed or approved the fabrication.
Treaty Abuse – Shell Hong Kong Company Claiming DTA Relief

Statutory sanction: China's GAAR under CIT Law Art. 47 and Art. 120 of the Implementation Regulations permits the tax authority to disregard the Hong Kong structure, deny treaty benefits, re-source the income to China, and apply full 10% WHT plus interest and penalties under TCAL Art. 48. Hong Kong IRD may also refuse the tax residency certificate under DIPN 60 if the company lacks substance.

Director personal liability: Directors of the shell Hong Kong company face disqualification under Hong Kong Companies Ordinance s.168. In mainland China, the persons controlling the scheme are jointly liable for the unpaid tax under Civil Code Art. 83; they may be blacklisted as serious tax violators, denied exit from China, and prosecuted for tax evasion under Criminal Law Art. 201 if the scheme intentionally concealed taxable income.
Failure to File Transfer Pricing Documentation and Related-Party Transaction Schedule

Statutory sanction: TCAL Art. 62 imposes a fine of up to RMB 10,000 for failure to file statutory schedules; under SAT Bulletin [2016] No. 42, the tax authority may disregard the taxpayer's pricing and apply a deemed profit adjustment. If the omission is found to be intentional concealment, TCAL Art. 63 applies, with a penalty of 50% to 500% of the adjusted tax and possible criminal liability for tax evasion.

Director personal liability: Directors and partnership managers who knowingly fail to file documentation are personally liable under the partnership agreement and Civil Code Art. 83 for the resulting tax shortfall and penalties; they may be added to the non-compliant taxpayer blacklist, have their passports cancelled, face restrictions on company formation, and, if criminal intent is proven, are liable for imprisonment under Criminal Law Art. 201.
