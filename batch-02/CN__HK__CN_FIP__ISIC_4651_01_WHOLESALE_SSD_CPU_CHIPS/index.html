Stage 03
Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

      
Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

    

    

      
Tax ID Application Infrastructure

      

        
Competent tax authority
State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA); online portal: STA Electronic Taxpayer Bureau (e-Tax) through the official STA e-tax websites. For Hong Kong SAR territorial nexus, the Inland Revenue Department (IRD) is also competent, with the IRD eTAX online portal, when the FIP carries on business in Hong Kong.

        
E-government filing portal
After formation of the foreign-invested partnership (FIP), the FIP obtains a Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) from the competent Administration for Market Regulation; the USCC is the PRC statutory taxpayer identification number for VAT and income tax filing. Within 30 calendar days of the business license, the FIP must complete tax registration on the STA e-Tax portal, filing the partnership deed, partner list with foreign tax identification numbers, registered address, bank account details, expected taxable turnover, VAT classification application, and export tax refund eligibility data. The FIP then applies for VAT taxpayer qualification: small-scale taxpayer status is available while annual taxable sales are at or below RMB 5,000,000, and general VAT taxpayer registration is mandatory above that threshold. For export trade, the FIP must maintain its customs exporter registration and complete the STA export-tax-refund filing profile; non-resident foreign partners generally do not receive a separate PRC TIN unless they derive PRC-source income, in which case a withholding/non-resident registration record is created with the partner's home-country TIN. If a HK branch or HK business is established, the FIP must separately apply to the IRD for a Business Registration certificate under the Business Registration Ordinance using the eTAX portal.

      

      
Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible. The FIP is mandatorily fiscally transparent for PRC Enterprise Income Tax: tax is imposed on each partner's distributive share rather than at the partnership level. Corporate partners are taxed at the PRC 25% EIT rate; individual partners are taxed on business income under PRC Individual Income Tax progressive rates (5%-35%). For PRC VAT, the FIP is a separate VAT taxpayer and not pass-through. For Hong Kong Profits Tax territoriality, profits are assessed on the partners only if the profit source is in Hong Kong; genuine offshore trading profits outside Hong Kong are generally not chargeable to Profits Tax, subject to IRD source rules. — After formation of the foreign-invested partnership (FIP), the FIP obtains a Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) from the competent Administration for Market Regulation; the USCC is the PRC statutory taxpayer identification number for VAT and income tax filing. Default Tax Classification & Elections

      

        
Default tax classification
Pass_Through_Eligible. The FIP is mandatorily fiscally transparent for PRC Enterprise Income Tax: tax is imposed on each partner's distributive share rather than at the partnership level. Corporate partners are taxed at the PRC 25% EIT rate; individual partners are taxed on business income under PRC Individual Income Tax progressive rates (5%-35%). For PRC VAT, the FIP is a separate VAT taxpayer and not pass-through. For Hong Kong Profits Tax territoriality, profits are assessed on the partners only if the profit source is in Hong Kong; genuine offshore trading profits outside Hong Kong are generally not chargeable to Profits Tax, subject to IRD source rules.

        
Available election forms
None for partnership pass-through; PRC law does not provide a check-the-box election for partnerships or hybrid-mismatch entity elections. The only relevant election is for PRC VAT: while annual taxable sales remain at or below RMB 5,000,000, the FIP may elect small-scale taxpayer treatment; it may also elect general VAT taxpayer treatment to reclaim input VAT, and general taxpayer treatment becomes compulsory once the RMB 5,000,000 annual threshold is exceeded. Hong Kong does not provide a check-the-box election; a partnership is transparent by statute for Profits Tax.

      

    

    

      
VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

      

        
Standard VAT / GST rate
13%

        
Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)
RMB 5,000,000 annual taxable turnover for mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration; all PRC entities with taxable sales must complete tax registration upon formation regardless of turnover, and classification as a small-scale VAT taxpayer is automatic only below the RMB 5,000,000 threshold.

      

      
      

        Export 0% zero-rating conditions
        
The FIP's wholesale export of solid-state drives and server processors is eligible for the PRC 0% VAT export zero rate when: (i) the FIP is a general VAT taxpayer with an export refund file; (ii) goods are physically exported from mainland China through a PRC customs clearance point, including shipments to Hong Kong SAR as a separate customs territory; (iii) there is a genuine foreign buyer and an export contract with title transfer outside mainland China; (iv) the customs export declaration data is electronically transmitted to the STA; and (v) export proceeds are repatriated by the foreign buyer to a PRC-designated bank account within the statutory foreign-exchange receipt period. Qualifying offshore digital services are not within this FIP's goods-wholesale scope.

General VAT taxpayers must issue special VAT invoices and electronic invoices through the STA Golden Tax Phase IV fully digital e-invoice platform, with real-time encrypted invoice data transmission, anti-counterfeit invoice coding, and automatic cross-checking of issuance records against VAT returns, customs export declarations, logistics data and foreign-exchange receipts. Tax control equipment or digital U-Key authentication is mandatory for invoice issuance; unauthorized self-invoicing or invoice fabrication is subject to cancellation, penalties and clawback of any VAT refund claimed.

      
The STA and Customs jointly audit export zero-rating files through the Golden Tax export-rebate cross-check system. If the foreign exchange receipt is not obtained within 180 days after export declaration, or if the customs documentation bundle is incomplete or inconsistent, the export is transferred out of zero-rating and assessed at 13% VAT with late-payment surcharge at 0.05% per day and recovery of any refund previously paid. A fraudulent or false export zero-rating claim (including forged customs or logistics documents, false FX receipts, or artificial round-tripping of funds) triggers retrospective clawback of the full refund, administrative fines of up to five times the refunded amount and/or the tax underpaid, revocation or suspension of export-tax-refund eligibility, and criminal prosecution for tax fraud against the FIP and responsible partners, with cross-border enforcement cooperation and anti-money-laundering reporting to the competent authorities.

Stage 04
Banking & Treasury Architecture

      
Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

    

    

      
Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

      

        
Current-account convertibility
Hong Kong operates under a fully convertible currency regime anchored by the Linked Exchange Rate System, with the HKD trading in a 7.75 to 7.85 band against the USD. The HKMA as the central banking and foreign exchange authority imposes no current account or capital account restrictions, no mandatory conversion obligations, and no profit remittance permits for entities lawfully resident in Hong Kong. A Hong Kong FIP engaged in wholesale brokerage of SSDs and server processors can therefore invoice in USD, HKD, CNH, or any freely accepted currency, retain export proceeds abroad, and remit distributable profits to partners without seeking HKMA permission. The only practical friction arises when the ultimate investor chain touches mainland China: in that case, SAFE regulations govern the Chinese resident's outbound investment, including ODI filing, QDLP quotas, and the annual repatriation and settlement cycle for Chinese-funded export receipts. The HKMA oversees banks' liquidity, currency board operations, and transaction reporting, but it does not ration foreign exchange. Thus the currency convertibility risk is limited to the Mainland-HK interface, not the HK entity itself, and the treasury should focus on maintaining robust documentary evidence for both HKMA and SAFE if the capital flows are routed through China.

        
Export proceeds surrender rules
No statutory export proceeds surrender rule exists in Hong Kong SAR. There is no mandatory conversion ratio, no specified repatriation window, and no HKMA directive requiring an FIP to exchange foreign currency receipts into HKD. Export earnings in USD, CNH, or other currencies may be retained in the operating accounts indefinitely. However, if a mainland China affiliate is the exporter of record, SAFE's foreign exchange settlement rules require that affiliate to repatriate and settle export proceeds within the standard banking settlement period after customs validation unless a valid retention approval is obtained; that obligation does not attach to the Hong Kong FIP.

      

      
Central bank supervision: Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA); the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) is the primary supervisor only for capital flows originating from or returning to mainland China.

    

    

      
Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

      

        
        

          

            Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails
            Fit: High
          

          
Low-to-Medium for the primary treasury function, with significant legal constraints. Hong Kong does not recognise the European EMI licence class; an offshore fintech EMI that lacks authorisation under the Banking Ordinance cannot accept deposits or maintain a full corporate settlement account for the FIP. For money transmission and payment collection, such an entity must hold a Money Service Operator (MSO) licence under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Ordinance, and even then it cannot provide deposit-taking, capital escrow, FX hedging, or trade finance services. For a Medium_Risk_Enhanced semiconductor wholesale partnership, reliance on an overseas EMI would expose the treasury to licensing risk, sanctions screening gaps, and undue concentration in non-regulated rails. Licensed Hong Kong virtual banks can act as supplementary settlement accounts, but they are not viable substitutes for tier-1 clearing or escrow arrangements. The recommendation is to use fintech rails only for low-value disbursements and to maintain the primary capital and FX flows through tier-1 licensed institutions.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

      

        Capital injections from non-Chinese partners should be sent via SWIFT MT103 directly to the dedicated USD/HKD capital escrow account at the tier-1 bank, with the remittance narrative referencing the partnership deed and the specific capital contribution tranche; no HKMA approval is required, and the receiving bank will request a capital verification report from a Hong Kong certified public accountant to confirm the subscription schedule. For capital originating from mainland China, the Chinese investing partner must first complete SAFE ODI or QDLP approval, remit funds through its designated Chinese bank using the approved investment purpose code, and ensure the MT103 carries the ODI record number and underlying partnership contract reference. The Hong Kong bank will treat the credit as a capital transaction, complete HKMA transaction reporting, and apply enhanced beneficial-ownership screening. In all cases, the escrow account is released only after the general partner provides the partnership capital contribution certificate and the commercial bank confirms that no frozen or sanctioned parties appear in the final investor chain.Outbound trade payments to SSD and CPU suppliers will be routed through SWIFT MT103 for open-account settlements under standard purchase orders; for orders above a set threshold, the FIP will issue an irrevocable documentary letter of credit (MT700) governed by UCP600, with the supplier required to present transport documents, inspection certificates, and commercial invoices at a confirming bank. The bank may also process documentary collections under URC522 for inspection escrow releases, but the recommended rail for high-value spot semiconductor trades is a cash-secured LC or an escrow-instructed MT103 to avoid title and quality disputes. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

      
Master / Local File threshold: Contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation (Local File) is mandatory if the annual related-party transactions involving tangible goods such as SSD/CPU chips exceed RMB 200 million; intangible-property transactions exceed RMB 100 million; financial-asset transactions exceed RMB 100 million; or other related-party transactions exceed RMB 40 million, per SAT Public Notice [2016] No. 42. A Master File is also required when ultimate-parent group consolidated revenue exceeds RMB 5 billion and cross-border related-party transactions reach the filing threshold.

      
Economic substance scope: Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) and CIGA (Core Income-Generating Activities) tests, as applied in Hong Kong and China's general anti-avoidance rules, require the entity claiming treaty benefits or offshore margins to manage core business functions at its claimed residence location: negotiating contracts, maintaining inventory, billing, accepting commercial risk, keeping bank accounts, and employing senior staff with decision-making power. A shell FIP with no employees or premises but controlling chip inventories and sales would be recharacterized by the Chinese tax authority, causing treaty-relief denial, immediate 10% WHT, and possible criminal referral.

      

    

    

      
Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

      
      

        Transfer Pricing Manipulation / Tax Evasion
        
Statutory sanction: Article 41 and Article 47 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law empower the State Taxation Administration to make Transfer Pricing adjustments and charge 5% annual interest; the Law on Administration of Tax Collection Article 63 imposes a 50%-500% penalty on the underpaid tax; Article 201 of the Criminal Law criminalizes tax evasion, providing up to seven years imprisonment and unspecified monetary fines for serious cases.

        
Director personal liability: Chinese courts may pierce the corporate veil for entities used as alter-egos of their controllers; directors who cause the FIP to file false returns are jointly and severally liable for the tax debt, personal assets can be frozen, bank accounts seized, and the directors are placed under exit/entry control (travel ban) until restitution is made. In flagrant cases, the direct responsible manager faces prosecution.

      

      
      

        Shell Company and Treaty (DTA) Abuse
        
Statutory sanction: The competent authority will apply Article 24 of the China-HK DTA and the OECD BEPS Action 6 LOB/PPT principles to deny the treaty benefit; the entire distribution is resurrected as a 10% WHT charge with a late-payment surcharge and a 50%-500% false-benefit penalty; the company is listed on the 'high-risk taxpayer' alert ledger and loses simplified tax treatment.

        
Director personal liability: Directors knowingly signing beneficial-ownership declarations without substance may face civil restitution of the evaded WHT and monetary damages to creditors; cross-border travel is curtailed by a risk-control travel ban; repeated misrepresentations to tax authorities can result in a criminal conviction for forging financial certificates, punishable by 3-10 years of imprisonment in addition to confiscation of diverted profits.

      

      
      

        Anti-Money Laundering (AML) / Illegal Cross-Border Payment Rerouting
        
Statutory sanction: The PRC Anti-Money Laundering Law in its revised form empowers the People's Bank of China to impose fines of up to RMB 5 million on implicated entities, to suspend or revoke business approval, and to ban irresponsible senior managers from holding positions for up to 10 years; false declarations in auxiliary records also trigger tax fraud penalties of 50%-500% of evaded tax for the FIP.

        
Director personal liability: Directors involved in laundering money flows from semiconductor trading may be prosecuted under Article 191 of the Criminal Law for money laundering and face imprisonment of up to 10 years plus forfeiture of participation; the directors are jointly responsible for compensation in civil proceedings; exit restrictions are immediately placed by public security authorities until the AML risk is cleared.
