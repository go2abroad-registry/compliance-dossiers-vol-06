Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA) and the Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax) portal; customs export registration and export declarations are additionally executed through the China International Trade Single Window.

E-government filing portal

For the Chinese Foreign-Invested Partnership (FIP), the statutory tax identification number is the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) printed on the Business License issued by the Market Regulation Administration (AMR). Procedure: (1) reserve the FIP name and file the partnership formation application with AMR, providing the partnership agreement, foreign-investor identity or qualification certificates (notarized and legalized/apostilled), bank confirmation of capital contribution and a registered address in the PRC; (2) upon AMR approval, obtain the Business License; the AMR automatically transmits registration data to the STA, and the USCC functions as the enterprise TIN and VAT registration number; (3) within the statutory filing period, complete tax confirmation in the e-Tax portal, select small-scale or general VAT taxpayer status and update financial and customs information; (4) register as an exporter through the China International Trade Single Window to obtain the Customs Registration Code required for export declarations, export rebates and foreign-exchange collection; (5) if any non-resident partner claims treaty benefits, file the partner's Tax Residence Certificate and apply for treatment under the relevant double-tax agreement through the STA; the FIP acts as the withholding agent for non-resident partner income tax where applicable.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible; the FIP is not an enterprise income tax taxpayer; each foreign or domestic partner is taxed on its allocable share of partnership income, while the partnership is a separate VAT registrant for goods sold and services supplied.

Available election forms

None for check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election under PRC law; partnership pass-through is mandatory. A small-scale VAT taxpayer may elect general VAT taxpayer status under the VAT rules, and input VAT allocation may follow the statutory apportionment formula when both taxable and non-taxable supplies are made.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Renminbi (RMB) 5,000,000 aggregate taxable turnover in any consecutive 12-month period for mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration; below the threshold small-scale VAT taxpayer status applies, subject to voluntary election to register as a general taxpayer; basic tax registration is automatic upon issuance of the Business License.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A 0% VAT rate is available when the FIP, as registered exporter, exports physical goods out of PRC customs territory under a valid Customs Export Declaration submitted through the China International Trade Single Window under e-commerce supervision codes 9610, 9710 or 9810, and the goods are sold to non-resident buyers or shipped to an overseas Amazon FBA warehouse; the export must be supported by export invoice, packing list, transport document, customs value and foreign-exchange receipt; qualifying offshore-consumed services supplied to and wholly used by overseas customers may also be zero-rated or VAT-exempt, but the FBA physical-goods flow is zero-rated. Goods brought back into China or sold domestically are not eligible for zero-rating.

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronic Goods Export Manifest filed under the China International Trade Single Window with a unique customs declaration number)
  • Ocean Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing carriage from the PRC customs territory to the Amazon FBA overseas warehouse or foreign consignee
  • Commercial Export Invoice issued by the FIP showing the zero-rated export sale and export value
  • Export Packing List matching the customs declaration, commercial invoice and bill of lading
  • Bank foreign-exchange receipt voucher (Export Collection Verification) issued by a SAFE-designated bank confirming receipt of export proceeds in a permitted currency within the statutory deadline
  • Export VAT rebate declaration filed through the STA e-Tax export-rebate module together with the underlying Full Digitalized Electronic Invoices

All VAT and export invoices must be issued as Full Digitalized Electronic Invoices (FDEI) through the STA's Electronic Invoice Service Platform, with near-real-time issuance into the national e-invoice database, cryptographic signing via Tax UKey using State Cryptographic Algorithms (SM2/SM3/SM4), mandatory anti-counterfeit invoice number and QR stamp, and API-based filing for approved third-party invoicing systems; paper or unregistered invoices do not support zero-rating or export-rebate claims.

The STA and China Customs may audit export transactions for three years, extending to ten years for suspected fraud; unsubstantiated zero-rating is recharacterized as a domestic taxable supply, output VAT is assessed at 13% with a late-payment surcharge of 0.05% per day, and penalties range from 50% to five times the underpaid tax; export-rebate fraud attracts a separate fine of one to five times the refund obtained, suspension of export-rebate eligibility, customs credit downgrade, and criminal prosecution under PRC tax, customs and anti-money laundering laws where statutory thresholds are met.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China maintains current-account convertibility for genuine trade and service payments, while capital-account transactions remain subject to a foreign-exchange registration and approval framework. For a Foreign-Invested Partnership (FIP) established under PRC law, every external capital contribution is a capital-account inflow that must obtain SAFE foreign-exchange business registration before the remittance is accepted by an onshore bank. Once the foreign currency is received and recorded in a foreign currency capital contribution confirmation, it can be converted into RMB at market spot rates within the registered investment purpose. Profit remittance to Hong Kong partners is generally treated as a current-account outflow for after-tax earnings and does not require separate SAFE approval, provided the FIP submits audited financial statements, the partnership profit-distribution resolution, tax settlement records, and a bank-compliant declaration. The practical hurdles are documentary: the FIP must maintain impeccable source-of-funds evidence, complete trade/transaction declarations, and avoid commingling platform settlements with non-operating receivables. The RMB is not fully convertible at capital account, and all convertible operations must be conducted through banks or licensed payment institutions within the scope of the negative list and the partnership's approved business.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the current SAFE framework, export-generated foreign exchange must be repatriated onshore within 180 days from the date of export customs declaration unless an extension is granted. There is no fixed mandatory conversion ratio; the FIP can retain foreign exchange in a permitted settlement account or freely sell it to any licensed bank at the market spot rate. For Amazon FBA cross-border e-commerce, proceeds collected via licensed payment institutions must be supported by electronic order data, customs clearance records, and settlement manifests to evidence the genuine trade flow.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBoC) and State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) for onshore foreign-exchange control; Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) for offshore Hong Kong dollar and offshore CNY clearing and banking supervision.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Low for onshore account replacement, Medium as an ancillary payment-collection rail. A foreign fintech EMI such as Airwallex, Stripe, or Payoneer is legally barred from acting as the primary depository or capital-account bank for a PRC FIP, because PRC banking law and SAFE rules require a domestic banking license for foreign-exchange settlement, capital-account receipt, and export-proceeds reconciliation. A foreign EMI may facilitate marketplace payouts and provide multi-currency collection through licensed onshore partner banks or a PBOC-licensed payment institution, but it cannot substitute for a Tier-1 domestic bank account and cannot clear the FIP's statutory capital-account obligations. Therefore, the blueprint should treat EMIs only as an optional overlay for global receivable collection while keeping the liquidity and compliance spine with onshore banks.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Complete the SAFE foreign-direct-investment registration for the FIP before any remittance. The Hong Kong parent should originate an MT103 wire to the FIP's capital escrow account at the selected onshore Tier-1 bank. The wire must include the SAFE registration reference and the foreign-exchange purpose code for foreign-invested partnership capital contribution. Upon receipt, the onshore bank will issue a foreign exchange capital inflow confirmation and credit the foreign currency capital account. A licensed PRC CPA must then issue a capital verification report. After verification, the FIP can convert to RMB and move funds to its basic deposit account for permitted operating expenditure. If the injection is to be made in RMB, the route should use CIPS or an approved RMB settlement bank with the corresponding RMB FDI filing.Domestic suppliers are paid through the onshore RMB basic deposit account using CNAPS transfers. International logistics, FBA warehousing, advertising fees, and overseas service fees are settled via SWIFT MT103 from the onshore foreign-exchange settlement account, or through CIPS where the counterparty is RMB-denominated. For large inventory purchases sourced through Hong Kong, an irrevocable commercial letter of credit is the preferred clearing instrument; documentary collections can support smaller shipments. Amazon marketplace receipts from US, EU, and JP markets should be routed through the onshore bank or a licensed payment institution under the applicable customs e-commerce export regime, with each payout mapped to order-level data and customs declarations before funds are converted or retained in accordance with SAFE account rules.Onshore RMB/FX forward contracts with strictly documented underlying current-account or capital-account exposure transacted through a licensed Chinese bank.Onshore FX swaps and cross-currency swaps used for hedging cross-border trade and registered capital cash flows under SAFE norms.SAFE-approved physical-delivery FX options and option combination structures that are tied to the FIP's genuine exposure and do not create net naked positions.Offshore non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are not permissible as a hedge on the onshore FIP; such instruments may be used only by the offshore Hong Kong parent for its own account and cannot be booked against the FIP's RMB obligations.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low for onshore account replacement, Medium as an ancillary payment-collection rail. A foreign fintech EMI such as Airwallex, Stripe, or Payoneer is legally barred from acting as the primary depository or capital-account bank for a PRC FIP, because PRC banking law and SAFE rules require a domestic banking license for foreign-exchange settlement, capital-account receipt, and export-proceeds reconciliation. A foreign EMI may facilitate marketplace payouts and provide multi-currency collection through licensed onshore partner banks or a PBOC-licensed payment institution, but it cannot substitute for a Tier-1 domestic bank account and cannot clear the FIP's statutory capital-account obligations. Therefore, the blueprint should treat EMIs only as an optional overlay for global receivable collection while keeping the liquidity and compliance spine with onshore banks.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% (statutory CIT rate; the FIP itself is fiscally transparent and the tax is attributed to corporate partners)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% statutory withholding tax on China-source dividends, profit distributions, and deemed distributions paid to a non-resident enterprise; under the PRC-HK DTA, the rate may be reduced to 5% if the beneficial owner is a company directly holding at least 25% of the capital of the paying entity, otherwise the 10% rate remains.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: CNY 200 million for annual related-party tangible asset transactions; CNY 100 million for annual related-party intangible asset transactions; CNY 100 million for financial transactions; CNY 40 million for other related-party transactions; Master File is required when aggregate related-party transactions exceed CNY 1 billion, under SAT Announcement 2016 No. 42.

Economic substance scope: The FIP and its Hong Kong related partner must satisfy economic substance and beneficial ownership tests. China applies General Anti-Avoidance Rules (GAAR) under CIT Law Article 47 to recharacterize any arrangement lacking commercial substance. The Hong Kong FSIE regime requires substantial economic activity for passive income of pure equity-holding companies; in the cross-border Amazon FBA model, CIGA must occur in the purported profit-centre jurisdiction, including strategic decisions on marketplace entry, pricing, inventory risk, supplier selection, and treasury settlement functions.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Transfer Pricing / Shell Company Abuse

Statutory sanction: Special tax adjustment under CIT Law Article 47 recharacterizes the income as China-source; unpaid tax is recovered with late-payment surcharge at 0.05% per day; a penalty of 50% to 500% of evaded tax is imposed under Tax Administration Law Article 63; the DTA benefit is denied and a deemed distribution may trigger 10% WHT.

Director personal liability: Competent authorities can pierce the partnership veil when the general partner controls the shell HK entity as an agent; directors and managing partners may be jointly and severally liable for unpaid tax, interest, and penalties; asset freezing, travel bans, and blacklisting may be imposed; criminal prosecution under Article 201 of the PRC Criminal Law can result in imprisonment of up to 7 years and fines.
Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Under-Reporting

Statutory sanction: The China tax authorities may assess the omitted revenue, impose a late-payment surcharge of 0.05% per day, and apply a penalty of 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax under Article 63 of the Tax Administration Law; serious cases involving more than RMB 50,000 of evaded tax and a 10% evasion ratio may be referred to criminal authorities.

Director personal liability: Directors and partners who intentionally instruct or participate in the fraud are personally liable for restitution and civil damages; their personal assets can be seized; travel restrictions and blacklisting prevent them from leaving China; criminal liability under Article 201 of the PRC Criminal Law allows imprisonment for up to 7 years with confiscation of property and disqualification from managing any enterprise.
Withholding Agent Failure / DTA Abuse

Statutory sanction: As withholding agent, the FIP is liable for the uncollected tax; Article 69 of the Tax Administration Law authorizes recovery of the unpaid tax and a fine of 50% to 300% of the sum not withheld; interest accrues on the unpaid amount; in fraudulent cases, no statute of limitations applies.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil or limited liability shield is pierced where a director personally selected the Hong Kong conduit and directed the unwithheld remittance; directors may be held jointly and severally liable for the full tax debt, face civil asset seizure and restitution orders, receive travel bans and blacklisting, and, if the non-withholding is part of a deliberate tax evasion scheme, be sentenced to imprisonment up to 7 years under the PRC Criminal Law.
