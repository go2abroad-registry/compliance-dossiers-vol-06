Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA) and its local tax service bureaus, accessible online through the Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Taxation) portal; for Hong Kong SAR activities, the Inland Revenue Department (IRD) is the Hong Kong counterpart, accessible through the eTax portal.

E-government filing portal

A foreign non-resident investor establishing a Foreign-Invested Partnership in Mainland China first completes business registration at the competent Administration for Market Regulation to obtain the Business License and the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC). The USCC is the statutory Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN); tax registration is synchronised under the multi-license integration reform, and the local STA branch completes the file after verifying the partnership agreement, foreign investor identity documentation, legal representative or managing partner appointment, and place of business. The foreign partner does not receive a separate Chinese VAT ID; the FIP itself is the entity-level VAT taxpayer and uses the USCC, while the foreign partner's income tax position is addressed through the partnership profit allocation return and, where required, non-resident enterprise withholding registration. If Hong Kong registration is also triggered, the partnership must apply to the Business Registration Office of the Inland Revenue Department under the Business Registration Ordinance (Cap. 310) within one month of commencing business, obtain the Business Registration Certificate, and use the Business Registration Number as its tax reference; each non-resident partner without a Hong Kong address should appoint an authorised tax representative for service of assessments and correspondence.

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mainland China: RMB 5,000,000 annual taxable sales is the compulsory threshold for general VAT taxpayer registration, but every business carrying on taxable activities in China must obtain tax identity registration from commencement regardless of turnover. Hong Kong SAR: not applicable because no indirect tax registration regime exists.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The FIP may apply the 0% export VAT rate on cosmetics and personal care products only where the goods are sold to a buyer outside Mainland China, are physically exported from the PRC customs territory under an approved export customs supervision mode (including cross-border e-commerce retail export mode 9610 or cross-border e-commerce B2B export mode 9710), and the selling price is paid by non-resident counterparties in foreign exchange that is repatriated to China through an authorised bank. The zero-rating/refund is available to the registered exporter/taxpayer itself and is denied if the goods are in fact sold domestically or if the physical shipment, customs declaration, invoice, and foreign exchange receipts cannot be matched to the same transaction. Qualifying offshore digital services are zero-rated only where supplied to a non-resident recipient for use wholly outside Mainland China and separately satisfy the cross-border digital service zero-rating/exemption requirements.

  • Customs Export Declaration (export bill of entry) evidencing the export supervision code and physical exit
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill showing the consignee and destination outside Mainland China
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or authorised bank export proceeds receipt record for the foreign exchange collected
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List reconciling product description, quantity, and value with the customs declaration
  • Export sales contract or binding purchase order from the overseas buyer
  • Cross-border e-commerce export clearances / customs list for supervision modes 9610 or 9710 where used
  • Fully digitalised electronic invoice or export invoice required by customs and tax authorities for the shipment
  • Export tax refund/exemption declaration and the reconciliation return filed with the STA export refund system

Mainland China operates a real-time fiscal clearance regime through the National Electronic Invoice Service Platform under the Golden Tax Phase IV system; fully digitalised electronic invoices must be issued with a unique invoice number, a digital fiscal stamp, and encrypted QR validation, and all taxable supplies are transmitted to the STA for near-real-time matching. Hong Kong SAR does not mandate electronic invoicing or fiscal clearance; ordinary commercial documents suffice.

Export zero-rating is subject to cross-authority audit by the STA, customs, and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange. Failure to substantiate physical export, non-repatriation of export proceeds within the statutory period, falsified invoices or logistics documents, or discovery that goods remained in or returned to the domestic market will trigger mandatory reversal of the 0% rate, recovery of every VAT refund granted, late payment surcharge of 0.05% per day on the recovered amount, and an administrative penalty of 50% to five times the tax involved; fraudulent documentation may lead to criminal prosecution for fraudulent export tax refund, restriction on future refund applications, and retrospective clawback extending beyond the ordinary three-year assessment window for wilful concealment.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Hong Kong dollar operates under a Linked Exchange Rate System and is freely convertible on both current account and capital account items. Hong Kong has no foreign-exchange controls, no prior approval mechanism for cross-border fund transfers, and no surrender or mandatory repatriation rules for export proceeds. Dividends, partnership profit shares, royalties, trade payments, and loan repayments may be remitted freely once the maintaining bank's normal KYC, sanctions, and anti-money laundering checks are satisfied. The mainland Chinese leg is different: RMB is convertible on current account for genuine trade and service payments, but capital account transactions, including capital contribution into a Foreign-Invested Partnership, foreign debt, and repatriation of investment, require SAFE registration and settlement through a designated domestic bank. A Chinese FIP receiving capital from Hong Kong must complete SAFE foreign-investment registration, open a separate capital escrow account at a designated bank, and, where the bank's compliance policy requires it, produce an accountant-issued capital verification report. Profit remittance from the Chinese FIP to Hong Kong partners requires a partner resolution, an audited financial statement, proof of tax payment, and the standard practice of tax settlement before profit remittance. In the Hong Kong treasury layer, HKD, USD, and CNH funds can move in real time through CHATS or cross-border payment systems with no official conversion ceiling. The structure enjoys maximum liquidity efficiency in Hong Kong and only moderate regulatory friction on the mainland capital account leg.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For a Hong Kong treasury entity there is no statutory surrender timeline and no mandatory conversion ratio. HKD and all foreign-currency export receipts can be retained, freely converted, paid into operational accounts, or remitted outside Hong Kong without approval. For mainland China consolidated export proceeds generated from the Chinese supply side, SAFE goods-trade supervision applies: export receipts must be repatriated to a licensed domestic bank within the trade contract's settlement maturity, and in the absence of a contractual maturity no later than 180 days after the customs export declaration; deferred receipt periods must be registered or reported to SAFE. China no longer imposes a fixed mandatory conversion ratio for export proceeds. The exporter may choose to settle the foreign currency into RMB at the market rate or retain the foreign currency in its designated foreign-exchange account, subject to the bank's verification of the transactions.

Central bank supervision: Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA), the de facto central bank and monetary authority of Hong Kong SAR, in coordination with the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) for mainland China foreign-exchange and cross-border capital flow matters

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

PayPal Fit: High

Viability rating for offshore digital EMIs as primary treasury rails: Low. An offshore EMI holding a United Kingdom or European Union electronic-money licence has no automatic passporting or recognition in Hong Kong. To legally issue stored value products or provide payment services in Hong Kong, an institution must be licensed by the HKMA under the Payment Systems and Stored Value Facilities Ordinance or hold an authorised banking licence under the Banking Ordinance. Therefore, fintech players such as PayPal, Shopify Payments, Wise, Airwallex, and Revolut can only be used as customer-facing collection and payout channels, and monetary balances must in practical treasury terms be held with a licensed bank partner. Using a pure offshore EMI as the sole operational account for a Hong Kong entity is not a compliant substitute for corporate banking because the EMI cannot provide statutory deposit protection, trade finance, capital escrow, documentary credits, or bank-grade sanction screening controls. The recommended architecture is a Tier-1 corporate bank account as the master treasury vault, with Shopify Payments and PayPal as automated payment gateways whose net settlements sweep daily into that vault.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

For capital injection into the Hong Kong FIP, execute a partnership capital contribution or shareholder loan agreement, then route an SWIFT MT103 from the funding entity to the FIP's account at a licensed HK bank. The wire should display the remitter's full legal name, relationship to the payee, and a purpose narrative such as capital contribution to Foreign-Invested Partnership. HKMA imposes no purpose-code approval regime, but the bank will record capital contribution in its transaction monitoring; for the mainland China leg, outbound RMB or foreign-currency remittances must pass through a designated Chinese bank using SAFE-registered cross-border income payment purpose codes. If capital is being injected from Hong Kong into a mainland China FIP, the investor must complete SAFE FDI registration before wiring, obtain a CPA-issued capital verification report after funds are credited, and keep the capital escrow account designated solely for business operations. Conversion from HKD or USD to RMB must be executed at the onshore market rate through the designated domestic bank, not through offshore markets. Once capital is lawfully injected, it may be used for working capital, inventory purchases, marketing services, and e-commerce logistics payments, but cannot be used for securities investments or loans outside the approved business scope. For Hong Kong bookkeeping, a simple certificate of paid-up capital signed by the general partner is sufficient unless the lending bank requests a professional accountant's confirmation.Trade settlements for cosmetics and beauty retail should use standard institutional rails: SWIFT MT103 for vendor payments and intercompany disbursements, commercial documentary letters of credit for large-scale purchase orders of cosmetics inventory from mainland factories or Asian contract manufacturers, and documentary collections on shorter payment terms with vetted counterparties. The Hong Kong treasury account should keep HKD, USD, and CNH sub-ledgers so that supplier invoices can be paid directly in the invoiced currency and merchants can receive settlement in their preferred foreign currency. For e-commerce customer refunds and marketplace payouts, Shopify Payments and PayPal operate as retail rails, but the underlying merchant settlement must flow to the HKD operating account at HSBC or BOCHK. Repatriation of profits to non-Hong Kong partners is executed by MT103 with purpose narrative dividends or partnership distributions, supported by a partner resolution and tax computations if the Hong Kong profits tax exemption is being claimed. All outbound trade disbursements should be reconciled against the beauty retail inventory system to prevent fragmented or phantom payments.FX forwards and cross-currency swaps between HKD, USD, and CNH are permissible with an HKMA-authorised bank under an ISDA master agreement, provided the notional is aligned to actual cosmetics procurement or inventory commitments and not a speculative position.Offshore RMB non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) referencing CNH are permissible for hedging China supply-chain payment exposure; the maturity should be staggered around purchase order and inventory turnover dates, and the aggregate notional should not exceed the projected customs-cleared intake for the next six months.FX swaps on HKD or USD are freely permissible for normal liquidity conversion, but the treasury committee must maintain a documented hedging policy that separates commercial hedging from trading positions and requires bank approval for any exotic option or leveraged product.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Viability rating for offshore digital EMIs as primary treasury rails: Low. An offshore EMI holding a United Kingdom or European Union electronic-money licence has no automatic passporting or recognition in Hong Kong. To legally issue stored value products or provide payment services in Hong Kong, an institution must be licensed by the HKMA under the Payment Systems and Stored Value Facilities Ordinance or hold an authorised banking licence under the Banking Ordinance. Therefore, fintech players such as PayPal, Shopify Payments, Wise, Airwallex, and Revolut can only be used as customer-facing collection and payout channels, and monetary balances must in practical treasury terms be held with a licensed bank partner. Using a pure offshore EMI as the sole operational account for a Hong Kong entity is not a compliant substitute for corporate banking because the EMI cannot provide statutory deposit protection, trade finance, capital escrow, documentary credits, or bank-grade sanction screening controls. The recommended architecture is a Tier-1 corporate bank account as the master treasury vault, with Shopify Payments and PayPal as automated payment gateways whose net settlements sweep daily into that vault.
