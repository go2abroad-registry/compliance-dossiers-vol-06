Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China, exercised through the competent local tax authority and the national Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax) portal; the Hong Kong Inland Revenue Department (IRD) remains the separate tax authority for any Hong Kong-sourced profits tax exposure, as Hong Kong SAR has no VAT, GST or general sales tax.

E-government filing portal

Register the Foreign-Invested Partnership (FIP) with the local Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) to obtain a Unified Social Credit Code (USCC); under the one-licence-one-code reform this USCC is automatically transmitted to the STA to create the tax registration record, so no separate TIN certificate is issued. Next, log into the e-Tax portal to complete the basic tax information record, confirm the legal representative and responsible financial person, set the VAT taxpayer status, and apply for the export tax refund/exemption filing if exports will be made. A non-resident partner without an establishment in China must be registered for PRC tax identity and withholding by the FIP or by a statutory withholding agent where China-source income is distributed; the foreign partner provides its home-country registration and tax-residence certificate and the partnership agreement. The partnership's TIN for all tax invoices, customs declarations and VAT refund filings is the USCC. No minimum capital is required for establishment of the FIP, and no separate business-license pre-approval is required for smart-home IoT retailing absent a statutory licence requirement.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible - the FIP is transparent for PRC enterprise income tax; the partners are taxed on their distributive shares, whereas the FIP is a separate VAT taxpayer for Chinese VAT; non-resident partners are subject to source-based territorial taxation on China-source income, and any Hong Kong partnership element would be subject to Hong Kong's territorial profits tax.

Available election forms

None for entity classification; China and Hong Kong do not offer a check-the-box election or hybrid-mismatch election. The FIP may elect, where its accounting records are sound, to register as a VAT general taxpayer even if annual taxable sales are below RMB 5,000,000; once so elected, it must remain a general taxpayer for at least 12 months. It may also elect small-scale taxpayer treatment if it is below the RMB 5,000,000 annual turnover threshold, subject to the STA preferential rules then in force.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Tax registration is mandatory from the date of business registration without regard to turnover. For VAT classification, annual taxable sales of RMB 5,000,000 is the threshold above which registration as a VAT general taxpayer is mandatory; below that threshold the FIP can be a small-scale taxpayer. Small-scale taxpayers with monthly sales not exceeding RMB 100,000 may be exempt from VAT for that month under the STA's current small-scale taxpayer preferential policy.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export of smart-home IoT hardware is zero-rated under the PRC VAT regime (goods exported by a VAT taxpayer are outside the PRC VAT scope and the related input VAT is refundable). The FIP must be a registered VAT taxpayer, must have made the export customs declaration, must prove the goods left China, and must receive the sales proceeds in a foreign exchange/RMB form through a SAFE-supervised bank. In the cross-border e-commerce retail export mode, customs regulatory code 9610 applies, and the e-commerce order, payment data, logistics data and customs declaration must be interlocked in the customs e-commerce system. Bundled offshore digital services (e.g. IoT cloud features) are eligible for zero-rating only if the services fall in the STA cross-border taxable-services zero-rated/exempt catalogue and the service is supplied to an overseas recipient and consumed entirely outside China; if hardware is the dominant component the bundled supply is treated as goods.

  • Customs Export Goods Declaration (Export Customs Declaration; standard export declaration or cross-border e-commerce retail export declaration with customs supervisory code 9610 as applicable)
  • Commercial invoice and packing list for the export order; for e-commerce sales, the platform order generated on the Shopify/Stripe or Shopify Payments rails showing the overseas buyer and delivery address
  • Bill of Lading (sea), Air Waybill (air), or postal/courier logistics manifest showing the goods exited China and were delivered to the overseas customer
  • Bank foreign-exchange collection record or inward remittance certificate from a SAFE-designated bank, showing the export proceeds were repatriated to China and settled/retained in accordance with SAFE trade-monitoring rules
  • Export VAT input-tax verification record and the export tax refund/exemption application data submitted in the STA export tax refund system

The FIP must issue fully digitalised electronic invoices (digital e-invoices) through the STA electronic invoice service platform, which transmits invoice data to the tax authority in real time; each invoice carries an electronic seal and a unique dynamic invoice number. Traditional special VAT invoices, if used for input VAT credit, are issued through the VAT invoice management system with block/order control and must be authenticated/verified in the national invoice data platform before credit is taken. There is no physical fiscal stamp; anti-fraud control is enforced through real-time digital reporting, the Golden Tax Phase IV platform, and automatic locking of invoice data on the e-invoice service platform.

Export zero-rating is subject to verification by the STA and customs. If customs declarations, logistics evidence, foreign-exchange receipts, or invoice records are missing or inconsistent, the STA will disallow the zero rate, claw back the input VAT refund already paid, reverse the input-tax credit, and impose late-payment surcharge of 0.05% per day. Under the Tax Collection Administration Law, a false export-refund claim is treated as tax evasion/refund fraud and is punishable by an administrative penalty of 50% to 500% of the evaded amount, together with possible suspension of VAT invoice quotas and export refund filing rights. Customs may separately penalise false declarations under the Customs Law. Where fraudulent export-tax-refund conduct reaches the criminal threshold, the case is transferred for liability under the PRC Criminal Law for fraudulent export tax refund, with criminal fines and imprisonment for the natural-person persons responsible.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Under PRC law the RMB is conditionally convertible. Current-account receipts and payments for goods and services are accepted by licensed banks without individual SAFE approval when the enterprise is a verified "A" category trader and presents authentic commercial documents, including invoices, purchase and sale contracts, customs declarations, and transport evidence. The Foreign-Invested Partnership (FIP) can pay importers and receive e-commerce customer settlements in foreign currency or RMB through its current-account clearing bank, but the funds must be tied to a real transaction and must be recorded in the Trade Receivables and Payables Roster. The capital account, including partnership capital injections and partner loan repayments, remains subject to FDI registration and SAFE filing before the bank can release funds. Profit remittance from the China FIP to the Hong Kong partner is not a straightforward convertibility guarantee: the distribution timeline depends on provisional or consolidated audits, transfer pricing documentation, a tax clearance certificate, and a SAFE-approved payment purpose code. The bank will review prior capital verification, unpaid intercompany items, and outstanding bank charges before debiting any remittance, and any historically unreconciled export proceeds will block the request. No synthetic multicurrency treasury structure or private currency conversion facility is legal for onshore flows.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export proceeds of the FIP must be repatriated into China through a designated domestic current-account foreign exchange settlement account or sold to a licensed commercial bank. SAFE's time-limit rule is generally the export customs declaration date plus 180 days, subject to enterprise classification and banking practice; proceeds received after that deadline trigger a SAFE verification report and a possible downgrade of the trade roster. For standard goods exports, no mandatory fixed conversion ratio is currently imposed; an enterprise may retain up to 100% of eligible export proceeds in foreign currency or convert them to RMB at will. However, the retained foreign currency balance must remain within the approved current-account foreign exchange retention position, and trade surveillance ensures that repatriation is not delayed or used as a netting mechanism without documentary proof.

Central bank supervision: The People's Bank of China (PBOC), acting through the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE), with the National Financial Regulatory Administration (NFRA) licensing and supervising the authorized commercial banks

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: No legal viability for the onshore FIP. Offshore digital EMIs are not licensed by the PBOC, NFRA, or SAFE to open settlement accounts for China-domiciled legal entities, and a China-incorporated partnership cannot lawfully use an EMI as its principal banking venue for receiving or making cross-border payments or retaining export proceeds. If the Hong Kong parent uses an EMI, that treasury activity must remain entirely outside the China entity's books and cannot be presented as an onshore settlement rail. The FIP must clear CNY and FX flows through a SAFE-regulated Tier-1 bank; Stripe and Shopify Payments are payment aggregators, not corporate bank accounts, and may only be connected to the FIP through a verified onshore bank settlement process with full trade documentation.

Strike-off trigger

Mandatory Audit Threshold Small Company Audit Exemption Regime

Turnover: Under Hong Kong law, a partnership as such is not automatically subject to statutory audit solely because of balance sheet size or headcount; however, the Inland Revenue Department requires certified audited financial statements to accompany the BIR52 Partnership Profits Tax Return whenever the partnership's annual gross sales or income exceeds HKD 5,000,000. The IRD may also require audited accounts for any partnership whose accounts are considered 'large' or complex, or where income has been understated. A partnership with gross income at or below HKD 5,000,000 may file unaudited accounts with a partner-signed certification and tax computation. If the partnership has a corporate general partner or any corporate subsidiary, that corporate entity is legally required to commission an independent audit under the Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622) unless dormant, with no small-company turnover exemption. A partnership carrying on a regulated financial activity or holding a money service operator licence is additionally subject to the audit and assurance requirements of the SFC/AMLO. Headcount is not a statutory audit trigger in Hong Kong; the quantitative triggers are: (i) HKD 5,000,000 annual gross income for partnership tax filing; (ii) unlimited audit requirement for any non-dormant company; and (iii) regulator-specific audit for licensed financial businesses.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: HKD 10,000,000 aggregate controlled transactions with associated enterprises during the basis period triggers a mandatory Local File under IRD DIPN 59; a Master File is required where the MNE group's consolidated group revenue exceeds HKD 6,800,000,000. Simplified documentation applies for small taxpayers (annual turnover ≤ HKD 100,000,000, total assets ≤ HKD 100,000,000, or average staff ≤ 100).

Economic substance scope: Hong Kong does not have a statutory Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) regime comparable to BVI/Cayman. Instead, the territorial source principle functions as the fundamental test: profits derived from business carried on in Hong Kong are taxable, while offshore-sourced profits are exempt. A taxpayer claiming offshore status must demonstrate, through contemporaneous documentation, that the core income-generating activities (CIGA) — such as contract negotiation, order acceptance, deployment of the principal technology, and management decision-making — were performed outside Hong Kong. For an IoT hardware retail FIP, hosting the e-commerce platform overseas and executing sales contracts abroad may support an offshore claim, but if the sales activities, inventory holding, order fulfilment, or payment processing occur through Hong Kong, the profits will be treated as Hong Kong-sourced.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion by Deliberate Understatement

Statutory sanction: Section 82(1), Inland Revenue Ordinance (Cap. 112) – On conviction, maximum fine of HKD 50,000, imprisonment for up to 3 years, and an additional fine of treble the amount of tax undercharged. Civil penalties may also be imposed by the Commissioner in lieu of prosecution.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil will be pierced where directors, partners or managing agents knowingly authorise or participate in the evasion. The individual is jointly and severally liable for the underpaid tax, interest, and penalties, may be subject to a travel ban and blacklisting of their HKID / passport and immediate seizure of personal assets through civil recovery proceedings, and faces actual criminal imprisonment up to 3 years.
Wilful Failure to File / Notify Chargeability

Statutory sanction: Section 80(2), Inland Revenue Ordinance (Cap. 112) – Fine up to HKD 10,000, plus daily default penalty for continued failure; where tax is undercharged, the penalty may be trebled. Under section 80(5), a further fine of HKD 10,000 may be imposed for failure to comply with notice requiring statement of assets and liabilities.

Director personal liability: Directors and partners who cause the default cannot shelter behind the partnership; the IRD may issue a notice of assessment against the individual, enforce payment by distress and imprisonment under section 84 IRO. The offending officer's personal bank accounts may be garnished, and the court may also impose an involuntary bankruptcy / winding-up petition and a travel restriction order.
Anti-Money Laundering and Facilitation of Tax Fraud via Cross-Border E-Commerce Payments

Statutory sanction: Section 14 AMLO (Cap. 615) – Unlicensed or wilful failure to comply with AML requirements or engaging in money laundering can attract a maximum fine of HKD 5,000,000 and imprisonment up to 7 years. Further, under the Organized and Serious Crimes Ordinance (Cap. 455), proceeds of crime are subject to confiscation orders.

Director personal liability: Directors, general partners or beneficial owners who knowingly direct or consent to the money-laundering scheme are personally liable for confiscation of the proceeds; they may be placed on the Hong Kong financial blacklist, subjected to asset-freeze orders and travel bans, and sentenced to imprisonment up to 7 years. The court may also impose disqualification from acting as a director/partner for a defined period and issue civil restitution orders exceeding the value of the laundered property.
