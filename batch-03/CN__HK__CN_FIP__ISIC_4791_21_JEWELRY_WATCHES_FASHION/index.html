Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Inland Revenue Department (IRD) of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, including the Business Registration Office; official electronic filing portal: eTAX via GovHK.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Determine whether the Foreign-Invested Partnership is carrying on business in Hong Kong. Under the Business Registration Ordinance (Cap. 310), every person/partnership that carries on business in Hong Kong must register with the Commissioner of Inland Revenue within one month of commencement; there is no minimum capital or turnover condition for this registration. Step 2: If the FIP is constituted as a limited partnership under the Limited Partnerships Ordinance (Cap. 37) or as an LPF under the Limited Partnership Funds Ordinance (Cap. 637), complete the registration/incorporation with the Companies Registry and obtain the relevant certificate of registration. Step 4: IRD shall issue a Business Registration Certificate bearing a Business Registration Number (BRN), which is the functional Tax Identification Number for all Hong Kong tax compliance. Step 5: Maintain the BRC displayed/available at the Hong Kong place of business; file subsequent Profits Tax returns in the partnership's name or the partners' names as applicable; use eTAX for returns and correspondence.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Under section 22A of the Inland Revenue Ordinance, an eligible partnership carrying on a trade/profession in Hong Kong may elect in writing to be assessed in the partnership name and at the corporate Profits Tax rate; the election, once validly made, is generally irrevocable. For a Hong Kong partnership, no check-the-box regime is available, and there is no hybrid mismatch election analogous to the US Form 8832; the pass-through result is the default for partnerships, with each partner taxed on his/her/its respective share of Hong Kong-sourced profits only.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/Sales and Use Tax; Hong Kong imposes only narrow excise-like duties under the Dutiable Commodities Ordinance (Cap. 109) on specified commodities.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — no VAT/GST registration threshold exists in Hong Kong. Business Registration under Cap. 310 is mandatory within one month of commencing business, irrespective of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable. Hong Kong does not impose VAT/GST, so no 0% export VAT rate or export VAT refund scheme exists. For Profits Tax, cross-border e-commerce profits of a Hong Kong partnership are taxable only if the profits arise in or are derived from Hong Kong; export sales are assessed by reference to the source of profits, not by an indirect-tax zero-rating rule.

  • Not applicable for VAT purposes — no VAT zero-rating substantiation bundle is prescribed in Hong Kong because no VAT/GST is levied.
  • Customs/trade control support (only when an export declaration is required under the Import and Export (Registration) Regulations): electronic Trade Single Window export declaration receipt, commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading or air waybill, and any export licence issued by the Director-General of Trade and Industry.
  • If the goods are manufactured in mainland China and routed through Hong Kong, retain the mainland China export customs declaration and logistics manifests to evidence movement, ownership and value.
  • Inward remittance certificates are not statutory Hong Kong tax/customs documents; where a financing bank requires one for proceeds of jewelry/watch sales, a bank-issued inward remittance advice may accompany the books but no statutory Hong Kong tax deadline applies to its issuance.

None. Hong Kong has no real-time fiscal invoice clearance, no mandatory e-invoicing API, and no anti-fraud digital stamping. IRD accepts electronic records. For goods subject to import/export control, electronic trade declarations are lodged with the Customs and Excise Department through the Trade Single Window, but this is not a VAT clearance mechanism.

Because there is no VAT, there is no VAT zero-rating clawback. However, an offshore-profits/source claim will be scrutinized by IRD under the territorial source doctrine; false statements made knowingly in Profits Tax returns can trigger prosecution and, under the Inland Revenue Ordinance, the imposition of additional tax (commonly up to three times the amount of tax undercharged) plus fines and imprisonment. False export/trade declarations to Customs under the Import and Export (Registration) Regulations are punishable by monetary penalties, imprisonment and/or forfeiture of the goods.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The PRC operates a managed current-account convertible system in which trade and service transactions are permitted to clear through licensed banks without prior individual approval, but capital account movements remain strictly regulated and require case-by-case SAFE registration. For the CN_FIP, incoming equity and partner loan payments must enter through a dedicated capital account or FDI currency conversion account, be documented for capital verification, and be deployed only within the approved scope; profit remittances to the Hong Kong partner require audited financial statements, tax completion certificates, and SAFE remittance filing. The CNY is not freely convertible offshore onshore; all conversions must pass through the onshore interbank system at prevailing spot rates, although the HK market provides CNH liquidity at a free-floating offshore price. The seller of jewellery and watches using Stripe and PayPal is exposed to cross-currency mismatch because platform settlements arrive in multiple foreign currencies; repatriation into the mainland FIP account must be documented with trade background and reported under current account rules. There is no general right to retain or deploy capital proceeds without evidenced commercial risk, and restrictions on certain negative-list sectors further constrain the use of funds.

Export proceeds surrender rules

China has abolished mandatory fixed-ratio surrender for trade proceeds; the FIP must repatriate export earnings for goods to a domestic foreign exchange account within 90 days of the export customs declaration date and then either retain the foreign currency or convert it into CNY at a licensed bank. Where the FIP uses cross-border RMB settlement, the same trade background must be reported under CIPS/SAFE purpose codes, and deferred receipts or material deviations exceeding the prescribed grace period require a written SAFE explanation and registration.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the People's Bank of China (PBOC) for the mainland-resident Foreign-Invested Partnership, with the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) as the currency board authority for parallel HKD/CNH clearing.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Low feasibility for the onshore Chinese FIP. An offshore digital EMI without a PBOC licence cannot open a deposit account, conduct FOREX settlement, or perform SAFE capital verification for the FIP. Some licensed PBOC-instructed payment institutions can support collection of e-commerce proceeds from Stripe and PayPal, but they operate only as facilitators and do not displace the commercial bank account relationship. Legal constraints require the FIP to maintain a local corporate account at a licensed onshore bank for statutory capital and operational liquidity clearing.

0% - Hong Kong imposes no withholding tax on dividend distributions, and because the FIP is a pass-through partnership, allocations of profits to partners are not subject to distribution withholding. The partners are taxed directly on their shares of Hong Kong-sourced assessable profits.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within one month from the date of issue of the tax return under IRO s.51(1). Where an authorised tax representative is appointed and the accounting period ends on 31 December, the IRD block extension practice ordinarily extends the paper filing deadline to 15 August and the e-Tax filing deadline to 15 November of the following year.
