Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Inland Revenue Department (IRD), Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, including the Business Registration Office (BRO); electronic portal: eTAX at https://www.etax.gov.hk. The Business Registration Ordinance (Cap. 310) is administered by the IRD.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Apply to the Business Registration Office under the Business Registration Ordinance (Cap. 310) within one month of commencing business in Hong Kong, using the statutory application for business registration (Form IRBR1). Step 2: The IRD issues a Business Registration Certificate (BRC) containing the Business Registration Number (BRN); the BRN is the sole statutory tax identification number for the Foreign-Invested Partnership (FIP). Step 3: If the FIP is constituted as a limited partnership, it must also be registered with the Companies Registry under the Limited Partnerships Ordinance (Cap. 37) using the prescribed statement of particulars; the Limited Partnership Certificate does not replace the BRN. Step 4: No separate VAT identification number exists in Hong Kong because Hong Kong has no VAT/GST. Each foreign non-resident partner is assessed directly on the allocated share of partnership profits under the Inland Revenue Ordinance (Cap. 112), and the IRD may issue tax returns or assessments directly to a non-resident partner having assessable profits sourced in Hong Kong.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible; for Hong Kong Profits Tax purposes the FIP is transparent. The partnership itself is not taxed as a separate entity; partners are chargeable on their respective shares of the partnership profit under sections 22, 22A and 25A of the Inland Revenue Ordinance (Cap. 112), with territorial sourcing rules applying to each partner.

Available election forms

None. Hong Kong has no check-the-box or hybrid mismatch entity classification election. Partnership treatment is mandatory under the Inland Revenue Ordinance, and no election is available to change the pass-through characterization. Advance rulings may be requested from the IRD on specific transactions, but such rulings are not entity classification elections.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No general VAT, GST, or sales and use tax in Hong Kong; Hong Kong levies only targeted indirect duties such as those under the Dutiable Commodities Ordinance (Cap. 109) and the Hotel Accommodation Tax Ordinance (Cap. 348).

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable; Hong Kong has no VAT/GST and accordingly no monetary threshold triggering indirect tax registration. However, every business, including an FIP carrying on business in Hong Kong, must obtain a Business Registration Certificate under the Business Registration Ordinance (Cap. 310) irrespective of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable; Hong Kong does not operate a VAT or GST, so there is no export zero-rating election and no statutory mechanism for claiming a 0% VAT export rate. Exported goods and cross-border services from Hong Kong are outside the scope of indirect taxation. For Profits Tax purposes, an FIP in the refrigerated logistics warehousing sector may argue that certain income is not sourced in Hong Kong and thus not taxable, but that is a territorial profits tax analysis rather than an export VAT zero-rating claim.

  • Not applicable for VAT zero-rating: no Hong Kong export VAT declaration or VAT zero-rating substantiation bundle is required.
  • To support any offshore-source Profits Tax argument, retain contracts, invoices, purchase orders, warehouse receipts, temperature logs, customs export documents, bills of lading or airway bills, courier receipts, and communications evidencing where the refrigerated logistics services are performed and where the customer or recipient is located.
  • To evidence foreign exchange repatriation for group risk management, retain bank credit advisories, SWIFT messages, and settlement statements; Hong Kong maintains no foreign exchange control requirement or statutory repatriation deadline for export proceeds.

No mandatory electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, API-based reporting, or anti-fraud digital stamping regime exists in Hong Kong. Taxpayers must instead preserve sufficient business records for at least seven years under section 51C of the Inland Revenue Ordinance (Cap. 112).

However, if an FIP or partner makes an incorrect Profits Tax treatment of Hong Kong-sourced income as offshore-source income, the IRD may raise estimated assessments, impose penalties of up to three times the amount of tax undercharged under section 82A of the Inland Revenue Ordinance (Cap. 112), and in serious cases pursue criminal prosecution. Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Chinese FIP is domiciled in the People's Republic of China and therefore falls under SAFE's foreign-exchange control framework. The RMB is fully convertible on the current account (trade in goods/services, dividends and royalties) after genuine business documents and tax receipts are presented to a designated bank, but the capital account remains heavily regulated. Foreign capital contributed to a Foreign-Invested Partnership must flow through a designated FDI capital settlement account, and the original currency (usually USD or HKD) may be converted to RMB only after the bank has verified the subscription agreement, SAFE FDI registration, paid-in capital certificate and business scope compliance. Profit remittance to foreign partners is permitted but requires a copy of the audited annual financial statements, the board/partner resolution approving distribution, and the corporate income tax withholding voucher; banks may freeze remittance if the partnership is not in good standing with the State Administration of Taxation. There is no mandatory surrender of export proceeds for current account receipts since the 2012 reform, but all export earnings must be repatriated to China within the prescribed timeline and retained in a designated foreign currency account or voluntarily converted into RMB. Immediate conversion for capital injections is not automatic; a capital account FX conversion form must be lodged with the bank, and the proceeds cannot be used for working capital beyond the registered scope. Any profit deemed earned from impermissible activities or unreported ultimate beneficiaries will trigger AML review and potential SAFE penalties.

Export proceeds surrender rules

China abolished mandatory surrender percentages for current account export proceeds; enterprises may retain all foreign currency receipts in a settlement account or convert to RMB at their discretion. Export proceeds must be repatriated to the domiciled bank and go through trade credit verification within 180 days from the date of export customs declaration (extendable to 360 days with a valid SAFE reason). Receipts for pre-shipment on account must be registered as advance receipts and unreleased within 30 days after delivery. Failure to repatriate within the timeline is an administrative violation under the Foreign Exchange Control Regulations (Regulations of the People's Republic of China on Foreign Exchange Control, 2008).

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBoC) and State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE); Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) acts as the de facto central bank for the Hong Kong SAR leg where no FX controls exist.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Offshore digital EMIs (e.g., Wise, Airwallex, Revolut Business) are not licensed by the PBOC/CBIRC to maintain domestic current or capital accounts for a Chinese Foreign-Invested Partnership, and they cannot undertake SAFE foreign-exchange registration or capital verification. They may be used solely as ancillary cross-border payment channels for inbound disbursements after a licensed commercial bank has opened a sponsor bank account, but they are legally barred from acting as the primary account provider or holding the entity's capital injection. For a cold storage logistics FIP needing full bank guarantees, trade finance and SAFE compliance, a Tier-1 onshore bank is mandatory. Any EMI that cannot provide a China bank branch with a full KYC relationship should be rejected for the main treasury function.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

For the Chinese FIP, the foreign partner must first ensure the partnership is registered with the Market Regulation Authority and the foreign investment information report is filed. The bank then completes SAFE's FDI (Foreign Direct Investment) registration for the partnership and opens a fiduciary 'FDI Capital Settlement Account' in the same currency as the contribution. The investor remits via SWIFT MT103 with the beneficiary bank being the designated onshore branch, using the BOP transaction purpose code from the SAFE 6220 series (Foreign direct investment inflows – capital contribution) and the correct custody line 'FDI CAPITAL SETTLEMENT ACCOUNT'. Bank officers will perform capital verification against the signed partnership agreement; only after the capital has landed and the bank has issued a paid-in certificate may the partnership convert the funds into RMB (or use a 'payment before settlement' priority for designated expenditures) and transfer the RMB to its operational current account for permitted cold-storage operating expenses. Any deviation from the registered use (e.g., investing into brokerage or offshore lending) triggers a SAFE penalty.Outbound payments are cleared through the onshore branch using SWIFT MT103 for ordinary current-account trade (e.g., shipping refrigerated containers, maintenance parts from overseas vendors) supported by an invoice, contract and customs declaration where applicable. For recurring purchases of expensive compressors, insulation panels or bonded linings from overseas suppliers, a commercial Letter of Credit (MT700) issued by the Bank of China or ICBC with a 90-day sight tenor gives the partnership secure deferred settlement and better payment terms. Documentary Collections (MT400/MT540) can be used for low-risk, out-of-warranty spare parts that do not require title documents. All trade outflows must align with the 5210 cold-storage logistics scope; a mismatch in the goods description is an AML red flag.Onshore deliverable CNY/USD FX forward contracts (with a physical delivery to the designated settlement bank) under a real current-account exposure documentary file for procurement of cold-storage equipment and international electricity/fuel tariffs.Cross-currency swaps (CCS) to convert syndicated USD/HKD equipment-lease liabilities into RMB payment streams, provided the underlying loan has completed SAFE foreign-debt registration.FX options (vanilla purchase only) and zero-cost collars (two-bank simultaneous call/put) for protection against adverse translation of future capital contributions or dividend absorptions, clearly tied to verified committed flows. Offshore NDFs are not permissible for an onshore FIP without SAFE approval; therefore NDF contracts are excluded from this hedging blueprint.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Offshore digital EMIs (e.g., Wise, Airwallex, Revolut Business) are not licensed by the PBOC/CBIRC to maintain domestic current or capital accounts for a Chinese Foreign-Invested Partnership, and they cannot undertake SAFE foreign-exchange registration or capital verification. They may be used solely as ancillary cross-border payment channels for inbound disbursements after a licensed commercial bank has opened a sponsor bank account, but they are legally barred from acting as the primary account provider or holding the entity's capital injection. For a cold storage logistics FIP needing full bank guarantees, trade finance and SAFE compliance, a Tier-1 onshore bank is mandatory. Any EMI that cannot provide a China bank branch with a full KYC relationship should be rejected for the main treasury function.

Turnover: Under the Hong Kong Partnership Ordinance and Limited Partnerships Ordinance, no statutory audit is imposed on a pure Foreign-Invested Partnership solely by reference to turnover, total assets, or employee headcount. A certified independent audit by a qualified CPA becomes legally mandatory where: (a) the partnership agreement, investor side letter, or banking covenant requires an audited statement; (b) the Inland Revenue Department requires an auditor's report in support of the Profits Tax Return where accounts are material or unresolved; (c) the Department of Health / Pharmacy and Poisons Board imposes an audited financial statement condition for renewal of the Wholesale Dealer in Medicines licence; (d) the same business is carried on through a Hong Kong company under Cap. 622, in which case qualified auditor appointment is required unless the company is dormant or a valid members' dispensation applies; or (e) the partnership is consolidated into an offshore parent's statutory audit. No numerical audit threshold exists for a Hong Kong partnership vehicle itself.
