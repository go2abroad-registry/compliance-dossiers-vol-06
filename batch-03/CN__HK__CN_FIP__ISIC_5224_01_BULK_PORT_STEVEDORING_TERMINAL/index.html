Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Inland Revenue Department (IRD), Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region; online electronic portal: eTAX (https://www.etax.gov.hk); official IRD website: https://www.ird.gov.hk.

E-government filing portal

For a Foreign-Invested Partnership (FIP) carrying on business in Hong Kong, no VAT identification number is issued because Hong Kong does not levy VAT/GST/Sales Tax. The statutory taxpayer identification is the Business Registration Number (BRN) obtained under the Business Registration Ordinance (Cap. 310), and the Business Registration Certificate (BRC) functions as the de facto TIN for Hong Kong tax purposes. Procedure: (1) Determine whether the FIP is carrying on business in Hong Kong or has a place of business in Hong Kong; if so, apply to the Business Registration Office (BRO) of the IRD within one month after commencement. (2) If the FIP is formed as a limited partnership in Hong Kong, the relevant registration with the Hong Kong Companies Registry under the Limited Partnerships Ordinance or the Limited Partnership Funds Ordinance is required where applicable; no minimum capital is required and non-resident ownership is permitted. (3) Submit the prescribed Business Registration application through the eTAX portal or BRO counter, providing the FIP name, Hong Kong business address, nature of business (including ISIC 5224 bulk port stevedoring activities), date of commencement, names and addresses of all partners including foreign non-resident partners, and principal place of business information. (4) Pay the prescribed business registration fee and levy, after which the IRD issues the Business Registration Certificate. (5) Use the BRN as the taxpayer identification number for all IRD correspondence, profits tax filings, and trade declarations. (6) The partnership itself is not a separate taxable entity; it is administratively assessable in the name of the partnership, with profits being allocated to the partners under the territorial source principles of the Inland Revenue Ordinance (Cap. 112).

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible / Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

None. Hong Kong does not provide check-the-box elections, hybrid mismatch elections, or an election for a partnership to be taxed as a corporation. A Foreign-Invested Partnership is treated as tax-transparent, and each partner is separately chargeable on Hong Kong-source profits.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/Sales Tax (Hong Kong has no general indirect tax regime).

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable; Hong Kong has no VAT/GST/sales tax regime and therefore no turnover threshold triggering indirect tax registration. A Hong Kong business must obtain a Business Registration Certificate under the Business Registration Ordinance (Cap. 310) from the commencement of business regardless of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable. Because Hong Kong does not impose VAT/GST/sales tax, there is no 0% export VAT rate and no VAT refund mechanism for exported goods or services. For Hong Kong profits tax purposes, the territorial source rule may exclude profits derived from outside Hong Kong, but stevedoring and marine terminal activities physically performed at a Hong Kong bulk port are Hong Kong-source and are therefore assessable to profits tax. Export documentation is required for customs compliance and evidencing foreign exchange proceeds, but it does not substantiate a VAT zero-rating claim.

  • Import and Export Declaration (electronic export cargo manifest) lodged with the Hong Kong Customs and Excise Department under the Import and Export (Registration) Regulations.
  • Bill of Lading or seaway bill evidencing the loading and export movement of bulk cargo from the Hong Kong marine terminal.
  • Commercial invoice and packing list describing the cargo, quantity, port of loading, consignee, buyer, seller, and payment terms.
  • Bank credit advice, telegraphic transfer receipt, or SWIFT MT103 inward remittance message

None. Hong Kong does not mandate real-time electronic tax invoicing, fiscal clearance, API-based reporting, e-invoice digital stamping, or anti-fraud clearance. Ordinary invoices and supporting records are governed by the record-keeping provisions of the Inland Revenue Ordinance (Cap. 112) and must be retained for at least seven years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The PRC maintains a distinction between current account and capital account convertibility. A mainland FIP providing bulk port stevedoring services may receive HKD/USD/RMB payments from Hong Kong and other counterparties; current account receipts for service income are generally convertible at designated foreign exchange banks once the underlying transaction is supported by authentic service contracts, port authority invoices, cargo manifests, and tax payment records. Capital account inflows, including capital contributions from the HK investor, are not freely convertible: the FIP must complete SAFE foreign exchange registration before the capital is remitted, and the proceeds must be used only for approved partnership business activities. RMB conversion of foreign currency capital requires a payment-use declaration, and funds cannot be deployed for securities, entrusted loans, or repayment of domestic RMB lending unless expressly permitted by SAFE. Profit and partnership distribution remittances to the HK partner require audited financial statements, PRC tax clearance, and a valid partnership distribution resolution; non-resident partners may face withholding tax. The HK treasury vehicle itself enjoys full HKD/foreign currency convertibility under the HKMA currency board, but this does not exempt the onshore FIP from SAFE-based registration, repatriation, and designated-bank settlement duties.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory mandatory conversion ratio for export proceeds under the current SAFE regime; the FIP may retain 100% of export/stevedoring service receipts in a foreign exchange settlement account or sell them to a designated bank at market rates. All current account receipts must nevertheless be repatriated into China and reported through the bank's Foreign Exchange Monitoring System; for stevedoring services the remittance must be received within the contractual payment period, generally not exceeding 180 days after service completion unless a documented payment deferral is approved by the bank. In Hong Kong, no surrender requirement applies to HKD or foreign currency receipts.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the People's Bank of China (PBOC) for mainland China; Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) as the currency-board authority for the Hong Kong SAR treasury and clearing hub

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low for primary onshore treasury banking. Overseas and Hong Kong fintech EMIs cannot open domestic RMB settlement accounts or perform SAFE-regulated foreign exchange conversion for a mainland-incorporated FIP; under PRC commercial banking and payment institution rules, only licensed domestic banks are permitted to hold FIP deposits, execute capital account registration, and process foreign exchange. An HK-licensed EMI or Money Service Operator can be used as an ancillary payment message orchestrator for HK-side sub-accounts, pre-funding, or CNH/HKD treasury sweeps, but it cannot replace the onshore bank compliance and clearing rails. Therefore, the FIP should place all SAFE-related capital and operational FX flows through Tier-1 Chinese banks, with the HK treasury entity using an HKMA-authorized bank for offshore liquidity management.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The HK investor should transmit USD or HKD by SWIFT MT103 from its HK corporate account to the FIP's foreign exchange capital account at the chosen PRC Tier-1 bank. The wire must clearly reference the SAFE Foreign-Invested Partnership filing number and use the appropriate SAFE BOP foreign investment purpose code, typically the FDI capital contribution code applied by the receiving bank. The HK remitter must supply board/partnership resolutions, certificate of incorporation, UBO declarations, and source-of-funds evidence. On receipt, the onshore bank will complete foreign currency to RMB conversion under the payment-use declaration, and the FIP must obtain a PRC CPA capital verification report before any operating disbursement. All funds must remain in the capital account until SAFE registration and tax filing formalities are confirmed, and no payment can be made for prohibited capital-account uses.Operational cross-border payments for port equipment maintenance, agency fees, bunkering, and subcontractor stevedoring should be routed via SWIFT MT103 with underlying invoices and service contracts; imports of heavy terminal equipment should be settled by commercial letters of credit issued by the onshore bank (MT700) with full transport documents, insurance, and port authority inspection reports; low-risk related-party supplier payments can use documentary collections under URC522. Domestic CNY payments to the port authority, labor subcontractors, and utilities should be settled through CIPS or the onshore RMB clearing system, including the port authority clearing channel, with all outbound foreign exchange payments supported by tax clearance and SAFE filing records.Onshore deliverable FX forward contracts with a PRC designated bank to hedge USD/HKD/RMB revenue and cost flows, provided the term and amount are matched to genuine stevedoring, port dues, and equipment purchase exposuresOnshore FX swaps between CNY and HKD/USD for short-term working capital timing mismatches on verified trade and service paymentsCross-currency swaps or interest rate swaps with the PRC settlement bank to hedge long-term USD/HKD-denominated port equipment financing, subject to SAFE and PBOC non-speculative underlying-debt requirementsOffshore non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are not permissible for the onshore FIP; only the Hong Kong treasury affiliate may transact CNH/USD NDFs in the offshore market to hedge its own HK-entity exposure, not the mainland stevedoring partnership

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% (corporate partner CIT; the Foreign-Invested Partnership itself is a pass-through entity; individual partners may be taxed at IIT rates of up to 35%)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% statutory withholding tax on dividends/profit distributions paid by a Chinese resident enterprise to a non-resident enterprise. For the FIP, pass-through treatment means profit allocated to a non-resident partner is generally taxed as China-source business profits; where a distribution is treated as a dividend-like remittance, the 10% default WHT applies and may be reduced to 5% under the PRC-HK Tax Arrangement Article 10 if the Hong Kong partner is the beneficial owner and has held at least 25% of the relevant interest.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Annual return due within 5 months after fiscal year-end, i.e. 31 May for calendar-year FIPs; quarterly prepayment returns due within 15 days after each quarter-end, i.e. roughly 15 April, 15 July, 15 October and 15 January.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: RMB 200 million for related-party tangible-asset transactions; RMB 100 million for related-party intangible-asset and financial-asset transactions; RMB 40 million for other related-party transactions in a single fiscal year, triggering contemporaneous Local File documentation. Master File is expected where the consolidated global related-party transaction volume of the group exceeds RMB 1 billion.

Economic substance scope: The PRC tax authorities will apply the substance-over-form doctrine and the General Anti-Avoidance Rule under EIT Law Article 47 to an FIP that carries on bulk port stevedoring. Core Income-Generating Activities (CIGA) for the trade are operational dispatch, labor deployment, cargo handling, berth management, safety control and terminal maintenance at the PRC port. Hong Kong does not apply Caribbean-style ESR, but the Hong Kong partner must have genuine personnel, premises, risk-bearing capacity and management functions in Hong Kong to claim treaty benefits. A paper HK entity whose CIGA are actually performed in Mainland China will be stripped of treaty protection and recharacterized as a PE in China.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Transfer Pricing Mispricing

Statutory sanction: Under EIT Law Articles 41 and 46 and the Law on Tax Collection and Administration Articles 32, 63 and 64, the SAT will make a transfer pricing adjustment, impose additional CIT at 25%, add a daily late-payment surcharge of 0.05%, and impose a penalty between 50% and 500% of the underpaid tax. The entity may be included in the public tax-related dishonesty blacklist and face exit-control measures.

Director personal liability: The general partner and the executive partner/de facto director who approved the mispricing are jointly and severally liable for partnership debts under the PRC Partnership Enterprise Law Articles 2, 33 and 39. If the evasion exceeds RMB 100,000 and 10% of taxes payable, the officer may be convicted under Criminal Law Article 201 and sentenced to up to 7 years imprisonment, with fines and confiscation; personal assets may be attached and travel bans or exit restrictions will be imposed.
Shell Company / Treaty Abuse

Statutory sanction: The treaty benefit will be denied under SAT Announcement 2015 No. 60 and EIT Law Article 47; the payment is recharacterized as China-source income taxable at the statutory 10% WHT, with back tax, 0.05% daily late surcharges and an additional penalty of 50% to 500%. If the arrangement constitutes intentional tax evasion or false beneficial ownership declarations, the matter is referred to criminal investigation.

Director personal liability: Directors or de facto managers of the HK shell and the FIP can be held personally liable for the unpaid tax and civil restitution. They may be blacklisted and banned from exiting Mainland China. If the scheme is treated as tax evasion, Criminal Law Article 201 applies with imprisonment up to 7 years; if money laundering through the shell is established, Criminal Law Article 191 carries imprisonment up to 10 years and fines.
AML / Unlicensed Port Settlement Fraud

Statutory sanction: This violates the PRC Anti-Money Laundering Law Articles 20 and 21 and Criminal Law Article 191, triggering confiscation of proceeds, fines and possible revocation of the statutory port terminal operating license for ISIC 5224 stevedoring. Tax authorities will add the full undercharged VAT and CIT with penalties of 50% to 500% under Article 63 of the Tax Collection and Administration Law.

Director personal liability: Controlling executives who operate the settlement scheme face money-laundering criminal liability under Criminal Law Article 191 with imprisonment up to 10 years and personal fine. They are also subject to civil asset restitution, entry/exit travel bans, tax-related blacklisting and, for the FIP, unlimited joint liability of the general partner under the Partnership Enterprise Law when partnership assets are insufficient.
