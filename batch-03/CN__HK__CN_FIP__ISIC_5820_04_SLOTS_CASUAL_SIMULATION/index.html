None under Hong Kong law. The Limited Partnership Ordinance does not prescribe any minimum paid-up capital, and no blocked escrow account or statutory capital deposit is required. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA), accessible via the 'National Electronic Tax Bureau' (eTax portal) at https://etax.chinatax.gov.cn, with local counterpart administration at the municipal district tax service hall within the registered address jurisdiction.

E-government filing portal

For a Foreign-Invested Partnership (FIP) established under the PRC Partnership Enterprise Law and the Foreign Investment Law, the primary tax identifier is the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), automatically obtained upon completion of business registration with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR). Thereafter, the FIP must complete taxpayer registration with the competent tax authority through the 'New Electronic Tax Bureau' by submitting the partnership registration certificate, articles of partnership, a copy of the foreign investor's passport or company registration certificate, proof of the registered address (e.g., lease agreement and property ownership certificate), and the bank account opening permit or basic deposit account information. The tax authority will issue an electronic tax registration form and confirm the tax classification. For foreign non-resident partners or foreign enterprises without a permanent establishment in China that have Chinese-source income, a Non-Resident Taxpayer Identification Number (NTN) must be applied for through the withholding agent using the Tax Withholding Filing Form for Contracts (Contract Filing for Withholding Tax), with supporting documentation of the contract and the non-resident entity's foreign registration certificate.

Pass_Through_Eligible

None. China does not provide a check-the-box election under domestic law for partnership taxation. VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 annual assessable turnover for compulsory general taxpayer registration; foreign-invested partnership is subject to small-scale taxpayer registration from initiation if below this threshold, and a non-resident digital supplier triggers VAT registration when cumulative assessable digital services sales to Chinese customers exceed RMB 500,000 in any rolling 12-month period. Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is available for exported software publishing and digital services under STA Notice 2016 No. 36 and related implementing circulars, provided the service is supplied to an overseas recipient (outside mainland China), the actual consumption takes place wholly offshore, the contractual payment is received in foreign exchange from outside China, and the exporter is a general VAT taxpayer. Cross-border supply of simulated casino and social slots game publishing to Hong Kong SAR users can qualify as an offshore software-and-information-technology service only where the FIP has obtained approval for the relevant tax-exempt or zero-rated cross-border service filing and the destination of the digital service is substantiated as Hong Kong SAR. The FIP must complete the cross-border service exemption or zero-rating filing with the competent tax authority using the prescribed registration platform. Digital gaming services supplied through app store platforms may be zero-rated where the contractual service recipient is the overseas app store operator or a Hong Kong consumer, and where the payment chain and platform statements show foreign currency remittance minus platform commission.

China operates the 'Golden Tax Phase IV' digital infrastructure with mandatory 'Full Digital Electronic Invoice' (Full Digital E-Invoice / Shu Dian Piao) issuance and real-time transmission of invoice data to the STA's central electronic tax database upon issuance. The issuance system uses a unique nationwide invoice number, anti-fraud cryptographic stamping, and automated conformance checking of invoice limits against the taxpayer's prior declaration and tax credit rating. The FIP must apply for electronic invoice quotas and issuance authorizations through the eTax platform, and all sales must be recorded using the prescribed Goods and Services tax classification codes. No alternative paper invoice is permitted unless obtained from the tax authority in extraordinary cases. The platform payment rails (Apple IAP and Google Play Billing) must generate transactional records reconciling with invoice issuance for VAT filing obligations; if the FIP uses the foreign multinational platform as the collection agent, the contractual arrangement and platform reports must evidence the underlying trade for VAT and foreign exchange compliance.

For claimed export zero-rating or VAT exemption, the tax authority may conduct a post-refund audit requesting the entire documentation bundle. Failure to produce FIRC or equivalent foreign exchange receipt within 180 days from the date of export, or within the extended time approved, triggers reversal of the zero-rating, output VAT clawback with statutory late-payment interest at 0.05% per day, and a penalty of 50% to 500% of the tax underpaid if fraud or negligence is established. Fraudulent zero-rating declarations may be prosecuted as tax evasion under the PRC Criminal Law Article 204, with imprisonment and fines; an enterprise in violation may be deregistered as a general taxpayer or barred from future VAT zero-rating and export refund facilities.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Hong Kong operates a freely convertible currency regime with no exchange controls and no approval requirement for current or capital account remittances; the HKD is anchored through the Linked Exchange Rate system at between 7.75 and 7.85 per US dollar and multi-currency accounts are freely allowed. By contrast, mainland China applies a rigorous dual-tier system: current account convertibility is permitted after documentary verification, while capital account conversion, including equity injections into a Foreign-Invested Partnership, requires SAFE registration, a capital verification report by a certified public accountant, and a designated foreign exchange capital account that restricts the use of funds to the registered business purpose. Profit distribution and partnership remittances from a mainland FIP to an HK partner must be supported by audited financial statements, tax clearance certificates, and a written application through the bank for SAFE-affirmed repatriation. Non-financial RMB cannot be freely delivered from onshore China to Hong Kong; offshore CNH can be accumulated or hedged through the HK market. The practical convertibility position is therefore fully free in HKSAR, fee-based and document-controlled in mainland China, and non-deliverable for pure RMB capital arbitrage across the border.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Mainland China: export foreign currency receipts must be repatriated and verified through the SAFE trade-in-goods monitoring system, generally within 90 days from the date of export or the contractual settlement period; there is no fixed mandatory conversion ratio into RMB, but proceeds must be deposited in a designated foreign exchange settlement account or converted to RMB based on authentic trade background. Hong Kong: no surrender requirement, no statutory conversion ratio, and no timeline for retention of export or sales proceeds in HKD or foreign currency.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) and State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE); Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) for Hong Kong bank and clearing channels

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Offshore fintech EMIs incorporated outside Hong Kong and outside mainland China are not viable as primary treasury rails because they lack a SAFE licence for onshore FX and RMB settlement and generally do not hold a Hong Kong deposit-taking licence under the Banking Ordinance; using them to hold client funds would be unlawful in a regulated context. HKMA-authorized virtual banks such as ZA Bank, Mox Bank and Livi Bank may be used as secondary operating accounts, but they are not classified as offshore EMIs and still require the same corporate KYB plus sector review. For a medium-risk gaming-adjacent FIP, virtual banks may decline the relationship if the social casino classification is ambiguous; an MSO licence under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Ordinance would be required for any non-bank remittance business. The recommended structure is therefore a primary Tier-1 commercial bank account and, at most, one licensed virtual bank for contingency liquidity.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Group payments should be routed as SWIFT MT103 from an HK Tier-1 bank to the mainland FIP's SAFE-designated foreign exchange capital account, with the remittance purpose coded as equity contribution to a foreign-invested partnership and the SAFE registration number stated in the field-72 narrative. The onshore receiving bank will perform capital verification, compare the contribution to the registered partnership capital schedule, and allow RMB settlement only after tax and SAFE documentation checks. For capital movements initiated inside Hong Kong, no HKMA approval is required; the bank will apply CDD and request the investment agreement, source-of-funds questionnaire and declaration of commercial rationale. For a medium-risk gaming-adjacent FIP, virtual banks may decline the relationship if the social casino classification is ambiguous; an MSO licence under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Ordinance would be required for any non-bank remittance business. The recommended structure is therefore a primary Tier-1 commercial bank account and, at most, one licensed virtual bank for contingency liquidity.
