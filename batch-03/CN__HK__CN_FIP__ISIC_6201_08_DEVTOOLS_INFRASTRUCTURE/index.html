None. Hong Kong SAR does not provide a check-the-box or entity classification election. VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None (Hong Kong SAR imposes no general VAT, GST, or sales and use tax).

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable. No indirect-tax turnover threshold exists because there is no VAT/GST/sales tax. Every person carrying on a business in Hong Kong SAR must register under the Business Registration Ordinance (Cap. 310) regardless of turnover, unless expressly exempted.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable. Hong Kong SAR has no VAT/GST, so there is no 0% export VAT rate, no input VAT refund mechanism, and no statutory export zero-rating claim. This is a direct-tax territorial-source exemption, not an indirect-tax zero-rating.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Chinese renminbi is broadly convertible for current-account items such as software service exports, API subscription fees, and routine cross-border trade payments, but remains strictly managed on the capital account. The FIP must complete a SAFE foreign investment registration through a designated bank before any non-resident capital contribution is credited. Inbound capital is held in a Foreign Exchange Capital Account and can be settled into RMB under the capital-account settlement framework, but conversion back into foreign currency requires a genuine trade, refinancing, or exit event. Profit repatriation by the foreign partners is permitted only after annual audited financial statements, full corporate income tax and VAT settlement, and proof of no unpaid SAFE or tax liabilities. Although China has abolished mandatory percentage surrender of export proceeds, all foreign currency receipts arising from the FIP's current-account business must be repatriated to China, credited to a domestic foreign exchange settlement account, and supported by authentic transaction documentation. By contrast, the Hong Kong treasury leg has full currency convertibility under the linked exchange rate system, enabling free movement of HKD, USD, and offshore CNH; however, CNH converted into onshore RMB must enter through a licensed bank or the Cross-Border Interbank Payment System with a valid underlying.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no fixed statutory percentage of export proceeds that must be sold to domestic banks under SAFE's current current-account administration regime. Export-related foreign exchange receipts of the FIP must be repatriated to China through a licensed bank and may be either retained in a foreign exchange settlement account or settled into RMB at the FIP's discretion. The standard trade verification window requires that export proceeds be collected within the contractually agreed payment term; if collection occurs later than 90 days after the customs export declaration, the FIP must file a deferred collection registration in SAFE's Trade-in-Goods Monitoring System. Similarly, advance receipts received more than 30 days before customs declaration must be registered. Banks apply authenticity and compliance screening against customs declarations, contracts, invoices, and bills of lading before crediting or converting proceeds.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) through the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) for the mainland-incorporated Foreign-Invested Partnership; the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) supervises the HKD offshore clearing leg but does not impose FX controls.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Direct fintech EMI viability for the mainland FIP is Low. Stripe and Mercury are not licensed commercial banks in China and cannot maintain accounts for a Chinese legal entity under PBOC and SAFE rules; Mercury ACH is a US clearing rail and cannot be used for CNY-denominated operating or SAFE-required accounts. These rails are viable only as ancillary collection channels for a separate Hong Kong or US treasury vehicle that books SaaS revenue from global customers, then injects capital or shareholder loans into the mainland FIP through compliant FDI channels. Stripe Billing may be acceptable for international subscription collection in the HK treasury entity, but it must never hold the FIP's statutory capital or serve as the primary corporate bank account.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

16.5% Hong Kong Profits Tax for corporations; partnerships taxed as unincorporated businesses at 7.5% on the first HKD 2,000,000 of assessable profits and 15% on the remainder.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0%. Hong Kong does not impose dividend withholding tax under the Inland Revenue Ordinance; a distribution by the FIP to a non-resident partner is not subject to a statutory WHT, but the Hong Kong-source part of the partner's allocation is assessable to Profits Tax in the hands of the non-resident partner (unless a valid offshore/FSIE exemption applies).
