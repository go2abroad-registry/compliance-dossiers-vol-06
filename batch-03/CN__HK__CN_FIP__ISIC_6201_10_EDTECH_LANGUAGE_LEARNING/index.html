In Hong Kong, a virtual office address or commercial mailbox may be accepted by the Companies Registry and the Business Registration Office for basic registration purposes. However, the Inland Revenue Department and licensed banks generally reject a virtual office for an EdTech subscription entity without further commercial substance. E-government filing portal

Step 2: Under China's multi-license integration system, the tax registration is completed automatically upon market registration, and the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) issued by the SAMR functions as the FIP's Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN). Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Chinese mainland operates a managed floating exchange-rate regime in which current account transactions are generally freely convertible once a bank has performed genuine trade background verification, while capital account transactions remain strictly controlled and require prior registration, approval, and bank-level compliance review. For a Foreign-Invested Partnership (FIP) in China, foreign limited partners injecting equity capital must first complete foreign direct investment (FDI) information registration with SAFE or through the receiving bank's digital SAFE portal. The resulting registered capital is held in a foreign exchange capital account and can be settled into RMB only for permitted business expenditures, not for onward investment or speculative use. Profit remittances to non-resident partners are current account items but require a verifiable audited financial statement, a tax payment or tax exemption certificate, board or all-partner resolution, proof of paid-in capital, and confirmation that the distribution arises from genuinely earned operating profits. Convertibility of the current account is real but administratively conditional, and the bank or SAFE may freeze settlement if the EdTech subscription revenue flow appears inconsistent with the declared commercial scope. Overall, CNY is convertible for trade and service receipts, but capital account movement is gated by SAFE registrations and cannot be replaced by offshore fintech rails without substantial penalty and compliance risk.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export and cross-border service revenue receipts must be repatriated into China through a designated domestic bank and reported under SAFE's Trade Monitoring System; the statutory conversion model is market-based, meaning there is no fixed mandatory conversion ratio, and the FIP may choose to retain the foreign currency in its current account foreign exchange settlement account or convert it to RMB at the prevailing interbank market rate. Surrender-related verification must occur within the 90-day trade settlement reporting window, supported by valid contracts, invoices, customs data if physical goods are involved, and the bank's electronic verification of the related service delivery for EdTech subscriptions.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the People's Bank of China (PBOC), with SAFE provincial branches and designated domestic banks acting as the operational enforcement and verification channel

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low legality and viability for onshore mainland treasury operations. Foreign fintech EMI platforms such as Airwallex, Revolut Business, Currenxie, and similar digital banking providers generally hold financial licenses outside mainland China and are not licensed by the PBOC or SAFE to hold RMB basic deposit accounts, receive FIP registered capital, or clear domestic supplier payments in CNY. Such EMIs may only be used as offshore collection vehicles for foreign app store settlements if the FIP establishes a compliant outbound payment structure under a genuine services arrangement. However, the FIP's statutory capital contributions, SAFE-registered paths, tax filings, and all RMB-denominated operational flows must remain inside a domestic tier-1 bank account. Attempting to run the operating treasury through an offshore EMI will trigger severe AML blockages and capital control violations.

However, the Inland Revenue Department requires audited financial statements to accompany Profits Tax Return BIR52 where the partnership's annual gross turnover or gross income exceeds HK$2,000,000; for annual gross income of HK$2,000,000 or below, unaudited financial statements and tax computations may be accepted. There is no balance-sheet or headcount audit exemption threshold for partnerships in Hong Kong, but if the same business were operated through an incorporated Hong Kong company, an independent audit would be required under the Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622) irrespective of size.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% (standard corporate income tax at partner level; the Foreign-Invested Partnership itself is a pass-through vehicle and does not pay entity-level CIT)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% statutory corporate income tax withholding on outbound dividends and China-sourced non-resident distributions; reduced to 5% under the Mainland-HK DTA where the beneficial owner is a Hong Kong resident company directly holding at least 25% of the capital of the distributing entity; for the FIP, the entity itself is pass-through, so a cash remittance to a non-resident partner is taxed as partnership profit allocation rather than as a formal corporate dividend.
