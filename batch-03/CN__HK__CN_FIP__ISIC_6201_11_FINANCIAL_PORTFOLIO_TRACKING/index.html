Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Inland Revenue Department (IRD) of the Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region; online portal: eTAX (https://www.etax.gov.hk); business registration window: Business Registration Office of the IRD.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Confirm whether the foreign non-resident entity is a partner in a Hong Kong partnership carrying on business in Hong Kong; the partnership itself must register under the Business Registration Ordinance (Cap. 310) within one month of commencement of business, while a passive foreign partner without a separate Hong Kong place of business is not independently required to obtain its own Business Registration Certificate merely as an investor. Step 2: If the entity is a limited partner, ensure the limited partnership is first registered with the Companies Registry under the Limited Partnerships Ordinance (Cap. 321) before approaching the Business Registration Office. Step 3: Submit the prescribed Business Registration application to the Business Registration Office of the IRD, accompanied by the partnership registration certificate where applicable, a copy of the foreign partner's certificate of incorporation or equivalent documentary identity, the foreign partner's home-jurisdiction tax identification number, and proof of the authorized Hong Kong representative if the non-resident partner has no address in Hong Kong. Step 4: The IRD issues a Business Registration Certificate containing the Business Registration Number (BRN), which is the Hong Kong tax identification number used for the partnership's eTAX filing and all IRD correspondence. Step 5: The partnership files annual Profits Tax Returns in the partnership name; tax is assessed in the partnership name but is payable by the partners on their respective shares of Hong Kong-sourced profits, and the foreign non-resident partner may need to appoint a Hong Kong tax representative for notices and assessments. Step 6: No VAT/GST registration exists in Hong Kong, so there is no separate indirect-tax TIN to obtain.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

None. Hong Kong does not operate a check-the-box election regime and has no statutory hybrid-mismatch election; a partnership is automatically transparent for Profits Tax, and taxpayers may separately claim the territorial offshore-source exclusion under the Inland Revenue Ordinance (Cap. 112).

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No Hong Kong VAT/GST/sales tax; Hong Kong imposes only selective excise duties on a limited class of dutiable commodities under the Dutiable Commodities Ordinance (Cap. 109).

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable. There is no VAT/GST registration threshold in Hong Kong because no general consumption tax exists; business registration under the Business Registration Ordinance (Cap. 310) is required on commencement of business regardless of turnover, except for statutorily exempted activities.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable to indirect tax because Hong Kong has no VAT/GST to zero-rate. For cross-border software and financial portfolio-tracking services, the relevant relief is the territorial source principle under Hong Kong Profits Tax: profits derived from services performed and contracts concluded outside Hong Kong are treated as offshore-sourced and are not chargeable to Profits Tax. This is an income-tax exclusion, not a VAT zero-rating claim. Physical exports of goods are not subject to any Hong Kong VAT, and dutiable commodities remain subject only to excise duties where applicable.

  • Certified Hong Kong Export Declaration issued by the Census and Statistics Department (required for dutiable, controlled, or high-value physical consignments; not required for pure software services)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical movement of exported goods
  • Commercial invoice identifying the overseas customer, destination, export goods or services, and consideration
  • Bank Inward Remittance Advice or SWIFT credit confirmation evidencing receipt of export proceeds from outside Hong Kong

No mandate. Hong Kong does not operate real-time fiscal clearance, API-based tax e-invoicing, digital stamping, or continuous transaction controls. The eTAX portal is used for filing Profits Tax returns, Business Registration matters, and IRD correspondence, not for invoice clearance. Businesses may issue ordinary commercial invoices, and records supporting assessable profits must be retained for at least seven years under the Inland Revenue Ordinance (Cap. 112).

Because Hong Kong has no VAT, there is no VAT export zero-rating clawback or VAT fraud penalty. The audit exposure arises under the Inland Revenue Ordinance (Cap. 112): if the IRD rejects an offshore-source claim, the profits are assessed to Profits Tax with interest, and knowingly incorrect returns or fraudulent evasion can lead to prosecution, fines, imprisonment, and additional tax of up to three times the tax undercharged. No statutory FX repatriation deadline applies in Hong Kong; the 180-day window is the documentary benchmark for preserving bank and remittance evidence to substantiate the offshore receipt and to defend the export/offshore-source treatment on an IRD audit.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Chinese yuan is fully convertible for current account transactions under IMF Article VIII, but remains heavily managed and not freely convertible on the capital account. For this Hong Kong-invested FIP, inbound equity subscriptions from the Hong Kong SAR partner constitute foreign direct investment and require SAFE foreign exchange registration before any capital can be deployed. Once the foreign currency capital is received into a designated FDI capital account, the bank will settle the funds into RMB through a pending-settlement account, and every downstream payment from that account must be accompanied by supporting invoices, contracts, tax proof, and payment instructions. Profit remittance by the FIP to its Hong Kong partner is treated as current account repatriation and is generally permitted after annual financial audit, tax clearance and payment withholding; however, the foreign exchange bank will require an audited balance sheet, profit distribution resolution, tax settlement certificate and historical capital verification documentation. No full capital account convertibility exists, so any foreign debt from the Hong Kong parent, cross-border guarantee, or equity transfer in the FIP must be registered or approved under SAFE's macro-prudential framework. The practical result is that liquidity movements are legal but require documentary discipline.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For goods exports, export proceeds must be repatriated to a designated domestic bank and the export collection settlement completed within 180 days from the date of export customs declaration unless an extension is approved by SAFE. There is currently no mandatory surrender ratio: exporters may keep foreign currency in an export proceeds settlement account, voluntarily convert to RMB at market rate, or use the receipts to pay qualifying foreign currency expenditures. For services and software exports, including subscription receipts from Stripe and Apple IAP, the same repatriation principle applies under the service trade foreign exchange administration rules, and no fixed conversion ratio is imposed.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) / State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low. Offshore digital EMI providers such as Wise, Revolut, Airwallex or Currenxie are not licensed commercial banks or designated foreign exchange banks in mainland China and cannot legally operate the primary CNY operating account or the SAFE-registered FDI capital account for a PRC FIP. They may only be used outside China as auxiliary collection or expense accounts for the Hong Kong parent or offshore agent, and any eventual repatriation to the FIP must pass through an onshore licensed bank under a valid service or investment headroom. The FIP should not use an EMI for capital injection, profit remittance, or statutory FX settlement.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All cross-border capital injections must be executed by SWIFT MT103 from the Hong Kong investor to the FIP's designated FDI Foreign Exchange Capital Account at a SAFE-approved bank. The remittance message must contain the name of the PRC FIP, the SAFE FDI registration certificate number, and the transaction purpose code corresponding to foreign direct investment in a foreign-invested enterprise. Upon receipt, the bank verifies the remitter is exactly the registered foreign investor, checks the payment instruction letter and invoices for the future use of funds, converts the FX into RMB at market rate, and credits the funds to the RMB capital settlement pending-payment account. A registered Chinese CPA then issues a capital verification report, and every subsequent withdrawal from the pending-payment account must be supported by a payment instruction, contract, invoice and tax certificate, recorded in SAFE's system.Current-account outflows for licensing fees, cloud infrastructure, advertising and consulting are settled through SWIFT MT103 instructions from the onshore bank using the appropriate service trade custom declaration code, with each payment supported by a tax invoice, signed contract, withholding tax voucher and the bank's electronic tax payment proof. Commercial letters of credit and documentary collections are only relevant if the FIP purchases physical equipment or enters into goods trade; for a software portfolio-tracking application the primary rails are wire transfers and card-network settlements. Stripe and Apple IAP collections should be routed through an onshore licensed cross-border payment institution or the FIP's designated settlement bank, with aggregated consumer receipts reconciled against the service trade export declaration and monthly partner settlement statements.Onshore CNY/USD forward sale or purchase contracts with genuine trade or capital-inflow backgroundOnshore CNY/USD FX swaps used to match short-term receivables and payablesPlain-vanilla CNY/USD options for hedging the conversion of capital proceeds or subscription receiptsCross-currency interest rate swaps for approved foreign debt or financing exposure onlyOffshore NDFs are not permissible for the PRC FIP; the Hong Kong parent may use NDFs offshore outside the PRC boundary, and such instruments must not involve the FIP balance sheet

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

Hong Kong profits tax: 16.5% for corporate partners and 15% for individual partners; the Foreign-Invested Partnership (FIP) itself is tax-transparent and has no separate corporate income tax charge.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

Hong Kong statutory withholding tax on dividends paid by a Hong Kong entity: 0% (no domestic dividend WHT). If the FIP receives dividend distributions from PRC portfolio companies, the PRC statutory withholding tax on outbound dividends is 10%; under the China-HK DTA the rate is 5%, but only where the beneficial owner is a Hong Kong resident corporate holder of at least 25% of the PRC payer's capital. A transparent partnership itself generally cannot claim the DTA rate because it is not a 'resident' liable to tax in Hong Kong; the individual resident partners must separately substantiate treaty eligibility.
