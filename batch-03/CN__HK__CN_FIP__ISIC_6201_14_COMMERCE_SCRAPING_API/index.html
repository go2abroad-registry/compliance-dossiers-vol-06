Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A virtual office or commercial mailbox is permissible for initial registration with the Hong Kong Companies Registry, provided that the address is a physical street address in Hong Kong and a responsible individual accepts service of process and legal notices on the partnership. However, for a partnership engaged in public commercial web scraping and proxy API gateways, the Inland Revenue Department will scrutinize whether the partnership maintains real commercial substance, and a purely virtual address without local personnel, server hardware, or management control will be treated as a strong indicator that the partnership is a shell. Licensed banks in Hong Kong, particularly those expected to handle Stripe settlements and USDC cryptocurrency receivables, will reject a virtual office address for account opening and will instead require a physically leased office, a local partner or senior manager, and evidence of daily business operations. The tax authorities and banks will also look for a physical signage, utility bills in the partnership name, and availability of staff on site. Accordingly, a virtual office is permissible only in a transitional sense and should be replaced within 90 days by a serviced office or a dedicated physical office.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Inland Revenue Department (IRD) of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region; official electronic portal is eTax, accessible via GovHK at https://www.ird.gov.hk/etax/; Business Registration is administered by the Business Registration Office of the IRD under the Business Registration Ordinance (Cap. 310). Hong Kong operates as an offshore special territory with full fiscal autonomy under the Basic Law and is administratively separate from the State Taxation Administration of mainland China.

E-government filing portal

Foreign non-resident partners establishing a Hong Kong limited partnership obtain the statutory taxpayer identifier as follows: (1) register the partnership at the Companies Registry under the Limited Partnership Ordinance (Cap. 37), or under the Limited Partnership Fund Ordinance (Cap. 637) if constituted as a Limited Partnership Fund; (2) within one month of the commencement of business, submit the business registration application required by section 5 of Cap. 310 through the IRD eTax portal; (3) receive the Business Registration Certificate (BRC) bearing the eight-digit Business Registration Number (BRN), which serves as the partnership TIN for Profits Tax, employer returns and Stamp Duty; (4) appoint a tax representative ordinarily resident in Hong Kong to receive the Partnership Return (BIR Form 52) and all IRD notices; a partnership carrying on business in Hong Kong is also obliged to keep sufficient books and records at a place in Hong Kong under section 51C, Cap. 112; (5) respond to any IRD request for the partnership agreement, partner particulars and underlying commercial substance; no minimum capital is required and 100% non-resident ownership is permitted.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible - the Hong Kong limited partnership is fiscally transparent: profits are computed at partnership level but are taxable on the partners in their respective profit shares, and the partnership tax is collectible from partners jointly and severally; the two-tiered Profits Tax rate for unincorporated businesses applies (7.5% on the first HKD 2,000,000 and 15% above); under the territorial source principle in section 14, Cap. 112, only profits arising in or derived from Hong Kong are chargeable, so offshore-sourced web-scraping and API gateway revenues fall outside the charge. — Foreign non-resident partners establishing a Hong Kong limited partnership obtain the statutory taxpayer identifier as follows: (1) register the partnership at the Companies Registry under the Limited Partnership Ordinance (Cap. 37), or under the Limited Partnership Fund Ordinance (Cap. 637) if constituted as a Limited Partnership Fund; (2) within one month of the commencement of business, submit the business registration application required by section 5 of Cap. 310 through the IRD eTax portal; (3) receive the Business Registration Certificate (BRC) bearing the eight-digit Business Registration Number (BRN), which serves as the partnership TIN for Profits Tax, employer returns and Stamp Duty; (4) appoint a tax representative ordinarily resident in Hong Kong to receive the Partnership Return (BIR Form 52) and all IRD notices; a partnership carrying on business in Hong Kong is also obliged to keep sufficient books and records at a place in Hong Kong under section 51C, Cap. 112; (5) respond to any IRD request for the partnership agreement, partner particulars and underlying commercial substance; no minimum capital is required and 100% non-resident ownership is permitted.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible - the Hong Kong limited partnership is fiscally transparent: profits are computed at partnership level but are taxable on the partners in their respective profit shares, and the partnership tax is collectible from partners jointly and severally; the two-tiered Profits Tax rate for unincorporated businesses applies (7.5% on the first HKD 2,000,000 and 15% above); under the territorial source principle in section 14, Cap. 112, only profits arising in or derived from Hong Kong are chargeable, so offshore-sourced web-scraping and API gateway revenues fall outside the charge.

Available election forms

None - Hong Kong does not operate a check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election regime; the only relevant statutory options are: (i) the two-tiered Profits Tax rates, which apply automatically; (ii) the Unified Fund Exemption claim for an onshore Limited Partnership Fund under the Inland Revenue Ordinance, provided the fund satisfies the qualifying-transaction and investor conditions; and (iii) if the group also holds a mainland China Foreign-Invested Partnership, the PRC pass-through classification under the Enterprise Income Tax Law is likewise automatic and hybrid arrangements are addressed under PRC general anti-avoidance rules.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/sales-and-use tax system in force - the Hong Kong SAR has no general consumption tax; the only indirect levies are Stamp Duty (Cap. 117), Betting Duty (Cap. 108) and First Registration Tax on motor vehicles; mainland China VAT (standard rates 13%, 9%, 6%, and 3% for small-scale taxpayers) is outside Hong Kong fiscal authority and is administered separately by the State Taxation Administration.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - because Hong Kong has no VAT/GST statute, no economic turnover threshold triggers indirect tax registration; the sole mandatory registration is the Business Registration Certificate under Cap. 310, which arises from the first day of carrying on business irrespective of revenue.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The Hong Kong SAR has no export zero-rating provision because there is no VAT to zero-rate; for Hong Kong Profits Tax, offshore export income from digital data pipeline and proxy API services is excluded by proving the services were not rendered in Hong Kong under the source rule in section 14, Cap. 112, evidenced by non-resident customer contracts, off-Hong Kong performance, and in-bound foreign receipts; where the group's mainland China Foreign-Invested Partnership supplies software/IT services across the customs boundary, PRC VAT zero-rating applies under CaiShui [2016] No. 36 for qualifying cross-border services fully consumed outside China.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% (PRC Enterprise Income Tax applied at the corporate partner level; the Foreign-Invested Partnership is fiscally transparent under Cai Shui [2008] No. 159)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% statutory PRC WHT on dividends paid by a Chinese resident enterprise to a non-resident enterprise; reduced to 5% under the Mainland-Hong Kong DTA if the beneficial owner is a Hong Kong company directly or indirectly holding at least 25% of the paying enterprise.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Under SAT Public Notice 2016 No. 6, the annual related-party transaction threshold triggering mandatory Local File/Master File preparation is RMB 200,000,000 for tangible property transactions and RMB 100,000,000 for financial asset, intangible asset, service, or associated-party financing transactions.

Economic substance scope: While the PRC has no named Economic Substance Regulations statute, tax authorities apply the substance-over-form and General Anti-Avoidance Rule under PRC Enterprise Income Tax Law Articles 45-48 and SAT Public Notice 2017 No. 6. For a Hong Kong intermediary or partner, the Mainland-Hong Kong DTA, as amended by the 2019 Protocol, applies the Principal Purpose Test; a Hong Kong entity must demonstrate real presence, management and control, and core income-generating activities in Hong Kong. Shell vehicles with no substantive employees, premises, contracts, or decision-making in Hong Kong will be disregarded and profits will be re-sourced to mainland China.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Failure to Declare Cross-Border Digital Revenue

Statutory sanction: PRC Tax Administration Law Articles 32 and 63: recovery of unpaid tax plus 0.05% daily surcharge and a penalty from 50% to 500% of the underpaid amount; PRC Criminal Law Article 201: tax evasion with imprisonment up to seven years and criminal fines; Article 205 for unlawful VAT invoice issuance.

Director personal liability: Directors, general partners, and de facto managers are jointly and severally liable for restitution of evaded tax and pecuniary penalties; tax authorities may freeze personal bank accounts, impose an exit ban/blacklist preventing travel, seize assets, and refer the individuals for imprisonment up to seven years.
Shell Company / Treaty Abuse and Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: PRC Enterprise Income Tax Law Article 47 and SAT Public Notice 2017 No. 6 authorize the tax authority to recharacterize the enterprise structure, disregard the sham entity, deny DTA benefits, and reassess income at the 25% CIT rate or 10% withholding tax, plus Article 63 penalties for unlawful tax reduction.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced: individual directors and general partners are required to make civil restitution for the unpaid tax and interest; their assets are attachable; an exit ban/blacklist is imposed while the distressed tax liability remains; deliberate treaty abuse may be prosecuted as criminal tax fraud under PRC Criminal Law Article 201, resulting in imprisonment up to seven years.
AML and Personal Information / Cryptocurrency Payment Enforcement Failure

Statutory sanction: Personal Information Protection Law Article 66: fines up to RMB 50,000,000 or 5% of prior-year turnover, suspension of business, and revocation of licenses; Criminal Law Article 253-1: imprisonment up to seven years for unlawful collection, sale, or provision of personal information; Criminal Law Article 191: money laundering with penalties including imprisonment and confiscation.

Director personal liability: Directors, data protection officers, and managers who directed the unlawful scraping or crypto payment scheme bear civil liability for restitution to affected data subjects and the state; personal assets may be frozen, travel bans/blacklisting may be imposed, and responsible individuals face criminal imprisonment under Articles 253-1 and 191 with personal fines, the corporate veil being pierced for those who knowingly benefited.
