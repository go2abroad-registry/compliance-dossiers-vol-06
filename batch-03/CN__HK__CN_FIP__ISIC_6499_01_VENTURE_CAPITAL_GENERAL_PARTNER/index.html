Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A virtual office or commercial mailbox alone is not permissible for the venture capital general partner entity. The Hong Kong Inland Revenue Department and the Companies Registry require a physical registered office address in Hong Kong, not a post office box, for service of process and tax communications. Mainland tax authorities and banks will reject a mailbox address for a Foreign-Invested Partnership because it indicates absence of commercial substance, blocks on-site VAT inspection, and prevents capital-call wire verification. An outsourced serviced office with a dedicated meeting room, physical signage, and employee presence may be accepted only if the bank and the tax authority separately approve the substance arrangement in writing.

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Inland Revenue Department (IRD) of Hong Kong SAR; electronic portal: eTAX (https://www.gov.hk/en/residents/taxes/etax/)

E-government filing portal

A foreign non-resident entity establishing a Hong Kong partnership must engage a Hong Kong tax representative (if non-resident) and apply to the Business Registration Office (BRO) under the Inland Revenue Department (IRD) for a Business Registration Certificate (BRC) within one month of commencement of business. For partnership registration, the responsible person must file Form IRBR1 (Business Registration Application). Upon issuance of BRC, the Business Registration Number (BRN) is assigned and serves as the tax identifier for profits tax purposes. If the partnership is required to file Profits Tax Returns, the IRD may issue a separate Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) under the Inland Revenue Ordinance; in practice the BRN is the primary tax reference. Non-resident partners without a permanent establishment may be subject to withholding tax on certain Hong Kong-sourced income; they must register with the IRD for tax file numbers through the relevant IRD forms if any Hong Kong-sourced profits are derived.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Goods and Services Tax (GST) – Hong Kong operates no general VAT/GST; no sales tax is imposed. The only indirect tax relevant is the Business Registration Fee and, for certain specified goods, the Dutiable Commodities Ordinance duties, but no broad-based VAT/GST exists.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable – no GST/VAT registration threshold exists in Hong Kong; all businesses must obtain a Business Registration Certificate regardless of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable – Hong Kong does not levy VAT/GST, so there is no export zero-rating mechanism. All exports are outside the scope of Profits Tax unless the profits arise from Hong Kong-sourced trading activities. For the venture capital general partner, management fees received from offshore funds may be non-Hong Kong-sourced if the services are performed outside Hong Kong, and are taxed on territorial basis.

  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill
  • Commercial Invoice
  • Packing List
  • Evidence of payment / Foreign Exchange Repatriation: Inward Remittance Certificate (IRC) or bank statement showing foreign currency proceeds credited to the Hong Kong bank account

Not applicable – no electronic invoicing clearance or real-time fiscal stamping mandate under Hong Kong law. Businesses may issue ordinary invoices. However, for export documentation, an electronic Customs Export Declaration must be lodged with the Customs and Excise Department (C&ED) through the DTI (Digital Trade and Transportation Network) system or the Trade Single Window for goods subject to import/export declaration.

Since no VAT zero-rating applies, there is no VAT clawback mechanism. However, failure to lodge accurate export declarations may attract penalties under the Import and Export Ordinance (Cap. 60) and the Dutiable Commodities Ordinance. For profits tax, misdeclaration of offshore claims may result in additional tax, penalty up to 200% of tax undercharged, and prosecution under the Inland Revenue Ordinance.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Hong Kong operates as a separate monetary jurisdiction from mainland China, and the HKD is fully convertible under the linked exchange rate system with no prior approval or licensing requirement for current account or capital account transactions involving HKD, USD, EUR, or CNH (offshore RMB) held in Hong Kong. Capital remittances into and out of a Hong Kong registered partnership, whether FDI-style contributions, management fee distributions, or profit remittances, are unrestricted in currency movement and do not require surrender to any authority; the only friction comes from sanctions screening, AML compliance, and tax withholding obligations. Because this entity is a venture capital general partner managing early-stage technology allocations, the material exchange-control tensions arise when funds cross from Hong Kong into mainland China: SAFE registration, a dedicated RMB capital account for the foreign-invested partnership, purpose-code verification, and possible quota or scope limitations on capital account conversion would then apply. Hong Kong has no currency inconvertibility risk; the CNY remains a managed float requiring a genuine underlying trade or investment transaction for onshore settlement. Overall liquidity is deep in HKD, USD, and CNH, while mainland RMB restrictions create operational friction that must be managed through a licensed Tier-1 bank with a mainland clearing network.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Hong Kong law does not impose statutory export proceeds surrender or mandatory conversion ratios. A Hong Kong VC GP may retain, use, or convert all export-related foreign currency receipts freely. If the partnership or its controlled portfolio companies generate export revenue from mainland China, SAFE current account rules require repatriation and bank verification within the statutory period, generally no later than the date specified in the underlying trade contract or within 90 days of the customs export declaration absent an approved extension, and enterprise proceeds may be held in a designated foreign exchange account or settled into RMB. There is no universal surrender quota in Hong Kong.

Central bank supervision: Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA); for any China-mainland foreign exchange command, the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the People's Bank of China (PBoC) remains the competent authority.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low as the primary treasury rail for a medium-risk enhanced-due-diligence VC GP. Hong Kong does not legally bar authorised stored value facilities or money service operators from serving corporates, but an EMI cannot provide regulated escrow or custody, capital-call trust banking, documentary credit, commercial letter of credit services, or broad cross-border correspondent clearing. An EMI may be used only for minor administrative expense accounts and is not acceptable for LP capital injection, portfolio company deal wires, or investor distributions. Core treasury must sit with a licensed Tier-1 commercial bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Foreign LP capital should enter the Hong Kong GP's account through SWIFT MT103 payer-to-payee wires or MT202 COV correspondent clearing, with full beneficiary reference. Since Hong Kong has no exchange controls, no prior HKMA or SAFE approval is required at intake. If the fund later capitalises a mainland China foreign-invested partnership or portfolio company, the GP must complete SAFE foreign investment information reporting through the receiving bank, open a dedicated RMB capital account in mainland China, and provide the remittance advice, capital verification report, and payment order. The receiving bank will map the transaction to the applicable SAFE balance of payments purpose code for foreign-invested partnership capital injection, and RMB settlement from that capital account will be permitted only for approved operating, investment, or merger and acquisition purposes.Portfolio disbursements and general operating outflows should use SWIFT MT103 with detailed beneficiary data for capital call funding and management fee payments. Interbank treasury sweeps between the GP and fund custodians should use MT202 COV. For transactions involving physical technology components, inventory, or structured trade where a payment against title documents is required, commercial letters of credit issued under UCP600 via MT700 and documentary collections on D/P or D/A terms are the appropriate clearing rails. Mandatory Audit Threshold Mandatory Audit for All Entities

Turnover: Under the Hong Kong Companies Ordinance (Cap.622), every active company must appoint a certified public accountant holding a practising certificate as auditor and must deliver audited financial statements annually; there is no size-based turnover, balance-sheet, or headcount de-minimis for active companies, so any balance-sheet total above zero, any gross revenue above zero, or any employee count above zero triggers the audit unless the dormant private company exemption under Cap.622 s.395 applies, which requires an absence of significant accounting transactions. For a registered Foreign-Invested Partnership or limited partnership under Cap.37, the partnership itself is not automatically required to file audited accounts, but a mandatory independent certified CPA audit is legally triggered by any of the following: (a) the general partner is a Hong Kong incorporated company that is not dormant; (b) the general partner is licensed or registered by the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) for Type 9 asset management or any regulated activity and must file audited accounts and a capital adequacy return under the Securities and Futures (Financial Resources) Rules, including the HK$5 million (approx. USD 641,000) minimum paid-up capital threshold and liquid capital requirements; (c) the venture capital fund is authorized as a collective investment scheme under the Securities and Futures Ordinance and the fund manager or custodian is required to produce audited financial statements; or (d) the limited partnership agreement, fund constitutive documents, or external investor commitment requires an audit. The applicable accounting framework is HKFRS, which fully aligns with IFRS; an eligible private entity may apply HKFRS for Private Entities or the HK SME-FRS where not publicly accountable, while partnership standalone tax-basis accounts are prepared under HKFRS/HKGAAP with pass-through profit and loss allocations. Books and records must be retained for at least 7 years under the Inland Revenue Ordinance (Cap.112 s.51C).

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15.0% standard Profits Tax rate applicable to an unincorporated body (Foreign-Invested Partnership); two-tiered concession reduces the rate to 7.5% on the first HKD 2,000,000 of assessable profits with the balance charged at 15.0%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% – Hong Kong imposes no withholding tax on dividends and does not tax dividend distributions or profit repatriation; a FIP may freely distribute profits to its China resident partners without any Hong Kong dividend withholding

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The return is issued by IRD on 1 April (bulk issue) or upon commencement and is statutorily due within one month of the date of issue; IRD block extension schemes for e-filing and taxpayers represented by authorized tax representatives extend the due date (e.g., for 31 December year-end non-corporate businesses, an extended deadline is generally granted); the FIP must file even if no tax is payable

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Transfer Pricing Documentation (Local File and Master File) applies to specified transactions with related parties that are 'substantial': HKD 50 million or more for transfer of property (other than financial assets and intangibles) and for provision of services, and HKD 5 million or more for financial transactions and intangibles; a Country-by-Country Report is required for MNE groups with consolidated group revenue of at least HKD 6.8 billion; the FIP's management fee, carried interest, and capital call arrangements with the PRC parent, the fund, and underlying portfolio companies must be benchmarked and documented even below the mandatory thresholds

Economic substance scope: Hong Kong does not maintain a statutory Economic Substance Regulation regime comparable to BVI/Cayman; however, the territorial source principle functions as an equivalent gatekeeper: profits are chargeable only where the CIGA are performed in Hong Kong. For a venture capital general partner FIP, economic substance is evidenced by an SFC Type 9 asset management licence, a physical Hong Kong office, a minimum of two full-time qualified investment professionals, at least HKD 2 million of annual Hong Kong operating expenditure, and records of investment committee and board meetings held in Hong Kong. Maintaining this substance is a precondition for the 0% carried interest concession and for defending the FIP's Hong Kong resident status in DTA claims; absence of substance exposes the entity to recharacterization under the GAAR (IRO s.61A) and potential deemed Hong Kong sourcing of income

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion via False Offshore Claims

Statutory sanction: On conviction, a fine of HKD 50,000 and three times the amount of tax undercharged, plus default interest; imprisonment of up to three years; the IRD may issue estimated assessments at 15% and deny deductions for false documentation

Director personal liability: Managing partners and the general partner of the FIP are jointly and severally liable for penalties; willful evasion may trigger disqualification, travel restriction orders preventing departure from Hong Kong pending settlement, and for the China resident general partner, cross-border criminal exposure under the PRC Criminal Law for tax evasion
Unlicensed Asset Management (SFO Breach)

Statutory sanction: Criminal liability under the Securities and Futures Ordinance s.114: a fine of HKD 5,000,000 and imprisonment for up to seven years on indictment; civil liability for restitution to investors; the SFC may issue cease-and-desist orders and bar the FIP from fund management

Director personal liability: Each officer, managing partner, and shadow director can be prosecuted for abetting the unlicensed activity; personal disqualification orders under the Securities and Futures Ordinance and reputational injury across the venture capital market
Shell Company / Substance Abuse and Treaty Shopping

Statutory sanction: The IRD may disregard the arrangement under the GAAR (IRO s.61A) and impose additional tax at 16.5%; the PRC tax authority may deny DTA benefits and levy 10% dividend withholding, 10% interest withholding, and 10% royalty withholding (increased penalties of up to 50% of the underpaid tax); adverse publicity and possible deregistration

Director personal liability: General partners remain personally liable for the FIP's obligations; directors of the corporate general partner face breach of fiduciary duty claims and personal assessment for unpaid taxes under the piercing-the-veil doctrine; travel bans may be imposed pending tax settlement
AML / CDD Violations (Anti-Money Laundering Ordinance)

Statutory sanction: Under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Ordinance, fines of up to HKD 1,000,000 per offence and imprisonment of up to two years for non-compliance by licensed corporations; the SFC may suspend or revoke the Type 9 licence; referred institutions may freeze the FIP's bank accounts and disrupt capital calls

Director personal liability: Approved persons and senior managers bear individual criminal responsibility; compliance officers and money laundering reporting officers face personal fines and imprisonment; in egregious cases, the Generalized Scheme of Arrangements and insolvency proceedings may expose the general partner to claims for restitution
Misclassification of Carried Interest / Misuse of Concession

Statutory sanction: Disallowance of the concession and recapture of Profits Tax at the applicable two-tier rates (7.5%/15%) on the full carried interest; penalty of up to 3x the tax undercharged and HKD 50,000 fine; interest charges on the underpayment from the original due date

Director personal liability: The managing general partner and the investment manager are severally liable for the underpaid tax; false declarations on the tax return constitute a criminal offence punishable by imprisonment; PRC tax authorities may also recharacterize the carried interest as PRC-source service income of the China resident partner, triggering additional CIT and Individual Income Tax exposure
