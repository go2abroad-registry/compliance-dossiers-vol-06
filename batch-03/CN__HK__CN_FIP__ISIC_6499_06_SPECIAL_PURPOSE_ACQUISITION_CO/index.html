Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A virtual office or commercial mailbox is not absolutely prohibited for Hong Kong registered office purposes, but it is operationally unacceptable for this SPAC-class entity. The Inland Revenue Department, HKMA, and licensed banks will typically reject a pure virtual address for a blank-check entity because it lacks demonstrable physical substance, management presence, and adequate records-keeping capacity. For the mainland PRC FIP, SAMR and PBOC require a fixed, non-residential registered address capable of receiving official correspondence and permitting unannounced inspection; a commercial mailbox without lease or staffing fails this test. Accordingly, a physical Hong Kong office, even if small or in a serviced-office building, is mandatory for bank onboarding, trust escrow arrangements, and any public-market listing preparation; virtual office solutions may only serve as an auxiliary back-office and cannot be the sole registered office.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Hong Kong Special Administrative Region Inland Revenue Department (IRD) with online eTAX portal; for People's Republic of China mainland tax nexus, the State Taxation Administration (STA) with provincial electronic tax declaration portals.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Confirm whether the Foreign-Invested Partnership carries on business in Hong Kong. Step 2: Within one month of commencing business, submit the statutory Business Registration application to the Business Registration Office of the Hong Kong IRD, using the eTAX portal or paper channels, together with the partnership agreement, partner identification documents, registered address proof, and any authorization to an appointed agent. Step 3: After processing, the IRD issues a Business Registration Certificate and Business Registration Number (BRN) which serves as the Hong Kong taxpayer identifier. Step 4: If a permanent establishment or fixed place of business is created in mainland China, register with the competent STA tax bureau within 30 days of establishment via the electronic tax registration procedure, providing the unified social credit code certificate or equivalent, the partnership registration certificate, the partnership agreement, bank account opening permit or notice, and the authorized person's identity documents. Step 5: The STA will issue a statutory taxpayer identification number (TIN) and classify the partnership for VAT purposes; the same TIN is used for enterprise income tax filings, VAT filings, and withholding filings. Step 6: Any separate registration with the Hong Kong Companies Registry is not a substitute for the IRD Business Registration, and any PRC market registration must be followed by the STA tax registration before the partnership can lawfully issue invoices in mainland China.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible — the Foreign-Invested Partnership is a fiscally transparent partnership for Hong Kong Profits Tax and PRC enterprise income tax on partnerships. Profits are assessable in the hands of the partners. The Hong Kong territorial source principle limits taxability to profits arising in or derived from Hong Kong, while PRC source rules apply to income derived from mainland China. The entity is not eligible for corporate-style entity-level taxation unless a separate corporate wrapper is interposed.

Available election forms

None. Hong Kong does not provide a check-the-box election, and the PRC tax system requires partnerships to be treated as flow-through entities for enterprise income tax. Individual partners may elect personal assessment under the Hong Kong Inland Revenue Ordinance to aggregate salaries, profits and property income for progressive tax relief, but this is not a classification election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mainland China: VAT registration is immediate upon commencement of taxable activities; mandatory general VAT taxpayer status is triggered when annual taxable turnover exceeds RMB 5,000,000 (small-scale taxpayer threshold). Hong Kong: no VAT registration threshold, but a Business Registration Certificate is required on commencement of business regardless of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under PRC VAT law, zero-rating applies to genuine cross-border exports of goods where the exporter is a general VAT taxpayer registered for export tax refunds, the goods are physically declared to Chinese customs for export, the export invoice matches the customs declaration, logistics documents and payment, and foreign exchange is received or legally repatriated. Qualifying cross-border services listed by the Ministry of Finance and STA may also be zero-rated or VAT-exempt. For Hong Kong, no VAT is levied, so exports are outside the scope of indirect tax and no zero-rating filing is made to the IRD.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Hong Kong operates a fully convertible currency regime under the HKMA's currency board system, with the Hong Kong dollar pegged to the U.S. dollar in a 7.75-7.85 band. There are no surrender requirements, capital account controls, or profit repatriation restrictions in Hong Kong. Mainland China, however, maintains a managed floating exchange rate regime with a two-tier convertibility structure: the RMB is fully convertible for current account transactions (trade in goods and services, dividends, interest) upon submission of supporting documents, but capital account transactions, including outbound direct investment, portfolio flows, and cross-border financing, require prior approval or registration with SAFE. For a China-based Foreign-Invested Partnership (FIP) seeking to inject capital into an HK SPAC vehicle, the outbound investment must be recorded under the Outbound Direct Investment (ODI) framework or through qualified outbound investor schemes (QDLP/QDIE). Profit remittances from the HK SPAC back to mainland FIP partners are subject to Chinese foreign exchange registration, tax filing with the State Taxation Administration, and verification of the genuine nature of the funds. Onshore CNY remains restricted, while offshore CNH is freely traded in Hong Kong; the two are distinct and cannot be fungible without a dedicated regulatory gateway. All treasury operations must therefore maintain strict separation between onshore CNY settlement and offshore CNH/USD/HKD flows to avoid inadvertent creation of an unregulated cross-border channel.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For the Mainland China operating segment, SAFE mandates that export proceeds in foreign currency must be repatriated into a domestic foreign exchange settlement account within 90 days of the customs export declaration, and 100% of such proceeds must be surrendered or settled into RMB unless the exporter is licensed to retain FX under a valid exemption. Advance export receipts must be swept into the designated account within 30 days of collection. In contrast, the Hong Kong SAR, as the offshore special territory, imposes no surrender rules; all foreign currency receipts may be retained indefinitely and deployed freely across the escrow and operational treasury frameworks without mandatory conversion or repatriation deadlines.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Bank of China (PBOC) for sovereign mainland China cross-border RMB and FX approval matters; Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) as the de facto central bank and banking regulator for the Hong Kong SAR's offshore CNH clearing and U.S. dollar interbank settlement infrastructure.

Strike-off trigger

Mandatory Audit Threshold Mandatory Audit for All Entities

Turnover: Hong Kong imposes no size-based audit exemption for an incorporated SPAC or a licensed financial entity; therefore the quantitative threshold is effectively zero. Audit is compulsory from the first accounting period regardless of balance sheet size, turnover, or headcount if the vehicle or any of its general partners is incorporated under the Companies Ordinance, Cap 622, in which case a practising certified public accountant must be appointed under sections 405 and 411 and audited financial statements must be laid before the annual general meeting within 9 months of the financial year end. If the Foreign-Invested Partnership is an unincorporated Limited Partnership Fund under Cap 637, audit becomes compulsory when the partnership agreement, the SFC licence condition, or an HKEX requirement mandates it; in practice a SPAC sponsor and investment manager licensed by the SFC must submit audited annual financial statements under the SFC Code of Conduct and the Securities and Futures (Financial Resources) Rules irrespective of size. The HKEX SPAC regime requires public accountability, so the vehicle cannot use the HKFRS for Private Entities framework; it must prepare financial statements under HKFRS or IFRS and the auditor must apply Hong Kong Standards on Auditing. No headcount or revenue threshold can exempt the SPAC or its licensed manager from audit.
