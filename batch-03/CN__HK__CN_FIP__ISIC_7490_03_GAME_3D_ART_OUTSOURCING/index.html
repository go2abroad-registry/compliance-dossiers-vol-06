Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A virtual office or commercial mailbox is generally not acceptable for a limited partnership in Hong Kong for tax and banking purposes. The Inland Revenue Department requires a physical address for correspondence and service of documents. Banks typically reject virtual office addresses for account opening due to anti-money laundering concerns. For a game art outsourcing business, a physical office is recommended to demonstrate commercial substance and to satisfy the 'place of effective management' test for tax residency. However, a registered office can be a service provider's physical address if it is a real office, but it must not be a mere virtual mailbox. The partnership must maintain a physical presence, including staff and business records, to avoid being classified as a shell entity.

None. The Limited Partnership Ordinance (Cap. 352) imposes no minimum paid-up capital and no blocked escrow account requirement. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China; taxpayer registration, VAT account activation and electronic filing are administered through the STA's Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax) platform and the Golden Tax System Phase IV.

E-government filing portal

For a Foreign-Invested Partnership (FIP) in the PRC established by one or more non-resident foreign partners, the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) printed on the partnership Business License constitutes the statutory Tax Identification Number (TIN) and VAT registration number. Procedure: (1) Foreign and domestic partners conclude a partnership agreement; foreign partners must provide notarized and authenticated incorporation certificates, registered office particulars, and director/partner identity documents. (2) Apply for name pre-approval and file the FIP formation documents with the local office of the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) in the place of business registration. (3) Upon issuance of the Foreign-Invested Partnership Business License, tax registration is completed automatically via the government data-sharing system; the local competent tax authority assigns the VAT taxpayer profile using the USCC as the primary taxpayer identity. (4) Activate the taxpayer account on the STA e-Tax platform using the USCC and a China tax digital certificate (CA/USBKey), then complete the supplementary taxpayer information file, including legal form (Partnership_Limited), industry (ISIC 7490), and business scope. (5) Elect VAT general taxpayer status if annual taxable turnover is expected to exceed RMB 5,000,000 or voluntarily from inception; otherwise remain a small-scale VAT payer. (6) Because the FIP is fiscally transparent, the partnership itself does not file enterprise income tax; each partner is responsible for its own corporate/individual income tax on allocable shares under the relevant PRC tax regime.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

None. The PRC tax law does not provide a check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election for a Foreign-Invested Partnership; pass-through income tax treatment is mandatory. The only optional status election is the VAT general taxpayer vs. small-scale taxpayer choice, which is confined to indirect tax administration.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 annual taxable sales for mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration; all FIPs obtaining a business license must complete tax registration irrespective of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rated VAT for exports applies to physical goods exported outside the PRC customs territory, and under Caishui [2016] No. 36 and the STA's cross-border taxable service implementation rules, to cross-border modern services including design and development services supplied to non-resident customers and wholly consumed outside China. For game 3D art and rigging outsourcing, the FIP must demonstrate that (a) the overseas recipient is non-resident, (b) the services are performed from China but enjoyed and used entirely outside China, (c) the consideration is settled in foreign currency through lawful bank channels, and (d) the service contract, delivery records, and milestone acceptance evidence establish the offshore supply. Goods export zero-rating likewise requires a formal customs export declaration and physical departure of goods.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bill of Entry) for physical export consignments
  • Bill of Lading or air waybill as transportation evidence
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or bank foreign-exchange settlement receipt
  • Zero-rated export service contract and scope-of-work signed by overseas recipient
  • Commercial invoice and zero-rated VAT invoice
  • Evidence of non-resident recipient status (e.g., registry extract, tax residence certificate)
  • Proof of foreign consumption (e.g., digital delivery logs, acceptance certificates, user location data)

China operates a real-time invoice clearance and fiscal-control mandate under the Golden Tax System Phase IV. All VAT special/ordinary invoices issued by the FIP must be created through the STA e-Tax/electronic invoice platform using an approved digital certificate; invoice data are transmitted to the tax authority in real time, each invoice receives a statutory digital fiscal stamp/anti-counterfeit watermark, and any manual alteration or offline generation of invoices without tax-authority clearance is prohibited. For cross-border zero-rated supplies, the e-invoice must be annotated with the zero-rate basis and linked to the export transaction file.

During audit, the STA may re-open any zero-rated export claim. If the required bundle is incomplete, contradicted by customs or foreign-exchange data, or proves to be fabricated, the refunded VAT will be clawed back in full, late payment surcharge will accrue at 0.05% per day from the original refund date, and an evasion penalty of between 50% and 500% of the VAT evaded will be imposed. For fraudulent export refund claims, criminal liability under the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China on tax evasion and fraudulent export tax refunds may be pursued, including penalties up to life imprisonment in severe cases.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The RMB is not freely convertible under the capital account. Current account service-trade receipts, including 3D game art outsourcing fees, are generally convertible once genuine trade documentation is presented and the remittance is processed through a SAFE-regulated bank using the online trade monitoring system. Capital account inflows, such as a foreign partner injecting capital into the China-registered Foreign-Invested Partnership, require SAFE foreign direct investment registration and the funds may only be settled for verified business purposes. Profit and dividend remittances to the Hong Kong parent or non-resident partners require audited financial statements, corporate income tax payment certificates, a SAFE-compliant remittance form, and proof that no liquidation or tax arrears exist. The absence of a statutory minimum capital requirement does not waive these controls. Export proceeds from overseas clients must be repatriated into a domestic foreign currency account, although the entity may retain the foreign currency or convert it into RMB at its discretion. The entity must not use parallel channels, personal accounts, or unlicensed payment platforms to move capital across the border, because such structures are treated as illegal cross-border fund transfers and will trigger immediate AML suspension.

Export proceeds surrender rules

China has abolished mandatory export foreign exchange surrender ratios; there is no fixed statutory conversion percentage. Export receipts from game art outsourcing services must be credited to a SAFE-authorized foreign currency current account and matched against the underlying service contract, commercial invoice, delivery acceptance record, and bank transaction reference. For service trade, the generally monitored collection window is the contractual due date, with a bank-level convention that unexplained delays exceeding 180 days from the commercial due date require written explanation and supporting escalation evidence. Retained foreign currency balances are permitted but remain subject to bank monitoring and SAFE reconciliation, and any conversion into RMB must be completed with the recorded purpose code of service trade income.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC); State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) as the designated foreign exchange administration authority

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Offshore fintech EMI platforms such as Revolut, Wise, and Airwallex cannot replace PRC-licensed commercial banks for the China-registered FIP. These EMIs are not licensed by SAFE or the PBOC to operate domestic foreign exchange settlement, capital verification, or cross-border reporting accounts in mainland China. They may be used only as an ancillary collection tool for low-value overseas client payments, but they are legally barred from serving as the primary treasury clearing rail for the PRC entity. The Hong Kong parent may maintain a Hong Kong treasury account with a licensed bank or virtual bank, but that account cannot process onshore SAFE-regulated capital flows for the FIP. Viability rating: Low for PRC entity clearing; Medium only for HK-adjacent ancillary collections.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital from the Hong Kong parent into the China FIP must be routed by SWIFT wire transfer from a licensed Hong Kong bank account to the FIP's SAFE-registered capital account at a PRC Tier-1 commercial bank. The remittance must cite the SAFE FDI or foreign-invested partnership registration number and the approved balance-of-payments transaction code for direct investment capital injection. Upon arrival, the bank will verify the funds against the partnership agreement and SAFE filing. If the partners elect to recognise contributed capital, a Chinese certified public accountant must issue a capital verification report. The funds may only be settled into RMB for bona fide operating expenses such as salaries, rent, software licensing, and outsourced artist fees. The entity must not route shareholder funds through PayPal, digital wallet accounts, or unnamed intermediary accounts.For cross-border trade settlements, the FIP should use SWIFT MT103 payment messages for high-value disbursements to overseas 3D artists, subcontractors, and software vendors. Cross-border RMB payments may be settled through CIPS via the PRC relationship bank. Commercial letters of credit and documentary collections are not appropriate for game art services because performance is intangible and delivery acceptance is not documentary in the traditional trade sense. For low-value platform receipts from global clients, PayPal may be used as an ancillary receivable rail, but it must not be used as an outbound vendor settlement rail because it does not provide a robust structured AML and counterparty verification trail. Mandatory Audit Threshold Small Company Audit Exemption Regime

Turnover: Under Hong Kong Companies Ordinance Cap. 622 ss.359-363, a private company is exempt from statutory audit in a financial year if it satisfies any two of the following three conditions: (i) total annual revenue does not exceed HK$20,000,000 (approximately US$2,564,000); (ii) total assets do not exceed HK$10,000,000 (approximately US$1,282,000); and (iii) the number of employees does not exceed 100. Public companies, listed companies, banking/insurance/MPF companies, companies required to be audited by their constitution or by another ordinance, and companies in non-exempt groups are not eligible for the exemption and must appoint a Hong Kong certified public accountant (CPA) to conduct an audit under HKFRS. For this FIP, which is a pass-through partnership rather than a corporation, there is no Companies Ordinance audit filing; however, the Inland Revenue Ordinance Cap. 112 requires the partnership to keep accounting records sufficient to verify profits for at least 7 years, and the IRD may expressly demand audited or certified accounts if the partnership's gross income, volume of offshore-source claims, or scale of transactions exceeds what can be readily verified from unaudited accounts. In practice, an FIP with annual gross receipts above HK$5,000,000 (approximately US$641,000) or with an active offshore profit claim should secure an HKICPA-certified audit or independent accountant's report to avoid a protective IRD assessment and the inability to substantiate a profits tax exemption.
