Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None. Hong Kong law imposes no minimum paid-up capital for a Foreign-Invested Limited Partnership and no blocked escrow or callable-capital account requirement. Contributions are determined by the written Partnership Deed and may be in cash, in-kind digital content assets, software licences, or property. If a Chinese mainland investor remits outward FDI, PRC State Administration of Foreign Exchange filing and bank remittance procedures apply, but no Hong Kong escrow is required.

Funding & unlock path: Not Applicable - no civil defense fire permit, industrial structural inspection, or occupational clearance is required for a pure digital publishing and syndication enterprise. If an ordinary commercial office is later occupied, the landlord's Fire Services Certificate under the Fire Services Ordinance (Cap. 95) and Building Ordinance compliance should be verified, but no separate industrial permit attaches.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA), administered via the National Electronic Tax Administration Platform (e-Tax) at etax.chinatax.gov.cn and provincial electronic tax bureaus; for the Hong Kong SAR offshore coordination, the Inland Revenue Department (IRD) of Hong Kong SAR via the GovHK eTAX portal.

E-government filing portal

For the mainland China foreign-invested partnership (FIP), the general partner or appointed representative must first complete the partnership establishment registration with the State Administration for Market Regulation through the Integrated Online Government Services Platform and obtain the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which is the statutory taxpayer identification number for all PRC tax matters. Within 30 days of issuance of the business license, the FIP must complete tax registration on the e-Tax platform, linking the USCC to VAT, enterprise income tax, payroll withholding, contract stamp duty and financial reporting. Foreign non-resident partners without a Chinese permanent establishment that receive PRC-source income, including profit distributions, royalties or service fees, must enter the non-resident enterprise tax registration or temporary tax registration module on the e-Tax platform, upload a certified foreign registration certificate, constitutional documents and the income-generating contract, obtain a Chinese non-resident taxpayer ID, and appoint a withholding agent before the central bank foreign-exchange repatriation clearance can be obtained.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

None. The PRC does not provide a check-the-box election; an FIP is pass-through for enterprise income tax, and no hybrid mismatch election is available. Foreign entity classification will be tested under PRC general anti-avoidance rules.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 annual taxable sales; mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration applies above RMB 5,000,000, while entities at or below that threshold may remain small-scale VAT taxpayers. Digital webtoon syndication services supplied domestically are generally subject to VAT at the 6% modern-services rate, and qualifying cross-border supplies may be zero-rated.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For exported goods, 0% VAT applies only where the FIP is a VAT general taxpayer, the goods are physically exported from PRC customs territory under a customs export declaration in the FIP's own name, an overseas sales contract exists, and export proceeds are received from outside the PRC and reported into the SAFE monitoring system. For the digital webtoon syndication business, cross-border digital content supplied to foreign subscribers through Apple IAP or Google Play Billing can be zero-rated or exempt only if the service is consumed outside mainland China and falls within the published catalogue of zero-rated or exempt cross-border services; the app-store periodic settlement statements must demonstrate that the revenue is for chapter unlocking and subscription access by non-PRC users, and the platform commission and fee invoices must be treated separately.

  • For physical export: Export Goods Customs Declaration (customs export declaration form) in the exporter's own name; for digital-only export: cross-border service agreement and app-store settlement report in lieu of a customs declaration
  • Bill of Lading or transport waybill for physical export; for digital-only export, the platform payout statement from Apple IAP or Google Play Billing
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or SAFE-compliant foreign exchange receipt record from the settlement bank

All VAT invoices, including fully digitalized electronic invoices, must be issued from the National Electronic Invoice Service Platform, transmitted in real time to the STA under the Golden Tax System Phase IV, and carry the national anti-fraud digital tax-control stamp and unique invoice code. The FIP must reconcile invoice data, VAT returns, customs export declarations and SAFE foreign-exchange receipts before any export zero-rating or refund can be processed.

For any export zero-rated claim, the STA and China Customs cross-match electronic invoices, customs export declarations, logistics waybills, bank settlement records and SAFE foreign-exchange returns. Unverified or fraudulent claims are denied, the VAT refund or exemption is clawed back as underpaid tax, late-payment interest accrues at 0.05% per day, administrative penalties range from 50% to five times the tax amount, and fraudulent export refund cases are referred to criminal enforcement under the PRC Criminal Law. There is no limitation period for recovery of false export tax refunds.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The RMB is fully convertible on current account but remains administratively managed on capital account. Digital webtoon platform revenue collected from Apple IAP and Google Play Billing is service-trade income and may be repatriated into the mainland Foreign-Invested Partnership's domestic foreign-currency account through a licensed bank after tax and authenticity verification. Capital contributions from the Hong Kong partner into the mainland FIP are capital-account transactions requiring SAFE foreign direct investment registration; the funds must be received into a foreign-exchange capital account or an RMB capital account and settled only for genuinely documented operating purposes. Profit remittances to the Hong Kong partner are current-account outbound payments after annual audit, Chinese tax settlement and withholding by the FIP, and issuance of applicable tax certificates; no separate SAFE administrative approval is required if the remittance is genuine, correctly classified, and supported by audited accounts. In practice banks will review the SAFE registration certificate, service contracts, tax vouchers, source-of-wealth evidence from the Hong Kong investor, and the economic substance of the digital content distribution arrangement before releasing funds.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Mainland China does not impose a mandatory surrender ratio on export foreign-exchange receipts; a mainland FIP may retain up to 100% of eligible current-account receipts in a domestic foreign-currency account or settle them voluntarily. For goods-trade exports the proceeds must be repatriated and verified through a licensed bank within the prescribed period, typically 90 days from customs declaration for standard open-account terms, with longer tenors requiring SAFE or trade-monitoring registration. For digital webtoon syndication, receipts are services-trade proceeds and carry no statutory conversion ratio; banks require an SAFE service-trade declaration code, the underlying distribution agreement, platform payout statements, and an MT103 credit trail to evidence economic reality before permitting any transaction.

Central bank supervision: The People's Bank of China (PBOC) is the ultimate monetary authority, while the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) is the operative foreign-exchange control regulator for mainland China; the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) is the offshore clearing and currency oversight authority for the Hong Kong treasury leg.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Not viable for the mainland FIP. Foreign digital EMIs such as Wise, Revolut Business, Mercury, and Payoneer are not licensed by the PBOC to accept deposits, hold capital-account funds, or act as a primary treasury for a mainland-registered foreign-invested partnership. These EMIs may be used only by a separate Hong Kong treasury vehicle for offshore aggregation. The mainland FIP must maintain its capital escrow and operational liquidity with a licensed domestic commercial bank; a licensed PBOC cross-border payment institution may only support ancillary low-value trade remittance mechanics and cannot substitute for the bank's compliance and capital-verification function.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The Hong Kong investor must remit funds by SWIFT MT103 through an HKMA-regulated bank (for example Bank of China Hong Kong or HSBC) to the mainland FIP's foreign-exchange capital account at a designated domestic tier-1 bank. Each remittance must reference the SAFE foreign-investment registration certificate and carry the appropriate balance-of-payments direct investment purpose code for capital contribution to a foreign-invested partnership, together with a copy of the investment agreement and the capital contribution schedule. The receiving bank will perform a second-layer SAFE compliance check, settle the FX in RMB only against genuine contemporaneous expenditure, and record the deposit in the SAFE information system. A Chinese licensed CPA must then issue a capital verification report certifying the subscribed capital, the contributed amount, the exchange rate, and the date of receipt before the FIP can deploy the funds for production expenses, content acquisition, or operating costs.For recurring royalty, licensing, translation, and content-production payments to overseas vendors, the primary clearing rail is SWIFT MT103 using the FIP's domestic bank account, supported by the underlying agreement, invoice, tax withholding certificate, and service-trade FX declaration. Commercial letters of credit and documentary collections are reserved for physical-goods transactions; for digital webtoon syndication they are rarely used. If the FIP also sells printed comic books or merchandise, a UCP-600-compliant commercial L/C or documentary collection can be used for those goods trades, with the customary transport documents, packing lists, and customs declarations attached. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Not viable for the mainland FIP. Foreign digital EMIs such as Wise, Revolut Business, Mercury, and Payoneer are not licensed by the PBOC to accept deposits, hold capital-account funds, or act as a primary treasury for a mainland-registered foreign-invested partnership. These EMIs may be used only by a separate Hong Kong treasury vehicle for offshore aggregation. The mainland FIP must maintain its capital escrow and operational liquidity with a licensed domestic commercial bank; a licensed PBOC cross-border payment institution may only support ancillary low-value trade remittance mechanics and cannot substitute for the bank's compliance and capital-verification function.

Mandatory Audit Threshold Small Company Audit Exemption Regime

Turnover: A Hong Kong limited partnership is not automatically subject to the statutory audit regime of the Companies Ordinance (Cap 622). There is no balance-sheet or headcount audit trigger for a partnership in Hong Kong. The principal quantitative trigger is turnover-based for taxation purposes: where the gross income of the partnership exceeds HK$5,000,000 (approximately USD 641,026) for the basis period, the Inland Revenue Department practice requires the Form BIR52 to be supported by audited/certified financial statements prepared by a Hong Kong certified public accountant. If any partner is a Hong Kong-incorporated company, that corporate partner is independently required to have its financial statements audited annually under Cap 622 irrespective of size, turnover, or headcount; no small-company audit exemption exists other than for dormant companies. In addition, lending or commercial syndication agreements commonly impose an independent audit covenant at an even lower threshold.
