Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Inland Revenue Department (IRD) of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region; online electronic portal: eTAX (www.etax.gov.hk). Business Registration Office is the operational unit for entity registration.

E-government filing portal

For a foreign non-resident entity establishing a Hong Kong joint-venture operation: (1) if the JV is incorporated outside Hong Kong, first file a non-Hong Kong company registration with the Companies Registry under the Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622) Part 16 within one month of establishing a place of business; (2) apply for Business Registration with the Business Registration Office under the Business Registration Ordinance (Cap. 310) through the eTAX Business Registration module, accompanied by the certificate of incorporation, the foreign company registration certificate, the articles of association or equivalent constitutional documents, the Hong Kong registered office or principal place of business address, director and shareholder identification, and evidence of the local business address; (3) receive the Business Registration Certificate containing the eight-digit Business Registration Number (BRN), which serves as the functional tax identification number for profits tax, employer tax, customs and bank reporting; (4) if a branch office is opened, register the branch and obtain a separate branch certificate; (5) no separate VAT/GST registration is required because Hong Kong has no general indirect tax.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None. Hong Kong does not provide check-the-box, look-through, or hybrid mismatch elections. The Inland Revenue Ordinance (Cap. 112) imposes profits tax on an entity-level, territorial-source basis, and claims for treaty relief or foreign tax credits are made by way of administrative relief, not entity classification elections.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No_General_Indirect_Tax (Hong Kong SAR imposes no VAT, GST, or sales and use tax; the only indirect imposts remaining are excise duties under the Dutiable Commodities Ordinance (Cap. 109) and Hotel Accommodation Tax (Cap. 348) where applicable).

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable. There is no VAT/GST threshold. Every person carrying on business must obtain a Business Registration Certificate under Cap. 310 irrespective of turnover; low-turnover businesses may apply to the Commissioner for exemption from the business registration fee only if the statutory small-business conditions are met.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable. Hong Kong has no VAT/GST regime, so there is no export VAT zero-rating claim or indirect tax refund mechanism. For profits tax purposes, export income may be treated as non-taxable offshore income if the profit arises outside Hong Kong; the taxpayer must prove that the goods were exported from Hong Kong and that the essential trade contracts and operations were performed outside Hong Kong.

  • Customs export declaration lodged with the Customs and Excise Department under the Import and Export (Registration) Regulations (Cap. 60E)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing export of the wooden puzzle goods
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or equivalent bank foreign-exchange receipt record evidencing repatriation of sale proceeds

No mandatory real-time fiscal clearance, continuous transaction control, API furnishing, or anti-fraud digital stamping regime exists for indirect tax invoicing in Hong Kong. E-invoicing is voluntary. For exported goods, the statutory evidence base is the Customs and Excise Department export declaration/cargo manifest filing and the underlying commercial shipping documents under the Import and Export (Registration) Regulations (Cap. 60E).

While there is no VAT clawback mechanism, the Inland Revenue Department may reopen assessments within six years for ordinary cases, and without time limit for fraud or negligence. Under section 82A of the Inland Revenue Ordinance, a taxpayer who makes an incorrect return or fails to furnish information may be charged additional tax up to 300% of the tax undercharged; criminal prosecution is possible under sections 80 and 82 for tax evasion. Exporters should retain customs declarations, shipping documents, sales contracts, and bank remittance records for at least seven years to substantiate any offshore income treatment and to avoid retrospective assessment.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The PRC maintains a managed floating RMB exchange-rate system; current account items are substantially convertible while capital account items remain controlled. For this Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture, the initial registered capital injection by the Hong Kong shareholder is capital account activity requiring SAFE FDI registration and a dedicated foreign-exchange capital account at a domestic designated bank. Once capital is settled into RMB, routine operating payments for wood, laser consumables, packaging, wages, utilities and inventory are current account payments supported by genuine invoices and contracts, and are typically released by the bank after standard screening. Profit and dividend remittance to the Hong Kong shareholder is permissible after the entity has completed statutory annual auditing, paid enterprise income tax, and compiled a lawful profit-distribution resolution; banks will require the audited financial statement, tax settlement certificate, SAFE registration and confirmation that the distribution does not exceed distributable reserves. There is no fixed statutory surrender percentage for export receipts, but export proceeds must be repatriated to the PRC within the prescribed timeframe unless an offshore retention approval is obtained. In practice, operational clearing in mainland China requires a tier-one licensed commercial bank; overseas EMIs and non-bank PSPs are not licensed to open capital accounts or perform SAFE verification for a PRC JV.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE rules and the current trade-content verification framework, general-trade export foreign currency receipts must be repatriated into the JV's designated foreign exchange current account or settled into RMB within 180 days from the date of customs export declaration, unless SAFE has approved overseas retention for working capital. There is no mandatory conversion ratio; the JV may keep the proceeds in a foreign currency account or settle any portion into RMB at its discretion. Every receipt must be matched to the corresponding customs export declaration, commercial invoice, packing list and transportation evidence through the bank's Trade Monitoring and Verification System; a mismatch, late repatriation, or false trade background can trigger a SAFE penalty and the freezing of the non-compliant account.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China, operating under the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The Hong Kong shareholder wires the registered capital in USD or HKD via SWIFT MT103 to the JV's designated foreign exchange capital account at the selected tier-one bank, using the transaction purpose code for foreign direct investment capital injection and quoting the SAFE FDI registration code in the remittance field. The receiving bank verifies that the remitter is the registered shareholder, checks the amount against the SAFE capital account system, and then settles the funds into RMB through a capital-account foreign-exchange settlement application. The settled RMB is credited to the JV's Basic Deposit Account and may be used only for approved operating purposes such as purchasing laser cutting machinery, timber materials, packaging, paying wages, rent, utilities, and third-party logistics. The full receipt and settlement record must be reported into SAFE's capital project information system; any change to the investment amount, shareholding ratio, or planned use requires a fresh SAFE filing before disbursement.For imports of laser consumables and raw timber, the JV should use a SWIFT MT103 foreign-currency trade remittance or a cross-border RMB wire through the bank if the supplier accepts CNY; for large or escalating orders, an irrevocable Commercial Documentary Letter of Credit under SWIFT MT700 or a Documentary Collection with terms DP or DA provides payment security while preserving title documents. For exports of wooden puzzles to Amazon, Stripe and B2B marketplaces, the JV must use a licensed cross-border e-commerce payment institution or the bank's e-commerce collection product, aggregating card receipts and settling them into the designated onshore current account; direct SWIFT MT103 payments are suitable for B2B wholesale buyers and should be matched with the customs declaration, invoice and packing list. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File mandatory if annual related-party transactions exceed CNY 20 million (purchase or sales with related parties) or other thresholds: financial asset transfers > CNY 100 million, intangible asset transfers > CNY 10 million; Master File is mandatory if the group's annual consolidated revenue exceeds CNY 5 billion and cross-border related-party transactions exceed CNY 100 million (or other specified thresholds).

Economic substance scope: China applies the substance-over-form doctrine. The JV must show that the Hong Kong parent has actual decision-making and economic substance for any intercompany arrangement (loans, IP licensing, service fees). The Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) of Hong Kong apply only to Hong Kong resident entities; if the Hong Kong shareholder is within the scope of HK ESR (e.g., passive holding business), it must demonstrate sufficient economic substance in Hong Kong. Core Income-Generating Activities (CIGA) for a Hong Kong holding company include shareholder meetings, board decisions, dividend approval, and loan management; failure to maintain substance may expose the Hong Kong shareholder to HK ESR penalties and also to Chinese CFC/anti-abuse rules if the JV distributes passive income.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion – False Business Expense (Inflated Related-Party Royalties)

Statutory sanction: Article 63 of the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection (tax adjustment) applies: the STA may re-assess taxable income based on the arm's length principle; if the adjustment exceeds certain amounts, a penalty of up to 5 times the underpaid tax (Article 63) and daily surcharge of 0.05% applies.

Director personal liability: Directors of the JV who knowingly approve the sham royalty are jointly liable for the underpaid tax and penalties; the court may pierce the corporate veil under PRC Company Law Article 20 (abuse of corporate personality to evade tax), causing director personal property to satisfy the JV's liabilities; criminal prosecution under PRC Criminal Law Article 201 (tax evasion) can result in up to 7 years' imprisonment and an unlimited fine for individuals directly responsible, plus exit bans (travel restrictions) and a blacklist in China's anti-tax-evasion credit system.
Shell Company / Abusive Treaty Shopping

Statutory sanction: Under the PRC General Anti-Avoidance Rules (GAAR) (Article 120 of CIT Law Implementation Regulations) and the OECD Multilateral Instrument if applicable, the treaty benefit is denied, and the shareholder's dividend is reclassified as taxable at 10%; a 20% penalty on the under-withheld tax may be imposed under Article 63 of the Tax Collection Law (if fraud is proven).

Director personal liability: The directors of the Hong Kong shareholder may face personal liability under HK law for breach of duty (Sec. 465 of Companies Ordinance – if reckless signature on false substance declarations); they are exposed to disqualification from acting as directors for up to 15 years, civil asset restitution claims, and criminal liability under HK Crimes Ordinance if they make a false declaration to obtain a tax residency certificate, carrying imprisonment of up to 14 years and a fine; in mainland China, the beneficial owner is subject to a blocked exit (出境限制) and a denial of all treaty benefits, and the STA can look through the shell to the ultimate beneficial owner.
AML / Payment Channel – Circumvention of Foreign Exchange Control

Statutory sanction: Under PRC Foreign Exchange Management Regulations (Article 39), evasion of mandatory foreign exchange repatriation is punishable by confiscation of the illegal gain and a fine of up to 30% of the illegal amount; under the Anti-Money Laundering Law, the financial institution may report a suspicious transaction to the Public Security; the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) may impose a civil fine of up to RMB 300,000; serious cases constitute the crime of illegal foreign exchange transfer under Criminal Law Article 190.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign the fake foreign exchange settlement documents are personally criminally liable for 'False Foreign Exchange Lending/Remittance' under Criminal Law Article 190 (up to 10 years' imprisonment) and under Article 405 (if they are government involved) or Article 175 (secondary offense); they face civil tort liability to the JV for losses; SAFE can impose a lifetime ban on serving as the legal representative or director of any foreign-invested entity in China; the director's personal bank accounts in China can be frozen, and a travel ban is automatically issued pending payment of the fine or before criminal trial.
