Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; online filings are processed through the STA's Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax) national online service platform maintained by the provincial tax services.

E-government filing portal

The Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture (CN_JV_LTD) is a PRC-resident enterprise formed under the PRC Foreign Investment Law and Company Law. After the company is incorporated at the local Administration for Market Regulation, it is granted a Uniform Social Credit Code, which automatically serves as the enterprise's tax identification number and VAT taxpayer identification number. The joint venture must complete consolidated tax registration through the Electronic Tax Bureau within 30 days of obtaining its business license, using the Uniform Social Credit Code as the TIN. For the Hong Kong non-resident shareholder, no standalone registration is required for the shareholder merely to hold equity; however, before the JV distributes dividends or makes any payment of China-source income to the Hong Kong parent, the Hong Kong parent must obtain a Non-resident Enterprise Tax Identification Code through the competent STA office at the place where the income arises, or be identified in the withholding registration. The Hong Kong parent must also furnish a Hong Kong resident certificate if it intends to claim treaty benefits under the Mainland-Hong Kong Double Taxation Arrangement. The JV itself is not a pass-through vehicle: it files corporate income tax and VAT under its own TIN.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation. The JV is a PRC resident enterprise taxed on worldwide income under the Enterprise Income Tax Law at the standard 25% rate; all VAT obligations are borne by the JV. The Hong Kong SAR jurisdiction applies a territorial profits tax and has no VAT/GST, but Hong Kong treatment does not alter the PRC entity-level classification of the mainland JV.

Available election forms

None. The PRC tax system does not provide check-the-box entity classification elections or hybrid-mismatch elections. The JV may apply for preferential EIT treatment (e.g., High and New Technology Enterprise, small low-profit enterprise) by administrative application, but such applications do not change entity-level taxpayer status. The HK shareholder may elect to apply the reduced 5% dividend withholding rate under the Mainland-Hong Kong Double Taxation Arrangement if it meets the beneficial ownership and 25%+ holding conditions.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

All established enterprises must register for tax regardless of turnover; the Uniform Social Credit Code is the VAT TIN. The compulsory VAT general taxpayer registration threshold is annual taxable sales in excess of RMB 5,000,000. Below that threshold, the JV can remain a small-scale VAT taxpayer at a reduced collection rate, and monthly sales of RMB 100,000 (quarterly RMB 300,000) are exempt from VAT under current small-scale preferential policies.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

To claim the 0% export VAT rate for the manufactured epoxy-resin liquid-core RPG polyhedral dice, the JV must be the actual exporter, the goods must be physically declared to and cleared by PRC Customs and leave the PRC customs territory to an overseas destination (including Hong Kong SAR, which is outside the PRC customs territory for export purposes), and the JV must prove the sale, the real foreign buyer, the actual shipment, and the receipt of payment from outside China. The sale must be supported by a valid customs export declaration, an overseas transport document, a commercial invoice/contract, and foreign exchange receipt evidence. The export may be carried out under ordinary trade declarations or, for crowdfunded/online orders via Kickstarter/Stripe/PayPal, under cross-border e-commerce export customs clearance; every parcel must have a customs declaration rather than being shipped only as untracked fulfilment. The qualifying production exporter then uses the export tax refund (exemption, set-off and refund) procedure to obtain refund of the input VAT embedded in manufacturing costs.

  • Customs Export Declaration (export bill of entry / customs clearance form) bearing the export reference number, HS classification, quantity, unit value and actual export release date
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (for direct-to-backer crowd shipments, the international express courier's shipping manifest and detailed delivery tracking proving departure from PRC customs and delivery to the overseas customer)
  • Commercial invoice, sales contract or order record (including Kickstarter backer ledger, Stripe/PayPal transaction statements) identifying the overseas buyer and the RPG polyhedral dice sets sold
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / bank foreign-exchange receipt (the Chinese export verification equivalent) and the Stripe/PayPal settlement-payout reports showing that export proceeds were actually collected and repatriated to China within the statutory period

VAT invoices must be issued through the STA-managed digital electronic invoice platform (all-digital e-invoices), with real-time transmission to the taxation authority at the moment of issuance. The STA clears the invoice by validating the counterparty tax IDs, verifying the applicable rate, and applying a cryptographic anti-fraud digital stamp and unique invoice number. ERP/API machine-to-machine issuance is supported for high-volume sellers, while manual issuance on the Electronic Tax Bureau portal is permitted. Since export zero-rating is applied at the customs export declaration level, the e-invoice in an export transaction is normally not a zero-rated VAT invoice; the export price is declared as VAT-exempt for output VAT and the supported refund is recovered through the export tax rebate filing.

The STA will cross-match the customs export declarations with the e-invoices, logistics tracking events, bank FX receipts and crowdfunding/payment-aggregator settlement data under the Golden Tax Phase IV data warehouse. If the zero-rating/export refund is not supported by the required documentation, or if payment is not received within the 180-day repatriation period, the claimed zero-rating will be reversed, the output VAT will be clawed back with daily late payment surcharge, and an administrative fine of at least 50% and up to 5 times the tax evaded or falsely refunded may be imposed under the PRC Law on Administration of Tax Collection. Where false export documents are used intentionally to obtain a refund, the entity and responsible personnel face criminal liability for fraudulent export tax refund under the PRC Criminal Law, with penalties including imprisonment and, for especially large amounts, up to life imprisonment and confiscation of property.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The CN_JV_LTD is a PRC-resident joint venture and is therefore within PBOC/SAFE foreign exchange administration. The renminbi is convertible on current account items, so export proceeds from tabletop dice sales, logistics fees, royalties and normal trade disbursements may be settled through banks under authentic trade documentation. Capital account items remain restricted: the Hong Kong shareholder's capital contribution must enter through an FDI foreign exchange capital account, be registered with SAFE, and be supported by a capital verification report before deployment. Profit remittances to the Hong Kong parent are permitted as current account transfers only after annual audit, tax payment certificates and board resolutions are presented; remittance is not an automatic right. Export receipts must be repatriated to China and verified against customs declarations. There is no mandatory universal surrender ratio at present, but retention is capped by the SAFE-authorized settlement account ceiling and surplus balances must be converted into renminbi. The HKD leg is fully convertible under HKMA's linked exchange rate regime, but the PRC JV cannot circumvent PRC capital controls by routing through Hong Kong unless the underlying transaction is lawful and reported.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export proceeds earned by the JV must be repatriated into its designated Chinese settlement bank within 180 days from the date of export customs declaration unless a deferred-payment extension is filed through the SAFE trade monitoring system. At present no fixed mandatory conversion ratio applies to current-account export receipts; the JV may retain foreign currency in an approved foreign exchange settlement account up to the ceiling set by SAFE based on its verified trade turnover, and any amount above that ceiling must be settled into RMB. All receipts must be matched against customs export records and bank trade verification filings.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) and State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) for the mainland-resident Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture; Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) governs the separate HKD clearing corridor for the Hong Kong corporate layer.
