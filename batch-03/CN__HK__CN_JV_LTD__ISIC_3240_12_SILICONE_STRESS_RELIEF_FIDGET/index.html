Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Mainland China: State Taxation Administration (STA) through the national unified electronic tax bureau (e-Tax Bureau) accessible from the STA website (www.chinatax.gov.cn). Hong Kong SAR: Inland Revenue Department (IRD) through the eTAX online portal (www.etax.gov.hk).

E-government filing portal

Step 1: The Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture is first registered with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR), which issues a Business License containing the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC); the USCC is the statutory Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) for all PRC tax filings. Step 2: Tax registration is completed through the multi-registration integration system and by filing the taxpayer registration particulars with the competent STA office through the e-Tax Bureau. Step 3: VAT taxpayer status is determined at the same time; annual taxable sales exceeding RMB 5,000,000 trigger mandatory general VAT taxpayer status, while smaller enterprises may be registered as small-scale VAT taxpayers. Step 4: For a non-resident enterprise without an establishment in China but with China-source income, the non-resident enterprise must apply for a non-resident enterprise TIN with the competent tax authority of the withholding agent, or directly if no withholding agent exists; the application must include the home-jurisdiction tax residence certificate, the commercial contract, and the foreign entity registration certificate when treaty relief is claimed. Step 5: For the Hong Kong SAR vehicle, incorporation is effected at the Companies Registry, followed by an application to the Inland Revenue Department for a Business Registration Certificate (BRC); the BRC number functions as the Hong Kong taxpayer identifier. No VAT ID is issued or required in Hong Kong because the territory imposes no VAT or GST.

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mainland China: no de minimis turnover threshold for basic tax and VAT registration; all newly registered enterprises must complete VAT registration using their USCC. Annual taxable sales exceeding RMB 5,000,000 trigger mandatory general VAT taxpayer status; below that threshold, small-scale VAT taxpayer classification is available. Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Mainland China: export of physical goods qualifies for the zero rating and refund of input VAT if the exporter is a general VAT taxpayer, the goods are loaded and exported through a customs-supervised port, the export customs declaration is matched with the corresponding VAT and export-rebate declarations, and the export proceeds are repatriated through the banking system within the statutory foreign exchange period. Small-scale VAT taxpayers may use an export agent or obtain a refund under the simplified export-tax-rebate mechanism. Hong Kong SAR: no export VAT exists because Hong Kong has no indirect tax; goods exported from Hong Kong are outside the scope of any Hong Kong VAT, and profits derived from offshore export trading may be reported as offshore-sourced and not subject to Hong Kong profits tax if the economic activities and contracts are performed outside Hong Kong.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

RMB is fully convertible on current-account items such as trade-related receipts and payments for the JV's export of silicone fidget toys, subject to SAFE verification through designated banks. However, capital-account convertibility remains restricted: foreign-direct-investment capital contributions, cross-border financing, and profit repatriation must be registered or approved by SAFE. The JV must use the bank-managed foreign-investment system for capital account settlement, and each capital injection must have a clear economic purpose and a matching branch registration. Hong Kong, by contrast, maintains full convertibility under the linked exchange rate system, with the HKD free from controls. In practice, profit remittance from the Chinese JV to the Hong Kong shareholder requires audited financial statements, a formal shareholders' distribution resolution, corporate and tax registration certificates, and proof of Chinese enterprise income tax withholding (or treaty-reduced rate) on the dividends, and must be converted from RMB in a designated bank. Dividend payments cannot exceed accumulated distributable profit; capital-gain repatriation after liquidation is subject to additional tax certification. No surrender margin applies to export proceeds, but all export earnings must be repatriated and accounted for through SAFE channels.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE rules, export proceeds must be repatriated into the PRC through a designated foreign-exchange bank, generally within 180 days from the customs declaration date of the silicone fidget toy shipment unless an approved extension or contract-based exception exists. The JV may retain the foreign currency in its current-account foreign-exchange savings balance or convert it into RMB voluntarily at market rate; there is no fixed mandatory conversion ratio or obligatory surrender percentage because China removed the export-proceeds surrender requirement.

Central bank supervision: The People's Bank of China (PBOC), acting through the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE), is the FX authority for Mainland China, where the JV is resident. The Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) is the central banking authority for Hong Kong SAR, which applies no FX controls.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound FDI capital from the Hong Kong shareholder must be remitted via SWIFT MT103 to the JV's designated domestic Capital Foreign Exchange Account at a bank licensed to handle SAFE registration. The wire must state the purpose 'Equity injection for Sino-Foreign Joint Venture' and include the SAFE Foreign-Investment Registration Code, the investor's name, and the JV company name. The receiving bank completes the SAFE FDI base account registration, verifies the transaction against the approved total investment amount and joint-venture contract, and may then convert the funds into RMB inside the capital-settlement-to-operating-account system.
