Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China, administered through the National Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax) portal; for any Hong Kong SAR branch or separate HK presence, the Inland Revenue Department of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region issues the Business Registration Certificate and administers Profits Tax, but levies no VAT, GST or sales tax.

E-government filing portal

For the Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture, the Chinese and foreign shareholders complete business license registration with the State Administration for Market Regulation and obtain the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code, which is the statutory Chinese TIN and VAT registration number. The JV must complete tax registration with the locally competent State Taxation Administration office through the National Electronic Tax Bureau portal within 30 days of business license issuance, activate taxpayer files, and elect VAT general taxpayer or small-scale taxpayer status. For export sales, the JV must also finish the Ministry of Commerce foreign trade operator filing and China Customs enterprise registration before being eligible for export VAT exemption or refund. A foreign non-resident entity with no Chinese establishment is not issued the JV's Unified Social Credit Code; if it receives China-source income, the withholding agent files a non-resident enterprise tax registration and the non-resident is assigned a withholding tax identification number for tax treaty and reporting purposes. A Hong Kong corporate shareholder claiming treaty benefits should obtain a Hong Kong tax-resident certificate.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation: the Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture is a PRC tax-resident enterprise taxed on worldwide income under the Enterprise Income Tax Law and is not a pass-through entity. Hong Kong territorial source taxation applies only to any profits arising in or derived from Hong Kong if the JV establishes a Hong Kong branch or is otherwise within the Inland Revenue Ordinance source rules.

Available election forms

None comparable to a US check-the-box election; PRC entity classification is mandatory and follows legal form. The JV may elect the small and low-profit enterprise preferential Enterprise Income Tax rate if it meets statutory thresholds, and a small-scale taxpayer may elect the simplified VAT collection method. Hybrid mismatch elections are not available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

In mainland China, cumulative annual taxable sales exceeding RMB 5,000,000 (VAT-exclusive) triggers mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration; at or below RMB 5,000,000, the JV may remain a small-scale VAT taxpayer unless it opts into general taxpayer status. A general taxpayer applies the standard 13% rate to taxable goods supplies in China; export supplies are at 0% only when all statutory export conditions are met.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods physically exported from the customs territory of mainland China, including hard enamel lapel pins sold through Etsy, qualify for the 0% VAT export rate when the JV is a registered VAT general taxpayer and exporter, the goods are cleared through customs under an export declaration, title and risk pass to an overseas buyer, payment is received in foreign currency from outside mainland China, and export proceeds are repatriated to a designated foreign exchange bank. For cross-border retail parcels sold on Etsy, customs supervision mode code 9610 may apply; larger B2B export shipments use the standard export clearance process. The JV must retain evidence linking Etsy orders, payment rail settlements from Etsy Direct Pay or Stripe, logistics movement, and the customs declaration.

  • Customs Export Goods Declaration Form with export declaration number and customs release notation
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill, or for Etsy cross-border parcels an integrated cross-border logistics manifest, showing movement from mainland China to the overseas destination
  • Commercial invoice, packing list, and Etsy order report with overseas buyer information and foreign-currency sales price
  • Bank foreign exchange receipt advice or inward remittance advice from a SAFE-designated bank evidencing receipt of export proceeds from Etsy Direct Pay or Stripe settlements
  • Export contract or purchase order and, where VAT output credit is claimed, the VAT general taxpayer export rebate declaration filed through the State Taxation Administration module

The State Taxation Administration operates the Golden Tax Phase IV integrated VAT invoice administration system. VAT invoices must be issued through the approved electronic invoice service platform, each invoice receives a unique authorization number before issuance, and fully digitalized electronic invoices are uploaded in real time to the State Taxation Administration central database. The JV must connect its ERP or billing software to an approved electronic invoice interface or use the National Electronic Tax Bureau portal. Etsy Direct Pay and Stripe payment data do not replace Chinese VAT invoice and reporting obligations for domestic taxable supplies; export transactions are substantiated through the China Customs electronic export declaration system.

Export VAT zero-rating is subject to tax authority risk screening and post-export customs/tax audits. If the STA determines that the export declaration, logistics evidence, or foreign exchange receipt proof is missing, false, or fails to demonstrate physical export and payment repatriation, the 0% rate is denied, the export VAT exemption or refund is clawed back, late payment surcharge is imposed, and a penalty of 50% to five times the evaded or falsely refunded tax amount may be assessed under Article 63 of the Law on Tax Administration and Collection of the People's Republic of China. Falsifying customs declarations or refund invoices can constitute criminal export refund fraud under Article 204 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, with imprisonment and monetary penalties for responsible persons, and the JV may be added to the abnormal taxpayer risk list and disqualified from future export VAT refund and exemption privileges.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The currency-account assessment for the onshore joint venture is dominated by China's legal framework. RMB is fully convertible for current-account trade items such as goods sold and services provided, but requires the submission of a genuine trade background and a SAFE declaration. Capital-account transactions are not freely convertible: the foreign investor's capital contribution must be supported by an FDI registration and a foreign-exchange capital account; once converted to RMB, funds must be used in the enterprise's operating scope. Export receipts must be repatriated to a bank and reported under the trade-integrated monitoring system; there is no mandatory conversion, so the JV can keep USD/HKD in an onshore account or settle to RMB with the bank at the market rate. Profit remittance is permitted after the tax authorities verify taxes and the bank receives an audited financial statement, a board resolution, and the annual SAFE/foreign investment information report. The Hong Kong SAR limb is not subject to these controls; its treasury function can move funds freely in HKD/USD, but any inward capital to the mainland JV must cross through SAFE-regulated rails. Practical hurdles include bank-level documentation reviews, the negative-list restrictions, and the fact that a distressed shell balance-sheet profile can lead to enhanced due diligence and deferred settlement.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the SAFE goods-trade foreign-exchange monitoring framework, there is no fixed mandatory surrender ratio for exports; receipts may be retained in an onshore current-account foreign-exchange balance or converted to RMB at commercial rates. All export proceeds must be collected through an authorized bank or a licensed cross-border e-commerce settlement institution, and reported in the enterprise's Trade Foreign Exchange Income and Payment Account. As a statutory guardrail, export receipts must be repatriated within the normal customs and declaration cycle; a payment received more than 90 days after shipment should be reported as delayed collection to avoid a bank break-of-trade alert. The JV must be listed in the SAFE Trade Exporter/Importer Directory before the first settlement.

Central bank supervision: The People's Bank of China (PBOC) as the monetary authority, with foreign exchange control enforcement delegated to the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Not viable as a primary treasury rail for the onshore JV. Under current PRC law, offshore digital EMIs such as Airwallex, Currenxie, and WorldFirst are not permitted to maintain the JV's principal FDI capital account or collect export proceeds directly on the entity's behalf without a SAFE/PBOC cross-border payment license. A narrow exception exists for cross-border e-commerce collection services through SAFE-registered payment institutions; those institutions settle in aggregate to a domestic bank account using receivables reconciliation and must not act as unlicensed deposit-takers. For the Hong Kong sibling treasury hub, an EMI may be used for fee, royalty, or trade payable flows, but it should not be a substitute for an onshore corporate account when repatriating capital or settling current-account trade proceeds.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% (PRC Corporate Income Tax Law, Article 4) for the Chinese-resident Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

Domestic statutory rate is 20% reduced to 10% for non-resident recipients on gross dividends paid by a PRC resident enterprise to a Hong Kong non-resident shareholder. Under the China-Hong Kong DTA, the rate is reduced to 5% if the beneficial owner is a Hong Kong tax resident company that has directly held at least 25% of the capital of the JV for the preceding 12 months; otherwise the treaty rate remains 10%.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File must be prepared if the JV's related-party transactions in a fiscal year reach any of the following thresholds: (1) transfers of tangible assets totaling RMB 200 million or more; (2) financial asset transfers totaling RMB 100 million or more; (3) intangible asset transfers totaling RMB 100 million or more; (4) other related-party transactions totaling RMB 40 million or more. Master File is mandatory if the aggregate related-party transaction volume of the group exceeds RMB 10 billion, or if the enterprise is required under other rules.

Economic substance scope: Because the entity is a PRC resident JV, it must demonstrate substantive business operations in China: real manufacturing, personnel, and assets for enamel pin production, marking CIGA in China. If the Hong Kong shareholder is a pure conduit without HK economic substance, the PRC tax authority may deny treaty benefits under the beneficial ownership and principal purpose test. Hong Kong's own Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) do not directly apply to the PRC JV, but the HK shareholder must satisfy HK ESR if it conducts relevant activities. In the PRC, the general anti-avoidance rule (Article 47 of CIT Law) requires commercial substance; transactions lacking economic purpose may be disregarded and recharacterized. The JV should maintain board minutes, operational premises, payroll, and decision-making records to substantiate its active business and intercompany contracts.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Digital Sales Underreporting

Statutory sanction: Article 63 of the PRC Tax Administration Tax Collection Law: the authority shall recover the unpaid tax plus late payment surcharge and impose a penalty of 50% to 500% of the underpaid amount. If a criminal threshold is met, Article 201 of the Criminal Law punishes tax evasion with imprisonment up to 7 years for the directly responsible officer.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil may be pierced under the Company Law where directors abuse legal person status to evade debts/taxes; directors are jointly and severally liable for the unpaid tax and fines. A criminal conviction can lead to personal criminal fines, imprisonment, and disqualification; the tax authority may apply for property preservation and travel bans (blacklisting the legal representative).
Transfer Pricing Manipulation / Treaty Abuse

Statutory sanction: Under Articles 41 and 48 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, the tax authority may make transfer pricing adjustments with an interest surcharge on the resulting additional tax. For treaty abuse, the General Anti-Abuse Rule (Article 47, Implementation Measures under Bulletin 2012 No. 13) may recharacterize the structure, deny treaty relief, and impose taxes together with interest. If fraudulent, penalties up to 50%-500% plus criminal liability under Article 201 if evasion is intentional.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false transfer pricing documentation may face administrative penalties; where the adjustment shows evasion, the directors can be held civilly liable for restitution, blacklisted, and potentially criminally prosecuted if they had actual knowledge of the pricing scheme.
Failure to Withhold and Remit Cross-border WHT / Money Laundering

Statutory sanction: Article 69 of the PRC Tax Administration Tax Collection Law: the withholding agent who fails to withhold shall be fined 50%-300% of the amount that was not withheld. Unremitted withheld taxes may be recovered from the withholding agent. For AML, the Financial Institutions Customer Due Diligence Measures can impose fines; tax fraud can invoke Criminal Law Article 201. Additionally, under the Tax Collection Law, the director may be ordered to rectify and pay personal liability.

Director personal liability: Piercing the corporate veil: courts may hold the director personally liable for the unpaid withholding tax and penalties if the director directed the payment without deduction. Personal assets may be frozen, the individual may be placed on a tax evasion blacklist, and under serious circumstances, imprisonment up to 3 years for evasion, or 7 years if the amount is especially large. If foreign exchange evasion occurs, SAFE may impose fines and travel restrictions.
