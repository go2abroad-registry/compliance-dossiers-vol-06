Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA), acting through the local competent tax authority and the National Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax Bureau) online portal operated by each provincial-level tax service under STA direction.

E-government filing portal

For the Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture (CN_JV_LTD), the company obtains a Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which functions as the Chinese Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) and VAT taxpayer identity, during company registration with the State Administration for Market Regulation. Within 30 days of obtaining the business license, the JV must complete tax registration through the e-Tax Bureau, file the entity profile, designate the legal representative and tax officer, select the accounting system and tax filing basis, and register for VAT. If annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000, the JV must apply for general VAT taxpayer status; below that threshold, the JV may either remain a small-scale taxpayer or voluntarily register as a general VAT taxpayer if accounting records are sound. The JV must also apply for tax-control UKey or electronic invoice quota through the e-Tax Bureau and complete goods-trade foreign exchange registration with the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) for future export receipts. A foreign non-resident shareholder does not automatically obtain a Chinese TIN through equity ownership; any China-source income of the non-resident is handled through withholding procedures, and a non-resident supplier making taxable supplies in China would separately apply for non-resident tax registration through the e-Tax Bureau.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Basic tax registration is mandatory within 30 days of obtaining the business license with no monetary threshold. The mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration threshold is annual taxable sales exceeding RMB 5,000,000; taxpayers below this threshold may register as general VAT taxpayers voluntarily under specified conditions.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The JV, as a general VAT taxpayer, may claim a 0% VAT rate and input VAT refund on exported goods only where the goods are physically exported from China under customs supervision, the export customs declaration is filed and released through China International Trade Single Window, the sale is made to a non-resident overseas buyer with title and risk passing outside China, the export proceeds are received in foreign exchange and repatriated through a Chinese bank or SAFE-monitored channel within the statutory period, the goods are not prohibited or restricted for export without required licenses, and the JV maintains a complete audit trail reconciling the customs declaration, logistics documents, sales contract, commercial invoice, input VAT invoices, and foreign-exchange receipt records. Qualifying offshore digital services are not part of the current robotic hardware scope; any future cross-border service supply would separately require service consumption outside China and delivery to a non-resident recipient.

  • Customs Export Goods Declaration (export declaration) filed through China International Trade Single Window and released by customs, with electronic data transmitted to the STA export-rebate verification system
  • Ocean Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, Rail Consignment Note, or International Road Consignment Note (CMR) evidencing physical export movement of the manufactured robotic goods
  • Commercial invoice, packing list, and export sales contract or purchase order identifying the overseas buyer, shipment terms, and export consideration
  • Bank export-proceeds receipt voucher or SAFE Goods Trade Foreign Exchange Monitoring System record evidencing repatriation of the export proceeds within the statutory deadline
  • Input VAT Special VAT Invoice(s) or customs import VAT payment certificate evidencing the refundable input VAT attributable to the exported goods
  • Agency export agreement and Agency Export Goods Certificate, if the export is routed through a licensed export agent

All VAT invoices, including Special VAT Invoices and ordinary VAT invoices, must be issued through the STA-approved Electronic Invoice Service Platform or STA tax-control equipment (tax-control UKey). Invoice data is transmitted to the STA VAT invoice management system in near real time, and each invoice carries a statutory 20-digit invoice number and encrypted digital tax-control signature. Unauthorized private invoice generation is prohibited. The tax authority may impose quota controls, prior online verification, or concentrated invoice issuance on high-risk taxpayers; fully digitalized electronic invoices do not require a physical stamp but must pass the STA digital anti-counterfeit validation.

The STA cross-matches the export VAT zero-rating or refund claim against customs export declarations, logistics movement records, VAT invoice system data, and SAFE foreign-exchange receipt records. If the export zero-rating claim is found to be false, unsubstantiated, or fraudulent, the STA orders repayment of the refunded VAT plus late-payment surcharge, imposes an administrative fine of one to five times the amount of tax defrauded, and may suspend or cancel the taxpayer's export rebate qualification. Serious cases are referred to criminal authorities for prosecution of fraudulently obtaining export tax rebates under Chinese criminal law, with potential imprisonment and confiscation of property.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Hong Kong SAR does not impose exchange controls; the Hong Kong dollar is fully convertible under the HKMA currency-board system, and offshore renminbi (CNH) is freely traded in the Hong Kong interbank market. The mainland People's Republic of China, however, operates a managed convertibility framework that distinguishes current account from capital account items. Current account flows, including goods trade, ordinary services, and investment income distributions, are convertible once the verifying bank confirms authenticity and consistency under SAFE rules; capital account flows, such as equity injections into a Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture, shareholder loans, overseas guarantees, and pure financial FX trades, require prior registration, approval, or filing with SAFE and must flow through designated foreign exchange accounts. Profit remittance from a mainland JV is treated as current account investment income but is subject to documentary review of audited financial statements, tax filing certificates, tax clearance for withholding, and a board resolution approving the dividend. In Hong Kong, the JV treasury can freely repatriate dividends to shareholders through normal banking channels without FX approval, but any transfer between the HK entity and the mainland operating entity must be fully documented under SAFE balance-of-payments statistics and retained for at least five years. The practical assess is that trade-backed flows are genuinely convertible, while capital-account and financing flows remain carefully gatekept and require licensed bank infrastructure.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Mainland export entities are no longer subject to mandatory forced surrender or a fixed conversion ratio; they may retain 100% of eligible export proceeds in a designated foreign exchange account or convert to renminbi at the market rate after the verifying bank matches the receipt to the customs declaration and the exporter is on the SAFE Goods Trade Foreign Exchange Monitoring register. The statutory collection period is the underlying commercial contract term, but export proceeds must be repatriated to China and reported in the monitoring system; where collection extends beyond the ordinary benchmark of 90 days from customs export declaration, the exporter must register the delayed receipt with SAFE through the bank and produce contractual evidence and risk explanation. If the trade receipt remains offshore in the HK JV as a HK resident entity, it is not subject to SAFE surrender rules; the surrender, repatriation, and verification obligations attach only when the funds enter the mainland banking system as export collection.

Central bank supervision: The Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) is the central banking authority for Hong Kong SAR; the People's Bank of China (PBC) and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) exercise foreign exchange administration for mainland China, including FDI registration, trade settlement, and capital account controls.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Fintech EMI viability is conditional and mostly ancillary. In Hong Kong, licensed digital banks and authorized EMIs such as Airwallex, Neat, and Statrys can open multi-currency operational accounts, issue virtual IBANs, and route payments for trade settlement, but they are not licensed as full commercial banks and cannot provide onshore mainland RMB capital account services, SAFE foreign exchange surrender/verification, FDI capital escrow, or statutory capital verification. The PBC/SAFE framework bars offshore digital EMIs from servicing mainland resident entities for capital account settlement; all mainland cross-border trade FX receipts and capital injections must pass through a designated domestic foreign exchange bank. For the HK JV, an EMI may be used for low-value e-commerce payouts, ancillary operating expenses, and transactions that do not require statutory FX registration, but the treasury-critical clearing rails, FDI injection, profit repatriation, commercial letters of credit, and RMB cross-border settlement must remain with tier-1 commercial banks. Viability rating: Low-Medium for core treasury workflows; acceptable as payment collection rails under the JV's stack.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound FDI capital injection from the HK JV or HK shareholder into the mainland PRC entity must be routed through a SAFE-designated domestic foreign exchange bank. The remitter sends a SWIFT MT103 with the 70 field purpose code reflecting direct investment equity injection, such as SAFE transaction code 621/622 as applicable, together with the FDI foreign exchange registration reference number and the joint venture contract identifier. Before remittance, the mainland JV must complete incorporation, SAFE FDI registration, open an FDI Capital Account, and obtain the bank reference number from the designated branch. The required onboarding package includes the joint venture contract, articles of association, mainland business license, shareholder resolution approving the capital injection, FDI registration certificate, and directors'/UBO identities. After receipt, a certified public accountant must perform capital verification for registered capital obligations, and the funds may be converted or used only after trade-background and payment-purpose verification. Any shareholder loan in addition to equity is subject to cross-border financing macro-prudential limits and must be registered with SAFE; the HK JV treasury must maintain a complete SWIFT trail, board approval minutes, and regulatory filings for later bank and SAFE audits.Outbound trade disbursements by the HK JV to component suppliers and production partners should use standard clearing rails selected by transaction size and trust. For ordinary component purchases and service fees, SWIFT MT103 payments with the 70 field purpose code for goods trade are appropriate, supported by purchase orders, commercial invoices, transport documents, and contract files retained by the instructing bank. For larger procurement orders or where the supplier requires conditional payment, an irrevocable Commercial Letter of Credit issued by the JV's HK bank and advised through the supplier's bank gives the greatest settlement certainty; presentations must include compliant invoices, bills of lading, packing lists, insurance certificates, and certificates of origin. Documentary collections under documents against payment or documents against acceptance provide a lower-cost alternative for recurring low-risk shipments but do not carry a bank payment undertaking. For mainland RMB disbursements to PRC suppliers or service providers, the HK JV should use the Cross-Border Interbank Payment System (CIPS) through a RMB clearing bank in Hong Kong such as Bank of China (Hong Kong) or ICBC (Asia), with the same trade documentation and, where required, withholding tax clearances and SAFE reporting.Deliverable CNY/USD, CNY/HKD FX forward contracts with SAFE-licensed onshore banks for genuine current-account trade and investment exposure, including flexible delivery within a five-day window.Offshore CNH non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) booked through the HK treasury to hedge conversion risk where the mainland balance-sheet exposure is not eligible for onshore deliverable hedging.FX swaps used to manage HKD, USD, and RMB payment-mismatch timing across dividend, procurement, and sales cycles, with same-notional reversal and documented trade exposure.Cross-currency interest rate swaps and currency swaps limited to balance-sheet and procurement-financing hedges under ISDA/CSA documentation, internal hedge policy, and board approval; prohibited for speculative trades.Simple FX options, including vanilla call and put structures, where approved by the bank's KYC and suitability review and supported by an explicit treasury mandate with options premium risk limits.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Fintech EMI viability is conditional and mostly ancillary. In Hong Kong, licensed digital banks and authorized EMIs such as Airwallex, Neat, and Statrys can open multi-currency operational accounts, issue virtual IBANs, and route payments for trade settlement, but they are not licensed as full commercial banks and cannot provide onshore mainland RMB capital account services, SAFE foreign exchange surrender/verification, FDI capital escrow, or statutory capital verification. The PBC/SAFE framework bars offshore digital EMIs from servicing mainland resident entities for capital account settlement; all mainland cross-border trade FX receipts and capital injections must pass through a designated domestic foreign exchange bank. For the HK JV, an EMI may be used for low-value e-commerce payouts, ancillary operating expenses, and transactions that do not require statutory FX registration, but the treasury-critical clearing rails, FDI injection, profit repatriation, commercial letters of credit, and RMB cross-border settlement must remain with tier-1 commercial banks. Viability rating: Low-Medium for core treasury workflows; acceptable as payment collection rails under the JV's stack.
