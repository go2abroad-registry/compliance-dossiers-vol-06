Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; electronic sovereign portal: National Unified Electronic Tax Bureau (E-Taxation) at the official STA e-tax platform, and the China International Trade Single Window for export/import customs and tax coordination.

E-government filing portal

Foreign non-resident entities establishing a Sino-foreign equity joint venture in the PRC obtain their statutory Tax Identification Number (TIN) through the following procedure: (i) Complete the foreign investment filing with the Ministry of Commerce or the provincial branch under the Foreign Investment Negative List, if applicable; (ii) Incorporate with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) to obtain the Business License, which includes the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) — this USCC operates nationwide as the taxpayer’s TIN; (iii) Within 30 days of license issuance, log into the National Unified Electronic Tax Bureau platform with the USCC, submit the taxpayer registration information (including financial controller details, banking account, proposed VAT status, and authority delegation), and complete the initial basic tax profile; (iv) The STA district office reviews the submission, assigns a resident enterprise taxpayer status, and activates the electronic tax account; (v) If the entity intends to export, additionally register with the General Administration of Customs (GACC) as an import/export enterprise and file an export tax rebate eligibility record with the local STA. For foreign entities with no Chinese establishment but deriving Chinese-sourced income, a withholding tax registration is effected through the payer, or the entity may apply for a temporary tax number solely for refund administration.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None. The PRC Enterprise Income Tax Law does not provide a check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election analogous to the U.S. IRC §7701; a Chinese-incorporated Sino-foreign equity joint venture is mandatorily treated as a resident enterprise subject to entity-level tax on worldwide income. Any tax structure utilising an offshore intermediate entity must rely on treaty benefit limitation and substance rules rather than a unilateral classification election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VAT taxpayer registration is compulsory for all enterprises immediately upon commencement of taxable activity. For compulsory General VAT Taxpayer classification, the annual taxable sales threshold is CNY 5,000,000; below that, an enterprise is a Small-Scale Taxpayer unless it opts for General status.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of tangible goods, including Class III coronary drug-eluting stents, are eligible for zero-rating (commonly referred to as export VAT refund) if the seller is a VAT general taxpayer, the goods are physically shipped out of the PRC customs territory, the export transaction is declared through the China International Trade Single Window, the proceeds are repatriated through a PRC bank within the statutory foreign exchange verification period, and the export declaration and required invoices are submitted for refund processing prior to the statutory refund deadline.

  • Customs Export Goods Declaration (Export Goods Declaration generated by the General Administration of Customs)
  • Ocean Bill of Lading or Air Waybill in the name of the PRC exporting entity
  • Foreign Exchange Income Declaration (SAFE cross-border receipts) evidencing actual receipt of export proceeds

All VAT invoices must be issued through the State Taxation Administration's VAT Invoice Management System, now the Fully Digitalized Electronic Invoice (FDEI) system. Each invoice is transmitted in real-time to the STA tax control servers, assigned a unique digital invoice number and cryptographic stamp, and automatically cleared against the taxpayer's VAT return. Input VAT credit is disallowed unless the VAT invoice is validated via the VAT Invoice Comprehensive Service Platform.

The STA and GACC jointly monitor export zero-rating. Any claim unsupported by the required customs declaration, transport document, and bank receipt is subject to full clawback of the VAT refunded, an additional penalty of 50% to 500% of the tax amount involved, daily late-payment surcharge (0.05%), and potential downgrading of tax credit rating. Where fraud is demonstrated, Article 204 of the Criminal Law imposes criminal liability for fraudulent export tax refunds, with penalties up to life imprisonment, fines, and confiscation of property.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China maintains a conditional current-account convertibility regime under the RMB managed floating exchange rate system. Trade-related receipts and payments for goods and services are generally convertible through designated banks, provided the transaction is genuine and documented under the SAFE electronic monitoring system. Capital account items, however, remain strictly controlled: foreign direct investment, loans, and securities flows require registration or approval by SAFE, an onshore licensed bank, and sometimes the People's Bank of China. The Chinese joint venture can receive foreign equity capital through a dedicated FDI capital account, but conversion of those proceeds into RMB must be based on actual operational use and cannot be used for speculative purposes. Profit remittances are permitted once audited annual accounts are approved, tax clearance certificates are obtained, and the bank verifies that the distribution matches registered capital and ownership ratios; withholding tax on dividends must be settled first. Practical hurdles include inconsistent documentation across tax and FX filings, delays in bank compliance reviews for the medical device sector, and the need to reconcile import/export data with customs. Surplus foreign exchange must be either retained in approved foreign currency accounts or converted to RMB, but cannot be lent offshore without SAFE approval.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the SAFE trade monitoring framework, export foreign exchange receipts must be repatriated to China and credited to a current-account foreign exchange settlement account within 30 calendar days of receipt from the overseas buyer, unless approved otherwise. There is no fixed mandatory conversion ratio; the exporting joint venture may retain up to the approved account ceiling or convert the full amount to RMB through the designated bank. All receipts must be matched against customs export declarations within the 30-day verification cycle, and any unreconciled balance is a red flag for SAFE.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

For a mainland China entity, offshore fintech EMIs are not a viable settlement rail. China's FX and banking regulations reserve RMB clearing, foreign exchange settlement, and cross-border payment services to licensed banking institutions. An EMI licensed only in the EU or UK cannot open a China-based account or provide CNY clearing without a Chinese banking partner. Some Hong Kong-licensed stored value facilities and virtual banks can provide ancillary multi-currency accounts for the Hong Kong parent, but they cannot replace a domestic tier-1 bank for the Chinese joint venture's capital account or true trade settlement. Feasibility rating: Low; legal constraint is prohibitive without a mainland bank sub-license.

The receiving bank will require the capital injection to be matched against the joint venture contract and the FDI Foreign Exchange Registration Certificate. A Chinese certified public accountant must then issue a capital verification report within 30 days after receipt.
