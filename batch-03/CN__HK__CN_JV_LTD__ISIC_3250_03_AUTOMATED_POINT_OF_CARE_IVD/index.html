Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None. The People's Republic of China does not provide check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch entity classification elections. The Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture is mandatorily an entity-level resident corporate taxpayer under the Enterprise Income Tax Law, taxed on worldwide income; pass-through treatment is not permitted. VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 (CNY 5 million) in annual taxable sales; exceeding this threshold mandates registration as a general VAT taxpayer. All newly established entities must complete tax registration within 30 days of business license issuance regardless of turnover. Non-resident enterprises selling taxable goods or services within mainland China may have VAT registration or buyer-withholding obligations even without a Chinese establishment.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is available to a VAT general taxpayer for physical goods actually exported from mainland China customs territory under an export contract, including finished IVD analyzers and microfluidic cartridges. The exporter must have valid customs registration, must file and complete the customs export declaration, the goods must leave China under customs supervision and not return without a formal re-importation, and the export proceeds must be repatriated through a Chinese bank and matched in the SAFE Trade Foreign Exchange Monitoring System. The zero-rated export VAT treatment operates as exemption plus refund of input VAT, and requires a complete documentation and payment trail.

  • Customs Export Goods Declaration Form (Customs Export Declaration record with export-rebate special seal)
  • Export Commercial Invoice and detailed packing list
  • Ocean or air Bill of Lading or multimodal transport waybill evidencing physical export from mainland China customs territory
  • Export sales contract or confirmed purchase order
  • Bank-issued export proceeds receipt entry in the SAFE Trade Foreign Exchange Monitoring System proving timely cross-border receipt of funds
  • Input VAT special invoice and VAT export refund declaration package supporting the zero-rated export claim

Under the STA Fully Digitalized Electronic Invoice reform and Golden Tax Phase IV, VAT invoices must be issued through the STA-controlled digital electronic invoice platform or authorized tax-control equipment. Invoice data are transmitted to the National VAT Invoice Verification Platform in near real-time with unique encrypted digital stamps and invoice codes for anti-fraud matching. Buyers must confirm and verify special VAT invoices before claiming input VAT credits. The system automatically cross-matches invoice data with customs export declarations, logistics records, bank settlement confirmations, and SAFE foreign exchange monitoring information.

The STA, GACC, and SAFE jointly review export zero-rating claims using Golden Tax Phase IV data matching across invoices, customs release records, logistics waybills, and bank FX receipts. If exports are found not to have occurred, to involve circular trade arrangements, to be supported by false or inconsistent documentation, or to lack timely repatriation of export proceeds, the STA will revoke the zero-rating treatment, recharacterize the sale as taxable domestic supply at 13% VAT, claw back any input VAT refund, and impose late payment surcharges. Under the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection, fraudulent export refunds are subject to a penalty of 100% to 500% of the falsely refunded amount, possible suspension of export refund and customs clearance privileges, and criminal liability under the Criminal Law for export-rebate fraud.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China maintains an administered managed float for the RMB with significant restrictions on capital account items, while Hong Kong SAR applies no foreign exchange controls. For the mainland Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture, current account trade transactions are largely convertible: export proceeds may be retained in foreign currency or converted to RMB through designated banks, but must be repatriated and verified through the SAFE monitoring system. Capital account flows, including registered capital injection, shareholder loans, equity transfers, and dividend remittances, require FDI registration with SAFE and a valid Foreign Exchange Business Registration Form. Profit remittance is not blocked but entails banks undertaking post-tax distribution verification: audited financial statements, board profit-distribution resolution, tax filing receipts, and clear evidence of accumulated profits. The JV cannot freely convert RMB for investments or debt repayments outside authentic commercial or listed-use purposes; each conversion requires concrete underlying documentation under the payment authenticity principle. RMB may be used cross-border into Hong Kong via the RMB cross-border settlement mechanism but remains subject to trade documentation. Thus treasury planning must separate onshore RMB liquidity, capital-account buffers, and offshore HKD/USD clearing liquidity, with all flows executed through a SAFE-designated bank rather than unregulated fintech channels.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the PRC Foreign Exchange Administration Regulations and SAFE trade-in-goods monitoring rules, export foreign-currency proceeds attributable to the JV must be repatriated into a domestic current-account FX settlement account. No mandatory percentage conversion to RMB is imposed under the current SAFE framework; the exporter retains 100% of verified export proceeds in its FX account or may convert at the onshore market rate. The receiving bank must submit the cross-border receipt declaration through the SAFE Digital Platform on the same banking day for standard wire collections. Export receipts with payment terms exceeding the SAFE monitoring threshold, normally 90 days for deferred receipts, must be reported in the Trade Monitoring System before the bank releases funds. Failure to repatriate or verify creates discrepancy indicators and can restrict future current-account operations. The statutory surrender obligation is therefore a repatriation and verification requirement rather than a fixed conversion ratio.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) and People's Bank of China (PBOC) for the mainland China JV; Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) for the Hong Kong SAR treasury node

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: China Local File: annual related-party tangible property transfers >= CNY 200 million; intangible or financial asset transfers >= CNY 100 million; related-party service transactions >= CNY 40 million. China Master File: aggregate related-party transactions >= CNY 1 billion annually. HK follows IRD practice note with no monetary threshold but contemporaneous documentation is expected for offshore-related tax positions under Hong Kong Inland Revenue Ordinance Section 16.

Economic substance scope: China does not apply a UAE-style ESR regime; however, the PRC General Anti-Avoidance Rule (EIT Law Art. 47) and the SAT beneficial-ownership test (SAT Announcement 2015 No. 60) require the Hong Kong shareholder to conduct real substantive activities—board meetings, employees, premises, risk-taking and other core income-generating activities (CIGA)—in Hong Kong. The mainland JV must also prove that its R&D, manufacturing and distribution operation, not the HK shell, actually earns the taxable profits attributable to IVD product development. Failure to satisfy CIGA substance in Hong Kong treats the HK entity as a conduit and denies treaty benefits.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False CIT Return with Fabricated VAT Invoices

Statutory sanction: TAL Articles 63-64: recovery of evaded tax, 0.05% daily late surcharge and a penalty of 50% to 5 times the underpaid tax. Criminal Law Art. 201: tax evasion up to 7 years' imprisonment and unlimited fines; Criminal Law Art. 205: issuing/using false VAT invoices attracts imprisonment up to life imprisonment in egregious cases.

Director personal liability: Directors, actual controllers and finance officers who design or approve the scheme are 'directly responsible persons' and face criminal prosecution; the State Taxation Administration will blacklist the entity and impose exit bans on the legal representative; under the Company Law abuse of limited liability allows the court to pierce the veil and order personal restitution of the full evaded tax and penalties from directors.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation / Cross-Border Profit Stripping

Statutory sanction: EIT Law Arts. 41, 44, 48 and SAT Announcement 2016 No. 42: tax authority may reassess taxable income using arm's-length prices; imposed additional tax carries compulsory interest at PBOC benchmark rate plus 5 percentage points; if the arrangement is treated as evasion, TAL Art. 63 penalty is 50%-500%; failure to prepare contemporaneous documentation reverses the burden of proof.

Director personal liability: Directors who personally negotiate the non-arm's-length HK agreements may be targeted in special tax investigations; where transfer pricing is used to execute tax fraud, criminal liability follows; exit restrictions and property preservation orders can be issued for the outstanding tax; the court can pierce the veil and hold directors jointly and severally liable if the related-party scheme is found to have unjustly depleted the JV's assets.
Treaty Abuse / HK Shell Shareholder and Beneficial Ownership Failure

Statutory sanction: SAT Announcement 2015 No. 60 and EIT Law Art. 47: treaty benefits are denied; the full 10% statutory WHT must be paid with interest; penalties under TAL Art. 63 if the underpaid WHT is fraudulent; the Chinese tax authority can recharacterize the dividend as a different income flow and institute CFC or anti-avoidance adjustments.

Director personal liability: Directors who utilize a HK shell to fraudulently obtain treaty benefits can be prosecuted as joint actors in tax evasion; the HK entity's directors also face Hong Kong Inland Revenue penalties for false statements; Chinese courts may lift the corporate veil to recover underpaid dividend WHT from shareholders who directed the collapse and are mere conduits; the legal representative of the JV (as withholding agent) is personally blocked from exiting China until the underpaid withholding is settled.
