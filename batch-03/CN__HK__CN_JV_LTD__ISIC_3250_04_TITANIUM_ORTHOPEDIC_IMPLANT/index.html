Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No minimum paid-up capital and no blocked escrow account are required under Hong Kong law. A company limited by shares may be incorporated with an issued capital of HKD 1.00 or any higher amount stated in the statement of capital. For this titanium orthopedic implant JV, the shareholders should pay or contribute the agreed share consideration in cash or in kind, including electron-beam melting equipment and working capital, not later than the date specified in the shareholders' agreement. The contribution must be properly reflected in the share register and, where contribution is in kind, supported by an independent asset valuation and board minutes. There is no statutory escrow account obligation in Hong Kong; however, banks and the Medical Device Administrative Control System may require evidence of paid-up capital and operating budget before opening accounts or issuing manufacturer authorization.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of titanium orthopedic implants qualify for 0% VAT zero-rating when the goods are physically exported from mainland China through customs and the exporter is a registered General VAT Taxpayer. The exporter must evidence a genuine export sale, receive the foreign currency proceeds through approved repatriation channels, and apply for the export refund within the statutory declaration period. Qualifying offshore services, if supplied to an overseas customer and consumed entirely outside mainland China, are also zero-rated in accordance with the STA zero-rating catalogue. Since Hong Kong SAR has no VAT, exports from Hong Kong do not require a zero-rating substantiation bundle under Hong Kong law.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Export Goods Customs Declaration Form)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC)
  • Export commercial invoice and packing list

China mandates VAT invoices to be issued through the STA's electronic invoice platform. Digitalized e-invoices carry a unique anti-fraud invoice code and tax-authority-issued cryptographic identity; issuance data is transmitted to the STA in near-real time via the Electronic Tax Bureau. Third-party invoicing systems must integrate using the national e-invoice API and comply with the prescribed invoice data schema. Traditional paper invoices are valid only until the applicable digitalization phase-out schedule, and digital e-invoices do not require physical stamping.

Unverified or fraudulent export zero-rating triggers mandatory clawback of the entire VAT refund plus statutory late-payment interest, reassessment of output VAT at the 13% standard rate on a deemed domestic sale, and administrative fines of one to five times the amount defrauded under the PRC Tax Collection Administration Law. Criminal referral applies where export-refund fraud is established under the PRC Criminal Law. Hong Kong profits tax consequences may also follow if the Hong Kong parent/conduit lacks economic substance or the route is structured without a genuine offshore commercial purpose.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The People's Republic of China operates a managed floating exchange rate regime administered jointly by the PBOC and SAFE, with distinct treatment of current account versus capital account items. Current account transactions, including trade in goods, services, and dividends derived from bona fide operations, are effectively convertible upon presentation of a valid underlying contract, customs declaration, and tax clearance certificate. However, capital account transactions such as foreign direct investment into the equity joint venture, cross-border loans, and repatriation of equity capital require prior approval or filing with SAFE, followed by registration with a designated Chinese domestic bank that acts as the settlement bank. For the Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture, all inbound capital injections must be remitted in foreign currency, credited to a foreign currency capital account, and converted into RMB only after completion of foreign exchange registration and capital verification by a licensed accounting firm. Profit remittances to the Hong Kong shareholder are permitted only if the JV has accumulated audited retained earnings, has settled all PRC taxes, and obtains a board resolution declaring dividends; the remittance is then processed under the current account 'Profit/Loss Remittance' purpose code without requiring further SAFE approval, though the bank will review evidence of the profit distribution decision and tax payments. Export proceeds from titanium orthopedic implant sales must be repatriated into China within 90 days, and while there is no mandatory percentage-to-RMB conversion, the enterprise may elect to hold foreign currency in an export proceeds settlement account or convert to RMB subject to bank requirements. A significant constraint is that any cross-border intercompany loan from the Hong Kong parent exceeding a certain amount must be registered as a foreign debt with SAFE and is subject to macro-prudential parameters, including leverage ratio and maturity matching requirements. Overall, the regime is pragmatic for trade-linked flows but requires rigorous documentary discipline for capital transactions, making compliance with SAFE registration and bank-level screening indispensable for treasury operations.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export proceeds from the sale of titanium orthopedic implants must be repatriated into China within 90 days of shipment. There is no mandatory conversion ratio to RMB; the JV may retain foreign currency in a designated export proceeds settlement account or convert to RMB at its commercial discretion, provided the bank records the transaction under the appropriate SAFE trade monitoring system code. For any foreign currency receipts exceeding USD 50,000 per transaction, a valid customs declaration and commercial invoice must be retained for bank verification, and SAFE's cross-border receipt and payment information system must show no discrepancy.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) and State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Offshore digital EMI entities such as Wise, Airwallex, and Currencycloud are legally barred from acting as the primary settlement bank for a mainland Chinese Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture. The PBOC and SAFE regulations require all foreign exchange and RMB-denominated onshore corporate accounts to be held at either a licensed onshore commercial bank or a foreign bank branch approved for local currency business. Fintech EMIs lacking a Chinese banking license cannot issue Chinese CNAPS account numbers nor participate in the SAFE cross-border monitoring system. They may only serve as ancillary payment initiation tools if they partner with a licensed Chinese bank, but the JV's statutory corporate treasury operations must still be maintained at a Tier-1 domestic bank. Therefore, the viability rating for offshore EMIs is Low and any proposal to route capital injections or trade settlements through such firms would be rejected by Chinese regulators and onshore compliance committees.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital injection from the Hong Kong parent to the mainland JV must be executed as a SWIFT MT103 wire denominated in USD or HKD to the JV's foreign currency capital funds account at a designated Tier-1 Chinese commercial bank. The remitter must quote SAFE Foreign Exchange Business Code 622 (Equity Investment) or the appropriate FDI code, and includes the purpose of payment as 'Share Subscription Contribution'. The receiving bank will require the JV's SAFe FDI registration certificate, the joint venture contract, the approval certificate from the Ministry of Commerce (or its provincial counterpart), and a board resolution authorizing the capital call. After receipt, the funds are frozen in a non-interest-bearing escrow-type capital account until the JV completes its registered capital verification. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Offshore digital EMI entities such as Wise, Airwallex, and Currencycloud are legally barred from acting as the primary settlement bank for a mainland Chinese Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture. The PBOC and SAFE regulations require all foreign exchange and RMB-denominated onshore corporate accounts to be held at either a licensed onshore commercial bank or a foreign bank branch approved for local currency business. Fintech EMIs lacking a Chinese banking license cannot issue Chinese CNAPS account numbers nor participate in the SAFE cross-border monitoring system. They may only serve as ancillary payment initiation tools if they partner with a licensed Chinese bank, but the JV's statutory corporate treasury operations must still be maintained at a Tier-1 domestic bank. Therefore, the viability rating for offshore EMIs is Low and any proposal to route capital injections or trade settlements through such firms would be rejected by Chinese regulators and onshore compliance committees.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Local File preparation is triggered when related-party tangible-asset transfers exceed RMB 200 million in a year, intangible-asset or financial-asset transfers exceed RMB 100 million, or other related-party transactions exceed RMB 40 million. Master File preparation is triggered when aggregate related-party transactions exceed RMB 1 billion. Country-by-Country Reporting is required for the group when consolidated group revenue reaches the RMB 5.5 billion threshold.

Economic substance scope: Although Hong Kong does not apply the offshore Economic Substance Regulations of certain low-tax islands, the China-Hong Kong Double Tax Arrangement is interpreted using substance-over-form and beneficial ownership doctrines. The Hong Kong parent must prove Core Income-Generating Activities in Hong Kong, including real personnel, offices, board control, business decision-making, and economic risk assumption. The PRC JV must also maintain arm's-length substance in China for contract manufacturing, product development, quality control, and distribution functions, otherwise the tax authorities may recharacterise the arrangements and deny treaty benefits.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and False Accounting

Statutory sanction: Article 63 of the Tax Collection Law authorises the tax authority to recover the underpaid tax, impose the daily 0.05% surcharge, and levy a fine of 50% to 500% of the underpaid amount. Article 201 of the Criminal Law punishes tax evasion with imprisonment of up to 7 years and fines where the evaded amount is large and exceeds the statutory percentage of taxable income.

Director personal liability: Courts may pierce the corporate veil under Article 20 of the PRC Company Law where abuse of limited liability or corporate personality is present, making directors and actual controllers jointly liable for the company's unpaid tax and civil restitution. The legal representative may also face an exit ban/blacklisting under Article 44 of the Tax Collection Law for attempting to leave China without settling taxes, and individual directors can be named in criminal proceedings with personal imprisonment exposure.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation and Profit Shifting

Statutory sanction: Article 41 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law permits the STA to make an arm's-length adjustment to income, and Article 48 requires the payment of interest on the adjusted amount. Under the Special Tax Adjustment Measures, the interest rate is benchmark plus up to five percentage points. Where the mispricing is intentional and accompanied by false books, the 50% to 500% evasion penalty and criminal liability under Article 201 of the Criminal Law can apply.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly execute non-arm's-length contracts to strip China-source profit may be treated as persons responsible for the illegal act. Civil asset restitution can be claimed against them, they can be blacklisted and prohibited from leaving China, and where criminal tax evasion is proved they may be sentenced to imprisonment together with fines and confiscation of assets.
Treaty Shopping, Shell Company, and Withholding Tax Failure

Statutory sanction: China's anti-avoidance rules under Article 47 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law and STA Public Announcement 2018 No. 9 allow denial of the treaty benefit and application of the statutory 10% withholding rate. Article 69 of the Tax Collection Law penalises the withholding agent with a fine of 50% to 300% of the tax that should have been withheld but was not withheld. The tax may be recovered jointly from the non-resident shareholder and the JV as withholding agent, together with late-payment surcharge.

Director personal liability: Directors who approve the shell shareholder structure and direct dividend routing can have the corporate veil pierced, making them jointly liable for the unpaid withholding tax, interest, and penalties. Shareholders and directors may be placed on the tax non-compliance blacklist, subjected to exit bans, and, where the under-withholding forms part of a deliberate tax evasion scheme, prosecuted under Article 201 of the Criminal Law with personal imprisonment exposure.
