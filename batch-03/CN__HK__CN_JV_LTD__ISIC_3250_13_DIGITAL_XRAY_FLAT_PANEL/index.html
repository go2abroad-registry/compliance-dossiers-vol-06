Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA); electronic filing and tax registration are administered through the E-Tax Bureau online portal operated by the STA and the provincial/municipal tax services in the jurisdiction where the Sino-foreign equity joint venture is established.

E-government filing portal

For a Sino-foreign equity joint venture formed in mainland China, the Chinese partner and the foreign non-resident investor, including a Hong Kong SAR corporate shareholder, must first obtain the business license from the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR). Within 30 days of the issuance of the business license, the joint venture must complete tax registration with the competent tax authority. The 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) printed on the business license serves as the taxpayer identification number for corporate income tax, value-added tax, and withholding tax purposes. The joint venture must separately complete VAT qualification registration, appoint a financial official responsible for tax affairs, and file its bank account information with the E-Tax Bureau. A Hong Kong SAR non-resident shareholder does not obtain a mainland TIN merely by owning shares; if it carries on a taxable trade in mainland China, it must register separately as a non-resident taxpayer and obtain a TIN under the non-resident taxpayer filing framework.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None. The PRC tax system does not provide check-the-box elections, hybrid mismatch elections, or optional pass-through classification for a Sino-foreign equity joint venture. The entity is mandatorily taxed at entity level on its worldwide income. VAT general taxpayer status can be voluntarily elected only in accordance with turnover thresholds and is not an entity-level tax classification election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration is triggered when annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000. A business with taxable turnover below this amount is treated as a small-scale VAT taxpayer but must still register for VAT once it commences taxable activities; voluntary general taxpayer registration is permitted under the statutory conditions.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The joint venture may claim the 0% VAT export rate for digital X-ray flat panel detectors exported from mainland China to Hong Kong SAR or any other foreign destination provided that the seller is a VAT general taxpayer, the goods physically depart mainland China under customs supervision, an export customs declaration has been lodged and verified, title and risk pass to the overseas buyer, and the export sale is genuinely reflected in the VAT filing. Export VAT refund of input tax is available only after the customs export declaration is matched with the sales invoice and the export proceeds are repatriated into the mainland China bank account. Qualifying cross-border services are zero-rated only when supplied to customers outside mainland China and not effectively consumed in China.

  • Electronic Customs Export Declaration (export goods declaration) evidencing customs clearance and physical departure of the goods from mainland China
  • Original ocean bill of lading, multimodal transport document, or air waybill naming the joint venture as exporter/shipper and showing a destination outside mainland China
  • SAFE Export Proceeds Collection Verification Record or the designated bank's foreign exchange receipt certificate demonstrating full inward remittance of export proceeds within the statutory deadline
  • Commercial invoice, detailed packing list, and signed export sales contract matching the declared customs value and the goods description
  • VAT refund application electronic ledger and the general VAT taxpayer registration status record maintained in the STA E-Tax Bureau

All VAT invoices must be issued through the State Taxation Administration's nationwide all-digital electronic invoice service platform. Issuance requires real-time API-based transmission of invoice data to the Golden Tax Phase IV tax-control network, statutory digital signature by the taxpayer's tax-control device, and automatic insertion of anti-counterfeiting fiscal stamps. The STA can monitor invoice issuance in real time and block anomalous invoices prior to completion. Paper invoices, where still usable, must be printed from the tax-controlled invoice system and reconciled with the electronic fiscal ledger.

Export zero-rating or export VAT refund claims unsupported by the required customs declaration, transport evidence, and verified foreign currency repatriation will be rejected and clawed back in full, with re-assessment at the 13% VAT rate. Late payment surcharges accrue at 0.05% per day from the date the VAT was originally underpaid. Administrative penalties range from 50% to 500% of the evaded tax where false or fraudulent documentation is involved. Serious violations may expose the joint venture, its responsible officers, and the Hong Kong SAR shareholder to criminal liability for false export tax refund crimes under the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, loss of export tax refund eligibility, and inclusion in the national tax integrity blacklist.

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The foreign exchange regime is a managed floating rate system with current account convertibility and restricted capital account convertibility. The EJV's paid-in capital injections in USD or HKD from the Hong Kong investor are classified as foreign direct investment and must be registered with SAFE before entry. Funds received into the foreign exchange capital account may be converted into RMB on a real-need basis through a designated bank; arbitrary conversion for portfolio purposes is prohibited. Current account transactions including payment for imported amorphous silicon targets, cesium iodide chemicals, component procurement, patent royalties, and dividends can be remitted freely after bank verification of transaction authenticity and compliance. Profit repatriation from the Chinese EJV to the Hong Kong shareholder is permitted after annual audit, final tax settlement, and withholding tax settlement, with documentation including audited financial statements, tax payment certificates, and board dividend resolutions. Remittance of dividends is processed by the designated bank under the current account and requires no SAFE approval, but capital reductions, dissolution proceeds, and assignment of equity remain subject to capital account SAFE approval. The Hong Kong SAR jurisdiction imposes no exchange controls, but the mainland leg of the structure is the controlling constraint for all RMB funding and clearing decisions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export receipts from sales of digital X-ray flat panel detector products must be repatriated to the PRC through a designated foreign exchange bank for collection verification. The statutory collection period for export proceeds is 180 days from the date of export unless a longer period is approved by SAFE for high-ticket capital equipment sales. There is no mandatory conversion ratio: after verification, the exporter may retain 100% of the foreign currency proceeds in a current account foreign exchange settlement account or convert to RMB at the prevailing market rate, subject to the bank's periodic balance-monitoring and SAFE's documentary audit. For open-account or advance-payment trade with the Hong Kong sales arm, the EJV must maintain an accurate SAFE trade-in-settlement ledger and submit invoices, bills of lading, customs declarations, and bank receipts to evidence the trade cycle.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the People's Bank of China (PBOC) for the mainland Chinese Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture; Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) remains the separate currency and banking authority for the Hong Kong SAR intermediate jurisdiction.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Offshore digital EMIs and virtual account providers are not viable as the primary banking layer for a mainland Chinese EJV. SAFE regulations require cross-border receipts and payments to pass through licensed domestic banks capable of performing trade authenticity verification and SAFE reporting. Fintech EMIs such as Airwallex, Currenxie, and global neo-banks are not licensed as deposit-taking institutions in the PRC and cannot maintain RMB current accounts or foreign exchange capital accounts on behalf of the EJV. They may be used only as auxiliary collection agents in the Hong Kong leg to manage receivables from overseas distributors, provided that all funds intended for the mainland entity are swept to the designated domestic account through MT103 wires in compliance with SAFE. The Hong Kong intermediate entity can use licensed Hong Kong Stored Value Facility (SVF) networks, but this does not relieve the mainland EJV from its domestic settlement obligations. Overall feasibility: very low for mainland account substitution, medium for invoicing and trade documentation in Hong Kong.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The Hong Kong shareholder must first remit subscribed capital through its own corporate bank via SWIFT MT103 to the EJV's foreign exchange capital account at Bank of China or ICBC in the mainland. The remittance must be annotated with the SAFE registration notice number and the transaction purpose code corresponding to foreign direct investment contribution to registered capital. After the funds arrive, the designated bank trails the payment through SAFE's capital project information system and issues the inbound foreign exchange capital registration and verification notice. The EJV then obtains a capital verification report from a licensed PRC CPA and registers the contribution with the State Administration for Market Regulation. Conversion of the foreign currency into RMB may be made through the same designated bank at the daily central parity rate using a valid contract and invoice; funds credited to the RMB capital account are restricted to operating expenditures, payroll, raw materials, equipment purchases, and expenses directly related to the EJV's business scope. The EJV must not lend capital funds to related parties or use them for securities investments without specific SAFE qualification.For upstream component purchases and technology license fees, the EJV uses domestic bank settlement via SWIFT MT103 for direct wire payments after presenting the underlying invoice and contract. For large capital equipment from vendors overseas, it opens an irrevocable letter of credit (MT700) through BOC or ICBC with documents against payment. For mid-sized shipments of cesium iodide deposition consumables, unitized documentary collections are acceptable under Incoterms CIF or FOB with draft and bill of lading presented through the negotiating bank. For downstream sales to global OEMs, the EJV should receive funds via TT or sight LC. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Offshore digital EMIs and virtual account providers are not viable as the primary banking layer for a mainland Chinese EJV. SAFE regulations require cross-border receipts and payments to pass through licensed domestic banks capable of performing trade authenticity verification and SAFE reporting. Fintech EMIs such as Airwallex, Currenxie, and global neo-banks are not licensed as deposit-taking institutions in the PRC and cannot maintain RMB current accounts or foreign exchange capital accounts on behalf of the EJV. They may be used only as auxiliary collection agents in the Hong Kong leg to manage receivables from overseas distributors, provided that all funds intended for the mainland entity are swept to the designated domestic account through MT103 wires in compliance with SAFE. The Hong Kong intermediate entity can use licensed Hong Kong Stored Value Facility (SVF) networks, but this does not relieve the mainland EJV from its domestic settlement obligations. Overall feasibility: very low for mainland account substitution, medium for invoicing and trade documentation in Hong Kong.
