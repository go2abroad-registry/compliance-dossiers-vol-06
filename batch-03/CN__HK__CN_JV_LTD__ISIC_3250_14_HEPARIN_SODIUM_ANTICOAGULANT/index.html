Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA), with the electronic portal being the national Electronic Tax Administration (e-Tax) platform operated through the provincial-level STA local e-Tax access points. The Hong Kong SAR shareholder, where separately liable, is supervised by the Inland Revenue Department of Hong Kong through the eTAX portal; that separate filing does not replace the PRC tax registration of the mainland-established Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture with the State Administration for Market Regulation and obtain the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code, which simultaneously serves as the enterprise's legal identifier, taxpayer identification number, and VAT registration number under China's one-code-one-certificate reform. Step 2: Complete automatic tax registration; after company registration, the tax authority synchronizes the entity record into the STA taxpayer registry, and no separate application for a different VAT ID is required. Step 3: Supply the supplementary tax registration dataset to the competent local tax authority covering legal representative, registered address, financial accounting standards, bank account, capital contribution, shareholder structure, and foreign investment information. Step 4: For the foreign non-resident corporate investor that does not have a permanent establishment in mainland China but receives China-source income from the JV, the non-resident enterprise must obtain a Non-Resident Enterprise Taxpayer Identification Number through either the withholding agent's tax registration file or direct self-registration with the competent tax authority of the income-source district, before the JV can remit dividends, interest, royalties, or certain capital amounts. Step 5: If the Hong Kong shareholder claims China-Hong Kong tax arrangement benefits, it must maintain valid Hong Kong Business Registration and Taxpayer Identification records with the Inland Revenue Department, and supply a Hong Kong tax-resident certificate where required by the competent PRC tax authority.

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 annual taxable sales for mandatory VAT general taxpayer registration. Export 0% zero-rating conditions

VAT zero-rating applies to goods physically exported from the Chinese customs territory by the JV under a valid customs export declaration, provided the export sale is genuine, documented, and supported by actual movement of goods to the overseas buyer. The JV, as a manufacturing exporter, may apply the export exemption-offset-refund method, under which output VAT on exported goods is exempted, creditable input VAT is offset against domestic output VAT liabilities, and any remaining creditable VAT may be refunded by the competent tax authority. The export proceeds must be received from the overseas buyer in foreign currency in accordance with State Administration of Foreign Exchange requirements, and the export rebate claim must be submitted through the STA e-Tax export tax rebate module within the applicable rebate filing period. For qualifying offshore digital services, different rules apply; this dossier concerns physical pharmaceutical goods.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The system splits between mainland China and Hong Kong SAR. Mainland China's Renminbi is convertible on current account for international trade and services, but capital account transactions remain subject to prior registration, an effective exchange-control regime. The Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture, as a Chinese onshore entity, must obtain an FDI Foreign Exchange Registration and open a capital account at a designated bank before receiving equity from its Hong Kong investor. Once funds enter the capital account, they are placed in a capital verification process with a PRC CPA and can only be used for the JV's actual business operations. Dividend repatriation by the Hong Kong shareholder is permitted after completion of annual statutory audit, tax clearance, and distribution resolution, but the bank will verify the audited accounts, CIT payment evidence, and source of distributable profits. Hong Kong SAR, by contrast, imposes no foreign-exchange controls on HKD or foreign currency movements and operates a Linked Exchange Rate system; funds can flow freely within the Hong Kong banking system. However, any movement between the JV's onshore accounts and its Hong Kong parent/treasury hub triggers SAFE's current and capital account verification. For heparin crude procurement from overseas, the JV must support every payment with the contract, invoice, customs declaration, and inspection certificates; SAFE's trade verification system will compare remittance data with customs records. Therefore, convertibility should be considered full for current-account trade transactions, but conditional and documentary for capital injections, cross-border guarantees, and dividend remittances.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export proceeds from heparin sodium or crude heparin must be repatriated to China and collected into a bank-designated foreign exchange account no later than 180 days from the customs export declaration date. Since the 2012 repeal of compulsory surrender, no minimum conversion ratio is imposed; the JV may retain the proceeds in foreign currency at an onshore bank or sell them to the bank at market rate. The non-repatriation, delayed collection, or settlement of export proceeds through non-banking channels is prohibited without SAFE approval.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) and State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE), with Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) acting as the Hong Kong SAR central banking authority and currency board for HKD clearing

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Offshore digital EMIs are not viable as the primary treasury layer for this onshore Sino-Foreign Equity JV. PRC banking regulations permit only licensed banks to open the onshore RMB basic account, FDI capital account, and export income settlement account. SAFE requires that all cross-border capital flows be routed through a designated foreign exchange bank with a valid tax identification, customs registration, and SAFE filing. Airwallex, Revolut, or similar offshore EMIs are not licensed as deposit-taking institutions in China and cannot issue or hold the statutory account structure needed for capital verification. They may, at best, act as a front-end payment aggregator for low-value ancillary freight or marketing disbursements outside China, but they cannot replace the licensed bank line. HK-licensed SVFs can provide corporate wallet services for the Hong Kong subsidiary, but any transfer into mainland China must still end at a designated PRC bank. Overall legal constraint rating for a mainland JV is Low-to-Nil viability as a primary clearing rail; HK treasury shell services are Medium only for non-CNY third-party payments.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The Hong Kong investor shall remit the committed USD, HKD, or offshore RMB equity contribution through a licensed Hong Kong bank using SWIFT MT103 with the beneficiary being the JV's FDI Capital Contribution Account at a PRC designated bank. The wire instruction must state 'Investment capital contribution for establishment of Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture' and carry the SAFE FX purpose code corresponding to inbound foreign direct investment equity injection. The PRC receiving bank will require the valid SAFE FDI registration, the JV business license, a company resolution evidencing the capital call, and the foreign investor's certificate of good standing. Upon receipt, the bank submits a SAFE foreign exchange settlement form (International Transaction Reporting) and releases proceeds to the capital account subject to the CPA's capital verification report. No conversion of the foreign currency without an approved actual-use need is permitted; conversion into CNY for employee wages, procurement, or internal expense reimbursement must satisfy the bank's documentary review and is strictly monitored by SAFE. For an offshore RMB injection, the route can use a CNH account with the same documentation and cross-border RMB direct investment filing.Trade settlements for heparin sodium API purchase and export should be cleared through three primary rails. First, SWIFT MT103 telegraphic transfer: used for standard open-account import/export payments after the bank matches the invoice, contract, customs declaration and phytosanitary/pharma certificate in the SAFE trade verification database. Second, irrevocable documentary letter of credit: recommended for high-value cargoes; the Chinese JV or its buyer will issue/accept an LC governed by UCP600, normally confirmed by a top-tier bank in Hong Kong, with documents including commercial invoice, full set of clean on-board bills of lading, certificate of analysis and country-of-origin certificate; reimbursement occurs through the presenting bank's SWIFT MT202/MT799 claims. Third, documentary collections (D/P or D/A): permissible for repeat counterparties with short tenors, using the same trade document packet but without bank payment undertaking; risk allocation must be covered by export credit insurance. All outbound disbursements must include accurate invoice and purpose codes and, where the payment is in CNY, may be executed via the Cross-Border RMB Payment system using CNH liquidity from Hong Kong correspondent banks.Onshore CNY forward contracts booked with a PRC designated foreign exchange bank, strictly for hedging underlying trade exposure, maturing before the associated receivables/payables and subject to SAFE 'real demand' documentation.Cross-currency swaps (CNY/USD or CNY/HKD) permitted for hedging medium-term capital or trade liabilities, provided the JV maintains a documented exposure, the notional amount does not exceed the underlying contract, and the swap is executed through a licensed onshore bank.Offshore CNH non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) permissible only through the Hong Kong treasury company for the overseas arm, not the onshore JV; onshore PRC entities cannot routinely access NDF markets unless SAFE grants a specific cross-border hedging pilot approval.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Offshore digital EMIs are not viable as the primary treasury layer for this onshore Sino-Foreign Equity JV. PRC banking regulations permit only licensed banks to open the onshore RMB basic account, FDI capital account, and export income settlement account. SAFE requires that all cross-border capital flows be routed through a designated foreign exchange bank with a valid tax identification, customs registration, and SAFE filing. Airwallex, Revolut, or similar offshore EMIs are not licensed as deposit-taking institutions in China and cannot issue or hold the statutory account structure needed for capital verification. They may, at best, act as a front-end payment aggregator for low-value ancillary freight or marketing disbursements outside China, but they cannot replace the licensed bank line. HK-licensed SVFs can provide corporate wallet services for the Hong Kong subsidiary, but any transfer into mainland China must still end at a designated PRC bank. Overall legal constraint rating for a mainland JV is Low-to-Nil viability as a primary clearing rail; HK treasury shell services are Medium only for non-CNY third-party payments.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: RMB 200 million threshold for annual related-party tangible-goods transactions; RMB 40 million for related-party services; RMB 100 million for related-party intangible or financial-asset transfers; Master File is required when the multinational group's consolidated annual revenue exceeds RMB 2 billion and the Chinese entity is within the Local File regime

Economic substance scope: Although the PRC does not impose a separate Economic Substance Regulations form for ordinary mainland resident companies, the GAAR and substance-over-form doctrines under Enterprise Income Tax Law Article 47 and SAT Announcement 2015 No. 60 permit recharacterization of arrangements lacking commercial purpose. For the Hong Kong shareholder, Hong Kong ESR and CIGA analysis is critical: the HK company must have real premises, human resources, records, and decision-making power aligned with the dividend, interest, royalty, and sales activities, or the treaty-reduced withholding rate will be denied and the shareholder treated as a conduit

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Under-Reporting

Statutory sanction: PRC Tax Collection Administration Law Articles 32 and 63 and Criminal Law Article 201. The tax authority recovers the underpaid EIT, imposes daily late-payment interest at 0.05%, and assesses a civil penalty of 50%-500% of the tax understated; criminal tax evasion applies where the amount evaded exceeds approximately RMB 50,000 and exceeds 10% of the tax payable, with fines and imprisonment of up to 7 years; false VAT invoices additionally trigger Criminal Law Article 205

Director personal liability: Under PRC Company Law Article 20(3), the court may pierce the corporate veil and hold the directors and the Hong Kong shareholder jointly and severally liable for the JV's unpaid tax, penalties, and any unlawful distributions; directors face severely dishonest taxpayer blacklisting, travel bans and exit restrictions under Tax Collection Law Article 44, freezing of personal bank accounts, and personal criminal imprisonment if they directed or approved the fraudulent return
Treaty Abuse and Conduit Shell Arrangement

Statutory sanction: The State Taxation Administration denies treaty benefits under EIT Law Article 47 and SAT Announcement 2015 No. 60, recharacterizes the HK entity as a conduit, and imposes the full 10% statutory dividend WHT, plus late-payment interest and penalties; the HK company may also face Hong Kong profits tax reassessment and loss of offshore status for failure to satisfy ESR/CIGA requirements

Director personal liability: The arrangement is treated as an abuse of the corporate form, allowing the veil to be pierced; directors and controlling shareholders of both the JV and the HK holding company may be personally assessed for the under-withheld tax and penalties, plus travel bans, blacklisting, and criminal exposure for knowingly filing false beneficial-ownership declarations
Transfer Pricing and Contemporaneous Documentation Failure

Statutory sanction: EIT Law Articles 41, 44 and 48 and SAT Announcement 2016 No. 42 allow the tax authority to reconstruct the transaction using TNMM or cost-plus, reassess the JV's taxable income, and charge interest at the PBOC benchmark lending rate plus 5 percentage points; the failure to maintain contemporaneous documentation may be penalized separately, and intentional profit shifting may escalate to a 50%-500% evasion penalty and criminal liability under Criminal Law Article 201

Director personal liability: Directors who signed the pricing policy, related-party transaction schedule, or funds-out authorization may be pursued personally if the corporate distribution stripped the JV of assets; tax authorities can issue exit-control orders preventing the directors from leaving mainland China until the adjusted tax, interest, and penalties are settled, and a criminal prosecution may follow if the transfer-pricing documentation is forged or the understatement is deliberate
