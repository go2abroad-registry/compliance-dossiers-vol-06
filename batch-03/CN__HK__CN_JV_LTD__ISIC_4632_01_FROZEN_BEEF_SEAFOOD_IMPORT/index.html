Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; nationwide Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax Bureau) portal at https://etax.chinatax.gov.cn; import VAT and customs duties are collected at the border by the General Administration of Customs (GACC). Hong Kong SAR is a separate tax jurisdiction administered by the Inland Revenue Department (IRD) through eTAX for the non-resident shareholder, but the PRC JV is registered and taxed in mainland China.

E-government filing portal

After the State Administration for Market Regulation issues the business license for the Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture, the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) is automatically assigned and functions as the enterprise's Tax Identification Number (TIN) and VAT registration number; the JV must complete tax registration through the Electronic Tax Bureau within 30 days, record its financial accounting system, bank accounts, legal representative, tax personnel and VAT status, and apply for general VAT taxpayer status if its annual taxable sales exceed CNY 5,000,000. A foreign non-resident enterprise without a permanent establishment does not obtain a separate PRC VAT ID and is instead identified through withholding tax registration by the PRC payer; a foreign enterprise that establishes a PRC permanent establishment must register under the same USCC procedure.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — After the State Administration for Market Regulation issues the business license for the Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture, the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) is automatically assigned and functions as the enterprise's Tax Identification Number (TIN) and VAT registration number; the JV must complete tax registration through the Electronic Tax Bureau within 30 days, record its financial accounting system, bank accounts, legal representative, tax personnel and VAT status, and apply for general VAT taxpayer status if its annual taxable sales exceed CNY 5,000,000. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None; no check-the-box election and no hybrid mismatch election exists under PRC Corporate Income Tax Law. The PRC JV is mandatorily a resident enterprise taxed on worldwide income; the Hong Kong shareholder remains subject to Hong Kong's territorial profits tax only on profits sourced in Hong Kong, and no entity classification election can alter the PRC entity's tax status.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No VAT registration threshold exemption applies at importation: import VAT is payable to customs before release regardless of turnover. For domestic taxable sales, mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration applies when annual taxable sales exceed CNY 5,000,000 (RMB 5 million); small-scale taxpayers with monthly sales up to CNY 100,000 are VAT-exempt for those sales but must still be registered. The subject frozen beef and pelagic fish imports are edible agricultural products, attracting import VAT at the reduced 9% rate, while the standard VAT headline rate is 13%.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A PRC VAT general taxpayer may claim export VAT zero-rating/exemption-refund on frozen beef, pelagic fish and other goods only when the goods are physically exported from the PRC customs territory, the export customs declaration is filed in the name of the JV, sales are made to a non-resident buyer at arm's-length value, consideration is received in foreign exchange and repatriated into China, and the tax authority can verify the chain through VAT

China operates the Golden Tax Phase IV VAT invoice system. The JV must issue VAT invoices through the STA Electronic Invoice Platform or a certified e-invoice service provider integrated by API with the tax authority's real-time data warehouse. Fully digitalized e-invoices are cleared online with a unique invoice number and encrypted digital stamp, and issuing or red-letter reversal data are transmitted immediately; paper invoices are generated through anti-counterfeit tax-control equipment (Tax UKey) and must bear a tax-control password. Import VAT is paid and cleared through the Customs Single Window, and GACC/STA exchange electronic customs and VAT data in real time.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China maintains current account convertibility, permitting trade-related foreign exchange settlement without item-by-item approval, while the capital account remains subject to strict registration, verification and use monitoring. The Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture must channel its paid-in capital through a SAFE-registered foreign exchange capital account at a designated domestic bank, and each inbound remittance must be matched against the approved investment amount and the filed joint venture contract. RMB is freely convertible for bona fide import settlements, service payments, dividend payments and interest on external debt as current account items, provided the requisite underlying documents are presented to the settlement bank. Profit remittance hurdles include the requirement for audited financial statements, a tax clearance certificate, proof that the proposed dividend was deducted from after-tax earnings computed under PRC GAAP, and the practical impossibility of transferring funds for out-of-scope activities such as speculative FX trading or intra-group equity acquisitions. Capital account operations, including cross-border loans, equity purchase premia, and share transfers between non-residents, demand separate SAFE approvals or filings. The JV must also comply with the foreign debt concentration ratio and the use restriction that capital-settled RMB cannot be used for wealth management products or entrusted loans. In practice, banks treat each remittance as a transaction needing documentary evidence, and the single largest obstacles are the audit trail of the source of funds, the absence of a full tax filing record, and the exact codification of the purpose of each payment in the cross-border payment system.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export receipts must be repatriated into mainland China through a designated bank within 180 days of the export declaration (this timeline is extendable to 210 days upon a reasonable application to SAFE). Once repatriated, the JV may retain the foreign currency in an export settlement account or convert it into RMB at its own discretion; no mandatory conversion ratio applies. The receipt origin and trade background must be visible in the SAFE Monitoring System, and any prolonged non-repatriation triggers automatic bank and customs alert flags.

Central bank supervision: The People's Bank of China (PBOC) and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low feasibility. Mainland China's banking and foreign exchange framework requires a Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture executing onshore treasury and trade settlements to use a licensed domestic financial institution connected to the PBOC clearing systems and SAFE's cross-border monitoring network. Offshore fintech EMIs such as Revolut Business, Neat, Interpolitan and Airwallex are not licensed to open resident RMB settlement accounts, process capital account registrations, or discharge export-receipt collection obligations for an entity that is fiscally and operationally resident in mainland China. They may support an offshore Hong Kong treasury shell, but cannot legally serve as the JV's sole or primary payment rail. Use of an unlicensed EMI creates an unlawful account structure, an unfillable gap in the KYC audit trail, and elevated AML exposure. The JV must therefore maintain its core clearing relationship with a PRC Tier-1 commercial bank, and may only use fintech platforms peripherally for remote reconciliation in the offshore HK entity.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The foreign shareholder must first complete SAFE foreign-invested enterprise (FIE) registration. The capital contribution is then remitted from the shareholder's overseas account to the JV's foreign currency capital account at a designated domestic bank, using a wire marked with the SAFE capital injection purpose code for foreign direct investment (622001) and referencing the SAFE registration certificate number. The remittance must not be aggregated with non-equity proceeds. After receipt, the domestic bank verifies the identity of the remitter, the declared investment amount, and the validity of the capital account under the SAFE Monitoring System. The JV may settle the foreign currency into RMB upon a valid underlying payment or expenditure purpose and must ensure that the use is within the approved business scope. A PRC-registered certified public accountant must issue a capital verification report, and the JV must update the SAFE record if the paid-in capital is injected in tranches, with each tranche separately reflected in the bank's capital monitoring system.Trade settlements for frozen beef and seafood imports will rely primarily on SWIFT MT103 for telegraphic transfers, MT700/MT720 for issuing and amending irrevocable letters of credit, and documentary collections (D/P or D/A) for lower-credit counterparties. For a commodity flow, a revolving 90-day irrevocable letter of credit issued by the JV's Tier-1 bank is the standard rail, with reimbursement executed through SWIFT or CIPS if the beneficiary has a RMB settlement agreement with a Chinese bank in Hong Kong. All outbound payments must be matched against the underlying customs declaration, commercial invoice, bill of lading, and the import license, and must be declared under the correct trade purpose code. The bank will also check that the export proceeds, if any, have been collected and reported before approving incremental offshore settlement. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: The Chinese JV must prepare a contemporaneous Local File where related-party tangible asset transactions equal or exceed RMB 200 million, intangible or financial asset transactions equal or exceed RMB 100 million, or any other related-party services and royalty transactions equal or exceed RMB 40 million in a fiscal year. A Master File is mandated where the enterprise's total related-party transactions in the previous fiscal year exceeded RMB 1 billion. The JV's imports of frozen beef and seafood from a HK related trading company and its cold-chain logistics service payments will typically trigger the RMB 200 million and RMB 40 million thresholds respectively, thereby requiring Local File documentation, benchmark search, and economic substance narratives.

Economic substance scope: The Chinese tax authorities apply the economic substance doctrine through EITL Article 47 (GAAR) and SAT Notice 2016 No. 42. The Chinese JV must demonstrate Core Income-Generating Activities (CIGA) within China, including procurement negotiation, sanitary and phytosanitary compliance for imports, cold-chain inventory ownership, spoilage risk-bearing, and wholesale sales management. For the Hong Kong parent, the China-HK DTA beneficial ownership test and Hong Kong's Economic Substance Requirements (ESR) require full-time staff, premises, board decisions, and material revenue-active risk management in Hong Kong. If the HK company engages solely in dividend passage without CIGA, the Chinese tax authority may recharacterise the shareholding structure, deny treaty benefits, and attribute the economic rights back to ultimate Chinese shareholders.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion via Off-Book Frozen Meat Sales

Statutory sanction: Pursuant to the Tax Administration Law Article 63, the tax authority shall recover the evaded CIT, impose a daily late-payment surcharge of 0.05%, and levy an administrative penalty of 50% to 500% of the evaded tax. Criminal liability is triggered under PRC Criminal Law Article 201 where the tax evaded exceeds RMB 100,000 and constitutes more than 10% of the tax due, resulting in a fine and imprisonment of up to seven years for the principal offenders.

Director personal liability: If the JV is used as a personal fraud vehicle, the corporate veil is pierced and directors, shadow managers, and beneficial owners are jointly and severally liable for repatriation of the evaded tax and unreported profit. Their personal bank accounts, freezing orders, and equity assets are subject to seizure; exit bans and blacklisting are imposed by the tax authority under Article 44, and the principal executives face criminal imprisonment for defrauding state tax revenue.
Shell Holding Company in Hong Kong and Treaty Benefit Abuse

Statutory sanction: Under EITL Article 47 and the beneficial ownership clause of the China-HK Arrangement, the application of the 5% treaty rate is annulled; the shortfall between the statutory 10% and the claimed 5% becomes immediately recoverable as unpaid WHT, together with daily surcharge. Intentional misuse of a tax treaty constitutes a false tax preference, sanctionable under Tax Administration Law Article 63 at a penalty between 50% and 500% of the underpaid tax.

Director personal liability: Directors who know or approve the diversion through the shell are jointly and severally liable for the unpaid WHT and associated penalties. The legal representative, financial officer, and nominee director face personal asset freezing, a cross-border travel ban through the escort of the Supreme People's Court maritime and economic execution blacklists, and potential criminal liability for money laundering and fraudulent evasion where the diverted distributions are layered through multiple jurisdictions.
Transfer Pricing Documentation Failure and Unjustified Cross-Border Royalty Payments

Statutory sanction: The tax authority may adjust the JV's taxable income under EITL Article 41 and impose interest at the benchmark lending rate plus an additional 5 percentage points over the adjustment period. Failure to maintain contemporaneous documentation incurs an administrative fine of RMB 10,000 to RMB 50,000 under Notice 2016 No. 42, while intentionally inflated royalties that understate taxable profit attract a 50% to 500% evasion penalty under Tax Administration Law Article 63.

Director personal liability: Directors and financial officers who sign the intercompany agreements and certify the royalty deductions may be jointly and severally liable for the restitution of the artificial profit strip, including interest and penalties. The China tax authorities may apply the economic substance doctrine to pierce the veil of the HK affiliate, subject the directors to personal liability for the related-party debt, impose exit bans under the tax collection law, and refer cases to public security for fraud if fabricated royalty invoices are used to disguise profit stripping.
