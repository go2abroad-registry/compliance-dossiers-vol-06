Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

    

    

      
Tax ID Application Infrastructure

      

        
Competent tax authority
State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; online filing through the Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax Bureau) portal and the STA Electronic Invoice Service Platform; export clearance through the General Administration of Customs (GACC) China International Trade Single Window. The Hong Kong SAR Inland Revenue Department is the tax authority of the Hong Kong investor jurisdiction, but the China-resident Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture is registered for PRC tax exclusively with STA.

        
E-government filing portal
Step 1 - After incorporation with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) as a Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture under the Foreign Investment Law, the entity automatically receives the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), an 18-character legal-person code that is the statutory Tax Identification Number for PRC tax purposes and also functions as the VAT taxpayer identification number. Step 2 - SAMR transmits the incorporation record in real time to STA through the one-stop integrated government service platform; the JV is automatically registered for corporate income tax, VAT, payroll withholding, stamp duty, and urban maintenance and construction tax. No separate VAT registration number is issued because the USCC serves as the tax ID. Step 3 - The JV completes its VAT taxpayer classification through the e-Tax Bureau and must select or be classified as a general VAT taxpayer because wholesale supplies of tangible goods are taxable at 13% and annual taxable sales will exceed the RMB 5,000,000 threshold; it then activates the electronic VAT special invoice quota and digital invoicing capability. Step 4 - A foreign non-resident enterprise without a permanent establishment in China that invests in the JV does not need a PRC VAT ID; it completes a Non-Resident Enterprise withholding tax registration with the competent STA branch or effects withholding through the bank on outbound dividends, interest, and royalties. Step 5 - For export trade, the JV must register with GACC as a customs declaration unit and complete the export VAT rebate registration through the e-Tax Bureau export rebate module before claiming zero-rating.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

      

        
Default tax classification
Entity_Level_Taxation

        
Available election forms
No check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election exists under PRC law. The only relevant statutory election is the VAT taxpayer-model election: a small-scale taxpayer may elect general VAT taxpayer status below the RMB 5,000,000 annual taxable sales threshold, but general taxpayer status is mandatory once the threshold is exceeded. For corporate income tax, no consolidated group filing or pass-through election exists.

Standard VAT / GST rate
13%

        
Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

        Export 0% zero-rating conditions
        
Under PRC VAT policy, physically exported tangible goods, such as acetate ophthalmic eyeglass frames sold by a registered general VAT taxpayer for export, are zero-rated (0% VAT), with attributable input VAT recoverable through the export VAT rebate procedure. The statutory criteria are: (1) the JV is a general VAT taxpayer registered for export rebates; (2) the goods are declared to GACC and actually leave the PRC customs territory; (3) the goods are sold to a genuine overseas buyer and the sale proceeds are received and repatriated through banking channels under SAFE trade monitoring rules; (4) the export documentation bundle is complete, consistent, and retained for audit; and (5) the transaction is not circular, over-invoiced, or otherwise artificial. Qualifying cross-border digital services can also be zero-rated if consumed entirely outside China, the direct beneficiary is a non-resident, and payment is received in foreign exchange, but the core goods export is the relevant zero-rating event for this JV.

All B2B taxable supplies must be evidenced by a VAT Special Invoice or a fully digitalized electronic e-invoice generated through the STA Electronic Invoice Service Platform under the Golden Tax System IV. The JV must connect through the official tax-control API; every invoice includes the seller's and buyer's USCC, taxable rate, taxable amount, and tax amount, and carries an encrypted one-time invoice code and anti-fraud digital stamp. Invoice data are transmitted to STA in near real time for verification; unapproved offline invoice formats and private invoice software are prohibited. Invoice quota changes, voids, reversals, and red-letter invoices must be reported digitally through the e-Tax Bureau. Zero-rated export sales are substantiated by an ordinary export invoice/fapiao and the GACC customs export declaration rather than a VAT Special Invoice showing output VAT.

      
The STA and GACC jointly scrutinize export declarations and VAT rebate claims. If zero-rating substantiation is incomplete, inconsistent, or false, the export sale is reclassified as a domestic taxable sale at 13% VAT, the VAT output tax and any recovered input VAT are clawed back with a 0.05% per-day late-payment surcharge, and the taxpayer is subject to a penalty ranging from 50% to five times the evaded tax or falsely claimed refund. Fraudulent export rebate claims, forged customs documents, or unrepatriated export proceeds may lead to suspension or loss of export rebate qualification, criminal referral to public security and customs anti-smuggling authorities, and retrospective clawback for an unlimited period where fraud is established.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

      
Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

    

    

      
Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

      

        
Current-account convertibility
China maintains a managed floating exchange rate regime with CNY convertibility principally limited to current-account transactions under Articles 6-8 of the IMF Articles of Agreement. Current-account trade credits, service payments, trade-related commission remittances, and declared profit remittances are convertible provided the underlying transaction is genuine and document-backed. Capital-account items, including inward FDI equity contributions, capital injection settlement into RMB, shareholder loan repayments, cross-border guarantees, and portfolio investments, remain subject to SAFE registration, quotas, or administrative approvals. The Joint Venture benefits from a favorable negative-list environment for wholesale optical frame distribution, but profit remittance to the Hong Kong parent requires a mandatory SAFE pathway: audited financial statements, a bank capital verification report, a tax settlement certificate, and a corporate board resolution. Surplus RMB cannot be freely converted to USD beyond these compliance frameworks; any foreign-exchange hedging or swap must evidence real economic demand. The PRC retains discretionary rate-regulating macro-prudential tools, yet an onshore JV can achieve regular cross-border treasury velocity through a registered onshore RMB current account and a foreign-exchange current account, provided all capital items are carefully orchestrated through SAFE's capital item information system.

        
Export proceeds surrender rules
Under SAFE's Measures on Export Proceeds Repatriation and Settlement (2012, as amended), all export foreign-currency proceeds must be repatriated to a domestic designated foreign-exchange current account within five business days of the value date. The mandatory conversion ratio is nil; the exporter may elect to retain the full balance in foreign currency or settle into RMB, subject to the authenticity of the underlying commercial documents such as sales contracts, customs declarations, commercial invoices, bills of lading, and the bank's trade-monitoring data. For this optical frames JV, USD or EUR receipts from EU and ASEAN distributors must be swept into the onshore current foreign-exchange account no later than five days after collection, and may not remain parked offshore without SAFE approval.

      

      
Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) and State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Turnover: Hong Kong has no quantitative balance sheet, turnover, or headcount threshold for statutory audit under the Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622). Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

      
Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Local File preparation under SAT Announcement [2016] No. 42 for a taxpayer whose annual related-party transactions reach any of the following thresholds: tangible-asset transfer > RMB 200 million; intangible-asset transfer > RMB 100 million; financial-asset transfer > RMB 100 million; related-party service transactions > RMB 100 million. A Master File is mandatory where the consolidated revenue of the multinational group exceeds RMB 2 billion and cross-border related-party transactions exist.

      
Economic substance scope: The mainland PRC does not apply offshore-state Economic Substance Regulations, but the tax authorities invoke the Economic Substance / CIGA doctrine when scrutinising treaty benefits and transfer pricing. The JV must demonstrate that its wholesale distribution, inventory, logistics, invoicing, and customer contracting functions are performed in mainland China with payroll and premises. The Hong Kong interrelated entity, if claiming CDTA benefits, must separately demonstrate core income-generating activities (CIGA) in Hong Kong through substantive offices, employees, and decision-making; absence will cause denial of treaty relief, reclassification of payments, and application of the GAAR under Enterprise Income Tax Law Article 47 and Article 48 with possible penal consequences.

      

    

    

      
Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

      
      

        Tax Evasion / False Tax Return
        
Statutory sanction: Article 63 of the PRC Tax Collection and Administration Law imposes a penalty of 50% to 500% of the amount of tax underpaid. Under Article 201 of the Criminal Law, tax evasion exceeding RMB 100,000 and greater than 10% of taxable tax due is punishable by up to 3 years imprisonment; more serious amounts and over 30% of taxable tax due are punishable by 3 to 7 years imprisonment.

        
Director personal liability: Where the directors orchestrated or knowingly permitted the false filing, the corporate veil can be pierced under Company Law Article 20: directors shall be jointly and severally liable for the company's unpaid tax, interest, and penalties. Directors may face personal bank account freezing, asset attachment, civil restitution, travel bans (exit prohibition / tax defaulter blacklisting), and criminal imprisonment under Article 201 of the Criminal Law if convicted as a direct responsible person.

      

      
      

        Shell Company / Beneficial Ownership Abuse in DTA Claim
        
Statutory sanction: The tax authority will deny the treaty benefit and apply the 10% statutory withholding tax on the full dividend distribution, plus interest on underpaid tax, under the GAAR in Enterprise Income Tax Law Articles 47-48 and Tax Collection and Administration Law Article 36. If fraudulent documents are submitted for treaty relief, Article 64 of the Tax Collection and Administration Law applies with penalties of 50% to 500% of the tax avoided.

        
Director personal liability: Directors who arranged the shell structure or signed the counterfeit TRC application face personal civil liability for the difference in tax plus penalties; the blacklisting regime applies to the individual directors prohibiting e-commerce, high-consumption expenditure and outbound travel; criminal liability for intentional tax evasion under Article 201 may attach if the shell was used to conceal taxable income, with imprisonment up to 7 years and confiscation of personal assets.

      

      
      

        Transfer Pricing Manipulation / Related-Party Loss Shifting
        
Statutory sanction: Under Enterprise Income Tax Law Article 41, the tax authority shall perform a special tax adjustment using the arm's length principle. Article 114 of the Tax Collection and Administration Law and Article 48 of the EIT Law impose interest at the central bank benchmark rate plus 5 percentage points for underpaid tax, and the adjustment can be assessed for up to 10 years. Failure to file the related-party transaction form attracts a fine of RMB 2,000 to RMB 10,000; if the non-filing involves intentional concealment, the 50%-500% penalty under Article 63 applies.

        
Director personal liability: If directors have made fraudulent or false documents, or knowingly transferred enterprise assets to shield profits from tax, the court may pierce the corporate veil. Directors may be held personally liable for the adjusted tax, interest, and penalties; their names are placed on the tax defaulter public blacklist causing travel bans, restrictions on exit, property attachment, and disqualification from serving as directors or senior officers; intentional TP fraud with cross-border concealment can be prosecuted under Article 201 of the Criminal Law carrying terms of imprisonment up to 7 years.
