Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation (the PRC JV is a resident corporate taxpayer taxed on worldwide income; the Hong Kong partner remains subject to territorial profits tax on Hong Kong-sourced profits only).

Available election forms

None. PRC law does not provide check-the-box or hybrid mismatch elections; the Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture is mandatorily taxed as a taxable entity. Transfer pricing advance pricing arrangements and special reorganization elections may be applied for under the Corporate Income Tax Law, but these are not entity classification elections.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 of annual taxable sales for mandatory VAT general taxpayer registration; a newly established JV must nevertheless complete tax registration immediately upon formation even if its turnover is below this threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exported goods are eligible for the 0% VAT rate if they leave the PRC customs territory under a valid export customs declaration, the seller is a PRC VAT taxpayer, the export price is at arm's length, the goods are the subject of a genuine commercial sale, and the export proceeds are collected/repatriated in foreign currency within the statutory deadline. Clinical diagnostic reagents are zero-ratable if they are not subject to export prohibitions and the exporter holds the required diagnostics license/product registration.

  • Customs Export Declaration (People's Republic of China Customs Export Declaration/Bill of Entry)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export from PRC customs territory
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or bank receipt of cross-border inward remittance/report on international transactions under SAFE export proceeds collection monitoring
  • Foreign exchange settlement confirmation from the receiving PRC bank
  • Commercial invoice and packing list issued by the JV exporter
  • Export sales contract/purchase order and, where applicable, the diagnostic reagent export license

The STA mandates fully digitalized electronic invoices issued through the STA electronic invoice service platform and recorded in the taxpayer's tax digital account; invoice issuance data is reported to tax authorities in real time. The Golden Tax System applies digital anti-fraud control and invoice verification, and customs export declaration data is matched automatically through the China International Trade Single Window to substantiate export zero-rating and tax refund claims.

Tax authorities and customs cross-match customs export declarations, electronic invoices, transport documents, insurance records, and foreign exchange collection data. Failure to repatriate export proceeds within 180 days (unless an approved extension applies) triggers a retrospective clawback of the zero-rated VAT benefit, repayment of input VAT credited/refunded, and late-payment surcharge. Fraudulent or fabricated export transactions are treated as tax fraud under the PRC Tax Collection and Administration Law and the Criminal Law, attracting administrative penalties of 50% to 500% of the tax involved, confiscation of illegal gains, forfeiture of export tax-refund status, and criminal liability for the responsible persons.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The People's Republic of China maintains a managed floating exchange rate regime under the Foreign Exchange Administration Regulations. The RMB is convertible on current account items for genuine trade in goods and services, but capital account transactions require SAFE registration, approval, or filing. For this Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture distributing laboratory diagnostic reagents, operating cash flows will combine mainland sales revenue in RMB with Hong Kong procurement and settlement in USD or HKD. Export proceeds from cross-border reagent sales must be repatriated through designated banks within the statutory timeline and are subject to mandatory surrender ratios under SAFE Circular 10 and the Measures for Foreign Exchange Administration of Export Proceeds. Profit remittances from the mainland subsidiary to the Hong Kong JV require audited financial statements, board resolutions, and tax clearance under the Corporate Income Tax Law, plus SAFE verification via the Foreign Exchange Registration Certificate. Dividends are not freely convertible until the underlying tax liability and foreign exchange filing obligations are discharged. The Hong Kong entity itself enjoys full convertibility under HKMA's free capital movement regime, but every mainland-bound RMB remittance, either as registered capital injection or intercompany trade payment, will be screened under the PBOC Anti-Money Laundering Rules and the SAFE cross-border RMB settlement guidelines. The practical effect is a two-tier treasury architecture: an onshore current account in mainland China with strict purpose-code discipline, and an offshore HKD/USD account in Hong Kong with permissionless clearing, subject to beneficial ownership and sanctions screening.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE regulations, export foreign exchange receipts must be repatriated into the domestic foreign currency account or settled into RMB through the designated bank within a maximum of 180 days from the date of customs declaration for general trade. For this diagnostic reagent distribution JV, a mandatory conversion ratio of 100% applies to the declared export value, meaning no legally permissible retention of export proceeds in foreign currency outside the approved onshore FCY account; commodities classified under HS Chapter 3822 regulated as IVD reagents are subject to standard surrender mechanics, with SAFE comparing customs export data against banking receipts via the Digital Cross-Border Finance Platform.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) for mainland China; for the Hong Kong SAR treasury layer, the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) supervises licensed banks while the Linked Exchange Rate System operates without routine capital controls

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Digital EMI-only cross-border solutions hold Low viability for the onshore mainland treasury because China prohibits foreign fintech EMIs from operating as settlement institutions or maintaining RMB clearing accounts under the PBOC Payment Services Regulation. The Hong Kong treasury layer can use licensed Stored Value Facility holders that provide multi-currency virtual accounts, but they lack direct access to the HKMA Real Time Gross Settlement system for high-value trade flows and cannot fully replace a Tier-1 licensed bank for this JV's commercial letter of credit and SWIFT MT103 disbursements. The recommended strategy is to use a Hong Kong licensed bank as the principal clearing counterparty and to engage fintech EMIs only for pre-accounting reconciliation and invoicing analytics, not for actual fund movement or regulatory filing.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital contributions from the Hong Kong JV into the mainland operating entity must be booked as registered capital through a SAFE-approved Foreign Capital Account at the domestic designated Tier-1 bank. The sequence is: submit the Foreign Investment Information Report from the Market Regulation Bureau; open the RMB Basic Account and capital FX account; remit HKD or USD via SWIFT MT103 using purpose code 721010 for foreign-invested enterprise paid-in capital; instruct the receiving bank to complete capital verification against the audited registered capital schedule; then transfer the settled RMB only after SAFE releases the capital from the 'to-be-settled' deposit sub-account. Any injection above the registered amount will be classified as a shareholder loan and requires SAFE's foreign debt registration. Trade-based intercompany funding must route through the current account using purpose code 101010 for imported goods, supported by invoices, airway bills, and customs release notices.Payments to reagent manufacturers in Germany, the United States, and Japan are settled via SWIFT MT103 for open-account purchases after receipt of the tested shipment, with the invoicing currency either USD or EUR. For large recurring shipments exceeding one million US dollars, the JV should issue an irrevocable Commercial Letter of Credit at sight through Bank of China (Hong Kong) to protect the mainland buyer's deposit, with all bills of exchange and transport documents routed through the confirming bank. Documentary collections remain permissible for moderate-value monthly supplies, but the recommended treasury policy is to maintain the LC pipeline for the highest-value IVD raw-material contracts and to reserve MT103 TTs for operating purchases of packaging and secondary reagents. The only quantitative-style carve-out is the dormant-company exemption under section 5 of Cap.622, and only where no accounting transactions have occurred and the company has not traded or carried on any business during the relevant period. A company in the IVD reagent wholesale sector carrying active commercial distribution transactions cannot rely on a dormant exemption and must engage an independent certified public accountant holding a Hong Kong practising certificate.
