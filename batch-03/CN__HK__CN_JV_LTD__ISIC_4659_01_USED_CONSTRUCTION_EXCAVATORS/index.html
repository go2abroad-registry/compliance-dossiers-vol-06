Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China; online taxpayer services are administered through the State Taxation Administration Electronic Tax Bureau portal and the STA electronic invoice service platform.

E-government filing portal

A Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture formed in mainland China must complete taxpayer registration at the competent tax office of the State Taxation Administration within 30 days after the issuance of its business license, using the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) as the statutory Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN). For foreign non-resident shareholders or the Hong Kong SAR corporate participant, the foreign entity evidence required includes the home-country certificate of incorporation, constitutional documents, board resolutions, notarized passport or digital identity of the legal representative, power of attorney for tax filing agents, bank account confirmation, lease or property document evidencing the business premises, and a declaration that the shareholder has no inconsistent residency treatment under the PRC-Hong Kong double tax arrangement. The tax office issues/confirms the 18-digit USCC as the TIN, registers the entity as a legal-person VAT taxpayer, and activates the digital tax taxpayer file in the Electronic Tax Bureau. A non-resident enterprise with no establishment but with China-source income is registered separately through the withholding agent using the non-resident taxpayer identification number; the JV itself is registered as a resident enterprise and may not rely on offshore pass-through status.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None for entity classification; a Chinese Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture is mandatorily taxed at entity level on worldwide income and cannot elect pass-through treatment. Election mechanisms are available only at the VAT administrative layer, principally the voluntary election of general VAT taxpayer status when annual taxable sales are below the RMB 5,000,000 compulsory threshold, and the election to apply export VAT refund/zero-rating to qualifying overseas sales. There are no hybrid mismatch or check-the-box elections under Chinese tax law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

The compulsory general VAT taxpayer registration threshold is annual taxable sales exceeding RMB 5,000,000 for the sale of goods; all entities engaged in taxable activities must maintain tax registration from commencement of business, and small-scale VAT payer status with reduced filing/collection rules applies below the threshold, including the monthly RMB 100,000 and quarterly RMB 300,000 VAT exemption ceilings for small-scale taxpayers.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The export of used construction excavators qualifies for VAT zero-rating only when a VAT general taxpayer physically exports goods from China under customs supervision, files an official export customs declaration, transfers title and risk to a non-resident buyer, receives payment in a permitted foreign currency through sanctioned banking channels, and maintains contemporaneous evidence of actual shipment and the foreign exchange repatriation. The zero-rating claim also requires that the enterprise is not in an export tax refund risk-control list and that the transaction is a genuine commercial sale rather than a customs declaration-only or VAT-invoice-only arrangement.

  • Customs Export Declaration Form (Customs Bill of Entry / export goods declaration)
  • Bill of Lading or air waybill evidencing export of the excavators from a Chinese customs port
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / Cross-Border Foreign Exchange Income Declaration Form and bank receipt/proof of foreign exchange collection
  • Signed commercial invoice and export sales contract describing the used excavators, quantity, price, and Incoterms
  • Export Goods VAT Refund (Exemption) Application Form and export declaration data submitted through the State Taxation Administration Electronic Tax Bureau
  • Supporting documents such as certificates of origin, pre-shipment inspection certificates, used-machinery condition reports, and import-country customs clearances, when applicable

VAT reporting is integrated with the Golden Tax Phase IV system: invoices must be issued through the State Taxation Administration-approved electronic invoice platform as fully digital electronic VAT invoices using standardized OFD/XML data envelopes, each carrying an encrypted tax-control code and anti-fraud digital stamp; invoice issuance details are uploaded to the STA electronic invoice service platform in real time, and buyers may only claim input VAT credit after the issuing taxpayer's invoice data has been certified/cleared by the tax authority.

The State Taxation Administration, Customs, and State Administration of Foreign Exchange apply cross-agency data matching for every zero-rated export claim, comparing customs export declaration data, VAT invoice data, tax-control serial numbers, shipping records, and bank forex receipts. Missing or inconsistent documentation triggers automatic suspension of the refund, a compulsory tax assessment, and a retroactive clawback of the refunded input VAT together with late-payment surcharge and interest. Fraudulent or knowingly false zero-rating claims are punishable under the Tax Collection and Administration Law by fines of 0.5 to 5 times the tax refund or understated tax, suspension of export tax refund qualifications, customs penalties, and potential criminal liability for tax fraud and evasion, including penalties on responsible legal representatives and other directly liable persons.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Hong Kong operates a free capital-account regime with full HKD convertibility under the Linked Exchange Rate System, so no central approval is required for capital movements in the Hong Kong treasury layer. The PRC leg, however, applies a managed floating exchange rate, full current-account convertibility subject to verification, and stricter discretionary control over capital-account items. For this China-HK joint venture, current-account flows (export proceeds, import payments, fees, and dividend remittances) are convertible once the bank validates the supporting commercial invoice, customs declaration, tax certificate, and SAFE trade-monitoring record. Capital-account flows, including registered capital injections, shareholder loans, and guarantees, require SAFE foreign-investment registration or a bank-delegated SAFE verification, a valid purpose code, and a genuine economic underlying. Dividend/profit remittance from the PRC entity to the HK shareholder will additionally demand audited financial statements, the corporate income tax filing and payment certificate, and a board resolution; the withholding tax clearance must be settled before release. There is no mandatory conversion or Central Bank prior-approval burden in Hong Kong; the practical hurdle is the PRC requirement to demonstrate a real trade/investment relationship and maintain documentary integrity in every direction of flow.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under current SAFE regulations, PRC export proceeds in foreign currency must be repatriated into a designated domestic foreign-currency settlement account within 180 days from the date of the customs export declaration unless an extended payment term is registered with Customs/SAFE. There is no mandatory conversion ratio into RMB under the present verified-documentation regime; the exporter may retain the full 100% in foreign currency or convert any portion to RMB, subject to the bank's account ceiling and trade documentation requirements. For proceeds received by the Hong Kong treasury entity, there is no surrender obligation and funds may remain in HKD or USD without conversion or SAFE approval.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the People's Bank of China (PBOC) for the PRC-side exchange controls; Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) for the Hong Kong SAR monetary authority.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Offshore digital EMIs are rated LOW VIABILITY as primary treasury rails. Under PRC SAFE/PBOC rules, foreign-exchange accounts, capital-account settlements, and trade-proceeds collection must be conducted by licensed commercial banks within the SAFE reporting framework; an offshore EMI is not a permitted counterparty for an FDI capital account or trade-in-goods settlement. In Hong Kong, HKMA-regulated Stored Value Facilities (SVFs) and Money Service Operators (MSOs) can provide payment functionality, but they are not licensed to provide bank-grade foreign exchange derivative contracts, commercial letters of credit, import/export documentary collections, or trade-finance loans. An EMI may therefore be used only as a supplementary payment utility for immaterial operating expenses and cannot replace the mandated Tier-1 banking relationship.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Route the inbound shareholder injection via SWIFT MT103 to the receiving bank's FDI Foreign Exchange Capital Account, quoting the SAFE-verified purpose code for 'Foreign Direct Investment Capital Injection / Registered Capital Contribution' in field 70; use CIPS for RMB transfers or CHIPS/Fedwire for USD/HKD transfers and do not split the injection into multiple sub-threshold payments. After funds are credited, engage a certified public accountant to perform capital verification and issue a capital verification report before the JV may convert capital to RMB or disburse any funds. The JV must also complete the bank-delegated SAFE use-of-funds filing or obtain the Capital Account Payment Confirmation for each later drawdown. Every converted RMB payment must be supported by the underlying used-excavator purchase contract, tax invoice, and delivery evidence, and the funds must never be co-mingled with current-account trade proceeds.For open-account supplier payments, clear through SWIFT MT103 using a trade-in-goods current-account purpose code and the underlying invoice/purchase order. Use HK CHATS (RTGS) for HKD, USD CHATS/Fedwire for US dollars, and CIPS for RMB leg payments to mainland counterparties. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File (同期资料本地文档) mandatory if any of the following thresholds is met in the tax year: tangible asset related-party transfers exceed RMB 200 million (e.g., used excavators), financial asset transfers exceed RMB 100 million, intangible asset transfers exceed RMB 100 million, or other related-party transactions (including services, interest, royalties) exceed RMB 40 million in aggregate. Master File (主体文档) mandatory if the group's total consolidated annual related-party transaction volume exceeds RMB 10 billion. Documentation must be filed within 30 days after the tax authority's request (not automatically filed), but must be prepared before the annual filing deadline.

Economic substance scope: The PRC does not apply CIGA ESR laws similar to the BVI/Cayman regime, but the Beneficial Ownership rules (Announcement 2018 No. 9) and the General Anti-Avoidance Rule (CIT Law Article 47) require the Hong Kong company to demonstrate real economic substance and control over key value-driving functions. The tax authority can reattribute profits and deny treaty benefits if the Hong Kong entity lacks personnel, premises, decision-making, or assumes no risk in the purchase/sale of the used excavators. A Hong Kong company that is merely a nominee or conduit will be disregarded for DTA purposes.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Transfer Pricing Profit Shifting (特别纳税调整)

Statutory sanction: Under the PRC Enterprise Income Tax Law Article 41 and 48, the tax authority may make a special tax adjustment to reprice the related-party transaction and add interest equal to the benchmark loan rate plus 5 percentage points on the underpaid tax. If recharacterised as tax evasion, the fine under the Law on Tax Administration and Collection (LTAC) Article 63 is 50% to 500% of the additional tax due; failure to provide TP documentation shifts the burden of proof and allows the authority to estimate income (Article 44).

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false contracts or knowingly misstate transfer pricing documents may be personally liable for restitution under Company Law Article 20 (piercing the veil) and for criminal tax evasion under Criminal Law Article 201 (with intent and materiality required), with imprisonment up to 7 years and fines. Their personal bank accounts can be frozen; the legal representative may be placed on the exit-ban blacklist prevented from leaving China (LTAC Article 44).
Failure to Withhold Dividend / Interest Withholding Tax

Statutory sanction: Under LTAC Article 69, the withholding agent that fails to withhold or remit the withheld tax is fined from 50% to 300% of the amount that should have been withheld; the tax authority will also charge overdue interest at 0.05% per day (Article 32) and may collect the unpaid tax directly from the non-resident payee. In cases of deliberate deception or evasion, Criminal Law Article 201 applies with fines and imprisonment.

Director personal liability: Directors who authorised the distribution without setting aside the WHT may be held personally liable for the unpaid tax to the extent of assets distributed in violation of distribution rules (Company Law Article 166). If the company is liquidated and the deficiency is hidden, the court may pierce the veil and order directors to repay the company's debts. Criminal exposure: if a director deliberately conspired to evade tax, personal imprisonment and confiscation of assets; travel ban (exit ban) and tax blacklisting apply to the legal representative.
Treaty Abuse / Shell Conduit (Beneficial Ownership Failure)

Statutory sanction: Under the General Anti-Avoidance Rule (CIT Law Article 47) and the beneficial ownership rules (Announcement 2018 No. 9), tax authorities may deny the DTA benefit and impose the full 10% statutory WHT plus a special tax adjustment. A shell-company arrangement may be recharacterised as a direct payment to the ultimate shareholder, leading to penalties under LTAC Article 63 and a possible criminal referral for issuing false invoices or fraudulent tax returns.

Director personal liability: Directors who fabricated substantial evidence (e.g., sham board meetings, backdated service agreements, empty offices) to obtain treaty benefits may be personally liable for fraud under Criminal Law Articles 201 (tax evasion) or 266 (fraud); they can be stripped of the benefit of limited liability and be ordered to repay the company's tax debts from personal assets; the legal representative is subject to an exit ban and a 12-month public tax blacklist, and repeat offences lead to criminal imprisonment.
