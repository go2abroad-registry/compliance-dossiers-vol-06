Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Companies Registry, Hong Kong SAR, operating through the e-Registry/ICRIS digital portal, with the Business Registration Office of the Inland Revenue Department under the one-stop company and business registration arrangement.

Governing statute

Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622, Laws of Hong Kong), particularly sections 67 to 68 (incorporation and company status), 112 (allotment return), 451 (registered office), 474-477 and 479 (directors and company secretary), and the Companies (Fees and Regulations) Regulation (Cap. 622H); Business Registration Ordinance (Cap. 310), section 5, and the Business Registration Regulations. The one-stop system integrates the Companies Registry and Inland Revenue Department Business Registration Office.

Formation / constitutional filings

Form NNC1 (Incorporation Form - Company Limited by Shares), the Company's executed Articles of Association, and the integrated Notice to Business Registration Office (IRBR1) forming part of Form NNC1.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 18 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None. No minimum paid-up capital or blocked escrow account is required under Hong Kong law. A private company limited by shares may be incorporated with a nominal share capital determined in the Articles of Association, and each subscriber must subscribe for at least one share. The statement of capital in Form NNC1 must be accurate; any unpaid share consideration is a debt receivable, but no escrow, capital blocking, or capital verification certificate is required by the Companies Registry.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA) via the national electronic tax platform (e-Tax); for Hong Kong SAR matters, the Inland Revenue Department via the eTAX portal.

E-government filing portal

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

All PRC-established enterprises must complete tax and VAT registration upon incorporation without a size-based registration threshold. The compulsory general VAT taxpayer registration threshold is annual taxable turnover exceeding CNY 5,000,000; below that threshold, small-scale taxpayer registration rules apply.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The 0% VAT rate applies to exported tangible goods where the PRC-registered seller actually declares the goods to PRC Customs and the physical goods leave the customs territory; the transaction must be evidenced by a valid export declaration, international transport document, commercial contract, and export sales invoice. For qualifying offshore digital services or technology export, the service must be rendered to an overseas recipient, consumed wholly outside China, and settled in foreign exchange. The zero-rating right is substantiated only after the taxpayer completes customs and tax-bureau export information matching and bank foreign-exchange receipt verification.

  • Customs Export Goods Declaration Form (Bill of Entry)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) / Export Proceeds Collection Certificate issued by the receiving bank
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Export Sales Contract and Incoterms evidence
  • Export VAT Invoice and electronic export-tax-rebate data submitted through the STA e-Tax portal

All VAT taxpayers must issue invoices through the STA-sanctioned electronic tax invoice system. In the nationwide fully digitalised electronic invoice pilot, registered taxpayers issue e-invoices through the official e-Invoice platform or an integrated ERP API connection, with real-time automatic clearance and data filing to the STA. Invoices carry tax control numbers and anti-forgery digital stamps; credit/red-letter invoices require validation through the taxpayer electronic tax filing account. Companies must preserve invoice-level data and file periodic VAT returns electronically.

The STA and PRC Customs will retrospectively disallow the 0% VAT treatment and claw back output VAT, input VAT refunds, and export tax rebates if the supporting customs, export, or foreign-exchange documentation is false, incomplete, or non-matching. Under the PRC Law on Tax Collection and Administration, the taxpayer may be charged late-payment surcharge and an administrative penalty of 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax, with potential criminal liability under the PRC Criminal Law for VAT invoice fraud or export-tax-rebate fraud. In Hong Kong SAR, any unfounded offshore-profits claim is subject to additional assessments, penalties, and prosecution under the Inland Revenue Ordinance.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Under the PRC current-account and capital-account framework, the RMB is fully convertible for current-account trade in goods and services provided the underlying transaction is genuine and validated through SAFE's Trade in Goods system and the designated bank's authenticity checks. Capital-account transactions, including the Hong Kong parent's equity injection, the JV's shareholder loans, profit repatriation, and cross-border asset transfers, remain subject to SAFE registration, FDI information reporting, and periodic compliance examinations. The JV may purchase foreign exchange only on a real-need, documentary basis using invoices, contracts, customs declarations, tax payment references, and the applicable SAFE registration number; there is no unexamined spot market access. Dividend remittance to the Hong Kong shareholder requires audited annual financial statements, a completed corporate income tax settlement certificate, distributable profit verification, and statutory withholding tax clearance. Export proceeds are no longer forcibly surrendered, but must be repatriated to China in bookable form and either retained in a designated foreign-exchange current account or converted to RMB at the prevailing onshore rate through the designated bank. Commodity trading flows with volatile settlement lags, especially bulk petrochemical polymers, require prudent FX position tracking and advance relationship-manager engagement to prevent documentary mismatches and impossible-to-settle events under the genuine-transaction doctrine.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE's Measures for the Administration of Trade in Goods, export proceeds in foreign exchange must generally be brought back into the PRC within 180 calendar days from the export customs declaration date; the statutory surrender ratio is effectively zero because the 2012 abolition of forced surrender permits the JV to hold export receipts in a current-account foreign-exchange deposit account. Any conversion into RMB must be executed at the market rate at a designated domestic bank after that bank completes matching checks against the customs declaration, commercial invoice, contract, bill of lading, and collection record. For the Hong Kong parent, no mainland surrender requirement applies, but if the parent centralises export collections for the JV, each leg must be independently documented to avoid private settlement or illegal cross-border currency arbitrage.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China, exercising delegated authority from the People's Bank of China (PBOC), with the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) as the complementary supervisor for the Hong Kong clearing corridor and offshore CNH market.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Offshore digital electronic money institutions carrying Hong Kong MSO or EU EMI licences are not authorised by SAFE or PBOC to open onshore foreign-currency or RMB clearing accounts for a PRC-domiciled Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture, nor can they satisfy the statutory bank functions for FX settlement, capital verification, tax withholding remittance, or documentary trade processing. The viability of using any fintech EMI as the primary clearing rail for CN_JV_LTD is therefore Low. Such platforms may only act as a supplementary liquidity concentration tool for the Hong Kong parent treasury, with Medium viability, provided all underlying import and export settlements remain with a designated PRC bank. Attempting to use an unlicensed EMI for onshore settlement would trigger an AML compliance block, create an undeclared correspondent risk, and expose the JV to PBOC penalties.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Equity injection from the Hong Kong shareholder to CN_JV_LTD must be routed exclusively through the SWIFT network into the JV's designated capital settlement account at a tier-1 PRC bank, using a standard MT103 message with the remitter's full banking details, the beneficiary's unified social credit code, the SAFE FDI registration reference, and the purpose code for equity investment in field 70. The receiving bank will verify the board resolution approving the capital call, the SAFE FDI Foreign Exchange Registration Statement, the JV's business licence and joint-venture contract, and the Hong Kong parent's clean source-of-funds evidence before crediting. Mandatory Audit Threshold Mandatory Audit for All Entities

Turnover: A Hong Kong private company is exempt from statutory audit only if it qualifies as a small private company or a member of a small group under Part 7 of the Companies Ordinance Cap 622. Qualification requires satisfying at least two of the following three conditions in both the current and immediately preceding financial year: (1) total revenue does not exceed HK$20 million; (2) total assets do not exceed HK$10 million; (3) the average number of employees does not exceed 50. If the company fails at least two of those criteria, or if it is a public company, a parent required to prepare group accounts, or a company whose auditor is demanded by shareholders or creditors, an independent audit by a licensed Certified Public Accountant is legally mandatory. The audit must be conducted in accordance with Hong Kong Standards on Auditing and the auditor must be appropriately licensed.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File required when annual related-party transactions for tangible goods exceed RMB 200,000,000; Master File required when consolidated group revenue exceeds RMB 2,000,000,000. Related-party service transactions exceeding RMB 40,000,000 and intangible or financing transactions exceeding RMB 100,000,000 also trigger documentation.

Economic substance scope: Although the PRC does not have separate Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) in the Gulf format, the PRC General Anti-Avoidance Rules (GAAR), specifically the quotient covering tax avoidance in excessive reliance on legal form without commercial substance, and the Beneficial Ownership rules of Announcement 2018 No. 9 require taxpayer substance. Core Income-Generating Activities (CIGA) must be present in the claimed treaty jurisdiction; a Hong Kong shell lacking substance will be denied treaty benefits, and the PRC entity may be subject to dividend WHT at 10%. The PRC tax authorities apply the principle of substance-led recharacterization under which intercompany payments will be reclassified as deemed dividends or capital gains if no real business substance exists.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Understatement of Income

Statutory sanction: PRC Tax Collection Administration Law (TCAL) Article 63: penalty of 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax, recovery of unpaid tax, and daily late surcharge of 0.05% on overdue tax; Criminal Law Article 201: for-evasion amounts exceeding RMB 100,000 and exceeding 10% of taxable income, the criminal penalty is imprisonment of up to 7 years, plus fines.

Director personal liability: Directors who orchestrate or knowingly participate in the fraud are jointly and severally liable for restitution of the unpaid tax from personal assets. They may face criminal prosecution, exit bans (denial of exit from China), blacklisting in the social credit system, and seizure of personal bank accounts and real estate. In egregious cases, imprisonment of the responsible director for up to 7 years is statutorily possible.
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance (Lack of Arm's Length and Documentation)

Statutory sanction: TCAL Article 44 and Article 64: tax authorities may estimate taxable income using deemed methods; imposed tax adjustment, additional 5% annual interest assessed on the underpaid tax, and penalty of 50% to 500% of the adjusted tax; failure to provide documentation in the harmful extreme may attract criminal liability for fraudulent tax returns under Criminal Law Article 203 if the company repeatedly fails to pay tax after a demand.

Director personal liability: The board of directors personally order the transfer pricing policy; if they knowingly use it to defraud the tax authorities, they can be held jointly liable for the adjusted tax and penalties. The tax authority may petition courts to attach personal assets of the directors; directors may also be disqualified from corporate management and subjected to criminal investigation for evasion.
Shell Company / Treaty Abuse (Claiming DTA Benefits without Economic Substance)

Statutory sanction: Announcement 2018 No. 9, together with TCAL Article 120? Actually, the PRC tax authorities will deny treaty benefits retroactively and reclassify the distributions as taxable at the full 10% under domestic law; in the event of avoidance, a penalty of 50% to 500% of the under-withheld amount plus interest and late surcharge under TCAL Article 63 may apply; additionally, the PRC GAAR allows the tax authority to restructure the transaction and assess a penalty for evasion.

Director personal liability: Personally liable for the withholding shortfall if they sign false beneficial ownership declarations; civil restitution from assets, blacklisting as a tax evader (social credit), revocation of passports through court order, and imprisonment for 3-7 years under Criminal Law Article 201 for fraudulent treaty claim if deliberate intent is proven.
