Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A virtual office or commercial mailbox is generally not permissible for a marine bunkering company in Hong Kong. The Hong Kong Marine Department requires a physical office presence for licensing under the Shipping and Port Control Ordinance (Cap. 313) and the Merchant Shipping (Local Vessels) Ordinance (Cap. 548). For the bunkering license, the Marine Department will conduct a physical inspection of the office and operational facilities.

Competent corporate registry

Companies Registry, Hong Kong SAR, together with the Business Registration Office of the Inland Revenue Department.

Governing statute

Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622, Laws of Hong Kong), Part 3 and sections 67-74; Companies (Fees and Charges) Regulation (Cap. 622K); Business Registration Ordinance (Cap. 310). The joint venture vehicle is incorporated as a Hong Kong company limited by shares, and the Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture relationship is governed by a shareholders agreement expressed under Hong Kong law; the PRC Foreign Investment Law does not govern the Hong Kong entity itself except as a matter of parent shareholder regulation.

Formation / constitutional filings

Incorporation processing time

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None. Under the Hong Kong Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622), there is no statutory minimum issued or paid-up share capital for a company limited by shares. The Company may be incorporated with one or more issued shares of no par value; paid-up capital may be as low as HKD 1. There is no mandated blocked escrow account or government-mandated capital deposit. The shareholders agreement may nonetheless require a funded capital account for working capital, but such account is a contractual covenant and not a Companies Registry condition.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Services Department Certificate of Fire Services Installation and Equipment for any shore-side installation; Dangerous Goods Licence for storage and handling of marine fuel; Marine Department Certificate of Fitness or Certificate of Inspection for the bunker barge including structural load and stability; Occupational Safety and Health Ordinance Cap. 509 compliance for safe work systems; confined space entry permit and hot work permit for tank cleaning or maintenance; Civil Defense fire safety clearance and emergency response plan approved by the relevant authorities.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

People's Republic of China: State Taxation Administration (STA) via its nationwide Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax) online service; Hong Kong SAR: Inland Revenue Department (IRD) of the Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region via GovHK and IRD eTAX electronic portals, with the Business Registration Office as the statutory issuer of tax identity certificates.

E-government filing portal

For the Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture in the PRC, the foreign investor and Chinese partner first complete incorporation with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) or its local equivalent and obtain the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which is automatically recognised as the taxpayer identification number for Enterprise Income Tax and VAT. The JV must then complete tax registration with the competent local STA office within 30 calendar days of issuance of the business license, providing the business license, articles of association, shareholders list and legal representative identification. A non-resident foreign enterprise that carries on business in the PRC through a permanent establishment must also obtain PRC tax registration at the place where the permanent establishment is located; if it has only passive China-source income, no full tax registration is required and the payer performs withholding. For Hong Kong SAR, a foreign-incorporated entity or a PRC JV creating a Hong Kong branch must, within one month of commencing business, apply to the IRD Business Registration Office for a Business Registration Certificate under the Business Registration Ordinance (Cap. 310); the Business Registration number constitutes the Hong Kong taxpayer identification number. No separate VAT ID is issued because Hong Kong does not operate a VAT/GST system.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation (PRC resident JV taxed on worldwide income under the Enterprise Income Tax Law; Hong Kong profits tax applies territorially to profits sourced in Hong Kong and is levied on the legal entity or branch).

Available election forms

None (neither the PRC nor Hong Kong SAR provides U.S.-style check-the-box elections. VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

PRC: VAT general taxpayer registration becomes mandatory when annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000; agricultural and certain transport/services categories may use lower applicable rates and small-scale taxpayers can elect general status. Hong Kong SAR: no turnover threshold for indirect tax registration because there is no VAT/GST; every business must obtain a Business Registration Certificate under Cap. 310 within one month of commencement.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under PRC VAT, zero-rating is available for goods exported from Chinese customs territory and for certain cross-border services. For marine fuel and lubricants physically supplied to a vessel departing on an international voyage from a PRC port, zero-rating is recognised when the transaction is declared to China Customs as an export-type supply, the bunker delivery takes place in a bonded or customs-supervised zone or is otherwise treated as an export from customs territory, the seller is a VAT general taxpayer, and the related foreign currency proceeds are repatriated to the PRC in accordance with SAFE's goods-trade foreign exchange rules. Qualifying offshore digital services do not apply to the bunkering scope. In Hong Kong SAR, no VAT exists and therefore no export zero-rating claim is made; duty relief for ship stores on international voyages may be separately available under the Dutiable Commodities Ordinance (Cap. 109).

  • Electronic Export Declaration (Customs Declaration for Export Goods) accepted by China Customs and reflected in the China International Trade Single Window
  • Original commercial invoice and packing list for the marine fuel and lubricant delivery
  • Bunker Delivery Note signed by the receiving vessel's master or appointed engineer
  • Bill of Lading, sea waybill, voyage charter, or port clearance certificate evidencing departure on an international voyage from a PRC port
  • Bank foreign exchange receipt record from a licensed bank under the SAFE Goods Trade Foreign Exchange Monitoring System
  • Approved ship store declaration or Dutiable Commodities Permit when the bunker supply is delivered or withdrawn in Hong Kong waters

PRC: VAT invoices are issued through the nationwide Electronic Invoice Platform operated by the State Taxation Administration; special VAT invoices are digitally monitored under the Golden Tax Phase IV system, with real-time upload of invoice data to tax servers and anti-counterfeit digital stamping; no legacy paper fiscal stamp is used. Hong Kong SAR: no statutory real-time electronic invoicing clearance, fiscal API mandate, or anti-fraud stamping obligation; taxpayers simply maintain records and issue commercial invoices in compliant form.

China Customs and the State Taxation Administration may jointly audit the exported goods declaration, the bunker delivery documents, the vessel voyage records, and the foreign exchange repatriation data. If zero-rating is claimed without proof of actual export, without delivery to an outbound international voyage, or without timely repatriation of foreign currency, the tax authority will claw back the output VAT refund, impose late payment surcharge, and levy penalties under the Tax Collection Administration Law of the People's Republic of China; serious fraudulent export refund cases may additionally trigger criminal liability under the PRC Criminal Law. In Hong Kong, false ship store declarations can create penalties under Cap. 109, and the IRD may adjust assessments and add penalties if profit attribution or source rules are mis-stated.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Hong Kong operates as a separate customs and monetary territory within the People's Republic of China. The Hong Kong dollar is fully convertible and is issued under a Currency Board system, the Linked Exchange Rate System, which keeps the HKD money base backed by US dollars and the exchange rate between HKD and USD within the band of 7.75 to 7.85. There are no foreign exchange control restrictions on current-account or capital-account transactions. A company incorporated in Hong Kong may freely receive and retain HKD, USD, EUR, RMB (CNH) and other major currencies, and may convert funds domestically without application to the HKMA. Dividend remittances, repayment of intercompany loans, shareholder advances, royalty payments and trade settlements are not subject to convertibility approval. The practical constraints for a Sino-Foreign JV arise only where funds cross the mainland border: the mainland shareholder must complete the relevant outward direct investment (ODI) filing and SAFE foreign exchange registration, and mainland banks must observe trade and investment verification procedures. Profit remittances from mainland China require documentation of audited distributable profits and tax payment in accordance with SAFE and PRC dividend remittance rules. No surrender or mandatory conversion requirements apply in Hong Kong, making the HK treasury the natural netting and liquidity hub.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable to Hong Kong SAR. There is no statutory surrender timeline, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no obligation to convert export proceeds or bunkering income into HKD. HKD and other foreign currencies may be retained freely in ordinary operational accounts. For mainland-side flows, PRC SAFE rules require export proceeds to be repatriated into China and verified through the Trade in Goods Foreign Exchange Monitoring System; mandatory full surrender was abolished, so proceeds may remain in an approved foreign exchange settlement account, although conversion to RMB may be required for onshore expense payments.

Central bank supervision: Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA); for mainland China capital-flow frontiers, the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) and the People's Bank of China (PBOC) supervise the China side of cross-border remittances.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low viability as a primary treasury bank. In Hong Kong, the term 'fintech EMI' is not a defined banking category; an institution holding customer corporate deposits, issuing standby letters of credit, and undertaking documentary trade settlements must be an authorized institution under the Banking Ordinance. Digital banks in Hong Kong are licensed as virtual banks and are full authorized institutions, but most pure-play EMIs are either Stored Value Facility holders or Money Service Operators and are legally prohibited from taking deposits or issuing commercial documentary credits. For a medium-risk offshore marine bunkering joint venture with complex cross-border shareholders, the relationship must remain with a licensed Tier-1 bank capable of capital escrow, SWIFT-clearing, LC issuance, and sanctions-screening of vessels and counterparties. A fintech EMI may be used only as an auxiliary payment execution channel for small-value disbursements after a full bank-account structure has been established, and only if it holds a valid HKMA license for the relevant payment activity.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The mainland shareholder must first complete ODI approval with NDRC and MOFCOM and obtain the SAFE foreign exchange registration certificate. The injection should be transmitted by SWIFT MT103 or MT202 from the mainland parent's designated corporate bank account to a HKD, USD, or CNH capital escrow account at the Hong Kong JV's Tier-1 commercial bank. For RMB-denominated injections, the remittance should use the cross-border RMB payment and clearing infrastructure, either through CIPS or through the offshore CNH clearing bank in Hong Kong, with the remitting bank selecting the correct trade/investment FX purpose code for outward direct investment; current SAFE IRES codes include 621011 for outward direct investment in an overseas enterprise, but the exact code should be confirmed with the remitting bank at execution. The receiving bank should treat the funds as capital pending Hong Kong certified public accountant verification; after the share allotment, filing with the Hong Kong Companies Registry, and capital verification report are completed, the funds may be transferred to the operational treasury account. Commingling of capital injection funds with trade receivables is prohibited and would trigger re-underwriting.For marine fuel purchases from international suppliers and payments to ship owners or agents, the primary clearing rail is SWIFT MT103 for cash transfers in USD, HKD, CNH, or other major currencies, settled through CHATS in Hong Kong or through the bank's outbound correspondent network. For high-value cargo purchases where secure payment is required, the bank will issue a Commercial Letter of Credit via SWIFT MT700 under UCP600, with documents including a proforma invoice, bill of lading, surveyor report, bunker delivery note, quantity/quality certificate, and certificate of origin. For smaller or recurring payments to trusted counterparties, documentary collections under URC522 are available, using presentation through the remitting bank and payment or acceptance by the buyer's bank. The JV should also maintain a Standby Letter of Credit capability, issued via SWIFT MT760, for performance guarantees, customs bonds, or customer-credit arrangements in the bunkering business. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low viability as a primary treasury bank. In Hong Kong, the term 'fintech EMI' is not a defined banking category; an institution holding customer corporate deposits, issuing standby letters of credit, and undertaking documentary trade settlements must be an authorized institution under the Banking Ordinance. Digital banks in Hong Kong are licensed as virtual banks and are full authorized institutions, but most pure-play EMIs are either Stored Value Facility holders or Money Service Operators and are legally prohibited from taking deposits or issuing commercial documentary credits. For a medium-risk offshore marine bunkering joint venture with complex cross-border shareholders, the relationship must remain with a licensed Tier-1 bank capable of capital escrow, SWIFT-clearing, LC issuance, and sanctions-screening of vessels and counterparties. A fintech EMI may be used only as an auxiliary payment execution channel for small-value disbursements after a full bank-account structure has been established, and only if it holds a valid HKMA license for the relevant payment activity.
