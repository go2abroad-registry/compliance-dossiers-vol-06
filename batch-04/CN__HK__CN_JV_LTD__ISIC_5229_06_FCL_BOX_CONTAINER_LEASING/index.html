Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA) and its local provincial/municipal tax bureaus; online filing and registration are performed through the national unified e-Tax Bureau (electronic tax service portal).

E-government filing portal

The Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture is first incorporated with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR), which issues a Business License containing the 18-character Unified Social Credit Code. That Unified Social Credit Code is simultaneously the statutory taxpayer identification number under the STA's information-sharing mechanism, so no separate tax registration application is required. The JV must then complete the post-incorporation tax formalities in the e-Tax Bureau: file the deposit bank account report, financial accounting reporting system record, taxpayer qualification/status definitions, VAT general taxpayer application if annual taxable sales will exceed CNY 5,000,000, and application for Fully Digitalized E-Invoice issuance. A Hong Kong corporate non-resident with no permanent establishment in China is not required to obtain a PRC Unified Social Credit Code; Chinese-source income payable to it is registered and withheld by the JV under the withholding tax system, using the non-resident's Hong Kong Business Registration Number as its foreign tax identifier in the withholding return.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

CNY 5,000,000 in annual taxable sales is the mandatory threshold for general (standard-rate) VAT taxpayer registration; basic tax registration is mandatory for the incorporated JV at formation regardless of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods physically exported from mainland China to a destination outside mainland China, including Hong Kong and Macao SARs, qualify for VAT zero-rating under the export-refund (exempt-offset-refund) mechanism, provided the exporter is a VAT general taxpayer and the goods are not prohibited/restricted. For the active leasing of ISO shipping containers, zero-rating will be accepted if the containers physically leave mainland China under a customs-approved export/lease trade mode and are used outside China by a non-resident lessee; the cross-border lease service itself must fall within the STA's published Catalogue of Export Goods and Zero-Rated Cross-Border Services. If the containers remain within mainland China or are delivered for use inside the PRC, the lease is domestic supply taxable at 13% VAT.

  • Customs Export Goods Declaration issued by the Customs Administration of the People's Republic of China, with the export trade mode indicating leased/exported ISO containers
  • Original ocean Bill of Lading or multimodal transport document evidencing movement of the containers from a mainland China port to the overseas lessee
  • Fully Digitalized E-Invoice (export/lease invoice) issued through the STA platform
  • PRC designated-bank foreign exchange receipt record and Statement of International Payment for Trade in Goods, constituting the Chinese equivalent of an Inward Remittance Certificate
  • ISO container lease agreement, container serial-number/box inventory, and sublease ledger substantiating the cross-border use and the foreign lessee

The STA mandates invoice issuance through its national Fully Digitalized E-Invoice (FDEI) service platform. VAT invoices, including export invoices, must be issued electronically and transmitted in real time to the national tax data platform; the Golden Tax Phase IV system performs automatic digital data matching against customs declarations, export proceeds, and inventory. There is no physical fiscal stamp on an FDEI; anti-fraud controls are effected through dynamic digital tax-control records, mandatory red-letter invoice procedures, and automatic invalidation/cancellation of anomalous invoices.

The tax authorities, customs and SAFE jointly audit export zero-rating claims through matched digital records. If the export declaration, bill of lading, lease contract, or foreign-exchange receipt is found false, inconsistent, or non-repatriated within the prescribed window, the zero-rating is revoked and the transaction is recharacterised as a taxable domestic supply at 13% VAT. The taxpayer must repay the refunded VAT plus late-payment interest, and may be assessed an administrative penalty of up to 50%-500% of the erroneously refunded/underpaid tax under the PRC Tax Collection and Administration Law. Where false export declarations are used fraudulently to collect export tax refunds, the matter becomes criminal export-tax-refund fraud under the PRC Criminal Law, subject to fines, asset forfeiture and custodial penalties up to life imprisonment.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Hong Kong operates under a currency board regime and imposes no exchange controls on corporate treasury flows. The Hong Kong dollar is freely convertible within the statutory band of HKD 7.7500 to 7.8500 per US dollar, and current-account and capital-account transactions are permitted without general SAFE approval for an HK-incorporated entity. The JV can hold multi-currency accounts in HKD, USD, EUR, and offshore CNH, and can freely repatriate container-leasing rental income from international shipping customers. The mainland PRC leg is materially more restrictive: the CNY is a managed float with a daily trading band of plus or minus 2 percent around the PBOC fixing, and capital account movements into and out of mainland China require SAFE registration and documentation. An inbound equity injection from a PRC shareholder into the HK JV requires the PRC shareholder to complete ODI registration with SAFE before remitting funds. Profit distributions from the HK JV back to a PRC parent are also subject to investor-level tax, audited financial statements, and SAFE monitoring if converted to CNY onshore. Offshore CNH is fungible but distinct from onshore CNY, and the HK treasury pool can settle in HKD/USD without conversion hurdles.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Hong Kong has zero mandatory surrender rules; export proceeds may be retained indefinitely in any currency. For PRC-side export and container leasing receipts that must be funnelled through the mainland, SAFE applies the export proceeds collection regime: export proceeds must be repatriated to a designated domestic FX settlement account within 90 calendar days from the date of customs declaration, or up to 180 calendar days if a SAFE-approved deferred collection filing is in place. The mandatory conversion ratio is 0 percent; the exporter may retain the full amount in a foreign currency settlement account or voluntarily convert to CNY at market rates. The full verified export invoice value must be received and reconciled electronically against customs data before any onward payment is permitted.

Central bank supervision: Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA), with the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Bank of China (PBOC) as the PRC-side regulator for any cross-border RMB or foreign exchange flows into and out of mainland China.

Mandatory Audit Threshold Mandatory Audit for All Entities

Turnover: Under the Companies Ordinance (Cap.622), a Hong Kong private company must have its financial statements audited by an independent practising certified public accountant unless it qualifies as a small private company or a small private company group under sections 359C and 359D. A small private company must satisfy at least two of the following three tests for the relevant financial year and, where applicable, the immediately preceding financial year: (1) total annual revenue not exceeding HK$20,000,000 (approximately USD 2,564,103); (2) total assets at the balance sheet date not exceeding HK$20,000,000 (approximately USD 2,564,103); and (3) total number of employees not exceeding 50. An independent statutory audit is therefore mandatory where the company fails at least two of those tests, where a shareholder or shareholders holding at least 5% of the voting rights require an audit, or where the company is otherwise not eligible for the audit exemption. The audit must be performed under Hong Kong Standards on Auditing by a practising certified public accountant licensed under the Professional Accountants Ordinance (Cap.50).

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: RMB 200 million annual related-party tangible asset transfers trigger Local File preparation; RMB 100 million for intangible or financial asset transfers; RMB 40 million for other combined related-party transactions. Master File preparation is required when group related-party transaction turnover exceeded RMB 1 billion in the preceding tax year.

Economic substance scope: The PRC Enterprise Income Tax Law Article 47 and SAT Announcement No. 42/2016 apply substance-over-form and general anti-avoidance rules. For the Hong Kong shareholder, the Mainland-Hong Kong Double Taxation Arrangement requires the Hong Kong entity to perform Core Income-Generating Activities (CIGA) in Hong Kong. In the container-leasing context, CIGA include contract negotiation, asset sourcing, funding decisions, risk assumption, billing, and strategic management. If those activities are actually performed in mainland China, the Hong Kong entity will be treated as having a permanent establishment in China, the treaty benefits will be unavailable, and the profits will be taxed at 25% in China.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Shell Company / Artificial Avoidance of Permanent Establishment

Statutory sanction: The tax authority re-characterizes the structure under Article 47 of the PRC Enterprise Income Tax Law and denies treaty benefits; the Hong Kong shareholder is deemed to carry on business in China through a permanent establishment. The full 25% CIT is assessed on attributable profits, plus 0.05% daily interest and a 50%-500% penalty under Article 63 of the Tax Collection Administration Law for false declarations.

Director personal liability: Directors who orchestrated the letterbox arrangement are jointly and severally liable for the unpaid enterprise income tax and interest. Courts may pierce the corporate veil under the PRC Company Law, allowing creditors and tax authorities to attach directors' personal assets. The legal representative is also subject to a travel ban and entry into the blacklist of dishonest judgment debtors. Criminal conviction under Article 201 of the Criminal Law can result in imprisonment up to seven years where the evaded tax amount is large.
Transfer Pricing Documentation Default and Artificial Profit Shifting

Statutory sanction: Article 41 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law authorizes the tax bureau to make a transfer pricing adjustment and impose tax on the revised profit. Failure to file contemporaneous documentation attracts a fine up to RMB 10,000 under implementing rules. If the understated profit is concealed on the annual return, Article 63 of the Tax Collection Administration Law imposes a penalty of 50%-500% of the evaded tax, plus daily late-payment interest of 0.05%.

Director personal liability: The finance director and general manager are personally liable for the tax deficiency where the corporate veil has been abused; personal assets can be attached if the company is insolvent. Directors are placed on the joint punishment list for dishonest taxpayers, barred from leaving the country, and subject to criminal tax evasion prosecution with imprisonment up to seven years for serious cases.
Fraudulent Dividend Repatriation and Abusive DTA Relief Claim

Statutory sanction: The DTA benefit is denied under the beneficial ownership rules; the 5% vs 10% withholding difference is assessed as under-withheld tax. Article 69 of the Tax Collection Administration Law penalizes the withholding agent at 50%-300% of the amount that should have been withheld; the non-resident recipient additionally faces a 50%-500% evasion penalty under Article 63. Article 201 of the Criminal Law applies to the fraudulent scheme, with criminal fines and imprisonment for the responsible individuals.

Director personal liability: Directors of the joint venture, acting as withholding responsible persons, are personally and jointly liable for the under-withheld tax and penalties. Courts may pierce the corporate veil ordering civil asset restitution from directors who personally profited from the sham arrangement. The directors are blacklisted, travel-banned, disqualified from management positions for up to five years upon criminal conviction, and may be sentenced to imprisonment up to seven years for aggravated tax evasion.
