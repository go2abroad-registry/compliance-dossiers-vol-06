Stage 03
Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

      
Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

    

    

      
Tax ID Application Infrastructure

      

        
Competent tax authority
State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA) and, for Hong Kong SAR, the Inland Revenue Department (IRD); online portals are the Electronic Tax Bureau of the STA and the IRD eTAX portal

        
E-government filing portal
For the PRC Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture, the company must first complete business registration with the local Administration for Market Regulation and receive a Business License bearing the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC). The USCC is the statutory Tax Identification Number for all PRC tax filings. The entity then completes tax registration profile setup with the STA through the Electronic Tax Bureau, confirming VAT general taxpayer or small-scale taxpayer status and the Corporate Income Tax filing period. A foreign non-resident enterprise without a PRC establishment that derives China-source income must follow the non-resident enterprise tax registration procedure, or must be registered by a withholding agent, using a tax residency certificate, constitutional or incorporation documents, and a written description of the China-source activities. For a Hong Kong SAR entity, the Inland Revenue Ordinance requires registration with the Business Registration Office within one month of commencing business; the Business Registration Certificate Number serves as the primary tax file number. A foreign company registered under Part 16 of the Hong Kong Companies Ordinance obtains the same Business Registration Number, and no separate VAT registration is required because Hong Kong SAR does not impose VAT or GST.

      

      
Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation; the CN_JV_LTD is a PRC resident enterprise subject to Corporate Income Tax at the entity level on worldwide income and is not eligible for pass-through treatment. If the same structure operates through a Hong Kong SAR company, Hong Kong applies Territorial_Scope taxation to profits sourced in or derived from Hong Kong only. — For the PRC Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture, the company must first complete business registration with the local Administration for Market Regulation and receive a Business License bearing the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC). Default Tax Classification & Elections

      

        
Default tax classification
Entity_Level_Taxation; the CN_JV_LTD is a PRC resident enterprise subject to Corporate Income Tax at the entity level on worldwide income and is not eligible for pass-through treatment. If the same structure operates through a Hong Kong SAR company, Hong Kong applies Territorial_Scope taxation to profits sourced in or derived from Hong Kong only.

        
Available election forms
None. The PRC does not offer check-the-box elections or hybrid mismatch entity classification elections; the Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture is mandatorily classified as a resident corporation. Hong Kong SAR has no check-the-box elections and no hybrid mismatch elections; corporate profits tax applies on a territorial source basis.

      

    

    

      
VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

      

        
Standard VAT / GST rate
6%

        
Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)
Annual taxable sales exceeding RMB 5,000,000 in the PRC creates a mandatory VAT general taxpayer registration obligation; below the threshold, an eligible small-scale taxpayer may remain small-scale or voluntarily register as a general taxpayer. Non-resident suppliers of logistics, freight, or other taxable services into China have no RMB turnover threshold and must either register for VAT or appoint a PRC withholding agent for the first taxable supply.

      

      
      

        Export 0% zero-rating conditions
        
VAT zero-rating is available in the PRC for goods physically exported from the PRC customs territory and for qualifying cross-border services. For GDP-certified clinical logistics and pharma freight, zero-rating applies where the logistics service is supplied to a non-resident recipient, the service is performed in connection with a genuine export movement of goods, the freight forwarding or international transportation terminates outside PRC customs territory, and the service consideration is payable by a non-resident party in foreign exchange. The claimant must be a VAT general taxpayer, hold valid export customs declarations, repatriate export proceeds within the SAFE monitoring period, and file the export VAT refund application with the STA. Domestic cold-chain storage, warehousing, handling, or ancillary value-added services consumed within China are taxable at 6% and cannot be zero-rated.

The STA mandates issuance of Fully Digitalized Electronic Invoices through the Electronic Tax Bureau under the Golden Tax Phase IV system. VAT special invoices are fiscally cleared in real time by the STA platform, which validates the Unified Social Credit Code, invoice serialization, applicable VAT rate, and tax amount; an anti-fraud unique digital signature and verification code are applied automatically. Businesses must integrate their billing systems with the STA electronic invoicing application programming interface; physical fiscal stamping has been replaced by real-time digital clearance, and private or unauthorised fiscal stamps have no legal effect.

      
If the STA, customs, or SAFE audit determines that goods did not actually leave China, that export proceeds were not repatriated within the statutory period without a validly recorded extension, or that zero-rating documentation is false or unverifiable, the zero rate is retrospectively denied. The exporter must account for VAT at the domestic rate on the affected supplies, the VAT refund is clawed back, and a daily late-payment surcharge applies under the Tax Collection Administration Law. A fraudulent export VAT refund claim carries an administrative penalty of one to five times the refunded amount and, where a crime is established, criminal liability under the PRC Criminal Law. Customs may also impose penalties for export declaration discrepancies, and SAFE may fine the entity for failure to repatriate export proceeds under the foreign exchange administration regime.

Stage 04
Banking & Treasury Architecture

      
Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

    

    

      
Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

      

        
Current-account convertibility
Mainland China operates a managed current-account convertibility system under the Foreign Exchange Administration Regulations, while the capital account remains materially controlled. RMB is fully convertible for genuine trade and current-account items including freight, logistics, and GDP pharma distribution service payments, subject to bank verification of authentic supporting documents. Capital-account transactions such as foreign direct investment equity contributions, shareholder loans, cross-border borrowing repayments, and profit remittances require SAFE foreign investment registration, bank-verified quotas, and purpose-code compliance before funds may be converted. Profit remittance by a Sino-foreign equity joint venture is lawful after annual audited financial statements are filed, corporate income tax is settled, and the dividend board resolution is presented; banks then execute settlement using the SAFE-registered current-account framework, but the bank retains the duty to verify authenticity and reject round-tripping or disguised capital outflows. Hong Kong SAR has no exchange controls; the HKMA maintains the HKD link through a managed convertibility corridor and permits free capital movement. Treasury planning must therefore separate PRC onshore capital-account flows, where convertibility is conditional on registration and documentary authenticity, from HK offshore flows, where convertibility is unconditional, to avoid illegal redirection of onshore RMB through the CNH market.

        
Export proceeds surrender rules
For PRC mainland exporters, the statutory repatriation timeline is 180 days from the date of export customs declaration; the mandatory conversion ratio is 0% because full retention is permitted, and the exporter may voluntarily sell the foreign currency to a licensed domestic bank for RMB. Hong Kong SAR imposes no surrender requirement, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no statutory repatriation deadline for export proceeds.

      

      
Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China together with the People's Bank of China (PBOC) as the central banking authority for mainland China; for Hong Kong SAR, the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) is the effective central banking and currency management authority.

    

    

      
Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

      

        
        

          

            Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails
            Fit: High
          

          
Offshore digital EMI challenger banks are not viable for the onshore PRC leg. SAFE and PBOC rules require all RMB current-account and capital-account settlement to be executed by licensed domestic banks or qualified settlement banks. Offshore fintech EMIs are legally barred from holding SAFE-registered capital-account flows, issuing FX purpose-code compliant wires, or operating RMB current accounts for an onshore Sino-foreign equity joint venture. In Hong Kong, licensed virtual banks and stored-value facilities have limited viability for petty operating float, but they should not be used as primary clearing rails for registered capital or high-value GDP pharma trade settlements because their clearing membership, SWIFT direct connectivity, and trade-document handling parity remain inferior to Tier-1 licensed banks. The recommended legal structure is therefore Tier-1 bank-only clearing for treasury-critical flows; any fintech EMI wallet must be ring-fenced from capital-account funds and used solely for small incidental expenses.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

      
      

        UBO chain opacity caused by mixed PRC-HK ownership, nominee shareholders, and layered holding or trust structures concealing the final natural persons above the joint venture.
        
Root cause: UBO chain opacity caused by mixed PRC-HK ownership, nominee shareholders, and layered holding or trust structures concealing the final natural persons above the joint venture.

        
Defense evidence: Provide certified certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles of association, full shareholder registry, JV contract, board resolution approving the UBO declaration, signed UBO questionnaire, source-of-wealth statement for each 25% or more natural person, tax residence certificate, notarized passport copies, an organizational chart tracing every intermediate entity to the final individual owners, and audited financial statements of all intermediate HK holding entities.

      

      
      

        Sanctions and proliferation finance exposure from GDP pharma freight movement to jurisdictions or parties on OFAC, EU, UN, or HKMA sanctions lists.
        
Root cause: Sanctions and proliferation finance exposure from GDP pharma freight movement to jurisdictions or parties on OFAC, EU, UN, or HKMA sanctions lists.

        
Defense evidence: Submit real-time global sanctions and PEP screening output with matching percentage, transaction-level legal entity identifiers, export and import licenses, certificate of origin, route and conveyance schedule, freight forwarder license, end-user declaration, beneficial cargo owner declaration, GDP compliance certification, and a written sanctions compliance policy confirming zero transactions with blocked parties.

      

      
      

        Trade-based money laundering red flags such as invoice value mismatch, phantom freight, double invoicing, round-tripping of funds, or settlement to an unrelated third party without a genuine logistics or pharma service nexus.
        
Root cause: Trade-based money laundering red flags such as invoice value mismatch, phantom freight, double invoicing, round-tripping of funds, or settlement to an unrelated third party without a genuine logistics or pharma service nexus.

        
Defense evidence: Provide commercial invoices, purchase orders, bills of lading or air waybills, packing lists, customs clearance records, signed freight contracts with service descriptions, remaining bank statements of the beneficiary, evidence of import duty and VAT payment, GDP temperature-chain monitoring records, and a board-approved schedule of expected counterparties and their business purposes.

      

      
      

        Capital injection fund source mismatch or overstatement of in-kind contributions to the EJV causing non-completion of capital verification or unlawful conversion of registered capital.
        
Root cause: Capital injection fund source mismatch or overstatement of in-kind contributions to the EJV causing non-completion of capital verification or unlawful conversion of registered capital.

        
Defense evidence: Deliver the SAFE FDI foreign investment registration or Foreign Investment Certificate, board-approved capital call notice, SWIFT remittance records from the actual HK investing entity, source-of-funds bank statements, audited third-party valuation report for any non-cash assets, capital verification report issued by a PRC-certified public accountant, stamped JV contract, and the State Administration for Market Regulation filing receipt.

      

      
    

    

      
Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

      

        Capital contributions from the Hong Kong investor to the PRC joint venture must be routed only through a domestic licensed bank holding the SAFE foreign investment registration. The remitter must issue an SWIFT MT103 or equivalent domestic cross-border instruction, with the beneficiary being the onshore JV capital account. The receiving bank will verify the SAFE FDI registration and the BOP purpose code before crediting; foreign currency must first be deposited into the capital-account foreign exchange settlement account, and conversion to RMB is permitted only against verified current-account use documents. Capital verification by a PRC-certified public accountant is required after receipt, and the verification report must be filed with the relevant market regulator. Any injection exceeding the registered quota should be rejected or separately documented as a shareholder loan under the appropriate SAFE cross-border financing and reprocessing regime.Outbound trade and logistics disbursements should use SWIFT MT103 for high-value freight invoices and vendor payments, commercial letters of credit for high-value pharma imports or exports requiring bank examination of documents, and documentary collections for lower-trust settled consignments. RMB trade settlement may be executed through CIPS by the domestic Tier-1 bank, while HKD/USD settlement should be cleared through the Hong Kong Tier-1 bank network. Before debiting, the bank must verify the underlying logistics contract, customs or waybill data, and the consistency of beneficiary identity and value. Prohibited structures include cash-on-delivery flows to offshore shell freight providers, split payments below monitoring thresholds, and transit to unrelated third countries not supported by bill-of-lading or airway-bill evidence.Onshore CNY deliverable FX forwards executed with a licensed PRC bank only when genuine current-account or capital-account exposure is evidenced; tenors generally capped at 12 months and rollovers require updated bank-verified business background.CNH/HKD deliverable FX forwards and FX swaps via HKMA-licensed Tier-1 banks for hedging export proceeds, trade payable, and HK treasury balances; no speculative open position is permitted under the company treasury policy.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are permitted only offshore in the CNH/HKD or emerging-market context and may not be booked onshore against RMB because SAFE requires deliverable settlement onshore.Cross-currency interest rate swaps are permissible to convert HKD or USD funding liabilities into CNY or vice versa solely when the underlying facility is a qualified cross-border loan; swap-linked equity distribution or dividend arbitrage is barred absent explicit board and Asset and Liability Committee approval.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

      
Master / Local File threshold: HK$10,000,000 aggregate controlled transaction value in a fiscal year for taxpayers with turnover exceeding HK$200,000,000; a Group Master File and HK Local File are required for such entities; Country-by-Country Reporting threshold is consolidated group revenue of HK$6,800,000,000.

      
Economic substance scope: Hong Kong relies on territorial source charging rather than a nominal ESR, but the substance principle applies: any claim that freighting profit is offshore is rejected unless the core income-generating activities (CIGA) - contractual negotiation, dispatch control, GDP cold-chain custody, invoicing, quality release, and revenue risk adoption - are performed in Hong Kong by employees and physical assets. The JV must maintain a GDP temperature-mapped warehouse, licensed GDP person-in-charge, and auditable freight documentation; pure booking of China-originated pharma shipments without Hong Kong activity makes the entire profit assessable in Hong Kong and a potentially fatal shell-company finding.

      

    

    

      
Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

      
      

        Tax Evasion - False Offshore Income Claim
        
Statutory sanction: Inland Revenue Ordinance (IRO) s.82A; maximum fine of HK$50,000 plus additional tax of three times the tax undercharged, with imprisonment up to 3 years; the IRD may also increase civil assessments and deny the offshore exemption on a reconstruction basis.

        
Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced where the director caused or permitted the false declaration; the director becomes jointly and severally liable for the HK$50,000 fine, the additional tax, penal interest, and legal costs; a tax warrant may be issued resulting in a travel ban (blacklisting) and asset freezing; upon criminal conviction imprisonment is possible.

      

      
      

        Fatal Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance
        
Statutory sanction: Inland Revenue Ordinance s.61 and s.14 read with the Companies Ordinance Cap.622; the IRD may treat the JV as a sham, assess the entire local/foreign profit at 16.5%, impose an additional assessment of up to triple the tax, and the Companies Registry may deregister the shell for failure to prove a registered position and business ; upon liquidation, fraudulent preference rules deny any limited-liability shield.

        
Director personal liability: Directors are exposed to disqualification orders, personal contribution orders for insolvency assets, director liability for reckless trading, and a travel ban; where shell status is tied to understating Hong Kong-source profits, the same individual may be jailed under IRO s.82A and the IRD may apply for a restraint order under organized crime laws if related pharma dosage fraud is uncovered.

      

      
      

        AML / SARS Capital Evasion through Pharmacy Freight Payment Rails
        
Statutory sanction: Organized and Serious Crimes Ordinance (Cap. 455) s.25 imposes on conviction on indictment a fine of HK$5,000,000 and imprisonment for 14 years; the company may additionally be fined, and the proceeds of the freight business become subject to confiscation under s.10(1) of the same Ordinance.

        
Director personal liability: A director who signs payment instructions or who knows or has reason to believe the transaction is tainted is personally liable to conviction under Cap.455 s.25, with mandatory disqualification from managing any company for up to 15 years; the court may grant restitution orders against the director's personal assets, impose a travel ban, and on guilty plea or conviction impose direct imprisonment.
