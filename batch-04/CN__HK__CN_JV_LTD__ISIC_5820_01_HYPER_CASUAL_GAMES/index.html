Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA). Online electronic portal: China Electronic Tax Administration (e-Tax), operated by the STA and the provincial-level state tax service bureaus under the unified national Tax Administration portal.

E-government filing portal

The Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture is incorporated with the Administration for Market Regulation (AMR), which issues a business license containing the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC). The USCC is the statutory Tax Identification Number for Enterprise Income Tax, VAT, and all national tax filings. The AMR automatically transmits incorporation data to the competent local STA office. Upon receipt of the business license, the JV must complete post-registration tax administration items through the e-Tax portal, including confirmation of taxpayer status, legal entity information, financial accounting basis, bank account reporting, and invoice-type registration. Foreign non-resident shareholders that merely hold equity do not require a separate PRC TIN; China-source payments to such shareholders are subject to withholding by the JV as withholding agent. If the foreign non-resident entity creates a permanent establishment in China, it must register through the e-Tax portal and obtain a non-resident enterprise tax file.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No economic turnover threshold applies to registration for incorporated entities; every JV with a valid business license must complete tax registration. The RMB 5,000,000 annual taxable sales threshold is the statutory boundary between Small-Scale VAT Taxpayer and General VAT Taxpayer classification. Digital game publishing, advertising revenue, and cross-border digital services are treated as modern services at the statutory VAT rate of 6% in the general regime, while physical software carriers are taxable as goods at the standard 13% rate.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A resident Chinese supplier may claim VAT zero-rating or cross-border VAT exemption for qualifying cross-border taxable services under Cai Shui [2016] No. 36 and related STA/MOF export VAT regulations, provided that: (1) the recipient is an overseas entity or individual; (2) the service is actually supplied for consumption outside the People's Republic of China; (3) the consideration is paid from outside China in a convertible foreign currency; and (4) the JV maintains the statutory contract, invoice, and repatriation evidence. For hyper-casual mobile game publishing, qualifying services include game software services, digital game publishing services, and advertising-inventory services supplied to overseas ad platforms such as AdMob and AppLovin, where the games and advertisements are consumed by users outside mainland China. If the game software is physically exported on media, the export goods VAT zero-rate applies with a customs export declaration. For pure digital delivery through app stores and ad networks, no customs declaration is made; the substantiation is the cross-border service contract, FDEI invoice, and foreign-exchange receipt records evidencing payment from the offshore counterparty.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bill of Entry) as the Export Goods Customs Declaration under PRC Customs, applicable where software is shipped on physical media
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill for physical-export scenarios
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or the SAFE foreign-exchange receipt and payment declaration as bank evidence of repatriated export proceeds

The STA requires newly formed enterprises to use the Fully Digitalized Electronic Invoice (FDEI) platform. All statutory invoice data, including invoice XML, must be transmitted in real time to the STA through the national e-Invoice Service Platform. The Golden Tax Phase IV integrated tax administration system performs automated validation, anomaly detection, risk scoring, and cross-verification of invoice flows. No per-invoice administrative pre-clearance stamp is required; the anti-fraud mechanism is the real-time digital transmission of invoice data, the unique full-digital invoice code, and the continuous tax-control interface embedded in the FDEI platform. Legacy invoices not yet migrated to FDEI remain controlled through the VAT Invoice Integrated Management System with certified tax-control devices.

Under Articles 63 and 66 of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Tax Administration and Collection, any false claim of export VAT zero-rating or exemption results in recovery of the tax benefit, late-payment interest, and an administrative penalty of 50% to 500% of the tax amount. If the false declaration constitutes the crime of fraudulent export tax refund under Article 204 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, responsible persons are subject to criminal fines and imprisonment of up to five years, or up to ten years for especially large amounts. The STA may also revoke the entity's export tax refund/exemption qualification, downgrade its tax credit rating, apply enhanced Golden Tax Phase IV monitoring, and impose retrospective clawback for all unverified export benefits where the 180-day foreign-exchange repatriation and verification deadline is not satisfied.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Mainland China operates a managed current-account convertibility regime under the Foreign Exchange Administration Regulations of 2008 and SAFE's current-account rules. Cross-border payments for services such as advertising, software licensing, app-store distribution, and royalties are generally permitted if supported by authentic commercial contracts, invoices, tax certificates, and bank true-transaction verification. Capital-account transactions, including the Hong Kong investor's equity capital injection into the Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture, remain restricted and require SAFE's foreign direct investment registration, a dedicated capital account, a bank-filed capital account payment confirmation, and later a CPA capital verification report. The JV may keep export and service proceeds in a foreign-currency settlement account or convert them voluntarily to renminbi; there is no forced percentage mandatory conversion today. Profit remittance, although not requiring a separate SAFE approval after completion of the annual audit, is operationally gated by full tax payment, dividend resolution, audited financial statements, and application through the designated bank under the profit-remittance rule.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export and service foreign-currency receipts must be repatriated into China through a licensed bank and reported under SAFE's Trade in Services and Goods foreign-exchange monitoring framework. The statutory monitoring window for repatriation is generally no later than 180 days from the verified invoice or customs declaration date, although SAFE applies dynamic non-performing and excessive reconciliation thresholds. Mandatory conversion ratio: 0% - the JV may retain 100% in an export foreign-currency settlement account or convert to renminbi voluntarily; no fixed surrender ratio is imposed for ordinary current-account collections. Enterprises with classification A or B/C face different documentary and expedited-control procedures under the goods-trade and service-trade systems.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) and State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE); Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) supervises the Hong Kong offshore clearing node

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Offshore digital EMI/neobank viability is Low for the mainland JV's onshore CNY treasury and capital account because PBOC/SAFE require the entity to maintain its principal settlement, tax payment, and capital accounts with a licensed commercial bank in China. Offshore EMIs cannot legally act as deposit-taking institutions or execute the mandatory SAFE capital verification pathway. For the Hong Kong treasury node only, licensed HKMA Major Payment Institutions or Stored Value Facility issuers can be used as auxiliary payout rails for AdMob and AppLovin ad-network settlements; they cannot replace a Tier-1 bank account or provide CNY account services for the mainland legal entity. Any payments from Hong Kong to China should flow through SWIFT or CIPS to a licensed onshore bank rather than through an unlicensed offshore EMI.
