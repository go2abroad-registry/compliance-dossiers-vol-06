Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China, acting through the China Electronic Tax Administration online portal; for any Hong Kong SAR taxable nexus, the Inland Revenue Department (IRD) of Hong Kong SAR via the GovHK eTAX portal.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) and obtain the business license; the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) printed on the license is the statutory Tax Identification Number (TIN) for PRC tax purposes. Step 2: Complete tax registration and cross-border/zero-rating filing profile through the STA electronic tax administration portal immediately after incorporation, linking the USCC to Value-Added Tax, Enterprise Income Tax, and individual income tax withholding registrations; China maintains a unified taxpayer identifier system and does not issue a separate VAT ID. Step 3: For foreign non-resident shareholders or non-resident marketplace operators that derive PRC-sourced income without a mainland China establishment, register through the STA non-resident withholding tax registration procedure to obtain the applicable non-resident taxpayer identifier; no separate VAT registration is required where all PRC VAT obligations are discharged by the resident JV. Step 4: If the JV carries on business in Hong Kong SAR or has a Hong Kong place of business, apply to the IRD under the Business Registration Ordinance for a Business Registration Certificate and obtain the Hong Kong Business Registration Number/Taxpayer Identification Number through the eTAX portal; if the global game publishing activity creates no Hong Kong-source profits and no Hong Kong place of business, no mandatory IRD registration arises solely for that activity.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 in annual taxable sales; where annual taxable sales exceed this threshold, mandatory registration as a general VAT taxpayer applies. Below this threshold small-scale simplified VAT treatment may apply, but the JV must nonetheless complete VAT registration from business commencement.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero rating applies to exported goods where the export customs declaration is made and the goods physically leave mainland China. For cross-border digital game services and qualifying software-related services, zero rating is available when the service is supplied to an overseas customer, consumed outside mainland China, and the consideration is received from outside the PRC, supported by app-store payout reports, merchant agreements, and service delivery logs demonstrating the foreign recipient; sales to mainland China end users remain subject to VAT at the applicable domestic rate.

  • Customs Export Declaration (export customs declaration form with customs-assigned export declaration number)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export movement for goods; for pure digital services, the platform payout settlement report and service delivery log
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List stating the zero-rated VAT treatment
  • Bank foreign exchange receipt confirmation / Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or equivalent foreign exchange collection verification document
  • Signed export contract or Apple/Google merchant agreement evidencing the overseas counterparty

China mandates the issuance of Fully Digitalized Electronic VAT Invoices through the STA e-Tax platform, with real-time reporting, uniform invoice codes, digital signatures, tamper-proof anti-fraud controls, and high-volume API integration where applicable; every zero-rated export transaction must be cross-matched through Golden Tax IV supervision against the customs export declaration or digital-service export evidence, the electronic invoice, and bank settlement records.

The STA may disallow zero rating upon audit where the export, digital-service consumption, or foreign-exchange repatriation documentation is incomplete or contradictory; the taxpayer must repay any incorrectly refunded input VAT or denied zero-rated benefit together with late payment interest, and under the Tax Collection Administration Law penalties range from 50 percent to five times the tax evaded or one to five times the amount fraudulently obtained as an export VAT refund, with criminal liability for fraudulent export refund schemes; general recovery limitation periods are three years, extended to five years in special circumstances, with no limitation period for fraudulent evasion.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The RMB is fully convertible for current account transactions, including trade in goods and services, dividends, and interest related to foreign invested enterprises, subject to genuine transaction verification and SAFE reporting. Capital account transactions remain heavily regulated; inbound equity injections, foreign debt, and outward direct investments require SAFE registration, bank-level verification, and post-event supervision. For a Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture operating in mainland China, registered capital must be paid in through a designated foreign exchange capital account, and each inflow must be matched against the approved FDI information registration. Profit remittance to the Hong Kong or foreign shareholder is permitted only after annual statutory audit, final tax settlement, and board approval, and the dividend must be sourced from after-tax retained earnings. The joint venture must also comply with the requirement to settle foreign currency receipts into RMB via the banks where applicable, and any cross-border RMB payments must be reported under the RMB and foreign currency current account systems. Although the government has progressively liberalised many cross-border channels such as the Shanghai-Hong Kong Stock Connect and Bond Connect, unrestricted capital account convertibility is not available. Any attempt to bypass SAFE registration, commingle capital with operating accounts, or use offshore digital banking without onshore regulatory cover would constitute a serious compliance violation and jeopardise the entity's banking licence and ongoing treasury operations.

Export proceeds surrender rules

SAFE requires all foreign exchange received from current account transactions to be repatriated through licensed banks in China within the prescribed reporting timelines; NFRA-regulated banks must validate the economic substance of each transaction, and funds retained abroad without SAFE authorisation are treated as illegal parking. The joint venture should adopt a daily treasury reconciliation process to ensure that Apple App Store and Google Play proceeds are settled through the respective global payment rails, credited to the designated current account, and converted to RMB only after documentary verification.

Central bank supervision: The People's Bank of China (PBOC) acting through the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) and its local branches

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Offshore digital banks, electronic money institutions, and non-bank fintech EMIs have negligible legal viability as the primary treasury rail for a mainland China-incorporated Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture. PBOC and SAFE regulations require all cross-border receipts and payments for domestic legal entities to be channelled through licensed domestic banks and reported through the international payments and receipts system. Offshore EMIs are not licensed bank counterparties for domestic RMB conversion, cannot complete SAFE FDI registration, cannot act as designated custodian of capital escrow funds, and cannot issue RMB settlement accounts with a valid basic deposit account certificate. They may only be used in limited non-core pockets such as receiving overseas marketing spend or contractor payments, and even then only as a pass-through vehicle with strict reporting; in practice, the compliance risk and regulatory opacity make such use inadvisable. The CFO should therefore anchor all treasury operations on tier-1 domestic commercial banks and use Hong Kong banking relationships only for offshore liquidity buffers and trade financing outside the regulated onshore framework.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The JV must first complete foreign investment registration with the local Administration for Market Regulation and obtain the Foreign Investment Information Report record. The shareholders' capital contribution is then injected by wire transfer through a global bank, using SWIFT MT103 messages to the tier-1 domestic bank's designated branch. The remittance instruction must clearly state the purpose code 'FDI capital contribution', include the investor name, recipient JV name, and the SAFE foreign investment registration number. The domestic bank will credit the foreign exchange capital settlement account and, after verifying the funds against the SAFE FDI information form, will allow conversion into RMB for use in accordance with the JV's business scope. A qualified certified public accountant must issue a capital verification report after the contribution, confirming both the amount and the valuation of any in-kind contributions. The treasury team should ensure the offshore remitting bank uses the correct beneficial owner and charge code to avoid in-transit deduction issues, and must not allow the capital funds to enter the general operational account before the account activation and SAFE filing are complete.For operating expenditures inside China, payments are made in RMB through the basic deposit account using the domestic clearing system CNAPS. For cross-border trade and service payments, the primary rail is SWIFT MT103 via the tier-1 bank's international settlement platform, with the JV submitting contract, invoice, and tax declaration for each outward remittance. For large or recurring purchases of overseas software licences, cloud infrastructure, or content production, the bank may require either a commercial letter of credit (LC) or documentary collections, particularly when the counterparty is not a trusted long-term supplier. Digital app store payouts from Apple and Google should flow directly to the JV's foreign currency current account, after which the treasury team reconciles the net proceeds against platform settlement reports, prepares the required current account purpose code declaration, and converts to RMB only when needed for operational liquidity. For high-value trade settlements above the bank's discretion threshold, an accompanying set of original bills of lading, delivery certificates, and customs declarations must be presented for documentary collection.Onshore RMB deliverable forward contracts with domestic banks, requiring underlying cross-border receipts or payments and a genuine business need, executed under the NAFMII master agreement where applicable.Onshore FX swaps and currency swaps between RMB and major currencies, permitted for the tenor matching of anticipated capital injections, dividend remittances, and operating cash flows, but strictly requiring SAFE trade-linked documentation.Offshore non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are generally not permissible for a mainland China entity to execute directly for hedging onshore RMB exposure; such transactions are reserved for offshore counterparties and would constitute unapproved cross-border derivative activity, so the treasury should use deliverable onshore forwards instead.Option-type hedging, such as call/put options on major currencies, may be available from tier-1 domestic banks under the 'real demand' principle, but must be approved by the bank's risk committee and cannot be used for speculative positions.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: HKD 30,000,000 in aggregate cross-border related-party transactions triggering mandatory Local File preparation; CbC Report threshold applies where group consolidated turnover exceeds HKD 6,800,000,000.

Economic substance scope: Hong Kong has not adopted Caribbean-style Economic Substance Regulations, but the Foreign-Sourced Income Exemption (FSIE) regime requires economic substance in Hong Kong for passive income. The Hong Kong JV must demonstrate Core Income-Generating Activities (CIGA) in Hong Kong: hiring staff with suitable expertise, maintaining physical premises, holding board meetings, storing intellectual property contracts, negotiating and executing significant contracts, and managing bank treasury functions. For the hardcore MMORPG publishing business, the CIGA must include strategic commercial decisions, risk management, server deployment oversight and revenue collection systems maintained in Hong Kong. If the China parent exercises all major operational control and the Hong Kong entity is a letterbox or service shell, the IRD will deny FSIE, recharacterise the Hong Kong profits as China-source, and apply penalties for tax advantage.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Underreporting of MMORPG Revenue

Statutory sanction: Inland Revenue Ordinance (Chapter 112), Sections 80, 82 and 82A. A conviction fraud carries a fine of up to HKD 50,000, additional tax of up to 200% of tax undercharged, and imprisonment up to 3 years. The IRD Tax Investigation Unit may also impose assessed estimated profits and instigate criminal prosecution of the responsible officers.

Director personal liability: Piercing the corporate veil is available where a personally controlling director signs false profits tax returns, directs diversion of revenue, or uses the Hong Kong JV to evade Hong Kong tax. Directors face civil asset restitution, an export and travel ban or blacklisting issued by the Hong Kong Court, disqualification from acting as directors under the Companies Ordinance, personal joint and several liability for the JV's unpaid taxes and penalties, and imprisonment up to 5 years for fraud-related conspiracy.
Serious Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Under the Inland Revenue Ordinance Section 50AB and the IRD's Departmental Interpretation and Practice Note No. 59, the Commissioner may make transfer pricing adjustments, deny deductions, and impose a penalty of up to 100% of the additional tax assessed when avoidance is the main purpose. For failure to keep contemporaneous TP documentation, a separate default penalty of up to HKD 20,000 and additional assessments may apply. The IRD may also compound fraud or tax evasion and require full payment within 14 days; for deliberate concealment, criminal proceedings may follow.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly implement a sham TP structure or sign false tax computations become personally exposed to: restitution orders and civil assessment for the tax differential, judicial travel restrictions and blacklisting, disqualification from company management up to 3 years, and criminal liability under the Theft Ordinance (Cap 210) for misleading commercial contracts and fraudulent avoidance of tax liability.
Abusive Foreign-Sourced Income Exemption and Shell Company Arrangement

Statutory sanction: The Hong Kong FSIE rules effective from 1 January 2023 deny exemption where economic substance requirements are not met; assessable profits are taxed at the 16.5% headline rate. The IRD may also impose a penalty of up to 200% of the tax undercharged under Section 82A; the Hong Kong Companies Registry may strike off the shell company under the Companies Ordinance; and the Global Joint Tax Authority can apply coordinated penalties in accordance with CbC reporting and exchange of information.

Director personal liability: Directors of the shell JV may be ordered to restore ill-gotten tax benefits, be personally liable for the company's Hong Kong tax debts if they knowingly traded at risk, face a Hong Kong High Court travel ban for failure to comply, be disqualified as directors under Section 168E of the Companies Ordinance, and be subject to criminal imprisonment up to 12 months for fraudulent misuse of the FSIE regime.
