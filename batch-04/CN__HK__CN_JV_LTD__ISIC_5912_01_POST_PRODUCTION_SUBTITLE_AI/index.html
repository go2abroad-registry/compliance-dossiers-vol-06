Tax ID Application Infrastructure

      

        
Competent tax authority
State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; online via the nationwide Electronic Tax Bureau portal (www.chinatax.gov.cn and provincial e-taxation service platforms).

        
E-government filing portal
The Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture is incorporated in mainland China and obtains a Uniform Social Credit Code (USCC) from the municipal Administration for Market Regulation at registration. This USCC is automatically recognized as the TIN and VAT registration number under the STA One-Code-One-Certificate system. After incorporation, the entity must complete supplementary tax registration (accounting standards, bank account details) through the Electronic Tax Bureau within 30 days. For foreign non-resident entities that do not establish a legal entity but have China-sourced taxable transactions, the local tax authority will issue a Temporary Taxpayer Identification Number upon application via Electronic Tax Bureau, based on the foreign enterprise registration certificate, a signed local agent appointment, and the tax registration form.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

      

        
Default tax classification
Entity_Level_Taxation - the joint venture is a Chinese resident enterprise subject to Enterprise Income Tax on worldwide income at 25%, with no pass-through; statutory representative office and branch rules apply separately.

        
Available election forms
None - mainland China does not provide a check-the-box election or hybrid mismatch election. However, a VAT small-scale taxpayer may elect general taxpayer status where annual turnover is below the mandatory threshold; intra-group asset restructuring may follow special tax treatments upon application. No other elective tax classification is available.

Standard VAT / GST rate
6%

        
Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)
RMB 5,000,000 annual taxable turnover in any rolling 12-month period (mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration); below this threshold the entity may remain a small-scale taxpayer unless it opts to register as a general taxpayer.

      

      
      

        Export 0% zero-rating conditions
        
Under Caishui [2016] No. 36 and related STA circulars, zero-rate VAT applies to cross-border sales of qualifying services consumed entirely outside China, including broadcast, film and television production services, software services, and design/creative services. ISIC 5912 AI subtitle transcription and voice cloning studio services qualify as broadcasting/film post-production or software/design services when supplied to a non-resident enterprise and the deliverable is not used or consumed within mainland China. The supplier must retain evidence proving the recipient is foreign, the service is consumed abroad, and export receipts are received in foreign currency.

China mandates electronic invoicing using the All-In-One Digital Invoice (fully digitalized e-Fapiao) system under the Golden Tax IV infrastructure. Taxpayers must integrate their ERP or bookkeeping software with the national e-taxation platform via approved APIs. Every invoice is digitally signed, transmitted to the STA in real-time, and cleared before the buyer can legally deduct input VAT. Anti-fraud digital stamping is built in; no physical seal is required for digital invoices.

      
The STA and General Administration of Customs cross-check export data and foreign exchange records using Golden Tax IV and the logistics network. False or unverified zero-rating claims trigger recovery of the refunded VAT, late-payment surcharge at 0.05% per day, and administrative fines ranging from 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax. Serious cases are referred for criminal prosecution under Article 204 of the PRC Criminal Law (fraudulent export refund), which carries possible imprisonment up to life. Any refund may be clawed back retroactively if the required documentation or FX repatriation is not completed within the statutory deadline, or if the service is later determined to be a domestic taxable supply.

Stage 04
Banking & Treasury Architecture

      
Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

    

    

      
Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

      

        
Current-account convertibility
Mainland China maintains a managed floating exchange-rate regime with a dual-tier foreign exchange control system. The RMB is fully convertible only on the current account; bona fide trade in goods and services, dividends, royalties, and salaries may be settled through authorized banks upon satisfaction of documentary and tax requirements. The capital account remains heavily controlled, requiring SAFE registration, a bank-approved foreign currency capital account, and CPA-verified capital injection records for equity inflows from Hong Kong. Export proceeds from digital media and subtitling/voice services must be repatriated to China through a designated foreign-exchange account unless a SAFE exemption applies, and any surplus foreign exchange may be retained or converted at market rates but not freely deployed abroad. Profit remittance to the Hong Kong shareholder is permissible after annual audit, tax clearance, and foreign exchange declaration, but the bank must verify the distribution resolution and actual profit calculation. There is no fixed percentage surrender mandate at present; however, the bank applies a transaction-by-transaction authenticity review and monitors clumping/round-tripping patterns.

        
Export proceeds surrender rules
Export proceeds must be repatriated to a domestic foreign-exchange account within 180 days from the customs declaration date for goods exports; for cross-border services such as subtitling, voice cloning and AI dubbing, export income must be collected within the contractual payment terms and not later than 30 days after service completion unless a SAFE-approved extension is obtained. There is currently no mandatory minimum surrender ratio; the Sino-Foreign JV may retain eligible foreign-currency proceeds in its current-account retention quota or convert them into RMB at the onshore market rate. Amounts exceeding the bank-approved retention limit must be converted or repatriated promptly, and the bank will verify each credit against customs or contract data.

      

      
Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China, operating under the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

      
      

        Opacity in the Hong Kong intermediate holding company, creating a possible shell structure for the mainland JV capital injection.
        
Root cause: Opacity in the Hong Kong intermediate holding company, creating a possible shell structure for the mainland JV capital injection.

        
Defense evidence: Provide notarized Hong Kong Companies Registry certificates and annual returns, a full group structure chart disclosing all natural-person shareholders owning 25% or more, audited financial statements of the investor, board resolutions approving the capital injection, a source-of-wealth and source-of-funds declaration supported by 12 months of HK bank statements, signed JV service contracts, and a no-nominee confirmation letter from every director.

Defense evidence: File the updated foreign-invested enterprise business licence, signed board resolution on signatory change, notarized passport/identity copies of the new director, Chinese-consulate legalization of the Hong Kong director's identity and residence proof, bank signature cards opened under physical branch supervision, a non-PEP declaration, and a certificate of no criminal record from the relevant Hong Kong or police authority.

      

      
    

    

      
Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

      

        Route all equity injections from Hong Kong via SWIFT MT103 with remitter name strictly matching the HK shareholder's bank records and beneficiary being the JV's SAFE-registered foreign-currency capital account. Reference the SAFE foreign direct investment contract number and board capital-resolution number in the remittance field. The bank will map the purpose code to paid-in capital contribution and, after receipt, the JV must obtain a capital verification report from a Chinese certified public accountant. That report is filed to SAFE and the bank before capital funds can be transferred into the basic RMB operating account for approved working-capital use; speculative use, offshore deposit, and intercompany loan re-characterization are prohibited.Use SWIFT MT103 wire transfers for royalties, service fees, subtitling and AI dubbing contract payments, attaching invoices, service agreements, tax withholding certificates, and SAFE payment filings. For physical media or hardware collateral shipments, issue sight Commercial Letters of Credit through Bank of China or ICBC; use Documentary Collections (D/P and D/A) only with established counterparties after 12 months of clean settlement history. High-value transactions above CNY equivalent thresholds require additional customs/contract or IP evidence, and all outbound payments must be matched to a registered current-account purpose code.Onshore RMB foreign-exchange forward contracts with SAFE-approved banks for genuine current-account exposures such as export receivables and royalty payments; maximum tenor generally 12 months, extendable to 24 months on documented transaction evidence.FX swaps between CNY, HKD and USD for short-term liquidity rebalancing of operational cash only; no speculative overnight rollovers without an underlying exposure.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are not directly permissible for the mainland JV onshore; the Hong Kong shareholder/treasury centre may execute NDFs offshore solely to hedge its own translated exposure and must not attempt delivery into the PRC capital account.Cross-currency swaps (CCS) are permitted only for long-term capital-account liabilities after SAFE registration, with documented principal and interest repayment schedules linked to the foreign-currency loan; naked and speculative CCS contracts are strictly prohibited.
