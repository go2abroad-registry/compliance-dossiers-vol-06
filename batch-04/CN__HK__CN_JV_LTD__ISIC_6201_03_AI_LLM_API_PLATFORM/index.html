Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 annual taxable turnover is the mandatory threshold for general VAT taxpayer registration in mainland China; taxable entities must register from inception regardless of turnover, and Hong Kong has no indirect tax registration threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exported goods and qualifying cross-border digital services supplied by the Chinese VAT taxpayer are eligible for the 0% VAT rate where the services are provided to non-resident customers, are wholly consumed outside mainland China, and are supported by export-contract, delivery, payment, and foreign-exchange-repatriation evidence. AI and LLM API platform fees qualify where the overseas customer accesses the API from outside China, the service is performed outside the customs territory, and no domestic taxable consumption in mainland China occurs.

  • Customs Export Declaration (China Customs Export Goods Declaration Form with customs entry number)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or equivalent bank foreign-exchange receipt confirmation
  • Export contract and commercial invoice
  • Service export contract, offshore consumption evidence, API access logs, and digital delivery records for zero-rated digital services

China requires Fully Digitalized Electronic Invoices issued through the State Taxation Administration e-invoice service platform with API integration; invoice data is transmitted in real time to the Golden Tax System, and each invoice carries a tax-control auto-assigned serial number and anti-counterfeit QR verification. Unverified or fraudulent export zero-rating is subject to retrospective clawback of refunded VAT, a daily late-payment surcharge of 0.05%, and an administrative penalty of one to five times the refund fraudulently obtained; serious cases are referred for criminal prosecution under the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China for export-tax fraud, with possible revocation of export VAT refund registration and joint liability of responsible officers.

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Hong Kong operates a fully liberalized foreign exchange regime under the Linked Exchange Rate System. The Hong Kong dollar (HKD) is fully convertible between 7.7500 and 7.8500 per US dollar, and the HKMA maintains a currency-board-like mechanism through buy and sell points. There is no legal distinction between current and capital account conversion; dividends, royalties, service fees, investment redemptions, and liquidation proceeds can be converted and remitted freely without prior approval or surrender. Firms may hold, settle, and lend in any freely traded currency. Offshore renminbi (CNH) trades freely in Hong Kong's interbank and retail markets, but conversions involving onshore renminbi (CNY) remain subject to mainland SAFE rules and the local capital account restrictions. Therefore an AI API platform JV operated in Hong Kong suffers no on-shore convertibility bottleneck for USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, or CNH. The main practical constraints are banking counterparty credit limits, AML/KYC documentation obligations under HKMA's statutory guidelines, and the need to ensure that any mainland-sourced capital injection has already passed SAFE's outbound investment or current account compliance checks.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable in Hong Kong SAR. No statutory timeline, mandatory conversion ratio, or surrender obligation applies to export, service, or non-export foreign currency receipts. Enterprises may retain export proceeds in any currency, use them directly for outbound payments, or convert freely through licensed banks. If a mainland Chinese exporter is also involved, that mainland entity may be subject to SAFE's current account verification requirements, but the Hong Kong JV is not.

Central bank supervision: Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA); foreign exchange administration for mainland-origin RMB remains with the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the People's Bank of China (PBOC), but the Hong Kong SAR does not maintain a separate exchange control authority.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Rating: Low as a primary banking rail and Conditionally Medium as a supplementary payment collection channel. Offshore digital EMIs are not a substitute for a licensed Hong Kong bank because the Banking Ordinance restricts deposit-taking to authorized institutions. An offshore EMI without an HKMA license cannot hold domestic HKD/CNH settlement accounts or provide CHATS direct clearing membership.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Under the STA related-party transaction reporting regime, the mandatory Local File threshold is RMB 200 million for annual tangible-property related-party transactions, RMB 100 million for intangible-property or financial-asset related-party transactions, and RMB 40 million for related-party service transactions. A Master File is also required where the PRC filer meets the CbC reporting standard or the group exceeds RMB 5 billion in consolidated revenue. The JV must prepare contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation before the annual CIT return filing date and submit the related-party transaction disclosure schedules (G-form series) with the annual return if the relevant thresholds are met.

Economic substance scope: Although there is no UAE-style Economic Substance Regulations statute in Mainland China or Hong Kong, the substantive economic substance doctrine applies through the PRC General Anti-Avoidance Rule, the Beneficial Ownership anti-treaty-shopping rules, and the Hong Kong IRD mutual agreement procedures. The entity claiming treaty benefits or foreign-sourced income treatment must demonstrate Core Income-Generating Activities (CIGA) in the claimed jurisdiction. For AI LLM and API platform arrangements, CIGA includes strategic decisions about software development, model maintenance, customer contracting, risk acceptance, and significant management functions. A Hong Kong affiliate that only executes invoices, holds bank accounts, or holds legal title to IP while the PRC JV performs all substantive research, development, marketing, and technical support is not the beneficial owner and will be disregarded for treaty and transfer pricing purposes.
