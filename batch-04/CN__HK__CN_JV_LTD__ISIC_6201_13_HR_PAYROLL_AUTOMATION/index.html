Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA) and its national electronic tax office platform, together, for Hong Kong SAR jurisdictional purposes, with the Inland Revenue Department (IRD) of Hong Kong and the IRD's eTAX online filing portal.

E-government filing portal

For the PRC Sino-foreign equity joint venture, the State Administration for Market Regulation issues the business license and, through the integrated multi-license and tax registration reform, automatically transmits the 18-character Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) to the State Taxation Administration; the USCC is the entity's unified taxpayer identification number for VAT and corporate income tax purposes. The JV must then use the electronic tax office to confirm its tax registration profile, banking and settlement details, electronic invoice capacity, payroll withholding registration, and fiscal ledger setup. A foreign non-resident enterprise without a PRC establishment or place of business that derives China-sourced income must separately apply to the competent tax authority of the PRC payer or withholding agent for a non-resident taxpayer identification number and complete the relevant withholding registration. In Hong Kong SAR, the JV must file with the Business Registration Office of the Inland Revenue Department under the Business Registration Ordinance to obtain a Business Registration Certificate and, if it has Hong Kong employment activities, separately register as an employer for payroll, tax and MPF compliance purposes.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

6%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For goods, zero-rated VAT of 0% is available only after a valid Customs Export Goods Declaration is accepted and endorsed by the General Administration of Customs, the goods leave PRC customs territory, title passes to the overseas buyer, and the export price is received through a Designated Foreign Exchange Bank. For qualifying cross-border services, including qualifying payroll/SaaS software services supplied to an overseas customer, zero-rating applies only where the service is supplied wholly from the PRC to a non-resident customer, the service is consumed outside the PRC, the contractual documentation is maintained by the PRC supplier, the overseas payment is received in foreign currency, and the transaction is supported by the STA's cross-border taxable service zero-rating catalogue.

  • Customs Export Goods Declaration Form issued by the General Administration of Customs of the PRC
  • Ocean Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing actual physical export from the PRC
  • Commercial invoice and packing list matching the customs declaration
  • Bank-generated export collection or foreign exchange remittance receipt evidencing receipt from the overseas buyer in foreign currency
  • SAFE foreign exchange collection verification or export proceeds reconciliation record
  • Export sales contract or purchase order with destination, Incoterms, and payment terms

China operates a unified electronic invoice service platform under the State Taxation Administration and the Golden Tax IV digital control and anti-fraud system. VAT taxpayers must issue fully digitalized electronic invoices with unique digital identifiers, real-time upload to the STA data center, and digital fiscal clearance; API integration, invoice classification code mapping, and automatic reconciliation of invoice records to VAT filing positions are required as applicable. Paper special VAT invoices remain subject to tax-control equipment registration and online verification through the STA's nationwide invoice verification system. Hong Kong SAR has no general VAT/GST/sales tax and therefore imposes no indirect-tax electronic invoicing or clearance mandate.

The STA can retrospectively disallow zero-rated treatment and claw back export VAT refunds where the export or qualifying offshore service supply is not substantiated, the foreign exchange receipt is absent or delayed, documents are false, or the transaction is redocumented to obtain a 0% rate. Penalties include compulsory VAT top-up at the applicable rate, late payment interest, administrative fines of 50 percent to five times the evaded tax, loss of future export tax rebate qualification, and criminal liability for fraudulent export tax refund under the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, including fines and imprisonment of the responsible officers.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The PRC maintains a bifurcated convertibility regime. Current account items, including trade in goods and services, dividends, and wages, are generally freely convertible after SAFE verification and bank review, with no prior approval required provided the underlying transaction is authentic and compliant with documentary requirements. Capital account items remain subject to strict administrative controls administered by SAFE under delegated authority from PBOC. Inward foreign direct investment (FDI) requires completion of FDI registration and opening of a designated capital account at a domestic bank; foreign exchange may be settled into RMB only after verification of the injection and earmarked for approved use. Profit remittance for a Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture is permitted after audited annual financial statements, tax payment certificates, and board resolutions; however, banks will require proof that the retained earnings were generated through legal operations and that no outstanding external debt liabilities are overdue. Export proceeds surrender requirements have been substantially liberalized: no statutory ratio mandate exists since implementation of the foreign exchange account cap system; enterprises must repatriate export earnings to China within 180 days of customs clearance and either settle them into RMB or maintain them in a designated foreign exchange current account subject to the bank-approved retention limit. Capital controls are enforced through the cross-border financing ratio, macro-prudential parameter, and negative list restrictions. In practice, converting proceeds from payroll SaaS subscriptions into foreign exchange for distribution abroad may require tax certificates, invoice documentation, and a justified service trade purpose through SWIFT MT103 channels. The overarching theme is authenticity-first supervision, requiring comprehensive documentation and periodic SAFE reporting.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under current SAFE rules, exporters must repatriate foreign exchange proceeds from trade in goods into China within 180 days of the customs declaration date for exports. There is no obligatory surrender percentage; enterprises may retain up to 100 percent of foreign exchange earnings in their current account foreign exchange settlement account, but the balance cap is calculated based on the bank-approved quota and the enterprise's actual trade volume. Should the enterprise elect to convert, the transaction must be carried out through a designated domestic bank using the appropriate trade purpose code, and the bank will validate against the customs export declaration and goods trade monitoring system.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China, operating under the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Rating: NOT VIABLE for onshore RMB treasury. PRC banking law and SAFE foreign exchange regulations require all physical RMB accounts and FX conversions to be handled through licensed domestic banks; offshore digital EMI (electronic money institution) providers are not recognized as valid in-country settlement channels for a JV's mainland capital account, trade settlement, or payroll disbursement on the China side. An offshore EMI may potentially handle a Hong Kong treasury vehicle's non-RMB auxiliary flows, but it cannot open a basic deposit account, obtain SAFE FDI registration, perform capital verification, or act as a counterparty for onshore CNY swaps or forwards. Legal constraints include the Administrative Measures for the Reporting of Large-Value and Suspicious Transactions and the Measures for the Administration of Foreign Exchange, which mandate a licensed bank intermediary. Therefore, use only Tier-1 Chinese banks for the onshore blueprint; offshore EMIs are restricted to a pure compliance back-office facilitation role outside China.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: RMB 40,000,000 aggregate controlled related-party transaction volume for Local File preparation; RMB 1,000,000,000 group-level related-party transaction volume for Master File preparation; lower category-specific thresholds apply to financial asset and intangible transfers under SAT Bulletin No. 42/2016.

Economic substance scope: The PRC applies the substance-over-form rule and may deny treaty benefits if the Hong Kong affiliate lacks Core Income-Generating Activities (CIGA), real staff, premises, and decision-making authority. For a payroll-automation platform, the China-HK arrangement must show that the Hong Kong entity performs actual payroll processing, owns the relevant intellectual property, controls client relationships, and bears real economic risk. Empty conduit structures are vulnerable to general anti-avoidance recharacterization under PRC CIT Law Article 47 and denial of the 5% dividend treaty rate.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Transfer Pricing Abuse

Statutory sanction: PRC CIT Law Articles 41 and 47, PRC Tax Administration Law Articles 36 and 63, and SAT Special Tax Adjustment rules permit re-determination of taxable income at arm's length, imposition of late-payment surcharge at 0.05% per day, and an administrative fine of 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false tax returns or originate sham contracts are jointly liable for restitution of the shortfall, may have personal bank accounts frozen, may be placed on the dishonest-taxpayer social credit blacklist, may be subjected to exit bans, and may face criminal imprisonment of up to seven years plus fines under PRC Criminal Law Article 201 for tax evasion.
Treaty Shopping / Shell Company DTA Abuse

Statutory sanction: The tax authority may deny treaty benefits under CIT Law Article 47 and SAT Public Notice No. 35/2019, reassert the full 10% domestic WHT on the gross dividend, impose a 50% to 500% penalty for wilful underwithholding, and add daily surcharge on the unpaid amount.

Director personal liability: Directors who execute the circular dividend and procure false beneficial-ownership declarations are personally liable for the unpaid withholding tax shortfall, may be blacklisted, banned from leaving China, and may face imprisonment for fraudulent tax arrangements where the diverted profits are laundered through the Hong Kong entity.
AML / Cross-Border Payroll Fund Irregularities

Statutory sanction: PRC Anti-Money Laundering Law Articles 32 and 33 impose fines of up to RMB 2,000,000 on institutions and up to RMB 100,000 on responsible directors; unlicensed cross-border payment settlement can be punished as illegal business operations under Criminal Law Article 225, and the JV may face revocation of its EFT rails and payroll outsourcing licences.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly route payroll funds without payment licences or AML filings are personally liable for restitution of lost or frozen funds, may be subjected to travel restrictions and social-credit blacklisting, and face criminal liability for illegal business operation or money laundering with maximum imprisonment of seven years and confiscation of assets.
