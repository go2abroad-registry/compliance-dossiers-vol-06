Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No minimum paid-up share capital under Hong Kong Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622). The issued capital may be denominated in any currency and shares must be paid on such terms as specified in the articles of association. For a single-family office, the subscriber memorandum may provide for a moderate initial capital (e.g. HKD 10,000) with the balance payable on call; however, any deemed capital infusion from PRC parent must be duly recorded in the shareholder register and if it forms cash contribution, the proceeds must be held in the company's licensed bank account for the stated investment purposes. No escrow or blocked capital account is required by the Companies Registry for ordinary private companies. Such requirement may arise only if banking consent conditions are imposed by the Hong Kong Monetary Authority under a licensed trust or securities business, which is not engaged here.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Physical goods exported from the PRC and cleared by China Customs are zero-rated for VAT; a General VAT taxpayer may claim a refund of input VAT attributable to the exported goods. Qualifying cross-border services supplied to non-resident recipients and consumed entirely outside the PRC may be zero-rated or VAT-exempt under the STA cross-border service catalogue; for the family office holding scope, offshore investment advisory, custody, or management services must be supplied to non-residents and supported by contracts, invoices, and overseas actual-consumption evidence to obtain zero-rating or exemption. Domestic services, local custody or securities services, and services without credible extraterritorial consumption remain taxable.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Export Goods Declaration generated through the China International Trade Single Window and cleared by China Customs)
  • Bill of Lading, Sea Waybill, Air Waybill, International Consignment Note, or equivalent proof of export shipment
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or bank/SAFE export proceeds collection voucher showing repatriation of foreign exchange from the overseas buyer
  • Commercial invoice and export sales contract
  • Packing list with export cargo descriptions
  • General VAT taxpayer special invoice or export invoice issued under the tax-controlled invoicing system

All VAT invoices, including Special VAT Invoices and Full Digital Electronic Invoices, must be issued only through State Taxation Administration-approved mechanisms, namely the Tax UKey and the nationwide Electronic Taxpayer Service Hall / Electronic Invoice Service Platform. Invoice data is transmitted to the STA in near real time under the Golden Tax Phase IV anti-fraud digital administration framework; each Full Digital Electronic Invoice carries a unique nationwide invoice code and tax control number. Private invoicing systems, separate fiscal servers, and manual paper-only stamping systems are not permitted; tax authorities perform real-time digital clearance, invoice code registration, and abnormal invoice risk monitoring.

STA audits zero-rated export refunds using cross-matched customs declarations, logistics records, invoices, shipping documents, and foreign exchange receipts. Failure to substantiate physical export, service consumption outside China, or FX repatriation within 180 days from customs clearance converts the transaction to a taxable domestic sale and the refund is clawed back, with daily late-payment surcharge of 0.05% and administrative penalties of 50% to 5 times the underpaid tax. China Customs may also impose penalties for false declarations under the Customs Law; fraudulent or false export rebate filing is a criminal offense under the PRC Criminal Law covering export tax refund fraud and can result in confiscation of illegal gains, substantial fines, and imprisonment up to life imprisonment.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The PRC operates a managed float with partial capital account convertibility. Current-account items, including dividends and interest remitted to foreign partners, are convertible upon submission of authentic trading documents and corresponding tax clearance certificates. Capital-account flows remain subject to a strict filing/approval regime: inbound equity contributions require an FDI registration with SAFE, a capital account opening at a designated domestic bank, and periodic cross-border flow reporting. For the proposed Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture (JV) structured as a single-family office, every remittance triggering a capital change (e.g., capital increase, equity transfer) must satisfy the Foreign Investment Law and the Negative List screening, and must be individually registered with SAFE. RMB is an IMF reserve currency, but onshore RMB and offshore CNH are not freely interchangeable; households and corporates may access only current-account services and approved hedging. Profit remittance hurdles include the need for an audited financial statement, a board resolution declaring dividends, verified tax payments (including corporate income tax and withholding tax), and proof that the source of the remitted funds is not part of a disguised capital flight scheme. The JV must also ensure that all foreign-currency receipts originate from legitimate trade or investment and are repatriated through legally recognized banking channels. No fintech, cryptocurrency, or off-shore EMI can act as a clearing intermediary for such remittances.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the SAFE Foreign Exchange Administration for Trade, there is no mandatory surrender ratio for export receipts. Exporters may retain 100% of their foreign currency proceeds in designated foreign-currency accounts. The statutory reporting timeline requires that export-related foreign currency receipts be entered into the SAFE trade monitoring system and cross-checked with customs declarations generally within 30 days from the date of export declaration (customs exit). In practice, banks require the exporter to present the shipping documents, customs declaration, and commercial invoice for electronic verification at the time of receipt; any mismatch must be rectified via a supplementary declaration. The JV, being formed under ISIC 6430, does not conduct traditional exports but may receive foreign exchange as capital contributions or service fees; such funds are not subject to surrender requirements but are captured by the capital account filing and current account verification procedures respectively.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC); State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under PBOC

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Not viable. Under the PRC Regulations on the Administration of Foreign Exchange and the Payment Clearing Rules promulgated by the PBOC, only licensed banks and qualified non-bank payment institutions approved by PBOC may lawfully provide cross-border RMB and foreign exchange settlement services to PRC-domiciled entities. Offshore digital EMI platforms (e.g., Revolut, Wise, Payoneer) do not hold a PBOC payment institution license and are barred from opening internal settlement accounts for Chinese JVs. Attempting to route any inbound capital contribution or dividend remittance through such channels would constitute an illegal payment clearing act, triggering SAFE penalties, forced repatriation, and criminal liability for unlawful business operations. The only permissible clearing rails are the onshore CNAPS and the SWIFT network operated via a licensed domestic commercial bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital injections must be routed through SWIFT MT103 messages to the JV's SAFE-registered Capital Escrow Account at a designated Tier-1 domestic bank. The wire must clearly state the purpose code (e.g., 'Foreign Direct Investment - Equity Contribution' or '602' under the SAFE BOP classification), the JV's unique FDI registration number, and the reference to the Chinese joint venture contract. Upon receipt, the bank conducts a compliance check against the SAFE FDI Register and then credits the funds to the foreign exchange capital account. Before converting any amounts into RMB, the JV must submit a Payment Purpose Verification Form (with supporting invoices, contracts, or declarations) for each transaction. Within 15 days of receipt, the JV must file an update with SAFE by confirming the cross-border income via the bank's reporting system. The subsequent renminbi use must follow the 'negative list' approach — the funds shall not be used for repaying RMB loans, real estate speculation, or unapproved investments. A certified public accountant (CPA) in PRC must issue a capital verification report after the contribution is settled, and the report is filed with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR).For a single-family office undertaking investment holding, outbound payment volumes are predominantly dividend remittances, professional advisory fees, and possibly acquisition consideration. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Not viable. Under the PRC Regulations on the Administration of Foreign Exchange and the Payment Clearing Rules promulgated by the PBOC, only licensed banks and qualified non-bank payment institutions approved by PBOC may lawfully provide cross-border RMB and foreign exchange settlement services to PRC-domiciled entities. Offshore digital EMI platforms (e.g., Revolut, Wise, Payoneer) do not hold a PBOC payment institution license and are barred from opening internal settlement accounts for Chinese JVs. Attempting to route any inbound capital contribution or dividend remittance through such channels would constitute an illegal payment clearing act, triggering SAFE penalties, forced repatriation, and criminal liability for unlawful business operations. The only permissible clearing rails are the onshore CNAPS and the SWIFT network operated via a licensed domestic commercial bank.
