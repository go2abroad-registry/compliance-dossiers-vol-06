E-government filing portal

A foreign non-resident entity without a Hong Kong establishment that derives Hong Kong-sourced profits must nonetheless notify the IRD and deliver Profits Tax Returns when required.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Hong Kong operates under a freely convertible currency regime with no foreign exchange controls, no surrender requirements, and no restrictions on the movement of capital into or out of the territory. The HKD is fully convertible against all major currencies under the Linked Exchange Rate System, managed by the HKMA within the HKD 7.75 to 7.85 per USD band. Current account payments for portfolio management fees, subscription and redemption proceeds, dividends, interest, and internal group service charges are freely remitted without prior Hong Kong approval. There are no statutory surrender rules for foreign exchange receipts and no obligation to convert export or custodial proceeds into local currency. However, from the perspective of the Chinese shareholders and the Chinese sovereign parent, the PRC State Administration of Foreign Exchange may impose capital account controls: any capital injection from the mainland into the Hong Kong entity must be registered as outbound direct investment or be structured through permitted channels such as QDII/QDLP, and the underlying purpose must be consistent with the approved commercial scope. Dividend or profit repatriation back to mainland China, in the form of HKD or RMB conversions, will be treated as foreign exchange payment under current account and will require transactional vouchers, board resolutions, audited accounts, tax payment certificates, and SAFE circular monitoring once the payment is settled through a mainland bank. The practical effect is that while Hong Kong itself imposes no hurdles, the fund source jurisdiction of China creates upstream compliance gates that determine the timing and ability to deploy liquidity.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Hong Kong: nil. There is no surrender timeline and no mandatory conversion ratio for any foreign currency receipt in the Hong Kong SAR. For the mainland China parent or exporter, goods-trade foreign exchange proceeds must be repatriated to a designated PRC foreign exchange bank within 180 days from the date of export customs declaration; after repatriation, the enterprise may hold the proceeds in a retained foreign exchange account or convert into RMB at its own election, with a 0% mandatory conversion ratio, subject to SAFE's annual goods-trade verification and on-site inspections.

Central bank supervision: Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) as the de facto central banking authority; for PRC cross-border renminbi flows, the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China is the controlling foreign exchange administration.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low to nil for primary treasury, custody, and interbank settlement purposes. While Hong Kong permits licensed digital banks and stored value facility operators, an MFO with ISIC 6430 discretionary portfolio advisory and custody wire requirements is classified as High_Risk_Special_License. Overseas non-bank EMIs, such as Payoneer or Airwallex, unless they hold a Hong Kong Authorized Institution licence, are legally barred from providing custodial services, executing SWIFT interbank transfers, or holding client money under the Securities and Futures Ordinance. Their use is confined to ancillary expense cards or receivables collection with strict daily limits, and only after the HKMA and SFC licensing overlay is satisfied for higher-risk activity. The HKMA does not issue banking licences to pure fintech EMI firms without full prudential and AML/CTF compliance, so domestic Tier-1 banks remain the compliant treasury rails.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound capital must be routed by SWIFT MT103 or MT202 from a licensed PRC bank to the Hong Kong Tier-1 corporate account, with the SAFE ODI registration number, the applicable foreign exchange purpose code, and the underlying share subscription agreement reference listed in field 70. The Hong Kong receiving bank will perform an enhanced KYC check, apply FATCA and CRS classification and, after funds pass through interbank clearing layers (CHATS, RTGS, SWIFT), issue a credit advice. Capital verification is conducted by an independent Hong Kong CPA, who reconciles the bank in-credit memorandum, board minutes, share certificate register, and SAFE ODI forms filed by the PRC investor; for the multi-family office, the capital is then held in a custodial client money account under the client money rules, segregated from the operating treasury.Outbound settlements are executed via SWIFT MT103 for client payments, SWIFT MT202 COV for interbank reimbursement, and CHATS or RTGS for HKD and USD clearing. Portfolio purchases are settled through custodial instructions over SWIFT or via the Central Moneymarkets Unit (CMU) in Hong Kong. For any trade-related movement, documentary letters of credit (UCP600), standbys (ISP98), and documentary collections (URC522 and URDG758) are permitted, but the multi-family office must obtain an independent directors' resolution and the bank must verify the underlying trade documentation to avoid misuse of the trade corridor. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low to nil for primary treasury, custody, and interbank settlement purposes. While Hong Kong permits licensed digital banks and stored value facility operators, an MFO with ISIC 6430 discretionary portfolio advisory and custody wire requirements is classified as High_Risk_Special_License. Overseas non-bank EMIs, such as Payoneer or Airwallex, unless they hold a Hong Kong Authorized Institution licence, are legally barred from providing custodial services, executing SWIFT interbank transfers, or holding client money under the Securities and Futures Ordinance. Their use is confined to ancillary expense cards or receivables collection with strict daily limits, and only after the HKMA and SFC licensing overlay is satisfied for higher-risk activity. The HKMA does not issue banking licences to pure fintech EMI firms without full prudential and AML/CTF compliance, so domestic Tier-1 banks remain the compliant treasury rails.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: HK$78,000,000 (intercompany transaction threshold triggering Local File / Master File preparation under IRD DIPN 58; transactions below HK$8,000,000 are generally not subject to full documentation).

Economic substance scope: Hong Kong follows the territorial source principle; economic substance is evaluated through the core income-generating activities (CIGA) of the investor. The 2023 family office exemption requires a single family office to carry out distinct business activities in Hong Kong with adequate substance. For a multi-family office, the advisory service price must be properly remunerated under the arm's length principle; adequate substance documentation must be maintained even though no separate ESR statute exists.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Violation of the Inland Revenue Ordinance (Cap. 112) s.82 – upon summary conviction, a fine of HKD 100,000 and imprisonment for 3 years; in addition, tax undercharged may be recovered up to 3 times.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil may be pierced: directors are jointly and severally liable for the outstanding tax and penalties. A travel ban and asset restraint order may be issued under s.85; criminal imprisonment up to 3 years applies.
Failure to Maintain Transfer Pricing Documentation

Statutory sanction: Under the Inland Revenue (Amendment) Ordinance 2018 (Cap. 112, s.51C), penalties of up to HKD 50,000 on first offence and HKD 100,000 on subsequent offence; a daily fine of HKD 500 may apply, and the tax authority may assess additional profits at the arm's length price.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly authorise the omission are personally liable for the penalties and may be blacklisted; criminal prosecution may impose fines and imprisonment up to 3 years.
Money Laundering / AML Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Unlicensed regulated activity under the SFO (Cap. 571) s.114 carries a maximum fine of HKD 5,000,000 and imprisonment for 7 years; AML failures under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Ordinance (AMLO) may attract a fine and up to 7 years' imprisonment.

Director personal liability: Personal criminal liability attaches to directors directly involved; corporate veil-piercing orders may compel restitution of laundered assets, travel bans apply, and the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) may debar the individual from holding office.
