Stage 03
Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

      
Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

    

    

      
Tax ID Application Infrastructure

      

        
Competent tax authority
Inland Revenue Department of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region (IRD), acting through the GovHK/eTAX electronic portal, together with the Companies Registry of Hong Kong; where the Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture has a mainland China taxable establishment, the State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China remains the competent authority for Corporate Income Tax, VAT, and withholding tax, administered through its electronic tax service platform.

        
E-government filing portal
For a foreign non-resident entity establishing or owning the Hong Kong-incorporated JV: first register the private limited liability company at the Companies Registry under the Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622) and obtain the Certificate of Incorporation; if a non-Hong Kong company is used, register it as an overseas company under Part 16 of the Companies Ordinance. Within one month of commencing business in Hong Kong, submit the statutory business registration application to the Business Registration Office of the Inland Revenue Department under the Business Registration Ordinance (Cap. 310), attaching the incorporation documents, registered office address, director and shareholder particulars, identity proofs, and Hong Kong place of business. The IRD will issue a Business Registration Certificate whose Business Registration Number functions as the Hong Kong tax identification number for profits tax, stamp duty, and employer tax returns. If the JV also establishes a mainland China entity or permanent establishment, the enterprise must first obtain a foreign-invested enterprise business license from the State Administration for Market Regulation, then complete taxpayer registration with the competent State Taxation Administration office under the Tax Administration Law; the Unified Social Credit Code (18-digit) issued at incorporation or registration is the PRC taxpayer identification number and VAT registration identifier. A foreign non-resident shareholder without a mainland permanent establishment does not require a separate PRC TIN solely for withholding on dividends; the PRC payer acts as withholding agent, and a non-resident taxpayer number is assigned only if treaty relief or non-resident business registration is applied for. Hong Kong does not require a VAT/GST identification number because it levies no VAT, GST, or sales tax.

      

      
Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation. The CN_JV_LTD is a limited liability corporate vehicle taxed separately from its shareholders; it is not a pass-through entity. In Hong Kong, profits tax is assessed at entity level on profits with a Hong Kong source; in mainland China, a resident foreign-invested enterprise is taxed on worldwide income at entity level under PRC Corporate Income Tax. The default status is therefore Entity_Level_Taxation, with Territorial_Scope principles governing Hong Kong-sourced income only and no tax-exempt-zone pass-through treatment. — For a foreign non-resident entity establishing or owning the Hong Kong-incorporated JV: first register the private limited liability company at the Companies Registry under the Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622) and obtain the Certificate of Incorporation; if a non-Hong Kong company is used, register it as an overseas company under Part 16 of the Companies Ordinance. Within one month of commencing business in Hong Kong, submit the statutory business registration application to the Business Registration Office of the Inland Revenue Department under the Business Registration Ordinance (Cap. 310), attaching the incorporation documents, registered office address, director and shareholder particulars, identity proofs, and Hong Kong place of business. The IRD will issue a Business Registration Certificate whose Business Registration Number functions as the Hong Kong tax identification number for profits tax, stamp duty, and employer tax returns. Default Tax Classification & Elections

      

        
Default tax classification
Entity_Level_Taxation. The CN_JV_LTD is a limited liability corporate vehicle taxed separately from its shareholders; it is not a pass-through entity. In Hong Kong, profits tax is assessed at entity level on profits with a Hong Kong source; in mainland China, a resident foreign-invested enterprise is taxed on worldwide income at entity level under PRC Corporate Income Tax. The default status is therefore Entity_Level_Taxation, with Territorial_Scope principles governing Hong Kong-sourced income only and no tax-exempt-zone pass-through treatment.

        
Available election forms
None. Hong Kong's Inland Revenue Ordinance does not provide a check-the-box election; corporations are automatically separate taxpayers. Mainland China also does not offer a U.S.-style check-the-box entity classification; anti-hybrid mismatch rules under the PRC Enterprise Income Tax Law operate to deny improper mismatch outcomes. Treaty-based relief under the Mainland-Hong Kong double taxation arrangement is available only by claim and applies to eliminate double taxation, not to reclassify the entity.

      

    

    

      
VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

      

        
Standard VAT / GST rate
Mainland China Value-Added Tax (VAT) applies to taxable sales, with financial intermediary services generally taxable at 6% and goods at 13%; Hong Kong SAR has no VAT, GST, or Sales and Use Tax. The target HK-incorporated JV is outside the mainland VAT net unless it has a mainland establishment or makes taxable supplies in mainland China.

        
Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)
Hong Kong SAR: none / nil because there is no VAT/GST. Mainland China: mandatory VAT general taxpayer registration is required when annual taxable sales exceed CNY 5,000,000; below that threshold, simplified small-scale taxpayer rules apply unless the taxpayer elects general taxpayer status. The HK-based JV is not required to register for PRC VAT merely for executing the equity crowdfunding platform outside mainland China; registration arises only for mainland taxable activities.

      

      
      

        Export 0% zero-rating conditions
        
For mainland China exports of goods, the statutory criteria are: a genuine cross-border sale to an overseas buyer, physical export of goods from the PRC customs territory under a valid customs declaration, full collection of export proceeds from outside China, and compliance with VAT invoice and export verification requirements. Qualifying cross-border services, including certain fully consumed offshore digital services, may be zero-rated or exempted from PRC VAT if the beneficiary is located outside mainland China, the services are wholly used outside mainland China, and statutory supporting documentation is retained; an equity crowdfunding portal is likely to qualify only for the portion of intermediary services provided and consumed outside mainland China, not for any mainland-regulated activities requiring a financial-services licence. In Hong Kong, no VAT zero-rating is required because Hong Kong imposes no VAT; Hong Kong-sourced profits may still be exempt from profits tax if sourced outside Hong Kong, subject to IRD territorial source rules.

Hong Kong SAR: no statutory real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing mandate, or anti-fraud digital stamping regime; commercial invoices and accounting records satisfy Inland Revenue Ordinance record-keeping. Mainland China: VAT invoices and fully digitalized electronic invoices must be issued through the State Taxation Administration's national electronic invoicing and clearance systems, with invoice data transmitted in real time to the tax authorities; the platform applies anti-counterfeit tax control mechanisms, digital stamps, and invoice-existence verification before a mainland VAT taxpayer may claim input VAT credits or export refunds.

      
The State Taxation Administration, the General Administration of Customs, and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange jointly scrutinize export zero-rating refund claims. Falsified customs declarations, forged Bills of Lading or FIRCs, unrepatriated export proceeds, or offshore shell transactions trigger retrospective clawback of VAT refunds, late payment surcharges, administrative penalties up to five times the tax involved, disqualification from export tax refund status, VAT invoice controls, and referral for criminal investigation under the PRC Criminal Law for fraudulent export tax refunds. In Hong Kong, false records or fraudulent profits tax claims may lead to IRD estimated assessments, penalties of treble the tax avoided as provided in the Inland Revenue Ordinance, and prosecution; clawback applies to any refund or relief obtained through misrepresentation.

Stage 04
Banking & Treasury Architecture

      
Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

    

    

      
Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

      

        
Current-account convertibility
The Hong Kong dollar is fully convertible in a free market under the linked exchange rate system administered by the HKMA; there are no surrender requirements, no approval gates for dividend remittances, and no restrictions on holding foreign currency in Hong Kong. However, the parent company is in mainland China, so any cross-border RMB or FX movement between mainland China and Hong Kong is governed by SAFE. China maintains a managed float and strict capital account controls; current account trade convertibility is generally available once banks verify genuine trade transactions. Capital account flows such as equity subscription by mainland investors into the offshore joint venture, loans, or overseas direct investment require SAFE registration or approval. For an equity crowdfunding portal, the mixture of retail investor money and founder capital increases verification complexity: a bank will characterise each credit individually. Profit remittance from Hong Kong to mainland shareholders is a dividend flow, requiring tax certification and, for mainland resident corporate investors, possibly SAFE balance-of-payments reporting. The practical hurdle is not currency convertibility but compliance-evidence density.

        
Export proceeds surrender rules
Hong Kong SAR imposes no statutory surrender timeline or mandatory conversion ratio for export receipts; all foreign currency may be retained or converted at market rate. For mainland China export-related flows, SAFE requires exporters to repatriate trade proceeds into China and complete trade-monitoring reconciliation. Since the 2012 reform, exporters may voluntarily retain foreign currency earnings and are not subject to mandatory conversion. However, if proceeds are not received within 90 days after the Customs export declaration, the exporter must register a delayed collection filing; otherwise the bank may suspend trade verification. Any export receipt exceeding USD 5 million equivalent remaining unrepatriated beyond 120 days triggers additional SAFE documentary review. The conversion ratio is enterprise-determined and no fixed statute percentage applies.

      

      
Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBoC) and State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE); Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) is the banking and monetary authority for Hong Kong SAR.

    

    

      
Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

      

        
        

          

            Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails
            Fit: High
          

          
Low viability for the core treasury function. Hong Kong does not grant full banking privileges to offshore electronic money institutions; an entity accepting deposits or conducting money servicing must be authorised under the Banking Ordinance or the Payment Systems and Stored Value Facilities Ordinance. Offshore fintech EMIs such as neobanks and remittance apps are not licensed to hold segregated client escrow for a regulated equity crowdfunding portal. The platform's investor fund custody requirement, segregated trust ledger, and HKMA AML/CFT supervision necessitate a licensed Tier-1 commercial bank as the sole clearing and settlement counterparty. A fintech EMI may only provide ancillary FX conversion or card issuance if it operates through a licensed partner bank, but it cannot be the principal account-holding intermediary and is not recommended for this mandate.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

      
      

        The platform is a non-bank fiduciary that can move third-party investor funds into escrow, creating an automatic higher-risk money laundering and terrorist financing red flag under HKMA AML/CFT guidelines.
        
Root cause: The platform is a non-bank fiduciary that can move third-party investor funds into escrow, creating an automatic higher-risk money laundering and terrorist financing red flag under HKMA AML/CFT guidelines.

        
Defense evidence: Provide the executed trust or escrow custody agreement with a licensed Hong Kong bank, the statutory securities regulator licence for equity crowdfunding, a legal opinion confirming the platform does not operate an unauthorised deposit-taking business, audited segregated escrow ledgers, a real-time investor transaction monitoring protocol, and independent audit certification of the AML control environment.

      

      
      

        The Sino-Foreign equity joint venture structure blends mainland China shareholders with Hong Kong corporate layers, making source of wealth and business rationale unclear and triggering suspicion of round-trip cross-border financing.
        
Root cause: The Sino-Foreign equity joint venture structure blends mainland China shareholders with Hong Kong corporate layers, making source of wealth and business rationale unclear and triggering suspicion of round-trip cross-border financing.

        
Defense evidence: Provide the complete corporate ownership chart from mainland parent to Hong Kong entity, SAFE outward direct investment or ODI registration for each mainland shareholder, notarised board resolutions, certified certificates of incumbency, three years of parent company audited financial statements, source-of-funds evidence for the initial capital, and a detailed business plan proving the crowdfunding operations are distinct from depository banking.

      

      
      

        Equity crowdfunding receipts may be commingled with founder contribution or unverified subscription fees, producing a laundering pattern of mixed investor funds and unexplained rapid credit turnover.
        
Root cause: Equity crowdfunding receipts may be commingled with founder contribution or unverified subscription fees, producing a laundering pattern of mixed investor funds and unexplained rapid credit turnover.

        
Defense evidence: Supply the investor suitability and verification procedure for each subscription, a contractual waterfall that releases funds only when a funding round closes, dual-approval escrow release matrices, automated reporting APIs that give the bank read-only access to transaction ledgers, external audit letters over the platform's segregation of accounts, and the named AML compliance officer's professional certification and police clearance certificate.

      

      
      

        The purpose of opening a capital escrow account for a niche fintech crowdfunding vehicle is not standard corporate banking activity and may be rejected by the bank's product risk committee as unviable without proof of regulatory standing.
        
Root cause: The purpose of opening a capital escrow account for a niche fintech crowdfunding vehicle is not standard corporate banking activity and may be rejected by the bank's product risk committee as unviable without proof of regulatory standing.

        
Defense evidence: File the official HK licensing or exemption confirmation for ISIC 6499 crowdfunding activity, a schedule of the specific securities laws being relied upon, proof that the platform is not accepting deposits or issuing loans, a FATCA and CRS classification, the statutory auditor's opinion on internal controls over financial reporting, and a default protection or investor compensation arrangement issued by a licensed insurer.

Turnover: Hong Kong does not impose quantitative balance-sheet, turnover, or headcount thresholds that create a mandatory audit obligation; all active Hong Kong companies must be audited under Companies Ordinance Cap 622 section 359 unless dormant or in a narrow small-company exemption.
