Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None. Hong Kong has no check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election allowing a limited company to be treated as a pass-through entity. The HK JV captive reinsurer is separately chargeable at the entity level under the two-tier profits tax rates: 8.25% on assessable profits up to HKD 2,000,000 and 16.5% on the excess; the two-tier rates apply automatically and do not require an election. An offshore allocation for reinsurance income is an evidentiary claim based on territorial-source principles, not a statutory check-the-box election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None (Hong Kong SAR does not operate a VAT, GST, or general sales and use tax; the only indirect levies potentially relevant are stamp duty, duties on hydrocarbon oil, alcohol, tobacco and methyl alcohol, and insurance levies administered by the Insurance Authority).

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable in HKD because no VAT/GST/SUT registration regime exists. However, every person carrying on a business in Hong Kong must apply for Business Registration under the Business Registration Ordinance (Cap. 310) within one month of commencement, irrespective of turnover; the annual BR fee/levy is not an indirect tax and there is no consumption-tax threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable to VAT/GST because there is no VAT/GST in Hong Kong and therefore no export zero-rating or refundable input VAT. For Hong Kong profits tax, the analogous relief is the territorial-source principle: a captive reinsurer may claim that reinsurance premium income is wholly offshore and thus untaxed in Hong Kong if the insured risk is located outside Hong Kong, the contract is arranged, negotiated, and executed outside Hong Kong, and all profit-producing activities are performed outside Hong Kong; the burden of proof lies on the taxpayer. If instead the entity is a PRC mainland taxpayer, cross-border reinsurance may qualify for PRC VAT zero-rating, but that requires PRC tax registration and is outside this Hong Kong jurisdiction dossier.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Hong Kong operates a free convertibility regime under the Basic Law, with the HKD fully convertible against all foreign currencies. The Linked Exchange Rate System pegs HKD to the United States Dollar within the range of 7.75 to 7.85 per USD, eliminating sovereign exchange-control risk for the captive reinsurance SPV. There are no surrender requirements, no mandatory conversion ratios, and no advance deposit or licensing hurdles for current account reinsurance premium, claims, or underwriting reserve movements. Profit remittance from the HK SPV to overseas shareholders is unrestricted at the HKMA level; however, when the ultimate beneficial owners are PRC entities, the remittance becomes an inbound capital flow into China and must comply with SAFE's capital account verification, tax withholding under pillar two rules, and the foreign investment negative list. For the onshore RMB leg, the capital account remains insufficiently convertible, so transfers between the HK SPV and mainland parent require pre-approval for foreign debt or equity investment, or qualified current account compliance such as reinsurance premium payments supported by licensed contracts and actuarial certificates. The absence of exchange control is therefore administrative and structural rather than absolute for cross-border RMB, but for HKD, USD, EUR, and other tradeable currencies, the HK captive can freely hold, deploy, and remit funds.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None under Hong Kong law. HKD and foreign currency receipts from export-related underwriting and reinsurance activities are not subject to any statutory surrender or conversion requirement. Should the underlying commercial flows involve mainland export proceeds being repatriated to China, SAFE mandates the repatriation of export proceeds into China within the customary 90-day period from the date of receipt (subject to current law and enterprise trading credit rating), but allows enterprises to retain the earnings in foreign currency without mandatory conversion, provided the business is registered and the revenue is reconciled through the State Administration of Foreign Exchange's monitoring system.

Central bank supervision: Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) as the de facto central bank and currency board authority for the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region; the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the People's Bank of China for any cross-border RMB and mainland China capital account transactions involving the Sino-Foreign equity joint venture shareholders.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low Feasibility for core treasury operations. The captive reinsurance SPV is a licensed regulated financial institution under the Insurance Ordinance (Cap. 41), expressly subject to the Hong Kong Insurance Authority (HKIA) and the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Ordinance (Cap. 615). HKIA's 'Guideline on the Miscellaneous Insurance Business' and the 'Guidance Note on Reinsurance' require premium income, claims reserves, and underwriting reserves to be held in licensed bank accounts in the name of the insurer or its licensed trust vehicle. Fintech EMI licensees and Money Service Operators (e.g., Airwallex, Currenxie, Statrys) are not authorized to provide custodial banking services to insurance undertakings, cannot issue capital verification certificates, and generally are not covered by the HKMA's Deposit Protection Scheme. Their settlement rails are also incompatible with the HKIA's 'unearned premium reserve' segregation requirements. The only permissible use of an EMI would be for petty-cash operating expenses, subject to a strict single-purpose account and a cap below 10% of monthly opex, but even then the bank's compliance committee will likely reject the arrangement given the enhanced risk classification.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Covered transaction local file threshold of HKD 5,000,000 per transaction category; master file also required when the MNE group annual consolidated revenue exceeds HKD 6,800,000,000 and a local file obligation is otherwise triggered.

Economic substance scope: Hong Kong does not apply an EU-style Economic Substance Regulations code, but territorial-source doctrine and Insurance Ordinance licensing impose equivalent substance requirements: a captive reinsurance SPV must perform core income-generating activities, including underwriting strategy, pricing, claims management, reserving, treasury management, board governance and compliance, in Hong Kong. Profits are taxable only if the source of the profit is Hong Kong; profits from offshore risk reinsurance are treated as non-resident offshore profits. A captive that cannot demonstrate real CIGA in Hong Kong will be challenged as a sham for tax avoidance under IRO sections 61 and 61A, with recharacterization of the captive's contracts and transfer pricing adjustments.
