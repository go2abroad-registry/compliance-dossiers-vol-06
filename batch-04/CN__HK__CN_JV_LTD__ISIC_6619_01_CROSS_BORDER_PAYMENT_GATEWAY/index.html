Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA); online filing is performed through the China Electronic Taxpayer Service Platform (e-Tax / Electronic Tax Bureau). For Hong Kong SAR sourced profits, the Inland Revenue Department (IRD) and eTAX portal are separately relevant because this is a Sino-Foreign Equity JV with an HK connection.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: The CN_JV_LTD, as a Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture under Chinese law, is established in the State Administration for Market Regulation and obtains the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which is the legal taxpayer identification number (TIN) used for both Corporate Income Tax (CIT) and Value-Added Tax (VAT). Step 2: The JV completes the statutory tax registration through the STA electronic taxpayer service platform by filing the articles of association, joint venture contract, foreign investment information report, proof of registered address, legal representative identity document, and bank account information; no separate tax registration certificate is issued under the current integrated registry. Step 4: The JV must also register for foreign exchange receipt/payment directory purposes with SAFE via its bank, since export zero-rating and charge collection require bank-level foreign exchange evidence.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation (the Chinese resident JV is taxed on worldwide income under the Corporate Income Tax Law; Hong Kong’s territorial profits tax does not override mainland China entity-level taxation for the JV unless the same income is separately sourced and taxed in Hong Kong, with foreign tax credit or tax exemption policy applied by STA).

Available election forms

None for US-style check-the-box or hybrid mismatch elections. The only relevant elections are VAT reporting-status elections: an eligible small-scale taxpayer may elect general VAT taxpayer status, and a taxpayer may elect simplified VAT collection methods only where the STA law expressly permits. Hybrid mismatch outcomes are addressed through General Anti-Avoidance Rule (GAAR) and contemporaneous documentation rather than an elective regime.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 in annual taxable sales for mandatory VAT general-taxpayer registration; an incorporated JV is required to obtain tax registration immediately upon establishment regardless of turnover. The applicable VAT rate for online payment and clearing services is 6% before any export zero-rating; the 13% is the headline standard goods rate.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A Chinese VAT taxpayer may claim 0% VAT on exported goods by completing customs export clearance and proving the goods leave China. For cross-border services, including payment acquiring/clearing for overseas merchants, the 0% rate applies only if: (i) the service is supplied to an overseas entity/individual for use wholly outside China; (ii) the service falls within the STA and related catalogues of zero-rated cross-border services; (iii) the foreign exchange consideration is received from outside China and recorded through a SAFE-designated bank; (iv) the service is not rendered to a domestic Chinese consumer or connected with real estate/goods in China; and (v) the taxpayer retains contemporaneous contracts, invoices, bank receipts, and service performance records. Absent those conditions, the payment gateway service is taxed at 6% VAT.

  • Export Goods Declaration filed on the China International Trade Single Window (Customs export declaration number)
  • Original Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, or multimodal transport document evidencing exit of goods from China
  • Commercial invoice, packing list, and export sales contract
  • For cross-border services: signed cross-border service agreement and service completion/performance report
  • Bank foreign exchange receipt evidence from a SAFE-designated bank (SWIFT MT103 / bank receipt for export proceeds)
  • SAFE trade-in-services/exchange receipt declaration record (foreign exchange payment/receipt confirmation)

All VAT-inclusive sales must be documented with invoices issued through the STA Full Digital Electronic Invoice platform. Under the Golden Tax Phase IV modernization, invoice data is transmitted to STA in real-time via the e-Tax platform and clearing APIs; full digital electronic invoices carry a unique STA-generated invoice number and an anti-fraud digital source-identification mark (digital stamping). No offline manual invoice can substantiate an input VAT credit for cross-border payment services.

If the 0% export rate is claimed without the statutory documentation, the tax authority will claw back the zero-rated benefit, require output VAT at the otherwise applicable 6% (or 13% for goods), charge late-payment interest from the original due date, and impose an administrative penalty of 50% to 500% of the unpaid tax under the Tax Administration and Collection Law. Where forged customs declarations, false export receipts, or counterfeit special VAT invoices are used, the case is treated as export tax fraud and may be referred to the public security authorities under the Criminal Law; responsible officers are subject to criminal liability and the taxpayer remains liable for full restitution, VAT, interest, and penalties within the statutory recovery period.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Mainland China operates a managed float with strict current-account convertibility and controlled capital-account convertibility. RMB is convertible for trade and services under the current account, but capital-account transactions such as FDI injections, shareholder loans, and outbound investment are subject to SAFE registration, quotas, and real-trade documentation. The Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture (CN_JV_LTD) will maintain a capital account for equity injections and a current-account RMB and foreign-currency account for operational receipts. Profit remittance to the Hong Kong parent requires audited financial statements, tax clearance certificate, board resolution confirming distributable profits, and SAFE-verified bank release. Dividend remittance is permitted only after corporate income tax is settled and must be declared under the specified profit-remittance purpose code. For cross-border payment gateway services, domestic merchant settlement received in RMB may be converted to USD or HKD through an onshore bank at the daily central parity rate under the CFETS framework, subject to authenticity review and verifiable transaction records. The Hong Kong offshore layer enjoys CNH convertibility without SAFE approval, but onshore capital flows remain strictly regulated.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE's goods-trade administration, export proceeds received in foreign currency must be repatriated to a domestic export collection account within 180 days of the customs declaration date. There is no mandatory fixed conversion ratio; the exporting entity may retain 100 percent of the foreign exchange in account or settle it into RMB at a licensed bank. The bank checks the transaction against customs manifests and trade documents, and the entity must report receipts through the SAFE monitoring platform within five working days of bank credit. For services and cross-border payment flows, receipts must be settled or retained under the applicable service-trade code within the same authenticity-check framework.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBoC) and State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE), with the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) as the de facto monetary and clearing authority for HKD and offshore Renminbi (CNH) settlement in Hong Kong

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low viability. Offshore digital EMIs are legally barred from directly onboarding a mainland-domiciled entity such as CN_JV_LTD because PRC non-bank payment institution rules require a PBoC license and an onshore legal presence. Such EMIs cannot open local RMB current accounts, access CIPS directly, or act as the primary clearing institution for domestic merchant settlement. They may only operate as downstream white-label FX or conversion services behind a licensed Chinese commercial bank or a licensed onshore payment company, which increases AML opacity and is not recommended as the main treasury rail.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% statutory withholding tax on dividends paid by a Chinese resident enterprise to a non-resident shareholder; reduced to 5% under the Mainland-Hong Kong DTA where the beneficial owner is a Hong Kong resident company that directly holds at least 25% of the JV's equity and satisfies beneficial ownership and substance requirements.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

For a calendar-year Sino-foreign equity joint venture, quarterly CIT prepayments are due within 15 days after each quarter-end; the annual Form A return and supporting transfer pricing documentation must be filed no later than May 31 of the following fiscal year.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File is mandatory if: (a) related-party tangible asset transactions exceed RMB 200 million; (b) related-party intangible asset transactions exceed RMB 100 million; (c) related-party financial asset transactions exceed RMB 100 million; or (d) other related-party transactions exceed RMB 40 million in the tax year. Master File is required when total related-party transactions in the prior year exceed RMB 1 billion. Country-by-Country reporting is required for multinational groups with consolidated revenue of RMB 5.5 billion or more in the previous fiscal year.

Economic substance scope: The Chinese JV must have full commercial substance in its licensed payment gateway business; the Hong Kong affiliate must satisfy the China-HK DTA beneficial ownership test, the PRC GAAR under CIT Law Article 47 and SAT 2017 No. 6, and if applicable, Hong Kong Economic Substance Regulations. Core income-generating activities (CIGA) - strategic decision-making, staff, premises, risk management, and compliance oversight - must actually be performed in Hong Kong; a paper conduit HK company receiving payment gateway fees, royalties, or interest without local substance will be denied treaty benefits, lose intercompany deductions, and have payments recharacterized as taxable dividends.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Treaty Shopping Through a Hollow Hong Kong Shell

Statutory sanction: The tax authority may apply substance-over-form and GAAR to recharacterize all Hong Kong fees, royalties, and interest as taxable dividends; under Tax Collection Administration Law Articles 63-64, false reporting causing a tax deficiency exceeding RMB 50,000 and exceeding 10% of tax payable triggers a monetary penalty of 50% to 5 times the underpaid tax plus 0.05% daily interest; treaty rates of 5% and 7% are denied, and full 10% WHT plus late interest is assessed.

Director personal liability: Under PRC Company Law Article 20, the corporate veil is pierced for directors who abuse the limited liability form; directors are jointly and severally liable for the JV's tax debts, face asset freezing and court-enforced restitution, may be placed on the tax blacklist with an exit ban and travel restrictions, and face criminal liability under PRC Criminal Law Article 201 for tax evasion with imprisonment up to 7 years and fines.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation and Contemporaneous Documentation Failure

Statutory sanction: The State Taxation Administration may make a primary transfer pricing adjustment using the most appropriate method, add 5% to 15% late interest for the adjustment period, and impose penalties; under TCAL Article 44 and SAT 2016 No. 42, failure to provide documentation permits the tax authority to use deemed methods and assess underpayment penalties of 0.05% daily, with a 50% to 5 times fraud penalty if concealment or false documents are found.

Director personal liability: Directors who signed false intercompany contracts, caused false invoices, directed profit shifting, or obstructed the TP audit personally lose the corporate veil; their personal assets may be preserved and applied to the tax debt; they are subject to civil restitution, travel bans via the joint disciplinary blacklist, and criminal prosecution under Criminal Law Article 201 with imprisonment up to 7 years where the shifted profit is substantial.
Unlicensed Payment Institution Operation, AML Breach, and Concealment of Offshore Settlement Income

Statutory sanction: Operating payment services without PBOC approval under the Administrative Measures for Payment Services of Non-Financial Institutions attracts confiscation of illegal gains and fines of 1 to 5 times illegal income or up to RMB 3 million where no illegal income is identified; under the PRC Anti-Money Laundering Law, institutions face fines up to RMB 2 million and responsible personnel face separate penalties; concealed offshore income triggers CIT fraud penalties under TCAL Article 63.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced for the most serious misconduct: directors are personally liable to return illegal gains, compensate merchants and clearing banks, and may be subject to asset seizure, travel bans, and the cross-border financial and tax blacklist; directors can face criminal liability under Criminal Law Article 191 (money laundering) for imprisonment of 5 to 10 years and fine, and Article 225 (illegal business operation) for imprisonment up to 5 years or more for especially serious offenses; if the unlicensed gateway is used in cross-border fraud, directors may be charged under Article 266 with potential life imprisonment in extreme cases.
