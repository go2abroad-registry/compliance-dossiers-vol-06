Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A virtual office address may satisfy the Companies Ordinance registered-office requirement only where a licensed registered agent maintains a physical Hong Kong receiving address. However, for an AEO certification and supply-chain security auditor, the Inland Revenue Department, Hong Kong Customs, and issuing banks treat a virtual mailbox as a red flag. Remote-only arrangements are rejected under the Business Registration Ordinance (Cap. 310) because the IRD requires a place where documents can be personally served. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA); online portal: Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax). For Hong Kong SAR presence, the Inland Revenue Department (IRD) through eTAX is the separate competent authority.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: incorporate at the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) and obtain the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which the STA uses as the taxpayer's TIN. Step 2: tax registration is completed automatically through the SAMR-STA one-stop business registration process; no separate PRC tax registration application is required for the JV itself. Step 3: complete VAT taxpayer qualification, choosing between small-scale taxpayer status and general VAT taxpayer status; annual taxable sales above RMB 5,000,000 force general taxpayer status. Step 4: for a foreign non-resident enterprise without a Chinese establishment, no permanent TIN is needed in advance; before paying China-source dividends, interest, royalties, or service fees, the JV must register the non-resident with the local STA under the withholding registration procedure and obtain a non-resident tax code. Step 5: if the JV creates a Hong Kong branch, separately register with the Hong Kong Business Registration Office under the Inland Revenue Ordinance and obtain a Business Registration Number and, if chargeable to profits tax, an IRD file number.

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None. No check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election exists for a Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture under PRC tax law. The JV is mandatorily taxed as an entity-level PRC resident enterprise. VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VAT registration arises at business registration; general taxpayer registration is mandatory once annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000 in any rolling 12-month period. The JV's AEO certification and supply-chain audit consulting is a modern service subject to 6% VAT domestically, while the PRC headline VAT rate for general goods is 13%.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For goods, 0% VAT applies when the goods are physically exported from China's customs territory, a valid customs export declaration is released, and the export proceeds are repatriated to a PRC bank account through SAFE-regulated channels within the prescribed period. For qualifying cross-border services and offshore digital services, zero-rating is available only if the service is listed in the statutory cross-border zero-rating schedule and the entire service is supplied to and consumed by a non-resident outside China. Management consulting and AEO supply-chain audit services are generally treated as cross-border VAT-exempt services rather than automatically zero-rated, unless the specific engagement is reclassified as a listed zero-rated modern or digital service by the STA. In every zero-rating claim, the JV must show a written offshore contract, actual service delivery documentation, absence of mainland Chinese consumption, and bank receipt of foreign currency.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bill of Entry) with electronic export release record
  • Commercial invoice, packing list, and signed export sales contract or purchase order
  • Bill of Lading, airway bill, or multimodal transport document showing physical export
  • Bank Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or equivalent foreign exchange receipt and settlement proof
  • SAFE trade-monitoring system export proceeds collection confirmation
  • VAT export invoice or export-fapiao record and STA export tax refund application trail
  • AEO supply-chain security audit file and customs logistics traceability records

All VAT invoices must be issued through the STA-approved Electronic Invoice Service Platform or tax-control UKey equipment, with issuance data transmitted to the STA on a real-time or near-real-time basis. Digital e-invoices carry a national invoice number, QR code, and digital signature; no separate physical stamping is required after platform clearance. General-taxpayer input VAT recovery is preconditioned on invoice verification and matching in the STA VAT invoice confirmation system before the monthly return is filed. Authorized large taxpayers may use STA-approved e-invoicing APIs and ERP integration, but every invoice series remains fiscally controlled and monitored by the STA.

The STA, General Administration of Customs, and SAFE may jointly audit or validate any export zero-rating claim, including AEO supply-chain records. If a declaration is false, service consumption occurs in China, or export proceeds are not repatriated within the 180-day deadline, the tax authority will claw back all VAT refunded or exempted, charge overdue interest, and impose a fine of 50% to five times the tax evaded under the PRC Tax Collection and Administration Law. Fraudulent claims may lead to criminal referral, AEO certification revocation, export VAT rebate suspension, and cancellation of e-invoicing quotas.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Mainland China maintains a managed floating exchange rate regime with full current account convertibility for genuine trade and service transactions, but a strictly registered capital account. The RMB is convertible for trade, dividends, royalties, and service payments once the underlying transaction is verified by a licensed bank; however, capital flows such as foreign direct investment, shareholder loans, reinvestment, and capital account profit movements require SAFE registration, FDI foreign exchange registration, and purpose-code matching. The former mandatory export proceeds surrender requirement has been abolished, yet export receipts must be repatriated into China and can be retained in foreign exchange settlement accounts or converted to RMB at the onshore rate. A Sino-foreign equity joint venture faces specific profit remittance hurdles: remittances must be sourced from audited after-tax profits, supported by a board-approved distribution resolution, certified financial statements, tax clearance evidence, and a SAFE-verified foreign exchange payment application. Hong Kong SAR has no foreign exchange controls under the linked exchange rate system; HKD is fully convertible. The critical friction in this structure is the mainland-to-HK capital and payment bridge, where every CNY/HKD or CNY/USD flow must be supported by eligible SAFE codes and real commercial or investment documentation.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE current account administration, the mainland EJV must repatriate export proceeds no later than 90 days after the customs export declaration date unless a justified extension is approved through the Trade Foreign Exchange Monitoring System. The mandatory surrender ratio is zero; the exporter may retain all eligible export receipts in a foreign exchange settlement account or convert them, wholly or partially, into RMB at the onshore bank rate. Hong Kong SAR imposes no export surrender obligation, no collection timeline, and no mandatory conversion ratio.

Central bank supervision: The People's Bank of China (PBOC) and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) for mainland China; the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) for Hong Kong SAR.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Viability rating: Low for the mainland EJV; Medium for the Hong Kong treasury vehicle as an ancillary payout channel. Offshore digital EMIs such as Wise are not PBOC/SAFE-licensed commercial banks in mainland China and cannot open RMB clearing accounts, process FDI capital injections, perform capital verification, issue LCs, or act as the primary clearing rail for the EJV. They are legally barred from replacing a domestic Tier-1 bank for statutory corporate banking in mainland China. In Hong Kong, Wise and similar EMIs are typically licensed under the Payment Systems and Stored Value Facilities Ordinance and can handle low-value current account disbursements from the HK treasury entity, but they remain an ancillary rail only; all inbound FDI, trade settlements, profit repatriation, and FX hedging must run through a licensed commercial bank.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% domestic withholding tax on dividends paid by a Chinese resident enterprise to non-resident enterprise shareholders under EIT Law Article 37, absent treaty relief. Under the China-Hong Kong Double Tax Arrangement, the rate is reduced to 5% where the Hong Kong shareholder is the beneficial owner and directly holds at least 25% of the capital of the Chinese JV.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Quarterly EIT prepayments must be filed and paid within 15 days after the end of each calendar quarter. The annual final EIT return must be filed within 5 months after the end of the tax year (i.e., on or before 31 May following a 31 December fiscal year-end).

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Contemporaneous Local File preparation is mandatory where annual related-party tangible asset transfers exceed RMB 200,000,000; intangible asset transfers exceed RMB 100,000,000; financial asset transfers exceed RMB 100,000,000; or other related-party transactions exceed RMB 40,000,000. A Master File is also required where the MNE group consolidated revenue exceeded RMB 5,500,000,000 in the prior fiscal year, aligning with the Country-by-Country Reporting threshold.

Economic substance scope: China does not apply UAE-style Economic Substance Regulations as such, but the substance-over-form principle applies under EIT Law Article 48 and SAT anti-avoidance rules. For the China-Hong Kong DTA benefits, the Hong Kong shareholder must evidence that its Core Income-Generating Activities — strategic decisions, financing risk, board meetings, staff employment, and contract execution — are performed in Hong Kong. A letterbox Hong Kong entity or a Chinese JV with no operational substance will be recharacterized by SAT, with treaty benefits denied and the 10% WHT rate restored.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Invoicing

Statutory sanction: Under PRC Tax Collection Law Article 63, the tax authority will recover the underpaid CIT and levy a daily late-payment surcharge of 0.05% plus a penalty of 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax. Use of false VAT invoices invokes Criminal Law Article 205, carrying fines and imprisonment of up to 15 years for large-amount invoice fraud, and Article 201 for tax evasion carrying imprisonment of up to 7 years.

Director personal liability: Where directors personally order, sign, or benefit from the false invoicing scheme, the corporate veil is pierced under Company Law Article 20: each director is jointly and severally liable for the JV's tax arrears, penalties, and surcharges to the extent of personal wrongdoing and company undercapitalization. The legal representative and senior managers are subject to PRC exit-control blacklisting / travel bans under Tax Collection Law Article 44; upon criminal conviction they face personal fine and imprisonment up to 7 years, plus disqualification from serving as company directors.
Treaty Abuse / Shell Company / General Anti-Abuse Rule

Statutory sanction: EIT Law Article 48 authorizes SAT to recharacterize the transaction and deny treaty benefits. Public Notice SAT [2015] No. 7 empowers the tax authority to redetermine tax, impose a 10% WHT on the entire distribution, and assess interest. Tax Collection Law Article 63 applies a penalty of 50% to 500% of the extra tax assessed; SAT may also impose a specific anti-treaty-abuse penalty where the Chinese entity is used as a conduit.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly signed off on the shell arrangement will be jointly and severally liable for the unpaid WHT and penalties; the corporate veil may be pierced to allow attachment of personal bank accounts, real property, and directors' salaries. A travel ban/blacklist will prevent the legal representative from leaving China until tax liabilities are discharged, and criminal referral can lead to imprisonment of up to 7 years under Criminal Law Article 201.
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance and Failure to Withhold

Statutory sanction: Under Tax Collection Law Article 62, failure to file the related-party return or withholding declaration attracts a fine of RMB 2,000 to RMB 20,000, or up to RMB 100,000 in serious cases. Under Article 63, concealment and non-withholding of tax due on dividend remittance triggers a penalty of 50% to 500% of the unpaid WHT plus the 0.05% daily late-payment surcharge. Transfer pricing adjustments and interest are mandatory under EIT Law Article 41 and SAT Public Notice No. 42 (2016), including penalty interest up to 2 percentage points above the benchmark lending rate for defective contemporaneous documentation.

Director personal liability: Directors and the principal officers responsible for statutory filing are personally liable where their willful neglect or fraudulent conveyance of JV assets creates an uncollectible tax claim; civil courts may pierce the corporate veil to require restitution of the unpaid tax and penalties from personal assets. The legal representative will be subjected to a travel ban/blacklisting by SAT jointly with the immigration authorities. Where the evaded withholding tax exceeds the criminal threshold, directors face personal criminal prosecution and imprisonment of up to 7 years for tax evasion; those who abscond overseas can be extradited and charged under Criminal Law Article 203 for failure to pay taxes using concealment of assets.
