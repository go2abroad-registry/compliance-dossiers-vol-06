Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA) through the national Electronic Taxpayer Portal and the Electronic Invoice Service Platform; local tax service bureaus exercise delegated administration for taxpayer registration, filing and invoice supervision.

E-government filing portal

For the PRC Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture, tax registration is integrated with commercial registration: after the State Administration for Market Regulation issues the business license, the STA automatically records the taxpayer and uses the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code as the single statutory Taxpayer Identification Number for corporate income tax, VAT and all other taxes. The joint venture must complete tax registration, appoint a tax accountant, activate its electronic taxpayer account and file all required initial declarations within 30 days of business license issuance through the competent local tax service bureau. A foreign non-resident enterprise that has no PRC establishment but derives China-sourced taxable income or provides taxable services in China must either have a withholding agent and obtain a Non-Resident Enterprise Tax Registration through that agent or, if no agent exists, apply directly through the same Electronic Taxpayer Portal; the STA will assign a separate taxpayer identification number for the non-resident enterprise for China-sourced income reporting. No separate VAT certificate number is issued because the Unified Social Credit Code is the universal TIN.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election is available under PRC tax law. The entity may elect small-scale VAT taxpayer status if annual taxable turnover does not exceed RMB 5,000,000, which allows simplified VAT calculation at a lower collection rate but forfeits input VAT credit. Once annual taxable turnover exceeds RMB 5,000,000, registration as a general VAT taxpayer is mandatory. No other elective entity-classification regimes apply; China applies statutory resident enterprise, non-resident enterprise, CFC and general anti-avoidance rules.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

6%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 annual taxable turnover is the mandatory VAT general taxpayer registration threshold; below this level small-scale taxpayer status applies. Irrespective of turnover, a non-resident supplier with no PRC establishment providing taxable services sourced in China must ensure VAT is withheld and paid through the recipient or statutory withholding agent, and no turnover threshold relieves that withholding obligation.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For physical exports, 0% VAT zero-rating is available when goods are genuinely exported and a lawful customs export declaration is filed. For cross-border services, the PRC joint venture may claim 0% VAT only if the M&A advisory service is supplied to a non-resident recipient, is actually consumed wholly outside mainland China, and falls within the statutory list of cross-border zero-rated services; advisory services that instead fall within the cross-border VAT-exempt category are exempt rather than zero-rated. The supplier must be a general VAT taxpayer, maintain a written cross-border service contract, issue a compliant VAT invoice, receive the consideration from outside China, and report the foreign exchange receipt to the State Administration of Foreign Exchange within the statutory time limit.

  • Physical export goods: Customs Export Declaration (Export Goods Customs Declaration), bill of lading or airway bill, commercial invoice and packing list, inward remittance certificate (FIRC) or bank foreign exchange settlement certificate, and export VAT rebate application file.
  • Cross-border services (M&A advisory): signed cross-border engagement contract with the overseas recipient, detailed service description and completion report, VAT invoice marked with the applicable zero-rate or exemption basis, foreign exchange receipt and remittance certificate (FIRC or SAFE-bank settlement certificate), STA cross-border service zero-rating or exemption filing, and, for pure services, no customs export declaration is required.

The joint venture must use STA-approved invoice-issuing software and the national Electronic Invoice Service Platform. VAT special invoices and ordinary VAT invoices must be transmitted in real time to the STA system, carry a tax-control digital anti-counterfeit code, and pass automatic real-time verification against the issuer's TIN, tax rate, item description and declared revenue before being recognized by the buyer. Fully digital e-invoices are mandatory in STA digital invoicing pilot jurisdictions, and all paper or electronic invoices must be reconciled with the VAT filing. The STA Golden Tax system performs the fiscal clearance function through real-time invoice verification; no physical fiscal stamp is required.

Unverified or fraudulent zero-rating claims trigger retrospective VAT clawback of refunded input VAT, re-assessment of output VAT at the standard rate, late-payment surcharge of 0.05% per day from the original filing date, and administrative penalties of 50% to 5 times the tax involved under the Tax Administration Law of the People's Republic of China. Where zero-rating or export VAT rebate is obtained through false or fictitious documentation, criminal liability for fraudulent export tax refund may arise under the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, exposing responsible persons to imprisonment and fines, and the taxpayer may be placed on the tax non-compliant blacklist and disqualified from future export VAT rebate privileges.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Hong Kong operates a fully convertible currency regime under the Linked Exchange Rate System with the United States Dollar at a band of 7.75-7.85. There are no surrender requirements, no current account restrictions, and no capital account controls within the HK jurisdiction. The Hong Kong dollar (HKD) and the offshore renminbi (CNH) are freely exchangeable at market-determined rates. However, the sovereign nexus to the People's Republic of China imposes significant convertibility frictions at the cross-border capital layer. Chinese resident shareholders of the JV must obtain SAFE registration for any outward direct investment (ODI) and must undergo a commercial authenticity check before remitting equity or debt funds to the Hong Kong entity. On the distribution leg, dividend remittances from the HK JV to the mainland corporate parent are permitted under the current account only after the mainland parent has presented a board resolution, audited financial statements, and the Hong Kong profits tax certificate, and the transaction is subject to a 10% withholding income tax under the China-Hong Kong double taxation arrangement. For service trade payments such as success fees sourced from mainland clients, the JV may receive RMB via CIPS, but every receipt must be supported by a service trade contract and a foreign-exchange filing. The capital account convertibility of the RMB is therefore restricted, while the HKD is fully convertible, resulting in a dual-track convertibility posture that must be navigated with treasury discipline.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the PRC SAFE Good Trade Regulation (Circular 2013-58 and its successors), any export goods' proceeds declared in the mainland customs system must be repatriated into a domestic registered bank account before the contractual payment maturity. Specifically, the exporter must collect the full invoice evidence within 30 days of the date of shipment unless a contract condition allows longer credit; a deferred payment term exceeding 90 days requires pre-registration with the SAFE observation system. There is no mandatory conversion ratio of exported foreign exchange into CNY; the entity may hold 100% of the foreign currency in its bank account, or convert fully at the interbank spot rate. However, for service trade exports (the JV's M&A advisory would be classified as 'services trade'), the surrender requirements are materially relaxed; proceeds are treated as current account items, and the recipient may directly receive the HKD/USD into the HK JV's account without SAFE conversion as long as the income is adequately documented and reported under China's country-level balance of payments statistics.

Central bank supervision: Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) as the de facto central banking authority for Hong Kong SAR; concurrently, the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the People's Bank of China (PBOC) governs the cross-border FX posture of the mainland Chinese investors. The bifurcated regulatory framework requires dual-tracking for any RMB conversion or equity capital movement.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound equity capital from mainland Chinese shareholders must be routed through a SAFE-registered ODI channel. The designated bank on the mainland side will issue an 'ODI Emission Letter' and the wire originates as an MT103 from the mainland shareholder's bank, with the purpose code '621021 - Outward Direct Investment – Equity Capital' to the JV's Escrow Account at BOCHK. For the HKD/CNH legs, the remittance should clear via the Hong Kong Interbank Clearing Limited RTGS system; for USD legs, use SWIFT. The bank requires a WAAD (Wire Against Authorized Documentary) dossier comprising the SAFE Capital Item Filing, the ODI certificate from the Ministry of Commerce of the PRC, a notarized capital call letter from the JV board, and a CPA's capital verification report within 60 days of receipt.Primary clearing rail is SWIFT MT103 for service success fees and dividend distributions. For any outbound payment from the HK JV to a mainland beneficiary in RMB, the payment must be queued through the CIPS system and include a service trade certificate (Form SF-VT) and an invoice; the bank will authenticate the underlying advisory contract. For goods-related disbursements (if the M&A advisory includes a trade-asset component), a Commercial Letter of Credit (LC) can be opened in the name of the JV as the applicant, using the UCP600 rules; documentary collections (CAD) are acceptable for trade payments against Bills of Lading. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

16.5%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% under Hong Kong domestic law; dividends paid by a Hong Kong company to non-resident shareholders are not subject to Hong Kong withholding tax and are exempt from Hong Kong profits tax. A Mainland China shareholder is separately subject to PRC Corporate Income Tax on the dividend receipt at 25%, subject to foreign tax credit and the Mainland-Hong Kong Double Tax Arrangement.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The BIR51 must be filed within one month after the date of issue by the IRD. Under the block extension for corporations with a financial year ending on 31 December, an authorized tax representative may obtain an extension to 15 May of the following year for paper filing or 15 August of the following year for eTAX filing. Missing the applicable deadline triggers automatic penalties and daily surcharges.
