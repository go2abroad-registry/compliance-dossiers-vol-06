None. Hong Kong does not impose a minimum paid-up capital on a private company limited by shares, and there is no blocked escrow capital account requirement at incorporation. Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA) via the STA Electronic Tax Bureau e-tax portal; for Hong Kong SAR investor or parent identity matters, the Inland Revenue Department of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region via the eTax portal.

E-government filing portal

For the Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture (CN_JV_LTD) in mainland China: (1) obtain the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) on the business license issued by the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR); (2) complete mandatory tax registration with the local competent tax authority within 30 days of business license issuance through the STA Electronic Tax Bureau; (3) file tax registration particulars including legal representative identity, articles of association, accounting policies, capital structure, bank account details, and the foreign investment filing record for the equity joint venture; (4) receive the PRC tax identification number (USCC) and register VAT taxpayer status as small-scale or general VAT taxpayer; (5) apply for Digital Electronic Invoice issuing capability or tax-control invoice equipment; (6) for the Hong Kong foreign investor/non-resident entity, procure the Hong Kong Business Registration Certificate (BRC) and Business Registration number from the Hong Kong Inland Revenue Department solely to evidence the investor's tax identity; the PRC-resident EJV must not use the Hong Kong registration number for PRC tax filings.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

6%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mainland China: mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration when annual assessable turnover exceeds CNY 5,000,000 (RMB 5 million); below that threshold, small-scale taxpayer status is available. Non-resident digital or remote service suppliers have no registration threshold and must register for PRC VAT when supplying services into mainland China. Hong Kong SAR: no indirect tax registration threshold because no VAT/GST applies.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For PRC VAT purposes, zero-rating or export refund eligibility for goods requires physical export from the PRC customs territory under a customs export declaration and receipt of foreign exchange from the overseas buyer. For cross-border services, including App Store Optimization (ASO), multilingual SEO localization and related digital agency services, zero-rating is available where the services are provided to an overseas enterprise or individual, are wholly performed and consumed outside mainland China, are not used or consumed in China, payment is received in foreign currency from outside China, and the taxpayer retains contemporaneous contracts, performance evidence and bank receipts. Pure domestic services supplied to Chinese customers, or services consumed in China, are taxable at 6% VAT as standard services.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Export Goods Declaration / Bill of Entry)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (or equivalent transport document)
  • Commercial invoice and packing list for exported goods
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or bank foreign exchange receipt confirmation confirming overseas payment repatriation
  • Foreign exchange collection verification record from the bank (SWIFT credit advice or equivalent)
  • Export contract or international service agreement naming the overseas recipient
  • Registration Form for Service Trade and Other Foreign Exchange Transactions (for cross-border services)
  • Digital or electronic VAT invoice or export invoice issued under STA requirements with export mark

Mainland China: the STA Golden Tax Phase IV system requires VAT invoices to be issued through authorized e-invoicing or tax-control software; the Digital Electronic Invoice (DEI) pilot mandates real-time API transmission of invoice metadata to the STA, with automatic anti-fraud digital stamping (tax-control signature) and online clearance before an invoice is valid for tax credit. Hong Kong SAR: no e-invoicing or fiscal clearance mandate.

The STA and SAFE may jointly inspect export zero-rating substantiation. Failure to repatriate export proceeds within 180 days, or submission of incomplete or false customs or foreign exchange documentation, results in denial of the 0% rate, reversal of claimed output VAT zero-rating, clawback of any VAT input credit previously claimed, and imposition of late-payment interest. Under the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Tax Collection, fraudulent export zero-rating or refund claims attract administrative penalties ranging from 50 percent to 500 percent of the evaded or underpaid tax; fraudulent export refunds specifically attract penalties of 100 percent to 500 percent of the amount fraudulently obtained, plus revocation of export refund qualification. Repeated or criminal conduct may be referred for criminal liability under the Criminal Law of the PRC for export tax refund fraud and VAT evasion.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Mainland China applies asymmetric convertibility: current-account items are convertible after authenticity review, while capital-account items require SAFE registration and purpose-locked account handling. The JV can freely convert RMB and foreign currency for permissible ASO/SEO localization service transactions, including client collections and vendor payments, provided the bank obtains the contract, invoice, tax voucher and deliverables evidence. Equity capital injected by the Hong Kong shareholder must flow through a SAFE-registered FDI foreign exchange capital account and can only be converted/used on a payment-after-verification basis. Dividend and profit remittance to Hong Kong is not blocked but is administratively heavy: the bank requires audited statutory accounts, a board dividend resolution, corporate income tax completion/withholding certificates, and a SAFE foreign-exchange payment filing, after which CNY is converted at the onshore rate. The HK side has no conversion limits, no repatriation quota and full account discretion under HKMA's linked rate system. The principal practical hurdle is maintaining the complete documentary chain for cross-border digital service revenues and preventing any co-mingling of capital-account and current-account proceeds.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE current-account administration, export/service receipts must be repatriated to a domestic designated foreign-exchange settlement account within 180 days of the underlying export declaration or service invoice date; overdue items trigger bank enquiry and possible inclusion in SAFE's pending-receipt monitoring. There is no statutory mandatory sale into RMB in normal circumstances: the applicable mandatory conversion ratio is 0% and the JV may retain 100% of its export/service foreign-currency receipts in a designated current-account foreign-currency account, or voluntarily sell any amount at the onshore rate. Hong Kong imposes no surrender rules on the treasury entity.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) and its delegated State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) govern the onshore Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture; the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) is the currency-board and banking regulator for the Hong Kong SAR treasury hub.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Payoneer Fit: High

Fintech EMIs such as Wise_B2B and Payoneer cannot act as the primary SAFE settlement bank for the mainland JV because they do not hold a Chinese commercial-bank or licensed payments-institution authorization to receive and verify statutory foreign-exchange capital inflows or to issue purpose-coded FX declarations. They are legally viable in Hong Kong as registered Money Service Operators and may support collection/disbursement workflows for the HK treasury entity, but the JV cannot rely on them for FDI capital verification, profit remittance filing or conversion into RMB. Viability rating: Low for principal clearing, Medium as an ancillary current-account aggregation layer for small-value foreign vendor payments, High only for the offshore HK disbursement hub, provided funds are reconciled back into a tier-1 bank account and all underlying service contracts are retained.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The Hong Kong shareholder must complete SAFE FDI registration and file the joint-venture capital schedule with the receiving bank before any injection. The inbound wire is sent by SWIFT MT103/MT202 from the HK treasury account to the JV's designated FDI foreign-exchange capital account with the remittance purpose clearly stated as 'equity investment / FDI capital injection' and referenced to the SAFE FDI registration code. The receiving tier-1 bank matches the incoming foreign currency against the FDI registration, credits the capital-account foreign-exchange ledger, and permits RMB conversion only after the JV presents a payment-after-verification request supported by the approved purpose, underlying expenditure or current-account obligation. An independent Chinese CPA then issues a capital contribution verification report, completing the statutory capital verification; after verification, the funds may be used only for the registered business purposes and may not be transferred to the HK parent without an approved dividend/tax process.
