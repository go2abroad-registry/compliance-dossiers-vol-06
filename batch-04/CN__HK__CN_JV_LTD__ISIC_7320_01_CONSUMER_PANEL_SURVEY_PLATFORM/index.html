VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Because the JV is a PRC domestic legal person, VAT registration is mandatory upon business license issuance with no economic turnover threshold for basic VAT registration. The relevant turnover threshold is the general VAT taxpayer threshold: annual taxable sales exceeding RMB 5,000,000 make general taxpayer status mandatory; below that threshold the entity may remain a small-scale taxpayer, subject to the statutory collection rate of 3% with applicable temporary reductions and the small-scale monthly VAT exemption of RMB 100,000, or RMB 300,000 per quarter, where statutory conditions are satisfied.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For goods exported from the PRC, the 0% VAT rate and export tax rebate are available where the exporter is a registered VAT general taxpayer, the goods are physically exported under customs supervision, the export declaration is made in the exporter's own name, the sale is made to a non-resident buyer with foreign-currency settlement, and export proceeds are received and repatriated in accordance with State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) rules. For cross-border services, zero-rating is limited to specified statutory catalogue services, including offshore software, information system, and business-process-management services, supplied to overseas recipients and consumed wholly outside the PRC. The consumer panel survey market-research SaaS platform may qualify if the payment originates from an offshore client, the service deliverables are used exclusively outside the PRC, and the arrangement is not characterized as domestic market-research or consultancy; services outside the zero-rated catalogue may instead be VAT-exempt without input recovery or subject to 6% VAT.

  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing export departure
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or bank foreign-exchange receipt voucher

All VAT invoices must be issued through the STA-approved electronic invoice infrastructure. General taxpayers are required to use Digital E-Invoices and fully digitalized electronic invoices issued through the nationwide Electronic Invoice Service Platform; invoice data files use OFD and XML formats and are transmitted to the tax authority in near-real time at issuance. Each invoice carries a unique 20-digit invoice number and an anti-counterfeiting dynamic QR code. Special VAT Invoices used for input-credit claims must match the buyer's Unified Social Credit Code and be recorded in the tax authority's invoice reconciliation system. No third-party physical stamping is required, but the STA digital seal, invoice quota control, and real-time reporting remains mandatory. API or batch-file integration with the electronic invoice platform is required for high-volume B2B invoicing.

Tax and customs authorities require retention of the full export documentation bundle, electronic invoice files, and remittance records for at least 10 years. Failure to substantiate zero-rating, or failure to repatriate export proceeds within the 180-day deadline, invalidates the 0% rate and triggers VAT recomputation with late-payment surcharge of 0.05% per day and penalties under the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection. Fraudulent or unverified export-rebate claims are subject to recovery of the refunded tax plus a fine of up to five times the tax involved; serious cases constitute the criminal offence of defrauding export tax rebates under the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China. Customs administrative penalties, suspension or loss of export-tax-rebate qualification, tax-credit downgrade, and restricted access to digital invoice issuance may also be imposed.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Hong Kong applies full current-account and capital-account convertibility under the linked exchange rate system. The HKMA currency board guarantees convertibility of the Hong Kong dollar within the 7.7500 to 7.8500 per US dollar zone, and there is no surrender requirement, no foreign-exchange approval, and no profit-repatriation restriction for a Hong Kong-incorporated treasury vehicle. Dividends, management fees, service fees, and intercompany settlements can be wired out of Hong Kong freely on presentation of a signed board resolution and supporting transactional documents. The controls that materially affect this blueprint are the mainland Chinese rules administered by SAFE when the ultimate business is a Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture. For the PRC operating entity, current-account RMB payments require an underlying genuine trade or service contract and must be processed through designated banks with verification of authenticity and conformity; capital-account inflows to the JV require an SAFE foreign investment registration before funds can be credited. If profit after tax is to be remitted to the Chinese shareholder or foreign investors, the JV must file for dividend remittance with its onshore bank, and the bank will require audited financial statements, a corporate income tax settlement certificate, a joint-venture profit-distribution resolution, and evidence that the remittance amount is within undistributed profits. From the Hong Kong treasury perspective, the HKD/USD and offshore CNH markets are fully liquid, while onshore CNY hedging and settlement requires bank registration and documentary evidence of the underlying commercial flows.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Hong Kong has no statutory export-proceeds surrender or mandatory conversion ratio; all export receipts may be retained in any eligible currency and repatriation occurs on normal settlement cycles. Where the mainland counterpart of the structured entity exports goods, SAFE's current-account regulations require export foreign-exchange proceeds from overseas buyers to be repatriated and credited to the onshore company's bank account within 90 days of the customs export declaration. Since the 2012 deregulation there is no prescribed mandatory conversion percentage; an onshore exporter may retain up to 100% of export proceeds in foreign currency or convert as it wishes, subject to bank-observed matching against the customs trade-monitoring system and the transaction's declared purpose code.

Central bank supervision: Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) as the de facto central bank, currency board, and prudential supervisor; the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the People's Bank of China (PBOC) is the applicable authority for CNY capital and trade flows linked to mainland China.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

PayPal Fit: High

In Hong Kong, non-bank fintech players such as Wise and PayPal can operate lawfully as Money Service Operators under the Money Service Operators Ordinance and, for stored value, under the HKMA's Stored Value Facility licensing regime; they are permitted to provide remittance and disbursement services to Hong Kong corporate entities. However, they are not authorized deposit-taking institutions and cannot provide the regulated capital escrow, trade-finance, FX-forward, or documentary-collection rails required for this multinational treasury structure. They also do not satisfy the bank-equivalent beneficiary and wire-purpose-code verification standards of onshore mainland Chinese regulators for FDI or JV dividend remittances. For the consumer-panel platform's operational mass payouts to panelists, Wise and PayPal Mass Payout can be integrated as settlement sub-rails subject to the agreeing bank's delegated-third-party due diligence and the platform's transaction-monitoring obligations; but they cannot be used for the statutory FDI foreign-exchange registration or as the sole banking layer. Overall viability is Medium for operational payment liquidity and Low for the treasury-capital and FX-compliance layer.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

For the Hong Kong layer, capital injection should be executed as an inbound SWIFT MT103 from the shareholder's bank to the designated tier-1 corporate account; the message must carry the full legal name and account number of the recipient, the purpose code classified under Capital Investment or Equity Contribution in the bank's FX purpose-code table, and a payment reference string corresponding to the board-approved capital injection resolution. The HK bank will independently verify the source of funds; no government capital authorization is required in Hong Kong. If the same group seeks to move funding into the mainland Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture, the flow must first be registered with SAFE under the foreign-investment reporting regime; the onshore JV should hold a SAFE-registered capital account. The remittance is documented with the FDI registration information, the business incorporation certificate of the JV, and the shareholders' capital call; after receipt the domestic receiving bank completes the capital verification and the JV's statutory audit trail records the paid-in capital as foreign currency or RMB converted at the bank's published exchange rate.For settlement with overseas suppliers, licensors, and panelists, the primary rail is SWIFT MT103 for direct funds transfers, with a purpose code such as Services or Market Research and Polling Services. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File is mandatory when annual cross-border related-party transactions exceed RMB 200 million for tangible-property transfers, RMB 100 million for intangible-property transfers or financial-asset transfers, or RMB 40 million for all other related-party transactions. Master File is mandatory when the annual aggregate related-party turnover of the group reaches RMB 1 billion.

Economic substance scope: The PRC entity is assessed under substance-over-form rules: Article 47 of the PRC Enterprise Income Tax Law and SAT Announcement 2017 No. 71 authorize the tax authorities to disregard schemes whose sole purpose is tax avoidance. Economic Substance Regulations as such apply where the Hong Kong element is a special-territory company; for treaty relief under Mainland/HK DTA, the Hong Kong shareholder must demonstrate Core Income-Generating Activities (CIGA) in Hong Kong, meaning board meetings, strategic decisions, contract negotiation, management, risk absorption, and operational staff located there. A pure mailbox Hong Kong company that records invoicing but carries out zero substantive activity will not be treated as beneficial owner, and the reduced 5% or 7% treaty rate will be denied.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and False Deductions

Statutory sanction: Article 63 of the PRC Tax Administration Law: recovery of underpaid tax plus daily late interest at 0.05% per day and a fine of 50% to 5 times the tax shortfall. Criminal tax evasion under Article 201 of the PRC Criminal Law can lead to fines and imprisonment of up to 7 years for serious fraud.

Director personal liability: Where the corporate veil is abused, the PRC courts pierce the limited liability of the joint venture and hold directors personally liable for the unpaid tax and civil restitution from their own assets. The legal representative and CFO may be placed on a travel-ban/blacklist by the tax authorities, have passports cancelled, and face criminal prosecution with custodial sentences for signing false tax returns.
Shell Company and Treaty Abuse

Statutory sanction: SAT Notice 2018 No. 9 denies beneficial-ownership status, so the 10% statutory WHT applies to the full dividend; the difference in tax is collected with interest and late-payment penalties. The arrangement may be disregarded under the PRC general anti-abuse rule, and interposed invoices may be disallowed with 50%-to-5x penalties.

Director personal liability: Directors who signed false residency declarations or controlled the Hong Kong shell will face civil joint and several liability for the avoided withholding tax. Travel restrictions and asset freezes are imposed under tax collection enforcement powers. If forged certificates or fabricated corporate documents are used to claim treaty benefits, criminal liability arises under Articles 201 and 280 of the PRC Criminal Law, with imprisonment of up to 7 years.
Transfer Pricing Non-Disclosure and Documentation Failure

Statutory sanction: The State Taxation Administration may make a recharacterization under Articles 41 and 48 of the PRC Enterprise Income Tax Law, adjust taxable income to an arm's length result, impose a 50%-to-5x penalty on the underpaid tax under Article 63 of the Tax Administration Law, and levy interest at the benchmark loan rate plus up to 5 percentage points for special tax adjustments.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers who knowingly approved false related-party reporting or omitted a statutory TP disclosure are personally liable for civil damages to the joint venture and for the tax shortfall if the entity is unable to pay. The competent tax authority may blacklist the responsible director, issue an exit-control order, and refer the case to criminal authorities if the understatement is fraudulent, leading to imprisonment and personal fines.
