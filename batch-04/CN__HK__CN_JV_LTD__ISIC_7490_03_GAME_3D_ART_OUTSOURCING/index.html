Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

    

    

      
Tax ID Application Infrastructure

      

        
Competent tax authority
State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China via the national e-tax portals and official STA website www.chinatax.gov.cn; Hong Kong SAR: Inland Revenue Department (IRD) via the eTax platform at www.ird.gov.hk.

        
E-government filing portal
For the PRC Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture: after incorporation with the State Administration for Market Regulation, the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) printed on the business license is automatically used as the enterprise Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN); the JV must complete supplementary tax registration with the competent STA tax bureau by filing legal representative data, registered address, bank account details, accounting system and tax-filing basis through the e-tax portal, and must register as a general VAT taxpayer where cumulative taxable turnover exceeds RMB 5,000,000 in any continuous 12-month period, or voluntarily, in order to issue export-valid VAT special invoices and access zero-rating/refund. For the Hong Kong parent as a foreign non-resident investor: shareholding alone does not confer a PRC TIN; if the non-resident has a taxable establishment in China, it must register for tax and obtain a TIN under the non-resident tax registration measures, otherwise withholding agents report and remit China-source tax. The Hong Kong entity separately obtains a Business Registration Certificate from the IRD Business Registration Office under the Business Registration Ordinance within one month of commencement of business; the Business Registration Number is its Hong Kong tax identifier.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

      

        
Default tax classification
Entity_Level_Taxation

        
Available election forms
None. The PRC JV is compulsorily taxed as a resident enterprise and there is no US-style check-the-box election under PRC law; a small-scale VAT taxpayer may voluntarily elect general taxpayer status if accounting and input-credit conditions are met. Hong Kong applies territorial source-based profits tax and has no elective entity classification regime.

      

    

    

      
VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

      

        
Standard VAT / GST rate
6%

        
Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)
All entities must complete tax registration upon commencement by obtaining the Unified Social Credit Code; mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration is triggered when cumulative taxable turnover excluding VAT exceeds RMB 5,000,000 in any continuous 12-month period. General taxpayer status is required to issue VAT special invoices and to claim zero-rated export service refunds.

      

      
      

        Export 0% zero-rating conditions
        
A 0% VAT rate may be claimed by the PRC JV for qualifying cross-border supplies of 3D game-art sculpting and character-rigging services classified as design or software-related services supplied to an overseas recipient, provided the service is received and consumed entirely outside China, the contract clearly evidences the overseas place of performance, ownership of the deliverable IP and the non-resident customer, the consideration is collected through the designated foreign-exchange channel, and the service is separately accounted for in the VAT return with proper input-VAT allocation. For tangible goods exports, zero-rating requires lawful customs export declaration, physical export out of China through a customs-supervised channel, and proof of shipment and payment.

VAT special invoices and fully digitalized electronic Fapiao must be issued through the STA National Electronic Invoice Service Platform, with issuance data transmitted to the tax authority in real time. Golden Tax Phase IV enforces invoice code and number control, a statutory tax-control code on each digital Fapiao, and anti-fraud matching against bank receipts, customs export declaration data and VAT returns. Taxpayers must use the e-tax bureau interface/API to file invoice data and VAT returns; invoices not cleared by the platform are invalid for input VAT credit or export zero-rating.

      
The STA and Customs Administration perform automated matching of VAT refund claims, invoice data, customs export data, logistics documents and foreign-exchange collection records. Any missing, inconsistent or false document will deny zero-rating and the refund will be clawed back with a late-payment surcharge of 0.05% per day. Fraudulent export refund claims are penalized under the Law on Administration of Tax Collection by recovery of the refunded VAT plus a monetary fine of one to five times the tax evaded; serious cases are referred for criminal prosecution for fraudulent tax refund, carrying imprisonment up to life. In Hong Kong SAR there is no export VAT clawback, but false statements to the IRD can trigger penalties under the Inland Revenue Ordinance, additional tax of up to three times the amount under-assessed and imprisonment.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

      

        
Current-account convertibility
The mainland RMB is accepted on the current account for trade in goods and services under Article VIII of the IMF Articles of Agreement, but convertibility does not mean unrestricted settlement. For a Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture exporting 3D game art services, every current-account receipt and payment is subject to the 

        
Export proceeds surrender rules

      

      
Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the People's Bank of China (PBOC) for mainland China foreign exchange controls; the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) as monetary authority and bank supervisor for the Hong Kong clearing satellite.

    

    

      
Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

      

        
        

          

            PayPal
            Fit: High
          

          
Onshore legal viability: nil. Mainland China does not permit offshore digital EMIs such as Wise, Airwallex, Currenxie, or Revolut to open local corporate current accounts because they do not participate in the PBOC/CBIRC clearing system and do not hold a domestic commercial banking licence. They cannot act as the JV's primary clearing bank, cannot perform SAFE export-import verification, and cannot issue the capital verification report needed for FDI. PayPal is not an EMI but remains only an auxiliary collection rail; it must be contractually mapped to invoices and be settled through a licensed domestic bank. The Hong Kong treasury satellite may use a regulated HKMA-authorised EMI or licensed virtual bank for low-value collections and operating expenses, but all material FDI, export-import settlement, and dividend remittances must be executed through a Hong Kong licensed bank or a SAFE-registered onshore bank.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

      
      

        Cross-border equity injection from the Hong Kong shareholder is considered round-tripping risk or unverified source-of-wealth, particularly if the HK parent has thin operating substance and the ultimate beneficial owner is opaque.
        
Root cause: Cross-border equity injection from the Hong Kong shareholder is considered round-tripping risk or unverified source-of-wealth, particularly if the HK parent has thin operating substance and the ultimate beneficial owner is opaque.

        
Defense evidence: Notarised Hong Kong Certificate of Incorporation and Certificate of Good Standing; audited financial statements of the Hong Kong shareholder for the last three years; banker's reference letter from its HKMA-licensed bank; register of members and ultimate beneficial owner register; individual source-of-wealth reports for all natural-person shareholders; board resolution approving the investment; SAFE FDI registration evidence; Chinese counsel's legal opinion on the ownership chain and tax residency.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

      

        Inbound capital from the Hong Kong parent must be remitted to the JV's dedicated FDI capital account at a SAFE-licensed onshore bank. The remitter should use SWIFT MT103 with beneficiary bank details in the JV's name and cite in field 70 a clear reference to 'CN_JV_LTD equity injection' together with the approved FDI registration certificate reference. The remittance purpose code must be classified under SAFE's Balance of Payments category as Foreign Direct Investment - Equity Contribution, commonly captured under bank-specific FDI transaction code 621011 or the bank's current SAFE-approved direct investment capital code. Within the time limit set by the bank after receipt, the JV must complete the capital account registration, produce the SAFE FDI information update, and obtain a capital verification report from a licensed CPA. The funds may then be converted into RMB under the bank's domestic capital account management rules, but they cannot be used for prohibited purposes such as securities investment, placement of deposits, repayment of personal debt, or expenditures outside the registered business scope. Every RMB payment from the capital account must be supported by contracts, invoices, or a board-approved use-of-funds resolution.For supplier, freelancer, and software licensing payments, the primary clearing rail is SWIFT MT103 with the beneficiary bank details, invoice number, contract reference, and service-trade purpose code in the remittance narrative. The onshore bank must hold a full service-trade payment dossier containing the underlying contract, invoice, milestone acceptance, tax withholding certificate where applicable, and the SAFE service-trade transaction code. Commercial letters of credit and documentary collections are generally unsuitable for pure 3D digital asset outsourcing because there is no physical shipment; they should be reserved for high-value hardware deliveries, software escrow releases, or a strategic publisher's request for an LC with an electronic acceptance clause. PayPal may be used only for low-value auxiliary collections by the Hong Kong satellite or as a sub-threshold customer preference; it cannot replace the bank-cleared MT103 rail for material outbound disbursements and must be reconciled daily to avoid SAFE reporting gaps. All outbound settlements should remain fully traceable from contract to invoice to bank statement.Onshore deliverable USD/CNY forward contracts with a SAFE-licensed commercial bank, based on authentic export receivables and with a tenor not exceeding 12 months; speculative positions are prohibited.Offshore non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) in the Hong Kong treasury satellite to hedge CNH/USD or HKD/USD exposure arising from the same project cash flows, provided the onshore JV does not back-to-back its onshore exposure into the offshore NDF contract.Currency swaps in USD/CNY or HKD/CNY executed through the relationship bank to align FDI capital inflow and dividend outflow timing, supported by board approval and the underlying SAFE capital-account documentation.

              Business Registration Certificate Renewal (Business Registration Fee plus Protection of Wages on Insolvency Fund Levy)
              Annually on or before the expiry of the one-year Business Registration Certificate; IRD issues a renewal demand note and payment must be made within one month after the expiry date to avoid penalties. The certificate is renewable every 12 months.
              $294.87 USD
              If renewal is made after the expiry date but within one month: extra late fee of HK$300. If not renewed within one month, liability to a fine of up to HK$5,000 and, for a continuing offence, HK$500 per day under Cap. 310. Failure to display the certificate may also incur a HK$1,000 fine.
            

            
            

              Profits Tax Return (Form BIR51) together with Audited Financial Statements and Tax Computation
              Within 1 month from the date of issue of the return (IRD ordinarily issues on 1 April; an electronic filing extension automatically grants an additional 2 months, making the usual e-filing deadline 3 months from issue, typically 31 July).
              $0 USD
              For failure without reasonable excuse, the company is liable to a fine of up to HK$10,000 under Cap. 112 s.82. Where tax has been understated, the court may order an additional penalty of up to three times the tax undercharged. Persistent non-filing may also trigger estimated assessments.
            

            
            

              Employer's Return of Remuneration and Pensions (BIR56A) and IR56B Information Forms
              Within 1 month of issue; if issued on 1 April (the standard annual date), the due date is 1 May for the preceding year of assessment. For electronic filing, the IRD may allow extensions.
              $0 USD
              Late filing or non-filing renders the employer liable to a fine of up to HK$10,000; if there is an under-assessment of tax, an additional three times the tax undercharged may be imposed. Failing to provide receipts, notices or returns is punishable under Cap. 112.
            

            
            

              Significant Controllers Register (SCR) Update and Inspection Readiness
              Continuous obligation; any change in significant controllers (e.g., shareholding or control shifts of 25% or more) must be recorded in the SCR within 7 days of the company becoming aware of the change. The annual return (NAR1) is the regular annual confirmation of shareholding details.
              $0 USD
              Failures under Cap. 622 Sch. 5D: fine of up to HK$25,000 and continuing daily default fine of HK$2,500; materially false statements knowingly made: fine of up to HK$150,000 and up to 2 years imprisonment.
            

            
            

              Annual Members/Directors Meeting Minutes and Statutory Register Audit
              Annually, concurrently with the preparation of the financial statements and directors report; minute books must be signed and maintained (no fixed statutory deadline for private companies, but good practice is within the accounting period).
              $0 USD
              Deficiency in registers/minutes may be cited in prosecution; failure to keep minutes of directors meetings under Cap. 622 s.620 is an offence attracting a fine of up to HK$20,000; failings in accounting records may be a daily default fine and can also obstruct the audit exemption status.

          Mandatory Audit Threshold
          
            Mandatory Audit for All Entities
          
        

        
Turnover: A mandatory independent audit is required for all Hong Kong incorporated companies except (i) dormant private companies under Cap. 622 and (ii) small private companies or small private company groups that validly pass an audit-exemption resolution. A small private company qualifies if it meets at least two of the following three thresholds in a financial year: (a) total annual revenue not exceeding HK$20 million; (b) total assets not exceeding HK$10 million; (c) average number of employees not exceeding 100. A small private company group qualifies if it meets at least two of: (a) aggregate annual revenue not exceeding HK$40 million; (b) aggregate total assets not exceeding HK$20 million; (c) aggregate average employees not exceeding 100. Any company that does not qualify, does not pass the exemption resolution, is a public company, a listed company, a financial services/licensed company, or whose shareholders require an audit, must appoint a certified public accountant (practising) to audit its financial statements under Part 9 of Cap. 622 and the Professional Accountants Ordinance (Cap. 50). The Inland Revenue Department also requires reliable and/or audited accounts in support of the Profits Tax Return.

Box-by-Box Return Breakdown (non-resident focus)

      

        

          

            BoxField
            Non-resident guidanceTax-evasion pitfall
          

          
            
            

              Box 1 (Line 1)
              Operating revenue: aggregate invoiced income from 3D art outsourcing, sculpting, rigging, and character design services provided to global game publishers.
              Report all revenue earned from non-resident clients at the spot exchange rate on the invoice date; do not net bank transfer charges; reconcile inflows with contracts and transfer pricing documentation. Cross-border services are taxable in China if the value is created in mainland China; if services are performed through a foreign PE, exclude that PE profit only if treaty-recognized and properly supported.
              Late filing attracts a penalty of RMB 2,000 per return (or RMB 2,000 to RMB 10,000 for serious cases) under Article 62 of the Tax Collection Administration Law, plus a daily late-payment surcharge of 0.05% on unpaid tax. Under Article 63, false returns or concealment causing underpayment attract recovery of unpaid tax, daily surcharge, and a civil penalty of 50% to 500% of the evaded tax. Under PRC Criminal Law Article 201, large-scale evasion may lead to imprisonment of up to three years, and massive evasion to three to seven years, with fines and confiscation; responsible directors may also face joint liability.
