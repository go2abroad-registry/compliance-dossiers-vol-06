Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA) and its national unified Electronic Tax Bureau (ETAX) internet portal at etax.chinatax.gov.cn; provincial and municipal STA offices administer registration and audits. The Hong Kong Inland Revenue Department remains the separate competent tax authority for the Hong Kong SAR shareholder for profits tax and income matters in Hong Kong.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: The Administration for Market Regulation issues the business license for the Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture and generates the Unified Social Credit Code, which constitutes the taxpayer identification number for enterprise income tax and VAT. Step 2: The market regulator transmits the establishment record to the STA Electronic Tax Bureau; the JV must complete supplementary tax registration, including real-name authentication of the legal representative and financial officer, within 30 days of establishment. Step 3: The JV determines its VAT taxpayer identity as a general taxpayer or small-scale taxpayer and obtains digital electronic invoice allocation through the Electronic Tax Bureau. Step 4: A foreign non-resident enterprise without a permanent establishment that derives China-source income must separately complete non-resident tax registration through its withholding agent at the competent STA office and is assigned a non-resident taxpayer code distinct from the JV Unified Social Credit Code. Step 5: The JV opens a PRC bank account, reports the account to the STA, and registers in the State Administration of Foreign Exchange system for export proceeds collection or cross-border service fee receipt if it will export goods or services.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation; the Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture is a PRC resident enterprise taxed on worldwide income at the statutory 25% enterprise income tax rate unless a reduced rate applies. The Hong Kong shareholder is a non-resident enterprise for PRC tax purposes, taxed only on China-source income and potentially eligible for relief under the Mainland and Hong Kong tax arrangement. The study abroad and immigration visa advisory activity is a VAT-taxable consulting service in the PRC at the applicable 6% service VAT rate, with entity-level taxation rather than pass-through treatment.

Available election forms

None; the PRC does not operate a check-the-box or elective entity classification regime equivalent to the United States, and hybrid mismatch outcomes are addressed through the special tax adjustment and general anti-avoidance rules rather than by taxpayer election. The JV may, however, apply for advance pricing arrangements or adopt enterprise income tax preferences only to the extent expressly provided by statute and approved by the STA.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory VAT registration applies to all PRC-registered businesses; the mandatory general VAT taxpayer threshold is annual taxable turnover exceeding RMB 5,000,000. Below that threshold the JV may remain a small-scale VAT taxpayer at the simplified 3% collection rate, with temporary legislative reductions where applicable, but a small-scale taxpayer cannot claim input VAT credits or export VAT rebates. The JV should register as a general taxpayer to claim effective 0% export zero-rating and cross-border service refunds.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating under PRC VAT is available for goods that are declared to Chinese customs, physically exported from the Chinese customs territory, and supported by bank-collected export proceeds in accordance with State Administration of Foreign Exchange regulations. For services, only specifically listed cross-border taxable services such as international transportation, offshore research and development, software, information technology, and design services consumed wholly outside the PRC qualify for the 0% rate. A study abroad and immigration visa advisory service normally qualifies for the cross-border VAT exemption, or for 0% where it is bundled in an approved offshore digital service package, provided the recipient is a non-resident, the service is not performed for PRC domestic consumers, and payment is received through permissible escrow or wire-transfer rails in foreign exchange.

  • China Customs Export Goods Declaration (electronic customs export declaration with customs export number)
  • Bill of Lading, air waybill, or multimodal transport document evidencing physical exit of the goods from the PRC customs territory
  • Export sales invoice and the procurement VAT special invoice chain substantiating input tax for export rebate computation
  • Bank-issued export proceeds collection and remittance verification record in the State Administration of Foreign Exchange system, being the PRC functional equivalent of an Inward Remittance Certificate
  • Signed export contract or cross-border service agreement, plus electronic service consumption evidence such as IP address, user location, and delivery records for qualifying offshore digital services

China operates a nationwide fully digitalized electronic invoice mandate under the Golden Tax Phase IV anti-fraud architecture. Newly registered taxpayers issue invoices through the STA National Electronic Invoice Service Platform inside the Electronic Tax Bureau; each invoice carries a unique digital invoice number and tax-control code, with structured data transmitted in real time to the STA. B2B transactions above platform thresholds require buyer acceptance confirmation, and the platform exposes open API interfaces for ERP integration, red-flush reversals, and reconciliation with customs and foreign-exchange data. Physical pre-printed invoices are not accepted for general VAT taxpayers, and the risk-control engine automatically flags mismatched customs declarations, missing foreign-exchange records, or abnormal zero-rating claims.

Fraudulent or unverified export zero-rating claims are prosecuted as export tax refund fraud. The STA and customs audit the full documentary chain; where supporting documents are missing, inconsistent, or forged, the claimed VAT rebate or zero-rate exemption is retroactively clawed back with statutory late-payment surcharge of 0.05% per day from the due date, plus an administrative penalty of 50% to five times the amount of tax involved, and potential disqualification from simplified export refund formalities. Criminal referral may follow under the PRC Criminal Law for fraudulent export tax refunds and false issuance of VAT special invoices, exposing responsible individuals to imprisonment and fines. Retrospective clawback extends up to ten years in fraud cases and up to five years in ordinary tax audits, in addition to the costs of foreign-exchange compliance audits and tax investigation.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The PRC operates a managed floating exchange rate regime with full convertibility on the current account for bona fide trade, service, and remittance transactions, while the capital account remains controlled. For a Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture, the forex administration framework requires SAFE registration of the foreign-invested enterprise, use of a designated onshore foreign currency/capital account, and a genuine, documentation-backed transaction for every FX conversion. Profit remittance by the Hong Kong/foreign shareholder is permissible after annual audit and tax filing, but it is administratively blocked if the tax clearance certificate, audited financial statements, board resolution regarding dividend distribution, and the SAFE remittance purpose code are not complete. Export proceeds are no longer forcibly surrendered to the central bank; however, there is a statutory duty to repatriate overseas receipts and to go through bank collection verification. Any attempt to layer offshore HK treasury vehicles while ignoring the onshore SAFE account and tax documentation will legally fail. Capital inflows for equity subscription also require a capital verification report issued by a licensed PRC certified public accountant. Thus, the convertibility at current account level is strong but conditional; capital account actions such as equity injection, borrowing, and dividends are highly workflow-driven and require dual filing with SAFE and the market supervision authority.

Export proceeds surrender rules

The current PRC rules do not impose a mandatory conversion ratio into RMB. However, 100% of export foreign currency receipts from cross-border trade and service activities are subject to repatriation into a licensed domestic bank, with the exporter obliged to complete the bank's export-conduct verification within 180 days of customs declaration for goods exports; service export proceeds must be repatriated no later than the contractually agreed payment due date and recorded in the SAFE monitoring system. Retention in a foreign currency account is permitted for bona fide operational needs, but invoices, transport/logistics records, service contracts, and customs declarations must be retained as the bank-level audit trail.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China, acting under the People's Bank of China (PBOC), with associated regulatory coordination through the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) for cross-border clearing channels into the Hong Kong SAR.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Offshore fintech electronic money institutions are legally and operationally barred from providing the core treasury banking services required by an onshore PRC Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture. A digital EMI from Singapore, the UK, or the EU does not hold a PRC commercial banking licence, cannot open an RMB basic deposit account, cannot appear in SAFE's cross-border foreign exchange reporting system, and cannot issue credible SWIFT MT103 covers or FX settlement reports for capital verification. The feasibility rating is therefore nil or low for any onshore capital inflow, profit remittance, export collection, or tax-related clearing. Such EMIs may at best serve ancillary Hong Kong-side travel cards or expense accounts for overseas principals, but they must never receive the JV's statutory capital or operate as the entity's payment clearing utility in mainland China.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity capital from the Hong Kong shareholder must be wired to the EJV's onshore SAFE-registered capital account using the correct foreign exchange purpose code for a direct foreign investment capital injection, with the remittance narrative identifying the Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture name and the equity subscription. The receiving Tier-1 PRC bank will complete SAFE authenticity checks against the FDI basic registration, the approved capital account payment form, the joint venture contract, and the business licence. Each inbound SWIFT MT103/MT202 credit must be matched to the capital verification process, and a licensed PRC certified public accountant must issue a capital verification report before the shareholder is credited with its equity ratio. The bank will then allow conversion into RMB for lawful operating expenses or retention in USD/HKD as the entity's cash position permits, but no early distribution or dividend remittance can occur without annual audit and tax settlement.Outbound payments for tuition fees, student welfare deposits, immigration lawyer retainers, visa application fees, and destination-government golden visa costs should be settled through SWIFT MT103 for foreign currency or through CIPS (Cross-Border Interbank Payment System) for cross-border RMB, accompanied by the service-trade purpose code and the full supporting dossier known to the paying bank. Commercial letters of credit and documentary collections are not appropriate for advisory and education expenses; at most, a standby letter of credit can back a performance bond or an escrow arrangement with a regulated destination-country institution.
