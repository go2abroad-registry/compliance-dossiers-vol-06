Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA) via the STA Electronic Tax Bureau online taxpayer portal (e-Tax); the Hong Kong SAR parent/investor is separately supervised by the Inland Revenue Department (IRD) via the eTax/eTax Mobile portal.

E-government filing portal

For CN_JV_LTD, a Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture incorporated in mainland China, the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) issued by the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) is the statutory Taxpayer Identification Number. The SAMR one-stop incorporation platform shares registration data with the STA; the entity must complete supplementary taxpayer registration through the STA e-Tax portal or at the competent local tax service hall within the statutory registration window, supplying the business license, articles of association, proof of registered address and actual business premises, identity documentation for the legal representative, and investor/corporate identity documents for the Hong Kong foreign partner. Upon completion, the STA activates the entity's taxpayer status (VAT general taxpayer or small-scale VAT taxpayer) and VAT invoice authorization. A foreign non-resident enterprise without a mainland permanent establishment is instead registered by the STA under the non-resident enterprise tax registration procedure for withholding and compliance purposes, with TIN issuance tied to the China-source income filing obligation; CN_JV_LTD itself is a resident Chinese entity and uses the USCC as its TIN.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

6%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 annual taxable turnover is the mandatory VAT general taxpayer registration threshold; below that limit the JV may remain a small-scale VAT taxpayer, subject to a 3% collection rate on taxable supplies. A Sino-Foreign Equity JV is still required to complete basic tax registration regardless of the VAT threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Cross-border supplies of modern services by a VAT general taxpayer can be zero-rated under the STA cross-border taxable service zero-rating catalogue, which extends to qualifying information technology and data-processing/BPO services such as computer vision bounding-box annotation and RLHF data curation, provided the recipient is outside mainland China (including Hong Kong SAR), the service is consumed outside mainland China, and the JV retains contractual, delivery, and payment evidence. Physical exports of goods qualify for zero-rating/export tax rebate upon customs export clearance.

  • Customs Export Declaration Form for physical goods; for cross-border services, the service export contract and delivery/acceptance confirmation
  • Bill of Lading, airway bill, or other international transport evidence for physical goods; for services, electronic delivery logs, IP access records, and overseas user acceptance evidence
  • Commercial invoice issued by CN_JV_LTD and the underlying cross-border service agreement
  • Bank-issued Foreign Exchange Income Declaration Form under SAFE (the Chinese statutory equivalent of an Inward Remittance Certificate) or equivalent cross-border receipt confirmation from PayPal/Wise and the funding bank, evidencing foreign currency repatriation from the overseas customer

China operates fully digitalized electronic invoices (digital e-invoices) through the STA e-invoice service platform. VAT invoices must be created on or integrated with the STA invoice system, with invoice data transmitted to the tax authority in near real time; no private-sector anti-fraud fiscal stamp is required. Digital e-invoices carry a unique invoice code and are authenticated by the STA. For domestic B2B supplies to VAT general taxpayers, electronic VAT special invoices must be issued and reported.

If zero-rated export treatment is claimed without complete customs, delivery, or foreign exchange repatriation evidence, the STA may recharacterize the transaction as domestic taxable supply, require output VAT at the applicable rate (6% for modern services or 13% for goods), impose daily late-payment surcharge at 0.05%, assess administrative penalties from 50% to 5 times the tax shortfall for fraudulent or grossly negligent claims, claw back any VAT refund/rebate with interest, and refer egregious invoice or export-rebate fraud to criminal prosecution under the Criminal Law.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Hong Kong maintains a fully convertible currency under the Linked Exchange Rate System (USD 7.75-7.85 per HKD). Both current-account and capital-account transactions are freely convertible for a Hong Kong-incorporated company; there are no approvals, quotas, or surrender requirements applicable to export proceeds, capital injections, loan drawdowns, or dividend remissions. A Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture domiciled in Hong Kong may hold operating receivables in CNY, USD, EUR, or any major currency and convert on demand through the offshore CNH market. Profit remittance is unrestricted under the Companies Ordinance because dividends are paid out of distributable profits and no additional exchange-control permit is required. The only practical restriction originates upstream when the mainland China parent remits equity or shareholder funding: that payment must satisfy SAFE's overseas direct investment registration and the PRC remitting bank's capital-account purpose-code screening. Once the funds land in Hong Kong, no further convertibility hurdle exists. The HKMA's FX regime therefore is not classified as stringent exchange control; it operates through the Currency Board mechanism, and foreign investment can be serviced without swap-file applications or repatriation lock-in periods.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable in Hong Kong. There is no statutory timeline, mandatory conversion ratio, or surrender obligation for export foreign-currency receipts under HKMA or the Companies Ordinance. The JV may retain export earnings in the original currency, convert at market rates via licensed banks, or apply proceeds to operating expenses, subject only to standard accounting, invoicing, and anti-money-laundering documentation controls.

Central bank supervision: Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) - Exchange Fund Division; the PRC leg is governed by the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) for outbound investment only.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

PayPal Fit: High

Feasibility rating: High for the named mass-payout rails (Wise and PayPal) as supplementary disbursement channels, but Low for serving as the primary capital-escrow or settlement bank. In Hong Kong, Wise operates under a Money Service Operator licence granted by the Customs and Excise Department under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Financing of Terrorism Ordinance, while PayPal operates under an SVF licence from the HKMA. An unlicensed offshore digital EMI is legally barred from maintaining a Hong Kong-dollar settlement account or from soliciting the JV as a customer without a HK banking partner. The prudent structure is to keep all capital inflows in the Tier-1 bank account and push mass AI annotator payouts through these EMI rails using API batch files that include full payee KYC and transaction-purpose data.

Mandatory Audit Threshold Small Company Audit Exemption Regime

Turnover: There are no balance-sheet, turnover, or headcount thresholds in Hong Kong that exempt an active incorporated company from statutory audit. Every Hong Kong incorporated company that is not dormant must prepare financial statements that give a true and fair view and have them audited by a certified public accountant (practising) under the Companies Ordinance, Cap. 622, Sections 408 to 411. The sole general exemption applies to a private company that is dormant, meaning it has no significant accounting transactions during the relevant financial year other than transactions relating to incorporation, the allotment of shares to a subscriber, or the payment of administrative fees necessary to maintain the company; the dormant status must be supported by a special resolution and is not available to a company actually carrying on any income-generating activity. This Sino-Foreign Joint Venture entity, engaged in AI data-labeling and AI-training BPO services, is therefore statutorily required to undergo an independent certified audit every financial year, without regard to asset size, revenue, or headcount.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due within one month of the date on which the BIR51 is issued by the Inland Revenue Department (IRD). For accounting periods ending 31 December with standard extension via a Hong Kong tax representative, the e-filing deadline is 15 August of the following year; paper returns due 1 August. Other year-ends receive extensions up to 8 months where the company provides audited financials.
