Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA), electronic portal: https://tpass.chinatax.gov.cn (e-Tax/电子税务局). For Hong Kong SAR: Inland Revenue Department (IRD), portal: https://www.ird.gov.hk

E-government filing portal

For a Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture established in mainland China, the entity must first complete the unified social credit code registration with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR), which simultaneously issues the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) serving as the taxpayer identification number for all taxes administered by the STA. The JV must then register for tax with the competent local tax authority within 30 days of obtaining the business license, completing the tax registration form, providing the articles of association, lease agreement, legal representative identification, bank account information, and capitalization verification. The tax authority will issue a tax registration certificate and link the entity to the electronic tax bureau. For the Hong Kong parent or related entity, a foreign non-resident company deriving income from or carrying on business in Hong Kong must apply to the IRD for a Business Registration Certificate (BRC) under the Business Registration Ordinance and, if chargeable to profits tax, obtain a separate Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) via the IRD's 'eTAX' system; a non-resident without a Hong Kong permanent establishment may still need a BRC if it is an overseas company registered under the Companies Ordinance (non-Hong Kong company) carrying on business in Hong Kong. For pure offshore cross-border webtoon syndication, the China JV should use its USCC for VAT registration and export zero-rating, while any Hong Kong sales entity should obtain a HK BRC and tax file number, with profits tax on an territorial source basis.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The China JV may claim zero-rated VAT on exports of digitally published webtoons (electronically delivered services) only if it qualifies as an 'outbound cross-border sales of services' transaction under Caishui [2016] No. 36 and supplemental STA announcements. The digital services must be rendered to overseas entities/individuals, the service contract and payment must be in foreign exchange and repatriated, the recipient must be outside China (or the service is fully consumed outside China), and the JV must be a general VAT taxpayer. Cross-border digital services, including copyright licensing and syndication of webtoon content, are normally zero-rated or VAT-exempt for services, with input VAT refund available under the 'exemption and refund' (免税并退税) or 'exemption without refund' (免税不退税) policy; goods export would require an export declaration, but the digital nature of webtoon syndication falls under the services export regime. For the Hong Kong jurisdiction, there is no VAT/GST, so zero-rating does not apply; the profit tax territorial source principle will exempt offshore-sourced syndication income from Hong Kong profits tax.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bill of Entry) - for any physical goods or carrier media, as issued by China Customs (出口报关单)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill, or electronic delivery log/proof of server transfer for digital services
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) / Bank Foreign Exchange Repatriation Certificate (跨境人民币收款/收汇凭证)
  • Export contract and invoice, including the digital IP licensing/syndication agreement
  • Foreign recipient qualification certificates (overseas business registration or passport/ID)
  • Platform settlement reports showing app-store sales to non-PRC consumers for Apple_IAP and Google_Play_Billing

China operates mandatory electronic invoice (电子发票) issuance through the STA's 'Golden Tax System Phase IV' (金税四期) with full digital invoice (全电发票) pilots; taxpayers must use the 'Digital Tax Certificate UKey' or the e-invoice service platform to issue VAT invoices in real-time. Invoice data is cleared through the national VAT invoice management system, and high-value or suspicious invoices may require 'risk control' verification. There is no external API provided to third parties; instead, enterprises integrate through the e-tax bureau portal or certified invoice software. Hong Kong has no electronic invoicing clearance mandate; private e-invoicing is contractual, and the IRD does not require real-time clearance.

China Customs and the STA may jointly inspect export zero-rating claims. If the claimed export is found to be false, unqualified, or not actually consumed outside China, the taxpayer must repay all zero-rated output VAT plus 0.05 per day overdue interest and may be subject to a penalty of 50%-500% of the unpaid tax under the Law on Tax Administration and Collection. Falsely obtaining export tax refunds constitutes 'counterfeiting export tax refund' (骗取出口退税) and attracts criminal liability under Article 204 of the Criminal Law, with fines and imprisonment up to 10 years or more for aggravated fraud; special audit scrutiny applies to digital services exports involving related-party webtoon platforms or intra-group IP payments, and the STA can perform transfer pricing adjustments and VAT recapture.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The CNY component remains current-account convertible for genuine trade, royalties and services, while capital-account flows such as equity injections, shareholder loans, guarantees and dividend distributions require SAFE registration, approval or filing plus bank-level authenticity review. The HKD component is fully convertible: Hong Kong imposes no exchange controls, applies no surrender obligation, and allows the joint venture to hold HKD, USD, CNH and other currencies in the same liquidity pool. On the Mainland side, export proceeds of the manufacturing and content supply chain must be repatriated into a designated bank and matched in the State Administration of Foreign Exchange goods-trade monitoring system; no fixed conversion ratio is presently forced, so the exporter may retain eligible foreign currency or settle to CNY. Profit remittance hurdles are mainly evidentiary: the paying Mainland entity must show audited distributable profits, tax payment/clearance certificates, a board resolution, currency conversion through the local bank, and an updated SAFE direct-investment record. For the Hong Kong treasury vehicle, outbound profit or dividend payments are unrestricted but must satisfy the bank's source-of-funds, sanctions and tax-residency review.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Mainland China: export proceeds must be repatriated onshore and reconciled against the SAFE goods-trade monitoring framework; if receipts are not collected within the normal 180-day monitoring window from customs declaration, the exporter must present contracts, customs documents, logistics evidence and a written explanation. No statutory percentage is mandatorily converted; the exporter elects whether to retain foreign currency or settle to RMB. Hong Kong: no surrender rule applies to the HK JV.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE), People's Bank of China, together with the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) as the de facto central bank of Hong Kong

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Rating: Conditional-Low. HKMA-regulated digital banks and stored-value facilities may support the Hong Kong JV's HKD/USD collections and outbound disbursements, but offshore digital EMIs are legally barred from servicing the Mainland China entity as a primary settlement bank, holding CNY capital/equity proceeds, or obtaining SAFE capital-registration status. The Mainland RMB treasury functions must be executed through a licensed Chinese commercial bank under SAFE. Digital fintech rails may only be used as supplementary collection infrastructure for Apple IAP and Google Play Billing proceeds in the HK entity, not as the corporate banking backbone.

Mandatory Audit Threshold Small Company Audit Exemption Regime

Turnover: Hong Kong does not apply any quantitative balance sheet, turnover, or headcount threshold for exemption from audit. Every company incorporated under the Companies Ordinance Cap 622, regardless of size, turnover, assets, or number of employees, must prepare annual financial statements and have them audited by an independent certified public accountant practising in Hong Kong, unless the company qualifies as a dormant company and passes the required special resolution. A dormant company retains the exemption only if it has undertaken no significant accounting transactions during the relevant period; any material transaction removes the exemption for that financial year. The audit must be performed in accordance with Hong Kong Standards on Auditing and the auditor must be independent under the professional and statutory independence requirements of the Hong Kong Institute of Certified Public Accountants.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% (standard Corporate Income Tax rate under China's EIT Law)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% statutory withholding tax on dividends paid by a Chinese resident enterprise to a non-resident enterprise shareholder; reduced to 5% under the PRC-HK Double Taxation Arrangement (DTA) where the beneficial owner is a Hong Kong resident company holding at least 25% of the issuing company's capital directly or indirectly, subject to the 365-day holding period test.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File: annual related-party transactions exceeding RMB 40 million (per type); Master File: aggregate related-party transactions exceeding RMB 200 million in the prior fiscal year. Entities with cross-border related-party transactions exceeding RMB 100 million must also submit a Country-by-Country Report if the ultimate parent group revenue exceeds RMB 5.5 billion. All documents must be contemporaneous and submitted within 15 days of audit request.

Economic substance scope: China's economic substance doctrine is embedded in the GAAR (Art. 47 EIT Law), the Specific Anti-Avoidance Rules (SAT Announcement No. 6 of 2017), and the BEPS Action Plan 6 treaty-shopping rules. For a Sino-Foreign JV, the Chinese entity must carry on actual business in China with its own employees, assets, risks, and management decisions; it cannot be a mere conduit receiving profits from China and passing them to Hong Kong without substantive activity. The Hong Kong shareholder also must have sufficient substance to qualify for DTA benefits. If a transaction (e.g., royalty payment to an offshore IP-holding company) lacks commercial rationale and economic reality, the tax authorities may re-characterize the transaction, allocate income to the real economic activities in China, and deny DTA relief. Application of CIGA (Core Income-Generating Activities) as defined under the OECD Model is used to identify where value is created; digital content development, platform management, and advertising decisions occurring in China would attribute income to the Chinese entity.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion (虚假申报/逃税)

Statutory sanction: Article 63 of the Tax Collection Administration Law: an order to pay the underpaid tax within a time limit and a penalty of 50% to 500% of the underpaid amount. If the case reaches criminal level, Article 201 of the Criminal Law prescribes imprisonment of up to 3 years (or 3-7 years if the amount is huge and constitutes more than 30% of the tax payable) and a criminal fine for the entity and responsible individuals.

Director personal liability: Directors who approve or knowingly participate in the fraudulent filing are deemed 'directly responsible persons' under Article 201. They face personal criminal liability: imprisonment and fines; additionally, their personal assets can be seized to satisfy unpaid tax and penalties under civil recovery actions. The director may also be subject to a travel ban/exit restriction under the 'Joint Disciplinary Measures for Major Tax Violation Cases', and disqualified from board roles for up to 3 years.
Cross-Border Transfer Pricing Manipulation (转让定价调查)

Statutory sanction: Article 41 of the EIT Law and SAT Announcement No. 6 of 2017 mandate a primary adjustment to the taxable income at arm's length, plus a compensating adjustment with an interest surcharge equal to the benchmark lending rate plus 5 percentage points (only if the documentation is incomplete). Failure to provide contemporaneous documents triggers a penalty under Article 44 of the EIT Law of up to RMB 200,000, and additional fines under Article 62 TCAL for failure to file.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign off on false TP reports or who deliberately create sham contracts to justify non-arm's length payments may be personally liable for the penalties if the corporate veil is pierced. Under the PRC Company Law, directors are liable for damages caused by violating laws; in criminal cases, they can be prosecuted as accomplices to tax evasion. The tax authority may also apply for a court-ordered confiscation of personal benefits derived from the scheme and issue a blacklisting/travel ban against the directors.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance and DTA Abuse (滥用税收协定)

Statutory sanction: Under Article 47 of the EIT Law (GAAR) and the provisions of the PRC-HK DTA on limitation of benefits, the tax authority may disregard the Hong Kong entity, recharacterize the payments as taxable in China, impose the full 10% statutory WHT (instead of treaty-reduced rate), apply a 50%-300% penalty for failure to withhold, and impose interest. Additionally, SAT Announcement No. 9 of 2015 on beneficial owner determination may deny treaty benefits retroactively.

Director personal liability: Directors of the Chinese JV who knowingly participate in the scheme (e.g., by preparing false ownership certifications) are jointly and severally liable for the unpaid withholding tax and penalties under civil law. The company may be 'pierced' to hold directors personally liable for the debts if fraud or abuse of the corporate form is found. On the criminal side, if the evasion exceeds the statutory threshold, directors may face imprisonment for up to 7 years and a personal fine. They may also be placed on the tax blacklist, leading to restrictions on travel abroad, high-consumption spending, and credit downgrades.
