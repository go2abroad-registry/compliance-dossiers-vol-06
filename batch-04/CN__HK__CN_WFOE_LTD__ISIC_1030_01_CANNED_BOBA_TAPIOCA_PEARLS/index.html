Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

The PRC Company Law does not impose a minimum amount of registered capital for a general WFOE. The amount is fixed by the shareholder and stated in the Articles of Association. Under Article 47 of the Company Law, subscribers must pay their subscribed capital in full within five years after establishment. No blocked escrow account is required before registration; subscribed capital must be paid into the company's bank account in China and accurately disclosed in the capital ledger and to the SAMR upon call-up.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; local administrative tax bureaus exercise delegated supervision; the statutory online filing and registration portal is the unified Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Taxation) platform, supplemented by the National VAT Invoice Verification Platform administered by the STA.

E-government filing portal

1) The WFOE is first established through the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR); the China business license carries the 18-character Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which is simultaneously accepted by the tax authorities as the entity's Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) and VAT registration number. 2) SAMR transmits the registration data electronically to the competent tax bureau, completing the one-stop tax registration; the WFOE itself does not need to submit a separate TIN application. 3) The legal representative and financial officer must complete real-name authentication on the Electronic Tax Bureau portal, bind the WFOE's tax registration, and confirm the taxpayer identity attributes, including resident enterprise status, industry codes, VAT taxpayer type, and tax collection jurisdiction. 4) The WFOE must then verify or elect its VAT classification: if annual taxable sales do not exceed CNY 5,000,000, it may remain a small-scale taxpayer; if the threshold is exceeded or export zero-rating/input-VAT refund is intended, it must register as a VAT general taxpayer through the VAT General Taxpayer Registration function in the e-Taxation portal. 5) For the foreign non-resident investor, typically the Hong Kong SAR parent company, no Chinese TIN is required for merely holding shares in the WFOE; the WFOE acts as the withholding agent for China-source dividends and remits withheld tax using the parent's home-jurisdiction tax identification number, supported by a Hong Kong tax-residence certificate where treaty benefits are claimed. 6) If the foreign non-resident entity itself becomes liable to register for Chinese tax because it carries on business in China, has a permanent establishment, or supplies taxable services in China, it must file a non-resident tax registration/filing declaration with the competent tax bureau, supported by the foreign certificate of incorporation, articles of association, board resolution, power of attorney, identity documents of the authorized representative, and notarization, legalization or apostille as applicable under the Hong Kong-Mainland document certification arrangement, together with a Chinese translation; after verification the tax bureau assigns a Chinese tax identification number for the non-resident entity and opens its access to the e-Taxation portal. 7) All subsequent changes to registered information, bank account details, legal representatives, ownership, address, or business scope must be filed through the e-Taxation portal within 30 days.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None of the U.S.-style check-the-box elections or hybrid-mismatch elections are available under PRC tax law. The only material statutory election is the VAT taxpayer status election: an entity with annual taxable sales not exceeding CNY 5,000,000 may elect small-scale taxpayer status, but an exporting production enterprise that needs the 0% export VAT and export input-tax refund treatment must be a VAT general taxpayer; registration as a VAT general taxpayer is compulsory once annual taxable sales exceed CNY 5,000,000.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No economic turnover threshold triggers basic VAT registration: every PRC-incorporated WFOE must complete VAT registration upon issuance of its SAMR business license. For VAT taxpayer classification, the small-scale taxpayer threshold is annual taxable sales of CNY 5,000,000; exceeding this threshold mandates compulsory VAT general taxpayer registration. Export-related 0% VAT treatment and export rebate eligibility are generally available only after VAT general taxpayer status is in place.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under the PRC VAT export tax rebate rules, goods manufactured in China and physically exported outside China's customs territory are subject to 0% VAT on the export sale, with input VAT attributable to the exported goods credited and refunded under the production-enterprise exemption-credit-rebate method. The WFOE must meet all of the following statutory criteria: (1) it is registered as a VAT general taxpayer; (2) the canned boba tapioca pearls are sold to an overseas buyer and the goods are physically exported through a PRC customs port, evidenced by an accepted Customs Export Declaration; (3) export sales proceeds are repatriated through permitted banking channels and the export collection is reported in SAFE's Trade in Goods Foreign Exchange Monitoring System; (4) the export value, product codes, quantities, units, destination, and payment terms are consistent across the commercial invoice, customs declaration, transport documents, and foreign exchange receipt record; (5) input VAT on raw materials and production costs directly attributable to the exported goods is substantiated by valid VAT special invoices or customs VAT payment vouchers; and (6) the product-specific export rebate rate, which may be equal to or lower than the 13% standard rate, is correctly applied in the exemption-credit-rebate calculation. Relevant qualifying offshore digital services are not part of this manufacturing scope.

The STA mandates the Fully Digitalized Electronic Invoice (e-invoice) framework under the Golden Tax Phase IV architecture. Every statutory VAT invoice, including the VAT special invoice used for input-VAT credit, is issued through the STA's Electronic Invoice Service Platform; the tax authority pre-assigns the invoice code and unique invoice number, and the complete invoice data is transmitted to the STA in real time before or at the moment of issuance, constituting the Chinese fiscal clearance event. The e-invoice carries a cryptographic anti-fraud tax-control authorization stamp in place of the former physical paper stamp and fiscal-device seal. Enterprises must integrate their ERP billing systems with the e-Taxation API or use the e-Taxation module, authenticated with a tax-control UKey or STA-compliant digital certificate. Red-letter credit notes, void invoices, and revisions are processed through the central platform with tax authority authorization; recipients verify e-invoices in real time on the National VAT Invoice Verification Platform. Legacy tax-controlled invoice equipment and paper invoices continue to be phased out under the provincial tax bureau transition schedules.

The STA and China Customs cross-check the Customs Export Declaration, e-invoice data, logistics bills, VAT special invoices, export rebate declarations, and SAFE export-collection records through automated data matching. If the 0% VAT export treatment cannot be substantiated or if any document is inconsistent, the tax authority may retroactively claw back the refunded input VAT, reverse the export zero-rating and assess output VAT at 13% on the affected export sales, impose late-payment surcharge at 0.05% per day, and impose a tax evaded penalty of 50% to 500% of the tax amount under Articles 63 and 64 of the Tax Collection and Administration Law of the People's Republic of China. Cases involving fabricated exports, counterfeit customs declarations, fraudulent input-invoice credits, or proceeds not repatriated within the statutory FX monitoring window are treated as export tax refund fraud; criminal liability arises under Article 204 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, which provides for heavy fines, imprisonment, and confiscation, and the entity will be listed in the national tax dishonesty blacklist, potentially triggering restrictions on finance, customs clearance, and foreign exchange operations.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a managed floating exchange-rate regime under the PBOC/SAFE framework. The current account is largely convertible for genuine trade and service transactions; banks perform authenticity and consistency checks on commercial invoices, contracts, customs declarations, and tax vouchers before allowing FX purchase/sale or RMB cross-border settlement. The capital account remains restricted: inbound equity capital to a WFOE must be registered as FDI with SAFE, wired into a designated capital account, converted at a licensed bank, and verified by a Chinese CPA before use. Profit remittances to the HK parent are permitted as dividends under the current account, but only after the company has filed and paid enterprise income tax and any dividend withholding tax, submitted an audit report, board resolution, and a foreign exchange payment application. If the WFOE posts no substantive operating activity, SAFE can challenge round-tripping or cross-border financing misclassification. Convertibility is therefore real but documentary; the bank is the first line of compliance. Currency convertibility is unrestricted for trade-related current items, but every transaction must have an economic substance and a clean audit trail.

Export proceeds surrender rules

All goods-export FX receipts must be repatriated to a domestic licensed bank account and reported in the SAFE Trade Foreign Exchange Monitoring System. There is no mandatory conversion ratio since the trade FX reform; exporters may retain the FX in a settlement account or sell it to the bank at market rate. Receipts should match the underlying export customs declaration and invoices. If payment is not received within 90 days after the export date, the WFOE must proactively register the delayed collection with SAFE before the deadline. Cross-border RMB receipts are settled via CIPS and are not subject to FX surrender.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File preparation is mandatory where annual related-party transactions exceed one of: RMB 200 million for tangible asset transfers, RMB 100 million for financial asset transfers, RMB 100 million for intangible property transfers, or RMB 40 million for all other related-party transactions (including services, management fees, and loans); a Master File is required when the consolidated annual revenue of the multinational group exceeds RMB 5.5 billion. These thresholds trigger mandatory contemporaneous documentation, which must be submitted within 15 days of a tax authority request.

Economic substance scope: China does not have an offshore Economic Substance Regulations regime, but the GAAR under Article 47 of the EIT Law and the substance-over-form doctrine require the WFOE to perform genuine manufacturing, employ personnel, own equipment, carry risk, and make substantive managerial decisions in China. The canned boba production process, including pearl cooking, canning, aseptic tubing, and quality inspection, must be the Core Income-Generating Activity of the Chinese entity; artificial allocation of profit to a shell Hong Kong entity without employees, assets, or functions will be recharacterized. For the Hong Kong parent or affiliate, Hong Kong ESR, if classified as a relevant entity, remains applicable in Hong Kong, and the Chinese WFOE must support any contractual allocation with third-party evidence.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Cross-Border Invoicing

Statutory sanction: Under Article 63 of the PRC Tax Collection Administration Law, tax evasion is penalized by an amount equal to 50%-500% of the underpaid tax; Article 201 of the Criminal Law imposes criminal liability for evasion exceeding RMB 50,000, with imprisonment of up to three years, or seven years for especially large amounts; Article 205 of the Criminal Law on false VAT special invoices imposes from three years to life imprisonment and fines.

Director personal liability: A court may pierce the corporate veil under Article 23 of the PRC Company Law (2023 revision) if shareholders or directors abuse corporate independence; directors who sign fraudulent tax returns are jointly and severally liable for restitution of the corporate tax, face asset freezing and bank account seizures, can be placed on the tax exit-control blacklist, which includes a travel ban prohibiting departure from China, and may personally receive criminal imprisonment terms of up to seven years, along with confiscation and fines.
Shell Company and Lack of Economic Substance in Cross-Border Structure

Statutory sanction: The GAAR under Article 47 of the EIT Law gives the tax authority power to restructure or disregard an arrangement that lacks reasonable commercial purpose; a reassessment may disallow all related-party deductions, reallocate profit to China, impose a penalty of up to 50% of the adjusted additional tax, and demand interest on the underpaid tax; treaty benefits are denied under the beneficial ownership and principal purpose test; the tax authority may additionally issue a public penalty and credit rating downgrade.

Director personal liability: Where the entity is a mere conduit, shareholders and directors can be held personally liable for corporate debts under the Company Law veil-piercing provision; directors who produce false substance documentation may face civil damages to creditors, a SAFE or tax blacklist that includes an exit ban, and criminal exposure for fraudulent fact-finding, including imprisonment for tax crimes; the legal representative may be barred from leaving China while the enterprise has overdue undisputed tax liabilities.
AML and Foreign Exchange Violations in Concealed Profit Remittance

Statutory sanction: Under Article 39 of the PRC Regulations on Foreign Exchange Administration, illegal cross-border fund transfer is penalized with 5%-30% of the illegal amount; non-withholding of tax on outbound payments triggers penalties equal to 50%-300% of the unwithheld amount under the Tax Collection Administration Law; money laundering under Article 191 of the Criminal Law carries a maximum sentence of ten years, plus fines and confiscation.

Director personal liability: Directors who orchestrate or approve underground remittances are subject to civil liability for the company's resulting losses, individualized SAFE and customs travel bans, freezing of personal assets, disqualification of the legal representative, and criminal imprisonment for money laundering or tax evasion; if foreign exchange fraud causes severe losses, responsible directors may be personally sued and held liable for the entire shortfall in tax and FX penalties, and asset recovery proceedings may be initiated against their personal holdings.
