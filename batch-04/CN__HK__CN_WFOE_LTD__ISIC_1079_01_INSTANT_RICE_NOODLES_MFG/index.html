Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum registered capital is required for a WFOE under the PRC Company Law; the registered capital amount and contribution schedule must be stipulated in the Articles of Association in RMB or convertible foreign currency. Under the 2023 revision, shareholders must make a full capital contribution within a maximum of five years from the incorporation date, subject to any shorter industry-specific period. There is no mandatory blocking escrow account for a standard WFOE; however, if local authorities require capital verification evidence for the Food Production License application, the company must maintain an unrestricted bank account in which paid-in capital is deposited and documented by a bank capital contribution certificate.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA), administered through the unified Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax) portal at https://etax.chinatax.gov.cn; customs export filings and tax-rebate matching are coordinated through the China International Trade Single Window at https://www.singlewindow.cn.

E-government filing portal

A Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE) is itself a PRC resident enterprise, so its statutory TIN and VAT ID do not require a separate non-resident tax file. The WFOE obtains an 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code from the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) upon issuance of the Business License, and this code is the TIN/VAT ID for all tax matters. Procedure: (1) incorporate the WFOE with SAMR and receive the Business License bearing the Unified Social Credit Code; (2) within 30 days of incorporation, log into the Electronic Tax Bureau and complete Tax Registration Information Confirmation, select the VAT taxpayer classification, and report bank account information; (3) obtain the Food Production License required for packaged food manufacturing before production begins; (4) register with the General Administration of Customs (GACC) as an import/export enterprise and complete the Export Food Production Enterprise filing through the China International Trade Single Window; (5) enroll with the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) in the trade foreign exchange receipt and payment directory and establish the export receipt verification profile; (6) submit the Export Tax Refund Registration (export tax rebate filing) through the Electronic Tax Bureau, designating the bank account for refunds. A Hong Kong corporate shareholder that is not a PRC taxpayer does not receive a WFOE VAT ID; the WFOE withholds PRC tax on dividends paid to the Hong Kong shareholder using the WFOE's own TIN, and the Hong Kong shareholder should obtain a Hong Kong tax residence certificate to apply treaty benefits under the Mainland/Hong Kong arrangement.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Annual taxable sales of RMB 5,000,000 (CNY 5,000,000) is the mandatory threshold for general VAT taxpayer status for a manufacturing and sales enterprise; all VAT registration commences from the date of Business License issuance, and export zero-rating and input VAT refund eligibility generally require general VAT taxpayer status.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods qualify for 0% export VAT with input VAT refund only where the WFOE is the actual exporter and a general VAT taxpayer; the goods are self-produced packaged rice noodles physically exported from PRC customs territory; the WFOE holds the GACC export-food producer registration and the consignment is covered by the required export inspection/quarantine or health certificates; the export sale is supported by an export contract, commercial invoice, packing list, and transport document; and the foreign exchange or cross-border RMB proceeds are received in a domestic bank account and matched through SAFE trade foreign exchange monitoring data within the statutory deadline.

  • GACC Customs Export Declaration (Customs Declaration Form) bearing the export tax refund seal and transmitted via the China International Trade Single Window
  • Commercial export invoice and packing list issued by the WFOE exporter
  • Ocean Bill of Lading, multimodal Bill of Lading, or Air Waybill evidencing physical departure from PRC customs territory
  • Export sales contract or purchase order with the overseas buyer
  • Bank SWIFT credit advice or inward remittance receipt issued by a licensed domestic bank evidencing foreign-exchange or cross-border RMB settlement
  • GACC Export Food Production Enterprise Filing Certificate and the export inspection/quarantine or health certificate for the consignment
  • VAT special input invoices or purchase VAT payment vouchers substantiating deductible input tax for the export tax refund calculation
  • SAFE trade foreign exchange receipt verification record matching the customs declaration to the bank receipt

All VAT invoices must be issued through the STA's Full Digital Electronic Invoice system (Golden Tax Phase IV Integrated Electronic Invoice Platform). Invoices are issued in real time through the Electronic Tax Bureau API, automatically uploaded to the national electronic invoice database, and validated by the tax authority before they acquire fiscal effect. Each invoice carries a nationally controlled invoice number and a tax-authority-approved digital seal; no private fiscal stamping device is recognized, and export declaration data transmitted through the China International Trade Single Window is matched against the invoice and refund application.

If the export zero-rating is found to be unsupported or fraudulent, the tax authority claws back the refunded VAT with statutory late-payment interest and may impose a fine of 50% to 500% of the tax involved under Article 63 of the Law on Administration of Tax Collection. Fraudulent export tax refund cases may also be referred under Article 204 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, with criminal liability including imprisonment and monetary fines in aggravated cases. The enterprise may be removed from the export tax refund roster, suspended from zero-rating benefits, and downgraded in taxpayer credit classification.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The WFOE is a China resident entity and is therefore subject to the foreign exchange administration of SAFE/PBOC. For current account items, China has accepted transitional convertibility: imported materials, packaging, machinery, and trade service payments can be bought and remitted freely once the bank has verified authentic documentary background. Export receipts from the sale of retort river snail noodles may be retained or settled into RMB, but the funds must be collected through China and reported in the SAFE monitoring system. Capital account transactions are not fully convertible. A foreign direct investment capital injection from the Hong Kong parent requires SAFE registration or bank-based documentary completion, opening of a special FDI foreign-exchange capital account, and customs/negative-list verification. Profit remittance to the Hong Kong parent is legally permissible but not automatic: the WFOE must first complete statutory annual audits, account for all Chinese taxes and transfer pricing, obtain the tax clearance certificate, pass the annual foreign investment information report, and use the bank's profit dividend remittance process. There are no strict surrender quotas nowadays, but statistical reporting, receipt verification, and the 180-day export collection window effectively prevent indefinite parking of export proceeds offshore. The HK jurisdiction itself has no FX controls; however, the ring-fence is at the mainland border, and all cross-border movements between the HK parent and the PRC WFOE must be channelled through a licensed PRC bank.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Goods-export proceeds must be repatriated to China through a licensed bank unless a SAFE-approved deferred collection registration has been filed before the maturity date. The standard collection window is 180 days from the date of export declaration. No fixed statutory mandatory conversion ratio is imposed on the WFOE; the entity may keep the receipt in a domestic designated foreign-exchange account or request voluntary settlement into RMB at the prevailing bank rate. For any export receivable older than 180 days, the exporter must lodge a deferred-receivable registration, otherwise the bank may flag the receivable as an export verification irregularity and administrative penalties may apply. For the low-risk instant rice noodle manufacturing scope, the bank will expect a documented collection plan or settlement for the full invoice value; the surrender requirement is satisfied by repatriation and SAFE receipt verification, not by forced conversion.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) acting through the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Not viable for the onshore WFOE. PRC commercial banking law and SAFE regulation restrict the handling of RMB settlements, FX receipts, and outward remittances to licensed onshore banks and their sanctioned clearing systems. Offshore digital EMIs, including Hong Kong licensed stored-value facilities and money service operators, have no PRC banking licence and cannot open PBOC basic deposit accounts, access SAFE registrations, or execute foreign-direct-investment capital flows. They may be used only by the Hong Kong parent for small operational treasury balances outside China. Overall feasibility rating: Low/Legally barred for regulated onshore cross-border flows; do not route WFOE disbursements, export surpluses, profit repatriation, or trade settlements through any fintech EMI.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound equity injection from the Hong Kong parent must be routed by SWIFT MT103 (or CIPS for cross-border RMB) into the WFOE's designated FDI foreign-exchange capital account at a licensed onshore Tier-1 bank. The remitter must be the SAFE-registered direct shareholder, and the wire reference must carry the appropriate SAFE/BOP purpose code for foreign direct investment capital injection, together with the company's FDI registration number. The receiving bank will perform authenticity verification, record the receipt in the SAFE foreign-exchange account and monitoring system, and complete the capital-item registration update. The WFOE may then settle into RMB only for permitted operating expenses after negative-list screening. Capital verification is evidenced by the bank's inward remittance confirmation, the SAFE FDI registration certificate, and the internal capital verification memorandum prepared by the account bank.Outbound settlements for rice, retort packaging film, seasoning and machinery are cleared through the onshore bank's trade settlement platform using SWIFT MT103 for clean telegraphic transfers, commercial letters of credit for large or documentary trades, and documentary collections where bilateral trust is lower. The WFOE must provide the import contract, commercial invoice, bill of lading/air waybill, customs import declaration, and any applicable hygiene or inspection certificates. For cross-border RMB flows, the same Tier-1 bank can clear via CIPS while retaining SWIFT-like references. Dividends, royalties and management fees to the Hong Kong parent must be separately routed with the income purpose code and are subject to audited financials, tax clearance, transfer pricing documentation, board resolution on distribution, and SAFE registration.Onshore SAFE-registered plain-vanilla USD/RMB forward contracts with real trade or current-account exposure, settled on a deliverable basis at maturity.Onshore FX swaps between RMB and USD for short-term trade-settlement liquidity, with the underlying transaction documented and the hedge amount limited to exposure.Cross-currency interest-rate swaps for hedging SAFE-registered foreign-currency debts, provided the swap does not create speculation and is booked with a designated bank.Boundary: non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are not permissible onshore and cannot be booked by the WFOE; an NDF overlay, if any, may be executed only through the Hong Kong parent in the offshore market and must exclude speculative intent.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Not viable for the onshore WFOE. PRC commercial banking law and SAFE regulation restrict the handling of RMB settlements, FX receipts, and outward remittances to licensed onshore banks and their sanctioned clearing systems. Offshore digital EMIs, including Hong Kong licensed stored-value facilities and money service operators, have no PRC banking licence and cannot open PBOC basic deposit accounts, access SAFE registrations, or execute foreign-direct-investment capital flows. They may be used only by the Hong Kong parent for small operational treasury balances outside China. Overall feasibility rating: Low/Legally barred for regulated onshore cross-border flows; do not route WFOE disbursements, export surpluses, profit repatriation, or trade settlements through any fintech EMI.
