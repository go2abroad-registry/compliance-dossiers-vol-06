Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies for a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise in the permitted toy manufacturing sector. The shareholder may subscribe any capital amount stated in the Articles of Association, but the subscribed capital must be fully paid within five years from establishment under the PRC Company Law. No blocked escrow account is required; upon capital injection, the contribution must be reported to SAFE, deposited in the entity's foreign-currency capital account, and converted through a local licensed bank only after domestic foreign-exchange settlement procedures are completed.

Funding & unlock path: The factory must obtain a Fire Safety Acceptance Certificate for civil-defence compliance, covering fire-resistance ratings, compartmentation, measurable egress widths, smoke barriers, fire alarms, sprinklers, emergency lighting, and evacuation signage. Structural load inspections must certify mezzanines, racking, and machine foundations. Occupational clearance under the Work Safety Law includes workplace hazard notification, medical surveillance, noise exposure limits, local exhaust ventilation verification, special equipment registration, and documented emergency response drills.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA); online electronic portal: National Electronic Tax Service Platform / Electronic Taxpayer Service Hall (e-Tax), administered through provincial offices of the STA and accessible to all PRC taxpayers.

E-government filing portal

For the CN WFOE (Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise): (1) Incorporate with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR). The business license automatically integrates tax registration under the multi-certificate integration system; the 18-character Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) printed on the license is the statutory taxpayer identification number for corporate income tax, VAT, and all PRC taxes. (2) The local STA office completes tax registration after SAMR data transmission; the WFOE files taxpayer profile details through the e-Tax portal and designates the applicable taxes (VAT, Corporate Income Tax, urban construction and maintenance tax, education surcharges, stamp duty, and payroll withholding). (3) For a foreign non-resident entity (e.g., the Hong Kong parent or an HK/offshore supplier) with no PRC establishment but deriving PRC-source income, the non-resident or the PRC withholding agent must file the non-resident enterprise tax registration procedure with the competent local STA office; the STA assigns a Non-Resident Enterprise Taxpayer Identification Number to the foreign entity. (4) If treaty benefits are sought, the Hong Kong resident/company obtains a Hong Kong Inland Revenue Department Tax Residency Certificate and files a treaty reduction claim using the Mainland and Hong Kong SAR Double Tax Arrangement; the withholding agent keeps the certificate in the tax file. (5) VAT registration is not a separate certificate: the USCC is the VAT identification code; the entity is automatically registered on the taxpayer roll at incorporation. If annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000, an application for general VAT taxpayer recognition must be filed; otherwise the entity may remain a small-scale taxpayer.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation. The WFOE is a PRC tax resident limited liability company, subject to 25% corporate income tax on worldwide income; there is no pass-through or disregarded-entity option. Non-resident shareholders are taxed through withholding at the prescribed treaty or domestic rate (10% dividend withholding, reduced to 5% for a qualifying Hong Kong company holding at least 25% capital under the Mainland-Hong Kong Double Tax Arrangement).

Available election forms

None. The PRC does not provide US-style check-the-box elections or hybrid entity/mismatch elections. The WFOE may, however, elect general VAT taxpayer status on a voluntary basis, elect simplified small-scale taxpayer treatment if annual taxable sales do not exceed the statutory threshold, apply for special tax treatment on qualifying corporate restructurings, and apply for an advance pricing arrangement with the STA to lock in cross-border transfer pricing methods.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 (CNY 5 million) of annual VAT taxable sales; exceeding this turnover makes general VAT taxpayer registration mandatory. A newly formed WFOE is already registered for VAT by virtue of its USCC tax registration; the threshold only governs small-scale taxpayer vs general taxpayer status. Small-scale taxpayers are currently chargeable at the 3% collection rate (or 1% when reduced by pandemic-era policy if applicable).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The export sale of designer blind-box toy physical goods qualifies for the 0% VAT rate and export tax exemption/rebate (exempt-offset-refund) only when: (a) the goods are physically exported from PRC customs territory under an electronic Export Customs Declaration; (b) ownership passes to an overseas buyer outside the PRC (including Hong Kong SAR in cross-border trade); (c) the WFOE is a VAT general taxpayer and holds lawful input VAT special invoices for the materials, rotocasting resin, injection inputs, packaging, and airbrush finishing supplies; (d) the export proceeds are collected in foreign exchange, remitted back to the PRC, and reconciled to the export declaration within the statutory or bank-prescribed period; and (e) the export is not listed in the prohibited/restricted export-tax-rebate catalogue. Offshore digital services, if separately sold to foreign customers, are outside PRC VAT scope when supplied to non-resident persons; for these physical goods the zero-rating follows the export-verification route.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bill of Entry), Bill of Lading, Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC); Export Contract; Commercial Invoice; Packing List; Export VAT invoice (full-digital e-invoice with export designation); bank receipt/proof of export proceeds; customs release receipt or port logistics confirmation; and the SAFE/STA electronic export exchange data reconciliation report.

Under Golden Tax Phase IV, all VAT invoices must be issued through the STA-integrated electronic invoice system. The fully digitalized e-invoice is the standard B2B/B2C VAT document; each invoice is digitally signed using State Cryptography Administration-approved algorithms, assigned a unique 20-digit invoice number and invoice code, and transmitted in near real-time to the National Electronic Invoice Platform. Taxpayers' ERP, Shopify Payments, Stripe, or local POS systems must interface with the STA e-invoice open interface/API through an STA-approved service provider or the e-Tax portal. The system performs total-amount control, invoice quota management, automatic comparison against VAT returns and customs/trade data, and anti-counterfeiting digital stamping. Paper invoices, when still used, must be opened through tax-control equipment (tax Ukey/tax-control disc) with periodic data reporting. Any deviation triggers invoice freezing, VAT credit denial, risk alerts, and audit referral.

The STA jointly monitors customs export data, SAFE foreign-exchange receipts, port logistics confirmations, invoice records, and VAT/EIT returns. If the zero-rating is claimed without a valid customs declaration, transport document, FIRC/bank receipt, or timely FX repatriation, the tax authority will reverse the zero-rating, require VAT at 13% on the export consideration, charge a daily late-payment fee of 0.05%, and impose a penalty of 50% to 5 times the tax shortfall under the Law on Tax Collection and Administration. Where forged documents or circular foreign-exchange arrangements are used to obtain an export rebate, the refund is clawed back, the exporter loses future export-refund access/blacklisted status, and criminal liability for fraudulent export tax refund may be pursued under Article 204 of the PRC Criminal Law, with penalties ranging from 5 years' imprisonment up to life imprisonment and confiscation of property for especially large amounts.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a managed floating RMB regime with tight capital controls and a clear split between current account and capital account convertibility. The WFOE is free to use RMB in its operational settlement account for payroll, raw materials, rent, utilities, and domestic services, and current account payments for cross-border trade in goods and services are permissible once a designated foreign exchange bank has completed authentic-basis verification of contracts, invoices, customs declarations, tax certificates, and the applicable foreign exchange payment purpose codes. Capital account convertibility remains restricted: the equity registered capital contributed by the Hong Kong parent must be routed through a SAFE-registered FDI foreign exchange capital account, and each disbursement from that capital account requires the receiving bank to verify the actual use against the FDI negative list and to reject any attempt to convert capital into RMB for prohibited uses such as securities investment, real estate speculation outside the permitted scope, or intercompany lending without a separate foreign debt registration. Profit remittance to the Hong Kong parent is processed as a current account item but only after the WFOE has completed its annual statutory audit, corporate income tax final settlement, board approval of a dividend distribution, and submission of the audited financial statements, tax payment proofs, and a remittance application demonstrating that the dividend amount does not exceed accumulated after-tax profits. In practice, banks also require confirmation that the WFOE has no outstanding foreign debt repayment obligations, no overdue taxes, and no unresolved SAFE reporting exceptions. The documentation burden, verification timelines, and purpose code discipline create real operational hurdles, but a well-documented manufacturing WFOE engaged in goods trade can reasonably execute current account conversions and cleared capital account transfers within two to four weeks through its licensed corporate bank.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Goods trade foreign currency receipts of the WFOE must be repatriated to its domestic bank through the cross-border receipt and payment reporting system. Under current SAFE rules there is no mandatory conversion ratio; the exporter may choose to retain the foreign currency in an export settlement account or convert it into RMB after the bank completes trade-in-goods verification. The statutory collection timeline for export proceeds is generally 90 calendar days from the date of customs export declaration, extendable to 180 days with SAFE registration for special trade terms or extended payment schedules. All export receipts must be reported with the correct trade purpose code in the Foreign Exchange Receipt and Payment Declaration, and the bank will match the customs export declaration data with the settlement instruction. Retained FX is not subject to mandatory surrender but is closely monitored, and the WFOE cannot use retained export proceeds for speculative investments or to repay foreign debt without the corresponding SAFE registration and supporting facility agreements.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) and the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Low; legally barred as a standalone onboarding channel for the China WFOE. Offshore and digital EMI providers such as Revolut Business, Airwallex, Currencycloud, or other foreign payment institutions are not licensed by the PBOC or SAFE to open RMB deposit accounts, access CNAPS, handle statutory tax or customs payments, or support SAFE capital account verification for a mainland WFOE. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Before any capital remittance, the WFOE must complete the Foreign Investment Information Report in the SAFE corporate bank reporting system and obtain the Foreign Exchange Registration Certificate for FDI from the receiving designated bank. The HK parent then instructs its remitting bank to send an inbound SWIFT MT103 or MT202 to the WFOE's dedicated FDI foreign exchange capital account at the mainland commercial bank. The payment details must carry the WFOE foreign investment confirmation code, the SAFE foreign exchange payment purpose code for equity contribution to a foreign-invested enterprise, and a clear remittance reference to the capital injection contract or board resolution. The receiving bank is required to verify the authenticity of the underlying FDI record, reject any remittance arriving in a standard operational RMB current account instead of the regulated capital account, and ensure the amount does not exceed the approved registered capital. Once the FX funds are credited, the WFOE's authorized signatories issue a settlement instruction to convert the foreign currency into RMB, and the bank posts the transaction to the capital account income ledger. A Chinese certified public accountant may then issue a capital verification report if required for the company registration file. The WFOE must maintain an actual use ledger for all capital account disbursements because SAFE requires periodical reporting on how the FDI capital has been deployed, and any amendment to the registered capital or business scope must be reported before further inbound capital is accepted.The WFOE should maintain segregated RMB and FX current accounts to preserve clean trade verification records. For urgent raw material purchases, royalty payments, and low-value service fees, direct SWIFT MT103 transfers are appropriate, with each payment supported by a trade contract, invoice, tax certificate, and the correct SAFE foreign exchange purpose code. For large bulk procurement of vinyl, injection molding equipment, packaging, and finished toy components, the recommended clearing rail is an irrevocable documentary letter of credit, either at sight or usance, issued or confirmed by the mainland bank, using SWIFT MT700 for issuance and MT202/MT754 for settlement. Documentary collections, including D/P and D/A, remain available for long-standing supplier relationships where the bank has complete historical payment data. Every physical goods disbursement must match the China Customs import declaration, the commercial invoice, packing list, and transport document in the goods trade payment control system. Service payments for licensing, design, marketing, or overseas logistics must be accompanied by a service agreement, proof of the service actually rendered, and a withholding tax certificate when the service income is deemed China-source. For cross-border RMB payments, the bank may use the CIPS rail; for USD, HKD, or other non-RMB currencies, settlement is executed through the bank's SWIFT correspondent network. Merchant proceeds from Shopify Payments, Stripe, and local POS should be settled through a licensed domestic acquiring arrangement into the WFOE's operating account; if such proceeds are instead collected by the HK parent treasury, the parent may only bring funds back into the mainland as lawful equity contribution, foreign debt, or payment for an underlying service or trade transaction, and cannot use trade channel misrepresentation to repatriate e-commerce receipts.Onshore deliverable CNY/USD and CNY/HKD forward contracts concluded by the WFOE with its licensed foreign exchange bank, supported by genuine current account or capital account exposure and governed by the real demand principle; no speculative net open positions are permitted.RMB cross-currency swaps for tenor-based matching of FX income and RMB operational expenditure, subject to SAFE underlying transaction validation and bank credit approval.Plain-vanilla CNY foreign exchange options, including call and put structures with bank counterparty, permitted only when tied to a documented commercial exposure and not exceeding the bank's internal exposure limits.Offshore non-deliverable forwards in CNH markets are not permissible for the mainland WFOE because onshore NDF trading is prohibited; any CNH NDF hedging must be executed only through the Hong Kong parent treasury for the parent's own RMB investment and royalty exposure.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low; legally barred as a standalone onboarding channel for the China WFOE. Offshore and digital EMI providers such as Revolut Business, Airwallex, Currencycloud, or other foreign payment institutions are not licensed by the PBOC or SAFE to open RMB deposit accounts, access CNAPS, handle statutory tax or customs payments, or support SAFE capital account verification for a mainland WFOE. Such providers may act only as auxiliary settlement interfaces for collecting overseas e-commerce receivables from Shopify Payments, Stripe, or local POS into a sponsor bank arrangement, but final clearing into the WFOE's corporate treasury must occur through a licensed Chinese bank. The WFOE should not structure its primary banking relationship around an offshore EMI because capital injections, trade payments, tax remittances, and regulatory reporting all require a domestic bank relationship with direct CNAPS, SWIFT, and CIPS participation.
