Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise limited liability company under PRC law. The sole shareholder must subscribe to registered capital in the Articles of Association and pay contributions within the period required by the Company Law (default maximum five years), but no blocked escrow account is required. For this polyresin statue manufacturing activity, the foreign shareholder should nevertheless fund an initial registered capital reasonably sufficient to cover leasehold improvements, EIA compliance, hazardous waste disposal deposits, fire safety retrofits and the first year of production overhead.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

China: State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA) via the Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax); Hong Kong SAR: Inland Revenue Department (IRD) via the eTAX portal.

E-government filing portal

Mainland China WFOE: The WFOE is incorporated through the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) under the Foreign Investment Law and the PRC Company Law. SAMR issues a business license with an 18-character Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which is the sole statutory taxpayer identification number (TIN). Tax registration is synchronized automatically from SAMR to the STA; the WFOE must then activate its tax profile in the Electronic Tax Bureau, confirm its VAT taxpayer status (small-scale or general VAT taxpayer), and update its bank, payroll, and accounting records. If it establishes a PE or directly receives reportable China-source income, it must complete non-resident taxpayer registration with the competent STA office to obtain a tax file number. Default tax classification

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies under PRC VAT to exports of physical goods by a VAT general taxpayer. The WFOE must be a VAT general taxpayer, hold valid GACC customs registration, actually export the resin statues through a Chinese customs port, file an electronic export declaration, and present evidence that the goods left the PRC customs territory and were paid for in foreign currency. For a production enterprise, export of self-manufactured goods is eligible for the exemption, credit, and refund method; input VAT attributable to the exported goods is refundable after verification. Physical goods do not rely on digital-services offshore consumption tests.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The renminbi is convertible on current account for genuine trade and service transactions, but capital account flows remain subject to SAFE licensing, registration, and quota regimes. Mainland China operates a managed floating exchange rate regime with a daily band against the US dollar and a reference basket; all FX transactions must be routed through banks with a SAFE-filed license. For a Hong Kong-owned WFOE engaged in resin statue production, investment capital may enter only after the WFOE has completed foreign exchange registration for direct investment with the local SAFE branch and has opened a dedicated foreign exchange capital account with a domestic designated bank. Once credited, capital injections can stay in foreign currency or be converted to RMB according to authentic business use; conversion is recorded under the bank's capital account 'capital verification' process. Current account payments for raw materials, logistics, and royalty fees are generally permitted without prior SAFE approval, but each payment requires transaction supporting documentation, invoice authenticity checks, and the use of specific trade purpose codes. Profit remittance to the Hong Kong shareholder is subject to corporate income tax filing and settlement, audited financials, and the bank's validation of retained earnings. The practical hurdle is compliance documentation, not statutory convertibility: the bank must verify no false trade, matching import/export data, and consistency of the entity's actual operations with its registered business scope.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE's current trade foreign exchange monitoring framework, export proceeds from the manufacture and sale of resin statues must be repatriated to the WFOE's domestic foreign exchange settlement account through a designated bank. The statutory maturity for export collection is generally within 90 days of the export customs declaration date, with extension up to 180 days permitted where deferred payment terms or overseas e-commerce shipping cycles are contractually demonstrated. There is no mandatory conversion percentage under the current policies: the enterprise may retain 100% of collected foreign currency in its export proceeds account indefinitely, or convert to RMB freely according to commercial needs. However, every receipt must be matched to a customs declaration through SAFE's trade monitoring system; unmatched receipts are flagged as anomalies and may trigger bank compliance investigations before funds are cleared. E-commerce preorder deposits collected via Stripe and PayPal must be settled by the payment processor to the enterprise's domestic bank in the currency nominated, with accompanying merchant settlement reports.

Central bank supervision: The People's Bank of China (PBOC), acting through the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low for treasury and clearing; restricted. Offshore digital EMI platforms are not licensed deposit-taking institutions in Mainland China and cannot legally maintain settlement accounts or execute CNY clearing for a WFOE. A Hong Kong or Singapore EMI may hold an offshore licence but has no PBOC payment institution licence; therefore using such an EMI as the WFOE's primary operating account is not viable. Cross-border collection through global PSPs remains acceptable only insofar as they settle to a PRC-designated bank account via a licensed acquiring bank or cross-border payment institution that has filed with SAFE. For trade disbursements, the WFOE must route SWIFT payments through a designated domestic bank; foreign EMIs can at most act as invoicing or treasury aggregation tools outside the PRC, not as the legal clearing rail. Recommended rating: not viable as a primary treasury rail; acceptable as a supplementary foreign-currency collection concentrator if liquidated daily to the domestic account.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The Hong Kong parent wires registered capital or capital reserve to the WFOE's dedicated FDI capital account using SWIFT MT103, with the remittance purpose expressly stated as foreign direct investment or equity injection into a wholly foreign-owned enterprise. Before the transfer, the WFOE must complete SAFE direct investment registration and obtain a foreign exchange business registration voucher so the receiving bank can code the credit under the applicable inbound direct investment purpose code. Upon receipt, the bank conducts capital verification against the SAFE system, matches the remitter name with the registered shareholder, and credits the amount without local clearing queues. Funds may be converted to RMB under the bank's capital account conversion limit for operating costs, and the bank will require a capital usage plan; the WFOE must not use capital to repay cross-border loans or equity investments unless explicitly approved. If the amount exceeds an internal threshold, a formal capital verification report issued by a certified public accountant in China is recommended.For operating expenditures and raw-material imports, the WFOE uses SWIFT MT103 through a designated domestic bank under the current account; the bank validates the import contract, invoice, customs declaration, and authenticity of the goods. For high-volume polyurethane or resin raw-material imports from long-standing suppliers, an irrevocable Commercial Letter of Credit can be opened through the MT700 message type, often supported by a short-term import trade finance facility. Documentary collections on a documents-against-payment or documents-against-acceptance basis are permissible for smaller counterparties, but the bank requires transport documents and insurance certificates with the draft. Payments to the Hong Kong parent or an affiliated marketing entity are permitted only if identifiable as genuine trade in goods or services and not disguised profit distribution; each outbound payment needs a SAFE-compliant purpose code, the underlying transaction documents, and transfer pricing support to avoid suspicion of capital flight.Onshore deliverable CNY/USD forward sale and purchase contracts booked with a PRC designated bank, limited to a real current-account trade exposure or FDI capital-account exposure evidenced by the WFOE's underlying invoices and SAFE records.Cross-currency swaps (CNY/USD) for long-term FX positions arising from the Hong Kong parent equity injection and shareholder loans, subject to bank credit-line approval and verification that the underlying exposure is not speculative.FX option strategies such as knock-in forwards, range forwards, and participating forwards with a documented commercial premium, restricted to hedge-to-arrive structures and prohibited from creating a naked speculative position.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are not permissible onshore for a PRC-incorporated WFOE as a direct hedging booking entity; the Hong Kong parent may access offshore NDF markets for synthetic hedging of CNY exposure, but the WFOE cannot book NDF contracts itself without violating SAFE current-account realism principles.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low for treasury and clearing; restricted. Offshore digital EMI platforms are not licensed deposit-taking institutions in Mainland China and cannot legally maintain settlement accounts or execute CNY clearing for a WFOE. A Hong Kong or Singapore EMI may hold an offshore licence but has no PBOC payment institution licence; therefore using such an EMI as the WFOE's primary operating account is not viable. Cross-border collection through global PSPs remains acceptable only insofar as they settle to a PRC-designated bank account via a licensed acquiring bank or cross-border payment institution that has filed with SAFE. For trade disbursements, the WFOE must route SWIFT payments through a designated domestic bank; foreign EMIs can at most act as invoicing or treasury aggregation tools outside the PRC, not as the legal clearing rail. Recommended rating: not viable as a primary treasury rail; acceptable as a supplementary foreign-currency collection concentrator if liquidated daily to the domestic account.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File preparation is required when annual related-party tangible-asset transfers exceed RMB 200m, intangible-property transfers exceed RMB 100m, financial-asset transfers exceed RMB 100m, or other related-party transactions exceed RMB 40m. Master File preparation is required when aggregate annual related-party transactions exceed RMB 1b.

Economic substance scope: China applies substance-over-form through CIT Law Art. 47 and its General Anti-Avoidance Rule (GAAR). A China WFOE must have full substance in manufacturing, warehousing, invoicing, and risk-bearing. The Hong Kong parent, if claiming DTA benefits, must demonstrate core income-generating activities (CIGA) such as board meetings, strategic decision-making, contract negotiation, and real economic operations in Hong Kong; otherwise treaty benefits and tax residency certificates may be denied.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Failure to Withhold Outbound Dividend Tax

Statutory sanction: Tax Collection Law Art. 63: confiscate illegal gains, recover underpaid tax, impose 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax, and charge daily late penalties. Criminal Law Art. 201: evasion of tax of RMB 50,000 or more and exceeding 10% of the payable tax is punishable by up to 3 years' imprisonment; huge amounts carry 3 to 7 years.

Director personal liability: Directors who ordered the evasion are personally liable for restitution of diverted corporate assets under Company Law Art. 149. Courts may lift the corporate veil under Company Law Art. 20 for abuse of corporate personality, subject a director's personal assets to tax recovery, impose travel bans/blacklisting under the joint tax enforcement memorandum, and imprison responsible directors.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation and Deficient TP Documentation

Statutory sanction: CIT Law Art. 41 and SAT Announcement [2016] No. 42 require arm's-length pricing and contemporaneous documentation. Under the Special Tax Adjustments Measures, tax authorities may perform a transfer pricing audit, reassess taxable income by reference to comparable independent enterprises, impose interest on the adjustment, and assess penalties under Tax Collection Law Art. 35 when the taxpayer fails to provide accurate documentation.

Director personal liability: Directors and the responsible finance officer face joint and several liability for resulting underpaid tax if they signed false financial declarations; civil asset restitution; delisting and blacklisting of the company can extend to travel restrictions for non-cooperation. In egregious cases of fraudulent invoicing, criminal liability follows under Tax Collection Law Art. 63 and Criminal Law Art. 205 for false special-value-added-tax invoices.
Shell Company / Beneficial Ownership Abuse and Treaty Shopping

Statutory sanction: CIT Law Art. 47 and Implementation Regulations Art. 120 allow tax authorities to recharacterize the arrangement and deny the DTA benefit; China may impose the full 10% dividend withholding and assess interest plus late-payment surcharges. The Inland Revenue Department of Hong Kong may refuse a TRC or revoke exemption on profits derived from China if the Hong Kong entity lacks commercial substance.

Director personal liability: Directors who make false treaty-benefit claims risk personal civil liability for the tax shortfall plus damages to minority members or creditors. They may be blacklisted for tax fraud, deprived of travel permits under cross-border enforcement arrangements, and prosecuted for criminal tax fraud with imprisonment up to 7 years for aggravated amounts.
