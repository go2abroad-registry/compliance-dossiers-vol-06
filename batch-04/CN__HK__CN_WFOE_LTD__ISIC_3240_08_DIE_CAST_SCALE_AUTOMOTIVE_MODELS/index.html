Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

Under the PRC Foreign Investment Law and the current Company Law, there is no statutory minimum registered capital for a general WFOE and no government escrow or blocked-account requirement solely for incorporation. However, shareholders must pay subscribed registered capital in full within five years from establishment unless another law or regulation prescribes a shorter period or a special licensing regime imposes a minimum paid-up capital. Contributions must be made into a bank capital-contribution account opened after incorporation and may not be reduced or returned except through lawful capital reduction proceedings.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA); online electronic tax portal: the Electronic Taxpayer Service (e-Tax system) under the national integrated tax information system (Golden Tax Project Phase IV), accessed through the provincial tax authority for the WFOE's registered location and used for tax registration, monthly/quarterly filings, VAT invoice administration, payment, and export rebate applications.

E-government filing portal

The WFOE obtains its statutory taxpayer identifier automatically through the multi-license integration procedure: upon issuance of the business license by the State Administration for Market Regulation, the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) is issued and serves as the taxpayer identification number (TIN/VAT registration number). Within 30 days after the business license is issued, the WFOE must complete tax registration information through the Electronic Taxpayer Service or at the in-charge tax office, submitting incorporation documents, legal representative identity, registered address, business scope, capital structure, bank deposit account information, and expected tax-filing arrangements. For a foreign non-resident entity that is not incorporated in mainland China but derives Chinese-source income, the non-resident enterprise must apply for a non-resident tax identification code with the in-charge tax authority by filing the foreign certificate of incorporation, constitutional documents, board resolution appointing a responsible person or Chinese tax agent, and the underlying contract or evidence of Chinese-source income; this registration enables proper withholding tax, VAT, and e-invoice compliance.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No turnover threshold for VAT registration: every WFOE must register for VAT. For general taxpayer status, the mandatory annual taxable sales threshold is RMB 5,000,000; if annual taxable turnover is below RMB 5,000,000 (or monthly taxable sales do not exceed RMB 100,000 for small-scale taxpayer exemption purposes), the entity may remain a small-scale taxpayer but has no legal right to avoid registration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A WFOE exporting die-cast scale automotive models is eligible for the zero VAT rate (0% output VAT) and applicable input VAT refund/export rebate on the statutory conditions that the goods are: (1) actually shipped out of the customs territory of the PRC and declared to China Customs under an export customs declaration in the WFOE's name (or through a licensed export agent); (2) sold to an overseas buyer under an export sales contract; (3) not designated as prohibited or restricted export goods; (4) paid by the overseas buyer through cross-border foreign exchange or approved cross-border RMB settlement routed through a designated bank; and (5) supported by commercial invoices, packing lists, shipping documents, and customs export records. Qualifying offshore digital services are not within this goods-based scope; for the die-cast collectibles business, only physical goods exports are zero-rated.

  • Customs Export Declaration Form (export clearance record from the General Administration of Customs) evidencing the date and value of goods departed from the PRC territory
  • Original Bill of Lading or Air Waybill issued by the carrier evidencing shipment to the foreign buyer's destination
  • Export sales contract, commercial invoice, and packing list matching the declared customs value
  • Foreign exchange receipt evidence from a designated bank under the SAFE goods trade monitoring system, showing repatriation of export proceeds from the offshore buyer in foreign currency or approved cross-border RMB
  • VAT invoice and export tax refund declaration (where an input VAT refund is claimed) together with export rebate supporting documents

China mandates VAT invoice issuance through the State Taxation Administration's unified VAT invoicing and anti-fraud tax-control system under Golden Tax Project Phase IV. Fully digitalized e-invoices are generated through the national e-invoice platform and submitted to the tax authority in real time; every invoice receives the SAT's anti-fraud digital validation marker, and invoice data is cleared through the taxpayer's e-tax account before delivery to the recipient. B2B export sales require issuance of a valid VAT special or ordinary invoice, and the taxpayer must synchronize invoice data, VAT return data, and export customs clearance data under the integrated e-tax system.

If an export zero-rating claim is found to be unsupported or fraudulent, the tax authority will deem the export sale taxable at 13% output VAT, withdraw any VAT refund or exemption already granted, recover the unpaid tax with a late-payment surcharge of 0.05% per day under Article 32 of the Tax Administration and Collection Law, and impose a penalty of 50% to 5 times the tax involved under Article 63 for fraudulent refund claims. Where customs exit records, export declarations, bills of lading, or foreign exchange repatriation evidence are falsified, the case may be referred for criminal prosecution under Article 204 of the PRC Criminal Law (fraudulent export tax rebate); the persons concerned may also be placed on the serious tax violation list and subjected to cross-border payment and exit restrictions.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China maintains current-account convertibility for goods trade, meaning that genuine export and import settlements can be converted freely at licensed banks after document inspection. However, the capital account remains regulated: foreign direct investment into the WFOE requires prior SAFE/FDI registration, a declared registered capital amount, and a licensed bank capital account. RMB cannot be unconditionally converted for capital outflow; profit remittance to the Hong Kong parent is an income item requiring board declaration, audited financial statements, tax clearance, and bank submission of the remittance application. Export proceeds do not need to be forcibly surrendered, but must be repatriated to China through the SAFE settlement and verification system, and every RMB conversion must correspond to an underlying trade transaction. The practical effect is that the treasury can achieve high liquidity for trade flows but must maintain a strict documentary file for each cross-border payment. The Hong Kong offshore CNH market is separate and open, yet the Mainland WFOE cannot use offshore CNH balances or NDFs to evade SAFE monitoring; any hedging must be executed onshore with a qualified domestic bank and supported by real exposure. Profit remittance to the Hong Kong parent also requires evidence that the WFOE has no outstanding tax disputes, that all customs and SAFE filings are current, and that the board resolution approving the dividend matches the registered corporate governance documents.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Statutory timeline: Export proceeds must be repatriated and settled within 180 days from the date of export customs declaration or within the contractually agreed collection period, whichever is earlier; banks must receive the export collection declaration and match it with customs data under the SAFE joint electronic verification system. Mandatory conversion: There is no fixed mandatory surrender ratio since China moved to a settlement-account system; the WFOE may retain 100% of eligible foreign currency receipts in an approved export settlement account or convert them to RMB at prevailing market rates. Each conversion requires supporting customs declaration, commercial invoice, packing list, logistics documents, and matching buyer identity.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE), the foreign-exchange regulatory authority of the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

PayPal Fit: High

Low for primary corporate treasury use. An offshore EMI such as Wise, Payoneer, or Airwallex cannot open a local RMB account in China in the name of the WFOE without a licensed domestic bank sponsor. Chinese FX regulations require all cross-border goods-trade receipts and payments to go through SAFE-regulated banking channels or licensed cross-border payment institutions. PayPal is permitted only as an auxiliary sales collection layer for direct consumer orders, not as a substitute for the onshore bank settlement account; all PayPal proceeds must be settled to the licensed bank account and declared to SAFE under export monitoring. Therefore, the recommended architecture is a domestic Tier-1 bank as the sole treasury spine and PayPal as a merchant acquisition front end.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The Hong Kong parent should remit USD or HKD via SWIFT to the SAFE-specified capital escrow account of the WFOE at a Tier-1 domestic bank. The remitter and beneficiary names must exactly match the FDI registration and the WFOE business license. The remittance instruction must include the SAFE FDI registration code, the purpose code for the foreign direct investment capital contribution, and the word 'capital injection' in the message. Upon receipt, the bank will perform a capital account transaction review, post funds to the capital account, and issue a receipt voucher. The WFOE must then instruct a Chinese certified public accountant to issue the capital verification report for the registered capital. Conversion of the capital funds into RMB for operating expenses must follow actual working capital needs and be accompanied by supporting documents such as purchase orders, lease agreements, payroll schedules, and utility invoices.For direct supplier payments, use SWIFT MT103 with the SAFE goods-trade purpose code, supported by customs declarations, commercial invoices, packing lists, and transport documents. For bulk raw-material imports, issue an irrevocable commercial letter of credit through the onshore relationship bank, with the Hong Kong parent or supplier as beneficiary and the WFOE as applicant. For stable open-account buyers, use documentary collection on D/P or D/A terms through the bank clearing rail. PayPal should be used only as a retail or marketplace collection channel for direct consumer orders, and all PayPal balances must be swept periodically into the licensed bank account with the transaction history retained for SAFE export verification.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low for primary corporate treasury use. An offshore EMI such as Wise, Payoneer, or Airwallex cannot open a local RMB account in China in the name of the WFOE without a licensed domestic bank sponsor. Chinese FX regulations require all cross-border goods-trade receipts and payments to go through SAFE-regulated banking channels or licensed cross-border payment institutions. PayPal is permitted only as an auxiliary sales collection layer for direct consumer orders, not as a substitute for the onshore bank settlement account; all PayPal proceeds must be settled to the licensed bank account and declared to SAFE under export monitoring. Therefore, the recommended architecture is a domestic Tier-1 bank as the sole treasury spine and PayPal as a merchant acquisition front end.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File (Contemporaneous Transfer Pricing Documentation) is mandatory if any of the following related-party transaction thresholds are exceeded: tangible asset transfers RMB 200 million; intangible asset transfers RMB 100 million; financial asset transfers RMB 100 million; other related-party transactions RMB 40 million. Master File is required when the multinational group's consolidated revenue exceeds RMB 5.5 billion.

Economic substance scope: China does not have a dedicated Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) code like offshore jurisdictions, but anti-avoidance rules under Articles 6 and 47 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law require that intercompany arrangements reflect genuine economic substance. The Core Income-Generating Activity (CIGA) doctrine is embedded in transfer pricing rules: profits must be allocated where significant people functions, assets, and risks are located. A WFOE receiving dividends or royalties without substantive activities may be disregarded or re-characterized, leading to adjustments, interest, and penalties.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Tax Administration Law Article 63: recovery of evaded tax plus late-payment interest; penalty of 50% to 500% of evaded tax; Criminal Law Article 201: if evaded tax amount exceeds RMB 100,000 and constitutes more than 10% of payable tax, imprisonment up to 3 years or criminal detention; if amount is huge and over 30%, imprisonment 3-7 years plus fines.

Director personal liability: Directors who order or conceal the evasion are deemed directly responsible. The corporate veil may be pierced under Company Law Article 20: directors and shareholders may be personally liable for company debts. Civil asset restitution, travel bans (blacklisting) under tax collection measures, and imprisonment.
Withholding Tax Failure

Statutory sanction: Tax Administration Law Article 69: the withholding agent is jointly liable to pay the unwithheld tax; penalty of 50% to 300% of the unwithheld amount. Criminal exposure for intentional non-collection under Article 203 (serious cases).

Director personal liability: Directors are held personally responsible for the decision to skip WHT; they may face personal tax liability, civil damages to the tax authority, blacklisting in cross-border customs and travel, and criminal liability if willful evasion is proven.
Shell Company / Abuse of Treaty Benefits

Statutory sanction: General Anti-Avoidance Rule (GAAR) under Enterprise Income Tax Law Article 47 and Implementation Regulation Article 120: tax authority may re-characterize the arrangement, impose adjusted tax, and charge an interest premium of up to 3 percentage points over the central bank benchmark rate (Special Tax Adjustment Measure, SAT Announcement 2017). No 50% penalty but a 0.5% monthly interest; criminal referral if fraudulent intent is found.

Director personal liability: Under the deeming rules, the Hong Kong company is a conduit, so the WFOE's Chinese tax liabilities may be imposed on the actual controllers. Directors may be subjected to civil asset clawback, personal travel bans, and criminal penalties for fraud under Article 201 of the Criminal Law if they knowingly execute sham structures.
