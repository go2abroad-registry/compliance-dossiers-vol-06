Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

For customs and foreign-exchange clearance, the China Customs Single Window and SAFE reporting are integrated with STA data.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE) with the Administration for Market Regulation and obtain the Business License bearing the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC). Step 2: The USCC is automatically transmitted to the tax authority through the national 'one-license-one-code' information sharing system; the WFOE is tax-registered and the USCC serves as the legal Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) and VAT taxpayer number. Step 3: Activate the E-Tax Bureau account, complete the taxpayer registration confirmation, record the legal representative, financial/accounting software, bank settlement account, contact information, and entity-level ownership details. Step 4: Apply for VAT General Taxpayer status if annual taxable sales will exceed RMB 5,000,000 or voluntarily if the statutory conditions are met. Step 5: Register for Full Digital Electronic Invoice capability and obtain any remaining tax-control/issuance facilities required for the taxpayer's invoicing category. Step 6: For a foreign non-resident corporate shareholder (including the Hong Kong parent), no separate Chinese TIN is required for passive holding income; where China-sourced income arises, the non-resident registers through the WFOE's withholding declarations in the E-Tax Bureau and may, where applicable, claim treaty benefits under the Mainland-Hong Kong DTA upon presenting a Hong Kong Tax Residency Certificate.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A WFOE manufacturing and exporting Color Doppler ultrasound consoles qualifies for the input-credit-refund export model for production enterprises and the export VAT zero-rating/exemption if it is a VAT General Taxpayer, the goods are physically exported from mainland China to an overseas customs territory (including Hong Kong SAR), the China Customs export declaration is issued in the exporter's name, the sales are accurately invoiced and declared in the E-Tax Bureau, the foreign currency proceeds are repatriated to China through an approved bank within 180 days (or a valid delay/dispensation is filed), and the export does not distort the underlying business purpose. Qualifying offshore digital services are not within this scope.

China operates nationwide real-time invoice clearance through the STA E-Tax Bureau and the Golden Tax Phase IV system. Full Digital Electronic Invoices (FDEI) are issued, number-stamped, encrypted and transmitted in real time through the STA electronic invoice platform; no artificial stamps are added after issuance. VAT Special Invoices must be verified and authenticated before the input tax credit can be recognized. Third-party SaaS invoicing tools must connect through STA-certified API interfaces. The invoice unique code and anti-counterfeit digital stamp operate as the fiscal seal; duplicate or backdated invoicing is blocked by the central tax database. For export sales, the e-invoice and export customs declaration data are matched automatically with the export tax refund declaration.

The STA and customs joint audit system verifies export declarations, logistics waybills, bank receipts, e-invoice data and tax refund claims. If zero-rating is claimed without proof of physical export, without timely foreign exchange repatriation, or with false or inconsistent documentation, the export tax refund and exempted output VAT are clawed back with late-payment surcharge (0.05% per day on overdue tax). For fraudulent export refunds, the enterprise must repay the amounts refunded and may be fined from one to five times the tax involved; the tax authority may suspend all future export tax rebate services and transfer the case for criminal prosecution (crime of fraudulent export tax refund) where imprisonment and criminal fines apply. The statutory limitation for retrospective clawback is five years; in cases of fraud involving the public tax fund, recovery is pursued without a limitation period.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The People's Republic of China operates a managed current-account convertibility regime administered by SAFE. Trade-related foreign exchange receipts and payments are permitted through designated banks after verification of customs declarations, sales contracts, invoices, and shipping documentation. Capital-account flows, including equity subscriptions by the Hong Kong parent, intercompany loans, debt financing, and profit repatriation, are not freely convertible and require prior SAFE registration, a valid FDI filing, and a licensed bank's compliance judgment before funds are credited or disbursed. Hong Kong SAR, by contrast, maintains full convertibility under the Common Law framework and the HKMA's Linked Exchange Rate system, with no surrender requirements for HKD, USD, or other major currencies. For the mainland WFOE, the most demanding hurdle is outward remittance of profits to the Hong Kong shareholder: the bank will require audited financial statements, completed withholding tax settlement at the treaty-reduced rate under the mainland-HK double tax arrangement, a board resolution approving the dividend, an SAFE profit repatriation filing, and proof that the capital account has been used only for the approved business scope. Current-account trade settlements remain practical for procurement of ultrasound console components and export proceeds, but equity injections, dividends, and any capital-account movement remain documentary and compliance-intensive.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE's current-account rules for goods trade, export foreign currency proceeds must be repatriated into China and credited to the WFOE's trade foreign exchange income account within 90 days of the customs export declaration. There is no statutory mandatory surrender ratio; the enterprise may retain USD, HKD, EUR, or other earned currencies in its settlement account or voluntarily convert them into RMB onshore at prevailing market rates. If payment is expected after the 90-day collection window, or if advance export proceeds have been received more than 90 days before the shipment will be exported, the WFOE must file a delayed collection or advance receipt report in the SAFE trade-monitoring system before the bank executes the transaction. Failure to report creates a compliance flag and may suspend the entity's A-class trade foreign exchange status.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the People's Bank of China (PBoC), with the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) as the separate central monetary authority for the Hong Kong SAR clearing hub.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Not viable for the mainland WFOE. PBoC and SAFE regulations restrict domestic CNY account opening and FX settlement to commercial banks and licensed non-bank payment institutions; no offshore fintech EMI legal wrapper is recognised by Chinese banking law as an authorised depository for FDI capital, export proceeds, or SAFE-registered payments. An offshore EMI could theoretically support the Hong Kong treasury vehicle's non-mainland operational cash flows, but it still requires an underlying Hong Kong bank account and cannot be used to book mainland FX settlements, perform capital verification, or satisfy export-import matching. Therefore, all capital injections, trade disbursements, and FX hedging for this medical-device exporter must be routed through a mainland Tier-1 licensed bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The Hong Kong parent shall transmit FDI capital via SWIFT MT103 to the WFOE's SAFE-registered foreign exchange capital settlement account at a mainland Tier-1 bank. The remittance instruction must cite the SAFE FDI registration number and the payment purpose code attributable to foreign direct investment capital injection. The receiving bank will place the credit into the capital settlement account pending verification. A licensed PRC certified public accountant must then issue an audited capital verification report before the capital can be converted into RMB and transferred into the general operating account. Subsequent disbursements must respect the foreign investment negative list for medical device manufacturing and cannot be deployed into securities, entrusted loans, non-operating real estate, or speculative financial products. If urgent trade payments arise, the WFOE may apply under the SAFE capital-account settlement convenience pilot, provided the full audit trail and internal governance structure remain intact.For export sales of color Doppler ultrasound consoles, the preferred clearing rail is a sight or usance commercial letter of credit issued by the buyer's bank and advised through the WFOE's mainland bank; SWIFT MT700 and MT707 messages govern LC issuance, amendments, and documentary compliance. For smaller distributors and after-sales service parts, the WFOE may accept SWIFT MT103 telegraphic transfers, but each payment must be supported by a signed sale contract, commercial invoice, transport document, packing list, certificate of origin, and inspection certificate. Documentary collections such as D/P and D/A are permissible only for low-risk repeat buyers in non-sanctioned jurisdictions and must be routed through the same advising bank to preserve continuity of the audit trail. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: Not viable for the mainland WFOE. PBoC and SAFE regulations restrict domestic CNY account opening and FX settlement to commercial banks and licensed non-bank payment institutions; no offshore fintech EMI legal wrapper is recognised by Chinese banking law as an authorised depository for FDI capital, export proceeds, or SAFE-registered payments. An offshore EMI could theoretically support the Hong Kong treasury vehicle's non-mainland operational cash flows, but it still requires an underlying Hong Kong bank account and cannot be used to book mainland FX settlements, perform capital verification, or satisfy export-import matching. Therefore, all capital injections, trade disbursements, and FX hedging for this medical-device exporter must be routed through a mainland Tier-1 licensed bank.
