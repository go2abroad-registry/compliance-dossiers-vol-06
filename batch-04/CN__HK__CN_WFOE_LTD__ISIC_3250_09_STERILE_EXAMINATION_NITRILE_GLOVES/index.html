Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum registered capital is prescribed for a general manufacturing WFOE under PRC Company Law. The Articles of Association must state the registered capital in RMB or an acceptable foreign currency, the contribution period, and the method of contribution (cash, equipment, or receivables). No government-blocked escrow account is required as a matter of national company law. However, for a sterile nitrile examination glove manufacturing project, the registered capital amount should be sufficient to cover industrial site acquisition or lease, dipping and curing lines, cleanroom and sterilization equipment, environmental infrastructure, and working capital, and paid-in funds must be remitted through a domestic foreign-currency capital account opened at a SAFE-designated bank. The foreign investor must report paid-in capital contributions and beneficial ownership through the Ministry of Commerce Foreign Investment Information System within the statutory deadline.

Funding & unlock path: The plant must obtain a Civil Defense Fire Safety Design Review Certificate and a Fire Safety Completion Acceptance Certificate from the Fire and Rescue Division for the production building, warehousing areas, sterilization rooms, and chemical storage rooms. Structural load approval must be obtained for dipping tank supports, drying ovens, curing lines, overhead conveyors, and mezzanine storage. Occupancies must comply with workplace safety and health codes, including emergency evacuation routes, fire suppression systems, gas detection, flammable liquid storage, and hazardous chemical safety assessment. Boilers, pressure vessels, sterilizers, and lifts must be registered with the market regulation special equipment safety supervision authority. The WFOE must also complete an occupational hazard risk evaluation and a control effect evaluation under the Law on Prevention and Control of Occupational Diseases before commencing operations.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA), accessed through the China Electronic Taxpayer Service Platform (e-Tax); the Hong Kong SAR element of the structure is under the Inland Revenue Department (IRD) for profits tax purposes only.

E-government filing portal

Upon incorporation, the WFOE receives an 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) from the State Administration for Market Regulation; the USCC is the taxpayer identification number. The WFOE must then complete tax registration through the e-Tax portal with the competent STA tax authority, filing legal representative details, accounting and bookkeeping rules, bank account information, and payroll data. For export of nitrile gloves, the WFOE must complete customs registration with the General Administration of Customs, complete the Ministry of Commerce foreign trade operator filing, register as a VAT general taxpayer, and enrol in the STA export tax refund/exemption system. A foreign non-resident parent should file Non-Resident Enterprise Tax Registration with the STA to obtain a non-resident tax record for PRC withholding tax; if relying on the Mainland/Hong Kong double tax arrangement, the parent should obtain a Certificate of Hong Kong Resident Status from the IRD and submit it to the STA.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The WFOE may claim the 0% export VAT rate for nitrile examination gloves only if it is a registered VAT general taxpayer; the gloves are physically exported from mainland China under customs supervision and reported on a GACC export customs declaration; the sale is made to a non-PRC buyer; the WFOE retains a commercial invoice, transport document, and payment instructions from outside China; the export proceeds are repatriated to a SAFE-designated bank account within the statutory deadline; and the STA export tax refund/exemption declaration is filed electronically. Qualifying cross-border offshore services may also be zero-rated if the service is supplied to a non-PRC customer and performed outside mainland China, but goods must satisfy the physical export test.

  • Customs Declaration Form for Export Goods with the GACC export declaration number
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical shipment from the PRC to the offshore destination
  • Export Proceeds Collection Certificate from a SAFE-designated bank, being the mainland equivalent of a foreign inward remittance certificate
  • Commercial invoice and packing list matching the export customs declaration
  • Export tax refund/exemption declaration filed through the STA electronic export tax refund system

The STA mandates issuance of fully digitalized electronic invoices through the STA Electronic Invoice Service Platform. Each invoice carries a unique national invoice number, dynamic QR code, and encrypted digital signature; issuance data is transmitted to the STA in real time. Golden Tax Phase IV automatically cross-checks invoice data, customs declarations, bank settlement records, and export logistics. VAT special invoices are cleared before input credit can be claimed; approved ERP and accounting systems must use the STA e-invoice API interface. Sales returns and credit notes must be processed as red-letter electronic invoices in the platform. For exports, the zero-rating claim is electronically linked to the GACC export declaration and the SAFE-designated bank's foreign exchange receipt record. Hong Kong SAR has no indirect-tax fiscal clearance mandate.

Under the Tax Collection and Administration Law of the People's Republic of China, the STA and GACC will deny or claw back the export zero-rating benefit if the customs declaration, transport documents, foreign exchange receipts, or invoice records are false, incomplete, or inconsistent. The enterprise will be required to repay the VAT refunded, pay late-payment surcharge of 0.05% per day, and may be subject to an administrative fine ranging from 50% to 500% of the tax shortfall for fraudulent conduct. Criminal tax fraud liability can arise under the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China for a deliberately fraudulent export tax refund. SAFE may also impose monetary penalties for failure to repatriate export proceeds within 180 days, and GACC can sanction false export declarations. Golden Tax Phase IV audit scrutiny remains open for the statutory limitation period and may require full documentary substantiation of the entire trade chain.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a current-account convertible but capital-account controlled currency regime. For this medical-PPE manufacturing WFOE, ordinary trade-related import and export receipts can be converted to CNY at onshore designated banks under SAFE's total-quantity trade monitoring framework, without case-by-case approval. However, capital account flows such as FDI equity injection, shareholder loans, cross-border debt, and profit repatriation require strict SAFE registration, true-to-use verification, and documentary compliance. The RMB is not freely convertible offshore; every cross-border movement must be linked to an underlying goods, service, or registered capital transaction. There is no statutory mandatory conversion ratio: since the SAFE reforms, export proceeds may be retained 100% in foreign currency or sold to banks at the onshore market rate. Nevertheless, goods-export proceeds must be repatriated to a licensed onshore goods-trade foreign exchange account, generally within 180 days from customs export declaration unless an approved extension is granted. Profit remittance to the Hong Kong parent is permitted only after annual audit, corporate income tax settlement, tax clearance, and bank verification of a board-approved profit distribution resolution, with SAFE-monitored outward remittance purpose codes.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory surrender percentage currently applies; the effective conversion ratio is 0% surrendered, meaning 100% of export foreign currency receipts may be retained in an onshore goods-trade foreign exchange settlement account or voluntarily converted into CNY at the prevailing spot rate. Statutory repatriation for ordinary exports is required within 180 days from the date of customs export declaration (goods shipment), with advance receipts requiring the enterprise to register under SAFE's trade monitoring system and declare the expected export date within 30 days. The designated receiving bank verifies customs declarations, commercial invoices, logistics documentation, and received remittances through the SAFE cross-border payment monitoring platform before allowing credit or conversion.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC), with the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) as the delegated foreign-exchange administrative enforcement authority

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Offshore digital EMI and fintech neo-banking platforms are not viable as primary operating accounts for a China-domiciled WFOE. They are not licensed deposit-taking institutions in mainland China, cannot hold the WFOE's capital account, and are legally barred from performing the onshore trade verification and SAFE reporting required for CNY clearing, export proceeds repatriation, or FDI capital settlement. They can at best function as auxiliary overseas receivables collection rails through a licensed onshore bank partner, but they cannot replace a Tier-1 domestic commercial bank account for regulatory, treasury, or tax purposes. The correct architecture is an onshore bank as the anchor account with the EMI prohibited from servicing any capital-account or statutory-trade flow.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity funding from the Hong Kong shareholder shall be routed as FDI through the WFOE's SAFE-registered capital settlement account at the designated Tier-1 onshore bank. The remitting bank in Hong Kong must issue an SWIFT MT103 with remittance purpose clearly stated as 'Direct Investment - Equity Injection for WFOE Paid-In Capital Increase', including the name of the Chinese investee company and the registered FDI filing confirmation code. Before settlement into CNY, the receiving bank will perform a true-to-use review against the WFOE's business permit, foreign investment information report, SAFE FDI registration, and board resolution. The converted CNY must be used only for approved operating expenditures within the business scope of sterile nitrile glove manufacturing; capital-fund diversion into equity, securities, entrusted loans, or real estate is strictly prohibited. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Offshore digital EMI and fintech neo-banking platforms are not viable as primary operating accounts for a China-domiciled WFOE. They are not licensed deposit-taking institutions in mainland China, cannot hold the WFOE's capital account, and are legally barred from performing the onshore trade verification and SAFE reporting required for CNY clearing, export proceeds repatriation, or FDI capital settlement. They can at best function as auxiliary overseas receivables collection rails through a licensed onshore bank partner, but they cannot replace a Tier-1 domestic commercial bank account for regulatory, treasury, or tax purposes. The correct architecture is an onshore bank as the anchor account with the EMI prohibited from servicing any capital-account or statutory-trade flow.
