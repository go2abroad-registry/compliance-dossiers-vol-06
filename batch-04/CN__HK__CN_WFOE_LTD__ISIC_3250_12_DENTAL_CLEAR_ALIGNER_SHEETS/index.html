Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods are eligible for 0% export VAT and refund where the WFOE is a general VAT taxpayer, the dental clear aligner sheets are physically exported from China through customs, a valid sales contract exists, an export declaration is completed at the port of departure, the goods are not re-imported, and the foreign sales proceeds are repatriated to China within the statutory timeline; qualifying cross-border offshore services also carry zero-rating when performed for overseas businesses.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Export Goods Declaration) confirming customs clearance of the aligner sheets
  • Bill of Lading or Combined Transport Bill of Lading covering the full export route

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a managed floating exchange-rate regime and maintains selective capital-account controls while honoring current-account convertibility for legitimate trade, service and income flows. For a mainland-incorporated WFOE, the functional currency is RMB; all foreign-exchange receipts must either be received into a domestic foreign-exchange settlement account or converted to RMB through a designated bank that undertakes payment-in/use-in verification. The capital account is not freely convertible: paid-in equity from the Hong Kong shareholder must be registered with SAFE under the FDI information registration, deposited in a SAFE-registered capital account, and each RMB conversion from capital funds must be matched against actual business invoices and a genuine goods/services background. Dividend remittances to the Hong Kong parent are permissible only after completion of the annual audit, corporate income tax clearance, proper withholding at 5% if the Hong Kong substance qualifies under the China-Hong Kong double-taxation arrangement, and board resolution approval. Since the Hong Kong parent is in an offshore-special-territory jurisdiction, the bank will treat the intra-group flow as a non-resident investor remittance and will require underlying contracts, invoices and equity/subscription documents. Overall, current-account payments for raw materials and trade services proceed with documentary verification; capital-account flows remain tightly monitored through the SAFE trade/transaction monitoring system.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export receipts from dental clear aligner sheet sales must be repatriated to China through a designated foreign-exchange bank within 180 days from the Customs export declaration date, unless SAFE grants an extension based on contractually justified open-account trade terms. There is no mandatory fixed conversion ratio in the current voluntary-settlement regime: 100% of export foreign-currency proceeds may be retained in the enterprise's foreign-exchange settlement account or converted to RMB voluntarily. Every settlement must correspond to genuine exported goods, and the exporter must maintain accurate entries in the SAFE Trade Monitoring System; A-class enterprises obtain automated verification, while B/C-class enterprises face prior review and enhanced documentation.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) as the central bank, with the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) as its delegated foreign-exchange administration authority for regulation, approvals and monitoring.

Master / Local File threshold: RMB 200 million annual related-party tangible-asset transactions, RMB 100 million annual intangible/financial-asset transactions, and RMB 40 million annual other related-party transactions trigger the mandatory Local File under SAT Announcement 2016-42. A Master File is required where the consolidated group's related-party turnover exceeds RMB 1 billion. The WFOE's transaction volume of dental clear-aligner sheet sales to the HK distributor must be monitored on an accrual basis; even if below the threshold, a sufficient transfer-pricing study should be prepared because cross-border intra-group sales of high-profit medical devices are a high audit priority.

Economic substance scope: Mainland China does not impose a separate Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) statute on ordinary WFOEs, but the WFOE must prove genuine business substance through physical manufacturing, payroll, R&D, board control and decision-making in China. For the Hong Kong parent claiming DTA benefits, Hong Kong's FSIE regime effective 1 January 2023 requires Core Income-Generating Activities (CIGA) for passive income (dividends, interest, royalties) to take place in Hong Kong; the parent must have a credible local office, employees and decision records. Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Corporate Income Tax Evasion through Transfer Pricing Manipulation

Statutory sanction: Enterprise Income Tax Law Article 41 and Tax Collection and Administrative Law (TCAL) Article 63 impose recovery of the underpaid tax plus a daily surcharge of 0.05% and an administrative penalty of 0.5 to 5 times the underpaid tax. Where the evaded tax amount is 'relatively large' and exceeds 10% of the tax payable, Criminal Law Article 201 imposes imprisonment up to three years and a fine; if the amount is 'huge' and exceeds 30% of the tax due, imprisonment of three to seven years applies.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil may be pierced under PRC Company Law Article 20 where the WFOE is a mere instrument of the HK group and understated income; directors are jointly and severally liable for the equalised state loss through civil restitution. Directors who knowingly sign false TP reports face personal criminal liability, a travel ban (blacklisting) under TCAL Article 44, freezing of personal assets, confiscation, and disqualification from acting as company directors in China.
Abusive Treaty Shopping / Fake Hong Kong Conduit (DTA Abuse)

Statutory sanction: The tax authority will apply the beneficial ownership rules in SAT Announcement 2018-9 and the General Anti-Avoidance Rule under EIT Law Article 47 to deny treaty relief; the WFOE, as withholding agent, will owe the shortfall (10% statutory minus 5% claimed) plus interest and a penalty of 50% to 300% of the under-withheld amount under TCAL Article 69. Fraudulent treaty claims may also constitute tax evasion under TCAL Article 63.

Director personal liability: Directors and the ultimate beneficial owner are treated as withholding agents and are personally liable for the tax certificate shortfall; courts may order restitution from their personal estates and revoke passports or impose exit bans until the tax liability is settled. Criminal liability for fraudulent use of treaty documents can be prosecuted under Criminal Law Article 201 for tax evasion, resulting in up to seven years' imprisonment, asset forfeiture and permanent blacklisting for the directors.
Shell-Company Fraud / Money Laundering through Cross-Border Payment Rails

Statutory sanction: Criminal Law Article 205 punishes false issuance of special VAT invoices with imprisonment from three years up to life; Article 191 imposes imprisonment of five to ten years and confiscation for money laundering; TCAL Article 63 also imposes the 0.5 to 5 times underpaid-tax penalty. PRC AML Law imposes additional civil sanctions including freezing of bank accounts and suspension of the business license.

Director personal liability: The legal representative and financial controller who signed the fake invoices face individual criminal liability, not merely corporate penalties; personal assets can be seized for restitution of the evaded tax and the invoicing fraud. The court may ban the directors from leaving China through a travel blacklist, and a criminal conviction triggers lifetime disqualification from corporate management in China as well as notice to Interpol where extradition is possible.
