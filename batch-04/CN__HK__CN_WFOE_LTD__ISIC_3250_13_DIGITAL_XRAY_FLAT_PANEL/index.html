No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to a WFOE formed for ISIC 3250 under the PRC Foreign Investment Law and Company Law. Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (www.chinatax.gov.cn) and the provincial/municipal tax services through the electronic tax bureau (e-tax) portal; the General Administration of Customs of the PRC is the customs counterpart for export clearance and export VAT refund data exchange.

E-government filing portal

1) The WFOE is incorporated under the PRC Foreign Investment Law and obtains a business license from the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR); the SAMR assigns the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which serves as the statutory tax identification number (TIN) for all PRC tax purposes, including VAT. 2) Through the multi-license integration reform, SAMR automatically transmits the incorporation record to the competent tax authority of the State Taxation Administration; the WFOE must complete the 'new taxpayer package' in the electronic tax bureau within 30 days of license issuance, confirming the legal representative, accounting system, bank settlement account, invoice requirements, and VAT taxpayer status. 3) The competent tax authority activates the taxpayer master file and issues an electronic tax registration certificate containing the USCC; no separate TIN is issued because the USCC is the unified taxpayer ID. 4) For a foreign non-resident entity, such as the Hong Kong parent or an offshore supplier without a permanent establishment in mainland China, the WFOE as payer or customs declearant is the statutory withholding agent; the non-resident entity is assigned a Chinese non-resident taxpayer file code when the WFOE files the withholding return with the tax authority. The non-resident must provide its home-jurisdiction tax ID and a Hong Kong tax residence certificate when claiming treaty benefits under the Mainland and Hong Kong Double Tax Arrangement. 5) A non-resident entity does not obtain a Chinese VAT ID merely because it has a Chinese withholding obligation; it becomes VAT-registered in China only if it itself makes taxable supplies in mainland China or imports goods.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None. The PRC does not operate a check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election system. A resident enterprise, including a WFOE, is statutorily taxed at entity level under the Enterprise Income Tax Law. The only material election is indirect-tax-oriented: a WFOE may elect to remain a VAT small-scale taxpayer subject to a 3% levy if annual taxable sales do not exceed CNY 5,000,000, or may voluntarily register as a VAT general taxpayer to claim input VAT credit and export refunds. No other entity classification election is available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No turnover threshold for VAT registration; registration is mandatory for all enterprises within 30 days of business license issuance. Mandatory VAT general taxpayer registration arises once annual taxable sales exceed CNY 5,000,000; entities below this threshold may elect small-scale taxpayer status or voluntary general taxpayer registration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating for exported goods applies under the PRC provisional VAT rules and Caishui [2012] No. 39 where: (i) the WFOE is a registered VAT general taxpayer; (ii) the manufactured goods are physically cleared through the PRC customs as exports and the customs release date is evidenced by the PRC Customs Export Declaration Form; (iii) the sale is made to an overseas purchaser, including a Hong Kong-related trading counterparty, with title and risk transferred outside mainland China; (iv) the export sales price is arm's length and expressed in a foreign/offshore currency; and (v) export proceeds are repatriated through a designated PRC foreign-exchange bank. Manufacturing exporters apply the 'exemption, credit, refund' method, and qualifying cross-border digital services, if separately supplied and consumed entirely outside China, may also be zero-rated when supported by cross-border contracts, service performance evidence, and offshore payment receipts.

  • PRC Customs Export Declaration Form (the statutory export declaration released by the General Administration of Customs, evidencing the customs clearance/export date)
  • Negotiable Bill of Lading, Master Air Waybill, or other customs-recognized transport document evidencing physical outward movement of goods from mainland China
  • Commercial invoice and packing list for the export sale
  • Export sales contract or purchase order with the overseas buyer
  • Export proceeds collection voucher issued by a PRC-designated foreign exchange bank, constituting the Chinese statutory equivalent of an inward remittance certificate and evidencing receipt of payment from the non-resident buyer
  • VAT special invoices for procurement of raw materials, components, and services used in producing the exported goods, plus customs import VAT payment vouchers for any imported inputs
  • Electronic export VAT refund declaration submitted through the electronic tax bureau under the export tax refund management system

All VAT invoices must be issued through the State Taxation Administration's VAT invoice control system. The WFOE must onboard to the national full-data electronic invoice platform, where each digital e-invoice carries a unique invoice number, a 20-digit invoice code, an anti-counterfeit QR code, and a digital signature/seal applied by the tax authority at the moment of issuance; the invoice data is transmitted to the tax authority in near real time and validated before the buyer may claim input VAT credit. Large-volume issuers may integrate their ERP with the electronic tax bureau through the official e-invoice API using the STA's prescribed mutual-authentication and encryption standards. Paper special VAT invoices remain subject to physical anti-counterfeit stamping and mandatory online authentication, while fully digital e-invoices require no manual stamping. The tax authority automatically cross-matches invoice issue data with VAT returns, customs export release records, and foreign exchange receipt data.

Export VAT refund claims are scrutinized through an automated cross-check of customs export declarations, bank foreign-exchange receipt records, transport documents, and purchase-side VAT special invoices. If the mandatory documentation package is incomplete, inconsistent, or unverifiable, the zero-rating is reversed and the export is recharacterized as a domestic taxable sale, requiring output VAT at the standard 13% rate plus statutory late-payment surcharge and interest. Where fraud is established through false customs declarations, forged transport documents, circular foreign-exchange arrangements, or fabricated invoices, the tax authority recovers the refunded tax and imposes a fine of one to five times the amount fraudulently obtained under Article 66 of the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection and Levy, and criminal liability for export tax fraud may attach. The retroactive clawback period is generally three years, extended to five years for special circumstances, and is unlimited for fraud. The exporter is also downgraded in the export tax refund classification system, which can suspend advance refunds and trigger enhanced post-clearance audits.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Mainland China maintains current-account convertibility for genuine trade and service transactions, but capital-account items remain subject to SAFE registration, designated bank validation, and post-payment monitoring. For the CN_WFOE_LTD Hong Kong-owned medical-device manufacturing entity, equity capital injections are treated as foreign direct investment (FDI) and require FIE registration before SAFE and declaration through the AMATS system. Once the capital account is registered, the HK parent may wire funds into the WFOE's capital account at a licensed domestic bank; the proceeds may be settled into RMB for permitted operating purposes, but not for stock market, real estate, entrusted loans, or non-FIE foreign debt unless separately registered. Profit remittance to the Hong Kong parent is a current-account-linked capital outflow but still requires audited financial statements, corporate income tax settlement and payment proof, board resolution, and SAFE declaration at the remitting bank. Export proceeds from x-ray detector sales are current-account receipts and may be retained in a foreign-currency account or directly settled into RMB; the bank will verify underlying export customs declarations and invoices. Convertibility is therefore functional for ordinary trade, but structurally conditional for capital flows.

Export proceeds surrender rules

After export, the WFOE is required to receive goods-trade foreign exchange proceeds back into a domestic goods-trade foreign exchange account as soon as the shipment is completed. If the receipt is delayed beyond 90 days from the export customs declaration date, the WFOE must register the overdue payment with SAFE and provide supporting documentary evidence. There is no mandatory national conversion ratio: 0% of export foreign currency receipts must be converted to RMB. The entity may keep 100% of proceeds in a retained foreign exchange account and independently choose when to settle into RMB through its designated bank, subject to the matching of the export contract, customs declaration data, VAT invoice, and the trade receipts verification platform.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China, operating under the People's Bank of China (PBOC) as the central bank authority

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Onshore clearing viability is Low. Digital EMI services such as Airwallex, Currenxie, and WISE are not licensed as deposit-taking banks or authorized foreign-exchange settlement banks by the PBOC or SAFE. They cannot open a CNY Renminbi current account for a WFOE, cannot clear WFOE VAT-included trade payments, and cannot obtain SAFE's decremental FX settlement license. Mainland WFOE must use domestic commercial banks for RMB and all cross-border FX. Offshore HK-side front office can use an EMI for trade facilitation such as collecting EUR/USD from international OEM customers before converting and upstreaming to the WFOE via the HK holding entity, but the EMI rail cannot be the sole clearing corridor; each underlying trade invoice must still be supported by banking-issued SWIFT instructions. Medium viability therefore exists only around HK treasury optimization and transaction reconciliation, not as a substitute for mainland corporate banking infrastructure.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File preparation is mandatory in a tax year where controlled transactions exceed the following thresholds: RMB 200 million for tangible property transfers; RMB 100 million for intangible property transfers; RMB 100 million for financial asset transfers; RMB 100 million for intercompany services; and RMB 50 million for related-party interest. Master File preparation is required when the multinational group's consolidated annual revenue in the preceding fiscal year is at or above RMB 5.5 billion and the Chinese constituent entity has controlled transactions meeting the applicable Local File threshold.

Economic substance scope: The PRC does not recognize offshore economic substance by itself. The WFOE must undertake its core income-generating activities in China, including manufacturing, inventory risk, marketing support, and operational decision-making. For the Hong Kong intermediate parent to receive treaty benefits, it must demonstrate genuine management and control in Hong Kong, substantive business premises, employees, and independent risk bearing. The China-Hong Kong DTA favorable rates will be denied under the general anti-abuse rule and the beneficial ownership doctrine if the HK company is a pass-through entity with no economic substance.

Shell Company / DTA Treaty Abuse

Director personal liability: Under PRC Company Law Article 23, directors and actual controllers who abuse the corporate form to evade taxes are jointly and severally liable for civil restitution. The legal representative of the WFOE or the HK parent can be blacklisted under the cross-department joint disciplinary measures for dishonest taxpayers, which includes a travel ban and exit restriction. Where the evasion is criminal, Criminal Law Article 201 subjects the responsible persons to imprisonment up to 3 years for a relatively large evasion and up to 7 years where the amount is especially large.
Transfer Pricing / Profit Shifting

Statutory sanction: A special tax adjustment under CIT Law Articles 41, 42, 46 and 48, with the tax authority adjusting the taxable income upward. A daily interest charge of 0.05% is applied from the year the tax should have been paid. If the mispricing is supported by false invoices or fraudulent books, the penalty escalates to 50% to 5 times the evaded tax under Tax Administration Law Article 63, and the transfer pricing documentation failure shifts the burden of proof to the taxpayer.

Director personal liability: The chief financial officer, legal representative, and actual controller are exposed to personal liability if they directed, signed, or approved false intercompany invoices or fabricated cost records. Criminal prosecution can be brought for tax evasion, with seizure and return of diverted assets, fines, and imprisonment under Criminal Law Article 201.
