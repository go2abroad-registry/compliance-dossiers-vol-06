Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China, acting through the competent local STA tax service bureau in the province/city where the WFOE is domiciled; online portal: National Electronic Tax Administration platform (e-Tax) at https://etax.chinatax.gov.cn.

E-government filing portal

The WFOE is first incorporated with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) and receives a business license containing the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC). Within 30 days of SAMR issuance, the WFOE must complete basic tax registration on the e-Tax platform; the USCC is the statutory TIN for VAT and corporate income tax, and the tax, customs, market regulation, public security, banking and foreign-exchange systems are linked electronically. The WFOE must then elect or be forced into VAT taxpayer status: mandatory general taxpayer status applies when annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000; below that threshold it may remain a small-scale VAT taxpayer or voluntarily register as a general taxpayer. A foreign non-resident entity that merely owns the WFOE does not obtain a PRC VAT registration; if it derives China-source income without an establishment, it must complete non-resident withholding tax registration on the e-Tax platform (or through a Chinese withholding agent) and receive a non-resident enterprise tax withholding registration code. Hong Kong SAR is a separate customs and VAT territory: a Hong Kong holding company is not automatically registered for mainland PRC VAT by reason of its WFOE ownership; goods sent from mainland China to Hong Kong SAR are exports for PRC VAT purposes.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The PRC maintains a managed floating exchange rate regime in which the renminbi is only partially convertible. Current account transactions, including cross-border procurement of organic matcha powder, can be processed with relative ease once the designated bank verifies the underlying trade documents and completes SAFE trade monitoring records. Payment for import goods may be settled in RMB or foreign currency through a licenced commercial bank after customs declaration, contract and invoice validation. Capital account transactions, however, remain tightly controlled. A wholly foreign-owned enterprise must register every inbound shareholder capital injection with SAFE, convert funds only after obtaining a valid FDI registration/QR code, and use the proceeds in accordance with the approved business scope. Dividends and profit remittances to the Hong Kong parent are not automatic: the WFOE must submit an annual audited financial statement, PRC corporate income tax filing, withholding tax voucher, and board resolution confirming the distributable amount. The absence of full RMB convertibility means that all conversions are transaction-specific and must be supported by documentary evidence; any mismatch among contracts, invoices, customs declarations and payment instructions can lead to SAFE inquiry, suspension of FX services, or account freezing.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the current SAFE legal framework, the old mandatory full surrender of export proceeds has been abolished. Exporters may retain 100% of eligible export receipts in foreign currency or convert any portion into RMB at the prevailing market rate, with no fixed compulsory conversion ratio. However, export proceeds must still be repatriated to the exporter's mainland China designated foreign exchange account within 180 days of the customs export declaration, unless an extension or deferred collection arrangement is approved by the designated bank/SAFE. The bank will match the incoming remittance against the customs declaration and the trade-funds electronic monitoring system, and any overdue collection must be reported through the monetary trade collection adjustment process.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China, acting under the statutory mandate of the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low - legally barred from acting as the primary banking layer. A mainland China-incorporated WFOE must hold its settlement accounts and capital accounts at a licensed PRC commercial bank. Foreign fintech EMIs such as Airwallex, Currenxie or Revolut Business are not licensed as deposit-taking institutions in China and cannot open a local RMB settlement account, receive FDI capital injections, or authenticate export proceeds with SAFE. They may only act as unpaid auxiliary payment-aggregation tools through a regulated partner bank, and even then the WFOE must maintain its own compliant domestic account. Relying on an EMI as the principal clearing rail would create a regulatory and AML gap unacceptable to SAFE, the PBOC, and the external auditor.

Mandatory Audit Threshold Small Company Audit Exemption Regime

Turnover: Under the Hong Kong Companies Ordinance, every company must have its annual financial statements audited by a certified public accountant unless it qualifies for the small private company audit exemption. A private company qualifies as small when it satisfies at least two of three thresholds for the financial year: (i) annual revenue not exceeding HKD 10,000,000; (ii) total assets not exceeding HKD 10,000,000; and (iii) not more than 50 employees. Where the company is a parent entity, the exemption is available only if the consolidated group also satisfies the same small-company tests on aggregate revenue, assets and employee headcount. Audit exemption is denied for banking, insurance, securities and other companies regulated by the Hong Kong Monetary Authority, Insurance Authority or Securities and Futures Commission. Any company crossing the balance-sheet, turnover or headcount thresholds, or required by its articles or a shareholder agreement, must undergo an independent statutory audit in accordance with Hong Kong Standards on Auditing.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% under PRC Enterprise Income Tax Law Article 3(3) and Article 37; reduced to 5% under the China-HK DTA Article 10 if the Hong Kong resident beneficial owner holds at least 25% of the WFOE's share capital and meets the substance and beneficial-ownership requirements.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Annual EIT return and payment must be filed within 5 months of fiscal year-end (by 31 May of the following year); quarterly prepayment returns plus tax installments are due within 15 days after each quarter-end.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: The PRC SAT Public Notice 2016 No. 42 threshold requires a Local File when annual related-party tangible-property transactions exceed RMB 200 million, or when related-party intangible, financial-asset or service transactions exceed RMB 100 million; a Master File is required when aggregate related-party transactions exceed RMB 1 billion.

Economic substance scope: Although the BVI-style Economic Substance Regulations do not apply directly to a PRC WFOE, Chinese revenue authorities apply the general anti-avoidance rule and substance-over-form to deny treaty benefits where the Hong Kong intermediate does not perform core income-generating activities (CIGA). The WFOE itself must have real employees, procurement contracts, quality control, inventory risk, and pricing decisions in China. A Hong Kong company that is only a mailbox with no office, no staff, no local board decision-making, and no risk management will be treated as a conduit and denied DTA relief, and the Chinese entity may be reclassified as a permanent establishment or a sham.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Transfer Pricing / Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Under PRC EIT Law Article 41 and SAT Public Notice 2016 No. 42, the tax authority may make a transfer pricing adjustment, impose interest on the underpaid tax, and levy a penalty under Tax Administration Law Article 63 of 50% to 5 times the underpaid tax. The unreported profit is treated as disguised dividend or service income subject to additional withholding.

Director personal liability: A director who knowingly signs the false tax return or approves the sham pricing faces piercing of the corporate veil under PRC Company Law Article 20, civil restitution to the tax authority, exit ban/blacklisting under the Exit Administration Law, and criminal prosecution under Criminal Law Article 201 for evasion, punishable by imprisonment for up to 7 years plus confiscation of personal assets.
Shell Company / Treaty Abuse / Beneficial Ownership

Statutory sanction: The Chinese tax authority will deny the benefit of the China-HK DTA under the PRC General Anti-Avoidance Rule (EIT Law Article 47 and SAT Notice 2018 No. 9), re-source the income as China-source, recapture the full 10% withholding difference, impose late payment interest and a 50% to 5 times evasion penalty. The HK company may be disregarded for tax purposes and the WFOE may be treated as having a permanent establishment in China.

Director personal liability: Directors of the WFOE and the Hong Kong shell may be personally liable for the WHT shortfall, with civil asset restitution, asset preservation orders, travel bans preventing exit from Mainland China, and criminal liability for organizing tax evasion under Criminal Law Article 201 or Article 205 where fraudulent invoices are used, carrying imprisonment from 3 to 10 years in serious cases.
Invoice Fraud / AML-Related Tax Fraud

Statutory sanction: Under PRC Invoice Management Law Article 21 and Criminal Law Article 205, issuing or using fraudulent special VAT invoices subjects the enterprise to administrative fines up to RMB 500,000, confiscation of illegal gains, VAT clawback, and a criminal penalty: up to 7 years for ordinary cases, and up to life imprisonment where the amount involved is enormous. The tax authority may also blacklist the enterprise and its legal representative.

Director personal liability: The legal representative and finance director are exposed as direct responsible persons under Criminal Law Article 205, facing imprisonment of up to life, personal fine, forfeiture of personal assets, and joint civil liability to restore evaded taxes. The Hong Kong Monetary Authority and Chinese anti-money laundering authorities may additionally impose asset freezes and travel bans under China's anti-money laundering law for the cash-smuggling or layering transactions.
