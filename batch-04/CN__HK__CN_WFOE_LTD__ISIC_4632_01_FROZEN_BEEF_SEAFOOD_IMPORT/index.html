Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A virtual office or commercial mailbox for a mainland WFOE is likely rejected by SAMR, tax bureaus, and banks for this cold-chain trading industry. The WFOE must maintain a physical registered office within China, with a lease term of at least one year, a site that can accommodate business records, and, if the WFOE handles physical product, cold-chain storage documentation. Some municipal SAMR bureaus require a physical inspection of the office before issuing the business license. Hong Kong permits a virtual office for the registered office requirement, but the Inland Revenue Authority and major banks will reject mailbox-only arrangements for the HK parent if the company seeks a bank account, import/export licences, or treaty benefits. The HK entity must have a local company secretary, local director (or, if all directors are overseas, a designated representative), and demonstrable commercial substance such as a physical mailing address with call forwarding and document storage, or a serviced office that provides board meeting rooms and records custody. For ISIC 4632, the perceived risk is heightened; banks and the Customs and Excise Department require actual supply-chain documentation and a substantive local presence, so a service office is the minimum acceptable.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA) — electronic portal: National Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax Bureau) at https://etax.chinatax.gov.cn

E-government filing portal

For a foreign non-resident entity establishing a WFOE in China: (1) first register with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) and obtain a Business License; (2) the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) issued on the license is deemed the taxpayer identification number (TIN); (3) complete tax registration with the competent STA office via the e-Tax Bureau within 30 days, at which point a separate VAT registration is created for indirect tax purposes; (4) the VAT registration is automatic under the USCC master-data system; (5) if annual taxable sales exceed the VAT registration threshold, the entity is mandatorily registered as a general VAT taxpayer; small-scale taxpayer status may apply below threshold. For non-resident entities without a permanent establishment, a non-resident tax registration is obtained for withholding tax purposes using the same portal, but the WFOE is taxed as a domestic resident entity.

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Annual taxable sales threshold of RMB 5,000,000 for mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration; below that, small-scale VAT taxpayer treatment at a lower rate applies unless voluntary registration is chosen. Import transactions trigger VAT payment at customs regardless of registration status.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exported goods (including frozen beef and seafood, whether originally domestic or imported and re-exported) qualify for VAT zero-rating when the goods are physically taken out of China through a customs export declaration, the export contract is in place, the goods are shipped, and the foreign exchange proceeds are repatriated to China within the statutory period. The exporter must be a general VAT taxpayer and hold valid export licences where required. interested services may zero-rate if supplied to a foreign recipient and the benefit is received outside China.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bill of Entry)
  • Bill of Lading
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC)

China requires the issuance of fully digitalized electronic invoices (FDEI) through the State Taxation Administration's e-invoice platform, transmitted real-time for fiscal clearance. Taxpayers with VAT liability must use the e-invoice system for all B2B and export transactions; the platform applies anti-fraud encryption, smart data matching, and no physical stamp verification. For exports, the customs declaration is filed electronically via the International Trade Single Window, and the export VAT refund is processed through the e-Tax Bureau with the matching of invoice data and customs export data.

If the tax authority or customs finds that export zero-rating was claimed on false or missing documentation, the VAT refund is clawed back with interest and late fees; penalties range from 50% to 500% of the tax amount evaded under the PRC Tax Collection and Administration Law. Serious cases may lead to criminal liability for tax fraud and prohibition on future export rebates.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The People's Republic of China operates a managed floating exchange rate regime with strict but gradually liberalized controls under SAFE and PBOC. The Chinese yuan is fully convertible for current account items - including import trade payments, dividends declared from audited profits, service fees, and salaries - provided that the underlying documentation is authentic and submitted to a designated foreign exchange bank. Capital account items, however, remain heavily regulated: foreign direct investment injections, offshore portfolio investment, cross-border shareholder loans, and most hedging structures require prior SAFE registration or approval. A WFOE importing frozen beef and seafood must complete FDI information registration before receiving capital, ensure every equity remittance is endorsed against the capital account, and verify the use of funds through a Chinese accounting firm. Profit remittance to the Hong Kong parent is lawful, but requires audited financial statements, corporate income tax settlement filings, withholding tax receipts for dividends, and a board resolution declaring the distribution. Export surrender is no longer enforced at a mandatory fixed ratio since 2012; nevertheless, export proceeds must be repatriated onshore and either held in foreign currency or settled at banks on a voluntary basis. The overall regime therefore offers reasonable current account convertibility but imposes strict evidentiary and documentary hurdles for every capital flow.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE's packaged foreign exchange administrative rules, export proceeds must be repatriated to a domestic foreign exchange account within 180 days of the customs export declaration date. There is no statutory mandatory conversion ratio currently applicable, meaning a Chinese exporting entity may keep the FX in a current foreign currency account or convert it to CNY at the bank's market rate according to its treasury policy. For this WFOE, which is primarily an importer of frozen beef and seafood, the same 180-day discipline applies in reverse: import payments must be settled before the related customs declaration, and the import vendor's invoice, bill of lading, quarantine license, and previous payment records must be retained for six years to evidence the genuine trade background.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) acting under the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Offshore digital EMI accounts are not viable for domestic transaction banking in mainland China. Under the Regulation on Foreign-Invested Banks and PBOC/SAFE licensing, only domestically licensed commercial banks can maintain RMB current accounts, FX capital accounts, and trade settlement accounts for a WFOE. Foreign EMIs such as Airwallex, Currenxie, or Revolut Business cannot provide an onshore in-country account number, accept RMB settlement, or interface with SAFE and the Customs trade monitoring system; at best they can be used for overseas ancillary clearing but not as a substitute for the mandatory Chinese bank account infrastructure. Hence viability rating is Low, legally barred from holding the principal WFOE account.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% statutory dividend withholding tax on distributions to any non-PRC resident enterprise shareholder. Under the China-Hong Kong Double Tax Arrangement (DTA), the rate is reduced to 5% if the Hong Kong shareholder directly holds at least 25% of the WFOE's paid-up capital for a continuous twelve-month period immediately preceding the dividend payment, and is the beneficial owner.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Annual return must be filed on or before 31 May following the December 31 fiscal year-end. Quarterly provisional returns plus payments are due no later than 15 days after the end of each quarter (Q1: April 15, Q2: July 15, Q3: October 15, Q4: January 15). The final annual settlement balance is due by 31 May with the annual return.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File is mandatory where aggregate annual related-party tangible property transactions (e.g., imports of frozen beef/seafood from the HK affiliate) exceed RMB 100 million; for related-party intangible asset transfers, the threshold is RMB 10 million; for financial transactions (including intercompany loans and interest), RMB 10 million; and for other service transactions, RMB 40 million. Master File is required where the consolidated group turnover (including the WFOE and its ultimate parent group) exceeds RMB 3 billion. In all cases, the annual related-party transaction schedules (Forms G100000-G108000) must be filed even if no threshold is met.

Economic substance scope: Under PRC CIT Law and the OECD BEPS framework, the WFOE must prove that its taxable profits correspond to core income-generating activities (CIGA) performed in China, namely: procurement negotiation, import customs clearance, cold-chain inventory management, branding/distribution strategy, and collection of trade debts. The Hong Kong intermediate holding company, to be respected for treaty purposes, must independently satisfy Hong Kong's Economic Substance Regulations by having adequate employees, premises, board meetings and business records in Hong Kong. If the HK entity is a bare nominee with no economic substance, the PRC tax authority will apply the substance-over-form principle and GAAR (CIT Law Art. 47) to re-characterise the flow and impose statutory withholding rates.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Transfer Pricing Fraud

Statutory sanction: CIT Law Art. 47 and Implementation Regulation Art. 120 empower the tax administration to make upward transfer pricing adjustments and charge special interest at the annual benchmark RMB loan rate plus 5 percentage points. Law on Tax Administration and Collection Art. 63 imposes an administrative penalty of 50%-500% of the underpaid tax; Criminal Law Art. 201 for tax evasion (evaded amount exceeding RMB 50,000 and representing more than 10% of due tax) triggers a criminal fine of 1-5 times the evaded tax and imprisonment.

Director personal liability: Under the PRC Company Law (new Art. 20/23), if a director uses the limited-liability form to defraud the State or evade taxes, he or she is personally and jointly liable to the extent of the underpaid tax, interest and penalties. The tax authority will impose an exit/出境 travel ban on the legal representative and responsible CFO. For severe evasion, directors face 3 to 7 years fixed-term imprisonment, and confiscation of personal property.
Treaty Abuse / Shell Company

Statutory sanction: The tax authority applies the Principal Purposes Test (PPT) under Article 29 of the Multilateral Instrument and the Limitation on Benefits provision of the China-HK DTA; treaty relief is denied. The WFOE is assessed for under-withheld tax (5% shortfall) plus daily late-payment surcharge of 0.05%; Art. 63 penalty of 50%-5x for false declaration. The WFOE and HK parent are blacklisted in the Taxpayer Credit File for 3 years, negatively impacting customs classification and foreign exchange approvals.

Director personal liability: Both the WFOE directors and the HK nominee directors are exposed to piercing the corporate veil: a court order for civil restitution of the shortfall if the company assets are insufficient; automatic travel bans against all directors issued by the State Administration for Foreign Exchange and the SAT; and criminal liability for organising tax evasion under Criminal Law Art. 203 (refusal to pay) or Art. 225 (illegal business conduct), punishable by up to 3 years.
TP Documentation Failure / ESR Violation

Statutory sanction: Under CIT Law Art. 44 and Implementation Regulations Art. 114, the tax authority may use a deemed profit method (not the WFOE's books) to determine taxable income, plus special interest under Art. 122 at the benchmark rate plus 5 percentage points. The related-party schedule failure triggers a RMB 10,000-50,000 administrative fine under the Tax Collection Law, and the taxpayer loses the right to rely on its own contemporaneous comparability study when disputing the assessment.

Director personal liability: The responsible finance director may be issued a personal civil restitution order if the WFOE cannot pay; tax authorities regularly place a travel ban on the compliance officer. In Hong Kong, the IRD may apply disqualification proceedings against the HK director for ESR violations, banning the individual from serving as a director in Hong Kong for 3 years. If the failure is part of a fraudulent scheme to evade tax, the PRC director faces criminal prosecution under Criminal Law Art. 201, with imprisonment of 2 to 7 years.
