Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-up capital and no blocked escrow account requirement for a general WFOE. The registered capital must be specified in the Articles of Association and subscribed by the sole Hong Kong shareholder; contribution may be made in installments within five years from the date of establishment under the Company Law of the PRC, unless the applicable Foreign Investment Negative List or sectoral regulator imposes a higher threshold. Actual capital injections must be effected through a bank capital contribution account, and inbound SWIFT remittances must be recorded with the State Administration of Foreign Exchange through the settlement bank.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification is mandatory under the Fire Protection Law of the People's Republic of China. The warehouse must obtain Fire Design Review and Fire Safety Acceptance Inspection from the local Fire and Rescue Department, including a Civil Defense fire permit for the alcohol storage area. Fire protection systems must include automatic sprinklers, smoke detection, fire hydrants, alcohol-compatible foam extinguishing equipment, explosion-proof electrical installations, spill containment dikes equal to 110% of the largest storage vessel, and ventilated flameproof storage cabinets. Occupational safety clearance requires a workplace safety registration, occupational disease hazard project filing with the Municipal Health Commission, forklift and dock safety procedures, personal protective equipment programs, and a safety management manual for employees. Structural load safety approval must be obtained from the municipal housing construction authority for racking, mezzanine, and palletized wine storage systems, and lightning protection acceptance must be secured from the Municipal Meteorological Bureau before bonded alcohol inventory is received.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

For the China WFOE: State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA), with electronic filings through the STA Electronic Tax Bureau (provincial e-Tax portals) and customs transactions through the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (GACC) via the China International Trade Single Window. For the Hong Kong parent: Inland Revenue Department of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region (IRD), with e-filings through the GovHK e-Tax portal.

E-government filing portal

The foreign investor incorporates the WFOE with the Administration for Market Regulation (AMR), which assigns the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC). The USCC is the sole statutory TIN for PRC VAT and Enterprise Income Tax; AMR pushes incorporation data to STA, after which the WFOE must complete tax registration in the Electronic Tax Bureau, select applicable taxes (VAT, consumption tax, Enterprise Income Tax, stamp duty), register as a general VAT taxpayer if turnover is CNY 5,000,000 or above, and obtain GACC customs registration plus bonded warehouse operator status through the China International Trade Single Window. For the Hong Kong non-resident parent, no PRC TIN is issued unless it derives China-source income; when it does (for example, dividends), it must file with STA for a non-resident taxpayer number and may claim treaty relief using an IRD Certificate of Tax Residence.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The WFOE may apply 0% VAT to wine/spirits exported from a PRC bonded warehouse when the goods are physically removed from the customs territory, the sale is made to an offshore buyer, the export declaration is lodged and released by GACC, title and risk pass overseas, and the proceeds are collected and repatriated through banking channels. Exporting or re-exporting bonded goods that do not enter domestic China consumption is not subject to VAT; consumption tax on re-exported goods is also relieved; the WFOE must retain the full documentary chain to support input VAT refund or output VAT zero-rating.

  • Export Goods Customs Declaration (submitted through the China International Trade Single Window)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (international transport document)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or equivalent bank export collection voucher confirming cross-border receipt of foreign exchange
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Bonded Warehouse Customs Transfer/Re-export Release Document

The WFOE must issue full digital electronic invoices (digital e-invoices) through the STA national e-invoice service platform under Golden Tax System Phase IV. Invoice data is uploaded and cleared in near-real time to STA; bonded warehouse exports and customs declarations are transmitted through the China International Trade Single Window. Paper VAT special invoices, where still in transition, require anti-fraud/cash-register control equipment (tax-control devices) with digital fiscal numbering. No private invoice system can bypass STA electronic clearance.

STA and GACC can retrospectively disallow the zero-rating, claw back the VAT refund with an additional collection of unpaid tax, and impose late payment surcharges from the original refund date. Under the PRC Tax Administration Law, fraudulent export refund claims may be punished with an administrative penalty of 50% to 500% of the tax obtained, suspension or revocation of export tax refund eligibility, blacklisting and enhanced monitoring, and criminal liability for export tax refund fraud under the Criminal Law for forged export declarations or fabricated foreign-exchange receipts.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Within the mainland Chinese legal perimeter of this Hong Kong owned WFOE, the RMB is not freely convertible on the capital account. Current account transactions, including goods trade, service fees, and ordinary dividend payments after proper verification, operate under a largely convertible and post-facto monitoring regime administered by SAFE-authorized banks. By contrast, every capital-account movement is license-gated: registered capital injection must follow FDI registration, foreign debt requires a SAFE quota letter if used under the macro-prudential foreign debt formula, and refunds on undistributed profits or liquidation surplus require separate clearance certificates. The real transaction need principle prohibits pre-financing of unrelated invoices, and any company seeking to convert RMB to Hong Kong dollars or retain forex earnings abroad must demonstrate a genuine underlying trade within the bonded alcoholic-beverage scope. Overseas investors' access to onshore debt proceeds is further limited: RMB funds obtained from settlement of foreign currency capital can only be deployed into operating purposes and are expressly prohibited from discretionary lending, securities investment, or wealth-management products. Profit remittance is allowed only after the annual audit, corporate income tax conclusively settled with the State Taxation Administration, and a dividend remittance verification certificate issued by the custodial bank.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE trade-in-goods administration, the WFOE must repatriate export proceeds to a domestic designated foreign-exchange account within 180 days of the customs export declaration; if collection is delayed beyond this SAFE monitoring window, the enterprise must apply for an extension through the Trade Foreign Exchange Monitoring System. There is no statutory percentage of foreign currency that must be converted into RMB; the WFOE may retain 100% of export proceeds in foreign currency in a designated account or voluntarily convert into RMB through a licensed bank. Each receipt must be matched by its customs declaration record number and invoiced cargo description, and any mismatch above USD 50,000 triggers a verification check and may block the funds or result in a blacklist rating.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) within the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Not viable. No overseas EMI or digital bank without a PBOC financial business permit can accept deposits or perform RMB settlement on behalf of this WFOE. The Electronic Money Institution passport regime used in the EEA is wholly inapplicable to mainland China. SAFE requires all cross-border trade receipts to enter a licensed designated foreign-exchange account, and China's cross-border payment infrastructure (CIPS and CNAPS) is accessible only through PBOC-network participants. A Hong Kong or UK EMI may serve as a custody or payment channel at the HK parent level, but it is legally barred from being the onshore settlement bank. Attempting to route WFOE export proceeds through an unlicensed EMI will trigger suspicious transaction reports, account freezing, and administrative penalties.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The HK parent injects USD or HKD through a SWIFT MT103 or MT202 to the WFOE's SAFE-designated FDI capital account at Bank of China or ICBC. Before remittance, the WFOE must complete SAFE FDI registration and obtain the Foreign Direct Investment Foreign Exchange Registration Certificate or disclosure sheet. The remitting bank must include the SAFE-designated FDI equity injection remittance purpose code and the WFOE's SAFE registration code in the beneficiary message. The receiving Chinese bank performs a capital-account authenticity inspection, logs the payment into the Capital Account Information Platform, and obtains the customer's conversion application before converting into RMB. A Chinese CPA must subsequently issue a capital verification report for the registered capital. The resulting RMB in the capital settlement account is restricted to operating purposes such as bonded inventory purchase, customs duties, rent, salaries, transportation, and licensing fees; prohibited uses include equity investment, non-operating real-estate purchases, entrusted loans, securities investment, and wealth-management products.For high-frequency low-value disbursements such as customs duties, warehousing fees, and logistics, SWIFT MT103 transfers are standard for supplier payments below USD 100,000. For high-value parcels of aged Scotch or Bordeaux wine, the preferred clearing rail is an irrevocable 90-day Commercial Letter of Credit opened through Bank of China's London or Paris branch, with required documents including the signed supplier invoice, duty-paid warehouse receipt, certificate of authenticity and valuation, packing list, certificate of origin, health certificate, marine insurance certificate, and airway bill or bill of lading. Documentary collections under DP or DA terms are permissible only after a verified trade-history record with the supplier. All bonded warehouse-to-warehouse transfers require 'bonded transfer settlement instructions' tied to the SAFE 'bonded transaction' trade code and to a customs cargo-transfer application. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

Statutory withholding income tax on dividends paid to a non-resident is 10%; under the Mainland-Hong Kong DTA the rate is reduced to 5% when the beneficial owner is a Hong Kong company holding directly at least 25% of the capital of the Chinese WFOE, otherwise the DTA cap is 10%.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

For a calendar-year WFOE, the annual final return is due on or before 31 May of the following year; quarterly provisional returns are due within 15 days after the end of each quarter.

Box-by-Box Return Breakdown (non-resident focus)

BoxField Non-resident guidanceTax-evasion pitfall
Box 1 (Line 1) Gross operating revenue from bonded wholesale trade in wine and spirits, including bonded warehouse sales, bonded transfers, commissions, storage service fees and incidental logistics income. Declare all gross turnover. If the Hong Kong parent owns the inventory and the WFOE only provides bonded-warehouse agency services, record service commission only and not the principal merchandise flows. Mischaracterisation creates a constructive permanent establishment risk for the Hong Kong principal and transfer-pricing recharacterisation risk for the WFOE.
Box 2 (Line 2) Operating costs including landed cost of imported aged Scotch and Bordeaux wines, customs and excise items not in bonded status, bonded warehouse rent, handling and processing costs inbound and outbound. Related-party purchases from the Hong Kong parent must satisfy the arm's-length standard under Article 41 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law. If the Hong Kong mark-up exceeds comparable margins, the SAT will make an upward adjustment to taxable income. Maintain complete customs-bonded movement records.
Box 3 (Line 3) Taxes and surcharges actually paid, including urban maintenance and construction tax, education surcharges, stamp tax, property tax and other deductible taxes other than enterprise income tax. Enterprise income tax itself, VAT paid on unlawful leakage into the domestic market, and consumption tax penalties are non-deductible. For goods released from bonded supervision, restate import duty and VAT only when the release is lawfully completed.
Box 4 (Line 4) Selling expenses, administrative expenses and financial expenses including intercompany interest, storage, insurance, marketing, management fees, bank charges, foreign exchange losses and director compensation. Unsupported management fees paid to the Hong Kong parent are non-deductible. Related-party interest is capped by the 2:1 debt-to-equity thin capitalisation rule and must be at an arm's-length rate. Business entertainment expense is limited to 60% of the amount incurred and in any case to 0.5% of sales. Interest paid to a related non-resident must also satisfy beneficial-ownership substance.
Box 5 (Line 5) Loss carryforwards and prior-year tax adjustments arising from Customs, VAT or enterprise income tax audits. The WFOE can carry losses forward for no more than 5 years. Losses cannot be used to reduce withholding tax on dividends paid to the Hong Kong parent. Loss deductions require availability of filed returns, ownership continuity and substantiated audit trail.
Box 6 (Line 6) Taxable income after all accounting-to-tax adjustments, prior to loss offset and tax credits. Reconcile all related-party declarations attached to the annual return with the contemporaneous transfer-pricing documentation. An unadjusted mispricing will be corrected by SAT with a 0.05% daily late-payment surcharge and possible penalty multiples.
Box 7 (Line 7) Final enterprise income tax computed at 25%, applying any preferential effective rate, foreign tax credit, and withholding tax credits not yet utilised. This box does not compute the outbound withholding tax on dividends, interest or royalties. Those cross-border payments are separately reported on the withholding income tax return submitted by the payer, with the applicable Mainland-Hong Kong DTA rate claimed before withholding.
Box 8 (Line 8) Final enterprise income tax payable or refundable after deduction of quarterly prepayments and pre-paid amounts. If the 5% dividend DTA rate is to be used, the Hong Kong parent must obtain the Tax Residency Certificate and deliver the treaty-benefit claim package before the dividend payment. If 10% is mistakenly withheld, a refund may be pursued through the treaty claims procedure.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Contemporaneous Local File is mandatory when annual related-party tangible-asset transfers exceed RMB 200 million, or when related-party intangible-asset transfers, service transfers or financial-asset transfers exceed RMB 100 million; an aggregate annual related-party transaction amount exceeding RMB 1 billion triggers the Master File.

Economic substance scope: The Hong Kong entity claiming treaty benefits must establish genuine economic substance to obtain a TRC; a shell holding company with no staff, premises or decision-making functions will be denied beneficial-ownership status. For the Chinese WFOE, China applies the General Anti-Avoidance Rule under Enterprise Income Tax Law Article 47 and requires Core Income-Generating Activities for the bonded wine and spirits trade to be performed in China, including warehouse operations, inventory control, sales negotiation, customs declaration preparation, billing and risk management. A WFOE that is reduced to a passive invoicing conduit with no commercial substance will have profits reattributed to the entity bearing the real economic functions.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Related-Party Expense / VAT-Invoice Fraud

Statutory sanction: Tax Collection and Administration Law Article 63 authorises recovery of the unpaid tax, a penalty of 50% to 500% of the evaded amount, and a 0.05% daily late-payment surcharge. False VAT invoices engage Criminal Law Article 205. Repeated income-tax evasion triggers Criminal Law Article 201.

Director personal liability: A director who signs false financial statements, approves fictitious invoices or directs the evasion scheme may be jointly and severally liable where the corporate veil is abused under Company Law Article 20. Personal assets are subject to civil restitution and attachment; the responsible person is placed on the tax blacklist, faces exit and travel restrictions under the Exit-Entry Administration Law, and may be criminally sentenced to imprisonment of up to 7 years for especially large evasion.
Treaty Abuse / Shell HK Holding Company / Beneficial Ownership Misrepresentation

Statutory sanction: EIT Law Article 47 and the General Anti-Avoidance Rule permit SAT to deny DTA benefits, recharacterise the transaction and recompute taxable income. Under Tax Collection and Administration Law Article 63, under-withholding caused by false declarations attracts a 50% to 500% penalty on the underpaid withholding tax. Forgery of tax residency documents engages Criminal Law Article 280.

Director personal liability: Directors who arrange circular financing, backdated board minutes, or sham substance to claim DTA relief may be personally ordered to repay under-withheld tax and to restore diverted assets. Corporate personality is pierced where the HK entity is found to be a mere conduit under the director's personal control. Exit bans and blacklisting apply; forged TRCs carry imprisonment up to 3 years, and the directors' Hong Kong and mainland assets may be preserved for confiscation.
Bonded Warehouse Smuggling / Customs Duty Evasion / AML Breach

Statutory sanction: Customs Law Article 82 imposes confiscation of smuggled goods and illegal proceeds, recovery of unpaid customs duty and a fine of up to five times the value of the smuggled goods. Criminal Law Article 153 punishes large-scale smuggling of ordinary goods with imprisonment of 3 to 10 years, life imprisonment in extremely serious cases, and property confiscation. The Anti-Money Laundering Law permits asset-freezing and heightened penalties for funds originating from duty-evasion offences.

Director personal liability: Directors who organise or personally benefit from bonded-goods diversion face unlimited personal restitution through veil-piercing, customs blacklisting, and denial of exit until duties and penalties are paid. Criminal conviction for smuggling by responsible corporate officers carries fixed-term imprisonment and, where amounts are extremely serious or repeated, life imprisonment. Personal real estate, vehicles and cross-border bank balances are subject to judicial attachment and confiscation.
