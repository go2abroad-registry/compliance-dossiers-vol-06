Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None. There is no statutory minimum paid-up registered capital for a general wholesale and export WFOE under current PRC law, and there is no blocked escrow account requirement. The registered capital amount is fixed in the Articles of Association and must be fully paid by shareholders within five years from establishment or within the shorter period stated in the Articles. The Hong Kong corporate shareholder must remit its capital contribution through a foreign exchange capital account opened at a SAFE-designated bank in China, but no advance deposit into an escrow or blocked account is required.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification is mandatory. The WFOE must obtain a Fire Protection Design Review and Fire Protection Acceptance Certificate under the Regulations on Fire Protection in Construction Projects for any premises used for the storage or handling of bottled alcoholic spirits, and a Fire Safety Inspection Certificate for premises open to employees or visitors. The storage of high-proof baijiu in quantity must comply with the Safety Specification for Storage of Flammable Liquids GB 15603, and the company must appoint a work safety officer under the Work Safety Law of the People's Republic of China. Structural load inspections and shelving stability certificates are required if the warehouse uses racking to store packaged spirits. If the company operates only from a serviced commercial office and uses third-party bonded warehousing, the occupier must retain the landlord's fire safety record and verify that the third-party logistics provider holds the applicable fire safety and dangerous goods storage permits.

Paid-in capital rule: None. There is no statutory minimum paid-up registered capital for a general wholesale and export WFOE under current PRC law, and there is no blocked escrow account requirement. The registered capital amount is fixed in the Articles of Association and must be fully paid by shareholders within five years from establishment or within the shorter period stated in the Articles. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; online filing and administration via the Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax) platform at etax.chinatax.gov.cn

E-government filing portal

The Hong Kong parent incorporates the WFOE with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR); the issued business license carries the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which is the definitive statutory Tax Identification Number (TIN) for all PRC tax purposes. Within 30 calendar days of obtaining the business license, the WFOE must complete tax registration with the competent local STA office through the Electronic Tax Bureau, linking the USCC to Corporate Income Tax, Value Added Tax, stamp duty, property tax, and individual income tax withholding accounts. The WFOE must then register as a VAT general taxpayer when projected annual taxable turnover exceeds RMB 5,000,000, or voluntarily when below that threshold, and activate the fully digitalized electronic invoice system. For customs and export purposes, the WFOE must register with the General Administration of Customs, obtain export goods enterprise registration, and activate the China International Trade Single Window. The WFOE must also complete foreign exchange registration with the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) for export proceeds collection. Foreign non-resident entities with no PRC permanent establishment are not separately assigned a stand-alone TIN; their China-sourced income is subject to withholding by the resident payer using the payer's USCC, and treaty relief is applied through the e-Tax non-resident procedure.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating of 0% VAT is available for goods physically exported from the PRC customs territory where the exporter is a registered VAT general taxpayer, the export customs declaration has been recorded by the General Administration of Customs, title and risk have passed to an overseas buyer, and export proceeds are repatriated to China within 180 days of customs declaration. Qualifying offshore services and cross-border digital services supplied to recipients outside the PRC and consumed entirely outside the PRC are also zero-rated, subject to the statutory 'completely consumed outside China' test. For baijiu, the exporter must also satisfy PRC food safety export registration, origin, quality, and alcoholic beverage licensing requirements.

  • Customs Export Goods Declaration (electronic declaration via China International Trade Single Window)
  • Bill of Lading or multimodal transport document
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Official export VAT invoice data record from the STA e-Tax platform
  • Bank receipt of export proceeds / Foreign Exchange Income Declaration form
  • Export sales contract with the overseas buyer
  • Export health, quality, and alcoholic beverage certification for baijiu

All VAT invoices must be issued through the STA fully digitalized electronic invoice platform. Invoice data is transmitted to the STA in near-real-time through API/ERP integration, and each invoice carries a unique tax authority code and cryptographic digital seal. Physical pre-printed invoice stock and manual fiscal stamping do not satisfy PRC requirements. For exported goods, the invoice record is electronically matched with the customs export declaration through the China International Trade Single Window before zero-rating or export VAT refund can be processed.

The STA and General Administration of Customs cross-match export declarations, transport documents, digital e-invoices, and bank foreign exchange receipts. Unverifiable or fraudulent zero-rating claims trigger compulsory clawback of the VAT refund, daily late-payment interest, fines from 50% to 500% of the tax evaded or refund obtained, suspension of export rebate eligibility, and criminal liability for export-rebate fraud under PRC Criminal Law, including imprisonment up to life and confiscation of property. Fraudulent claims are not subject to the ordinary three-year tax assessment limitation period.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China maintains a managed floating exchange-rate system with convertibility granted on the current account for genuine trade in goods; export proceeds must be repatriated into China and deposited in a designated current-account foreign exchange settlement account while the exporter may voluntarily convert to RMB or hold the foreign currency. The capital account remains supervised: FDI registered capital injection requires prior SAFE registration and the funds are subject to verified intended use. For the WFOE engaged in baijiu export, remittance of dividends or profits to the Hong Kong parent is processed on the current account only after the annual statutory audit, corporate income tax settlement, and withholding tax payment; banks require the tax clearance certificate and audited financial statements. Liquor export is a legitimate physical trade stream, but high-proof spirits heighten document-scrutiny expectations from the clearing bank. Overall convertibility is real but not unconditional; every flow must be matched to a SAFE-registered commercial purpose.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export proceeds must be repatriated within 180 days from the date of export customs declaration. There is currently no mandatory conversion ratio; the WFOE may retain the foreign currency in an onshore current-account foreign exchange settlement account or voluntarily convert to RMB at the bank's spot rate, provided each credit is supported by customs declarations, commercial invoices, contracts, and transport evidence. SAFE will monitor the retention balance against the enterprise's trade volume and may require documented justification for any period beyond 90 days.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE), operating under the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low viability for a China-registered WFOE. Under PBOC licensing and SAFE account rules, only domestic deposit-taking banks may open onshore settlement accounts and process currency conversion for PRC entities; offshore digital EMIs do not hold a RMB clearing licence and cannot legally act as the WFOE's primary treasury provider. An offshore EMI may be used only as a technical instruction aggregator in partnership with a licensed Chinese bank, and it cannot replace the statutory bank relationship, KYC interview, or SAFE filing obligations. The compliance risk and account-freezing potential make reliance on an EMI inadvisable.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The Hong Kong parent should initiate a SWIFT MT103 transfer to the WFOE's designated Foreign Exchange Capital Account at a domestic bank using the SAFE-registered FDI capital injection purpose code. Turnover: Mandatory audit under Companies Ordinance Cap.622 s.404 applies to every non-dormant Hong Kong incorporated company, including a WFOE engaged in liquor export, irrespective of balance-sheet size, turnover, or headcount; there is no general SME exemption for active trading companies. The only statutory dispensation is for a private company qualifying as a 'small private company' under s.405, which requires all of the following to be met cumulatively for the financial year: (i) annual revenue does not exceed HK$10,000,000 (approximately USD 1,280,000); (ii) total assets do not exceed HK$10,000,000 (approximately USD 1,280,000); (iii) the company is not a bank, an insurer, a licensed corporation under the Securities and Futures Ordinance, or a member of a group that is not itself exempt; and (iv) the shareholders have passed an appropriate special resolution to waive the audit. Accordingly, any company with total assets exceeding HK$10,000,000, annual turnover exceeding HK$10,000,000, or a business profile that falls outside the narrow exemption is compulsorily subject to an independent certified CPA audit. A certified auditor must be appointed and the audited financial statements are filed with IRD as part of the Profits Tax Return and with the Companies Registry only when required.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: RMB thresholds under SAT Public Notice [2016] No. 42: contemporaneous Local File documentation is required for related-party tangible-asset transactions above RMB 200 million and for intangible-asset or service transactions above RMB 100 million; the Master File is generally expected when aggregate annual related-party transactions reach approximately RMB 1 billion or the group has significant PRC presence.

Economic substance scope: The WFOE must maintain real business management and operating substance in mainland China. The Hong Kong shareholder is subject to Hong Kong's Economic Substance Requirements (ESR) and must perform Core Income-Generating Activities (CIGA) in Hong Kong to justify tax residence and treaty benefits. A Hong Kong shell with no office, staff, contracts or board decisions cannot claim the 5% dividend rate or 7% interest/royalty rates; the PRC SAT may also apply controlled-foreign-company and general anti-avoidance rules to recharacterise the structure.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion and false invoicing in baijiu export trade

Statutory sanction: PRC Tax Collection Administration Law Art. 63: recovery of underpaid CIT plus daily late-payment interest and an administrative penalty of 50% to 500% of the underpaid amount. Criminal Law Art. 201 imposes up to 7 years' imprisonment and fines for tax evasion; Art. 205 imposes up to life imprisonment and fines for issuing/using false VAT special invoices.

Director personal liability: A director, legal representative, financial officer or actual controller who engineers the false invoices is personally liable for the company's unpaid tax under veil-piercing doctrine where limited liability is abused to defraud creditors; personal assets may be attached, the individual is subject to high-consumption restrictions and exit bans, and criminal conviction results in imprisonment and confiscation.
Shell/letter-box Hong Kong structure and fraudulent DTA relief

Statutory sanction: The PRC tax authority denies treaty treatment under SAT Public Notice [2018] No. 9 for non-beneficial owners, recovers the 5% WHT shortfall, adds late-payment surcharge, and imposes a 50% to 500% tax evasion penalty. Hong Kong Inland Revenue may also impose penalties for false tax-residence declarations and revoke the tax residence certificate.

Director personal liability: Directors who signed false beneficial-owner declarations and tax-residence certificates risk criminal liability for aiding tax evasion under PRC Criminal Law Art. 201, and civil restitution for wrongful extraction of dividends. In Hong Kong, knowingly making false statements to tax authorities is punishable under Inland Revenue Ordinance s. 82A with treble tax and imprisonment; the director may be blacklisted and travel-restricted.
Transfer pricing manipulation, thin capitalisation and missing documentation

Statutory sanction: CIT Law Art. 41 and Art. 48 allow re-pricing under the OECD-compliant arm's-length standard and special tax adjustment interest; excess interest is recharacterised as deemed dividends subject to 10% WHT. Failure to retain documentation is separately penalised under Tax Collection Administration Law Art. 62, and deliberate mispricing rises to Art. 63.

Director personal liability: The responsible director/CFO who signs false transfer-pricing documentation and related-party contracts can be held personally liable for the adjusted tax if the corporate veil is abused, subjected to civil damages, and criminally prosecuted under Criminal Law Art. 201 where the underpayment is intentional; the director is also entered into the tax integrity blacklist, impairing travel, credit and board eligibility.
