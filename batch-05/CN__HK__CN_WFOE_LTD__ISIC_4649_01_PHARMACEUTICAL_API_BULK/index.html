Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA), operating through the National Electronic Taxpayer Service Platform (e-Tax Bureau) and the competent provincial/municipal tax service offices; customs registration and export declaration processing are performed by the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (GACC) through the China International Trade Single Window.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the WFOE with the local Administration for Market Regulation and obtain the Business License carrying the Uniform Social Credit Code (USCC). Step 2: Tax registration is completed through the integrated multi-item linkage registration; the STA uses the USCC as the sole statutory Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) for the WFOE, and no separate domestic tax registration number is issued. Step 3: Within 30 days of the Business License date, the WFOE must complete supplementary taxpayer registration, including legal representative, finance officer and tax-filing calendar, through the e-Tax Bureau. Step 4: Register for VAT in the competent STA office at the WFOE's registered address and select the VAT taxpayer classification; mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration applies when annual taxable sales exceed CNY 5,000,000, while a WFOE that expects to exceed that threshold or needs to issue Special VAT Invoices for input VAT recovery or export rebates must submit the general VAT taxpayer qualification election. Step 5: Open a RMB and foreign-currency bank account, file the bank account details with the STA, and ensure the account is linked to the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) export-proceeds collection monitoring process. Step 6: Apply for electronic invoice issuance, including activation of the STA-approved Tax UKey and invoice quota, through the e-Tax Bureau. Step 7: For export activities, complete GACC import/export goods registration to obtain the Customs Registration Code, complete the applicable foreign-trade operator/customs filing, and maintain the NMPA/GACC pharmaceutical export license and wholesale drug distribution license required for active pharmaceutical ingredients. A non-resident shareholder does not obtain a mainland TIN solely from equity ownership; any Chinese-source income of the non-resident shareholder is subject to withholding tax filing through the WFOE, and a Hong Kong shareholder may claim treaty relief under the Mainland and Hong Kong SAR tax arrangement by obtaining a Hong Kong resident certificate.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation. The CN WFOE is a resident enterprise incorporated under PRC law and is taxable on worldwide income as a separate legal entity; it is not eligible for pass-through treatment, is not eligible for Hong Kong offshore territorial-scope treatment, and is not located in a tax-exempt zone.

Available election forms

None under PRC Corporate Income Tax: PRC law does not provide a check-the-box election or hybrid-mismatch election. VAT-specific elections available are (i) small-scale VAT taxpayer status versus general VAT taxpayer status, (ii) voluntary general VAT taxpayer registration upon demonstrating sound accounting and adequate records, and (iii) election to apply the general VAT method once statutory conditions are met; there is no VAT group registration or consolidation, and no mainland tax election is available to mimic Hong Kong offshore territorial taxation.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

All WFOEs must register for VAT within 30 days of establishment irrespective of turnover. Mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration applies when annual taxable sales exceed CNY 5,000,000; enterprises with annual taxable sales of CNY 5,000,000 or below qualify for small-scale VAT taxpayer treatment, and may voluntarily register as general VAT taxpayers if they meet the accounting-record and input-credit conditions.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A VAT zero-rating and corresponding input VAT refund is available for the physical export of bulk pharmaceutical API goods from the PRC, provided that: (i) the WFOE is a registered VAT general taxpayer; (ii) the goods have actually left the PRC customs territory and are supported by a GACC export customs declaration; (iii) the transaction is a genuine commercial sale to a non-PRC buyer with title and risk transferred at the export border; (iv) the export proceeds are collected in foreign exchange through a bank and recorded in the SAFE goods-trade foreign-exchange monitoring system within the statutory time limit; (v) the exporter holds the required GACC customs registration, foreign-trade operator/customs filing, and any NMPA/GACC pharmaceutical export license applicable to active pharmaceutical ingredients; and (vi) the WFOE issues the appropriate export invoice and retains the full documentary chain proving physical export. Sales of the same API within Mainland China remain subject to 13% VAT, and supplies to Hong Kong SAR qualify for zero-rating only if the goods are physically exported and declared to GACC for departure to Hong Kong as a separate customs territory; offshore digital services or triangular arrangements without physical PRC customs export do not qualify.

  • Customs Export Goods Declaration Form (Customs Export Declaration) released by GACC through the China International Trade Single Window
  • Original negotiable Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing shipment from a PRC port or customs area to the foreign destination and showing the WFOE as exporter or shipper
  • Commercial invoice, packing list, and export sales contract or purchase order corresponding to the customs declaration
  • Bank-issued foreign-exchange receipt voucher and SAFE export-proceeds collection monitoring record, being the PRC statutory equivalent of an Inward Remittance Certificate, proving actual repatriation of the export proceeds
  • Special VAT Invoice or purchase documentation for the exported API and any direct logistics and freight invoices supporting the input VAT refund computation

VAT invoices are issued through STA-controlled invoicing systems under the Golden Tax Phase IV administration system. Special VAT Invoices and General VAT Invoices must be digitally reported to the STA in near-real time through the National Electronic Taxpayer Service Platform. Fully Digitalized Electronic Invoices do not require a physical tax-control seal and are authenticated through STA digital stamping, invoice quotas and the taxpayer's Tax UKey. The system applies automated cross-checks among invoice data, VAT returns, customs declarations and foreign-exchange collection records; there is no private-sector fiscal pre-clearance device, but the STA may require real-time invoice metadata transmission and may restrict invoice quotas or block abnormal issuers as part of the anti-fraud digital stamping mandate.

The STA and GACC jointly verify export zero-rating claims by matching the customs declaration, invoice data in Golden Tax Phase IV, the SAFE export-proceeds collection record and the bill of lading or air waybill. If the goods are not physically exported, the buyer is not genuinely foreign, the proceeds are not repatriated, or the documentation is false, the zero-rating is revoked and output VAT plus previously refunded input VAT is clawed back with interest under the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection. Fraudulent export-tax-refund claims attract administrative fines of up to five times the refund or tax evaded, suspension of VAT refund eligibility, and penalties under the Customs Law of the People's Republic of China. Serious cases involving false export declarations to defraud export tax refunds are prosecuted under the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, with penalties including imprisonment and confiscation of assets; the recovery period is effectively unlimited for fraudulent or concealed schemes, and export documentation should be retained for at least ten years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The People's Republic of China operates a managed current-account convertible but capital-account restricted foreign exchange regime. For the WFOE, ordinary trade-related current account transactions, including payment for imported active pharmaceutical ingredients, logistics, commission, and operating expenses, can be settled through a domestic Tier-1 bank under the genuine-transaction rule, with invoices, contracts, customs records, and tax bills submitted to the bank for SAFE monitoring. Capital account flows, however, require specific registration: FDI registered capital injection must pass through a SAFE-registered capital account and be settled under strict regulatory scrutiny. Export proceeds are current account receipts but remain subject to repatriation oversight; despite the historical mandatory surrender system having been removed, receipts must be repatriated or retained in compliance with SAFE trade-reporting rules. Profit remittance is conditionally available after the WFOE completes its annual corporate income tax filing, provides audited financial statements, produces the board resolution approving distribution, and demonstrates that accumulated losses do not impair paid-in capital. The bank will verify the source of after-tax retained earnings and apply the relevant tax withholding on dividends paid to the Hong Kong parent, often invoking treaty relief documentation to lower withholding. Therefore, the treasury blueprint must treat every cross-border fund movement as a documentary event and maintain a complete SAFE/regulatory audit trail.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export foreign currency receipts must be repatriated to the WFOE's domestic foreign exchange settlement account through a designated bank; the standard SAFE goods-trade monitoring framework requires collection within the payment term stated in the underlying contract, generally not exceeding 180 days after export customs declaration unless an extended trade-credit period is registered. Mandatory conversion ratio is zero; the exporter can retain 100% in foreign currency within the bank-approved current account quota, or convert the entire 100% into RMB at market rates. If export proceeds are retained outside China, prior approval or filing with SAFE is required, and the balance must be reported. The WFOE must file transaction details in the SAFE monitoring system within five business days of any trade payment or receipt.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE), acting under the delegated authority of the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low viability and legally constrained for this WFOE. China prohibits non-bank fintech EMIs from operating as primary holders of SAFE-registered FDI capital accounts, performing foreign exchange conversion for capital account transactions, or issuing verified bank account numbers for an onshore entity's statutory cash management. Offshore EMI services may assist with collection of e-commerce receivables, but they cannot act as the treasury clearing venue for bulk pharmaceutical API transactions subject to China's goods-trade FX control. All meaningful cross-border settlement, FX conversion, and regulatory reporting must be executed at a licensed domestic commercial bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All registered capital from the HK parent must be remitted by SWIFT MT103 or equivalent through banking channels to the WFOE's SAFE-registered foreign exchange capital base account at the chosen Tier-1 domestic bank. The remittance must be in the shareholder's name, reference the new FDI registration code, and use the SAFE-recognised purpose code for foreign direct investment paid-in capital. After receipt, the WFOE must instruct the bank to settle the funds into RMB through the SAFE settlement system, and a licensed Chinese certified public accountant must issue a capital verification report for the company registration and tax bureaus. No third-party prepaid cards, e-wallet deposits, or non-bank EMI transfers can be used for capital injection.For import of bulk APIs and ancillary products, disbursement rails include SWIFT MT103 from the WFOE's domestic forex/RMB account for open-account payments, commercial letters of credit issued by BOC/ICBC and advised/confirmed through an overseas bank, and documentary collections for post-shipment settlements. For export collections, the WFOE should present shipping and invoice sets under export LC or collection instructions; proceeds are received by the settlement bank, verified against the customs database, and posted to the foreign exchange account. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low viability and legally constrained for this WFOE. China prohibits non-bank fintech EMIs from operating as primary holders of SAFE-registered FDI capital accounts, performing foreign exchange conversion for capital account transactions, or issuing verified bank account numbers for an onshore entity's statutory cash management. Offshore EMI services may assist with collection of e-commerce receivables, but they cannot act as the treasury clearing venue for bulk pharmaceutical API transactions subject to China's goods-trade FX control. All meaningful cross-border settlement, FX conversion, and regulatory reporting must be executed at a licensed domestic commercial bank.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File threshold: RMB 200 million for tangible-asset related-party transfers; RMB 100 million for intangible-asset related-party transfers; RMB 100 million for financial-asset related-party transfers; RMB 40 million for other aggregate related-party transactions. Master File is mandatory when the annual aggregate of related-party transactions exceeds RMB 1 billion or when the ultimate parent group has prepared a Master File; Country-by-Country reporting is required where the consolidated group revenue exceeds RMB 5.5 billion.

Economic substance scope: China applies substance-over-form and general anti-avoidance rules under EIT Law Article 47 and SAT anti-abuse guidance. The PRC WFOE must demonstrate that its Core Income-Generating Activities - procurement negotiation, inventory management, quality control, contract execution, banking and logistics decisions - are actually performed by Chinese staff in China. The Hong Kong parent claiming treaty benefits and ownership returns must likewise prove real economic substance in Hong Kong; a letter-box conduit company will be denied DTA benefits, recharacterized, or pierced. Pharmaceutical API wholesale additionally requires statutory licensing, product registration and compliance with drug administration rules to evidence a genuine physical business.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Transfer Pricing Manipulation

Statutory sanction: TCAL Article 63: fine of 50% to 500% of underpaid tax, plus 0.05% daily late-payment surcharge under Article 32; transfer-pricing re-assessment under EIT Law Article 41 and SAT Public Notice 2016 No. 42; inclusion in the Serious Tax Violation Disclosure List and tax blacklisting.

Director personal liability: Under PRC Company Law veil-piercing rules, directors who direct or sign fraudulent tax filings may be jointly and severally liable for the unpaid tax and civil restitution; personal bank accounts and assets can be attached; the legal representative may be placed on exit-control and refused permission to leave China. Criminal liability under Criminal Law Article 201 can result in up to 3 years' imprisonment for large evasion and 3 to 7 years' imprisonment plus asset confiscation for particularly large evasion.
Failure to Withhold Dividend WHT / Fraudulent Treaty Relief

Statutory sanction: TCAL Article 69: withholding agent is liable for the unwithheld tax and a fine of 50% to 300% of the under-withheld amount, plus late-payment surcharge; EIT Law Article 47 and the general anti-avoidance rules permit the tax authority to deny treaty benefits and recharacterize the distribution as taxable profit.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false withholding returns expose themselves to personal civil liability, asset seizure, tax blacklisting and exit/travel bans; willful use of false official documents such as forged TRC or IRD certificates triggers Criminal Law Article 280 for forging official instruments and Article 201 for tax evasion, carrying maximum imprisonment of 7 years.
Shell Structure, AML and Pharmaceutical-License Violations

Statutory sanction: PRC Drug Administration Law Articles 112-115 authorize confiscation of illegal gains, seizure of illegal goods and equipment, revocation of licenses and severe fines; China's Anti-Money Laundering Law imposes institutional monetary penalties and potential business restrictions on financial transactions lacking beneficial-ownership due diligence; tax authorities may also invoke GAAR to void the structure and impose 50%-500% penalties on the resultant underpaid tax.

Director personal liability: Directors who abuse the corporate form to conceal the true transactional structure face personal asset execution and joint and several restitution liability; they may be blacklisted and subjected to exit bans; knowing participation in money-laundering through shell entities is punishable under Criminal Law Article 191 with imprisonment of up to 10 years, and pharmaceutical license violations can lead to director disqualification.
