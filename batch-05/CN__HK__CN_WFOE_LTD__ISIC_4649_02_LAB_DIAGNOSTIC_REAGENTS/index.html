Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

      
Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

    

    

      
Tax ID Application Infrastructure

      

        
Competent tax authority
State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA); online electronic portal: the Unified Electronic Tax Administration (e-Tax) platform, including the China Electronic Tax Administration national portal and the provincial electronic tax bureau portal where the WFOE is registered.

        
E-government filing portal
Step 1: The WFOE is incorporated with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR). The business license issued by SAMR contains the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which is the single statutory Tax Identification Number (TIN) for the WFOE for enterprise income tax, VAT, payroll, and withholding purposes. Step 2: Within 30 days after the business license is issued, the WFOE must complete the tax registration information collection in the Unified Electronic Tax Administration (e-Tax). The submission package includes the business license, articles of association, shareholder register, non-resident shareholder identity documents (including the Hong Kong Certificate of Incorporation and Business Registration Certificate), legal representative and financial officer identity documents, registered address proof, bank account confirmation, and projected business scope with estimated VAT taxable sales. Step 3: The competent tax authority records the WFOE as a VAT taxpayer. If the estimated annual taxable sales amount to RMB 5,000,000 or more, general VAT taxpayer registration is mandatory; below that threshold the WFOE is registered as a small-scale VAT taxpayer, with the right to elect general VAT taxpayer status if proper accounting and fixed place of business are established. Step 4: A foreign non-resident enterprise without an establishment or place of business in mainland China is not required to obtain a full TIN; where a China-source income payment is made by the WFOE to such non-resident enterprise, the WFOE registers as the withholding taxpayer and, where treaty relief is sought, the foreign enterprise must obtain a Hong Kong tax residence certificate from the Inland Revenue Department of Hong Kong. Step 5: If the foreign non-resident enterprise establishes an actual establishment or place of business in mainland China, it must complete the non-resident enterprise tax registration procedure through e-Tax and obtain a non-resident taxpayer code or USCC as applicable, after which it must file and pay taxes through the same portal.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

      

        
Default tax classification
Entity_Level_Taxation

        
Available election forms

The WFOE may elect to register as a general VAT taxpayer even when annual taxable turnover is below the RMB 5,000,000 small-scale threshold, subject to STA conditions, and may apply for preferential tax incentive classifications such as Small and Low-Profit Enterprise or High and New Technology Enterprise where statutory conditions are met; these are preferential tax elections, not entity classification elections.

      

    

    

      
VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

      

        
Standard VAT / GST rate
13%

        
Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)
No turnover threshold triggers initial tax registration; every incorporated WFOE must complete tax registration after issuance of the business license. Mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration arises at annual taxable sales of RMB 5,000,000 or more. Below RMB 5,000,000 the WFOE is a small-scale VAT taxpayer, with a small-scale monthly sales VAT exemption of RMB 100,000 (RMB 300,000 per quarter) under the current policy period.

      

      
      

        Export 0% zero-rating conditions
        
Goods physically exported from the customs territory of China, including IVD reagents and laboratory diagnostic products legally exported by the WFOE, are subject to VAT at the zero rate provided the WFOE is the exporter of record, the goods are actually exported under customs supervision, the sale is made to an overseas buyer, the export proceeds are received and repatriated in accordance with SAFE rules, and the export is declared in the export tax rebate system. The diagnostic reagents must not be prohibited from export and must hold any required medical device or in-vitro diagnostic product license under the National Medical Products Administration rules. The refundable amount is the input VAT attributable to the exported goods at the applicable export rebate rate, generally 13% for finished medical device products. Qualifying offshore digital services supplied to non-resident recipients and wholly consumed outside China may also qualify for VAT zero-rating under the cross-border services zero-rating catalogue; the distribution of lab diagnostic reagents is a goods transaction and must follow the physical export route.

Stage 04
Banking & Treasury Architecture

      
Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

    

    

      
Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

      

        
Current-account convertibility
The Renminbi is not a freely convertible currency for capital account purposes; current account items are largely liberalised but remain subject to genuine trade documentary review. A wholesale WFOE engaged in distribution of diagnostic reagents must treat equity and debt flows from Hong Kong as capital account transactions. Inward equity capital is permissible only after SAFE foreign direct investment (FDI) registration is completed, a dedicated foreign-exchange settlement capital account is opened, and the bank receives the necessary business registration filings. Once received, registered capital cannot be deployed for off-permit activities; the bank monitors the funds' disbursement under a payment instruction mechanism and requires vouchers such as purchase contracts, invoices, and customs declarations for each release. Profit remittance to the Hong Kong shareholder is treated as a current account item: the bank will require audited annual financial statements, a corporate income tax final settlement certificate, a board resolution declaring dividends, and proof that accumulated losses have been recovered. There is no statutory remittance quota, but the amount cannot exceed the audited distributable reserve. Foreign-currency loans from the parent require SAFE debt registration; their repayments are subject to maturity and drawdown restrictions. Thus, convertibility exists but is documentary conditional, not a fungible market right.

        
Export proceeds surrender rules
Under the existing SAFE goods-trade verification regime, export proceeds must be received through a licensed domestic bank into the WFOE's trade settlement foreign-currency account and reported into the Trade in Goods Foreign Exchange Monitoring System on the same banking day. The historical mandatory surrender ratio has been abolished, so there is no fixed percentage conversion requirement. An A-class trade enterprise may keep receipts in foreign currency, sell them at market rates for CNY, or use them directly for legitimate import/trade payments. Any receipt that remains unmatched against an export customs declaration for a prolonged period (commonly 30-90 days under bank's internal thresholds) will trigger an authenticity review and possible conversion restriction.

      

      
Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC), with exclusive regulation delegated to the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

      

        Complete SAFE FDI registration and open a dedicated foreign-exchange capital account at a Tier-1 domestic bank. The HK parent then executes a SWIFT MT103 wire with the payment narrative clearly identifying the WFOE, the SAFE FDI registration reference, and the purpose classification 'direct investment capital contribution' in the bank's international balance-of-payments reporting. If the capital is remitted in RMB, the transaction can be cleared through CIPS or through the HK RMB clearing bank with the same FDI purpose code. The receiving bank will record the capital inflow for SAFE monitoring and apply its payment instruction regime, releasing funds only against supporting vouchers such as supplier contracts, customs declarations, payroll, or local tax notices. For export sales to hospitals or distributors, the WFOE receives customer TTs via MT103 into its trade settlement foreign-currency account, or uses bank collection services for documentary draft settlement, while preserving the full audit trail from sales order to delivery confirmation.Onshore deliverable foreign exchange forward contracts with a genuine trade or capital underlying, typically up to 12 months, executed through a licensed domestic bank and supported by the required SAFE real-risk documentation.Cross-currency swaps (CCS) and FX swaps for matched shareholder loan or trade cash flows, each requiring SAFE registration of the underlying debt or transaction and a clear net-open-exposure test.Vanilla FX options and forward structures for committed invoices, while offshore CNH non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are not permissible for the onshore WFOE; the Hong Kong parent may use offshore NDFs/CNH products to hedge its RMB exposure.
      

      
E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Rating: Not viable for onshore treasury operations. Offshore digital EMI platforms are not licensed by PBOC/SAFE to open domestic RMB/foreign-exchange accounts, access CIPS, process PRC tax payments, or manage SAFE capital account files for a mainland WFOE. They can be used only as an ancillary payment-notification or settlement-tracking layer by the Hong Kong parent. Routing WFOE customer receipts or supplier payments through an unlicensed EMI would breach China's foreign exchange controls and create material AML risk.
