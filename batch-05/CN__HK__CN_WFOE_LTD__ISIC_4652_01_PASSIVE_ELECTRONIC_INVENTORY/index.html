Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

Virtual offices and commercial mailbox addresses are generally not accepted for a mainland China WFOE. SAMR and the State Taxation Administration require a registered address consisting of a physically leasable premises, and banks require lease contracts, rent receipts, utility invoices, and evidence of business operations before opening corporate accounts. Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital is imposed for a WFOE engaged in wholesale distribution of electronic components. The registered capital is fixed in the Articles of Association and may be subscribed by the sole shareholder without immediate block account or escrow arrangements. Cash contributions must be paid in within the capital contribution period set in the Articles (commonly 10-20 years; for service/trading companies often 10-25 years). If non-monetary capital is used, a statutory valuation report is required and must be verified by an accountant. The registered capital and paid-in contributions are disclosed on the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System.

Funding & unlock path: Not Applicable for the pure distribution business; however, the WFOE must comply with the Work Safety Law when leasing office or storage space, including regular fire safety inspections and obtaining a fire safety inspection certificate if the premises are open to the public or exceed regulatory thresholds. The enterprise should also register its workplace with the local emergency management bureau and conduct occupational hazard declaration if any employees are exposed to warehouse lifting activities.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA) and its Electronic Tax Administration (e-Tax) online portal, administered locally by the provincial or municipal tax office where the Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise is registered; the Hong Kong Inland Revenue Department is relevant only for the non-resident shareholder's profits tax and residence certificate under the PRC-Hong Kong Double Taxation Arrangement.

E-government filing portal

The Hong Kong investor first completes foreign investment information registration with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR). Upon approval, the WFOE receives an 18-character Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which is the statutory Tax Identification Number. The WFOE must complete tax registration with the competent STA office within 30 calendar days of the business license by submitting through the e-Tax portal or in-person counters: the business license, articles of association, registered address proof, legal representative and finance officer identification, Hong Kong shareholder incorporation documents, and bank account details. The STA activates VAT invoice registration, determines small-scale or general VAT taxpayer status, sets filing frequency, and registers the WFOE as withholding agent for employee income tax and non-resident distributions. A Hong Kong non-resident shareholder does not obtain a PRC TIN unless it directly derives PRC-source income subject to withholding; in that case, the WFOE must complete non-resident income tax withholding registration and the shareholder should obtain a Hong Kong resident certificate from the Inland Revenue Department to claim treaty benefits.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None. PRC enterprise income tax law subjects the WFOE to mandatory entity-level taxation; there is no check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election. The taxpayer may elect general VAT taxpayer status once eligible, but that is a VAT collection mechanism, not an entity classification election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

All WFOEs must obtain basic tax and VAT registration from inception, with no monetary threshold. Mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration applies when annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000; below that level, small-scale taxpayer status and simplified VAT collection apply.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exported goods are zero-rated under PRC VAT if the WFOE has a completed customs export declaration, the goods physically leave the PRC customs territory, the buyer is an offshore counterparty, export proceeds are collected in foreign exchange and repatriated in compliance with State Administration of Foreign Exchange rules, and the export documentary chain is complete. Zero-rating for goods also entitles the exporter to an export VAT refund of input VAT. Passive distribution of electronic components is not an offshore service; zero-rating for services applies only to the cross-border services enumerated in the STA's zero-rating rules.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Export Goods Customs Declaration Form)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (or other recognised international transport document)
  • Commercial Invoice
  • Packing List
  • Export Sales Contract
  • Bank Foreign Exchange Collection Receipt / Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC)
  • Special VAT invoices for purchase inputs and the Export VAT Refund Application Declaration

China mandates the State Taxation Administration's Unified Electronic Invoice Platform under Golden Tax Phase IV. Special VAT invoices and full digital electronic invoices must be issued through STA-authorised channels or an STA-certified system-to-system interface; invoice data are transmitted to STA servers in real time at issuance, with anti-counterfeit invoice codes, QR codes, and digital stamping. General taxpayers require prior invoice quota authorisation, and hand-written or unauthorised invoice formats are not allowable for input VAT credit.

Where zero-rating is not substantiated, tax authorities will require repayment of the export VAT refund, claw back the corresponding input VAT credit, and impose late-payment surcharge at 0.05% per day. Administrative fines range from 50% to 500% of the tax evaded or falsely refunded; fraudulent export refund claims may trigger criminal liability, imprisonment, confiscation of property, suspension of export VAT refund qualification, and public tax blacklisting.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a managed floating exchange-rate system under the PBOC/SAFE. The RMB is convertible for current-account items without prior SAFE approval, while capital-account convertibility remains controlled. The WFOE's HK shareholder contribution is a capital-account inflow that requires FDI registration, foreign-exchange capital account opening, and post-entry authenticity verification. Profits distributed to the HK parent in USD or HKD are permissible only after audited financials, tax clearance, and Chinese CPA capital-verification; each dividend remittance requires a SAFE-authorized bank holding the FDI dividend-remittance filing and corporate income tax payment evidence. Export receipts must be repatriated but may be retained in a current-account foreign-exchange balance; no fixed mandatory conversion ratio applies. The practical hurdle is documentary: Chinese banks scrutinise the underlying trade, inventory movement, and electronic-component classification to distinguish passive stockholding from speculative currency flows. Treasury centralisation therefore gives transparency but limited currency flexibility; convertibility is operational, not absolute.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Statutory repatriation: export proceeds must be returned onto the WFOE's goods-trade foreign-exchange account in mainland China within 180 days after shipment/declaration, and the bank validates each receipt against the SAFE trade-monitoring electronic system. Mandatory conversion ratio: none; 100% of qualifying export proceeds may be either settled into CNY at market spot rates or retained in the current-account foreign-exchange quota, provided the quota is correctly registered with SAFE and supported by open export contracts/customs declarations. Non-compliance risks suspension of the enterprise's trade-foreign-exchange verification qualification.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) through the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE), with the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) supervising CNH offshore clearing and treasury activity in Hong Kong SAR

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Fintech EMI providers such as Airwallex, Revolut Business, and Wise Business are legally barred in mainland China from operating as licensed banks, accepting WFOE deposits, maintaining segregated client-fund accounts, issuing documentary credit, or onboarding the PRC entity as a domestic account holder. PBOC-regulated cross-border online payment institutions may assist e-commerce collection and cross-border RMB payment, but they are not a substitute for the Tier-1 bank rails that must carry FDI capital, export proceeds, LC issuance, and SWIFT clearing. Therefore fintech EMIs have only ancillary viability for marketplace collection channels; standalone treasury use is rated Low.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The receiving Chinese bank performs authenticity verification, then obtains the forex settlement application before converting funds into CNY through the capital-account FDI settlement form. The WFOE must hold its business licence, FDI information registration record, board resolution, share subscription agreement, and a payment-use table for foreign-exchange capital settlement before the CNY can be moved to the RMB operating account for permitted working-capital purposes. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: The PRC Master File is required when the total related-party transactions exceed RMB 1 billion in a single fiscal year or when other threshold tests under STA Announcement [2016] No. 42 are met. The Local File is mandatory when: (a) related-party tangible asset transfers exceed RMB 200 million for the year; (b) related-party intangible property transactions exceed RMB 100 million; or (c) other related-party transactions exceed RMB 40 million. The Country-by-Country Report is required for groups with annual consolidated revenue of at least RMB 5.5 billion. For this distribution WFOE, the most relevant threshold is related-party inventory purchases exceeding RMB 200 million, which immediately triggers the contemporaneous Local File obligation.

Economic substance scope: The WFOE and its Hong Kong intermediate parent both face Economic Substance scrutiny. The PRC General Anti-Avoidance Rules (GAAR) under Article 47 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law and STA Announcement [2017] No. 6 permit recharacterization of transactions lacking commercial reasonableness. The Hong Kong parent must satisfy the substantive Hong Kong Economic Substance Requirements (ESR) for pure equity holding: maintain registered office, local director, company secretary, statutory records, and adequate records demonstrating that it has substance proportionate to the dividend income received. More critically, if the Hong Kong company performs no real procurement, sales, inventory management, or risk-assumption functions, the China WFOE is treated as the actual source of the trading profit, and the Hong Kong entity may be regarded as a conduit subject to reassessment of the entire group profit in China.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Cross-border transfer pricing manipulation and round-tripping of profits through a substance-less Hong Kong intermediate entity

Statutory sanction: Article 41 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law and Article 110 of the Implementation Regulations authorizes a transfer pricing audit adjustment. The tax authority may impose a deemed profit rate of 15% on turnover under Article 55, require principal-to-principal recharacterization, and assess a 5% annual interest penalty on the underpaid tax through Article 56 of the Tax Collection Law. In severe cases, an administrative penalty of 50% of the underpaid tax is imposed under Article 63 of the Tax Administration Law.

Director personal liability: Directors of the WFOE and the Hong Kong parent are jointly and severally liable for the unpaid corporate tax under Article 4(2) of the PRC Criminal Law doctrine and Article 1 of the Criminal Law Interpretation on Tax Evasion. Directors who knowingly signed false intercompany agreements, approved mispriced invoices, or structured the round-tripping scheme are exposed to criminal prosecution under Article 201 of the Criminal Law for tax evasion, carrying imprisonment of up to 7 years, criminal fines, asset confiscation, and a travel ban preventing departure from mainland China until the tax liability is settled.
Fabricated intercompany expense and invoice fraud (假发票/虚开发票) disguised as logistics, warehousing, or group services

Statutory sanction: Submission of false expense invoices violates the Invoice Administration Measures and Article 63 of the Tax Administration Law, allowing recovery of the evaded tax, an administrative fine of up to 500% of the evaded amount, and disallowance of the false deductions. The issuance or use of falsified special VAT invoices triggers Article 205 of the Criminal Law (Crime of Issuing Falsified Special Invoices), punishable by life imprisonment in severe cases; for ordinary invoice fraud, the fine and imprisonment terms under Article 205-1 apply.

Director personal liability: The CFO and general manager of the WFOE who approved the false invoices face criminal detention immediately upon criminal filing, with the property preserved for civil restitution. The director of the Hong Kong entity who profited from the circular fund flows is subject to the PRC Criminal Procedure Law's cross-border asset recovery, freeze of bank accounts, a red-notice level travel restriction through the National Immigration Administration, and imprisonment ranging from 3 to 10 years for tax evasion if the aggregate amount exceeds RMB 1 million.
Failure to meet statutory non-resident withholding obligations for dividends repatriated to the Hong Kong parent without CIT declaration

Statutory sanction: Article 37 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law mandates the WFOE to withhold 10% on the full RMB 8 million distribution within 7 days of payment. The withholding agent's failure to do so triggers Article 69 of the Tax Administration Law, imposing a penalty of 50% to 300% of the un-withheld tax and a daily surcharge for late payment. The tax authority will additionally deny any later attempt to claim the 5% DTA benefit unless the beneficial ownership and 12-month shareholding requirements are proven retroactively; a demanding rigor practically impossible once the offshore bank transfer is complete.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for the un-withheld tax as statutory withholding agents. Civil liability includes full restitution of the RMB 800,000 un-withheld amount plus penalties and interest, enforceable against the directors' personal assets in mainland China. The criminally culpable director who knowingly engineered the loan label scheme is subject to the corporate veil-piercing doctrine of Article 20 of the PRC Company Law and criminal liability under Article 201 of the Criminal Law, with a non-discretionary travel ban (黑名单) imposed through exit control lists until the tax authority certifies full payment of the outstanding liability, and potential imprisonment from 2 to 5 years given the evasion exceeds RMB 500,000.
