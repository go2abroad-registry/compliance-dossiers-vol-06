Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None. Under the Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622), a private company limited by shares may be incorporated with a nominal share capital of one share of HK$1.00. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; taxpayer filing is conducted through the STA Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax Bureau) portal at www.chinatax.gov.cn and the relevant provincial Electronic Tax Bureau systems.

E-government filing portal

A foreign non-resident investor establishing a WFOE must first complete company incorporation with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR). The Business License contains the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which is the sole statutory Tax Identification Number (TIN) and VAT registration number of the WFOE. No separate TIN certificate is issued. Within 30 days of the Business License date, the WFOE's taxpayer information is automatically transmitted to the STA through the multi-license integration system, and the WFOE must file supplemental taxpayer information through the e-Tax Bureau or the local tax service hall. Required documentation includes: the SAMR Business License; the foreign investor's notarized and China-certified corporate registration documents (or apostilled equivalent); the board resolution establishing the WFOE; the memorandum/articles of association; the registered office lease or proof of address; legal representative identity documents; appointment documents for the finance officer/principal accounting personnel; and bank account opening confirmation. After verification, the STA activates the tax registration, assigns tax type attributes, determines VAT taxpayer type (small-scale or general taxpayer), and registers the entity for corporate income tax, VAT, stamp duty, urban construction tax and surcharges. If annual taxable sales are expected to exceed RMB 5,000,000, VAT general taxpayer registration is mandatory; otherwise the entity may remain a small-scale taxpayer until the threshold is exceeded. The foreign non-resident investor itself does not obtain a Chinese TIN merely by owning the WFOE; any PRC-source income payable to the non-resident is subject to withholding tax by the WFOE.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation (PRC resident enterprise; the WFOE is a separate limited liability legal person subject to enterprise income tax at 25% and is not eligible for pass-through or check-the-box transparency. The Hong Kong SAR parent is treated as a non-resident shareholder, not a branch of the WFOE.)

Available election forms

None (no check-the-box election or hybrid mismatch election exists under PRC tax law; entity-level taxation is mandatory for a WFOE. The only related elective measure is the VAT small-scale/general taxpayer status election below the statutory turnover threshold, which is not an entity classification election.)

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

CNY. No turnover threshold applies to the basic tax registration of an incorporated entity: formation as a WFOE triggers automatic tax registration. For VAT classification, annual taxable sales not exceeding RMB 5,000,000 qualify as small-scale taxpayer status; once annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000, the WFOE must register as a VAT general taxpayer.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A WFOE may claim 0% VAT for the export of used construction excavators only if: (1) the goods are physically exported from mainland China customs territory under a Customs release; (2) the export transaction is supported by a valid Export Goods Customs Declaration; (3) the WFOE is the supplier and receives the export sales consideration in foreign exchange through a designated bank; (4) the proceeds are repatriated within the statutory period; and (5) the input VAT on the purchase of the used machinery is substantiated by VAT Special Invoices or customs import VAT payment documents. The particular export commodity classification must fall within the applicable export zero-rating/tax rebate coverage and must not be subject to export prohibition. Exports that fail these conditions, including exports to bonded zones without actual customs departure or transactions with uncollected foreign exchange, are not zero-rated.

  • Export Goods Customs Declaration Form (customs export declaration with electronic release message)
  • Bill of Lading (or equivalent multimodal, airway, or railway transport document evidencing physical export)
  • Export Commercial Invoice
  • Packing List
  • Export Sales Contract or Purchase Order
  • Bank Foreign Exchange Receipt Voucher / Export Proceeds Collection Confirmation (bank-confirmed inward remittance certificate)
  • VAT Special Invoice(s) for the domestic purchase of the used excavators, or the Customs Import VAT Payment Certificate if previously imported, to substantiate the input VAT refund

All VAT invoices must be issued through the STA-controlled e-invoicing system. The fully digitalized electronic invoice carries a unique tax control code and anti-counterfeiting QR code, and invoice data is uploaded in real time to the STA Golden Tax system. Special VAT invoices are transmitted to the national VAT invoice verification and cross-matching platform in real time, enabling automatic matching of input VAT credits and export declarations. Taxpayers use the e-Tax Bureau's online invoice issuance functions and API-based interfaces; no manual cash register invoice is accepted for a WFOE required to issue Special VAT Invoices.

The STA and China Customs cross-match export declarations, Special VAT Invoice data, logistics records, and foreign exchange receipts. If the 180-day FX repatriation deadline is missed, or if documentation is false, incomplete, or inconsistent, the tax authority will disallow the 0% rate, require output VAT at the applicable rate, recover any refunded/offset input VAT, and impose late payment surcharge (0.05% per day on overdue tax). Under the Law on Administration of Tax Collection, deliberate false export zero-rating may attract a fine of 50% to 5 times the tax underpaid, suspension or cancellation of export tax rebate qualification, downgrade of taxpayer credit rating, and criminal referral for fraudulently obtaining export tax rebates. The clawback is retrospective to the original refund/credit period.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The mainland-resident WFOE operates under SAFE's bifurcated currency regime. Current-account transactions are convertible in principle after banks perform real-transaction verification through the Goods Trade and Service Trade monitoring systems; every payment or receipt must reference an authentic contract, invoice, customs declaration, or service receipt. Capital-account transactions are not freely convertible. Inbound FDI equity injections require a SAFE FDI registration, a designated capital-foreign-exchange account, and use that conforms to the Foreign Investment Negative List and the registered business scope. Capital proceeds cannot be diverted into unapproved wealth management, real estate, securities, or related-party lending. Profit remittance to the Hong Kong shareholder is treated as a current-account outward remittance only after the WFOE completes its annual statutory audit and final corporate income tax settlement; the bank will demand the audited statements, the shareholders' dividend resolution, tax filing certificates, and beneficial-ownership declarations. If the Hong Kong shareholder is a qualifying beneficial owner of more than 25 percent, the China-Hong Kong double tax arrangement supports a 5 percent dividend withholding rate rather than the usual 10 percent, subject to a Hong Kong Tax Residency Certificate and treaty-benefit analysis. The WFOE must repatriate all export proceeds to a domestic bank rather than leaving them on an offshore payment platform; retention in foreign currency is permissible, but no fixed percentage must be sold, and the receipt must be declared under the goods-trade monitoring framework. The Hong Kong jurisdiction itself has no exchange controls, but the WFOE cannot use Hong Kong as a bypass around onshore capital restrictions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the PRC Goods Trade Foreign Exchange Administration, export proceeds must be repatriated to a domestic bank and entered into the Goods Trade Foreign Exchange Account or settled into RMB. The standard SAFE administration deadline is the 90-day collection period from the date of the Customs export declaration; if proceeds are not credited by that point, a trade-data discrepancy report is mandatory. There is no fixed mandatory conversion ratio: 100 percent must be repatriated, but 0-100 percent can be held in foreign currency or sold to the bank at market rate, so the true surrender obligation is full physical repatriation and prompt reporting rather than a quota-based conversion.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the People's Bank of China (PBOC) for the mainland China WFOE, with Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) supervising the Hong Kong clearing node as a fully convertible offshored liquidity layer.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Rating: LOW / NOT AUTHORISED for primary onshore banking. Offshore digital EMIs do not hold a PBOC payment institution license sufficient to operate the SAFE goods-trade monitoring and capital-account settlement window for a China-resident WFOE. The WFOE must use a licensed bank for export receipt repatriation, capital injection settlement, and dividend remittance. An HK-licensed EMI can function only as an ancillary collection or payout utility for the Hong Kong treasury node, never as the final channel for onshore RMB clearings; all onshore CNY obligations and export proceeds must move through CNAPS, CIPS, or SAFE-monitored banking rails. The EMT relationship therefore cannot replace a statutory Tier-1 commercial bank account.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

SWIFT MT103 routed from the HK shareholder's account at a licensed bank to the WFOE's SAFE-registered Capital-Foreign-Exchange Account at an onshore Tier-1 bank. The remitter must exactly match the registered shareholder and the SAFE FDI registration confirmation must be produced before crediting. The message should carry the bank's international balance-of-payments declaration category for inbound direct-investment equity capital and an explicit narrative such as WFOE registered capital injection under SAFE FDI registration number XXXX; no goods invoice or secondhand-machinery import code should be used for an equity wire. The onshore bank will post the credit to the capital-foreign-exchange account, validate the business against the Foreign Investment Negative List, settle permitted amounts to RMB for operating expenses, and the WFOE's PRC certified public accountant will issue a capital verification report to update the corporate capital ledger. For the HK intermediate treasury layer, the HKMA licensed bank may also require a board borrowing resolution and source-of-funds pack before releasing the transfer.For payments to overseas suppliers of used construction machinery and implements, the WFOE should use a clean SWIFT MT103 for low-value open-account disbursements, an irrevocable documentary letter of credit with MT700 structure for high-value imported earthmovers, and documentary collections using D/P or D/A when there is an established trade relationship. Every MT103 must include the underlying goods-import payment declaration reference and the Trade in Goods monitoring code; the bank should be given the commercial invoice, packing list, transport document, insurance certificate, auction or remarketing award if any, and condition survey. Where the beneficiary is a Hong Kong seller and settlement is in CNH, the payment can clear through CIPS; where settlement is in USD or EUR, the transfer moves through the commercial bank's nostro and Vostro clearing rails. The WFOE must retain all Customs Import Declarations and condition reports in the bank transaction file.Onshore CNY/USD and CNY/EUR outright forwards and swaps with a licensed PRC bank on real-demand background, with trade or capital exposure documentation and no net-open-position speculation.Cross-currency interest rate swaps for long-term approved external borrowings or FDI term liabilities after SAFE external-debt registration, with notional and tenor aligned to the underlying obligation.Hong Kong offshore CNH forwards and NDFs are permissible for the HK treasury and clearing vehicle with genuine CNH receipts; they are not permissible onshore for the WFOE as standalone speculative instruments, and the boundaries must prevent offshore NDFs from acting as an unlicensed capital-account conversion channel.Structured forwards with zero-cost collar or knock-in and knock-out features are permissible only where the embedded options are supported by the same real-demand underlying trade and are executed through a licensed commercial bank, not through unregulated digital derivative platforms.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Rating: LOW / NOT AUTHORISED for primary onshore banking. Offshore digital EMIs do not hold a PBOC payment institution license sufficient to operate the SAFE goods-trade monitoring and capital-account settlement window for a China-resident WFOE. The WFOE must use a licensed bank for export receipt repatriation, capital injection settlement, and dividend remittance. An HK-licensed EMI can function only as an ancillary collection or payout utility for the Hong Kong treasury node, never as the final channel for onshore RMB clearings; all onshore CNY obligations and export proceeds must move through CNAPS, CIPS, or SAFE-monitored banking rails. The EMT relationship therefore cannot replace a statutory Tier-1 commercial bank account.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% standard PRC resident corporate income tax rate; 10% is the statutory non-resident withholding rate on China-source passive income such as dividends, interest and royalties under Articles 3 and 37 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% statutory Chinese withholding tax on outbound dividends paid by the WFOE to its non-resident shareholder; reduced to 5% under the China-Hong Kong Double Taxation Arrangement (DTA) where the beneficial owner of the dividend is a Hong Kong company that holds directly at least 25% of the capital of the Chinese paying company
