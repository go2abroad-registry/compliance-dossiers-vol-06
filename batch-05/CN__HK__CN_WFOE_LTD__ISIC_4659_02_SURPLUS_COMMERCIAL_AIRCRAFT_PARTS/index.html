Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; online electronic tax service portal: National Electronic Tax Service (etax.chinatax.gov.cn) and the relevant Provincial/Regional Electronic Tax Office platform

E-government filing portal

For a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE) established in mainland China with a Hong Kong SAR corporate shareholder, the entity obtains its statutory taxpayer identity upon issuance of the business license by the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR); the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code on the business license automatically constitutes the Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) and VAT registration identification. Within 30 days of receiving the license, the WFOE must complete initial tax registration with the local competent tax office through the National Electronic Tax Service, activate the tax registration record, confirm the taxpayer classification, and link the entity to the VAT general/small-scale taxpayer system. The foreign nonprofit-resident parent in Hong Kong SAR must obtain a non-resident taxpayer identification from the Chinese tax authority for withholding and China-source income purposes through the tax withholding agent or direct registration; this non-resident TIN is not the WFOE's TIN but is required for cross-border payments and tax files.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The export sale of surplus commercial aircraft parts and avionics may qualify for VAT zero-rating/export-rebate treatment only where the WFOE has physically exported the goods through a Chinese customs port, declared the goods under an export customs declaration, sold the goods to a genuine foreign buyer, received payment in foreign currency diverted through statutory banking channels, and repatriated the export proceeds to a bank account in mainland China within the prescribed regulatory time limit. The transaction must not involve domestic consumption in China and must be supported by authenticated contracts, commercial invoices, packing lists, and logistics records reflecting an overseas destination. For trading enterprises, the export rebate method is the exemption-and-refund method based on the exact export purchase invoices and customs data.

  • Customs Export Declaration (China Customs export cargo declaration form) for the surplus aircraft parts and avionics
  • Commercial invoice issued by the WFOE to the overseas buyer
  • Packing list containing HS classifications, quantities, and unit values
  • Bill of Lading (or air waybill in the case of airfreight) evidencing export from a Chinese port or airport to the offshore consignee
  • Bank inward remittance receipt / export foreign-exchange collection voucher issued by an authorized Chinese foreign exchange bank evidencing repatriation of the export proceeds
  • Export sales contract and booking confirmation establishing the genuine cross-border trade

The WFOE must issue electronic invoices through the STA's all-digital electronic invoice (full digital invoice) platform under the Golden Tax Phase IV system, with real-time secure transmission of invoice data to the national electronic tax network. The system applies automated tax registration numbers, invoice geometries, tax control serial numbers, and digital authentication codes; no separate paper fiscal stamp is required beyond the STA-authorized digital invoice identity and online verification application. VAT special invoices are further screened through the national VAT invoice verification/inspection system.

The STA may retrospectively disallow zero-rating/export rebate claims and require the WFOE to repay the refunded VAT together with daily late-payment interest from the date of refund. Penalties for fraudulent or unverified export zero-rating range from 50% to five times the evaded tax and may include revocation of export tax-rebate qualification. Where facts indicate deliberate export-tax-refund fraud, criminal liability may be pursued under the Criminal Law, including imprisonment, fines, and confiscation of illegally obtained rebates; the Golden Tax IV system cross-references customs declarations, bank FX settlement records, supplier invoices, and logistics data to identify fictitious or circular export transactions.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Mainland China's RMB is fully convertible on current account for genuine trade and payment items, while capital account transactions remain heavily regulated and require case-by-case SAFE registration. A China WFOE routinely operates through an FDI capital settlement account and a goods trade foreign exchange account. Inbound equity injections must be declared, verified, and tracked through the SAFE Capital Project Information System, with domestic Tier-1 banks acting as statutory gatekeepers. Export proceeds must be collected under the goods trade foreign exchange administration and repatriated to mainland China within the applicable statutory collection period. There is no mandatory conversion to RMB; a WFOE may lawfully retain USD, EUR, or HKD liquidity in its designated foreign exchange settlement account. Profit repatriation to the Hong Kong shareholder requires audited annual financial statements, an unqualified Chinese tax clearance, a board resolution approving dividend distribution, and submission to the bank for real-need verification before the outward remittance is executed under the correct current-account dividend purpose code. Because the commercial scope is surplus civil aircraft rotables and avionics, all foreign exchange declarations must be aligned with customs declarations, import/export licenses, and CAAC aviation regulatory permits. The HKMA component ensures full offshore convertibility, no surrender obligations, and no regulatory barriers to FX hedging in Hong Kong; however the mainland WFOE-to-HK treasury funding route is a capital or trade payment requiring a documented underlying transaction.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the SAFE Goods Trade Foreign Exchange Administration framework, no mandatory conversion ratio applies. Export proceeds from the aviation parts trading business must be repatriated to a designated foreign exchange settlement account in mainland China within 180 days of the export customs declaration, unless the WFOE obtains a prior SAFE offshore retention permit. The company must complete trade foreign exchange income and expenditure verification through the banking platform by matching foreign currency receipts with customs declarations, trade contracts, invoices, and shipping documents. The WFOE may retain the foreign currency or sell it voluntarily through a licensed Tier-1 domestic commercial bank; any outward foreign exchange sale for trade settlement must be supported by the underlying goods trade transaction and recorded in the Foreign Exchange Monitoring System.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange of the People's Republic of China (SAFE) acting under the People's Bank of China (PBOC) for mainland China treasury operations; Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) as the currency board authority for the Hong Kong SAR clearing hub

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Viability rating: Not viable as a primary banking or FX clearing layer for the mainland WFOE. Under PRC law and SAFE regulations, only banks licensed by PBOC and SAFE may lawfully conduct foreign exchange settlement, RMB/foreign currency conversion, and cross-border payments for a mainland-incorporated entity. Offshore digital EMIs such as Wise, Revolut Business, Payoneer, and Airwallex are legally barred from providing regulated capital account services or goods-trade settlement for a mainland WFOE because they do not hold a Chinese banking license and cannot access SAFE's monitoring and verification systems. Using an unlicensed EMI for inbound capital injection or statutory export collections would violate the PRC Foreign Exchange Administration Regulations and could trigger balance-of-payments penalties, loss of the CAAC aviation trading license, and freezing of the WFOE's banking relationships. The Hong Kong treasury vehicle may use an EMI for incidental offshore operating expenses, but all mainline inbound and outbound clearing must pass through Tier-1 domestic banks with full SWIFT MT103/MT202 audit trails, China customs declarations, and SAFE purpose codes.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

From the Hong Kong treasury parent or ultimate foreign investor, remit via SWIFT MT103 directly to the WFOE's designated Foreign Currency Capital Account held at a Tier-1 Chinese bank. The SWIFT field 70 remittance information must state 'FDI Capital Injection' and include the SAFE FDI registration code, the WFOE's corporate name, and the capital account number. The receiving bank will verify the payment against the SAFE Capital Project Information System, record the purpose code as 'Equity Investment in a Resident Enterprise', and block any funds not matching the foreign exchange registration. After receipt, a licensed PRC certified public accountant must issue a registered capital verification report confirming the actual infusion and the valuation basis. The proceeds may only be settled into RMB for genuine operating purposes after the bank receives the capital settlement application and the corresponding payment instruction, and the WFOE must preserve all underlying subscription agreements and board resolutions for the annual SAFE reporting cycle.For payments to overseas suppliers of surplus aircraft parts, use SWIFT MT103 for open-account settlement and pre-payments, with full invoice, contract, customs declaration, and CAAC import/export license references in the payment narrative. For large-value rotable purchases, a commercial letter of credit issued by a mainland Tier-1 bank and confirmed through a global or Hong Kong correspondent bank is the preferred clearing rail, as it incorporates document scrutiny against the aviation part certificates and shipping terms. For established suppliers, documentary collections (D/P or D/A) may be used where short-term trade credit is acceptable and the transaction value is below the bank's enhanced due diligence threshold. All outbound wires must carry the correct SAFE goods-trade purpose code and be supported by the underlying purchase order, proforma invoice, bill of lading/airway bill, and applicable export control classification; any discrepancy between the payment amount and the declared trade value will trigger a bank compliance hold.Onshore RMB/foreign currency executed forwards with documentary trade or investment exposure; the notional amount must not exceed the underlying receivables, inventory purchase obligations, or foreign debt principal and the contract must be closed within the actual settlement window under SAFE real-need verification.RMB/USD cross-currency swaps (CCS) for long-dated capital account conversions such as project finance repayments, FDI capital settlement, or dividend remittance streams; requires an approved underlying exposure, board authorization, and the Chinese bank's internal derivatives qualification.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are prohibited as an onshore corporate hedging instrument for mainland Chinese WFOEs; NDF trading may only be executed by the Hong Kong treasury vehicle under HKMA/SFC market rules and cannot be booked into the mainland WFOE's financial statements.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Viability rating: Not viable as a primary banking or FX clearing layer for the mainland WFOE. Under PRC law and SAFE regulations, only banks licensed by PBOC and SAFE may lawfully conduct foreign exchange settlement, RMB/foreign currency conversion, and cross-border payments for a mainland-incorporated entity. Offshore digital EMIs such as Wise, Revolut Business, Payoneer, and Airwallex are legally barred from providing regulated capital account services or goods-trade settlement for a mainland WFOE because they do not hold a Chinese banking license and cannot access SAFE's monitoring and verification systems. Using an unlicensed EMI for inbound capital injection or statutory export collections would violate the PRC Foreign Exchange Administration Regulations and could trigger balance-of-payments penalties, loss of the CAAC aviation trading license, and freezing of the WFOE's banking relationships. The Hong Kong treasury vehicle may use an EMI for incidental offshore operating expenses, but all mainline inbound and outbound clearing must pass through Tier-1 domestic banks with full SWIFT MT103/MT202 audit trails, China customs declarations, and SAFE purpose codes.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File threshold: annual related-party trading of tangible assets exceeding CNY 200 million, intangible transactions exceeding CNY 100 million, or financial transactions exceeding CNY 100 million; Master File threshold: annual aggregate related-party transactions exceeding CNY 1 billion. The WFOE must also file Schedule A105090 listing related parties, and prepare a Special File on value chain and profit allocation if requested.

Economic substance scope: For the HK parent to claim DTA benefits, it must demonstrate Core Income-Generating Activities (CIGA) in Hong Kong (contracting, actual decision-making, staff, premises, corporate governance). Hong Kong does not impose Caribbean-style ESR, but the IRD requires management and control tests; the PRC tax authorities will apply substance-over-form and Article 47 GAAR to disregard a pure conduit, recharacterize transfers as domestically sourced, and deny the 5% dividend rate. The WFOE itself must also have economic substance in Mainland China - physical office, qualified personnel, inventory custody, contract execution - to avoid being regarded as a shell or putting the Hong Kong parent's treaty position at risk.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Under PRC Law on Tax Administration and Collection Article 63 and Criminal Law Article 201, the tax authority imposes a penalty of 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax and orders repayment with daily surcharge (0.05%); criminal liability applies when the evaded tax exceeds CNY 10,000 and represents over 10% of taxable income, punishable by imprisonment up to 7 years and a fine of up to 5 times the evaded tax.

Director personal liability: Directors and financial officers who orchestrated or knowingly signed false returns are personally liable for civil restitution of the underpaid tax, interest, and penalties; they may be subject to exit restrictions and travel bans (blacklisting) under the PRC social credit enforcement law while a criminal investigation is pending; if convicted, individuals may face personal imprisonment and lifetime disqualification from corporate management positions.
Transfer Pricing Mispricing

Statutory sanction: PRC Enterprise Income Tax Law Article 41 authorizes the tax authority to re-determine taxable income at arm's length and impose additional tax plus interest at the central bank benchmark plus 5 percentage points per annum. Article 44 allows the authority to estimate taxable income if TP documentation is absent or inadequate; penalties of up to RMB 10,000 for failure to provide documentation and corresponding adjustments to tax refunds.

Director personal liability: Directors who signed or approved the manipulated intercompany price may be jointly and severally liable with the WFOE for the adjusted tax and interest to the extent the company's assets are insufficient; enforcement measures include freezing personal bank accounts, seizure of assets, travel bans, and public listing as a dishonest person. Where false TP documentation is submitted to the tax authority for the purpose of underpaying tax, directors may face criminal liability under Article 201 of the Criminal Law.
Shell Company / Treaty Abuse

Statutory sanction: Under the PRC GAAR (Enterprise Income Tax Law Article 47 and SAT Announcement No. 118/2019), the tax authority may disregard the Hong Kong conduit, re-source the dividends as from a PRC resident, re-impose WHT at 10% plus interest and late-payment penalties, and deny DTA treatment retroactively. Under the Inland Revenue Code of the Hong Kong SAR, submitting false statements to obtain a Tax Residency Certificate triggers penalties and imprisonment; serious cross-border abuse is prosecuted as tax fraud.

Director personal liability: Directors of the Hong Kong parent and the WFOE are exposed to personal restitution for the extra withholding tax and penalties if they knowingly used the shell structure; the PRC court may pierce the corporate veil, impose a travel ban, freeze assets in mainland accounts, and refer the case to the criminal authorities. Singapore/Hong Kong regulators may revoke the Hong Kong corporate registration and blacklist directors from acting as company officers; prison terms of up to 2 years apply under the Inland Revenue Ordinance for knowingly making false statements to obtain DTA benefits.
