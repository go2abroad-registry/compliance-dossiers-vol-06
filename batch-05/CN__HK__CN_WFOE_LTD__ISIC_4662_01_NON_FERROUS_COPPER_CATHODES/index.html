Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA) and its National Electronic Tax Administration platform (e-Taxation), integrated with the China International Trade Single Window for export customs and foreign exchange data.

E-government filing portal

For the mainland China WFOE, the foreign investor first obtains a business license from the State Administration for Market Regulation; the Unified Social Credit Code printed on that license is the statutory taxpayer identification number under China's multi-license integration regime. Within 30 calendar days of business license issuance, the WFOE must complete taxpayer registration through the Electronic Tax Administration platform, submit entity and beneficial-owner particulars, appoint the legal representative and registered finance officer, and report all opened bank accounts to the STA. VAT taxpayer identity is then confirmed either as a small-scale taxpayer or, if annual VAT-taxable turnover reaches the mandatory threshold, as a general VAT taxpayer. A foreign non-resident enterprise without an establishment or place of business in mainland China that derives Chinese-source income must complete a non-resident enterprise tax registration with the competent STA office or through its withholding agent, receive a non-resident tax identification number, and, where relevant, file a treaty-benefit application to claim reduced China withholding rates under the applicable double taxation arrangement.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None. China does not permit US-style check-the-box elections or hybrid mismatch elections for a WFOE; the WFOE is a Chinese resident enterprise subject to entity-level Enterprise Income Tax and transactional VAT. A small-scale VAT taxpayer may voluntarily elect general VAT taxpayer status if below the RMB 5,000,000 annual threshold, but this is a VAT collection election and not a tax classification election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 annual VAT-taxable sales for mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration; small-scale taxpayers may voluntarily register as general VAT taxpayers below that threshold; eligible small-scale taxpayers retain a monthly sales exemption of RMB 100,000 where the statutory conditions are satisfied.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The WFOE must be a VAT general taxpayer in good standing, have completed export registration with Customs, conclude a genuine sale of Grade A refined copper cathodes to an overseas non-resident buyer, obtain a valid Customs Export Declaration evidencing physical departure from mainland China, and repatriate the export proceeds in foreign currency to a domestic designated foreign exchange account within the statutory deadline. The transaction must be declared in the China International Trade Single Window and matched to the VAT invoice and customs data before the VAT export zero rate and the corresponding input-VAT refund can be sustained. Qualifying offshore digital services are not relevant to this physical-goods scope, and any deemed export treatment would require separate factual justification.

  • Customs Export Declaration (export customs bill of entry)
  • Bill of Lading
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Export Contract or Sales Agreement
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC), where required, or equivalent bank foreign exchange receipt evidence
  • Documentary Letter of Credit or LME Warrant Transfer documents, as applicable
  • Electronic export tax refund declaration submitted through the China International Trade Single Window

VAT invoices, including special VAT invoices, must be issued through the STA-controlled Golden Tax Phase IV electronic invoice system using a tax control device, Tax U-Key, or the fully digitalized e-invoice module of the Electronic Tax Administration platform. Special VAT invoices require pre-issuance validation against the buyer's Unified Social Credit Code and the seller's invoice quota in the VAT invoice comprehensive service platform; each invoice is encrypted, digitally signed, and transmitted to the STA in near-real-time. Digital stamping and invoice inventory reconciliation are mandatory; ERP/API integrations are allowed only via STA-approved e-invoice service providers, and the same invoice data must automatically match customs export declarations and bank foreign exchange receipt records.

The STA and Customs jointly verify export zero-rating by cross-matching Golden Tax Phase IV VAT invoice data, China Customs export declarations, and SAFE trade foreign-exchange records. If an export VAT refund or zero-rating claim is found to be false, materially incomplete, or not supported by genuine cargo movement and foreign-exchange repatriation, the tax authority will deny the refund, retrospectively claw back any refund already paid, impose late-payment interest, and apply administrative penalties of 50% to 500% of the tax underpaid. Confirmed fraudulent export refund activity constitutes the criminal offense of export-tax-refund fraud under the Criminal Law, subject to fines and imprisonment, automatic downgrade of customs and foreign-exchange facilitation status, and referral to the public security anti-smuggling authorities.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The RMB is a managed floating currency with full current account convertibility and tightly controlled capital account convertibility. The WFOE, as an onshore PRC legal entity, must obtain SAFE registration for all cross-border capital movements, and its daily copper cathode arbitrage will be treated as goods trade and must be matched, line by line, against Customs declarations and the SAFE trade monitoring system. Inbound equity and shareholder loans from the Hong Kong parent are capital account items: funds must enter via a SAFE-registered capital account and cannot be converted into RMB until a valid business purpose, contract, and tax certificate are shown; conversion into CNY for working capital requires a payment-to-application rule and unjustified conversion is prohibited. Surplus proceeds from export sales may be retained in USD or converted, but the bank and SAFE will query any prolonged mismatch between payable cycles and receivable cycles. Profit remittance to Hong Kong is a current-account outward transfer tested by the WFOE's audited annual accounts, corporate income tax payment, and careful application of withholding tax and permanent establishment rules; the bank will not release dividends unless the board resolution and tax receipt are present. In practical metals trading, the more severe convertibility constraint is documentary: if the copper cathode HS code, warehouse warrant chain, invoice amount, and LME pricing terms are inconsistent, SAFE will suspend settlement and FX conversion, even when the underlying transaction is real. Treasury policy must therefore block-box elastic USD hedges and maintain an extremely high paperwork standard for every MT103 and LC instruction.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the current PRC regulatory framework there is no mandatory surplus surrender percentage; SAFE administers export proceeds through a retention account model. All export-related foreign exchange receipts must be repatriated to the PRC banking system within the statutory goods-trade repatriation window applied by the receiving bank under SAFE monitoring, generally not later than 90 calendar days from the export Customs declaration date unless the contract lawfully provides for a later instalment and is declared in advance. The mandatory conversion ratio is zero percent. The WFOE may retain export receipts up to the SAFE-assigned macroprudential ceiling of its current-account foreign exchange settlement account and may convert amounts fully backed by trade invoices or import payment orders. Converted CNY must not be used for prohibited investment categories or for unauthorized repayment of shareholder loans.

Central bank supervision: The People's Bank of China (PBC) acting through the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE); the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) retains regulatory relevance only for the offshore CNH treasury leg of the group.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All HK-to-CN capital injections must be made after initial FDI registration at the local SAFE branch and MOFCOM filing, followed by opening of a SAFE-designated capital account at a Tier-1 PRC bank. The remittance should be sent by SWIFT MT103 or MT202 to the WFOE capital account with the beneficiary name and a clearly stated remittance purpose such as shareholder equity contribution or subordinated shareholder loan disbursement. The receiving bank will report the transaction using the SAFE direct investment transaction composition form, quoting the foreign exchange purpose code for FDI equity subscription or external debt drawing. Funds cannot be used until the bank recognizes the receipt; within one month the WFOE must obtain a capital verification report from a PRC certified public accountant. For shareholder loans, the HK parent must secure inbound external debt registration with SAFE and comply with the applicable macroprudential ceiling; each drawdown must be separately traced and repaid strictly through the registered external debt account.For outbound payment to overseas copper suppliers, the WFOE will use: first, SWIFT MT103 for direct wholesale transfers, quoting the sales contract, commercial invoice number, HS code 7403, and LME warrant reference in field 70; second, a domestic commercial letter of credit issued by the Tier-1 bank in favor of the supplier, payable against warehouse warrant, bill of lading, certificate of origin, and assay certificate; third, documentary collection on either D/P or D/A terms only for stable and pre-approved counterparties with insurance cover. For RMB-denominated invoices, clearing must use the Cross-Border Interbank Payment System or an RMB agent bank clearing arrangement. No disbursement may be made to a beneficiary, country, or account number that differs from the supporting trade contract; any deviation triggers a full enhanced due diligence re-run before payment.Onshore forward foreign exchange contracts executed by the WFOE with the licensed Tier-1 bank, supported by current trade invoices or SAFE-registered capital transactions and aligned with the payable or receivable maturity; no naked rollover is permitted.Onshore CNY/USD FX swaps for short-term liquidity management of capital inflows and outbound trade payments, subject to the bank's macroprudential position and the production of trade paper or SAFE registration documentation.Cross-currency swaps limited strictly to converting the registered external debt from the Hong Kong parent into the currency of the loan repayment schedule; the notional amount cannot exceed the SAFE-registered external debt and must match drawdown and interest dates.Offshore non-deliverable forwards in CNH are permitted only at the Hong Kong parent level with a licensed bank as a proxy hedge against the PRC investment; the onshore WFOE is prohibited from executing direct NDF contracts unless and until a future SAFE pilot clearly authorizes them.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File (China-specific file) must be prepared when the amount of related-party transactions in the current year exceeds RMB 400 million for tangible asset transfers (including copper cathode commodity sales/purchases), or RMB 100 million for transfers of financial assets or intangibles/service transactions. Master File is required if the group's consolidated revenue exceeds RMB 5.5 billion and the Chinese entity is engaged in significant related-party transactions. CbC notification is required for a Chinese resident entity that is the ultimate parent of a multinational group with consolidated revenue of at least RMB 6.5 billion (or if designated as a surrogate entity). A simplified TP documentation requirement applies to low-risk distribution enterprises: if the Chinese enterprise assumes no significant risk and earns a capped gross margin, a full Local File may be substituted with a profit-level indicator table. Related-party transaction reporting forms (G100000 and G100000/ F forms) are mandatory for an entity with annual aggregate related-party transactions exceeding RMB 200 million for commodity trading.

Economic substance scope: The WFOE must satisfy China's economic substance requirements under the general anti-avoidance rule (GAAR) and the 'beneficial ownership' / 'substance over form' doctrine: the entity must have substantive management, operational personnel, assets, and risks appropriate to its role in the copper cathode trading chain. For the Hong Kong parent to obtain treaty benefits, it must also demonstrate economic substance in Hong Kong (per the OECD Base Erosion and Profit Shifting final reports and China's Circular 9/2020 beneficial ownership guidance). The WFOE's activities should include: local contract negotiation, order processing, inventory risk management, logistics coordination, and credit risk assessment. If the WFOE is only a contract-signing shell or the Hong Kong parent directs the business from outside, China may assert that the WFOE is a paper company and allocate the trading profit to a Chinese permanent establishment of the Hong Kong company, or the Hong Kong company may be denied treaty benefits as a conduit. Under China's new 'main purpose test' for treaty abuse (General Anti-Abuse Rule in the DTA, replaced by the Multilateral Instrument / Principal Purpose Test), treaty relief will be denied if obtaining the treaty benefit was one of the principal purposes of the arrangement. Note: Hong Kong's own Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) do not apply to Hong Kong domestic entities; instead, Hong Kong IRD applies an 'adequate economic substance' requirement only for treaty benefits and under the territorial-source principle. for foreign-sourced income exemption, the Hong Kong entity must show real business activities in Hong Kong.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion – Shell/Conduit Entity Without Economic Substance (Tax Fraud)

Statutory sanction: CIT Law Article 47 (Special Tax Adjustment) and Article 48; Implementation Regulations Article 120; STA Notice 68/2004. The tax authority may adjust the transfer price, recharacterize the WFOE as a permanent establishment of the Hong Kong company, deem the under-reported profit as a dividend, and impose a 50%-300% penalty on the underpaid tax and a daily surcharge of 0.05% on the overdue amount. The taxpayer also faces a CIT deficiency assessment of up to 25% plus late-payment interest.

Director personal liability: The legal representative / director in charge of finance and accounting who intentionally causes or participates in the preparation of false accounts can be personally liable jointly and severally for the corporation's unpaid taxes to the extent of the benefit received under Article 20 of the Tax Collection Administration Law; a travel ban (出境限制) may be imposed under Article 44 of the Tax Collection Administration Law, and the director can be included in the national tax blacklist (重大税收违法失信案件) rendering the individual subject to restrictions on high-consumption travel, flight, and high-speed rail. Criminal liability: under Article 201 of the Criminal Law, evasion of tax exceeding RMB 100,000 and more than 10% of the tax payable is punishable with up to 7 years' imprisonment, a fine of 1-5 times the evaded tax, and additional penalties for perjury/fraud. If the offshore arrangement is determined to be a sham, the Hong Kong director/beneficial owner can also be liable for fraud (e.g., forging TRC or invoices), with possible extradition risk under the China–Hong Kong mutual legal assistance framework.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation – Related-Party Copper Trading and Expense Shifting

Statutory sanction: CIT Law Articles 41, 44, and 114; Implementation Regulations Articles 108-116; STA Public Notice 42/2016 (TP documentation); Tax Collection Administration Law Articles 35 and 36. The STA can re-determine taxable income using the CUP/TNMM methodology based on LME copper price benchmarks, impose a TP adjustment, assess 5%-15% fine plus daily late-payment surcharge, and impose a documentation penalty of RMB 10,000-100,000. If the adjustment results in deemed dividend distribution, an additional 10% (or 5% if treaty applies, denied due to treaty abuse) WHT is imposed. Criminal prosecution under the Criminal Law Article 201 may follow if the underpayment exceeds the criminal threshold. Bona fide taxpayer adjustment with full documentation may reduce the penalty to an interest-only payment under the voluntary-correction program.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly sign or approve false intercompany contracts, issue fake invoices, or cause the failure to keep books of account can be personally fined RMB 10,000-100,000 under Article 63 of the Tax Collection Administration Law; if the company is unable to pay the tax due and the director commingled corporate funds or used the company as a facade, a court can pierce the corporate veil under the PRC Company Law Article 20 and hold the director/shareholder jointly and severally liable for the tax debts. A director who discovers the falsity and fails to prevent it may be deemed negligent and subject to administrative liability; criminal liability for transfer pricing fraud rarely attaches to mere pricing judgment absent intentional evasion, but if the director also undertook a 'round-tripping' arrangement through Hong Kong to repatriate profits without withholding, the risk of imprisonment up to 7 years is material.
Offshore Tax Evasion / Fraudulent DTA Relief & AML Red Flags (Illegal Profit Repatriation and Use of Fictitious Invoices / False LME Warrant Structure)

Statutory sanction: PRC Tax Collection Administration Law Articles 63, 64, and 70 (tax evasion penalty 50%-300%); Criminal Law Articles 201 (tax evasion), 205 (false issuance of special VAT invoices, punishable up to life imprisonment and confiscation of property); Article 176 (illegal deposit-taking), Article 191 (money laundering), Article 153 (smuggling) if customs value is manipulated; DTA main purpose test under the MLI and STA Announcement 9/2020 denying treaty benefits; withholding tax deficiency of 10% plus penalties, and the enterprise is added to the tax blacklist. AML: failure to conduct customer due diligence for transactions exceeding RMB 200,000 cash or involving sanctioned counterparties may violate the PRC Anti-Money Laundering Law, resulting in fines up to RMB 5 million and suspension of business licenses.

Director personal liability: The legal representative and financial officer face automatic joint and several liability for the corporation's tax debts under Article 20 of the Tax Collection Administration Law; restriction of exit (出境限制) is mandatory for arrears exceeding RMB 10,000; the director is blacklisted (税收违法黑名单) with restrictions on travel, credit, and public procurement. Criminal liability: false issuance of VAT invoices by the legal representative can result in a fixed-term imprisonment of 10 years to life, or death penalty (abolished for non-violent crimes but replaced by life imprisonment) under Article 205; tax evasion can yield up to 7 years; money laundering can yield up to 10 years plus fines. The director can also be sued in civil liability by the tax authority for the amount of underpaid WHT as a tax withholding agent, and under the 'piercing the corporate veil' doctrine, shareholders who abuse the limited-liability structure to evade taxes or defraud creditors bear unlimited liability (PRC Company Law Article 20 and Article 63 for one-person companies).
