Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None: there is no statutory minimum registered capital for an ordinary wholly foreign-owned enterprise limited-liability company. The shareholder determines the registered capital and payment schedule in the Articles of Association. Under the 2023 Company Law, subscribed capital must be fully paid within five years from incorporation unless a shorter period is specified in the Articles of Association. No blocked escrow account is required by law; however, the paid-in capital must be deposited into a dedicated capital contribution account in the company's name at a bank licensed in the People's Republic of China and must be used for lawful business purposes.

Funding & unlock path: Not Applicable for the registered office-based wholesale and procurement operation. The office space must nevertheless comply with the Fire Protection Law of the People's Republic of China through a simple fire safety inspection by the local Fire and Rescue Section covering emergency exits, fire extinguishers and electrical safety. If a warehouse is subsequently operated, the company must obtain the Fire Safety Acceptance Certificate, complete fire control design review, arrange Civil Defense protected area approval where applicable, and conduct occupational hazard factors testing under the Work Safety Law before commencing storage operations.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA); electronic portal: Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax platform) and the STA-controlled digital tax control system for VAT invoice issuance and export rebate filing.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: The foreign parent entity must provide notarized and apostilled constitutional documents, board resolutions, and a certificate of tax residence from its home jurisdiction. Step 2: The Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE) is first incorporated with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) and obtains the business license bearing the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC). Step 3: Within 30 days of obtaining the business license, the WFOE must complete tax registration through the Electronic Tax Bureau or the competent local STA office. The USCC is automatically recognized as the taxpayer identification number (TIN) for enterprise income tax, VAT, and consolidated tax returns. Step 4: The WFOE must register its bank account details, accounting system, legal representative, and finance officer in the STA taxpayer profile, and must apply for the applicable VAT taxpayer qualification based on projected turnover. Step 5: The WFOE applies for general VAT taxpayer status if its annual taxable sales exceed the statutory threshold or if such status is necessary to claim input VAT credits and export zero-rating. Step 6: The WFOE must activate electronic invoicing via the STA e-invoice platform, obtain the tax-control digital certificate or UKey, and complete the fiscal registration required before issuing Special VAT Invoices. Step 7: If a foreign non-resident entity receives Chinese-source income and has no permanent establishment in China, it does not undergo ordinary VAT registration; instead, it is registered for withholding tax purposes and is identified by its home-country TIN, while the WFOE as payer performs withholding and reporting.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 of annual taxable sales for mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration. All entities, regardless of turnover, must complete tax registration within 30 days of business license issuance. Below the threshold, the WFOE may remain a small-scale taxpayer unless it elects general taxpayer status to claim input VAT credits and export zero-rating.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A Chinese VAT general taxpayer may apply the 0% export VAT rate and claim export tax rebate for battery-grade lithium carbonate sold to an overseas customer provided the goods are lawfully exported from mainland China through Chinese customs to a destination outside mainland China, including Hong Kong SAR as a separate customs territory. The sale must be between non-associated parties or at arm's length if between related parties. The exporter must receive the export proceeds in foreign currency or RMB through approved banking channels within the statutory repatriation period, retain the customs export declaration and transport documentation, and match the VAT invoice, customs declaration, logistics receipts, and FX collection records for tax authority risk control.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Export Goods Customs Declaration, equivalent statutory export bill of entry)
  • Bill of Lading or other transport document evidencing physical movement of the goods from China to the overseas destination
  • Commercial invoice and detailed packing list
  • Bank foreign exchange collection voucher, Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC), or SAFE export proceeds collection verification record

China mandates the issuance of VAT invoices through the STA-controlled digital system, including the Fully Digitalized Electronic Invoice (e-invoice) regime and the Golden Tax IV platform. Special VAT invoices must be transmitted to the STA in near real time through authorized e-invoice service platforms or tax-control UKey devices. The STA automatically validates taxpayer identifiers, commodity/service codes, tax rates, invoice amounts, and anti-fraud digital fiscal seals. Cross-checking against VAT returns and customs export data is performed electronically. Paper invoices may remain in parallel for limited legacy scenarios, but the digital e-invoice is the standard clearance mechanism for new commercial transactions.

The STA and customs authorities perform cross-agency risk matching of export declarations, VAT invoices, logistics documents, and foreign exchange collection records. Any false declaration, counterfeit bill of lading, non-delivery of goods, circular financing, artificial related-party transaction, or failure to repatriate export proceeds within the statutory deadline triggers retrospective cancellation of the 0% VAT rate, mandatory output VAT re-assessment, recovery of any VAT refund already received, daily late-payment surcharge, administrative penalties ranging from 50% to 500% of the evaded VAT, suspension or permanent disqualification from export tax rebate filing, and potential criminal liability for fraudulent export VAT rebate under Chinese law.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Chinese Renminbi is freely convertible for current account transactions under Article VIII of the IMF Articles of Agreement, including cross-border trade settlement and dividend distribution, but the capital account remains heavily controlled by SAFE. A CN WFOE importing lithium carbonate must settle payments through a designated domestic bank after submitting the trade contract, commercial invoice, customs declaration and transport documents, while all FDI equity injections from the Hong Kong parent require prior SAFE Foreign Exchange Registration and must be credited strictly to a restricted capital escrow account. Export proceeds must be repatriated to an onshore FX settlement account within the statutory period, although no mandatory conversion ratio is imposed. Dividend remittance to the Hong Kong parent, although classified as a current account remittance, is de facto reviewed by the bank against the audited annual financial statements, corporate income tax clearance certificates, retained earnings reconciliation and board resolutions, and any conversion of working capital or cross-border lending without a real trade background is prohibited. Delays of five to ten business days are common for profit repatriation because the bank and SAFE must verify tax payment, capital verification and authenticity of the underlying commercial flows, and the WFOE cannot freely convert surplus RMB into offshore currencies without an underlying genuine need.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export proceeds in foreign currency must be repatriated into the WFOE's onshore foreign exchange settlement account within 90 days of the export customs declaration date, or within 30 days where payment was received in advance; the exporter may retain the proceeds in a supervised FX settlement account or convert them into RMB at the prevailing market rate through the designated Tier-1 bank, and no mandatory conversion ratio is applied, but any offshore retention beyond the approved time limit requires an SAFE exemption filing and is subject to administrative penalties.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the People's Bank of China (PBOC) for the mainland WFOE, with the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) as the offshore regulatory authority for the Hong Kong SAR treasury parent and the CNH market

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low and legally barred for the mainland-incorporated WFOE. Offshore digital EMI platforms such as Wise, Airwallex or Revolut are not licensed by PBOC or SAFE to maintain the mandatory capital escrow account, RMB basic deposit account or foreign exchange settlement account of a Chinese legal entity, and any attempt to route WFOE trade settlements through such providers violates China's foreign exchange administration rules. These EMIs may serve the Hong Kong treasury parent for CNH balances and offshore receivables only, but every onshore capital injection, import payment, export repatriation and profit remittance of the CN WFOE must clear through a licensed domestic designated foreign exchange bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Equity capital from the Hong Kong parent is transmitted by SWIFT MT103 or MT202 to the WFOE's Foreign Exchange Capital Escrow Account at the designated Tier-1 onshore bank, quoting the SAFE Foreign Exchange Registration Certificate number, the FDI transaction purpose code for establishing a new onshore foreign-invested enterprise and the Hong Kong parent's board resolution. The receiving bank verifies the economic substance of the capital contribution, retains the correspondent-bank advice, and triggers the statutory capital verification carried out by a licensed Chinese certified public accountant; the resulting capital verification report is then filed with the State Administration for Market Regulation. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Under SAT Public Notice [2016] No. 42, the Local File must be contemporaneously prepared where related-party transactions exceed: RMB 200 million for tangible asset transfers; RMB 100 million for intangible asset transfers; RMB 100 million for financial asset transfers; and RMB 40 million for related-party services. The Master File is required where aggregate annual related-party transactions exceed RMB 1 billion. Country-by-Country Reporting obligations apply separately to multinational groups meeting the consolidated revenue threshold.

Economic substance scope: Economic substance and Core Income-Generating Activities (CIGA) apply at two levels. For the China WFOE, Article 47 of the Tax Collection and Administration Law and Article 120 of the CIT Implementation Regulations require real commercial substance in China; a pure pass-through entity with no procurement, logistics, quality control, staffing, warehousing, invoicing, risk assumption, or decision-making functions will not be respected. For the Hong Kong shareholder claiming treaty benefits, the Hong Kong Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) require the offshore company to conduct its CIGA in Hong Kong, including board direction, management decisions, staff, and premises proportionate to the dividend income derived. Failure to satisfy either substance test allows the tax authorities to recharacterize the structure, deny DTA benefits, and assess CIT at 25% plus withholding tax at source.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Shell Company / Tax Treaty Abuse and Dividend WHT Evasion

Statutory sanction: Denial of the DTA reduction under the Principal Purpose Test and the beneficial ownership requirement; general anti-avoidance recharacterization under Article 47 of the Tax Collection and Administration Law and Article 120 of the CIT Implementation Regulations. The underpaid 10% WHT and CIT are collected with daily 0.05% interest, and Article 63 TCAA imposes a penalty of 50% to 500% of the tax evaded.

Director personal liability: Directors who caused the WFOE to distribute to a sham shell and who submitted false beneficial ownership documentation may be held personally liable for restitution of the unpaid withholding tax and interest through corporate-veil piercing. Tax authorities may also request court-ordered exit restrictions, travel bans, and blacklisting of the responsible directors; where intentional fraud is established, Article 201 of the Criminal Law applies with imprisonment of up to 7 years and fines.
Transfer Pricing / Artificial Profit Shifting in Battery-Metal Cross-Border Trade

Statutory sanction: Article 41 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law authorizes special tax adjustment; Articles 107 to 123 of the CIT Implementation Regulations and SAT Public Notice 42/2016 impose the documentation obligations. Upon adjustment, taxable income is recomputed, underpaid CIT is payable with interest, and the false declaration may trigger Article 63 TCAA penalties of 50% to 500% of the evaded tax.

Director personal liability: Directors who approved or signed the intercompany contracts and TP documentation without a reasonable good-faith basis are exposed to civil veil-piercing for the unpaid tax and penalties. The tax authority may blacklist the entity and the directors and impose cross-border exit bans; knowing creation of fabricated transfer pricing documentation and fraudulent accounts invokes Article 201 of the Criminal Law with imprisonment up to 7 years and disqualification from corporate management.
False Invoicing / VAT and CIT Fraud on Lithium Carbonate Procurement

Statutory sanction: Article 63 of the Tax Collection and Administration Law treats this as tax evasion with a penalty of 50% to 500% of the evaded tax; Article 32 imposes daily 0.05% late-payment surcharge; Article 64 penalizes false filing. Criminal liability arises under Article 201 of the Criminal Law for tax evasion and under Article 205 for issuance of false special VAT invoices, with cumulative imprisonment exposure and separate fines.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial controllers are personally liable for restitution of the fraudulent VAT credits, CIT, penalties, and legal costs. They may be subjected to asset freezing, travel bans, and cross-border blacklisting. Criminal conviction carries imprisonment up to 7 years for tax evasion and additional terms for false VAT invoice offences; upon conviction, the directors are disqualified from serving as legal representatives, directors, supervisors, or senior executives, and all unlawful gains are confiscated.
