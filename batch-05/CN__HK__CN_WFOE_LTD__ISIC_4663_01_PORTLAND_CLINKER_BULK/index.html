Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA), through the Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax Hall) at etax.chinatax.gov.cn, with customs interface through the General Administration of Customs China International Trade Single Window.

E-government filing portal

For the Hong Kong SAR non-resident shareholder forming this PRC WFOE, the WFOE itself is the registered taxpayer. (1) Incorporate the WFOE with the local Administration for Market Regulation and obtain the Business Licence; the Unified Social Credit Code on the licence is the statutory Chinese TIN/VAT taxpayer identification number. (2) Within 30 calendar days of formation, access the STA Electronic Tax Bureau, confirm the taxpayer profile transferred from SAMR, and complete supplementary registration by filing the legal representative, registered address, accounting standards, bank account details, and foreign investor information. (3) Register for VAT and elect general taxpayer status, mandatory once annual taxable sales exceed CNY 5,000,000; for an export-oriented wholesale business, general taxpayer status should be elected immediately to preserve input VAT credits and export-refund eligibility. (4) Complete the China International Trade Single Window customs profile for future warehousing and export declarations of portland clinker. (5) Complete SAFE foreign-exchange registration with the settlement bank for export proceeds; the bank files collection data digitally to SAFE and STA. (6) Apply for the Fully Digitalized Electronic Invoice quota and tax-control UKey. The Hong Kong parent does not automatically obtain a PRC TIN from passive shareholding; if it has a PE or renders services in China, it must separately register with the STA.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

VAT zero-rating applies to portland clinker/bulk hydraulic cement sold by the WFOE to overseas buyers when the goods are physically exported from the PRC customs territory, declared on a Customs Export Declaration, shipped by ocean vessel, and sold for foreign exchange. The WFOE must be a general VAT taxpayer, issue a zero-rated export VAT invoice, and substantiate receipt of the export proceeds. Qualifying offshore services supplied to non-residents may also be zero-rated, but the controlling route for this bulk-goods transaction is export of goods.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bill of Entry) accepted by the General Administration of Customs through the China International Trade Single Window
  • Original ocean Bill of Lading or sea waybill evidencing shipment of the clinker to the overseas destination
  • Foreign exchange collection evidence (Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or bank-issued export collection confirmation under SAFE) showing receipt from the overseas buyer
  • Commercial invoice and packing list for the portland clinker consignment
  • Zero-rated export VAT invoice, matched by electronic data to the customs declaration reference
  • Sales contract or proforma invoice evidencing the cross-border trade terms and foreign-currency settlement

The issuer must use the STA Fully Digitalized Electronic Invoice (FDEI) platform for real-time fiscal clearance. Each invoice receives a nationally unique electronic serial number, anti-counterfeit QR code, and tax-control digital stamp, and invoice data is uploaded to the STA in real time through the Electronic Tax Bureau. Legacy paper VAT invoices, when permitted, require the taxpayer tax-control UKey and are reconciled under the Golden Tax Phase IV system. Export zero-rated invoices must be cross-referenced with the accepted Customs Export Declaration through the China International Trade Single Window.

The STA and the General Administration of Customs jointly risk-score export declarations and VAT refund claims. If a zero-rated export or VAT refund is later found to be unsupported, based on false customs declarations, forged shipping documents, or unrepatriated foreign exchange, the tax refund is clawed back in full, with late-payment surcharge and an administrative fine of up to 50% of the refunded tax under the Law on Administration of Tax Collection. Where fraudulent export-tax-refund behavior is established, the responsible director and legal representative may face criminal liability for tax-refund fraud under the PRC Criminal Law, and the clawback is retrospective until the fraud is detected.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a managed floating exchange-rate system with partial currency convertibility. Current-account transactions are broadly convertible through licensed banks on the strength of authentic underlying documents; trade receipts may be retained or settled, and trade-related service payments can be remitted upon presentation of contracts, invoices, and tax certificates. Capital-account transactions remain heavily administrated: FDI capital contributions must be deposited into a SAFE-registered capital account, verified against the FDI registration, and then converted into RMB only for purposes consistent with the approved business scope. Profit remittance by the WFOE to its Hong Kong shareholder is lawful after the annual audit and tax settlement, but the bank will require an audited financial statement, tax filing receipts, a board resolution declaring the dividend, and evidence that corporate income tax has been fully settled. The same capital-account gate applies to liquidation, equity transfer, and debt repayment proceeds. Because the wholesale of Portland cement clinker is a real goods-trade business capable of producing customs declarations, bills of lading, and third-party survey reports, the convertibility risk is manageable but administrative latitude remains significant; treasury should maintain a 45-day planning buffer for SAFE and bank review.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For goods exports, SAFE does not maintain a fixed mandatory conversion or surrender ratio for current-account receipts. The exporter is required to repatriate proceeds to a designated trade FX collection account and the bank must complete trade-monitoring matching. The general statutory collection window is 180 days from the customs export date; if payment has not arrived by day 180, the WFOE must file a deferred-receipts explanation or register exceptional circumstances with its bank and local SAFE. The enterprise may convert at spot into RMB or retain the FX, subject to the account retention quota calculated from its average trade volume. Any such proceeds are not free of compliance: the bank matches each receipt against the export customs declaration, contract, invoice, packing list, and ocean bill of lading. For inbound FDI capital, in contrast, the mandatory prerequisite is 100% SAFE registration before the capital account can receive funds; conversion is permitted only after capital verification.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Rating: Low and legally insufficient as the primary banking rail for a mainland China WFOE. Offshore digital EMIs holding Hong Kong money service operator, U.S. MSB, or United Kingdom EMI licenses do not constitute licensed banks in the People's Republic of China. They cannot open a legal-person RMB basic deposit account, cannot receive SAFE-registered FDI capital, cannot issue the capital verification documents required by Chinese accountants, and cannot perform the customs-matching trade verification required by SAFE. At best, a PBOC-licensed domestic payment institution can act as an ancillary sub-processor for certain clearly identified current-account expenses, but the clearing backbone for bulk cement trade, LC operations, capital injection, and FX hedging must remain with a licensed domestic commercial bank. Any proposal to route inbound WFOE capital through an offshore EMI is operationally non-compliant and will be rejected by the corporate bank's compliance committee.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: RMB 100 million aggregate annual related-party transactions triggers mandatory Local File; RMB 40 million triggers Master File. Annual related-party transaction disclosure form must be filed with the annual CIT return.

Economic substance scope: The WFOE must satisfy substance requirements by performing core income-generating activities (CIGA) in mainland China, including procurement, inventory risk, logistics contracts, and sales negotiations. A Hong Kong affiliate without real employees, board meetings, and decision-making in Hong Kong will be treated as a shell under PRC GAAR and CFC rules; profits will be reattributed to the Chinese entity and treaty benefits denied.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Shell company arrangement exploiting Hong Kong treaty rates without substance

Statutory sanction: Under PRC CIT Law Article 47 and SAT Announcement 2017-6, tax authorities may recharacterize the arrangement, impose 25% CIT plus daily surcharge and a 50%–500% penalty under the Tax Collection and Administration Law Article 110

Director personal liability: Directors may have the corporate veil pierced under PRC Company Law (2023) for abuse of corporate independence, leading to joint liability for company taxes; travel restrictions through the tax blacklist (重大税收违法失信案件) and criminal liability for tax evasion under Criminal Law Article 201 (up to 7 years imprisonment) apply
Fraudulent misdeclaration of cross-border freight income and use of forged invoices

Statutory sanction: Under Tax Collection and Administration Law Article 63, the taxpayer is liable for underpaid tax plus 50%–500% penalty and daily surcharges; criminal prosecution under Criminal Law Article 201 if evasion exceeds RMB 50,000 and is 10% of taxable income, imprisonment up to 7 years

Director personal liability: Directors who approve false invoices face personal criminal liability for invoice fraud under Criminal Law Article 205 (life imprisonment possible for special VAT invoices) and may be jointly liable for the company’s tax debt; asset freezes and exit bans may be enforced
DTA abuse / treaty shopping by claiming Hong Kong beneficial ownership without contractual or operational substance

Statutory sanction: Beneficial ownership denial under SAT Bulletin 2018-9 and the Principal Purpose Test; recharacterization of the entire transaction, imposition of statutory WHT at 10% or 10%/7% plus daily surcharge and penalty of 50%–500% under Tax Collection and Administration Law Article 63

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false beneficial ownership declarations may be personally liable for the underpaid withholding and face criminal fraud sanctions under Criminal Law Article 204 (imprisonment up to 3–7 years); blacklisting and disqualification from director roles in PRC may follow
