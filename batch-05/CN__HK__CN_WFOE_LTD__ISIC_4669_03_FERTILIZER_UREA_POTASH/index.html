None. No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to a WFOE conducting wholesale fertilizer trading under the current Foreign Investment Negative List. Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA), accessed through the Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax) official online portal. The Hong Kong SAR parent/subsidiary side is separately within the competence of the Inland Revenue Department of Hong Kong SAR.

E-government filing portal

The WFOE obtains its statutory Tax Identification Number as part of the integrated one-stop registration: (i) incorporation with the State Administration for Market Regulation and receiving the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code, which is the corporate TIN for all tax purposes; (ii) automatic linked tax registration through the one-stop platform, including VAT and corporate income tax registration; (iii) filing of supplemental tax registration data in the Electronic Tax Bureau, covering legal representative, finance officer, bank account information, accounting policy, and authorized tax agents; (iv) VAT taxpayer type determination, including compulsory general taxpayer registration when annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000 and optional election for general taxpayer status below that threshold; (v) application for digital fiscal invoice quota and issuance qualifications; and (vi) connection to the STA-approved electronic invoice / tax-control system. A foreign non-resident enterprise, including the Hong Kong holding company, without a permanent establishment in China obtains a Non-Resident Taxpayer Identification Number through the Electronic Tax Bureau for China-source income withholding and filing obligations. The Hong Kong entity separately obtains a Business Registration Number from the Hong Kong Inland Revenue Department; Hong Kong profits tax follows territorial sourcing.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rated export VAT treatment applies when the WFOE is a VAT general taxpayer, the prilled urea and potassium chloride have been physically exported from the PRC customs territory under an export customs declaration made in the exporter's name, the goods qualify under the prevailing export tax refund (exemption) catalogue and are not on the export tax rebate negative list, the export consideration is received in foreign exchange under SAFE rules, and the complete supporting document chain is retained. Offshore digital services are not in the scope of this goods-based export transaction.

The WFOE must issue fully digitalized all-electronic invoices through the STA Electronic Tax Bureau or an STA-approved e-invoice application. Invoices are transmitted to the Golden Tax Phase IV platform, which performs automatic validation, unique invoice coding, cryptographic signing, and digital stamping before the invoice becomes effective. Real-time fiscal clearance and anti-fraud controls require the WFOE to maintain bank account linkages, invoice quota approvals, and automated matching of sales, purchases, customs, and foreign exchange data with the tax authority. API-based integration is permitted only through compliant service providers; issuers remain fully liable for false or mismatched invoice data.

If export zero-rating or VAT refund claims are found to be unsupported, false, or fraudulent, the STA and PRC Customs will disallow the claim, claw back the refunded VAT plus statutory late-payment surcharge, and impose administrative penalties under the Law on Tax Collection and Administration of the PRC, generally ranging from 50% to five times the tax amount involved. Deliberate export tax refund fraud is a criminal offense under the Criminal Law of the PRC punishable by fines and imprisonment up to life imprisonment. The audit cross-checks customs declarations, bill of lading data, foreign exchange receipts, input VAT invoices, and buyer credentials, and the recovery period is extended or unlimited where fraud or evasion is established.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The renminbi is fully convertible for current account transactions such as goods trade, services, and profit remittances once the underlying genuine business background is authenticated by the designated foreign exchange bank. Capital account transactions, however, remain tightly controlled and require SAFE registration, approval, or filing. A WFOE in the agricultural commodity wholesale sector must repatriate all export proceeds from urea and potassium chloride sales into mainland China via designated banks and report them through the SAFE trade monitoring system, though no mandatory surrender percentage is imposed post-2012. Profit remittances to the Hong Kong parent are lawful after a statutory audit, tax clearance, and the submission of the Foreign Exchange Payment of Dividends form, with banks verifying the absence of accumulated losses and the settlement of corporate income tax. Increases in paid-in capital, external debt, or cross-border guarantees require separate SAFE registration. The regime also imposes strict look-through review on round-tripping arrangements, and any currency conversion for capital account purposes must be tied to verified commercial activity, with banks driving the 'payment after confirmation' rule to prohibit speculative conversion.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export proceeds from physical fertilizer shipments must be repatriated to a domestic foreign currency account within 90 days from the date of customs declaration, extendable to 180 days for approved sectors under SAFE guidelines. There is no statutory mandatory conversion ratio; the enterprise may retain foreign currency or sell it to any licensed bank at the prevailing market rate. Every transaction must be matched against customs data in the SAFE goods trade monitoring system, and supporting documents such as the customs declaration, commercial invoice, bill of lading, and settlement records must be retained for at least five years.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC); the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) is the delegated foreign exchange regulatory authority

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Offshore digital EMI and payment platforms such as Airwallex, Revolut Business, or PingPong are not licensed to operate as banks in mainland China and cannot legally open RMB settlement accounts, access CIPS/SWIFT clearing as direct members, or process FX conversion for the onshore WFOE. Their permissible use is limited to offshore treasury pockets in Hong Kong for pre-import working capital or supplier prepayments, where the WFOE maintains a separate HK treasury account. For any onshore transaction, the bank-led SAFE verification framework excludes unlicensed EMIs; therefore, the viability for the PRC WFOE core banking is structurally Low, while their offshore auxiliary role is Medium with a clear prohibition on handling mainland-issued invoices or customs-linked payments.

Turnover: Under Section 746 of the Hong Kong Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622), a private company is mandatorily required to have an independent certified audit unless it qualifies as a small company by satisfying ALL of the following quantitative thresholds for the financial year: (1) total annual revenue / turnover does not exceed USD 1,282,051 (HK$10,000,000); (2) total assets / balance sheet total does not exceed USD 1,282,051 (HK$10,000,000); and (3) number of employees does not exceed 50. The exemption is unavailable if the company is a banking, insurance, or securities firm; if it is a member of a group that does not meet the small company group threshold; if any shareholder holding 5% or more of the voting rights has served a written notice requiring an audit; or if the company has previously been required to file audited statements. Any company exceeding even one threshold, or falling outside the statutory exemption, MUST undergo an audit by a licensed Certified Public Accountant (CPA) registered with the Accounting and Financial Reporting Council (AFRC).

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: A Local File is mandatory if any related-party transaction category exceeds the following RMB thresholds: tangible property transfers RMB 200,000,000; intangible property transfers RMB 100,000,000; financial asset transfers RMB 100,000,000; and other related-party transactions RMB 40,000,000. A Master File is required when aggregate related-party transactions exceed RMB 1,000,000,000 in the prior fiscal year. The annual related-party transaction report itself is triggered by total related-party transactions exceeding RMB 20,000,000 or by any category threshold being met.

Economic substance scope: The China WFOE is not directly subject to Hong Kong's Economic Substance Regulations, but the substance-over-form principle, the beneficial ownership condition in the China-HK arrangement, and PRC anti-avoidance rules require both the WFOE and its HK parent to evidence substantive commercial activities. The HK parent claiming treaty benefits must carry on procurement, marketing, financing, and risk-management functions with adequate local staff and offices in Hong Kong. The WFOE must exercise real control over fertilizer inventory, logistics, customs, and counterparty credit risk; if either entity is a shell, the tax authority may recharacterize transactions, deny treaty relief, and impose special tax adjustments including interest.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Transfer Pricing

Statutory sanction: Special tax adjustment under PRC Enterprise Income Tax Law Article 41 and SAT transfer pricing regulations; the tax authority may recharacterize, increase taxable income, charge interest at the central bank benchmark lending rate plus 5 percentage points for special tax adjustment, and impose a civil penalty of 50% to 500% of underpaid tax under Tax Administration Law Article 63; where intentional falsification is proven, Criminal Law Article 201 applies.

Director personal liability: Directors, legal representatives, and finance officers who knowingly prepare false transfer pricing books face criminal liability for tax evasion, with imprisonment from 3 to 7 years for large amounts and fines; the court may pierce the corporate veil under PRC Company Law Article 23 in the 2023 revision where company and personal assets are commingled or the company is used to evade tax, leaving directors personally liable for unpaid taxes and surcharges; tax exit controls and asset freezing are available during investigations.
Shell Company / Double Tax Agreement Abuse

Statutory sanction: Denial of treaty benefits under the beneficial ownership clause of the China-Hong Kong SAR Arrangement and PRC general anti-avoidance rules; the tax authority reimposes 10% withholding tax plus late payment surcharge of 0.05% per day and penalties of 50% to 500% of the under-withheld tax; criminal liability for fraudulent tax reduction applies under Criminal Law Article 201 if false declarations are made.

Director personal liability: Directors and ultimate beneficial owners who sign false beneficial-ownership certificates or cause treaty abuse may be prosecuted for tax fraud; the company's limited-liability shield can be lifted, and shareholders who abuse corporate personality to strip the Chinese WFOE's cash illegally are jointly and severally liable to make restitution; blacklisting for AML, travel bans, and seizure of personal assets are possible where the court finds the HK entity to be a sham.
AML / False Export Invoicing and VAT Rebate Fraud

Statutory sanction: Issuing false VAT invoices is punishable under PRC Criminal Law Article 205 with imprisonment up to life and fines; fraudulent export tax rebate claims are punishable under Criminal Law Article 204 with imprisonment of 3 to 10 years, or up to life for extremely large amounts, plus fines of 1 to 5 times the fraudulent rebate amount; AML violations under Criminal Law Article 191 and PBOC regulations add separate forfeiture and anti-money laundering fines.

Director personal liability: Directors, compliance officers, and beneficial owners involved in the false-export web will be prosecuted as directly responsible persons; the corporate veil is pierced where the WFOE is found to be a vehicle for crime, exposing directors' personal bank accounts, residences, and business assets to criminal confiscation and civil restitution; cross-border travel blacklisting and personal blacklisting by financial institutions are automatic in the customs, tax, and AML enforcement processes.
