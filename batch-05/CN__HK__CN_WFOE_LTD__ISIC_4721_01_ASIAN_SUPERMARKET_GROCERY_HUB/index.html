        Export 0% zero-rating conditions
        
Zero-rated export treatment with input VAT refund is available only to registered general VAT taxpayers that physically export goods from China Customs territory under a qualifying export customs declaration and collect export proceeds in accordance with State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) rules, ordinarily within 180 days of customs declaration. The goods must not fall within the statutory list of commodities ineligible for export tax rebate. Exporters classified as small-scale VAT taxpayers are exempt from VAT on exports but cannot claim input VAT refund. Offshore digital services qualify only if expressly listed in the cross-border taxable services zero-rating/exemption catalogue issued by the Ministry of Finance and the STA.

Stage 04
Banking & Treasury Architecture

      
Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

    

    

      
Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

      

        
Current-account convertibility
Under the PRC foreign exchange control framework, the current account is broadly convertible but remains subject to true-transaction verification for every payment or receipt. The WFOE's supermarket imports, grocery disbursements, and associated logistics or insurance payments are current-account items that can be processed through a domestic bank after review of contracts, invoices, customs declarations, and shipping documents. The capital account, however, is not freely convertible: equity injections from the Hong Kong shareholder must be registered with SAFE, deposited into a designated FDI capital account, and used only in accordance with the capital-account positive-list rules. Export proceeds are subject to repatriation but not to mandatory conversion; an exporter may retain 100 percent of its receipts in a foreign exchange current account or voluntarily convert them into RMB at the onshore market rate. Profit remittance to Hong Kong is permissible only after the statutory annual audit confirms distributable profits, PRC corporate income tax and withholding tax are paid, the board distribution resolution is passed, and the remitting bank validates the submission. In practice, the main hurdles are capital verification of registered capital, SAFE direct-investment data registration, tax payment or exemption certificates, and the strict BOP reporting code attached to each dividend remittance.

        
Export proceeds surrender rules
Goods-trade export proceeds must be repatriated to the PRC through a licensed domestic bank in accordance with the underlying payment terms. SAFE's Trade Monitoring System compares each export customs declaration with the eventual receipt; if the receipt is abnormal or more than 90 days overdue from the declared payment term in the absence of a valid extension record, the bank will require a written explanation and a supporting extension registration. There is no mandatory conversion ratio in the current-account regime: the statutory surrender ratio is 0 percent, and the WFOE may retain 100 percent of its export receipts in its foreign exchange settlement account or voluntarily sell the currency into RMB. For capital-account foreign exchange, conversion must follow the capital project FX settlement positive-list system and the bank must report each settled item to SAFE.

      

      
Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBoC) and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

    

    

      
Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

      

        
        

          

            Airwallex
            Fit: High
          

          
Offshore fintech EMIs such as Airwallex, Wise, Currenxie, or Revolut are not licensed by PBoC or SAFE as deposit-taking institutions and cannot act as the primary treasury clearing account for a mainland WFOE. PRC foreign exchange regulations require current-account receipts to be repatriated into a designated domestic bank account and FDI capital to be received in a SAFE-registered capital account; an offshore EMI cannot meet these requirements. At most, an EMI may be used by the Hong Kong parent company for treasury acceleration or supplier payments outside China, but the WFOE itself will still need a Tier-1 domestic commercial bank account to satisfy SAFE reporting, capital verification, customs matching, and tax clearance. The legal viability of replacing the domestic clearing bank with an EMI is therefore negligible.

PRC foreign exchange regulations require current-account receipts to be repatriated into a designated domestic bank account and FDI capital to be received in a SAFE-registered capital account; an offshore EMI cannot meet these requirements. At most, an EMI may be used by the Hong Kong parent company for treasury acceleration or supplier payments outside China, but the WFOE itself will still need a Tier-1 domestic commercial bank account to satisfy SAFE reporting, capital verification, customs matching, and tax clearance. The legal viability of replacing the domestic clearing bank with an EMI is therefore negligible.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

      
Master / Local File threshold: RMB 400 million of annual related-party tangible property transactions triggers the Local File; RMB 100 million of related-party intangible, financial asset or financing transactions likewise trigger contemporaneous documentation; other related-party transactions above RMB 40 million also require local documentation; a Master File is required when the Chinese entity is part of a multinational group with consolidated group revenue exceeding RMB 1 billion in the prior tax year

      
Economic substance scope: For a PRC-resident WFOE, substance is evidenced by central management, control and core income-generating activities exercised in mainland China. With respect to the Hong Kong parent claiming DTA benefits, Announcement 2019 No.35 allows tax authorities to deny treaty relief if the Hong Kong entity lacks beneficial ownership and core substance. Hong Kong's own territorial system and Economic Substance Regulations, where they apply under its regulatory framework, require the Hong Kong parent to maintain premises, employees, expenditure and active decision-making in Hong Kong for the receipt of dividends, interest and royalties. A pipeline or mailbox arrangement in Hong Kong will be disregarded, the DTA reduction will be denied, and the WFOE will owe the full statutorily reduced rate plus penalties.

      

    

    

      
Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

      
      

        Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Under-Reporting
        
Statutory sanction: Violation of Article 63 of the PRC Tax Collection and Administration Law: recovery of the underpaid tax plus a fine of 50% to 500% of the evaded tax; daily late interest of 0.05% on the unpaid balance; where fraudulent transfer-price manipulation is found, the tax authority may perform a special anti-avoidance adjustment and impose interest under Article 48 of the EIT Law; criminal tax evasion under Article 201 of the PRC Criminal Law is punishable by imprisonment of up to seven years and significant monetary fines.

        
Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced when a director signs or causes the filing of false documents or knowingly diverts corporate assets to evade CIT. The director becomes personally liable for restitution of underpaid taxes and fines, may be prohibited from leaving mainland China under the customary exit-restriction mechanism, and may face criminal imprisonment up to seven years with a lifetime ban on serving as a company director in severe cases.

      

      
      

        Shell Company / Denial of DTA Beneficial Ownership
        
Statutory sanction: The tax authority will recharacterize the Hong Kong recipient as not being the beneficial owner under Announcement 2019 No.35 and deny the DTA reduction; the full 10% statutory WHT will be assessed as under-withholding; a general anti-avoidance adjustment under Article 120/121 of the PRC Enterprise Income Tax Law will be applied; the WFOE as payer can be fined between 50% and 500% of the underpaid withholding and exposed to daily late payment interest on the shortfall.

        
Director personal liability: Directors who structure or approve dividend distributions to an empty Hong Kong shell may be held jointly and severally liable for the deficit caused to the tax authority; they may face civil restitution from personal assets, travel bans and blacklisting, and criminal liability for fraudulent evasion if they knowingly arranged the abuse to lower tax; imprisonment of up to seven years is available under the tax crime articles.

      

      
      

        AML and Financial Integrity Failure in Cash-Heavy Retail Operations
        
Statutory sanction: Breach of the PRC Anti-Money Laundering Law: the institution can be fined RMB 500,000 to RMB 5,000,000 for serious violations; the responsible director can be fined RMB 50,000 or more; the regulator may suspend business licenses and revoke the entity's qualified regulatory status; criminal money laundering under Article 191 of the PRC Criminal Law carries punishments of up to ten years' imprisonment, depending on the circumstances.

        
Director personal liability: Directors can be personally fined and imprisoned for approving or participating in laundering arrangements; civil liability requires restitution of laundered funds and compensation for harmed parties; entry-exit control and disqualification orders prohibit the director from leaving China and from holding directorships in regulated entities; the individual's personal bank accounts are subject to freezing and asset seizure.
