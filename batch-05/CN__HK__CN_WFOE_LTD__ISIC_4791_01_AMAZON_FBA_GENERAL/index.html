E-government filing portal

The WFOE is a PRC-resident enterprise, not a foreign non-resident entity; its statutory taxpayer identification number is the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) issued by the Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR). Step 1: Complete SAMR incorporation and obtain the business license bearing the USCC. Step 2: Within 30 days of SAMR registration, complete consolidated tax registration at the in-charge STA office; the USCC is automatically activated as the VAT tax identification number and corporate income tax number. Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

For VAT classification, an enterprise with annual taxable sales in excess of RMB 5,000,000 is mandatorily required to register as a general VAT taxpayer; below that threshold it remains a small-scale taxpayer. A new WFOE is subject to tax registration at incorporation because AMR registration automatically triggers STA taxpayer registration, so there is no separate low-threshold turnover waiver for the legal entity. For cross-border e-commerce exports, the entity must separately register with the STA for export tax rebate procedures and with the General Administration of Customs for the applicable export supervision mode.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods exported by the WFOE qualify for VAT zero-rating and export tax rebate only if the following statutory conditions are met: physical export from mainland China under a customs supervision mode appropriate for cross-border e-commerce, namely 9610 for retail export, 9710 for B2B direct export, or 9810 for overseas warehouse/FBA export; the export constitutes a genuine sale to an overseas buyer or e-commerce marketplace; the order, payment, logistics, customs declaration and invoice data are consistent; the goods are not on the State Council prohibited or restricted export-rebate list; and the foreign-currency consideration, when applicable, is received through a PRC bank account with supporting SAFE trade-record documentation. For qualifying offshore digital services, zero-rating applies only when the service is wholly consumed outside mainland China and the recipient is a non-resident outside the PRC. The 0% rate is substantiated through the STA export tax rebate declaration, not merely by the VAT invoice.

China's electronic invoice framework is mandatory for all VAT taxpayers. Invoices must be issued through the National Electronic Invoice Service Platform and the Golden Tax IV system, which performs real-time validation, real-time transmission, and statutory anti-counterfeit digital stamping with the electronic QR code. The WFOE must apply for a tax-control UKey and an approved invoice quota before issuing VAT invoices. API integration for high-volume cross-border e-commerce operations must be certified by the STA and the General Administration of Customs; the WFOE may transmit invoice and customs declaration data via the China International Trade Single Window API. Fully digitalized electronic VAT invoices are the current national standard, and once enabled, no parallel paper invoice quota is required.

The STA and customs authorities jointly verify export zero-rating through four-stream matching and the 180-day foreign-exchange repatriation record. Failure to substantiate the export or any false, overstated or unverified zero-rating claim triggers compulsory removal of the goods from the zero-rating/export rebate scheme, retrospective clawback of all VAT exemptions or rebates, daily late-payment surcharges of 0.05% on the amount incorrectly claimed, administrative fines of 50% to 500% of the falsely refunded amount under the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection, suspension or revocation of export tax rebate qualifications, and customs administrative penalties for false export declarations. Serious cases involving forged documents or fraudulent export tax rebate claims are subject to criminal liability under the PRC Criminal Law, including confiscation of property and imprisonment.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The RMB is convertible on current account items for trade in goods and services, while capital account items remain heavily regulated. The CN WFOE is treated as a foreign-invested enterprise (FIE) and its cross-border receipts/disbursements are subject to SAFE's 'bank-based verification and reporting' framework. Current account transactions - primarily Amazon FBA merchandise export proceeds, platform service fees, warehouse logistics, and third-party payment collections - are freely convertible provided the bank reviews genuine trade documentation (invoices, customs declarations, logistics bills, settlement statements). The WFOE may lawfully retain foreign currency in its foreign currency settlement account or convert to RMB at its own discretion. Capital account items, including the HK parent's equity capital injection, intercompany loans, and dividend repatriation, require SAFE registration, a valid FDI record, and a capital account bank settlement mechanism. Profit remittance to the HK shareholder is permitted after annual audit, tax clearance, and submission of the required SAFE declaration, but the residual distributed profit must be remitted via the designated bank using the appropriate benefit-code (profit remittance). No centralised surrender requirement exists for export receipts; however, the actual receipt must be repatriated into China within the statutory period, and the underlying cross-border transactions must be settled in the designated local bank with full documentation to avoid over-aggregation or mismatch allegations.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export proceeds are not subject to mandatory FX surrender in China, but must be repatriated to the WFOE's own foreign exchange account at a designated bank within 180 days from the export customs declaration date (extendable only via exceptional SAFE approval). There is no statutory minimum conversion ratio; the exporter may retain the foreign currency or convert to RMB at market rate.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE), operating under the People's Bank of China (PBOC), as the central licensing and regulatory authority for all foreign exchange transactions in the People's Republic of China.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The HK parent must perform an inbound remittance via SWIFT MT103 to the WFOE's designated foreign exchange bank in China. The remittance instruction must specify purpose code 8221 (Industrial Commercial) or 6220 (Payment of Investment Funds) derived from the SAFE Catalogue of Payment Codes, and must name the WFOE as beneficiary. The bank will first verify the WFOE's FDI registration confirmation (given by SAFE under the Foreign Investment Information System). Upon receipt, the bank repays the RMB equivalent if the WFOE chooses to settle, or credits the capital account in the original foreign currency; the funds are then transferred to the settlement account with the 'Capital Account Settlement Reserve' flag. A CPA-issued capital verification report (验资报告) and the bank's Capital Verification Confirmation are filed with SAFE and the Customs/Commerce authorities. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation (Local File) is mandatory in China when related party transactions in a single year exceed: RMB 200 million for tangible property transfers; RMB 100 million for intangible property transfers, financial asset transfers, loans, or other service transactions. The Master File is required when aggregate related party income exceeds RMB 1 billion. The WFOE engaged in Amazon FBA with a Hong Kong related distributor must prepare, or retain access to, a Local File before the annual CIT filing if the intercompany transaction thresholds are met, even if the actual cross-border payment is small, and must include benchmarking studies for the distribution, procurement, trademark royalties and loans performed in the structure.

Economic substance scope: China does not operate a named Economic Substance Regulations regime of the kind used in certain offshore jurisdictions, but the State Taxation Administration applies an economic substance principle through the General Anti-Avoidance Rule and the substance-over-form doctrine. The WFOE conducting Amazon FBA commerce is expected to demonstrate real operational substance in mainland China, including employees, office space, inventory management, logistics decision-making, business risks, and commercial autonomy. Conversely, if the Hong Kong shareholder is a shell company that lacks employees and decision-making capacity, Chinese tax authorities may pierce the intermediate entity, deny the 5% treaty dividend rate, reattribute all profits to the Chinese WFOE, and assess additional CIT, late interest and penalties. Hong Kong territorial tax rules similarly require the Hong Kong entity to carry on substantive economic activities in Hong Kong to be accepted as a treaty resident; a passive HK holding company may be treated as not entitled to treaty protection.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Transfer Pricing Evasion and Sham Intercompany Profit Reallocation

Statutory sanction: PRC Enterprise Income Tax Law Articles 41 and 44 authorize the tax authority to adjust related-party transactions to an arm's length result. The Tax Collection and Administration Law Article 63 may impose a penalty of 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax, plus daily late interest at 0.05%, and transfer pricing documentation failure can lead to an upward gross-profit adjustment based on official margin statistics.

Director personal liability: Senior executives who knowingly executed the underpricing sales invoices, signed false financial statements, or directed accountants to conceal the related-party relationship may have the corporate veil pierced under PRC Civil Code Article 83 or general anti-abuse doctrine. Directors are personally liable for restitution of unjust enrichment and civil damages to the company. The tax authority can impose exit ban or blacklisting measures preventing travel, and criminal prosecution under Criminal Law Article 201 as the person directly in charge can result in imprisonment from three months to seven years, depending on the amount, proportion of underpaid tax, and absence of timely correction.
Treaty Shopping and Dividend Repatriation Abusive Structure

Statutory sanction: The State Taxation Administration may invoke the General Anti-Avoidance Rule and recharacterize the Hong Kong entity as a conduit, deny the 5% treaty rate, recapture the 5% withholding difference, and impose interest and penalties. The withholding agent may be penalized under Tax Collection and Administration Law Articles 63 and 69. Falsifying beneficial ownership declarations also constitutes a false tax declaration.

Director personal liability: Directors who approve the dividend distribution and execute false beneficial ownership statements are personally exposed to civil liability for the unpaid tax and penalties. Their personal assets may be frozen, the taxpayer blacklisted in the national tax credit system, and an exit ban may be imposed under the Exit Administration Law. Criminal liability for assisting tax evasion can be prosecuted under Criminal Law Article 201 or Article 211 for unit crime, subjecting directors to imprisonment of up to seven years and fines.
Failure to File Corporate Income Tax Return, Omission of Revenue and Understating Taxable Income

Statutory sanction: Late or non-filing incurs penalties under Tax Collection and Administration Law Article 62 from RMB 2,000 to RMB 10,000 and daily late-payment interest of 0.05% on the overdue tax under Article 32. Willful omission of revenue constitutes tax evasion under Article 63, with a monetary penalty of 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax. If the evaded tax amount exceeds RMB 50,000 and is at least 10% of the tax payable for that year, the matter becomes a criminal tax fraud case.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil may be pierced if the directors commingled company funds, concealed bank accounts and seller receipts, or directed destruction of accounting records. Civil liability attaches for the full tax debt and restitution. Directors are individually liable in administrative enforcement and may receive travel bans and blacklisting. Criminal prosecution under Criminal Law Article 201 and Article 211 subjects the person directly in charge to up to seven years' imprisonment, fines, confiscation of personal assets, and disqualification from serving as a director of any PRC company.
