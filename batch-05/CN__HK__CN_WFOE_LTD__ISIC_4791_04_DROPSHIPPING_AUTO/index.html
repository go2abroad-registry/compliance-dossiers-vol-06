Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-up capital for a Hong Kong company limited by shares. The issued share capital may be as low as HK$1.00. However, for the purposes of bank account opening and the present WFOE cross-border structure, the subscriber(s) must state the proposed share capital in the NNC1; no escrow account, blocked deposit, or capital verification certificate is required under the Companies Ordinance. Where the company will later receive a banking facility or trade credit, the directors may call up unpaid capital as ordinary resolution dictates.

Funding & unlock path: Not Applicable for pure digital dropshipping: no physical premises require a Fire Services Certificate (Cap. 95, Fire Services Ordinance) or a structural safety approval. The Hong Kong registered office, however, must display the company name and maintain standard fire safety measures under the Occupational Safety and Occupational Health Ordinance (Cap. 509) and the Building Ordinance (Cap. 123) insofar as it is used as an administrative office. If the WFOE later operates a cloud-based fulfillment ordering hub without handling goods, it must still obtain a general 'Fire Safety Certificate' if it employs more than 20 persons in a non-industrial workplace, and must file a 'Fire Protection Inspection' with the local Fire and Rescue Department for any automated parcel conveyor station exceeding 200 m².

Entity_Level_Taxation

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 of annual taxable sales for mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration; all WFOEs must complete basic tax and VAT registration within 30 days of SAMR business license issuance regardless of turnover, and small-scale taxpayers with monthly sales up to RMB 100,000 may be VAT-exempt.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exported goods qualify for 0% VAT and input VAT refund if they are physically exported from China through a customs declaration under an export-dedicated customs supervision code, the sale is made to an overseas non-resident buyer, export proceeds are received in foreign currency and verified through the SAFE foreign exchange monitoring system, and the exporter holds a complete matching set of customs, logistics, invoicing and payment documents. Cross-border services wholly consumed outside China may also be zero-rated when supplied to non-residents and supported by a contract, overseas consumption evidence and repatriation proof.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Export Goods Customs Declaration Form)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill
  • Commercial Invoice
  • Packing List
  • Foreign Exchange Settlement and Export Proceeds Verification Form issued by bank under SAFE (Inward Remittance Certificate)
  • VAT Export Rebate Application Data matching special VAT input invoices

All VAT invoices must be issued through the STA full-digital e-invoice system under Golden Tax Phase IV and uploaded in real time to the E-Tax platform. The system enforces invoice quotas, automatic data matching, and real-time anti-fraud verification; special VAT invoices are digitally stamped without paper copies. High-volume automated dropshipping merchants must integrate their billing/ERP APIs with the e-invoice platform and cannot manually override clearance controls.

The STA and customs jointly audit zero-rating by cross-matching customs declarations, VAT invoices, logistics documents, foreign exchange receipts and tax rebate applications. If the export proof is false, missing or materially inconsistent, the refunded VAT is clawed back with a daily late-payment surcharge of 0.05% and an administrative penalty of 50% to 5 times the tax amount under Article 63 of the Tax Administration Law. Fraudulent export tax refund cases are additionally referred for criminal prosecution under Article 204 of the Criminal Law, carrying penalties up to life imprisonment for amounts found to be enormous.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The PRC operates a hybrid regime: current account transactions are generally convertible under SAFE's surrender and verification framework, but capital account transactions remain heavily controlled and require case-by-case registration, approval, and post-event reporting. A WFOE enjoys full access to RMB conversion for operational purposes, but the receipt of registered capital from the Hong Kong parent must be routed through a capital account designated at a domestic bank after SAFE foreign direct investment registration. Working capital loans, cross-border guarantees, and profit remittances to the Hong Kong parent are each treated as distinct capital sub-accounts with separate authorizations. Dividend distribution requires audited financial statements, tax clearance certificate, board resolution, and proof that the underlying profits were generated by the entity's certified business scope. Repatriation of liquidation proceeds also requires de-registration certificates from the market regulator and tax authority. In practice, conversion for imports through dropshipping suppliers is permissible under the current account with a commercial invoice; however, mismatches between payment card aggregators and the original export/import declaration can trigger hold-the-conversion and cause banks to demand reconstructed documentary evidence. Thus, despite nominal current-account convertibility, the administrative burden of proving the real trade under the automated dropshipping model creates de facto partial inconvertibility until the compliance file is fully standardized.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Goods export proceeds must be repatriated to China through a designated foreign exchange bank within 180 days from the date of export customs declaration. Since 2012, SAFE has eliminated a fixed mandatory surrender ratio; exporters are free to retain the proceeds in a current account foreign currency ledger or convert to RMB at market rates. Enterprises must nevertheless report the receipt under SAFE's monitoring system and match each inflow to the corresponding customs and invoice data; the bank will verify 100 percent of inward receipts against electronic export records, and any unrepatriated balance after the deadline triggers a compliance penalty threshold.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE), operating under the People's Bank of China (PBoC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Fintech/EMI viability is negligible for a PRC-licensed WFOE. Foreign digital banks or EMIs cannot open operational accounts without a domestic banking license; PBoC has not authorized any foreign EMI as a settlement bank for corporate entities. Stripe and PayPal serve only as online payment acceptance aggregators, not as lawful treasury deposit-taking institutions for the WFOE. Bank acceptance of PayPal/Stripe settlements is conditional upon the WFOE's underlying bank account receiving the settlement via regular SWIFT wires; therefore, the firm's core liquidity rails must reside in a domestic tier-1 bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The Hong Kong parent remits registered capital via SWIFT MT103 with purpose code FDI - injection of registered capital for Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise. The receiving bank requires SAFE FDI registration certificate, capital account control form, and the bank instruction matching the entity name and the amount recorded in the foreign exchange business licence. After receipt, the bank credits the foreign currency capital account; subsequent conversion to RMB is executed inside the entity's operational account under SAFE records. A PRC certified public accountant then issues a capital verification report for the company registration authority and tax bureau.For supplier payments, the WFOE clears via SWIFT MT103 using bank-approved trade purpose codes on current account, with each instruction referencing invoice number, purchase order, and customs or e-commerce record. Domestic suppliers are paid via RMB CNAPS within China; cross-border suppliers use the same SWIFT rail. Commercial Letters of Credit and Documentary Collections are reserved for high-value bespoke manufacturing purchases where bank financing or title documents exist, but the automated dropshipping layer makes such instruments rare; when used, the WFOE must submit transport documents, commercial invoice, and the uploaded export manifest.Onshore RMB foreign-exchange forwards with commercial underlying exposure, executed through SAFE-authorized tier-1 banks under the real demand principle.Cross-currency interest rate swaps permitted only for onshore foreign debt or funding with SAFE registration; not accessible for retail dropship trade settlement.Offshore non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are permissible only in the Hong Kong treasury intercompany book to hedge the net equity exposure in RMB; the PRC WFOE may not execute NDFs onshore.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% standard China Corporate Income Tax (EIT); 15% for qualified high-new-tech enterprises; small low-profit enterprises enjoy a 20% rate with an effective 5% preferential outcome where taxable income is below RMB 3 million during the 2023-2027 policy window

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% statutory China withholding tax on dividends remitted by the WFOE to a foreign enterprise shareholder; reduced to 5% under the China-Hong Kong Double Tax Arrangement only where the beneficial owner is a Hong Kong resident company holding at least 25% of the WFOE's capital, satisfies the Limitation-on-Benefits test, and holds no PRC permanent establishment to which the shareholding is effectively connected.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Under SAT Public Notice 2016 No. 42, the China WFOE must prepare and maintain a Transfer Pricing Local File if annual related-party tangible-asset transactions exceed RMB 200 million, intangible-asset or financial-asset related-party transactions exceed RMB 100 million, or other related-party service transactions exceed RMB 40 million. A Master File is required where aggregate related-party transactions exceed RMB 1 billion or where the enterprise meets the consolidated revenue and related-party volume thresholds applicable to large multinational groups.

Economic substance scope: The PRC Corporate Income Tax Law General Anti-Avoidance Rule in Article 47 and the substance-over-form doctrine require the China WFOE to demonstrate real decision-making, personnel, assets and risk assumption in its automated dropshipping operations. For any Hong Kong intermediary in the distribution chain, the Hong Kong Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) require adequate physical presence, qualified employees, and Core Income-Generating Activities performed in Hong Kong. If the HK entity is a shell that merely holds contracts while the WFOE performs all logistics, order routing, pricing and customer service, the DTA relief is denied, the HK entity may be treated as creating a PRC permanent establishment, and payments may be recharacterized as dividends.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Fatal Red Flag 1 - Cross-Border Tax Evasion and Concealment of Dropshipping Revenue

Statutory sanction: Under PRC Tax Collection Law Article 63, the taxpayer is ordered to pay the evaded tax with a daily 0.05% late-payment surcharge and a penalty of 50%-500% of the evaded tax. Under PRC Criminal Law Article 201, deliberate concealment of taxable income may lead to criminal fines, confiscation of illegal proceeds and imprisonment of up to 7 years.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced where the director or beneficial owner controlled the offshore payment accounts and signed false financial statements. Directors and actual controllers are jointly and severally liable for the unpaid tax, penalties and interest, and may be subject to PRC tax blacklisting, exit bans, travel restrictions, asset freezing and civil restitution. Criminal prosecution may result in detention and imprisonment.
Fatal Red Flag 2 - Sham Transfer Pricing and Thin-Capitalization Profit Stripping

Statutory sanction: Under PRC Enterprise Income Tax Law Articles 41 and 46 and SAT Public Notice 2016 No. 42, the tax authority may recharacterize the payments, disallow excess deductions, adjust taxable income, and impose daily interest with an additional 5 percentage-point increase for missing contemporaneous documentation. Where the arrangement is fraudulent, Tax Collection Law Article 63 adds a 50%-500% financial penalty and criminal referral under Criminal Law Article 201.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly sign inflated related-party contracts are exposed to personal civil liability, asset restitution, travel bans and blacklisting. Courts may lift the corporate veil of both the WFOE and the HK shell to attach personal assets where the structure is a sham. Criminal liability attaches to directors who use transfer pricing as an instrument of intentional tax evasion.
Fatal Red Flag 3 - Shell Hong Kong Shareholder and Withholding Tax/DTA Abuse

Statutory sanction: Under PRC Tax Collection Law Article 69, a withholding agent that fails to withhold is liable for a fine of 50%-300% of the unwithheld tax, in addition to recovery from the taxpayer. Under the PRC GAAR and SAT Announcement 2018 No. 9, treaty benefits are denied, the payment is recharacterized, and the underlying profits are taxed at the statutory rate. The Hong Kong ESR may also impose profits-tax consequences and penalties on the shell entity.

Director personal liability: Directors who authorize an unlawful distribution and non-withholding are jointly and severally liable for restitution of the unwithheld tax and penalties. Personal asset tracing, travel bans, blacklisting and exit restrictions apply. Deliberate evasion using a treaty-shell structure may result in criminal prosecution, imprisonment and confiscation of assets.
