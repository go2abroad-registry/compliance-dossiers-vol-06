Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None. For a general internet retail WFOE under ISIC 4791, PRC law does not impose a minimum registered or paid-up capital amount. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA), acting through the in-charge local tax service office; electronic registration and filing are processed on the STA Electronic Taxpayer Portal (the e-Tax platform).

E-government filing portal

For a PRC Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE), the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) obtained from the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) constitutes the statutory taxpayer identification number. The WFOE must complete taxpayer registration with the in-charge STA office through the e-Tax platform within 30 days after SAMR issuance, verify the registered enterprise type, select the VAT taxpayer classification (small-scale or general taxpayer), bind the USCC to all tax accounts, and enroll for electronic invoice issuance. A foreign non-resident enterprise without an establishment in China is generally not separately registered for a PRC TIN unless it is obliged to withhold China-source tax; in that case the payer registers the non-resident in the withholding taxpayer registration module and obtains a temporary tax code.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None. The PRC does not maintain a check-the-box election. A WFOE is treated as a PRC resident enterprise taxed at the entity level on worldwide income. Hybrid mismatch arrangements are addressed through the Enterprise Income Tax Law, the anti-avoidance rules, and the administrative guidance on preferential arrangements.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 annual taxable turnover for mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration; small-scale taxpayers below this threshold may voluntarily register as general taxpayers if required by commercial activities.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A WFOE may apply zero per cent VAT on goods physically exported from the PRC customs territory, provided the export is a genuine and verified export transaction, the goods are actually shipped abroad, the export proceeds are collected and repatriated within the statutory foreign exchange window, and the export is supported by lawful export declaration records. Cross-border e-commerce retail exports are zero-rated when cleared under the cross-border e-commerce retail export customs supervision model and reported in the relevant export summary declaration. Cross-border services consumed wholly outside the PRC and wholly performed outside the PRC may similarly qualify for VAT zero-rating or exemption if the statutory supporting documents are retained.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Customs Bill of Entry)
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Bill of Lading or airway bill
  • SAFE goods trade foreign exchange monitoring system export collection and verification record
  • Bank receipt of export proceeds
  • Cross-border e-commerce order and logistics data where applicable

All VAT taxpayers are required to issue invoices through the STA-approved e-invoice platform; fully digitalized electronic invoices are transmitted to the STA in real time and carry automatic digital anti-fraud control features. Special VAT invoices must pass real-time online verification and reporting through the VAT invoice system; printed invoices require the use of the official tax-control equipment. E-commerce exporters must transmit order, shipment and customs clearance data to the tax authorities through the prescribed electronic interfaces to support export invoices and zero-rating claims.

If the tax authority determines that an export zero-rating or refund claim is unsupported, fraudulent, or not evidenced by genuine cross-border movement, the VAT exemption and refund are clawed back, overdue tax is assessed with daily surcharge interest, and administrative penalties of fifty per cent to five times the tax evaded may be imposed under the PRC Tax Administration and Collection Law. Serious cases involving false export declarations or forged documents may be referred to the customs anti-smuggling and tax criminal authorities for criminal liability, revocation of export tax refund qualifications, and downgrading of the enterprise's customs and foreign exchange compliance rating.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The PRC operates a managed floating exchange-rate regime with strict capital-account controls and general current-account convertibility for bona fide trade transactions. Under SAFE authority, a Chinese WFOE may convert remitted equity capital into CNY only after completing foreign direct investment (FDI) registration at the local SAFE branch and only through a designated Tier-1 PRC bank; unused foreign exchange must be maintained in a restricted capital account and converted on an as-needed basis with evidence of actual expenditure. Export proceeds from cross-border POD e-commerce sales are treated as current-account goods trade and are therefore eligible for conversion or retention in an export-collection foreign-currency account, but the receipts must be physically repatriated to China and reconciled with the electronic customs declaration. Profit remittances by the WFOE to its Hong Kong parent are permitted only after annual financial statements have been audited, corporate income tax has been settled, the board has resolved to distribute dividends, and SAFE has verified the remittance through the bank on a real trade/dividend basis. No offshore RMB convertibility, speculative currency swaps, or unregistered capital inflows are permitted; all capital-account transactions need SAFE approval or prior registration.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE regulations, exporters are obliged to repatriate export foreign-exchange receipts to China within 180 days of the date of customs declaration, unless an approved extension is obtained for genuine commercial or trade reasons. Mandatory conversion is not applied on a fixed percentage basis; the exporter may keep the full amount of eligible export proceeds in a dedicated foreign-currency current account or voluntarily settle into CNY at commercial bank quoted rates. Each receipt must be reported in the SAFE digital foreign-exchange administration system and reconciled with the customs export declaration via the China Electronic Port single-window to satisfy the goods-trade verification requirement.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) acting through the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Low to non-viable for onshore WFOE treasury. Under the PRC banking and payment regulations, only commercial banks licensed by the PBOC and a limited number of domestic non-bank payment institutions with the relevant cross-border payment licence can handle CNY clearing and foreign-exchange conversion for resident entities. Offshore EMI brands such as Wise, Airwallex, Payoneer, Stripe and PayPal are not authorised to open domestic bank or payment accounts or perform onshore CNY settlement for the WFOE; PayPal and Stripe can only be used as overseas e-commerce collection and payment gateways through the WFOE's own contractual merchant accounts, with the proceeds repatriated to a licensed PRC bank through trade channels. A Hong Kong intermediate company may lawfully hold an account with an offshore EMI for collecting foreign sales, but the HK entity must then remit the funds to the WFOE via SWIFT trade payments or dividend channels that are subject to SAFE reporting. Therefore the recommended treasury architecture is a licensed Tier-1 PRC bank as the primary clearing rail, with the HK treasury as an offshore revenue pooling and trade-finance point; offshore EMIs should not be presented to Chinese regulators as part of the onshore account structure.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The HK parent must ensure the remittance is made only after the WFOE has obtained a SAFE FDI registration and currency quota and has opened the dedicated foreign-currency capital account at the chosen Tier-1 PRC bank. The inbound wire is sent in HKD or USD via SWIFT MT103 or MT202 to the WFOE capital account with remittance information containing the SAFE FDI registration code, the foreign-exchange purpose code for direct-investment equity contribution, and a reference to the HK parent board capital call. Upon receipt, the bank verifies the beneficiary name, enters the funds in the SAFE capital-account information system, and requires the WFOE to submit a capital-use application, CPA capital verification report, board resolution, and underlying business contracts before converting the foreign currency into CNY and transferring it to the basic operating account. Any conversion is executed at the prevailing bank exchange rate, and every subsequent use must remain traceable to the registered business scope; conversion of capital funds for securities investment, entrusted loans, domestic equity investment, or prohibited fields is prohibited.Three clearing rails are used. First, for low-value POD supplies and routine offshore platform or software fees, the bank executes SWIFT MT103 directly from the WFOE CNY or FX account after presentation of invoice, purchase order, and service contract. Second, for bulk production runs, the WFOE or HK parent opens a commercial letter of credit with transport documents, insurance certificate, and inspection and compliance documents, settled under UCP600. Third, for established trade buyers, documentary collections using D/P or D/A are processed through the negotiating bank with title documents released per collection instructions. Export-side clearing from overseas POD customers flows through PayPal or Stripe to an offshore collection bank or HK treasury and then via SWIFT MT103 to the WFOE export proceeds current account in China; each receipt is matched to the customs export declaration and SAFE goods-trade verification system. Import disbursements for fabric, blanks, print equipment, packaging, and logistics are supported by the relevant contracts, invoices, customs declarations, and transport documents to satisfy bank authenticity checks.Deliverable onshore forward foreign exchange contracts for CNY/USD, CNY/HKD, CNY/EUR and other trade-invoiced currencies, supported by real current-account underlying trade documents and capped at a one-year tenor, executed with a licensed PRC bank under the recognised master agreement.Plain vanilla foreign exchange swaps used to manage temporary timing mismatches between receipt and payment dates for the same underlying trade exposure.Cross-currency swaps for hedging approved cross-border financing and debt principal and interest, only where SAFE registration or pilot approval exists and a genuine exposure to long-term foreign-currency liabilities is demonstrated.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) and speculative leveraged exotic option structures are not permissible for the onshore WFOE; any offshore NDF treasury activity may be performed only by the Hong Kong parent and must not be booked to, or risk-shared by, the Chinese entity.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low to non-viable for onshore WFOE treasury. Under the PRC banking and payment regulations, only commercial banks licensed by the PBOC and a limited number of domestic non-bank payment institutions with the relevant cross-border payment licence can handle CNY clearing and foreign-exchange conversion for resident entities. Offshore EMI brands such as Wise, Airwallex, Payoneer, Stripe and PayPal are not authorised to open domestic bank or payment accounts or perform onshore CNY settlement for the WFOE; PayPal and Stripe can only be used as overseas e-commerce collection and payment gateways through the WFOE's own contractual merchant accounts, with the proceeds repatriated to a licensed PRC bank through trade channels. A Hong Kong intermediate company may lawfully hold an account with an offshore EMI for collecting foreign sales, but the HK entity must then remit the funds to the WFOE via SWIFT trade payments or dividend channels that are subject to SAFE reporting. Therefore the recommended treasury architecture is a licensed Tier-1 PRC bank as the primary clearing rail, with the HK treasury as an offshore revenue pooling and trade-finance point; offshore EMIs should not be presented to Chinese regulators as part of the onshore account structure.

Under the Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622), a private company may be exempt from statutory audit for a financial year if it is not a specified regulated company, not listed, not a member of a non-qualifying group, and it satisfies all of the following thresholds on the balance sheet date: annual revenue not exceeding HKD 10,000,000, total assets not exceeding HKD 10,000,000, and total employees not exceeding 50. The exemption is also subject to the absence of a written notice from any shareholder holding at least 5 percent of voting rights requiring an audit.
