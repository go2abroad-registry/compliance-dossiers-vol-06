Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None. The PRC capital subscription system imposes no minimum registered capital and no blocked escrow account for an electronic-retailing WFOE outside the Negative List. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; online electronic tax declaration portal: National Electronic Tax Administration (etax.chinatax.gov.cn)

E-government filing portal

Upon incorporation, the WFOE automatically receives a Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which serves as the statutory Tax Identification Number for income tax, VAT and customs purposes. Procedure: (1) submit Certificate of Incorporation, Articles of Association, lease agreement, legal representative identity documents and bank account opening certificate to the local office of the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR); (2) SAMR issues the Business License with the USCC; (3) complete tax registration through the integrated multi-licence tax registration process at the competent State Taxation Administration office or via the National Electronic Tax Administration portal; (4) for VAT purposes, the entity is registered as a Small-Scale Taxpayer if annual VAT-taxable sales do not exceed CNY 5,000,000, or as a VAT General Taxpayer upon exceeding the threshold or by voluntary election; (5) for export operations, register the same USCC as the customs declarant in the General Administration of Customs of China through the China International Trade Single Window. A non-resident foreign entity without a permanent establishment may obtain a Non-Resident Taxpayer Registration Number through a withholding agent in China when it derives China-source income.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A VAT General Taxpayer that physically exports goods, including pet supplies, out of Chinese customs territory may apply the 0% export VAT rate and claim a refund of attributable input VAT provided the export sale is made to an overseas buyer, the goods leave mainland China through a registered customs process, title and risk pass outside China, and the export proceeds are collected in foreign currency from overseas. Cross-border e-commerce retail exports may qualify under the applicable cross-border e-commerce retail export customs supervision mode if the parcels are cleared through a designated cross-border e-commerce customs hub. Qualifying cross-border services are subject to separate zero-rating conditions.

  • Bill of Lading, Air Waybill or Parcel Delivery Manifest
  • Commercial Invoice
  • Packing List
  • Export Proceeds Collection Return (Foreign Exchange Receipt Verification)
  • Export VAT Rebate Declaration with matched customs, invoice and foreign exchange data

China mandates fully digitalised electronic invoice issuance through the National Electronic Invoice Service Platform. All invoice data is transmitted in near real-time to the State Taxation Administration under Golden Tax Phase IV. Each electronic invoice bears a unique tax control number and QR code; fiscal clearance is achieved through digital validation rather than physical stamping. Enterprises with automated systems may use API integration for real-time invoice issuance and reporting.

The State Taxation Administration cross-checks export declarations, electronic invoices, customs data and foreign exchange repatriation through Golden Tax Phase IV. If the 0% VAT rate is claimed without verifiable export movement or foreign exchange repatriation within the 180-day deadline, the VAT refund is clawed back, the taxable output VAT becomes payable at 13%, a daily late payment surcharge of 0.05% applies, and Article 66 of the Tax Collection and Administration Law authorises a fine of 100% to 500% of the fraudulently refunded amount. Fraudulent export refund schemes are referred to criminal authorities under the Criminal Law for the offence of fraudulent export tax refund.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a hybrid convertibility regime. The RMB is fully convertible on current account for bona fide trade, e-commerce and service payments after the designated bank completes truthfulness review, but remains subject to strict capital account control under PBC/SAFE. The WFOE, as a mainland resident foreign-invested enterprise, may receive foreign currency equity injections only after FDI registration is completed; every inward remittance is tracked in the Foreign Exchange Capital Account and must be settled against a genuine capital transaction. Export proceeds from pet supplies e-commerce are required to be repatriated to mainland China and collected through the domestic banking system; there is no fixed mandatory percentage conversion, so the WFOE may retain foreign currency or convert to RMB at market rates. Profit remittance to the Hong Kong shareholder is permitted only after the annual audit, corporate income tax clearance and SAFE dividend remittance confirmation; the local bank will require audited financial statements, tax payment proof, board resolution approving the dividend, and confirmation that there are no undistributed losses or illegal capital reserve distributions. The Hong Kong SAR side has no exchange controls, no surrender requirements and no profit remittance approval, giving the HK holding layer free treasury mobility under the HKMA's linked exchange rate system. Thus, the treasury blueprint must keep onshore China capital account documentation fully segregated from offshore Hong Kong liquidity and investible cash.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE trade monitoring, export foreign currency receipts from the ISIC 4791 e-commerce business must be repatriated to China and reported through the bank's foreign exchange receipt system. The statutory chase-back window is normally within 180 days after the customs export declaration date, or the contractual payment maturity if shorter; overdue receipts above USD 500,000 trigger validation alarm. There is no mandatory surrender ratio; the WFOE may retain proceeds in a current account foreign-exchange account or convert to RMB at prevailing interbank rates, but the proceeds may not be held abroad without SAFE registration.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBC) and State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China; for the Hong Kong SAR leg, the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) operates the currency board and supervises the local banking market

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Low for primary treasury rails; conditionally viable as an ancillary collection gateway. Under PRC law, a mainland WFOE cannot open a bank account or receive FDI capital through an unauthorized fintech EMI, including EU, UK or Singapore digital banks, because only SAFE-licensed banks and licensed non-bank payment institutions may process cross-border receipts. Stripe and PayPal may be used for low-risk retail e-commerce collections, but their proceeds must be swept into a domestic bank account and cannot replace the required bank clearing rail for profit remittance or capital injection. The HK parent should not rely on online EMIs for the equity contribution wire because the PRC beneficiary bank must see a clean MT103 from a regulated corporate bank account in the name of the Hong Kong shareholder; an EMI-hosted account without full bank ownership or audit trail will be rejected.

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% statutory withholding tax on dividends paid by a Chinese resident enterprise to a non-resident enterprise, including a Hong Kong corporate shareholder, under PRC Enterprise Income Tax Law; qualified Hong Kong beneficial owner companies holding at least 25% of paid-in equity capital may obtain the reduced 5% rate under the Mainland-Hong Kong Double Taxation Arrangement.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: RMB 1 billion for the Master File if aggregate related-party transactions reach that annual threshold; RMB 200 million for tangible property controlled transactions triggering the Local File; RMB 100 million for financial asset and intangible property controlled transactions; RMB 40 million for other controlled transactions; related-party transaction schedules in Form A are due with the annual return and contemporaneous Local File and Master File should be ready before filing.

Economic substance scope: China does not apply a separate ESR regime but relies on substance-over-form, the general anti-avoidance rule under Enterprise Income Tax Law Articles 47 and 48, special anti-avoidance rules for controlled foreign companies and thin capitalisation, and the beneficial ownership and place of effective management tests for treaty application. Core Income-Generating Activities must be verified for any Hong Kong intermediary: procurement, inventory risk, logistics coordination, marketing decision-making, customer contracting and treasury functions cannot be merely paper transactions; the absence of CIGA in Hong Kong combined with a China-based WFOE performing all functions exposes the group to PE identification in mainland China, treaty denial and recharacterization of profits.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion through Unreported Cross-Border E-Commerce Revenue

Statutory sanction: PRC Tax Collection Administration Law Article 63 imposes a penalty of 50% to 5 times the amount of tax underpaid, plus daily late interest at 0.05% under Article 32; under Criminal Law Article 201, tax evasion of RMB 100,000 or more and a 10% tax evasion ratio triggers criminal liability, with larger amounts carrying up to three years' imprisonment; fines up to 5 times the evaded tax; aggravated fraud may reach seven years.

Director personal liability: The relevant director and legal representative face piercing of the corporate veil under PRC Company Law, making them jointly and severally liable with the WFOE for unpaid taxes and penalties if personal assets were used to conceal revenue; tax authorities impose exit bans and blacklisting through the joint punishment memorandum, freezing personal bank accounts and securities, prohibiting re-entry into China, and public criminal referral can lead to detention and custodial sentencing of the director for organized tax evasion.
Transfer Pricing Mispricing and Intercompany Profit Shifting

Statutory sanction: Enterprise Income Tax Law Articles 41 and 44 authorize the State Taxation Administration to make a special tax adjustment and assess income by reference to arm's length conditions; SAT Announcement 2016 No. 42 and Tax Collection Administration Law impose penalties for failure to supply documentation, interest at the benchmark loan rate plus 5 percentage points on the adjusted underpayment, and recharacterization of royalties as dividends subject to withholding tax.

Director personal liability: Directors approving the artificial transfer pricing policy are liable for causing the enterprise to underpay CIT; where the transfer pricing documentation is fabricated, personal exposure mirrors tax evasion; the director can be blacklisted, have passport revoked, be disqualified from serving as director, and be personally sued by the tax authority; criminal liability for false accounting documents and corruption could be raised under Criminal Law provisions applicable to fraudulent tax filings.
DTA Treaty Abuse / Shell Company Beneficial Ownership Failure

Statutory sanction: The tax authority may deny treaty benefits under the beneficial ownership test and the general anti-abuse rule under Enterprise Income Tax Law Article 47, recharacterize the HK company as tax resident in China or its income as China-source income, impose the full 10% statutory withholding tax plus penalty and interest, and may assess the WFOE for unpaid withholding tax as withholding agent under Tax Collection Administration Law Article 69.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign tax residency certificates or beneficial ownership declarations knowing them false commit fraud; the corporate veil is pierced where the company is a facade for individual interests, exposing directors to personal restitution of the evaded WHT; the legal representative and financial controller face blacklisting and travel bans, personal bank attachment, and prosecution under Criminal Law for issuing false declarations; imprisonment of up to seven years may result for aggravated cross-border tax fraud.
