Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA); online electronic portal: Electronic Tax Administration (e-Taxation) platform at etax.chinatax.gov.cn

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the WFOE with the Administration for Market Regulation and obtain the Business License bearing the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), an 18-character alphanumeric code that constitutes the statutory taxpayer identification number (TIN) for corporate income tax and VAT. Step 2: Within 30 days after the Business License is issued, complete tax registration information confirmation through the STA e-Taxation platform or the competent tax service hall, submitting the USCC, registered address, business scope, legal representative and financial officer identity data, capitalization, and bank account details. Step 3: The STA validates the data and opens the enterprise tax registration master file; no separate paper TIN certificate is issued because the USCC is the TIN/VAT registration number. Step 4: Determine VAT taxpayer status (small-scale taxpayer or general VAT taxpayer) and apply for VAT digital invoice issuance rights through e-Taxation. Step 5: For a non-resident investor without a permanent establishment in China, no PRC TIN is required; the WFOE must withhold PRC corporate income tax and VAT on any China-source payments, and treaty relief must be evidenced by the investor home jurisdiction tax residence certificate. Step 6: If the WFOE undertakes exports, complete the export tax rebate (zero-rate) registration filing and link banking settlement records with the State Administration of Foreign Exchange export proceeds monitoring system.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

There is no turnover exemption from tax registration for a WFOE; the mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration threshold is CNY 5,000,000 of annual taxable turnover. Below CNY 5,000,000 the taxpayer qualifies as a small-scale VAT taxpayer; crossing the threshold makes general VAT taxpayer registration compulsory.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods, including baby and maternity supplies, qualify for the 0% VAT zero-rating when the WFOE is a general VAT taxpayer registered for export tax rebates, the goods are actually exported from PRC customs territory under an approved customs export declaration, the export sales proceeds are received and repatriated through a designated foreign-exchange bank, and the export transaction is included in the STA export tax rebate declaration with matching customs, logistics, and payment data. Qualifying cross-border e-commerce retail export shipments may also use the consolidated export supervision model to obtain zero-rating; services exported to non-residents must fall within the statutory offshore service zero-rating catalogue.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bill of Entry) with customs declaration number
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Bill of Lading (or air waybill for air cargo)
  • Export order or sales contract
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or bank-issued foreign exchange collection certificate evidencing repatriation of export proceeds

VAT special invoices must be issued through the STA National Unified Electronic Invoice Service Platform as full-digital electronic invoices using the tax control UKey or an STA-certified system-to-system API. The e-invoice platform assigns a unique 20-digit invoice number, applies the taxpayer digital signature, and receives the invoice data in real time at issuance; no lawful VAT special invoice can be issued outside this digital fiscal clearance environment. Anti-fraud controls include automatic cross-matching of invoice data with VAT returns and customs/export records.

The STA export tax rebate audit cross-checks the customs export declaration, logistics documents, upstream VAT invoice chain, and bank foreign-exchange receipts. If any document is false, incomplete, or inconsistent, the zero-rating/refund is clawed back with daily late payment surcharge at 0.05% and, under Article 66 of the PRC Tax Collection Administration Law, a monetary penalty of one to five times the amount of the refund fraudulently obtained. Serious cases involving counterfeit documents or fictitious exports are referred for criminal liability for fraudulent export tax rebates, and the taxpayer may be downgraded to restricted foreign-exchange/customs supervision status.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

For the mainland China WFOE with a Hong Kong parent, the renminbi is not fully convertible under capital account rules. Current account transactions, including cross-border e-commerce sales proceeds and payments for goods and services under ISIC 4791, are generally convertible once the bank verifies the underlying trade documents, invoices, logistics records, and SAFE filing codes. Capital account flows remain tightly controlled. Inbound FDI registered capital injections from the Hong Kong shareholder must be routed through a designated SAFE-registered foreign exchange bank into a capital account facility, and the subsequent conversion of foreign currency into renminbi requires a capital account settlement declaration with the bank. Profit repatriation or dividend remittance to the Hong Kong parent is permissible only after the annual financial audit is completed, corporate income tax is settled, statutory reserves are allocated, and SAFE verification of the dividend resolution and audited financial statements is passed. The WFOE may hold foreign exchange in an operational current account, but no unfettered overseas conversion or offshore clearing is allowed. Stripe and PayPal can serve only as front-end merchant collection tools; the underlying funds must be routed back into the Chinese banking system through licensed commercial banks. The overall regime therefore allows trade-led convertibility but imposes strict documentary substance, reporting, and capital account filing burdens.

Export proceeds surrender rules

China has abolished mandatory surrender of export foreign exchange receipts. There is no fixed statutory conversion ratio. Export proceeds must be repatriated into the Chinese banking system within 180 calendar days after the export customs declaration, unless SAFE grants an extension. The WFOE may retain 100% of verified export foreign currency receipts in a designated foreign exchange current account or sell them to an authorized bank at the prevailing market rate. Conversion to renminbi is optional and must be supported by underlying trade documents, customs records, shipping bills, and e-commerce platform settlement statements.

Central bank supervision: The People's Bank of China (PBOC) together with the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Low. Offshore digital EMIs, including neobanks and payment service providers, are not legally permitted to replace a licensed Chinese commercial bank as the treasury and FX clearing bank for a mainland WFOE. Such entities are not licensed by SAFE or the PBOC to operate renminbi corporate settlement accounts, execute capital account conversions, or provide foreign exchange verification for FDI flows. Stripe and PayPal may be used only as auxiliary merchant collection rails for low-value cross-border e-commerce sales; the collected funds must ultimately be channeled into the WFOE's domestic bank account through lawful trade settlement procedures. Any proposal to place primary corporate liquidity or FX execution with an offshore EMI is non-compliant and must be rejected.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The Hong Kong shareholder must remit the approved FDI registered capital via SWIFT MT103 to the WFOE's SAFE-registered capital escrow account held at a licensed Chinese bank. The wire must reference the SAFE FDI registration number and use the foreign exchange purpose code for inbound direct investment registered capital, typically recorded by the receiving bank under SAFE's capital project information system. Upon receipt, the bank issues an inbound foreign exchange payment notice and handles the capital account settlement into renminbi under the negative list. A Chinese certified public accountant is then engaged to issue a capital verification report, which is filed with SAMR and SAFE and serves as evidence of paid-in capital.For settlements with domestic Chinese suppliers, the WFOE will clear renminbi payments through CNAPS or the RMB Core Clearing System. For cross-border trade settlements with overseas vendors and logistics providers, the permitted rails include SWIFT MT103 telegraphic transfers, irrevocable commercial letters of credit for bulk inventory purchases, and documentary collections where deferred payment terms are agreed. For cross-border renminbi trade settlements, CIPS can be used to reduce costs and improve traceability. All outbound payments must carry the correct SAFE trade purpose code and supporting commercial invoices, contracts, and transport documents.Onshore plain-vanilla renminbi or foreign currency FX forwards executed with a SAFE-approved domestic bank, supported by underlying trade or capital flows.Cross-currency swaps for intercompany funding and working capital hedges, limited to actual exposure and subject to bank counterparty and SAFE reporting approval.Plain-vanilla FX options for hedging confirmed trade receivables; exotic derivative structures and speculative options are not permissible for the WFOE.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are not permitted onshore for the mainland WFOE; only the offshore Hong Kong treasury vehicle may access NDF markets outside mainland China.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low. Offshore digital EMIs, including neobanks and payment service providers, are not legally permitted to replace a licensed Chinese commercial bank as the treasury and FX clearing bank for a mainland WFOE. Such entities are not licensed by SAFE or the PBOC to operate renminbi corporate settlement accounts, execute capital account conversions, or provide foreign exchange verification for FDI flows. Stripe and PayPal may be used only as auxiliary merchant collection rails for low-value cross-border e-commerce sales; the collected funds must ultimately be channeled into the WFOE's domestic bank account through lawful trade settlement procedures. Any proposal to place primary corporate liquidity or FX execution with an offshore EMI is non-compliant and must be rejected.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% statutory withholding on dividends paid by a Chinese resident enterprise to a non-resident enterprise without a permanent establishment in China (the nominal 20% law rate being effectively reduced to 10% under implementing rules); reducible to 5% under the Mainland-Hong Kong Arrangement if the beneficial owner is a company resident in Hong Kong directly holding at least 25% of the capital of the WFOE and all treaty conditions are met.
