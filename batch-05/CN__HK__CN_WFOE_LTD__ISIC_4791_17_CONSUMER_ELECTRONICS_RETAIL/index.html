Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No minimum paid-up capital is required under Hong Kong law. The Company may be incorporated with an issued share capital of one ordinary share at HKD 1.00 per share or such higher amount as the subscribers determine. There is no blocked, escrowed, or pre-funded capital account requirement, and no exchange-control approval is required for a non-resident shareholder. The directors must, however, comply with Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622) allotment and share-capital provisions and file the prescribed notice of allotment within the statutory time limit if shares are issued.

Funding & unlock path: Not Applicable for a pure online retail company with no physical industrial premises. No Civil Defense fire permit or fire safety certificate is triggered at Stage 2. If the entity later occupies an office or warehouse, it must obtain a Fire Services Installation compliance certificate from an approved fire service installation contractor under the Fire Services Ordinance (Cap. 95), secure structural approval under the Buildings Ordinance (Cap. 123) for any storage loads, and comply with the Occupational Safety and Health Ordinance (Cap. 509) for workplace safety and employee welfare.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China, including provincial/municipal tax service branches; all filings and issuance are centralized through the China Electronic Tax Bureau (E-Tax Bureau) online portal, which is the STA's official taxpayer electronic service platform.

E-government filing portal

For the PRC WFOE, there is no separately issued TIN or VAT ID. The statutory taxpayer identification number is the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) printed on the WFOE's Business License. The procedure is as follows: (1) The foreign investor causes its Hong Kong incorporation certificate and good standing documentation to be notarized and apostilled as required, and appoints the proposed legal representative of the WFOE. (2) The foreign investor files the establishment application with the local Administration for Market Regulation using the official one-window foreign-investment business registration system; the AMR approves the WFOE and issues the Business License containing the USCC. (3) The AMR automatically transmits the registration record to the State Taxation Administration through the multi-department data-sharing mechanism, and the WFOE completes its tax registration at the competent tax authority via the E-Tax Bureau; the USCC is activated as the VAT and Enterprise Income Tax identifier. (4) The WFOE then activates the Electronic Tax Bureau account, confirms VAT taxpayer status as small-scale or general taxpayer, registers for e-invoice services, and registers with the China International Trade Single Window for customs and export VAT purposes. A foreign non-resident enterprise that has not established a WFOE but has China-sourced income is instead subject to withholding tax administration by the payer and, if it has a permanent establishment, must separately complete registration under the same USCC/TIN framework and obtain an E-Tax Bureau account.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation - the WFOE is a PRC resident enterprise taxed at entity level on worldwide income; it is not eligible for pass-through treatment, and the Hong Kong shareholder's offshore status does not flow through to the WFOE.

Available election forms

None. The PRC tax system does not provide a U.S.-style check-the-box classification election and does not permit an entity-level resident WFOE to be treated as a pass-through. Hybrid mismatch outcomes are addressed through the General Anti-Avoidance Rule, controlled foreign corporation legislation, and business substance requirements.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 annual taxable sales is the mandatory threshold for VAT general taxpayer registration. All WFOEs are VAT-registered upon business license issuance without regard to turnover; there is no registration threshold for the initial tax registration itself.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The WFOE may claim PRC VAT zero-rating on export sales of consumer electronics only if the goods are sold to an overseas buyer, the goods physically leave mainland China customs territory under customs supervision, the WFOE is named as exporter in the customs export declaration, and the export proceeds are collected in foreign currency through a PRC-designated bank within the statutory 180-day repatriation period. For VAT general taxpayers, zero-rating is implemented through the export tax refund and exemption-credit-refund method under which output VAT is 0% and input VAT attributable to the exported goods can be refunded. Cross-border e-commerce retail export sales qualify under the cross-border e-commerce retail export regulatory model if the customs declaration, order data, payment data, and logistics data are electronically reconciled.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Export Bill of Entry) generated through the China International Trade Single Window showing the WFOE as exporter and the exported goods
  • Ocean Bill of Lading or Master Air Waybill evidencing physical export from PRC customs territory
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Bank foreign-exchange inward remittance receipt (FIRC-equivalent export collection verification certificate) issued by the settlement bank
  • Export VAT refund declaration voucher and supporting VAT special purchase invoices where input VAT credit or refund is claimed
  • Cross-border e-commerce retail export order, payment platform settlement, and logistics trace records

The PRC operates real-time fiscal invoice monitoring through the Golden Tax Phase IV system and the fully digitalized electronic invoice platform. Qualified taxpayers must issue VAT invoices through the STA e-invoice service platform or through independently developed systems connected to the e-invoice platform using the STA's electronic invoice data interface specifications. Each e-invoice is cleared at issuance with a unique tax control number and an anti-fraud QR code; issuance data is transmitted electronically to the tax authority in near-real time. The system matches invoice data against customs declarations, export logistics, and bank foreign-exchange receipts, and any non-conforming e-invoice is blocked or flagged for audit.

Export zero-rating is automatically scrutinized by the STA's Golden Tax Phase IV cross-referencing of customs release records, foreign-exchange receipts, e-invoices, and logistics data. If the WFOE fails to substantiate the export or fails to repatriate the foreign-exchange proceeds within 180 days, the zero-rating is reversed and output VAT is assessed at 13% on the export sale, together with statutory late payment surcharge and interest. Deliberate falsification of export documents, fake foreign-exchange receipts, or fraudulent refund claims trigger recovery of all refunded VAT, administrative fines up to five times the tax involved, denial or suspension of export-tax-refund qualifications, and referral for criminal prosecution under the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China for fraudulent export tax refund offences.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

RMB is fully convertible on current account for trade and service operations, while capital account transactions are strictly controlled through authorization, registration, and quota systems. The China WFOE, as a foreign-invested enterprise, can receive equity from its Hong Kong shareholder only after completing the Foreign Investment Filing and SAFE's Direct Investment Foreign Exchange Registration. Capital inflows must enter a dedicated Foreign Currency Capital Account and be credited against the registered contribution schedule; the funds cannot be used for securities investment, real estate unrelated to operations, entrusted loans, or offshore lending. RMB converted from FDI capital is segregated from current-account funds, and reconversion for equity repatriation requires liquidation or SAFE approval. Dividend/profit remittance is a current-account transaction, but the bank will require audited financial statements, tax clearance, and proof of withholding tax (5% under the PRC-HK DTA when the 25% direct ownership and beneficial owner tests are met). Export proceeds from cross-border e-commerce must be repatriated to a domestic SAFE-registered account and monitored under the Trade Monitoring System; proceeds may be kept in foreign currency or converted to RMB at the prevailing bank rate. In practice, SAFE treats large, unexplained, or late transactions as high-risk, so treasury operations must be supported by complete trade and FDI documentation.

Export proceeds surrender rules

SAFE has abolished mandatory surrender of export proceeds; the exporter may retain foreign exchange in a settlement account or convert it to RMB at the bank's quoted rate. The operational requirement is to repatriate all goods-trade export proceeds to a domestic SAFE-monitored bank account within 180 days from the customs export declaration (extensions can be applied for under the Trade Monitoring System in legitimate cases). No fixed conversion ratio applies. For e-commerce retail, each batch of proceeds must be matched with order data, logistics tracking, and customs declarations; if the e-commerce platform uses a licensed Chinese payment institution to collect and settle, the payment institution must transmit the necessary background information to the settling bank.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE); for the Hong Kong SAR shareholder conduit, the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) is the relevant monetary authority, but the mainland WFOE's banking and FX transactions are governed by PBOC/SAFE only.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Low regulatory viability for onshore statutory banking; conditional viability as a supplementary collection channel. Offshore digital banks and EMIs (Revolut, Airwallex, Payoneer, etc.) do not hold a PRC banking license and cannot open domestic deposit or settlement accounts for a WFOE. They are also not permitted to conduct RMB clearing or handle SAFE-reportable trade receivables directly. E-commerce platforms (Stripe, PayPal) may collect foreign-currency consumer payments, but those funds must be settled through a licensed Chinese partner bank or a licensed payment institution and ultimately credited to the WFOE's onshore settlement account. Therefore, the WFOE must not fund its capital account or pay taxes through offshore EMIs; the primary treasury relationship must be with a Tier-1 Chinese domestic bank.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low regulatory viability for onshore statutory banking; conditional viability as a supplementary collection channel. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Under SAT Public Notice 2016 No. 42, a Local File is mandatory where annual related-party transactions reach any of: tangible property transfers of RMB 400 million or more, intangible property transfers of RMB 100 million or more, related-party service transactions of RMB 100 million or more, or related-party equity transfers of RMB 100 million or more. A Master File is required where the aggregate annual related-party transactions exceed RMB 1 billion or the group otherwise meets the BEPS Action 13 filing conditions.

Economic substance scope: PRC tax law applies substance-over-form principles under Article 47 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law and SAT Public Notice 2018 No. 9 for treaty benefits and anti-avoidance purposes. The WFOE must demonstrate that its people, assets, functions and risks are aligned with the profits reported; the Hong Kong parent must perform core income-generating activities in Hong Kong, including strategic investment management, dividend receipt decision-making and risk management, to qualify as beneficial owner and to satisfy Hong Kong's Foreign-Source Income Exemption economic substance requirements. A mailbox or conduit company with no real board oversight, no local employees and no decision-making records will be disregarded and denied treaty benefits.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion Through Concealment of Online Sales and Offshore Payment Settlement

Statutory sanction: PRC Law on Administration of Taxation Article 63: recovery of unpaid tax and late-payment surcharge plus an administrative penalty of 50% to 5 times the underpaid tax. Criminal Law Article 201: tax evasion with a significant evaded amount generally exceeding RMB 50,000 and exceeding 10% of payable tax carries imprisonment or criminal detention up to 3 years and a fine; evasion of a huge amount exceeding 30% of payable tax carries imprisonment from 3 to 7 years and a fine.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly instructed staff to use offshore payment accounts or signed false financial statements may be subject to piercing of the corporate veil under PRC Company Law Article 20 where abuse of limited liability injures creditors. Directors face joint and several personal restitution of the corporate tax debt, freezing of personal assets, travel bans and exit restrictions under Article 44 of the Law on Administration of Taxation, publication on the tax-related blacklist, restrictions on high-consumption spending and cross-border movement, and criminal prosecution as directly responsible persons.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation and Failure to Maintain Contemporaneous Documentation

Statutory sanction: Article 41 of PRC Enterprise Income Tax Law authorizes the SAT to make a transfer pricing adjustment under the arm's length principle; Article 48 requires payment of special tax adjustment interest at the benchmark loan rate plus 5 percentage points. Failure to provide contemporaneous documentation may trigger a deemed assessment under Article 44 and administrative penalties under the Law on Administration of Taxation; if the false documentation results in understated tax, a 50% to 5x penalty can be imposed.

Director personal liability: A director or CFO who signs false transfer pricing declarations, participates in circular invoicing, or approves the destruction of pricing records may be personally joined in tax recovery where corporate assets are insufficient. The director may face civil asset restitution, exit restriction and blacklisting for unpaid special tax adjustment interest and penalties. In aggressive cross-border profit-stripping cases, criminal tax evasion charges can extend to the director as a directly responsible person under Criminal Law Article 201, with imprisonment up to 7 years in aggravated cases.
Shell Company and Treaty Shopping Abuse Lacking Economic Substance in Hong Kong

Statutory sanction: Under SAT Public Notice 2018 No. 9, the claim for DTA relief is denied because the Hong Kong recipient is not the beneficial owner and the arrangement lacks a reasonable business purpose; the SAT will impose the statutory 10% dividend withholding tax, recover the shortfall with late-payment surcharge and interest, and may invoke Article 47 of the EIT Law to recharacterize the structure. If false declarations were submitted to obtain treaty benefits, an administrative penalty of 50% to 5 times the unpaid tax and criminal penalties under Criminal Law Article 201 may apply.

Director personal liability: Directors who signed the treaty-benefit application, Tax Residency Certificate declaration or beneficial ownership statement with false content face personal criminal liability for fraudulent tax avoidance. The corporate veil may be pierced if the Hong Kong entity is found to be a mere agent of the ultimate shareholder; directors and ultimate owners may be jointly liable for the additional tax, subject to entry onto the tax blacklist, travel bans across mainland China and Hong Kong, seizure of personal assets for restitution, and imprisonment for up to 7 years in aggravated tax evasion or fraud cases.
