Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR), acting through the local Administration for Market Regulation (AMR) at the city/district level where the WFOE’s registered office is located; foreign investment information filing is administered by the local commerce department through the Foreign Investment Comprehensive Management Information System.

Governing statute

Company Law of the People’s Republic of China (2023 Revision, effective 1 July 2024), Articles 22, 23, 29, 30, 31, 32, 40 and 47; Foreign Investment Law of the People’s Republic of China (2020), Articles 2, 6 and 28; Implementing Regulations of the Foreign Investment Law of the People’s Republic of China (State Council Order No. 723); Market Entity Registration Administration Regulations (State Council Order No. 746).

Formation / constitutional filings

Market Entity Establishment Registration Application (single integrated application covering business license, tax registration, social insurance, statistics and enterprise information) together with the Articles of Association and the Foreign Investment Information Report Filing Form.

Incorporation processing time

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None. There is no statutory minimum paid-up capital for a WFOE engaged in online retail of jewelry, watches and fashion accessories under ISIC 4791. The registered capital stated in the Articles of Association is the subscribed capital. Under Article 47 of the PRC Company Law (2023 Revision), all shareholders must contribute their subscribed capital in full within five years from establishment unless the Articles prescribe a shorter period. No blocked escrow account is statutorily required; however, the Hong Kong shareholder’s contributions must be remitted through a licensed bank and recorded under the SAFE Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) registration so that the inbound foreign-exchange capital account is used only for lawful operating purposes.

Funding & unlock path: Not Applicable for pure e-commerce retail under the current business scope. The WFOE must still perform office fire-risk assessments under the Work Safety Law, maintain portable fire extinguishers, and ensure safe use of electrical equipment. A Civil Defense fire permit is not required in the absence of a factory, warehouse, chemical installation or assembly line. If the WFOE later leases a fulfillment or bonded warehouse, the local Fire and Rescue Division must issue a Fire Safety Inspection Certificate, and the building must pass structural-load and fire-code review under GB 50016-2014 (2018 edition) before occupancy.

Paid-in capital rule: None. There is no statutory minimum paid-up capital for a WFOE engaged in online retail of jewelry, watches and fashion accessories under ISIC 4791. The registered capital stated in the Articles of Association is the subscribed capital. Under Article 47 of the PRC Company Law (2023 Revision), all shareholders must contribute their subscribed capital in full within five years from establishment unless the Articles prescribe a shorter period. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA) - online portal: Electronic Tax Bureau at https://etax.chinatax.gov.cn; for export/import trade, customs filing and tax-refund clearance through the General Administration of Customs China International Trade Single Window (https://www.singlewindow.cn).

E-government filing portal

For the WFOE (a Chinese resident limited liability company): (1) incorporate with the State Administration for Market Regulation; the system issues an 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) which is legally the TIN/VAT identification number; (2) the tax record is opened automatically at the competent in-charge tax office under the 'one license, one code' mechanism; no separate VAT registration is needed; (3) complete real-name authentication for the legal representative, financier, and tax personnel on the Electronic Tax Bureau portal within 30 days after the business license; (4) file bank account deposits and financial accounting regime with the tax office; (5) elect VAT taxpayer classification (small-scale or general). For a foreign non-resident enterprise without an establishment in China: (i) it is not required to obtain a general VAT ID for export transactions because the WFOE is the exporter; (ii) if it receives China-sourced income (e.g., dividends, interest, royalties) and is a Hong Kong resident, it must present a Hong Kong Tax Residency Certificate to the STA withholding agent to access the China-Hong Kong double tax arrangement; the withholding agent registers the contract and a non-resident TIN is allocated by the in-charge tax authority for the specific income; (iii) for VAT on digital/offshore services supplied into China by a non-resident, the Chinese buyer is the withholding agent and must obtain a VAT remittance registration number for the non-resident seller before issuance of the withholding VAT certificate.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation - the WFOE is a Chinese resident enterprise subject to the Enterprise Income Tax on its worldwide income; the limited liability form is not transparent, and no pass-through is permitted for income tax or VAT.

Available election forms

None for entity classification under Chinese enterprise income tax law; China does not provide check-the-box or hybrid mismatch elections. The only relevant statutory elections are: (a) under the VAT rules, an eligible small-scale taxpayer may apply to become a general taxpayer; and (b) cross-border e-commerce retailers may elect between the standard export refund (exemption) declaration route and the simplified retail export declaration route if qualifying conditions are met.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

All WFOEs are registered VAT taxpayers from the date of establishment; there is no registration threshold. A RMB 5,000,000 annual taxable turnover (rolling 12 months) is the statutory threshold separating 'small-scale taxpayers' (simplified 3% collection rate, with temporary 1% relief) from 'general taxpayers' (13% standard rate and input VAT credit subtraction). Turnover above RMB 5,000,000 makes general-taxpayer registration mandatory; below it the taxpayer may still elect voluntary general-taxpayer status.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to goods physically exported from China when all of the following statutory conditions are met: (1) the goods have been declared to customs under an export customs declaration (including cross-border e-commerce retail export operations in the 9610/1210/9710/9810 supervision modes) and have actually left the customs boundary; (2) the WFOE is the registered exporter and holds export right status; (3) the exported goods are commercial and not on the catalog of commodities prohibited or restricted from export tax refunds; (4) the WFOE receives payment from a foreign buyer in foreign currency (or approved settlement) through a designated bank and the export proceeds are repatriated into China within 210 days from the customs declaration date; (5) the input VAT on the exported goods has been separately identified and included in the export refund declaration; and (6) a valid export invoice/contract and the statutory documentation bundle are retained. Qualifying offshore digital services may be zero-rated or exempt under cross-border service rules; for jewelry/watches goods, the goods regime applies.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bill of Entry): the official Export Goods Customs Declaration Form issued and released by the General Administration of Customs (GACC), bearing the export declaration number and release/discharge endorsement, or the Cross-border E-commerce Retail Export Declaration List for e-commerce parcels under supervision codes 9610/1210/9710/9810.
  • Bill of Lading (sea transport), Air Waybill (air transport) or courier consignment note from a licensed international express carrier (for e-commerce small parcels), evidencing movement of the goods out of China.
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) equivalent: the Export Proceeds Collection Record and the Foreign Exchange Income Declaration Certificate issued by a designated SAFE-authorized bank, reconciled with the Trade in Goods Foreign Exchange Monitoring System to prove foreign buyer payment receipt within the 210-day repatriation deadline.
  • Commercial invoice and packing list issued by the WFOE, matching the customs declaration values.
  • Export sales contract or purchase order with the overseas buyer (including platform order records for marketplace e-commerce).
  • Input VAT special invoices (purchase VAT invoices) and payment vouchers that establish the export input-tax-credit pool.
  • Export Tax Refund (Exemption) Application Form and any additional attestation letters required under the STA Export Rebate Administration Measures (e.g., certificates from the e-commerce platform and logistics provider for cross-border e-commerce retail exports).

Since the nationwide digitalization, VAT invoices must be issued through the state Electronic Invoice Service Platform as Fully Digitalized Electronic Invoices (FDEI). The FDEI is cleared in near-real-time by the STA: each invoice receives a nation-wide unique invoice number and a digitally signed anti-fraud code (QR code); invoice data is transmitted automatically via the platform's API for enterprise resource planning (ERP)/e-commerce systems. No physical paper or mechanical stamp is permitted. Special VAT invoices are additionally checked across the national input-credit verification system. For exports, the fiscal clearance is via customs export declaration and the Export Tax Refund (Exemption) electronic system, rather than the ordinary invoice issuance.

The State Taxation Administration and the General Administration of Customs jointly operate a data-matching audit system (Customs-Tax-Foreign Exchange triangulation) for every export refund. If the export is subsequently found not to have met the zero-rating substantiation requirements, the refunded VAT is fully clawed back with statutory late surcharge at 0.05% per day from the refund date, and the exporter must reclassify the transaction as a domestic taxable sale at 13% VAT. Where documents are false or misdeclared: a 50% to 500% fraudulent tax penalty under the Tax Collection Administration Law; suspension or revocation of export refund qualifications; inclusion in the national social credit blacklist; referral to the public security authority for criminal liability for export tax fraud under Article 204 of the Criminal Law, with penalties ranging from serious economic fines up to life imprisonment for especially large cases. The limitation period for audit reassessment is 3 years normally, extending to 5 years or indefinitely in fraud scenarios.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a managed floating exchange rate system with strict segregation between current account and capital account transactions. Current account trade-related foreign exchange is broadly convertible after banks perform authenticity verification, which for a WFOE jewellery and timepiece exporter means presenting customs declarations, commercial invoices, logistics evidence, and sales platform data. Export proceeds from cross-border e-commerce may be received into a domestic foreign exchange settlement account through a licensed domestic bank or a SAFE-registered payment institution, with transaction-level reconciliation expected. Capital account flows, however, remain tightly controlled: FDI equity injections into a WFOE must be made into a SAFE-designated foreign exchange capital account, registered with SAFE through the bank, and settled into RMB only for approved operational purposes. Profit remittances are current account transfers but are subject to tax certificate, audited financial statement, and board resolution checks before the bank will execute the dividend wire. There is no general compulsory conversion ratio for export proceeds into RMB today, but all offshore receipts must be repatriated into China and reported under the trade foreign exchange monitoring system. Foreign currency loans, offshore guarantees, and related-party treasury arrangements require separate SAFE registration and file a genuine economic substance test.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For general goods trade, export proceeds must be repatriated to China within 90 days from the export customs declaration date. There is no mandatory conversion ratio into RMB; exporters may retain foreign currency in a domestic settlement account or voluntarily convert through a licensed designated bank. Cross-border e-commerce export receipts may be collected via SAFE-registered payment institutions, subject to order-level verification against platform settlement reports. Any receipt exceeding the standard 90-day window requires SAFE registration through the bank with documentary support such as trade financing, open account terms, or disputed shipment evidence.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) acting under the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Master File required where total annual related-party transactions exceed RMB 1 billion. Local File required where one type of related-party transaction reaches: tangible asset transfers > RMB 200 million; financial asset transfers > RMB 100 million; intangible asset transfers > RMB 100 million; or other related-party transactions (e.g., services) > RMB 40 million. Contemporaneous TP documentation must be prepared by the filing deadline and submitted within 15 days upon tax authority request.

Economic substance scope: Although China does not apply territorial Economic Substance Regulations akin to Caribbean jurisdictions, the tax authorities apply the substance-over-form doctrine, the general anti-abuse rule under Article 47 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, the beneficial ownership test, and the Principal Purpose Test under the OECD Multilateral Instrument (MLI). A Hong Kong intermediate holding company must have its own premises, employees, and substantive decision-making functions; its core income-generating activities (CIGA) must justify the benefits claimed under the DTA. Absence of economic substance may lead to denial of treaty benefits, reclassification of income, and imposition of Chinese WHT at the statutory 10%.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Corporate Income Tax Return

Statutory sanction: Article 63 of the Tax Collection and Administration Law: recovery of unpaid tax plus daily surcharge plus a penalty of 50% to 500% of the amount underpaid. Article 201 of the Criminal Law: tax evasion crime subject to imprisonment up to seven years and fines if the evasion amount is substantial and exceeds 10% of the tax payable.

Director personal liability: Under Article 57 of the Tax Collection and Administration Law, the responsible directors may be personally liable for the unpaid tax if they willfully disregarded corporate formalities. A court may pierce the corporate veil and order restitution of assets. Directors may be placed on a no-exit/blacklist restricting travel, their personal bank accounts may be frozen, and, upon criminal conviction, they face imprisonment of up to seven years plus monetary fines.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation and Thin Capitalization

Statutory sanction: Under Article 41 and Article 46 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, tax authorities may make a special tax adjustment and impose interest on the adjusted tax. If fraud is proven, Article 63 of the Tax Collection and Administration Law applies with penalties of 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax; for severe cases, Article 201 of the Criminal Law may impose imprisonment.

Director personal liability: Directors who approved the sham transactions may be held jointly and severally liable under civil liability rules; they may be prohibited from leaving China (exit ban), have personal assets seized to cover tax and interest, and if the adjustment is treated as intentional tax evasion, criminal prosecution with imprisonment up to seven years.
Treaty Benefit Abuse / Shell Company Arrangement

Statutory sanction: Under Article 47 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, the tax authority may apply a general anti-avoidance rule and re-determine the amount of taxable income. Under the DTA limitation of benefits rules and the OECD Multilateral Instrument Principal Purpose Test, the dividend treatment is denied and the statutory 10% withholding tax is collected. The State Taxation Administration may also impose a penalty under Article 63 of the Tax Collection and Administration Law if underpayment resulted from false declarations.

Director personal liability: Directors of both the Hong Kong shell and the Chinese WFOE may face administrative fines; directors who certified false beneficial ownership statements may be subject to criminal liability for fraudulent evasion. Asset tracing may pierce the corporate veil to recover the additional WHT and penalties; travel bans and blacklisting can be applied; criminal imprisonment of up to three years for tax evasion offenses may be imposed.
