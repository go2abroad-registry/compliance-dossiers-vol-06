Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A pure virtual office or commercial mailbox is generally rejected for a newly incorporated WFOE. SAMR requires a fixed, verifiable business address in the registration locality evidenced by a lease contract or property title certificate. The local tax bureau expects the same address to support invoice issuance, payroll reporting, VAT filings, and audit visits. Banks applying AML/KYC rules will reject a mail-only address because it lacks operational substance and physical control. However, a licensed co-working space or serviced office with a dedicated numbered room, signage, meeting facilities, and a real lease may be accepted by SAMR and tax authorities at their discretion, provided the WFOE can demonstrate actual occupancy, access control, and business records at that address. For an online retail ISIC 4791 WFOE, banks also expect a warehouse or logistics arrangement consistent with 3D printer consumables distribution, although a separate warehouse is not required at the registered office.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a managed float with a current-account convertibility regime and a conditionally open capital account. The WFOE is a domestic legal person; its operational receipts in CNY from domestic e-commerce sales are usable within China, but all cross-border payments, shareholder loans, dividends, and export proceeds are subject to SAFE registration, bank verification of authenticity, and transaction-level documentation. Current-account payments such as import purchases, technology licensing royalties, and service fees are generally convertible once the bank has verified the underlying commercial contracts, invoices, tax payment certificates, and customs declarations. Dividend remittance to the Hong Kong parent is capital-account related: it requires audited annual financial statements, a formal board dividend resolution, PRC corporate income tax settlement or withholding tax receipt, and a SAFE profit remittance compliance check; the funds are then converted from CNY to HKD or USD and sent overseas. Equity capital under the WFOE's registered capital must be injected through a registered FDI foreign exchange capital account, not through ordinary CNY accounts. The negative-list principle applies to the sector: online retail of 3D printers and resin filaments is not a restricted foreign investment sector under the current Access Negative List, so full foreign ownership is permitted. Nevertheless, capital-account transactions require real transactions supporting every settlement, and banks maintain strict limits on capital account settlements to fixed asset purchases, operating expenses, or trade payments within the WFOE's business scope. Any offshore funding or repayment of cross-border loans requires separate SAFE debt registration and a quota-controlled arrangement.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export proceeds from overseas sales of 3D printers and consumables must be repatriated to China within 180 calendar days from the date of export customs declaration unless SAFE has approved overseas retention. There is no mandatory surrender-to-CNY ratio currently applied; the WFOE may retain foreign currency in an onshore export proceeds foreign exchange account or voluntarily sell the proceeds to a designated bank. Every receipt must be matched against customs, logistics, and order data in SAFE's cross-border payment verification system, and any unrepatriated balance requires documentary justification.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China, functioning under the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Not viable as a treasury settlement wall and strictly supplemental at best. Fintech EMI platforms and offshore virtual banks may facilitate collection of card payments from overseas retail buyers through Stripe or Shopify Payments, but they are not licensed PRC banks, cannot maintain a WFOE's RMB basic account, cannot accept SAFE FDI capital registration, and cannot perform onshore CNY/HKD conversion or foreign exchange settlement. All legally compliant cross-border receipts and payments must clear through a licensed PRC designated foreign exchange bank. Therefore the WFOE must maintain primary banking with a Tier-1 domestic commercial bank and treat any EMI as a front-end payment aggregator whose funds are ultimately settled into the onshore bank account.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The Hong Kong parent must remit equity capital to the WFOE's dedicated SAFE-registered FDI foreign exchange capital account at a designated Tier-1 PRC bank using an international SWIFT MT103 wire. The remittance instruction must specify the SAFE FDI registration code and the standard balance-of-payments purpose code for foreign direct investment equity injection, and the bank will validate the transaction in SAFE's digital capital project management system. After receipt of the foreign currency, the WFOE may settle the capital into RMB only after the bank verifies the intended use; settlement funds must be used for legitimate operating expenses, procurement of inventory, salaries, rent, or other business-scope payments. A PRC-registered accountant is required to issue a capital verification report confirming the subscribed capital has been lawfully injected before the bank releases full to the settlement account. No part of the capital may be used for equity investment into another Chinese entity, short-term securities investment, or repayment of unlawful loans outside the capital account control rules.For overseas supplier payments, the WFOE should use SWIFT MT103 direct remittances with underlying purchase contracts, invoices, packing lists, and customs declarations where goods are cross-border. For larger supply orders, irrevocable documentary letters of credit (MT700) can be opened through the settlement bank to protect against vendor performance risk; documentary collections (MT400/MT500 series) are permissible for open-account or trusted-trade transactions but offer less protection. Domestic inbound payments for e-commerce sales in China will clear via CNAPS through the RMB basic deposit account; offshore sales collected by Stripe or Shopify Payments will be swept from the payment processor into the WFOE's onshore export proceeds FX account after settling the processor's merchant agreement and then converted to RMB at spot or forward. Mandatory Audit Threshold Mandatory Audit for All Entities

Turnover: Under the Companies Ordinance (Cap.622), every Hong Kong company must be audited unless it is a 'small private company' (or a member of a 'small group') qualifying for statutory audit exemption. A company qualifies only if it satisfies at least two of three size tests for both the current and immediately preceding financial year: (a) annual revenue not exceeding HKD 10,000,000; (b) total balance-sheet assets not exceeding HKD 10,000,000; (c) average number of employees not exceeding 50. If it fails at least two of those tests, independent CPA audit is legally mandatory. Audit exemption is also unavailable for listed companies, licensed banking, insurance, money-lending, securities or futures firms, MPF trustees and similar regulated entities, and is overridden if shareholders holding at least 10% of the voting shares require an audit.
