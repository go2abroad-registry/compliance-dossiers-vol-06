Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to a WFOE limited liability company engaged in online retail under ISIC 4791, and no blocked escrow account is required. Under the 2023 PRC Company Law, the subscribed capital must be paid in full within five years from establishment unless a shorter term is fixed in the Articles of Association. Contributions may be in cash or in non-cash assets permitted by law. The registered capital amount should be commercially reasonable and consistent with the projected business scale, banking requirements, tax filings, and annual reporting obligations.

Funding & unlock path: Not Applicable as an industrial civil-defense permit for the pure online retail business. The office or light commercial premises must still comply with the Fire Protection Law of the People's Republic of China and the workplace safety obligations under the Work Safety Law; if the leased premises are required to have a fire safety inspection or filing, the landlord or occupier shall obtain the Fire Safety Inspection Certificate from the local Fire and Rescue Department. In any future battery/solar panel warehouse, a Fire Safety Approval (civil defense clearance) would be mandatory, including compliance with lithium battery storage codes, separation distances, extinguishing systems, and employee occupational hazard notification under the Occupational Disease Prevention and Control Law.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; electronic clearance and self-service registration portal: the Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax) under the STA, accessible via provincial State Tax Service platforms with full integration into the Golden Tax-IV digital monitoring system.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the wholly foreign-owned enterprise (WFOE) as a limited liability company with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) and obtain the business license containing the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC). Step 2: Within thirty calendar days of the business license issuance, complete tax registration information confirmation through the Electronic Tax Bureau; the USCC is the statutory Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) and is also used for enterprise income tax, VAT, stamp duty, and withholding tax accounts. Step 3: Register the foreign dominant management facts and bank account details; for general taxpayer status, submit the VAT general taxpayer registration if annual taxable sales are expected to exceed CNY 5,000,000 or elect voluntary registration if annual sales are below that threshold. Step 4: Complete the export tax rebate (exemption) recordal, i.e., export tax refund qualification filing, in the e-Tax export tax module before claiming zero-rating. Step 5: Register as a foreign trade operator with the local Department of Commerce and file an enterprise customs registration/declaration filing with the competent Customs district. Step 6: Complete trade-in-goods foreign exchange operations qualification with a designated foreign exchange bank under SAFE's trade-in-goods truth-verification system. No additional physical TIN card is issued; the business license and tax registration information confirmation record are the official tax identity evidence.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None for entity level classification. The PRC Enterprise Income Tax Law does not recognize check-the-box or hybrid mismatch elections; a WFOE is automatically a Chinese tax resident enterprise. In VAT, the only election available is choice of small-scale taxpayer classification for annual taxable sales below CNY 5,000,000 or voluntary general taxpayer status, subject to accounting capability and input-output invoice management requirements.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

CNY 5,000,000 in annual taxable sales is the threshold above which VAT general taxpayer registration becomes mandatory; below CNY 5,000,000 the WFOE may retain small-scale taxpayer status. Irrespective of turnover, tax registration must be completed within 30 days of the business license issuance, so the economic threshold does not relieve a newly formed WFOE from initial tax registration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A registered VAT general taxpayer WFOE engaged in the e-commerce export of portable battery generators and solar panels may claim zero-rating where: (i) the goods are actually exported from the customs territory through a customs export declaration under the applicable e-commerce supervision code, such as 9610 for cross-border e-commerce retail export, 9710 for B2B direct export, or 9810 for overseas warehouse export; (ii) the underlying export order, logistics record, and payment flow can be matched on a one-to-one basis; (iii) the goods are new, materially conform to the documented product description and customs commodity classification, and are sold to an overseas buyer outside mainland China; (iv) the export proceeds are repatriated into a China-designated bank in foreign currency or RMB under an approved cross-border settlement arrangement; and (v) the input VAT has been lawfully certified. Qualifying offshore digital services are not relevant to this goods-export WFOE.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Customs Declaration Form for Exported Goods) containing the export supervision code and the verified product HS classification
  • Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, cross-border vehicle manifest, or international consignment note proving physical departure from the PRC customs territory
  • Export invoice (commercial invoice) issued by the WFOE to the overseas customer
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or bank export proceeds receipt confirmation showing receipt of foreign exchange; for e-commerce platform sales, the payment settlement report and foreign exchange settlement record must be provided
  • Export contract or electronic purchase order and payment terms
  • VAT special invoices of domestic suppliers for purchased inventory and input services
  • Packing list, logistics waybill, and e-commerce platform transaction data supporting the three-flow consistency of order flow, logistics flow, and payment flow
  • SAFE trade-in-goods external payment/collection declaration record and the export tax rebate electronic declaration file

The Golden Tax-IV system requires real-time transmission of special VAT invoice details to the STA invoice verification platform. Invoices must be issued through the official e-Tax electronic invoice service or an STA-approved tax control U-Key device. Comprehensive digitalized electronic invoices carry a statutory invoice number, QR code, and tax-control metadata; red-letter invoice substitution is only permitted through the e-Tax system. Enterprises must match electronic invoice data with customs export declarations, export proceeds data, and logistics data before export tax refund claims; anti-fraud screening applies to invoice frequency, counterparty risk, commodity tax classifications, price anomalies, and transport-geolocation consistency.

Export tax refund claims are subject to the STA's classified export-tax-rebate management categories I-IV, which determine the intensity of document review and on-site inspection. If the STA or customs determines that the zero-rating was based on fictitious export orders, false customs declarations, recycled goods, falsified transport or exchange settlement documents, or failure to repatriate proceeds within the required 180-day period unless duly registered as an exception, the refund/zero-rating will be reversed, the underlying input credits will be clawed back, and overdue interest will be imposed under the Tax Collection and Administration Law. Administrative penalties may range from 50 percent to 500 percent of the tax involved; the WFOE may be disqualified from export tax rebate treatment for a period of 6 months to 3 years; and in severe cases, criminal liability for false export tax refunds under Articles 201 and 204 of the Criminal Law can lead to fines and imprisonment. Retrospective review periods extend to 3 years for ordinary adjustments, 5 years for special circumstances, and an unlimited period for deliberate evasion, fraud, or withholding on non-resident payments.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File is mandatory when annual intercompany transactions exceed: RMB 20 million for transfers of tangible assets, RMB 100 million for financial asset transfers, RMB 100 million for intangible asset transfers, or RMB 40 million for other related-party transactions (including services). Master File is required if the total related-party revenue exceeds RMB 1 billion.

Economic substance scope: Mainland China has no standalone Economic Substance Regulations, but the WFOE must demonstrate physical presence (office, staff, management decisions) in China to rebut any sham or agency characterisation; the Core Income-Generating Activities (CIGA) concept applies to the Hong Kong parent for treaty claims, and China's de facto management test and General Anti-Avoidance Rule (CIT Law Article 47) require that intercompany transactions reflect real economic functions and risk assumption.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion through Revenue Understatement

Statutory sanction: Tax Collection Administration Law (TCAL) Article 63: confiscation of illegal gains, order to repay the evaded tax, late-payment surcharge of 0.05% per day, and a civil fine between 50% and 500% of the evaded tax amount. Criminal Law Article 201: if the evaded tax exceeds RMB 50,000 and constitutes more than 10% of total tax payable, imprisonment up to 3 years; if the amount is huge and exceeds 30%, imprisonment up to 7 years, plus fines and forfeiture.

Director personal liability: Directors who direct or participate in the offshore diversion are personally and jointly liable for the tax deficiency, fines, and unpaid WHT; the court may pierce the corporate veil and attach personal assets of the legal representative and CFO; a travel ban (exit denial) is imposed under TCAL Article 44; criminal conviction can result in 3–7 years’ imprisonment and debarment from holding directorships.
Transfer Pricing Misreporting and Lack of Documentation

Statutory sanction: CIT Law Article 41 (arm’s length principle) and Article 48 (special tax adjustment); STA Announcement 2016/42 issuances: the tax authority may recharacterise the transaction and impose an interest charge on the additional tax equal to the benchmark lending rate plus 5 percentage points. If inaccurate documentation is deemed a false submission, TCAL penalties of 50%–5x unpaid tax may apply, and under Criminal Law Article 201 fraudulent means to evade tax are prosecutable.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign or approve false transfer pricing documentation are exposed to personal liability for the additional tax and interest if the corporate veil is lifted; travel restrictions and asset freezes can be ordered; a director who intentionally causes under-reporting is similarly exposed to criminal liability under Article 201, with imprisonment up to 7 years in egregious cases.
Treaty Abuse / Shell Company Lack of Beneficial Ownership

Statutory sanction: STA Announcement 2018/9 (Measures for the Administration of Non-resident Enterprises Enjoying Tax Treaty Treatment): denial of treaty benefits and recovery of the additional withholding tax (e.g., from 5% to 10%) plus late-payment interest; General Anti-Avoidance Rule (CIT Law Article 47) allows re-computation of the income as belonging to the Chinese WFOE and re-assesses tax. Civil fines under TCAL Article 44 for fraudulent reporting and issuance of false certifications may also apply.

Director personal liability: Directors who orchestrate the shell arrangement are personally liable for the under-withheld tax if the structure is disregarded and the corporate veil is pierced; a blacklisting (travel ban) and bank seizure order can be issued; criminal penalties for fraudulent use of tax treaties can extend to imprisonment up to 3 years for evidence forgery under Criminal Law Article 280, plus restitution of the full tax shortfall.
