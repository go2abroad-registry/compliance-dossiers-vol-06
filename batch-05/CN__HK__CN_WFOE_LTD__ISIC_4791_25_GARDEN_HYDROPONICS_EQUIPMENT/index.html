E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE) with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) and obtain the Legal Business License incorporating the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC). Step 2: Complete tax registration through the STA Electronic Tax Bureau within 30 days of business license issuance; the USCC is activated as the taxpayer identification number (TIN), replacing any separate tax registration code. Step 3: File the VAT taxpayer status registration (general taxpayer or small-scale taxpayer) and, if exporting, register for the export tax rebate mechanism. Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For goods: a VAT general taxpayer exporting physical goods from China customs territory may apply zero percent VAT and claim refund of attributable input VAT, provided the export is substantiated by genuine customs export declarations, the goods physically leave China, payment is received from overseas in foreign currency, repatriation occurs within the statutory window, and no export-fraud warning indicators are present. For qualifying offshore digital services: zero-rating is available where the services are rendered to non-resident customers, wholly consumed outside China, and paid in foreign currency; for the current garden hydroponics equipment online retail scope, the physical goods export route applies.

  • Bill of Lading (or Air Waybill / multimodal transport document evidencing export shipment)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or bank foreign exchange receipt evidencing repatriation of export proceeds to China

China operates the Golden Tax Phase IV digital compliance framework and the fully digitalised electronic invoice (DEI) platform under the State Taxation Administration. VAT special invoices and zero-rated export invoices must be issued through the STA tax-control system (Tax UKey or e-invoice platform) with real-time upload, automated fiscal clearance checks, e-invoice serial number verification, red-letter invoice controls, and anti-fraud digital risk monitoring. High-volume e-commerce taxpayers are expected to use system-to-system API connectivity. The China International Trade Single Window interlinks customs export declarations with tax invoice data for export rebate validation.

The STA, the General Administration of Customs and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange perform cross-agency matching of customs declarations, VAT invoice data, logistics documents and foreign exchange receipts. If export zero-rating is claimed without substantiation or is fraudulent, the STA may refuse or claw back the VAT exemption/input credit/refund, impose statutory late-payment surcharges and administrative fines in the range of 50% to five times the tax or refunded amount as applicable, suspend or revoke export tax rebate qualification, and refer the case for criminal prosecution for export tax refund fraud under the Criminal Law of the PRC. Retroactive tax reassessment and recovery periods apply under the Tax Collection and Administration Law, with extended periods for deliberate concealment or fraudulent conduct.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Mainland China maintains current-account convertibility under Article VIII of the IMF Articles of Agreement; as a result, bona fide trade settlements, service payments, dividends, repatriation of profits and repayment of trade-related loans can in principle be settled with the bank upon documentation review. The capital account remains regulated: a wholly foreign-owned enterprise (WFOE) may only receive equity contribution or shareholder loans after obtaining a SAFE FDI registration and opening a designated foreign currency capital account; the RMB settlement of the capital proceeds is subject to capital-account settlement restrictions requiring the funds to be used within the approved business scope and Negative List constraints. Profit remittance is permitted annually after an external audit and tax settlement, with board resolutions and the SAFE/BOP reporting code; however, any deemed dividend distribution must not prejudice creditors, and banks may request vouchers for expenses incurred before conversion. The result is an asymmetric regime in which current-account convertibility is broad but subject to transaction authenticity screening, while capital-account mobility remains conditional, requiring pre-registration, dedicated accounts, and post-event reporting. A Hong Kong parent is not insulated from these rules when injecting funds into the mainland WFOE; the WFOE is treated as a Chinese resident legal person for FX purposes.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the SAFE Goods Trade Foreign Exchange Administration framework, export proceeds from overseas e-commerce sales, including Stripe/PayPal settlements received by the WFOE's licensed acquiring bank or via a cross-border payment institution, must be repatriated to a mainland China current account through a designated authorized bank within 90 calendar days from the date of export shipment, i.e., the customs declaration or bill of lading date that triggers the SAFE monitoring point. There is no statutory mandatory conversion ratio: the WFOE may retain 100% of the export foreign exchange in a foreign-currency current account or may sell the full amount to the bank at market rates; 0% is legally required to be surrendered to the central bank. The receiving bank must submit export collection verification data to the SAFE Goods Trade Monitoring System, and any overdue balance exceeding 90 days requires an explanation and documentary support, including export contract, invoices, and customs declaration, to avoid a compliance violation.

Central bank supervision: The exchange-control authority for the mainland WFOE is the People's Bank of China (PBOC), delegated to the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) and its local branches; the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) is the banking regulator for the Hong Kong treasury parent but applies no foreign exchange controls in Hong Kong SAR.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Very low. Under the PRC Administrative Measures for Payment Services and SAFE Circulars, an offshore digital EMI such as Revolut Business, Wise Business, or WorldFirst is not licensed to open a domestic RMB/FX settlement account in mainland China or to execute SAFE cargo-trade verification on behalf of a WFOE. These platforms may function as payment gateway signposts or foreign-currency virtual accounts in Hong Kong, but the WFOE must feed all cross-border proceeds into a mainland authorized bank within the statutory collection period. Any assumption that the WFOE can use solely an offshore EMI as its operational bank is legally untenable and would fail the bank's KYC review. The Hong Kong parent may use an EMI for incidental operational funds only when the EMI holds a Hong Kong Money Service Operator (MSO) licence or banking licence from the HKMA, but this must not absorb the underlying export receipts without onshore repatriation. Stripe and PayPal are not EMIs; they are payment processers whose funding flow must ultimately settle into a SAFE-monitored domestic banking channel.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The Hong Kong parent initiates a SWIFT MT103 from its HKD or USD account at a licensed HK bank to the WFOE's SAFE-designated foreign currency capital account in mainland China, referencing the WFOE business registration number and the SAFE BOP purpose code for FDI capital contribution, such as 621011 or the code mapped by the receiving domestic bank, together with the FDI registration reference. The receiving bank performs capital verification, enters the inbound FX transaction into the SAFE monitoring system, and may settle the capital into RMB only after all documentation is approved. The capital verification milestone requires submission of the SAFE FDI registration notice, a board resolution authorizing the capital call, source-of-funds bank statements from the Hong Kong investor, and a Chinese accountant's certification of the subscribed capital.For supplier payments and operating expenses, the WFOE can issue SWIFT MT103 in USD or EUR for ordinary open-account invoices, or in RMB via CIPS when the counterparty accepts RMB. For large-volume procurement of garden hydroponics components or raw materials, use commercial letters of credit at sight and documentary collections such as D/P or D/A, backed by full marine bills of lading. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Very low. Under the PRC Administrative Measures for Payment Services and SAFE Circulars, an offshore digital EMI such as Revolut Business, Wise Business, or WorldFirst is not licensed to open a domestic RMB/FX settlement account in mainland China or to execute SAFE cargo-trade verification on behalf of a WFOE. These platforms may function as payment gateway signposts or foreign-currency virtual accounts in Hong Kong, but the WFOE must feed all cross-border proceeds into a mainland authorized bank within the statutory collection period. Any assumption that the WFOE can use solely an offshore EMI as its operational bank is legally untenable and would fail the bank's KYC review. Stripe and PayPal are not EMIs; they are payment processers whose funding flow must ultimately settle into a SAFE-monitored domestic banking channel.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File preparation is mandatory where annual related-party transaction categories exceed the statutory thresholds: RMB 200 million for tangible goods transfers, RMB 100 million for intangible property transfers, RMB 100 million for financing, and RMB 40 million for intercompany services or a combination of other controlled transactions. Master File preparation is required where the multinational group has a PRC entity in its chain and group-wide related-party revenue reaches the thresholds stipulated by SAT Announcement No. 42 of 2016; in addition, annual related-party transaction reporting must be filed whenever controlled relationships and transactions exist, irrespective of amount.

Economic substance scope: Both PRC and Hong Kong substance rules are engaged. The Hong Kong parent must satisfy Hong Kong's Economic Substance Requirements and demonstrate that its Core Income-Generating Activities, including management decisions, procurement, financing, and contractual negotiations, occur in Hong Kong with real employees, premises and records. In China, treaty relief and arm's length pricing presuppose economic substance and beneficial ownership; a Hong Kong shell that merely receives dividends or claims treaty rates will be attacked under PRC General Anti-Avoidance Rules and the beneficial ownership doctrine, and the Hong Kong parent may also fail Hong Kong's territorial source and ESR tests.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Transfer Pricing Fraud / Artificial Profit Shifting

Statutory sanction: PRC Corporate Income Tax Law Articles 41 and 48; State Taxation Administration Announcement No. 42 of 2016; Tax Collection and Administration Law Article 63. The tax authority may re-determine taxable income using the arm's length principle, impose a daily late-payment surcharge of 0.05%, and penalise underpaid tax by 50%-500% of the amount evaded. The adjustment may also trigger interest on the additional tax and recharacterisation of the Hong Kong parent's role.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly approved fabricated invoices, false transfer pricing studies, or sham service agreements are jointly and severally liable for the unpaid tax, surcharges and penalties through piercing of the corporate veil under PRC Company Law. Criminal tax evasion under PRC Criminal Law Article 201 may lead to imprisonment of up to seven years, fines, asset confiscation, and an exit-control travel ban until the tax liability is settled.
Withholding Tax Evasion on Outbound Profit Repatriation

Statutory sanction: PRC Corporate Income Tax Law Articles 37 and 38; Tax Collection and Administration Law Articles 37, 55 and 69. The withholding agent is liable to be fined 50%-300% of the unwithheld amount; the tax authority may recover the unpaid tax from the non-resident payee, impose the daily late-payment surcharge, and deny treaty benefits for abusive arrangements.

Director personal liability: Directors who instructed or allowed profit remittance without tax clearance are jointly and severally liable for the withheld tax, penalties and surcharges; they may be subject to civil recovery of company assets, personal asset attachment, exit blacklisting and travel restrictions, and criminal prosecution for tax evasion or fraudulent misrepresentation if the non-payment is willful and material, attracting imprisonment as permitted by PRC law.
Shell Company / Treaty Abuse and Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: The Mainland and Hong Kong SAR Double Taxation Arrangement benefits will be denied; PRC General Anti-Avoidance Rules under CIT Law Article 47 permit recharacterisation of the arrangement. Hong Kong's Economic Substance Regulations impose penalties for failure to satisfy economic substance requirements, including HKD 50,000 for non-compliance and HKD 100,000 plus continuing daily penalties for default; false declarations can lead to criminal sanctions in Hong Kong.

Director personal liability: Directors of the Hong Kong shell may be personally subject to Economic Substance Regulation penalties and disqualification orders in Hong Kong; in China, the corporate veil is pierced where the shell is a sham or alter ego, rendering shareholders and directors personally liable for unpaid taxes, penalties and surcharges. If treaty abuse is used to evade substantial Chinese tax, criminal referral may follow, with asset seizure, blacklisting, travel restrictions, and imprisonment for the directors involved.
