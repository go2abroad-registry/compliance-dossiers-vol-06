Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None. No statutory minimum registered capital applies to a wholly foreign-owned enterprise engaged in ISIC 5210 refrigerated warehousing, but the registered capital must be subscribed in the Articles of Association and fully paid within five years of establishment under the 2023 Company Law. No blocked escrow account is required by SAMR for this low-risk cold-chain logistics activity, although a legally compliant bank account must be used for payroll, supplier payments, and tax transactions.

Funding & unlock path: Fire approval per the Fire Protection Law of the PRC: the cold storage must obtain a Fire Safety Acceptance Inspection Certificate before operation, including automatic sprinklers, gas extinguishing systems, temperature-sensing alarms, and fire compartmentation compliant with GB 50016. Civil defense structural filing: the owner must submit structural load calculations and rack anchorage certificates to confirm that the floors withstand the designed pallet loads; a Structural Load Inspection Report must be certified by a registered structural engineer. Occupational safety clearance: the site is to be registered in the State Work Safety Supervision system; ammonia monitoring, personal protective equipment, cold-storage emergency exit drills, and refrigeration plant risk assessments are mandatory. For pharmaceutical storage, the local drug administration will verify temperature mapping at 2 to 8 degrees Celsius and minus 20 degrees Celsius with qualified monitoring devices.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For physical goods exports, the WFOE may claim 0% VAT export exemption/refund only if the goods are actually declared to and leave Chinese customs territory, sold to a non-resident buyer, invoiced in compliance with VAT rules, and the export proceeds are received and repatriated through banking channels. For services, zero-rating applies only to cross-border services listed in STA zero-rating and VAT-exemption circulars; cold-chain warehousing for perishable pharmaceuticals supplied to overseas customers is not automatically zero-rated unless the service is part of a qualifying export logistics service chain and is supported by the statutory documentation. Qualifying offshore digital/automated services are zero-rated only where the customer is outside China, the service is performed outside China, and there is no domestic consumption or Chinese fixed establishment involved.

  • Bill of Lading or air/rail waybill issued by the carrier evidencing physical export
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Bank Foreign Exchange Receipt/Export Proceeds Confirmation evidencing repatriation of the export sales proceeds
  • Export VAT rebate application file maintained in the e-Tax export rebate module

Mandatory use of VAT special invoices and ordinary invoices through STA-controlled issuance systems. The STA Fully Digitalized Electronic Invoice (FDED) platform requires real-time transmission of invoice data via taxpayer e-Tax API integration, unique invoice codes, anti-fraud digital stamps, and automatic reconciliation with the Golden Tax Phase IV risk-monitoring system. Paper invoices, where still permitted, must be issued via tax-control enabled devices. All invoice data is cleared in real time against taxpayer registrations, commodity/service descriptions, and transaction counterparties; any mismatch triggers automatic compliance alerts and suspension of invoice issuance.

The STA and China Customs cross-validate export declarations, invoice data, logistics manifests, and bank FX receipts. If zero-rating cannot be substantiated, the claimed VAT exemption/refund is retrospectively clawed back, output VAT at the applicable rate (6% for services, 13% for goods unless a lower export-transaction rate applies) is assessed, late payment interest accrues, and administrative penalties under the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection (0.5 to 5 times the tax shortfall) may apply. Deliberate export-rebate fraud is prosecuted under the Criminal Law with criminal fines and imprisonment for responsible persons.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The People's Republic of China maintains a strict foreign exchange control regime under the State Administration of Foreign Exchange. The current account is generally free for trade and service transactions, subject to a genuine transaction background check. Capital account transactions, including foreign direct investment (FDI) and outbound loans, require registration and approval. For a WFOE, capital contributions from abroad must be registered under SAFE's FDI rules and are held in special capital accounts. The RMB is not fully convertible on the capital account; however, current account items such as dividend remittances are permissible once the enterprise has paid corporate income tax and obtained audit reports. There are no mandatory surrender requirements for export proceeds; companies can retain foreign exchange earnings or settle them at market rates. Profit repatriation to the Hong Kong parent involves a tax clearance certificate, audit report, and a resolution on profit distribution, then a bank verifies and executes the remittance. Overall, the system is administered with high documentation compliance, and any deviation can freeze corporate liquidity. Therefore treasury operations must be meticulously aligned with SAFE timelines and supporting document packets.

Export proceeds surrender rules

As of the latest regulations (Circular 30/2019 and subsequent reforms), China imposes no mandatory conversion ratio for export proceeds. Export receipts may be retained in foreign currency accounts or converted into RMB at the enterprise's discretion. Nevertheless, all export proceeds must be repatriated to the country within the statutory timeframe—typically 30 days after customs clearance for goods; for service exports or warehousing receipts, the applicable period is 90 days from the date of invoicing. Banks are required to verify the authenticity of the underlying transaction through cross-border payment reporting and customs data matching. If the entity fails to repatriate within the deadline, SAFE may impose fines or restrict future FX purchases.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) and State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility: Low. The PRC does not recognize a foreign fintech EMI license as a substitute for a domestic banking license. Cross-border digital banks such as Wise, Revolut, or Airwallex cannot serve as the primary settlement vehicle for a PRC WFOE. Under the Regulations on the Administration of Remitting Foreign Exchange and the Measures for the Management of Online Payment, only domestically licensed payment institutions (e.g., Alipay, Tenpay) can process RMB settlements. Foreign EMIs may partner with a local bank, but they cannot provide an on-shore account number, and their services are not permitted for receiving foreign direct investment or paying statutory trade debts. Therefore, the WFOE must rely on local Tier-1 banks for all treasury functions. EMIs may be used incidentally for non-core, low-value cross-border disbursements, but the legal risk outweighs operational benefit.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound investment from the HK parent must be wired via SWIFT MT103 to the WFOE's foreign currency capital account opened at a designated PRC bank. The remittance instruction must clearly state the payment purpose as 'Equity Injection for WFOE Registration' and include the FDI capital contribution reference number (Foreign Exchange Registration Certificate) issued by SAFE. After receipt, the bank performs electronic verification against SAFE's FDI system. The capital can only be settled into RMB for the company's operational expenses under the real demand principle and must not be used for equity investment, loan repayment, or non-operational expenditures without specific approval. For amounts above USD 500,000, the bank may request additional due diligence including the preceding source and the ultimate beneficial owner.Outbound payments for goods or services (e.g., purchase of refrigeration machinery, spare parts, technology licensing) should be executed via SWIFT MT103 for direct remittances, or through documentary collections (CAD/DP) and commercial letters of credit for higher-value transactions. The WFOE must provide the underlying contract, invoice, Bill of Lading or airway bill, and customs declaration. For service trade payments, a tax payment certificate and Form A0105 for SAFE reporting is required. Dividends to the HK parent are treated as current-account transactions and require an audited financial statement, tax clearance, and a board resolution, then processed via SWIFT MT103. Mandatory Audit Threshold Small Company Audit Exemption Regime

Turnover: Under sections 359 and 360 of the Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622), a Hong Kong private company may be exempt from a statutory audit only if it qualifies as a small private company or a small member of a group and passes a unanimous shareholder resolution waiving the audit. To qualify as small, the company must satisfy at least two of the following three criteria for the current financial year: annual turnover not exceeding HK$10,000,000; total assets not exceeding HK$10,000,000; and average number of employees not exceeding 50. A company that fails the small-company criteria, or that does not adopt the waiver resolution, must appoint one or more certified public accountants registered under the Professional Accountants Ordinance to perform the audit. Even when audit exemption applies, the company must prepare financial statements that present a true and fair view in accordance with Part 9 of the Companies Ordinance.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% statutory withholding tax on dividends paid by a Chinese resident enterprise to a non-resident enterprise; under Article 10 of the China-Hong Kong Double Tax Arrangement, the rate is reduced to 5% where the beneficial owner is a Hong Kong resident company directly holding at least 25% of the share capital of the Chinese payer.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Annual corporate income tax return must be submitted within five months after the fiscal year-end (for calendar year filers, by 31 May); quarterly pre-payments are due within 15 days after each quarter-end; requests for extension to August 31 are rarely granted and only for special bookkeeping emergencies with advance approval.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: RMB 200 million of aggregate tangible property related-party transactions triggers the Local File; RMB 100 million aggregate intangible property or financial asset related-party transactions triggers the Local File; RMB 40 million of aggregate other related-party service or leasing transactions triggers the Local File; a Master File is mandatory if the aggregate annual related-party transactions exceed RMB 1 billion, or where certain thresholds of assets and sales are met. The WFOE must prepare contemporaneous documentation within 15 days after the tax authority issues a transfer-pricing investigation notice, and the documentation must include a business analysis, a functional analysis, industry benchmarks, and selection of the tested party.

Economic substance scope: The WFOE itself, as a resident enterprise operating an operating business in China, must possess physical premises, cold-chain employees, logistics contracts and actual daily management in mainland China; a structural absence of these attributes triggers the GAAR under EIT Law Art. 47 and Special Tax Adjustment Notice No. 2 (2009). For the HK parent, the Hong Kong Economic Substance Regulations (Inland Revenue (Amendment) Ordinance 2018) apply: a pure equity-holding parent whose only income is dividends from the China WFOE must satisfy a reduced substance test by maintaining at least one HK office and one part-time HK employee, and filing the Economic Substance Return (ESR) annually. Core Income-Generating Activities (CIGA) of a cold-chain holding company are the board meetings and control functions performed in Hong Kong; to be eligible for the 5% dividend treaty rate, the HK parent must evidence that these CIGA are actually in Hong Kong.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Shell company fraudulent DTA claim

Statutory sanction: The Chinese tax authority applies the general anti-avoidance rule under EIT Law Art. 47, re-characterises the HK parent as not being the beneficial owner, and retroactively denies the treaty benefit; it demands the 5% under-withheld amount plus 50% penalty under PRC Tax Collection Administration Law Art. 63; the HK authorities may also issue an assessment or penalty for failing to file the Economic Substance Return under the Inland Revenue (Amendment) Ordinance (Cap. 112) Section 80(2), together with a fine of HK$10,000 and possible additional fines of three times the avoided tax.

Director personal liability: Directors of both the WFOE and the HK parent are personally liable to restore the unpaid withholding tax as civil restitution; the director is placed on the tax blacklist, triggering travel bans and denial of exit until the tax is settled; if the fraud exceeds the threshold of RMB 50,000, criminal prosecution under PRC Criminal Law Art. 201 applies, carrying up to 7 years imprisonment, a fine of at least RMB 10,000, and confiscation of personal assets.
Artificial transfer-pricing shifting of profits to Hong Kong

Statutory sanction: The tax authority applies EIT Law Art. 41 and Art. 46, plus SAT Public Notice No. 2 (2009) and No. 42 (2016), to adjust taxable income by adding back the excessive royalty and interest; the ineligible interest portion over the 2:1 debt-equity safe harbour is also disallowed; the taxpayer is charged late interest at the benchmark rate plus 5 percentage points, plus a 50% to fivefold fine for fraudulent underpayment of tax under the Tax Collection Administration Law Art. 63.

Director personal liability: Directors who signed or approved the false related-party contracts are jointly and severally liable for civil restitution; personal bank accounts are frozen, departure restrictions are placed, and any use of false fapiao to inflate expenses attracts a criminal liability for issuance of false invoice under PRC Criminal Law Art. 205 with up to 7 years imprisonment and a fine of RMB 500,000 or more; the company and the responsible officer are collectively fined.
Improper loss carryforward and non-recognition of deemed dividend

Statutory sanction: Under EIT Law Art. 18, losses may only be carried forward for five years and only the accurate proven balance is allowed; the tax authority recalculates taxable income under Art. 49, imposes a penalty of 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax under Tax Collection Administration Law Art. 63, and additionally charges a daily late fee; if a deemed dividend or constructive loan benefit is found, the WFOE must withhold 10% on the deemed dividend and if the reduced DTA is claimed, the HK parent's lack of substance is flagged by the INR (HK) as an abuse.

Director personal liability: The director who certified the fraudulent financial statements is personally liable for restitution of the lost tax and for the late payment interest; under the 2014 Joint Memorandum for Blocklisting Discredit Action, the director is denied high-speed rail travel and air travel to Hong Kong; the criminal exposure is imprisonment up to 7 years for tax evasion under PRC Criminal Law Art. 201, disqualification from company directorship, and lifetime prohibition from serving as a legal representative of any Chinese enterprise.
