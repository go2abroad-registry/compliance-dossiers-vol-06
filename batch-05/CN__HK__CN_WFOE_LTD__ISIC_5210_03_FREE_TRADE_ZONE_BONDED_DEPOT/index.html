Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-up registered capital applies to this WFOE. The foreign shareholder may subscribe any registered capital consistent with the contemplated bonded warehouse business, provided the subscribed capital is paid up within five years from establishment as required by Article 47 of the PRC Company Law (2023 Revision). SAMR does not require a blocked escrow account or paid-up capital deposit for ordinary WFOE incorporation. The General Administration of Customs may separately require a customs guarantee or bonded warehouse bond in connection with the bonded warehousing license, but that is a regulatory security and not a shareholder paid-up capital condition.

Funding & unlock path: The project must obtain fire-fighting design review and fire acceptance recordation from the Municipal Fire and Rescue Department, evidenced by the fire safety acceptance certificate and the occupancy permit for the warehouse. Civil defense construction obligations under the Civil Air Defense Law apply only if the warehouse includes underground construction in an urban area; otherwise this item is not applicable. Structural load verification must confirm that floor slabs and racking systems meet the design live loads, usually not less than 2.5 tonnes per square meter unless the racking engineer specifies otherwise. The company must complete the Three Simultaneous work safety facility review and acceptance under the PRC Work Safety Law, arrange occupational hazard testing for dust, noise, and temperature under GBZ 158 and GBZ 188, and ensure all staff are trained in the safe handling of goods stored in the bonded depot.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China, with the National Electronic Tax Bureau e-Tax portal (https://etax.chinatax.gov.cn) as the online filing system; customs registration and bonded-zone administration are handled by the General Administration of Customs of China (GACC) through the China International Trade Single Window (https://www.singlewindow.cn).

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE) with the local Administration for Market Regulation (AMR); the business license will show the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which is the legal Tax Identification Number (TIN) for the WFOE. Step 2: Within 30 calendar days after issuance of the business license, complete consolidated tax registration in the STA e-Tax portal by logging in with the USCC, filing the taxpayer registration particulars, confirming legal representative, registered address, business scope, bank account details, and accounting methodology. Step 3: If the Hong Kong investor is a non-resident enterprise without a permanent establishment in China, no Chinese TIN is required for the investor; any China-source income or contract with the WFOE must be reported by the WFOE as withholding agent using the non-resident's foreign tax identifier and tax residence certificate. Step 4: For VAT, the WFOE must determine whether it is a small-scale taxpayer or general taxpayer; if annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000, it must obtain general taxpayer status, register VAT invoice quotas, and access the fully digitalized electronic invoice (FDEI) system. Step 5: For the bonded depot operations, complete customs registration at the competent customs office, obtain the customs registration code linked to the USCC, register the bonded warehouse/special supervision zone site, and link the e-Tax export tax refund module to the China International Trade Single Window records.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None; the PRC does not provide a check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election. The WFOE may elect general taxpayer versus small-scale taxpayer VAT treatment when turnover is below the statutory threshold, may elect free trade zone preferential enterprise income tax rates if the bonded depot activity is in the encouraged industry catalogue, and may claim Mainland-Hong Kong Double Taxation Arrangement relief where the Hong Kong investor is the beneficial owner. No entity pass-through election exists.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No turnover-based exemption from basic tax registration exists because registration is mandatory from incorporation; the statutory threshold for mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration is annual taxable sales exceeding RMB 5,000,000 in any 12-month period. Below RMB 5,000,000, the WFOE may remain a small-scale taxpayer with simplified VAT calculation, but sales of taxable goods and services still attract VAT at the applicable rate.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero rating and export VAT refund apply to goods physically exported from PRC customs territory and to qualifying offshore services. In a customs special supervision zone or bonded depot, sales of goods to overseas buyers are zero-rated where the goods are actually shipped out under customs supervision; goods sold from the bonded zone into domestic China are taxable imports and cannot be zero-rated. The WFOE must hold export tax refund registration, have general VAT taxpayer status for input credit, and file the export rebate/exemption claim through the STA e-Tax export tax refund module after the customs export declaration is released.

  • Customs Export Declaration Form released by GACC with the export customs clearance number and supervising customs seal/log record
  • Bill of Lading, airway bill, or rail consignment note evidencing physical departure from PRC customs territory
  • Export VAT invoice or fully digitalized electronic invoice matching the customs declaration
  • Bonded depot outbound delivery list and customs warehouse/zone exit audit trail confirming the goods left the special supervision area
  • Foreign exchange receipt evidence, including the bank export collection record and Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or electronic bank receipt issued by a licensed PRC bank
  • Commercial invoice, sales contract, packing list, and inspection certificates required by the export commodity catalogue
  • Certificate of Origin and related customs valuation documents where preferential tariff or origin claims are made
  • Customs registration code and the bonded warehouse entity-specific customs electronic account records

The PRC operates Golden Tax Phase IV real-time fiscal clearance. VAT invoices must be issued through the STA e-invoice platform, either as fully digitalized electronic invoices (FDEI) or under controlled invoice inventory rules, with real-time encrypted transmission to the tax authority, automatic anti-fraud matching, and QR-code verification. For bonded depot transactions, customs declarations, e-invoice data, bonded warehouse inventory records, and foreign exchange settlement data must align through the China International Trade Single Window; any mismatch triggers tax authority risk control and suspension of invoice quotas.

Export zero-rating claims are cross-checked in real time against GACC customs release data, STA e-invoice data, SAFE foreign exchange settlement records, and logistics manifests. If the WFOE fails to substantiate physical export or repatriate export proceeds within the 180-day statutory deadline (or an approved SAFE/STA extension), the tax authority will deem the transaction a domestic taxable sale, claw back any VAT refund or exemption, and assess value-added tax, enterprise income tax, late-payment surcharge at 0.05% per day, and a penalty ranging from 50% to 5 times the underpaid tax under the PRC Tax Administration and Collection Law. Document falsification, fraudulent customs declarations, or circular export arrangements can lead to criminal tax fraud prosecution, permanent disqualification from export tax refund status, customs smuggling penalties, and personal liability of the legal representative and responsible directors.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The People's Republic of China operates a managed floating exchange rate system with strict foreign exchange controls. Current account items, including genuine trade in goods and services, are broadly convertible, but every payment must be supported by an underlying contract, customs declaration, commercial invoice, and bank compliance review. The capital account remains heavily restricted; foreign direct investment into a China-based WFOE, inbound equity injections, cross-border shareholder loans, external debt, and profit repatriation all require prior or concurrent registration with SAFE before funds may enter or leave the banking system. Export receipts in foreign currency must be repatriated to an onshore bank account, but mandatory conversion has been abolished, so enterprises may retain foreign currency or convert to RMB at their discretion through a licensed bank. Dividend and profit remittance to the Hong Kong parent can only be completed after the WFOE has undergone its annual statutory audit, filed its corporate income tax return, settled all outstanding tax liabilities, and submitted a valid board resolution and distributions schedule to the remitting bank. For a bonded warehouse operator inside a free trade zone, the core FX control framework is identical to that of mainland companies, while the bonded customs regime separately defers customs duty and import VAT. Practical hurdles include branch-level compliance discretion, enhanced beneficial ownership checks on offshore parents, and strict matching of every capital account flow to a SAFE purpose code. Capital injections must be fully verified by a certified public accountant before being used for construction, rent, inventory, or operational expenses.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export foreign currency receipts must be repatriated to a designated domestic foreign exchange account within 180 days of the related export customs declaration, unless the local SAFE branch approves a written extension. There is no statutory mandatory conversion ratio; the mandatory surrender ratio is effectively 0%, meaning no portion of export proceeds must be forcibly sold to the banking system. If the WFOE elects to convert export proceeds into RMB, conversion must be executed through a licensed onshore bank at the prevailing interbank spot rate and reported to SAFE under the appropriate trade purpose code. Bonded depot operators must reconcile every export customs declaration with the corresponding SWIFT receipt to avoid foreign-exchange receipts without matching customs data flags.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE), with SAFE executing delegated foreign exchange administration, registration, and supervisory powers

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Not viable as a primary account provider. Offshore digital EMI platforms are not licensed deposit-taking institutions in mainland China and cannot access CNAPS, CIPS, or SAFE purpose-code submission channels on behalf of a WFOE. The PBOC and the National Financial Regulatory Administration prohibit unlicensed non-bank entities from holding client funds, opening RMB basic deposit accounts, or performing foreign-exchange surrender and registration functions. An offshore EMI may only act as an ancillary expense management layer or invoicing dashboard integrated with a licensed onshore bank's virtual account system, and all clearing must ultimately pass through the licensed bank. Feasibility rating: Low.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound capital from the Hong Kong shareholder must be remitted via SWIFT MT103 directly to the WFOE's designated foreign-exchange capital account at a licensed onshore bank, using the SAFE foreign-exchange purpose code for equity injection under foreign direct investment. The remitter name in the MT103 must exactly match the registered shareholder; third-party payment intermediaries are not permitted. On receipt, the receiving bank credits the temporary capital account and reports the flow to SAFE through the Capital Account Information System. The WFOE then applies to convert the foreign currency into RMB for permitted operating uses under the settlement-at-source principle, presenting the FDI registration certificate, board resolution, capital contribution schedule, and a written description of intended use. A registered CPA must issue a capital contribution verification report once funds are received. Bonded warehouse construction, rental, equipment, inventory, and operational expenses must be traceable to the verified capital pool. If the HK shareholder injects offshore RMB instead of foreign currency, the transfer should be routed through CIPS with the same FDI equity purpose code and matching underlying registration.Outbound trade payments from the bonded depot should be executed using SWIFT MT103 for direct foreign-currency settlements, commercial letters of credit transmitted through SWIFT MT700 and MT707 for structured supplier payments, and documentary collections handled through SWIFT MT400 and MT422 messages. RMB-denominated cross-border trade should clear through CIPS with the appropriate RMB settlement purpose code, while domestic disbursements for warehouse rent, logistics, electricity, customs bond deposits, and taxes should use CNAPS local clearing through the RMB basic deposit account. All settlement documentation must match the customs declaration numbers, bonded warehouse entry and exit records, commercial invoices, packing lists, and transport documents. Bank of China and ICBC can act as issuing banks for letters of credit payable to overseas suppliers; for high-value commodities, a sight letter of credit is recommended so that the issuing bank verifies customs and logistics documents before releasing payment.
