Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; electronic compliance is performed through the STA Integrated Electronic Tax Bureau and the China International Trade Single Window for customs/export declarations. The Hong Kong SAR Inland Revenue Department is not the VAT registration authority for the mainland WFOE but remains relevant for the Hong Kong parent's territorial profits tax treatment and the Mainland-Hong Kong Double Tax Arrangement relief procedures.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: The foreign non-resident shareholder of the WFOE, including the Hong Kong SAR parent, must execute notarization and apostille of its constitutive documents, certificate of incorporation, business registration certificate, and certificate of good standing; China is an apostille jurisdiction for documents originating in Hong Kong SAR since the PRC accession, and the apostille replaces consular legalization. Step 2: Apply to the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) for the WFOE business license; the approved business license carries the Uniform Social Credit Code (USCC), which is the statutory Chinese Taxpayer Identification Number and the default VAT registration number. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None in the sense of a US-style check-the-box or hybrid mismatch classification election. The WFOE is automatically a PRC resident limited liability company subject to entity-level Enterprise Income Tax on worldwide income at the statutory 25% rate. The only indirect-tax election is the VAT small-scale taxpayer versus general taxpayer election; a taxpayer may elect general taxpayer status even below the RMB 5 million annual threshold if it meets accounting and fixed-place criteria, and the WFOE engaged in tangible movable property leasing will normally be required to be a general taxpayer to issue 13% VAT invoices and to recover input VAT. The Hong Kong parent may elect to apply treaty benefits under the Mainland-Hong Kong Double Tax Arrangement, including the reduced 5% dividend withholding rate, subject to beneficial ownership and the principal purpose test, but this is a treaty relief claim rather than a domestic entity classification election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Annual taxable sales exceeding RMB 5,000,000 in any consecutive 12-month period triggers mandatory general taxpayer VAT registration for the WFOE; below this threshold the entity is registered as a small-scale VAT taxpayer. Independent of the general taxpayer threshold, the WFOE must complete VAT registration as part of its standard tax identity registration, and small-scale taxpayers with monthly sales not exceeding RMB 100,000 or quarterly sales not exceeding RMB 300,000 may qualify for the prevailing VAT exemption, unless a general taxpayer election is made.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The WFOE can claim 0% VAT export zero-rating for aircraft engine dry leasing only if the transaction falls within the PRC export-of-goods regime or the cross-border taxable services zero-rating rules. For physical export, the engine must be removed from PRC customs territory under an export customs declaration and must be leased to a non-resident operator for use wholly outside the mainland. For cross-border services without physical export, the supply must be made to an overseas enterprise or individual, the leased aircraft engine must be situated and used entirely outside mainland China during the lease term, the lease consideration must be paid by the offshore lessee, and the foreign exchange proceeds must be collected and repatriated into China through licensed banking channels. The WFOE must substantiate that the engine is not for domestic mainland use, and the contractual, payment, usage, and customs records must all consistently evidence the offshore destination; dry leasing with crew or any taxable auxiliary service supplied in mainland China would preclude zero-rating on that portion.

  • Customs Export Goods Declaration Form validated by China Customs through the International Trade Single Window, being the Chinese statutory equivalent of a bill of entry for export
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical removal of the aircraft engine from PRC customs territory into the international transportation chain
  • Lease agreement, commercial invoice, engine serial number/tail number records, and lessee acceptance certificate confirming offshore use
  • Bank-issued Export Proceeds Receipt Voucher and SAFE trade-in-goods foreign exchange settlement record, being the Chinese statutory equivalent of the inward remittance certificate and export proceeds verification document
  • Zero-rated VAT invoice issued through the STA Fully Digitalized Electronic Invoice system with the cross-border zero-rated export code and matching contract reference

The WFOE must use the STA Fully Digitalized Electronic Invoice system. All VAT special invoices and ordinary invoices must be issued from the STA-controlled cloud platform with nationwide unique invoice numbers and near-real-time upload to the Golden Tax infrastructure; there is no separate physical fiscal stamping, but the digital serialization and automatic reconciliation of output VAT against input VAT are mandatory. The invoice must state the correct tax classification for tangible movable property leasing services, the lessee's taxpayer identification, the contract number, and, for a zero-rated export transaction, the cross-border zero-rated export indicator. The Hong Kong lessee or non-resident counterparty is not in the Chinese VAT invoice system unless it separately obtains a mainland taxpayer identification; therefore the WFOE must maintain a complete contract and payment file in addition to the digital invoice.

The STA and China Customs cross-match the customs export declaration value, the digital VAT invoice amount, the bank foreign exchange receipt amount, and the lease contract terms using the Golden Tax data platform. Any missing, inconsistent, contradicted, or fraudulent export documentation will cause the zero-rating to be revoked, output VAT at 13% to become payable with late payment interest from the original tax filing due date, and a penalty of 50% to five times the underpaid tax under the Tax Administration Law. False export declarations, forged customs or bank documents, circular foreign exchange flows, and lease arrangements structured through a Hong Kong intermediary without commercial substance are subject to anti-fraud investigations, export tax fraud penalties, criminal referral, and potential recharacterization under China's General Anti-Avoidance Rules and the principal purpose test of the Mainland-Hong Kong Double Tax Arrangement.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a managed floating exchange-rate system with current-account convertibility under IMF Article VIII, but capital-account transactions remain administratively controlled by SAFE. For a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise engaged in aircraft engine dry leasing, operational lease rental receipts and payments constitute service-trade items falling under current-account convertibility, so cross-border lease collections can be received and converted onshore provided the settlement bank obtains a valid underlying master lease, invoice, collection confirmation and tax filing. Capital injections from the Hong Kong parent are capital-account transactions and require a separate SAFE foreign direct investment (FDI) registration through the designated mainland bank before a foreign-currency capital account can be funded. Profit remittance to the Hong Kong shareholder is permitted only after the annual statutory audit, corporate income tax reconciliation and tax clearance; the WFOE must evidence paid-in capital verification, historical undistributed profits, and beneficial-ownership compliance. Although the Renminbi is freely usable for trade settlement, onshore derivative transactions must pass the real-demand-risk test; offshore NDF arbitrage is not available to the mainland WFOE, and any forward, swap or option must be documented against an identified lease exposure. Dividends to the Hong Kong parent are generally subject to 5% withholding tax only if the Hong Kong shareholder satisfies the mainland beneficial-ownership test; otherwise the standard 10% withholding rate applies. No mandatory foreign-exchange surrender obligation currently exists for ordinary operating receipts, but SAFE can invoke temporary macroprudential adjustment measures if balance-of-payments stress emerges.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the current SAFE regime, there is no fixed mandatory surrender ratio for export foreign-currency receipts. For goods exports, proceeds must be repatriated into mainland China within 180 days from the date of customs declaration unless an extended timeline is approved by the settlement bank and recorded with SAFE. For service-trade and operating-lease receivables, including aircraft engine dry-leasing rentals, the WFOE must bring the foreign currency into its onshore current-account foreign-currency account and file the electronic balance-of-payments report within the bank's post-credit reporting window, typically within 30 calendar days. The WFOE may retain or voluntarily convert the receipts at the market rate. In exceptional crisis conditions SAFE may impose temporary surrender percentages, but under standard treasury operations no conversion quota applies to this transaction.

Central bank supervision: The People's Bank of China (PBOC) and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE); the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) supervises offshore CNH treasury activity for the Hong Kong parent vehicle.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Offshore fintech EMIs are not viable as primary treasury rails for a mainland WFOE. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Under SAT Public Notice 2016 No.42, a Local File is mandatory for the WFOE if annual related-party transactions exceed: (a) RMB 100 million in tangible asset transfer (aircraft engines or engine spare parts); (b) RMB 10 million in intangible asset transfer or royalties; (c) RMB 10 million in financial asset transfers; or (d) RMB 40 million in related-party services (including management, agency, maintenance services). A Master File must be prepared when the aggregate annual cross-border controlled transaction amount exceeds RMB 1 billion. Additionally, a contemporaneous TP documentation pack is required before filing the annual EIT return, and the related-party disclosure form (A000000) is always due.

Economic substance scope: Although the PRC does not have a UAE-style Economic Substance Regulation, the PRC Enterprise Income Tax Law (Article 47) and SAT Announcement 2019 No.35 impose a general anti-avoidance rule and a beneficial-ownership test that function as an economic substance doctrine. A WFOE engaged in aircraft engine dry leasing must have real office premises, qualified aviation-leasing personnel, core decision-making functions in the PRC, and actual management of engine procurement, lease negotiation, credit risk, and residual asset risk. For the HK shareholder to enjoy 5% dividend WHT relief, the HK company must show core income-generating activities in Hong Kong relating to its equity investment, such as strategic ownership decisions, dividend collection, and financing. A HK 'letterbox' company without employees, bank accounts, or physical premises will be denied treaty benefits and may be recharacterized as a conduit. Services rendered between the PRC and HK entities must be economically substantive; intra-group management fees must correspond to actual services that produce a benefit and cannot be simply profit-stripping.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion (false EIT return and fabricated lease deductions)

Statutory sanction: Under PRC Tax Collection Administration Law Article 63, the tax authority will recover the underpaid tax plus a daily late-payment surcharge (0.05%), and impose a fine of 50% to 500% of the unpaid tax. Under PRC Criminal Law Article 201, tax evasion exceeding the statutory threshold (generally RMB 100,000 and 10% of taxable taxes) is a crime punishable by criminal fines (up to 5 times the tax evaded) and imprisonment of up to 7 years; if the WFOE uses false invoices to support the evasion, additional criminal liability under Article 205 (false special VAT invoices) can apply.

Director personal liability: Courts may pierce the corporate veil under PRC Company Law Article 20 (new Article 23) when the WFOE is a mere instrument of fraud. The director or representative who signed false returns is jointly and severally liable for civil restitution, including payment of the tax, interest, penalties, and legal costs. The SAT will blacklist the company and its legal representative, and frontier control authorities will issue a travel ban / exit restriction against the individual. If the fraudulent amount is large, the director faces personal criminal fines and imprisonment for up to 7 years for tax evasion, and up to 10 years if the conduct is also prosecuted as money laundering.
Shell Company / Treaty Abuse (improper claims for 5% dividend WHT relief)

Statutory sanction: The PRC tax authority will deny treaty benefits under SAT Announcement 2019 No.35 and apply the domestic 10% dividend WHT rate, with late-payment interest and penalties. The withholding agent will be held responsible under PRC Tax Collection Administration Law Article 64 for failure to withhold, incurring a penalty of 50% to 300% of the unwithheld amount. Under EIT Law Article 47, the arrangement may be recharacterized entirely, including reclassifying the dividend as a taxable distribution to the true ultimate shareholder and disallowing the HK intermediary entity. If the sham is documented, SAT will apply the GAAR and may impose a 50% to 300% additional adjustment.

Director personal liability: Directors of the WFOE who participate in filing a false beneficial-owner declaration for the HK parent, or who sign false minutes for the HK board, are jointly liable to the tax authority for the additional 5% dividend WHT plus penalties. The HK resident director of the shell entity may also be personally named in the tax aggression/publicity list, blacklisted from entering mainland China, and subject to asset tracing and asset freezing in both mainland China and Hong Kong. If the conduct is characterized as fraud or tax evasion, criminal liability can extend to up to 7 years imprisonment; an international arrest warrant may be issued under mutual legal assistance.
AML / Cross-Border Financial Infraction (money laundering through lease payments)

Statutory sanction: Under PRC Anti-Money Laundering Law (as amended in 2025), banks and the WFOE (as a reporting entity) face severe administrative penalties including fines (up to RMB 5 million for serious violations) and revocation of financial licenses. Under PRC Criminal Law Article 191, money laundering is punished with confiscation of the laundered funds and imprisonment of up to 10 years for natural persons; the WFOE will also be fined. Violations of PRC Foreign Exchange Administration Regulations subject the offender to fines of 0.5% to 30% of the illegal amount and closure of the foreign-exchange risk position.

Director personal liability: If the director ordered, participated in, or knew of the laundering, the corporate veil is pierced and the director bears personal criminal liability with imprisonment of up to 10 years and criminal fines; he is jointly and severally liable for civil forfeiture of the laundered assets and restitution to injured parties. The director will be placed on the individual blacklist for major AML/tax violations, subjected to a cross-border travel ban (with denial of exit), asset freezing orders, and professional disqualification from serving as a director of any PRC company. In cross-border cases, the PRC procures extradition and mutual legal assistance for asset recovery, extending the personal liability to any jurisdiction where assets are located.
