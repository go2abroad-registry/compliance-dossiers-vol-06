Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None. The PRC Company Law imposes no minimum registered capital for a wholly foreign-owned enterprise engaged in intermodal container leasing. Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Mainland China applies a 0% VAT rate to exported goods when the goods are declared to Customs under the Customs Law of the People's Republic of China, physically depart the PRC customs territory, the exporter is a VAT general taxpayer, and the export sale is made to an overseas purchaser in exchange for foreign currency. The export tax refund (exemption) regimes are the exemption, offset and refund method for production-oriented exporters and the exemption and refund method for foreign-trade exporters under the STA Measures for the Administration of Export Tax Refund (Exemption) on Goods and Services (STA Announcement No. 24 of 2012). For cross-border leasing of intermodal ISO shipping containers, the transaction must be analysed under the cross-border taxable services zero-rating and exemption catalogue issued by the Ministry of Finance and STA; if the container lease qualifies as a service provided to an overseas entity and wholly consumed outside China, the applicable treatment is zero-rated or exempt as provided by that catalogue; if the container physically leaves China under customs lease-export procedures, the export goods zero-rating and refund rules apply subject to the same documentation and foreign exchange repatriation requirements. Qualifying digital services supplied to overseas customers and wholly consumed outside the PRC are also eligible for zero-rating or exemption to the extent listed in the same cross-border service catalogue.

China operates mandatory state-controlled e-invoicing under the nationwide Fully Digitalized Electronic Invoice system, supported by the Golden Tax Phase IV infrastructure and the STA Electronic Tax Bureau. Fully digitalized e-invoices are issued through the STA electronic invoice service platform and immediately transmitted to the STA tax digital account, where each invoice is assigned a unique invoice code, number and tax-authority digital supervision mark; physical tax control devices are no longer required for qualifying taxpayers. Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a managed floating exchange rate regime and has accepted IMF Article VIII obligations for current account convertibility. Cross-border rental income and ordinary service trade receipts in CNY or foreign currency are therefore freely convertible for trade purposes, subject to truthful documentation and bank verification. The capital account, however, remains strictly regulated. All foreign direct investment into the WFOE, including equity injections from the Hong Kong parent, must be registered with SAFE under the Foreign Investment Law and the implementing rules for foreign exchange administration of direct investment. This requires the opening of a dedicated foreign currency capital deposit account; funds cannot be offset, netted, or redirected without SAFE approval. Dividend remittances to the Hong Kong parent are conditional upon completion of a statutory annual audit, corporate income tax settlement, and submission of tax clearance and verified financial statements. Since the 2012 SAFE reform, there is no mandatory surrender of current account export receipts; enterprises may retain foreign exchange or sell it to a bank at the market rate. Nevertheless, capital account transactions such as shareholder loan prepayments, intercompany lending, and offshore investment remain subject to quotas, registration, and maturity requirements. The renminbi is not freely tradable onshore beyond the daily central parity band; offshore CNH rates differ from onshore CNY. Any onshore hedging must be supported by a genuine underlying exposure. Overall, treasury management for the WFOE must separate current account operating cash flows from capital account injection proceeds and preserve a complete audit trail of SAFE reporting codes for every wire.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory mandatory surrender applies; the mandatory conversion ratio is 0% for export and service trade receipts. Under SAFE's current account administration measures, all export proceeds in foreign exchange must be repatriated to China and credited to the company's foreign exchange settlement account at a bank. For goods exports, receipt must be completed generally within 180 calendar days from the customs declaration date; for cross-border services such as container leasing, the receipt must be collected by the due date specified in the contract and reconciled with the bank. The enterprise may retain 100% of the foreign exchange earnings indefinitely or convert them into CNY at prevailing market rates. Any delay beyond the prescribed period requires prior SAFE registration, supporting explanation, and may trigger forex fine investigation.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) and State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Unviable as a primary banking rail for the mainland WFOE. Offshore digital EMI platforms, including Revolut Business, Wise Business, and Airwallex, are not licensed deposit-taking institutions under the National Financial Regulatory Administration (NFRA) and are not members of CIPS or the domestic clearing system. They cannot open a CNY current account, a basic deposit account, or a SAFE-designated foreign currency capital account for a China-registered WFOE. The practical use is limited to the Hong Kong parent entity, where the parent may hold a multi-currency wallet and initiate MT103 wires to the WFOE's domestic bank. Even then, a capital injection arriving from an unlicensed EMI will be rejected or frozen because the bank cannot verify the source through a supervised financial institution, and the SAFE capital verification process will fail. Accordingly, the treasury blueprint must rely exclusively on licensed Tier-1 commercial banks for all onshore and cross-border WFOE flows.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The Hong Kong parent will initiate a SWIFT MT103 wire to the WFOE's foreign currency capital account at the designated Tier-1 bank, using the SAFE foreign exchange code for foreign direct investment capital injection (investment principal). The remittance must be registered in advance with SAFE and match the amount and currency recorded in the FDI registration. Upon credit, the bank matches the inflow to the SAFE system, then either retains it in foreign currency or converts it to CNY at the market rate. A Chinese CPA is then engaged to produce a statutory capital verification report confirming paid-in capital. Only after verification may the business use the funds for container procurement, lease operations, and general working capital, under the SAFE payment-conversion rules requiring underlying contracts. All supporting documents, including the SAFE registration form, capital verification report, and payment conversion vouchers, must be retained for a minimum of five years for examination.For low to mid-value operational disbursements such as depot storage, cleaning, maintenance, and insurance premiums, the WFOE will use SWIFT MT103 wires from its FX settlement account, attaching underlying invoices and service contracts for bank review. For high-value purchases of new ISO containers from overseas manufacturers, an irrevocable commercial letter of credit (LC) is recommended, with the beneficiary's draft and shipping documents to secure the transaction and provide trade finance flexibility. Documentary collections (D/P and D/A) may be employed for mid-value periodic payments to trusted terminals and logistics providers, balancing LC costs against counterparty risk. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% statutory dividend withholding tax on outbound dividends sourced from the Chinese WFOE to a non-resident corporate shareholder; the China-Hong Kong DTA reduces the rate to 5% for a Hong Kong company that is the beneficial owner and directly holds at least 25% of the capital of the Chinese company for the 12 months preceding the dividend.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The annual return must be filed and final tax paid within 5 months after the fiscal year-end (31 May for a calendar-year company). Quarterly provisional CIT returns are due within 15 days after each quarter-end.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File and contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation are mandatory when annual related-party transactions are above: RMB 200 million for tangible asset transfers, RMB 100 million for intangible asset transfers, RMB 100 million for financial asset transfers, or RMB 40 million for related-party service transactions; a Master File must be prepared if the Hong Kong parent group's global consolidated revenue exceeds RMB 5.5 billion. Every WFOE with related-party transactions must also file the annual Related Party Transaction Report.

Economic substance scope: The PRC applies the substance-over-form doctrine and the General Anti-Avoidance Rule under Article 47 of the EIT Law. For FCL container fleet leasing, Core Income-Generating Activities include container procurement, lease solicitation and negotiation, credit and collection, maintenance supervision, repositioning decisions, and assumption of fleet utilization risk; these activities must be performed by the Chinese WFOE with local employees and assets. The Hong Kong parent separately must satisfy the Hong Kong Economic Substance Requirements under Cap. 615 for pure equity holding and finance/leasing activities: adequate office, employees, board direction, and operating expenditure in Hong Kong, with annual ESR reporting.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion / fraudulent corporate income tax filing

Statutory sanction: PRC Tax Administration and Collection Law Article 63 imposes recovery of underpaid tax, a penalty of 50% to 5 times the evaded amount, a daily late surcharge of 0.05%, and public blacklisting. PRC Criminal Law Article 201 imposes criminal liability for tax evasion: imprisonment up to 3 years for relatively large amounts and up to 7 years plus fines where the amount evaded is enormous.

Director personal liability: Directors who approve or sign fraudulent returns may have the corporate veil pierced under PRC Company Law and Civil Code where they used the entity to defraud the tax authorities. Responsible officers are named in the tax blacklist, may face travel bans and exit denial under the Exit-Entry Administration Law, and risk asset freezing, personal fines, and imprisonment.
Treaty abuse / shell company / lack of economic substance in Hong Kong

Statutory sanction: The tax authority may deny the treaty benefit under the beneficial-ownership rule and the Principal Purpose Test, recharacterize the dividend as a China-source payment taxed at 10%, impose 0.05% daily surcharge and a 50%-5x penalty for under-withholding, and re-tax indirect transfers of Chinese assets under SAT Announcement 2017 No. 6. The Hong Kong ESR regime also imposes fines, daily default penalties, and sanctions against the company and its responsible officers for false or non-compliant declarations.

Director personal liability: Directors of the Hong Kong shell and of the WFOE who sign false beneficial-ownership declarations are personally exposed to HK statutory penalties and imprisonment for knowingly providing false ESR or tax declarations; in China they may face joint and several civil restitution, travel restrictions, criminal liability for tax fraud, and cross-border asset recovery proceedings.
Transfer pricing manipulation / thin capitalization / profit stripping

Statutory sanction: Under EIT Law Articles 41 and 46, the tax authority may make a transfer pricing adjustment, disallow excess interest, recharacterize it as a dividend subject to withholding tax, impose daily surcharge, and assess penalty of 50% to 5 times the underpaid tax in cases of willful misstatement or fraudulent documentation. Failure to maintain contemporaneous TP documentation triggers risk assessment, adjustment without comparability credit, and exposure to special tax investigation rules.

Director personal liability: Directors and financial officers who execute artificially structured related-party contracts with no commercial reality may be pursued under the PRC Civil Code for damages, lose the benefit of limited liability through corporate-veil piercing, be placed on the tax blacklist and subject to exit bans, and face individual criminal prosecution under Article 201 of the PRC Criminal Law when the arrangement constitutes deliberate tax evasion.
