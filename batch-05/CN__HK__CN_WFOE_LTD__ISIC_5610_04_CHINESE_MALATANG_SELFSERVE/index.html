Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation - the WFOE is a PRC resident enterprise liable to Enterprise Income Tax on worldwide income at the 25% statutory headline rate; it is not pass-through, not a territorial company, and does not automatically enjoy tax-exempt zone treatment.

Available election forms

None for U.S.-style check-the-box or hybrid mismatch elections; PRC law does not permit the WFOE to be disregarded. The only operational election relevant to this entity is the VAT classification election: annual taxable turnover below RMB 5,000,000 permits small-scale taxpayer treatment at the 3% collection rate, while the WFOE may elect general taxpayer status to claim input VAT credits and apply the 6% catering/franchise service rate; simplified VAT treatment for certain cross-border service categories may also be elected where admissible.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

6%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

All business entities must complete tax registration at establishment. Mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration is triggered when annual taxable turnover exceeds RMB 5,000,000. Below RMB 5,000,000 the WFOE may remain a small-scale taxpayer at the 3% collection rate or elect general taxpayer status. Catering services and franchise licensing under this fast-casual self-serve model carry the 6% VAT rate for general taxpayers, while separate sales of tangible goods and equipment attract the standard 13% VAT goods rate.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For goods: the WFOE must be a general VAT taxpayer and must physically export goods from China's customs territory to an overseas customer; the sale must be evidenced by a customs export declaration, transport documents, commercial invoice, and receipt of export proceeds in foreign exchange. For qualifying cross-border services, zero-rating or VAT exemption applies only where the service is completely consumed outside mainland China and supplied to an overseas or Hong Kong recipient under the cross-border VAT rules, including the framework of Caishui 2016 No. 36 and related announcements. A franchise royalty or catering master-license arrangement with a Hong Kong licensee will qualify only if the licensed brand activity and delivery are substantiated as occurring outside mainland China; intra-group management services supplied to Hong Kong must be evidenced as not used in mainland China. Export VAT zero-rating is not automatic; the export declaration and rebate documentation must be filed through the ETB with supporting evidence.

China mandates issuance of digital e-invoices, known as fully digitalised electronic invoices, through the STA Electronic Invoice Service Platform in the National Unified Electronic Tax Bureau. Each invoice is assigned a unique 20-digit invoice number and official digital seal; the platform validates and records every invoice in real time before delivery to the buyer, operating as tax-authority clearance. Physical tax-control devices are being phased out; ERP-to-platform API integration is available, but all invoice issuance must be synchronised with STA central systems. Anti-fraud numbering, automatic reconciliation of invoice data, and mandatory electronic archiving apply; paper fiscal stamps are no longer required.

STA and customs jointly audit export zero-rating claims against export declarations, logistics events, invoice data, and SAFE foreign-exchange records. If zero-rating is disallowed, the WFOE must output-reverse the recovered VAT, pay the underlying VAT with a daily late-payment surcharge at 0.05% of the tax due, and may face an administrative penalty of 0.5 to 5 times the underpaid or fraudulently refunded tax. Falsely declared exports or forged documents attract criminal liability for export tax fraud, including fines, confiscation, and imprisonment up to life for major cases. Temporary or permanent suspension of export tax rebate status and loss of simplified trade privileges may also be imposed.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Chinese Renminbi is conditionally convertible on current account and controlled on capital account. Current account trade and service transactions are generally permitted for settlement and payment subject to truthfulness review by licensed banks, while capital account transactions such as FDI injections by foreign shareholders require prior SAFE registration or approval. A WFOE established under a Hong Kong parent must register the inbound equity investment through SAFE and open a distinct Foreign Exchange Capital Account before receiving the remittance. Once credited, the foreign currency capital may be settled into RMB for working capital using the capital settlement procedures, but it cannot be used for currency trading, lending, or investments outside the approved business scope. Profit remittance for dividends is permissible after annual audit, CIT settlement, and the bank's review of the board resolution and tax vouchers; there is no blanket repatriation tax, but the dividend withholding tax or treaty rate must be cleared. The practical constraint is not formal convertibility but documentary rigidity, transaction-by-transaction supervision through the SAFE monitoring system, and the requirement for all funding to have genuine underlying operational purpose.

Export proceeds surrender rules

China has moved away from compulsory surrender ratios. A WFOE engaged in food-service trade must repatriate export and cross-border e-commerce proceeds into a domestic foreign exchange settlement account through a designated bank. The general trade supervision timeline is tied to the customs export declaration and subsequent banking receipt verification; proceeds that are not verified within the standard SAFE monitoring cycle, commonly 180 days for trade credit terms unless extended by registration, require an extension application. The bank will execute settlement into RMB according to the enterprise's instruction; if the account retains foreign exchange above the approved retention ceiling, the surplus must be settled or moved into a controlled debt or liquidity structure.

Central bank supervision: The State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China, within the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low as a primary treasury and capital-account bank. Offshore digital EMIs and non-bank fintech platforms such as Airwallex, Currencycloud, and Revolut Business are not licensed deposit-taking institutions in the PRC and cannot open corporate bank accounts for a WFOE. Under SAFE and PBOC regulations, a WFOE must maintain its statutory registers, capital verification, and trade collection and repayment flows in a domestic SAFE-approved bank. A fintech EMI can only serve as a support ancillary rail for low-value merchant collection or international e-commerce settlement if the service is supplied by a PBOC-licensed payment institution acting through a domestic partner bank, but it cannot receive SAFE-registered FDI capital, cannot execute capital-factored trade settlements, and cannot replace the local banking account opening obligation.
