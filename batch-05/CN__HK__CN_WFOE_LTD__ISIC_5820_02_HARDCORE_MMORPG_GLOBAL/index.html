Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA); electronic portal: the New Electronic Tax Service Platform (e-Tax) of the STA; customs/export declarations are filed through the China International Trade Single Window.

E-government filing portal

For the China WFOE, the foreign investor first obtains the statutory Business License from the State Administration for Market Regulation; the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) printed on that license is the legal TIN and VAT identification number for all tax filings. Within thirty days of the license, the WFOE must complete initial tax registration with the competent local STA office through e-Tax, verify legal representative and address, and register for VAT and corporate income tax. For a foreign non-resident enterprise with no Chinese establishment receiving China-source income, the payor is required to withhold, and the non-resident applies for a Non-Resident Enterprise Tax Registration and obtains a non-resident taxpayer identification number through the competent STA office handling the withholding. This non-resident ID is also used when the foreign party is obliged to discharge Chinese VAT on cross-border services. After registration, the WFOE must maintain the USCC on all invoices, customs declarations, export refund, and foreign-exchange filings, and must update the registration within thirty days of any change to name, legal form, shareholders, business scope, legal representative, or address.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None. The PRC does not offer check-the-box, pass-through, or partnership-taxation elections for a wholly foreign-owned limited liability company. The WFOE is automatically treated as a Chinese tax resident subject to entity-level corporate income tax on worldwide income; relevant anti-avoidance rules, including CFC and general anti-abuse rules, apply to outbound structures. Hybrid mismatch elections are not available in China.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

There is no zero threshold for VAT registration: any entity conducting taxable sales in China must obtain tax registration and file VAT. The turnover threshold affecting status is annual taxable sales of CNY 5,000,000: a taxpayer at or below this amount may remain a small-scale taxpayer, while a taxpayer whose annual taxable sales exceed CNY 5,000,000 is required to register as a general VAT taxpayer, issue VAT special invoices, and comply with input VAT credit and export refund procedures.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For physical goods, the exporter must have the goods actually verified and released for export by Chinese Customs, declare the export under the customs export declaration procedure through the China International Trade Single Window, and retain proof that the goods left the PRC customs territory. For qualifying cross-border digital services such as MMORPG software publishing and in-app game services supplied to overseas platform operators or end-users, zero-rating is available when (i) the service is provided by a resident enterprise to a foreign entity or individual, (ii) the service is wholly consumed outside China, (iii) a written cross-border service agreement or platform settlement contract exists, (iv) all consideration is derived in foreign currency from abroad, and (v) the exporter can demonstrate that no onshore domestic recipient benefited from the service. Apple App Store and Google Play payout statements, royalty or service agreements, and bank foreign-exchange receipts must be retained to substantiate the offshore nature.

  • Customs Export Declaration (export goods declaration accepted and stamped by PRC Customs through the China International Trade Single Window)
  • Transport document evidencing physical departure, such as Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, or rail manifest (for digital services, the platform settlement report and server-usage/offshore-consumption evidence replaces the transport document)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or bank foreign-exchange receipts statement evidencing that export proceeds were repatriated into the exporter's foreign-exchange account within the prescribed deadline

China mandates VAT invoices be issued through the STA-controlled invoice issuance system. All VAT invoices, including fully digitalised electronic VAT invoices, must be registered and transmitted in real time to the STA Electronic Invoice Service Platform when issued. Each invoice carries a unique invoice code, digital signature, and verification element; there is no requirement for physical stamping, but the e-tax system validates invoice numbers, tax rates, amounts, and buyer/seller TIN before use. Anti-fraud controls include one-invoice-one-code, red-letter reversal requirements for cancelled invoices, automatic matching of invoice data with VAT returns, and cross-referencing with customs export and foreign-exchange reporting data.

Export zero-rating is aggressively audited by STA and China Customs through cross-referencing of customs declaration data, VAT records, freight documents, and SAFE foreign-exchange receipts. If the zero rating is claimed without actual export, without offshore consumption, without receipt of foreign currency, or based on forged platform/customs documentation, the tax authorities will claw back all zero-rated/refunded VAT, impose late payment surcharge at 0.05% per day, and impose administrative penalties ranging from 50% to five times the tax amount. Repeated or fraudulent export refund claims are referred for criminal investigation under the Law of the PRC on the Administration of Tax Collection and the Criminal Law; the crime of fraudulent export tax refund is punishable by substantial fines, confiscation, and imprisonment up to life imprisonment in serious cases. Additionally, the exporter may lose export tax refund qualification, be downgraded in tax credit rating, and become subject to mandatory customs inspection and bank foreign-exchange monitoring.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China maintains Article VIII current-account convertibility, meaning genuine cross-border trade and service payments, wages, and routine investment income may be settled through banks with supporting documentary evidence and without prior SAFE approval. However, capital-account items such as FDI equity injections, foreign debt drawdowns, cross-border guarantees, and securities-related flows remain tightly regulated. The WFOE may receive equity capital from its Hong Kong parent only after a bank-mediated FDI registration has been completed under SAFE. Any contribution exceeding the registered capital requires an amendment to the articles of association, SAFE re-filing, and fresh capital verification. RMB converted from the foreign-currency capital account must be parked in a settlement-pending account and may only be disbursed against approved operating expenditures. Profit remittance to the Hong Kong parent is feasible after the annual statutory audit, corporate income tax final settlement, withholding tax payment, and submission of audited financial statements to the remitting bank. The practical reality is that convertibility is robust for genuine trade and investment flows, but non-trade or speculative flows face strict authenticity and purpose checks. There is no mandatory surrender requirement for export proceeds; enterprises may retain foreign exchange or settle into RMB at a licensed bank.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE current-account rules, export foreign-currency receipts must be repatriated to China and must be reported through the Trade in Goods Foreign Exchange Monitoring System. There is no mandatory conversion percentage; the WFOE may retain up to 100% of its export proceeds in a current-account foreign-currency account or settle them into RMB at a designated bank. The operational timeline requires bank verification of export collection within 30 days of receipt. If customs export declaration occurs and matching foreign exchange has not arrived within 180 days, the WFOE must submit a written explanation, the export customs declaration, contracts, transport documents, and bank refusals or collection correspondences to SAFE or the facilitating bank for verification. Unapproved offshore retention of export proceeds is prohibited.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low viability. Offshore digital EMI platforms such as Revolut, Airwallex, Payoneer, and Wise are not licensed banks in mainland China and are legally barred from opening SAFE-registered capital accounts or acting as deposit-taking clearing institutions for a WFOE. They cannot perform FDI verification, issue capital-account receipts, or support statutory capital verification. They may only act as optional merchant payout facilitators where they partner with a licensed onshore bank under PBOC cross-border payment pilot rules, but all treasury, foreign-exchange conversion, tax filing, and liquidation rails must remain anchored to an onshore Tier-1 domestic bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The Hong Kong parent must remit USD, HKD, or CNH into the WFOE's SAFE-registered FDI Foreign Exchange Capital Account at the chosen Tier-1 domestic bank. Prior to the remittance, the WFOE completes FDI foreign-exchange registration through the bank and obtains the SAFE registration confirmation number. The remittance instruction must clearly cite the SAFE file number, identify the payment as 'FDI equity contribution', and reference the approved capital schedule. The receiving bank will complete an inbound FX transaction report using the SAFE balance-of-payments category applicable to foreign direct investment capital injection. Once the funds arrive, the bank issues an inward remittance receipt, the converted RMB is placed into a settlement-pending capital account, and the WFOE engages a registered Chinese CPA to perform capital verification. Funds may not be injected through third-party EMI wallets, crypto platforms, or unrelated intermediary accounts.For recurring global operating payments from the WFOE — cloud hosting, overseas co-development studios, marketing agencies, middleware licenses, and platform-related fees — the primary clearing rail is a SWIFT MT103 wire with SAFE current-account trade-in-services declaration, a governing services agreement, invoice, withholding tax certificate, and evidence of economic substance. For physical imports such as server equipment, network hardware, and localized distribution devices, the WFOE may use a commercial letter of credit (MT700) or documentary collection, with full customs declarations, transport documents, insurance, and commercial invoices. Mandatory Audit Threshold Mandatory Audit for All Entities

Turnover: No quantitative balance-sheet, turnover, revenue, asset, or headcount threshold exempts an active Hong Kong company from audit. Under the Companies Ordinance (Cap 622), every non-dormant Hong Kong company must appoint an independent certified public accountant (practising) licensed under the Accounting and Financial Reporting Council Ordinance to audit its annual financial statements, regardless of size, revenue, assets, or number of employees. The only statutory exemption applies to a dormant private company that has not commenced business, is not carrying on business, has no significant accounting transactions during the relevant period, and has passed the necessary special resolution to forgo an audit. A parent company preparing group financial statements must obtain a statutory audit of its consolidated accounts. The absence of any size threshold means an active HK WFOE or MMORPG publishing entity with in-app purchase revenue, Apple and Google payout streams, and cross-border group arrangements is unconditionally subject to a mandatory annual CPA audit.
