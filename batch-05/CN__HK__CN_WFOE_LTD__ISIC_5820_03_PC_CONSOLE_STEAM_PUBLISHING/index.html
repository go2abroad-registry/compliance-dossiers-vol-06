Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None. The PRC Company Law does not impose a statutory minimum registered capital for ordinary limited liability companies or WFOEs engaged in software publishing. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA) and its local tax services; online portal: China Electronic Tax Bureau (etax.chinatax.gov.cn), including the Export Tax Rebate (Exemption) module and the electronic invoice issuing platform.

E-government filing portal

The WFOE is incorporated with the Administration for Market Regulation and automatically receives an 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which constitutes the statutory Tax Identification Number (TIN) for PRC tax purposes. The market regulator pushes the incorporation record to the STA, effecting automatic tax registration. The legal representative and appointed financial officer must then log on to the China Electronic Tax Bureau to complete the new-taxpayer confirmation, install the tax UKey/digital certificate, elect VAT taxpayer status (general or small-scale), activate electronic invoice issuance authority, and register the correct tax types. No additional VAT registration certificate is issued. Non-resident shareholders do not require a separate PRC TIN solely to own the WFOE; if the non-resident person has direct PRC-source income or a permanent establishment, a separate withholding registration/procedure is required.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None. The PRC does not recognise US-style check-the-box entity classification and provides no elective hybrid mismatch treatment. A WFOE is mandatorily treated as a PRC resident enterprise taxed at entity level on worldwide income. The only indirect-tax election available is voluntary registration as a general VAT taxpayer where annual taxable turnover is below the CNY 5,000,000 threshold.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

CNY 5,000,000 annual taxable turnover; all WFOEs with a business license are tax-registered upon incorporation, and exceeding this threshold makes general VAT taxpayer registration mandatory.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is available where the WFOE exports physical goods out of the PRC customs territory or supplies qualifying cross-border taxable services, including digital software publishing/licensing to Valve, PlayStation Store, or overseas end customers. The supply must be received and consumed outside China, the payment must be received in foreign currency by the WFOE, and the transaction must be correctly reported in the China Electronic Tax Bureau as a zero-rated/export supply. Pure digital downloads do not have a customs border crossing; for such supplies the statutory documentation is the cross-border service contract, platform payout/settlement statements, and foreign-exchange receipts rather than a physical export declaration.

  • PRC Customs Export Goods Declaration Form bearing the export declaration number and customs clearance record (required for physical media/goods exports)
  • Bill of Lading or sea/air waybill issued by the carrier evidencing export from a PRC customs port (not required for pure digital services)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or bank-issued foreign exchange receipt confirmation from the SAFE trade monitoring system evidencing the Valve/PlayStation payout to the WFOE
  • Export sale, copyright licensing, or digital publishing agreement with the overseas platform/licensee
  • Steamworks or PlayStation Partner payout statements reconciled with invoices, customs declarations, and foreign-exchange receipts

Fully digitalized electronic invoices are used under the Golden Tax Phase IV system. VAT special invoices must be issued through the China Electronic Tax Bureau, signed with the taxpayer's tax UKey, assigned a unique electronic invoice number, and uploaded to the STA in real time. Invoice data is automatically cross-checked with customs declarations, foreign-exchange receipts, and input-credit claims. Anti-fraud controls include digital signatures, tax-control codes, and mandatory electronic reconciliation before a special VAT invoice may be used.

The STA compares export declarations, electronic invoices, foreign-exchange receipts, contracts, and platform statements through the Golden Tax IV risk-control system. If the zero-rating is unsubstantiated, proceeds are received late without an approved extension, or domestic digital sales are misstated as exports, the STA will restore VAT liability with retrospective effect, charge late-payment surcharge at 0.05% per day, impose an administrative penalty of up to five times the tax involved for fraudulent export refund claims, downgrade the export tax-rebate credit rating, and refer egregious cases for criminal prosecution for export-tax-refund fraud.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Under the PRC foreign-exchange control framework, the Renminbi is broadly convertible for current-account trade and service transactions but remains strictly managed on the capital account. The WFOE must complete SAFE's direct-investment foreign-exchange registration through its designated settlement bank before receiving any equity capital. Inbound capital must enter a segregated FDI capital account and may be settled into RMB only under the pay-use model with a negative list; prohibited uses include repayment of existing offshore loans, entrusted loans, securities investments, purchase of wealth-management products, real-estate purchases outside the approved scope, and non-business-related foreign-exchange speculation. Current-account receipts from Steam and console-store publishing are treated as cross-border service-trade income after documentary authenticity verification by the receiving bank; the bank will review the publishing agreement, platform payout statements, tax filing records and the SAFE payment-purpose declaration. Dividend remittances to the Hong Kong shareholder are conditional on audited annual financial statements, corporate income tax clearance, withholding-tax certificates, a board dividend resolution and current-account dividend-remittance verification. In practical liquidity terms, the bank must be provided with notarized copies of the WFOE's business licence, social-credit code certificate, legal representative identity documents and the SAFE FDI registration code. Because the onshore RMB spot market is managed, settlement is executed at the market middle rate with a bank spread; no free capital-account exchange is available.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Statutory surrender ratio is zero; China abolished mandatory foreign-exchange surrender for current-account trade receipts. The WFOE may either retain export/service proceeds in a foreign-currency settlement account with a SAFE-designated bank or convert them into RMB at prevailing market rates. The legal collection requirement is that proceeds must be repatriated to the domestic settlement account by the payment date specified in the underlying Steam/PlayStation agreement, and any uncollected receivable outstanding more than 90 days after the invoice date must be reported to SAFE through the company's bank with supporting contracts and overdue-receivable explanation. Extension beyond 180 days without an agreed amendment may lead to a trade foreign-exchange monitoring downgrade and restriction to the B/C classification, which forces full settlement and per-document review. All receipts require an income declaration showing the SAFE current-account transaction code, the payer's registration identifier and the associated publishing contract.

Central bank supervision: The State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE), the delegated foreign-exchange authority of the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Offshore digital EMIs are effectively legally barred from acting as the WFOE's principal onshore treasury rail because they do not hold a deposit-taking banking licence from the National Financial Regulatory Administration and are not connected to SAFE's direct-investment and current-account monitoring system. They cannot receive FDI capital, execute SAFE registrations, perform RMB capital settlement, or issue verification-compliant trade receipts in mainland China. An EMI may be used only by an offshore Hong Kong treasury affiliate for collections outside mainland; any transfer of funds back to the WFOE must cross SAFE-controlled banking rails. Overall viability rating is low for primary treasury operations and conditionally medium for offshore collection, pooling or escrow in tandem with a licensed Tier-1 Chinese bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity from the Hong Kong parent should be remitted via SWIFT MT103 to the WFOE's segregated FDI foreign-currency capital account at a Tier-1 designated bank. The remittance instruction must quote the WFOE's unique social-credit code and the SAFE FDI registration certificate reference number, with the purpose stated as inbound direct investment equity injection to a wholly foreign-owned enterprise. The received foreign currency is quarantined from current-account sweep until authenticity verification is completed; the bank then processes the capital-account receipt and, once supporting contracts and use-plan documents are submitted, permits conversion into RMB under the pay-as-you-use capital settlement model. A certified public accountant must issue a capital verification report after the injection is booked, and the WFOE shall update SAFE's foreign-investment register within 30 days. The enterprise should avoid splitting large injections into repeated small transfers because regulatory monitoring thresholds and banking-referral exclusions apply.For recurring outbound payments such as Steam platform-hosted royalty distributions, licensing fees to foreign IP owners and PlayStation Store promotion-service fees, use SWIFT MT103 for USD or EUR remittances or CIPS cross-border RMB payment instructions when counterparties accept RMB. The service-export disbursement dossier must contain the signed agreement, tax-withholding certificate, invoice and platform settlement statement. For physical collector's editions and console peripherals, use an irrevocable confirmed letter of credit issued by a Tier-1 Chinese bank or documentary collections such as D/P and D/A; a standby letter of credit is acceptable to support milestone escrow. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% statutory enterprise income tax rate under Article 4 of the PRC Enterprise Income Tax Law (“EIT Law”) for all resident enterprises, including the PRC WFOE

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% statutory PRC withholding tax on dividends paid by a PRC resident WFOE to a non-resident corporate shareholder; the rate is reduced to 5% under Article 10 of the Mainland and Hong Kong Double Tax Arrangement if the Hong Kong shareholder is a beneficial owner and a Hong Kong tax resident company directly holding at least 25% of the capital of the WFOE.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Contemporaneous Local File is required if annual related-party tangible asset transfers exceed CNY 200 million; intangible asset transfers exceed CNY 100 million; financial asset transfers exceed CNY 100 million; or other related-party transactions including services exceed CNY 40 million. Master File preparation is required where annual related-party transactions exceed CNY 1 billion or where the ultimate parent group is required to prepare a Master File. The related-party transaction disclosure schedules must be filed with the annual CIT return whenever any related-party transaction occurs, regardless of amount.

Economic substance scope: The mainland WFOE is not subject to a standalone Economic Substance Regulations code equivalent to offshore financial centres, but the substance-over-form doctrine applies through EIT Law Article 47 general anti-avoidance, SAT Announcement [2019] No. 35 beneficial-owner requirements, and PRC case law under the Civil Code and Company Law. The Hong Kong parent must show that its Core Income-Generating Activities for holding the WFOE shares, funding the WFOE, licensing IP, or receiving dividends are actually performed in Hong Kong: meetings, negotiations, contracts, financing decisions, accounting, and treasury functions. The WFOE, in turn, must have real physical premises, employees, payroll, board decisions, and documented operations in China for game development, localisation, Steam/PlayStation publishing relationships, platform settlement, customer support, and content updates. If either entity is a hollow shell or the Shanghai/Hong Kong structure exists mainly to obtain treaty benefits, tax authorities may disregard the structure, impose withholding, reassess profits, and deny treaty relief.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

DTA abuse and artificial Hong Kong shell company used to obtain 5% dividend withholding relief

Statutory sanction: The PRC tax authority may deny treaty benefits under SAT Announcement [2019] No. 35 and apply the general anti-avoidance rule under EIT Law Article 47. Under TCAL Article 63, the underpaid withholding of 5% plus interest is recovered, and a penalty from 50% to 500% of the unpaid tax can be imposed. If criminal tax evasion is established under Criminal Law Article 201, the punishment is up to three years imprisonment and fines, or up to seven years for large amounts or other serious circumstances. The entity and the responsible directors are jointly exposed to criminal fines and per-person liability.

Director personal liability: Courts may pierce the corporate veil under PRC Company Law Article 23 where shareholders abuse corporate personality to evade debts or taxes and seriously harm creditors or state interests. Directors who approved the sham dividend structure or false beneficial-owner declarations can be ordered to make personal asset restitution, be named on the tax-related serious dishonesty blacklist, suffer exit/travel bans and restrictions from leaving mainland China, and face criminal prosecution as directly responsible persons under Criminal Law Articles 30 and 201.
Transfer pricing and economic substance fraud in Steam/PlayStation digital publishing revenue flows

Statutory sanction: The tax authority may adjust profits under EIT Law Article 41 and assess taxable income by estimation under EIT Law Article 44 where documentation is absent or false. Under EIT Law Article 48 and the Implementation Regulations, an interest charge is added at the prevailing central bank benchmark loan interest rate plus 5 percentage points for transfer pricing adjustments. Failure to file returns and supplementary schedules is penalized under TCAL Article 62 with fines of RMB 2,000 to RMB 10,000, but deliberate false under-reporting escalates to TCAL Article 63 tax evasion penalties of 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax and criminal liability.

Director personal liability: Directors and the financial controller who sign false financial statements or direct the suppression of taxable revenue may be personally liable for tax restitution through civil joint liability under PRC Company Law Article 23 and Civil Code Article 1168. As directly responsible officers of a unit crime, they may face criminal fines and imprisonment for tax evasion under Criminal Law Article 201, prohibitions on serving as directors or senior managers, and blacklist measures restricting exit, passports, business registration changes, and high-consumption activity.
Failure to withhold PRC tax on outbound dividends, interest, and royalties to Hong Kong and circumvention of cross-border payment disclosure

Statutory sanction: Under TCAL Article 69, a withholding agent that fails to withhold or fails to obtain and remit tax is subject to a penalty from 50% to 300% of the amount that should have been withheld, in addition to recovery of the underlying tax from the non-resident taxpayer where collectible. Late payment surcharges accrue under TCAL Article 32. If the mischaracterization is used to avoid tax, TCAL Article 63 and Criminal Law Article 201 may apply, with the full suite of criminal fines and imprisonment for illegal tax reduction or evasion.

Director personal liability: The WFOE directors who authorize cross-border remittances without withholding may incur personal joint and several liability for the unpaid withholding and penalties based on complicity and false filings. The legal representative and directors face court-ordered travel bans, exit restrictions through the tax blacklist mechanism, seizure or freezing of personal assets if the corporate veil is pierced under PRC Company Law Article 23, and criminal prosecution as the persons directly in charge where the non-withholding is found to be intentional tax evasion rather than an ordinary error.
