Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to an unrestricted software/AIGC services WFOE. The registered capital amount may be set by the shareholders in the Articles of Association. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA), with the national website portal www.chinatax.gov.cn and the provincial STA Electronic Tax Office e-filing/registration platforms; customs-related export clearance is administered by the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China through the China International Trade Single Window.

E-government filing portal

For the PRC Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE), the statutory Tax Identification Number is the 18-character Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) printed on the business license. After market-administration company formation, the WFOE must complete tax registration within 30 calendar days. The WFOE logs into the provincial STA Electronic Tax Office or visits the competent local STA service hall, then submits the business license, articles of association, legal representative identity certificate, registered address proof, bank account information, and shareholder/capital information. The tax authority assigns the taxpayer registration file, tax jurisdiction, VAT taxpayer type, and tax categories. Because China uses multi-license integration, no separate paper VAT registration certificate is issued; the USCC is the VAT and corporate income tax ID for the WFOE. For a foreign non-resident enterprise without a permanent establishment but with PRC-source income, the non-resident enterprise must complete non-resident tax record-filing via the Electronic Tax Office through the payer/withholding agent, submit a home-jurisdiction tax residence certificate and relevant contracts, and obtain a non-resident tax identification record for withholding-tax administration.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

An enterprise with annual taxable sales exceeding RMB 5,000,000 excluding VAT is obliged to register as a general VAT taxpayer; enterprises at or below that threshold may remain small-scale VAT taxpayers. A non-resident enterprise engaged in taxable sales in the PRC must either VAT register or have VAT withheld by the domestic purchaser.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Tangible goods exported from the PRC customs territory are zero-rated for VAT if they are physically exported under a valid Customs export declaration and all export sales proceeds are repatriated through licensed PRC banks and reported to the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE). Cross-border sales of qualifying digital software services, including AI avatar video generation platform services supplied to an overseas customer where the service is wholly performed and consumed outside the PRC, may be zero-rated or exempt if the WFOE is a general VAT taxpayer, the contract is with an offshore party, the service has no PRC domestic beneficiary, and the foreign currency consideration is repatriated to a PRC bank account within the prescribed FX period.

  • Customs Export Goods Declaration (the electronically transmitted and Customs-released export declaration)
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, or equivalent transport document evidencing physical export
  • Foreign Exchange Receipt Voucher / bank export-proceeds collection record domiciled through SAFE
  • Export sales contract or purchase order
  • Export VAT rebate filing documentation and underlying input-VAT special invoices of the exporting entity

All VAT invoices are issued through the STA tax-control invoice system (Golden Tax system). Special VAT invoices and fully digitalized electronic invoices must be transmitted in real time to the STA's national electronic invoice platform, carry the tax authority's digital signature and anti-fraud encryption, and be cleared against the taxpayer's VAT return. Qualified taxpayers may integrate ERP systems with the STA e-invoice application programming interface API for real-time issuance and reporting; unauthorized offline invoice printing, false invoice issuance, or non-reconciliation with the tax return triggers automatic tax-control alerts and penalty actions.

Export VAT zero-rating and refund claims are jointly scrutinised by STA, Customs, and SAFE. Missing, false, or mismatched customs declarations; non-arrival of goods; unrepatriated or circular FX receipts; sham offshore service arrangements; or forged bills of lading will result in denial of the claimed zero rate, full recovery of refunded VAT, daily overdue interest at 0.05%, late-payment surcharges, and administrative fines of 50%-500% of the tax evaded under the Tax Collection and Administration Law. Fraudulent conduct may be prosecuted as false invoice issuance or export tax refund fraud, with criminal liability including imprisonment and confiscation of property, and the entity may be downgraded in Customs credit classification, lose export rebate facilitation, and be placed on tax risk control watch-lists.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a distinctly bifurcated foreign exchange regime. The current account, covering trade in goods, services, and conventional profit remittances associated with verified business operations, is substantially convertible after genuine transaction verification. Banks under SAFE supervision exercise post-hoc auditing and require underlying contracts, invoices, customs declarations, tax certificates, and board resolutions. The capital account remains closely controlled. A WFOE established as a foreign direct investment vehicle receives inbound equity capital through a designated FDI capital settlement account, with the proceeds tracked under SAFE's Foreign Exchange Integrated Management Platform. RMB conversion for capital items is restricted and must be aligned with a declared negative-list business purpose. Profit repatriation to the Hong Kong parent is permitted only after annual audit, corporate income tax settlement, and satisfaction of the remittance bank's evidence requirements; a 10% withholding tax may apply unless a tax treaty claim under Mainland-HK arrangements reduces the rate. Currency convertibility for a software licensing and AI video generation business is therefore workable for current operational liquidity but requires disciplined documentary discipline for dividends, IP royalty outflows, and overseas SaaS subscription receipts. The WFOE cannot freely swap RMB for foreign currency without an underlying real transaction; capital flight transactions, round-tripping schemes, and fabricated service agreements are actively scrutinized. The practical effect is that a China WFOE needs to maintain a domestic RMB and dual-currency treasury stack, appoint a relationship manager with cross-border expertise, and pre-clear each material outward remittance with the branch compliance team to avoid frozen transfers.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export foreign currency proceeds must be repatriated to a domestic foreign exchange account in China within 180 days from the date of expected collection, subject to the contractually agreed settlement period. The mandatory conversion ratio is zero percent: since 2012 China abolished forced surrender, so the WFOE may freely retain export receipts in foreign currency or voluntarily settle them into RMB at commercial bank rates. SAFE requires the underlying customs declaration, commercial invoices, shipping/airway bills, and service delivery evidence for AI software exports to be archived for verification. Failure to repatriate within the statutory window or falsification of export documentation triggers SAFE penalties, blacklist revision, and forced suspension of the entity's cross-border collection rights.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) acting under the mandate of the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Offshore fintech EMIs and cross-border digital banking providers are not directly viable for the China WFOE as stand-alone bank accounts. PBOC and SAFE prohibit unlicensed foreign payment institutions from servicing onshore Chinese legal entities without an onshore payment business license and a clearing bank partner. Global platforms such as Stripe, Paddle, and Airwallex cannot open deposit accounts or conduct foreign exchange settlements inside China for the WFOE directly. They can be used only as foreign collection gateways for overseas B2B and B2C customers, with net funds periodically swept through a licensed domestic receiving bank via SWIFT MT103 or through a licensed cross-border payment institution under SAFE's cross-border payment regulatory pilot. The WFOE should treat such fintech rails as low-value collection conduits, not as treasury counterparties, and should maintain every customer subscription invoice and platform payout report for local bank KYC reviews. Any claim that a foreign EMI can replace a Chinese-tier-one bank account is a material compliance failure.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The Hong Kong shareholder must initiate a SWIFT MT103 transfer from its corporate account to the WFOE's designated FDI capital settlement account at a tier-one Chinese bank. The remittance instruction must clearly state the FX purpose code for direct investment capital injection and reference the SAFE Foreign Investment Enterprise registration file. The domestic bank will verify the capital injection against the registered foreign exchange information, the shareholder resolution, and the articles of association. Post-receipt, the WFOE must obtain a certified public accountant capital verification report and file it with the local market supervision authority. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File mandatory if any of the following related-party transaction thresholds are met in a tax year: RMB 200 million for tangible assets, RMB 100 million for intangible assets, RMB 100 million for financial assets, and RMB 40 million for services. Master File mandatory if aggregate related-party transactions exceed RMB 1 billion; a Country-by-Country Report is required when the MNE group's consolidated revenue exceeds RMB 5.5 billion.

Economic substance scope: China applies the OECD substance-over-form and Principal Purpose Test in relation to the DTA. The WFOE must carry on legitimate business operations in China, and the Hong Kong shareholder must have real economic substance in Hong Kong to qualify for treaty benefits. Both China's special tax adjustment rules and Hong Kong's FSIE economic substance rules require that risks, functions, and assets match the income. A WFOE with CIGA in China and supporting employees, software server infrastructure, and board oversight is necessary to avoid an attribution challenge; the lack of substance leads to denial of treaty benefits or PE exposure.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Article 63 of the Tax Administration Law imposes a penalty of 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax; Article 201 of the Criminal Law imposes imprisonment up to 7 years and fines for tax evasion exceeding the criminal threshold.

Director personal liability: Directors who order or knowingly permit false declarations are personally liable under the Company Law for the corporate tax debt and may have personal assets subject to restitution. The competent tax authority can restrict legal representatives from leaving China under the exit-clearance rules, and criminal liability (detention or imprisonment) may attach to the directly responsible director.
Shell Company / Treaty Abuse

Statutory sanction: The Chinese tax authority will deny the 5% DTA relief under the Beneficial Ownership rule and the Principal Purpose Test (Article 7 of BEPS MLI), back-charge the 10% withholding tax difference, and apply default interest and late-filling penalties. Under the Tax Administration Law Article 94, treaty abuse constitutes a ground for special tax adjustment.

Director personal liability: The directors of the WFOE who approve dividend distributions without verifying the HK shareholder's substance can be held personally responsible for the under-withheld tax, with restitution of the corporate assets; civil travel bans, asset freezes, and a director disqualification order may be issued. If the structure is construed as criminal tax evasion, the directors face imprisonment up to 7 years.
Anti-Money Laundering Transparency

Statutory sanction: Article 50 of the PRC Anti-Money Laundering Law imposes fines up to RMB 5 million on the entity, suspension or shutdown of operations; Article 191 of the Criminal Law punishes money-laundering with imprisonment from 5 to 10 years and fines for severe violations.

Director personal liability: The legal representative and responsible senior managers are subject to individual fines up to RMB 500,000, cannot depart the jurisdiction without tax clearance, and may be criminally prosecuted for money laundering, with civil asset seizure and a ban against acting as company directors for up to 10 years.
