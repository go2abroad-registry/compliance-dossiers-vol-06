Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA) and its in-charge local tax service bureau; electronic self-service portal: China Electronic Taxpayer Service System (e-Tax / Electronic Tax Bureau) at etax.chinatax.gov.cn. For the Hong Kong parent or shareholder, the Inland Revenue Department (IRD) of Hong Kong SAR and its eTax portal at www.ird.gov.hk are relevant for tax-residency and treaty-benefit certification.

E-government filing portal

A foreign non-resident entity that is not establishing a Chinese permanent establishment does not receive a Chinese Uniform Social Credit Code; it is identified in the STA withholding tax system by its home-country tax identification number. For the China-resident WFOE, the procedure is: (1) incorporation before the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) issues the Business License containing an 18-digit Uniform Social Credit Code (USCC), which is automatically transmitted to the STA; (2) within 30 calendar days of license issuance, the WFOE completes tax registration and duty identification in the Electronic Tax Bureau; (3) the USCC becomes the statutory TIN and VAT registration number; (4) the WFOE applies for e-invoice quota and, if its annual turnover will exceed RMB 5,000,000 or it so elects, General VAT taxpayer status; (5) the foreign investor records its shareholding through the capital account and State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) filing; (6) the Hong Kong shareholder applies to the IRD for a Hong Kong Tax Residency Certificate if treaty-reduced withholding on dividends, interest, or royalties is claimed; and (7) export zero-rating capabilities are flagged with the STA. No separate national tax registration form beyond the integrated SAMR-STA data exchange is required for the WFOE.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation - The WFOE is a PRC resident enterprise subject to enterprise income tax on worldwide income; no pass-through or fiscal transparency applies.

Available election forms

None - PRC tax law does not provide check-the-box or hybrid mismatch entity elections. Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory General VAT taxpayer registration is triggered when annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000 (CNY 5 million). Below this threshold a taxpayer may remain a small-scale taxpayer using simplified VAT calculation, but all WFOEs must complete tax registration at incorporation without any turnover waiver.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A PRC-resident WFOE may apply the zero VAT rate to helpdesk and SaaS services supplied to overseas customers where the buyer is located and consumes the service entirely outside China, the service is in a zero-rated category such as software service, information system service, business process management service, or offshore service outsourcing, consideration is contemporaneously received from outside China or in cross-border RMB, the transaction is properly documented, and the export is filed in the STA zero-rated service refund and exemption procedure. Zero-rating permits refund of residual input VAT. Physical exported goods are zero-rated only upon customs export clearance and collection of foreign exchange.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a managed floating exchange rate regime with a statutory distinction between current account and capital account transactions. Current account items, such as software subscription receipts from overseas customers and payments for cloud infrastructure procurement, are generally convertible once banks verify the genuineness of the underlying transaction against invoices, contracts, customs or service-export evidence. Capital account items, including FDI equity injections from the Hong Kong parent, cross-border loans, and dividend repatriations to the non-resident shareholder, require mandatory SAFE registration, an approved legal capital scheme, and any applicable risk-based screening. The WFOE must first complete the SAFE Foreign Exchange Business Registration and open an FDI capital exchange settlement account at a PRC domestic bank. Foreign-currency capital proceeds may be settled into RMB only upon presentation of documentation proving the intended use, and any proceeds exceeding the permitted current-account balance cap must be held within the settlement account or converted subject to free movement constraints. Profit repatriation to the HK parent is subject to audited financial statements, tax clearance for withholding income tax, and bank compliance review, but no separate approval beyond SAFE registration is required for bona fide dividends. Export proceeds from software services have no mandatory surrender obligation; however, receipt must be collected through designated banks within the regular trade-receipt reconciliation window, effectively 180 days, and foreign currency may be retained under the bank's account cap.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE current-account administration rules, exporter foreign exchange receipts must be brought back into the PRC through the bank that completed the export collection verification, normally within 180 days of the service completion date or customs export declaration. The mandatory surrender ratio is 0%; there is no forced conversion into RMB. The WFOE may hold export and service receipt proceeds in a foreign exchange settlement account subject to SAFE's account cap, or convert into RMB at the spot rate on commercial terms.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) / State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Very Low. Foreign fintech EMIs cannot open domestic WFOE accounts or serve as a settlement channel because they are not licensed as PRC banking institutions by the National Financial Regulatory Administration and have no direct access to SAFE's capital registration, foreign exchange conversion, or CIPS clearing rails. They may support only the front-end collection of cross-border SaaS subscription payments, but the treasury-accounting and SAFE-reportable elements must still be distilled into a licensed PRC bank. Consequently, no foreign EMI can serve as the WFOE's operative bank account for capital injections, RMB conversion, tax payments, or repatriation.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The HK parent should remit the FDI equity subscription funds from a corporate bank account in Hong Kong to the WFOE's certified FDI Capital Settlement Account at an approved PRC settlement bank using SWIFT MT103 with the SAFE-approved foreign exchange purpose code for Foreign Direct Investment Equity Subscription. Before the transfer, the WFOE must complete SAFE foreign exchange registration through the bank and obtain the FDI registration voucher and Unique Business Number. Once received, the foreign-currency capital is held under capital account supervision and can be converted into RMB only on a payment-by-payment basis with supporting use-of-funds documentation. A licensed PRC certified public accountant must then issue a capital verification report based on the bank settlement receipt and company registration confirmation to complete equity recording.For software and helpdesk service settlements, the primary clearing rail is an SWIFT MT103 wire executed through the relationship bank, carrying a current-account payment purpose code and accompanied by the service contract, invoice, tax certificate, and where required, delivery or usage evidence. For physical hardware or equipment procurement embedded in the helpdesk solution, the WFOE may use a sight commercial letter of credit under UCP600 or documentary collections to protect title, though those instruments remain inefficient for recurring SaaS subscriptions. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Very Low. Foreign fintech EMIs cannot open domestic WFOE accounts or serve as a settlement channel because they are not licensed as PRC banking institutions by the National Financial Regulatory Administration and have no direct access to SAFE's capital registration, foreign exchange conversion, or CIPS clearing rails. They may support only the front-end collection of cross-border SaaS subscription payments, but the treasury-accounting and SAFE-reportable elements must still be distilled into a licensed PRC bank. Consequently, no foreign EMI can serve as the WFOE's operative bank account for capital injections, RMB conversion, tax payments, or repatriation.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: RMB 100 million in annual related-party service transactions, RMB 200 million in related-party tangible-property transactions, and RMB 100 million in related-party intangible-property or financing transactions trigger mandatory Local File preparation; a Master File is required when cross-border related-party transactions in the aggregate exceed RMB 1 billion or the group meets the STA's higher reporting thresholds.

Economic substance scope: For the Hong Kong parent, Hong Kong's Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) require that core income-generating activities, strategic decisions, senior management, employment, premises and record-keeping functions be performed in Hong Kong for relevant activity categories; for the Chinese WFOE, the STA imputes economic substance through the beneficial owner and General Anti-Avoidance Rules, and requires real employees, helpdesk infrastructure, customer contracts, call-center management, pricing discretion, billing systems and risk assumption. A letterbox Hong Kong holding company with no CIGA or a Mainland WFOE with no operational substance will fail the beneficial ownership and commercial substance tests, causing treaty benefits to be denied and the transaction to be recharacterized.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion by False Invoicing and Fictitious Cross-Border Service Fees

Statutory sanction: PRC Tax Collection and Administration Law Article 63 imposes a penalty of 50% to 500% of the unpaid tax plus a 0.05% daily late-payment surcharge; Criminal Law Article 205 punishes the issuance or use of false special VAT invoices with fines and imprisonment, and Criminal Law Article 201 applies to tax evasion exceeding RMB 50,000 and more than 10% of tax payable.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil may be pierced under PRC Company Law Article 23 where the company is used to evade taxes and corporate assets are insufficient; directors and beneficial owners are personally liable to restore misappropriated assets, face freeze orders on personal bank accounts, entry-exit travel bans and blacklisting under the Joint Punishment for Tax Dishonesty regime, and may receive criminal sentences of up to seven years for tax evasion or up to seven years for false-invoice fraud.
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance in Intercompany Helpdesk and SaaS Contracts

Statutory sanction: PRC CIT Law Article 41 empowers the STA to make a special tax adjustment to the arm's length price; CIT Law Article 44 permits deemed assessment where contemporaneous documentation is absent; TCAL Article 63 or 64 applies for inaccurate or false returns with a penalty of 50% to 500% of unpaid tax, plus interest at the benchmark lending rate plus five percentage points under special tax adjustment rules.

Director personal liability: Directors who execute the intercompany agreements and approve fee payments without commercial justification may be required to reimburse the company for the tax adjustment, surcharges and penalties; where the transferred funds are distributed to shareholders, the court may peel back limited liability, impose asset restitution, and issue travel bans/blacklisting. If the documentation is supported by fictitious contracts and false invoices, the directors are criminally exposed under Criminal Law Article 205 with potential imprisonment, in addition to civil liability for the shortfall.
Treaty Abuse, Dividend Stripping and Shell-Company Beneficial Ownership Failure

Statutory sanction: Under STA Public Notice No. 9 of 2018, the beneficial ownership test fails, treaty benefits are denied, and the WFOE must withhold the difference between the 10% statutory rate and the 5% claimed rate; CIT Law Article 47 permits the recharacterization of the shell under the General Anti-Avoidance Rules, and TCAL Article 69 imposes a 50% to 300% penalty on the withholding-agent failure; Hong Kong's ESR may also impose additional Hong Kong tax and penalties on the parent.

Director personal liability: The beneficial owner and the directors causing the artificial structure are subject to corporate-veil piercing under PRC Company Law Article 23, civil restitution equal to the unpaid withholding tax and interest, travel bans and tax-dishonesty blacklisting; where the dividend scheme is supported by false beneficial-ownership certificates or fabricated board minutes, criminal liability under Criminal Law Article 201 may result in imprisonment of up to seven years, and the personal bank accounts and exit-entry permits of responsible directors may be restrained during investigation.
