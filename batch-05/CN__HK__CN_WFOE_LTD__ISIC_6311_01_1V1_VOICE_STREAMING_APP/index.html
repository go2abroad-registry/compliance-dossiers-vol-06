Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

For the interactive audio chatroom and companion social application industry, which is classified as a Medium-Risk Enhanced AML/CFT business, the use of a virtual office or a commercial mailbox as the sole registered address is categorically rejected by most Chinese municipal tax authorities and licensed banks. The PRC Company Law (2023 Revision) and the Measures for the Administration of the Registration of Enterprise Name and Address require a physical business premise that can be inspected. Both the local Market Supervision Administration and the tax authority (State Taxation Administration) expect to find active employees, operating equipment, and lease agreements. A virtual office may be accepted briefly during the early pre-incorporation phase but will prevent bank account opening and trigger immediate AML red flags, as it fails the 'place of effective management' test and the commercial substance test under the PRC General Anti-Avoidance Tax Rules. The only circumstance where a commercial mailing address is tolerated is when it is an ancillary correspondence address alongside a physical registered office located within the same city; still, the physical office must be fully furnished and staffed. Therefore, the mandatory statutory registered address must be a non-residential, purpose-built office space with a verifiable lease term of at least 12 months, and the company must maintain the original lease deed, utility bills, and employee roster available for authority inspection.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; electronic filing and registration through the national Electronic Tax Service portal (e-Tax platform) administered by STA

E-government filing portal

The WFOE is incorporated under the Company Law of the PRC and obtains its 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) from the State Administration for Market Regulation at incorporation. The USCC is the statutory Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) for both Corporate Income Tax and VAT, and no separate VAT ID is issued. Within 30 days of the business license issuance, the WFOE must complete tax registration at the competent STA tax office, linking the USCC to the tax filing account. For a foreign non-resident entity without a permanent establishment or Chinese establishment but with China-sourced income, the non-resident entity must obtain a separate non-resident enterprise TIN by filing the statutory non-resident tax registration declaration with STA, either directly or through a PRC withholding agent, accompanied by incorporation certificates, notarized constitutional documents, authorisation and identity proof, proof of China-sourced income contracts, and financial statements evidencing the taxable nexus. The WFOE, being a PRC resident enterprise, is automatically treated as a domestic taxpayer; non-resident shareholders do not automatically receive a PRC TIN unless they are directly subject to a PRC filing or withholding obligation.

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

All VAT taxpayers must register within 30 days of business establishment. Mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration is triggered when annual VAT taxable turnover exceeds CNY 5,000,000 over any rolling 12-month period. Small-scale taxpayers remain below CNY 5,000,000 annual turnover, but non-resident suppliers of digital services to non-VAT-registered customers in China have no VAT registration threshold and are generally required to register or have VAT withheld, irrespective of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exported tangible goods are eligible for VAT zero-rating or export VAT rebate if the goods are physically exported from Chinese Customs detention territory with a valid export customs declaration and the sales proceeds are received in a permitted foreign currency and repatriated to China within the statutory deadline. Qualifying cross-border services, including offshore digital voice streaming and social application services supplied by the WFOE to overseas users or non-resident enterprises, are also zero-rated under PRC VAT Law where the services are rendered entirely outside the mainland or consumed abroad; the exporter must evidence the cross-border nature through the offshore service contract, export service invoice, platform settlement records, foreign-currency collection from foreign counterparties, and the corporate trail showing the customer is a non-resident. The voice streaming app revenue collected through Apple IAP and Google Play Billing qualifies for zero-ratings subject to adequate documentation that the digital service is supplied to overseas consumers outside the customs territory.

  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (transport document proving physical export from the PRC)
  • Commercial Invoice issued to the overseas buyer or offshore platform

China operates the Fully Digitalized Electronic Invoice (digital e-invoice) system under the Golden Tax Phase IV initiative. VAT-registered enterprises must issue all statutory invoices through the STA e-Tax portal or API-integrated software system, which performs real-time validation, assigns the invoice number and tax-control code, and clears the fiscal invoice on the national tax control blockchain before delivery. The digital e-invoice must contain the anti-fraud unique electronic verification code and cryptographic stamp issued by STA. Enterprises must transmit e-invoices electronically to buyers and cannot generate valid VAT invoices outside the cleared electronic invoicing system. Compliance with STA digital invoice data standards and special invoice rules is mandatory.

STA performs cross-audits combining customs export records, transport logistics, service performance logs, foreign exchange receipt and repatriation records, and Apple/Google platform settlement statements to substantiate export zero-rating. Where zero-rating is claimed on the basis of fraudulent documentation, inflated values, unverified customs declarations, or failure to repatriate export proceeds within the statutory deadline, the tax authority will reverse the zero-rating, require the taxpayer to pay the output VAT underpaid, collect retroactive interest and late payment surcharges at 0.05% per day, and impose administrative penalties ranging from 0.5 to 5 times the tax amount involved. Serious cases of export VAT rebate fraud trigger criminal liability under Article 204 of the Criminal Law of the PRC, including imprisonment, criminal fines, freezing of capital, and confiscation of property. Additionally, the exporting enterprise may be downgraded in STA tax credit rating and barred from export rebate facilitation for a statutory correction period.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The People's Republic of China maintains a managed floating exchange rate regime in which the yuan (CNY) is convertible only for genuine current-account transactions, such as trade in goods and services, royalties, cross-border e-commerce settlement, and profit distributions derived from operating profits, after documentary verification. Capital-account items remain subject to a registration-and-approval model administered by SAFE; inbound equity capital for a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise must be declared and registered under the Foreign Investment Framework, injected through an onshore capital escrow account, and monitored until the paid-in capital is reported to the SAFE system. Profit remittance by the WFOE is permitted as a current-account dividend when the enterprise has completed its annual audit, the board has passed a lawful distribution resolution, the corporate income tax withholding certificate is presented, and foreign-exchange verification is satisfied. In practice, dividend outflows require submission of audited financial statements, tax filing proof, and bank-level token-level checks against the declared capital contribution. Thus CNY is not freely convertible outside the boundary of genuine trade and investment; any attempt to treat offshore CNH liquidity as fungible with onshore CNY would violate the capital administration rules and expose all parties to SAFE penalties and the bank to regulatory enforcement.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE's Trade in Goods Foreign Exchange Administration Measures, export proceeds must be repatriated to a domestic foreign-exchange settlement account within 90 days of the customs export declaration date, unless a deferred-receipt filing has been approved. There is no mandatory conversion ratio: the exporter may retain 100% of the foreign currency in the settlement account or sell it to a designated bank at the market rate. The reconciliation through the China International Trade Single Window must match the export declaration value, the invoice, the customs record, and the actual FX receipt; otherwise the bank will suspend settlement and refer the matter to SAFE.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The inbound injection must comply with SAFE's FDI registration. The Hong Kong parent must first complete the SAFE foreign-exchange registration for the WFOE and obtain the FDI payment confirmation. The parent then issues a SWIFT MT103/MT202 or CIPS instruction to the designated onshore bank account, using the SAFE-prescribed foreign-exchange purpose code for equity injection into a foreign-invested enterprise and inserting the SAFE registration number in the remittance information field. The bank verifies that the remitter matches the registered investor, credits the funds to the capital escrow foreign-exchange account, and, after the SAFE system confirms receipt, allows conversion into RMB. A certified public accountant issues the capital verification report, and the WFOE files the report with the market regulation authority. No drawings against the capital account for daily operations are permitted until the SAFE capital-item information system is updated and the bank releases the funds against the approved use list.All disbursements should be made from the onshore operational CNY account using CIPS for RMB counterparties or SWIFT MT103 for cross-border FX payments; for high-value trade and service contracts, an irrevocable commercial letter of credit or documentary collection may be used to provide payment security and title control. Every MT103 must contain the underlying contract and invoice reference, SAFE current-account purpose code, detailed trade or service description, counterparty bank details, and, where applicable, proof of service completion such as streaming traffic reports. Dividend payments to the Hong Kong parent require the board resolution, audited financial statements, corporate income tax withholding certificate, and SAFE profit-remittance declarations. The bank will reject any split or de-aggregated payment pattern designed to evade transaction monitoring.Onshore deliverable RMB/foreign-currency forward contracts with a genuine current-account or verified capital-account exposureRMB/foreign-currency foreign-exchange swaps for short-term liquidity and working-capital matchingCross-currency swaps and currency options that have SAFE-recognized underlying transactions and are executed through a licensed onshore bankCombination structures such as participating forwards, collars, and ratio forwards limited to the amount covered by trade receivables or payablesOffshore non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are not permissible for the onshore WFOE; NDFs may be used only by the offshore group level with no direct onshore mandate unless a specific SAFE/PBOC no-action or qualification is obtained

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Rating: Low; legally constrained for onshore use. Offshore fintech EMI providers such as Wise, Revolut, or Airwallex do not hold a PBOC Payment Business License or SAFE foreign-exchange license, and therefore cannot open a domestic RMB current account, obtain a CNAPS sort code, interface with CIPS, pay PRC taxes, or process payroll for the WFOE. They are legally barred from serving as the principal treasury bank for a mainland China entity. Their only permissible role is to support the Hong Kong parent in offshore HKD/CNH collection before capital is injected into China through a licensed onshore bank. Relying on an offshore EMI for operational clearing inside China would create unauthorised cross-border money-transmission exposure and can trigger AML/CTF and SAFE enforcement.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File: RMB 200 million for related-party tangible asset transfers; RMB 100 million for intangible-asset transfers, financial-asset transfers, and service provision; RMB 40 million for other connected-party transactions. Master File: aggregate annual related-party transactions of RMB 1 billion or more.

Economic substance scope: China does not apply an offshore ESR regime, but the general anti-abuse rule under CIT Law Art. 47 and the beneficial-ownership substance rules in SAT Statements 7/2015 and 9/2018 require the WFOE to perform substantive Core Income-Generating Activities in China: strategic management and board decision-making, app/platform development, user and streamer contracting, content moderation, billing via IAP, and treasury oversight. A HK conduit that merely holds IP or invoices Apple/Google without employees, premises, and decisions in Hong Kong will be pierced and all profit re-attributed to the Chinese WFOE; the WFOE must also file contemporaneous TP documentation and benchmark its transactions under the most appropriate arm's-length method.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion through offshore revenue diversion

Statutory sanction: CIT Law Arts. 41, 44, and 47 permit the tax authority to disregard the arrangement and assess under-reported Chinese taxable income; TCAL Art. 63 imposes a fine of 50% to 5 times the evaded tax; in severe cases, PRC Criminal Law Art. 201 imposes up to 7 years' imprisonment and fines.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil may be pierced under PRC Company Law Art. 20 for abuse of corporate form to evade taxes; directors who signed false returns or issued fake invoices are jointly and severally liable for the tax and penalties, face freezing of personal assets and an exit ban under TCAL Art. 44, and can be prosecuted as criminal co-principals with imprisonment.
Withholding tax failure and fraudulent DTA claim

Statutory sanction: TCAL Art. 69 requires the withholding agent to withhold and remit; failure to do so results in a penalty of 50% to 3 times the withheld amount plus late interest, and the non-resident taxpayer remains liable. SAT may recover the shortfall with interest and deny the treaty relief.

Director personal liability: The board that resolved the unlawful dividend or signed the false treaty-benefit statement is exposed to civil restitution under Company Law Art. 149; travel restrictions and blacklisting under the SAT/immigration joint punishment mechanisms apply; intentional withholding evasion can trigger criminal liability under Criminal Law Art. 201.
Shell-company abuse and transfer-pricing documentation non-compliance

Statutory sanction: CIT Law Art. 44 allows the tax bureau to make a deemed income assessment based on comparable non-connected parties; TCAL Art. 62 and 63 impose filing fines and 50%-5x understatement penalties; GAAR re-attribution under CIT Law Art. 47 with a 10-year back-assessment period applies.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly concealed the absence of substance or refused to provide TP documentation risk personal joint liability under Company Law Art. 20, asset seizure, denial of exit via exit-control lists, and criminal 'tax evasion by fraud' with sentences of up to 7 years.
