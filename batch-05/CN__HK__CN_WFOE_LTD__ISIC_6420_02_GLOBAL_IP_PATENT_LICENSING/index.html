Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None. Hong Kong has no minimum paid-up or authorized share capital requirement and no blocked escrow account rule. A private company limited by shares may be incorporated with one subscriber share of HK$1.00; any issued shares must be paid per the statutory terms, and capital can later be increased by ordinary or special resolution.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA); electronic filing through the STA-designated 'China Tax Electronic Tax Bureau' (e-Tax) portal and the dedicated Export Tax Rebate System.

E-government filing portal

A Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE) must first secure a PRC business license containing the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) from the local Administration for Market Regulation. The USCC is automatically the statutory taxpayer identification number (TIN) for the WFOE. Within 30 days of business licence issuance, the WFOE must complete PRC tax registration at its competent district-level STA office through the e-Tax Bureau, filing the corporate articles of association, investor identity documentation, board resolution on tax matters, evidence of registered address, banking account information, and financial institution capital verification data. The WFOE is registered as a VAT general taxpayer or small-scale VAT taxpayer, an income tax payer for corporate income tax (CIT), and a withholding agent for employee individual income tax. For a foreign non-resident parent in Hong Kong: no PRC TIN is required if the non-resident has no permanent establishment in China; the WFOE, as payer of royalties, must withhold CIT and VAT on cross-border royalty remittances and file the withholding remittance returns. To claim treaty benefits under the Mainland and Hong Kong Arrangement for the Avoidance of Double Taxation, the Hong Kong parent obtains a Certificate of Hong Kong Resident from the Inland Revenue Department of Hong Kong SAR and files the application for treaty relief with the STA before or at the time of withholding.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation - the WFOE is a PRC resident enterprise taxed at a 25% corporate income tax rate on worldwide income; no pass-through or transparent treatment exists under PRC tax law.

Available election forms

None. The PRC does not provide a check-the-box pass-through election for limited liability enterprises. The WFOE is automatically an entity-level taxpayer. It may elect simplified VAT treatment as a small-scale taxpayer while annual taxable sales remain below the CNY 5,000,000 threshold, and it may apply for treaty-based withholding reductions with the STA; hybrid mismatch elections are not recognised.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

For incorporated enterprises there is no turnover-based VAT registration threshold; registration is mandatory upon business license issuance. The compulsory general VAT taxpayer threshold is annual taxable sales (exclusive of VAT) exceeding CNY 5,000,000; below that, the WFOE can remain a small-scale VAT taxpayer subject to the simplified 3% VAT collection rate, unless voluntary general taxpayer status is applied for.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export zero-rating is available where the supply is a physical export of goods declared to Chinese Customs, or where the WFOE supplies a service or intangible asset to an overseas party that is expressly enumerated as zero-rated in Annex 4 of Cai Shui [2016] No. 36 (e.g. qualifying R&D, design, software, information system, business process management or offshore outsourcing services) and the service/asset is consumed entirely outside the PRC. Patent and trademark sub-licensing royalties, however, are normally subject to VAT at 6% when the income is consideration for use rights; if the arrangement constitutes a qualifying technology transfer or technical service supplied from China to an offshore recipient, the WFOE may instead claim VAT exemption or zero-rating only by proving: (i) the overseas party is the true recipient; (ii) the rights are exploited entirely outside China; (iii) no goods or real estate within China benefit from the service; and (iv) the foreign-exchange consideration is received from outside China. The exact statutory list in Annex 4 must be matched, and the e-Tax export zero-rating/refund declaration must be filed before the statutory deadline.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Export Goods Declaration) generated through the China International Trade Single Window for physical goods exports
  • Transport document evidencing export movement - Bill of Lading, Air Waybill or multimodal transport document
  • Commercial invoice, packing list and, for IP licensing, the underlying trademark/patent licence agreement and chain-of-title evidence
  • Bank receipt and cross-border settlement voucher showing the inward foreign-currency remittance from the overseas counterparty (SAFE-monitored foreign exchange receipt record)
  • Export VAT zero-rating/refund declaration record in the STA Export Tax Rebate System, matching the export customs declaration, e-invoice (with QR code validation), payment receipt and forex repatriation data

All VAT invoices must be issued through the STA Digital Electronic Invoice (All-Electronic Invoice) platform operated under the Golden Tax Phase IV system. Each invoice is transmitted to the STA fiscal database in real time, assigned a unique nationwide invoice number, and digitally signed with an anti-counterfeiting QR code before delivery to the buyer. Enterprises must integrate their ERP systems with STA-approved APIs for direct e-invoice generation and clearance; manual offline use of legacy mechanical tax-control equipment is no longer permitted for general taxpayers, and prescribed substitutes/fallback procedures must be lodged with the e-Tax Bureau before any permitted offline issuance.

The STA and Chinese Customs may launch a joint desk audit comparing export declarations, VAT invoices, logistics movement, royalty contracts, overseas beneficial ownership and SAFE foreign-exchange receipts. If zero-rating is unsubstantiated or fraudulent, the claimed VAT refund is withdrawn, the WFOE is charged late payment interest at 0.05% per day, and an administrative penalty of 50% to 5 times the tax evaded may be imposed under the PRC Tax Collection Administration Law. Deliberate use of forged customs, forex or invoice documentation to obtain export VAT refunds is criminal tax fraud under Article 204 of the PRC Criminal Law, punishable by imprisonment or criminal detention for up to three years for minor amounts, from three to ten years for large amounts, and from ten years to life imprisonment and confiscation of property.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a managed current-account-convertible system in which genuine trade, service, royalty, dividend and interest payments can be converted and remitted through designated domestic banks on the strength of underlying documentation, without requiring prior SAFE approval in each case. Capital account transactions, however, remain tightly controlled: the inbound equity capital injection into the WFOE is a capital account item requiring SAFE Foreign Direct Investment registration, a local bank capital account, and post-entry reporting, while dividends and royalty payments are treated as current account items requiring only documentary verification. For profit remittance, the WFOE must complete its annual statutory audit, corporate income tax final settlement, and any withholding tax settlement; the bank will independently examine the board profit distribution resolution, audited financial statements, tax payment and clearance certificates, and proof of shareholder identity. Any mismatch between the licensing agreement, beneficiary name, tax identification number or source of funds will delay or block the remittance. The HK parent structure does not remove PRC capital controls; SAFE still sees through the offshore holding layer for the onshore WFOE's capital, dividends and royalty flows.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE trade administration, export proceeds must be repatriated into a domestic bank account within the collection period evidenced in the export contract, generally not exceeding 180 days from the customs declaration date; longer credit terms require a SAFE trade-credit registration or bank-supported extension. The mandatory conversion ratio is currently zero: a WFOE may retain up to 100% of legitimate export proceeds in a foreign exchange current account or sell them into RMB at the prevailing market rate through a designated bank. All export receipts remain subject to SAFE's electronic monitoring and the bank must be able to reconcile the underlying customs declarations, invoices, bills of lading and contract terms before any subsequent use of the retained funds.

Central bank supervision: The People's Bank of China (PBOC) and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) are the joint central bank and foreign exchange administration authorities for the PRC; SAFE administers all FX regulatory approvals and bank-level compliance for the onshore WFOE, while the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) is relevant only for treasury operations conducted through the Hong Kong parent outside mainland China.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low and legally non-viable for custody or rails. Mainland China does not issue standalone EMI licences; only PBOC-licensed commercial banks may open corporate settlement accounts, perform FX conversion, and execute SAFE verification duties. A Hong Kong or overseas fintech EMI cannot lawfully hold onshore funds or act as bank of record for the WFOE. At best, a fintech can provide a white-label reporting interface over a licensed Tier-1 bank account, but the underlying treasury rails must remain with a qualified PRC commercial bank.

Mandatory Audit Threshold Mandatory Audit for All Entities

Turnover: Hong Kong has no turnover, balance-sheet, or headcount exemption for an active company. Every company incorporated under the Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622) that is not a dormant company must prepare financial statements and appoint a certified public accountant who holds a practising certificate to audit them. A company qualifies as dormant only if it has no significant accounting transactions during the period; otherwise, because an IP licensing HoldCo earns royalty income and incurs intercompany expenses, independent CPA audit is mandatory. This requirement applies regardless of the company being a small private company or an SME.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% statutory withholding enterprise income tax on dividends paid by a Chinese resident enterprise to a non-resident enterprise shareholder; reduced to 5% under the Mainland-Hong Kong DTA if the Hong Kong beneficial owner directly holds at least 25% of the WFOE and meets the beneficial ownership and anti-abuse conditions.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Annual return must be filed and final tax settled on or before 31 May of the year following the end of the fiscal year; quarterly prepayment declarations are due within 15 days after the close of each quarter.

Box-by-Box Return Breakdown (non-resident focus)

BoxField Non-resident guidanceTax-evasion pitfall
Line 1 - Total Revenue (Operating Revenue) Gross operating revenue from all business sources, including patent royalty income, trademark licensing fees, technical service fees and sub-licensing revenue. Report gross royalty revenue from Chinese and foreign licensees on a gross basis; do not net out China VAT, overseas withholding taxes, or related-party deductions. For a WFOE receiving sub-licensing royalties from a Hong Kong IP HoldCo, ensure the contract payment structure and transfer pricing policy are consistent with the tax invoice and foreign-exchange filings, and recognize income under accrual accounting in the year to which it is economically attributable. Failure to file: statutory penalty of RMB 2,000 to RMB 10,000, and RMB 2,000 to RMB 10,000 for serious circumstances; late-payment surcharge of 0.05% per day on unpaid tax; under-declaration or tax evasion: fine of 50% to 500% of underpaid tax; criminal referral for tax evasion where unpaid tax exceeds RMB 50,000 and is more than 10% of the tax payable; directors and legal representatives also face exit restriction and tax blacklisting and may be banned from leaving China before all tax liabilities are settled.
Line 2 - Total Cost of Sales (Operating Costs) Direct costs deducted against operating revenue, including patent amortization, IP legal registration and maintenance costs, technical and licensing fees paid to third parties, and capitalized acquisition costs being amortized. Deduct only arm's-length intercompany royalty payments to the Hong Kong related party that are evidenced by valid contracts, patent assignment records, and amortization schedules. Payments made overseas without proper foreign-exchange registration or without withholding tax clearance cannot be deducted until fully compliant. Failure to file: statutory penalty of RMB 2,000 to RMB 10,000, and RMB 2,000 to RMB 10,000 for serious circumstances; late-payment surcharge of 0.05% per day on unpaid tax; under-declaration or tax evasion: fine of 50% to 500% of underpaid tax; criminal referral for tax evasion where unpaid tax exceeds RMB 50,000 and is more than 10% of the tax payable; directors and legal representatives also face exit restriction and tax blacklisting and may be banned from leaving China before all tax liabilities are settled.
Line 3 - Taxes and Surcharges Local taxes and surcharges other than Enterprise Income Tax and VAT, such as urban maintenance and construction tax, education surcharges, stamp duty and real estate tax. Do not deduct corporate income tax itself, any late payment surcharge, tax penalties, or withholding tax paid on behalf of the Hong Kong non-resident. The payer's inability to pass on foreign WHT to the WFOE is a non-deductible expense. Failure to file: statutory penalty of RMB 2,000 to RMB 10,000, and RMB 2,000 to RMB 10,000 for serious circumstances; late-payment surcharge of 0.05% per day on unpaid tax; under-declaration or tax evasion: fine of 50% to 500% of underpaid tax; criminal referral for tax evasion where unpaid tax exceeds RMB 50,000 and is more than 10% of the tax payable; directors and legal representatives also face exit restriction and tax blacklisting and may be banned from leaving China before all tax liabilities are settled.
Line 4 - Selling Expenses Marketing, royalty collection, sales commission, and distribution expenses. Allocate expenses in accordance with the transfer pricing policy; expenses paid to non-resident related parties must be supported by service agreements, proof of actual service delivery, and Core Income-Generating Activities (CIGA) evidence of the non-resident service provider. Failure to file: statutory penalty of RMB 2,000 to RMB 10,000, and RMB 2,000 to RMB 10,000 for serious circumstances; late-payment surcharge of 0.05% per day on unpaid tax; under-declaration or tax evasion: fine of 50% to 500% of underpaid tax; criminal referral for tax evasion where unpaid tax exceeds RMB 50,000 and is more than 10% of the tax payable; directors and legal representatives also face exit restriction and tax blacklisting and may be banned from leaving China before all tax liabilities are settled.
Line 5 - General and Administrative Expenses Management fees, office rent, professional fees, R&D expenditures, and head-office cost allocations. Management service fees paid to a Hong Kong parent are deductible only if the parent demonstrates substantive service provision, benefit to the WFOE, duplication-free charges, and arm's-length profit markup; otherwise the expense is disallowed and taxed as a disguised dividend or constructive distribution. Failure to file: statutory penalty of RMB 2,000 to RMB 10,000, and RMB 2,000 to RMB 10,000 for serious circumstances; late-payment surcharge of 0.05% per day on unpaid tax; under-declaration or tax evasion: fine of 50% to 500% of underpaid tax; criminal referral for tax evasion where unpaid tax exceeds RMB 50,000 and is more than 10% of the tax payable; directors and legal representatives also face exit restriction and tax blacklisting and may be banned from leaving China before all tax liabilities are settled.
Line 6 - Financial Expenses Interest expense, bank charges, exchange losses and financing costs. Interest expense on intercompany debt from the Hong Kong parent must comply with the thin capitalization rule (2:1 debt-to-equity ratio for non-financial enterprises) and special tax adjustment rules; excess interest is non-deductible and may be recharacterized as a dividend, triggering dividend WHT and additional late-payment interest. Failure to file: statutory penalty of RMB 2,000 to RMB 10,000, and RMB 2,000 to RMB 10,000 for serious circumstances; late-payment surcharge of 0.05% per day on unpaid tax; under-declaration or tax evasion: fine of 50% to 500% of underpaid tax; criminal referral for tax evasion where unpaid tax exceeds RMB 50,000 and is more than 10% of the tax payable; directors and legal representatives also face exit restriction and tax blacklisting and may be banned from leaving China before all tax liabilities are settled.
Line 7 - Operating Profit Revenue less cost of sales and expenses, plus loss allowances, fair-value changes and investment income. This line must not reflect any treaty-reduced withholding tax rates. The operating profit is the starting point before transfer-pricing adjustments and taxable income reconciliation. Failure to file: statutory penalty of RMB 2,000 to RMB 10,000, and RMB 2,000 to RMB 10,000 for serious circumstances; late-payment surcharge of 0.05% per day on unpaid tax; under-declaration or tax evasion: fine of 50% to 500% of underpaid tax; criminal referral for tax evasion where unpaid tax exceeds RMB 50,000 and is more than 10% of the tax payable; directors and legal representatives also face exit restriction and tax blacklisting and may be banned from leaving China before all tax liabilities are settled.
Line 8 - Non-Operating Income and Expenses Extraordinary items, government subsidies, donation expenses, and asset disposal gains or losses. Include subsidies received from any government that are taxable unless an explicit exemption applies; donation deductions are capped at 12% of total profit and must be made through legally recognized charities. Failure to file: statutory penalty of RMB 2,000 to RMB 10,000, and RMB 2,000 to RMB 10,000 for serious circumstances; late-payment surcharge of 0.05% per day on unpaid tax; under-declaration or tax evasion: fine of 50% to 500% of underpaid tax; criminal referral for tax evasion where unpaid tax exceeds RMB 50,000 and is more than 10% of the tax payable; directors and legal representatives also face exit restriction and tax blacklisting and may be banned from leaving China before all tax liabilities are settled.
Line 9 - Total Profit Accounting profit before tax, comprising operating profit plus non-operating income less non-operating expenses. Reconcile the audited profit and loss statement to the tax return basis. Any intercompany royalty withholding tax borne by the WFOE on behalf of the Hong Kong beneficial owner is excluded from deductible expenses. Failure to file: statutory penalty of RMB 2,000 to RMB 10,000, and RMB 2,000 to RMB 10,000 for serious circumstances; late-payment surcharge of 0.05% per day on unpaid tax; under-declaration or tax evasion: fine of 50% to 500% of underpaid tax; criminal referral for tax evasion where unpaid tax exceeds RMB 50,000 and is more than 10% of the tax payable; directors and legal representatives also face exit restriction and tax blacklisting and may be banned from leaving China before all tax liabilities are settled.
Line 10 - Taxable Income after Adjustments and Loss Carryforward Total profit adjusted for tax-exempt income, non-deductible items, transfer-pricing adjustments, and carryforward losses; the base on which the 25% headline CIT is assessed. Apply the transfer-pricing adjustment schedule where the actual royalty charges deviate from the arm's-length price; only losses within the 5-year statutory carryforward cap may be deducted; losses used to shelter profit created by intra-group patent licensing in low-tax jurisdictions will be denied. Failure to file: statutory penalty of RMB 2,000 to RMB 10,000, and RMB 2,000 to RMB 10,000 for serious circumstances; late-payment surcharge of 0.05% per day on unpaid tax; under-declaration or tax evasion: fine of 50% to 500% of underpaid tax; criminal referral for tax evasion where unpaid tax exceeds RMB 50,000 and is more than 10% of the tax payable; directors and legal representatives also face exit restriction and tax blacklisting and may be banned from leaving China before all tax liabilities are settled.
Line 11 - Tax Payable and Credits Computation of tax payable at 25%, reduced by tax exemptions, preferential rates, and foreign tax credits. Claim HNTE 15% or Hainan 15% preferential rate only if the WFOE separately satisfies the substantive business activity, R&D ratio, patent ownership, and intellectual-property core operations tests; foreign tax credits on royalty income received from abroad are capped at the Chinese CIT payable on the same foreign-source income. Failure to file: statutory penalty of RMB 2,000 to RMB 10,000, and RMB 2,000 to RMB 10,000 for serious circumstances; late-payment surcharge of 0.05% per day on unpaid tax; under-declaration or tax evasion: fine of 50% to 500% of underpaid tax; criminal referral for tax evasion where unpaid tax exceeds RMB 50,000 and is more than 10% of the tax payable; directors and legal representatives also face exit restriction and tax blacklisting and may be banned from leaving China before all tax liabilities are settled.
Line 12 - Actual Tax Payable, Prepayments and Final Balance Annual tax payable after reductions and Foreign Tax Credits, less quarterly prepayments, producing the final balance due or refund. For non-resident profit repatriation, do not confuse the WFOE's corporate income tax with the 10% or treaty-reduced 5% dividend withholding tax on actual distributions; the WFOE must file a separate withholding declaration immediately when dividends are declared and paid. Failure to file: statutory penalty of RMB 2,000 to RMB 10,000, and RMB 2,000 to RMB 10,000 for serious circumstances; late-payment surcharge of 0.05% per day on unpaid tax; under-declaration or tax evasion: fine of 50% to 500% of underpaid tax; criminal referral for tax evasion where unpaid tax exceeds RMB 50,000 and is more than 10% of the tax payable; directors and legal representatives also face exit restriction and tax blacklisting and may be banned from leaving China before all tax liabilities are settled.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Under STA Public Notice [2016] No. 42, the Local File must be prepared when annual related-party tangible-asset transfers exceed RMB 200 million, intangible-asset transfers exceed RMB 100 million, financial-asset transfers exceed RMB 100 million, or other related-party expense payments exceed RMB 40 million. The Master File is generally required for an enterprise whose total related-party transaction volume reaches RMB 1 billion in the preceding fiscal year; annual related-party transaction reporting must be filed with the annual CIT return.
