Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA); electronic filing and registration are performed through the Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax) portal, whose authoritative access point is published on the official STA website chinatax.gov.cn.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: The Hong Kong investor incorporates the WFOE with the local State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR); SAMR issues the Business License that contains the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC). Step 2: SAMR transmits the registration data to the STA under the joint one-stop registration reform; within 30 calendar days after issuance of the Business License, the WFOE must complete tax registration in the Electronic Tax Bureau and adopt the USCC as its statutory Tax Identification Number for Enterprise Income Tax, VAT, withholding, and customs purposes. Step 3: The WFOE must designate the legal representative and tax accounting personnel, report its accounting system, open a domestic RMB and foreign-exchange bank account, register for VAT invoice quotas, and be classified as either a VAT general taxpayer or a small-scale taxpayer. Step 4: The non-resident Hong Kong shareholder does not obtain a Chinese TIN merely by owning WFOE equity; if the shareholder derives China-sourced income subject to tax, the Chinese payer or the shareholder files under the non-resident tax withholding administration and the STA issues a taxpayer registration record or withholding tax registration identifier for that non-resident filing.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None. China's Enterprise Income Tax Law does not provide check-the-box or hybrid mismatch elections; the WFOE is mandatorily taxed at entity level. The only classification election is voluntary registration as a VAT general taxpayer when annual taxable turnover does not exceed RMB 5,000,000, subject to STA approval and once elected generally irrevocable for 36 months.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No turnover threshold exempts a newly incorporated WFOE from tax registration; SAMR business registration triggers mandatory tax registration with the STA within 30 days. For VAT classification, annual taxable sales in excess of RMB 5,000,000 make registration as a VAT general taxpayer compulsory; at or below RMB 5,000,000 the entity remains a small-scale taxpayer unless it elects voluntary general taxpayer status.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For goods: 0% VAT and export tax refund are available to a VAT general taxpayer that exports physical goods from mainland China (including to Hong Kong SAR) under a genuine sales contract, declares the goods to Chinese Customs, physically transports them out of the customs territory, receives foreign-currency consideration, and retains the complete export evidence package. For cross-border services: only services expressly enumerated as zero-rated under Caishui [2016] No. 36, Annex 4 (for example, certain international transportation, research and development, design, software, information technology, consulting, and offshore outsourcing services) are eligible; discretionary portfolio advisory/financial services must be consumed wholly outside China and must fall within an enumerated zero-rated scope to claim 0% VAT, otherwise the 6% financial-services VAT rate applies.

  • Customs export declaration (Customs Declaration Form for Exports) bearing the customs-assignment number and export date
  • Bill of Lading, airway bill, or international consignment note evidencing physical movement of goods out of mainland China
  • Commercial invoice, packing list, and export sales contract between the WFOE and the non-resident buyer
  • Bank foreign-exchange receipt or SAFE verification record evidencing inward remittance of export proceeds from the overseas buyer
  • Zero-rating substantiation file containing the VAT invoice stream, Customs data matching record, payment/remittance evidence, and, for services, the contract and evidence of overseas place of supply and offshore consumption

VAT invoices must be issued through the STA-licensed electronic invoicing or tax-control system, with invoice data transmitted in real time to the STA Golden Tax administration platform. Each special VAT invoice carries a unique invoice code and digital anti-counterfeit stamp, and buyers/sellers authenticate invoices through the National VAT Invoice Verification Platform before the VAT deduction is cleared. Application Programming Interface (API) integration is permitted only through certified e-invoice service platforms approved by the STA, and the WFOE must complete the STA e-invoicing quota and system-clearance registration before issuing VAT invoices.

The STA, Customs, and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange match export declarations, VAT invoices, and foreign-exchange receipts electronically. If export proceeds are not repatriated and verified within 180 days from the export date (absent an approved extension), the export zero-rating is disallowed and the WFOE must pay VAT at the applicable positive rate with statutory late-payment surcharge of 0.05% per day. If the 0% rate was obtained by fictitious export, forged documents, or non-genuine transactions, the STA claws back the refund and imposes an administrative fine of not less than 1 time and not more than 5 times the refund fraudulently obtained, with criminal liability for fraudulent export tax refunds under the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Chinese-WFOE operates within the PRC mainland where foreign exchange is regulated under a 'managed float' regime. The RMB is fully convertible on the current account for trade and services, subject to submission of authentic underlying commercial documents and completion of a transaction authorities' registration under the goods trade or service trade system. The capital account, however, is not freely convertible: incoming FDI equity injections, shareholder loans, and outbound remittances of dividends, loan repayments, royalties, and liquidation proceeds require explicit SAFE filings or approvals. For this discretionary portfolio advisory MFO, the principal cross-border flows will be capital injections from the Hong Kong parent and subsequently regulated profit remittances. Dividends declared by the WFOE to its Hong Kong shareholder must be sourced from audited after-tax profits, evidenced by the annual CIT settlement certificate, then processed through the bank within the registered FDI quota, with 10% withholding tax (or reduced rate if a tax treaty applies). Any false or non-trade-related conversion may trigger administrative penalties, revocation of the foreign exchange account, and inclusion on SAFE's compliance blacklist, impairing future treasury operations.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export proceeds from trade in services or goods must be repatriated into the WFOE's designated foreign exchange account in China within 90 days from the date of export declaration or service completion, with a maximum extension to 180 days upon SAFE filing for approved open-account terms. There is no mandatory conversion ratio for current-account receipts; the entity may retain the funds in a foreign currency current account or convert to RMB at its discretion. However, the proceeds must be fully repatriated and documented; no overseas parking is permitted. For payments received in advance of export, the receipt must be tracked against the subsequent customs declaration, and if not matched within the statutory period, the bank must reverse the amount and impose a fine. All settlements must be made through the bank's Trade Settlement and Receipt/Payment system (e.g., 国家外汇管理局数字外管平台 English equivalent), and the exporting entity must maintain a trade credit classification rating; any late repatriation without valid justification triggers heightened monitoring and potentially suspension of the trade licence.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China, operating under the mandate of the People's Bank of China (PBOC); the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) separately governs the free-convertibility offshore channel in the Hong Kong SAR.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Digital-only financial institutions and offshore EMI platforms (e.g., Revolut, Airwallex, Wise Business) are not standalone viable for this WFOE's core banking operations. They are legally barred from holding or settling foreign exchange on behalf of a Chinese domestic entity because they lack a PRC banking licence and SAFE regulatory designation for FDI capital injection, capital verification, or dividend remittance. Such EMIs may only serve as ancillary payment facilitation tools for low-value, current-account transactions (e.g., subscription fees from overseas clients) provided they route through a licensed Chinese bank, but they cannot maintain the mandatory SAFE registration or exchange-control reporting. The primary treasury relationship must reside with a domestic Tier-1 commercial bank; the fintech EMI is rated 'Low' for capital account, 'Medium' only for local inbound collections with proper underlying contracts, and 'Not applicable' for hedging.

Turnover: Under Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622) ss.359-361, every Hong Kong company must appoint a qualified certified public accountant as auditor unless it qualifies as a small private company or small guarantee company and validly opts out. The small entity audit exemption is available only if the company satisfies at least two of the following three financial-year criteria: annual revenue not exceeding HKD 25,000,000, total assets not exceeding HKD 15,000,000, and not more than 50 employees. The exemption is expressly unavailable to an SFC-licensed corporation under the Securities and Futures Ordinance, an authorized financial institution, an authorized insurer, or other regulated/prudentially licensed entities. This multi-family office is licensed for SFC Type 4 and Type 9 regulated activities, so a compulsory certified CPA audit applies irrespective of any small company threshold. The SFC also requires audited financial statements under the Securities and Futures (Financial Resources) Rules, including an auditor's report, liquid capital verification and the filing of Financial Resources Returns within prescribed deadlines.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File and contemporaneous documentation are mandatory when the WFOE's related-party transactions in a fiscal year reach: tangible property transactions RMB 200 million or more; intangible property transactions RMB 100 million or more; financial transactions (including loans, intercompany guarantees) RMB 100 million or more; and other related-party service transactions RMB 40 million or more. A Master File is required if aggregate related-party transactions reach RMB 1 billion; a Country-by-Country Report is required when the MNE group consolidated revenue exceeds RMB 5.5 billion.

Economic substance scope: The PRC General Anti-Avoidance Rule (EIT Law Art. 47 and Implementation Rules Art. 120) empowers tax authorities to recharacterise arrangements lacking reasonable commercial purpose. Under SAT Announcement 2015 No.70 and 2009 No.601, the 'beneficial ownership' doctrine denies treaty benefits to letterbox companies without substantive personnel, premises, decision-making and management functions; for a Multi-Family Office WFOE, the Ethiopia? No. The WFOE must demonstrate that portfolio advisory decisions, custody wire instructions, and client relationship management are core income-generating activities performed by its own professionals in mainland China; failure to prove such core substance in China or in the Hong Kong parent will result in reclassification of income, denial of treaty relief, and possible deemed dividend treatment under PRC tax law.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Return

Statutory sanction: Under PRC Tax Administration Law Art. 63, fraudulent underpayment of tax attracts a monetary penalty of 50% to 500% of the underpaid amount, plus daily late-payment surcharge of 0.05%; if the evaded amount is large and exceeds 10% of taxable income, the offence constitutes Tax Evasion under Criminal Law Art. 201, punishable by imprisonment of up to 7 years and a fine.

Director personal liability: Civil: directors can be personally liable to restore corporate assets and compensate the tax authority for unpaid taxes if they directed or concealed the fraud; personal bank accounts and properties may be seized. Administrative: directors face travel bans (exit restrictions) under Tax Administration Law Art. 44, 'blacklisting' in the national tax integrity system, and disqualification from serving as senior managers. Criminal: a director who caused the false filing can be sentenced to up to 7 years' imprisonment plus a criminal fine and confiscation of assets.
Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Financial Conduct Breach

Statutory sanction: Under the PRC Anti-Money Laundering Law (Art. 32 for institutional penalties, Art. 33 for responsible officers), serious violations can attract fines of up to RMB 5 million, suspension or revocation of business licences; where the transferred proceeds are a result of predicate criminal activity, Criminal Law Art. 191 punishes Money Laundering with fines and up to 10 years' imprisonment.

Director personal liability: Civil: directors must compensate defrauded clients and the firm for losses caused by failure in AML controls; personal assets may be frozen in administrative and civil asset-recovery proceedings. Administrative: directors may be permanently disqualified from financial industry positions, placed on the onshore and offshore financial blacklists, and subjected to travel restrictions. Criminal: knowingly facilitating placement of criminal proceeds through SWIFT and custody wires renders directors jointly and severally liable, leading to imprisonment up to 10 years and criminal forfeiture.
Transfer Pricing Non-Disclosure and Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Under EIT Law Art. 44 and SAT Announcement 2016 No.42, the tax authorities may make a deemed assessment based on available facts and impose a 50%-500% penalty for underpaying tax under Tax Administration Law Art. 63 when the TP failure is fraudulent or deliberate; interest on TP adjustment can be assessed at 5% above the benchmark rate plus a 0.05% daily surcharge.

Director personal liability: Civil: the corporate veil will be pierced if directors used the WFOE as a mere instrumentality to divert profits; directors may be held personally liable for the entire tax shortfall and related civil damages. Administrative: shareholders and directors face registration of the entity on the serious-tax-violation blacklist, travel bans and prohibition against leaving Chinese territory; directors may be disqualified from serving in any enterprise in the PRC. Criminal: where the false TP arrangement concealed taxable income above the criminal threshold, directors will be prosecuted for tax evasion and may face imprisonment up to 7 years and fines.
