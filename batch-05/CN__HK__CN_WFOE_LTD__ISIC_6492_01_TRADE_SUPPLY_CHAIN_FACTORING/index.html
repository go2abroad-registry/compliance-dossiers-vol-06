A virtual office or commercial mailbox is not permissible as the sole registered address for a Mainland WFOE engaged in accounts receivable factoring and cross-border reverse factoring. The Company Law of the PRC and the Measures for the Administration of Registered Addresses of Market Entities require a fixed and verifiable physical office address that is consistent with the actual place of business. Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None. Hong Kong law imposes no minimum issued or paid-up capital. The company may be incorporated with a share capital as low as one ordinary share, denominated in HKD or any other currency. No blocked escrow account or capital verification account is required by the Companies Registry. If an onshore mainland WFOE is later established as a subsidiary, the PRC Company Law also imposes no general paid-up capital minimum for a trade-supply-chain finance company unless a sector regulator prescribes otherwise.

Funding & unlock path: Not Applicable for pure financial services; no Fire Services Department industrial permit is required. No structural load inspection is needed because no racking or heavy warehousing equipment will be installed. As an employer, the company must comply with the Occupational Safety and Health Ordinance (Cap. 509), maintain emergency evacuation plans and obtain any labour registration required for employees.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA); online electronic portal: National E-Tax Bureau (etax.chinatax.gov.cn), operated through provincial STA branches; export customs filings are handled through the China Single Window portal administered by the General Administration of Customs of China (GACC).

E-government filing portal

The China WFOE is formed under the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR). After the SAMR issues the business license, the WFOE automatically receives the statutory Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which functions as the Tax Identification Number for enterprise income tax, VAT, and local surtaxes. The WFOE must complete tax registration through the E-Tax Bureau within 30 days of the license date, providing the articles of association, legal representative identification, financial accounting system document, registered address proof, and business license. The STA then activates the taxpayer file, determines Small-scale or General VAT taxpayer status, and enrolls the entity for monthly or quarterly VAT and annual corporate income tax filings. For a foreign non-resident enterprise with no Chinese establishment but with Chinese-source income, no USCC is required; the payer or the non-resident may apply for a non-resident taxpayer registration with the STA for withholding and treaty-claim purposes, using the home jurisdiction TIN. For the WFOE, the Chinese entity is the taxpayer and no separate offshore registration is needed for its local tax identity.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None. China does not permit a check-the-box or pass-through election for a WFOE; the WFOE is mandatorily a Chinese resident enterprise subject to entity-level taxation on worldwide income. No hybrid mismatch election is available. The only elections are administrative: a business below the RMB 5,000,000 VAT general-taxpayer threshold may choose to become a general VAT taxpayer, and eligible taxpayers may elect to apply tax treaty benefits under the PRC/Hong Kong SAR double taxation arrangement, but neither changes the default entity-level classification.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

6%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

All VAT-taxable enterprises must complete tax registration; annual taxable sales exceeding RMB 5,000,000 trigger mandatory registration/classification as a General VAT taxpayer. Below RMB 5,000,000, the WFOE may be a Small-scale VAT taxpayer subject to simplified collection unless it voluntarily elects general status.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A General VAT taxpayer may apply the 0% VAT rate to exported goods when the goods physically leave China Customs territory, the taxpayer is the actual exporter/seller, the VAT invoice, customs declaration, logistics documents, and bank receipt are mutually consistent, and the foreign currency export proceeds are collected from the overseas buyer. Zero-rating of services is available only for categories expressly listed in the STA cross-border taxable services zero-rating and refund catalogue; factoring services supplied to non-residents are not zero-rated and may qualify only for VAT exemption if the service is wholly used outside China. Accordingly, the WFOE must separate the underlying goods export trade from the factoring commission and finance charges: the goods export is substantively zero-rated, while the factoring income is generally taxable at 6% unless an independent cross-border service exemption applies.

  • China Customs Export Declaration for Goods (Customs Export Declaration Form / Bill of Entry) generated in the China Single Window
  • Bill of Lading (ocean full set), Air Waybill, or international railway/road consignment note evidencing physical departure from China
  • Commercial invoice, packing list, and the underlying export sales contract or purchase order
  • Bank-issued Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or Export Proceeds Collection Confirmation evidencing receipt of foreign exchange from the overseas buyer
  • SAFE and bank foreign exchange receipts/payments records and settlement records matching the customs declaration value
  • Export VAT refund application data transmitted through the E-Tax Bureau and cross-referenced to the customs declaration and proceeds receipt

China operates the Golden Tax Phase IV electronic invoice administration framework and the STA all-digital electronic invoice platform. VAT invoices (Fapiao) for factoring fees, including Special VAT Invoices, must be issued through STA-approved invoicing systems or system-to-system API interfaces connected to the E-Tax Bureau. Each invoice receives a unique tax-control number and digital anti-counterfeiting signature at issuance; invoice data, VAT returns, and customs declarations are reconciled in near-real-time by the STA. There is no foreign-style physical fiscal stamp; anti-fraud clearance is performed digitally by the national VAT invoice identification and verification platform, and every issuance, red-letter reversal, and void is reported immediately to the tax authority.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Under China's foreign exchange administration framework, RMB is fully convertible for current-account transactions, including payments for goods, services, and remittance of lawfully declared dividends. However, the capital account remains regulated, and all cross-border capital movements require prior SAFE registration or approval. A WFOE establishes an FDI capital account; injected equity funds must be registered with SAFE through a designated domestic bank, converted in compliance with the foreign-invested enterprise capital settlement policy (positive/negative list approach), and may not be diverted into securities or entrusted loans unless specifically permitted. The repatriation of profits is permitted, but is conditioned on mandatory tax clearance, audited financial statements, tax withholding on dividends, and documentary proof that the profits arose from genuine business operations. In practice, banks require the annual audit, preferential tax or standard 10% withholding forms, and a formal board resolution before effecting outward remittance. Export receipts require receipt within statutory timeframes and are subject to the genuine-invoice verification system, although the mandatory surrender ratio has been zero since 2012; exporters may hold or convert proceeds subject to bank monitoring and trade data matching. For a foreign-invested factoring/licensing entity, each cross-border transfer must also be mapped to an approved SAFE business code, making the pre-transaction documentary compliance a make-or-break requirement.

Export proceeds surrender rules

China abolished mandatory export surrender. Receipts from exports must be collected within 90 days after the date of export declaration, unless a longer grace period is approved; the full amount must be brought into a domestic account and reported through the bank under SAFE's trade monitoring system. Export proceeds may be retained in foreign currency or converted into RMB at the market rate, but any cross-border transfer must be matched to the customs declaration and invoice data to satisfy the 'three-way reconciliation' (goods, funds, declarations). Failure to collect within the statutory window triggers an exceptional reporting and penalty regime.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBoC) and State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Offshore digital EMI platforms (e.g., Wise, Airwallex, Payoneer, Revolut Business) are not licensed deposit-taking institutions in the PRC and are legally prohibited from maintaining RMB current accounts or foreign-exchange dominions for a WFOE. Under the Administrative Measures for the Payment Services of Non-Bank Institutions, non-bank entities cannot engage in cross-border renminbi settlement as a settlement bank in the sense of conducting KYC, foreign exchange verification, and SAFE reporting autonomously. Consequently, their viability for this structure is effectively zero. They may only function as a low-value collection gateway for e-commerce, not as the primary clearing infrastructure for factoring or trade settlement. Any use of such EMIs as an operational account would trigger a systemic AML breach and could lead to account freeze, licence revocation, and exclusion from the SAFE registry. The only compliant route is a domestic commercial bank account alongside licensed cross-border payment institutions that partner with banks and perform filings under SAFE supervision.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The WFOE should first complete FDI registration with the local Market Supervision Administration and obtain the SAFE Foreign Investment Registration through a designated bank. Inbound equity wires are settled as capital injection for equity subscription, using the relevant SAFE business code for FDI. The funds enter the USD/CNY capital account and must be converted under the capital settlement payment policy; each conversion is tied to a bona fide transaction such as rent, salaries, procurement, or licensed business expenses. A capital verification report by a Chinese-certified public accountant is required after the injection, and the bank must receive the proof of use (e.g., payment orders, contracts, invoices) within the regulatory monitoring period. Use of capital for securities, entrusted loans, or non-operating investments is prohibited except under specifically approved pilot schemes.For domestic RMB disbursements to suppliers, use CNAPS/IPS clearing with reference to the underlying trade documents. For cross-border payments, SWIFT MT103 is the default direct settlement rail; commercial letters of credit are recommended for high-value import transactions where bank payment undertakings and document checking are required; documentary collections are acceptable when the WFOE has an established trade relationship. Every outward remittance must be supported by the invoice, contract, customs declaration where applicable, and a SAFE payment purpose code. Payments related to factoring must also reference the receivables assignment notice, the debtor payment undertaking, and the credit facility agreement to satisfy the underlying real transaction test. All foreign-exchange transactions are priced at the market rate and reported under the bank's head-office SAFE gateway.Onshore RMB forward contracts (delivery forwards) executed with a domestic designated bank, restricted to real trade exposure evidenced by export/import contracts and invoices; settlement occurs against the daily central parity rate on the value date.Cross-currency swaps and FX swaps with commercial banks for hedging the principal and interest cash flows of SAFE-registered foreign-currency borrowings; the underlying debt must be properly registered and the hedging notional cannot exceed the outstanding exposure.Client option products may be used to hedge firm committed foreign-currency receivables or payables, but naked options, leveraged FX derivatives, and speculative anticipatory hedging are prohibited.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are not permitted for onshore WFOE entities; any offshore NDF or quanto hedging arrangement violates the real-demand principle and is subject to regulatory enforcement, so only onshore deliverable instruments should be used.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Offshore digital EMI platforms (e.g., Wise, Airwallex, Payoneer, Revolut Business) are not licensed deposit-taking institutions in the PRC and are legally prohibited from maintaining RMB current accounts or foreign-exchange dominions for a WFOE. Under the Administrative Measures for the Payment Services of Non-Bank Institutions, non-bank entities cannot engage in cross-border renminbi settlement as a settlement bank in the sense of conducting KYC, foreign exchange verification, and SAFE reporting autonomously. Consequently, their viability for this structure is effectively zero. They may only function as a low-value collection gateway for e-commerce, not as the primary clearing infrastructure for factoring or trade settlement. Any use of such EMIs as an operational account would trigger a systemic AML breach and could lead to account freeze, licence revocation, and exclusion from the SAFE registry. The only compliant route is a domestic commercial bank account alongside licensed cross-border payment institutions that partner with banks and perform filings under SAFE supervision.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% on gross dividends paid to a non-resident enterprise under EIT Law Article 37 and Article 3 of the implementation regulations. Under the China-Hong Kong Double Taxation Arrangement, the rate is reduced to 5% where the Hong Kong shareholder is the beneficial owner and directly holds at least 25% of the WFOE's paid-in capital; because the WFOE is a 100% subsidiary, the 5% treaty rate applies if beneficial ownership and substance are proven. If the beneficial owner is not established, the rate is 10%.

Quarterly provisional enterprise income tax returns must be filed and paid within 15 days after the end of each calendar quarter.
