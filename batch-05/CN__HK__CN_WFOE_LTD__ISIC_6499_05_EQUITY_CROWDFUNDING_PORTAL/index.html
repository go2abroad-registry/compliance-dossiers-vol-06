Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA) for the mainland China WFOE; official online portal: www.chinatax.gov.cn and the STA Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax) platform. For the Hong Kong SAR parent or offshore profits, the competent authority is the Inland Revenue Department (IRD) of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region with the eTAX portal at www.ird.gov.hk.

E-government filing portal

The foreign investor must first register the Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE) with the local Administration for Market Regulation (AMR), complete foreign investment information reporting under the MOFCOM negative-list framework, and obtain a business license containing the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC). The WFOE must then complete tax registration with the STA within 30 calendar days via the e-Tax bureau or the local tax service hall. The USCC is the statutory Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) and VAT registration number. The WFOE must complete real-name authentication, bank-account linking for tax payment, and VAT general taxpayer qualification if annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000 or if its business model requires input VAT crediting. Supporting documents include the articles of association, legal representative identity proof, registered office lease, evidence of paid-in capital remittance, and bank account registration. A foreign non-resident enterprise without a permanent establishment that derives China-source income obtains a non-resident tax registration or is administered through a withholding agent in accordance with the STA non-resident tax administration framework.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation; Territorial_Scope applies to the Hong Kong SAR parent because Hong Kong profits tax is territorial, but the mainland China WFOE is a Chinese resident enterprise subject to entity-level Corporate Income Tax at 25%, VAT, urban maintenance and construction tax, and education surcharges. The WFOE is not a pass-through entity and has no tax-transparent classification.

Available election forms

China does not provide a check-the-box election. The WFOE may elect small-scale VAT taxpayer treatment while annual taxable sales remain below RMB 5,000,000; once it registers as a general VAT taxpayer, the election is irrevocable and the normal VAT credit-offset method applies. Small-scale taxpayers may apply simplified levy rates under STA temporary relief measures. Hong Kong SAR does not have a check-the-box regime; territorial source rules are non-elective, and no hybrid mismatch election is available under PRC domestic tax law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

6%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 annual taxable sales: annual sales above this threshold require compulsory VAT general taxpayer registration. All WFOEs must complete statutory tax registration within 30 days of business license issuance; below the threshold the entity may remain a small-scale VAT taxpayer. Hong Kong SAR has no VAT/GST registration threshold because no such tax exists.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under the PRC VAT export tax rebate framework, goods physically exported from China may obtain a 0% VAT rate or exemption/rebate if the export is declared by the registered VAT taxpayer in its own name and the foreign exchange receipt is repatriated through designated banks. Cross-border services may qualify for zero-rating or VAT exemption under Cai Shui [2016] No. 36 and implementing STA rules only where the recipient is outside China, the service is wholly consumed outside China, and the consideration is paid in foreign currency from outside China. An equity crowdfunding portal's facilitation fees are generally taxable at 6% as financial or direct charging intermediary services; zero-rating is not automatic and will apply only if the WFOE can prove a qualifying offshore digital service transaction, offshore recipient, offshore actual consumption, and timely foreign-exchange repatriation.

  • Customs Export Declaration (export goods declaration) bearing the China Customs release serial number and the exporter's customs registration code
  • Bill of Lading (ocean) or Air Waybill (air) evidencing physical export from China
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or bank foreign-exchange settlement record from a designated SAFE bank proving repatriation of export proceeds within 180 days
  • Commercial invoice, export sales contract, and packing list connecting the transaction to the exporting WFOE
  • PRC VAT export invoice or special VAT invoice and the export tax rebate application filed with the STA
  • For zero-rated cross-border services: offshore service contract, proof of offshore recipient and offshore consumption, and proof of foreign-currency settlement from outside China

Mainland China enforces real-time e-invoice clearance through the STA VAT Invoice Management System and the Full Digital Electronic Invoice Platform (all-electronic invoice). Taxpayers must issue Special VAT Invoices or digital e-invoices through the STA platform and upload transactional data in real time under the Golden Tax Phase IV architecture, with API integration for enterprise billing systems. Anti-fraud digital stamping uses unique invoice codes, encrypted invoice numbering, taxpayer real-name authentication, and automated cross-matching of VAT invoices. Hong Kong SAR imposes no electronic invoicing or fiscal clearance mandate because it operates no VAT.

China Customs and the STA jointly audit export declarations, logistics documents, foreign-exchange receipts, and VAT credit flows. Failure to substantiate zero-rating is treated as fraudulent export tax rebate or false zero-rated declaration. The STA will claw back refunded VAT, impose a late-payment surcharge of 0.05% per day from the due date, and impose an administrative fine of one to five times the tax amount fraudulently obtained under the Law on Administration of Tax Levy and Collection. The WFOE's export tax credit classification will be downgraded, zero-rating and export rebate privileges will be suspended, and the case may be transferred for criminal prosecution under the Criminal Law where the false declaration amount is significant. Records must be retained and made available to the STA for audit; failure to repatriate foreign currency within the 180-day deadline or falsification of offshore consumption will result in retroactive re-taxation at the standard VAT rate, clawback of input VAT refunds, and withholding tax assessments.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The WFOE operates under China's controlled convertibility framework. RMB is convertible for current account transactions such as merchandise trade, service payments, royalties, dividends on foreign direct investment, and reasonable operating expenses, but capital account transactions, including inbound equity injections, shareholder loans, and overseas direct investment, require mandatory SAFE registration and bank-level authenticity verification. In Hong Kong, the HKMA maintains the linked exchange rate system with free capital and current account convertibility, so the HK parent treasury can transfer equity capital into the Chinese WFOE without FX restriction. The WFOE's inbound foreign currency capital contribution must be credited to a SAFE-monitored FX capital account, and the bank will require the underlying foreign investment filing, the equity subscription agreement, and a post-receipt capital verification report from a Chinese certified public accountant. Profit remittances to the Hong Kong shareholder are permitted only after the WFOE completes its annual audit, corporate income tax settlement, and withholding tax filing; under the Mainland-Hong Kong double taxation arrangement, dividends may qualify for a reduced 5% withholding tax if Hong Kong is the ultimate beneficial owner. The convertibility gap is therefore structural rather than transactional: operating flows are freely convertible, while capital movements remain tightly controlled and require a paper trail that the corporate treasury must maintain for regulatory inspections.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE's current account administration, there is no mandatory surrender ratio. Export proceeds in foreign currency may be retained in full in an onshore FX current-account bank account or voluntarily converted into RMB through the bank's settlement system. The statutory repatriation window for export receipts is 180 days from the customs declaration date unless the exporter obtains prior SAFE approval for offshore retention. Every receipt must be reported through the SAFE Trade Monitoring System, and the bank performs documentary reconciliation against bills of lading, customs declarations, and invoices.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) as the central bank, acting jointly with the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE); the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) is the de facto central bank and monetary authority for the Hong Kong SAR leg.

Strike-off trigger

Mandatory Audit Threshold Mandatory Audit for All Entities

Turnover: Every Hong Kong private limited company must be audited by a certified public accountant unless it qualifies for the small private company audit exemption under the Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622). The qualifying conditions are satisfied when a small private company meets at least two of the three tests in a financial year: total annual revenue not exceeding HK$10,000,000; total assets not exceeding HK$10,000,000; and average number of employees not exceeding 50. However, this exemption is unavailable to the present entity because it holds a statutory license as a high-risk equity crowdfunding portal and is regulated under the Securities and Futures Ordinance (Cap. 571) and anti-money laundering legislation; therefore a compulsory independent certified CPA audit is mandatory regardless of turnover, balance sheet size, or headcount. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% statutory dividend withholding tax under Article 37 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law and Article 3 of the Implementation Regulations for dividends distributed by a Chinese resident enterprise to a non-resident enterprise shareholder. The rate is reduced to 5% under the Mainland-Hong Kong Double Taxation Arrangement if the Hong Kong resident shareholder is the beneficial owner of the dividend and holds at least 25% of the WFOE’s shares. Where treaty benefits are denied, statutory 10% withholding tax applies to gross dividends and no deduction is available to the WFOE for the distribution.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: China requires a Local File to be prepared where annual related-party transactions meet any of these thresholds: tangible asset transfers above RMB 200 million; transfers or use of intangible property above RMB 100 million; financial asset transactions above RMB 100 million; or other related-party transactions above RMB 40 million. A Master File is required where the aggregate amount of related-party transactions in the year exceeds RMB 1 billion. The WFOE must also file the annual related-party transaction reporting form A109000 with its annual CIT return whenever any related-party transaction occurs, irrespective of value.

Economic substance scope: The economic-substance doctrine under Chinese law is embodied in the general anti-abuse rule of Article 120 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, the specialty anti-avoidance rules for controlled foreign companies, thin capitalization, and the beneficial-ownership requirements of the Mainland-Hong Kong DTA. For an equity crowdfunding portal, Core Income-Generating Activities (CIGA) include platform development, investor onboarding, issue listing, operational risk management, escrow administration, and compliance decisions surrounding securities offerings. Where the WFOE performs those CIGA in mainland China while the HK parent provides only nominee services or title to the platform code, the HK entity will be disregarded for Chinese tax purposes, and the income will be reattributed to the WFOE or to a mainland permanent establishment. The Hong Kong parent must substantiate its own decision-making, staff, premises, and assumption of risk in Hong Kong to satisfy economic-substance requirements and to qualify for treaty benefits. A paper-thin HK holding company that merely invoices the WFOE for services or receives dividends without functional substance is a fatal treaty-shopping structure.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Corporate income tax evasion through transfer-pricing manipulation

Statutory sanction: The tax authority may apply the transfer-pricing adjustment rules under Articles 41 and 123 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law and Articles 112-115 of the Implementation Regulations, re-imputing the full arm’s-length profit to the WFOE, denying the management fee deduction, reassessing CIT at 25% on the adjusted profit, and imposing a late-payment surcharge of 0.05% per day. A penalty of 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax applies under Article 63 of the Tax Collection Administration Law. If the amount of tax evaded exceeds RMB 50,000 and accounts for more than 10% of the tax due, Criminal Law Article 201 imposes imprisonment for up to three years for minor cases and three to seven years for serious cases, plus fines.

Director personal liability: Under the veil-piercing doctrine of the PRC Company Law, directors and the legal representative who caused the WFOE to underpay CIT and divert profits to the HK parent may be jointly and severally liable for the unpaid corporate tax and penalties where they abused corporate separation or participated in fraud. Tax authorities may place the legal representative on the serious tax untrustworthiness blacklist, impose a travel ban preventing departure from mainland China, restrict high-consumption activities, and freeze personal assets. Upon criminal conviction for tax evasion, directors face imprisonment sentences of three to seven years, and confiscation of personal property derived from the evasion.
Shell-company structure and fraudulent claim of treaty benefits

Statutory sanction: Under Article 120 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law and the special tax collection measures applicable to treaty benefits, the tax authority may deny the treaty-claim, apply the general anti-abuse rule, disregard the existence of the HK parent, recharacterize the payments as taxable dividends or service revenue, and assess 25% CIT on the underlying profit plus 10% withholding tax on outbound distributions. The taxpayer will owe late-payment interest from the original due date and a penalty of 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax. Where forged Tax Residency Certificates or false beneficial-ownership declarations are used, the matter is treated as tax fraud under Criminal Law Article 201, and the relevant legal documents are reported to Hong Kong authorities.

Director personal liability: Directors who deliberately organized the HK shell, signed false beneficial-ownership declarations, or directed dividend payments to the HK conduit are personally exposed to civil liability for the unpaid taxes and interest through corporate-veil piercing. The legal representative is subject to exit restrictions and blacklisting by the Chinese tax authorities, and may be placed on the cross-border travel-ban list. If the fraudulent treaty claim is criminalized, imprisonment of three to seven years is possible, together with confiscation of bank accounts and shares held in the HK shell.
Unlicensed equity crowdfunding and anti-money-laundering non-compliance

Statutory sanction: Unlicensed securities intermediary operation is an illegal business operation under Criminal Law Article 225 and may also constitute an illegal public offering or illegal securities business under the PRC Securities Law. Operating without the required license is punishable by confiscation of illegal income and fines up to five times the illegal turnover; serious criminal cases carry imprisonment from five to ten years with fines and asset confiscation. Under the PRC Anti-Money Laundering Law, failure to meet AML obligations subjects the institution to fines ranging from RMB 1 million to RMB 5 million and the responsible directors and officers to fines from RMB 50,000 to RMB 500,000, plus removal from office and disqualification from the financial sector.

Director personal liability: Directors and the legal representative who knowingly operated an unlicensed crowdfunding portal and laundered investor funds through the escrow account are personally liable for investor losses through tort and fraudulent-trading doctrines. The Chinese regulator may ban them from serving as directors of any licensed financial, securities, or payment entity, blacklist them, freeze their personal bank accounts, and prohibit them from leaving the country. Criminal conviction for illegal business operation is punishable by five to ten years’ imprisonment in serious cases; AML violations may add separate imprisonment terms and monetary fines, with personal restitution ordered for diverted investor funds.
