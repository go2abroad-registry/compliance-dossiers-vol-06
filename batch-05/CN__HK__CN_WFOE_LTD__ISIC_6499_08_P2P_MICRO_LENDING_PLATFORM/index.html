Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China, administered through the National Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax Bureau) electronic portal and local STA branches. The Hong Kong SAR Inland Revenue Department (IRD) eTax portal is the competent authority for the Hong Kong parent's tax-residence certificate for the China–Hong Kong Double Tax Arrangement (DTA) relief. The WFOE itself is a PRC resident taxpayer, not an HK taxpayer.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Apply for company incorporation with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR); upon incorporation the WFOE receives an 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which is the statutory Tax Identification Number (TIN) for all PRC tax filings. Step 2: Complete post-incorporation tax registration through the STA e-Tax Bureau within 30 days; the tax registration is data-linked to SAMR and requires the USCC, legal representative identity, registered address, business scope, bank account information, and the articles of association. Step 3: Set up the e-tax taxpayer profile and obtain the digital certificate/TaxUkey or fiscal-control equipment from the tax authority. Step 4: Apply for VAT taxpayer status on the e-Tax Bureau; the entity must be registered as a general VAT taxpayer once annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000; below that threshold, it can elect small-scale taxpayer treatment or apply voluntarily. Step 5: For the non-resident Hong Kong parent, obtain a Hong Kong Business Registration Certificate and IRD Resident Certificate for DTA purposes; the HK parent's TIN in Hong Kong is the Business Registration Number (BRN). The HK parent must also obtain a PRC non-resident enterprise tax registration number or rely on the WFOE's withholding registration before dividend remittances are made; the withholding agent files the non-resident enterprise income tax withholding documentation through the e-Tax Bureau. Step 6: Keep all SAMR, tax, and foreign-exchange registrations in sync; any change in business scope, capital, or cross-border lender relationships must be updated within the legal filing periods.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation. The PRC WFOE is a resident enterprise under Article 2 of the PRC Enterprise Income Tax Law and is taxed at 25% corporate income tax on worldwide income. It is not a pass-through entity. The Hong Kong parent is a non-resident enterprise for PRC tax purposes; dividends paid by the WFOE to the HK parent are subject to PRC withholding income tax at 10%, reduced to 5% under the China–Hong Kong DTA if the HK parent is the beneficial owner and has directly held at least 25% of the WFOE's capital for a continuous 12-month period.

Available election forms

None. The PRC does not provide check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch entity classification elections; the WFOE's resident-enterprise classification is mandatory under the Enterprise Income Tax Law. The only elective mechanism relevant to this structure is the VAT general-taxpayer election (available below the RMB 5,000,000 threshold) and the treaty-relief claim mechanism: the HK parent may file for the 5% dividend withholding rate under the China–Hong Kong DTA by submitting an IRD Resident Certificate and a beneficial-ownership self-assessment through the STA e-Tax Bureau before dividend remittance. No entity classification election is available in Hong Kong SAR with respect to the WFOE.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

6%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 of annual taxable sales (or the equivalent monthly/quarterly apportionment) triggers mandatory VAT general-taxpayer registration; financial services are taxable at 6%, and the WFOE should register for VAT regardless of the threshold if it issues Special VAT Invoices or wishes to claim input credits. Small-scale taxpayers with monthly sales not exceeding RMB 100,000 are currently exempt from VAT under the relief effective through 31 December 2027.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export zero-rating for goods applies when goods are declared to the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China (GACC) for export, leave PRC customs territory, and the exporter collects the foreign exchange. Cross-border taxable services are zero-rated or VAT-exempt only where the recipient is outside the PRC and the service is consumed wholly outside the PRC. This fintech entity's core P2P micro-lending and underwriting services are supplied to PRC domestic borrowers and are therefore domestic taxable financial services, not export zero-rated supplies. If the WFOE separately provides pure offshore software or underwriting services to the HK parent or other non-residents, zero-rating or exemption is available only if such service is not an excluded financial service and the contract, invoice, payment, and overseas-consumption evidence are contemporaneously documented.

  • Customs Export Goods Declaration (customs Bill of Entry) recorded in GACC's export clearance system
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill (original or authenticated electronic transport document)
  • Commercial invoice and matching VAT invoice/fapiao generated in the STA e-invoice system
  • Bank foreign-exchange receipt voucher or Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) evidencing repatriation of export proceeds
  • Cross-border service contract, service acceptance certificate, and proof that the service was performed and consumed wholly outside the PRC (for services zero-rating)

The WFOE must issue electronic VAT invoices ('fapiao') through the STA-controlled VAT Invoice Management System / All-Electronic Invoice Platform. General taxpayers must use Special VAT Invoices (SVIs) for business customers who claim input VAT credits; each invoice is assigned a unique invoice code and anti-counterfeit QR/digital signature generated by tax-control equipment (TaxUkey/e-tax certificate). Invoice data is uploaded to the STA in near real-time; the STA performs risk-based, rather than per-invoice stamped, fiscal clearance. Manual invoices without government-issued invoice numbers are not enforceable VAT documents.

The STA and GACC use data cross-matching (Golden Tax Phase IV) to reconcile export declarations, VAT invoices, customs records, and SAFE foreign-exchange receipts. Any false export declaration or unverified zero-rating is recharacterized as domestic taxable supply and clawed back with late-payment surcharge (0.05% per day) and a penalty of 50% to 500% of the underpaid VAT under the PRC Tax Collection Administration Law. Deliberate fraud may be prosecuted under Article 204 of the Criminal Law as fraudulently obtaining export tax rebates, with penalties including confiscation of refunded VAT, criminal fines, imprisonment, and restrictions on responsible persons; the entity will also be placed on the tax non-compliant blacklist with restrictions on invoicing, export rebates, travel privileges, and financing.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The renminbi (RMB) is convertible under the current account for genuine trade and service transactions pursuant to China's IMF Article VIII obligations, but the capital account remains heavily controlled and subject to case-by-case approval, filing, or quota limits. The CNY is not freely convertible offshore for domestic entities. Inbound foreign direct investment into the Chinese WFOE requires SAFE registration and the opening of a dedicated capital foreign exchange account; proceeds must be settled through the 'capital project settlement' process and each RMB conversion must be matched to a genuine payment order. Outbound profit remittance from the WFOE to its Hong Kong parent is lawful only after the annual audit, income tax clearance, and the submission of a board resolution, audited financial statements, and a tax settlement certificate from the local State Tax Administration. There is no general profit surrender requirement, but any remittance exceeding USD 500,000 may trigger a SAFE documentary review. All foreign exchange purchased or settled for current account transactions must be supported by genuine documents such as contracts, invoices, customs declarations, and tax vouchers. As a P2P micro-lending fintech platform, the entity receives heightened scrutiny from SAFE and the PBOC's anti-money laundering division because any capital flow crossing the border for purposes other than the permitted loan origination or equity contribution already sanctioned by the banking regulator may be deemed illegal. Since the 2016 regulatory tightening on internet lending, the WFOE must hold a separate local financial regulatory filing for online micro-lending and must not execute quasi-banking treasury services without the explicit consent of the local Financial Regulatory Bureau. Therefore, convertibility is achievable for operational expenses, trade settlements, and lawful dividend distributions, but the documentary burden and approval timeline are substantial and non-transparent for a category of business that Chinese regulators view as high-risk.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Goods-trade export proceeds must be repatriated into China through a designated foreign exchange bank within 90 days after the customs export declaration, or within 180 days if the exporter has obtained a SAFE-approved extension for open-account credit terms. The mandatory conversion ratio is zero percent — since August 2012, China has abolished forced foreign currency surrender, so export receipts may be retained in a foreign exchange settlement account indefinitely. However, each foreign currency credit entry must be reconciled against the corresponding customs declaration data through SAFE's total-amount verification system. If export proceeds are not received or repatriated within the statutory timeline, the WFOE will be placed on SAFE's 'suspicious list' and may be restricted from making any subsequent foreign exchange payments until the overdue items are cleared.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China, acting under the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Offshore fintech EMIs (e.g., Revolut Business, Wise, Payoneer) are not chartered or licensed in mainland China and are legally barred from providing deposit-taking, payment, or lending services to PRC entities under the PBOC's Administrative Measures for Non-bank Payment Institutions and the State Council's Rules on the Regulation of P2P Lending. These EMIs may be used only by the Hong Kong parent for out-of-country treasury purposes, and even then, a complete paper trail must exist. For the mainland WFOE, the onboarding viability tier is 'Low' — the onshore entity must maintain its full operating cash flow in a licensed Chinese commercial bank. A letter of undertaking from an offshore EMI to the Chinese bank will not satisfy Know-Your-Customer requirements, and any attempted use of an EMI for capital injection would trigger immediate AML suspicious transaction reporting to the China Anti-Money Laundering Monitoring and Analysis Center (CAMLMAC). The only permissible non-bank rail is a licensed third-party payment institution approved by the PBOC, and that institution may not be an offshore EMI.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital is routed from the HK parent via SWIFT MT103 to the WFOE's SAFE-registered Capital Account (Foreign Exchange Capital Project Settlement Account) held at a Tier-1 domestic commercial bank. Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

Statutory withholding tax on dividends paid by a Chinese resident enterprise to a non-resident enterprise is 10% (CIT Law Article 37). Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File preparation is mandatory if any of the following related-party transaction amounts are exceeded in a tax year: related-party tangible property transfers > RMB 200 million; related-party intangible property transfers > RMB 100 million; related-party financial asset transfers (including loans, guarantees, equity) > RMB 100 million; related-party service transactions > RMB 40 million. Master File preparation is required if the group's consolidated annual revenue is above RMB 5.5 billion or if the enterprise's aggregate related-party transactions for the year exceed RMB 1 billion. The enterprise must also complete the A109000 related-party transaction reporting schedule in the annual CIT return on the official electronic filing system.

Economic substance scope: For the Chinese WFOE itself, economic substance is required to sustain tax residency and deductibility of expenses; the tax authorities will look at actual business premises, employees, decision-making in China, and the location of key credit-risk management functions. Under Chinese GAAR (CIT Law Article 47) and the substantive taxation doctrine, the WFOE cannot be a pure booking company for intra-group lending. For the Hong Kong parent or affiliate claiming treaty benefits, the IRD's DIPN 60 and the OECD Multilateral Instrument require that the HK entity have genuine economic activity and a Principal Purpose Test (PPT) is satisfied; all Core Income-Generating Activities (including the strategic asset and liability management of the micro-lending platform, credit appetite modelling, and capital allocation decisions) must be substantiated in Hong Kong. A shell HK conduit without CIGA will be stripped of treaty benefits and the arrangement may be recharacterized, causing additional Chinese WHT and penalties.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Transfer Pricing Profit Shifting / Intra-Group Financing Abuse

Statutory sanction: CIT Law Articles 41, 42, 44, 113 and 114; SAT Announcement 2016 No. 42 and No. 64; TCAL Article 63 (tax evasion) and Article 69 (withholding). The tax authorities may adjust taxable income to the arm's length standard, disallow the excessive interest or fee deduction, impose a fine of 50% to 500% of the tax shortfall plus a daily 0.05% late penalty, and launch a special tax investigation. In egregious cases criminal tax evasion prosecution may ensue.

Director personal liability: The legal representative and executive directors who knowingly sign false intercompany contracts or approve artificially priced payments may be jointly and severally liable for the re-determined tax and penalties under TCAL Article 57 and relevant judicial interpretations on corporate veil piercing. Personal bank accounts and assets can be seized, travel bans (blacklisting) under the exit-control coordination mechanism will be triggered, and criminal conviction under Criminal Law Article 201 can lead to imprisonment of up to 7 years, commensurate with the amount evaded.
Dividend Distribution Without Withholding Tax / DTA Abuse

Statutory sanction: CIT Law Articles 37 and 38; Implementation Rules Article 105; TCAL Article 69 (withholding agent fine of 50% to 300% of the un-withheld tax) and Article 63 (tax evasion). The tax authority will recover the unpaid WHT, impose the fine and daily interest, and reverse the reduction under the DTA if the principal purpose test and beneficial ownership requirements are not met.

Director personal liability: Directors who pass the dividend resolution and approve the outbound transfer without ensuring WHT compliance are 'responsible persons' under TCAL; they face personal liability for the collection of the unpaid tax, asset freezing, and exit denial (blacklisting). If the arrangement was carried out with fraudulent intent to evade taxes, personal criminal liability under Criminal Law Article 201 can result in imprisonment of 3 to 7 years and substantial fines, depending on the amount and degree of malice.
Shell Company / No Economic Substance in Hong Kong and Fraudulent Treaty Claim

Statutory sanction: CIT Law Articles 47 (General Anti-Avoidance Rule), 48 (Special Tax Investigation), and Article 120; SAT Public Notice 2018 No. 9 (Beneficial Ownership); Circular on the China-HK DTA Article 27 and the OECD BEPS MLI. The tax authority may deny the treaty benefits, recharacterize the HK entity as not the beneficial owner, and impose the full statutory WHT (10% on dividends, interest, and royalties) on all outbound payments, with interest accumulated at the statutory rate. A fine of 50% to 500% of the tax shortfall can be imposed for intentional evasion.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil can be pierced under PRC Civil Code Article 83 and the Supreme People's Court Circle Case Group No. 15 where the corporate form is abused to achieve an illegal tax objective; directors and shadow managers who control both the shell HK company and the WFOE may be held personally liable for the entire unpaid tax, interest, and penalties. Exit bans under TCAL Article 44 are routinely imposed on responsible persons in foreign-related tax fraud, and the issuance of a false TRC or forged board documents may lead to criminal labour under Criminal Law Articles 205 and 210, carrying imprisonment up to 10 years in serious cross-border fraud scenarios.
