Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA); electronic filing and compliance via the 'Electronic Tax Bureau' (eTax) at etax.chinatax.gov.cn, with the official 'China Tax Service' mobile application and the National VAT Invoice Verification Platform (invoices.chinatax.gov.cn) for invoice controls.

E-government filing portal

The PRC has integrated tax registration with business licensing under the 'five certificates in one' reform. The Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE) is first incorporated as a limited liability company with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) through the National Enterprise Registration Online Service. On issuance of the business license, the SAMR system automatically allocates an 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which is the sole statutory Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) and VAT registration number. The WFOE must then log into the Electronic Tax Bureau with the USCC to complete the taxpayer status confirmation ('shui fei zhong lei ren ding'), register bank accounts and accounting policies, and elect VAT taxpayer status (small-scale or general). For a foreign non-resident parent entity with no establishment in China (including a Hong Kong SAR shareholder), no separate PRC TIN is issued; instead, the parent is identified through the WFOE's withholding tax filings. To claim treaty benefits under the Mainland and Hong Kong Closer Economic Partnership Arrangement (CEPA) tax provisions, the Hong Kong shareholder must obtain a Certificate of Resident Status from the Hong Kong Inland Revenue Department and provide it to the STA for the reduced withholding rate. The WFOE's USCC is the sole identifier for enterprise income tax, VAT, customs, and foreign-exchange filing purposes.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch pass-through election exists under PRC Enterprise Income Tax Law. For VAT, a WFOE whose annual taxable turnover does not exceed CNY 5,000,000 may elect small-scale taxpayer treatment (currently a 1%, 3%, or reduced levy) or voluntarily register as a general VAT taxpayer; general registration, once made, is subject to change only on statutory conditions. For dividends paid to a qualifying Hong Kong resident shareholder, an election/claim is available under the PRC-HK double-tax arrangement to reduce the dividend withholding tax from 10% to 5% if the beneficial owner holds at least 25% of the WFOE's capital and satisfies anti-abuse substance requirements.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

6%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

CNY 5,000,000 annual taxable turnover for mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration. All enterprises already possess an automatic VAT taxpayer identity; those with annual taxable sales at or below CNY 5,000,000 are treated as small-scale VAT taxpayers and may still register as general taxpayers if they meet the collateral requirements.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exported goods qualify for 0% VAT when they are physically removed from mainland China customs territory for sale to a foreign buyer and the transaction results in foreign-exchange settlement. Cross-border services may be zero-rated or VAT-exempt only where the service is consumed entirely outside China and falls within the STA's catalogue for offshore services; for this algorithmic proprietary trading activity, trading for the WFOE's own account is outside the scope of VAT because no consideration is derived from a customer. Separately invoiced offshore trading-analytics or market-data services supplied to overseas clients may be zero-rated if the services are performed wholly outside China and meet the relevant documentation tests. Any zero-rating claim requires a valid export customs declaration (for goods) or the supply-character criteria for offshore services, plus proof that the overseas recipient is entitled to receive the service outside China.

  • Export Goods Declaration (Customs) generated through the China International Trade Single Window and bearing the export-declaration number
  • Bill of Lading, air waybill, or other transport document evidencing physical export from mainland China
  • Commercial invoice and detailed packing list signed by the seller and matching the customs declaration data
  • Foreign Exchange Receipt Declaration issued by a designated foreign-exchange bank under SAFE, together with the corresponding inward remittance advice
  • VAT invoice (ordinary or special) and the purchase-side input VAT documentation sufficient to trace the exported goods
  • Signed export contract and any transport insurance certificates required by the STA and customs for export-refund eligibility

All VAT invoices must be issued through the STA's Golden Tax Phase IV infrastructure. The 'Fully Digitalized Electronic Invoice' regime (digital e-invoice) is mandatory for an expanding list of taxpayers; issuance requires real-time machine-to-machine transmission to the STA's invoice database through certified e-invoice APIs or service providers. Each invoice receives a cryptographic QR code and digital signature that is cleared and authenticated instantly; there is no separate physical tax stamping. For export transactions, the China International Trade Single Window transmits customs declaration data electronically to the tax and foreign-exchange authorities, enabling automatic invoice-clearance matching.

Export zero-rating and refund claims are subject to ex post audit by the STA and customs. Where a claim is based on false declarations, forged transport documents, unmatched foreign-exchange receipts, or non-qualifying offshore services, the tax authority will claw back the zero-rated output adjustment and corresponding input VAT credits, add late-payment interest, and impose penalties from 50% up to five times the tax amount (and, for fraudulent export refunds, one to five times the refunded amount) under Articles 63 and 66 of the Law on the Administration of Tax Levying and Collection. In serious cases involving counterfeit invoices or money laundering, the STA will refer the matter to the customs anti-smuggling authorities or the police for criminal investigation under Article 204 of the Criminal Law, downgrade the taxpayer's credit rating, freeze invoice quotas, and apply enhanced anti-money-laundering scrutiny to all related payment rails.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China maintains a managed floating exchange rate regime with current-account convertibility and a regulated capital account. For a mainland WFOE, RMB and foreign currency current-account payments, including trade-related receipts and payments, service fees, royalties, and dividends, are generally convertible against genuine transaction documents and tax certificates. Capital-account transactions, such as FDI capital contribution, shareholder loans, foreign debt drawdown, reduction of capital, and liquidation proceeds, require SAFE registration, a domestic designated bank, and purpose-bound settlement. Profit remittance is treated as a current-account outflow: the WFOE must first complete annual statutory auditing, file and settle corporate income tax, obtain a withholding tax certificate for the HK parent, prepare a board resolution for dividend distribution, and present the audited financial statements to the remitting bank. The bank then executes the outbound transfer under the verified profit-remittance purpose code; no special SAFE approval is needed, but every document must be retained for inspection. RMB is not freely convertible offshore for speculative purposes. Any conversion must be supported by a real economic demand, a compliant invoice or contract, and must satisfy PBOC and SAFE anti-money-laundering, anti-tax-evasion, and cross-border payment integrity checks. The treasury blueprint must therefore use segregated RMB and foreign currency accounts with disciplined SAFE reporting and complete transaction-file retention.

Export proceeds surrender rules

China does not impose a fixed mandatory surrender ratio; the statutory conversion ratio is effectively 0% of export proceeds that must be compulsorily sold. Instead, export proceeds must be repatriated to mainland China and credited to a foreign-currency account at a designated bank, with receipt reported through the Goods Trade Monitoring System. The normal collection period is 90 days from the export customs declaration; if collection is expected beyond 90 days, the WFOE must register the deferred export collection with SAFE before the 90th day. A-class enterprises enjoy simplified verification, B-class enterprises face additional documentary checks, and C-class enterprises are subject to supervised settlement and enhanced monitoring.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC), acting through the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) and its provincial and municipal branches

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Offshore digital banks and financial-technology EMIs, including Hong Kong-licensed virtual banks, do not hold a mainland PBOC banking licence and are legally barred from opening basic deposit accounts, receiving FDI capital-account flows, or participating in renminbi clearing on behalf of a mainland WFOE. They cannot replace a SAFE-regulated commercial bank and are therefore rated non-viable as a primary treasury rail. A licensed mainland non-bank payment institution may be used only for narrow low-value collection or disbursement workflows in e-commerce scenarios, but it cannot provide SWIFT, FX settlement, capital verification, or trade-finance clearing. All core corporate banking and liquidity functions must remain with Tier-1 Chinese commercial banks.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The remittance must use SAFE foreign exchange purpose code 622011 for initial foreign-investor capital contribution or 622012 for capital increase, and the receiving bank will credit the dedicated FDI capital escrow account only after verifying the SAFE FDI registration code and the matching entity name. The capital must be verified by a licensed mainland CPA and documented in a capital verification report before conversion into RMB for operating expenses. All conversion records, bank statements, and purpose codes must be retained for SAFE and PBOC inspection. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% on dividends distributed by the Chinese WFOE to its non-resident Hong Kong parent company (deemed to be a non-resident enterprise for China-sourced dividend income), payable on the gross amount at distribution.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File (contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation) is mandatory if any of the following annual related-party transaction amounts are exceeded in RMB: (1) tangible property transfers > RMB 200 million; (2) intangible property transfers > RMB 100 million; (3) financial asset transfers > RMB 100 million; (4) other related-party transactions (service, interest, royalties) > RMB 40 million. Master File is required if the enterprise group has consolidated revenue above RMB 2 billion and the Chinese entity is required to file the local file. Failure to prepare a Local File on time triggers a RMB 100,000 penalty and potentially recharacterization of transactions with a 5-year retrospective adjustment.

Economic substance scope: China applies economic substance principles through the CIT General Anti-Avoidance Rules (GAAR) and the Beneficial Ownership framework for treaty claims. The Chinese WFOE must maintain real substance: a physical office, employees with decision-making authority for trading strategies, management of market-making risks, and execution of core income-generating activities. If the HK holding company is deemed a pure conduit without personnel or management functions, the Chinese tax authority will disregard it and deny treaty benefits, reclassifying dividends as fully taxable and confiscating preferential rates. Additionally, the Hong Kong parent should comply with Hong Kong's Economic Substance Regulations (if applicable) insofar as its claimed offshore profits must be supported by real business activities in Hong Kong.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion Through Fraudulent Transfer Pricing

Statutory sanction: PRC Tax Collection Administration Law Article 35 allows the tax authority to assess taxable income using deemed profit methods; Article 62 imposes penalties for incomplete tax filings; Article 63 imposes a penalty of 50% to 5 times the underpaid tax, plus daily surcharge at 0.05% of the overdue amount; PRC Criminal Law Article 201 may impose criminal fines and imprisonment of up to 7 years for fraudulent tax evasion exceeding statutory thresholds.

Director personal liability: Under PRC Company Law Article 20, courts can pierce the corporate veil if a director abuses the corporate personality to evade taxes or harm creditors, holding directors jointly and severally liable for the WFOE's unpaid taxes, penalties, and interest. Directors who knowingly sign false financial statements or instruct fraudulent TP arrangements may face criminal prosecution; principals can receive up to 3 years (or 3-7 years for repeated or large-scale evasion) in prison, travel restrictions (exit bans) under the tax blacklisting mechanism, and personal asset restitution orders.
Shell Company Conflict of Laws and Treaty Abuse

Statutory sanction: Article 2 of the PRC General Anti-Avoidance Rules (Notice of the State Taxation Administration [2009] No. 698 and CIT Law Article 47) allows the authorities to recharacterize the transaction, deny the treaty benefit, restore the 10% dividend WHT plus VAT and late surcharges; penalty up to 5 times the tax shortfall; concurrent penalties under Tax Administration Law Article 62 and Criminal Law Article 201 for intentional false filings.

Director personal liability: Directors and shadow directors of the WFOE and HK parent who structure the shell arrangement are personally liable for the resulting tax debt when the corporate veil is pierced; travel bans and asset freezes under the joint enforcement mechanism of tax authorities and the exit-control system; imprisonment of up to 3 years for defrauding tax revenue and up to 7 years for aggravated fraudulent tax crimes; additionally, regulatory blacklisting under the 'Memorandum on Joint Disciplinary Actions for Tax Law Violations' restricts directors' entry/exit, financing, and professional licenses.
Failure to Withhold and Remit Tax on Outbound Payments

Statutory sanction: PRC Tax Collection Administration Law Article 69 imposes a penalty of 50% to 3 times the undeclared withheld tax on the withholding agent; Article 64 imposes penalties for false withholding declarations; late payment surcharges of 0.05% per day on the unpaid amount compound until full settlement; in severe cases, the tax authority may blacklist the entity under the Major Tax Violation Case Publishing and Joint Punishment Scheme, triggering multi-agency enforcement.

Director personal liability: Directors and the financial officer are jointly and severally liable for unremitted withholding taxes if they participate in the decision to omit withholding; civil liability includes restitution of the withheld sum, payment of fines, and disgorgement of unauthorized profit; criminal prosecution for tax evasion or misappropriation of public funds can lead to imprisonment up to 5 years under PRC Criminal Law Article 201; all directors may be barred from leaving China via the exit-control system until the tax liability is settled, and personal assets may be seized to satisfy the unpaid claims.
