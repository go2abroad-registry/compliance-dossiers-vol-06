Stage 03
Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

      
Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

    

    

      
Tax ID Application Infrastructure

      

        
Competent tax authority
State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA), administered through the official STA website (www.chinatax.gov.cn) and the provincial-level Electronic Tax Bureau portals forming the national E-Tax system. The State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) operates the One-Window Service for incorporation and simultaneous tax registration. For the Hong Kong SAR parent investor, the Inland Revenue Department (IRD) administers profits tax and business registration, but the mainland WFOE is a Chinese resident taxpayer registered exclusively with the STA.

        
E-government filing portal
Step 1: The foreign shareholder, typically the Hong Kong SAR company, must be validly registered and in possession of its Hong Kong Business Registration Certificate; its constitutional documents, board resolutions, directorship evidence and registered office certificates must be notarized and apostilled for submission to the PRC market regulator. Step 2: The WFOE is incorporated through the SAMR One-Window Service, which issues the statutory 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code; this code is the taxpayer identification number (TIN) for all PRC tax purposes and no separate TIN certificate is issued. Step 3: Within 30 days of issuance of the business license, the WFOE must complete tax registration with the competent local STA through the E-Tax system, declaring the legal representative, financial controller, registered address, bank accounts, ownership chain, business scope and accounting arrangements. Step 4: The STA then assigns the VAT taxpayer classification (general taxpayer or small-scale taxpayer), activates the electronic invoice module, export tax rebate registration, payroll individual income tax filing, social insurance collection linkage and the digital tax-control certificate. Step 5: The WFOE must open a foreign currency account at a PRC designated foreign-exchange bank and register trade-related foreign exchange details in the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) monitoring system to repatriate export proceeds. A pure non-resident enterprise without a permanent establishment in China does not receive a Unified Social Credit Code; it is registered only through the PRC withholding agent in the STA non-resident tax file system.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

      

        
Default tax classification
Entity_Level_Taxation

        
Available election forms
None. The PRC tax system does not provide check-the-box entity classification, hybrid mismatch elections, or any pass-through election. The WFOE is mandatorily a Chinese resident limited liability company subject to entity-level Enterprise Income Tax at the 25% statutory rate on worldwide income, and no tax-transparent election is available.

      

    

    

      
VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

      

        
Standard VAT / GST rate
13%

        
Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)
CNY 5,000,000 in annual taxable sales is the compulsory general VAT taxpayer registration threshold; a newly established WFOE must complete tax registration with the STA within 30 days of SAMR incorporation irrespective of turnover. Financial services, including digital wallet services and virtual IBAN issuance, are generally taxable at the 6% VAT rate in the standard regime rather than the 13% goods rate.

      

      
      

        Export 0% zero-rating conditions
        
Goods physically exported from the PRC customs territory, and qualifying cross-border services supplied to non-resident recipients under the STA zero-rating/exemption catalogue, may be eligible for the 0% VAT rate and/or the export VAT rebate. To substantiate zero-rating for cross-border services, the WFOE must demonstrate that the service is rendered entirely outside China, that the beneficial recipient is a non-resident enterprise, that no benefit or use occurs inside China, and that the contract, invoice, payment and delivery evidence prove offshore supply. Virtual IBAN issuance, digital wallet reserve management and payment processing services must determine whether they fall within the cross-border service zero-rating/exemption rules or the financial services exemption; if the services are consumed inside China or supplied to domestic counterparties, 6% VAT applies.

China operates the Golden Tax Project Phase IV fully digitized electronic invoice system. All VAT taxpayers must issue Special VAT Invoices or Ordinary VAT Invoices through the STA-controlled Electronic Invoice Service Platform; every invoice is created with a unique tax-control digital code, and invoice data is transmitted in real time to the national tax database. Buyers must complete electronic invoice confirmation in the STA invoice verification platform before input VAT credit is allowed. The mandate requires API or web-service integration, digital seal management, real-time reporting, electronic archiving and automated cross-matching among VAT returns, customs declarations, foreign exchange records and invoice data to detect circular invoicing and export fraud.

      
The STA, Customs and SAFE jointly operate electronic data matching that automatically flags inconsistencies among customs declaration values, invoice amounts, contractual terms, transport documents and foreign exchange receipts. If export proceeds are not repatriated within 180 days, or the zero-rated export claim is unsupported, the zero-rating is withdrawn and the supply is reclassified as a domestic taxable sale. The STA will collect the underpaid VAT, impose a late payment surcharge of 0.05% per day, and levy administrative fines ranging from 50% to five times the tax underpaid for evasion, or one to five times the defrauded refund for fraudulent export refund claims. Serious cases are referred for criminal prosecution for false export declaration, smuggling and fraudulent export tax refund under the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

Stage 04
Banking & Treasury Architecture

      
Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

    

    

      
Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

      

        
Current-account convertibility
China maintains a managed floating exchange rate regime with RMB convertibility restricted at the capital account layer. For a PRC WFOE, current-account transactions such as trade-related fees, royalties, salaries and operating expenses are broadly convertible and may be settled through banks under the Foreign Exchange Verification system, provided the underlying transaction documents are genuine and consistent with the declared business scope. Capital-account operations—including the registration of the WFOE, the injection of paid-in capital from the Hong Kong parent, the later conversion of registered capital into RMB, foreign debt borrowings and the eventual repatriation of profits—require prior SAFE registration or approval. WFOE paid-in capital must be received in a dedicated FDI capital account; conversion into RMB is then processed at the bank under the capital account settlement of foreign exchange framework. Profit remittance is legally permissible after the annual foreign exchange inspection and the submission of audited financial statements, tax clearance certificates and the board resolution for dividend distribution, but practical hurdles appear when cross-border flows are inconsistent with the approved business scope, when registered capital has not been fully injected, or when tax certificates are delayed. Convertibility therefore exists but is conditional, evidence-based and transaction-specific.

        
Export proceeds surrender rules
PRC exporters are no longer subject to a fixed mandatory surrender ratio; the statutory requirement is repatriation of trade proceeds to a domestic foreign exchange settlement account within 180 days from the date of export customs clearance unless SAFE approves a longer window. The mandatory conversion ratio into RMB is 0%; the enterprise may retain foreign exchange in bank accounts or convert voluntarily. However, the entire amount of export receipts must be reported under the trade monitoring system, and each receipt is automatically matched to electronic customs declarations, logistics and invoice data; unexplained lags, split payments and over or short receipts trigger documentary verification and possible administrative penalties.

      

      
Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China, operating under the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

    

    

      
Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

      

        
        

          

            Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails
            Fit: High
          

          
Low. Offshore digital EMIs, including Hong Kong-licensed stored value facilities and virtual bank apps, are legally barred from servicing a PRC WFOE directly unless they hold a PBOC-issued payment business license. Virtual IBAN issuance is not a recognized standalone license in mainland China, and any fintech EMI without a mainland branch or PBOC approval cannot open settlement accounts, clear RMB, or provide cross-border payment services to the WFOE. The only viable route is to use licensed domestic tier-1 banks for treasury flows and to engage a licensed PRC non-bank payment institution only as a white-label processor under strict regulatory supervision.

Turnover: Every Hong Kong company, regardless of size or turnover, must prepare financial statements that give a true and fair view and be audited by a Hong Kong Certified Public Accountant (CPA) or a licensed corporate practice. Mandatory Return Breakdown

      
      

        
        
Deadline:  · Frequency: 

        
The final annual CIT return must be filed and the final tax balance paid by 31 May following the end of the calendar tax year. Quarterly provisional returns and prepayments are due within 15 days after the end of each quarter, i.e. by 15 April, 15 July, 15 October and 15 January.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

      
Master / Local File threshold: Under SAT Public Notice [2016] No. 42, the Chinese WFOE must prepare and maintain a Local File (contemporaneous documentation) if its annual related-party transactions exceed RMB 200 million in tangible asset transfers, RMB 100 million in intangible asset transfers, RMB 100 million in financial asset transfers, or RMB 40 million in other related-party transactions such as services, royalties, management fees, and interest. A Master File is required where the group's aggregate annual related-party transaction amount exceeds RMB 1 billion.

      
Economic substance scope: The Chinese WFOE must demonstrate substantive economic activity through licensed operations, physical premises, employees, KYC/AML compliance processes, and treasury management in China. The Hong Kong shareholder must satisfy the economic substance test under the China-Hong Kong DTA beneficial ownership analysis: real business premises, devoted staff, actual revenue, and decision-making authority in Hong Kong. While Hong Kong's Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) and Core Income-Generating Activities (CIGA) test apply to certain Hong Kong business sectors, the DTA substance test independently applies to Hong Kong holding companies claiming treaty benefits. Lack of substance in either the Chinese WFOE or the Hong Kong parent may cause reallocation of profits, denial of treaty benefits, and application of China's General Anti-Abuse Rule under Article 47 of the CIT Law.

      

    

    

      
Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

      
      

        Tax Evasion / False Return
        
Statutory sanction: Under Article 63 of the PRC Tax Administration Law, the tax authority will recover the underpaid tax plus daily surcharges of 0.05% and impose a penalty from 50% to 500% of the evaded tax. Under Article 201 of the PRC Criminal Law, evading a large amount of tax constitutes criminal tax evasion punishable by imprisonment of up to seven years and criminal fines. The taxpayer's name is publicly listed in the tax dishonesty blacklist.

        
Director personal liability: The court may pierce the corporate veil under Article 23 of the PRC Company Law, holding the controlling shareholder and directors jointly and severally liable for the unpaid corporate tax and penalties. The responsible legal representative, chief financial officer, and tax director may be subjected to personal travel bans, asset preservation orders, blacklisting as seriously dishonest persons, and criminal imprisonment from three to seven years.

      

      
      

        Treaty Abuse / Shell Company / Conduit Arrangement
        
Statutory sanction: The tax authority applies the General Anti-Abuse Rule under Article 47 of the CIT Law, recharacterizes the transaction, denies the DTA benefits, and re-imposes 10% statutory dividend withholding tax. Under Article 69 of the Tax Administration Law, the withholding agent that fails to withhold is liable for payment of the tax and revocation of withholding qualification; fraudulent treaty claims also trigger a penalty from 50% to 500% of the underpaid WHT and daily surcharges.

        
Director personal liability: Directors who design the shell structure may be personally liable for the short-withheld dividend tax, jointly liable under veil-piercing principles, banned from serving as directors of financial institutions, and subject to travel bans and exit controls. Criminal misuse of a shell company to evade tax or launder proceeds may result in up to ten years of imprisonment and confiscation of personal assets.

      

      
      

        Unlicensed E-Money Business / AML Non-Compliance
        
Statutory sanction: Unlicensed payment services violate the PRC Payment Institution Regulation and PBOC rules, leading to confiscation of illegal gains, fines from one to five times the illegal gains, closure of accounts, and revocation of business licenses. Money laundering under Article 191 of the PRC Criminal Law carries imprisonment from five to ten years, criminal fines, and confiscation; the AML violation compounds the CIT evasion because illegal income and related expenses are not deductible.

        
Director personal liability: The board members and the Anti-Money Laundering compliance officer will face personal criminal prosecution, asset seizure, travel bans, and disqualification from financial industry positions. The corporate veil is pierced to recover unpaid taxes, AML fines, and customer restitution; directors and senior managers may be sentenced to up to ten years of imprisonment, with career bans from payment services and financial technology management.
