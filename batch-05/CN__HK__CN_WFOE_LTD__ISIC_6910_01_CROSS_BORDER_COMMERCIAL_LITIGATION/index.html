Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

Virtual office or commercial mailbox addresses are generally rejected by SAMR, tax authorities, and banks for a WFOE in the legal services and arbitration-consulting industry. The State Taxation Administration requires a physical address for tax registration and for the issuance of electronic invoices; local banks conducting KYC and account-opening due diligence will refuse a purely virtual address because the industry is professional and requires invoices with the office address. A dedicated physical office in a proper commercial building, a lease contract registered with the local housing administration, utility bills under the company name, and a visible company signboard are mandatory. A co-working space with a private lockable office and an assigned meeting room may be accepted if the lease is for at least 12 months and the property is zoned for commercial use. P.O. boxes or agent-registered virtual offices are not permissible.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None. The Foreign Investment Law and the Company Law impose no statutory minimum registered capital for a WFOE limited liability company; the capital is determined solely in the Articles of Association. No blocked escrow account is required. The Hong Kong shareholder may subscribe capital in RMB or convertible foreign currency and must contribute the subscribed amount by the date specified in the Articles of Association, subject to State Administration of Foreign Exchange registration and reporting.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; electronic filing and registration portal is the STA Electronic Tax Office (e-Tax) platform operated by the provincial branches of the STA.

E-government filing portal

The WFOE obtains a business license from the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR); the license carries the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which is automatically recognised as the taxpayer identification number (TIN) for all tax purposes. The foreign non-resident shareholders then complete tax registration with the local STA branch through the one-stop combined registration mechanism within 30 calendar days of business licence issuance, or directly via the e-Tax portal. Required items include the articles of association, Chinese address proof for the registered office, legal representative identity document, banking details, and board resolutions. The tax authority creates the taxpayer profile, assigns VAT and income tax obligations, determines the VAT taxpayer identity (small-scale taxpayer or general VAT taxpayer), and activates the electronic invoice U-Key / fully digitalised electronic invoice capability. No additional VAT ID is required for a WFOE; the USCC is the statutory TIN. If the WFOE makes cross-border payments to its non-resident parent, a non-resident enterprise withholding registration is also opened for enterprise income tax withholding on dividends, interest, royalties, and service fees.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None. The People's Republic of China does not operate a check-the-box or similar flow-through election for a WFOE. A WFOE is a resident enterprise taxed at entity level on worldwide enterprise income and is a separate VAT taxpayer. No hybrid mismatch election is available. The only selectable tax status is the VAT registration category (small-scale taxpayer vs general VAT taxpayer) and, where applicable, the cross-border supply classification under tax treaties.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

6%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No de minimis turnover threshold for registration; every WFOE engaged in taxable activities must register on incorporation. Mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration applies when annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000; the small-scale taxpayer monthly sales exemption threshold is RMB 100,000 or annually RMB 1,200,000, with exact thresholds depending on the prevailing STA relief announcement.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export of physical goods qualifies for the 0% VAT rate only when the goods are actually shipped out of the PRC customs territory, a formal export customs declaration is filed, the sale is made to an overseas buyer, and the foreign exchange proceeds are repatriated to China. For qualifying offshore services and digital services, the supply must be rendered to a non-resident customer, wholly consumed outside China, have no direct connection with PRC real estate or PRC taxable economic activity, and be evidenced by a supply contract, service completion record, and foreign-currency receipt. For the WFOE's cross-border commercial litigation and legal services, the statutory treatment is generally the cross-border services exemption (not zero-rating) under Caishui [2016] No. 36, Annex 4, provided the service recipient is offshore and the service is completely consumed outside China.

  • Customs Export Declaration Form of the People's Republic of China (customs-cleared export bill of entry)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical movement of goods
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or bank-issued foreign exchange receipt voucher confirming repatriation of export proceeds
  • VAT special invoice or fully digitalised electronic invoice issued for the export sale
  • For services: Service Trade Cross-Border Payment/Receipt Filing Form filed with SAFE or designated bank, service contract, and a statement of complete offshore consumption

China mandates invoice issuance through the STA's Golden Tax Project Phase IV and the Electronic Invoice Service Platform. All VAT invoices, including fully digitalised electronic invoices, must be issued in the STA-prescribed data format, assigned a unique invoice code and digital signature, and transmitted to the STA in real time for fiscal clearance. Special VAT invoices are subject to U-Key authentication and batch-check reporting; no physical fiscal stamp is required for fully digital invoices, but the data must pass the STA's consistency, encryption, and anti-fraud validations before the buyer can deduct input VAT.

Export zero-rating or exemption is audited by the STA against Customs and SAFE data. If the documentation is incomplete, the 0% rate is disallowed and the output VAT must be paid with late-payment surcharge. If the zero-rating is obtained using false declarations, Article 63 of the Tax Collection Administration Law empowers the STA to recover the underpaid tax and impose a penalty of 50% to 500% of the underpaid amount; serious cases are prosecuted under Criminal Law Article 204 for fraudulently obtaining export tax rebates, with penalties up to life imprisonment and confiscation of property. The WFOE must retain all export and FX documentation for at least 10 years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The CNY is convertible on current account for genuine international trade and services but remains heavily controlled on capital account. A Hong Kong-owned WFOE must first complete SAFE foreign direct investment registration before any inbound equity funding can be credited to a domestic foreign currency capital account. Each conversion of capital-account FX into CNY must be tied to the registered business purpose and documented project or operating expense; reversing the flow from CNY back into foreign currency for distribution is treated as a fresh capital-account remittance. Cross-border service receipts from commercial litigation clients are current-account proceeds and may be settled through licensed banks after verification of engagement letters, invoices, and the SAFE trade-in-services filing. Dividend remittance to the Hong Kong parent requires audited financial statements, corporate income tax filing and clearance, a shareholder resolution, and proof that the PRC-Hong Kong DTA reduced withholding tax rate is available. Without a valid underlying commercial or capital transaction, no conversion or remittance will be processed. Hence dual ledgers - segregated capital settlement and operational FX current accounts - must be maintained under banking supervision.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For trade in goods and cross-border services, export and supply-of-service foreign exchange receipts must be repatriated to a licensed bank in mainland China and entered in the relevant SAFE verification system. The statutory benchmark for collection is the contractually agreed payment date and, in any event, not later than 90 days after the service completion or customs declaration date unless a formal SAFE or bank registration of deferred payment is made; overdue balances beyond 180 days require an individual reconciliation explanation. There is no fixed mandatory conversion ratio: the WFOE may retain 100% of verified receipts in a foreign currency current account or convert all or part into CNY at a commercial bank. In practice, the bank reviews each conversion under current-account authenticity rules and the company's prior-year receipts and settlements, and any conversion of capital-account funds into CNY is subject to separate negative-list control.

Central bank supervision: The People's Bank of China (PBOC) delegated through the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Route inbound equity from the Hong Kong parent via SWIFT MT103 to the WFOE capital account at a Tier-1 bank. The remittance must reference the SAFE FDI registration certificate number and use the SAFE-approved inbound direct investment equity contribution purpose code. The receiving bank credits the foreign-currency capital account and completes the capital account inflow filing; a PRC CPA must then issue a capital verification report. Proceeds may be settled into CNY only for permitted operating purposes within the WFOE's business scope, and each RMB conversion must be recorded in the SAFE bank system. For litigation chambers, capital funds may not be swept into the client trust account.For disbursements to overseas expert witnesses, foreign counsel, arbitral institutions, and judgment enforcement agencies, the WFOE will initiate SWIFT MT103 with the SAFE service-trade purpose code for cross-border legal services. The bank requires the underlying engagement contract, an itemized invoice, proof of service, and tax clearance or withholding certificate if needed. Commercial LCs are not appropriate for intangible legal services; documentary collections may be used only for reimbursable costs with original invoices and a bill of exchange, but MT103 is the efficient clearing rail. Domestic court fees and PRC arbitral institution payments are settled in CNY through central bank clearing systems in real time.Onshore CNY forward contracts with a licensed PRC bank, documentary-backed by genuine current-account legal service receipts or payments, with tenor normally capped at one year and rollover only after re-verification.Cross-currency swaps approved for matched foreign-currency debt or FDI capital repayment exposure; not available for speculative conversion of litigation fee income.Offshore non-deliverable forwards are not available to the mainland WFOE; the Hong Kong parent may execute CNH NDFs in Hong Kong outside SAFE's onshore perimeter under HK bank suitability and derivatives licensing rules.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% under PRC domestic law for dividends paid by a WFOE to a non-resident enterprise shareholder; reduced to 5% under the China-Hong Kong Arrangement if the Hong Kong recipient is a company beneficially owning directly at least 25% of the WFOE's shares and satisfies the principal purpose and substance tests.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The annual return and final settlement payment must be filed and discharged within 5 months after the fiscal year-end, i.e., on or before 31 May following a calendar tax year; extension time is not automatic and must be applied for before the stated due date through the e-Tax system.
