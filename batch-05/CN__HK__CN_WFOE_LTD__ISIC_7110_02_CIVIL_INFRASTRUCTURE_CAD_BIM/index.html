Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA); online portal: China Tax Electronic Administration (e-Tax) at https://tpass.etax.chinatax.gov.cn/ with local e-Tax platforms linked through the STA national portal

E-government filing portal

1) Incorporate the Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise with the Administration for Market Regulation (AMR) to obtain the business license bearing the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which is the statutory TIN for all Chinese resident enterprises. 2) Within 30 days of license issuance, complete the tax registration through the e-Tax portal by filing the enterprise registration details, bank account information, and designated tax officer; the USCC is automatically activated as the VAT and EIT identifier. 3) If the foreign non-resident parent or any non-resident counterparty has China-source income, it must obtain a non-resident enterprise TIN through the simplified registration procedure on the e-Tax platform (or via its withholding agent) before entering into taxable contracts, using the 'Tax Identification Number for Non-resident Enterprises' application module and presenting the notarised/certified home-country registration document.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None. The People's Republic of China does not provide check-the-box or pass-through classification elections. A WFOE is mandatorily treated as a resident enterprise subject to Enterprise Income Tax on its worldwide income. Hybrid mismatch relief is not available; the anti-abuse framework under the Enterprise Income Tax Law (including General Anti-Abuse Rule) governs.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

All enterprises must complete VAT registration upon incorporation. Mandatory general taxpayer status applies once annual taxable turnover exceeds CNY 5,000,000 (RMB 5 million). Below that threshold, the enterprise may elect small-scale taxpayer status with a 3% simplified collection rate, subject to legal conditions.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under Caishui [2016] No.36 and related STA circulars, the WFOE's offshore BIM/structural drafting services may be eligible for the 0% VAT rate as cross-border design services if: (a) the service is provided to an overseas client (non-resident); (b) the service is entirely performed for use outside China; (c) the consideration is paid in foreign exchange from outside China; and (d) the export service is within the statutory zero-rated catalogue (including engineering design and R&D services). The service provider must file the electronic declaration for zero-rated cross-border services.

  • China Customs Export Declaration (Export Goods Customs Declaration)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / Foreign Exchange Collection Receipt
  • Cross-border Service Zero-Rating Declaration (e-Fapiao record)
  • Signed export service contract with offshore client and statement of sole offshore consumption
  • VAT e-Fapiao bearing the STA digital stamp and reflecting the zero rate

China's VAT system operates under the Golden Tax III (Jin San) administration and the new Full Digitalised Electronic Fapiao (all-electric invoices) revolution. Invoices must be issued via the STA-approved e-Fapiao platform or certified electronic invoice APIs. Real-time fiscal clearance is mandatory: every Fapiao receives a unique 20-character electronic invoice code certified by the STA's encrypted digital stamp (anti-forgery tax-control seal). Batch or offline issuance without STA digital signature is not permitted.

The STA may retroactively audit zero-rated export transactions for up to 10 years. If the export is found not to be substantively consumed outside China or proceeds are not repatriated within the statutory period, the zero-rated treatment is revoked, the VAT refund must be repaid with daily late payment interest (0.05% per day), and an administrative penalty of 50% to 500% of the tax amount may be imposed. Fraudulent zero-rating or false refunds may constitute criminal tax fraud, subject to confiscation of illegal gains, criminal fines and imprisonment of up to life for serious export rebate fraud.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Mainland China operates a current-account-convertible but capital-account-controlled FX regime. The WFOE may receive foreign currency for exported engineering and CAD-BIM services and convert it into RMB through licensed banks without SAFE pre-approval, as long as the underlying transaction is genuine and supported by standard trade documents. Capital-account transactions are not freely convertible: a capital injection from the Hong Kong parent must be registered under SAFE Foreign Direct Investment rules before the remittance is accepted, and every inbound and outbound payment is matched to documentary evidence. Profit remittance is possible only when the WFOE has completed its statutory annual audit, maintained full capital-account registration records, settled corporate income tax and withholding tax liabilities, and passed a bank-level profit-remittance review based on a lawful profit distribution resolution. In practice, the bank will also ask for audited financial statements, tax filing receipts and a board resolution, and may escalate disproportionate or premature dividends to SAFE. Thus, the corridor supports the business only if the treasury function sustains disciplined documentation; otherwise the capital account will block dividend outflows, loan repayments and cross-border supplier payments.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory conversion or surrender ratio is currently imposed on export-FX receipts under SAFE trade-facilitation rules. The WFOE may retain 100% of eligible export foreign-currency proceeds in a designated foreign-exchange settlement account or convert them to RMB at a licensed bank. The statutory repatriation timeline is generally 180 calendar days from the export customs declaration, or from the due date for service-trade receivables where no customs declaration exists; longer deferral up to 360 days may be permitted only with a filed reason in the SAFE trade-monitoring system. RMB conversion requires an underlying invoice, contract, or customs declaration and the correct FX settlement purpose code.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC); State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE); the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) is relevant only to the offshore Hong Kong treasury vehicle, as the operating WFOE is subject to mainland China FX controls.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Low viability for the mainland WFOE. Foreign fintech EMIs such as Wise are not licensed by PBOC to open domestic RMB accounts, to clear onshore payments, or to act as designated banks for SAFE capital verification and trade-monitoring purposes. Wise remains feasible only for the Hong Kong treasury vehicle for low-value administrative disbursements and receivables, and cannot replace a Tier-1 bank for capital injection, export-proceeds repatriation, profit remittance, tax settlement or documentary trade operations. The correct structure is a Tier-1 bank as mandatory onshore rail plus the HK treasury entity for offshore FX risk management, with Wise restricted to minor HK-side payments outside the PRC clearing system.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound foreign direct investment must be wired via SWIFT MT103 to the WFOE's dedicated foreign-exchange capital account at the chosen Tier-1 bank, with the remittance purpose clearly stated as FDI capital injection and referencing the SAFE FDI registration number. The bank uploads the credit to the SAFE capital-project system, performs electronic capital verification and requires a PRC-certified CPA capital verification report before allowing RMB conversion. RMB proceeds are then transferred to the WFOE's RMB basic deposit account and restricted to operating expenses aligned with the approved business scope; no equity on-lending, securities investment, non-business real estate purchase or loan substitution is permitted without SAFE approval.For engineering and CAD-BIM service payments, the WFOE should use SWIFT MT103 from its foreign-exchange settlement account under a current-account service-trade transaction, attaching the commercial contract, invoice, project progress certificate, timesheet summary and non-resident enterprise tax withholding certificate. For physical procurement of construction materials and infrastructure equipment, commercial letters of credit at sight, deferred payment L/C or documentary collections are appropriate, all tracked in the bank's trade-services system and SAFE trade-monitoring framework. Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% on dividends paid by a PRC resident enterprise to a non-resident enterprise under CIT Law Articles 27 and 37.

Annual final return must be filed within 5 months after the end of the calendar tax year, normally by 31 May of the following year; quarterly prepayments are due within 15 days after the end of each quarter; no automatic extension of the annual final settlement.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File is mandatory when the WFOE s related-party transactions in a single year exceed: RMB 200 million for tangible property transfers, RMB 100 million for intangible or financial asset transfers, and RMB 40 million for service transactions; Master File is required when the multinational group s aggregate related-party revenue in China reaches RMB 1 billion; Country-by-Country Report applies where group consolidated revenue exceeds RMB 5 billion. Contemporaneous TP documentation must be prepared before the annual filing due date of 31 May.

Economic substance scope: The Chinese WFOE must demonstrate substance through CIT Law Article 41, Article 47 (GAAR), and STA Announcement 2016 No. 42. Core income-generating activities (CIGA) must be performed in China: hiring civil infrastructure engineers, making design decisions, managing project risk, and owning the relevant software assets and operational bank accounts. If the WFOE functions as an invoice-only conduit while CIGA occurs elsewhere, the tax authority will re-characterise income and deny treaty benefits. The Hong Kong parent, when claiming DTA relief, must also satisfy the Hong Kong Economic Substance Requirement under Inland Revenue Ordinance Article 110 for the architectural and engineering service activity.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion through Transfer Pricing Manipulation

Statutory sanction: CIT Law Article 41, Article 47; Tax Administration Law Articles 63 and 64. The tax authority reattributes income, imposes a penalty of 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax, and applies interest; Criminal Law Article 201 triggers fines and imprisonment up to 7 years when the evaded tax exceeds RMB 50,000 and more than 10% of the tax payable.

Director personal liability: Directors who authorise the false pricing may be personally liable for restitution to the company; the legal representative may be blacklisted and barred from leaving China under the Exit-Entry Administration Law until the tax liability is settled; criminal arrest and civil execution against personal assets follow an adverse judgment.
Failure to Withhold and Remit Withholding Tax on Distributions

Statutory sanction: Tax Administration Law Article 69: penalty of 50% to 300% of the amount that should have been withheld; interest at 0.05% per day; the withholding agent remains jointly liable for the uncollected tax.

Director personal liability: The legal representative, CFO and tax handler may be ordered to make restitution from personal assets if they knowingly diverted withheld sums; entry-exit restriction and the serious-tax-dishonesty blacklist will prohibit travel on high-speed rail and civil aviation until payment is made.
Shell Company, Economic Substance Failure and False Invoicing

Statutory sanction: GAAR under CIT Law Article 47 reconstructs the transaction and disallows treaty benefits; Tax Administration Law Article 36 permits a deemed assessment; false VAT invoicing invokes Criminal Law Article 205, with fines and imprisonment up to life sentence where amounts are substantial; market regulation organs may revoke the business licence.

Director personal liability: Under PRC Civil Code Articles 43 and 44, directors who abuse the independent personality of the enterprise bear joint and personal liability for company debts; in taxation matters, the legal representative is blacklisted, an asset seizure order may be enforced, and responsible directors face imprisonment of 3 to 10 years for false-invoice or evasion offences.
