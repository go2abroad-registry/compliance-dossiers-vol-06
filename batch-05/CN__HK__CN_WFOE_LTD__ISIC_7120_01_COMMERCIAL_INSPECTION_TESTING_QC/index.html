None. No statutory minimum paid-up capital or blocked escrow account is required for a WFOE engaged in commercial inspection, testing and quality-control services. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; statutory electronic filing is performed through the China Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax) online portal operated by the competent provincial STA branch, with the national Full-Digital Electronic Invoice platform integrated into each taxpayer's electronic filing account.

E-government filing portal

For a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise, the Unified Social Credit Code issued on the business license by the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) constitutes the statutory Tax Identification Number (TIN) for all PRC tax filings. The WFOE must complete taxpayer registration with the competent branch of the STA within 30 days of obtaining the business license through the e-Tax portal, activating value-added tax (VAT), corporate income tax, payroll individual income tax, and additional surcharge categories. A foreign non-resident enterprise without an establishment in China must instead apply for a separate non-resident tax registration identification number or rely on its PRC withholding agent to file contract and payment records with the STA, and must claim treaty benefits, including the 5% dividend withholding rate under the Mainland/Hong Kong Double Tax Arrangement, through the non-resident treaty benefit filing procedure. The WFOE must also complete withholding-agent registration for cross-border payments to non-residents within the same electronic tax account.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None - the PRC does not operate a check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch classification election; the WFOE is mandatorily taxed as an entity-level enterprise income taxpayer. The only relevant elections are the VAT taxpayer status choice between small-scale VAT taxpayer and general VAT taxpayer, subject to the RMB 5,000,000 annual turnover threshold, and the enterprise income tax quarterly-estimate-annual-settlement filing regime.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 annual taxable sales: mandatory VAT general taxpayer registration once annual taxable turnover exceeds RMB 5,000,000; below the threshold, small-scale VAT taxpayer status applies with a reduced levy rate, and entities with monthly sales not exceeding RMB 100,000 are exempt from paying VAT under current preferential policy, subject to policy expiry and renewal.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For physical exports of goods, zero-rating applies automatically where the goods leave Chinese customs territory and are supported by a valid export customs declaration; qualifying cross-border services enumerated in the VAT law, such as international transportation, research and development, design, software, and information technology services supplied to overseas recipients, are zero-rated or VAT-exempt. On-site pre-shipment inspection and testing services physically performed inside China, including factory quality-control audits for overseas buyers, do not by themselves qualify as zero-rated offshore services because the service is rendered within China; such services are generally subject to 6% VAT unless the WFOE separately exports goods or the service falls within an enumerated exemption. The export zero-rating and refund path available to the WFOE is therefore the standard goods export rebate regime for its own shipments or the matched zero-rating for offshore services where the statutory condition that the service be wholly used outside China is satisfied.

  • Export Goods Declaration Form issued by the General Administration of Customs of the PRC
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical carriage out of China
  • Export Proceeds Repatriation Record (Foreign Exchange Receipt Voucher) issued by the settlement bank and reported to the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) monitoring system

All VAT invoices are digitized through the national Golden Tax System Phase IV, requiring issuance through the national Full-Digital Electronic Invoice platform or, for legacy taxpayers, the licensed UKey tax-control device; every invoice must be transmitted in real time to the STA data pool before delivery to the purchaser is legally effective, and the purchaser verifies the digital signature and anti-fraud encryption stamp through the national e-invoice verification platform. Cross-checks between invoice issuance, bank settlement flows, and customs declarations are automated, and any mismatch triggers risk-control alarms, invoice quota suspension, and on-site audit.

Export VAT refund claims are cross-checked in real time by the Golden Tax System Phase IV export-rebate module against export customs declaration data, invoice pools, and SAFE foreign exchange receipt records. Where zero-rating is claimed without a matching export clearance or without foreign exchange repatriation within the statutory monitoring period, the STA claws back the refunded VAT, imposes a statutory penalty of one to five times the refunded amount together with late-payment surcharges, and, if fraudulent intent is found, refers the case for criminal prosecution for fraudulent export tax refunds under Article 204 of the PRC Criminal Law, with the retroactive recovery period unlimited in cases of fraud and customs penalties additionally applying to false export declarations.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a managed current-account convertibility regime under the Foreign Exchange Administration Regulations while retaining a registration-based, positive-list capital account framework. The WFOE's HK parent may inject equity only after SAFE FDI registration and a bank has opened a dedicated FDI foreign-exchange capital account; each remittance must be individually matched against the registration, the shareholders' resolution and the required BOP declaration. In contrast, genuine service-trade income derived from factory inspection, testing and quality-control work is current-account revenue and may be collected in RMB or foreign currency, with proceeds retained in an approved current-account FX account or settled at a licensed bank. Export-related proceeds are subject to repatriation rather than mandatory surrender; since the 2012 goods-trade reforms there is no fixed conversion percentage. Profit repatriation to the Hong Kong parent faces documentary rather than convertibility hurdles: audited financial statements, declared dividend, corporate income tax and withholding-tax clearance, and bank authenticity review. Every cross-border flow, including RMB cross-border payments, produces a SAFE/International Balance of Payments record.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE's Goods Trade Foreign Exchange Administration Guidelines, export-related receipts must be repatriated to China and are typically due within 90 days of the customs export declaration; longer settlement terms must be reported in the goods-trade monitoring system as deferred collection with the underlying contract. There is no mandatory conversion ratio: enterprises may retain 100% of export foreign currency receipts in a permitted current-account FX account or freely settle them with a bank. The WFOE should maintain customs declarations, shipping documents, invoices and collection ledgers to pass SAFE's total-volume authenticity review.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Low - not viable as a primary banking or clearing rail for the onshore WFOE. Wise and comparable offshore EMIs are not licensed by the PBOC/CBIRC as deposit-taking institutions and are legally barred from opening domestic settlement or foreign-exchange capital accounts. They cannot perform SAFE-driven trade reporting, FDI capital registration or corporate account KYC at the required PRC bank standard. The WFOE may not lawfully centralize incoming inspection/testing fees on a Wise wallet because such receipts would be deemed offshore settlement outside the domestic banking system and could create unverified export receipts. Wise may only be used by the Hong Kong parent as a low-value disbursement tool for third-country expenses, not by the WFOE for inbound regulatory capital or outbound trade payments.

Turnover: Every Hong Kong incorporated company must be independently audited by a certified public accountant under the Professional Accountants Ordinance unless it is dormant and has validly filed an exemption, or qualifies as a small private company that validly passes a shareholder resolution to waive audit. Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% (general Enterprise Income Tax Law rate for resident enterprises)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% imposed on gross dividends paid by a Chinese resident enterprise to a non-resident enterprise (Enterprise Income Tax Law Articles 3 and 37; SAT Circular 2009 No. 81). Dividends are not subject to VAT or business tax.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Annual enterprise income tax settlement must be completed and filed within 5 months of the fiscal year-end (May 31 for a calendar-year taxpayer). Quarterly prepayment filings are due by April 15, July 15, October 15, and January 15 following each quarter, respectively.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Under SAT Public Notice No. 42 (2016), a Local File is mandatory when annual related-party transactions exceed RMB 200 million in tangible property, RMB 100 million in intangible property or financial assets, or RMB 40 million in other related-party transactions (including management and technical services). For this inspection/QC enterprise, intercompany service outsourcing and head-office recharges will trigger the RMB 40 million threshold. A Master File is required where the consolidated group revenue exceeds RMB 5 billion and the China enterprise meets any of the category thresholds; Country-by-Country reporting arises at RMB 5.5 billion group revenue.

Economic substance scope: China applies a substance-over-form doctrine (Enterprise Income Tax Law Article 47) and an OECD-aligned Principal Purpose Test through GAAR practice. The WFOE must have real premises, qualified inspectors, operating assets, and actual performance of inspection/QC activities in mainland China as its CIGA. The Hong Kong shareholder must independently satisfy Hong Kong's substantive economic substance requirements under the FSIE regime (Inland Revenue (Amendment) (No. 6) Ordinance 2021) to avoid recharacterisation as a conduit; otherwise the treaty rates (5% dividend, 7% interest, 5% royalty) and foreign-sourced-income exemption will be denied.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Invoicing

Statutory sanction: Criminal Law of the PRC Articles 201 and 205: up to 7 years' imprisonment for tax evasion; up to life imprisonment for false special VAT invoicing; financial penalties of 50%-500% of unpaid tax (LATC Article 63); daily late-payment interest at 0.05%.

Director personal liability: Piercing the corporate veil: directors, actual controllers, and financial officers are jointly and severally liable for the unpaid tax and penalties. Tax authorities may impose exit restrictions, blacklist the individuals, and freeze personal bank accounts. Criminal courts can impose 3-7 years' imprisonment for evasion and up to life imprisonment for principals in false-invoicing rings, plus property confiscation and civil restitution orders.
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Enterprise Income Tax Law Articles 41 and 44; SAT Public Notice No. 6 (2017). The tax authority may adjust taxable income, add interest at the floating benchmark rate plus 5 percentage points, impose a penalty of 50%-500% of underpaid tax (LATC Article 63), and make a secondary adjustment reclassifying excess cash as a deemed distribution subject to 10% WHT. Failure to file documentation also carries a fine of up to RMB 50,000.

Director personal liability: Responsible directors are exposed to personal liability for withholding failures and for signing false TP documentation. Alongside corporate adjustments, directors may be placed on the dishonest-taxpayer blacklist with travel restrictions; personal assets may be seized for restitution. If the conduct is wilful, criminal liability for tax evasion under Criminal Law Article 201 can lead to 3-7 years' imprisonment.
Treaty Abuse / Shell Company / AML

Statutory sanction: General anti-avoidance rule (EIT Law Article 47), SAT Public Notice No. 9 (2015), and the China-HK DTA mutual agreement procedure. The tax authority will disregard the treaty claim, impose 10% statutory WHT plus 50%-500% penalty for under-declaration (LATC Articles 63-64), charge 0.05% daily late-payment interest, and hold the payor as withholding agent jointly liable. If intentional, criminal prosecution follows.

Director personal liability: Directors who design the shell structure have the corporate veil pierced under PRC Company Law Article 20: they must personally compensate the tax authority for the evaded amount and penalties. Courts may order asset restitution, freeze personal real estate and bank accounts, impose an exit ban on mainland-HK travel, and for money-laundering aspects (Criminal Law Article 191) impose 5-10 years' imprisonment; for the underlying tax evasion, 3-7 years' imprisonment and fines.
