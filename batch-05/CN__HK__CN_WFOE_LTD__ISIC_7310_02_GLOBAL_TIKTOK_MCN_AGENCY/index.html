Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

Mainland China rejects a virtual office, P.O. box, or commercial mailbox as the registered office of a WFOE. The PRC Company Law and SAMR registration practice require a physical, non-residential address with lease contract, property ownership certificate, and actual signs of business operations. Local tax authorities and banks, particularly for a TikTok/MCN agency processing creator payouts and Wise B2B rails, view virtual offices as strong indicators of shell risk and will reject tax registration, invoice quota applications, and bank account openings. Hong Kong permits a virtual registered address for the holding vehicle, but the Mainland WFOE must have a substantive operational office.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None. Under the Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622), Hong Kong SAR imposes no statutory minimum paid-up capital for a private company limited by shares, and no blocked escrow account or capital verification certificate is required. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA), administered locally through the provincial-level tax bureau of the registered address, and accessed through the National Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax Bureau) online portal.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Register the WFOE with the local branch of the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) and obtain the Business License carrying the 18-character Unified Social Credit Code, which simultaneously serves as the enterprise's Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN), VAT registration number, and social credit identifier. Step 2: Within 30 days of issuance of the Business License, complete tax registration information confirmation through the e-Tax Bureau with the competent local tax authority, declaring legal representatives, registered address, accounting policies, bank accounts, invoicing scope, and anticipated VAT status. Step 3: If the Hong Kong or other foreign non-resident parent does not itself carry on a trade or business in China and does not have a permanent establishment, it is not separately VAT-registered; any China-source payments made to that non-resident are subject to withholding by the WFOE as paying agent, and the non-resident enterprise is registered for tax identification purposes using the withholding agent's e-Tax Bureau filing. Step 4: The WFOE must register for VAT status; if annual taxable sales exceed CNY 5,000,000, Compulsory General VAT taxpayer registration applies. Step 5: Activate the electronic invoice platform through the e-Tax Bureau and apply for the appropriate VAT invoice quotas and categories. Step 6: For cross-border service receipts, complete foreign exchange receipt and repatriation registration with the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) through the designated domestic bank.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None. The People's Republic of China does not provide check-the-box entity classification elections, US-style pass-through elections, or elective hybrid mismatch treatments. A WFOE is mandatorily taxed as a Chinese resident enterprise at entity level under the Enterprise Income Tax Law. No consolidated or group relief election is available with the Hong Kong parent.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No turnover threshold applies to the compulsory tax registration obligation of a newly established WFOE; tax registration is required within 30 days of incorporation. For VAT status, annual taxable sales exceeding CNY 5,000,000 trigger compulsory General VAT taxpayer registration; below that threshold the small-scale taxpayer status applies with a simplified levy rate generally 3%, with qualifying temporary reductions available by policy.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods: zero-rating applies to goods physically exported from mainland China under a valid China Customs export declaration, supplied to an overseas buyer, and paid for in foreign exchange, provided the exporter is a General VAT taxpayer and the export refund is substantiated with the complete customs and fx document bundle. Services: zero-rating applies only to services that (i) are supplied to an overseas entity or individual, (ii) are wholly consumed outside mainland China, and (iii) fall within the statutory zero-rating catalogue issued by the Ministry of Finance and STA, including eligible offshore outsourcing, R&D, software, design, information system, broadcast and film program production/distribution, and technology transfer services. The Tiktok MCN agency's creator representation, advertising, marketing, commission and licensing services are not automatically zero-rated; if the service fails the wholly-consumed-outside-China test or is outside the statutory zero-rated categories, it may instead be VAT-exempt with no input credit or taxable at 6%.

  • Customs Export Declaration Form (Export Bill of Entry) issued by China Customs evidencing that the goods left Chinese customs territory
  • Commercial Invoice, Packing List, and the underlying export sales contract
  • Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, or multimodal transport document evidencing the physical export route from China to the overseas destination
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or bank foreign-exchange settlement receipt proving that the overseas buyer remitted payment from outside China
  • Export VAT refund declaration and related export refund statement filed through the e-Tax Bureau
  • Service agreement, service completion report, and overseas consumption evidence where the zero-rating claim is for qualifying cross-border services in lieu of physical customs documents

Mandatory. VAT invoices must be issued through the STA's official Full Digitalized Electronic Invoice platform or through the e-Tax Bureau's e-invoice module. Each invoice must carry a unique nationwide invoice number, cryptographic digital signature, and anti-fraud QR stamp, and invoice issuance data is cleared in real time to the STA's Golden Tax Phase IV central tax-control system. Certified API integration is available for qualified taxpayers and certified fiscal service providers; red-letter/credit invoices require pre-clearance against the original invoice through the platform; manual offline invoice issuance is generally not permitted for General VAT taxpayers.

China Customs and STA tax authorities may scrutinize export zero-rating and refund claims retrospectively. If any required document is missing, incomplete, or not received and repatriated within 180 days, the zero-rating is reversed, the input VAT refund is clawed back, and the output VAT becomes payable with daily late-payment surcharge at 0.05% from the original due date. Under the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection, falsifying export declarations to evade VAT or obtain fraudulent refunds attracts penalties of 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax. Under Article 204 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, export tax refund fraud is a criminal offense punishable by fines, confiscation, and imprisonment up to life imprisonment. SAFE may separately impose fines and compulsory repatriation orders for failure to repatriate foreign-exchange proceeds within the statutory deadline.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The PRC regime gives current-account items broad convertibility for genuinely documented trade, services, and creator or royalty receipts, while the capital account remains controlled. The WFOE may receive foreign exchange or purchase foreign exchange through a licensed domestic bank for invoices, service agreements, tax invoices, and platform settlement reports, all subject to SAFE value-for-money and total-volume monitoring. Capital inflows from the Hong Kong parent require FDI foreign-exchange registration, a dedicated capital account, and a CPA capital verification before conversion to RMB. There is no fixed mandatory surrender ratio for export receipts; proceeds must be repatriated and may be retained or settled at spot. Profit remittance to the Hong Kong parent faces documentary hurdles: audited distributable profits, corporate income tax settlement, board dividend resolution, and bank and SAFE cross-border remittance review must all align. The Hong Kong leg is freely convertible in CNH, HKD, and USD, but the onshore WFOE cannot use offshore CNH as a substitute for onshore RMB settlement. Negative-list business restrictions and capital-account registration remain binding.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export foreign-currency receipts from the WFOE's TikTok Shop and trade flows must be repatriated to a domestic foreign-exchange account within 90 days of the customs export declaration or the invoice date for services; deferred collection beyond 90 days requires SAFE registration or a bank-supported arrangement. There is no mandatory statutory conversion ratio; the WFOE may keep 100 percent of eligible export proceeds in a foreign-currency account or settle them into RMB at the market spot rate. For service and creator receipts, collection must be supported by a valid service contract and platform settlement report before the bank allows retention or settlement.

Central bank supervision: The People's Bank of China (PBOC); the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) as the designated foreign-exchange administration for mainland China; the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) as de facto central bank for the Hong Kong parent and offshore CNH clearing.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low as a primary treasury rail for the PRC WFOE; Medium for the Hong Kong parent only. Offshore digital EMI providers such as Wise B2B and Airwallex are not licensed by PBOC or SAFE as bank or payment-institution account providers for the mainland entity. The WFOE must maintain its principal RMB, export-proceeds, capital-account, and dividend-remittance flows through a licensed domestic bank. TikTok Creator Payouts can be received into a domestic bank account and reconciled under SAFE rules. The Hong Kong parent may use a licensed Hong Kong EMI for incidental low-value creator disbursements to non-PRC beneficiaries, but Wise B2B is not a substitute for the Chinese WFOE statutory banking relationship.

Mandatory Audit Threshold Small Company Audit Exemption Regime

Turnover: All Hong Kong incorporated companies are required to have an independent Certified Public Accountant audit their financial statements unless they qualify for audit exemption as a small private company or a dormant company. A small private company is exempt only if it passes a special resolution and satisfies at least two of the following three criteria: annual revenue HK$10 million or less, total assets HK$10 million or less, and average number of employees during the year of 100 or fewer. A dormant company must have no significant accounting transactions and is not a banking or insurance company. The thresholds are quantitative and statutory under the Hong Kong Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% on outbound dividends paid by the China WFOE to its Hong Kong shareholder, reduced to 5% under the China-Hong Kong Double Tax Arrangement where the beneficial owner directly holds at least 25% of the capital of the dividend-paying company.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File contemporaneous documentation is statutorily required when annual related-party tangible-asset transfers exceed CNY 200 million, annual related-party intangible-asset or financial-asset transfers exceed CNY 100 million, or annual related-party service transactions exceed CNY 40 million; the China WFOE must also submit the related-party transaction annual disclosure form when these thresholds are met.

Economic substance scope: China applies substance-over-form, beneficial-ownership, and General Anti-Avoidance Rule (GAAR) principles; no Chinese Economic Substance Regulations equivalent to offshore free-zone ESR regimes apply, but the Hong Kong parent must perform Core Income-Generating Activities in Hong Kong to obtain treaty benefits. A paper HK entity without personnel, premises, assets, or active decision-making will lose the 5% dividend WHT rate and may be disregarded.
