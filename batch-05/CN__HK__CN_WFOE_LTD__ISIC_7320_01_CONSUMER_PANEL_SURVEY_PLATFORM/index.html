Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum registered capital applies to an ordinary WFOE engaged in market research and consumer polling under ISIC 7320. No blocked escrow account rule applies. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation; the WFOE is treated as a resident enterprise subject to corporate income tax at the statutory rate of 25% on worldwide income, and as an independent VAT taxpayer in its own right.

Available election forms

None; China provides no check-the-box election for limited liability WFOEs. The only administrative election is between small-scale VAT taxpayer status and general VAT taxpayer status, subject to turnover thresholds and STA approval.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

CNY 5,000,000 annual aggregate taxable sales for mandatory registration as a general VAT taxpayer; below this threshold, entities operate as small-scale taxpayers with mandatory general registration triggered by exceeding the threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to market research and consumer panel survey services supplied to non-resident clients located outside the customs territory of China, where the service contract is executed with an offshore recipient, the commercial delivery is made offshore, consideration is received in foreign currency and repatriated to a Chinese bank account, and documentary evidence substantiates the cross-border nature of the supply. Domestic services provided to Chinese clients remain taxable at standard rates.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bill of Export) issued by the General Administration of Customs where physical goods or embedded deliverables cross the border
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill for physical exports; for digital survey services, an electronic service delivery log or platform usage report
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) issued by a SAFE-designated bank evidencing foreign currency repatriation from the overseas client
  • Executed cross-border service contract between the WFOE and offshore client specifying scope, deliverables, and consideration
  • Foreign exchange settlement statement or SAFE trade collection confirmation
  • Zero-rate VAT export invoice generated through the fully digitalized electronic invoice system

China mandates issuance of fully digitalized electronic invoices (shu dian piao) through STA-approved electronic invoicing systems integrated with the e-Tax Bureau. Invoices are generated with unique fiscal codes, encrypted transaction metadata, real-time transmission to the STA upon issuance, and automated anti-fraud reconciliation against VAT returns. Taxpayers must use the official electronic invoice service platform or certified third-party providers operating through STA-certified APIs. For cross-border SaaS and market research exports, the WFOE must issue zero-rate export invoices through the same digital system and report them separately in the VAT return.

The STA and GACC may jointly audit zero-rated claims; complete documentation must be retained for at least 10 years. Fraudulent or unsupported claims result in retroactive repayment of VAT at standard rates, daily late surcharges of 0.05%, administrative penalties of 50% to 500% of underpaid tax, and potential criminal liability under PRC Criminal Law for tax fraud. Failure to repatriate export proceeds within 180 days, unless justified with an approved extension, retroactively revokes zero-rating and triggers VAT liability with penalties from the original supply date.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The PRC operates a managed floating exchange rate regime with RMB convertibility on genuine current-account items, such as service fees, software subscriptions, market research payouts, and royalties, generally permitted through licensed banks after submission of underlying contracts, invoices, tax proof, and SAFE current-account verification. Capital-account convertibility remains heavily regulated. A PRC WFOE receiving inbound equity capital from its Hong Kong parent must complete bank-delegated SAFE FDI registration, open a dedicated foreign-exchange capital account, and receive funds under a strict foreign exchange purpose code. After conversion into RMB, the funds are still subject to the four negative lists: they cannot be used for prohibited securities investment, entrusted loans, repayment of unrelated domestic RMB loans, or transactions outside the WFOE's actual operating scope. Profit repatriation to the Hong Kong shareholder is current-account treatment but requires a statutory audit, tax-loss offset, a formal dividend resolution, PRC corporate income tax clearance, and dividend withholding tax; a properly structured Hong Kong parent can claim the China-Hong Kong DTA reduced rate of 5% if it meets the beneficial ownership and 25% or greater substantive holding requirement. Export proceeds and service revenue received in foreign currency are generally repatriable through designated bank accounts, with no mandatory surrender ratio, but the bank and SAFE will monitor collection timing, trade authenticity, and eventual tax payment. These controls shape treasury liquidity, counterparty bank selection, hedging limits, and the need for meticulous documentary support for all capital and current-account flows.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export proceeds, where applicable, must be repatriated into a SAFE-registered trade foreign-exchange account held at a licensed domestic bank. The standard statutory monitoring window for ordinary export trade is 90 days from customs declaration, extendable to 180 days where contractual deferred payment terms are documented; overdue amounts must be reported through the SAFE Goods Trade Monitoring System and may trigger bank verification holds. There is no current mandatory conversion ratio; the WFOE may retain 100% of foreign-currency export receipts or convert them to RMB at market rates at its discretion. For service export revenue related to the consumer panel platform, the clearing is governed by current-account service fee rules, which require contracts, invoices, tax withholding certificates, and internal payout ledgers instead of customs declarations.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China, operating under the People's Bank of China (PBoC); the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) is the relevant authority for HKD clearing and the offshore CNH market in Hong Kong SAR.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

PayPal Fit: High

Not viable as a primary corporate treasury rail and legally constrained in mainland China. A PRC WFOE cannot maintain its statutory foreign-exchange capital account, settlement account, or basic deposit account with Wise, PayPal, or other offshore non-bank EMIs because they are not licensed as PRC commercial banks and cannot perform SAFE transaction declaration, negative-list settlement, capital verification, or tax-linked foreign exchange monitoring. PayPal Mass Payout and Wise Business may be used only as ancillary micro-payout channels through a licensed Chinese cross-border payment institution for small-value consumer panel rewards, provided every funding leg begins and ends through the WFOE's licensed domestic bank; they cannot be used for FDI capital injection, dividend repatriation, large trade settlements, or as a banking counterparty acceptable to the compliance committee. On the Hong Kong treasury side, such EMIs may support small expense accounts but should not be relied upon for group liquidity, since the mainland bank and SAFE will require the financial data of every controlling entity.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity capital from the Hong Kong parent must flow only through a licensed bank channel. The WFOE should first complete foreign investment information reporting under the PRC Foreign Investment Law, confirm the consumer survey business is not on the negative list, and then complete bank-delegated SAFE FDI registration. At the chosen domestic Tier-1 bank, the WFOE opens a dedicated FDI foreign-exchange capital account. The remittance is initiated as a SWIFT MT103 from the HK parent with the remitter name exactly matching the registered shareholder and the beneficiary exactly matching the WFOE corporate name. The payment narrative must include the SAFE registration reference and the appropriate BOP/FDI purpose code, typically mapped by the bank to 622011 for foreign direct investment capital contributions. After receipt, the bank performs cross-border income declaration, anti-money-laundering screening, and authenticity checks. A PRC registered CPA firm then issues a capital verification report for the registered capital. RMB funds can be drawn only into the settlement-payment account and used against invoices, contracts, payroll, tax, rent, and other operating expenses; no fund is permitted for prohibited third-party lending, entrusted loans, securities investment, or domestic RMB loan repayment.Outbound clearing is handled through the WFOE's licensed bank using SWIFT MT103 for direct service and trade payments. For the survey platform core model, consumer panel rewards and market research subcontractor fees are processed as current-account service payments with each transaction backed by a service contract, invoice, payee bank proof, tax withholding certificate or cross-border tax filing, and an aggregate reward disbursement report where bulk payouts are used. For goods-related imports such as survey devices or promotional products, the bank can arrange Commercial Letters of Credit under UCP600 and Documentary Collections under URC522 with transport documents, commercial invoices, packing lists, and inspection certificates. PayPal and Wise are not standalone clearing rails for the WFOE; they may be integrated only through a licensed Chinese cross-border payment institution that sweeps proceeds into the domestic licensed bank and provides the required transaction declaration. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Not viable as a primary corporate treasury rail and legally constrained in mainland China. A PRC WFOE cannot maintain its statutory foreign-exchange capital account, settlement account, or basic deposit account with Wise, PayPal, or other offshore non-bank EMIs because they are not licensed as PRC commercial banks and cannot perform SAFE transaction declaration, negative-list settlement, capital verification, or tax-linked foreign exchange monitoring. PayPal Mass Payout and Wise Business may be used only as ancillary micro-payout channels through a licensed Chinese cross-border payment institution for small-value consumer panel rewards, provided every funding leg begins and ends through the WFOE's licensed domestic bank; they cannot be used for FDI capital injection, dividend repatriation, large trade settlements, or as a banking counterparty acceptable to the compliance committee. On the Hong Kong treasury side, such EMIs may support small expense accounts but should not be relied upon for group liquidity, since the mainland bank and SAFE will require the financial data of every controlling entity.
