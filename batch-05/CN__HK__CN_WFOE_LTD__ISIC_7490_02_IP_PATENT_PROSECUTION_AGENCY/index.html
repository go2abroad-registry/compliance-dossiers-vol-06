Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum registered capital applies to a general wholly foreign-owned enterprise under the PRC Company Law; the Articles of Association may set registered capital commensurate with the IP prosecution business scope and operating scale. For patent agency licensing, the entity must satisfy the professional qualification requirements under the Patent Agency Regulations. Any capital stated in the Articles of Association must be fully subscribed and paid in cash within the time limits specified therein; no blocked escrow account is required by SAMR, but capital contribution remittances must be documented through bank receipts and foreign exchange filings.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety Compliance: the leased office must obtain a fire safety inspection certificate or fire safety acceptance record for commercial office use from the local Fire Rescue Brigade. The landlord must provide structural load certificates and property safety approvals for the building. Under the Work Safety Law of the People's Republic of China, the Company must designate a workplace safety officer, maintain basic fire extinguishers, evacuation signage, and emergency exits, and complete an occupational safety self-assessment. For an office-only IP agency, no hazardous chemical approval, pollutant discharge permit, or special civil defense license is required.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China; online registration and compliance filings are processed through the Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax) portal at https://etax.chinatax.gov.cn, with the WFOE administered by the provincial and county-level tax service bureaus.

E-government filing portal

The foreign parent must first complete the incorporation of the WFOE with the State Administration for Market Regulation; the business license issued by the competent market regulation authority contains the Unified Social Credit Code, which is the legal Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) for the WFOE. Within 30 days of issuance of the business license, the legal representative or authorized tax agent must complete tax registration through the Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax) portal at https://etax.chinatax.gov.cn, using the WFOE's digital certificate and providing the business license, articles of association, proof of registered address (ownership certificate or lease), legal representative identity document, identity document of the authorized tax officer, bank account opening certificate, and forecast of taxable activities. The competent tax bureau will confirm the taxpayer status, assign the enterprise to the appropriate tax bureau and filing cycle, and determine the VAT taxpayer type. If the WFOE has not yet exceeded the general taxpayer threshold but elects general taxpayer status, the registration is completed through the same e-Tax portal. For a foreign non-resident enterprise that is not incorporated in mainland China but derives China-sourced income from the WFOE or another payer, tax registration must be completed by the payer or withholding agent in the non-resident enterprise registration module of the e-Tax portal before payment, and the non-resident enterprise will be assigned a non-resident tax identification code; treaty benefits require a resident certificate from Hong Kong's Inland Revenue Department where applicable.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

6%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5 million annual taxable sales, above which mandatory registration as a general VAT taxpayer applies; all WFOEs must complete VAT registration at establishment, with small-scale taxpayer monthly VAT exemption thresholds applicable where statutory conditions are met.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For export of goods, zero rating is available when the goods are actually physically exported from mainland China, the exporter holds a valid customs export declaration, the sale is made to an offshore customer, and the consideration is paid in foreign currency or purchased foreign exchange; the exporter must be registered and satisfy the export tax rebate administration requirements. For the patent and trademark agency services, zero rating or VAT exemption is available only to the extent the services are supplied to a non-resident person, are wholly used outside mainland China, and fall within the qualifying cross-border services category; the WFOE must retain the service agreement, invoice, evidence of non-resident status, proof that the benefit and use of the service occur outside mainland China, and payment received from outside mainland China. If the criteria are not satisfied, the domestic VAT rate of 6% applies.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bill of Entry or China export customs declaration form)
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill / Multimodal Transport Document
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or bank-issued foreign exchange receipt record evidencing repatriation of export proceeds

China has introduced fully digitalized electronic invoices, and taxpayers must issue qualified VAT invoices through the State Taxation Administration's e-invoice service platform embedded in the Electronic Tax Bureau. Invoice data are transmitted to the tax authority in real time; each invoice receives a tax-authority-controlled invoice code and encrypted digital seal. There is no private-sector fiscal clearance stamp, but issuance quotas, red-letter reversal, and abnormal invoice blocking are managed centrally, and VAT special invoices used for refunds must be validated in the national invoice verification platform.

The tax authority, together with customs and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange, may conduct retrospective audits of exports and cross-border services. If zero-rated treatment is disallowed because the required documentation, physical export, or foreign-exchange repatriation is missing or fraudulent, the VAT exemption or refund is clawed back with late-payment surcharge; administrative fines of 0.5 to 5 times the tax involved may apply; export rebate status may be suspended; and criminal liability may arise under the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China for fraudulent export tax rebates or false issuance of VAT special invoices, including penalties and imprisonment up to life in severe cases.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates an asymmetric convertibility regime. Under IMF Article VIII obligations, current-account payments including trade settlement, service fees, IP royalties, and ordinary profit remittances of a foreign-invested enterprise are generally convertible through licensed banks. The capital account remains tightly managed: an inbound equity contribution into a WFOE requires SAFE foreign-investment registration, and the foreign-currency capital account may be settled into RMB only on declared genuine operating use. For outward remittance of dividends or patent-agency fees, the bank must verify audited annual financial statements, the corporate income tax settlement voucher, any withholding tax certificate, a lawful board resolution, and the underlying IP/client contracts. SAFE and PBOC require banks to perform attributional review and transaction-purpose scrutiny on every outward remittance. A WFOE cannot batch-sweep intangibles proceeds offshore without demonstrating the real business purpose, completing tax clearance, and reporting the transaction to SAFE. Foreign debt, cross-border guarantees, and other capital-account flows are registered within the PBOC/SAFE macroprudential framework. Failure to obtain the correct registration will block the remittance at the clearing bank, regardless of the digital rail used.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For a goods-exporting WFOE, SAFE and PBOC require export proceeds in foreign currency to be collected and repatriated through a licensed bank within the applicable trade-in-goods verification cycle, generally 90 days from the date of export customs declaration; a deferred-payment term beyond 90 days must be registered in the SAFE/bank monitoring system, and a term beyond 180 days requires exceptional justification. No fixed percentage of export proceeds must be compulsorily converted into RMB; the enterprise may retain the full amount in a foreign-exchange settlement account or convert the full amount at market rates. SAFE applies bank-monitored aggregate retention ratios and transaction-nature conformity checks, so an unexplained accumulation of FX or a conversion pattern inconsistent with the business scope will trigger enhanced review and documentary requests.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) and State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Low. Under PBOC licensing rules, non-bank payment institutions are not authorized to provide unrestricted corporate current-account deposit or FX clearing services within China. Foreign EMIs, including Wise, cannot open SAFE-supervised RMB or foreign-currency operating accounts for a mainland WFOE and may only be used as a payment-origination front end where the actual clearing and FX settlement are executed by a licensed Chinese bank or a qualified cross-border banking partner. Using Wise as the sole treasury rail would fail SAFE's attributional bank review, trade-in-goods reporting, and outward-remittance verification requirements. The compliant design must designate a Tier-1 domestic commercial bank as the primary clearing institution; Wise can be reserved for sub-threshold disbursements originated from Hong Kong where the underlying beneficiary international patent office expressly accepts non-bank payment, with all China-side accounts, FX records, and tax declarations remaining inside the licensed banking system.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The Hong Kong parent must send equity capital by SWIFT MT103 to the WFOE's SAFE-registered foreign-currency capital account. The remitter field must show the exact certificated shareholder name; the remittance information field must include the WFOE name, SAFE FDI registration number, and the words FDI Capital Injection. The Chinese receiving bank will match the incoming credit against the SAFE foreign-investment registration and assign the standard cross-border receipt code for foreign direct investment capital contribution. If RMB settlement is required, the WFOE must complete a settlement payment order supported by contracts, invoices, or board resolutions, and the converted funds go into a RMB capital settlement account restricted to self-use. A Chinese certified public accountant must issue a capital contribution verification report for each tranche of paid-in registered capital; the report is used by the bank and market regulator to confirm compliance.Since patent and trademark prosecution are intangible services, the primary clearing rails are SWIFT MT103 for foreign-currency payments and CIPS for cross-border RMB payments. Commercial letters of credit and documentary collections are not suitable for pure IP service disbursements and should be reserved for physical supporting goods if any. Before outward remittance, the WFOE must submit the service contract, the taxpayer's withholding tax certificate, the itemized invoice, and the bank's required cross-border purpose code for royalty or professional consultancy payment. The beneficiary account must match the official patent office or law firm banking details, and the payment reference must include the patent or trademark application number and office name. The compliant design must designate a Tier-1 domestic commercial bank as the primary clearing institution; Wise can be reserved for sub-threshold disbursements originated from Hong Kong where the underlying beneficiary international patent office expressly accepts non-bank payment, with all China-side accounts, FX records, and tax declarations remaining inside the licensed banking system.

Turnover: In Hong Kong the audit obligation is unconditional for a non-dormant company. There are no balance-sheet, turnover, or headcount safe harbours: once the WFOE is active and has any trading transaction, chargeable asset, or business receipt, it must appoint a practising certified public accountant and deliver audited financial statements with its Profits Tax Return. The only theoretical exemption is the dormant company route under Companies Ordinance Section 5, which requires no significant accounting transactions in the period plus a members' special resolution; an IP patent prosecution agency earning fees and incurring costs will not qualify. Quantitative illustration: any book value of assets above HKD 1, any turnover of HKD 1, or employment of any staff member makes the statutory audit mandatory.
