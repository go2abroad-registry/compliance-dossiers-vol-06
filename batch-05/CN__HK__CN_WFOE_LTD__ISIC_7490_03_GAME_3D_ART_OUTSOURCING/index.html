Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is available under State Taxation Administration rules for cross-border taxable services supplied to an overseas purchaser, consumed entirely outside mainland China, not connected to PRC immoveable property or goods located inside the PRC, and paid for in foreign currency or offshore RMB settlement. The 3D game-art sculpting and character-rigging outsourcing service must fall within the STA-published Cross-Border Taxable Services Zero-Rating Catalogue as a qualifying design/creative service. If any mainland China nexus exists, only VAT exemption may be claimed rather than zero-rating.

  • Cross-border service agreement or work order specifying the overseas recipient and foreign-currency consideration
  • Electronic delivery evidence, acceptance certificate, or asset handover log for the 3D sculpting and rigging outputs
  • Full Digitalized Electronic Invoice referencing the export service and the zero rate, where required
  • Bank inward foreign-exchange receipt or offshore RMB settlement confirmation evidencing collection of export proceeds
  • PRC Customs Export Declaration (Bill of Export), cargo manifest, and Bill of Lading for any physical media or prototypes shipped abroad

All VAT invoices must be issued as Full Digitalized Electronic Invoices through the national Electronic Tax Bureau under the Golden Tax Phase IV system. Each invoice is transmitted in real time to the central tax platform and must carry the anti-fraud digital stamping and verification identifier generated by the State Taxation Administration. No manual or offline invoice issuance is permitted for WFOE taxable output, and invoicing/clearance interoperability is maintained through API connection to the digital e-invoice account.

The tax authorities will disallow zero-rating if the statutory documentation bundle is incomplete, the counterparty is a PRC-linked party, or the proceeds are not repatriated within the prescribed foreign-exchange window. In such cases the taxpayer must re-file the supply at the 6% VAT service rate and remit the fresh VAT together with late-payment interest and daily surcharges. Fraudulent or unsupported zero-rating claims trigger administrative penalties of 50% to 500% of the evaded VAT under the Tax Administration and Collection Law, revocation of export zero-rating privileges, and criminal liability for deception on export tax refunds under the Criminal Law when the amount is significant.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The PRC operates a current-account convertible but capital-account regulated system. SAFE administers registration, verification and statistical reporting for all cross-border receipts and payments. For the WFOE's 3D art BPO, ordinary service export receipts are current-account items and may be converted into RMB or retained in an onshore FX settlement account at a designated bank, subject to contract authenticity and SAFE reporting. Capital inflows from the Hong Kong parent are capital-account transactions; they must be registered under SAFE's FDI regime and credited to a dedicated foreign-exchange capital account before any conversion. Converted RMB capital can be used only for permitted operating purposes such as payroll, rents, materials and equipment; prohibited uses include equity investment, securities, trust loan and real estate. Profit remittance to Hong Kong is classified as current-account outward remittance and can be made after annual audit, tax clearance and completion of bank real-name verification, together with dividend tax withholding under the China-Hong Kong tax arrangement. The main hurdles are documentation completeness, tax payment proof and the bank's real-use check on converted capital.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For goods exports, export foreign exchange must be repatriated into the PRC banking system and collected within 180 days from customs declaration, with any extension requiring SAFE approval. Service-trade export proceeds for the 3D art outsourcing business must be collected according to the commercial contract due date and reported via the bank's service-trade verification module. There is no mandatory conversion ratio in the current regime: an enterprise may retain 100% of export proceeds in an onshore FX account or settle them to RMB at the spot onshore rate, provided the full amount is repatriated to a designated domestic bank and reconciled against customs or service-trade declarations.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China, acting under the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The Hong Kong parent must first complete SAFE FDI registration through the onshore bank or local SAFE authority to obtain an FDI registration number. The WFOE then opens a dedicated foreign-exchange capital account in its own name at the chosen domestic bank. The HK parent remits the subscribed equity via SWIFT MT103, adding the FDI registration code and a purpose description of direct investment equity injection. The receiving bank validates the code, source of funds and remitter identity; after receipt, the WFOE submits a capital-account foreign-exchange settlement application to convert funds into RMB. The use of converted RMB is restricted to approved operating expenses. A Chinese certified public accountant issues a capital verification report after the injection, and the WFOE files any changes to SAFE within the prescribed reporting period. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Under SAT Public Notice [2016] No. 42, the Local File must be contemporaneously prepared where annual related-party tangible-asset transactions exceed RMB 200 million, intangible-asset or financial-asset transactions exceed RMB 100 million, or other related-party service transactions exceed RMB 40 million; the Master File is mandatory where the group's annual related-party transaction aggregate exceeds RMB 1 billion or the ultimate offshore parent has already prepared a Master File for the group.

Economic substance scope: The WFOE is a mainland China resident company and is not directly subject to offshore Economic Substance Regulations. However, the PRC General Anti-Avoidance Rule under CIT Law Article 47 and the Special Tax Adjustment Rules require the multinational arrangement to reflect real Core Income-Generating Activities: the 3D art directors, modelers, riggers, render managers and client-facing contract negotiators must be located in mainland China. A superficial Hong Kong invoice route with no Hong Kong decision-making or staff will trigger recharacterization, denial of DTA benefits, and reallocation of profit to the place where the substance and people actually operate.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Shell Company / Treaty Benefit Abuse

Statutory sanction: CIT Law Article 47 and SAT Announcement [2018] No. 9: the tax authority may disregard the shell structure, deny the DTA reduction, impose the statutory 10% withholding tax on the dividend, charge 0.05% daily interest, and impose an administrative penalty of 50% to 500% if the conduct amounts to tax evasion; the taxpayer is also named on the Major Tax Default Cases blacklist with multi-government joint punishment.

Director personal liability: Under the PRC Company Law veil-piercing provision, courts can hold directors jointly and severally liable for unpaid tax, withheld dividends and civil damages where the shell is used to evade tax or defraud creditors. Responsible de facto managers face entry-exit control under PRC exit restrictions, public credit blacklisting, and asset seizure for restitution. If false Tax Residency Certificates or beneficial-ownership declarations are used to evade tax, Criminal Law Article 201 imposes criminal liability on directors, including imprisonment for up to seven years and fines.
Tax Evasion through Revenue Diversion and False Invoicing

Statutory sanction: Tax Administration Law Article 63: the tax authority recovers the underpaid tax, assesses 0.05% daily interest, and imposes a penalty of 50% to 500% of the evaded tax; the enterprise, responsible directors and the invoicing party are listed on the tax dishonesty platform and subjected to joint punishment by dozens of government departments.

Director personal liability: Civil liability: diverted revenue is recovered as company assets; directors who personally converted the funds are jointly and severally liable for restitution plus damages. Travel restriction: entry-exit control may be imposed on directors under tax collection and criminal procedure rules. Criminal liability: tax evasion offenses under Criminal Law Article 201 can result in imprisonment up to seven years for the responsible director; invoicing offenses under the criminal invoice provisions can result in even longer custodial sentences in aggravated cases involving forged VAT invoices.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation and Documentation Failure

Statutory sanction: CIT Law Articles 41 and 44, together with the Special Tax Adjustment Rules: the tax authority can make an upward transfer-pricing adjustment, disallow the excessive related-party payment, charge interest, and impose administrative penalties of 50% to 150% under the tax collection framework; the taxpayer may also be publicly listed as a tax non-compliant unit.

Director personal liability: Directors who intentionally engineered the pricing may face personal civil liability under fraudulent-transfer doctrines and the PRC Company Law, including asset tracing into personal bank accounts, real property and game IP registered in their own names. Directors and financial officers may also face personal blacklisting, exit control and disqualification from future management. Where false documentation is submitted during a tax audit or profit is deliberately stripped out of China to evade tax, directors face criminal prosecution for tax evasion with imprisonment up to seven years.
