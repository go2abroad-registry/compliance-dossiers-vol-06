Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No minimum paid-up capital is imposed by national law for a WFOE engaged in clinical trial CRO management under ISIC 7490. The registered capital is a subscribed amount set in the Articles of Association and must be fully paid within five years of establishment under Article 47 of the Company Law, unless the Articles prescribe a shorter schedule. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; electronic filing and registration portal: China Electronic Taxpayer E-Tax platform (etax.chinatax.gov.cn); VAT invoice and clearance infrastructure administered through the Golden Tax System Phase IV; Hong Kong tax matters, if relevant to the non-resident parent, are administered by the Hong Kong Inland Revenue Department (IRD).

E-government filing portal

Upon incorporation of the Chinese WFOE, the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) issues a business license containing the 18-character Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which is the statutory Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) and VAT registration number for the Chinese enterprise. The WFOE must complete tax registration with the competent STA office within 30 days of the business license issuance, file the taxpayer registration information statement, register bank settlement accounts, enable electronic tax filing, and activate the electronic invoice platform. For a Hong Kong non-resident parent entity with no permanent establishment in mainland China, the Chinese withholding agent must complete non-resident income tax withholding registration with the STA and obtain a non-resident tax code/one-time tax registration number for China-sourced dividends, interest, royalties, or service fees; there is no separate standard TIN card issued to such non-residents.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation (the Chinese WFOE is a PRC resident enterprise and standalone taxpayer liable to Enterprise Income Tax on worldwide income; pass-through treatment is not available; the Hong Kong parent remains separately subject to Hong Kong territorial taxation). — Upon incorporation of the Chinese WFOE, the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) issues a business license containing the 18-character Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which is the statutory Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) and VAT registration number for the Chinese enterprise. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation (the Chinese WFOE is a PRC resident enterprise and standalone taxpayer liable to Enterprise Income Tax on worldwide income; pass-through treatment is not available; the Hong Kong parent remains separately subject to Hong Kong territorial taxation).

Available election forms

None in the form of a US check-the-box election. Available statutory elections include VAT general taxpayer versus small-scale taxpayer classification below the RMB 5,000,000 services turnover threshold, simplified VAT collection for small-scale taxpayers, deemed-profit method for limited non-resident enterprise scenarios, and the right to claim benefits under the Mainland and Hong Kong Double Tax Agreement by furnishing a Hong Kong tax residence certificate and Chinese tax resident certification where required.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

6%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No minimum turnover threshold applies for initial VAT registration; every WFOE must register for tax from incorporation. Mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration applies once annual taxable turnover for services exceeds RMB 5,000,000. Below RMB 5,000,000, the entity may elect small-scale taxpayer status, and non-resident service suppliers without a Chinese establishment are generally subject to VAT withholding through the Chinese counterparty unless they independently register.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating at 0% VAT is available for physical goods that are actually exported and cleared through a PRC customs port, and for qualifying cross-border services, including clinical trial CRO management and related R&D services, supplied to a non-resident sponsor where the services are wholly consumed or used outside the customs territory of the PRC. The exporter/service provider must retain documentary evidence of physical export or overseas consumption, foreign exchange receipt, and contract terms. Services that fail the zero-rating criteria may instead qualify for VAT exemption under the cross-border services exemption catalogue, but zero-rating is not automatic and is subject to STA audit.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Export Goods Declaration / Chinese customs clearance record)
  • Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, or other international transport document proving physical exportation
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or bank foreign exchange receipt/collection confirmation evidencing receipt of export consideration in foreign currency
  • Foreign exchange settlement and verification records issued by the receiving bank for repatriated export proceeds
  • Export sales contract or service agreement with the overseas buyer or non-resident sponsor
  • Commercial invoice and packing list; for services, the service invoice and detailed work statement
  • Service Trade Foreign Exchange Payment/Tax Filing Certificate where cross-border service payment filing applies
  • Evidence of service consumption outside China, including offshore trial site locations, overseas sponsor jurisdiction, and foreign regulatory submission records
  • Certificate of Origin when required for customs or preferential tariff purposes

All VAT invoices are controlled under the Golden Tax System Phase IV. The WFOE must issue invoices through the STA electronic invoice service platform using certified tax-control equipment (Tax UKey) or the fully digitalized electronic invoice mode, with real-time transmission of invoice data to the STA clearance/API system before the invoice is recognized for input VAT credit. Each invoice must contain a national-standard anti-counterfeit QR code, a digital tax-control seal, and a unique invoice number; duplicate issuance, voiding, and cross-invoice reconciliation failures are automatically flagged by the platform.

Export zero-rating claims are reviewed by the STA through the Golden Tax System Phase IV risk-control mechanisms. If the required documentation is missing, inconsistent, or false, the STA will deny the zero-rating, assess VAT at the applicable domestic rate, claw back input VAT previously claimed, impose a late payment surcharge of 0.05% per day, and levy administrative fines ranging from 50% to five times the tax amount under the PRC Tax Collection and Administration Law. Failure to repatriate or verify export proceeds within the applicable foreign exchange deadline is separately penalized by the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE). Fraudulent or repeated violations may give rise to criminal tax evasion liability and revocation of VAT general taxpayer status.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Under the mainland China regime, the renminbi is convertible for current-account items (clinical trial service fees, licensing, royalties, salaries and non-capital operating transactions) once the bank verifies genuine commercial documentation; capital-account items, including foreign direct investment equity, shareholder loans, dividends and disposal proceeds, are not freely convertible and require SAFE registration, quota and purpose monitoring. The WFOE may receive foreign currency capital contribution from the Hong Kong parent into a designated FDI capital escrow account, convert it at the onshore market rate upon submission of the capital account payment confirmation, and thereafter use RMB for approved clinical trial operating expenses. There is no mandatory surrender of export proceeds, but all cross-border receipts must be repatriated into a PRC domestic foreign exchange settlement account or converted through a licensed bank. Dividend and profit remittance to Hong Kong is possible only after the annual statutory audit, corporate income tax filing and payment, and production of the WFOE's audited financials, board resolution, and foreign exchange payment confirmation. Additional practical hurdles include strict negative-list review for research clinical activities involving human genetic data, bank-level matching of invoices against completed milestones, and the need for a full Chinese accounting trail before any capital outflow becomes lawful.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory surrender ratio applies since SAFE's 2012 reform. Export receipts from CRO service contracts must be credited to the WFOE's foreign exchange settlement account maintained with a SAFE-designated bank and either retained or converted to RMB at the bank's real-time quoted rate. Banks must complete post-transaction verification against the customs/SAFE service trade declaration and the underlying invoice/contract, normally within 30 days of the nominal remittance due date; unverified receipts cannot be used for speculative or unrelated outbound payments. Partially matched receipts may require a written explanation from the WFOE and supporting clinical trial milestone documentation.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China, operating under the People's Bank of China (PBOC); the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) supervises the offshore CNH banking side for the Hong Kong parent but does not administer onshore WFOE FX controls.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Not viable for the onshore WFOE and legally barred from serving as the primary deposit-taking and FX clearing institution. Offshore digital EMIs, even if licensed in the UK, Lithuania or Hong Kong, do not possess a PRC banking licence, cannot open RMB current accounts, cannot hold SAFE FDI capital account registrations, and cannot deliver the required foreign exchange payment confirmation for capital injection. Their only permissible role is as a pre-funding or treasury wallet for the Hong Kong parent before funds enter the regulated Chinese banking system; all WFOE operational receipts and payments must clear through tier-1 PRC-licensed commercial banks.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The Hong Kong parent bank executes a SWIFT MT103 to the WFOE's PRC foreign investment capital account at a tier-1 Chinese bank. The remittance must identify the Hong Kong parent as the registered shareholder, reference the SAFE FDI registration or Confirmation of Foreign Exchange Registration, and use a capital-account purpose code for direct investment equity contribution. The receiving bank obtains the SAFE capital account payment confirmation, verifies the amount against the approved registered capital, debits the capital escrow account and, upon the WFOE's instruction, converts the foreign currency to RMB using the on-market CNY rate. Mandatory Audit Threshold Mandatory Audit for All Entities

Turnover: Every Hong Kong incorporated private limited company must appoint a Certified Public Accountant (CPA) to audit its financial statements unless it qualifies for audit exemption under Part 9 of the Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622). A company is ineligible for audit exemption and is therefore subject to mandatory statutory audit if it is not a small private company; a small private company must satisfy at least two of the following three conditions: annual turnover not exceeding HKD 10,000,000, total assets not exceeding HKD 10,000,000, and number of employees not exceeding 100. Audit exemption is also unavailable if any member is a non-natural person (e.g., a corporate shareholder) or if members do not unanimously waive the audit by written resolution. Consequently, a contract research organisation managing multinational clinical trials with corporate shareholders is effectively always subject to mandatory independent audit, regardless of size thresholds.

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% on gross dividends distributed by the PRC WFOE to its non-resident enterprise shareholder; reduced to 5% under the Mainland/Hong Kong Double Tax Arrangement if the Hong Kong resident company beneficially owns at least 25% of the WFOE's paid-in capital and satisfies the anti-treaty-shopping and beneficial ownership tests.

Quarterly provisional CIT returns must be filed and tax prepaid within 15 days after the end of each calendar quarter. The annual final return and tax settlement must be filed by 5 months after the fiscal year-end (May 31 for a calendar-year WFOE).

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: The Local File is generally required when related-party transactions in any single category reach: RMB 200 million for tangible asset transfers, RMB 100 million for intangible property transfers or related-party financing, and RMB 40 million for service transactions or other related-party transactions. The Master File is required where the PRC related-party transaction aggregate exceeds RMB 1 billion or where the group-level standards are otherwise met; contemporaneous documentation must be prepared before the annual return deadline.

Economic substance scope: The PRC GAAR and anti-abuse rules require that the WFOE has real operational substance for its clinical trial CRO activities: actual project managers, phlebotomy and laboratory coordination staff, data management, patient recruitment, SOP compliance, and clinical site monitoring in China. The Hong Kong intermediate entity must also demonstrate commercial substance under the Mainland/Hong Kong DTA beneficial ownership test: its core income-generating activities (dividend holding, investment decisions, contract negotiation, funding management) should be performed in Hong Kong with office, personnel, and board control. Hong Kong is not an Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) jurisdiction in the Caribbean sense, but an offshore holding with no CIGA in Hong Kong is vulnerable to denial of treaty benefits, deemed taxable presence in China, and PRC transfer pricing recharacterization.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent R&D Incentive Claim

Statutory sanction: PRC Tax Collection Administration Law Articles 63 and 64 impose a monetary fine of 50% to 5 times the underpaid tax, plus daily late interest. False invoices additionally engage Article 205 of the Criminal Law and the VAT invoice enforcement regulations. The R&D deduction is reversed and the WFOE may be assessed substantial additional penalties.

Director personal liability: The courts may pierce the corporate veil where the director or actual controller deliberately used the WFOE to evade tax, causing personal civil liability for restitution of corporate tax debts. Criminal liability under Criminal Law Article 201 includes imprisonment of up to 7 years for serious tax evasion; the competent tax authority may blacklist the company, instigate a travel ban against the legal representative, and seize assets of responsible directors who assisted in the fraud.
Shell Company and Treaty Benefit Abuse

Statutory sanction: The PRC tax authority disallows treaty relief under the beneficial ownership doctrine and reassesses the WFOE's withholding at the statutory 10% rate for dividends, plus interest and late payment surcharges. The GAAR under PRC CIT Law Article 48 permits recomputation of the taxable income and denial of deductions for the conduit arrangements; penalties may be imposed under Articles 41 and 110 of the same Law and supporting anti-avoidance announcements.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly established the Hong Kong shell to evade Chinese dividend WHT may be jointly and severally liable for the under-withheld tax under corporate veil-piercing principles. The legal representative may be subject to an exit ban and blacklisting for serious tax violations; if the arrangement is deemed criminal tax evasion, imprisonment of up to 3 years can apply to the directly responsible officer, with fines and confiscation of assets.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation and Thin Capitalization

Statutory sanction: Under PRC CIT Law Articles 41 and 46, the tax authority may apply arm's length adjustments for up to 10 years, disallow non-arm's length charges, impose a 5-year look-back period or extended investigation cycle, and levy a penalty of up to RMB 10,000 or higher under the transfer pricing administration rules for failure to provide documentation. Late payment interest accrues on every year of additional tax due.

Director personal liability: The directors who signed the fraudulent service agreements and caused the tax loss may be personally liable for restitution if the corporate veil is pierced; they can be named as responsible persons in tax enforcement proceedings, face asset freezes, be banned from leaving China, and be listed on the serious tax violation blacklist. Where the manipulation is criminalized as tax evasion, imprisonment for up to 7 years and substantial personal fines may be imposed.
