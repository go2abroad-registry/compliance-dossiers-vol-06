Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-up capital and no blocked escrow account required for a consulting WFOE. Under the PRC Company Law 2023, all shareholders must fully contribute their subscribed registered capital within five years from the date of issuance of the business license, unless the Articles of Association provide a shorter period. The Hong Kong corporate shareholder's foreign currency capital contribution must be remitted through a SAFE-designated capital account opened with a domestic bank, and the bank may require source-of-funds and beneficial ownership documentation.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China; electronic portal: National Electronic Tax Administration (e-TAX) at etax.chinatax.gov.cn; for WFOE CN_WFOE_LTD in mainland China, local filing is with the STA subsidiary bureau at the registered address.

E-government filing portal

For the WFOE, the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) issued by the State Administration for Market Regulation upon incorporation constitutes the sole TIN and VAT registration number. The mandatory sequence is: (1) complete SAMR incorporation and obtain the Business License; (2) SAMR electronically transmits the USCC and corporate data to the local STA bureau; (3) within 30 calendar days of the Business License date, the legal representative must finalize tax registration through the e-TAX portal by verifying the identity, registered address, bank settlement account, accounting method and reporting period, and selecting the applicable taxes; (4) the STA activates the USCC as the TIN and assigns VAT status; (5) the entity receives a tax-control UKey and digital invoice signing tool for issuance of VAT invoices. For a foreign non-resident entity without a permanent establishment, no separate Chinese TIN is needed for withholding obligations; if it has a Chinese PE or real estate, it must submit a non-resident tax registration through the e-TAX 'Non-Resident Enterprise Registration' function, providing the foreign incorporation certificate, board resolutions, audited Chinese/English translation, and the PE/real estate ownership evidence; the STA then issues a non-resident TIN valid for self-assessment and payment.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None. The PRC tax system does not have a check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election. A WFOE is automatically a resident enterprise subject to entity-level taxation. Under VAT, a small-scale taxpayer (below RMB 5 million annual sales) may elect to register as a general taxpayer for input VAT credit purposes; this is a status election, not a passthrough or mismatch election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

6%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VAT registration is mandatory for a newly incorporated WFOE immediately upon issuance of the SAMR Business License, with no turnover threshold. For VAT taxpayer status, annual taxable sales exceeding RMB 5,000,000 requires mandatory General VAT taxpayer registration; smaller businesses may remain Small-scale VAT taxpayers (collection rate 3% / 1% pending policy) and may opt in to General status to obtain input credit capability.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A zero VAT rate on cross-border services is claimed under Caishui [2016] No.36 and related STA circulars. The WFOE's anti-dumping trade defense consulting service qualifies when: (i) the client is an overseas entity or individual, including a Hong Kong SAR resident, without a Chinese establishment; (ii) the service is performed outside China and wholly consumed outside China, evidenced by foreign trade remedy procedures or legal proceedings not related to any Chinese taxable activity; (iii) the fee is payable and received in foreign convertible currency (e.g. USD or HKD) through an onshore bank; (iv) the WFOE is recognized as a VAT taxpayer and obtains a tax-control invoice for the zero-rated supply; and (v) the monthly VAT return is accompanied by an export services listing and underlying support.

  • Cross-border service contract/agreement identifying the overseas client, the anti-dumping / trade defense scope, and the place of service performance
  • Zero-rated export sales invoice issued through the National Unified Invoicing Platform, bearing the 0% VAT rate and digital tax seal
  • Foreign exchange receipt voucher from an onshore bank or SAFE-approved settlement system evidencing the inward SWIFT/electronic wire transfer
  • Overseas consumption certificate issued by the foreign legal client or the overseas trade remedy authority that the defense work was deployed outside China
  • Overseas client's business registration certificate and tax residency certificate to prove non-resident status
  • Zero-rated export services detail schedule annexed to the VAT return and electronically filed with the STA
  • Where the WFOE ships physical exhibits or legal document bundles related to the defense case: China customs export declaration (Bill of Entry), shipping bill of lading, and if applicable, consular invoicing evidence

All VAT invoices must be issued through the National Unified Invoicing Platform connected to the STA Golden Tax Phase IV system. Taxpayers use an encrypted UKey to affix a digital tax seal and a machine-readable anti-counterfeiting QR code to every invoice. Invoice line-level data is transmitted to the STA database in near real-time, generating a unified invoice verification record accessible from the National VAT Invoice Verification Platform. No external third-party fiscal discounting is allowed. Enterprises using automated ERP billing must integrate with the official e-invoice interface/API for issuing fully digital all-electronic invoices. The STA runs automated anomaly detection on invoice chains, input credits and payment inconsistencies; any mismatch triggers a tax inspection without prior notice.

The STA retains a five-year record retention and audit window. If the zero rating is reversed, the WFOE is assessed with output VAT at 6% on the gross receivable, plus a daily late-payment surcharge of 0.05% on unpaid tax from the statutory filing deadline, and a penalty of 50% to 500% of the amount under-collected under Article 63 of the Law on Tax Administration and Levy. Where wilful falsification of export or FX documentation is found, the case becomes criminal tax evasion under Article 201 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, for which responsible officers or shareholders are subject to fines and imprisonment up to seven years. The STA will also claw back any input VAT credits wrongfully credited and may place the entity under a 12-month enhanced control period requiring pre-approval of all zero-rated invoices.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Renminbi is fully convertible on the current account for legitimate trade in services and goods, including legal consulting and anti-dumping trade defense advisory fees, provided each transaction is supported by genuine contracts, invoices and SAFE-approved purpose codes routed through a licensed domestic commercial bank. Capital account items are heavily controlled: foreign direct investment into a Chinese Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise requires SAFE foreign exchange registration, bank-level foreign currency capital account opening, and post-injection capital verification. Registered capital received from the Hong Kong parent may be converted to RMB only for the WFOE's approved business scope; proceeds cannot be diverted to domestic equity investment, fixed asset speculation, or lending. Profit remittance to the Hong Kong shareholder is permitted after annual statutory audit, tax settlement, and submission of a board resolution approving dividends. The bank will verify the audited financial statements, tax payment certificates, and the authenticity of the profit distribution; withholding tax on dividends depends on the China-Hong Kong double taxation arrangement and beneficial ownership documentation. Foreign currency service receipts must be repatriated to China and may be retained in a foreign exchange current account or converted to RMB at a licensed bank; there is no mandatory conversion ratio today, but SAFE requires complete trade authenticity verification and monitoring of large and suspicious cross-border flows. The WFOE must maintain separate compliance chains for capital account injection and current account fee settlements to avoid cross-contamination of funds.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under current SAFE practice, exporters of goods and providers of cross-border services must repatriate foreign currency receipts into their domestic bank current accounts within 30 days from the payment date or as otherwise stipulated by trade-verification guidelines, and then either convert at a market rate or retain in a foreign exchange settlement account. There is no mandatory percentage surrender requirement for legitimate export proceeds; the full amount may be retained in a foreign currency account, but the underlying customs or service declaration, contract, invoice, and payment routing must be fully documented and verifiable through the bank's digital verification system.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the People's Bank of China (PBOC) of the People's Republic of China, with Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) as the supervisor for any HK-resident treasury or payment intermediary leg

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Fintech EMI cross-border viability is Low for the mainland WFOE. Mainland China does not permit unlicensed non-bank fintech electronic money institutions or foreign EMIs to open domestic settlement accounts, issue RMB account identifiers, or clear payments for a legally registered WFOE. Offshore fintech EMI platforms such as Airwallex, Currenxie or Revolut Business may facilitate treasury needs for the Hong Kong parent vehicle, but they cannot serve as the WFOE's primary clearing bank in China. All SAFE-administered FX and RMB current account flows must pass through a licensed bank with direct access to CNAPS, CIPS and the SWIFT correspondent network. Any foreign EMI attempting direct account onboarding for a mainland entity would trigger AML licensing violations and reputational red flags.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File must be prepared when annual related-party tangible property transfers exceed RMB 200 million, or when intangible property transfers, financial asset transfers, or service transactions exceed RMB 100 million. Master File must be prepared when aggregate annual related-party transactions exceed RMB 1 billion.

Economic substance scope: China applies substance-over-form principles. For the WFOE, economic substance requires genuine business operations, employees, office premises, contracts, risk management, and decision-making in mainland China. For the Hong Kong shareholder to obtain treaty benefits, the Hong Kong company must demonstrate core income-generating activities in Hong Kong, including strategic management, invoicing, negotiation, and risk assumption. Absence of such substance will result in treaty shopping denial, GAAR recharacterization, and transfer pricing adjustments.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Transfer Pricing Manipulation and Abusive Profit Shifting

Statutory sanction: PRC Corporate Income Tax Law Article 41 and Article 48, State Administration of Taxation Announcement No. 42/2016, and Tax Collection and Administration Law Article 63. The tax authority may make a recharacterization adjustment, add interest at the benchmark loan rate plus 5 percentage points, impose a penalty of 50% to 500% of the evaded tax, and make a twelve-year or ten-year reassessment window.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil may be pierced under PRC Company Law Article 20(3) and Civil Code Article 83. Directors who approved or signed the sham intercompany invoices are personally and jointly liable for the unpaid tax and penalties. Their personal bank accounts and assets may be seized; they may be blacklisted for tax enforcement and travel-banned under PRC tax collection rules; criminal conviction under Criminal Law Article 201 carries up to seven years of imprisonment, fines, confiscation of assets, and civil restitution.
Withholding Tax Evasion on Outbound Profit Repatriation

Statutory sanction: Tax Collection and Administration Law Article 69 imposes a fine of 50% to 300% of the amount not withheld or remitted, in addition to an order to pay the withholding tax. Late payment surcharges accrue at 0.05% per day under Article 32. If the unpaid non-resident tax is recharacterized as profit shifting, the TP adjustment and penalty provisions also apply.

Director personal liability: Directors who authorized the cross-border wire transfer without confirming withholding compliance will face personal liability for the unremitted tax. Tax authorities may freeze corporate and personal bank accounts, restrain assets, apply for exit bans and blacklisting of the legal representative, and pursue criminal liability where the evasion is willful. Imprisonment of up to seven years can be imposed under Criminal Law Article 201.
Shell Company Substance Abuse, Treaty Shopping, and Fraudulent Preferential-Rate Claim

Statutory sanction: PRC Corporate Income Tax Law Article 47 (General Anti-Avoidance Rule) and Tax Collection and Administration Law Article 63. The tax authority will deny DTA benefits, recharacterize the entire ownership and payment chain, reassess income at the full 25% CIT rate, apply 10% withholding on deemed dividends, impose 50% to 500% penalties, and charge late interest. The Hong Kong Inland Revenue Department may also cooperate through exchange of information and deny residency certificates where no tax is paid in Hong Kong.

Director personal liability: Director liability is triggered through veil piercing doctrines and anti-abuse rules. Directors of both the WFOE and the Hong Kong shell may be personally ordered to repatriate assets to satisfy the unpaid tax and penalties. They are subject to civil asset restitution, court-authorized asset freezing, travel bans, tax blacklisting, and professional disqualification. A criminal conviction for tax fraud can lead to imprisonment of up to seven years, heavy fines, and permanent immigration and banking sanctions.
