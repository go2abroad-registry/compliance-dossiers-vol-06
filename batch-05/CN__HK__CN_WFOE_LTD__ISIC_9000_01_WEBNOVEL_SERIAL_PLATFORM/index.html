Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; online administration through the national Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax) platform at etax.chinatax.gov.cn, with local filing made to the in-charge State Taxation Administration branch for the WFOE's registered office in the People's Republic of China.

E-government filing portal

The China WFOE is registered with the State Administration for Market Regulation and automatically obtains an 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code under the 'one license, one code' reform; the same code is recognised by the STA as the mandatory taxpayer identification number for all Enterprise Income Tax, VAT, withholding, and e-invoice purposes. No separate TIN application is required. The WFOE completes tax registration, including VAT general taxpayer registration if applicable, Enterprise Income Tax registration, payroll and Personal Income Tax withholding registration, and stamp duty registration, via the Electronic Tax Bureau within 30 days of issuance of the business license, using the Unified Social Credit Code and the legal representative's identity verification. For a foreign non-resident entity, including a Hong Kong holding company, that does not establish a Chinese branch or permanent establishment but derives China-sourced income, the STA normally captures its identity through withholding registration by the Chinese payer; the WFOE records the foreign entity's statutory registration number and tax residence certificate in its withholding files. If the foreign non-resident entity requires a direct Chinese TIN for treaty withholding relief, capital gains self-assessment, or another statutory purpose, it may apply for a non-resident taxpayer identification number through the in-charge tax authority at the place where the payer or the underlying asset or income source is located, supported by the entity's foreign incorporation certificate, a tax residence certificate issued by the Hong Kong Inland Revenue Department, the cross-border contract, and evidence of the Chinese source income.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation. The WFOE is a PRC resident enterprise taxed at entity level under the Enterprise Income Tax Law; it is subject to PRC Enterprise Income Tax on its worldwide income and is not eligible for pass-through or flow-through treatment.

Available election forms

None. The PRC does not operate US-style check-the-box elections or entity-classification elections. Hybrid mismatch outcomes are addressed through specific anti-hybrid measures in the Special Tax Adjustments framework and the general anti-avoidance rules; no elective mismatch relief is available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 annual taxable turnover from taxable sales of goods, services, or intangible assets. Exceeding this threshold requires compulsory general VAT taxpayer registration. Below the threshold the WFOE may voluntarily register as a general VAT taxpayer; small-scale taxpayers are subject to a temporary VAT exemption for monthly sales up to RMB 100,000, or quarterly RMB 300,000. The WebNovel subscription platform's taxable services generally fall under the reduced 6% VAT category for information or cultural services, while tangible or electronic publication sales may fall under 9% or 13% if not zero-rated export supplies.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A China WFOE may claim 0% VAT on physical goods exported from China after customs clearance with export title passing to an overseas buyer, and on qualifying cross-border services, including WebNovel digital subscription services, supplied to recipients outside mainland China, fully consumed outside mainland China, with payments received from abroad in convertible foreign exchange or through offshore settlement channels such as Apple IAP and Google Play Billing. Hong Kong SAR is treated as outside mainland China for PRC VAT export purposes. The zero rating is substantiated by contemporaneous documentation proving offshore recipient status, actual export or offshore delivery, cross-border payment settlement, and repatriation of export proceeds to China within the statutory foreign exchange window.

  • Customs Export Goods Declaration Form (Customs Export Declaration/Bill of Entry) issued after actual customs clearance from a PRC export port
  • Original or electronic Bill of Lading or air waybill evidencing export shipment and destination outside China
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or bank foreign-exchange receipt and settlement voucher issued by a SAFE-designated bank in China
  • Signed cross-border service agreement, subscription terms, or platform supplier agreement identifying the overseas recipient, including Apple and Google developer settlement contracts where applicable
  • Apple IAP and Google Play Billing settlement reports, app store transaction logs, and country-of-sale reports for offshore digital subscription revenue
  • Commercial invoice or e-invoice, packing list, and any other STA or customs supporting documents demonstrating that the goods or services were exported and paid for from outside China

The WFOE must issue VAT invoices through the STA's nationwide Electronic Invoicing System under the Golden Tax System (Phase IV). Transactions must be recorded and invoiced in real time using fully digitalised e-invoices generated via the Electronic Tax Bureau; each e-invoice carries a statutory invoice code and digital tax-control stamp, is transmitted to the STA for anti-fraud clearance and reconciliation, and is made available to the buyer online. For export transactions, the zero-rated sale is supported by export documents and platform settlement reports; for domestic transactions, e-invoice issuance may be required to complete tax clearance.

The STA may require full export-revenue substantiation for every zero-rated supply. If the required customs declaration, FIRC, or offshore-consumption evidence is unavailable, the zero rating is disallowed, output VAT is reassessed at the applicable domestic rate, and the WFOE must repay any input VAT credits claimed together with a late payment surcharge at 0.05% per day. Under the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection, non-payment caused by evasion, refusal to pay, or deception carries a penalty of 50% to 500% of the tax underpaid, and fraudulent export-refund claims can trigger unlimited-period retrospective clawback, suspension or revocation of export-tax-refund treatment, and criminal referral for export-tax-refund fraud or duty evasion; responsible officers may face criminal liability.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The RMB is only conditionally convertible. For the CNY sovereign jurisdiction, current account convertibility is broadly permitted once truthful trade and service backgrounds are established, while capital account transactions are heavily regulated and require case-by-case SAFE registration, approval, or filing. A WFOE may freely retain or convert verified current account export/income proceeds such as content royalties, platform subscriptions, and app store settlement proceeds, subject to authenticity checks and the matching of goods/services flow with funds flow. However, capital account items - foreign direct investment injection, shareholder loans, reinvestment of capital, equity transfer, dividend remittance, and repatriation of liquidation proceeds - require registration on the SAFE capital project management platform, specialized capital account bank accounts, and a compliance-certified cause-of-payment file. Profit remittance to the Hong Kong parent is legal after annual audit, corporate income tax filing, and a board distribution resolution, but the bank will need the WFOE's tax clearance and audited financial statements. Abolition of mandatory current-account forex surrender means that 100% retention is achievable, but every receipt must be tied to an underlying contract and, where relevant, customs declaration or digital service reconciliation. The effective convertibility risk is therefore operational rather than legal, and delays occur where the corporate registry does not match SAFE registration, where capital is injected before the bank receives SAFE capital project registration, or where a cross-border payment lacks a statutory tax certificate.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no mandatory surrender percentage in force today. China's SAFE allows WFOEs to retain 100% of current-account proceeds in a current-account foreign exchange settlement account or to sell them to a designated bank at the prevailing market rate. However, export-related proceeds must be repatriated to China within the standard export collection period, normally 90 days from the date of the customs declaration, with advance or deferred collection reported to SAFE where the period is exceeded. For digital service-trade income such as webnovel app receipts, the bank performs a monitoring and verification procedure using the platform contract and Apple/Google settlement statements; conversion into RMB is then executed through the standard foreign exchange purchase/settlement form. No statutory percentage surrender is triggered, but the documentation trail must fully evidence the underlying cross-border digital sale in the books of the WFOE.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) together with the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility is low and effectively unavailable as an onshore statutory clearing rail. China's foreign exchange regime requires a domestic WFOE to conduct all cross-border current and capital account payments through banks that are SAFE-designated foreign exchange banks or through bank-authorized clearing participants. Offshore digital EMIs such as Wise, Revolut, Airwallex, and similar platforms generally hold no PRC banking licence, cannot operate domestic CNY accounts, cannot connect as direct participants to CNAPS or CIPS, cannot issue the SAFE capital registration evidence required for capital injection, and cannot provide the tax certificate needed for service-trade outflows. They may be used only as an ancillary offshore treasury collection tool outside China, and even that must be carefully documented because it can create adverse KYC, sanctions, and source-of-funds concerns in the Chinese bank's compliance review.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Opaque Hong Kong parent ownership chain with apparent nominee shareholders or unclear ultimate beneficial ownership structure.

Root cause: Opaque Hong Kong parent ownership chain with apparent nominee shareholders or unclear ultimate beneficial ownership structure.

Defense evidence: Certified certificate of incorporation and current business registration of the Hong Kong entity; complete statutory share register; UBO declaration form; board resolution appointing the WFOE legal representative; Hong Kong registered office or physical office lease evidence; audited or management accounts of the HK parent; passport copies of all ultimate beneficial owners; and an organizational chart linking the HK parent to the final natural persons.
Capital injection pattern showing possible round-tripping, back-to-back funding, or excessive share premium without commercial substance.

Root cause: Capital injection pattern showing possible round-tripping, back-to-back funding, or excessive share premium without commercial substance.

Defense evidence: SAFE Foreign Exchange Business Registration Certificate; bank-issued capital contribution notice; source-of-funds statements from the HK parent; share subscription agreement and shareholders' resolution; CPA capital verification report; board minutes approving the capital call; tax filing status of the WFOE; and, if the capital is used to acquire IP or assets from related parties, a formal valuation report and transfer pricing document.
Digital app revenue from Apple IAP and Google Play Billing creating anonymous counterparty concerns and difficulty verifying the existence of the underlying commercial activity.

Root cause: Digital app revenue from Apple IAP and Google Play Billing creating anonymous counterparty concerns and difficulty verifying the existence of the underlying commercial activity.

Defense evidence: Signed platform developer agreements with Apple and Google; monthly payout settlement statements; backend transaction summaries showing user volumes and gross billings; royalty calculation and remittance schedules; digital content licensing or copyright registration certificates; app store statistics reports; marketing and user-acquisition invoices; and an independent audit report confirming revenue recognition.
Potential sanctions exposure or high-risk jurisdiction traffic in global reader payments and cross-border royalty recipients.

Root cause: Potential sanctions exposure or high-risk jurisdiction traffic in global reader payments and cross-border royalty recipients.

Defense evidence: Internal sanctions screening report covering corporate counterparties, authors, IP licensors, and app store payout accounts; end-user geo-IP traffic summary with privacy redactions; geoblocking policy demonstrating denial of service in prohibited jurisdictions; negative media and PEP search results; declared list of high-risk jurisdictions; and a written AML/CFT policy committing to periodic transaction monitoring and enhanced due diligence.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File is statutorily required when any of the following related-party transaction thresholds is met in the prior fiscal year: (a) tangible asset transfers exceeding RMB 200 million; (b) financial asset transfers exceeding RMB 100 million; (c) intangible asset transfers, including IP royalty payments, exceeding RMB 100 million; (d) comprehensive other related-party transactions, including intercompany services and interest, exceeding RMB 40 million. Master File is required if the enterprise's annual aggregate related-party transactions exceed RMB 1 billion.

Economic substance scope: The WFOE must possess substantive business operations: employees, server/licensing capacity, genuine board oversight, and decision-making in China. For the Hong Kong shareholder claiming treaty or FSIE benefits, Hong Kong's Foreign-Source Income Exemption (FSIE) regime, effective 2023, imposes economic substance requirements for passive offshore income, including IP income, interest, and dividends; if the Hong Kong company lacks Core Income-Generating Activities (CIGA) in Hong Kong, both the China dividend DTA relief and the Hong Kong FSIE may be denied. Chinese GAAR under CIT Law Article 47 permits the tax authority to re-characterize a shell arrangement that has no bona fide commercial purpose and ignore it for tax purposes.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Compliance (Corporate Veil Piercing)

Statutory sanction: TCA Article 63: recovery of the underpaid corporate income tax plus late-payment interest at 0.05% per day and a fine of 50% to 5 times the amount evaded. PRC Criminal Law Article 201 provides criminal liability for tax evasion; where the amount evaded is large, the responsible person is punishable by imprisonment up to 3 years, and up to 7 years for extremely large amounts; the entity is also subject to fines.

Director personal liability: Under PRC Company Law Article 20(3), the court may disregard the corporate entity when a shareholder abuses independent legal personality to evade tax; directors and financial officers who signed the false returns are personally liable for civil restitution and unpaid tax. The tax authority publishes a blacklist of serious tax violators under the Joint Disciplinary Memorandum, imposing travel restrictions on legal representatives, revoking high-consumption privileges, restricting exit visas, and making directors personally responsible for the tax claim.
Transfer Pricing Base Erosion and Aggressive Intellectual Property Migration

Statutory sanction: China's transfer pricing audit powers under STA Announcement 2017 No. 6 allow a special tax adjustment recomputing taxable income at arm's length, adding an interest uplift of five percentage points over the benchmark lending rate; under TCA Article 63, if false documentation is used, a penalty of 50% to 5 times the underpaid tax and a late surcharge are imposed. Additionally, the tax authority may apply the general anti-abuse rule under CIT Law Article 47 to disallow royalty deductions entirely.

Director personal liability: Directors who designed the shell royalty chain and knowingly signed false intercompany invoices can be jointly and severally liable for the restitution of the imputed profit plus penalties; if the arrangement is characterized as fraudulent tax evasion, the responsible manager can receive up to 7 years' imprisonment and be barred from leaving China while the criminal or administrative file is pending.
Shell Company and Beneficial Ownership Treaty Shopping (DTA Abuse)

Statutory sanction: Under the Mainland-Hong Kong DTA and China's anti-treaty-abuse administrative rules, the Hong Kong shell is not entitled to treaty relief, and the full statutory 10% dividend withholding tax is re-invoked with back interest and penalties. PRC anti-treaty-shopping measures and general anti-abuse rules under STA Announcement 2019 No. 35 permit recharacterization of the dividends as taxable income and may deny beneficial-owner status, classifying the entity as a conduit and triggering TCA Article 63 penalty exposure.

Director personal liability: The directors of both the Chinese WFOE and the Hong Kong vehicle who arranged the shell may face civil claims by the tax administration; the court may pierce the corporate veil of the Hong Kong shareholder to reach individual personal assets, place the individuals on the tax violation blacklist, inhibit cross-border travel, and impose criminal liability for fraudulent misrepresentations contained in the Tax Residency Certificate application, including imprisonment up to 7 years for tax fraud.
