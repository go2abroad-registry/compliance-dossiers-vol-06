Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China, operating the National Electronic Tax Bureau ('e-Tax') online taxpayer portal and the China Electronic Invoice Service Platform; local in-charge tax authority is the applicable District or County Tax Service Sub-branch of STA at the WFOE's registered address. For a Hong Kong SAR group entity, the competent authority is the Inland Revenue Department (IRD) of Hong Kong, with the Business Registration Office and the eTAX online portal for Profits Tax and registration matters.

E-government filing portal

For the PRC WFOE: (1) complete incorporation with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) and obtain the business license bearing the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which is the statutory Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) for all PRC tax filings. (2) Within 30 days after business license issuance, complete tax registration and taxpayer information confirmation through the Electronic Tax Bureau, including legal representative identity, financial accounting system and fiscal calendar, bank account filing, and initial tax declaration settings. (3) Apply for VAT General Taxpayer registration if annual taxable sales are expected to exceed RMB 5,000,000 or on voluntary election, and obtain the correct VAT taxpayer classification and invoice issuance limits. (4) Register for the Fully Digitalized Electronic Invoice (FDEI) platform and apply for an electronic invoice quota. (5) For foreign non-resident investors that are not a PRC establishment, perform non-resident tax registration or withholding-agent registration through the e-Tax portal to obtain a non-resident TIN for PRC-sourced income and treaty relief claims under the China-Hong Kong Double Tax Arrangement. For a Hong Kong SAR parent entity, register with the Business Registration Office under the Business Registration Ordinance and obtain a Business Registration Certificate; the IRD eTAX portal is then used for Profits Tax filings.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation - The PRC WFOE is a limited liability resident enterprise subject to enterprise income tax on worldwide income under the PRC Enterprise Income Tax Law; it is not a pass-through vehicle. A Hong Kong SAR parent, if separately formed, is subject to territorial-source Profits Tax on profits arising in or derived from Hong Kong, with offshore income outside the Hong Kong taxing scope.

Available election forms

None under PRC law for WFOE entity classification; no check-the-box election exists to treat a limited liability company as a pass-through. The STA applies special tax adjustment rules to hybrid mismatch arrangements and may recharacterize cross-border payments between China and Hong Kong to eliminate deduction-without-inclusion outcomes. In Hong Kong, no entity classification election exists; offshore profit treatment is an administrative claim based on factual sourcing and can be confirmed through IRD advance ruling, but it is not a statutory check-the-box election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 annual taxable sales is the mandatory threshold for VAT General Taxpayer registration in the PRC; below that, the WFOE may remain a Small-scale VAT Taxpayer with simplified levy treatment, but tax registration is mandatory regardless of turnover. For digital webtoon publishing and modern services, the applicable VAT rate is generally 6% when sold to PRC recipients; physical goods are taxed at the headline 13% standard rate. Hong Kong has no VAT registration threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exported goods qualify for the 0% VAT rate. Cross-border taxable services may qualify for zero-rating under Caishui [2016] No.36 when supplied to non-resident recipients, consumed entirely outside the PRC, and paid in foreign currency. For the digital webtoon syndication model, the WFOE must evidence that the publishing platform is a non-resident recipient, the digital chapters are unlocked or downloaded by non-PRC end users, the economic activity is wholly outside the PRC, and the Apple IAP or Google Play Billing settlements are received from outside China; otherwise, the service may only qualify for VAT exemption, not zero-rating, or may remain taxable at 6%.

The WFOE must issue VAT invoices through the STA-approved tax control system or the Fully Digitalized Electronic Invoice (FDEI) service platform. FDEI invoices are transmitted in real time to the STA Electronic Invoice Data Center; each invoice must carry an anti-fraud QR code and digital signature for authenticity verification. Special VAT invoices issued by General Taxpayers require tax authority authentication before input VAT credit can be claimed. ERP or API integration with the Electronic Tax Bureau is required for electronic invoice delivery, declaration data exchange, and tax control reporting. Hong Kong imposes no electronic fiscal clearance, real-time invoice reporting, or digital tax stamping mandate.

The STA may disallow zero-rating where the documentation bundle is incomplete or inconsistent, retroactively claw back the 0% VAT rate and any refunded input VAT, assess VAT at the applicable 6% or 13% rate, and impose a daily late-payment surcharge of 0.05% of the delinquent tax. Under the PRC Tax Collection Administration Law, fraudulent tax refund claims may attract penalties ranging from 50% to 500% of the tax underpaid. Criminal liability may arise under Criminal Law Article 204 for fraudulent export tax refunds, with public security and Customs involvement. The WFOE may also face taxpayer credit downgrade, restriction or suspension of electronic invoice issuance, enhanced audit scrutiny, and SAFE penalties for late or non-repatriation of export proceeds or submission of false foreign exchange verification records.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The PRC operates a managed floating exchange rate system under which current-account transactions are broadly convertible, consistent with IMF Article VIII status, but capital-account transactions remain subject to multi-layered registration, approval, and verification controls. For a WFOE licensed for digital webtoon publishing and multilingual chapter syndication, the basic operational account will hold RMB only, while the capital settlement room permits conversion of foreign investor contributions under a Foreign Investment Enterprise (FIE) capital account framework. Dividend remittance to the Hong Kong parent is treated as a current-account profit transfer, requiring a fully audited financial statement, the corporate income tax branch-payment certificate, an enterprise resolution authorizing the dividend, and a valid foreign exchange payment application. The practical effect is that funds can enter and exit only via designated banks that have accrued SAFE authorization, and each outward remittance is reviewed individually. Service-trade export proceeds, such as syndication licensing fees and platform settlement reports, are not subject to mandatory physical surrender, but the enterprise must provide authentic transaction evidence and statistical declarations through the Bank of China digital platform. Inward FDI capital is barred from speculative use and must be employed in the registered business scope within a reasonable window, failing which SAFE may impose a penalty or force re-export. Profit repatriation is therefore not prohibited but is procedurally heavy, and tax clearance plus FX form-filling requirements effectively make a Tier-1 Chinese bank mandatory for the treasury cycle.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For goods-trade export receipts, WFOEs must repatriate proceeds through a designated bank within 90 days of customs export declaration. The mandatory conversion ratio is flexible under the current account retention policy; enterprises may retain up to 100% in a foreign exchange settlement account, but any conversion into RMB for taxable domestic operations must be documented. Digital content and licensing export receipts are treated as service-trade flows and are generally exempt from the 90-day physical surrender timeline, requiring instead a service-trade transaction certificate and statistical reporting to SAFE via the bank.

Central bank supervision: The People's Bank of China (PBOC) and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Non-viable. Digital EMIs and fintech electronic money institutions are not licensed as deposit-taking banks in the PRC and cannot open RMB or foreign currency settlement accounts for WFOEs. They also lack SAFE authorization to handle FDI, export payment verification, or dividend remittance. The only potential role is as a downstream payment aggregator for platform settlement data, but the primary banking layer must be a licensed Chinese commercial bank.

Mandatory Audit Threshold Mandatory Audit for All Entities

Turnover: Under the Hong Kong Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622), every company is required to appoint a qualified auditor and have its financial statements audited unless the company is a 'small private company' that validly passes an audit-exemption resolution. A private company is exempt only if it satisfies at least two of the following three thresholds for the financial year: (i) total annual revenue not exceeding HKD 25,000,000; (ii) total assets not exceeding HKD 15,000,000; and (iii) the average number of employees not exceeding 50. If the company fails to satisfy the small-company test, if any member holding at least 5% of voting rights objects to the audit-exemption, or if the company is public, a full independent certified CPA audit is mandatory. The audited financial statements must be prepared under statutory accounting standards and submitted to the Inland Revenue Department together with the Profits Tax Return.
