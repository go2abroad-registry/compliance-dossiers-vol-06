Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; official portal: https://chinatax.gov.cn; e-tax platform: https://tpass.chinatax.gov.cn (电子税务局)

E-government filing portal

A Foreign-Invested Partnership (FIP) must first obtain a Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) via the Administration for Market Regulation (AMR) and then complete tax registration within 30 days of business license issuance; the USCC serves as the TIN. Procedure: (1) file establishment with AMR; (2) obtain business license containing USCC; (3) perform mandatory tax registration at the district-level tax authority, filing accounting system, legal representative/taxpayer identity, and bank account details; (4) activate electronic taxpayer services and e-invoicing; (5) elect or be deemed general VAT taxpayer status if annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5 million, or remain small-scale taxpayer below that threshold.

Procedure: (1) file establishment with AMR; (2) obtain business license containing USCC; (3) perform mandatory tax registration at the district-level tax authority, filing accounting system, legal representative/taxpayer identity, and bank account details; (4) activate electronic taxpayer services and e-invoicing; (5) elect or be deemed general VAT taxpayer status if annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5 million, or remain small-scale taxpayer below that threshold.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

China's Partnership Enterprise Law and tax rules mandate full pass-through transparency for all partnerships (including FIPs); no U.S.-style check-the-box election exists. For cross-border hybrid partnerships, the STA applies anti-hybrid mismatch principles under BEPS Action 2, requiring alignment of asymmetric deductions and inclusion; partnerships may elect an annual accounting period and certain allocation methods, but no optional entity-level taxation is available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VAT registration is mandatory upon business establishment regardless of turnover; the threshold for compulsory general taxpayer registration is annual taxable sales exceeding RMB 5,000,000 (five million Renminbi). Below this threshold, small-scale taxpayer status applies with a simplified collection rate (generally 3%, temporarily 1% under recent preferential policies).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports from mainland China to Macao SAR are treated as exports out of the customs territory and qualify for VAT zero-rating (0% VAT) with input VAT refund under the export tax rebate system. The seller must be a general VAT taxpayer, must complete customs export declaration, and must prove physical shipment abroad through transport documents. Offshore services (including related logistics and software) also qualify for zero-rating if fully consumed outside mainland China and documented with contracts and evidence of offshore supply.

  • Customs Export Declaration (出口报关单)
  • Commercial Invoice (商业发票)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill (提单/空运单)
  • Foreign Exchange Collection/Receipt Voucher (出口收汇核销单/银行收汇水单)
  • Export Contract and Proforma Invoice (出口合同/形式发票)
  • VAT Special Invoice for Purchased Goods (用于退税申报的增值税专用发票)

China operates a nationwide fully digital e-invoice system (全面数字化的电子发票) via the STA's e-tax platform. Issuance requires use of the Golden Tax IV system or the STA electronic invoice service, with APIs for integration into ERP systems. All invoice data is transmitted in real time to the STA for automated validation; there is no separate per-invoice fiscal stamping, but continuous digital synchronization is mandatory and non-compliance blocks invoice issuance and may suspend export rebate entitlements.

The STA and State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) jointly audit export rebates. Any false declaration, overstatement of refund amount, or failure to repatriate foreign exchange within 180 days triggers invalidation of zero-rating, clawback of refunded VAT with daily interest penalty (0.05% per day), and an administrative fine of 50% to 500% of the tax defrauded. Persistent or fraudulent conduct may result in suspension or permanent revocation of export rebate qualification. Criminal liability under Article 204 of the Criminal Law can lead to imprisonment of up to life imprisonment for large-scale fraud.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The FIP operates under partial convertibility. Current account: full convertibility for genuine trade in self-heating instant meal manufacturing (ISIC 1079) once the FIP completes SAFE trade-name-list registration (名录登记) and obtains A-class designation; service fees, royalties, and shipping disbursements are convertible after bank post-verification of the underlying contract and invoice. Capital account: restricted; the Macao partners' capital contribution requires FDI basic FX registration (外汇登记) administered through the onshore bank, followed by funds being held in a restricted capital FX account (外汇资本金账户) and applied only to permitted uses under the negative-list framework. Profit distribution to Macao partners is permissible after enterprise income tax settlement and partner-level tax compliance, supported by audited financial statements, tax clearance certificates, and a certified distribution resolution; capital reduction and dissolution repatriation require SAFE deregistration and are subject to full tax and banking compliance clearance. Self-heating food manufacturing is a permitted (允许类) sector under the Foreign Investment Access Negative List (2021 edition) with no foreign ownership cap, preserving maximum convertibility and exit flexibility.

Export proceeds surrender rules

China abolished mandatory FX surrender (强制结汇) in 2012. The FIP may retain 100% of export proceeds in an onshore current-account FX settlement account (经常项目外汇账户) or voluntarily convert to RMB at market rates under the voluntary settlement (意愿结汇) regime. Statutory repatriation timeline: export proceeds must be received onshore no later than 90 days from the customs export declaration date (报关出口之日起90天内收汇), extendable to 180 days for complete-plant and large-equipment contracts; each receipt must be reconciled to the corresponding customs declaration within the SAFE Trade Foreign Exchange Monitoring System (货物贸易外汇监测系统). Late or non-repatriation triggers B-list administration (9-month facilitation restrictions) or C-list administration (transaction-by-transaction pre-verification), and may also generate suspicious-transaction reporting under PBOC AML rules. No minimum conversion ratio applies to retained export FX.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC, 中国人民银行) as the sovereign central bank, with the National Financial Regulatory Administration (NFRA, 国家金融监督管理总局) as banking licensor and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE, 国家外汇管理局) as the exclusive FX control authority for the onshore Foreign-Invested Partnership (CN_FIP). Macao SAR regulatory nexus: the Monetary Authority of Macao (AMCM, 澳門金融管理局) supervises Macao-investor-side banks (Bank of China Macau Branch, Tai Fung Bank, Banco Nacional Ultramarino, ICBC Macau) whose remittances fund the FIP. All onshore FX conversion, registration, and compliance filings occur under SAFE's capital and current account monitoring framework, with Macao not exercising extraterritorial FX authority over mainland entities.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Local File and Master File are required when the aggregate value of intercompany controlled transactions exceeds MOP 2,000,000 in a single fiscal year and the related-party group turnover exceeds MOP 200,000,000. An FIP manufacturing self-heating pots for a Chinese parent should prepare contemporaneous documentation even below this threshold if its transactions exceed MOP 500,000 to mitigate the risk of ex officio transfer pricing adjustments.

Economic substance scope: Macao applies an economic substance doctrine through the Commercial Registry's supervision of partnerships and DSF anti-abuse rules in deciding complementary tax exemptions. For a manufacturing FIP under ISIC 1079, economic substance is satisfied by owning or leasing a physical production facility in Macao, employing full-time staff responsible for production and quality control, making substantial local operational expenditures, and keeping board and treasury functions within Macao. A pure conduit or shell FIP without premises, staff, or production activities is presumed to lack substance; the MOP 600,000 exemption may be denied and the profits recharacterised as taxable income of the beneficial owner.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Criminal fraud penalty of up to MOP 1,000,000 or three times the tax evaded, whichever is greater; imprisonment of up to 8 years for controlling partners/directors; confiscation of books, assets, and bank funds; DSF may issue additional assessed tax without the MOP 600,000 exemption.

Director personal liability: Where the FIP is insolvent or a mere veil over the commercial activities, the Macao courts may pierce the corporate/partnership veil and impose joint and several liability on the directors and general partners. Travel bans and seizure of passports are routinely ordered during tax fraud proceedings; civil restitution proceeds are attached from the partners' personal estate.
Shell Company / Artificial Arrangement

Statutory sanction: DSF may disallow the exemption, reclassify the partnership as a taxable entity, assess the full 12% complementary tax, and impose a penalty of 10% of the undercharged tax plus interest. The Commercial Registry may order deregistration of the FIP and refuse future incorporation by the same partners. Treaty relief under the China-Macao DTA is denied where the arrangement lacks substance.

Director personal liability: General partners and directors who knowingly directed the artificial arrangement are personally liable for the tax shortfall. Disqualification orders banning them from holding a directorship or partnership management role in Macao for up to 2 years may be issued; court-ordered civil asset tracing and freezing orders extend to shareholders' indirect investments.
Transfer Pricing Documentation Failure

Statutory sanction: DSF may adjust taxable profit to arm's length, applying a surcharge of 5% per unpaid month capped at 60% of the corrected tax, in addition to the assessed tax difference. A separate fixed fine of up to MOP 500,000 applies for gross documentation noncompliance. If the failure is deemed fraudulent, criminal tax evasion penalties may be triggered.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign incorrect return annexes or knowingly approve inflated intercompany prices are jointly and severally liable for the additional assessments and fines. Macao courts may issue a declaration of civil responsibility and impose a personal prohibition from holding managerial positions in corporate entities registered in Macao for up to 2 years; failure to pay assessed amounts may lead to attachment of the director's personal bank account.
