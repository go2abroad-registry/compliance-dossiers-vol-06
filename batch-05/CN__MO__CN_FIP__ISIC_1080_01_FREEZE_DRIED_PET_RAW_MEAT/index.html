Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

RMB is fully convertible for current-account items: export receipts from freeze-dried pet treats, import payments for raw meat, freight, and platform fees may be settled freely at banks against authentic documentation, subject only to SAFE total-amount monitoring and the A/B/C enterprise classification. Capital-account items remain restricted: the Macao partner's capital contribution requires (a) a foreign-investment information report filed with SAMR at FIP establishment; (b) FDI foreign-exchange registration in SAFE's Capital Account Information System, executed by the settlement bank, producing the Foreign Exchange Business Registration Certificate (业务登记凭证); and (c) receipt into a dedicated FDI Capital Account (资本金账户) for FX, or an RMB capital account under the cross-border RMB FDI regime, with conversion into RMB strictly on a payment-based basis (支付结汇) into a settlement-pending sub-account tracked against the SAFE negative list. Profit and capital repatriation to Macao require audited financial statements, a partner distribution resolution, settlement of enterprise income tax on the pass-through allocation (including the PE-attribution analysis of the CEPA 5% preferential dividend withholding for qualifying Macao resident partners), and bank verification; no case-by-case SAFE approval is needed thereafter, but the remittance must be recorded in the SAFE system.

Export proceeds surrender rules

China has abolished mandatory surrender of export proceeds. The FIP may retain 100% of export receipts in FX in a goods-trade current-account FX account or convert them to RMB at its discretion. Statutory constraints: (i) repatriation - all export-related FX must be collected into a domestic account; offshore retention is not permitted except in approved bonded/overseas-warehouse e-commerce models; (ii) timing - receipts should be collected within the contractually agreed period, with a 90-day baseline from the export customs declaration date; collections beyond 90 days must be reported as delayed receipts in the SAFE Trade Monitoring System (货物贸易外汇监测系统); (iii) verification - the settlement bank matches each receipt against customs declarations under the total-amount verification algorithm (money flow, goods flow, document flow); persistent mismatches downgrade the FIP from A-class status and trigger per-transaction scrutiny; (iv) e-commerce export (customs codes 9610/9710/9810) proceeds may be collected through SAFE-registered payment institutions, which net-settle into the FIP's corporate account against the e-commerce customs declaration lists.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC, 中国人民银行) and its statutory foreign-exchange agency, the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE, 国家外汇管理局), are the sole FX and capital-control authorities over the onshore CN_FIP. SAFE exercises current-account total-amount monitoring, capital-account registration, and delegated-bank verification over the FDI capital account. On the Macao side, the Monetary Authority of Macao (AMCM, 澳門金融管理局) supervises the Macao-resident partner and the remitting banks (e.g., Bank of China (Macau) Limited, Banco Nacional Ultramarino) that execute the outward SWIFT transfer; AMCM licensing is a precondition for a clean inbound capital flow.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: MEDIUM for licensed payment-institution rails; LOW for an offshore digital EMI as the primary treasury bank. PRC law requires the FIP's RMB and FX accounts to be held at PBOC-licensed deposit-taking banks; an offshore EMI (e.g., Wise Business, Currenxie, or the non-China entity of Airwallex) cannot lawfully open a domestic settlement account for the FIP and cannot receive its export proceeds. The FIP may, however, use: (1) SAFE-registered cross-border payment institutions (e.g., LianLian Pay, PingPong, WorldFirst-China, Alipay) under the Payment Institution Foreign Exchange Business Measures (汇发〔2019〕13号) for collection of platform e-commerce proceeds in bulk settlement to the onshore bank account; and (2) the Macao parent's offshore EMI for non-China working-capital pooling. Routing operating funds through an unlicensed EMI creates a fatal gap in SAFE total-amount verification, risks B-class downgrade, and should be avoided.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No published statutory threshold; in Macao tax practice, the authority may require Local File and Master File for controlled transactions with an annual aggregate value above MOP 2,000,000.

Economic substance scope: Macao has not enacted formal Economic Substance Regulations; however, the tax authority applies general anti-avoidance principles and substance-over-form doctrine, requiring that core value-creating functions (freeze-drying, quality control, inventory risk) are performed in Macao for treaty access and tax residence certification.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Under the Macao Tax Infraction Regime, wilful evasion carries a fine of up to three times the tax evaded, plus imprisonment not exceeding five years.

Director personal liability: Directors may be jointly and severally liable for tax debts if they act in bad faith; travel bans and seizure of personal assets can be ordered by the competent court.
Transfer Pricing Misreporting

Statutory sanction: Adjustment of taxable income to arm's length and a penalty of up to 75% of the additional tax due, or a fixed fine between MOP 5,000 and MOP 100,000.

Director personal liability: Directors who approve false or incomplete transfer pricing documentation may be subject to restitution orders, disqualification from management, and civil liability for the partnership's tax losses.
Shell Company and AML

Statutory sanction: Under the Macao AML Law (Law 3/2022), failure to declare beneficial ownership carries fines up to MOP 500,000; shell companies may be dissolved by the Commercial Registrar.

Director personal liability: Directors and partners face personal exposure for fraudulent misrepresentation, including travel bans and up to three years' imprisonment; the corporate veil may be pierced to recover civil restitution.
