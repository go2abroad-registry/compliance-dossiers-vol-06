Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None. Under the Macao Commercial Code, a Foreign-Invested Partnership may be formed with immediate effect upon registration without statutory minimum paid-up capital; partners may contribute in cash, in kind, or by bank guarantee, and no escrow or blocked-account rule applies.

Funding & unlock path: Mandatory Fire Safety Technical Opinion from the Fire Services Bureau (Corpo de Bombeiros) under Decree-Law No. 24/95/M before starting operations, covering emergency exit capacity, fire load classification, extinguisher placement, and sprinkler requirements for plastic injection molding areas. Structural clearance from the Land, Public Works and Transport Bureau (DSOP) is required for mezzanines, heavy injection press foundations, or warehouse racking. Occupational Safety and Health registration and annual machinery inspection with the Labour Affairs Bureau (DSAL) are required under the Occupational Safety and Health Regulations, together with a personal protective equipment program and an emergency action plan.

Paid-in capital rule: None. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Finance Services Bureau of the Macao Special Administrative Region (DSF), operational electronic tax service portal at www.dsf.gov.mo; for the mainland China manufacturing and export component, the competent authority is the State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA), electronic filing platform etax.chinatax.gov.cn.

E-government filing portal

A foreign non-resident partnership or foreign-invested partnership must first complete the applicable commercial registration in the jurisdiction where it establishes its place of business. For a PRC-registered foreign-invested partnership, the local Administration for Market Regulation issues the Unified Social Credit Code, which functions as the taxpayer identification number for STA electronic tax filing; tax registration is completed automatically through the one-stop business-registration workflow. For a Macao SAR permanent establishment or taxable presence, the foreign entity must register at the Commercial and Beneficial Ownership Registration Bureau, submit an activity declaration to the Finance Services Bureau, and obtain a Macao Taxpayer Registration Number. Macao does not issue a separate VAT identification number because it does not operate a VAT, GST or general sales tax system; the same taxpayer number is used for Complementary Tax, Consumption Tax and Stamp Tax obligations.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible; the foreign-invested partnership is fiscally transparent under both PRC Corporate Income Tax and Macao Complementary Tax where applicable, with partners assessed on their distributive shares and the partnership entity required to file information returns and, where necessary, withhold on behalf of non-resident partners.

Available election forms

None; neither the PRC nor Macao SAR provides a US-style check-the-box entity classification election or explicit hybrid-mismatch election. Statutory pass-through treatment is the default, and any cross-border mismatch arising from dissimilar treatment of the partnership must be documented in the partnership agreement and transfer-pricing documentation.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Macao SAR has no broad-based VAT, GST or sales and use tax; indirect taxation is limited to the selective Consumption Tax on specified goods. Where the FIP exports goods from mainland China, the PRC VAT system applies and qualifying export sales are subject to the zero rate.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable in Macao SAR because there is no general indirect-tax registration threshold; entities only require Consumption Tax registration when engaged in the licensed import, manufacture or sale of designated excisable goods. For the mainland China PE, mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration is triggered when annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000; entities below that threshold are small-scale taxpayers and may elect general taxpayer status.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Macao SAR itself has no export VAT zero-rating mechanism because it has no VAT. However, for goods physically exported from mainland China by the foreign-invested partnership, PRC VAT zero-rating is available when the goods leave the PRC customs territory to a destination outside mainland China, including Macao SAR; the exporter is properly VAT-registered; the export is evidenced by an electronic Customs export declaration with an actual release date; and export proceeds are repatriated through a licensed bank and reported in the State Administration of Foreign Exchange monitoring system. The zero-rating is applied as exemption and refund of input VAT, not merely exemption without credit.

  • PRC Customs Export Declaration issued through the China International Trade Single Window, including the customs release record and export item data
  • Ocean Bill of Lading or Air Waybill, as applicable, evidencing that the goods physically departed the PRC customs territory
  • Commercial invoice and complete packing list signed by the foreign-invested partnership
  • Export sales contract or purchase order identifying the overseas buyer and the Macao or other foreign destination
  • Bank-issued foreign exchange receipts declaration and settlement record under SAFE monitoring; China does not issue an Indian-style FIRC, so the statutory FX repatriation evidence is the bank receipt declaration and payment-credit record
  • Input VAT special invoices, customs VAT special payment vouchers and the export tax refund/exemption return filed with the STA
  • Electronic invoice records maintained in the STA full-digitalized electronic invoice system and any required export manifest or terminal release data where a customs agent is used

Not applicable in Macao SAR; DSF does not operate a real-time commercial-invoice fiscal clearance or anti-fraud digital stamping mandate. In the PRC, the STA requires use of the national electronic invoice system, including the full-digitalized electronic invoice pilots, which transmit invoice data in real time to the STA taxpayer account; export zero-rating and refund claims are further matched electronically with Customs export declarations, bank receivables data and the e-invoice records before approval.

Export zero-rating claims are subject to audit by the General Administration of Customs and the STA, including post-clearance checks. If the Customs export declaration, physical-exit proof, invoice or foreign-exchange receipt is false, incomplete or not substantiated within the statutory period, the STA will reverse the zero-rating, require the export to be treated as a domestic taxable sale, recover the VAT refund, impose late-payment surcharge, and apply a penalty of one to five times the fraudulently obtained refund under the Law on Tax Collection and Administration. Serious cases are referred for criminal prosecution under the PRC Criminal Law on fraudulent export-rebate offenses, and any false documentation used in Macao commercial or consumption-tax filings may separately trigger Macao fiscal and administrative penalties.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Macao SAR operates under the 'One Country, Two Systems' framework and maintains an independent currency, the Macanese Pataca (MOP), pegged to the Hong Kong Dollar (HKD) within a band of 1 HKD = 1.03 MOP. The Macao SAR has no sovereign debt and maintains a fully convertible current account, as trade in goods and services is unrestricted. Capital account transactions are generally free on both inward and outward flows for FDIs and FIPs, subject to KYC-compliant routing via the AMCM-authorized banks. For a China-based Foreign-Invested Partnership (FIP) manufacturing high-precision injection-molded action figure kits, the critical convertibility point is that the mainland China parent or investor engaging outbound FDI into Macao must first obtain SAFE registration and complete outbound FDI filing under Circular 3 of 2018. Once the capital is lawfully subscribed in Macao, profit remittance and liquidation proceeds are freely convertible to HKD or USD; nevertheless, remittance back to mainland China in RMB triggers strict onshore controls and requires the mainland receiving bank to file RCPMIS reports and verify the underlying FDI or trade documentation. A significant practical hurdle is that mainland Chinese banks will not accept a direct MOP remittance; conversion must be done via HKD or USD corridors, and any RMB remittance from Macao to mainland China requires an onshore purpose code and supporting evidence. Thus, the FIP should hold operational liquidity in HKD and USD, with MOP only for local statutory obligations, to avoid structural conversion friction in settlement rails for anime figure trade with US/EU retailers.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Macao SAR imposes zero mandatory surrender requirements on export proceeds; there is no stipulated timeline or conversion ratio under AMCM rules, and exporters may retain FX balances in HKD, MOP, USD, CNY, EUR, or any permitted currency. For exports of ISIC 3240 goods that ultimately route through mainland China as the physical origin, the mainland exporter (if any) must comply with SAFE's 90-day settlement window for export proceeds under current account verification; if the Macao FIP is the invoice-issuing principal, no mandatory conversion applies, however the FIP must file accurate trade declarations with the Macao Customs and AMCM for the purpose of bank debiting and crediting.

Central bank supervision: Monetary Authority of Macao (AMCM) and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

PayPal Fit: High

There is no viable path for a pure fintech EMI to serve as the primary bank for this FIP. The Monetary Authority of Macao (AMCM) does not issue EMI-style money service operator licenses to non-resident fintechs; only a limited number of authorized payment institutions may provide electronic money transfer in MOP/HKD, and those are domestic scheme-specific (e.g., MPay) and barred from cross-border corporate treasury. As such, digital EMI providers (e.g., Airwallex, Payoneer, Wise Business) are not permitted to open merchant or operational accounts domiciled in Macao; their available accounts are either Hong Kong or mainland China legal entities, not Macao FIPs. A limited workaround is that the FIP may use an offshore EMI to collect PayPal proceeds only if the beneficial owner's Hong Kong holding entity is separate, but that would break the FIP treasury trail and should be rejected under corporate governance. Therefore, the FIP must maintain its primary operational account with a Tier-1 Macao commercial bank, and may use a foreign EMI only as a disbursement sub-rail from HKD/USD funding after amending the account-opening contract to reflect the Macao FIP as the contractual principal.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: There is no viable path for a pure fintech EMI to serve as the primary bank for this FIP. The Monetary Authority of Macao (AMCM) does not issue EMI-style money service operator licenses to non-resident fintechs; only a limited number of authorized payment institutions may provide electronic money transfer in MOP/HKD, and those are domestic scheme-specific (e.g., MPay) and barred from cross-border corporate treasury. As such, digital EMI providers (e.g., Airwallex, Payoneer, Wise Business) are not permitted to open merchant or operational accounts domiciled in Macao; their available accounts are either Hong Kong or mainland China legal entities, not Macao FIPs. A limited workaround is that the FIP may use an offshore EMI to collect PayPal proceeds only if the beneficial owner's Hong Kong holding entity is separate, but that would break the FIP treasury trail and should be rejected under corporate governance. Therefore, the FIP must maintain its primary operational account with a Tier-1 Macao commercial bank, and may use a foreign EMI only as a disbursement sub-rail from HKD/USD funding after amending the account-opening contract to reflect the Macao FIP as the contractual principal.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: MOP 5,000,000 in annual related-party turnover used by the DSF as an administrative review marker; Macao law does not specify a formal Local File or Master File threshold, but the FIP should hold a full transfer pricing file whenever intercompany purchases from the China parent exceed MOP 1,000,000.

Economic substance scope: The Macao offshore and tax-benefit regime under Decree-Law 58/99/M and DSF anti-abuse guidance requires real economic substance and Core Income-Generating Activities (CIGA) in Macao. A Foreign-Invested Partnership that manufactures color snap-fit plastic mecha kits must show actual production personnel, local stockholding, raw-material purchasing decisions, mold maintenance, invoicing and board or partnership decisions in Macao. A purely administrative shell with mailbox presence and decision-making in China will be treated as controlled from China, denied the pass-through and abatement benefits, and reassessed at the 12% maximum rate.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion by transfer pricing manipulation

Statutory sanction: DSF invocation of the arm's length principle under the General Tax Procedure and adjustment of the FIP taxable income; penalty of up to three times the tax evaded, a fine of MOP 10,000 to MOP 500,000 under Decree-Law 37/99/M, and default interest.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false M/1 returns are joint and severally liable for the evaded tax and penalties; the DSF may register them on the tax debt blacklist, impose a travel ban, and the Public Prosecutions Office may prosecute under Article 8 of Decree-Law 37/99/M, carrying imprisonment up to 3 years and asset restitution.
Shell company and sham economic substance

Statutory sanction: Denial of tax benefits under Decree-Law 58/99/M and anti-abuse law; reclassification of the FIP as a Macao-resident entity with the entire actionable profit taxed at 12%; fine of MOP 20,000 to MOP 200,000 for failure to satisfy economic substance, plus reassessed tax and three-year back interest.

Director personal liability: Piercing the corporate veil: directors are personally liable for the whole underpaid tax and interest, are disqualified from acting as directors of any Macao company, face registration in the DSF irregular-taxpayers list, travel restrictions, and imprisonment up to 3 years for serious tax fraud where the shell structure was intentionally used.
Anti-money laundering and cross-border payment concealment

Statutory sanction: Violation of Law 2/2006 on money laundering and the Macao Anti-Money Laundering regulations, with fines of MOP 20,000 to MOP 500,000 for each offense, seizure of the underlying proceeds, and criminal liability for money laundering with imprisonment up to 8 years in aggravated cross-border cases.

Director personal liability: Directors face personal asset confiscation and civil restitution of the concealed proceeds, freezing and seizure orders on personal bank accounts, a travel ban and Interpol alert on departure from Macao, disqualification from managing any licensed commercial undertaking, and imprisonment for money laundering or tax fraud, the penalties potentially doubling for continuing offenses.
