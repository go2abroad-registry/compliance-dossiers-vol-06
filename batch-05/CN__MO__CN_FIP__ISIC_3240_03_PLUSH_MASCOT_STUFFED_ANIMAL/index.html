Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None. No statutory minimum paid-up capital is imposed on a foreign-invested limited partnership in Macao SAR. Partners may specify any capital amount in the deed. No blocked escrow account or prior bank deposit certificate is required; subscribed cash contributions may be paid directly into the partnership capital account at a licensed Macao bank within the period set out in the capital subscription schedule.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Macao Special Administrative Region Financial Services Bureau (Direcção dos Serviços de Finanças - DSF); online portal: DSF Tax e-Services (https://www.dsf.gov.mo). Customs/indirect trade oversight: Macao Customs Service (Serviços de Alfândega de Macau).

E-government filing portal

For a Foreign-Invested Partnership constituted in Macao, first execute the partnership deed before a Macao notary and register the partnership with the Macao Commercial and Movable Property Registry (Conservatória do Registo Comercial e de Bens Móveis). After registration, apply to the DSF for taxpayer registration using the DSF taxpayer registration procedure, either in person or through the official Tax e-Services portal, and obtain the Macao Taxpayer Number (Número de Contribuinte) and Taxpayer Card. Macao does not issue a separate VAT identification number because no VAT/GST regime exists. A foreign non-resident entity acting only as a partner of the FIP does not require a Macao taxpayer number solely by reason of that partnership interest unless it has Macao-source taxable income or a statutory withholding/stamp duty obligation; in such cases the DSF may require the appointment of a local tax representative. If the FIP will directly engage in cross-border goods movement, it may also need to register with the Macao Customs Service for external trade declaration purposes, which is a customs registration rather than a tax registration.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible - the FIP is treated as fiscally transparent for Macao Complementary Tax purposes, with partners taxed on their allocable share of profits; Macao territorial scope applies, meaning only Macao-source profits are taxable.

Available election forms

None. Macao has no check-the-box election, no elective entity classification, and no OECD hybrid mismatch election for partnerships; pass-through treatment is mandatory and derives from Macao's Commercial Code and tax legislation.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/Sales and Use Tax; Macao indirect taxation consists of Stamp Duty, Tourist Tax, Consumption Tax and Motor Vehicle Tax.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable (MOP); no VAT/GST registration threshold exists. Taxpayer registration is required upon commencement of activity, while stamp duty and other indirect tax liabilities are triggered by the underlying transaction or document.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable. Macao is a free port and has no VAT/GST regime; therefore no statutory criteria exist for claiming a 0% export VAT rate, and no VAT refund procedure attaches to exports of plush mascot stuffed animals or offshore digital services.

  • Macao Customs Service external trade declaration (electronic export/re-export declaration, where applicable under the External Trade Regime)
  • Commercial invoice describing the plush mascot stuffed animals and their FOB value
  • Packing list
  • Bill of lading or air waybill evidencing physical export from Macao
  • Overseas buyer purchase order or contract
  • Bank credit evidence (e.g., SWIFT MT103 or bank credit advice) evidencing inbound payment of export proceeds; FIRC is not applicable in Macao

No real-time fiscal clearance, no mandatory e-invoicing API, and no anti-fraud digital stamping regime exists for indirect taxes. Taxpayers must preserve normal commercial invoices and receipts to substantiate transactions for stamp duty and Complementary Tax purposes; DSF electronic filing through Tax e-Services is available for returns but does not constitute real-time tax clearance.

No export zero-rating or VAT clawback regime exists because Macao has no VAT/GST. However, false or inaccurate external trade declarations to the Macao Customs Service may trigger administrative fines and penalties under the External Trade Regime, and inaccurate books/records or tax evasion may trigger penalties under the DSF-administered tax laws, including criminal liability where fraud is proven.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Macao Special Administrative Region has a legally separate monetary system from mainland China. The Macao Pataca (MOP) is the domestic legal tender and is issued through an Exchange Fund mechanism administered by AMCM. MOP is freely convertible into HKD and other major currencies through AMCM-licensed banks; there is no foreign-exchange control law requiring approval for current-account or capital-account transactions, no surrender requirement for exporters, and no restriction on repatriation of profits, dividends, loan interest, or liquidation proceeds. In practice, a commercial bank may require evidential documents to understand the economic substance and AML profile of the remitter and beneficiary, but convertibility itself is not constrained. Banks may convert MOP to HKD, USD, EUR, GBP, or CNH at negotiated margins. The peg is maintained at approximately HKD 1 = MOP 1.03, with AMCM intervention to keep the band stable; therefore FX risk is concentrated in the HKD/USD linkage and in any CNH flows from mainland parties. Capital contributions from mainland China remain subject to PRC outbound-direct-investment and SAFE formalities before funds can leave the mainland; on the Macao side no additional exchange-control approval is imposed. Profit remittances from the FIP to non-resident partners are not subject to any Macao withholding or FX authorization, although the paying bank will screen transactions against sanctions and request proof of the underlying distribution resolution and tax position.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Macao has no statutory surrender rules and no mandatory conversion ratio. A Macao FIP may retain all export receipts in MOP, HKD, USD, or any convertible currency. If a mainland China affiliate performs the physical manufacturing and exports goods on behalf of the Macao principal, that mainland affiliate remains subject to SAFE export-proceeds repatriation and verification requirements under PRC currency regulations; those obligations do not fall on the Macao FIP but should be documented to avoid any cross-border circular-payment audit.

Central bank supervision: Monetary Authority of Macao (AMCM) — Autoridade Monetária de Macau, acting as the de facto central bank, foreign-exchange authority, and banking regulator of the Macao SAR

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low as a primary banking avenue; Medium only as an ancillary payment-bridge solution. Macao's Financial System Act (Decree-Law 32/93/M, as amended) restricts deposit-taking, payment processing, foreign-exchange dealing, and corporate account services to entities licensed by AMCM. Offshore digital EMIs such as Wise, Revolut, Airwallex, and similar platforms do not hold AMCM depository or payment-license privileges; they cannot issue MOP account numbers, provide MOP clearing, or serve as the FIP's regulated banking home. Their viable function is limited to receiving settlement in selected foreign jurisdictions and then sweeping funds through an AMCM-licensed Tier-1 bank in an approved end-to-end AML chain, but the entity must maintain a Macao domestic bank account for statutory capital, tax, customs, and operational liquidity.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory Local File / Master File thresholds are enacted in Macao; however, where annual turnover exceeds MOP 10,000,000 or related-party cross-border transactions exceed MOP 5,000,000, DSF expects contemporaneous documentation including segmented profit statements, OEM pricing sheets, and comparability analyses

Economic substance scope: Macao does not currently maintain BVI/Cayman-style Economic Substance Regulations; however, tax authorities apply the general anti-abuse rule and treaty-limitation rules that require genuine business management, local office, staff, and booking activities. A FIP manufacturing or distributing plush dolls must demonstrate real economic substance in Macao or the entity may be deemed tax transparent and its profits re-characterised as Chinese-source income.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion through False or Omitted M/1 Filing

Statutory sanction: Fine ranging from 10% to 100% of the evaded Complementary Tax, plus default interest; criminal conviction may impose imprisonment of up to 3 years and judicial liquidation of the FIP

Director personal liability: Directors and managing partners are personally liable for the evaded tax through piercing of the corporate veil; courts may order civil restitution, seizure of personal bank accounts, 3-year travel bans, and disqualification from partnership management
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Denial of treaty benefits, withdrawal of Macao-source income classification, reassessment of the entire profit as Chinese-source taxable income, plus an administrative penalty of up to 15% of reassessed tax and business registration cancellation

Director personal liability: Directors and partners may be held jointly and severally liable for the reassessed corporate taxes and penalties; in severe cases, a 5-year travel ban and prohibition on holding directorship positions in Macao are imposed
AML/CFT Red Flags in Payment Rails (SWIFT_TT and Platform_Pay)

Statutory sanction: Administrative fines of MOP 200,000 to MOP 500,000 per violation under Macao AML/CFT rules; freezing and confiscation of related assets; criminal money laundering penalties include imprisonment of up to 8 years for responsible persons

Director personal liability: Directors and senior officers face personal criminal liability, seizure of personal assets, travel bans of up to 5 years, and, upon conviction, custodial sentences applicable to money laundering and organized crime offenses
