Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

None. No minimum paid-up capital is required for partnerships under the Macao Commercial Code; however, the partnership deed must specify the capital contributions of each partner, which may be in cash, kind, or services, and must be fully subscribed at formation.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety Certificate (Certificado de Segurança contra Incêndios) issued by the Fire Services Bureau (Corpo de Bombeiros) after inspection of the premises, including verification of fire extinguishers, emergency exits, and proper storage of flammable materials. Occupational Safety and Health (OSH) clearance from the Labour Affairs Bureau (Direcção dos Serviços para os Assuntos Laborais – DSAL) is required, including a workplace safety audit covering ventilation, chemical handling, and personal protective equipment (PPE) for workers.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Financial Services Bureau (Direcção dos Serviços de Finanças – DSF) of the Macao Special Administrative Region; electronic lodgement and taxpayer services are accessible through the Macao SAR Government e-Pass (ePass) single sign-on and the DSF online tax services platform.

E-government filing portal

Procedure for a non-resident foreign-invested partnership (FIP) to obtain the Macao Taxpayer Identification Number (Número de Contribuinte): (1) Execute the partnership deed in the legally required form, using Portuguese or Chinese, and authenticate the identity and legal existence of all foreign partners; (2) Register the FIP with the Commercial and Movable Property Registry (Registo Comercial e de Bens Móveis) of Macao SAR to establish legal personality; (3) Submit to the Financial Services Bureau the statutory commencement of activity declaration (Declaração de Início de Actividade), together with the notarised partnership deed, registry certificate, partner identification documents, appointment of the local manager (gerente) and the Macao registered office address; (4) Where a foreign corporate partner is involved, provide a certificate of incorporation or registry extract from the home jurisdiction, notarised, legalised and translated into Portuguese or Chinese as required; (5) DSF will issue the taxpayer card and registration proof containing the Número de Contribuinte; there is no minimum capital requirement and no separate VAT ID is issued because Macao has no VAT/GST system; the same number is used for all tax relations with DSF and for customs and consumption tax purposes.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

None. Macao has no check-the-box election, no fiscal-transparency election and no hybrid-mismatch election for partnerships. Tax characterisation follows the Macao Commercial Code and the Complementary Tax Regulations and cannot be altered unilaterally by the taxpayer. Territoriality is inherent: only Macao-sourced profits are assessable to Complementary Tax, while non-Macao-source profits are excluded; general anti-abuse and anti-avoidance rules may apply if DSF recharacterises the arrangement.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Imposto de Consumo (Consumption Tax) – Macao SAR does not operate a VAT, GST, sales-and-use tax or digital-service tax regime.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No VAT/GST registration threshold exists. Under Consumption Tax, the taxable event is the importation or local production of enumerated goods (e.g., alcoholic beverages, tobacco, fuels, lubricants and motor vehicles); polyresin statues, garage kits and collectible figurines are not enumerated and therefore attract no consumption tax, no registration requirement and no turnover threshold. A general taxpayer registration (Número de Contribuinte) is nevertheless mandatory before commencing any business activity in Macao.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable: there is no VAT/GST in Macao and therefore no export zero-rating claim is available or required. Polyresin statue garage kits are outside the Consumption Tax schedule, so no indirect tax is chargeable on local sales or exports. If a future product line falls within the Consumption Tax schedule, export exemption may be claimed only where the goods are physically exported out of Macao and the exporter holds the corresponding customs export declaration evidencing departure; no VAT refund input-side mechanism exists because there is no input VAT.

  • Export Declaration (Declaração de Exportação) submitted to the Macao Customs Service (Autoridade Alfandegária de Macau) via the Macao Trade Single Window (Janela Única)
  • Commercial invoice issued in the name of the overseas buyer, showing the FIP’s Número de Contribuinte, product description, unit value, currency, Incoterms and country of origin
  • Packing list and weight/cube manifest
  • Bill of Lading (Conhecimento de Embarque) or Air Waybill (Guia Aérea) evidencing the export movement
  • Customs-system export clearance report or port/airport cargo movement record
  • Foreign payment settlement evidence from PSP/bank channels: Stripe/PayPal settlement reports, bank credit advice, SWIFT MT103 or a bank-issued foreign inward remittance certificate when requested for customs or anti-money laundering evidence

No real-time e-invoicing or fiscal-clearance mandate applies in Macao. There is no government API validation, SAF-T submission or anti-fraud digital stamping and no state-invoice system. Commercial invoices must comply with the Macao Commercial Code: sequential numbering, identifiable issuer and taxpayer number, date, description of operation, quantity and price, and any taxes. Electronic/PDF invoices are accepted. Customs declarations for goods subject to Consumption Tax are lodged electronically through the Macao Trade Single Window (Janela Única) with the Macao Customs Service (Autoridade Alfandegária de Macau).

Because Macao has no VAT, VAT zero-rating clawback is not applicable. However, any fraudulent or unsupported export exemption or Consumption Tax relief may be challenged by the Macao Customs Service and the Financial Services Bureau in audit. The consequences include disallowance of the exemption, assessment of the underpaid Consumption Tax, default interest from the original due date and fines under the tax and customs enforcement rules; deliberate falsification of export documents exposes the FIP and its managers to criminal customs and anti-fraud penalties. The 180-day export-proceeds control is applied as an internal documentation discipline; the FIP should retain PSP and bank settlement records for at least the statutory limitation period to evidence genuine payment from non-Macao buyers and to negate artificial export structures.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Macao Special Administrative Region operates as a separate customs and monetary territory within the People's Republic of China, and it maintains a fully free capital-account regime with no foreign exchange controls on current or capital transactions. The Macanese Pataca (MOP) is pegged to the Hong Kong Dollar within a band of +/-0.5%, which in turn is pegged to the US Dollar, thereby creating a stable and fully convertible currency environment for corporate treasurers. Convertibility for trade in goods and services is unrestricted, and profit remittances, dividend distributions, and capital repatriation may be executed without prior approval from the AMCM. However, because the entity is a Chinese Foreign-Invested Partnership (FIP) that may have cross-border flows with mainland China, the Chinese State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) does impose registration and verification requirements for capital injections into the FIP and for the settlement of goods exports. Under Macao law, there is no surrender requirement and no mandatory conversion of export proceeds, but banks still apply standard anti-money-laundering screening and the AMCM's stringent KYC obligations. Residual barriers to convertibility arise only from documentary deficiencies or from mainland China's capital-account controls when funds move between the FIP and Chinese counterparties. Overall, Macao offers a frictionless FX regime for treasury purposes, subject to robust licensing and AML oversight by the AMCM.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under Macao's Foreign Exchange Act and AMCM regulatory guidance, there is no statutory timeline or mandatory conversion ratio for export foreign currency receipts. Entities may retain 100% of export proceeds in any licensed currency, and the MOP/HKD peg means the only practical conversion requirements are those imposed by the operating bank's internal policies. For the Chinese-FIP component, mainland SAFE rules require export proceeds to be repatriated into a China-based foreign-exchange account and then, after verification of the trade transaction, may be either converted into RMB or retained in foreign currency; since 2019, SAFE has abolished mandatory conversion, but the exporter must file a receipt declaration and the FIP must reconcile its cross-border money trail with customs declarations and payment processor records.

Central bank supervision: Monetary Authority of Macao (Autoridade Monetária de Macau, AMCM)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Low. Macao's Banking Act and the AMCM's prudential licensing framework require any institution accepting deposits or providing payment services to be licensed as a bank or as a licensed financial institution; there is no separate electronic-money-institution (EMI) regime. Offshore digital EMIs such as Revolut, Wise, or Stripe Treasury are not permitted to open accounts or execute FIX/RTP clearing for Macao-domiciled entities. PayPal can be used as a payments collection rail for customer preorders, but it does not substitute for a domestic banking relationship for foreign exchange, liquidity management, or documentary trade instruments. Any attempt to route corporate treasury flows through an unlicensed EMI would expose the FIP to AMCM enforcement, reputation risk, and possible freezing of funds.
