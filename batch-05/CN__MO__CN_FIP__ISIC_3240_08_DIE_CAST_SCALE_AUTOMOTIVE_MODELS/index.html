Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None. The Macao SAR Commercial Code imposes no minimum paid-up capital for a foreign-invested partnership. Contributions may be cash or in kind as stated in the deed. Any cash contribution must be deposited into a blocked escrow account with an AMCM-licensed bank in the name of the partnership in formation, and disbursement is permitted only after registration or as expressly required by the partnership contract.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety Certificate from the Macao Fire Services Bureau certifying means of escape, emergency lighting, fire extinguishers, hydrant coverage, and safe storage of tampo inks and thinners. Structural load inspection certificate from the Land and Public Works Bureau (DSOP) confirming that the floor is rated for die-casting machine and zinc ingot storage loads. Occupational safety clearance from the Labour Affairs Bureau (STPS) covering machine guarding on die-casting presses, molten metal splash protection PPE, noise exposure within 85 dB(A) over an eight-hour shift, local exhaust ventilation in tampo printing, and chemical safety data sheets for all solvents and inks.

Paid-in capital rule: None. The Macao SAR Commercial Code imposes no minimum paid-up capital for a foreign-invested partnership. Contributions may be cash or in kind as stated in the deed. Any cash contribution must be deposited into a blocked escrow account with an AMCM-licensed bank in the name of the partnership in formation, and disbursement is permitted only after registration or as expressly required by the partnership contract.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China, with tax filings administered through the official Electronic Tax Bureau e-Tax portal operated by the State Taxation Administration; cross-border goods registration is coordinated with the General Administration of Customs and the China International Trade Single Window.

E-government filing portal

For a Foreign-Invested Partnership in the PRC: complete business registration with the State Administration for Market Regulation and obtain the partnership business license bearing the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code, which is automatically recognized as the taxpayer identification number for PRC tax purposes. If not automatically synchronized, register through the Electronic Tax Bureau within 30 days using the Unified Social Credit Code and open a permanent tax file at the competent tax office for the registered address. Submit the partnership agreement, foreign investor identification documents and tax residency certificate, capital contribution schedule, appointment letter for the financial responsible person, and proof of registered address. If exporting goods, separately register as an importer/exporter with the General Administration of Customs to obtain the 10-digit customs registration code. For trade settlement, complete the SAFE trade foreign exchange registration through the designated bank. A non-resident partner without a permanent establishment is not issued a separate PRC tax identification number merely because the partnership is tax-transparent; the partnership's Unified Social Credit Code is used for withholding and reporting, and the non-resident partner may rely on its own jurisdiction tax residency certificate, including the Macao SAR resident certificate, for benefits under the applicable double taxation arrangement.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

None. The PRC Enterprise Income Tax Law makes partnership taxation transparent as a mandatory statutory rule; no check-the-box election, hybrid mismatch election, or similar entity classification election is available. The Foreign-Invested Partnership is not an enterprise income tax taxpayer, while remaining a registered VAT and customs taxpayer.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

CNY 5,000,000 in annual taxable sales is the mandatory threshold for registration as a General VAT Taxpayer, but VAT registration is automatic and compulsory for a manufacturing Foreign-Invested Partnership upon issuance of the Unified Social Credit Code even below that threshold; the partnership must file VAT returns through the Electronic Tax Bureau.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export of zinc alloy die-cast scale model cars qualifies for the VAT zero rate if the Foreign-Invested Partnership is a VAT General Taxpayer holding a General Administration of Customs importer/exporter registration code, the goods are physically removed from mainland China customs territory and sold to a non-mainland customer, including a Macao SAR buyer, the customs export declaration is filed in the name of the partnership through the China International Trade Single Window, the sale is supported by a genuine export contract and commercial invoice, and export foreign exchange proceeds are received through designated banks and reported under SAFE trade foreign exchange monitoring. This zero rate operates as exemption with credit and refund for manufacturing general taxpayers: output VAT on exports is 0% and related input VAT may be credited against domestic output VAT, with any residual refundable through the State Taxation Administration export tax refund system. Export of goods by a small-scale taxpayer is exempt from VAT but does not generate input VAT refund. Cross-border services, if any, are zero-rated only when they fall within the statutory zero-rated services catalogue.

  • Customs Export Declaration Form filed electronically through the China International Trade Single Window showing the applicable HS code and actual shipment details
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List issued by the Foreign-Invested Partnership
  • Bill of Lading or air/sea waybill issued by the carrier and evidencing departure from mainland China customs territory
  • Export Contract or overseas purchase order in the name of the Foreign-Invested Partnership and the foreign buyer
  • Bank export collection record and SAFE trade foreign exchange receipt declaration evidencing repatriation of export proceeds

China operates the Golden Tax Phase IV real-time electronic invoice clearance system. VAT taxpayers must issue statutory special VAT invoices through the State Taxation Administration fully digitalized e-invoice platform or a certified third-party e-invoicing service interoperable with the tax authority; invoice data is uploaded in near real time and each invoice receives a tax control number and anti-counterfeiting QR code. For system-to-system issuance, taxpayers use the tax authority accredited e-invoice data interfaces through the digital tax account. No unregistered billing software may issue statutory VAT invoices. Export sales are substantiated by the customs export declaration and supporting logistics records; Customs does not add a separate digital fiscal stamp to the VAT invoice.

The State Taxation Administration and General Administration of Customs jointly audit export tax refund claims through cross-checked export declaration, logistics, foreign exchange receipt, and invoice data. If the supporting documentation is false, materially inconsistent, or missing, the VAT refund is retrospectively clawed back together with late payment interest and the taxpayer is liable under the Law on Tax Collection and Administration for a penalty of up to five times the amount of tax evaded. Fraudulent export tax refund conduct may additionally trigger customs penalty measures, suspension of export tax refund eligibility, SAFE foreign exchange penalties, and criminal liability under the Criminal Law for fraudulently obtaining export tax refunds, including imprisonment and criminal fines.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Macao SAR operates as a distinct customs territory with no generalised exchange-control mechanism. The Pataca (MOP) is fully convertible on current and capital account for residents and non-residents; the AMCM administers a linked exchange-rate system against the Hong Kong dollar, which in turn trades against the United States dollar. In practice, an FIP may hold MOP, HKD, USD, EUR and RMB in onshore Macao accounts, can freely convert balances at commercial bank dealing rates, and may distribute partnership profits without prior AMCM approval. The principal hurdles are not Macao-side but involve the mainland China link: RMB funds crossing the border must flow through the PBOC/SAFE cross-border channel on the basis of genuine invoices or investment filings; remittances from mainland partners require the remitting bank's SAFE-approved purpose code and supporting documentation. Macao banks will not help structure a transaction to evade mainland capital controls. Profit distribution to a mainland partner is processed on the usual SWIFT rail but must be reported for Macao withholding-tax purposes and, where applicable, under the double-taxation arrangement.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No surrender requirement exists in Macao SAR. AMCM law prescribes no percentage or bank purchase requirement; export receipts may be collected directly in MOP, HKD, USD, EUR or RMB, and retained indefinitely as long as the account's aggregate source-of-funds evidence satisfies internal AML monitoring. There is also no statutory timeline requiring repatriation to the Macao FIP, since Macao is not a capital-control jurisdiction. Where the export documents are issued from the mainland manufacturing plant, the mainland exporter remains within SAFE's current-account framework and must demonstrate via the bank's post-loan/import-export monitoring system that the export proceeds are repatriated to its designated foreign exchange account and matched to customs declarations; the mandatory conversion ratio is zero, and proceeds may remain in an approved current-account foreign-exchange account.

Central bank supervision: Monetary Authority of Macao (Autoridade Monetária de Macau / AMCM), in coordination with the People's Bank of China (PBOC) and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) for mainland-linked Renminbi flows.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

For capital coming from mainland China, the remitter must first complete the relevant SAFE direct-investment procedure (FDI or ODI, depending on the direction) and obtain a banking-registration reference code. The remitter then sends an MT103 from its designated bank to the FIP's Macao account using a purpose code matching capital injection or partnership contribution and identifies the beneficiary as the FIP with its Macao registration number. The receiving bank will credit only the dedicated capital escrow account, never the operating account. A local CPA verifies the funds against the partnership agreement and issues a capital verification report before the balance is unlocked for operational disbursement. For investment from non-mainland investors, no prior exchange-control approval is needed in Macao but the bank still requires a board/partner resolution, copies of the source bank statements, and the partnership's KYC pack.Clearing rails should be segregated by instrument: SWIFT MT103 for direct remittances to raw-material suppliers and third-party logistics providers; irrevocable commercial letters of credit for high-value zinc-alloy ingot purchases or tooling expenditures where the supplier requires bank settlement security; documentary collections (D/A or D/P) only for tested, non-sanctioned counterparties with an established payment history.
