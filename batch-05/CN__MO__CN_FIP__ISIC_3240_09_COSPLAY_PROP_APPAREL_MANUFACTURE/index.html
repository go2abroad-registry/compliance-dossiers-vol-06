Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None. A foreign-invested partnership formed under Macao law is not subject to any statutory minimum subscribed or paid-up capital, and no blocked escrow account requirement is imposed. The capital contributions are fixed by the partnership deed and may be made in cash or in kind. Unpaid contributions are enforceable as contractual obligations, and the managing partner may issue contribution calls without any pre-funding condition precedent to registration.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Bureau (Corpo de Bombeiros) fire safety certificate confirming means of egress, fire compartments, extinguishing equipment, emergency lighting, and safe storage of flammable materials; Civil Defense clearance for any chemical or resin inventory; Labour Affairs Bureau (DSAL) occupational safety and health clearance covering machine guarding, ventilation, noise control, handling of chemicals, personal protective equipment, and workplace training for all production staff.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Financial Services Bureau of the Macao Special Administrative Region (Direcção dos Serviços de Finanças, DSF), operating the official Macao tax e-services portal and Tax e-Declaration platform. Customs-related registration and import/export controls for Consumption Tax goods are administered by the Macao Customs Service (Direcção dos Serviços de Alfândega, DSA).

E-government filing portal

A foreign non-resident entity such as the CN_FIP must register with the Financial Services Bureau before carrying on any Macao-source revenue activity or, at the latest, immediately upon the first Macao-source income event. The entity must first confirm whether a permanent establishment or taxable presence is created under Macao territorial source rules. If a Macao presence exists, the foreign-invested partnership must execute and notarise the partnership deed, obtain an extract from the Macao Commercial Registry (Registo Comercial) when required, and appoint a resident fiscal representative if the partnership has no place of effective management in Macao. The DSF application is presented with the partnership formation instrument, identification documents for each partner, proof of registered address, and, where applicable, a document evidencing the appointment of the resident fiscal representative. After examination and data capture, the DSF issues the Número de Contribuinte (Taxpayer Identification Number) and the taxpayer registration card. If the entity will engage in import, export, or production of goods subject to the Consumption Tax (Imposto de Consumo), a separate prior registration or authorisation from the Macao Customs Service is required. Minimum capital is not required, but all foreign-language documents must be authenticated or legalised as required by Macao law and accompanied by Portuguese or Chinese translations where the authorities so require.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

None. Macao SAR has no check-the-box election and no hybrid mismatch election analogous to other common law tax systems. The CN_FIP's tax classification follows the legal form registered in the Macao Commercial Registry and the attribution rules of the Macao Complementary Tax (Imposto Complementar de Rendimentos). No separate entity-level or pass-through election is available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No general VAT/GST/Sales and Use Tax is imposed in Macao SAR. The relevant indirect tax framework consists of the selective Consumption Tax (Imposto de Consumo), Tourism Tax (Imposto de Turismo), and Stamp Duty (Imposto de Selo). For the manufacture and retail of cosplay prop apparel, there is no general turnover-based indirect tax charge.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable. Macao SAR does not operate a VAT or GST regime and therefore imposes no turnover-based indirect tax registration threshold. Producers and importers of goods listed under the Consumption Tax regime must obtain prior DSA authorisation; this requirement is product-specific and is not determined by a monetary turnover threshold. The Macao Pataca (MOP) is the local currency for any applicable taxes.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable in a VAT/GST zero-rating sense because Macao imposes no VAT or GST. For goods that fall within the Macao Consumption Tax regime, exportation from the Macao SAR is outside the charge to Consumption Tax if the goods are formally exported under a customs export declaration and are not released for domestic consumption in Macao. There is no VAT refund entitlement because no VAT has been charged. Qualifying offshore digital services are also outside the scope of Macao indirect taxation. The substantive eligibility criterion is physical exportation from Macao and, for Consumption Tax goods, full compliance with DSA customs procedures and export controls.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaração de Exportação) lodged with the Macao Customs Service (DSA)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical removal of the goods from Macao SAR
  • Commercial Invoice issued by the exporting foreign-invested partnership
  • Packing List
  • Bank SWIFT MT103 credit advice or bank inward remittance statement evidencing receipt of the export payment in the exporting entity's bank account; Macao does not issue India-style FIRC documents, so the SWIFT or bank credit record is the recognised substitute for foreign exchange repatriation substantiation
  • Export authorisation, movement permit, or other DSA clearance record required for any goods subject to Macao Consumption Tax or customs control

None. Macao SAR does not currently mandate a real-time electronic invoice clearance system, national digital tax stamping, or an anti-fraud e-invoicing API for domestic B2B or B2C transactions. Commercial invoices must be issued and retained in accordance with the Macao Commercial Code and Financial Services Bureau requirements. Cross-border trade declarations and related clearances are processed electronically through the Macao Customs Service (DSA), which maintains cargo and declaration records for audit and verification purposes.

Because Macao has no VAT/GST, there is no VAT zero-rating refund and therefore no VAT clawback mechanism. However, false or unverified declarations used to claim Consumption Tax export exemption or to avoid customs and stamp obligations are subject to retrospective assessment, monetary fines, seizure and confiscation of goods, suspension or cancellation of customs and tax registration, and criminal liability for smuggling or tax fraud under Macao Customs Law and the Macao Penal Code. If Consumption Tax goods originally declared as exports are subsequently diverted into Macao domestic consumption, the unpaid Consumption Tax is immediately due together with applicable penalties. The 180-day foreign exchange repatriation benchmark is relevant to banking documentary compliance and export payment matching; failure may create payment/cargo flagging but does not constitute a Macao VAT clawback because no Macao VAT exists.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Macao SAR operates a liberalised foreign exchange regime with no exchange controls and no prior approval requirement for cross-border current or capital transfers. The Macanese Pataca (MOP) is freely convertible within Macao and is pegged to the Hong Kong Dollar (HKD) at approximately MOP 1.032 to HKD 1.00; foreign currency deposits and payments in MOP, HKD, USD and CNY are permitted through AMCM-licensed banks. For mainland China, the RMB is fully convertible on current account transactions, including trade in theatrical costumes, prop apparel and synthetic wigs, but capital account flows such as inbound partnership capital injections, offshore borrowing and outbound direct investment remain subject to SAFE registration, real-trade verification and anti-circumvention review. Profit remittance and partnership distributions to foreign partners are permitted after the enterprise has paid tax and submitted contract, invoice and customs documentation to the declaration bank, but the bank will reject any payment where the trade is not evidenced. The Macao FIP may hold and deploy multiple currencies without surrender, while its mainland counterparty must repatriate export earnings, retain them in a bank-designated FX account, convert only through SAFE-qualified banks, and report each transaction through SAFE's monitoring system. There is no fixed mandatory conversion ratio under current mainland rules.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Macao SAR imposes no statutory surrender or conversion ratio. Exporters may retain all foreign currency proceeds in their AMCM-licensed bank accounts and use them freely for operating settlement. In mainland China, SAFE abolished mandatory surrender, but export proceeds must be repatriated into a domestic foreign exchange account through the banking system within the contractual payment period, normally 90 days from the export date and no later than 180 days, and must be reported using the Trade Monitoring System. The exporter may choose to keep the proceeds in foreign exchange or convert them to RMB at the prevailing market rate; no mandatory conversion percentage applies.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC), State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) for mainland China, and Monetary Authority of Macao (AMCM) for the Macao SAR

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion and fraudulent omission of revenue

Statutory sanction: Under Decree-Law 8/96/M, the Macao tax offence regime, tax fraud is punishable by a fine of up to three times the tax evaded and by imprisonment up to 5 years if committed with fraudulent intent. The DSF may also impose a separate administrative fine of up to MOP 500,000.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil may be pierced where the partnership is found to be a sham or where directors acted with intent to defraud. Managing partners and directors are jointly and severally liable for unpaid tax, penalties, and interest, and the Macao authorities may impose a preventive asset attachment, travel ban, blacklisting, and disqualification from holding office in any Macao company.
Shell company / lack of economic substance

Statutory sanction: Under Decree-Law 58/99/M and the Macao offshore regime, an offshore license may be revoked and a fine of up to MOP 200,000 imposed. The DSF can reclassify profits as fully taxable Macao-source income and add the unpaid complementary tax.

Director personal liability: Directors and managing partners who knowingly use a shell arrangement face personal civil liability for the tax underpayment, restitution of diverted assets, a travel ban pending settlement, and potential criminal liability for tax fraud with imprisonment up to 5 years.
Transfer pricing mispricing and thin capitalization

Statutory sanction: Under Macao transfer pricing rules (Decree-Law 24/2021), the tax authority may make a transfer pricing adjustment, disallow excessive deductions, and impose additional assessment with a penalty of up to MOP 200,000. Intentional mispricing may be prosecuted as tax fraud under Decree-Law 8/96/M.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign or approve false intercompany contracts, prepare fictitious transfer pricing documentation, or knowingly cause thin capitalization may be held personally liable for the adjusted tax and penalties. They can be subjected to a cross-border travel ban, asset seizure, disqualification from serving as directors, and imprisonment for up to 5 years in serious cases.
