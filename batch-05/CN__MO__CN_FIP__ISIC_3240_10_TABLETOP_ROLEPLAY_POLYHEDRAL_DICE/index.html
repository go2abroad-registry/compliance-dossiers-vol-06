Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-up capital is imposed on a sociedade em comandita simples under the Macao Commercial Code; the partners freely set registered capital in the partnership deed. There is no blocked escrow account requirement. However, limited partners' liability is capped at their subscribed contribution, and the registered capital must be adequate for the intended industrial dice casting establishment. In-kind contributions must be independently appraised and fully transferred before registration evidence is accepted.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety Certification from the Macao Fire Services Bureau (CB), evidencing compliant means of escape, emergency lighting, fire extinguishers, flammable liquid storage in an approved cabinet, isolation of ignition sources, and hot-work controls. A structural inspection certificate by a Macao registered engineer is required for production floor loads, storage racks, mezzanines, and equipment anchoring. Occupational clearance includes compliance with Decree-Law No. 33/99/M on industrial occupational safety, machine guarding for saws, drills, sanders, and mold presses, resin-ventilation adequacy, and mandatory personal protective equipment for eye, hand, and respiratory protection during manual sharp-edge trimming.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA), acting through the nationwide Electronic Tax Bureau online tax portal maintained by each provincial-level tax office.

E-government filing portal

Upon formation, the Foreign-Invested Partnership (FIP) receives from the Administration for Market Regulation a business license containing the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which automatically constitutes the national Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) and VAT registration number. The FIP must then complete tax information confirmation and financial and accounting registration with the competent local tax office through the Electronic Tax Bureau within 30 days of license issuance. Foreign non-resident partners do not need a separate Chinese TIN merely by owning an interest in the FIP; any foreign partner that itself carries on a taxable activity in China must apply to the STA-in-charge for a Non-Resident Enterprise Tax Identification Number. A Macao-resident partner's separate Macao tax registration with the Macao Financial Services Bureau is a parallel foreign tax obligation and does not replace the Chinese TIN.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

None - China does not maintain a check-the-box election or hybrid mismatch classification for partnerships. The FIP is statutorily transparent for Enterprise Income Tax purposes; profits are taxed at the partner level. For VAT, the FIP may voluntarily elect general taxpayer status even when annual taxable sales do not exceed the RMB 5,000,000 general registration threshold.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No economic turnover threshold triggers initial VAT registration; every business entity must register for VAT upon formation. Mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration is triggered when annual taxable sales excluding VAT exceed RMB 5,000,000. Voluntarily obtaining general taxpayer status below this threshold is permitted.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Supplies of finished RPG dice manufactured in China qualify for 0% VAT only if they are physically exported from the PRC customs territory to an overseas destination, are accompanied by a valid customs export declaration, and the export sale proceeds are received and settled through a domestic bank under SAFE trade foreign-exchange monitoring. The FIP must be a general VAT taxpayer to claim export zero-rating; goods must be ordinary goods not listed as prohibited or restricted for export. Cross-border e-commerce channel sales qualify when the customs clearance is declared under the applicable e-commerce export customs supervision code.

  • Customs Export Declaration form accepted by PRC Customs, bearing the official customs declaration number and release stamp
  • Ocean Bill of Lading or Air Waybill showing the FIP as shipper and an overseas consignee, proving physical exportation
  • Commercial export invoice issued by the FIP to the foreign buyer showing the goods description, quantity, and pricing
  • Packing list and export sales contract or purchase order that match the customs declaration values and specifications
  • Bank inward remittance receipt or SAFE foreign exchange receipt declaration, commonly called the Inward Remittance Certificate, showing the foreign buyer's remittance received and settled by a designated bank

The FIP must issue VAT invoices through the STA's national electronic invoice platform using a cryptographic digital certificate and unique anti-counterfeit QR code; invoice data is transmitted to the tax authority in real time. Taxpayers using ERP billing systems must integrate through STA-approved electronic-invoice APIs or tax-control software. There is no discretionary pre-approval of individual invoices, but the tax authority may suspend invoice issuance or impose real-time blocking for high-risk taxpayers.

The export zero-rating claim is subject to tax-office and customs audit. If it is shown that goods were not actually exported, the customs declaration is false, the foreign exchange was not repatriated within 180 days, or the documents do not form a coherent chain, the tax authority will claw back the rebated VAT, require payment of the headline 13% VAT with a daily late-payment surcharge, and impose a penalty under Article 63 of the Tax Collection Administration Law. Fraudulent export refund behavior may be referred for criminal prosecution under Article 204 of the Criminal Law, carrying fines, imprisonment, and possible confiscation of assets.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Macao operates as a separate customs and monetary territory under the One Country Two Systems principle. The AMCM maintains a linked exchange rate system under which the Macao pataca (MOP) is pegged to the Hong Kong dollar (HKD) at approximately MOP 1.03 = HKD 1.00; because HKD in turn is pegged to USD, MOP/USD convertibility is effectively stable. Macao does not impose exchange controls or surrender requirements on current or capital account transactions. Residents and non-residents may freely convert MOP into HKD, USD, CNH and other major currencies through licensed banks. There is no prior AMCM approval for remittance of share capital, loans, trade settlement or profit distributions by an FIP to non-resident partners, provided the remittance is supported by normal banking documentation. The practical hurdles are not exchange control but documentary: the bank must verify the FIP's legal existence, the underlying trade or investment, the beneficial owners' identity and source of funds, and the satisfaction of Macao tax clearance on any distributable profits. Capital account movements are captured in Macao's balance-of-payments statistics and may require direct investment declarations to AMCM for statistical and reporting purposes, but these are not licensing controls. Thus convertibility is unrestricted, with liquidity dependent on interbank markets.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export proceeds surrender or mandatory conversion ratio exists under Macao law. Exporters, including the FIP, may retain export receipts in the original invoiced foreign currency, hold them in MOP, HKD, USD or CNH accounts, and convert them freely through AMCM-licensed banks. The only statutory requirements are to maintain underlying commercial documents such as sales contracts, invoices, packing lists and customs declarations for AML and banking compliance review, and to report balance-of-payments trade data when requested by AMCM or the Macao Statistics and Census Service.

Central bank supervision: Monetary Authority of Macao (Autoridade Monetária de Macau - AMCM)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low as a primary banking rail; Medium only as an ancillary collection gateway. Macao does not recognise foreign electronic money institution (EMI) licences by passport. Holding client funds, executing payment transactions and issuing electronic money are regulated financial activities under the Macao Financial System Act, and any entity performing these functions must be licensed by AMCM as a credit institution or payment institution. Offshore EMIs such as Wise, Airwallex, Payoneer or Revolut Business cannot independently open a local MOP/KYD account for the FIP or clear payments through Macao's domestic systems. They may be used only as technical payment facilitators for global crowdfunding and B2C card collection, with subsequent settlement to an AMCM-licensed bank. The FIP must therefore select a tier-one domestic bank as the treasury anchor and use any fintech EMI strictly as a collection aggregator, never as the sole custodian of operating capital.

Non-resident partners inject capital via SWIFT MT103 from their home banking relationship into the FIP's Capital Escrow Account at an AMCM-licensed bank. For Mainland China source funds, the remitter must complete PRC SAFE outbound direct investment filing and use the SAFE-designated outward direct investment purpose code; the receiving bank in Macao will then perform an investment remittance compliance check against the CRCM registration, partnership deed and AMCM reporting thresholds. The bank must issue an inward remittance advice, and a Macao certified public accountant must prepare a capital verification statement documenting each partner's paid-in contribution in MOP-equivalent. Capital cannot be credited to an operational account before full AML/KYC onboarding is cleared; if funds arrive before account opening, they must be held in suspense or returned to source.Settlement of supplier invoices for epoxy resins, molds, packaging and freight should use SWIFT MT103 for open-account trade, Commercial Letters of Credit (MT700) for large or consignment imports, and Documentary Collections (MT400/MT410) for trusted distributor relationships. All outbound payment instructions must reference the underlying commercial contract, invoice, packing list and customs declaration. For Mainland China suppliers, the FIP may settle in CNH through Industrial and Commercial Bank of China (Macau) Limited or another CNH-clearing licensed bank, with MOP, HKD or USD conversion executed only when the trade documentation supports the currency. Payments originating from the operational account after a Kickstarter or PayPal payout must match the name and invoice of the trade counterparty; no anonymous third-party disbursements are permitted.Deliverable FX forwards on MOP/USD, MOP/HKD and USD/HKD up to 12 months or matching the tenor of the underlying trade receivable or payable; no speculative open forward positions are permitted.Offshore renminbi (CNH) non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are permissible only to hedge CNY/CNH exposure arising from Mainland China supplier invoices or future trade settlements, provided the NDF is backed by a documented commercial contract and executed through an AMCM-licensed bank authorised for CNH markets.Cross-currency swaps between MOP/USD, USD/CNH or HKD/CNH are permitted on a fully matched principal and interest basis for capital account obligations such as partner loans or trade financing, with underlying asset-liability matching and standard ISDA documentation.Plain vanilla FX options (purchased call or put) on CNH/USD or CNH/MOP are permitted with upfront premium payment only; exotic, barrier, knock-in, knock-out and leveraged structures are disallowed for this low-risk non-financial corporate.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: Low as a primary banking rail; Medium only as an ancillary collection gateway. Macao does not recognise foreign electronic money institution (EMI) licences by passport. Holding client funds, executing payment transactions and issuing electronic money are regulated financial activities under the Macao Financial System Act, and any entity performing these functions must be licensed by AMCM as a credit institution or payment institution. Offshore EMIs such as Wise, Airwallex, Payoneer or Revolut Business cannot independently open a local MOP/KYD account for the FIP or clear payments through Macao's domestic systems. They may be used only as technical payment facilitators for global crowdfunding and B2C card collection, with subsequent settlement to an AMCM-licensed bank. The FIP must therefore select a tier-one domestic bank as the treasury anchor and use any fintech EMI strictly as a collection aggregator, never as the sole custodian of operating capital.
