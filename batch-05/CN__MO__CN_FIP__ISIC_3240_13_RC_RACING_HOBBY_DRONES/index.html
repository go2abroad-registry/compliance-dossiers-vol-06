Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None. The Commercial Code of Macao SAR does not impose a statutory minimum paid-up capital on a Foreign-Invested Partnership. Partners must subscribe and pay the contributions fixed in the partnership contract. For cash contributions, a bank capital deposit certificate or notarial capital payment certificate is required before or immediately after filing; for in-kind contributions, a notarial evaluation report is required. No blocked escrow account is mandated by statute unless the partnership agreement itself requires an escrowed reserve.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Financial Services Bureau (Direcção dos Serviços de Finanças - DSF) of the Macao Special Administrative Region; online electronic portal: DSF e-Services and the Macao one-stop company/tax registration counter at https://www.dsf.gov.mo.

E-government filing portal

A foreign non-resident entity establishing or participating in a Macao Foreign-Invested Partnership (FIP), structured as a Macao commercial partnership under the Macao Commercial Code, must first register the partnership at the Macao Commercial and Movable Property Registry (Conservatória dos Registos Comercial e de Bens Móveis). Tax registration with the DSF is triggered by the commencement of business: (1) file the standard DSF commencement declaration (Declaração de Início de Actividade / M/1) with the partnership deed, registry certificate, identification of all partners, and proof of the Macao business address; (2) obtain the Taxpayer Identification Number (NIF - Número de Identificação Fiscal) for the FIP; (3) non-resident partners without a Macao address must appoint a Macao resident representative or tax agent for service of process and DSF correspondence; (4) where export activity is anticipated, register the business activity as industrial/commercial with the DSF and maintain the relevant customs/import-export records; and (5) retain the DSF tax registration certificate and all supporting records. The NIF is used for Complementary Tax, Stamp Duty and all DSF interactions; no separate VAT ID is issued because Macao does not operate a VAT/GST system.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

None. Macao law does not provide check-the-box elections or hybrid mismatch elections. VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None - Macao SAR operates no value-added tax (VAT), goods and services tax (GST), or general sales-and-use tax. Limited indirect fiscal duties consist of Stamp Duty, Tourism Tax for specific service sectors, and customs import duties/consumption taxes on a small list of controlled goods.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not_Applicable - Macao does not impose a VAT/GST/SUT registration threshold. Any person or partnership carrying on industrial or commercial activity in Macao must register for Complementary Tax through the DSF commencement declaration irrespective of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable in a VAT sense: because Macao has no VAT/GST, exports are neither standard-rated nor zero-rated, and no input VAT refund claim can arise. For direct tax purposes, FPV racing drone export revenue may be classified as non-Macao-source and therefore outside Complementary Tax where the profit is demonstrably generated through offshore trade, contracts, risks, financing and shipping rather than through a Macao business establishment. The exporter must prove physical movement of the goods out of Macao's customs territory and preserve the underlying economic substance of each export transaction.

  • Macao Customs export declaration (Declaração de Exportação) filed by the exporter and processed by the Macao Customs Service for the FPV drone shipment
  • Outbound transport document: ocean Bill of Lading, air waybill or combined transport document evidencing departure from Macao and delivery to the overseas consignee
  • Commercial documentation: commercial invoice, packing list, purchase order and sales contract describing the carbon-fiber milling and brushless motor FPV racing drone products, quantities, prices and Incoterms
  • Bank settlement and foreign exchange repatriation evidence: Macao bank inward remittance credit advice or Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC-equivalent) showing receipt of export proceeds from the overseas buyer within the normal settlement cycle

Macao currently imposes no mandatory real-time fiscal invoice clearance system, tax-authority API approval, or anti-fraud digital stamping on commercial invoices. Taxpayers may issue paper or electronic invoices, provided the invoices comply with the Macao Commercial Code, identify the supplier and transaction, and are retained for DSF audit. Export supporting documents such as customs declarations, transport documents and banking settlement evidence must be kept separately and reconciled to the goods exported.

Because Macao does not operate a VAT export zero-rating refund mechanism, no VAT clawback can arise. However, if the DSF audits the FIP and finds the export/non-Macao-source documentation incomplete, inconsistent or contradicted by shipping/banking records, the tax authorities may re-characterise the profits as Macao-source and assess Complementary Tax, default interest and tax-fraud penalties under the Macao tax offences regime. Fraudulent or false customs export declarations also expose the FIP and responsible partners to Macao Customs penalties, forfeiture or seizure of goods, loss of customs facilitation treatment, and criminal liability for tax and customs fraud. The FIP must preserve all export records and exchange settlement evidence for the full statutory limitation period.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Macao SAR operates a currency board system under which the Macao Pataca is freely convertible into Hong Kong dollars and U.S. dollars, with open current and capital account convertibility inside Macao. There is no Macao surrender requirement for export proceeds, no foreign exchange rationing, and no restriction on profit repatriation within the Macao banking system. However, because this structure is a Chinese Foreign-Invested Partnership with production, export, and operational cash flows routed through Mainland China, the effective convertibility ceiling is governed by SAFE. In Mainland China, current account items such as trade settlement and ordinary service payments are convertible against genuine underlying documentation, while capital account items such as cross-border equity injection, partner loans, dividends, and derivative transactions require SAFE registration, specified foreign-exchange purpose codes, and utilization within the approved partnership business scope. Profit remittances from the onshore FIP to Macao partners must be supported by audited financial statements, tax clearance certificates, and SAFE remittance forms; any distribution exceeding accumulated audited profits may be reclassified as unauthorized capital outflow. Macao's free convertibility therefore cannot be used to bypass Mainland Chinese exchange controls, and all funds crossing the boundary must be settled through a SAFE-designated bank with a valid trade or investment background.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Mainland SAFE rules require export proceeds from FPV drone sales to be repatriated through designated foreign-exchange banks within 180 days after customs clearance. There is no general mandatory percentage conversion ratio under current regulations; the exporter may voluntarily sell foreign exchange to the bank or retain up to 100% of lawful receipts in an approved foreign-exchange settlement account. Macao SAR, by contrast, imposes no export surrender obligation, and the FIP may hold U.S. dollars, HKD, MOP, or RMB freely inside Macao. Any Mainland export proceeds must be matched with customs declarations and invoices in SAFE's trade monitoring system before onward conversion or transfer to the Macao treasury account.

Central bank supervision: Monetary Authority of Macao (AMCM) as the financial regulator and de facto central bank for Macao SAR; the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Bank of China retains jurisdiction over all cross-border RMB capital flows between Macao and Mainland China.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Low; not legally viable as primary banking infrastructure. Macao's Financial System Act requires any deposit-taking or payment-clearing entity to be licensed as a credit institution by AMCM. AMCM does not issue a general Electronic Money Institution license equivalent to the EMD regime in Europe, so offshore fintech EMIs such as Wise, Revolut, or similar platforms cannot open local MOP accounts, hold client funds, or provide corporate trade clearing in Macao. PayPal and Stripe are permissible only as third-party online retail payment aggregators for the FPV drone e-commerce storefront; their settlement proceeds must be swept daily into a licensed Tier-1 commercial bank account and cannot serve as a treasury clearing rail.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound capital contributions from Macao partners or offshore parent entities must be remitted by SWIFT MT103 to the FIP's designated SAFE-bank account in Mainland China. The remittance instruction must carry the statutory foreign-exchange purpose code for direct investment capital injection, as recorded in the SAFE Capital Account Information System, and the receiving bank will verify the authenticity of the underlying partnership agreement and business license. HKD or USD payments are converted into RMB at the bank's market rate, with the bank issuing a foreign-exchange settlement certificate for each contribution. No capital injection may be made through personal accounts or via fintech EMI rails. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: MOP 2,000,000 aggregate annual related-party transactions in goods, services, intangibles or financing require a contemporaneous Local File; MOP 5,000,000 triggers a Master File; financing instruments require the additional minimum-statutory return for intercompany interest where the principal exceeds MOP 3,000,000.

Economic substance scope: Macao has no standalone ESR statute, but Article 21 of the Complementary Tax Regulation applies an arm's length and economic-substance principle. The FIP must prove local management, physical premises, local business decisions and CIGA for the FPV drone sales activity; mainland Chinese partners are also subject to CIGA recharacterization under China Announcement [2018] No. 9 and Chinese CFC rules for an offshore pass-through with no real Macao substance.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Revenue Omission

Statutory sanction: Article 54 of the Macao Complementary Tax Regulations and Article 137 of the Macao Penal Code apply; a surcharge of three times the evaded tax is imposed together with a fine of MOP 200,000 to MOP 500,000 and seizure of the suppressed revenue.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced: managing directors and general partners must restore the evaded tax from personal assets, are placed under a DSF travel ban and judiciary blacklisting, and face imprisonment for two to eight years where the intentional omission exceeds MOP 150,000.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation of Intercompany Royalty and Interest Accounts

Statutory sanction: Articles 55 and 58 of the Complementary Tax Regulations permit recharacterization at arm's length, disallowance of the excess deduction, a 50% surcharge on the denied payment and an administrative penalty of up to MOP 100,000 per infringement.

Director personal liability: General partners are jointly and severally liable for the disgorgement of the artificial deductions; directors who signed the false TP benchmark have their personal bank accounts frozen and are blacklisted from leaving Macao, and those convicted of fraudulent misrepresentation face one to three years of criminal imprisonment.
Shell Company Abuse of Pass-Through Status and Deficient Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: The DSF may disregard the partnership's pass-through election, deem the activity to have a permanent establishment in Macao, tax the entire rescinded profit at the 12% Complementary Tax rate and issue prior-year corrected assessments under Articles 21 and 62 of the Complementary Tax Regulations; the Chinese CFC regime also includes the undistributed profit in the Chinese partner's taxable income.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is fully pierced: directors are required to make civil restitution of the unpaid tax from personal assets, are prohibited from leaving Macao until settlement, are blacklisted by the financial authorities, and face imprisonment for one to five years for abuse of legal personality and fraudulent tax avoidance.
